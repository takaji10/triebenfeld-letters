## Provenance

The source is the town and commune of Zagórów's own presentation of itself,
on the municipality's site **zagorow.pl** (www.zagorow.pl), a digital
flip-book in the style of the Codrops "Flips" layout: its text stands in
boxes on four turning pages, dragged over like leaves. The page is at
`/z/index.html?page=2` — the `?page=` is the viewer's, not the text's: the
one HTML carries the whole flip-book, cover to back. The original address
no longer serves the flip-book (the site was rebuilt); the Wayback Machine
kept it, 24 captures between 16 March 2016 and 14 August 2022, and the
editor's address is the capture of **14 June 2022** (19:35:16 UTC). The
page is anonymous and undated; the capture is the only date the source
carries, and the slug carries it, after the rule of the web items of the
newspapers (`twojaslupca20150706`): `zagorowpl20220614`.

The site's own metadata names the page "Historia miasta Zagórów" (the
`description` of the HTML head). The h1 reads "Miasto i gmina Zagórów"; the
cover story "Zagórów — historia, położenie, atrakcje, kultura".

## How it was read

No OCR, no spot sheet: the text is the page's own HTML, fetched from the
Wayback Machine and taken as printed, **nothing tidied** — the transcription
rule (EDITORIAL_RULES.md) as the newspapers keep it for their web item. The
text was taken from the HTML with the tags read off, the paragraphs as the
page renders them (the HTML's own line breaks inside a paragraph render as
spaces and are not carried); the boxes stand in the order of the HTML as
captured, each under its own heading, the flip-book's page titles above
them. The fourth content page's own title row is empty in the HTML and no
title stands above its boxes.

**Not kept, and recorded here:** the flip-book viewer's own furniture — the
two instruction lines at the top ("Przeciągnięcie otwiera koleją stronę"
[Dragging opens the next page], "Kliknięcie rozwija treść." [Clicking
expands the content]), the cover's flip label ("< Strona"), and the
"Strona główna" (Home page) links that stand on every content page's title
row. They are the viewer's, not the town's text, as the portal's reader
comments were not part of the TwojaSłupca article (its notes.md).

**The photographs are not carried** (the editor's ruling above). The page's
boxes each carry a photograph in the layout; most are illustration (the
church, the market square, the park), and three carry content: the
photograph of the document of 1240 in the box "Położenie", captioned by the
page's own line "Fragment tekstu dokumentu z 30.IX.1240 roku.", the coat of
arms in its two boxes, and the flag. Their places are marked "[Obraz]"
(English "[Picture]"), the convention of the literature's figures. The
caption of the 1240 photograph stands where the page puts it, after the
paragraphs on the town's plan; the transcription places "[Obraz]" directly
before the caption it belongs to, and this note records the placing.

**The page contradicts itself, and both readings stand:**

- **1262 or 1261.** The prose of the box "Położenie" dates the confirmation
  naming Zagorowo to **10 XII 1262**; the caption under the quoted fragment
  dates it to **10.XII.1261** drawn up in Sechów. The edition's Places page
  records "1261: Zagorowo" from the Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski (the
  editor, 2026-10-16); where the page and the edition's documents differ,
  the documents stand (the documents-first rule), and the page keeps both
  its dates as printed.
- **1869 or 1870.** The box "Lokalizacja miasta" has the Russian
  authorities depriving Zagórów of its town rights in **1870**; the box
  "Historia" has the post-uprising repressions doing it in **1869**. The
  literature of the Congress Kingdom dates the measure to 1869 (the ukaz
  of 1 June 1869, in force 1870); the page's own two dates are both kept as
  printed, and which the editor wants recorded where is open
  (NEEDS_CONFIRMATION, "The places").
- The paragraph of the box "Historia" on the coat of arms and the town's
  history is printed **twice**, word for word: in the box "Historia" and
  again under "Godła Zagórowa" on the last content page. Both stand.

The Polish quotation marks of the page are kept as they print — the
straight `"` of the two quoted document fragments and of "BirdLife
International", and the page's „..." in "Orlik", "bycia w mieście",
"Zagórowskie tango", "Quarta".

## What it bears on

- **The town's own voice about the town.** The page is the municipality's
  presentation, written for its inhabitants and visitors; its history
  summarises what the literature records, without naming any of it, and its
  pages on nature and development are the commune's account of itself in
  its own day. Nothing here is judged; the edition keeps to its documents
  where they differ (the documents-first rule).
- **The medieval fragments match the edition's own documents' story.** The
  fragment quoted names "Zagorowo" among Marcinkowo, Jaroszyno, Gorzyza,
  Skokum and Oleszyno — the form the edition's Places page records from the
  KDW for 1240 and 1261 ("1240: Zagorovo", "1261: Zagorowo"). The KDW
  holding's entries run 1173 to 1440; whether the king's location act of
  1407, which the page gives whole, is among the entries the editor chose
  is not asserted here.
- **The Olędrzy.** The box "Historia" names "potomkowie olędrów wyznania
  ewangelickiego osiedlonych w Puszczy Pyzdrskiej w XVIII w." among the
  town's interwar population — the settlers the edition's documents follow
  on the Trąbczyn estate.
- **The places the page names that the edition knows:** Ląd (the abbey,
  whose copy-book of privileges is APP 53/1455/0/2/21), Pyzdry, Konin,
  Lądek, Ujście, Grodziec (the forest district), Rzgów (the narrow-gauge
  railway's intended course), Drzewce, Wrąbczyn, Imielno, Kościołków,
  Kleczew, Golina, Witaszyce, Myszaków. Whether the index of people and
  places should run over the sources of the places section, as it runs over
  the newspapers, is open (NEEDS_CONFIRMATION, "The places").
- **The narrow-gauge railway through Rzgów** (1947-1991, the plans to
  extend it to Konin) and **the Nadwarciański Landscape Park** touch the
  edition's ground only at the edges; recorded, not followed up.
