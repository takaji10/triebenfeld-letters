## How it was read

OCR be installed for the project, and it was: Tesseract 5.5.3 with the Polish
models, wrapped by `pipeline/ocr.py`). The text was read as the agreement of
five passes over the whole clipping:

1. Tesseract 5.5.3, `pol` from tessdata_best, the page upscaled ×3, psm 6;
2. the same, ×4;
3. the same, psm 4, ×4;
4. Tesseract with the standard `pol` model, ×4;
5. the Windows.Media.Ocr engine (pl), ×3 — the roughest, used only as a third

## What the clipping is, and what was left out

- The dateline "Z Zagórowa" is cut at its top by the clipping; it reads whole.
- A word-sized fragment (the passes read "są") stands between the first note
  and the second lead, where no text belongs: a sliver of a neighbouring
  column. Left out at the editor's word.
- A small stray mark stands between the lines after "gotówki" (the passes read
  a comma below the line); it belongs to neither line and is not transcribed.
- The printer's fragments "Pd" (above the dateline) and "PDZ" (the foot):
  slivers of neighbouring text. Left out at the editor's word.
- "osób." carries a period mid-sentence; both Tesseract passes read it and the
  spot sheet's crop showed it. Kept as printed.
- After "gotówki" and after "wiek" the passes read no close: the comma of
  "wiek," is what the word boxes carry. Nothing was added.
- "(mat)" closes all three notes. Read as the paper's siglum for its own
  material (materyały własne) — a reading of convention, not of the page; the
  English leaves it as printed.

## What it bears on

Zagórów and Konin are places of the edition; Oleśnica is the village on the
road from Zagórów to Konin (there was a gmina Oleśnica, seat at Oleśnica, in
the Konin district). The deputy mayor Franciszek Łukaszewski, the widow
Wiznerowa and the heirs of Ignacy Ciszewski are not in the edition's people.
The item is set in the era `twentieth-century` (its working name). No links to
documents yet: nothing in the item names the estate or its people; what it
gives the edition is Zagórów in 1937, the town the twentieth-century holdings
circle. Whether the index of people and places should run over the newspapers
is open (NEWSPAPERS_PLAN).

## Rights

The item is anonymous press of 1937. Under the Polish copyright act an
anonymous work is protected 70 years from its first publication, so the term
ended in 2007: public domain. That is Claude's reading of the law, recorded
here and in NEEDS_CONFIRMATION for the editor to confirm; the Licences page
names open licences only and was not touched (the Mielewczyk precedent).
