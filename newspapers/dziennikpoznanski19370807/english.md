From Zagórów

Recruitment of the unemployed for Germany

During the last recruitment of seasonal agricultural workers for Germany,
about sixty people were sent off from the town of Zagórów, more than one of
whom has already sent his family a little of his hard-earned cash. Through
the workers' departure the municipal budget has been relieved, (mat)

The moving of the old people's home

The old people's home, so far housed in a building rented from Mrs Wiznerowa
in Zagórów, as its rooms did not answer, has been moved by the Municipal
Board to a property belonging to the heirs of Ignacy Ciszewski, in the
village and commune of Oleśnica, some 300 metres from the home as it has
stood. The property, now let, is in a brick building by the highway leading
from Zagórów through Oleśnica to Konin, in which, besides the home, nobody
else will live, and the old folk will have the fresh air and the full freedom
of the adjoining garden, which will in addition tell well on their aged
years. The supervision of the home has been entrusted to the deputy mayor of
the Municipal Board at Zagórów, Mr Franciszek Łukaszewski, (mat)

From the town's management

The Board of the town of Zagórów, wishing the town to have a comely
appearance, has set about building pavements; the work has been begun, and
along these pavements trees will be planted, adorning the town, (mat)
