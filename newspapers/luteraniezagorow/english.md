Zagórów, the filial of the Evangelical-Augsburg Parish in Konin

The more important events in the life of the Parish in Zagórów

In the sixteenth century the Reformation movement was conducted by the monks of Ląd.

[Picture]

In the years 1720 - 1817 evangelicals arrived from Silesia, Pomerania and Prussia. They founded villages, raised chapels, and were given pastoral care by the incumbent of the evangelical parish at Września.

In the years 1817 - 1856 the care was exercised by the incumbents of the parishes
of Grodziec, Konin and Żychlin.
In Zagórów a filial of the parish of Grodziec was founded.

[Picture]

1856 - on 24 December the erecting of the Parish
in Zagórów.

1857 - the Parish bought the plot for the building of the Church.

1878 - on 8 May the laying of the foundation stone for the Church.

1882 - on the first Sunday in Advent, the consecration of the Church.

1913 - the building of the cantor's house.

1938 - on 16 October the consecration of the rectory.

1954 - on 18 July the first service after the Second World War. It was celebrated by the Rev. Karol Świtalski.

1995 - on 16 June the consecration of the former cantor's house as the Evangelical Senior Citizens' Home, performed by
His Excellency the Rev. Bishop Michał Warczyński.

2008 - on 17 August a new electronic clock and electronic bells.

2009 - on 30 November the Evangelical Senior Citizens' Home ended its activity (read more).
