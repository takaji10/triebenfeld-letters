## What it bears on

Zagórów is the estate's place. Słupca is the powiat's seat — not a place
of the edition (NEEDS_CONFIRMATION, "The newspapers", the Lidmanowski
note). The item names **nobody**: not the commission's chairman, not the
starosta, not one of the arrested — no person of the edition and none for
/people/. The index of people and places runs over the items (the editor,
2026-10-22): the item's hits are Zagórów's forms ("Zagórowie" the
headline's and "Zagórowa"), ruled in `newspapers/index.yml` as the 1937
item's forms were. The item is set in the era `twentieth-century` (its
working name).

What it bears on beyond itself: the recruitment of seasonal workers for
Germany, which the "Z Zagórowa" column of Dziennik Poznański of
7 August 1937 records as routine — the sending off of some sixty
unemployed of the town, the cash their families waited for. This item is
the same trade six years earlier and its violence: the stoning of the
recruiting commission's premises when the quota ran out, the starosta's
police, the second storming, the arrests. The two items stand either side
of the same practice, both from the blog's post of 2 October 2024. The
navy-blue uniforms (granatowe mundury) are the state police the period
called the granatowa policja.
