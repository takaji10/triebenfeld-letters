# Kuryer Polski, nr 75 (25 December 1754): how the item came in

The report on the funeral of Paweł Prusimski at Trąbczyn, 5 December 1754,
in the Warsaw newspaper Kuryer Polski, no. 75 of 1754 (25 December), from
the copy in the Kórnik Library of the Polish Academy of Sciences (PAN
Biblioteka Kórnicka, shelfmark Cz2271), published in the Wielkopolska
Digital Library (https://www.wbc.poznan.pl/dlibra/publication/85857/edition/99614/content).

## Two lives: holding, then item of the newspapers

- **Added 2026-10-16 as a holding** (`units/kuryerpolski1754075/`): the
  first holding from print and from a library, the editor's word then being
  units "that might not necessarily come from archives - think
  articles/announcements from newspapers". Read from the editor's crop of
  the left page of the second opening (the page that carries the report),
  translated by the editor, summarised and claim-checked in session,
  published at their word the same day. The full record of how it was made
  is in the git history of `units/kuryerpolski1754075/` (the intake
  `holding.py` carried every step; the spot sheet "The faint word in the
  Kuryer Polski" settled its one doubtful word, "[Cleri?]", kept with its
  doubt).
- **Moved 2026-10-22 into the newspapers** at the editor's word ("Move
  Kuryer Polski"), when the section was made for Dziennik Poznański (1937).
  The editor's ruling: newspaper articles belong in neither the archival
  sources nor the literature. The text, the English, the summaries and the
  page image come over unchanged in their words; what changed is where the
  item stands and what it is counted in — it is no longer one of the
  edition's documents (the edition counts one document and one holding
  less), its page is /newspapers/kuryerpolski1754075/ (a redirect stands at
  the old /documents/ addresses), and the timeline's event of 1754 and the
  story's link point at it there.

## The text

Line by line as printed, the printer's letters kept: the long s (ſ), the
ligature ß in "Kalißa" (the italic ſz), æ in "Sæcularis", accented á; "¬"
at a word broken at the line's end. Five words the line-break pass had
split wrongly ("Pru¬ſińſkich", "Gło¬wiecki") were ruled as joins by hand.
One mark of doubt: "[Cleri?]" before "Sæcularis", faint print, kept by the
editor on the spot sheet (2026-10-16). "Łędzki" is Ląd, the Cistercian
abbey (the editor); Kaźmierz is probably Kazimierz Biskupi, near Konin —
said on the record, not confirmed, and not in the place index. The report
fills the lower half of the left column of the page and three lines of the
right; the rest of the page carries other news, which is not transcribed.

the Kórnik Library's, published in the WBC. Shown as the newspapers show
their items, with the link to the WBC's copy.

## What it bears on

The funeral of the man who held Trąbczyn before the Starost: the clergy
present, the sermon, the oration of his nephew Antoni, the exequies his
widow Katarzyna had said at Pyzdry, Konin and Kaźmierz. The inspection of
Trąbczyn made for Katarzyna in July 1754 (Konin Gr.136) already calls
Paweł not long dead; the visitation of 1791 (ADWł 85) says she enlarged
the parish church. The timeline's event of 1754 rests on this item, as do
the story's words on the funeral.

## Why it is here

Kept from the work's account, which said it on the page until the voice review of 2026-10-29 (the editor: the reasoning for why a work is relevant is for the notes, not the reader):

> Paweł Prusimski held Trąbczyn before his nephew Antoni. Each issue gathers
> short reports from the towns of the Commonwealth and abroad. Printed Polish
> of 1754 in roman type, with italic for the dateline and for Latin words, the
> long s (ſ) and accented á; only this report of the page is transcribed.
