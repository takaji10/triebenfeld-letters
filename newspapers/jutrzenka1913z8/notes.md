## The attribution: open

The piece speaks of Dr. Lidmanowski **in the third person**. Its second
paragraph: "In the March issue of Zdrowie, Dr. Lidmanowski draws attention
to various deficiencies of today's folk attire" — the March 1913 issue of
the Hygiene Society's journal, where his article on the folk attire stood
under this same title. The piece presents itself as the article's matter
carried to the educational fortnightly's readers.

- The file's name attributes all three texts to Lidmanowski; this one the

## The journal

The print's own masthead (visible in the digitised issue) styles
Jutrzenka a "Dwutygodnik popularno-naukowy" — a fortnightly of popular
science, printed in Łódź; the library's metadata styles it "dwutygodnik
ogólno-kształcący, ilustrowany". Both stand recorded: the citation uses
the library's form, the account the print's own.

## The English: corrections of the session (2026-10-22)

Three corrections, each with the Polish that decided it:

- "It costs several dozen rubles" → "It costs some fifteen rubles" —
  "Kosztuje on kilkanaście rubli": kilkanaście is a dozen and a half, not
  several dozen (kilkadziesiąt would be).
- "woolen sailor suit" → "a woolen jacket suit" — "ubranie marynarkowe
  wełniane": the marynarka is the jacket suit, not a sailor's outfit (the
  "American's" attire, the paragraph of the returnee from America).
- "without heel straps" → "without heel counters" — "bez napiętków": the
  napiętek is the shoe's stiff heel part, the counter (the clogs,
  "trepek ... z drewnianymi podeszwami, bez napiętków").

## What it bears on

- **The era.** `twentieth-century`, like its fellows: the attire of the
  countryside of 1913.
- **"Abroad" is the Prussian border.** The piece's "za granicę" — the
  workers who go out, the suspenders and starched collars and the
  padded overcoats "bought abroad for cheap money" — is the same going
  abroad the housing and nutrition texts describe: the summer work in
  Prussia that text 3 names ("in summer go to work mainly to Prussia").
  A reader of the edition's documents, which also speak of workers
  abroad, is reading the same geography. The America of the piece is
  another thing: real returnees, described as a type.
- **The author.** Dr. Lidmanowski's person entry (`lidmanowski`) carries
  the piece in its account; the index hit "dr. Lidmanowski" is ruled to
  him (newspapers/index.yml).
- **The underlying article** — Lidmanowski on the folk attire, Zdrowie,
  March 1913 (R. XXIX, z. 3) — is not in the edition. It could be
  brought in the same way if the editor wants it.

## Rights

The underlying article is Dr. Lidmanowski's, and he died 6 January 1943,
so Polish copyright (life + 70 years) lapsed at the end of 2013; an
anonymous text of 1913 is out of copyright since 1984 (70 years from
publication). Either way the piece is in the public domain. Shown whole
(the editor's word, 2026-10-22).
