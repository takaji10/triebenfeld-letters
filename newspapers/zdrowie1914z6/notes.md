## The dating: 1914, not 1912-1913

The file's name says 1912-1913, and the other two texts of the file are of
1912 and 1913; this one is of **1914**:

- Polona's metadata for the linked issue: "1914 R. XXX, z. 6" — the
  journal's thirtieth year, zeszyt 6.
- The issue's table of contents (visible in the Crispa copy of the same
  issue, object 701206) lists "Lidmanowski K. Dr. z Zagórowa. Jak mieszka
  lud nasz? . . 432" — the page where this text begins.
- Modern scholarship cites it the same way: A. Boldyrew's study of social
  pathology in the Kingdom of Poland, 1864-1914, cites "K. Lidmanowski,
  Jak mieszka lud nasz, „Zdrowie" 1914, nr 6, s. 432-440".

## The English: corrections of the session (2026-10-22)

The English came with the editor's file (made by Claude) and was read
against the Polish original; four corrections, each with the Polish that
decided it:

- "The hallway only from the courtyard leads on one side to the kitchen,
  on the other to a storage room." → "The hallway is entered only from
  the courtyard; from it one side leads to the kitchen, the other to a
  storeroom." — the Polish: "Sień tylko od podwórza prowadzi z jednej
  strony do kuchni z drugiej do schowanka" (the hall of the wealthy
  farmer's house is entered from the courtyard; the front half of it
  gives the two rooms).
- "they pierce straw mats through such a damp wall" → "they pin straw
  mats against such a damp wall" — "na ścianę tak wilgotną przebijają
  słomianki" (the manor servant's defence against the wet wall).
- "At the room in 8 dwellings I found a pantry" → "Beside the room, in 8
  dwellings, I found a pantry" — "Przy izbie w 8 mieszkaniach znalazłem
  komorę" (przy izbie = beside the room).
- "Education and the development of skillful cooperation can do much
  here" → "Education and the development of sound cooperatives can do
  much here" — "Oświata i rozwój umiejętnej kooperatywy": the kooperatywa
  is the cooperative (the consumers' and credit societies of the time),
  not a manner of working together.

The duplicated sentence of the nutrition text (below) is that text's; this
one has none.

## What it bears on

- **The era.** `twentieth-century`, the era of the holdings from 1900 on:
  the article is of 1914, and its subject is the people the era's
  ownership line ends with — the village after the manor's world had
  been wound up.
- **The edition's ground, observed.** The article does not name the
  district surveyed. The doctor's study of nutrition in the same journal
  two years earlier (Zdrowie 1912, beside it in the Newspapers) names the
  Słupca powiat — the edition's own — and extends its figures to the
  whole Prussian border strip; the two articles are one work of
  observation, and the account says so without overclaiming (the housing
  article itself names no place).
- **The author.** Dr. Konstanty Lidmanowski (30 March 1865, Słupca - 6
  January 1943, Zagórów), physician: settled in Zagórów in 1893,
  practised there to his death; founded the town's volunteer fire brigade
  (1899), loan-and-savings society (1901) and Polish-language school
  (1906), kept its hospital from 1910 (the Polish Wikipedia's article,
  with S. Kumosa, *Lekarze medycyny i stomatologii pracujący w
  lecznictwie zagórowskim*, Zagórów 1992). The issue's table of contents
  styles him "Dr. z Zagórowa". He stands on /people/ as `lidmanowski`
  (the Głowiecki shape: an entry, no documents; the dates rest on
  Wikipedia — NEEDS_CONFIRMATION).
- **What no other source of the edition records**: the housing of the
  people the edition's documents move among — the one-room cottage of 91
  per cent of the farmers, the damp walls, the well by the manure pile,
  the farmhand's room. The documents name land, court rights and money;
  this is how the same people housed.
