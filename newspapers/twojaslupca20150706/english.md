Trąbczyn celebrated 775 years

6 July 2015

[Picture]

Trąbczyn is 775 years old this year. The jubilee was celebrated yesterday.
There was a little history, thanks and honours, and plenty of good fun.

The history of Trąbczyn is very rich. The place of Trąbczyn — or rather
Trampczin — is named for the first time in a medieval document of the year
1240. It concerns the grant by the Archbishop of Gniezno of tithes from
several villages, Trąbczyn among them, to the Cistercian monastery at Ląd.
The first documented owner of the village, from the grant of Duke Bolesław
the Pious, was Janek, son of Szymon. After him come the further heirs: the
count of Ląd, Mikołaj, the count Jan, and Przedpełk. To the end of the
seventeenth century the estates of Trąbczyn were in the hands of the
Trąbczyński family of the coat of arms Dryja. Later the owners changed
several times more. Many of the heirs of Trąbczyn and their descendants took
an active part in the wars of national liberation. Among them were, among
others, Antoni Prusimski, Stanisław Count Miączyński, and Aleksander and
Włodzimierz Miączyński. To honour their memory and to bring the history of
the little homeland near to the young generation, the celebrations of the
775th anniversary of the village's foundation were held yesterday in
Trąbczyn.

The celebrations were opened by a Mass in the parish church of St Stanislaus,
Bishop and Martyr. After it the memorial tablet and the obelisk were
unveiled. The further part of the jubilee took place on the ground by the
School-and-Gymnasium Complex named for the European Union in Trąbczyn. To
begin, all the guests were welcomed and the honours "Zasłużony dla wsi
Trąbczyn" — "Meritorious for the village of Trąbczyn" — were presented. They
were received by: Wiesław Radniecki Andrzej Kin [the print runs the two names
together], Marcin Urycki, Jan Nowak, Andrzej Przygoński, Robert Kaczmarek,
Stanisław Grajewski, Maria Urbaniak, Elżbieta Bączkiewicz, Ryszard Duda,
Paweł Kin, Irena Kubacka, Sławomir Swędrowski, Joanna Kicińska, Gabriel
Kubiak, Renata Kałużna, Kamila Witkiewicz, Marzena kazimierska [as printed],
Alicja Szymkowiak, Lech Wojciechowski, Krystyna Wojciechowska, Maciej
Urbaniak, Tadeusz Osowiec, Irena Zielińska, Tadeusz Krotoszyński, Juliusz
Fagasiński, Grzegorz Kucharczyk, Roman Kaczmarek, Stanisława Musielska,
Jadwiga Bartczak, Wanda Matecka, Maria Antkowiak, Zofia Wojtyra, Ryszard
Musiałowski, Anna Siuba, Marianna Nowaczyk, Marianna Graczyk, Krystyna
Kozioł, Stanisław Przydryga, Artur Paprzycki, Andrzej Drop, Adrian
Krajewski, Janusz Lewicz, Jadwiga Grajewska, Leon Parus, Anna Siuba [named
above], Józef Wolicki and Józef Kozioł. Posthumously the title "Zasłużony
dla wsi Trąbczyn" was also conferred on Krystyna Reszkiewicz. All the
invited guests were also given commemorative statuettes.

On the occasion of the jubilee the exhibition "775 lat Trąbczyna" —
"775 years of Trąbczyn" — describing the history of the village was also
officially opened. Local young people presented themselves on the stage;
many interesting games and competitions were prepared for the children; and
the star of the evening was the band Skaner. The day closed with a dance.
