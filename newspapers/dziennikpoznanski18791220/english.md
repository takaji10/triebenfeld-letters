Włodzimierz, Count Miączyński,
proprietor
of the estates of Trąbczyn
in the Kingdom of Poland,
died after long sufferings, fortified with the holy sacraments, on the
18th of December of the current year at Trąbczyn.
The dispatch of the body will take place on Monday the 22nd of the
current month, the funeral the next day, of which the grieving nephew
Władysław, Count Miączyński
of Pawłowa, gives notice.
