## How it bears on the edition

- **The black register.** The register's reprint in the Materialien (pp.
  162-164, Triebenfeld's entry) is in the literature, with four hand-made
  links to ten documents. Łukasiewicz (1997) is the modern source study of
  that same register: his finding that the title page's 20 million is false
  and the real value nearer 4 million bears directly on the question the
  editor means to put to the whole literature ("was Triebenfeld falsely
  inflating the value of the estates?").
- **Triebenfeld.** Named on pp. 44-46 (the giveaway's technical manager, his
  purchase at 50 000 and sale at 450 000/750 000), p. 51 (the most notorious
  of the malversators), and in the tables (Tabela 1: 700 000 official, 51 000
  tax, 700 000 "true"; Tabela 3: Kalisz (2), Odolanów (3), Ostrzeszów (3); 
  Tabela 4: 51 000 official, sold 400 000-750 000 in 1801). The register's own
  entry (Materialien pp. 162-163) gives the same 700 000/51 000 and the sale
  400 000-750 000; the edition's documents (the letters of 1799-1801,
  Oe 1 Bü 9454 docs 104, 108, 112) give the same valuation and resale.
- **The debts of donataries** (p. 45): Hohenlohe 263 700 thalers of Prussian
  credit — the Prince of the letters; also Treskow, Zastrow, Wurtemberg.
- **Rüchel** (pp. 45-46, 56, 61): his purchase for 30 000, sale to Reibnitz of
  Kalisz for 130 000, the judicial valuation at 380 000 and the suit — the
  register's Rüchel, as the edition ruled for letter 103's "Ruchts".
- **The register's figures against the edition**: Łukasiewicz gives 245
  estates for 48 donataries and 52 donations (p. 36, p. 53); the register's
  own title (241 estates, 52 donataries) as it is printed in the Materialien.
  His Tabela 3's entry 39, Prince Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Międzyrzecz (6),
  Brześć (1), Konin (5), answers the Konin court books' lord.

## Why it is here

Kept from the work's account, which said it on the page until the voice review of 2026-10-29 (the editor: the reasoning for why a work is relevant is for the notes, not the reader):

> It cites works that also stand on the Literature page (Wąsicki, the
> Materialien, the Neue Feuerbrände, Grünhagen, Philippson) and bears directly
> on the black-register passages and the Prince's gift. The register is

> 1796-1797 followed.
