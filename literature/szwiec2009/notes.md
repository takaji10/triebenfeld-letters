# Szwiec (2009): notes

Piotr Szwiec, *Katalog architektury regionalnej dla obszaru „Puszczy
Pyzdrskiej”. Opracowanie badań naukowych przeprowadzonych jesienią 2009 roku*
(Pyzdry - Poznań 2009), 100 pages; made for the research programme "PUSZCZA
PYZDRSKA - Olędrzy i ich żelazne domy" of the MECENAT Association, financed by
the Ministry of Labour and Social Policy (the credits, p. 3).

## The text

Never tidied. Kept as printed, among others: "nie występuję" (for
występuje, p. 12), "na poddasz" (p. 12), "Wrąbczynkowskich Holędrów" (note 3,
beside "Holendry" on p. 9), "pławi położnej" and "belkach stopowych" (p. 35),
"Bartosz Stańda.." and "f fot." in two captions. **Page 12 ends in mid-sentence**
("Dachy tych obiektów mają szczyty deskowane") and page 13 begins another: the
print breaks off there, nothing is missing from the PDF.

## The English

Claude's (`make_english.py`, paragraph by paragraph; the captions by their
parts, "N. Dwelling house – photograph 2009, by ..."). The bibliography's
entries are left as printed; its headings are translated. Terms: *ruda
darniowa* bog iron ore; *zagroda* farmstead; *chałupa* cottage; *budynek
gospodarczy* farm building; *konstrukcja zrębowa* log construction;
*sumikowo-łątkowa* post-and-plank; *szachulcowa* half-timbered; *ryglowa ze
strychulcem* timber framing filled with clay-wrapped staves; *glinobitka*
rammed clay; *jednotraktowy* single-pile; *czarna kuchnia* black kitchen;
*rzędówka bagienna* marsh row village; *ulicówka* street village; *mała
architektura* small structures; *wrota* double doors.

## How it bears on the edition

Trąbczyn, Wrąbczynek, Wrąbczynkowskie Holendry and Orlina are among the 22
villages studied (p. 9), and the history of settlement names Huta Trąbczyńska
and Huta Łukomska (p. 11, from Chodyła). The captions do not say which village
a photograph shows, so no photograph can be tied to Trąbczyn. Note 3 (p. 35)
names a building in Wrąbczynkowskie Holędry (inventory card 021). "Nowa Wieś"
is a common place name (the editor): no link is made to one unless the text
means the Trąbczyn estate's; this catalogue names none.
