# Guradze (1898): notes

## How it came in (2026-10-19)

From the editor's folder "Books": "Der Bauer in Posen (Guradze, 1918)
(Fixed).md" is `original.md`, "(English).md" is `english.md`, copied
unchanged. The year in the file names is wrong: the work is of 1898 (the
editor; the preface is dated "Halle a. S., im Frühjahr 1898"). The English
is Claude's (the editor).

A scan is at archive.org, `derbauerinposen00guragoog` (digitised by Google
from the University of California, 108 pages). It says the work appeared in
the *Zeitschrift der historischen Gesellschaft für die Provinz Posen*.

## Alignment

**No page numbers yet.** The editor means to correct the text and add them.
Until then the work is cut at its eight top headings: `c01` the title, `c02`
the preface and contents, `c03` to `c08` chapters I to VI. A way to add the
pages: archive.org's page-by-page OCR (`derbauerinposen00guragoog_djvu.xml`)
gives the first words of every printed page, which can be found in the
text.

**The footnotes are numbered differently in the two files**: the original
has 192, the English 189, and by chapter IV the English numbers run four
behind (the original's note 125 is the English's 121). To be looked at
when the editor corrects the text.

## Spot check of the English

The opening of chapter IV read against the German (2026-10-19): right in
substance but weaker than the German: "die größten Vortheile" (the greatest
advantages) is "benefited significantly", "die ersten gewaltigen Schritte"
