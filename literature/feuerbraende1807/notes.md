# Neue Feuerbrände (1807): notes

## How it came in (2026-10-19)

From the editor's folder "Books": `feuerbrand-book.md` is `original.md`,
`feuerbrand-book-en.md` is `english.md`, copied unchanged. The English is
Claude's (the editor).

## Alignment

Page marks `[p. N]`, the same in both files. **Each of the three parts
numbers its pages from 1 again**, and each opens with unnumbered and roman
pages (title, contents). A passage is keyed by part and page: `3-133` is
part 3, page 133, cited "Neue Feuerbrände III. 133" as the Materialien
cites it. Each part's unnumbered opening pages are its `front`.

## Cölln on the black register

Part 3, pages 129 to 134: the "Erklärung" of the author of the Vertraute
Briefe. He says he edited part 1 but did not see part 2 before it was
printed, calls the register a mixture of truth and lies, and corrects six
entries, by the page of part 2 they stand on (67, 69, 71, 75, 76, 83). The
last, page 83, is Triebenfeld: "Unter der Taxe der Triebenfeldschen Güther
und ihrem Verkaufspreise, stecken seine ihm eigenthümlich gehörigen Güter."
The black register itself is in part 2 from page 65; Triebenfeld's entry
(16/38) is on pages 83 and 84.
