# Materialien (1861): notes

## How it came in (2026-10-19)

From the editor's folder "Books": `materialen-book_de.md` is `original.md`,
`materialen-book_en.md` is `english.md`, copied unchanged. The English is
Claude's (the editor). The editor's `blackregister.txt` in the same folder
is pages 140 to 171 of this book, word for word, and was not brought in a
second time.

## Alignment

Printed page marks `— N —` at the head of each page, 1 to 236, the same in
both files. What stands before page 1 (title, preface, contents) is the
passage `front`; the English front also has a translator's note and a
glossary of words left untranslated, which the original has not. The book's
notes, gathered after page 236 under "Anmerkungen", are the passage `notes`.

## The black register

Pages 140 to 171, the list of the Crown and church estates given away in
South Prussia in 1794 to 1798, printed (the book's notes say) from a
manuscript found among Hans von Held's papers. Triebenfeld's entry (16/38) is on pages
162 to 164. **It concerns other estates than Trąbczyn**, printed as
Piotrow (district of Kalisch), Koscielna Wies, Glogowa and Krzywo Sondowo
(Adelnau), and the lordship of Lasky with Pawlowo, Nowa Wies and
Szmardzece (Ostrzeszow). It gives their value at the gift (31,000 thalers for the first
four; for Lasky he paid 20,000 as an Erbstand), the valuation of the Kalisz government in
spring 1797 (over 700,000 thalers net), the sale to Prince George of
Hessen-Darmstadt for 400,000 thalers and the resale to Schirnding for
750,000 (9 March 1801), Cölln's remark from the Neue
Feuerbrände (III. 133), and Held's account of Triebenfeld from the "black
book" (Held 1801, p. 11 ff). Page 135 says Cölln corrected six entries, "nos. 7, 10, 14, 18, 20 and
28", and that his corrections are printed at the entries; Cölln's remark on
Triebenfeld stands at his entry, which is numbered 16/38, so the "28" is
not his number. Not looked into further.
