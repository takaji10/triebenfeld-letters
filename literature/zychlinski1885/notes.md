# Żychliński (1885): notes

## How it came in (2026-10-19)

From the editor's folder "Books_2": two photographs, "Złota księga szlachty
polskiéj. R. 7 - Żychliński, Teodor (1836-1909) - 187.jpg" and "- 188.jpg".
The editor: "Złota księga vol. 7 begins with the entry for Krzysztof
Prusimski on 187". There was no text: the two pages were read in session
from the photographs (cut into quarters with PyMuPDF, as PIL is not
installed), and `original.md` was written by hand. The book is cited by its
printed pages, 187 and 188.

The year, 1885, is reckoned from the series (rocznik I appeared in 1879, one
volume a year), not read off a title page; the editor may correct it.

## How the text is given

- **As printed.** The old spelling of the 1880s is kept ("Iwińskiéj",
  "Wiktoryą", "Trąmbczynie", "Kaliskiém", "dzierzy"); so are "Ostrorog"
  without the accent, "pmo voto" and "sdo voto", and the punctuation.
- **Spaced type as bold.** The book prints names to be noticed, and the
  heading, in letter-spaced type ("K r z y s z t o f"); here they are in
  bold, in the original and in the English, as in Żychliński (1891), where
  bold keeps spaced type apart from the book's italics. (First given as
  italics; changed the same day, after the heading was checked on the
  photograph and found spaced, not italic.)
- **Words broken at the line end** are joined; the lines of the page are not
  kept.
- **The heading** "1. Odnoga na Trąmbczynie." is a heading (`##`), as on the
  page.

## Slips in the print, kept

- **"wrzysztof"** (p. 187, the first word): for Krzysztof. The editor noted
  it. The English gives "[K]rzysztof" and says what was printed.
- **"(zob. R. VI str. 50."** (p. 187): the bracket is never closed.
- **"rodziny. któréj"** (p. 187): a full stop where a comma is meant.
- **"Roczn. I str. 187--189"** (p. 188): a doubled dash.
- **"Wesslowny"** (p. 188): for Wesslówny, daughter of the Wessel family.

## Not in the photographs

- The **footnote 4)** after "piszący się zawsze Ostrorogiem" (p. 188, that
  Antoni always signed himself Ostroróg): the photograph ends at the printed
  frame, and the note is not on it. If the editor has it, it belongs on
  p. 188.
- The **beginning of the family's entry** (generations 1 to 8) stands on the
  pages before 187; the editor sent only these two.

## The English

Claude's (2026-10-19). "pok." (pokolenie, generation, counted from the first
of the family in Żychliński's account) is "generation"; "pmo voto" and "sdo
voto" are "married first" and "married secondly". The titles that make a
woman the daughter or wife of an office-holder (kasztelanka, wojewodzianka,
kasztelanowa, starościanka) are spelled out ("daughter of the castellan of
Nakło"). Place names are in today's form; Trąmbczyn is given once in
brackets. The references in brackets are kept as printed: "Inscr." and
"Rel." are the books of entries (inscriptiones) and of reports (relationes)
of the castle courts, "Valc." Wałcz, "Gnesn." Gniezno, "Posn." Poznań; "R."
and "Roczn." are earlier volumes of the Złota księga.

## Points for the reader, held against the edition

1. **The line agrees with the edition's documents.** Krzysztof (11th
   generation) is the Starost's grandfather, Stefan his father, Michalina his
   only child. The boundary decree of 1775 (Konin Gr.145) calls Krzysztof the
   Starost's grandfather, and the citation of 1780-82 in Konin Gr.118
   (document 23) names the Starost "son of the late Stefan Prusimski begotten
   with the late Wiktoria Malczewska, grandson of the late Krzysztof
   Prusimski and Teresa Golińska". The PSB entry (Pachoński 1982) makes him
   Stefan's son too. The edition's `prusimski-krzysztof` stands.
2. **Krzysztof's sons: Żychliński and the court book differ.** Żychliński
   gives four sons: Jan Paweł († 1773) and Teofil, unmarried; Antoni, married
   to Barbara Rayska, without children; and Stefan. The citation in Konin
   Gr.118, document 23, gives five: Jan, Bogumił, Antoni, Paweł and Stefan,
   and says that Jan and Bogumił died without issue before the decree of 1738
   and that Paweł was "the third brother" to die without issue. Bogumił is
   the Polish form of Teofil, so the two agree on him; but where the court
   book has Jan and Paweł as two brothers, Żychliński has one "Jan Paweł",
   dead in 1773.
3. **Paweł the chamberlain: one generation apart.** Żychliński puts "Paweł
   Apollo, chamberlain of His Royal Majesty", husband of Katarzyna
   Rozdrażewska, among the sons of Jan (10th generation), that is, as
   Krzysztof's brother and the Starost's great-uncle. The edition's documents
   make him the Starost's uncle: the Kuryer Polski of 1754 calls Antoni at
   his funeral his "Synowiec Rodzony", his brother's own son
   (`kuryerpolski1754075`); the Starost calls him his paternal uncle
   ("Patruus") in 1778 (Konin Gr.117); and Konin Gr.118, document 23, has
   him among Krzysztof's sons. The PSB also has the Starost inherit from "his
   paternal uncle Paweł". The edition follows its documents
   (`prusimski-pawel`); Żychliński is very likely wrong here. One thing
   remains unclear: the Kuryer's Antoni could be the elder Antoni, the
   Starost's uncle, who was Paweł's brother; neither reading puts Paweł in
   Krzysztof's generation.
4. **Michalina's second marriage.** Żychliński gives no year. The PSB says
   "from 1809"; the edition's `prusimska` and `miaczynski` say "about 1814".
   Volume 13 (Żychliński 1891, p. 221) gives "August 1809", with the
   marriage settlement, and agrees with the PSB. Asked in
   `docs/NEEDS_CONFIRMATION.md`; the bios are not changed without the
   editor's word.
5. **Władysław Miączyński.** "Which her grandson, Count Władysław Miączyński,
   holds today" agrees with the editor's ruling of 2026-10-15: after
   Włodzimierz's death in 1879 his nephew Władysław was heir of Trąbczyn.
   The nephew of Michalina's son is Michalina's grandson.
6. **Three daughters of Krzysztof.** Konstancja (Kowalska), Maryanna
   (Tomicka, then Zakrzewska) and Wiktorya (Tworowicz). The same families
   are cited in Konin Gr.118, document 23: Kunigunda Kowalska, daughter of
   Jan Kowalski and Konstancja Prusimska; Paweł and Józef Tomicki, sons of
   Marianna Prusimska; Elżbieta Tworowicz, wife of Jakub Taczanowski.
