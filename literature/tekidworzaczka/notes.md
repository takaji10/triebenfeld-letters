## How the text is kept

Each regest stands as supplied, the breadcrumb line, the number line and
the text, not tidied (the print's spaces in "15. 000" and "6. 000", "Br."
for the father's surname in c04). The heading over each ("# Nr. 1420
(1642)") is the edition's, so that the work can be cut and each regest
cited by its number. c01 is the line as Minakowska's page quotes it,
"sw.35437" and all: the edition does not have the regest from the files
themselves, and its series path is the page's. A further regest goes at
the end as c05, so the keys already cited do not move.

## Reading the abbreviations

The English expands the abbreviations; where a reading is Claude's it is
said here:

- **"s. o.", "c. o."** ("s. o. Wojc. Otha Tr.", "c. o. Mikoł.", "s. o.
  Władysława Tr."): read "son of", "daughter of". The same regests write
  "ol." for "the late" ("ol. Stanisł. Chw.", "ol. Teresy Gajewskiej"), so
  "o." is not read as "the late", and **no death is inferred from it**.
- **"pos. i t. w."**: posag i tyleż wiana, the dowry and as much again of
  dower, the usual form of a marriage settlement.
- **"wyd."** (c02, "za 20.000 złp. wyd."): read as wyderkaf, a sale the
  seller may redeem. Claude's reading, not checked against the files'
  list of abbreviations. It fits the record: Debrzyński stands in 1644 as
  a "possessor" of Trąbczyn beside the widow and sons (the boundary decree
  of 1775, APP 53/17/0/-/Konin Gr.145), and by 1663 Władysław Trąmpczyński
  holds the estate again (c03).
- **"BB."** (c03): the Bojanowskis, Stefan and Samuel, "bracia stryjeczni",
  her first cousins on the father's side.
- **"Br."** (c04, "c. Jana z Kolna Br."): so printed. The boundary decree
  of 1775 names her father "Jan na Kolnie Prusimski", and the Poznań decree
  of 1784 "Joannes in Kolno Prusimski" with the same wife, Teresa Gajewska:
  the "Br." is a slip of the regest or of the copy. Kept, and marked in the
  English.

## Against the documents (the documents first)

- **c01 (1614)**: the bride is the daughter of Jan Słonecki, son of
  Marcin, by Elżbieta Chwalibowska, who was the daughter of the late
  Stanisław Chwalibowski, pantler (podstoli) of Kalisz. The account of this
  regest in `minakowska2026ottotrampczynski/notes.md` (written 2026-10-22)
  made her the daughter of "Stanisław Słonecki, cup-bearer of Kalisz": a
  misreading, corrected there on 2026-11-12. The documents call her "Anna z
  Słończyc" (the decree of 1644, APP 53/6/0/-/36: "Annam de Słończyce").
- **c02 (1642)**: Stanisław alive in 1642, his wife consenting. The widow's
  manifest of the Saturday before Trinity Sunday 1644 (21 May 1644; the
  decree of 1775 recites it) gives the other end: he died between the two.
  The regest is why Maciej Debrzyński (the decree's "Dybrzyński") stands
  among the "heirs and possessors" of 1644; no family link between him and
  the Trąmpczyńskis is in the edition's sources. Minakowska's page makes
  him the husband of a Maria Otto-Trąmpczyńska, which nothing the edition
  holds confirms.
- **c03 (1663)**: Anna is already Władysław's wife: the regest dates the
  settlement of her dowry, not the wedding ("married by 1663"). The mill
  Drzazgi on which her dowry stands is the "Drzezgow Molendini" among the
  Trąbczyn estates of the Poznań decree of 1784 (APP 53/20/0/-/Poznań
  Gr.1181).
- **c04 (1692)**: Jan, son of Władysław, is the Jan who, with his brothers
  Andrzej and Władysław, divided Trąbczyn on 6 October 1693 and who, with
  Andrzej and their mother Anna Bojanowska, sold it in 1698 to Jan na Kolno
  Prusimski (the decree of 1775): his father-in-law by this regest.
