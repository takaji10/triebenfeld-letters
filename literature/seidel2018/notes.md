## Alignment

Page marks `[p. N]`, the same in both files: 3, then 7 to 266 (pages 4 to
6 are not marked in either), with unnumbered pages at the front and two
at the end. **The notes** ("Anmerkungen", pages 257 to 266): the editor's
files carry all 168 as Markdown footnotes at the end, numbered 1 to 168 straight
through, while their pages 257 to 266 stood empty; this note said until
2026-11-28 that they were not transcribed, which was wrong (the editor: "they
should be included!", to-do t034/t920). They are in the edition, cut with the
last passage (the back cover, `266-u2`). Moving them onto their printed pages
under the chapter headings was done and undone on 2026-11-28: on their pages a
note becomes the longest line of a passage, and the check that a hidden work's
text is not on the site (verify_site.py) then finds a note's citation of Kraus
(2001) in that work's title on the Literature page. Changing the check is the
editor's to allow (to-do t920). The page breaks are worked out (each page's
first note matched against the editor's photographs, `Pages/Hohenlohe_261.jpg`
to `_270.jpg` = pp. 257 to 266), and every break agrees with the print's own
numbering. **The print numbers its notes afresh in each chapter**: the print's
number is the footnote number minus the chapter's first footnote, plus one.
