# Seidel (2018): notes

**Not on the site** (the editor, 2026-10-19; `work.yml` and
`docs/LITERATURE_PLAN.md`, section 4). The book is under copyright. The
editor chose to keep it in this public repository for questions, knowing
that committing it publishes it.

## How it came in (2026-10-19)

From the editor's folder "Books": `hohenlohe-book.md` is `original.md`,
`hohenlohe-book-en.md` is `english.md`, copied unchanged. The English is
Claude's (the editor). Until then the book was kept out of the repository
(`.gitignore`, from 2026-09-04).

## Alignment

Page marks `[p. N]`, the same in both files: 3, then 7 to 266 (pages 4 to
6 are not marked in either), with unnumbered pages at the front and two
at the end. **The notes ("Anmerkungen", pages 257 to 266) are not
transcribed**: those pages are empty in both files, so the footnote markers
in the text have no notes.

Appendix 6 (page 241) transcribes Triebenfeld's
letter of 7 March 1809 from Kontopp, which stands in the edition as letters
48 and 302 of Oe 1 Bü 9454.
