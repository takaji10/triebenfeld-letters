## The subject and why he matters

Stanisław Adam Miączyński (1780-1845) is the edition's `miaczynski`: the
second husband of Michalina Prusimska, the only daughter of the Starost
Antoni Prusimski - the first Minakowska page's subject. The letters know him
only by his wife's names for him (Moscinski, Moszynski, "M. de Mięczynsky"
of 1815); this page names him fully: born 24 December 1780 at Warsaw, died
12 April 1845 at Berlin, buried at Trąbczyn, captain of the army of the
Duchy of Warsaw, adjutant of Prince Poniatowski, member of the Sejm of
1830-31, colonel of the Polish army, knight of several orders. The
legitymacja of 1838 that Sęczys records calls him "płk WP, dz. d. Trąbczyn,
pow. koniński i Wieniec, pow. brzeski" - **the heir of the edition's
Trąbczyn**, and of Wieniec.

His son Włodzimierz (1811-1879) is the edition's `miaczynski-wlodzimierz`,
whose identity already carries the editor's finds (heir of Trąbczyn, died
20 December 1879, succeeded by his nephew Władysław). The page agrees with
all of it and adds the brothers.

## Against the documents and the edition's entries

- **The marriage date - the work's most important find, and a conflict.**
  The page gives the marriage **"ślub: dnia 17 VIII 1810, Warszawa,
  Cyrkuł IV"**, resting on the marriage act it cites (Warsaw ASC Cyrkuł
  IV, act 67 of 1810, indexed on geneteka, with a szukajwarchiwach link).
  The edition's entries say the marriage was **"about 1814"** - inferred
  from the letters of 1815 (9454 letters 265b of 15 January 1815 and 270)
  that already write him as the husband, and the bio of `miaczynski`, the
  identity of `miaczynski`, and the genealogy block of `prusimski` all
  carry "about 1814". The act is the stronger source and four years
  earlier. **Nothing was changed**: the bio, the identity and the
  Prusimski block keep their "about 1814" until the editor rules
  (NEEDS_CONFIRMATION); the new page's marriage line carries the 1810
  date with its source and the flag beside it, so the site shows both and
  hides nothing.
- **Włodzimierz.** The page's Włodzimierz (1811-1879, Pyzdry district of
  the Agricultural Society) is the edition's `miaczynski-wlodzimierz`;
  the editor's finds of 2026-10-14/15 stand (heir of Trąbczyn, died 20
  December 1879, the nephew Władysław his successor). The page adds
  nothing against them.
- **Michalina.** The page's Michalina (1778-1851) agrees with the
  edition's `prusimska` (died 1851; the editor's 19 January 1851 stands).
  Her parents as the page gives them (Antoni 1744-ca 1806, Marianna née
  Dąmbska ca 1763-) agree with the first Minakowska page.
- **The dates.** The subject's dates, his parents' (1751-1801; ca
  1749-1843 with the page's own "prawdopodobnie" on the age), the
  children's and grandchildren's (1810-1869, 1811-1879, 1818-1887,
  1823-1880, and the grandchildren's) are stated by the page and stand on
  his with the work as their source; the "ca" of Stanisław (ca 1849-1860),
  Olga (ca 1888-) and Teresa Zaborowska (ca 1894-1978) kept as it stands.
  The estimates ("?1790-" of the half-sisters Dorota and Honorata,
  "?1870-" of Paulina Suchorzewska) are not used anywhere.
- **PSB t. 20 s. 562 (psb.17772.5)** - the Uwagi cite the Polski Słownik
  Biograficzny's article on him. The edition holds no PSB as a literature
  work (the PSB t. 28/27 question of the first work is the editor's file):
  whether to seek the volume is the editor's (NEEDS_CONFIRMATION).
- The other Uwagi sources (Sęczys's legitymacja, the Nekrolog of Kurier
  Warszawski 111/1845 with its ebuw link, the Czartoryski database,
