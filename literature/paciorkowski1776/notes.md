# Paciorkowski (1776): notes

## How it came in (2026-10-19)

From the editor's folder "Books": "Paciorkowski - Regula processus
granicialis campestris Regni Poloniae (French).md" is `original.md` (it is
the Latin, not French), "(English).md" is `english.md`, copied unchanged.
The English is Claude's (the editor).

## Alignment

Page marks `[p. N]`, with i to iii for the censor's approbation (Kraków,
15 May 1749) and a page "115x" before 115, as in the original. Two
differences:

- **The English has no mark for page 27**: the English of pages 26 and 27
  stands together under page 26. The passage `27` has the Latin only.
- **The English adds translator's notes** ("[T.N. 1]" and following) after
  some pages, and an index and a translation glossary after page 174, which
  the original has not.

## Spot check of the English

Page 10 read against the Latin (2026-10-19): faithful in substance. One
thing to know in reading it: **"heir" renders the Latin *heres*, which in
Polish law means the owner of a hereditary estate (dziedzic), not an heir**;
a "collateral heir" (*collateralis*) is the owner of the adjoining estate
summoned as a third party, as the English glossary at the end explains.
