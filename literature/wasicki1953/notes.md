# Wąsicki (1953): notes

Jan Wąsicki, "Kolonizacja niemiecka w okresie Prus Południowych 1793 – 1806",
*Przegląd Zachodni* 1953, no. 9-10, pp. 137-179 (Poznań: Instytut Zachodni).
Cited so in *Przegląd Historyczny* 1964 (t. 55, nr 3, s. 520-522); the
digitiser's footer on every page reads "Przegląd Zachodni, nr 9-10, 1953 /
Instytut Zachodni".

## How it came in (2026-10-21)

The editor: "Now can you add Kolonizacja niemicka w okresie Prus południowych
1793-1806 (Wąsicki).pdf as a new literary source, also in the \Books
folder?" The file name's "niemicka" is a slip; the print reads
"niemiecka".

- The PDF: 41 images of the printed pages (ABBYY FineReader 11), with an OCR
  text layer full of spaces inside words. The OCR was not taken over. Every
  page was rendered at 300 dpi and read in two halves; a draft rebuilt from
  the OCR's character positions (`scratch/was/draft.py`, not kept in the
  work) was corrected against the image line by line. Afterwards the reading
  was compared word by word with that draft: nothing was found missing (the
  only differences are running heads, the footer and the colony list moved
  as below).
- The PDF's order: pp. 137-161; four spreads printed sideways (pp. 162-169);
  pp. 170-176; a fold-out sheet without a page number (the costs of the
  colony of Nowe Gałęzewo, keyed "176-u1"), and its blank back, left out;
  pp. 177-179.

- Notes as "[^n]", 101 of them, with the print's "12a" and the unnumbered

## The English

Claude's, paragraph for paragraph and note for note. German quotations in
the notes keep their German, with an English rendering in brackets where
the Polish gives none. Terms: *łan* (Hufe) kept as łan/łany; *mórg* morgen;
*kamera* chamber (the War and Domains Chamber); *Generalne Dyrektorium*
General Directory; *domena* domain; *folwark* manor farm; *chałupnik*
cottager; *zagrodnik* smallholder; *budnik* Büdner; *wieczysta dzierżawa*
perpetual lease; *lata wolne* free years; *kanton* canton (military
conscription); *sołtys* sołtys (village headman); *podymne* hearth tax.

## How it bears on the edition

- Page 138: the Olęder settlements of the seventeenth and eighteenth
  centuries, founded by Polish lords on land to be cleared and drained, were
  the private initiative the Prussian state took over.
- Page 143: minister Schulenburg-Kehnert met the "Olęders" on his journey
  and wanted them supported; page 158: the General Directory put the
  requests of the local "Olęders" first for empty peasant farms.
- **Pages 174-176: the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen's Zagórów and
  Trąbczyn estates** (the manor farms of Drzewie, Kopojno, Świątniki,
  Święcia, Bronniki, Nowa Wieś, Przybyłów, Alt Hütte and Trąbczyn, the
  villages of Oleśnica and Szetlewek; 250 łany in all); 18 families of
  colonists brought in 1805 and given 100 łany of forest and meadow on the
  Warta; the villages founded there (Sophiental, Ludwigslust, Friedrichsfelde,
  Adelshof, Adolfsberg, Ingelfingen, Emilienheim, Augustinhof, Schwentz and
  the Wrąbczyn colony); the contract's rent of 30 thalers a year for each
  Magdeburg łan after four free years; 21 morgen for the teacher, the sołtys
  and the cemetery. All from Breyer (1938), pp. 50, 60, 71 (his notes 86,
  87, 89), already in the literature as `breyer1938`. These figures are to
  be read against the edition's documents before they are relied on: the
  lease of 1806 (APP 53/968/0/-/801, document 3) gives 100 Hufen of the
  Drzewce forest to eighteen settlers. Nowa Wieś here is the estate's
  manor farm, in Wąsicki's list of the estate's farms (the editor's rule on
  the common name).
