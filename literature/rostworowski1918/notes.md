# Rostworowski (1918), *Dziennik czynności Komisyi Rządzącej* — how it came in

Added 2026-10-26 on branch `rostworowski1918` at the editor's word: "I want to
add a new literary unit ... I only want to add some passages that are relevant
to Michalina Prusimska/Dąmbska", with the four passages named by their PDF
pages (66, 344, 76-77, 325) and the SBC address. Asked, they ruled: the whole
sitting (Sesya) is brought in for clarity and context, and for p. 322, which
has no session heading, the whole protocol that contains the passage (Nr. 3).

## The source

- Michał Rostworowski (ed.), *Materyały do dziejów Komisyi Rządzącej z r.
  1807*, T. I, *Dziennik czynności Komisyi Rządzącej*, Kraków 1918 (title
  page: nakładem Akademii Umiej. i Tow. Popierania Wydawnictw, drukarnia
  Uniwersytetu Jagiellońskiego pod zarządem J. Filipowskiego). The imprint
  gives no year on the first title leaf; the second (the SBC scan repeats it)
  has "1918".
- Out of copyright: Rostworowski died in 1940, the print is 1918. Shown whole
  as far as it is brought in; no licence question.
- The SBC digitisation (publication 94492, edition 89178): 842 PDF pages,
  five empty leading images, then title leaf. **PDF page = printed page + 22**
  throughout the checked range (printed 43 = PDF 65 ... printed 323 = PDF 345).
- The PDF has a text layer of uneven quality: letterspaced small caps come
  out with spaces inside every letter ("k o n f i s k a t y"), the dotless ż
  reads "ź", and there are plain misreads. Not used as the text; see below.

## The reading

- Two witnesses held against each other, as in Kraus (2001) and Werner (1904):
  the PDF's own text layer, and a lunaroute OCR pass over each of the nine
  kept pages rendered at 300 dpi. Where the two agreed, the reading stood;
  where they differed, it was settled from Polish sense and the print's own
  habits, and every settlement is listed below. **The images could not be
  read in the session** (the harness returned them stripped of content), so
  no zoom pass was possible: the settlements rest on the two witnesses and
  sense alone, and the doubtful spots went to the spot sheet
  (`review/rostworowski1918/`), where the editor has since answered.
- The print's spaced small caps (names, offices, the key terms) are joined
  into words, as in Kucharski (2017) — the joined word is the one the print
  emphasises; case follows the print's capitals. Where the spaced run crossed
  a word boundary, the boundary was restored by sense ("P. Daru
  rekwirował", "o kupno wołów").
- **A word the print divides over a page turn is given whole on the first
  page** (the Kucharski rule): Styczniowa pp. 43-44, przedmiotem pp. 54-55,
  pozwolono pp. 303-304, powinien pp. 322-323.
- No footnote of the print is brought in: the three footnotes on the kept
  pages' feet ("Ob. alleg. 1. 44.", "Por. alleg. 1. 45.", "Ob. alleg. 1.
  193.") all belong by their raised markers to text not brought in (the tail
  of sitting 50 on p. 43, sitting 52 on p. 44, sitting 209 on p. 303), and
  none of the four kept passages carries a marker. The printer's signature
  "21*" at the feet of printed pp. 321 and 323 and the stray "✓" at the foot
  of printed p. 322 are the print's chrome, dropped.

## The excerpt

- Printed pp. 43-44 — **Sesya 51, 13 February 1807**, whole: the Dąmbska
  memorial's first appearance in the Dziennik (nothing earlier names it; the
  preceding sittings were read and checked). Cut at "SESYA 51." and at the
  sitting's close ("Sesya na po obiedzie zapowiedziana. / Stanisław
  Małachowski, Prezes.") before "SESYA 52." on the same page.
- Printed pp. 54-55 — **Sesya 58, 18 February 1807**, whole: the Director of
  Justice's opinion and the Commission's resolution. Cut at "SESYA 58." and
  before "SESYA 59." on p. 55.
- Printed pp. 303-304 — **Sesya 210, extraordinary, at Dresden, 21 July
  1807**, whole: the Emperor's Wybicki decree extended to Dąmbska,
  Niemojewski and Wichrowski. Cut at "SESYA 210." (mid-page; sitting 209's
  close stands before it) and at the close before "SESYA 211." on p. 304.
- Printed pp. 321-323 — **the Directorium Generalne's protocol Nr. 3 of 15
  July 1807**, whole: the numbered series of Directorium dispatches ("Nr. 1"
  on printed p. 317, "Nr. 2" on p. 319, "Nr. 4" on p. 323, "Nr. 5-ty" on
  p. 324), presented to the Commission at its extraordinary sittings at
  Dresden. The Dąmbska/Dresden paragraph stands inside it, after the heading
  "Różne partykularne petita brevi manu rezolwowane"; the protocol carries
  its date, its first-person author (the Director of Police Łubieński) and
  his postscript asking approval of the Kościan court nominations. Cut at
  "Nr. 3-ci." and before "Nr. 4." on p. 323.
- Left out with the "[…]": on p. 43 the tail of sitting 50 (with its
  footnotes 1-2 and "Gutakowski."); on p. 44 sitting 52 from its heading; on
  p. 54 the tail of sitting 57 (the Gorzeński election to the seat at the
  head of the Department of Poznań); on p. 55 sitting 59 from its heading;
  on p. 303 sitting 209's close; on p. 304 sitting 211 from its heading; on
  p. 321 the end of protocol Nr. 2 (through its "Łubieński." signature) and
  one stray line; on p. 323 protocol Nr. 4 from its heading.
- **The stray line on p. 321** ("Nominowani zostali do konsystorza
  Luterskiego w Płocku.") stands between Nr. 2's signature and the Nr. 3
  heading; it is taken for Nr. 2's matter (a closing item after the
  signature, as Nr. 3 closes with its own postscript after the signature)
  and is left out. If the print meant it inside Nr. 3, one sentence of Nr. 3
  is missing from the excerpt — for the editor to see on the scan.

## The spot sheet, answered by the editor (2026-10-26)

The sheet (`scratch/rostworowski1918_sheet.py`, port 4110; the crops from
the PDF's own word positions at 600 dpi) was answered; the rulings below
are in the transcript:

1. **p. 322, the sum**: the editor read **"300/m"** — the OCR's reading,
   against the transcript's 500/m (taken from the layer's "SOO/m"). The
   text now carries 300/m.
2. **p. 322, the word**: the editor read **"anzeatycznym"** — the OCR's
   form, against the layer's "anzeatyeznym". Still no Polish word traced;
   the text now carries anzeatycznym (the English keeps the citation form
   "anzeatyczna").
3. **p. 322, "X-żę Dyrektor zapewnił"**: the editor read **"X-żę"**, the
   layer's form; it stands.
4. **p. 322, "Riviera Załuski"**: the editor read it **as printed**;
   "Riviera Załuski" stands, read as the man Riviera beside Załuski,
   Wołoski and Nakwaski.
5. **p. 322, "ściągniąnym"**: the editor read **"ściągniąnym"**, the
   layer's form; it stands, the print's form of what sense would write
   "ściągnionym".
6. **p. 323, "Kaiena"**: the editor read **"Kaiena"**; it stands.
7. **p. 321, the stray line** ("Nominowani zostali do konsystorza
   Luterskiego w Płocku.", between protocol Nr. 2's signature and the
   "Nr. 3-ci." heading): **the editor could not tell** ("I have no
   idea"). The transcript's take stands — Nr. 2's matter, left out — and
   the question stays open in NEEDS_CONFIRMATION.

## Identifications (Claude's, for the editor to accept or refuse)

- The **Dąmbska/Dąbska/Dambska** of all four passages is the edition's
  **Michalina z Prusimskich Dąbska, later Miączyńska** (people.yml
  `prusimska-michalina`): the February memorial and the July decree match
  the petitions the edition holds (AGAD 1/174/0/1/6 and 1/174/0/2/73, the
  restitution papers of 1807), and Pachoński (1982) tells the same story.
- **Wybicki** is Józef Wybicki, already in the edition's people (the
  Emperor's decree of 5 June 1807 at Finckenstein restoring his estates —
  also named in Łukasiewicz (1997) and ruled there); **Niemojewski** is
  General Józef Niemojewski, whose confiscated estates the same sources
  name; **Wichrowski** (p. 303) is the third beneficiary, not in the
  edition's people.
- **Stanisław Małachowski** the President, **Gutakowski**, **Stanisław
  Potocki**, **Działyński**, **Bieliński**, **Sobolewski** — the standing
  attendance of the Commission's sittings; Małachowski is in the edition's
  people, Sobolewski appears there as Ignacy Sobolewski of the February
  rachunki footnote (excluded text) and the PSB; the others are the
  Commission's own members.
- **Łubieński**, the protocols' first-person author, is Antoni Łubieński,
  Director of Police of the Duchy ("iż równie jak ja Direktor Policyi...").
- **Kamieniecki**, presented by the Prince as his deputy, and **Dembowski**,
  presiding in the Treasury Administration — the Directorium crisis of July
  1807; not the edition's people.
- The men and women of the Dresden list (Kownacka, Węgrzecki, Bórakowski,
  Paschalis, Gliszczyński, Czarnecki, Załuski, Wołoski, Nakwaski, and
  "Riviera") are left unidentified — one line of a police report, and the
  print's forms are uncertain.

## What it bears on

The February passages are the Dziennik's own record of the memorial the
edition's AGAD holdings preserve from Michalina's side, and the July decree
is the answer the petitions asked for — the hand-made links tie them to
`agad1174016-2` and `agad11740273-1`…`-4`. The Directorium protocol shows
her herself in movement to Dresden a week before the decree, in the police
director's own hand.
