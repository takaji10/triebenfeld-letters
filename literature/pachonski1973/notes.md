# Pachoński (1973): notes

Jan Pachoński, "Les Polonais dans la République de Venise (1794-1797)", in
*Italia, Venezia e Polonia tra illuminismo e romanticismo*, ed. Vittore Branca
(Florence: Leo S. Olschki, 1973), pp. 237-256: the papers of the third
conference of the Giorgio Cini Foundation and the Polish Academy of Sciences
(Venice, 15-17 October 1970), from the book's title page and imprint.

## The text

Read from the editor's scans (600 dpi, black and white) in session, each page
in five overlapping bands. Nothing was unreadable.
- By paragraph; the running heads and page numbers left out; italics not
  marked; the print's guillemets and apostrophes kept.
- The print's slips kept: "da grade", "contre-cadidat", "Néanmois",
  "suiverent", "plusiers", "italiannes", "officieurs", "lietenant", "la
  Russe"; "Kosciuszko" (p. 237) and "Kościuszko" (p. 239) both as printed.
- A word or note running over a page end is given whole on the page where it
  begins: "gouvernement" (237), note 4 (238), "s'efforçait" (241), "Légion"
  (242), "apportés," (243), "(contrada" (245), "attitude" (249), "défense"
  (254), "polono-italiannes" (255).
- Page 246 is the map (its picture, cut from scan 2026-10-17-0006_1L.tif) with
  its Italian legend as text.

## The English

Claude's, paragraph for paragraph and note for note. Names stay as the author
printed them (mostly French forms) except Stanisław August and Antoni
Prusimski, whom note 10 calls "Antoni Prusiński de Nieszczewice". Dates written
7.II.1795 are given as 7 February 1795. The slips of the print are not
reproduced.

## What it adds

Note 10 (p. 240) counts "Antoni Prusiński de Nieszczewice" among the three
starosts at Venice at the end of 1794: Antoni Prusimski, Starost of
Niszczewice. The edition's papers place him at Venice in December 1796
(I. HA GR, Rep. 7 C, Nr. 1414). The article does not say what the note rests
on. The Hohenlohe essay names it.

## The index
