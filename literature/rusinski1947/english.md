# Władysław Rusiński, *Osady tzw. „olędrów" w dawnym województwie poznańskim* — English translation

> **Translator's note.** This is a translation of the monograph
> (Poznań 1939, Kraków 1947). Conventions, agreed with the editor:
> British spelling; the ethnic term **Olęder(s)** (Pol. *olędrzy*,
> German *Hauländer*) is retained and glossed at first use; archival
> sigla (AW., Rel. Posn., Tp. 89 …), archive call marks and
> bibliographic titles are kept in their original form; numbers
> follow the print exactly, with Polish thousand/decimal separators
> converted to the English convention (13.737 → 13,737; 6,05 → 6.05)
> and no value altered — including the print's own internal
> discrepancies; measures are glossed at first use; quotations from
> archival Polish are translated, the original wording being given
> only where the author discusses it; quotations in German keep the
> original with a translation; the tables of ANEKS I are not
> translated in this edition. The bracketed `[p. N]` markers are the
> print's own pagination.
