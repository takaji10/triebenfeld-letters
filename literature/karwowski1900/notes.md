# Karwowski (1900): the convent of the Cistercian Nuns at Ołobok

Stanisław Karwowski, "Klasztor pp. cystersek w Ołoboku", *Roczniki
Towarzystwa Przyjaciół Nauk Poznańskiego*, T. XXVI, pp. 1-114 (Poznań 1900).
Added 2026-10-21 at the editor's word, "only this part, as the rest is not
relevant to the project".

## The source

The digitisation of the Wielkopolska Digital Library (WBC), id 118639,
https://www.wbc.poznan.pl/dlibra/docmetadata?id=118639, downloaded as a DjVu
folder in the editor's Downloads
("118639_Klasztor_pp_cystersek_w_O_oboku"). The DjVu files are **not** kept
in the repository (raw scans); the WBC page is recorded in `work.yml`. The
article is the volume's first (the contents list, scan 003: "Klasztor PP.
Cystersek w Oloboku. Skreślił Dr. Stanisław Karwowski", pp. 1-114). The
volume's title page (scan 001) reads: "Roczniki Towarzystwa Przyjaciół Nauk
Poznańskiego. Tom XXVI. Nakładem Towarzystwa Przyjaciół Nauk Poznańskiego.
Poznań. Czcionkami Drukarni Dziennika Poznańskiego. 1900."

The article's own table of contents (scan 124) has seven sections: I. the
convent's estates and privileges, II. the lawsuits, III. the nuns, IV. the
inner government, V. the convent's history from the third partition (the
estates taken, the winding-up of the house and the affairs after it, among
them the quarrel over the concealment of the convent's property), VI. the
convent building and its furnishings, VII. the churches of Ołobok. The
passage brought in stands in section V.3, "Materialne położenie PP.
Cystersek po zabraniu dóbr klasztornych" (the convent's material position
after the taking of its estates).

## What is brought in (the editor, 2026-10-21)

Only the two paragraphs of printed p. 87 (scan 097) the editor quoted:
"W tym razie Panny postąpiły sobie nieostrożnie ..." and "Tak więc przepadła
suma 40,000 złp. PP. Cysterskom ...", with their footnote 2). Everything
else on the page is left out, marked "[…]": before the passage the tail of a
paragraph on Szeliski and the paragraph naming the 40,000 złp. capital, and
after it the paragraphs on the Retorsions-Fonds, on the kompetencya of 1739
tal. 13 sbr. and on Gorzeński's loan of 25,000 złp. The page's footnotes 1),
3) and 4) belong to those other paragraphs and are not brought in; only
note 2) belongs to the passage.

**Context, Claude's summary of the left-out paragraph, not the text's**: the
passage's first words, "W tym razie" ("In this state of things"), refer back
to that preceding paragraph, which says the nuns' 40,000 złp. was lent at 5%
to Stefan Trzciński, heir of Trzcinica in the powiat of Ostrzeszów, in two
sums, 10,000 złp. on 1 May 1795 and 30,000 złp. on 20 June 1797 (in złoto
obrączkowe), and that his son Ignacy Trzciński, a radzca of the department
of Kalisz, mortgaged the debt on Zagórów in the powiat of Konin before the
notary Andrzej Bogusławski at Kalisz on 19 January 1814, the witnesses Józef
Miklaszewski and Wojciech Wyrzykowski. If the editor later wants the
paragraph, it is that one, from scan 097.

## The text

Transcribed from the page image, rendered at 300 dpi with ddjvu; doubtful
words were read again at word-level zooms. The scan's text layer (djvutxt)
and the editor's first OCR were used only as checks; neither was taken over.
The print's hyphens at line ends are joined ("Za-górów", "Trzciń-ski",
"za-ległą"); the running number 87 at the head of the page is left out (the
page mark `[p. 87]` says it). The old orthography stands as printed
("regulacyi", "licytacyi", "przyczem", "samem", "czem", "włości",
"nieostrożnie"); "obdłużone" is an old word for encumbered with debts
(zadłużone), not a misprint of "obciążone", and "PP." is the abbreviation of
the nuns (Panny), dative here: "PP. Cysterskom" = to the Cistercian Nuns.

Where the OCRs disagreed, the image settled it: "obdłużone" (not
"obciążone"), "z ciążącymi" (ciążyć, to weigh on; not "ciężającymi"),
"hipotece nowej regulacyi" (djvutxt read "nowey"; the image has "nowej"),
"zagórskich" (one r), "Bogumił" (with ł), "postąpiły", "spadł", "września",
and the year **1828** in the letter's date (the editor's OCR had "182?").
Between "r." and "1806" in "zaległą od r. 1806 prowizyą" the print shows a
stray ink fleck (djvutxt read "r. .1806"); transcribed "r. 1806".

The sentence ends "1828 roku[^2]" with no full stop in the print; kept. The
English adds the stop.

## The footnote

Note 2, "A. p. p. Kl. Ol. b, 7.", is kept as printed in both texts. The
article's own usage expands it (scan 030: "Archiwum państwowe poznańskie.
Kl. Ołobok, C. 1."; scan 036: "Archiwum państw. pozn. Kl. Ol. C. 17b."):
**A. p. p.** = Archiwum państwowe poznańskie (the state archive at Poznań),
**Kl. Ol.** = Kl. Ołobok, the convent's files, bundle b, no. 7. The other
notes on the page ("Akta konsystorskie gn. Ol. lit. O. N. 6.") are the files
of the consistory of Gniezno ("gn." = gnieźnieńskie), Ołobok, letter O,
no. 6. This expansion is Claude's, from the article's own notes.

## The English

Terms per the edition: **złp.** → Polish gulden (glossary); **prowizya** →
interest (the precedent is iiihamdaiiinr12366-006, "prowizyami" →
"interest"); **regulacya** → the regulation (the same document: "Akcie
Regulacyi Hypoteki" → "the regulation of the mortgage register"), so "w
hipotece nowej regulacyi" → "in the mortgage of the new regulation" (the
mortgage register set up under the new mortgage regulation). "PP.
Cysterskom" is dative: "lost to the Cistercian Nuns". Names keep the Polish
forms: Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Ignacy Trzciński, Karol Bogumił Weygel (the
letters spell "Weigel"), Sobolewski.
