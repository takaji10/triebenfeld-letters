# Pachoński (1982): notes

## How it came in (2026-10-19)

From the editor's folder "Books_2": "Antoni Ostroróg-Prusimski (Polski
Słownik Biograficzny T.27).md". The file holds the Polish entry with its
bibliography and the author's name (its first 25 lines), then a machine
English. `original.md` is the Polish half, unchanged, with the file's own
page marks ("\[582\]", "\[583\]", "\[584\]"; the backslashes are the file's).
The marks fall where the printed pages turn, mid-sentence ("już" / "jako")
and mid-bibliography ("Bonnefons" / "A.").

The editor dates the entry 1982 ("1982 is fine to use"); volume 27 of the
PSB came out in parts in 1982-1983.

## The English is Claude's, not the file's

The machine English in the editor's file was read against the Polish and is
not used: it is wrong in substance in several places, among them
- "his first wife being Tomaszowa Wierusz-Kowalska": the Polish says that
  his mother Wiktoria Malczewska was by her first marriage the wife of Tomasz
  Wierusz-Kowalski;
- "starost of Nieszczewicki": the adjective for the place, Nieszczewice;
- "voivode of Brzesko-Kujawski" for "wojewodzic brzesko-kujawski": Józef
  Dąmbski was the voivode's son, not the voivode.

`english.md` was written afresh from the Polish (2026-10-19). Polish terms
are glossed in brackets where the reader needs them (the Naczelnik, the
emphyteutic tenure of the starostwo, LB for the baptismal books). The
bibliography's connecting words are translated; titles stay as printed.

## Slips in the source, kept

- "zjeździć" (p. 583): for "zjeździe", at the assembly.
- "kaszt, kowalskiego" (p. 583): a comma where the abbreviation's full stop
  is meant.
- "Americain" (p. 584), in the title of the author's own article: for
  America.
- "Bie-ganowo" (p. 584): a word broken at a line end and not joined.

The English shows the slip in square brackets where a reader comparing the
two would look for it.

## Points for the reader, held against the edition

1. **His father Stefan, his grandfather Krzysztof.** The PSB makes Antoni
   (born 4 March 1744) the son of Stefan, owner of Kamionna, Kolno and
   Trąbczyn. This agrees with the edition: the boundary decree of 1775 (Konin
   Gr.145) calls Krzysztof the Starost's grandfather, and Konin Gr.118,
   document 23, names Stefan as his father; Żychliński (1885) gives the same
   line.
2. **His uncle Paweł.** The PSB has him inherit "from his paternal uncle
   Paweł" a fortune with Kolno and Prusinów. The edition has Paweł the
   chamberlain (`prusimski-pawel`, d. 1754) as his paternal uncle; Żychliński
   puts the chamberlain a generation higher (see Żychliński's notes, point 3).
3. **Michalina's second marriage "from 1809".** The edition's `prusimska`
   and `miaczynski` say "about 1814". The Miączyński entry of the Złota
   księga, vol. 13 (Żychliński 1891, p. 221), gives "August 1809", with the
   marriage settlement, and agrees with the PSB. Asked in
   `docs/NEEDS_CONFIRMATION.md`; the bios are not changed without the
   editor's word.
4. **Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen**, "the holder of the estate confiscated from P.",
   and the efforts after 1806 to have the estates given back to the family:
   these are the grant of 1796 and the restitution of July 1807 in the
   edition's documents (the AGAD holdings `agad11740273` and `agad1174016`).
5. **His wife.** The PSB has Marianna Dąmbska go with him to Venice at the
   end of 1794. The edition has Michalina suing in 1800 for the Brzyce
   estates as her "late mother's" property, so Marianna was dead by 1800.
   The two agree; the PSB gives no year of her death. The dispatch from
   Venice of 14 December 1796 (Nr. 1414, document 2) already speaks of
   Prusimski's grief at "la mort de son epouse", which is against
   Żychliński's year 1797 (Żychliński 1891, notes, point 2).
6. **"Deutsches Zentralarchiv w Merseburgu: Rep. ,7, C 14 M 1".** The files
   once at Merseburg are now in the Geheimes Staatsarchiv in Berlin. The
   citation may be the file the edition has as I. HA GR, Rep. 7 C, Nr. 1414
   ("Anton Prusimski Venice Residence", the dispatch from Venice and the
   letters on it, 1796-97), but the signature as printed cannot be matched
   to it with certainty. Asked in `docs/NEEDS_CONFIRMATION.md`.
7. **"Hoym, 9 May 1796"**: the PSB says Hoym pointed out his activity at
   Venice on that day. The edition's Nr. 1414 begins in December 1796; the
   letter of May is not in it.

## Links to the documents (written 2026-10-19, `literature/links.yml`)

All four from p. 583:
- `pachonski1982-commission`: the Sejm's commissions over the estates,
  Trąbczyn among them, to Konin Gr.145, the commission's decree of 1775 on
  the boundary between Trąbczyn and Łukomia.
- `pachonski1982-confiscation`: the confiscation after 1794 and Hohenlohe
  as holder, to I. HA GR, Rep. 7 C, Nr. 3570, document 1 (Hoym's proposal
  of 1796 and the cabinet order). The same file gives Colonel von Sanitz
  Pawłów, Dzwonów and Beerenbusch, three of the estates the PSB names
  among those the Sejm's commissions were to settle.
- `pachonski1982-venice`: Venice and Hoym, to Nr. 1414, documents 1 to 3.
- `pachonski1982-restitution`: the efforts after 1806 to have the estates
  given back, to the AGAD holdings `agad11740273` and `agad1174016` (July
  1807).
