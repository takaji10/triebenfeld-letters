Income in the Years 1550-1650", *Acta Poloniae Historica* 18 (1968), pp.
75-98, translated by Ewa Tymowska. RCIN's record: creator "Mączak, Antoni

## The text

The PDF's text layer is OCR with many faults and was not taken over. Every
page was rendered at 300 dpi, cut in three bands and read; the reading was
then compared word by word with the OCR (nothing was left out). The figures
of Tables 2 and 4 were checked by their own sums.
- By paragraph; running heads, page numbers, signature marks (6, 7) and RCIN's
  stamp left out; italics not marked (in Table 2 they set the percentages
  apart from the lasts: the note under the table says which is which).
- Note marks as "[^n]"; Table 3's lettered notes as raised letters (ᵃ, ᵇ).
- The print's slips kept: "wirtschafliche" (n. 1), "von Bath" beside "van
  Bath", "cummulative", "statical", "differenciated", "landlors",
  "achievments", "the sale or rye", "rythm", "according the figures",
  "Lustrácja", "oligarchial", "elswhere", "contituted", "Transilvanian",
  "cristalizing", "heberdashery", "The questions is", "quater", "consited",
  "nean", "200 Rdl." (for 200 thousand), "merchants'profits", "cautios",
  "enetered", "a sometimes mason's", "Krakowie a latach", "discused",
  "Ellehoj", "22 thous,", and 1663 in Table 6, which the text gives as 1653.
- Damaged print read as the word it is: the comma after "granen" (n. 6), "be"
  (p. 90).
- In the small type of the notes the stroke of ł does not always show at this
  resolution; Polish names are given as the body text prints them.
- The notes of p. 88 seem not to answer their marks; given as printed.
- Words running over a page end given whole where they begin: "prosperous"
  (77), "constituted" (86), "especially" (89), "existence" (94).
- Pages 81 (map) and 97 (chart of prices and wages) are shown as pictures,
  rendered from the PDF; their captions and labels are text.

## How it bears on the edition

It names neither Trąbczyn, nor Konin, nor the Olęders. Nearest: Great Poland
exported grain "at least from the regions near the Vistula, and a little along
the Warta river" (p. 93); Table 1 gives "Cuyavia and east Great Poland" 11 to
20 per cent of the grain at Włocławek; "the intensive agricultural and dairy
farming of the Dutch colonists" of Royal Prussia (p. 95), whose name the
Olęders carried. "Nowa Wieś" is a common place name (the editor): no link is
made to a Nowa Wieś unless the text means the Trąbczyn estate's.
