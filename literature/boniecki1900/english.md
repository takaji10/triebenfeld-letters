**The Chełmski family, arms Ostoja**

# 2.976.162-176

¶ Here the pedigree of the Chełmskis breaks off, so that it cannot be said with certainty from whom the members of the family given below descend. In an old pedigree I find that Mikołaj, son of Marcyan II, had a son Jerzy, who signed the election of Jan Kazimierz with the province of Kalisz. By Urszula Cielecka he is said to have had sons: Klemens Stefan and Mikołaj, who left no issue, and Krzysztof Ścibor and Andrzej Ścibor. The by-name Ścibor, borne by Jan, Master of the Hunt of Kraków, and then by Krzysztof, Master of the Hunt of Kraków, Andrzej, Standard-bearer of Sandomierz, and Marcyan, Camp Master, suggests rather that they were sons of Jan, and not of the Jerzy above.

# 2.976.177-182

¶ Krzysztof Ścibor made a deed of life interest with his wife, Teofila Ossowska, daughter of Aleksander and of Katarzyna née Koniecpolska, before the municipal court of Nowy Korczyn in 1676. He was the owner of Śladków, in the district of Wiślica, and of part of Łukomia in the province of Kalisz. In the acts he was called Master of the Hunt of Kraków and royal colonel. His widow entered a protest in the municipal court of Kraków in 1703 against her husband's brother Andrzej, that he alone held the estate of Łukomia in the province of Kalisz, half of which ought to belong to her son.

# 2.976.183, 196-198, 209-210

¶ His son Adam Ścibor, ensign of an armoured company, […]

¶ Ignacy, son of Adam, usually styled in the acts Cupbearer of Różan, […]

In 1766 Ignacy sold his share of the family estates of Łukomia, Łomów, Mielno and Bukowe to his cousin on his father's side, Stanisław Chełmski.

# 2.976.211-220

¶ Andrzej Ścibor Chełmski signed the election of Augustus II with the province of Kraków; he was called in the acts in 1690 Vice-Steward of Sandomierz and colonel of the Crown army, and in 1692 Standard-bearer of Sandomierz. He died in 1697, and Morsztyn became Standard-bearer after him. By his wife, née Pałuska, he had sons, Franciszek, who left no issue, and Józef, married to Helena Zielonacka, and daughters, Anna, wife of Józef Tworzyański, and Teresa, wife of Jan Czarnocki, Master of the Hunt of Chełm. A judgment of the Tribunal at Piotrków of 1748, given in the suit over the estate of Łukomia, shows us this.

# 2.976.221-225

Józef had a daughter Katarzyna and sons, Heliodor and Stanisław, as an agreement entered in the land-court acts of Wiślica in 1756 and a cession in the municipal court of Nowy Korczyn in 1768 show. In 1755 a commission was appointed to fix the boundary between their estate of Tyszaków (as printed, "Tyszakowa"), in the district of Konin, and the royal estate of Jadamiesz (Sig. 27 f. 349). Heliodor was parish priest of Jaksice in 1757 (Sig. 27 f. 391) and canon of Poznań in 1765 (Zs. Wiślic. of 1765).

# 2.976.226-240

Stanisław, Treasurer of Wschowa in 1779, who bought Łukomia from Ignacy Chełmski in 1766 (Zap. Oryg. Nowokorcz. 15 f. 418), had by Dorota Skórzewska a son, Franciszek Ksawery, married to Małgorzata Mielęcka, daughter of Andrzej, a general of the former Polish army, and of Balbina née Walchnowska. Their son Stanisław, president of the District Directorate of the Land Credit Society at Kalisz, died in 1877. By Marya Cielecka he left a daughter, Melania, wife of Romuald Podczaski, owner of Kamocka Wola, and a son, Władysław, owner of Łukomia, married in 1860 to Bronisława Tokarska. Their sons: Jan, born 21 October 1863, and Zygmunt, born 7 March 1867.
