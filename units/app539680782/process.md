### Division and dating

The holding is one document of two pages. It is dated from its own dateline: 14 March 1820.

### Translation

The English was written in a working session under the same rules as the other holdings: every figure, name and mark of doubt carried across, and names in their settled forms. The garbled stretches are kept or bracketed, never guessed. The money and measure abbreviations follow the standing forms; whether "6 gl." means gulden or Groschen in this document is still open.

The summary was written in English and German. Its 18 statements were then checked against the document, and all are supported. One was corrected in the draft: the one-year term belongs to the contract the board issues, not to the teacher's obligation.
