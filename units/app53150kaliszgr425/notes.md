# 53/15/0/-/Kalisz Gr.425 (APP)

One document on three pages: the adjournment of 28 September 1776 by five
commissioners in the boundary cause, with the record of its entry at Kalisz
on 30 September. Polish with Latin. Added on 2026-10-07 in the fourth
Prusimski-era batch. Status `translated`. Built and verified locally, **not
published**. How it was made is in the docstring of `intake/holding.py`.

- **Who these five are.** None signed the decree of the commission (Konin
  Gr.145: Dąmbski, Brzeziński, Stanisław Dąmbski, Kwilecki, Umiński,
  Gurczyński). Radoński is the surveyor to whom the other part of the
  divided court gave the pen in 1775 (same decree, English page 8). The
  holding's page says so and no more; that they are "Chełmski's side" is
  the natural reading but the document does not say it.
- **Folds** set by eye (the finder was off on these photographs).
- **Left as the editor has them:** "kłodamy Limituiemy", "przysztym",
  "Zieździe Mieli".
- Stanisław Otto-Trąmpczyński, commissioner, is of the family in the
  index (`trampczynski`); this document is added to that entry.
