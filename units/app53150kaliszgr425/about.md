One document of three pages in a book of the castle court of Kalisz for 1776, kept in the State Archive in Poznań (Archiwum Państwowe w Poznaniu). It is an adjournment made on 28 September 1776 by five commissioners in the boundary cause between Prusimski and Chełmski. It is in Polish, with a Latin record of entry. <!-- context -->

### Historical background

The Polish parliament appointed a commission in 1774 to end the boundary dispute between Trąbczyn and Łukomia. It opened on the ground on 13 September 1775. In its first days the commissioners fell out over who should keep the record, and the court divided. The part that sat under the president, Ludwik Dąmbski, walked the boundary, ruled for Prusimski, and put the rest of its business off to 21 May 1776; its decree was entered in the court records at Brześć Kujawski on 23 September 1776 with six signatures: [[unit:app53170koningr145]]. <!-- context -->

The five commissioners who sign this adjournment are not among those six. One of them, Tomasz Radoński, is the boundary surveyor to whom the other part of the divided court had given the pen in 1775. Five days after the decree was entered at Brześć Kujawski, they treat the cause as still open before them and adjourn it. <!-- context -->

### Contents

**The adjournment.** The commissioners appointed by the Estates of the Commonwealth to judge the cause between Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, heir of Trąbczyn, and Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, heir of Łukomia, declare that they cannot bring it to an end by a final judgment at the term now falling due. The ordinary parliament is sitting at Warsaw and the Crown Tribunal at Piotrków, and some of them have business at the one and some at the other. The term in question had itself come from the adjournment made in the decree of 13 September of the year before. They put the meeting off to the first Monday after Epiphany 1777 and keep for the parties the final term for appearing before the commission on the ground of Łukomia. The act is done at their residences on 28 September 1776 [[1]].

**The signatures.** Five commissioners sign, each with his seal in red wax: Stefan Leszczyc Zielonacki, deputy judge of the Kalisz land court; Maciej Leszczyc Mierzewski; Tomasz Radoński, boundary surveyor of Kalisz; Stanisław Jabłkowski; and Stanisław Otto-Trąmpczyński [[1]].

**The entry.** On Monday 30 September 1776 Antoni Czyżewski brought the act to the castle office at Kalisz to be entered, and the original was sewn into the book [[1]].

### Form and language

Leaf 497 is the original, a smaller sheet than the book, written on both sides, signed and sealed; leaf 498 has the office's Latin record. The Polish is in the spelling of the writer. One word at the end of a line runs into the fold of the book and its ending is supplied in square brackets. The back of the sheet has a docket, and the next leaf a register note, which are not transcribed. <!-- context -->
