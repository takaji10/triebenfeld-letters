Though most of the transactions were concluded within the same social class yet the resulting balance of property transactions is quite obvious. The Tęczyńskis family, once main exporters of grain from Little Poland, died out in 1636, yet their estates passed into the hands of several big families and there grew a new and even bigger property complex belonging to Stanisław Lubomirski.²⁷ Table 7 presents the results of the buying out of the estates of lesser gentry in the voivodship of Cracow by the big landlords.
The indicated trends of changes in the property structure were characteristic for most regions of the country. They did not occur where there was no germ, no cristalizing nucleus in the form of a large estate complex, e.g. in Masovia or in Royal Prussia, though even there we can trace similar endeavours by local and infiltrating nobility.
