No. 39: The Excellent Joannes Traholtz, Merchant and Resident of Witkowo, being sound of mind and body, by way of declaration \[T.N.1\], departing from \[T.N.2\], acknowledged: that he himself, wholly and in full, his own proper monetary sums — one, namely, of one thousand and fifty-three *imperials* \[T.N.3\], claimed against Paweł Prusimski for trees purchased by the present recognisant, the Honourable Traholtz, in the pine forests and woodlands of Trąbczyn, by means of a contract drawn up in respect of the purchase of those same trees, written out for the aforesaid late Honourable Prusimski, worked out from the personal funds and effort of the Honourable Traholtz, the recognisant (although the same late Honourable Prusimski had once received from the same recognisant full satisfaction in accordance with the tenor of the aforesaid contract) — intercepted in the said pine forests and woodlands of Trąbczyn by the said late Honourable Prusimski from the same recognisant by violent means, and sold to the Honourable Simon, Merchant of Stettin, by the same Honourable Prusimski, received in this sum (as attested by the written *reversale* \[T.N.4\] of the same Honourable Simon, Merchant of Stettin, commonly called an *Auszug* \[T.N.5\]) —

and then a second sum of one thousand five hundred and thirteen florins, expended partly by the same Honourable Traholtz, the present recognisant, in the making of sawn goods \[T.N.6\], that is, nine stacks of *Klapek* \[T.N.7\], commonly called *Rynek* \[T.N.8\], likewise intercepted by the late Honourable Antoni Prusimski, full brother of the same late Honourable Paweł Prusimski, following the extraction of the pine forest and woodlands between themselves \[i.e. the parties\] in dispute and under investigation, intercepted in this pine forest — expended partly by the recognisant Honourable Traholtz, and partly placed for the benefit and interest of the aforementioned late Honourable Paweł Prusimski, specified in a separate register written out by the present recognisant, and accepted by the said late Honourable Antoni Prusimski for these sawn goods, and disposed of for \[T.N.9\] and for his own benefit — these and other sums, set out in writings and documents and handwritten registers,

and also explained by various annotations, called \[T.N.10\] — pertaining to the recognisant by right of legacy \[T.N.11\], reserving and excepting nothing to himself and his successors from the aforesaid sums hereafter — into the person of the Right Honourable Stanisław Chełmski, as one paying these sums by his own proper right to the recognisant and fully satisfying \[him\] for the foregoing, and \[into the person\] of his successors — with full, total, and entire right, dominion, proprietorship, and competence, together with all and any \[rights\] pertaining to these sums and explaining these sums, with the legal process pertaining thereto instituted against the successors of the Prusimskis — ceded, conferred, transferred, conveyed, and assigned; as he cedes, confers, transfers, conveys, and assigns by the present act \[T.N.12\], granting \[T.N.13\] this his personal declaration freely acceding to the foregoing, upon the capital \[T.N.14\] from the interest arising from those same principal sums.

Jan Traholtz, by his held hand \[T.N.15\]

**Translator's Notes**

\[T.N.1\] Manifestatione: the ablative *Manifestatione* is grammatically unusual as a free-standing ablative of manner. The standard formula would be *Manifestando* (gerund). Translated as "by way of declaration."

\[T.N.2\] e\[dem?\]ce\~: unresolved. The formula *Recedendo e...* suggests *e hac Curia* (from this court) or *e hoc loco* (from this place). The lacuna is suppressed in the translation body.

\[T.N.3\] Imperialium: *imperial*, a German silver coin (*Reichsthaler*) in common use in commercial transactions in Greater Poland in this period.

\[T.N.4\] reversale: a written acknowledgement or receipt; a standard legal instrument. Retained in italics as a legal term.

\[T.N.5\] Auszug: German, literally "extract" or "summary" — here the vernacular name for the written *reversale* issued by Simon, the Stettin merchant. An *Auszug* in German commercial usage denoted a written statement of account or extract from a ledger serving as a receipt.

\[T.N.6\] secaminum: genitive plural of *secamen* (sawn goods, lumber). Translated as "sawn goods."

\[T.N.7\] Klapek: a Polish or German trade term for a type of sawn timber product. Possibly from German *Klappen* (boards, flaps) or a related term. Left in the original pending full identification.

\[T.N.8\] Rynek: the vernacular Polish name for *Klapek*, introduced by *vulgo* (commonly called). Polish *rynek* ordinarily means "market" or "market square," but in a timber trade context may denote a market-grade or market-standard cut of timber. Left in the original pending full identification.

\[T.N.9\] proque sisce\[?\]: *sisce\[?\]* remains unresolved. The phrase *proque sisce ac commodo suo* suggests "for \[something\] and for his own benefit." Translated with the unresolved word suppressed.

\[T.N.10\] alisculi\~ vocitatis: *alisculi\~* contracted remains unresolved. *Vocitatis* \= "called/named." The phrase *variis connotationibus alisculi\~ vocitatis* \= "by various annotations called \[something\]." Flagged pending resolution.

\[T.N.11\] jure legati: "by right of legacy/bequest." Traholtz holds these claims as a legatee and is now assigning them to Chełmski.

\[T.N.12\] actas praesenti Da\[m/s?\]: *actas praesenti Dato* (by the present act given) is the most likely reading, with the final letter of *Dato* uncertain. Translated as "by the present act."

\[T.N.13\] concedens \[?\]: *concedens* (granting) is followed by an unresolved word. Translated as "granting" with the lacuna suppressed.

\[T.N.14\] fun\[d?\]u\[m?\] provisionibus: *fundum provisionibus* \= "upon the capital, together with the interest." *Fundum* denotes the principal sum as the base on which interest (*provisiones*) accrues.

\[T.N.15\] ręką trzymaną: Polish, literally "by his held hand" — the standard Polish formula for a signature made by guiding the signatory's hand, used when the signatory signs with physical assistance.
