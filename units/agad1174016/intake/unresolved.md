- "Maniezki": the scan may have "Manieczki", the usual name of Wybicki's
  estate. Left as transcribed.
- A word is struck out after "w Kopii Autentyczney"; not transcribed.
- "Poznańskim,": the word is broken over the page ("Poznań-", catchword
  "skim", then whole on page 48). It stands once, at the foot of page 47.
- "podpisano" before the names and "(LS)" beside them are not transcribed,
  here or in document 2.

- "z Prusińskich": the clerk's form; the scan shows no accent on the n. Her
  maiden name is Prusimska.
- "Protokuł": the heading is in large display letters.
- The catchword "Pozwolenie" at the foot of the page opens the next entry and
  is not transcribed.
