### Scans

The archive supplied one image, which shows two facing pages of the bound volume. It was cut at the fold into two pages. Both are kept whole, although the entry begins only halfway down the first.

### Transcription

The editor transcribed the entry and supplied it by paragraph, not line by line, with the clerk's abbreviations filled out. The editor also transcribed the heading of the court's sitting from leaf 638. That leaf is not among the images, so the heading is quoted in the description of this holding and is not a page of the edition.

### Check against the scan

An AI model (Claude) read the whole entry word for word against the scan and corrected thirteen readings. Several were settled by the entry before it on the same page, which uses the same formulas written out a little more fully. Two words that had been left unread were read: the court's summoner, and what he was assigned to do. In three places the scan does not decide and the editor's reading stands. Each change is logged with its reason.

### Division and dating

The entry is one document. It has no date of its own. Its year is taken from the heading of the sitting it belongs to, which opened on 30 May 1763; the day of this decree within the sitting is not known.

### Translation

The English is the editor's own translation and was not made again. It was changed in six places where the Latin had been corrected. The largest is the middle of the decree: the earlier reading had the court adding a roll to the record and going on to a fourth session, where the scan has it assigning its summoner to speak the oath to the witnesses and going on to the reading of their testimony; and it is Prusimska who sent the potash-burners off. Two of the editor's seven notes are kept. The other five commented on readings that the scan does not have. An automatic check compared the terms of the two texts.

### Summary

The summary was written in German from the corrected Latin and translated into English. Each of its ten statements was then checked against the document. All held.
