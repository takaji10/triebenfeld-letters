### Historical background

The Kuryer Polski was a newspaper printed in Warsaw. Each issue gathers short reports sent in from towns of the Commonwealth and from abroad, each headed with the place and the date it was written, "Z Kalisza die 5. Decemb:", from Kalisz, 5 December. <!-- context -->

Paweł Prusimski, the King's chamberlain, held Trąbczyn before his nephew Antoni, the later Starost of Niszczewice. An inspection of the estate made for his widow Katarzyna in July 1754 already calls him not long dead: [[unit:app53170koningr136]]. Antoni Prusimski calls him his paternal uncle in a protest of 1778: [[unit:app53170koningr117]]. <!-- context -->

### Contents

The report, sent from Kalisz on 5 December 1754, describes the funeral held at Trąbczyn, "five miles from Kalisz", on the hereditary estate of the Prusimski family, for the late Paweł Prusimski, Chamberlain of His Royal Majesty. Many related houses came, and the Cistercians of the convent of Ląd. Their abbot, Głowiecki, had been invited to sing the High Mass, but an unexpected frost kept him from crossing the Warta. The sermon was preached by the prefect of the schools of the Jesuit college at Kalisz, and the funeral oration after the procession was given by Antoni Prusimski, the dead man's own nephew. At the order of the widow, Katarzyna Prusimska, born Rozdrażewska, exequies with vigils and many Masses were held the same day in the monasteries nearby, at Pyzdry, Konin and Kaźmierz, because the parish church of Trąbczyn had not room enough [[1]].

The newspaper writes the family "Prusiński" and the convent "Łędzki"; the edition gives them as Prusimski and Ląd. Kaźmierz is probably Kazimierz Biskupi, near Konin. <!-- context -->

### Form and language

Printed Polish of 1754 in roman type, with italic for the dateline and for Latin words, the long s (ſ) and accented á. The report fills the lower half of the left-hand column and three lines of the right; the rest of the page carries other news, which is not transcribed. <!-- context -->
