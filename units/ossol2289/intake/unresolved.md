# Unresolved readings, B. Ossol. rkp. 2289 II (document 1)

The editor's corrected reading of 2026-11-01 left no doubt marks in the
transcription. What follows are the places where the reading rests on the
copyist's formula rather than on clear letterforms, recorded so a later
pass knows where the ground is softer. The transcription stands as the
editor read it.

- **zachoway, chciey, przyimiy** (the closing sentence): three verb forms
  of difficult letterforms, read by the sentence's formulas (*zachowaj*,
  *chcę i*/chciej, *przyjmę i*/przyjmij). The sense is anchored; the
  exact spellings are the editor's.
- **strzedz** ("strzedz się należy"): read for *strzec się*; the form
  stands as written.
- **oczekuiącemi**: the participle's ending as the editor read it
  (*oczekuiącymi/oczekuiącemi*); the copyist's -emi is kept.
- **"o też"** ("którzy o też wraz ze mną ... walczyli"): as written; a
  period form of "o to też"/"o tę samą [sprawę]" is possible.
- **Sz[anowny] Ob[ywatel]u**: the superscript abbreviation, expanded by
  the editor. The expansion "Szanowny" is the editor's; the abbreviation
  itself (Sz. Ob.) is certain.
- **P[aryża]**: the superscript abbreviation of the dateline, expanded by
