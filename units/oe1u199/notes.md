# Oe 1 U 199

One document on 15 pages, 1805: Friedrich Wilhelm III's confirmation of the
family majorat and fideicommiss founded by Friedrich Ludwig, Prince of
Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, reciting the certified extract of his will.

## Provenance

Hohenloher Zentralarchiv Neuenstein (HZAN), Oe 1 U 199. Ten PDFs, one per
capture (0002-0011), exported and cropped before this unit was set up; the
fifteen text pages (0003_b to 0010_b) were taken from raw_dir/processed/.
Left out: 0002, the front cover in red leather, photographed with the hanging
wax seal and its silver seal box (worth showing as a front image one day);
0003_a, a blank endpaper; 0011, the back cover.

## The document

- `confirmation`, Berlin, 19 December 1805 (dates: read). "Zehn Tausend Thaler" at the head is the charter's fee or stamp value.
- The charter opens with the King's full title, then recites the Breslau Gouvernements-Gericht's certified extract of the will (7 October 1805): § XII the Breslau houses and the park at Scheitnig; § XIII Byttkow, joined to the Sławięcice works, with the order to finish the coke blast furnace and keep Baildon; § XIV Koschentin with Boronow, Olschin, Harbultowitz and Czyschowa; § XV the South Prussian estates; § XXIV who inherits if the male line fails (Adelheid, Emilie, Auguste). Then the confirmation itself.
- Estates in focus: Zagórów, Trąbczyn, Betsche, Wieniec, Bątkowo, Koszęcin, Boronów, Olszyna, Cieszowa, Bytków, Sławięcice, Lasowice, Birawa, Stara Kuźnia.
