### Scans

The folder has fourteen photographs of this book, each showing two facing pages. Seven carry an entry that is transcribed; each of those was cut at the fold, and eight halves are shown whole. Seven photographs show entries that the editor did not transcribe and are not used.

### Transcription

The editor transcribed eight entries and supplied them by paragraph, with the Latin written out.

### Check against the scans

An AI model (Claude) read against the scans every heading and date line, four entries whole and most of three more, and corrected 48 readings. One entry and the first half of another were not read.

Those that change the sense: in the search of September 1784 a line had been passed over, and the office certifies that the report of citation does "not exist"; in Chełmski's second citation a phrase was restored, that Łomowo "belongs to the plaintiff's principal estate of Łukom"; the acts are charged as done at "your" order, Prusimski's, not his steward's; the citation asks that "the production of the men be ordered and an inquiry held", where two words had been left unread; and in his protest Prusimski offers "to clear himself", not to make amends. Each change is logged with its reason.

### Division and dating

Each entry is a document. Three are dated by the line "Actum in Conin" directly above them and one by the heading higher on its page. Four have no dated heading on a photographed page: two are dated to March 1783 from the citation they report and the next heading, one to May 1783 from the heading three leaves before it, and Prusimski's protest to October 1783 from the land court's decree that cites it. Each says so.

### Translation

The English is the editor's own translation and was not made again. It was changed in ten places where the text had been corrected.

### Summaries

The summaries were written in German from the Latin and translated into English. Each of their 27 statements was then checked against its document. All held.
