## Words

- **Line 5: "Conte~".** The person before whom the citation was left on the
  table. The session's candidate is Contubernali, "household member": the
  parallel formulas of Konin Gr. 117 fill this slot with a person of the
  house ("praesente Economo" - the steward; "praesente virgine" - the
  maidservant). The editor is not sure. Left as written; the English carries
  it as "the Conte~ [as written] of the cited party".
- **Lines 5-6: "de Ju¬ris [F?]irma".** No parallel anywhere in the corpus.
  The editor notes that Forma is as plausible a reading as Firma. Left as
  read, with its bracket.
- **Line 2: "eam", with a scribal mark above the final m.** The editor
  guessed "eadem". The session's reading is "eam", the citation: it is the
  pronoun the sentence needs (it governs "detulisse" and is picked up again
  by "eamque ibidem ... posuisse et reliquisse"), and "eadem", a nominative,
  has no grammatical place in the sentence. If the mark completes the word,
  "eamdem" (= eandem, the accusative, the period's common spelling) is the
  likeliest form. Left as read.
- **Line 3: "(inserat~)".** The editor confirms the word is "inserat"
  followed by a scribal abbreviation - an insertion mark in the record,
  standing before the citation's address. Kept in the transcription in the
  editor's parentheses, as its own line.

## The date and the place of the sitting

The entry has no date of its own - only "Feria Quinta proxima praeterita",
the Thursday next past, relative to the sitting. It is dated 1727, year
only, as the editor's provisional assignment from the volume's title,
1726-1727. The date of the sitting is to be read from the preceding pages of
the book, and the ruling then moves to `dates: sitting:`; the same reading
settles the place of the sitting, which is also left open (the Kalisz land
court sat round the palatinate, in the roczki). NEEDS_CONFIRMATION,
