# I. HA GR, Rep. 7 C, Nr. 3709 (GStA PK)

Not yet transcribed. One provisional document on 19 scans, plus the cover as
front matter, 1800-1802: the ministry file on Michalina Prusimska's complaints
in her case against the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen. Added 2026-10-01 so
that the scans are in the edition; the editor will supply transcriptions.

## Provenance

Geheimes Staatsarchiv Preußischer Kulturbesitz, Berlin, I. HA Geheimer Rat,
Rep. 7 C Südpreußen, Nr. 3709. The cover reads "Acta betr. die Beschwerden der
Michalina von Prusimska in Sachen wider den Fürsten von Hohenlohe Ingelfingen"
and gives the span "den 18ten Juny 1800" to "den 29 Januar 1802". 22 scans:
0001 the cover, the rest openings photographed flat. Left out on the editor's
instruction, as blank: 0006 and 0022. The other 20 were copied into
raw_dir/processed/ as <capture>_a.jpg.

**Pages (editor, 2026-10-01).** An opening with one blank side is cut at the
fold to its written side; an opening with writing on both sides is NOT cut and
stays one page. This differs from Nr. 12765, where such openings were split.
- kept the right (`_a2`): 0002, 0003, 0004, 0005, 0007, 0011, 0012, 0014, 0016,
  0017, 0018, 0020
- kept the left (`_a1`): 0015, 0019, 0021
- whole (`_a`): 0008, 0009, 0010, 0013, and the cover 0001
The editor placed the fold on eight of the fifteen; the other seven were cut
at the proposed line, which the editor left in place. The blank halves are in
`processed/_blank_halves/`.

## What the scans show (not read closely)

Seen while setting the unit up, from the page images only:
- 0002: a petition to the King in a clerk's fair hand, with the Poznań
  government's marks of June 1800 and signatures of its members.
- 0003, 0005: copies of royal orders dated Charlottenburg, June and July 1800,
  to the government at Poznań.
- 0004, 0016: reports of the government at Poznań (July and December 1800).
- 0007-0008, 0010: a letter of the Prince, signed, Breslau, 30 November 1800.
- 0009: a letter over two pages in another hand.
- 0010-0015, 0017-0019: the ministry's drafts, Berlin, December 1800.
- 0020-0021: a petition in French to the King, January 1802, signed by
  Prusimska.
Several openings have writing on both sides, and several on one side only.
