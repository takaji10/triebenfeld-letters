A file of the Prussian central administration for South Prussia, kept in the Geheimes Staatsarchiv Preußischer Kulturbesitz, Berlin. According to its cover it concerns the complaints of Michalina von Prusimska in her case against Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen (1746-1818), and runs from 18 June 1800 to 29 January 1802. The file has not yet been transcribed. Its pages are shown as scans only. <!-- context -->

### Historical background

In 1796 the King of Prussia granted Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen estates in South Prussia that had been confiscated from the Starost Prusimski after the uprising of 1794. Michalina Prusimska was his daughter. In 1807, after Prussia had ceded the province, a Polish commission transferred the estates to her. <!-- context -->

### Contents

The contents have not been read.
