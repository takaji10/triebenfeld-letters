A court file kept in the Archiwum Państwowe w Poznaniu (State Archive in Poznań). It concerns a hereditary lease of land in the Trąbczyn forest, granted in 1806 by Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen (1746-1818) through his agent to the settler Christoph Erbet. The edition treats the file as one document of 21 pages, dated 1806 to 1811. <!-- context -->

### Historical background

Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen held the Trąbczyn estates in South Prussia by a royal grant of 1796. As lord of the estates he maintained a patrimonial court, which sat at Mariantów and recorded contracts made with the settlers. His general agent, Peter Friedrich von Triebenfeld, let parcels of forest and waste land in hereditary lease: the lessee paid a sum on entry and a fixed annual rent, and held the land heritably. After 1807 the district belonged to the Duchy of Warsaw, and its courts worked in Polish. <!-- context -->

### Contents

The record of the court, dated 31 March 1806, sets out the contract. Triebenfeld, as authorised agent of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, grants Christoph Erbet of the Trąbczyn Hauland one Hufe, Magdeburg measure, in the Trąbczyn forest for 100 Rthl entry money and an annual rent of 30 Rthl [[1]]. The first three years are free of rent, and the first payment, of 15 Rthl, falls due at Christmas 1809 [[1]]. The court's order of 23 May 1806 directs that the contract be issued [[1]].

Filed with the contract are the general power of attorney given to Triebenfeld on 19 February 1805, in a certified copy, and the issued contract with its covering notes to both parties [[1]]. A letter of 1811, in Polish, records that an official, Hawich, handed the file to the peace court of the Konin district and asked that costs of 9 Rthl 18 dgl be recovered from Erbet [[1]].

### Form and language

The file is in German, except the letter and bill of costs of 1811, which are in Polish. One image in the archive's sequence is absent; the text continues without a gap. <!-- context -->
