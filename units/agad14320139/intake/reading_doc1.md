# Reading of document 1 (scans 0318_a, 0322_a)

Pastor E. Erdmann's annual report of 1882 on the parish of Zagórów. Read
by Claude from the page images and enlarged bands, 2026-11-11. Not yet
checked by a second reader.

## Things that hold for both pages

- The hand writes capital C and K almost alike (a loop like an "L").
  The words are transcribed in the spelling the context and the period
  call for: Kirche, Kinder, Kantorhaus, Küster, Kopeken, Kasse, Knaben,
  but Consistorial, Colonie, Confessionen, Confirmirten, Candidaten,
  Collekten, Consistorium. A second reader should look at whether the
  scribe meant to tell C and K apart.
- Kurrent u always carries a bow; it is not read as ü unless the word
  needs it (Augsburg, Zustand, Kirchenbesuch).
- At a line end the scribe divides a word with "=" or a comma-like
  stroke (über=, Administra=, ge, Unterstü,); written "-" as the rules
  ask.
- Latin script, not Kurrent: Zagórow, October, Michalinow, Warschau,
  Baptisten, Boerner, Pastor (signature), the figures.

## 0318_a (page 1)

Doubtful readings:

- Line 1: only a few very faint strokes on the right of the hole in the
  paper; read "einer" (as of "Seiner"), possibly "euer" (as of "Euer").
  The rest of the line is lost under the hole.
- Line 2: "Herrn" begins at the edge of the hole. The hole covers the
  left of lines 1 and 2; whether line 2 had words there (the address is
  stepped and centred) cannot be seen, so "[...]" stands for the hole.
- Lines 8 and 10: the last legible letters, before the burn, are read
  "Ho" (Kurrent H with its descender, then o), both times; the next lines
  begin "würden" and "den". Alternative for line 10: "Ew" or "zu".
- Line 10: the sign before 267 looks like "#" with a loop (the scribe's
  numero sign), written "№".
- Line 9: after "October" a faint mark at the burnt edge, perhaps a
  figure; not read.
- Line 12: "vorzustel" - the last two letters "el" faint.
- Line 14: "Außeres" - the A carries no umlaut mark of its own; the only
  mark is the bow of the u. Kept as written.
- Line 15: after "Kirche" a comma with a dot above it, read as ";". Could
  be a comma. "derselb[en?]": "en" in the burn stain.
- Line 16: "Pastora" then two small dots, perhaps "t".
- Line 17: "und i" then, after a gap, an n-like letter: "in", doubtful.
- Line 23: "Küster" - "ter" faint at the edge.
- Line 24: a "d" after "Zustand", at the burn.
- Line 25: after "sind" faint traces (a, n?), not read.
- Line 26: "Kreuzen" - K, r, e, u (bow), z with descender, en. "Zäunen"
  (fences) does not fit the letters (no descender in it, and the capital
  is the C/K loop, not Z). Second reader please.
- Line 28: "Taufen" - the T is followed by a small loop that could be an
  r ("Trau..."), but there is a descender (f) and no g: "Taufen" taken.
- Line 29: the two words after "Abendmahls-Verächter im" are not read.
  Letter by letter: b (or G), a, n, z (descender) - gap - u (bow), long s
  or h, d, then "." Neither "im Ganzen" nor any other reading fits all
  the letters; written "[banz usd?]". Highest priority for a second reader.
  "Zahl d": a "d" at the burn.
- Line 30: after "72" an ink blot; a comma may lie under it. "159" is
  followed by a long down-stroke, read as a comma (72 + 87 = 159).

Lines where a "[...]" may stand for nothing: lines 14 ("besitzt"),
21 ("in") and 23 ("Küster") end close to the burn with nothing visible
after them, and the next line continues the sentence; they have no
"[...]". Lines 18 ("Verhältniß") and 22 ("der") have blank-looking
stained paper after the last word before the burn and are given "[...]",
since the next line needs words that are not there (zu/der; Ge-).

Not text:

- A hole at the top left of the address, with lighter paper (another
  leaf) showing through it.
- At the right edge, level with line 4, a curled burnt scrap with a few
  pen strokes (an "f"-like stroke); not read, probably from the ends of
  lines 3 and 4.
- Below the last line: a long thin curved line (a hair or fibre on the
  scan, or a stray pencil line) and two pairs of small dots; not writing.
- Brown burn stains along the right edge and over lines 14-29 at the
  right; small burnt flakes at the left edge; a brown spot left of
  line 18.
- No page number, no catchword.
