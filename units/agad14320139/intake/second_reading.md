## Which page to trust least

0345_a and 0318_a are the least trustworthy, for different reasons.
0345_a has the one plainly wrong reading in the set: "ol die ersten",
which is "[...Gottes]diensten". It sits in the line that carries the
filial's mission collection, at the most damaged stretch of the left edge
(lines 1-6). On 0318_a the end of the report is the weak part. Line 29's
"im [banz usd?]" is an unread formula that the parallel page shows to be
"unbewußt". Line 30's figures stand in the transcription, but the
reader's own note gives the competing reading 72 / 87 / 159, so the
page's one sum is not settled in the record. Overall, I trust 0345_a's
top six lines and 0318_a's last two lines least. On 0322_a, "kirchliche"
for "häusliche" and the unmarked stroke before "4 Rubel 61" make it the
next to check.
