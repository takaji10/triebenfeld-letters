# Reading of document 2 (scans 0322_b, 0345_a)

Pastor E. Erdmann's annual report of 1882 on the filial Lissewo-Pyzdry.
Read by Claude from the page images, the enlarged bands and tighter crops
(contrast-stretched, and with the stain's background flattened on the
blue channel), 2026-11-11. Not yet checked by a second reader.

## Things that hold for both pages

- Compounds: the scribe joins them with a long stroke as long as his
  dash (General–Superinten..., Evangelisch–Augsburgisch..., Lissewo–Pyzdry,
  Abendmahls–Verächter, Pastor–Administrator). Written "-" as in
  document 1 and NEW_UNIT ("a hyphen stays only in compounds"); the
  punctuation dash after a full stop (".–") is "–".
- A word divided at a line end is written with "-" (Mis-, Superintenden-,
  befrie-, Bap-, Unter-, geson-, Administra-).
- The hand's umlaut mark and the bow over u look alike (a small hook).
  Read as ü only where the word needs it (Hochwürden, Küsterhaus, fünf,
  Bethäusern, übersandt); u elsewhere (Augsburgisch, Zustand, Kreuzen).
- C and K are written much alike, as in document 1: Consistorial,
  Communicanten, Confirmirten, Candidaten, Collekten, Consistorium, Casse;
  Kirche, Kinder, Kantoren, Kopeken, Knaben.
- "№" on 0322_b line 10 is an N with two crossbars, written against the
  figure: "№267".
- "30ten" (0322_b line 9): "ten" is raised and underlined three times.

  word (strokes seen, not read; left "[...]"). After "Zahl" the stained

- Line 27: "Summa[?]": the S is under a blot; m m clear, the last letter

  through it, not read); "sind" after it, then the edge.
- Line 30: "Flecken" (alternative "Flacken"; the town); "zählt[?]": no

Not text: brown water or scorch stains over the lower right (lines 23-30),

below line 30 at the middle; the burnt notch at the top and the folded
blank strip at the upper right (lines 2-3). No page number, no catchword,
no second hand.
