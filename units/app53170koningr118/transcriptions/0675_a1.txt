Decreti Condescensorialis Granicialis Inter bona Trąmpczyn et Łukom Prolati Oblata
Videatur hoc loco oblata Decreti Condescensorialis Granicialis Inter Illustrem Magnificum Antonium Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen~ et Magnificum Stanislaum Chełmski Thesauararium Vschovensem et alios Inter bona Trąmpczyn et Łukom cum descriptione scopulorum prolati in productis
Pro parte JM Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen~ Hollandri Traditio
Videatur hoc Loco pro parte J Magnificis Antonii Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen~ Honesti Martini Hunt Hollandri Trąmpczynen~ Traditio, per M Josephum Łukaszewicz Commissarum Palatinatus Majoris Poloniae peracta prout in productis extat.
