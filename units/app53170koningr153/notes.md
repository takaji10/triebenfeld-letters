# 53/17/0/-/Konin Gr.153 (APP)

Two documents on twenty-six pages: the decree of the boundary chamberlain
of Gniezno that marked the boundary of Trąbczyn and Łukom, 28 November to
3 December 1782, with the Tribunal's decree of 17 October 1782 inside it
(25 pages), and the delivery of the settler Martin Hunt (1 page). Latin.
Added on 2026-10-07 in the fifth Prusimski-era batch. Status `translated`.
Built and verified locally, **not published**.

**Read the docstring of `intake/holding.py` first**: it has the pages, the
two passes of the check, and the words corrected throughout.

- **This is the end of the boundary dispute**: the Tribunal's decree of 23
  January 1782 (not in the edition; told here), its confirmation on 17
  October 1782, and the mounds raised. The decree of January, the map of
  1775 with the signed line, and the acts of the two field offices of May
  and June 1782 are named and are not in the edition.
- **Fully checked** (2026-10-07, at the editor's word, after a light check
  the same day): every page read against the scans. 67 corrections in
  `holding.py` (ROWS), 106 more in `intake/full_check.json`, applied with
  `--full`. Neither is to be applied again.
- **"jurato", "condescensio", "camerarialia", "sipatio"**, and "-bus" for
  "-busque", corrected throughout (holding.py; WORKING_NOTES).
- **Górski.** The clerk writes "Gorski" and the transcription keeps it; the
  English and everything written about him has Górski (editor, 2026-10-07;
  rule `gorski-name` in `reference/english_forms.yml`).
- **Who the officers are.** Górski (Inowrocław, engaged by Prusimski) and
  Orłowski (Dobrzyń, engaged by Chełmski) are boundary chamberlains, as
  Jasiński of Gniezno is; the editor's English calls the office "Boundary
  Surveyor". Orłowski is sentenced to pay Prusimski, Górski to pay Chełmski
  and to a week in the tower.
- **The editor's `info.txt`** says the commissioner's court was "held on
  October 17, 1782 in the house of Olęder Marcin Hunt": 17 October is the
  Tribunal's decree; the act at Hunt's house is of 28 November.
- **The Olęder of the burnt house** is "Hollandus Joannes" on leaf 1395
  verso, and Hunt is "Martinus": whether Jan's burnt holding and Hunt's are
  the same place the text does not say plainly. The summary keeps them
  apart.
- **The sum** Chełmski is to pay is in words ("sex millium centum sexaginta
  novem florinorum grosorum quatuordecim"); the summary does not give a
  figure.
- **Left as the editor has them:** "Orławski" / "Orłowski"; "Borzysławski"
  under the Tribunal's decree (the scan may have "Bordysławski"); "lecto
  per Ordęga"; the gap after "ex officio" on leaf 1391 verso (the edge of
  the page); "docere de standi" (a word may be lost at the edge);
  "tribunalium" for "Tribli-um" on leaf 1400 verso; "ad praesens executiis"
  there. "Illustrissimus" for "Illris" is left as the editor writes it out,
  except in a row that touched the words.
- The Konin register's notes of both acts: Konin Gr.118, documents 28 and
  29; the first recovery of the settlers (6 March, entered 18 March 1782):
  document 11 there.
- Not indexed as people: Jasiński, Woronowski, Łukaszewicz, Górski,
  Orłowski, Sierakowski, Kęszycki, Lubstowski, Wybieralski, Maszewski,
  Hunt.
