# I. HA GR, Rep. 7 C, Nr. 3570 (GStA PK)

One document on 22 scans, plus two pages of front matter, 1796 (registry notes
of 1826): the Prussian ministry file on the donation charters of August 1796.

## Provenance

Geheimes Staatsarchiv Preußischer Kulturbesitz, Berlin, I. HA GR, Rep. 7 C,
Nr. 3570. 28 scans, most of them two-page spreads photographed open. Left out
on the editor's instruction: 0001, 0027 and 0028, and 0007 (two blank pages).
The rest (0002-0006, 0008-0026) were copied unchanged into
raw_dir/processed/ as <capture>_a.jpg and staged; a "page" here is a scan.

## Transcription

One file per scan. 0006, 0008 and 0009 arrived later the same day and replaced
their placeholders. Not transcribed, by the editor's choice (2026-09-30): 0013-0024, the
same formulaic charters for the other six officers, skipped as less relevant; each carries the placeholder line "(not transcribed)", so
the page and its scan are in the edition. **When a transcription arrives,
replace that page's placeholder in corpus.txt and run the correction passes on
the new lines (units/oe1bu9454/new_text_checklist.md).**

## The document

- `donation`, Berlin, 9 August 1796 (dates: read, the charter's dateline).
  A package headed by the Prince's donation charter (the editor counts a file
  as one document).
- Front matter (0002-0003): registry notes of November 1826 calling up the
  file over von Sanitz's claim for his lost South Prussian estates, and the
  file's title.
- 0004-0005: the Expediatur docket of 6-8 August; Hoym to Minister von der
  Reck, Breslau, 30 July 1796 (the charters can all follow one form, and
  "cum appertinentia" must go in); the copy of Hoym's report to the King,
  Warsaw, 14 July 1796, proposing for the Prince the estates confiscated from
  the insurgent Anton Prusimski: Kamionna (Klemen) and Kulm; Trąbczyn,
  Szetlewek, Nowawies, Łazy, Old and New Laski, Osiny and the Trąbczyn
  Hauland; Brzyce in Kuyavia.
- 0006: the end of Hoym's report: the officers' grants (Pawłów, Dzwonów and
  Beerenbusch to Sanitz, also from Prusimski; Duninów to Blücher; Strzelce to
  Dolffs; Dziejorzów, Borschków and Jakubie to Pontanus; Głuchów, Malawieś and
  Grochole to Holtzendorff; Lekarzewice to Larisch), signed Graf von Hoym.
- 0008: copy of the King's cabinet order to Hoym, Pyrmont, 23 July 1796,
  approving all seven grants, signed Friedrich Wilhelm (II).
- 0009: Goldbeck to Hoym, Berlin, 9 August 1796, sending the engrossed
  charters and the covering report for countersignature.
- 0010: the covering report of 9 August presenting the seven charters (the
  Prince; Sanitz, Blücher, Dolffs, Pontanus, Holtzendorff, Larisch).
- 0011-0012: the draft of the Prince's charter, "So geschehen Berlin, d. 9.
  August 1796", countersigned Goldbeck and Hoym.
- 0013-0024: not transcribed (probably the officers' charters).
- 0025: Hoym to Goldbeck, Breslau, 20 August 1796. 0026: the fair copy of the
  covering report, dated Berlin 9 and Breslau 20 August 1796.
