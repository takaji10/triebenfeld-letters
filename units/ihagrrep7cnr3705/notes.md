# I. HA GR, Rep. 7 C, Nr. 3705 (GStA PK)

One document on 17 scans, plus the title page, 1805: Triebenfeld's petition to
the King for the sequestration of the Olesnica estate.

## Provenance

Geheimes Staatsarchiv Preußischer Kulturbesitz, Berlin, I. HA GR, Rep. 7 C,
Nr. 3705; the first holding in this edition from the Secret State Archives.
18 scans: 0001 is the file's title page ("Acta wegen des von dem Fürsten zu
Hohenlohe Oehringen verErbpachteten Guts Olesnica"); 0002-0018 are two-page
spreads photographed open.

## Transcription

## The document

- `letter`, Breslau, 18 September 1805 (dates: read). Triebenfeld, as general
  plenipotentiary, writes to the King in the Prince's first person.
- On 22 February 1804 the Prince let Olesnica, part of the Trąbczyn and
  Zagórów lordships, in hereditary lease to Johann Giese, Johann Bagans and
  Friedrich Gietzinger: 20,000 rt entry money in two halves, 2,000 rt a year,
  sub-letting only with his consent. They paid the first half out of the
  sub-lessees' money, then stopped paying. The Chamber refused to confirm
  the contract under the new cabinet order. Three suits were brought
  (Criminal-Rath Mondro). The Kalisch government refused sequestration on
  13 and 20 August 1805, and the petition answers both resolutions point by
  point from the Allgemeines Landrecht and the Gerichtsordnung, asking the
  King to call in the files and order the sequestration.
- The marginal letters (A)-(H) are enclosures that are not in this file.

## How it was prepared (the public text before 2026-10-30)

The editor ruled the tab too detailed: it now tells only what an AI decided that affected the text (`prepared:` in unit.yml). The full account, kept here:

### Scans

The archive photographed the file as eighteen images: a title page and seventeen openings of two facing pages. The openings were not cut, because the transcription treats each opening as one piece. A page here is therefore one opening.

### Transcription

The German text was transcribed by an AI system and supplied by the editor, one file for each scan.

### Corrections

An AI model (Claude) proposed corrections to the transcription. A word was changed only where an independent witness supported the change, such as the same word spelled out elsewhere in the petition or a fixed formula. Each change is logged with its reason. One of them is the closing formula, which was restored from the form Triebenfeld uses in his other letters; the English was edited to match. Words that remain doubtful were left as transcribed.

### Summary and translation

The model read the document whole and wrote a German summary. A second, separate run of the model checked the summary against the text. It removed the lessees' first names, which are known from another holding and not from this petition. The English summary is a translation of the checked German one. The model also translated the document. Three corrections it proposed during translation rested on sense alone and were not applied.
