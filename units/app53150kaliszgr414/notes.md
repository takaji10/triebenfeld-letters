# 53/15/0/-/Kalisz Gr.414 (APP)

One document on three pages: the delivery of possession of the Lusnia wood
and its twelve Olęder settlers to Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, 4 November
1771, with the record of its entry at Kalisz on 9 November. Latin with
Polish. Added on 2026-10-07 in the third Prusimski-era batch. Status
`translated`. Built and verified locally, **not published**.

How it was made is in the docstring of `intake/holding.py`.

- **Check**: opening, the Polish delivery with the names, closing and the
  record read against the scans; 6 readings corrected. Not compared: about
  200 words of the recital of decrees. The one that matters: "peracta nemine
  impugnante" (no one contesting), not "per acta nomine impugnante".
- **"[I2867]"** after Celmer was the editor's reference number: taken out.
- **Bogusław Celmer is the Zelmer of the edition**: the existing person entry
  `zelmer` ("the founding patriarch ... on the 1771 list of twelve annexed
  Olęders") matches him here, and the English gives him as Zelmer by the
  editor's ruling of 2026-09-11. The Polish keeps "Celmer".
- Left as the editor has them: "Bonorae" and "Tribunalibus" (the editor
  notes both as the clerk's slips), "W[ielmożnemu?] [Jegomości?]" (the scan
  has "W. JMci"), the name "Serlatkowski", "Frinderberg" (the scan may have
  "Frynderberk").
- **The decree of 1775** (Konin Gr.145, English page 113) names this very
  act: "the delivery of possession made in the Lusnia woodland on Monday
  after the Feast of All Saints" of 1771, by which Chełmski "received twelve
  olędry and the inn with stabling"; it sets it aside.
- New kind of document: `delivery` (delivery of possession), labelled in
  `site/_data/i18n.yml`.
- The fonds' name for 53/15 is not known here; the court is named from the
  text ("Acta Castrensia Calisiensia").
