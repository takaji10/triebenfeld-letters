## Document 1, the draft resolution (0005_a2)

- **A struck-out clause at the end is not transcribed, and stays out:** the
  editor ruled on 2026-10-05 that struck-out text is not transcribed
  (`docs/EDITORIAL_RULES.md`). It is recorded here only. After "wróciły" the
  draft went on "z zachowaniem praw Im służących" (with the rights belonging
  to them preserved), and the clause is struck through. "Im" is the least
  certain word. Worth the editor's eye: a clause about rights, drafted and
  then removed, in the act that the later dispute over the mortgages goes
  back to.
- Other struck words are not transcribed either: a word after "do Nas"
  replaced by "przez N° Cesarza", "wyświadczoney" replaced by "wymierzoney",
  "wszelkie" replaced by "tych wyżey wyrażonych Osob", "Czerwca" replaced by
  "Lipca". The text given is the corrected one.
- "z Pru[simskich]" and "dnia [5] Czerwca": the edge of the leaf is torn;
  the brackets are the editor's. The register copy has "z Prusinskich" and
  "dnia 5°".
- "Właścicieli": the end of the word is lost at the tear; the register copy
  has "Włascicielów".
- "Dóbr swoich dziedzicznych", "ścisłą", "wróciły": the scan has no accent
  on some of these ("Dobr", "scisłą", "wrociły") and a capital in
  "Dziedzicznych". Left.
