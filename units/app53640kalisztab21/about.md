In the summer of 1789 the Polish state collected a hearth tax in the Kalisz voivodeship, and its register records the sum paid by every town, village and part of a village of the Pyzdry, Kalisz and Konin districts, among them Trąbczyn and its neighbours – its Olęder settlement, Łazy, Osiny, Nowa Wieś and Łukom – a few years before Prussia took the region. <!-- context -->

### Historical background

The hearth tax (podymne) was a tax of the Polish Commonwealth laid on houses, counted by their chimneys, and paid in instalments. In 1789 Trąbczyn belonged to Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, and its neighbour Łukom to the Chełmski family: [[unit:app53170koningr153]]. <!-- context -->

A table of the villages around Konin made for the Prussian administration about eight years later counts the houses and inhabitants of many of the same places: [[unit:agad1169011745]]. <!-- context -->

Two colonies of Olęders, settlers who held their land by contract with the owner, had been founded on the Trąbczyn estate. Their names changed over time: first Trąbczyńskie Stare Olędry and Trąbczyńskie Nowe Olędry, the old and the new Olęder colony of Trąbczyn, and later Łazińskie Stare Olędry and Łazińskie Nowe Olędry; the sources write them in several forms. Łazy, which the register enters beside them, is a separate village of the estate. <!-- context -->

### Contents

The tax was due at midsummer (St John's) 1789. Part of it went to the treasury; part was paid by a village straight to the Crown army, on an order to pay (delata) [[1]].

Trąbczyn, written "Trąpczyn", paid 80 złoty. The rest of the estate followed: the subjects of the parish priest at Trąbczyn, 20 złoty; Łazy, 12 złoty 15 groszy; Osiny, 17 złoty 15 groszy; Nowa Wieś, 12 złoty 15 groszy; and "Olendry Trąbczynskie", the Olęder settlers of Trąbczyn, 35 złoty. Łukom paid 97 złoty 15 groszy to the army, and "Olędry Lusnie", the Olęder settlement in the Lusnia wood, 35 złoty. Szetlewo, held by four owners, paid in four parts, and Szetlewek 55 złoty [[1]].

The sums show the rate of the tax: in the Pyzdry and Kalisz districts every village paid a multiple of 3 złoty 15 groszy, and around Konin a multiple of 2 złoty 15 groszy; only the towns, and two other places, depart from it. <!-- context -->
