# Collation — Letter 48 ↔ Letter 302

Letters 48 and 302 are the **same document, transcribed twice** from two physical copies. This is the only place in the corpus where a second witness exists, so it is the only place readings can be chosen by comparison rather than conjecture.

- Word-level similarity: **72.1%** (635 vs 624 words)
- Divergences: **104**
- Requiring your decision: **1** (a numeric discrepancy — see below)


**Neither witness dominates** — each is right where the other is wrong. Letter 48 carries the date and wins most of the ordinal series; letter 302 wins most proper nouns and the critical 250000 figure.
