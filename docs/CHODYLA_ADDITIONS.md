## The editor's rulings (2026-10-28)

- **The primary source first.** For any fact that comes from Chodyła, the
  primary source he rests on is consulted first, is taken before him, and
  is cited wherever possible. Where the edition holds that source, it is
  the citation, and Chodyła is not needed; where it does not, Chodyła is
  cited and the source he names is given beside him, so it can be brought
  in later (`docs/MISSING_SOURCES.md`).
- **Nothing of Chodyła's on Trąbczyn's Olęder colonies** (Trąbczyńskie
  Stare and Nowe Olędry, Lusnie Olędry, the Łazińsk villages, the founding
  of 1746): the editor's information is better, and the primary sources
  speak for themselves. The first list's facts 1-14 and 18-21 are
  withdrawn.
- **A general observation on Miąsykąt's location** is welcome (no. 6).
- Of the seven points put to the editor, **A, B, C, D, F and G are not
  used**. E is answered below, and the editor ruled it: Turno.
- **The documents' own figures** for the places (met in this reading) are
  to be looked into later: `docs/TODO.md`, section 56.

## From the edition's own documents

These began as Chodyła's, but the edition holds the documents; the
documents are cited and Chodyła is not.

### Huta Trąbczyńska (`huta-trabczynska`) and Osiny (`osiny`)

1. "A judicial inspection of the Trąbczyn forests on 9 July 1754 found
   that past Łazy and Osiny, by Huta, where the finest timber had stood,
   it had been cut and only the poor trees were left; of the oaks, too,
   only the poor ones stood, the Olęders having taken the finer wood in
   the holdings (*huby*) allotted to them long before."
   Konin Gr.136, the inspection entered 12 July 1754
   (`/documents/app53170koningr136/1/#p4`): "zaś nimo Łazów i Osin między
   Hutą gdzie najpiękniejsze drzewo było i tam powycinane same też wybirki
   stoją. Także i dęby w lasach stojące dotychczas same też wybirki stoją
   tylko gdyż Olędry pozabierali hubami gdzie pieknięjsze drzewo było
   wydzielonymi z dawna każdemu z nich". The same sentence on both pages.
2. **Huta Trąbczyńska** only: "The Prussian register of about 1797 lists it
   as *Stara Huta Trąpcz.*, with 2 houses and 9 inhabitants."
   AGAD 1/169/0/1/1745, entry 283 (`/documents/agad1169011745/1/#p6`),
   the entry's existing reference for the name.

### Łazy (`lazy-trabczyn`)

3. "In 1791 the table of the grain levy entered Łazy with 5 farming
   hearths; the Prussian register of about 1797 gives it 5 houses."
   Pyzdry Gr.115 (`/documents/app5321pyzdrygr115/1/`), "Łazy | 5";
   AGAD 1/169/0/1/1745, entry 150 (`/documents/agad1169011745/1/#p1`),
   "Haul. Lazy. | 5" (the entry already cites this register for the 42
   inhabitants).

### Stawisko (`stawisko`) and Łukom (`lukom`)

4. "In 1767 people of Łukom, on the order of its owner, raised a dike at
   the end of the Stawisko, on Trąbczyn ground by the Łukom boundary; the
   dammed water flooded the fields and meadows of the Olęders settled in
   the Trąbczyn woods, so that four of them could not mow their meadows."
   Konin Gr.114, the two inspections entered 22 April and 6 August 1767
   (`/documents/app53170koningr114/1/`, `/documents/app53170koningr114/2/`):
   "Groblą niedawno w końcu Stawiska Usypaną przez której usypanie Woda z
   Stawiska tego płynąca ... Olendrów w Lesiech Trąmpczyńskich Osiadłych z
   Rolmi Ku Uprawienim Wymierzwionemi y Łąkamy zalała".

### Miąsykąt (`miasykat`)

6. **A general observation on its location**: "The boundary decree of 1775
   names Miąsykąt with the woods Lusnie, Dziadulina and Ostrowiec and the
   Stawisko marsh, the ground claimed by both Trąbczyn and Łukom on the
   south-west of the Trąbczyn estate: it lay among them, between Stawisko
   and the Łukom and Drzewce boundaries."
   Konin Gr.145, leaves 667v, 675v and 678v
   (`/documents/app53170koningr145/1/#p26`, `#p42`, `#p48`): "błota seu

   Dziaduline, Miąsykąt, Ostrowce i stawisko seu błota puste"; "lasów
   Lusnie Dziaduliny Miąsykąt Ostrowca tudzież i stawiska pustego". (The
   entry's footnote already cites leaf 692 of the same decree.)

## From Chodyła, the primary source not in the edition

### Łukom (`lukom`) and Łomów (`lomow`)

7. **Łukom**: "Before 1789 the owners of the Łukom and Łomów estates
   founded the Olęder settlements of Bukowe (Łomowskie Olędry), Imielno,
   Myszakówek and Stara Huta (Zagórowskie Olędry)."
   Chodyła 70, citing the grain-levy table of 1791, APP Pyzdry Gr.115,
   leaf 543v (not in the edition). The hearth-tax register of 1789, which
   he also cites, is in the edition and enters *Bukowa Olędry* (Kalisz
   Tab.21, leaf 8v, entry 44, `/documents/app53640kalisztab21/1/`): that
   one settlement's existence by 1789 can be cited to the document.
8. **Łomów**: "In 1791 the grain-levy table entered *Łomowo z Holl.*
   (Łomów with its Olędry) with 19 hearths." Chodyła 70, note 115; APP
   Pyzdry Gr.115, leaf 543v.

### Imielno, Myszaków (`imielno`, `myszakow`)

9. "In 1791 the grain-levy table entered *Holendry Jemielno* with 9 hearths
   and *Myszaków z Olędrami* (Myszaków with its Olędry) with 23." Chodyła
   70, notes 116-117; APP Pyzdry Gr.115, leaf 543v.

### Wrąbczynek (`wrabczynek`) and Wrąbczynkowskie Holendry (`wrabczynkowskie-holendry`)

10. **Wrąbczynek, 1776-1777**: "In 1776 a memorandum of the starost's agent
    I. Gałęski fixed the dues of 'the industrious people of Rąmpczyn', who
    still owed labour service, on 12 hubas; in 1777 the sworn surveyor
    Bogusław found only 11, and the commune was to elect a sołtys and two
    assessors." Chodyła 49; APP Pyzdry Gr.110, leaves 200-202.
11. **Both, the charter**: "Szczepan Turno, starost of Pietrzyków, gave
    Wrąbczynek and the Wrąbczynkowskie Olędry a joint charter at Pietrzyków
    on 20 October 1781: Wrąbczynek received the demesne farm with 11 hubas
    and 16 morgen of meadow, the Olędry 9½ hubas they had cleared in the
    forest, with Jakub Krysa (Kriese) as sołtys and Wojciech Kurtz and
    Krzysztof Szultz as assessors. King Stanisław August confirmed the
    charter on 2 February 1787." Chodyła 52 and 82; APP Pyzdry Gr.110,
    leaves 200-209v (the charter and its confirmation).
12. **Both, the commune**: "The sołtys held an extra five morgen in each of
    the three fields, the innkeepers half a huba each free of rent, and the
    school and cemetery were shared; the settlers owed three morgen of
    ploughing, two days of carting dung and three days of hand work at
    harvest. In 1789 Wrąbczynek had 14 farmers on 11 hubas, the Olędry 17
    farmers on 9½ hubas with 17½ morgen of meadow and a newly built
    school." Chodyła 82 and 127; APP Pyzdry Gr.110, leaves 200-203, and the
    survey of the starosty of Pietrzyków of 1 September 1789, AGAD ASK
    XLVI, 116, p. 22.
13. **Wrąbczynkowskie Holendry, today**: "A chapel built beside the school
    still stands, as do the remains of the cemetery." Chodyła 137-138 (his
    own observation, no document).

### Orlina Duża (`orlina-duza`)

14. "Founded before or in 1784 on the Królików estate by its owner Count
    Franciszek Stadnicki, starost of Ostrzeszów, through his agent Józef
    Koźmiński, with Bogusław Krol (sołtys) and Michał Arent as locators; the
    preliminary charter was issued at Królików on 5 May 1784. Twenty
    settlers came, most with Polish and some with German names, Catholics
    and Lutherans; eighteen held a whole huba and two half a huba, on 'the
    hubas beyond Otnie in the place called Orla' and the Królików wood
    Orlina." Chodyła 65 and 84; APP, SAB 732 (an unauthenticated copy of
    the charter). Also, Chodyła's observation (pp. 137-138): a chapel
    beside the school still stands, and the cemetery's wall is of bog iron
    ore (the entry's "low stone wall").

### Zbiersk Kolonia (`zbiersk-kolonia`)

15. "Founded as the Zbierskie Olędry by Stanisław Dzik Kożuchowski, land
    judge of Wieluń, by a charter given at Zbiersk on 16 December 1761: the
    colonists were settled in the forest with eight free years to clear it,
    fourteen of them on a huba each; half a huba each went to the teacher
    and the inn, and a plot of 15 by 15 rods to the burial ground
    (*kirchow*)." Chodyła 33 and 87; the charter, which Chodyła cites at his
    note 50 (to be looked up when the source comes in).

### Lisewska Kolonia (`lisewska-kolonia`)

16. "Kolonia Lisewo was founded with German Lutheran settlers in the
    Frederician colonisation of South Prussia, on the state domain of
    Pyzdry, as a colony by the Lisewo demesne farm; in 1804 it had 20
    families, 99 people, on 23 łany and 13 morgen. It is not the earlier
    Lisewskie Olędry (Olsz, Olszowiec), founded north-west of Lisewo after
    1765." Chodyła 32-33, citing Wąsicki (1953), p. 165. Wąsicki is in the
    edition, but that page as the edition has it does not name Lisewo: to
    be checked before this goes in.

## E answered: Turno, not Turna

The starost of Pietrzyków who founded Wrąbczynek and the
Wrąbczynkowskie Olędry is **Szczepan (Stefan) Turno**. The entries
`wrabczynek` and `wrabczynkowskie-holendry` call him "Szczepan Turna",
citing Chodyła 49.

Chodyła's p. 49 has the name only in the genitive: "pełnomocnika
Szczepana **Turny**, starosty pietrzykowskiego" (the agent *of* Szczepan
Turny). Polish surnames ending in *-o* decline like feminine nouns in *-a*
(Fredro, genitive Fredry; Kościuszko, Kościuszki), so *Turny* is the
regular genitive of *Turno*. Read back to a nominative, it gives *Turna*.
The same book, on p. 52, writes the name undeclined beside the declined
form: "przez Krystynę z Szembeków Turnową stolnikową poznańską, matkę i
Stefana/Szczepana **Turno** starostę pietrzykowskiego, syna", "przez
wspomnianego Szczepana Turno", "olędrzy tegoż p. Turny". His mother's
married name, *Turnowa*, is formed from Turno, as *Fredrowa* from Fredro.

No document in the edition names him, so the book is the only witness
here. **Ruled by the editor (2026-10-28): "Turno it is."** Both entries now
read "Szczepan Turno"; the change is recorded in GAZETTEER_PLAN, section 3.
