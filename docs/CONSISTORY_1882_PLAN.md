cemeteries, communicants 326 + 408 = 734 (131 more than in 1881), only 9
evangelical families in the town of Pyzdry, 23 confirmands (13 + 10), mission
collection 3 r. 40 k., 1 r. 76 k. for students, 2 r. 36 k. for the widows'
fund. **Why they matter:** the only documents in the edition that give the
Zagórów parish's own count of its churches, schools, cemeteries and communicants
in the Russian period, in the pastor's hand; they sit beside Kneifel, Werner,
the parish chronicle, Breyer and the editor's cemetery survey.

## 2. The damage, as the images show it

- **0318 (Zagórów, p. 1):** the **right edge** is burnt away on every line;
  from one to about three words are lost at the end of each line (the
  addressee block and the report's opening are the worst: the lines run into
  the burn). The top edge has a torn patch above the addressee. The left
  margin is intact. The last line is the line of communicants.
- **0322 side a (Zagórów, p. 2):** the **left edge** is burnt and flaking on
  every line; one to three words are lost at the *beginning* of each line.
  The top lines are the most damaged. The signature and the foot are whole.
- **0322 side b (Pyzdry, p. 1):** like 0318: the right edge lost, and the
  lower right of the page is stained as well (a brown mark over the last
  four lines).
- **0345 (Pyzdry, p. 2):** like 0322a: the left edge lost, the first line
  half gone at the top.
- So the loss is regular: **right edge on the first page of each report, left
  edge on the second**, the same few words per line, and the lost words are
  always at the edge of a line, never in its middle. The text between the two
  pages is continuous. This is what makes a bounded reconstruction possible.

## 3. The principle

A reconstruction is a *proposal with a witness*, never a reading.

1. **The transcription stays diplomatic.** What is on the page is written as it
   is, and each lost stretch is `[...]` at the edge of its line (the
   convention the existing `.txt` files and the editor's rules use;
   `docs/EDITORIAL_RULES.md`: a mark of doubt is never quietly removed, a word
   that cannot be read is not completed with a guess). **No reconstruction is
   written into the German transcription itself.**
2. **The reconstruction is a separate layer** (section 5), in which every
   supplied stretch is marked as supplied, names its witness, and carries a
   grade. A reader can switch it off.
3. **What may be supplied:** formula, boilerplate and grammar that the report
   gives elsewhere in the same words; headings; the ending of a word whose
   beginning is legible; words fixed by the other report, which is the same
   pastor's text on the same form. **What may never be supplied:** a figure, a
   name, a date, a place, a negation or its opposite, any word that adds a
   fact the surviving text does not state (is a thing present or absent, good or
   bad, more or fewer). Where such a stretch is lost it stays `[...]` in every
   layer, and the English says "[lost]" or its equivalent.
4. **The documents first** (CLAUDE.md): a supplied figure that disagrees with
   the surviving page is wrong and is dropped. Where a book disagrees with the
   page, the page stands and the difference is flagged.

**The English, 2026-11-13 (the editor):** the translation bridges the burnt
edges where the sense is certain - a broken word translated whole, a word from
the other report or the form in square brackets - so that it reads; what may
never be supplied (above) stays "[text lost]" in the English too. The same
day the editor had the transcription completed the same way, in square
brackets, and ruled the two negations of 0322_a "[not]" (the visible "t", the
twin, the form): the rule against a supplied negation gave way there.
units/agad14320139/notes.md, "The text completed".

## 4. The witnesses, strongest first

1. **The twin report.** The two reports repeat each other almost word for
   word: the opening ("In Folge der Anordnung [des Consistoriums] vom 30ten
   October ... N 267, habe ich die Ehre ..."), the headings (I. Kirchenwesen;
   a) Äußeres, b) Inneres; Kirchlichkeit und Kirchenbesuch befriedigend;
   Offenbare Abendmahlsverächter unbewußt; Missions-collect; Sittlichkeit so
   wie das häusliche Zusammenleben; Baptisten, Irwingianer ... nicht; Besondere
   Laster; Uebertritte zu andern Confessionen; Merkwürdige Ereignisse; Zahl der
   Confirmirten; Candidaten; Collekten, with the two standing collections),
   and the closing. A line lost in one report at the right edge is, in many
   places, whole in the other, and the reverse. This is the main source.
2. **The consistory's own form.** The order No. 267 of 30 October (1881)
   carried a questionnaire whose headings every report follows. The four
   sample scans the editor supplied (`samples/`, section 4a) show the form's
   whole skeleton, and it is the second main source. More scans of
   1/432/0/1/139 would still help (the circular, if the file holds it).
3. **Erdmann's own other reports.** Neighbouring files of the fond (other
   years, and the same pastor's reports for the same parishes) give his
   phrasing and his usual figures (children per school, communicants of the
   year before: the report says the Pyzdry number is 131 more than in 1881).
4. **Arithmetic inside the page.** Communicants 726 + 871 = 1,597; 326 + 408 =
   734; confirmands 36 + 31 = 67 and 13 + 10 = 23. These are all legible and
   already add up; they are a check on the read figures, and the model for
   testing any supplied figure (which is not supplied, but checked).
5. **The edition's own books**, for context and for flags only, not for
   supplying words: Kneifel (the Zagórów church of 1878-1896, pastors to
   1945), Werner, the parish chronicle (`luteraniezagorow`), Breyer's school
   counts, the cemetery survey (eight cemeteries), the gazetteer (Michalinowo,
   Pyzdry). Where the report's "8 schools" or "8 cemeteries" meets these, any
   difference goes to NEEDS_CONFIRMATION.
6. **The names of the office-holders** the report addresses or mentions
   (General Superintendent; Superintendent Manitius in Warsaw; Superintendent
   von Boerner in Płock): the printed church directories of the time, or the
   consistory's own files, settle spelling. These are the only names in the
   report; they stay as the page gives them until a witness shows otherwise.

## 4a. What the four samples show (read 2026-11-11)

The editor's `samples/` folder holds four spread scans from the same file,
from two other parishes (scan numbers 111109940, 111109942 = **Włocławek**,
the report for 1882, its signature not yet read; 111110137,
111110158 = **Konstantynów**, signed "Sig. Manitius, Pastor", with a filial).
They are on the archive's two-page spread format and are far better kept than
Zagórów's leaves: the burn takes only a few letters at the margins, and the
text is whole on nearly every line. What they give:

1. **The form's skeleton, in order.** Włocławek follows it with numbers, and
   the others under the same headings: **I. Kirchenwesen**, A/a) Äußeres
   Kirchenwesen (1. Zustand der kirchlichen Gebäude; 2. Friedhöfe; 3. Geschenke
   an die Kirche), B/b) Inneres Kirchenwesen (1. Gottesdienst, Kirchlichkeit
   und Kirchenbesuch; 2. Sakramentsgebrauch; 3-5. häuslicher Unterricht,
   Schulen, Konfirmandenunterricht; 6. Beteiligung der Gemeinde an Heiden- und
   Judenmissionen; 7. Armenpflege; 8. Sittlichkeit, häusliches und eheliches
   Leben; 9. besonders herrschende Unsitten und Laster; 10. Sekten; 11.
   Uebertritte zu andern Confessionen); **II. Merkwürdige Ereignisse; III.
   Zahl der Konfirmanden; IV. Kandidaten in der Gemeinde lebend; V. Collekten**
   (zum Besten der eigenen / fremder Gemeinden). Erdmann's two reports run
   in the same order in prose, so a lost line at the edge of one of his pages
   can be tested against *which question it answers*. This turns many lost
   stretches from "unknown" into "the answer to question X", which lets grade
   A cover the headings and the question words reliably.
2. **The standard answers.** Where a parish has nothing to report the reports
   use fixed formulas ("Besondere Laster ... nicht vorhanden", "Sekten sind
   nicht vorhanden", "Uebertritte zu andern Confessionen ... nicht statt",
   "Candidaten ... nicht vorhanden"). Seen in three hands, so they are the
   form's formulas and not one man's habit: this supports grade A for those
   exact phrases in Erdmann's reports (where the twin report also has them).
3. **What they do not give.** The reports are free prose under the headings:
   Konstantynów ignores the numbers and writes continuously; Włocławek writes
   point by point. So the samples support **headings, question order and the
   standard answers** but **not Erdmann's own wording** between them, which only
   the twin report and his other reports can. Their figures (families,
   communicants, collections) are other parishes' and are never used to fill
   a gap in Zagórów or Pyzdry.
4. **Calibration.** The burn takes about the same width at the edge on every
   leaf of the file; the samples show how many letters a line of the same
   ruling and hand carries per centimetre, which is the measuring rule for
   the fit test.
5. **Three things the samples raise, for the notes, not for supplying:**
   (a) the reports are dated at the foot in both calendars ("3/20 December
   1882" at Włocławek); Erdmann's two pages show no date at the foot, so the
   date, if he gave one, was in the burnt upper right and **may not be
   supplied**; (b) the reports of 1882 were addressed to the General
   Superintendent and sent through the diocese: Konstantynów speaks of "the
   Herr Superintendent der Kalischer Diöcese" presiding at the church-board
   election (the word is half burnt, "Ka[li]scher"); which diocese Zagórów
   belonged to is not in Erdmann's pages and is to be checked against Kneifel
   and Werner before the holding page says anything of it; (c) "Manitius" appears as the superintendent
   in Warsaw to whom the mission money went (Włocławek, Zagórów) and as the
   Konstantynów pastor "Sig. Manitius": whether these are two men or one is
   open and is not settled by supply.

## 5. The method

1. **Stage the pages.** Copy the four images into
   `units/agad14320139/intake/` (slug proposed: `agad14320139`; the repository
   is AGAD, a new fond: a new `consistory` source type is not needed, the
   documents are `report`s as in the Konin vocabulary; era `wlodzimierz` by
   date or the editor's choice, since the reports of 1882 fall in the Russian
   period the edition has put there). Crop the grey scan mat; do not fold
   (single leaves, no spread).
2. **Read the legible text line by line** from the images, in session,
   diplomatic (long s, ß, the scribe's orthography). Use the `.txt` files only
   as a second witness. Doubtful readings go on one spot sheet for the editor
   (`pipeline/review/queries.py`), the lacunae do not.
3. **Build a lacuna register** (`intake/lacunae.csv`), one row per lost
   stretch: page, line, edge (left/right), the text before and after, the
   *measured space* (the loss as a share of the line width; the letters it
   holds estimated against the same hand's letters on the same line), the
   *proposed supply*, the *witness* (the twin's line, the form, a sibling
   report) and a **grade**:
   - **A: formula or heading, whole in the twin or the form** (e.g.
     "Merkwürdige Ereignisse", the closing, "Baptisten, Irwingianer oder ...
     Sekten giebt es nicht"). Supplied in the layer.
   - **B: the word is certain from grammar and the visible beginning** (a
     compound or participle cut at the edge). Supplied in the layer.
   - **C: plausible but not fixed** (what fills the line is a sense, not a
     wording; or the twin differs). Offered on the sheet and in the register
     only; shown as `[...]`.
   - **D: a fact is lost** (number, name, date, presence/absence). Never
     supplied.
4. **A fit test for every A and B.** The supplied text must (a) fit the
   measured space within a stated tolerance, (b) make the sentence grammatical
   with what stands on both sides, (c) change no fact, and (d) match the
   twin's wording or the form's. A supply that fails any of the four is
   graded C.
5. **Translate the read text** in session, as for every holding
   (`intake/translation/`), keeping `[...]` where the German has it; the
   supplied layer's English is written beside it and marked supplied.
6. **Summaries** (both languages) are written from what is read and from the A
   and B supplies only; **no C or D content ever enters a summary** (the
   standing rule, EDITORIAL_RULES: a thought about what a word might be goes
   into `intake/unresolved.md`, never into a summary). The claim check is run
   as for every holding.
7. **The lacunae reported honestly** on the holding page: how much of each
   page is lost, where, and what rule governs what the reader sees. No
   method talk on a reader's page (HOUSE_STYLE): one factual sentence and the
   layer's switch.
8. **Index** the persons and places in the read text (Erdmann, Manitius,
   Boerner, Zagórów, Pyzdry, Lissewo, Michalinowo); Lissewo and Michalinowo
   need a place check against the register and the gazetteer; a supplied
   stretch is never indexed.

## 7. Which model does what (the editor, 2026-11-11)

The rule of thumb (CLAUDE.md, WORKING_NOTES): simple, checkable steps go to a
Sonnet sub-agent; anything that is a judgement about the text stays with an
Opus agent (or the main session). A sub-agent is given one step and its
inputs; its output is checked by the session before it counts.

**Opus (the judgement of the text):**
- Reading the legible text from the images, line by line (step 2): a
  nineteenth-century German Kurrent hand on burnt paper; a wrong reading here
  poisons everything after.
- Grading each lacuna A-D and the fit test (steps 3-4), and every decision that
  a supplied word changes no fact. This is the one step where a plausible
  mistake is a falsification of the record.
- The English of the read text, where the sense of a damaged sentence turns on
  a word, and the **summaries and the claim check** (a claim check is a second
  reading, so a different Opus pass from the one that wrote the summary).
- The spot sheet's questions to the editor, the notes, and the ruling on every
  difference between a witness and the page.

**Sonnet (bounded, checkable work), each result checked by the session:**
- Staging: copying the images into the unit, cropping the scan mat, the file
  and reference bookkeeping, `unit.yml` and the holding page scaffolding from
  `docs/NEW_UNIT.md`.
- Measuring the lost width of each line from the images and writing the
  `lacunae.csv` rows' mechanical columns (page, line, edge, text before and
  after, space measured, letters that fit), without any proposed supply.
- Collecting witnesses: finding the twin line in the other report and the
  matching formula in the other parishes' scans, and listing them beside each
  row (the *finding*, not the grading).
- Arithmetic and consistency checks (the four sums, the 131 more than 1881),
  and the mechanical checks that apply to any holding (the glossary gate,
  `verify_site.py`, link and marker counts before and after every pass).
- The index pass over the read text (hits listed for the session to rule) and
  the first English-German bookkeeping of names and places.
- Looking things up in the edition's own books for the flags (Kneifel, Werner,
  the cemetery survey) and returning the passage, not a conclusion.

**Never delegated:** the editor's rulings (section 6) and anything that enters
the supplied layer: those are written by an Opus agent and checked against the
register by the session.
