Farm labels — Pełzucki 1896 (working page)

Pick a farm in either list (or type its number and press Enter), then click its homestead - the farm disarms after one placement, so a stray click never becomes a label. A farmer can hold several farmsteads: to place several in one run, hold Shift while clicking, or just click the farm's row again for the next one. Drag a farm label to move it; select a label by clicking its pill, Delete removes that one only. A red number DRAGS to wherever its parcel really lies, and a CLICK corrects its reading (Enter saves - a [?] doubt stays bracketed); the change relinks at once and hides with its farm when Hide linked is on. Every change saves to the project the moment you make it; the server keeps dated snapshots under scratch/map4456/history/. Export JSON downloads the whole state - farms and red readings; Import JSON reads such a file back (the way back from a lost session).
