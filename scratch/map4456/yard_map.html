The flow: click a farm number on the map (a red dashed tag — it turns solid blue; draw mode comes on by itself), drag a box around the black n. / n figure, type the area as written — uncertainty in [..?] — and press Enter: the figure is linked to that farm, and the farm deselects for the next one. D toggles pan/draw from anywhere — it never types a letter (the area field takes digits, dots and dashes only). Esc clears the farm (or a pending rectangle, in the fields). The panel shows the farm's holder and total, but not its buildings figure — the yard read stays blind, so the check means something. Click a yard label to edit it; Export when done.
