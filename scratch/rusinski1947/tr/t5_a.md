In the Stare Borujskie Ol. we encounter a formally administrative fee, though by its character very close to a rent: the Olęders paid it every 50 years at the rate of 36 zł per huba in return for the manor's recognition of their right of ownership to the land they held[^89-3]. 5. The evolution of the manorial obligations. From the foregoing considerations it follows that the manorial obligations of the Olęders changed in the course of the colonisation. These changes might manifest themselves in this way, that either at the location of later settlements arrangements were made for services different from those in the earlier settlements, or else in one and the same settlement the lords altered the hitherto existing obligations in the course of time. The question of the evolution of the Olęders' obligations has already repeatedly been the subject of consideration in German literature.
