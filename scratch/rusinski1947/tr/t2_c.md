CHAPTER II Demographic conditions in the Olęder settlements

A. The numbers of the Olęders

The considerable number of Olęder settlements suggests the supposition that the Olęders constituted a substantial proportion of the population of the former voivodeship of Poznań. For that reason the detailed examination of the demographic structure of these settlements must be regarded as a matter of importance. Owing to the lack of sources it is not possible to present the changes in the size of the Olęder population over the whole period of the colonisation's existence; the source data do, however, make it possible to carry out a fairly exact calculation of this population in the period of the partitions 1. In view of the greatest abundance of the source tradition we shall adopt the year 1789 as the moment of the calculation. For Kościan county we possess from this period the following statistical materials: I. the “General table of the population” (Tol.), compiled by the Tax Commission of Kościan county on the basis of an ordinance of the Crown Fiscal Commission of 1789; 2. the “General table of hearths” (Tod. 89), compiled[^22-1][^22-2] by the same commission; 3. the Tariff of hearths and of the hearth tax for the years 1789 and 90 (Tp. 89, 90). The first table does not include the szlachta and the clergy, and moreover contains a considerable number of numerical errors and omissions. It is likewise, just like the second table, incomplete territorially. The third table, however, is compiled more exactly, and for that reason, despite certain gaps ?, it inspires considerably more confidence. The hearths are shown in it in two columns: in the first for 1789 on the basis of the declarations sworn by the owners, in the second for 1790 on the basis of the official lustration.
