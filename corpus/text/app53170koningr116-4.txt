M Prusimski contra P P Tomic et Alios Manifestatur
Videatur hoc Loco Manifestatio Certa ex re et Personali Comparitione Magnifici Antonii Prusimski Capitanei Niszczevicen~ Bonorum Trąmpczyn Haeredis In et Contra Magnificos Gliszczyński Bonorum Tomic Possessores[?] Stanislaus Chełmski Bonorum Łukomia Haeredes &c. Idque ratione Actus Campestris Granitialis Involutionis Aliarumque Circumstantiarum Facta et in Originali Porrecta in Productis.
Antoni na Kolnie Prusimski Sta: Nieszczew: mp

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Registervermerk über einen Protest, eingetragen in Konin im September 1775. Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice und Erbherr von Trąbczyn, ist persönlich erschienen und protestiert gegen die Gliszczyński von Tomice, gegen Stanisław Chełmski von Łukomia und andere, wegen der Verwicklung des Grenzverfahrens im Feld und anderer Umstände. Der Protest wurde im Original eingereicht und liegt bei den vorgelegten Schriftstücken. Prusimski hat unterschrieben.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A register note of a protest, entered at Konin in September 1775. Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice and heir of Trąbczyn, appeared in person and protests against the Gliszczyńskis of Tomice, Stanisław Chełmski of Łukomia and others, for the entangling of the boundary proceedings in the field and for other circumstances. The protest was handed in in the original and is among the documents produced. Prusimski signed.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Right Honourable Prusimski against the [Possessors of] Tomic and Others — Protest

Let there be seen here the Formal Protest made in fact and upon the personal appearance of the Right Honourable Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, heir of the goods of Trąbczyn, in and against the Right Honourables Gliszczyński, [uncertain: possessors] of the goods of Tomic, and Stanisław Chełmski, heir of the goods of Łukomia, etc. — and that by reason of the boundary field act, its entanglement, and other circumstances — made and in the original submitted among the Produced Documents.

Antoni of Kolno Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, by his own hand
