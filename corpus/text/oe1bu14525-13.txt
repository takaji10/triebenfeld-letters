--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A valuation of Kamionna and Kolno in the Międzyrzecz district, drawn up for the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen as hereditary lord on West Prussian principles of 1794, then corrected to South Prussian domain principles by Bading and attested by Beugnitz. It gives distances and boundaries, an area of 10,642 Morgen 35 rods, and describes the town of Kamionna with 466 inhabitants, its trades, livestock and the lordship's brandy and patronage rights, the two demesne farms and the village of Kolno with its labour-bound holdings, noting a disputed right to free wood from the Krobilewe heath.

The estates Kamionna and Kolno lie in the province of South Prussia, in the department of the Royal Government at Poznań, and in its Międzyrzecz district.

These estates stand in a single economic connexion, and are distant 3 miles from the district town of Międzyrzecz, 3 miles from Swerin, 1/2 mile from Birnbaum, 1 1/2 miles from Zircna [uncertain: Zircna], 2 miles from Pinne, 9 miles from Poznań, and 3 miles from the Neumark frontier, namely towards Driesen.

The nearest river is the Warta at Birnbaum, and the produce which is not consumed on the estate itself has been disposed of and sold in that town.

Kamionna borders to the east upon Mechnach, to the south upon Groß-Munche and Grolewo, and to the north upon Kolno.

Kolno borders to the east upon Prusim and Mechaiz, to the south upon Groß-Munche and Grolewo, to the west upon Bilski, Gorozin and Skrilewo, and to the north upon Popowo and Rzeitun.
