--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Desperate appeal for 200 ducats (later called 200 Th.), without which nothing can be accomplished and the writer fears disgrace and the Prince's anger. Urges that Prince August quickly demand the return of the power of attorney from Cosmar, warning that the young man is ill regarded despite his moustache, and that his written undertaking, apparently left behind at Öhringen, be carefully guarded. Notes that the King of Würtemberg has recognised the mediatised princes as Estates and sent a constitutional project, against which the Estates have protested.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Send Your Serene Highness the 200 ducats with all haste, or I must shoot myself dead; I could not bear the disgrace that the Prince himself should also be deluded, and in the end grow angry, for then, as good as matters now stand, all would nevertheless be lost. It is hard here to get through and to get at the people so as now to have any effect. But the devil may do that; only, one can accomplish nothing here.

when one has no money.

For the rest, do see to it that Prince August demands the power of attorney back quickly from Cosmar. People here have no very good opinion of the young gentleman — although he now wears a moustache — with respect to the written undertaking given by him, be well on your guard — As I hear, you have indeed left the written undertaking behind in Öhringen. It could be that you might be in great need of it — the King of Württemberg has, to be sure, already recognised the mediatised as Estates and has sent here a project for a constitution drafted by himself — but no notice was taken of it; besides, the Estates have already protested against it.
