--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Triebenfeld bittet dringend um die Übersendung der 200 #, da man hier ohne Geld nichts ausrichten könne und sonst trotz guter Lage alles verloren sei. Fürst August solle die Vollmacht rasch von Cosmar zurückfordern. Er warnt vor dem Revers des jungen Herrn, den der Fürst, wie er höre, in Öhringen gelassen habe. Der König von Württemberg habe die Mediatisierten als Stände anerkannt und einen Verfassungsentwurf geschickt.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
12 Your Serene Highness, send the 200 # with the greatest haste, or I must shoot myself dead; I could not bear the disgrace that the Prince himself should also [uncertain: have imagined it] and in the end grow angry, for then, as good as matters now stand, everything would nonetheless be lost. It is hard here to get through to people and to reach them, now, in order to have any effect. But the devil alone may [uncertain: do all that] when one has no money. Here one can accomplish nothing. For the rest, do see to it that Prince August promptly demands the power of attorney back from Cosmar. People here have no very good opinion of the young gentleman — although he now wears a moustache — as regards the written undertaking given by him, do be well on your guard — As I hear, you have indeed left the written undertaking in Öhringen. It could be that you would stand in great need of it — the King of Württemberg has, it is true, already recognised the mediatised as Estates and has sent here a draft constitution of his own drawing up — but paid no heed to it [uncertain: Moreover the Estates have] already protested against it.
