--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A royal attestation, made out at the Prince's request from the Betsche land and mortgage records, certifying that the Betsche estates in the district of Meseritz were conferred on the reigning Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen by a deed of donation of 10 June 1797 and a declaration of 1 December that year; that only the Treasury's resignation is still wanted to settle his title; that the estates formerly belonged to the bishopric of Posen; and that he has registered no debts upon them.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
We Friedrich Wilhelm, by God's Grace King of Prussia, do hereby make known and give to be understood, that the Betsche estates in the district of Meseritz have been actually conferred upon the reigning Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen by His Majesty the late King of most blessed memory, through the deed of donation of 10 June 1797 and the declaration thereof by the now reigning Royal Majesty of 1 December of the same year; that the settling of the said Prince's title of possession depends only upon the resignation still to be furnished by the Treasury; that these Betsche estates were formerly ecclesiastical estates, namely estates belonging to the bishopric of Posen; and that up to the present no debts have been contracted upon them by the said Prince, and no instruments of debt have been submitted for registration; concerning all which We have caused the present attestation to be issued hereby to the Prince of Hohenlohe upon his request of this day's date. In witness whereof, under the greater seal of Our South Prussian Government and the customary signature. Given at Posen, 9 February 1802. Steudener

Fest.

Attestation for the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen out of the Betsche land[illegible] and mortgage records.
