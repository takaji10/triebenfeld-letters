--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Triebenfeld meldet, der alte Weigel wolle den Pachtvertrag schließen und sei auf seinen Rat nicht abgeneigt, die Zagorower Güter zu kaufen: Er übernehme die Schulden und zahle 200000 Rthl zu, die er auf Koszęcin anrechnen wolle. Weigel rät, die Sache geheim zu halten und Koszęcin versteigern zu lassen. Er würde es per Revers dem Fürsten abtreten und glaubt, es sei unter 600000 Rthl zu erhalten. Triebenfeld bittet um geheime Weisung und um eilige Zusendung der Papiere.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Triebenfeld reports that old Weigel is willing to conclude the lease contract and, on his advice, is not averse to buying the Zagórów estates: he would take over the debts and pay a further 200000 Rthl, which he wishes to set against Koszęcin. Weigel advises keeping the matter secret and having Koszęcin sold at auction. He would make it over to the Prince by a written undertaking and believes it can be had for under 600000 Rthl. Triebenfeld asks for secret instructions and for the papers to be sent quickly.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---

Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

My messenger was held up 2 days and, being a blockhead, is only returning today. I am now sending the stupid beast by another road, and report to Your Serene Highness that old Weigel arrived here with me already today. He not only has the best will to conclude the lease contract, but he has disclosed to me that, following my advice, he is not disinclined to purchase the Zagórów estates. He assumes the debts and pays a further 200000 Rthl on top, which he wishes to set off against Koszęcin. In this matter, however, he advises keeping the affair secret and letting Koszęcin go to forced auction. He bids for it as a creditor and hopes to obtain it below the valuation; what is then lost, us Stössel

to lose, which would have cheated Your Serene Highness more than too badly. He engages himself to issue a written undertaking that he will cede Koszęcin, just as he should acquire it, to Your Serene Highness, or to whomever You should command. He believes that You would obtain it for still under 600000 Rthl — Only no soul must suspect that Your Serene Highness or Prince August wished to keep Koszęcin. Let Your Serene Highness write me Your will in this matter in secret, for the old man does what I advise him. As for the papers, may Your Serene Highness deign to send them with the utmost haste, for I know no counsel as to how to meet the costs; but the instrument must be drawn up as I have prescribed, otherwise it must be nothing. In reverence I remain

Your Serene Highness's

Blizanow

the 18th of July 1812

most humble and obedient

v Triebenfeld
