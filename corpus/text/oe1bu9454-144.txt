# 1804-11-03  Berlin
# /letters/oe1bu9454/144/

Durchleuchtigster Fürst!
Gnädigster Fürst und Herr!
Ew. Hochfürstlichen Durchlaucht befehle von
31. v. h. habe ich heute erhalten.
Morgen Früh werde ich zu den Greiff
von Schulenburg gehen, das an ihn
gerichtete Schreiben ihm überhändigen u seine
nähern Erklärung erwarten.
Mit der nächsten Post werde ich in Unter
thenigtes das Resultat meinen Conferenz
einzubreihl nicht unterlaßen.
cke ich erſtrete.
Er hochfürstlichen Sächleicht
Berlin den 3. Nov. 1834.
unterthänigste die
Amelang

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Acknowledgement by Amelang, writing from Berlin, that the Prince's instruction of the 31st of the previous month has arrived; he will call on Count von Schulenburg the following morning to hand over the letter addressed to him, hear his declaration, and report the outcome of the conference by the next post. A brief covering note containing no other business.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince! Most gracious Prince and Lord! Your Most Serene Highness's command of the 31st of last month I received today. Tomorrow morning I shall go to the Count von Schulenburg, deliver to him the letter addressed to him and await his further declaration. By the next post I shall not fail to submit in most humble [submission] the result of my conference. In deepest reverence I remain, until death, Your Most Serene Highness's Berlin, the 3rd Nov. 1834. most humble and obedient servant Amelang
