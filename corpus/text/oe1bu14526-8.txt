--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Hereditary-lease contract before the Hohenlohe Patrimonial Court by which War and Forests Councillor v. Triebenfeld, General Bevollmächtigter of Fürst Friedrich Ludwig, grants Christian Friedenberger of Szymanowicer Hauland one Hufe Magdeburg measure in the Trąbczyn forest: 100 Rthaler entry-money receipted, thirty Rthaler yearly canon in half-yearly instalments, three free years to 1809, half redeemable, with milling and drink compulsion, Laudemium of the tenth Groschen and no remission for misfortune. Filed with it are Triebenfeld's general power of attorney (Guhrwitz, 1805) and the royal Kalisch Chamber Consens for dismembering Zagorowo and Trąbczyn.

§. XII.

The hereditary lessee and his successors in possession are entitled to alienate his holding to another in any manner he pleases; but at every sale or exchange the tenth Groschen of the [uncertain: purchase] or exchange money must be paid to the ground-lordship as a Laudemium, and the [uncertain: consent] of the same must be obtained for every alienation, inasmuch as the ground-lordship hereby reserves to itself once and for all the right of pre-emption.

§. XIII. The hereditary lessee renounces, for himself and his successors in possession, all and every remission in cases of mis-

-fortune, be the same arising through fire from heaven, or the incendiarism of evil people, hail-storm, crop failure, cattle plague, inundation, war and pestilence, or in whatever manner it may arise, wholly for the present and for the future; and in cases occurring neither a bonification nor a remission of the hereditary rent shall take place.

§. XIV. All the costs attending this contract the hereditary lessee undertakes to discharge.

§. XV. His Most Serene Highness consents that the hereditary lessee may, on the ground of this contract, have his title of possession registered for himself.

§. XVI. So long as the hereditary lessee and his successors in possession fulfil all the obligations contained in this contract, His Most Serene Highness promises to maintain them in their rights and to defend them against every man's real claim.

But should the hereditary lessee or his successors remain in arrears with the yearly hereditary rents longer than two years, or else lay waste this holding and thereby diminish the revenues of the ground-lordship, then the ground-lordship shall be entitled to [uncertain: dispose of] this holding otherwise, and the hereditary lessee forfeits his whole

right and the entry-money paid in.

Both parties promise to keep this hereditary-lease contract firmly and inviolably; being judicially instructed as to the consequences, they renounce all subterfuges available against it, particularly the objection of an injury suffered above or below the half, of cunning persuasion, fear, compulsion, of matters otherwise agreed than written down, or not rightly understood; they moved that this contract be judicially engrossed, and, after previous verbatim reading out to them, in token of approval have

hereunder subscribed and signed with their own hands.

General Power of Attorney

I, the undersigned, do hereby make known and confess that I hereby [uncertain: expressly] empower the Royal Councillor of War and Forests, Herr Peter Friedrich August v. Triebenfeld, Your Right Honourable, of Schweinern etc., to manage in my name all and every affair touching the estates of Zagorowo, Wittow and Trąbczyn belonging to me, situate in South Prussia in the Kalisch Department, whether they concern a dismemberment, or purchase and sale, leasing or pledging, or by whatever other name they may be called and whatever bearing and influence they may have, both judicially and extrajudicially, to ad-

-minister; to petition, respectively to notify, all approbations or directives necessary in the matter, as well as all other requisites appearing to him necessary, both with the most laudable Chamber and with the most laudable Government at Kalisch and with all other requisite offices and authorities; and to manage all other requisite business for me; to settle accounts and conclude agreements with the tenants; to pay out moneys, to receive the like, and to give receipt for them; to issue to my officials and servants there the orders proper to them; to examine their conduct and service, and if he should not find such [uncertain: according] to his insight fitting and should find it prejudicial to me, to suspend them if need be; and in general un-

-restrictedly and freely to act in all authorities and affairs as he shall find good and most conformable to my interest.

Whatsoever my said Bevollmächtigter shall do by virtue of this commission received, I hereby promise to approve and to hold thereby so inviolably as if I had done it myself. At the same time I also bind myself to indemnify my Herr Bevollmächtigter in every quarter on account of this commission, and to reimburse him all [uncertain: costs] and outlays, upon the pledging of my present and future property, so far as shall be required thereto. In witness whereof I have subscribed this General Power of Attorney with my own hand and

confirmed it by the impression of my arms. Thus done at the Guhewitzsch Justice Office near Breslau, the 19th February 1805. (L. S.) read by myself and approved, Friedrich Ludwig Fürst zu Hohenlohe.

We Friedrich Wilhelm, by God's grace King of Prussia etc., make known and hereby give to be known that, whereas the possessor of the lordships of Zagorowo and Trąbczyn, situate in South Prussia in the Kalisch Chamber Department and in its Koninsch district, of the reigning Herr Fürst zu Hohenlohe Ingelfingen, has sought of us permission, reserving a part of the demesne farm Trąbczyn

together with the manorial parcels at Marianton, amounting in all to Seven hundred and Eighty-one Morgen and 77 square rods, and likewise four [uncertain: thousand] Nine hundred and Forty-seven Morgen of forest, to dismember the said lordships together with the remaining and greater part of the forests, namely

A.) the demesne farms belonging to the lordship of Zagorowo — Drzewce, Kopoyno, Swiątniki, Swięcia and Broniki — and likewise the demesne farms belonging to the lordship of Trąbczyn — Nowawies, Przybislaw, Stara Hutta — and a part of the demesne farm Trąbczyn;

B. to let out in hereditary lease the demesne farm and village Olesnice and likewise the demesne farm and village Szetlewek; and

C. to convert the services and prestations of all the subjects into a fixed money rent — we have therefore, in view of

the usefulness of this undertaking and of the profitable services rendered and still to be rendered to Us and Our Royal House by His Liebden the reigning Herr Fürst zu Hohenlohe Ingelfingen, Our General of Infantry and Governor of Breslau, by Cabinet Orders of the 22 October and 7 December of last year, resolved to comply with this his petition in such wise and to approve the same, provided that the following modalities shall obtain, namely

1., that the indemnification to be granted to the subjects for their hitherto pasturage and timber rights be more precisely determined, and that in the hereditary-rent instruments of each individual the extent of the area assigned to him rent-free in lieu thereof be

exactly noted; as likewise here in general the provisions contained in §. 12. of the Edict issued for the Province of Silesia under the 1st May 1804 must be duly followed, on the ground of a special and rectified survey;

2., that none of the present rustical holdings may lapse, but that if in case of a fire-damage or other misfortune no one is found who will re-establish such a holding out of his own means, the Dominium must restore the building at its own cost;

3., that all the sovereign dues now charged upon the said estates, or in future still to be laid upon them, and other charges likewise, shall as heretofore be

-livered to Our treasuries appointed thereto, within the prescribed terms; and that for their security the needful be on that account entered in the mortgage book of the lands and forests reserved by the Dominium;

4., that the timber rights belonging to the monastery and provostry of Ląd be likewise entered in the mortgage [book] upon the totality of the forests reserved from the dismemberment, and be in such manner secured; against which we consent that if hereafter the forests excepted from the dismemberment should be found insufficient for the timber rights belonging to the fisc in respect of the Domain Office of Ratyn,

an indemnification therefor shall be ascertained and secured by a yearly canon. In witness whereof we have caused the present Consens to be engrossed and executed under the usual signature and the greater seal of Our War and Domains Chamber. Thus done and given at Kalisch, the 28 January 1806. (L. S.) Royal South Prussian War and Domains Chamber
