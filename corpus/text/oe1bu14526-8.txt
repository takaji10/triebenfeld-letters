--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Hereditary-lease contract in fifteen sections by which von Triebenfeld, General Bevollmächtigter of Fürst Friedrich Ludwig zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, grants Christian Friedenberger of Szymanowicer Hauland one Hufe Magdeburg measure in the Trąbczyn forest for 100 Rthaler purchase-money, already receipted, and 30 Rthaler annual canon, half redeemable, with three free years, milling and drink compulsion, laudemium of the tenth Groschen, and no remission for misfortune. Filed with it: Triebenfeld's general power of attorney of 1805 with Guhrwitz court attestation, and the Kalisch Chamber's consent to dismembering Zagorowo and Trąbczyn.

His Most Serene Highness accordingly grants to Christian Friedenberger one Hufe — written out, One Hufe Magdeburg measure — reckoned at thirty Morgen to the Hufe and each Morgen at one hundred and eighty square rods, situated in [uncertain: um] the Trąbczyn forest, from now on for all perpetual time

in hereditary lease.

§. II. The said Christian Friedenberger pays for the one Hufe Magdeburg measure 100 rt., written out One Hundred Rthaler, as purchase-money; and since this purchase-money has already been paid in full, Christian Friedenberger is hereby receipted for it without further reservation.

hereby binds himself, for himself and his successors in possession of this holding, to pay from this one Hufe an annual hereditary canon of thirty Rthaler — written out thirty Rthaler — to the Dominium of this place; and this hereditary quit-rent shall be discharged yearly in two instalments, namely one half at Christmas and the other half at St. John's, each time in cash and in Courant, to the manorial treasury.

§. IV. For the wholly uncultivated arable the hereditary lessee receives three free years, which begin from Martinmas of the past year and end at St. John's 1809, so that the first half-yearly hereditary quit-rent must be paid at Christmas 1809 in the sum of 15 rt., written out fifteen Rthaler

§. V. The hereditary purchaser is at liberty to redeem the fixed hereditary quit-rent of the thirty Rthaler up to one half, that is to say, to pay off to the ground-lordship, according to his means, one, two, or up to three hundred [uncertain: Schuler] of capital, whereupon for every hundred Rthaler five Rthaler shall be

Likewise the hereditary lessee and his successors in possession of this holding must effect all future repairs and new buildings out of their own means, without subsidy or aid.

§. VIII. Outside his own holding the hereditary lessee has no right to firewood or to [uncertain: pasturage] in the manorial forests and fields.

§. IX. His drink the hereditary lessee must always take from

§. XII.

The hereditary lessee and his successors in possession are entitled to alienate their holding to another in any manner they please; but upon every sale or exchange the tenth Groschen of the purchase or exchange money must be paid to the ground-lordship as a laudemium, and the consent of the same must be obtained for every alienation, inasmuch as the ground-lordship hereby reserves to itself, once and for all, the right of pre-emption.

§. XIII. The hereditary lessee, for himself and his successors in possession, renounces all and every remission in cases of mis-

fortune — be they occasioned by fire from heaven or by the incendiarism of evil persons, hail-storm, crop failure, cattle plague, inundation, war and pestilence, or in whatsoever manner it may arise — entirely, for now and for the future; and upon the occurrence of such cases neither a bonification nor a remission of the hereditary quit-rent shall take place.

§. XIV. All costs attending this contract the hereditary lessee undertakes to discharge.

§. XV. His Most Serene Highness consents that the hereditary lessee may, on the ground of this contract, have his title of possession registered in his own name.

§. XII. So long as the hereditary lessee and his successors in possession fulfil all the obligations contained in this contract, His Most Serene Highness promises to maintain them in their rights and to defend them against every man's real claim.

Should, however, the hereditary lessee or his successors remain in arrears with the annual hereditary quit-rent for longer than two years, or lay this holding waste and thereby diminish the revenues of the ground-lordship, then the ground-lordship shall be entitled to dispose of this holding otherwise, and the hereditary lessee shall forfeit his whole

right and the purchase-money paid in.

Both parties promise to keep this hereditary-lease contract firmly and inviolably; being judicially instructed as to the consequences, they renounce all evasions that might be raised against it, particularly the objection of an injury sustained above or below the half [i.e. laesio enormis], of cunning persuasion, fear, compulsion, of matters otherwise agreed, otherwise written down, or not rightly understood; they moved that this contract be judicially drawn up, and, after previous reading aloud, have in token of their approval

subscribed and signed below in their own hands.

General Power of Attorney. I, the undersigned, hereby make known and declare that I do hereby expressly empower the Royal Councillor of War and Forests, Herr Peter Friedrich August von Triebenfeld, Your Right Honourable, of Schweinern etc., to attend, both judicially and extrajudicially, to all and every matter concerning the Zagorowo, Wittow and Trąbczyn estates belonging to me and situated in South Prussia in the Kalisch Department — whether they concern a dismemberment, or purchase and sale, leasing or pledging, or by whatever other name they may go and upon which they may have bearing and influence —

to procure all approbations or instructions necessary in the matter, as well as all requisites appearing to him necessary, both with the most laudable Chamber and with the most laudable Government at Kalisch and with all other requisite [authorities], and to petition, respectively to notify, [them], and to attend to all other requisite business on my behalf; to settle accounts with the lessees and conclude agreements with them; to pay out moneys, to receive the like, and to give receipt for them; to issue to my officials and servants there such orders as are appropriate to them; to examine their conduct and performance of duty, and, should he not find such [conduct] agreeable to his views and prejudicial to me, to suspend them if need be; and in general un-

restrictedly and freely to act before all authorities and in all matters as he shall find good and most conformable to my interest.

Whatsoever my said Bevollmächtigter shall do by virtue of this commission received, I hereby promise to approve and to hold thereto so inviolably as though I had done it myself. At the same time I also bind myself to indemnify my Bevollmächtigter in all respects on account of this commission, and to reimburse him all [uncertain: charges] and outlays, upon the pledging of my present and future property, so far as shall be required thereto. In witness whereof I have subscribed this General Power of Attorney with my own hand and have

corroborated it by the impression of my arms. Thus done at the Guhewitz Court of Justice near Breslau, the 19th of February 1805. (L. S.) Read by myself and approved. Friedrich Ludwig Fürst zu Hohenlohe.

Actuarius.

We, Friedrich Wilhelm, by God's grace King of Prussia etc., make known and hereby give to be understood that, whereas the possessor of the lordships Zagorowo and Trąbczyn, situated in South Prussia in the Kalisch Chamber Department and its Konin district, [namely] the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, has sought leave of Us, reserving one part of the demesne farm Trąbczyn

together with the manorial parcels at Marianton, amounting in all to seven hundred and eighty-one Morgen and 77 square rods, and likewise four thousand nine hundred and forty-seven Morgen of forest, to dismember the said lordships together with the remaining and larger part of the [estates], namely

A.) the demesne farms belonging to the lordship of Zagorowo — Drzewce, Kopoyno, Swiątniki, Swięcia and Broniki — and likewise the demesne farms belonging to the lordship of Trąbczyn — Nowawies, Przybislaw, Stara Hutta — and one part of the demesne farm Trąbczyn;

B. to let out in hereditary lease the demesne farm and village Olesnice, and likewise the demesne farm and village Szetlewek; and

C. to convert the services and prestations of all the subjects into a fixed money quit-rent: therefore We have, in respect of

the utility of this design and of the profitable services rendered, and yet to be rendered, to Us and Our Royal House by His Beloved the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Our General of Infantry and Governor of Breslau, by Cabinet Orders of the

22nd of October and 7th of December of last year, resolved to accede to this his petition and to approve the same in such manner that the following modalities shall obtain, namely

1., that the compensation to be granted to the subjects for their hitherto pasturage and timber rights be [uncertain: hereafter] determined, and that in the hereditary quit-rent instruments of each individual the extent of the areas assigned to him rent-free in lieu thereof be precisely noted

— just as in general the provisions contained in §. 12. of the Edict issued for the Province of Silesia on the 1st of May 1804 must here likewise be duly observed on the basis of a special and rectified survey;

2., that none of the present rustical holdings may be allowed to lapse, but that if, upon a fire damage or other misfortune, no one is found who will re-establish such a holding out of his own means, the Dominium must restore the buildings at its own cost;

3., that all the sovereign dues and other charges now resting upon the said estates, or hereafter to be laid upon them, [must], just as heretofore, be

paid over to Our treasuries appointed thereto, at the prescribed terms, and that for their security the necessary entry be made in the mortgage book of the lands and forests reserved by the Dominium;

4., that the timber rights pertaining to the monastery and the provostry of Ląd be likewise entered in the mortgage book upon the totality of the forests reserved from the dismemberment, and be secured in that manner; against which We consent that, if in the sequel the forests excepted from the dismemberment should be found insufficient for the timber rights pertaining to the fisc in respect of the Domain Office of Ratyn,

a compensation therefor shall be ascertained and secured by an annual canon. In witness whereof We have caused the present Consent to be drawn up and executed under the customary signature and the greater seal of Our Chamber of War and Domains. Thus done and given at Kalisch, the 28th of January 1806.

(L. S.) Royal South Prussian Chamber of War and Domains.
