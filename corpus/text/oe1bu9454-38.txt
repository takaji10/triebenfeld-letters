Triebenfeld, seriously ill, reports the receipt of 3000 Rthl from the banker Veit and the handing over of the order of payment to Amelang. He says he has used it to pay off part of new debts of 5176 Rthl. Instead of money, timber and boards from Konotop were accepted, with which 4176 Rthl are to be paid off. He says the Prince should make good to him the loss of 3480 Rthl. He advises raising 150000 Rthl in order to acquire papers worth 750000 Rthl and pay all the debts.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
38

Most gracious Prince and Lord, after all the vexation, which has quite undermined my health, I lie wretchedly ill and [uncertain: kunde] cannot write much. Only this for now: I have received the 3000 Rthl from the banker Veit, and only yesterday delivered the order to pay to Amelang.

Now I must still raise another 3000 Rthl, for the treasury received 5125 Rthl; of the 2/m I had only 3000 Rthl. I had to borrow through the help of His Excellency the Minister von Zastrow; one gave papers, we must [have] Courant it seems — these were now 5176 Rthl of new debt, which I have now had to discharge, so I have taken your 3000 Rthl for it. 2176 Rthl I thought to obtain upon the papers which Hecker had deposited as security; in the meantime, however, the papers had so fallen that upon 4000 Rthl of Märck Landschaft obligations no one would give even 500 Rthl.

The tavern too had some 2003[?] rt to pay on account of the defraying of the military execution; that now amounted afresh to 4209 Rthl Courant, which in these ghastly times were not to be raised — papers I could obtain, if I were willing to give Courant for them — that would then have come to 21000 Rthl. God helped me meanwhile: the Baupiner or Later took wood and boards from Konotop instead of money — by this means the 4176 Rthl will be discharged, and the Minister Zastrow already has his papers back — I suffer 3480 Rthl of loss on the wood, for I had to let it go at half price — this Your Serene Highness must one day make good to me. This loss is small against 25000 Rthl — As to the dahrliche let Your Serene Highness indeed take care — for you pay with 20000 Rthl in cash now hindert laufend that is — could you raise 150000 Rthl there, then you have papers to the value of 750000 Rthl and with them pay all your debts.

I can write no more today, I am too feeble; more in future — I am ever Your Serene Highness's

As soon as I have been, and I can quickly raise the Weise money, then I shall go to Vienna, and if it can be managed I shall come to Öhringen; but if business does not allow it, I shall return hither and write. Only let Your Serene Highness take care that you obtain 150000 Rthl, then Courland and all the stuff is paid. But it requires haste, the papers are rising wirdet. Berlin, the 14th of June

1808.

most faithful servant

v Triebenfeld

Something great must be afoot in the country: the Russian papers have risen unspeakably on the 8th of this month in Wrocław itself. There they hold the peace between Russia and England for certain. Now of our court I will say nothing. St: conducts himself badly, and by his money-in-paper manoeuvres he tears apart the last bond between the King and the nation. Also for the house St: only friendship —
