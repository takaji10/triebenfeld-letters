--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Kassierter Entwurf einer Antwort an den Fürsten: Die Territorialverhältnisse des Herzogtums Warschau seien für die an Preußen zurückfallenden Provinzen definitiv entschieden; die Entschädigung der Donatare sei einer besonderen Unterhandlung vorbehalten, zu der schon geschritten worden sei. Es werde Hardenberg ein besonderes Vergnügen sein, darin Gelegenheit zu finden, die Entschädigung für die entzogenen Trąbczyner Güter zu befördern. Der Entwurf verweist auf die anderweitige Verfügung vom 6. Juni 1815.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Cancelled draft of a reply to the Prince: the territorial arrangements of the Duchy of Warsaw have, he says, been settled definitively for the provinces returning to Prussia; the compensation of the holders of donations is reserved for a separate negotiation, which has already been begun. It would be a particular pleasure for Hardenberg to find in it an opportunity to further the compensation for the Trąbczyn estates taken from him. The draft refers to the other order of 6 June 1815.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Vienna, the __th of May 1815. To His Serene Highness and Lordship the Prince of Hohenlohe, etc.

at

Sławięcice

No. 3132

R. 28/5 to Rthl 28/5.

Cancelled, and see the further disposition of 6 June 1815. In most devoted reply to Your Highness's very kind letter of the 12th of this month I have the honour to respond: that at this moment the territorial relations of the Duchy of Warsaw are indeed definitively settled as regards those of its provinces reverting to Prussia; whereas as concerns the compensation of the respective donees in the said Duchy, the regulation of this matter is reserved to a separate negotiation, upon which, however, entry has already been made at this time. Given the sincere part I take in every occurrence concerning Your Highness etc., it will certainly afford me quite particular pleasure to find, in the course of this negotiation, an oppor-

-tunity to further the fulfilment of the wishes communicated to me by Your Highness in respect of compensation for the Trąbczyn estates taken from you. For the rest, I very gladly avail myself of the present occasion to renew to Your Highness etc. the assurance of that esteem with which I remain, etc. Vienna, etc.

In His Serene Highness's name
