--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Urges the Fürst to conclude the Sobottendorff business as vital: an 18-year lease of grounds, buildings and meadows for 36000 Rthl would satisfy a creditor owed 40000 Rthl without touching present revenues, made possible by settlements with Tschachmann or Cybalski and with Heinrich, whose 1300 Rthl withdrawal-money is secured on Blizanow. Presses for the prescribed instrument and the Sobottendorffs' speedy arrival, since all funds here are under arrest; complains of daily costs, judges, Lorenz, 300 Rthl demanded for the Warsaw suit, and expects 4000 Rthl interest from the Wytwiver case.

Appendix. 2) 6 July 1812

The affair with the Herr von Sobottendorff is of the greatest importance for Your Serene Highness. You put off a man to whom you owe 40000 Rthl, and you lose nothing of your present revenues, still less do you diminish the substance of your estates; [uncertain: 18 years throughout the] lease — the interest you keep free of encumbrance, and no creditor may [text lost] It is a great lease, but from where is the man to be satisfied? Meanwhile this too can only succeed now, because I have come to terms with Tschachmann or Cybalski and with Heinrich, to whom, however, on account of 1300 Rthl of withdrawal-money, I must give a mortgage upon my Blizanow; all these grounds, together with buildings and some remaining meadows, I lease to Herr v Sobottendorff for 18 years for 36000 Rthl; if [uncertain: 2t er] he is a good husbandman, he can make it come out; the soil is very good. — [uncertain: Mir] observe

that all this must happen very quickly. I await most longingly the arrival of the Herren v Sobottendorffs. Let Your Serene Highness see to it that the people come here quickly, and send me as speedily as possible the instrument as I have prescribed it to you, for Your Serene Highness can only authorise me to collect moneys which are free — but here at present everything is laid under arrest, and I can only seek to procure moneys in my capacity as creditor — the French [illegible] Decree entitles me before all others — by the instrument you are to issue I mean only to choose the shortest road to obtaining money and keeping the machinery in motion until I prevail. From nowhere can I take anything; daily there are expenses, the messengers' costs, without the carriage-hire, [uncertain: zahil] running over, so that today already 2 [uncertain: Ortl.] have again been sent to Kalisz and to [uncertain: Kopojer] and Ko; the accursed Lorenz eats up, altogether everything goes away with the judges, and when one has won one over, then

death and the devil fetch him away again. For the lawsuit at Warsaw 300 Rthl are now suddenly required; Almighty God knows where I am to take them from — but win the suit I must, for through it we obtain not only air on every side, but also hard money to offer [uncertain: um hätze bieten]. Concerning the Wytwiver suit I shall probably receive news today or tomorrow — that one must be won, and then you will receive 4000 Rthl in ready interest; the matter has nothing whatever to do with the one here. I am having it pursued through Salz, so that the creditors [uncertain: nothing further]. The coarse [uncertain: Schliefen] will thereby have his mouth stopped too, and a disgrace will be taken from me and from my [uncertain: Schweiner]. But do not, my Prince, let yourself be advised again by shifty people, and [uncertain: send/do] any other instrument than the one I have prescribed — for otherwise everything goes to the

devil — I mean it towards you heartily, honestly and well, both morally and physically; my children often go hungry along with me, and that because I am too grievously entangled in your affairs; I suffer and endure, with what is mine, everything possible — it must surely get better one day; we are once for all interwoven with one another and, so to speak, married; we must now [uncertain: abhobet] hold out and pull at the same rope together. We shall yet have many a struggle to sustain, but with God's assistance I hope still to obtain every advantage for us both.
