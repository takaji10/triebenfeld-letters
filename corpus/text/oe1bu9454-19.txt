--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Triebenfeld drängt auf eine Einigung mit Sobottendorff, dem der Fürst 40000 Rthl schuldet. Nach der Einigung mit Tschachmann und mit Hawich, dem er für 1300 Rthl eine Hypothek auf sein Blizanow geben muss, will er Gründe, Gebäude und Wiesen auf 18 Jahre für 36000 Rthl an Sobottendorff verpachten. Er bittet eilig um das Instrument nach seiner Vorschrift, da alles mit Arrest belegt ist und stellt dem Fürsten 4000 Rthl aus dem Witower Prozess in Aussicht, falls dieser gewonnen wird.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Triebenfeld presses for an agreement with Sobottendorff, to whom the Prince owes 40000 Rthl. After the agreement with Tschachmann and with Hawich, to whom he must give a mortgage on his Blizanow for 1300 Rthl, he intends to lease lands, buildings and meadows to Sobottendorff for 18 years for 36000 Rthl. He asks urgently for the instrument drawn up according to his instructions, since everything is under seizure, and holds out to the Prince the prospect of 4000 Rthl from the Witów lawsuit, should it be won.

6 July 1812 The business with Mr [uncertain: for] Sobottendorff is of the greatest importance for Your Serene Highness; you dispose of a man to whom you owe 40000 rt, and you lose nothing of your present revenues, still less do you diminish the substance of your estates, 18 years [illegible] it lease — you keep the interest free and no creditor may not grant It is a large lease, but from where is the man to be satisfied?

Let Your Serene Highness take care indeed that the people come here quickly, and send me as speedily as possible the instrument as I have drafted it for you, for Your Serene Highness can only collect monies, [uncertain: to me] authorise those that are free — but here at present everything is laid under arrest and I can only seek to procure monies as a creditor — the French G. decree entitles me before all others — by the instrument you are to issue I wish only to choose the shortest way to obtain money and to keep the machine running until I prevail. From nowhere can I take anything; daily there are expenses, the messengers' costs, without the carting, [illegible] running over, so that today already 2 [uncertain: Ortl.] have again been sent to Kalisz and to Kopojno and Konin, the cursed Lorenz devours, and altogether everything goes off with the judges, and when one has won one over, then

death and the devil fetch him away again. For the lawsuit at Warsaw 300 rt will now quickly be required; Almighty God knows where I am to take them from — but win the lawsuit I must, for by it we obtain not only breathing space in every direction but also hard money to [uncertain: hätze] offer. Concerning the Witów lawsuit I shall probably receive news today or tomorrow — that one must be won, and then you will receive 4000 rt in cash interest; the matter has nothing whatever to do with the one here, I am having it pursued through Salz so that the creditors get no scent of it. Thereby the coarse Schliefen will have his mouth stopped too, and a disgrace will be taken from me and from my Schweiner.

But do not, my Prince, let yourself be advised again by shifty people and send a different instrument from the one I have drafted — for otherwise everything goes to the

devil — I mean it towards you heartily, honestly and well, I both morally and physically, my children often go hungry with me, and that because I am too badly entangled in your affairs; I suffer and endure with my own, but everything possible — it must after all get better once; we are once and for all woven together and, so to speak, married; we must [uncertain: abhobet] now hold out and pull on the same rope with one another.
