--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Urges the Prince to close an 18-year lease of the remaining land, buildings and meadows to Mr. von Sobottendorff for 36,000 Rthl, satisfying a creditor owed 40,000 Rthl without touching present revenues, made possible by settlements with Tschachmann or Cybalski and Hainrich, the latter secured by a mortgage on Triebenfeld's Blizanow. Begs for the power of attorney worded exactly as prescribed, since everything is under arrest and he can only act as creditor; 300 Rthl is needed for the Warsaw suit, and the Wytwiver suit should yield 4,000 Rthl. Complains of costs, hunger and the lawyer Lorenz.

Appendix.

2) 6 July 1812 The business with the gentlemen before Sobottendorff is of the greatest importance to Your Serene Highness; you are putting off a man to whom you owe 40000 Rthl, and you lose nothing of your present revenues, still less do you diminish the substance of your estates — 18 years [uncertain: runs] the lease — the interest you keep free and no creditor may [text lost] It is a great lease, but from whence is the man to be satisfied?

Meanwhile this too can only succeed now because I have come to an agreement with Tschachmann or Cybalski and with Hainrich — to whom, however, on account of 1300 Rthl of relinquishment I must give a mortgage upon my Blizanow. All these grounds, together with the buildings and some meadows still remaining, I lease to Mr. von Sobottendorff for 18 years for 36000 Rthl; if he is a good husbandman he can bring it out, the soil is very good. — I note to myself

that all this must happen very quickly. I await most longingly the arrival of the gentlemen von Sobottendorff. Let Your Serene Highness see to it that the people come here quickly, and [uncertain: send] me as speedily as possible the instrument as I have prescribed it to you, for Your Serene Highness can only authorise me to collect monies which are free — but here everything is at present laid under arrest, and I can only seek to obtain monies as a creditor — the French G. decree entitles me before all others — by the instrument you are to issue I wish only to choose the shortest road to getting money and to keeping the machinery in motion until I prevail. From nowhere can I take anything; daily there are outlays, the messengers cost, without the carriage-hire, [uncertain: an overrunning sum]; today already 2 [uncertain: places] have again been sent to Kalisz and to Kopojer and Ko; the cursed Lorenz devours, makes away with everything along with the judges, and when one has won one over, then

death and the devil fetch him away again. For the lawsuit at Warsaw 300 Rthl will now quickly be required; almighty God knows where I am to take them from — but win the suit I must, for through it we obtain not only air on every side but also hard money to [uncertain: offer for baiting].

Concerning the Wytwiver suit I shall probably receive news today or tomorrow — that one must be won, and then you will get 4000 Rthl in cash interest; the matter has nothing whatever to do with the one here; I am having it pursued through Salz so that the creditors [text lost] nothing further. Then the coarse [uncertain: Schliefen] will also have his mouth stopped, and a disgrace will be taken from me and from my [uncertain: Schweiner].

But do not, my Prince, let yourself be advised again by shifty people, and [uncertain: issue] an instrument other than the one I have prescribed — for otherwise the whole goes to the

devil — I mean it toward you heartily, honestly and well; I [uncertain: suffer] both morally and physically; my children often go hungry with me, and that because I am too grievously entangled in your affairs; I suffer and endure with mine, but everything possible — it must after all one day become better; we are once for all woven together and, so to speak, married; we must now [uncertain: after all] hold out and pull at the same rope with one another. We shall yet have many a struggle to sustain, but with God's assistance I hope still to prevail in every way for us both. That [uncertain: Sommer] thus shot past beside the mark, and that you say the [uncertain: pressure] will not last long, gives me great joy — Mr. S. is a wicked man; he said he had not a Groschen for the return journey and that his horse was galled; I had 4 Rthl., which I gave him, and afterwards, one after another, he had much Gold about him.
