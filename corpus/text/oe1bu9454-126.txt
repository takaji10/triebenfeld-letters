Cabunis ist mit den Hussiten zurück — Es hat
die Leute außerordentlich gefallen
Sie wollen nun zurück eilen. Ihre Glaubensbrü¬
der zusammen ziehen und in 14 Tage alles abmachen
Cabanis hat aber viel Neuigkeiten mit gebracht
worüber ich mit Ewr Durchlaucht sprechen muß
Außerst nothwendig wird es daß gleich
Demand hingeht um alles ins beste Geleis
zu bringen.
Ich arbeite hier mit die Olesnicas Leute -
wolten Ewr Durchlaucht so gnädig sein und von
der Parade ein Virtel Stündchen bey mir kommen
den könten Höchstdieselben die Menschen alle Selbst
höhren, und gleich Höchſt Dero Befehle erteilen was
weiter gethan werden soll.
v Triebenfeld
[Breslau?] den 30ten April 1804

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports that Cabunis (Cabanis) has returned from the Hussites, who were exceedingly pleased and now wish to hurry back, assemble their co-religionists and settle everything within a fortnight; he has brought much news requiring discussion, and someone must go there at once to put matters on the best footing. Triebenfeld, meanwhile negotiating with the Olesnicas people, asks the Prince to call on him for a quarter of an hour after the parade to hear them himself and give orders.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Cabunis is back with the Hussites — The people liked it exceedingly, they now wish to hurry back, gather their brethren in the faith together and settle everything within 14 days. Cabanis has however brought back much news, about which I must speak with Your Serene Highness. It becomes most urgently necessary that someone go there at once to bring everything into the best track. I am working here with the Olesnicas people — would Your Serene Highness be so gracious as to come to me for a quarter of an hour from the parade, then Your Highness could hear all the people yourself, and issue Your most high commands at once as to what is further to be done. v Triebenfeld [Breslau?] the 30th April 1804
