--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Triebenfeld meldet, dass Cabanis mit den Hussiten zurückgekehrt ist, denen es außerordentlich gefallen habe. Sie wollen zurückeilen, ihre Glaubensbrüder sammeln und in 14 Tagen alles abmachen. Cabanis bringe viele Neuigkeiten, die Triebenfeld mit dem Fürsten besprechen müsse; dringend solle sogleich jemand hingehen. Triebenfeld arbeitet mit den Leuten aus Oleśnica und bittet den Fürsten, nach der Parade eine Viertelstunde zu ihm zu kommen, sie selbst anzuhören und Befehle zu erteilen.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Triebenfeld reports that Cabanis has returned with the Hussites, who he says liked it extremely well. They want to hurry back, gather their fellow believers and settle everything within 14 days. He says Cabanis brings a great deal of news that Triebenfeld must discuss with the Prince; urgently, someone should go there at once. Triebenfeld is working with the people from Oleśnica and asks the Prince to come to him for a quarter of an hour after the parade, to hear them himself and give orders.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
126

To His Most Serene

Highness. Cabanis is back with the Hussites — the people liked it exceedingly; they now wish to hurry back, gather their fellow believers together and settle everything within 14 days. But Cabanis has brought back a great deal of news, about which I must speak with Your Serene Highness. It becomes most extremely necessary that someone go there at once, to bring everything into the best track.

I am here working with the Oleśnica people — would Your Serene Highness be so gracious as to come to me for a quarter of an hour from the parade, for then Your Highness could hear all these persons yourself, and at once issue Your Highness's commands as to what is further to be done.

Wrocław, the 30th of April 1804

v Triebenfeld
