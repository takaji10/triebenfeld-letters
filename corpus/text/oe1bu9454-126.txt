Cabunis ist mit den Hussiten zurück — Es hat
die Leute außerordentlich gefallen
Sie wollen nun zurück eilen. Ihre Glaubensbrü¬
der zusammen ziehen und in 14 Tage alles abmachen
Cabanis hat aber viel Neuigkeiten mit gebracht
worüber ich mit Ewr Durchlaucht sprechen muß
Außerst nothwendig wird es daß gleich
Demand hingeht um alles ins beste Geleis
zu bringen.
Ich arbeite hier mit die Olesnicas Leute -
wolten Ewr Durchlaucht so gnädig sein und von
der Parade ein Virtel Stündchen bey mir kommen
den könten Höchstdieselben die Menschen alle Selbst
höhren, und gleich Höchſt Dero Befehle erteilen was
weiter gethan werden soll.
v Triebenfeld
[Breslau?] den 30ten April 1804

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A short note reporting that Cabanis has returned from the Hussites, who have taken an extraordinary liking to the land and mean to fetch their fellow believers and settle everything within a fortnight. Cabanis brings much other news that Triebenfeld must relay in person, and someone should go there at once. Triebenfeld is meanwhile dealing with the Olesnicas people and asks the Prince to call on him for a quarter of an hour after the parade to hear them himself and give orders.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Cabunis is back with the Hussites — The people have taken an extraordinary liking to it; they now mean to hurry back, gather their brethren in the faith together and settle everything within 14 days. Cabanis has, however, brought many pieces of news with him, about which I must speak with Your Serene Highness. It is in the highest degree necessary that someone go there at once, to bring everything into the best course.

I am working here with the Olesnicas people — would Your Serene Highness be so gracious as to come to me for a quarter of an hour from the parade, then Your Highness could hear all these people yourself, and at once issue Your Highness's commands as to what is further to be done.

v. Triebenfeld

[uncertain: Breslau] the 30th April 1804
