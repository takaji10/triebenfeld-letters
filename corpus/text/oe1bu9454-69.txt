--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports, via Lombardini, that the money could not be collected at once in Breslau and that dreadful roads and weather delayed the journey; nonetheless the writer reached Kontop by midday on the 25th, still finding Honrichs and Cabanis there, who left the same day in high spirits after Honrichs received 6000 f. and the original deeds of donation. Notes von Triebenfeld's badly afflicted right arm and his promise to come to Schlawentz for some weeks once his business is concluded. Signed Bally.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince, Most gracious Prince and Lord! for Most Serene Highness will already have learned through Mr. Lombardini that I was not able to receive the money at once in Breslau, and, even apart from the great impediments of the indescribable roads and weather, was detained. Notwithstanding this, however, I so hastened my journey from Breslau that on the 25th at midday I was here, and still found Honrichs and Cabanis

still found here, who then also, after Honrichs [had received?] 6000 f. and the deeds of donation in the original, departed that same day with the most joyful prospects. The Herr Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld finds himself suffering severely in the right[?]/legal arm, yet he has promised me that as soon as he shall have concluded his own affairs here, he will come at once for some weeks to Schlawentz. I answer After all the most graciously received instructions concerning this shall have been received, I shall hasten to return. In deepest reverence I remain, until death, as Your Most Serene Highness's Kontop, the 26th of February 1810. most humble, faithful and most obedient Bally
