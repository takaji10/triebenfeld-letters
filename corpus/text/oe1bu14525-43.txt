--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Abschlägige Antwort auf ein Gesuch des Fürsten von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen vom 31. Januar, der eine vidimirte Abschrift des gegen von Prusimski ergangenen Konfiskationsurteils samt Rechtskraftbescheinigung erbeten hatte, um seinen Besitztitel an den Trąbczyn'schen Gütern in Ordnung zu bringen. Die Regierung erklärt, das Urteil sei nicht bei ihr, sondern bei der Regierung in Toruń (Thorn) ergangen; der Fürst müsse sich dorthin wenden. Die eingereichte Anlage wird zurückgegeben. Unterzeichnet Danckelmann und Enge[r].

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A royal reply to the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, who had asked for a certified copy of the judgment confiscating von Prusimski's property, attested as having force of law, so that he could put in order his title to the Trąbczyn estates granted to him. The petition is refused as misdirected: the judgment was drawn up not before the Government at Poznań but before that at Toruń, and he must apply there. His original enclosure is returned.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Copy

Received the 9th of January 1799. By the Grace of God, Friedrich Wilhelm, King of Prussia. Our royal grace and favourable disposition to you beforehand, High-born Prince, particularly dear and faithful one! Upon the petition submitted by Your Lordship in the matter of the Treasury against von Prusimski on the 31st of January of this year, requesting a certified copy of the judgment of confiscation drawn up against the said von Prusimski, furnished with the attestation of its having force of law, for the purpose of putting in order the title of possession of the Trąbczyn estates conferred upon the same, we hereby grant Your Lordship, with the return of the original enclosure, this resolution: that the judgment of confiscation against the aforementioned von Prusimski was drawn up not before our Government here, but before our Government at Toruń, and that You must therefore apply to the latter in the matter. We remain disposed toward Your Lordship with royal grace and favourable will. Given at Poznań, the 4th of February 1799. Danckelmann

Enge[uncertain: r]

To His Lordship the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen
