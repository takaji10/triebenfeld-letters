Von Gottes Gnaden Friedrich Wilhelm König von Preußen
Unsern gnädigen Gruß zuvor, Ehrenvester Rath, Lieber, Getreuer
Wir eröffnen Euch hiermit auf Euren Antrag wegen
Resignationsleistung sämtlicher Betscher und in Cuja¬
wien belegenen Güther des Fürsten von Hohen¬
lohe Ingelfingen durchlaucht und Liebden
vom 13 d. M. daß der Cammerfiskal Beyer
bereits die erforderlichen Aufträge zu dieser Re¬
signationsleistung Fisci erhalten hat. Sind
Euch mit Gnaden gewogen.
Posen den 20 Februar 1802
Königl. SüdPreuß. Krieges- und Domanien Cammer¬
Münling[?]
An
den Fürstlich Hohenlohischen Bau
und Oekonomie Rath Hr Wilh. Glenk
zu Breslau

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Answering an application of the 13th, the Royal South Prussian War and Domains Chamber at Posen tells Wilhelm Glenck, building and economy councillor to the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen at Breslau, that the chamber's fiscal officer Beyer has already been instructed to carry out the formal resignation by the Treasury of its claim to the Betsche estates and those lying in Cujavia belonging to the Prince. A brief notice of authority given, with no further terms set out.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
By the Grace of God, Friedrich Wilhelm, King of Prussia. Our gracious greeting first, honourable Councillor, dear and faithful subject. We hereby inform you, upon your application of the 13th of this month concerning the making of the resignation of all the Betsche estates and those situated in Cujavia belonging to His Serene Highness and Lordship the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, that the Chamber fiscal officer Beyer has already received the requisite instructions for this making of the resignation of the Treasury. We remain graciously disposed towards you.

Posen, the 20th February 1802. Royal South Prussian War and Domains Chamber. Münling[uncertain: Münling]

To the Princely Hohenlohe Building and Economy Councillor Mr Wilh. Glenck at Breslau
