--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Royal confirmation, under the South Prussian Government at Posen, of the purchase contract of 16/25 January 1805 by which Friedrich Ludwig, Fürst zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, sold Kolno and Kaemen (Kamionna) in the Meseritz district to Bank Rendant George Conrad Leixner for 142,000 rtl., already paid, partly 53,139 rt. to von Broniewski. Filed with it are the notarial recognitions, the Posen protocol of 6 February 1805 (Melzer, Glenk, Guderian), church capitals, the 100,000 rt. Triebenfeld mortgage, the Krabielew timber suit against von Kwilecki, and Leixner's powers of attorney and their extension.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The original hereof is on 137 rl. stamped paper, and has been taken to the mortgage files of Kolno and Kaemen. We Friedrich Wilhelm, by the Grace of God King of Prussia p. p.

Do hereby make known and give to all and everyone, especially where it is needful, to know, that before the Commissarius appointed by Our Government here, Our Government Assessor Bewert, the reigning Prince Friedrich Ludwig zu Hohenlohe Ingelfingen, his Liebden, and the Bank Rendant George Conrad Leixner, both through their Mandatarien legitimated ad Acta for this purpose, Our Justice Councillor Melzer and Ew. Rath Guderian, appeared on the 6th of February 1805, expressly acknowledged the purchase contract concluded on the 16th and 25th of January ejusd. concerning the estates Kolno and Kaemen situated in the District of Posen, and requested that We might graciously be pleased to furnish this purchase contract with Our most high confirmation. The same, however, in its true content, reads as follows:

Between the Most Serene Prince and Lord, Lord Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Royal General of Infantry and Governor at Breslau, and the Royal Bank Director at Franckfurth an der Oder, Herr George Conrad Leixner, the following purchase contract has been concluded.

§. 1. His Most Serene Highness sells to Herr Bank Director Leixner the estates Kolno and Kaemen or Kamionna, situated in South Prussia, in the Department of Posen and its Meseritz district, acquired in property out of the deed of donation of the late King Friedrich Wilhelm the Second's Majesty, of the 9th of August 1796, with all appurtenances, mills, inhabitants, fields, woods, lakes, waters, services, hunting, propination rights, and generally with all rights and privileges, burdens and en-

cumbrances, as his true property, for a purchase Pretium of 142,000 rtll., one hundred and forty-two thousand Reichsthaler, according to the coinage standard of 1764.

§. 2. The purchase money of the 142,000 rtlr. is already fully paid. His Most Serene Highness gives receipt therefor, and acknowledges generally that He has no further demand upon the purchaser out of this transaction of sale.

It is here noted historically that the payment of the purchase moneys took place in the following manner.

a, 53,139 rt. 13 gg. 8 1/5 d. the purchaser has paid to the former Polish Rittmeister von Broniewski, who, as an immitted creditor, stood in the natural possession of the estates, as the sum legally awarded to him

Carried over 53,139 rt. 13 gg. 8 1/5. d., paid upon the Anweichung [assignment/instruction] of His Most Serene Highness.

b, 88,860 rt. 10 gg. 3 1/5. d. have been paid in cash by the purchaser to His Most Serene Highness Himself and upon His Highness's instructions (Anweisungen).

i. e. 142,000 rttl.

§. 3. Besides the purchase Pretium, the purchaser assumes the following items entered in the mortgage books upon Kolno and Kaemen:

a, a capital of 100 Florin, one hundred Polish florins, for the church at Kaemen, the interest whereof is fixed at four Polish marks yearly.

b, for the same church a capital of 250 rt., two hundred and fifty Reichsthaler, or 1500, fifteen hundred Polish florins, which bear interest at 3 1/2, three and one half per cent yearly.

The interest on these two items the purchaser assumes from St. John's Day 1804.

§. 4. The present sale takes place without valuation, in Pausch und Bogen. His Most Serene Highness nevertheless gives warranty for this:

a, that the surface area of Ankkorn [uncertain term], meadows, heaths and lakes set out in the survey registers, which together with the maps have been delivered up to the purchaser, is actually present. His Most Serene Highness herewith assures that no claim by anyone whatsoever to the parcels of land set out in these survey registers has hitherto come to His Highness's knowledge.

The obligation of His Most Serene Highness to answer for the correctness of the survey register in the above manner is limited to

three years.

b, that in public taxes no more is charged upon these estates than yearly for Kaemen 153 rt. 22 gg. 8 pf., for Kolno - 125 - 21 - 2, and in ecclesiastical dues for Kaemen 16 - 16 gg.

§. 5. The natural delivery of the estates to the purchaser has already taken place in the month of September 1804, and the proceedings recorded thereupon shall stand.

With a view to the warranty, the following principles have been adopted between the contracting parties:

a, that the usufruct of the estates is due to the purchaser from St. John's Day 1804, from which time the purchaser has also to bear the

burdens. Any older arrears will be answered for by His Most Serene Highness. His Most Serene Highness also bears the Justitiariat salary up to Michaelmas of last year.

b, that the estates shall be delivered to the purchaser as they stood and lay on the 15th of June of last year.

c, that the purchaser may however demand only that Beilaß, in field, cattle and farming inventory, which von Broniewski received upon his immission in the year 1786, according to the Acta already handed over to the purchaser in tens schon Sprache [unparseable], and was obliged to restore.

§. 6. Besides the church capitals noted in §. 2, there stands upon

the estates a mortgage capital of 100,000 rt., one hundred thousand Reichsthaler, entered out of a bond of indebtedness of His Most Serene Highness of the 1st of June 1804 in favour of the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, who has ceded this mortgage to the purchaser. This debt is however already fully discharged, and the purchaser hereby expressly renounces all claims arising therefrom, both against His Most Serene Highness and against the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld. It is likewise solely the purchaser's business to effect the cancellation of this nexus in the land and mortgage books. The costs of cancellation, however, shall be borne half by His Most Serene

Highness and half by the purchaser.

§. 7. The costs for the drawing up and confirmation of this sale, for the natural and civil tradition, and generally all costs of the sale, by whatever name, shall likewise be borne half and half by both contracting parties.

§. 8. Passive servitudes are not known to the vendor's Serene Highness. On the other hand, these estates enjoy a right of timber-taking in the Krabielew woods.

In a possessory suit against von Kwilecki such right was awarded to His Serene Highness, but in petitorio it was denied at first instance. The purchaser

takes over this rem litigiosam without demanding any warranty from the vendor's Serene Highness.

§. 9. All documents and files belonging to the estates the vendor's Serene Highness promises faithfully to deliver up to the purchaser, so far as it has not already been done; likewise also those which are still at present required in His Serene Highness's accounting suit against von Broniewski, [which] shall be handed over to the purchaser after the conclusion of that suit. It is however, as goes without saying, also free to the purchaser to have these papers not yet extradited inspected as often as he finds it necessary.

§: 10.

The contracting parties bind themselves to the maintenance of this contract in all its points and clauses, renounce all objections thereto, especially the objection of injury beyond the half (laesio enormis) and by whatever other name they may be called, and have executed this instrument by their own-hand signatures and appended seals.
