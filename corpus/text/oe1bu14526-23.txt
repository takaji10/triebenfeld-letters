--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Royal confirmation by the South Prussian Government at Posen of the sale of the estates Kolno and Kaemen (Kamionna), Meseritz district, granted to Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen by Friedrich Wilhelm II in 1796, to bank director George Conrad Leixner for 142,000 Rthl, paid partly by settling 53,139 Rthl with von Broniewski. The package includes the ten-paragraph contract sold in Pausch und Bogen, church capitals assumed, the disputed Krabielewo timber right, notarial attestations and Leixner's powers of attorney to Guderian.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The original of this is on 137 Rthl stamped paper, and has been taken to the mortgage records of Kolno and Kaemen. We Friedrich Wilhelm, by the Grace of God King of Prussia etc. etc.

Do hereby make known and give everyone, especially where it is needful, to know, that before the Commissioner appointed by Our Government here, Our Government Assessor Bewert, the reigning Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Your Lordship, and the bank cashier George Conrad Leixner, both through their agents legitimated hereto ad Acta, Our councillor of justice Melzer and Your Honour's councillor Guderian, appeared on the 6th of February 1805, expressly acknowledged the purchase contract concluded on the 16th and 25th of January ejusd. concerning the estates Kolno and Kaemen situated in the Posen district, and have requested that We might graciously be pleased to furnish this purchase contract with Our most high confirmation. The same, however, reads in its true content as follows:

Between the Most Serene Prince and Lord, Lord Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Royal General of Infantry and Governor at Breslau, and the Royal Bank Director at Frankfurt an der Oder, Mr George Conrad Leixner, the following purchase contract has been concluded.

§ 1. His Most Serene Highness sells to the Bank Director Leixner the estates Kolno and Kaemen or Kamionna, situated in South Prussia, in the Posen Department and its Meseritz district, acquired in property by virtue of the deed of donation of His Majesty the late King Friedrich Wilhelm II of the 9th of August 1796, with all appurtenances, mills, residents, fields, woodlands, lakes, waters, services, hunting, propination rights, and generally with all rights and privileges, burdens and encum-

brances, to his true property, for a purchase price of 142,000 Rthl, one hundred and forty-two thousand Reichsthaler, according to the coinage standard of 1764.

§ 2. The purchase money of the 142,000 Rthl has already been fully paid. His Most Serene Highness grants receipt thereof and acknowledges generally that He has no further claim upon the purchaser out of this transaction of sale.

It is noted here historically that the payment of the purchase moneys was effected in the following manner.

a, 53,139 Rthl 13 Groschen 8 1/5 d the purchaser has paid to the former Polish cavalry captain von Broniewski, who, as a creditor put in possession, stood in actual possession of the estates, as the sum legally awarded to him

Carried forward 53,139 Rthl 13 Groschen 8 1/5 d, paid upon the instruction of His Most Serene Highness.

b, 88,860 Rthl 10 Groschen 3 1/5 d have been paid in cash by the purchaser to His Most Serene Highness himself and upon His Highness's instructions.

i. e. 142,000 Rthl.

§ 3. Besides the purchase price, the purchaser assumes the following items entered in the mortgage books upon Kolno and Kaemen:

a, a capital of 100 florins, one hundred Polish florins, for the church at Kaemen, the interest whereon is fixed at four Polish marks yearly.

b, for the same church a capital of 250 Rthl, two hundred and fifty Reichsthaler, or 1500, fifteen hundred Polish florins, which bear interest at 3 1/2, three and one half per cent yearly.

The interest on both these items the purchaser assumes from Midsummer 1804.

§ 4. The present purchase is made without valuation, in Pausch und Bogen. His Most Serene Highness however gives warranty for this:

a, that the surface content of arable, meadows, heaths and lakes set out in the survey registers, which together with the charts have been handed over to the purchaser, is really present. His Most Serene Highness assures herewith that to His Highness no claim by anyone whatsoever upon the parcels of land set out in these survey registers is hitherto known.

The obligation of His Most Serene Highness to answer for the correctness of the survey register in the above manner is limited to

three years.

b, that no more in public dues encumber these estates than yearly for Kaemen 153 Rthl 22 Groschen 8 Pfennig, for Kolno - 125 - 21 - 2, and in ecclesiastical dues for Kaemen 16 - 16 Groschen.

§ 5. The actual delivery of the estates to the purchaser has already taken place in the month of September 1804, and the proceedings recorded thereon shall stand.

With a view to the warranty the following principles have been adopted between the contracting parties:

a, that the usufruct of the estates is due to the purchaser from Midsummer 1804, from which time the purchaser also has to bear the

burdens. Any older arrears shall be answered for by His Most Serene Highness. His Most Serene Highness also bears the justiciary's salary up to Michaelmas of last year.

b, that the estates shall be delivered to the purchaser as they stood and lay on the 15th of June of last year.

c, that the purchaser may nevertheless demand only that stock, in field, cattle and farming inventory, which von Broniewski received upon his being put in possession in the year 1786, according to the records already handed over to the purchaser in [uncertain: the Polish] language, and which he was obliged to restore.

§ 6. Besides the church capitals noted in § 2, there stands upon

the estates a mortgage capital of 100,000 Rthl, one hundred thousand Reichsthaler, entered by virtue of a bond of indebtedness of His Most Serene Highness of the 1st of June 1804 in favour of the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, who has ceded this mortgage to the purchaser. This debt, however, has already been fully satisfied, and the purchaser hereby expressly renounces all claims on that account, both against His Most Serene Highness and against the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld. It is likewise solely the purchaser's business to effect the cancellation of this encumbrance in the land and mortgage books. The costs of cancellation, however, shall be borne half by His Most Serene

Highness and half by the purchaser.

§ 7. The costs for the drawing up and confirmation of this purchase, of the actual and civil delivery of possession, and generally all costs of the sale, by whatever name, shall likewise be borne half by each of the two contracting parties.

§ 8. Passive servitudes are not known to His Serene Highness the seller. On the other hand there belongs to these estates a right of timber in the woods of Krabielewo.

In a possessory suit against von Kwilecki such right was awarded to His Serene Highness, but in the petitory suit it was denied him in the first instance. The purchaser

assumes this rem litigiosam without demanding any warranty from His Serene Highness the seller.

§ 9. All documents and records belonging to the estates, His Serene Highness the seller promises faithfully to deliver up to the purchaser, so far as this has not already been done; likewise also those which are still at present required in His Serene Highness's action of account against von Broniewski, to hand over to the purchaser after the conclusion of that action. It remains, however, free to the purchaser, as goes without saying, to have these papers not yet handed over inspected as often as he finds it necessary.

§ 10.

The contracting parties bind themselves to hold fast to this contract in all its points and clauses, renounce all objections against it, especially the objection of injury beyond the half, and by whatever other name they may be called, and have executed this instrument by their own-hand signatures and appended seals.
