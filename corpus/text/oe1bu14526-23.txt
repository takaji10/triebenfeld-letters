--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Royal confirmation by the South Prussian Government at Posen of the sale of Kolno and Kaemen (Kamionna) in the Meseritz district, granted to Friedrich Ludwig, Fürst zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen by the donation of 9 August 1796, to Banco Rendant George Conrad Leixner for 142,000 rtl. — 53,139 rt. 13 gg. paid to von Broniewski, the rest in cash — sold in Pausch und Bogen, with church capitals, the disputed Krabielew timber right, and cancellation of von Triebenfeld's 100,000 rt. mortgage. Enclosed: the contract, notarial attestations, and Melzer's, Glenk's, Guderian's and Leixner's powers of attorney.

The original hereof is on 137 rl. stamped paper, and has been taken to the mortgage files of Kolno and Kaemen. We Friedrich Wilhelm, by the Grace of God King of Prussia p. p.

Do hereby make known and give everyone, particularly where it is needful, to know, that before the Commissarius appointed by Our Government here, Our Government Assessor Bewert, the reigning Prince Friedrich Ludwig zu Hohenlohe Ingelfingen, his Liebden, and the Banco Rendant George Conrad Leixner, both through their Mandatarien legitimated ad Acta for this purpose, Our Justice Councillor Melzer and Ew. Rath Guderian, appeared on the 6th of February 1805, expressly acknowledged the purchase contract concluded on the 16th and 25th of January ejusd. concerning the estates Kolno and Kaemen situated in the Posen District, and requested that We might graciously deign to furnish this purchase contract with Our supreme Confirmation. The same, however, runs in its true content as follows:

Between the Most Serene Prince and Lord, Lord Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Royal General of Infantry and Governor at Breslau, and the Royal Banco Director at Franckfurth on the Oder, Herr George Conrad Leixner, the following purchase contract has been concluded.

§. 1. His Most Serene Highness sells to the Herr Banco Director Leixner the estates Kolno and Kaemen or Kamionna, situated in South Prussia, in the Posen Department and its Meseritz district, acquired in property by virtue of the deed of donation of His Majesty the late King Friedrich Wilhelm the Second, of the 9th of August 1796, with all appurtenances, mills, inhabitants, fields, woods, Senn, waters, services, hunting, propination rights, and generally with all rights and privileges, burdens and encum-

brances, to his true property, for a purchase price of 142,000 rtll., one hundred and forty-two thousand Reichsthaler, according to the coinage standard of 1764.

§. 2. The purchase money of the 142,000 rtlr. has already been paid in full. His Most Serene Highness gives receipt therefor and acknowledges generally that He has no further claim upon the Herr purchaser out of this transaction of sale.

It is noted here historically that the payment of the purchase moneys took place in the following manner.

a, 53,139 rt. 13 gg. 8 1/5 d. the Herr purchaser has paid to the former Polish Rittmeister von Broniewski, who as an immitted creditor stood in natural possession of the estates, as the sum legally adjudged to him

Carried over: 53,139 rt. 13 gg. 8 1/5. d., paid upon the [uncertain: instruction] of His Most Serene Highness.

b, 88,860 rt. 10 gg. 3 1/5. d. have been paid in cash by the Herr purchaser to His Most Serene Highness Himself and upon His most high instructions.

i. e. 142,000 rttl.

§. 3. Besides the purchase price the Herr purchaser assumes the following items entered in the mortgage books upon Kolno and Kaemen:

a, a capital of 100 Florin, one hundred Polish florins, for the church at Kaemen, the interest whereof is fixed at four Polish marks yearly.

b, for the same church a capital of 250 rt., two hundred and fifty Reichsthaler, or 1500, fifteen hundred Polish florins, which bear interest at 3 1/2, three and one half per cent yearly.

The interest on both these items the Herr purchaser assumes from Johannis 1804.

§. 4. The present purchase is made without valuation, in Pausch und Bogen. His Most Serene Highness however gives warranty for this:

a, that the surface area of [uncertain: arable], meadows, heaths and lakes set forth in the survey registers, which together with the maps have been extradited to the Herr purchaser, is really present. His Most Serene Highness assures hereby that up to now no claim by anyone whatsoever upon the parcels of land set forth in these survey registers is known to Him.

The obligation of His Most Serene Highness to answer for the correctness of the survey register in the above manner is limited to

three years.

b, that no more in public taxes lies upon these estates than yearly for Kaemen 153 rt. 22 gg. 8 pf., for Kolno – 125 – 21 – 2, and in ecclesiastical dues for Kaemen 16 – 16 gg.

§. 5. The natural delivery of the estates to the Herr purchaser has already taken place in the month of September 1804, and the proceedings drawn up thereupon shall stand.

With regard to the warranty, the following principles have been adopted between the contracting parties:

a, that the usufructs of the estates are due to the Herr purchaser from Johannis 1804, from which time the Herr purchaser must also bear the

burdens. Any older arrears shall be answered for by His Most Serene Highness. His Most Serene Highness also bears the Justitiariat salary up to Michaelmas of last year.

b, that the estates shall be handed over to the Herr purchaser as they stood and lay on the 15th of June of last year.

c, that the Herr purchaser may however demand only that supplement of field, cattle and husbandry inventory which von Broniewski received at his immission in the year 1786, according to the Acta already handed out to the Herr purchaser [uncertain: in tens schon Sprache], and was obliged to restore.

§. 6. Besides the church capitals noted in §. 2, there stands upon

the estates a mortgage capital of 100,000 rt., one hundred thousand Reichsthaler, entered by virtue of a bond of debt of His Most Serene Highness of the 1st of June 1804 in favour of the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, who has ceded this mortgage to the Herr purchaser. This debt however has already been fully satisfied, and the Herr purchaser hereby expressly renounces all claims in this regard, both against His Most Serene Highness and against the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld. It is likewise solely the business of the Herr purchaser to effect the cancellation of this nexus in the land and mortgage books. The costs of cancellation, however, shall be borne by half by His Most Serene

Highness and by half by the Herr purchaser.

§. 7. The costs for the drawing up and confirmation of this purchase, of the natural and civil tradition, and generally all costs of the sale, by whatever name, shall likewise be borne by half by each of the two contracting parties.

§. 8. Passive servitudes are not known to His Serene Highness the seller. On the other hand there belongs to these estates a right of timber in the Krabielew woods.

In a possessory suit against von Kwilecki this was adjudged to His Serene Highness, but in petitorio it was disallowed in the first instance. The Herr purchaser

assumes this rem litigiosam without demanding any warranty from His Serene Highness the seller.

§. 9. All documents and files belonging to the estates, His Serene Highness the seller promises faithfully to deliver over to the Herr purchaser, so far as this has not yet been done; likewise also those which are still for the time being required in the accounting suit of His Serene Highness against von Broniewski shall, upon conclusion of that suit, be handed to the Herr purchaser. It stands however free to the Herr purchaser, as goes without saying, to have these papers not yet extradited inspected as often as he finds it necessary.

§: 10.

The contracting parties bind themselves to hold fast to this contract in all its points and clauses, renounce all objections against it, in particular the objection of lesion beyond the half and whatever others they may bear the name of, and have executed this instrument by their own-hand signatures and appended seals.
