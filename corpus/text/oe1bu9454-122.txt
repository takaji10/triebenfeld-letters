Beigehendes Billet habe ich heute früh erhalten — Es war
aus Versehen in mein altes Logis liegen geblieben -
Herr Homuth hat desfals so eben wieder zu mich
geschickt und läst sehr bitten, daß diese Post bald
berichtiget wird damit er den Boten sogleich
den Eingang der 166 Rthl 16 g. mit anzeigen Kan¬
Cabanis reiſet ſo eben mit der Poſt nach Hus
sinietz ab - ich verspreche mir von dieſer Seite
den ſchnelleſten und glücklichſte Erfolg.
Von Berlin oder Franckfurth habe ich heute keine
Sylbe erhalten.
Breslau d 6ten April
1804.
v Triebenfeld

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Forwards a note received that morning, having been left by oversight at his former lodgings, and reports that Herr Homuth has sent to him again, pressing urgently for the matter to be settled so that he can notify the messenger of receipt of the 166 Rthl 16 Groschen. Cabanis is leaving by post for Hussinietz, from which Triebenfeld expects swift and happy success; nothing has come from Berlin or Frankfurt an der Oder.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The enclosed note I received this morning early — It had been left lying, by an oversight, in my old lodging - Herr Homuth has on that account just now sent to me again, and begs most earnestly that this item be settled soon, so that he may at once notify the messenger of the receipt of the 166 Rthl 16 g. Cabanis is departing this very moment by the post for Hussinietz - I promise myself from this quarter the swiftest and happiest success. From Berlin or Franckfurth I have received not a syllable today. Breslau, the 6th of April 1804. v Triebenfeld
