v Triebenfeld

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Der Schreiber leitet ein Billet weiter, das versehentlich in seinem alten Logis liegen geblieben war. Herr Homuth bittet dringend, diese Post bald zu berichtigen, damit er durch den Boten den Eingang der 166 Rthl 16 g anzeigen kann. Cabanis reist mit der Post nach Hussinitz ab; der Schreiber verspricht sich davon schnellen, glücklichen Erfolg. Aus Berlin oder Frankfurth hat er heute keine Nachricht erhalten.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The writer forwards a note that had been left behind by mistake at his former lodgings. He says Mr Homuth urgently asks that this item be settled soon, so that he can report through the messenger the receipt of the 166 Rthl 16 Groschen. Cabanis is leaving by post coach for Hussinitz; the writer says he expects a quick and happy outcome from this. He has had no news today from Berlin or Frankfurth.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
122 The enclosed note I received early this morning — it had by an oversight been left lying at my old lodging — Mr Homuth has just now sent to me again on this account and begs most earnestly that this post be settled soon, so that he may at once, by the messenger, report the receipt of the 166 Rthl. 16 Groschen as well — Cabanis is at this very moment setting off by post for Hussinitz — I promise myself from that quarter the swiftest and happiest success. From Berlin or Frankfurth I have not received a syllable today.

Wrocław, the 6th of April

1804.

v Triebenfeld
