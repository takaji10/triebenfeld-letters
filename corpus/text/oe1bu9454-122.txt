Beigehendes Billet habe ich heute früh erhalten — Es war
aus Versehen in mein altes Logis liegen geblieben -
Herr Homuth hat desfals so eben wieder zu mich
geschickt und läst sehr bitten, daß diese Post bald
berichtiget wird damit er den Boten sogleich
den Eingang der 166 Rthl 16 g. mit anzeigen Kan¬
Cabanis reiſet ſo eben mit der Poſt nach Hus
sinietz ab - ich verspreche mir von dieſer Seite
den ſchnelleſten und glücklichſte Erfolg.
Von Berlin oder Franckfurth habe ich heute keine
Sylbe erhalten.
Breslau d 6ten April
1804.
v Triebenfeld

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A brief note forwarding an enclosure that had been left behind at Triebenfeld's former lodgings: Herr Homuth has sent again and presses urgently for settlement of 166 Rthl 16 Groschen so that he can confirm receipt to the messenger. Cabanis is leaving by post for Hussinietz, from which Triebenfeld expects a quick and favourable outcome. Nothing at all has come in that day from Berlin or Frankfurt an der Oder.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The enclosed note I received early this morning — it had by oversight been left lying in my old lodgings. Herr Homuth has on that account just now sent to me again and begs most earnestly that this item be settled soon, so that he may at once notify the messenger of the receipt of the 166 Rthl 16 g. as well. Cabanis is this very moment departing by the post for Hus sinietz — I promise myself from that quarter the swiftest and happiest success. From Berlin or Frankfurt an der Oder I have not received a syllable today.

Breslau, the 6th of April

1804.

v. Triebenfeld
