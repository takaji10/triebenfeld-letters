--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Michalina Prusimska bittet den König auf Französisch um Beschleunigung: Vor achtzehn Monaten habe sie ihm in Posen eine Denkschrift überreicht und um das Vermögen gebeten, das ihr nach dem Tod ihrer Mutter zustehe; er habe versprochen, die Justiz zu beschleunigen. Nach vier Jahren sei der in Posen begonnene Prozess erst in erster Instanz entschieden, und die Entscheidung sei nun an den Gerichtshof in Berlin gegangen. Als verlassene Waise klage sie gegen den Fürsten von Hohenlohe. Die Bitte wird am 26. Januar 1802 dem Minister von der Reck zur Beschleunigung zugewiesen; er lässt ihr am 29. Januar mitteilen, sie habe die Publikation des Appellationsurteils zu erwarten.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Michalina Prusimska asks the King, in French, to hasten her case: eighteen months ago she handed him a memorandum at Poznań asking for the fortune due to her after her mother's death, and he promised to speed justice. After four years the suit begun at Poznań has been decided only in the first instance, and the decision has now gone to the court in Berlin. She is, she writes, an abandoned orphan pleading against the Prince of Hohenlohe. The petition is passed to Minister von der Reck on 26 January 1802 to be hastened; on 29 January he has her told that she may expect the appeal judgment to be published.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Sire!

Eighteen months ago, having had the good fortune to be presented to Your Majesty at Poznań, I handed you a memorial in which I begged you to have restored to me the fortune which, after the death of my mother, was mine by just right. You received my prayer with kindness and promised me to hasten the justice I asked of you. — And yet, Sire, it is now four years that this lawsuit, begun at Poznań, has been decided only in the first instance, and at the moment when I was awaiting a second decree, my guardian writes to me that the decision has just been referred to the Court of Justice at Berlin.

[Written by the receiving office:]

29 Jan.

To the Minister of State Baron [uncertain: Freyht] von der Reck, for expediting. Berlin, 26 January 1802.

Received the 28th.

Let the petitioner be notified that the appeal judgment, as appears from inquiries made, has been [uncertain: beim] sent back by the Superior Appeal Senate, and that she [uncertain: ist] therefore has its publication to await.

Berlin, 29 January 1802 Reck

Done

the 31st of the same month.

I know nothing of the degrees of the laws, but every unfortunate, and all the more a forsaken orphan, dares to have recourse to Your Majesty's benevolence and to beg you to deign to hasten the judgment that is to restore me to the property of which I have been deprived for so long. It is with confidence, Sire, that I ask of your fatherly heart the grace of fulfilling the promise you deigned to make me. I know it: I am pleading against the Prince of Hohenlohe; if, then, it is not Your Majesty's protection that sustains me, bereft of all help I shall be abandoned to the harshest poverty. May pity, joined to Your Majesty's goodness and clemency, receive favourably the prayer that I dare to address to you; as for me, I shall never cease to be, Sire,

Your Majesty's

Dresden, 22 January

1802.

most devoted and submissive

subject Michaline Prusimska.
