--- SUMMARY (English) ---
An urgent appeal for 900 Rthl: with the Prince having paid not a Groschen of interest, Triebenfeld must himself meet 700 Rthl promised for the inventory under his guarantee, 175 Rthl interest since 1799 and 35 Rthl costs, some 910 Rthl in all. His cash is gone after paying 1537 Rthl 12 gg the day before in the Nudnichisch matter; Wildegans and Heyman Pincus promise settlement but nothing comes, and execution threatens. He offers repayment from timber monies at Midsummer.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
All pleading is of no avail; I am to pay and must pay. The Prince has, as the enclosure shows, not settled so much as a single Groschen of interest.

I must therefore pay the

700 Rthl promised by the Prince for the inventory under my guarantee, and from 1799 the interest at 175 b

likewise legal costs

35 Rthl

altogether

910 Rthl. My cash-box is exhausted, for only yesterday I paid out 1537 Rthl 12 gg in the Nudnichisch matter – which the Prince, according to fourfold judicial documents, so solemnly promised to settle. Wildegans and Heyman Pincus promise to settle everything – only God knows when. In the meantime I must pay, if I do not want execution in the house. Could Your Serene Highness help me with the 900 Rthl? Then have the grace to do so – at Midsummer I can support Your Serene Highness again with the timber monies.

Breslau, the 2nd of May 1804

v. Triebenfeld
