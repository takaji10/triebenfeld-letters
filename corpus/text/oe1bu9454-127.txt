--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Triebenfeld bittet den Fürsten um Hilfe mit 900 Rthl, da er, um Execution zu vermeiden, 910 Rthl zahlen muss: 700 Rthl für das vom Prinzen unter seiner Garantie zugesagte Inventarium, 175 Rthl Zinsen von 1799 und 35 Rthl Prozesskosten. Der Prinz habe keine Zinsen berichtigt. Seine Kasse sei erschöpft, da er gestern bereits 1537 Rthl 12 gg in einer weiteren Sache des Prinzen gezahlt habe. Wildegans und Heyman Pincus versprechen Berichtigung. Zu Johanni will er den Fürsten mit Holzgeldern unterstützen.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Triebenfeld asks the Prince for help with 900 Rthl, since to avoid enforcement he must pay 910 Rthl: 700 Rthl for the inventory promised by the Prince under his guarantee, 175 Rthl of interest from 1799, and 35 Rthl of legal costs. He says the Prince has settled no interest. His cash is exhausted, as yesterday he already paid 1,537 Rthl 12 Groschen in another of the Prince's affairs. Wildegans and Heyman Pincus promise settlement. At Midsummer he intends to support the Prince with timber money.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
127 All pleading is of no avail, I am to and must pay. The Prince, as the enclosure shows, has not settled so much as a single Groschen of interest.

I must therefore pay the = 700 Rthl. promised by the Prince for the inventory under my guarantee, and the interest since 1799 amounting to = 175 Rthl., likewise court costs = 35 Rthl.

in all = 910 Rthl.

My funds are exhausted, for I already paid out 1,537 Rthl 12 Groschen yesterday in the Nudnich matter — which the Prince, according to fourfold court documents, so solemnly promised to settle — Wildegans and Heyman Pincus promise to settle everything — only God knows when — Meanwhile I must pay, if I am not to have distraint in the house. Can Your Serene Highness help me with the 900 Rthl? Then have the goodness to do so — At Midsummer I can again support Your Serene Highness with the timber monies.

Wrocław, the 2nd of May 1804

v Triebenfeld
