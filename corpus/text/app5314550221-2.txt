--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Abschrift (Nr. 15 des Kopialbuchs, überschrieben „Privilegium Gradziem Ruchowia“) einer lateinischen Urkunde, gegeben in Pyzdry am Montag nach Kreuzerhöhung 1461 (21. September). Piotr von Szamotuły, Kastellan von Posen und Generalstarost von Großpolen, bezeugt, dass die ungeteilten Brüder Jan Wochneth, Pfarrer in Koscielec, Mikołaj Wochneth von Bartodzieje und ein dritter Bruder von Osiecza, zusammen mit ihren Neffen Marcin, Jan und Maciej Bochneth, dem Abt Cornelius, dem Prior Jan, Wolmar, dem Rektor der Pfarrkirche in Ląd, und dem ganzen Konvent von Ląd mit Zustimmung König Kasimirs auf ewig aufgelassen haben: drei ganze Hufen mit Gärten im Dorf und Erbgut Łukom im Bezirk Konin, in der Pfarrei Zagórów, dazu den Wald Grądzyń mit seinen Gehölzen, die Wälder Chmielniki und Lome bis zu dem Weg namens Rosiszewska, der von Łukom und dem Dorf Lomowo zum Hügel Karczemne gory führt, die Sümpfe Karczemne Lugi und ein Feld oberhalb von Imielno, mit allen Rechten und Nutzungen, ohne sich etwas vorzubehalten. Der Preis sind 400 Mark breiter Groschen, 48 Groschen auf die Mark. Unter den Zeugen ist Jan Sobocki, Burggraf von Posen. Am Rand steht: „und mit dem Wald Lomy, von den Grenzen“.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A copy (no. 15 of the copy-book, headed "Privilegium Gradziem Ruchowia") of a Latin charter given at Pyzdry on the Monday after the Exaltation of the Holy Cross 1461 (21 September). Piotr of Szamotuły, castellan of Poznań and general captain of Greater Poland, attests that the undivided brothers Jan Wochneth, parish priest at Koscielec, Mikołaj Wochneth of Bartodzieje and a third brother of Osiecza, together with their nephews Marcin, Jan and Maciej Bochneth, resigned for ever to Abbot Cornelius, Prior Jan, Wolmar, rector of the parish church at Ląd, and the whole convent of Ląd, with King Kazimierz's consent: three whole mansi with gardens in the village and heritage of Łukom in the district of Konin, in the parish of Zagórów, with the forest of Grądzyń and its woods, the forests of Chmielniki and Lome as far as the road called Rosiszewska, which runs from Łukom and the village of Lomowo to the hill of Karczemne gory, the marshes of Karczemne Lugi and a field above Imielno, with all rights and profits, reserving nothing to themselves. The price is 400 marks of broad groschen, 48 groschen to the mark. Among the witnesses is Jan Sobocki, burgrave of Poznań. In the margin is written "and with the forest of Lomy, from the boundaries".

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Privilege "Gradziem Ruchowia". 15.

In the name of the Lord, Amen. For the perpetual memory of the matter. We, Piotr of Szamotuły, castellan of Poznań, general captain of Greater Poland, make known by the tenor of these presents to all whom it concerns: that, coming to our knowledge and to the presence of other nobles of the same land, the honourable and noble lords Jan

Jan Wochneth, parish priest at Koscielec, Mikołaj Wochneth of Bartodzieje, and [uncertain: Barlyus] Wochneth of Osiecza, three brothers german, undivided; and also the honourable and noble Marcin Bochneth, Jan Bochneth and Maciej Bochneth, brothers german, heirs german of the aforesaid Jan Bochneth, parish priest at Koscielec, of Mikołaj of Bartodzieje and of [uncertain: Wardzch] of Osiecza, undivided in their said goods with their said paternal uncles; not compelled, not forced, nor led astray by any other error, but sound in mind and body, having used the wholesome counsel of their friends and having first deliberated maturely among themselves, resigned in perpetuity three whole mansi with gardens in the village and heritage of Łukom in the district of Konin, lying in the parish of Zagórów; the forest Grądzyń with the woods and groves and the Pyzdry mounds; with the forest Chmielniki and with the forest Lome, up to the road called Rosiszewska, which goes from Łukom and the village of Lomowo to the hill Karczemne gory, and the marshes otherwise called Karczemne Lugi; and with the field above Imielno; and with all right, dominion and property, as they alone held, had and possessed them, reserving for themselves and their lawful successors no right whatever of dominion or property in them; with all and singular plots, courts, houses, barns, fields, meadows, pastures, grasslands, forests, woods, groves, thickets, enclosures, brushwood, oak woods, scrub, pine woods, hills, rises, [uncertain: Stenis], marshes, lakes, ponds, peasants, labours, burdens, gardens and gardeners, and generally with all and singular useful fruits, incomes and revenues, by whatever name and word they are called, belonging and pertaining in any way to the said mansi and the other goods described and designated above, far and wide, as the said mansi are marked off and bounded round about in their bounds and limits; for four hundred marks of good broad middle groschen of the coin and count customary in Poland, reckoning forty-eight groschen to each mark; to the Reverend Father, Lord Cornelius, abbot, and the religious Jan, prior, and Wolmar, rector of the parish church at Ląd, and to the whole convent of the monastery of Ląd, having for this the consent and special will of the Most Serene Prince and Lord, Lord Kazimierz, by the grace of God King of Poland etc., our most gracious lord: for the said lords the abbot, the prior and the whole convent and their successors to hold, have and possess, to use and enjoy, to sell, alienate, exchange, give, donate, and turn to their own uses and pleasure, as shall seem better and more useful to them and their successors for the time being. In witness of which our seals are hung beneath these presents. Done and given at Pyzdry on the Monday next after the feast of the Exaltation of the Holy Cross, in the year 1,461. Present there the well-born and noble lords Jan Swidwa the younger, Jan Sobocki, burgrave of Poznań, Mikołaj Kamski, [uncertain: Gavisso] Gorecki, Mikołaj Dabrowski, Krystyn Goszdychowski, and many others worthy of trust in the matters above.

[Written in the margin:]

And with the forest of Lomy, from the boundaries
