--- SUMMARY (English) ---
News from Vienna of Napoleon's return: Grenoble and Lyon taken, some 30,000 men under arms, Soult and Berthier named as his chief supporters, a 10 million franc price on his head with Massena pledging 3 million of his own, and alarm at his appeals to the rabble; rumours swing hourly. Amid this, requests favours for Court Councillors Barbe, Kraibe, Heym and Philipsborn: a wicker waggon and horses to Schlawenschitz, passes for servants, blank forms, and urgently that Eberhard be sent.
