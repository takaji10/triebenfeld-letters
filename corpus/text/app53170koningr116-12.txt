M Chełmski Decretum Commissoriale Campestre Requisivit
Ad Officium et Acta praesentia Castrensia Coninensia personaliter venientes Magnificus Stanislaus Scibór Chełmski Bonorum Łukom cum Attinentiis haeres Requisivit ab Officio praesenti Sibique Extradi Petiit Decretum quoddam Commissoriale Campestre Inter haeredes Villarum Nowawieś Łazy Osiny Trąmpczyn Łukom et Alias in Eodem Decretum expressas vi Commissionis A Statibus Regni Republicae Constitutione Anni Millesimi Septingentesimi Septuagesimi Quinti emanatae Prolatum et per Illustres Magnificos Commissarios ex parte Magnifici Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevice~ Subscript~ ac si More oblatae in Actis Officii reperibile Cui requisitioni Annuendo[?] Idem Officium praetens~ Decretum extradere Voluit Verum Quoniam requisito temporis spatio in Actis non reperitur Ideo illud uti Irreperibile extradere non valet Eodem Nomine Publica litt[?] [?]atin Juda[?] Superequisitionem.
Stanisław Scibor Chełmski mp

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Eintrag ohne Datum, wohl vom Oktober 1777. Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Erbherr von Łukom, erscheint persönlich vor dem Burgamt Konin und verlangt die Herausgabe eines Feldkommissionsdekrets, das zwischen den Erbherren der Dörfer Nowa Wieś, Łazy, Osiny, Trąbczyn, Łukom und anderer ergangen sei, kraft der von den Ständen mit der Konstitution von 1775 erteilten Kommission, und das die Kommissare auf Seiten Prusimskis unterschrieben hätten. Das Amt findet es in seinen Akten nicht, kann es nicht herausgeben und bescheinigt die Nachsuche. Chełmski hat unterschrieben.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
An undated entry, probably of October 1777. Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, heir of Łukom, appears in person before the castle office at Konin and asks to be given a field commission decree pronounced between the heirs of the villages of Nowa Wieś, Łazy, Osiny, Trąbczyn, Łukom and others, under the commission granted by the Estates in the constitution of 1775, and signed by the commissioners on Prusimski's side. The office does not find it in its records, cannot give it out, and certifies the search. Chełmski signed.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The Right Honourable Chełmski Requisitioned the Field Commissorial Decree

Coming in person before the present Municipal Court and Records of Konin, the Right Honourable Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, heir of the goods of Łukom with its appurtenances, requisitioned and requested from the present Office that there be issued to him a certain Field Commissorial Decree — issued between the heirs of the villages of Nowawieś, Łazy, Osiny, Trąbczyn, Łukom, and others named in the same Decree, by virtue of the Commission issued by the Estates of the Republic of Poland by the Constitution of the Year 1775, and subscribed by the Most Illustrious Right Honourable Commissioners on behalf of the Right Honourable Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — as if submitted in the manner of an Oblata, findable among the records of the Office. The same Office, consenting to this Requisition, was willing to issue the said claimed Decree; but since within the required period it is not found in the Records, it therefore cannot issue it as irretrievable, and makes public attestation [...] regarding the Requisition.

Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, by his own hand
