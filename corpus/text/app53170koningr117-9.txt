--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Bericht des Gerichtsboten Jan Jankowski von Łukom, eingetragen in Konin am 25. April 1778. Er hat Antoni Prusimski eine königliche Ladung vor das Kalischer Landgericht in Konin zugestellt, ausgestellt in Kalisz am Karfreitag 1778, auf Betreiben von Stanisław Chełmski, Erbherr von Łukomia. Prusimski soll das Dekret hören, mit dem das Gericht des Ortstermins in Trąbczyn die Sache an das volle Gericht verwiesen hat; Chełmski verlangt, dass ihm die von Jan Tracholz auf ihn übertragene Summe samt Zinsen und Kosten zugesprochen wird. Der Bote hat die Ladung im Gutsamt von Trąbczyn in Gegenwart des Verwalters hinterlegt.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A report of the court messenger Jan Jankowski of Łukom, entered at Konin on 25 April 1778. He has served on Antoni Prusimski a royal citation before the Kalisz land court at Konin, dated at Kalisz on Good Friday 1778, at the instance of Stanisław Chełmski, heir of Łukomia. Prusimski is to hear the decree by which the court of the sitting on the ground at Trąbczyn sent the cause to the full court; Chełmski asks that the sum made over to him by Jan Tracholz be adjudged to him with interest and costs. The messenger left the citation in the estate office at Trąbczyn in the presence of the steward.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
On behalf of the Honourable Chełmski against the Honourable Prusimski — Relatio

To the office and present records of the Konin Municipal Court, appearing in person, the Honest Jan Jankowski of the village of Łukom, Royal Court Summoner, General, sound of health, acknowledged the authenticated citation letter, issued for the Kalisz Land Court sessions of the district to be held at Konin, of the following tenor:

Stanislaus Augustus, by the Grace of God King of Poland, Grand Duke of Lithuania, of Russia, of Prussia, et cetera — to you, the Honourable Antoni of Kolno Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, hereditary owner of the estates of Trąbczyn — regarding your person and estates, generally all claims and monetary sums, held and to be held — we command that you appear in person and peremptorily before us and our Kalisz Land Court sessions, first and next to be held at Konin, at the instance of the Honourable Stanisław Chełmski, hereditary owner of the estates of Łukomia, plaintiff; who, adhering to his manifest and likewise to the counter-manifest made or to be made, cites you; or rather

— [cites you] to the prepared decree of the remission made to the full court by the Condescension Court at the condescension term at Trąbczyn, issued expeditiously between himself the plaintiff on one part and you the cited on the other part — cites you to see and hear:

the suit of himself the plaintiff, instituted against you, to be adjudged, notwithstanding any manifests and prosecution terms of yours, if such have intervened, in connection with the plaintiff's documents; the payment of the sum transferred to him the plaintiff from the Honest Jan Tracholz, together with provisions and reimbursement of litigation costs and any processes and delays — from the reasons to be set out at the prosecution term — to be adjudicated to him the plaintiff; and all other things required by the matter, the documents, and justice — to be decreed, decided, and sentenced against you. For which, being cited, be ready to obey, to attend the term and to respond judicially. Given at Kalisz on Good Friday in the Year of the Lord 1778.

At the estates of Trąbczyn, in the estate office there, the letters on the table of the heated room, with the estate manager present — on today's day, as contained in the act, he placed and left.
