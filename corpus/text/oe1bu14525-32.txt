--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Ein Schreiben aus Kalisz, unterzeichnet Schultz, ersucht die Oberamtsregierung zu Wrocław, dem regierenden Fürsten Friedrich Ludwig von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, wie am 24. September des Vorjahres erbeten, baldigst den Huldigungseid abzunehmen und das Protokoll darüber zu übersenden.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A letter from Kalisz, signed Schultz, asks the Oberamtsregierung, the provincial high court at Wrocław, to take the oath of homage from the reigning Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen as soon as possible, as requested on 24 September of the year before, and to send the record of it.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
We hereby most humbly request a Royal and Most Laudable Supreme Government Board to have the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Friedrich Ludwig, ad requisitionem of the 24th of September of last year, now perform the homage (Homagium) as soon as may be agreeable, and to be so good as to transmit to us the record taken thereof. Kalisz, the 18th of March 1800. Schultz

To a Royal and Most Laudable Supreme Government Board at Wrocław.
