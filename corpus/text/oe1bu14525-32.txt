--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A request from Schultz at Kalisz to the Supreme Government Board at Wrocław, following up an earlier requisition of 24 September, asking that Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, be required to perform his oath of homage for the granted estates at the earliest convenient date, and that the record made of the ceremony be sent back to Kalisz. A single short administrative note with no financial content.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
We hereby most humbly request a Royal and Most Laudable Supreme Government Board to have the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Friedrich Ludwig, ad requisitionem of the 24th of September of last year, now perform the homage (Homagium) as soon as may be agreeable, and to be so good as to transmit to us the record taken thereof. Kalisz, the 18th of March 1800. Schultz

To a Royal and Most Laudable Supreme Government Board at Wrocław.
