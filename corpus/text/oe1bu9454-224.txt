--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports at length on General Schimmelpfennig's terms for acquiring the Zagorow estates at 23000: he would bring in Seliz and Modkewo for 110000, Johnston's Goła wies (6000 Rthl), and 66000 Rthl of East Prussian mortgage bonds al pari, with further sums in cash or Silesian bonds; his sons-in-law Rittmeister von Kotzow and v. Johnston are to inspect after the holidays with Lieut. Sommer. The rival sale to Weigel and Stössel has collapsed, as Rittmeister v. Stössel of Ruzynowice confirms. Urges speedy conclusion of the lawsuits, money being indispensable, and mentions an express courier to Öhringen summoning his son.
