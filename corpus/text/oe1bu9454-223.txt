--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Written from Schlawenschitz by the Fürst himself, replying to Triebenfeld's letter of 20 November: he presses for rapid sale of the Zagorow estates to clear his private debts, doubts any bargain with Weigel and Stössel unless both agree, and declines Ruszynowią and Blizanow. General v. Schimmelpfennig and his sons want Zagorow, offering the debt-free Szelce and Maderzewo (bought for 26000 ducats, now valued above 100000 Rthl), plus Gollawia; Lieut. Sommer is to be sent ahead and rewarded. Also reports Adele's betrothal to the Hereditary Prince of Hohenloh Kirchberg, and his need of ready money by Christmas.

I believe I have overlooked nothing, nor sinned in the matter by any want of circumstantiality.
