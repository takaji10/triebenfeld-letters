--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Der Fürst berichtet über ein Tauschangebot des Generals v Schimmelpfennig, der die Zagorower Güter gegen seine Güter Szelce und Maderzewo bei Mysłowice eintauschen will (Ertrag 8000 Rthl, nach anderen 6000 Rthl). Lieutenant Sommer soll die Besichtigung in Zagórów vorbereiten. Einen Abschluss mit Weigel bezweifelt er. Er braucht bis Weihnachten dringend Bargeld, fragt nach dem Trąbczyner Prozess nicht mehr und erlaubt, Trąbczyn vorteilhaft in einen Handel einzubeziehen. Einem Werber aus Hohenlohe-Kirchberg gab er seine Zustimmung zur Heirat mit Adele.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The Prince reports an exchange offer from General v Schimmelpfennig, who wants to trade the Zagórów estates for his own estates Szelce and Maderzewo near Mysłowice (yield 8,000 Rthl, according to others 6,000 Rthl). Lieutenant Sommer is to prepare the inspection at Zagórów. He doubts a deal with Weigel will be concluded. He urgently needs cash by Christmas, no longer asks about the Trąbczyn lawsuit, and permits Trąbczyn to be included in a transaction if this is advantageous. He gave a suitor from Hohenlohe-Kirchberg his consent to marry Adele.

God grant that I may soon obtain peace, [uncertain: as one leaves off] being unjust towards me, and then my limited wishes are [uncertain: fully] attained; one of the foremost means to this is the achievement of the business here set forth, or some [illegible] early alienation of the Zagórów estates suited to my circumstances. You have the liberty to employ Trąbczyn in the bargain, on this or another side, if it can be done with advantage, just as I will refuse no useful way of attaining the end; everything else being left to your prudence and experience. Do not let me wait long for news, for every definite intelligence can

lead also to [uncertain: decisive] ends, just as to leave me in [uncertain: suspense] is a cruelty. God keep you in health; I was ailing some days, but since yesterday it goes better; only my eyes still plague me greatly through their inflammation. Hence I make use of a strange hand and dictate this. I believe I have overlooked nothing, nor sinned in the matter by any want of fullness. The wish to make it also quite [uncertain: intelligible] has led me to an abuse of your patience, and I therefore beg you, my esteemed friend, to pardon the length of this epistle. It is with the most excellent esteem that I remain Your Right Honourable's

Sławięcice, the 4th of December 1811.
