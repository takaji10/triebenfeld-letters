Trąbczyn, 15 maja 1434
I. N. Kanonik kruszwicki Wojciech z Trąbczyna upoważnia swego brata Jana z Trąbczyna do odbioru długu od scholastyka kruszwickiego Jaranda.
Kop.: Kal. 9175, s. 373. Tytuł: Mandatum ad levandum debitum.
Uw.: 1. O dacie zob. niżej, przypis a; 2. Zob. też dok. następny.
In nomine Domini amen. Sub anno a nativitate eiusdem Mo CCCCo XXXo quintoa, indicione XII, pontificatu sanctissimi in Christo patris et domini nostri, domini Ewgenii divina providencia pape quarti, anno ipsius quarto, sabbato in vigilia Pentecostes, hora terciarum vel quasi, in domo nobilis Msinchneb conthoralis Otthe heredis in Trampcz(i)no constitutus personaliter honorabilis dominus Albertus Otte de Trampczino canonicus Cruschui(censis)1, omnibus melioribus modo, via, forma iuris et stilo, quibus potuit, fecit, constituit et solemniter ordinavit suum verum, legitimum et indubitatum procuratorem, actorem, factorem suorumque negociorum dispositorem nobilem Johannem suum germanum, heredem in Trambczino2, presentem et onus procuracionis in se sponte suscipientem, dans sibi integram et omnimodam potestatem a venerabili viro, domino Jerando scolastico Crusuiczensis, Wladislauiensis et Wyeluniensis ecclesiarum canonico3 LXX florenos Vngaricales, occasione concordie per venerabiles dominos Johannem de Nyewesch decanum4, Swethoslaum de Wrancza archidiaconum et officialem Wladislau(ienses)5, Mathiam Drya decanum6 et Nicolaum de Glambocz archidiaconum Poznanien(ses)7 super canonicatu et prebenda ecclesie collegiate Wyeluniensis inter ipsos Albertum ab una et Jerandum parte ex altera facte, exigendi, tollendi, levandi et ad placitum suum disponendi, percipiendi et pactum de ulterius non pettendo faciendi, literas quittacionis sub nomine et sigillo suis dandi et generaliter omnia et singula, que circa huiusmodi percepta et levata ac quittata fuerint necessaria, exercendi. Super quibus omnibus etc.
Et ego Jo(hannes)8 etc., quia predictis constitucioni aliisque omnibus etc.
atak Kop., zamiast quarto, jak wynika z innych elementów datacji (indykcja, pontyfikat); ale nie można wykluczyć, że to datacja wedle stylu pizańskiego
btak Kop.
1Trąbczyn, wieś par. na pd.-wsch. od Zagórowa w Konińskiem (Koz. 5 s. 414). Otto, Ota, wyst. jeszcze 1426 (Roty V nr B455). Mścichna żona Otty skądinad nie znana. Jej syn Wojciech kanonik kruszwicki skądinąd nie znany.
2Jan z Trąbczyna syn Otty, brat kanonika Wojciecha i młodszego brata, Tomasza Ocica, trudny do rozdzielenia od swego imiennika wyst. równocześnie w Trąbczynie.
3Jarand syn Stanisława scholastyk kruszwicki i kanonik włocławski już 1432 (BullPol V nr 111), najpewniej ident. z Jarandem z Niewiesza, potem również gnieźn. kanonikiem 1441 i oficjałem 1450-51, zm. 1451 (GąsSkOf s. 79, 85).
4Jan syn Chebdy z Niewiesza.
5Świętosław syn Alberyka z Wrzący (na pn.-wsch. od Koła), kanonik włocławski 1412, kustosz 1426, kanonik płocki 1413, pozn. 1429, archidiakon i oficjał włocławski, zm. 1471 (Radzimiński II s. 147-148).
6Maciej Dryja z Modliszewa.
7Mikołaj z Głęboczka.
8Notariusz publiczny Jan z Łęczycy (jak dok. następny) nie zidentyfikowany. [Ju]

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Notariatsinstrument: Wojciech, Sohn des Otto von Trąbczyn, Kanoniker von Kruszwica, bestellt im Haus seiner Mutter Mścichna in Trąbczyn seinen Bruder Jan, Erben in Trąbczyn, zum Bevollmächtigten, um von Jarand, Scholaster von Kruszwica, siebzig ungarische Gulden einzuziehen, die ihm aus einem Vergleich über eine Pfründe in Wieluń zustehen. Trąbczyn, 15. Mai 1434; die Abschrift hat 1435, nach den Herausgebern statt 1434. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski XI, Nr. 2000.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Notarial instrument: Wojciech son of Otto of Trąbczyn, canon of Kruszwica, in the house of his mother Mścichna at Trąbczyn, appoints his brother Jan, heir in Trąbczyn, his proctor, to collect from Jarand, scholastic of Kruszwica, seventy Hungarian florins owed him under an agreement over a prebend at Wieluń. Trąbczyn, 15 May 1434; the copy has 1435, which the editors read as 1434. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski XI, no. 2000.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 2000

Trąbczyn, 15 May 1,434

Notarial instrument. Wojciech of Trąbczyn, canon of Kruszwica, authorises his brother Jan of Trąbczyn to collect a debt from Jarand, scholastic of Kruszwica.

Copy: Kal. 9,175, p. 373. Title: Mandatum ad levandum debitum.

Remarks: 1. On the date see below, note a; 2. See also the next document.

In the name of the Lord, amen. In the year from His birth 1,435ᵃ, in the twelfth indiction, in the fourth year of the pontificate of the most holy father in Christ and our lord, Lord Eugenius the Fourth, Pope by divine providence, on Saturday, the eve of Pentecost, at about the hour of terce, in the house of the noble Mścichnaᵇ, wife of Otto, heir in Trąbczyn, the honourable Lord Wojciech, son of Otto of Trąbczyn, canon of Kruszwica¹, being present in person, in all the better manner, way, form of law and style in which he could, made, set up and solemnly appointed as his true, lawful and undoubted proctor, agent, factor and manager of his affairs the noble Jan, his brother german, heir in Trąbczyn², who was present and took the charge of the proxy upon himself of his own will, giving him full and every power to demand, take, collect and dispose of at his pleasure, receive, and make a pact of not asking further, from the venerable man Lord Jarand, scholastic of Kruszwica, canon of the churches of Włocławek and Wieluń³, seventy Hungarian florins, on account of the agreement made by the venerable lords Jan of Niewiesz, dean⁴, Świętosław of Wrząca, archdeacon and official of Włocławek⁵, Maciej Dryja, dean⁶, and Mikołaj of Głęboczek, archdeacon of Poznań⁷, concerning the canonry and prebend of the collegiate church of Wieluń between Wojciech on the one part and Jarand on the other, to give letters of quittance under his name and seal, and in general to do all and each of the things that are needful concerning what has been so received, collected and acquitted. Concerning all which etc.

And I, Jo(hannes)⁸ etc., since at the said appointment and all the other things etc.

ᵃSo the copy, instead of quarto, as follows from the other elements of the dating (indiction, pontificate); but it cannot be ruled out that this is a dating in the Pisan style.

ᵇSo the copy.

¹Trąbczyn, a parish village south-east of Zagórów in the Konin land (Koz. 5, p. 414). Otto, Ota, occurs still in 1,426 (Roty V, no. B455). Mścichna, Otto's wife, not otherwise known. Her son Wojciech, canon of Kruszwica, not otherwise known.

²Jan of Trąbczyn, son of Otto, brother of the canon Wojciech and of a younger brother, Tomasz Ocic, hard to tell apart from his namesake who occurs at the same time in Trąbczyn.

³Jarand son of Stanisław, scholastic of Kruszwica and canon of Włocławek already in 1,432 (BullPol V, no. 111), most probably the same as Jarand of Niewiesz, later also canon of Gniezno in 1,441 and official in 1,450–51, d. 1,451 (GąsSkOf, pp. 79, 85).

⁴Jan son of Chebda of Niewiesz.

⁵Świętosław son of Alberyk of Wrząca (north-east of Koło), canon of Włocławek 1,412, custodian 1,426, canon of Płock 1,413, of Poznań 1,429, archdeacon and official of Włocławek, d. 1,471 (Radzimiński II, pp. 147–148).

⁶Maciej Dryja of Modliszewo.

⁷Mikołaj of Głęboczek.

⁸The public notary Jan of Łęczyca (as in the next document) not identified. [Ju]
