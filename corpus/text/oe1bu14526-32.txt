--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Closing certification of a translated chapter extract: the notarial subscription of Melchior Janowski, Notarius Apostolicus and secretary of the Reverendissimum Capitulum Cathedrale Posnaniense, followed by the attestation of the sworn translator George Wilhelm Behr, who certifies under oath that the preceding German rendering agrees word for word with the Polish original extract. Only these two authenticating clauses survive on the page; the substantive text of the extract itself is not present here.
