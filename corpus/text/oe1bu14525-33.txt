--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Glenck, writing for the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, presses the Royal Chamber at Posen to have the treasury resign its claim quickly over the whole of the princely estates in Cujavien and Betsche. He recalls that the government at Posen had itself demanded correction of the title of possession within fourteen days on pain of proceedings, and that the Prince then supplied the missing original deeds of donation. A draft of the same request to the government follows.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
To the Royal Chamber at Posen.

Most Serene [Lord],

Already under the xxx th of xxx of last year a Royal Government at Posen did already, according to the enclosed copy, demand of His Serene Highness the correction of the title of possession of the princely Hohenlohe estates as being so necessary and urgent that the treasury (fiscus) was to be moved against any longer delay [uncertain: renn] should the same not have been done within a set period of fourteen days; whereupon His Highness the Prince of Hohenlohe sent in on the 6[uncertain: 6] of March the original deeds of donation that had been wanting for this purpose. As much, then, as His Serene Highness has been pressed herein to the [uncertain: zug.] [uncertain: tilige] correction of the title of possession, so much has [he] since [seen? been] most concerned [uncertain reading] towards the conclusion of this matter; and I have now been charged, in the name of my Lord's Serene Highness, most urgently to beg Your Majesty for the speedy resignation of the treasury over the whole of the princely [uncertain: G]estates in Cujavien and Betsche. Breslau, the 10th of February 1802.

To Your Royal Majesty, Glenck
