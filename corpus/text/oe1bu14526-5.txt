--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Sale of the Posen-department estates Kollno and Kamionne, with mills, waters, propination, hunting and jurisdiction, by Friedrich Ludwig Fürst zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen to Banco Rendant George Conrad Leixner of Frankfurth an der Oder for 142,000 Rthl Courant, in Pausch und Bogen. Payment falls in 76,000 Rthl to Berg-Rath Bernhardi, 53,139 Rthl 13 Gg 8 4/5 Pf composition money to Rittmeister von Broniewsky at Posen, and 12,860 Rthl at the delivery on 10 September. Includes onera perpetua, taxes, the Gory and Krobilaw cutting right, Justitiarius Metzig, and royal concession to dismember.

Be it known and made public that between His Serene Highness the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe Ingelfingen, Governor of Breslau etc., on the one part, and the Bau Banco Pendant [uncertain: Bau Banco Pendant] Herr Leixner of Frankfurth an der Oder, the following contract of sale and purchase has been well-considered, freely and irrevocably agreed, concluded and executed.

§. 1. Namely, His Most Serene Highness the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe Ingelfingen, Governor of Breslau etc., sells His Highness's estates situated under the Department of the Government at Posen in the [text lost] District,

Kollno and Kamionne, with all appurtenances and mills belonging thereto, rights and privileges, with all resident holdings, fields, forests, lakes and waters, with the rents and the propination rights, the services, the hunt and the jurisdiction, also all remaining rights and appurtenances, just as His Most Serene Highness received the said estates and their appurtenances by delivery from the Royal Commission pursuant to the deed of donation - of which a vidimated copy is to be handed over to the Herr Purchaser, and for the correctness and truth of which His Serene Highness gives eviction-warranty in respect of the estates Kollno and Kamionne - and has possessed and enjoyed them, and can still use and enjoy them, with everything as it stands and lies, and finally also with all rights and privileges which may belong to His Serene Highness against the

resident holders and subjects of these estates, which they have arrogated to themselves and believe they may claim - to His Honour the Royal Banco Rendant Herr George Conrad Leixner in Frankfurth an der Oder, for a fixed purchase sum of One Hundred and Forty-Two Thousand Reichsthaler Royal Prussian 64-piece Courant, as his full and absolute property.

§. 2. Today's purchase of the estates Kollno and Kamionne is made without valuation, in Pausch und Bogen; for that reason His Most Serene Highness gives eviction-warranty solely in respect of the survey register drawn up by the Royal Commission and of the real assets listed in the [uncertain: Tan] which are to be handed over at the delivery. All revenues and farming yields the Herr Purchaser must take over as they exist, and according as they may hereafter increase or diminish, with which the Herr Purchaser likewise declares himself content.

His Most Serene Highness has had a composition concluded with Herr Rittmeister von Broniewsky on the 3rd of July of this year, according to the content of which the same will forthwith give back the estates Kollno and Kamionne upon receipt of a sum of 53139. Pf. shall be made on the 8th of September at Posen, but the natural delivery of the estates shall take place on the 10th of September at Kollno and Kamionne, His Serene Highness promises to send an authorised representative to Posen and to Kollno and Kamionne at the said time, in order to be present at these acts; and since a Government Commissioner from Posen will carry out the natural handing-over, His Serene Highness stipulates that the estates Kollno and Kamionne shall be delivered immediately on the 10th of September to the Herr Purchaser.

§. 4.
