--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Contract of sale by which Friedrich Ludwig, Fürst zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Governor of Breslau, conveys the donated estates Kollno and Kamionne, with mills, propination, hunt and jurisdiction, to Banco-Rendant George Conrad Leixner of Frankfurth an der Oder for 142,000 Rthl in Pausch und Bogen: 76,000 already paid to Berg-Rath Bernhardi, 53,139 Rthl 13 Gg 8 4/5 Pf to Rittmeister von Broniewsky at Posen, 12,860 Rthl at delivery. Terms cover the Gory and Krobilaw felling right, church rents and taxes, Justitiarius Metzig, royal concession and Leixner's intended dismemberment.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Be it known and understood that between His Serene Highness the reigning Prince zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Governor of Breslau etc., on the one part, and the Building-Bank Rendant Herr Leixner of Frankfurth an der Oder, the following contract of sale and purchase has been, after due deliberation, freely and irrevocably agreed, concluded and executed.

§. 1. Namely, His Most Serene Highness the reigning Prince zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Governor of Breslau etc., sells His own estates situated under the Department of the Government at Posen in the [text lost] District,

Kollno and Kamionne, with all appurtenances and mills belonging thereto, rights and privileges, with all inhabitants, fields, forests, lakes and waters, with the rents and the propination rights, the services, the hunt and the jurisdiction, also all remaining rights and appurtenances, just as His Serene Highness received the said estates and their appurtenances delivered over from the Royal Commission by virtue of the instrument of donation, of which a vidimated Copy is to be handed to the Herr Purchaser, and for whose correctness and truth His Serene Highness furnishes eviction-warranty in respect of the estates Kollno and Kamionne, and has possessed and enjoyed them, and can also use and enjoy them, with everything as it stands and lies, and finally also with all rights and privileges that may pertain to His Serene Highness against the

residents and subjects of these estates, which [rights] they have arrogated to themselves and believe themselves entitled to claim, — to His Honour the Royal Banco-Rendant Herr George Conrad Leixner in Frankfurth an der Oder, for a fixed purchase sum of One Hundred and Forty-Two Thousand Reichsthaler Royal Prussian 64-piece Courant, as his complete property.

§. 2. This day's purchase of the estates Kollno and Kamionne is made without valuation, in Pausch und Bogen; for that reason His Most Serene Highness furnishes eviction-warranty solely in respect of the survey register taken up by the Royal Commission and of the real assets listed in the [uncertain: Tan], which are to be handed over at the delivery. All revenues and farming yields the Herr Purchaser must take over as they exist, and according as they may hereafter increase or diminish, with which the Herr Purchaser likewise declares himself content.

His Most Serene Highness has caused a settlement to be concluded with Herr Rittmeister von Broniewsky on the 3rd of July of the current year, according to the content of which the same is willing, upon receipt of a sum of 53139 Rthl. 13 Ggl. 8 4/5 Pfl., to give back the estates Kollno and Kamionne forthwith and to restore the [estates] to His Highness. Now since the merchant Herr Simon, as authorised representative of Herr Leixner, has with Herr

von Broniewsky agreed at Posen on the 6th of this month that payment of the 53139 Rthl. 13 Gg. 8 4/5 Pf. shall be made on the 8th of September at Posen, but the natural delivery of the estates shall take place on the 10th of September at Kollno and Kamionne, His Serene Highness accordingly promises to send an authorised representative to Posen and to Kollno and Kamionne at the said time, in order to be present at these Actibus; and since a Government Commissary from Posen will carry out the natural handing-over, His Serene Highness stipulates that the estates Kollno and Kamionne be delivered directly to the Herr Purchaser on the 10th of September.

§. 4. Both in the settlement of the 3rd of July and also in a later declaration Herr von Broniewsky has bound himself to redeem entirely all his Intabulata upon Kollno and Kamionne, and thereby to have these estates cleared of all Nexibus charged upon them on his account. The Herr Purchaser therefore receives these estates wholly free of debt.

§. 5. Herr von Broniewsky retains all usufructs of the estates until the 24th of June of the current year. From that day onward all usufructs and burdens pass over to the Herr Purchaser, and Herr von Broniewsky will render the account thereof at the natural handing-over, just as he will at the same time restore the Beilass in field, cattle and farming inventory which the said von Broniewsky received in the year 1786 upon taking over the estates as manorial

Beilass, as the deed of delivery of that time states. The Herr Purchaser has all the less to receive anything further, inasmuch as His Most Serene Highness has hitherto not yet been in natural possession of the estates, and therefore also cannot hand over more than what is only now being delivered to His Highness.

§. 6. To the Herr Seller's Serene Highness no servitudes are known which burden the estates Kollno and Kamionne passively. On the other hand, there belongs to these estates the free right of felling, as a right due to the same, in the forests at Gory and Krobilaw, which right of felling has also been established by a recent judgment in Possessorium. The

5) Herr Purchaser is therefore likewise entitled to this right, and he is to carry on the further process concerning it, in so far as von Kwilecki should pursue the same further. The judgment in Possessorium has also already been handed to the Herr [uncertain: Schufer], and he declares himself content with the declaration of His Serene Highness.

§. 7. All documents and writings which concern these estates, and which the Herr Seller's Serene Highness or His authorised representatives may still have in hand, shall be faithfully extradited to the Herr Purchaser.

§. 8. His Serene Highness has engaged the justice-burgomaster

Metzig at Schwerin as Justitiarius in Betsch, Kollno and Kamionne. He will give information on all that concerns the administration of justice and its records, just as he also has the deposit business under his charge. It rests with the Herr Purchaser to retain him or to engage another. His Serene Highness pays the said Metzig his salary until Michaelmas of the current year, with which the Herr Purchaser is likewise content.

§. 9. His Majesty the King has already in the month of July of the current year granted the Herr Purchaser the All-Highest concession to purchase these estates, so that there is nothing to be objected against his capacity to possess; and since this point goes at the Herr Purchaser's expense, the matter rests thereupon.

§. 10. The Herr Purchaser's intention is to dismember the estates Kollno and Kamionne. His Serene Highness will lay no obstacles in the way of this business, but rather approves it and will also further it, that it be carried out on the Herr Purchaser's account; as His Highness has also already caused the consent thereto to be applied for at the Cameral Department. To do anything further lies beyond the powers of His Serene Highness, and it is therefore solely the Herr Purchaser's affair to pursue this matter and bring it to a conclusion.

§. 12. All costs concerning the drawing-up and execution of this contract of purchase, likewise the natural and civil delivery costs, as also the cancellation fees for the intabulated hundred thousand Thaler, both contracting parties bear in equal shares.
