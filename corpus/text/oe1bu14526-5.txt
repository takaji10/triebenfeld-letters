--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Sale of the estates Kollno and Kamionne, in the Posen government district, by Friedrich Ludwig, Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen to the bank cashier George Conrad Leixner of Frankfurt an der Oder for 142,000 Rthl in Pausch und Bogen, with mills, propination and jurisdiction. Payment runs 76,000 Rthl to mining councillor Bernhardi, 53,139 Rthl 13 Groschen 8 4/5 Pfennig to cavalry captain von Broniewsky on settlement, the rest at the handover; the purchaser assumes church rents, dues and the timber-cutting suit against von Kwilecki.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Be it known and understood that between His Serene Highness the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Governor of Breslau etc., on the one part, and the building-bank cashier Herr Leixner at Frankfurt an der Oder, the following contract of sale and purchase has been deliberately, freely and irrevocably agreed, concluded and executed.

§ 1. His Most Serene Highness the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Governor of Breslau etc., namely sells His Highness's estates lying under the Department of the Government at Posen in the

district of

Kollno and Kamionne, together with all the appurtenances and mills belonging thereto, rights and privileges, with all residents, fields, forests, lakes and waters, with the rents and the propination rights, the labour services, the hunt and the jurisdiction, also all remaining rights and appurtenances, just as His Most Serene Highness received the said estates and their appurtenances delivered over from the Royal Commission by virtue of the deed of donation, of which a certified copy is to be handed over to the purchaser, and for the correctness and truth of which His Serene Highness furnishes eviction with regard to the estates Kollno and Kamionne, and has possessed and enjoyed them, and can use and enjoy them, with everything as it stands and lies, and finally also with all the rights which His Serene Highness may have against the

inhabitants and subjects of these estates, which they have arrogated to themselves and believe they may claim, to His Honour the Royal bank cashier Herr George Conrad Leixner at Frankfurt an der Oder, for a fixed purchase sum of One Hundred and Forty-Two Thousand Reichsthaler Royal Prussian 64-piece Courant, as his complete property.

§ 2. Today's purchase of the estates Kollno and Kamionne is made without valuation, in Pausch und Bogen; therefore His Most Serene Highness furnishes eviction only with regard to the survey register taken up by the Royal Commission and the real properties listed in the [uncertain: Tan], which are to be handed over at the delivery of possession. All revenues and farming yields the purchaser must take over as they exist, and according as they may hereafter increase or diminish, with which the purchaser likewise declares himself content.

His Most Serene Highness has caused a settlement to be concluded with the cavalry captain von Broniewsky on 3 July of this year, according to the contents of which the same wishes, upon receipt of a sum of 53139 Rthl.

13 Groschen 8 4/5 Pfennig, to give back the estates Kollno and Kamionne forthwith and to restore the [text lost] to His Highness. Now since the merchant Herr Simon, as authorised representative of Herr Leixner, has agreed with Herr

von Broniewsky at Posen on the 6th of this month, that the payment of the 53139 Rthl. 13 Groschen 8 4/5 Pfennig shall be made on 8 September at Posen, but the delivery of possession in kind of the estates on 10 September at Kollno and Kamionne, His Serene Highness promises to send an authorised representative to Posen and to Kollno and Kamionne at the said time, in order to be present at these acts; and since a Government Commissioner from Posen will carry out the handing over in kind, His Serene Highness stipulates that the estates Kollno and Kamionne shall be delivered over to the purchaser immediately on the 10th of September.

§ 4. Both in the settlement of 3 July and also in a later declaration, Herr von Broniewsky has bound himself to redeem all his intabulations upon Kollno and Kamionne completely, and thereby to have these estates cleared of all obligations charged upon them on his account. The purchaser accordingly receives these estates entirely free of debt.

§ 5. Herr von Broniewsky retains all the usufructs of the estates up to 24 June of this year. From that day onward all usufructs and burdens pass over to the purchaser, and Herr von Broniewsky will, at the handing over in kind, render the account thereof, just as he will at the same time restore the stock of field, cattle and farming inventory which von Broniewsky received on taking over the estates in the year 1786 as the lordship's

stock, as the delivery-of-possession instrument of that time states. The purchaser has the less to receive beyond this, inasmuch as His Most Serene Highness has not until now been in possession in kind of the estates, and the same can therefore deliver over no more than what is only now being delivered over to His Highness.

§ 6. To the vendor, His Serene Highness, no servitudes are known which burden the estates Kollno and Kamionne passively. On the other hand, these estates are entitled to free felling as a right due to them in the forests at Gory and Krobilaw, which felling has also been established by a recent possessory judgment. The

5) purchaser is accordingly likewise entitled to this right, and has to continue the further suit over it in so far as von Kwilecki should pursue it further. The possessory judgment has also already been handed over to the purchaser, and the same declares himself content with the declaration of His Serene Highness.

§ 7. All documents and writings which concern these estates, and which the vendor, His Serene Highness, or His Highness's authorised representatives still have in hand, shall be faithfully delivered over to the purchaser.

§ 8. His Serene Highness has engaged the justice-burgomaster

Metzig at Schwerin as justiciary in Betsche, Kollno and Kamionne. He will give information on everything that concerns the justice administration and its records, as he also has the deposit business under him. It depends upon the purchaser whether to retain him or to engage another. His Serene Highness pays the said Metzig his salary up to Michaelmas of this year, with which the purchaser is likewise content.

§ 9. His Majesty the King has already in the month of July of this year granted the purchaser the All-Highest concession for the purchase of these estates, so that nothing is to be objected against his capacity to possess; and since this point goes at the purchaser's cost, the matter rests there.

§ 10. The purchaser's intention is to dismember the estates Kollno and Kamionne. His Serene Highness will lay no obstacles in the way of this business, but approves it and will also further it, that it be carried out at the purchaser's cost; as His Highness has also already caused the consent thereto to be sought from the Cameral Department. To do anything further lies beyond His Serene Highness's powers, and it is therefore solely the purchaser's affair to pursue this matter and to bring it to an end.

§ 12. All costs concerning the drawing up and execution of this contract of purchase, likewise the costs of the delivery of possession in kind and in civil form, as also the cancellation fees of the intabulated one hundred thousand Thaler, both contracting parties bear in equal shares.

In witness whereof the foregoing instrument of sale and purchase has been executed by His Most Serene Highness and by the purchaser in their own Most High and proper hands. Done at Breslau, the 30th of August 1804. Friedrich Ludwig, Prince of Hohenlohe. Read and approved by myself.
