--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Sale of the estates Kollno and Kamionne, in the Posen government district, by Friedrich Ludwig, Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen to the bank cashier George Conrad Leixner of Frankfurt an der Oder for 142,000 Rthl in Pausch und Bogen, with mills, propination and jurisdiction. Payment runs 76,000 Rthl to mining councillor Bernhardi, 53,139 Rthl 13 Groschen 8 4/5 Pfennig to cavalry captain von Broniewsky on settlement, the rest at the handover; the purchaser assumes church rents, dues and the timber-cutting suit against von Kwilecki.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Be it known and made manifest that between His Serene Highness the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Governor at Breslau etc., of the one part, and the building bank cashier Mr. Leixner at Frankfurt an der Oder, the following contract of sale and purchase has been deliberately, freely and irrevocably agreed, concluded and executed.

§ 1. Namely, His Most Serene Highness the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Governor at Breslau etc., sells his estates situated under the Department of the Government at Posen in the

district of

Kollno and Kamionne, with all appurtenances and mills belonging thereto, rights and privileges, with all residents, fields, forests, lakes and waters, with the rents and the propination rights, the services, the hunt and the jurisdiction, and likewise all other rights and appurtenances, just as His Serene Highness received the said estates and their appurtenances delivered over by the Royal Commission pursuant to the deed of donation, of which a vidimated copy is to be handed to the purchaser, and for whose correctness and truth His Serene Highness gives warranty of title (Eviction) in respect of the estates Kollno and Kamionne, and has possessed and enjoyed them, and can also use and enjoy them, with everything as it stands and lies, and finally also with all rights and privileges which His Serene Highness may have against the

inhabitants and subjects of these estates, which they have arrogated to themselves and believe they may claim, to His Honour the Royal bank cashier Mr. George Conrad Leixner at Frankfurt an der Oder, for a fixed purchase sum of one hundred and forty-two thousand Reichsthaler Royal Prussian 64-piece Courant, as his full property.

§ 2. The present purchase of the estates Kollno and Kamionne takes place without valuation, in Pausch und Bogen; for that reason His Most Serene Highness gives warranty of title (Eviction) solely in respect of the survey register drawn up by the Royal Commission and of the real assets listed in the [uncertain: Tan], which are to be handed over at the delivery of possession. All revenues and economic yields the purchaser must take over as they exist, and according as they may hereafter increase or diminish, with which the purchaser likewise declares himself content.

His Most Serene Highness has caused a settlement to be concluded with the cavalry captain von Broniewsky on 3 July of this year, according to the content of which the same will forthwith give back the estates Kollno and Kamionne upon receipt of a sum of 53139 Rthl. 13 Groschen 8 4/5 Pfennig, and will restore the [text lost] to His Highness. Now since the merchant Mr. Simon, as authorised representative of Mr. Leixner, has with Mr.

von Broniewsky agreed at Posen on the 6th of this month that the payment of the 53139 Rthl. 13 Groschen 8 4/5 Pfennig shall be made on 8 September at Posen, but the delivery of actual possession of the estates on 10 September at Kollno and Kamionne, His Serene Highness accordingly promises to send an authorised representative to Posen and to Kollno and Kamionne at the said time, in order to be present at these acts; and since a government commissioner from Posen will carry out the delivery of actual possession, His Serene Highness stipulates that the estates Kollno and Kamionne shall be delivered over to the purchaser directly on the 10th of September.

§ 4. Both in the settlement of 3 July and in a later declaration Mr. von Broniewsky has bound himself to redeem entirely all his intabulations upon Kollno and Kamionne, and thereby to have these estates purged of all encumbrances (Nexus) charged thereon on his account. The purchaser therefore receives these estates wholly free of debt.

§ 5. Mr. von Broniewsky retains all the profits of the estates up to 24 June of this year. From that day forward all profits and burdens pass to the purchaser, and Mr. von Broniewsky will render the account thereof at the delivery of actual possession, as he will at the same time also restore the stock of field, cattle and farm inventory which von Broniewsky received on taking over the estates in the year 1786 as the lordship's

stock, as the delivery deed of that time states. The purchaser has the less to receive anything further, inasmuch as His Most Serene Highness has to this day not yet been in actual possession of the estates, and therefore cannot deliver over more than what is only now being delivered over to His Highness.

§ 6. His Serene Highness the vendor knows of no servitudes burdening the estates Kollno and Kamionne. On the other hand these estates are entitled to free timber-cutting, as a right due to them, in the forests at Gory and Krobilaw, which right of cutting has also been established by a recent judgment in possessorium. The

5) purchaser is therefore likewise entitled to this right, and has to carry on the further litigation over it insofar as von Kwilecki should pursue the same further. The possessorium judgment has also already been handed to the purchaser, and he declares himself content with His Serene Highness's declaration.

§ 7. All documents and writings concerning these estates, and which His Serene Highness the vendor or his authorised representatives may still have in hand, shall be faithfully delivered over to the purchaser.

§ 8. His Serene Highness has engaged the justice-burgomaster

Metzig at Schwerin as justiciary in Betsche, Kollno and Kamionne. He will give information on everything concerning the administration of justice and its records, as he also has the deposit business under him. It rests with the purchaser to retain him or to engage another. His Serene Highness pays the said Metzig his salary up to Michaelmas of this year, with which the purchaser is likewise satisfied.

§ 9. His Majesty the King has already in the month of July of this year granted the purchaser the All-Highest concession to purchase these estates, so that nothing is to be objected against his capacity to possess; and since this point goes at the purchaser's cost, it shall rest thereby.

§ 10. The purchaser's intention is to dismember the estates Kollno and Kamionne. His Serene Highness will place no obstacles in the way of this business, but rather approves it and will also further it, so that it may be carried out at the purchaser's cost; as His Highness has likewise already caused the consent thereto to be applied for at the Chamber Department. To do anything beyond this lies outside His Serene Highness's powers, and it is therefore solely the purchaser's affair to pursue this matter and bring it to a conclusion.

§ 12. All costs concerning the drawing up and execution of this contract of purchase, likewise the costs of the delivery of actual and civil possession, as also the cancellation fees for the intabulated hundred thousand Thaler, both contracting parties shall bear in equal parts.

§ 13. His Serene Highness the vendor, as also the purchaser, renounce each and every legal objection and evasion opposed to this agreement, in particular the objection of injury beyond or below the half, that no general renunciation is valid where no special one has preceded it, and that this agreement was written down otherwise than it was agreed.

In witness whereof the foregoing instrument of sale and purchase has been executed by His Most Serene Highness and by the purchaser in their own hands. Thus done at Breslau, the 30th of August 1804. Friedrich Ludwig, Prince of Hohenlohe. Read and approved by myself.
