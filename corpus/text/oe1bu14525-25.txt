--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A mortgage certificate for the lordship of Pszczew or Betsche with its villages, the bailiwick of Zielomyśl and Silna, the Hollander settlements and the Heidemühle, held by the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen as confiscated church land of the Posen Cathedral Chapter. It records a yearly maintenance allowance of 2,470 Rthl 16 Groschen 3 Pfennig to the former ecclesiastical holders, and secured debts of 48,000 Rthl and 5,000 Rthl to Building and Economy Councillor Johann Wilhelm Glenck, 2,000 Rthl to Abraham Moses Hirschel, and 5,000 Rthl each to Baron von der Marwitz and Christiane Dorothea Michaelis. Issued to Glenck.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The noble lordship of Pszczew or Betsche, lying under the jurisdiction of the Royal South Prussian Government at Posen in the Posen District, with the following appurtenances: the village of Świechocin, the village of Stoki, the village of Łowyń, the village of Głażewo, the village of Dormowo, the village of Stołuń, the village of Kuligowo, the village of Szarcz, the bailiwick of Zielomyśl and Silna, the Hollander settlements, the Pomcher Hollander settlement, and the Heidemühle: these the reigning Prince, the Lord Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, has received in hereditary and proprietary grant as formerly ecclesiastical estates, having once belonged to the Cathedral Chapter at Posen, following the confiscation of all ecclesiastical estates in South Prussia, by virtue of the instruments of grant of His Majesty Friedrich Wilhelm the Second, dated Berlin the 19th of June 1797, and of His Majesty King Friedrich Wilhelm the Third, Berlin the 1st of December of the same year; and the title of possession for the same has been entered on the

basis of these instruments of grant and of the deed of resignation made out on the 29th of March 1802, by virtue of the decree of the 29th of March 1802.

Thereupon are entered:

I. Perpetual burdens and restrictions upon the property, or upon the disposition thereof.

1. is cancelled

2. is cancelled.

3. 2470 Rthl 16 Groschen 3 Pfennig, written out two thousand four hundred and seventy Reichsthaler, sixteen Groschen three Pfennig, being the annual maintenance allowance accruing therefrom to the former ecclesiastical possessors of these estates, payable to the War and Domains Treasury at Posen in quarterly instalments in advance; which allowance, together with those 100 Rthl, 11 1/10 d, written out one hundred Thaler four Groschen, eleven and one tenth Pfennig, which were provisionally noted above the line as an allowance out of the forest revenues pending the closer ascertainment of the latter, and the demanding of which has been further deferred, have been entered upon the basis of the produced attestation of the War and Domains Chamber at Posen of the 4th of January 1803, at the application of the Lord proprietor, in the place of the indeterminate allowance intabulated under No. 1 at the entry of his title of possession by the decree of the 29th of March 1802 and now cancelled, by virtue of the decree of the 17th of January 1803.

II. Judicially secured debts and other real obligations.

1. is cancelled

2. 48000 Rthl, written out forty-eight thousand Thaler Courant, which the Lord

proprietor has become owing to the Building and Economy Councillor Johann Wilhelm Glenck, who stands in his service, according to the judicial instrument of debt given at Breslau the 2nd of January 1803, at interest of 5 per cent and with six months' notice of termination free to both parties, likewise against the pledging of this lordship, save however for the Łowyń heath, for capital, interest and costs; and which have been entered at the Lord proprietor's own application, presented the 15th of January 1803, by the decree of the 17th of January 1803.

3. 2000 Rthl, that is two thousand Thaler in Courant, according to the coinage standard of the year 1764, which the Lord proprietor, according to the judicially acknowledged instrument of debt of the 26th of April 1803, has borrowed from the merchant Abraham Moses Hirschel at Breslau, at interest of five per cent in half-yearly instalments, with reservation of half a year's notice of termination and the pledging of this lordship, on account of capital, interest, damages and costs; and which have been entered at the creditor's request, presented the 14th of May 1803, by the decree of the 16th of the same month and year.

4. 5000 Rthl, written out five thousand Thaler Prussian Courant according to the coinage standard of 1764, which the Lord proprietor has become owing to the Baron von der Marwitz of Rudnik, at five per cent interest payable yearly at St George's Day, against repayment within six years reckoned from St George's Day 1803, further against the right of half-yearly notice of termination, which stands open to the Lord debtor at all times but to the creditor within the six years only if either the interest remains in arrears for more than a year after the expiry of an interest term, or if a change of possession occurs with these estates; and has, for security on account of the capital, the interest, damages and costs, pledged this lordship, save however with the exclusion of

the Lewiner oak wood. Entered upon the basis of the judicial bond of debt given at Breslau the 26th of April 1803, at the application of the special authorised representative of the Lord proprietor, presented the 2nd of July 1803, by virtue of the decree of the 11th of the same month and [illegible].

5. 5000 Rthl, written out five thousand Reichsthaler Courant, according to the coinage standard of 176, which the Lord proprietor has become owing to the widowed chief district councillor's wife Christiane Dorothea Michaelis, born Eichborn, at Breslau, at five per cent in half-yearly instalments, namely payable at Midsummer and Christmas, against repayment within six years reckoned from the 26th of March 1803, and against a right of half-yearly notice of termination standing open to the Lord debtor at all times but to the lady creditor within the six years only [uncertain: if] either the interest remains in arrears longer than a year, or if a change of possession of these estates occurs; and has, for security on account of the capital, the interest, damages and [uncertain: otherwise], pledged this lordship. Entered upon the basis of the judicial bond of debt dated Breslau the 26th of March 1803, at the application of the special authorised representative of the Lord proprietor, presented the 2nd of July 1803, by virtue of the decree of the 11th of the same month and year.

6. 5000 Rthl, written out five thousand Reichsthaler Courant according to the coinage standard of 1764, which the Lord proprietor has become owing to the Building and Economy Councillor Johann Wilhelm Glenck, who stands in his service, at five per cent interest to be paid yearly at St George's Day, against repayment after six years reckoned from the 26th of March 1803, and after half-yearly notice of termination given thereupon; the right of half-yearly notice, however, stands open within those six years also, to the Lord debtor at any time, but to the creditor only upon a change of possession of these estates occurring; and this lordship is pledged for capital, interest,

interest, damages and costs. Entered upon the basis of the judicial bond of debt dated Breslau the 26th of March 1803, at the application of the special authorised representative of the Lord proprietor, presented the 2nd of July 1803, by virtue of the decree of the 11th of the same month and year. Apart from the foregoing items, nothing is entered in the mortgage book against the lordship of Pszczew or Betsche; and for the rest, the present mortgage certificate is hereby granted to the Building and Economy Councillor Johann Wilhelm Glenck by way of acknowledgment, on account of the 5000 Rthl Courant entered for him under No. 6 of the judicially secured debts upon the basis of the judicial bond of debt dated Breslau the 26th of March 1803. In witness whereof, under the signature appointed by the Royal South Prussian Government and the greater seal. Given at Posen the 29th of August 1803. von Braunschweig

Bormann

Fest.
