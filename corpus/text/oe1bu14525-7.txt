--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Eine vidimirte Abschrift einer königlichen Schenkungsurkunde Friedrich Wilhelms, die dem regierenden Fürsten Friedrich Ludwig von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen über die schon am 19. Juni verliehenen Güter Betsche, Zagórów und Wienietz hinaus auch deren Pertinenzstücke erb- und eigentümlich überträgt: bei Betsche Stołuń, Kuligowo, Szarcz, Vogtey, Zielomyśl und Silna mit den Hauländereien, dem Pommser Hauländer und der Heÿdemühle, bei Zagórów das Dorf Swięcin, bei Wienitz Konty, Siedlemyn, Bądkowo und Karczyn, gegen unveränderlichen Canon und die Abgaben an geistliche Stiftungen. Beigefügt sind der Titulus possessionis für Swięcia mit dem Vorwerk Broniki im Koniner Kreis (Hypothekenbuch Bd. 3 fol. 94) und ein Hypothekenschein, der als einzige Last das Verkaufsverbot an Nichtinländer verzeichnet.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A royal letter of grant from Friedrich Wilhelm adds to the estates already given to Friedrich Ludwig, Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, on 19 June the outlying parcels belonging to Pszczew, Zagórów and Wienietz: the Stołuń, Kuligowo, Szarcz, Vogtey, Zielomyśl and Silna settlements with their Hauländer holdings and Heÿdemühle, the village of Swięcin, and Konty, Siedlemyn, Bądkowo and Karczyn, subject to a fixed canon and church stipends. Filed with it are a mortgage certificate and registration of title for Swięcia and the farm Broniki, which may not be sold to a native without consent.

Certified copy.

We Friedrich Wilhelm, by the Grace of God King of Prussia etc. etc., Margrave of Brandenburg, Arch-Chamberlain and Elector of the Holy Roman Empire; sovereign and supreme Duke of Silesia, Sovereign Prince of Orange, Neuchâtel and Valangin, as also of the County of Kłodzko, Duke in Geldern, at Magdeburg, Cleve, Jülich, Berg, Szczecin, Pomerania, of the Cassubians and Wends, at Mecklenburg and Krosno Odrzańskie, Burgrave of Nuremberg above and below the mountains, Prince of Halberstadt, Minden, Camin, Wenden, Schwerin, Ratzeburg, East Frisia and Mörs, Count of Hohenzollern, Ruppin, of the Mark Ravensberg, Hohenstein, Tecklenburg, Schwerin, Lingen, Bühren and Leerdam; Lord of Ravenstein, of the lands of Rostock, Stargard, Limburg, Lauenburg, Bütow, Arlaÿ and Breda etc. etc.

Do hereby make known and declare, and by virtue of this Our open letter, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors in the Crown, in the Electorate and in Our

other lands, that out of the particular friendship and affection We bear towards the High-Born Prince, Our friendly dear Uncle Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, and also in order to give him a new proof of Our royal gratitude for the distinguished and beneficial services rendered to Us and to Our Royal House, We have found Ourselves moved to make over, grant and convey to Your Lordship, heritably and in full property, in addition to the estates already granted to him by the instrument of the 19th of June of this year, namely Pszczew, Zagórów and Wieniec, the appurtenant parcels belonging to those estates, namely

1.) at Pszczew, the settlements of Stołuń, Kuligowo, Szarcz, Vogtey, Zielomyśl and Silna together with the Hollander holdings, the Pommser Hollander holding and Heÿdemühle

2.) at Zagórów the little village of Swięcia, and finally

3.) near Wieniec, Konty, Siedlemyn, Bądkowo and Karczyn, against payment

1.) of the sovereign dues hitherto rendered from the said settlements, Hollander holdings and parcels of land, as a fixed and unalterable canon, and

2.) of the stipends due therefrom to the ecclesiastical corporations and foundations.

We also do this hereby and by virtue of this present instrument, out of Our royal power and plenitude, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors in the Crown, in the Electorate and in Our other lands, in such manner and to the effect that Our aforesaid friendly dear Uncle, the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen,

shall from now and for all time, for himself and his descendants and heirs, irrevocably have, hold, possess and enjoy from Us and Our descendants and successors, as a true, well-acquired and unrestricted property, the above-named appurtenant parcels belonging to the estates of Pszczew, Zagórów and Wieniec, with all rights and privileges attaching thereto as these have hitherto been exercised or may lawfully be exercised, with all the lands belonging thereto in fields, woodlands and meadows, likewise in ponds and waters, according to the lawful boundaries and bounds, just as everything has hitherto been used or may of right be used; and that the power and authority shall be vested in him to dispose thereof at his pleasure under the conditions mentioned above,

without hindrance from any man.

Accordingly We command and enjoin all Our Supreme Provincial Colleges, as also all other authorities whom this concerns, hereby as graciously as earnestly, that they in no wise hinder or trouble either the aforementioned Lord Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Your Lordship, in the heritable and proprietary possession thus granted to him of the above-named appurtenant parcels belonging to the estates of Pszczew, Zagórów and Wieniec, nor him or those upon whom he shall in time bequeath the same by virtue of this Our letter of grant, or otherwise lawfully transfer them by other acts inter vivos aut mortis causa, but rather shall let His Lordship, and in the event also them, enjoy the same freely and quietly, themselves doing nothing

against it, nor occasion or permit such to be done by others, upon pain of Our most high displeasure and of unfailing severe punishment. In witness whereof under Our own most high signature and appended Royal seal. Thus done and given in Our residence city of Berlin, the first of December after the birth of Christ our Lord in the one thousand seven hundred and ninety-seventh year, and in the first year of Our royal reign. Friedrich Wilhelm

The noble estate Swięcia situated in the province of South Prussia and

in the Konin district thereof, to which the demesne farm Broniki belongs as an appurtenance, has been conveyed to the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Friedrich Ludwig, Serene Highness, by His Majesty King Friedrich Wilhelm the Third, with heritable and proprietary rights, by the letter of grant dated Berlin, the 1st of December 1797; and the Titulus Possessionis has been entered for him on the ground of that instrument ex Decreto of the 31st of July 1801. Thereupon are entered:

I. Onera perpetua: restrictions upon the property or upon the disposition thereof.

1. By virtue of the ordinance of the year 1796 and the Court Rescript of the 24th of February 1800, this estate may not be sold without special consent to a native of the land. Which has been noted here ex officio, ex Decrete of the 31st of July 1801.

II. Judicially secured debts and other real obligations.

Nothing.

Apart from the foregoing restriction, nothing is entered against the estate Swięcia in the mortgage book; and for the rest this mortgage

certificate is hereby issued, in vim recognitionis, to the present possessor, the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Friedrich Ludwig, Serene Highness, on account of the Titulus Possessionis entered for him on the ground of the letter of grant of the 1st of December 1797. In witness whereof under the greater seal of the Royal South Prussian Government and the usual signature. Given at Kalisz, the 29th of August 1801. (L. S.)
