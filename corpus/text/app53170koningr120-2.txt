--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Bericht des Gerichtsboten Sebastian Biegański von Sławsk, eingetragen in Konin Ende 1786. Er hat Antoni Prusimski von Kolno, Starost von Niszczewice, eine königliche Ladung vor das Krontribunal in Petrikau zugestellt, ausgestellt in Petrikau am 2. Oktober 1786, auf Betreiben von Stanisław Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa. Prusimski soll unter Eid angehalten werden, eine bestimmte Urkunde vorzulegen, die bei ihm verblieben sei: den mit dem Kaufmann Jan Tracholz geschlossenen Vertrag über den Verkauf eines Eichenwaldes. Er soll ferner dafür bestraft werden, dass er die Sache vom Gericht des Ortstermins an das volle Landgericht und von dort an das Tribunal hat verweisen lassen; die Dekrete des Kalischer Landgerichts in Konin, die den Ortstermin ansetzen, sollen vollzogen werden. Der Bote hat die Ladung im Gutshof von Trąbczyn hinterlegt.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A report of the court messenger Sebastian Biegański of Sławsk, entered at Konin at the end of 1786. He has served on Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice, a royal citation before the Crown Tribunal at Piotrków, dated at Piotrków on 2 October 1786, at the instance of Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa. Prusimski is to be bound under oath to produce a particular document said to have stayed with him: the contract made with the merchant Jan Tracholz for the sale of an oak wood. He is also to be punished for having had the cause sent from the court of the sitting on the ground to the full land court and from there to the Tribunal; the decrees of the Kalisz land court at Konin that fix the sitting on the ground are to be carried out. The messenger left the citation at the manor of Trąbczyn.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
On the part of Right Honourable Chełmski against His Worship Prusimski — Return of Service to the Tribunal

Coming personally, Providus Sebastianus Biegański of the village of Słask, General Royal Summoner, sound [of mind], acknowledged that he had cited [with] authentic letters of citation of the Ordinary General Sessions of the Crown Tribunal of Piotrków, the below-written edict of the following tenor:

Stanisław August, by the Grace of God King of Poland, etc. To you, the Illustrious Right Honourable Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, starosta [district administrator] of Niszczewice, Knight of the Order of Saint Stanisław, regarding your goods We command that at our Ordinary General Sessions of the Crown Tribunal of Piotrków, properly within four weeks from the date and delivery of this present citation, or at such time when the present cause, called and proclaimed for judgment from the register competent to it, shall fall due, you appear personally and lawfully, at the instance of Right Honourable Stanisław Chełmski, skarbnik [treasurer] of Wschowa, plaintiff, who cites you to the prepared, connected and competent inscriptions, to see and hear yourself — to the production of a specific document, namely the contract for the sale of oak woodland, entered into and concluded with the Honourable Joannes Trachultz,

remaining with you, the oath having been administered beforehand, to be bound, or the same presentation to be fulfilled at the designated place, to be prescribed. Now indeed in our Court, with penalties against you the cited party — both with respect to the remission procured from the Condescensorial Court to the Plenary Court, and thereafter from our Plenary Court to the Tribunal Court, and contrary to the decrees duly and legitimately passed accepting a condescension designating the institution of terms and of the cause not yet decided in the lesser bench, extended separately to the Court of Supreme Instance of Evocation, and from the circumstances of the matter and the cause occurring — the Land Court Decrees of Kalisz, passed in the jurisdiction of the Konin district, the whole matter having been suspended with the consent also of you the cited party, [decrees] designating a condescension to be carried out, to be arranged; all manner of satisfaction to be found for the plaintiff and ordered to be rendered; the rest according to the conclusion to be put; and the earlier terms of the plaintiff to be decided and resolved.

Given at Piotrków on 2 October in the Year of the Lord 1786.

In the manor court of the goods of the village of Trąbczyn, on the table of the heated room, with the representative of the cited party present, on the 2nd day of the month and year expressed in the act above, [he states that he] placed [it] and left [it] notified.
