Actum in Conin Feria Tertia post Dominicam Reminiscere Quadragesimalem proxima Die scilicet 18va Mensis Martii Anno Domini 1783
Pro Parte M Chełmski Thesaurarium Vschoven~ editt~ Citt~ Requisitio
Ad Officium et Acta praesentia Castrensia Coninen~ personaliter veniens Providens Stanislaus Stachowski de Villa Łukomie Ministerialis Regni Generalis Nomine et ex Speciali Commissio Magnifici Stanislai Chełmski Thaesaurarii Vschoven~ Bonorum Łukomia cum attinen~ haeredis Domini Sui Requisivit ab Officio praesenti, utrum Relationes Citationum binarum ex Parte Illustris Magnifici Antonii Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen~ Bonorum Trąmpczyna et Aliorum haeredis contra Sui Requirentis Principalem Dominum pro Judiciis Castren~ Calissien~ editarum et ad Bona Łukomie exportatt~ Coram Actis praesen~ per Ministeriales Recognitae et Termini ad Easdem porrecti sunt vel ne, Cui Requisitioni Officium praesens annuendo Reviso Protocollo Relationum ab Actu Feriae Tertiae post Festum Sanctae Catharinae Virginis et Martyris Die scilicet Vigesima Sexta Mensis Novembris Anni Immediate praeteriti Millesimi Septingentesimi Octuagesimi Secundi usque ad Actum Diei hodiernae Actu Contentae nisi Unam Relationem Sub Actu Feriae Sextae post Festum Sanctae Catharinae Virginis et Marthyris Die scilicet Vigessima Nona Mensis et Anni Eisdem per Providum Mathieum Matuszkiewicz de Villa Podbiele Ministerialem Regni Generalem Recognitam, ad Bonaque Łukomia exportatt~, et nude sine Termino per Officium praesens extraditt~, non provenit, Ideo de inexistentia Earundem publicam facit fidem
Stanisław Stachowski Wozny Swym y Pana Imieniem

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Eintrag vom 18. März 1783. Der Gerichtsbote Stanisław Stachowski von Łukomia fragt im Namen von Stanisław Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa, ob die Berichte über zwei Ladungen, die Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice, gegen Chełmski vor das Burggericht Kalisz erwirkt und nach Łukomia hat bringen lassen, vor dem Amt erklärt worden sind. Das Amt hat das Register vom 26. November 1782 bis zu diesem Tag durchgesehen und nur einen Bericht vom 29. November gefunden, erklärt vom Gerichtsboten Matuszkiewicz von Podbiele; es bescheinigt, dass weitere nicht vorliegen. Stachowski hat für sich und seinen Herrn unterschrieben.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
An entry of 18 March 1783. The court messenger Stanisław Stachowski of Łukomia asks, in the name of Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, whether the reports of two citations that Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, obtained against Chełmski before the castle court of Kalisz and had taken to Łukomia have been declared before the office. The office has looked through its register from 26 November 1782 to that day and found only one report, of 29 November, declared by the court messenger Matuszkiewicz of Podbiele; it certifies that there are no others. Stachowski signed for himself and his master.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Done in Konin on Tuesday next after the Quadragesimal Sunday Reminiscere, being 18 March 1783.

Requisition of the Issued Citations — On behalf of the Right Honourable Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa

To the office and present records of the Konin Municipal Court, appearing in person, the Honest Stanisław Stachowski of the village of Łukomia, General Royal Court Summoner, in the name and by special commission of the Right Honourable Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, heir of the estates of Łukomia with its appurtenances, his principal — requested of the present office whether the relationes of two citations issued on behalf of the Illustrious Right Honourable Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, heir of the estates of Trąbczyn and others, against his requesting party's principal, for the Kalisz Municipal Court sessions, and exported to the estates of Łukomia, had been recognized before the present records by the court summoner, and whether return dates had been attached to the same — or not.

To which requisition the present office, granting it and reviewing the Protocol of Relationes from the act of Tuesday after the Feast of Saint Catherine, Virgin and Martyr, being 26 November 1782, up to the act of the present day, found within it only one relatio: under the act of Friday after the Feast of Saint Catherine, Virgin and Martyr, being 29 November of the same month and year, by the Honest Mateusz Matuszkiewicz of the village of Podbiele, General Royal Court Summoner, recognized, exported to the estates of Łukomia, and delivered bare without a return date by the present office — nothing further was found; and therefore makes public certification of the non-existence of the same.

Stanisław Stachowski, court summoner, in his own name and in the name of his principal.
