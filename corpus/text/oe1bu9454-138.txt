--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Amelang reports that the renewed first attempt at deposition in the v. Broniewski matter failed, as shown by the enclosed resolution of 25 June, and that he pressed for a rescission. Acting on v. Triebenfeld's assurance that the Prince would lodge security to gain possession of the estates, he offered 10000 Rthl; the deposition and the clearing of Kolno and Kaemen have accordingly been ordered to the Government at Posen.
