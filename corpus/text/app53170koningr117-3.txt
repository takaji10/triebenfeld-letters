--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Protest von Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice, eingetragen in Konin im März 1778: ein Vermerk mit seiner Unterschrift und, auf dem gegenüberliegenden Blatt, der Text nach einer vorbereiteten Abschrift. Er richtet sich gegen Stanisław Chełmski, Erbherr von Łukom, und gegen die Richter des Kalischer Landgerichts. Chełmski habe von dem Bürger Jan Tracholz eine unrechtmäßige Forderung erworben, die aus einem 1744 mit Prusimskis verstorbenem Onkel Paweł Prusimski geschlossenen Geschäft über Eichenholz stamme, und verfolge sie nun gegen ihn. Ihm fehlten die nötigen Urkunden: Sie lägen bei den Erben der Witwe seines Onkels, und er habe sie eingeklagt, aber noch nicht erhalten. Das Landgericht habe die Sache mit Tracholz früher auf einen Ortstermin mit diesen Erben verwiesen; dennoch sei auf Chełmskis Betreiben ein entgegengesetztes Dekret ergangen, das einen Ortstermin auf dem Grund von Trąbczyn anordnet. Prusimski erklärt dieses Dekret, ergangen in Konin am 20. Oktober des Vorjahres, für nichtig und protestiert gegen die Übertragung der Forderung auf Chełmski.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A protest of Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, entered at Konin in March 1778: a note with his signature and, on the facing leaf, the text from a prepared copy. It is directed against Stanisław Chełmski, heir of Łukom, and against the judges of the Kalisz land court. Chełmski, he says, has acquired from the townsman Jan Tracholz an unlawful claim arising from a contract for oak timber made in 1744 with Prusimski's late uncle Paweł Prusimski, and now pursues it against him. He lacks the papers he needs: they are with the heirs of his uncle's widow, and he has sued for them but not yet received them. The land court had earlier sent the cause with Tracholz to a sitting on the ground with those heirs; yet at Chełmski's instance a contrary decree was given, ordering a sitting on the ground of Trąbczyn. Prusimski declares that decree, given at Konin on 20 October of the year before, null, and protests against the transfer of the claim to Chełmski.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The Honourable Prusimski against the Honourable Chełmski and the Condescension Court — Manifest Filed

Let there be seen in this place, arising from the matter and the personal appearance of the Honourable Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, against the Honourable Stanisław Chełmski and the Condescension Court — the manifest made, inserted opposite.

Upon the Manifest of Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice

Certified copy.

To the office and present records of the Konin Municipal Court, appearing in person, the Honourable Antoni of Kolno Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, hereditary owner of the estates of Trąbczyn — looking to the integrity of his cause and fortune and to remove danger from them — solemnly, before the same office and its records, in and against the Honourable Stanisław Chełmski, hereditary owner of the estates of Łukom, and simultaneously against the prejudicial judges of the Kalisz Land Court in the cause described below, manifests:

of the fact that the same Honourable Chełmski — going beyond all bounds and seeking litigation against the manifestant — came forward and presumed to pursue against his property and person the illegal right acquired from the Honest Jan Tracholz, in the matter of oak timber or woodland contracted with the late Honourable Paweł Prusimski, the manifestant's uncle, already in the year 1744, confirmed and assumed by him for himself — to whom the manifestant's late uncle had not been debtor, but who had himself been debtor to the uncle — and having thus acquired that same right, without attending to the fact that the manifestant is without resources and without the documents — adhering in the hands of the successors of the late widow of his uncle, vindicated through a process by the manifestant but not yet recovered — necessary both for this cause and for the general estate of his uncle; and without attending to the fact that by reason of those same documents, first the Kalisz Land Court decree of the enjoined condescension and then that of the Piotrków Tribunal had intervened together with those same successions; and hence also the cause with the same Honest Tracholz, the assignor of his right, had been remitted by decree of the Kalisz Land Court to that same condescension term designated together with the successors of the widow of his uncle; and furthermore that regarding those same documents, together with the successors of the widow of his uncle, the Commission of the Estates of the Realm had been issued — not yet decided, and due to be decided by the Crown Tribunal;

yet with all these things in no way considered or attended to — and notably the Land Court decree of remission to the condescension term — contrary to which no other decree could validly follow in that same entangled court — he dared to improperly summon the manifestant to that same Kalisz Land Court without even a term available within the remission decree; and in that same court to have issued another decree, different and contrary to the prior, of the enjoined condescension to the estates of Trąbczyn — without a term, as stated above — contrary to the remission decree, the same remission decree not reviewed, nay rather remaining untouched to this day — into a mere entanglement of the manifestant's cause and the boundary cause

pending in the Crown Tribunal — to be issued — and thereby to burden the manifestant, and to seek to draw him and his boundary cause and the discrepant decree into some entanglement;

and against the Honourable Kalisz Land Court judges: because they — not attending to the reasons and documents set out above, nor to their own decree of remission of this cause together with the legal assignment of the Honourable Chełmski to the condescension term together with the successors of the widow of the manifestant's uncle, contrary to which another judgment could not be issued — and that same remission decree likewise not reviewed, and unable to be reviewed without a term by the Honourable Chełmski — and without attending to the decrees of the Piotrków Tribunal and the Commission of the Estates of the Realm issued together with the successors of the widow of his uncle, by virtue of which the present cause did not fall to their forum and decision — and finally without attending to the fact that in this cause — since it is so long-standing and begun more than thirty years ago — the more extensive communication of documents ought entirely, according to the prescription of the new statute of the law and the determined degree of the right, to have been granted and decided — yet with all these things unattended to, dared and presumed to issue another decree of the enjoined condescension to the estates of Trąbczyn, entirely different from their prior decree and likewise outside the proper forum — and thereby to burden the manifestant, and to prepare danger for the boundary cause, and to refuse to admit any motion from that decree —

wherefore the manifestant, having set out these matters — not only by reason of all the foregoing but also regarding the nullity and want of substance of that same Kalisz Land Court decree issued at Konin on the Monday after the Feast of Saint Hedwig, Widow and Chosen of God, namely 20 October of the preceding year, between the same Honourable Chełmski and the manifestant, prejudicially — staying the execution of it and referring himself to the laws of the Realm, and simultaneously also against the transfer or the illegal right acquired for the Honourable Chełmski from the Honest Tracholz — again and again, most solemnly, manifests;

with all things preserved that ought to be preserved by law.
