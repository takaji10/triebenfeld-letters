nur Ewr Durchlaucht, laßen Ihnen alle beiden Fremd werden, und schande Ihnen
Ruhe und die dauerhafteste Gesundheit, damit Sie noch eine lange reihe von
Jahren die Stüzze Ihres Hauses und der Wohlthäter guter Menschen bleiben mögen.

eben 4 Haupt Sachen durch dem Departements Minister entschieden werden,

habe ich die Bestätigung und den Approbirten Transact erhalten,
daß die Bank und Invaliden Schulden, nebst den verlegenen Zutrahm

Weigel auf gleiche Art abgefunden werden wird.

die Ewr Durchlaucht oft sehr wahr thuthen, um mit dieser weisen all
Gemeinen Verfügung zufrieden sein müßen.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Condolences on the death of the Princess Sanken, then news that the Departmental Minister has confirmed a settlement spreading the Bank and Invalids' debts over 41¾ years at 6 per cent, with a like moratorium expected for private debts and Herr Weigel. Hereditary lessees pay nothing; suits over Olemizur, Werbizier and Swiecia should annul the contracts and lift revenues to 22–24,000 Rthl. Hecker, dismissed at the Tribunal, owes some 2500 Rthl; Triebenfeld defends himself, reports personnel cut to 1000 Rthl yearly, and plans a Berlin journey on the 25th.

Most Serene Prince, Most gracious Prince and Lord! In the death of the Princess Sanken I take the liveliest part; my [uncertain: dearest] condolence I lay at Your Serene Highness's feet. May Heaven preserve Your Serene Highness in return, let both of them become strangers to you, and [uncertain: grant] you rest and the most durable health, so that you may yet for a long series of years remain the support of your House and the benefactor of good people. The news of the most blessed Princess's death was already in my hands on the 11th. Gladly would I have hastened to Your Serene Highness, had not just now 4 principal matters been due to be decided by the Departmental Minister, on which the preservation of the Pat. estates depended. Only yesterday did I receive the confirmation and the approved settlement, that the Bank and Invalids' debts, together with the [uncertain: overdue arrears], are to be paid off over 41 3/4 years at 6 per cent. The copies follow herewith; according to these the estates are 2. 41 3/4 quite clear. According to the hint given me by the Minister in confidence, an equally proportioned moratorium will follow with respect to the private debts, whereupon Herr Weigel will be settled with in the same manner. I shall thank God on my knees in [uncertain: places] that those usurers, who often did Your Serene Highness very [uncertain: true/grievous] harm, must be content with this wise and general ordinance. Now there remains only the business of working out and fixing the revenues definitely,

so that a well-founded budget may be formed. The hereditary lessees pay absolutely nothing – the suits concerning this are pending; the outcome will be that all contracts are annulled and the [uncertain: stipulation] must be thrown out, against which one must lay out demesne farms on the [uncertain: cleared] lands. Beer and brandy [uncertain: cultivation] is already being administered for Your Serene Highness from the 1st of July – this branch will yield most highly at [illegible], instead of the barely 1500 Rthl got out of the lessees by wicked lawsuits. The Olemizur and Werbizier suit is pending; it may yet be 6 weeks before the matters are decided at the Tribunal – this much, however, I know for certain, that the contracts will be [uncertain: annulled/caused], and then Your Serene Highness will receive 40000 Rthl on each of them there, the annual interest of 2000 Rthl. With Swiecia it comes out exactly the same, for the present holders pay not a Groschen at all; they must go, and be announced to the Poles – these are now the only ones who pay; the Germans absolutely nothing. The revenues, when everything has been won through lawsuits, will come to stand at 22 to 24000 Rthl. At present not a devil pays anything; even all the executions bear no fruit. Hecker and Hawich set the people on to this, and this makes the [uncertain: last ones] still more obstinate – Meanwhile Hecker was yesterday entirely [uncertain: refused/dismissed] in the Tribunal; as soon as the [uncertain: Prefect] returns from the baths the rogue will be arrested. The thief demands wages notwithstanding that, so far as I have learnt the matters, he already has more than 5000 Rthl behind him. He was engaged in February 1808, gave himself notice on the 25th of 9br of the same year, and he could demand of me

only 8 months' salary – that amounted to 400 Rthl – But according to the audit he has taken some 5000 Rthl – his paper security amounts at most to 2100 d at the current rate, so he must still pay out 2500 Rthl; his interference from 10 April 1810 to 8 January 1811 is not valid and is rejected — that I have moreover not written to Your Serene Highness since the month of February, I dispute, and shall prove the contrary – I only [uncertain: gathered], since I have been a landed proprietor here, that Hecker, Hawich and these rogues represented me as a dangerous [uncertain: perjurer] who held with the enemies of Poland, on which account my letters have been intercepted – But as soon as the General Fiscal returns, [uncertain: he/I] shall frame a criminal process against S. – For the rest I have [illegible], for whose honesty I have always stood surety, instructed to send in to Your Serene Highness the most punctual accounts of every Groschen taken in and paid out. I have wretchedly struggled through, often gone hungry and lain in a hole, against which Your Serene Highness's dogs may be called a lesser [uncertain: kennel]. My fine Kontopper estates the devil has carried off over this; I was, from that cursed Berlin affair, as also to Blockadg and Stolzapfel, still some 6500 Rthl in debt; more than 2000 Rthl I had brought here and travelled [uncertain: myself and after]; the usurers pursued me as far as here into Poland; I had to enter, from their mal gré, into the barter deal so that I saved myself and was not once again arrested as in Berlin. Meanwhile I have been received here with love and kindness; I believe I shall find here a quiet [uncertain: physician] — For the rest I have reduced the personnel, engaged cheap men for Your Serene Highness – the Commissary of Husbandry, the [uncertain: Word] (which every hereditary lord should himself be), the [uncertain: Rentmeister], the Chief Forester, 2 under-foresters, one steward over the propination rights, cost 1000 Rthl yearly; I think Your Serene Highness can be content with that.

Gladly would [uncertain: I speak]; the times are pregnant with great things. [uncertain: to speak with] Your Serene Highness even but one little hour — M[inister] Hardenberg is my [uncertain: greener/friend] and Konstan: means well – the vacations now set in here, when the judges rest; if I knew where Your Serene Highness now is, I would lay myself at your [uncertain: feet] in person.
