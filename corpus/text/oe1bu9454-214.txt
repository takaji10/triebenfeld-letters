--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Condolences on the death of the Princess Sanken, then news that the Departmental Minister has confirmed a settlement spreading the Bank and Invalids' debts over 41¾ years at 6 per cent, with a like moratorium expected for private debts and Herr Weigel. Hereditary lessees pay nothing; suits over Olemizur, Werbizier and Swiecia should annul the contracts and lift revenues to 22–24,000 Rthl. Hecker, dismissed at the Tribunal, owes some 2500 Rthl; Triebenfeld defends himself, reports personnel cut to 1000 Rthl yearly, and plans a Berlin journey on the 25th.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

In the decease of the Princess Sanken I take the liveliest part; my [uncertain: dearest] condolence I lay at Your Serene Highness's feet. May Heaven in return preserve Your Serene Highness alone, let both of them become strangers to you, and [uncertain: grant] you rest and the most durable health, so that you may yet for a long series of years remain the support of your House and the benefactor of good men. The news of the death of the Princess, now most blessed, was already in my hands on the 11th. I should gladly have hastened to Your Serene Highness, had not just now 4 principal matters been due to be decided by the Departmental Minister, upon which the preservation of the patrimonial estates depended. Only yesterday did I receive the confirmation and the approved settlement, namely that the Bank and Invalids' debts, together with the arrears of interest [uncertain: Zutrahm], are to be paid off over 41 ¾ years at 6 per cent. The copies follow herewith; according to these the estates are, in 2. 41 3/4, entirely clear. According to the hint given me by the Minister in confidence, a like moratorium will follow in respect of the private debts, whereupon Mr. Weigel will be settled with in the same manner.

God will I thank on my knees in [uncertain: Stellen] that those usurers, who often did Your Serene Highness very [uncertain: true] harm, must now be content with this wise general disposition.

Now there remains only the business of working out and fixing the revenues definitely

so that a sound budget may be formed.

The hereditary leaseholders pay nothing whatever – the suits on that account are pending; the result will be that all contracts are annulled and the [uncertain: Bestimung] thrown out, against which one must lay out demesne farms on the [uncertain: geradeten] lands.

The beer and brandy rent-roll is already being administered for Your Serene Highness from the 1st of July – this branch will yield the utmost [uncertain: an Büro d], instead of the 1500 Rthl scarcely got out of the lessees by way of bitter lawsuits. The Olemizur and Werbizier suit is pending; it may yet be 6 weeks before the matters are decided at the Tribunal – so much, however, I know for certain, that the contracts will be annulled, and then Your Serene Highness will receive 40000 Rthl upon each, there the annual interest of 2000 Rthl; with Swiecia it comes out exactly so, for the present possessors pay not a Groschen whatever, they must go, and be given notice to the Poles; those are now the only ones who pay, the Germans absolutely nothing. The revenues, once everything has been won through litigation, will come to stand at from 22 to 24000 Rthl. At present not a devil pays anything; even all the executions bear no fruit. Hecker and Hawich set the people up to it, and this makes the [uncertain: leester] still more obstinate – meanwhile Hecker was yesterday wholly [uncertain: dismissed] in the Tribunal; as soon as the [uncertain: Prifat] returns from the baths the rogue will be arrested. The thief demands wages notwithstanding that, so far as I have unlearnt the matters, he has already more than 5000 Rthl behind him. He was engaged in February 1808, released himself of his own accord on the 25th of November of the same year, and he could only

demand his salary for 8 months – that amounted to 400 Rthl – but after the audit he has some 5000 Rthl gone – his paper security amounts at most to 2100 d at the rate of exchange, so he must still pay out 2500 Rthl; his interference from 10 April 1810 to 8 January 1811 does not hold good and is rejected — that I have, for the rest, not written to Your Serene Highness since the month of February, I do contest, and shall prove the contrary – I only know [uncertain: erthegt], since I have been an estate proprietor here, that Hecker, Hawich and these rogues gave me out for a dangerous [uncertain: perjurer] who sided with the enemies of Poland, on which account my letters have been intercepted – But as soon as the Attorney-General returns, a criminal prosecution will be framed against them, S. – For the rest I have [uncertain: habenis], for whose honesty I have always been surety, charged to send in to Your Serene Highness the most punctual accounts of every Groschen taken in and paid out. I have wound my way through in wretchedness, often gone hungry and lain in a hole against which Your Serene Highness's dog-kennel could be called the lesser [uncertain: ein fallest]. My fine Kontop estates the devil has taken over it; I was, from that [illegible] Berlin affair, as well as to the [uncertain: Blockadg] and Stolz[uncertain: apfel], still some 6500 Rthl in debt; over 2000 Rthl I had brought here and [uncertain: travelled away with myself and after]; the usurers pursued me here into Poland, I had to enter into the barter-bargain with them malgré, so that I saved myself and was not once again arrested as in Berlin. Meanwhile I have been received here with love and kindness; I believe I shall find here a quiet [uncertain: physician] — Otherwise I have reduced the personnel and engaged cheap men for Your Serene Highness – the economy commissary, the [uncertain: Wort] (which every hereditary lord should be himself), the rent-master, the head forester, 2 under-foresters, a bailiff over the propination rights, cost 1000 Rthl yearly; I think Your Serene Highness can be content with that.

The lawsuits alone cost a furious deal of money, and before everything is set in order Satan may yet [uncertain: fetch] a couple of thousand more – for this time, however, nothing shall be thrown away for nothing, for if I now lose a suit in Your Serene Highness's affairs, I will suffer boxes on the ear from the Professor.

On the 25th I go to Berlin and on the 12th of August I am back here again; they wish to speak there as well — The Minister and Prefect-General Count Gacczynali also desires that I should attend to a matter for him. Gladly — the times are pregnant with great things. I [would speak with] Your Serene Highness even but a little hour — M. Hard: is my [uncertain: Grüner] and Konstan: means well – the vacation now sets in here, when the judges rest; if I knew where Your Serene Highness now is, I should surely lay myself at your feet in person. In deepest reverence I remain, until death,

Your Serene Highness's

Kalisz, the 21st of July

1811.

humble and faithful servant

v. Triebenfeld
