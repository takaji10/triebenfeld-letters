--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Ein Bericht aus Kalisch vom 5. Dezember 1754 in der Warschauer Zeitung Kuryer Polski, Nr. 75 von 1754, auf Polnisch. In Trąbczyn, fünf Meilen von Kalisch, auf den Erbgütern der Familie Prusimski (im Druck „Prusiński“), wurde das Begräbnis des verstorbenen Paweł Prusimski, Kammerherrn Seiner Königlichen Majestät, gehalten, in Anwesenheit vieler verschwägerter Häuser und der Zisterzienser des Konvents von Ląd. Der Abt von Ląd, Głowiecki, war zum Hochamt eingeladen, doch ein unerwarteter Frost hinderte ihn, die Warthe zu überqueren. Die Predigt hielt der Präfekt der Schulen des Jesuitenkollegs in Kalisch, die Leichenrede nach dem Kondukt sein leiblicher Neffe Antoni Prusimski. Auf Anordnung der Witwe, Katarzyna Prusimska, geborene Rozdrażewska, wurden am selben Tag in den nahen Klöstern von Pyzdry, Konin und Kaźmierz Exequien mit Vigilien und vielen Messen gehalten, weil in der Pfarrkirche von Trąbczyn nicht genug Platz war.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A report from Kalisz of 5 December 1754 in the Warsaw newspaper Kuryer Polski, no. 75 of 1754, in Polish. At Trąbczyn, five miles from Kalisz, on the hereditary estate of the Prusimski family (the print has "Prusiński"), the funeral of the late Paweł Prusimski, Chamberlain of His Royal Majesty, was held, with many related houses and the Cistercians of the convent of Ląd present. The Abbot of Ląd, Głowiecki, had been invited to sing the High Mass, but an unexpected frost prevented his crossing the Warta. The sermon was preached by the prefect of the schools of the Jesuit college at Kalisz, and the funeral oration after the procession was given by his own nephew, Antoni Prusimski. At the order of the widow, Katarzyna Prusimska, born Rozdrażewska, exequies with vigils and many Masses were held the same day in the monasteries nearby, at Pyzdry, Konin and Kaźmierz, as there was not room enough in the parish church of Trąbczyn.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
From Kalisz, 5 December. At Trąbczyn, five miles from Kalisz, on the hereditary estate of the Honourable Prusimski family, the funeral of the late Honourable Paweł Prusimski, Chamberlain of His Royal Majesty, was held, with a large attendance of related houses, of the secular [uncertain: clergy] and of the Cistercian Fathers of the convent of Ląd. The Reverend Głowiecki, Abbot of Ląd, had been invited to sing the High Mass, but an unexpected frost prevented his crossing the river Warta. The sermon was preached by the Reverend Prefect of the schools of the College of the Society of Jesus at Kalisz. The funeral oration after the procession was given by the Honourable Antoni Prusimski, his own nephew. In the monasteries nearby, namely at Pyzdry, Konin and Kaźmierz, at the order of Her Grace Katarzyna Prusimska, born Rozdrażewska, his widow, exequies were held on the same day, with vigils and many Masses, for lack of room in the parish church of Trąbczyn.
