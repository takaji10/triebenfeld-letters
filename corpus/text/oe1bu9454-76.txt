--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports that the projected exchange of estates is under way and may soon be concluded. Triebenfeld announces that he will travel to Berlin at the beginning of September and has leave to stay until the end of that month, where he hopes to find the Prince in good health, present himself in person, and give a full verbal report on several items of business. Otherwise wholly formulaic in its expressions of devotion.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince, Most gracious Lord!

The certain exchange of estates is now in train, and perhaps the matter will shortly be settled. I go to Berlin at the beginning of September, and shall be permitted to remain until the end of the same month.

The foremost good fortune of which I hope to partake in Berlin is to see Your Most Serene Highness again in good health, and to be able to lay myself personally at your exalted feet, whereupon I shall render the most faithful report upon several matters of business.

In deepest reverence I remain, until death, Your Most Serene Highness's, Breslau, the 23rd of August 1798, most faithful and humble and obedient servant, v. Triebenfeld
