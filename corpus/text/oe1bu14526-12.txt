--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Hereditary quit-rent contract recorded before the Trąbczyn Patrimonial Court between War and Forests Councillor Peter Friedrich August v. Triebenfeld, general plenipotentiary of the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, and the forester George Friedrich Kammen (Kammer) of Pietrzykowo: 6 Hufen 20 Morgen Magdeburg measure from the Przybysław demesne farm with its buildings and timber, for 2,100 Rthaler (800 at Johanni, 1,300 at Christmas) plus 100 Rthaler yearly quit-rent, with obligations covering taxes, the Ląd monastery Competenz, seigneurial drink-compulsion, Laudemium and forfeiture on two years' arrears.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Actum Trąbczyn, the 10th of May 1805. Before the undersigned Patrimonial Court there appeared

1st, Herr Councillor of War and Forests v. Triebenfeld, as the known General Plenipotentiary of His Serene Highness the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, hereditary lord and lord of the court of the estates here, on the one part

and

2nd, the forester H. George Friedrich Kammen of Pietrzykowo on the other part. The parties are personally known to the subscribers, and their capacity to execute [uncertain: gewähtlicher] transactions is subject to no doubt.

The same declare, freely and after due deliberation, for the record, the following irrevocable hereditary quit-rent contract concluded between them, in nothing to be al-

tered, [text continues] into the record.

§ I. His Most Serene Highness accordingly grants in hereditary quit-rent, out of the demesne farm Przybysław belonging to the Trąbczyn estates, 6 Hufen [uncertain: keines] of arable land and, in meadow within the boundaries of the Trąbczyn estates, 20 Morgen, together therefore 6 Hufen 20 Morgen, in writing six Hufen twenty Morgen Magdeburg measure, to George Friedrich Kammer from now on and for ever-enduring times, together with all the farm buildings situated at Przybisław, nothing at all being excepted therefrom, as likewise the building timber lying there upon the demesne farm.

§ II. Kammer pays as purchase-money for the granted [uncertain: Rechtshafen], twenty Morgen

Magdeburg measure, together 2000 rt, in writing two thousand Rthaler, and for the buildings 100 rt, in writing one hundred Rthaler, thus in all 2100 rthl., in writing two thousand one hundred Rthaler, and that indeed at Johanni of this year with 800 rt, in writing eight hundred Rthaler, and at Christmas of this year with 1300 rt, in writing one thousand three hundred Rthaler, in cash and in good Prussian Courant of the standard of the treasury.

§ III. The hereditary lessee binds himself, for these acquired 6 Hufen 20 Morgen, from Johanni of this year onward to [pay] an ever-enduring hereditary quit-rent, which may never be raised or diminished, per Hufe Magdeburg measure at 15 rt, in writing fifteen Rthaler, hence for 6 Hufen 20 Morgen 100 rthl., in writing one hundred Rthaler, in

in Courant cash, and that annually in two instalments at Christmas and at Johanni, each time with 50 rt, in writing fifty Rthaler, yet not in advance, but rather after the lapse of each half-year, consequently to be paid at Christmas for the first time.

§ IV. His establishment he must effect himself at his own cost, and without receiving, either for the present or for the future, timber or other building assistance; nor does he receive any right of pasture or of wood-cutting on the remaining seigneurial territory.

§ V. The acquired [uncertain: bekaufen] 20 Morgen Magdeburg measure shall be measured out to Kammer as soon as possible,

and he receives the same, winter and summer field, unsown; yet for the tilling of the present summer field, for the carting out of the [uncertain: Muses], the requisite team-service will be furnished him by the ground lordship, and that for two Hufen, free of charge for this once.

§ VI. Besides the cash hereditary quit-rent, the hereditary lessee obliges himself, for himself and his descendants in possession of these six Hufen 20 Morgen, to discharge all and every Royal taxes, as likewise the Competenz for the monastery Ląd and the dues to the clergy, chimney-money, forage deliveries, and whatever else may arise, without any exception whatever,

without making or obtaining any remission of the hereditary quit-rent on that account.

§ VII. The hereditary lessee must take his drink in beer and brandy from the seigneurial [uncertain: Frage] or brew- and distilling-house, and in case of contravention must pay to the ground lordship, for every Garniec of foreign brandy brought in, one Rthaler, and for every barrel of foreign beer five Rthaler, as a penalty.

§ VIII. The hereditary lessee and his successors in possession are subject to the jurisdiction of the local Dominium, yet are wholly and for ever

enduring times exempt from all and every deliveries in kind and Hufe-services.

§ IX.

The hereditary lessee and his successors and [uncertain: Besizze] are at liberty to alienate this acquired holding to another by sale or by exchange, yet such must take place with the consent of the ground lordship, and upon every such change of possession there shall be paid to the ground lordship, out of the purchase-money or the exchange sum, the [uncertain: gehabte] Groschen as a Laudemium. In cases of inheritance to [uncertain: sonder] and collateral relations, no Laudemium is paid.

§ X. The hereditary lessee renounces, for himself and his successors and [uncertain: besag], all and every remission

in cases of misfortune which arise through fire from heaven, through the incendiarism of evil people, crop failure, [uncertain: smagelschlag], flood, cattle-plague, war and pestilence, or in any other manner whatsoever, and no compensation from the ground lordship, nor remission of the hereditary quit-rent, shall ever on that account accrue to them.

§ XI. The costs of this contract and of its engrossment are paid by the hereditary lessee.

§ XII. So long as the hereditary lessee and his successors and [uncertain: besitze] of this holding shall fulfil all the above obligations,

the ground lordship promises to protect them in their rights in every respect and to defend them against every man's real claims.

Should, however, the hereditary lessee and his successors [uncertain: verwüßen] this holding, or remain in arrears with the annual hereditary quit-rent for longer than two years, then this hereditary-lease contract shall cease, and the ground lordship shall be entitled to dispose of this holding otherwise.

Both parties promise to keep the foregoing hereditary quit-rent contract firmly and inviolably, and renounce all legal

evasions available against it, in particular the objection of cunning persuasion, of precipitancy, of an injury suffered above or below the half, of matters agreed otherwise than as set down in writing; and they have been duly instructed by the court as to the consequences of this renunciation.

His Most Serene Highness expressly consents thereto, that the hereditary lessee may, upon the ground of this contract, have the title of possession of this acquired holding entered in his name.

Further the contracting parties had nothing to bring forward; after this protocol had been read over to them word for word, they approved the same in every respect and subscribe it below in their own hand.

Peter Friedrich August v. Triebenfeld

I have read and approve this contract
George Friedrich Kammer
