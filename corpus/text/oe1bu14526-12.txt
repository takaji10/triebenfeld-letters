--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Hereditary quit-rent contract before the Trąbczyn Patrimonial Court between War and Forests Councillor v. Triebenfeld, general plenipotentiary of the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, and forester George Friedrich Kammer of Pietrzykowo: 6 Hufen 20 Morgen Magdeburg of arable and meadow from the Przybysław demesne farm, with its buildings and timber, for 2100 Rthaler (800 at Johanni, 1300 at Christmas) and a perpetual annual quit-rent of 100 Rthaler. Kammer bears all taxes, Kloster Ląd dues and clergy levies, must take beer and brandy from the manorial tavern, remains under the Dominium's jurisdiction, pays Laudemium on sale or exchange, and renounces all remission. Signed by Triebenfeld, Kammer, Schenck and Brauer.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Actum Trąbczyn, the 10 May 1805. Before the undersigned Patrimonial Court there appeared

1., Herr Councillor of War and Forests v. Triebenfeld, as the known General Plenipotentiary of His Serene Highness the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, hereditary lord and lord of the court of the estates here, on the one part

and

2., the forester H. George Friedrich Kammer of Pietrzykowo, on the other part. The parties are personally known to the Subscripti, and their capacities for the execution of [uncertain: valid] transactions are subject to no doubt.

The same declare, freely and after due deliberation, for the protocol the following hereditary quit-rent contract concluded between them, irrevocable and in nothing to be altered

-able hereditary quit-rent contract, to the protocol.

§. I. His Most Serene Highness namely grants in hereditary quit-rent, out of the demesne farm Przybysław belonging to the Trąbczyn estates, 6 Hufen of clear arable land and in meadows within the boundaries of the Trąbczyn estates 20 Morgen, together therefore 6 Hufen 20 Morgen, in words six Hufen twenty Morgen Magdeburg [measure], to George Friedrich Kammer from now on and for perpetual times, together with all the farm buildings situated at Przybisław, nothing at all excepted, as also the building timber lying there upon the demesne farm.

§. II. The said Kammer pays as purchase-money for the [uncertain: rightful holdings] granted to him, twenty Morgen

Magdeburg, together 2000 rt, in words two thousand Rthaler, and for the buildings 100 rt, in words one hundred Rthaler, altogether therefore 2100 rthl., in words two thousand one hundred Rthaler; and this, at Johanni of this year with 800 rt, in words eight hundred Rthaler, and at Christmas of this year with 1300 rt, in words one thousand three hundred Rthaler, in cash and in good Prussian Courant of the Treasury standard.

§. III. The hereditary lessee binds himself for these acquired 6 Hufen 20 Morgen, from Johanni of this year onward, to a perpetual hereditary quit-rent, which may never be raised or diminished, per Hufe Magdeburg at 15 rt, in words fifteen Rthaler, hence for 6 Hufen 20 Morgen 100 rthl., in words one hundred Rthaler, in

in Courant, in cash, and indeed yearly in two instalments, at Christmas and at Johanni, each time with 50 rt, in words fifty Rthaler, yet not in advance, but to be paid after the expiry of each half year, consequently at Christmas for the first time.

§. IV. His establishment [uncertain: he] must effect himself at his own cost, and without his receiving either now or in future timber or other building assistance; he likewise receives neither pasturage nor timber rights upon the remaining manorial territory.

§. V. The acquired [uncertain: six Hufen] 20 Morgen Magdeburg are to be measured out to the said Kammer as soon as possible

and the same receives the winter and summer field unsown; however, for the cultivation of the present summer field, for the carting out of the [uncertain: manure], the requisite team-service shall be furnished by the ground-lordship, and indeed for two Hufen, this once, free of charge.

§. VI. Besides the cash hereditary quit-rent, the hereditary lessee binds himself, for himself and his successors in possession of these six Hufen 20 Morgen, to discharge all and every Royal taxes, as also the dues to the Kloster Ląd and the levies to the clergy, chimney-money, forage deliveries, and whatever else may occur, without any exception whatever,

without making or receiving on this account any remission of the hereditary quit-rent.

§. VII. The hereditary lessee shall take his drink of beer and brandy from the manorial tavern or from the brew- and brandy-house, and in case of contravention shall pay to the ground-lordship as penalty, for every Garniec of foreign brandy brought in, one Rthaler, and for every barrel of foreign beer five Rthaler.

§. VIII. The hereditary lessee and his successors in possession are subject to the jurisdiction of the Dominium here, yet are wholly and for all perpetual

times exempted from all and every deliveries in kind and Hufe-services.

§. IX.

It stands free to the hereditary lessee and his successors in possession to [uncertain: alienate] this acquired holding to another by sale or exchange; yet such must take place with the consent of the ground-lordship, and upon every such change of possession there shall be paid to the ground-lordship, out of the purchase-money or the amount of the exchange, the [uncertain: Groschen accustomed] as a Laudemium. Upon inheritances to [uncertain: descendants] and collateral relations, no Laudemium is paid.

§. X. The hereditary lessee renounces, for himself and his successors in possession, all and every remission

in cases of misfortune, whether arising through fire from heaven, through the malice of wicked people, crop failure, [uncertain: hail-stroke], inundation, cattle plague, war and pestilence, or in any other manner whatsoever, entirely; and on this account no indemnification from the ground-lordship, nor any remission of the hereditary quit-rent, shall ever accrue to them.

§. XI. The costs of this contract and of its engrossment are paid by the hereditary lessee.

§. XII. So long as the hereditary lessee and his successors in possession of this holding shall fulfil all the above obligations,

the ground-lordship promises to protect them everywhere in their rights and to defend them against every man's real claims.

But should the hereditary lessee and his successors lay this holding waste, or remain in arrears with the yearly hereditary quit-rent for longer than two years, then this hereditary-lease contract ceases, and the ground-lordship shall be entitled to dispose of this holding otherwise.

Both parties promise to keep the foregoing hereditary quit-rent contract firmly and inviolably, and renounce all

legal evasions available against it, in particular the objection of cunning persuasion, of precipitancy, of an injury occurring above or below the half, of matters otherwise agreed than written down; and they have been duly instructed by the court as to the consequences of this renunciation.

His Most Serene Highness expressly consents that the hereditary lessee may, on the ground of this contract, have the title of possession of this acquired holding entered in his own name.

Further the contracting parties had nothing to bring forward; after this protocol had been read over to them word for word, they approved the same throughout and subscribed it below with their own hands.

Peter Friedrich August v. Triebenfeld

George Friedrich Kammer
