--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Hereditary quit-rent contract before the Trąbczyn Patrimonial Court between War and Forests Councillor v. Triebenfeld, general plenipotentiary of the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, and forester George Friedrich Kammer of Pietrzykowo: 6 Hufen 20 Morgen Magdeburg of arable and meadow from the Przybysław demesne farm, with its buildings and timber, for 2100 Rthaler (800 at Johanni, 1300 at Christmas) and a perpetual annual quit-rent of 100 Rthaler. Kammer bears all taxes, Kloster Ląd dues and clergy levies, must take beer and brandy from the manorial tavern, remains under the Dominium's jurisdiction, pays Laudemium on sale or exchange, and renounces all remission. Signed by Triebenfeld, Kammer, Schenck and Brauer.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Actum Trąbczyn, the 10th of May 1805. Before the undersigned patrimonial court there appeared:

1., the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, as is known the general attorney of His Serene Highness the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, hereditary lord and lord of the court of the estates here, on the one part,

and

2., the forester Mr. George Friedrich Kammer of Pietrzykowo on the other part. The parties are personally known to the undersigned, and their capacity to execute [uncertain: valid] transactions is subject to no doubt.

The same do voluntarily and after mature deliberation declare into the protocol the following hereditary quit-rent contract concluded between them, irrevocable and in nothing to be altered

hereditary quit-rent contract, into the protocol.

§. I. His Most Serene Highness does, namely, grant out of the demesne farm Przybysław belonging to the Trąbczyn estates 6 Hufen of clean arable land, and in meadow within the bounds of the Trąbczyn estates 20 Morgen, thus together 6 Hufen 20 Morgen, written out: six Hufen twenty Morgen Magdeburg measure, to George Friedrich Kammer from now on and for perpetual times in hereditary quit-rent, together with all the farm buildings standing at Przybisław, nothing at all excepted therefrom, as likewise the building timber lying there on the demesne farm.

§. II. Kammer pays as purchase money for the [uncertain: holdings] granted to him, twenty Morgen

Magdeburg measure, together 2000 rt, written out: two thousand Rthl, and for the buildings 100 rt, written out: one hundred Rthl, hence in all 2100 Rthl, written out: two thousand one hundred Rthl, and that at Midsummer of this year with 800 rt, written out: eight hundred Rthl, and at Christmas of this year with 1300 rt, written out: one thousand three hundred Rthl, in cash and in good Prussian Courant of full treasury weight.

§. III. The hereditary lessee binds himself for these acquired 6 Hufen 20 Morgen, from Midsummer of this year onward, to a perpetual hereditary quit-rent, which may never be raised or diminished, of 15 rt per Hufe Magdeburg measure, written out: fifteen Rthl, hence for 6 Hufen 20 Morgen 100 Rthl, written out: one hundred Rthl, in

in Courant cash, and that annually in two instalments, at Christmas and at Midsummer, each time with 50 rt, written out: fifty Rthl, yet not in advance but after the expiry of each half year, consequently to be paid for the first time at Christmas.

§. IV. He must effect his establishment himself, at his own cost and without receiving either now or for the future timber or any other building assistance; he likewise receives neither right of pasturage nor right of wood-taking on the remaining manorial territory.

§. V. The 20 Morgen Magdeburg measure acquired [uncertain: by purchase] shall be measured out to Kammer as soon as possible

and the same receives winter and summer field unsown; yet for the working of the present summer field, for the carting out of the dung, the requisite team-service shall be furnished him by the manorial lordship, and that upon two Hufen for this once without charge.

§. VI. Besides the cash hereditary quit-rent, the hereditary lessee obliges himself, for himself and his successors in possession of these six Hufen 20 Morgen, to discharge all and every royal tax, as likewise the quota due to the monastery of Ląd and the dues to the clergy, chimney money, forage delivery, and whatever else may occur, without any exception whatsoever,

without making or obtaining on that account any remission of the hereditary quit-rent.

§. VII. The hereditary lessee must take his drink, in beer and brandy, from the manorial tavern or the brew- and distilling-house, and in case of contravention must pay to the manorial lordship a fine of one Rthl for every garniec of foreign brandy brought in and five Rthl for every barrel of foreign beer.

§. VIII. The hereditary lessee and his successors in possession are subject to the jurisdiction of the manorial lordship (Dominium) here, yet are wholly and for ever

and perpetual times exempted from all and every delivery in kind and Hufe-service.

S. IX.

The hereditary lessee and his successors in possession are at liberty to [uncertain: alienate] this acquired holding to another by sale or by exchange; yet such must take place with the consent of the manorial lordship, and upon every such change of possession there shall be paid to the manorial lordship, out of the purchase money or the sum given in exchange, the [uncertain: Groschen received] as a transfer fee (Laudemium). Upon inheritance by [uncertain: direct] and collateral relations, no transfer fee is paid.

§. X. The hereditary lessee renounces, for himself and his successors in possession, all and every remission

in cases of misfortune arising through fire from heaven, through arson by evil persons, crop failure, hail, flood, cattle plague, war and pestilence, or in any other manner whatsoever, and on that account there shall never accrue to them any indemnification from the manorial lordship or remission of the hereditary quit-rent.

§. XI. The costs of this contract and of its engrossment are paid by the hereditary lessee.

§. XII. So long as the hereditary lessee and his successors in possession of this holding shall fulfil all the above obligations,

the manorial lordship promises to protect them everywhere in their rights and to defend them against every man's real claims.

Should, however, the hereditary lessee and his successors lay this holding waste, or remain in arrears with the yearly hereditary quit-rent for longer than two years, then this hereditary lease contract shall cease and the manorial lordship shall be entitled to let out this holding otherwise.

Both parties promise to hold the foregoing hereditary quit-rent contract firmly and inviolably, and renounce all legal

evasions available against it, particularly the objection of cunning persuasion, of precipitancy, of an injury suffered above or below the half, and of matters otherwise agreed than written down; and they have been duly instructed by the court as to the consequences of this renunciation.

His Most Serene Highness expressly consents that the hereditary lessee may, upon the ground of this contract, have the title of possession of this acquired holding [uncertain: registered] in his name.

Further the contracting parties had nothing to bring forward; after this protocol had been read over to them word for word, they approved the same throughout and have signed below in their own hands. Peter Friedrich August von Triebenfeld. I have read and approve this contract. George Friedrich Kammer
