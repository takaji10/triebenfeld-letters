--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Melzer reports from Poznań that the Chamber's fiscal officer again failed to appear at the hearing for the resignation of the Pszczew estates, having still received no authorisation, and that the business will not conclude until the Chamber instructs him plainly what he is to resign; he asks that the Prince press Chamber President von Harlem to expedite it. A postscript notes an appeal judgment in the case with the Cathedral Chapter and Kruhtswitz has been reversed in their favour.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Your Honour!

Particularly Highly Honoured Sir, Councillor of Building and Economy!

For the resignation of the Pszczew estates, the Chamber fiscal has once again failed to appear at today's hearing, because he has as yet received no authorisation from the Chamber. I have indeed once more requested that a new hearing be appointed, but the matter will never come to an end unless the Chamber at last gives the fiscal a properly drawn instruction that he is to resign, and what he is to resign. If His Most Serene Highness might be pleased to approach the Chamber President von Harlem for an expediting of the business, we should surely at some point attain our object.

I have the honour to be, with the most perfect [uncertain: esteem],

Your Honour's most devoted servant

Poznań, the 25th of January 1802

Melzer.

Turn over
