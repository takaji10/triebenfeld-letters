--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Melzer schreibt einem Bau- und Ökonomierat: Im heutigen Termin zur Abtretung der Güter von Pszczew ist der Kammerfiskal wieder nicht erschienen, weil ihn die Kammer noch nicht ermächtigt hat. Melzer hat um einen neuen Termin gebeten, sieht aber kein Ende, solange die Kammer dem Fiskal keine klare Anweisung gibt, und rät, der Fürst möge den Kammerpräsidenten von Harlem um Beschleunigung bitten. In einer Nachschrift meldet er, dass das Urteil zweiter Instanz in einer Sache mit dem Domkapitel abändernd ausgefallen und das Domkapitel abgewiesen worden ist.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Melzer writes to a councillor for building and estate management: at today's hearing for the formal surrender of the estates of Pszczew the chamber's fiscal officer again failed to appear, because the Chamber has not yet authorised him. Melzer has asked for a new hearing, but sees no end to it unless the Chamber gives the fiscal officer clear instructions, and advises that the Prince ask the Chamber's president, von Harlem, to hasten matters. In a postscript he reports that the judgment on appeal in a case with the cathedral chapter has reversed the first and dismissed the chapter.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Your Honour!

Particularly Highly Honoured Sir, Councillor of Building and Economy!

For the resignation of the Pszczew estates, the Chamber fiscal has once again failed to appear at today's hearing, because he has as yet received no authorisation from the Chamber. I have indeed once more requested that a new hearing be appointed, but the matter will never come to an end unless the Chamber at last gives the fiscal a properly drawn instruction that he is to resign, and what he is to resign. If His Most Serene Highness might be pleased to approach the Chamber President von Harlem for an expediting of the business, we should surely at some point attain our object.

I have the honour to be, with the most perfect [uncertain: esteem],

Your Honour's most devoted servant

Poznań, the 25th of January 1802

Melzer.
