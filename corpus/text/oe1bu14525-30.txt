--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Aufforderung der Königlich Südpreußischen Regierung an den fürstlich hohenlohischen Bau- und Wirtschaftsrat Glenck in Breslau, die Hypothekensachen des Gutes Swięcia und der dazugehörigen Güter Kopojno, Drzewce und Zagórów binnen 14 Tagen in Ordnung zu bringen. Andernfalls werde die Fiskalbehörde veranlasst, gegen seinen Auftraggeber, den Fürsten von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, vorzugehen. Unterzeichnet Meuser, mit Nummer 2092.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A royal warning from the South Prussian government at Kalisz to Glenck, building and economy councillor to the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, at Wrocław: unless the mortgage affairs of the estate Swięcia and the estates belonging with it, Kopojno, Drzewce and Zagórów, are put in order within fourteen days, the Treasury will be obliged to proceed against his principal, the Prince. A single short page, signed Meuser.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
By the Grace of God, Friedrich Wilhelm, King of Prussia. Our gracious greeting first: Honourable, dear and faithful subject. In the mortgage matter of the estate Swięcia, it is hereby made known to you that if within 14 days the mortgage affairs of this estate and of the remaining estates belonging thereto, Kopojno, Drzewce and Zagórów, are not [uncertain: unv] put in order, then the Treasury must without fail be moved to proceed against your principal, the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Your Lordship; whereby you are to govern yourself accordingly. We remain graciously disposed towards you. Given Kalisz, the 18th of November 1800. Royal South Prussian Government. Meuser [uncertain: Meuser]

To the Princely Hohenlohe Building and Economy Councillor Glenck at Wrocław
