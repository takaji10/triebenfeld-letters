--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A copy of a court order arising from the General Invalids' Fund's suit of 30 July before the Superior Court of Appeal in Berlin: Minister of State and Justice von Kircheisen is forbidden, on his own liability, to pay out anything to von Triebenfeld at Kontop near Grüneberg from a 50,000 Rthl legacy said to be due to the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen from the estate of Princess von Sacken and assigned to Triebenfeld against his claims. Like orders went to Chief Medical Councillor Cosmar and to Triebenfeld, forwarded through the Silesian Superior Provincial Court at Glogau.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The Royal Minister of State and of Justice, Mr. von Kircheisen, is hereby called upon, in the event that the assertion advanced by the General Invalids' Fund in its suit lodged on the 30th of last month with the Royal Superior Court of Appeal — namely, that the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen was to receive from the estate of the Princess von Sacken a legacy of 50000 Rthl, and that he had directed the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld at Kontop near Grüneberg to that sum for the settlement of the latter's claims against him — should prove to be well founded, to pay out nothing of this sum to the aforesaid von Triebenfeld, upon his own liability.

Berlin, the 1st of August 1811.

Royal Prussian Superior Court of Appeal.

[to] the Royal Minister of State and of Justice, Mr. von Kircheisen.

in like manner

To the Royal Chief Medical Councillor Mr. Cosmar here. To the Councillor of War and Forests Mr. von Triebenfeld at Kontop.

No. 1103.

We cause to be transmitted to you herewith a summons of the Royal Superior Court of Appeal at Berlin of the 1st of the current month, in the matter of the General Invalids' Fund against you, with the command to render in every respect the obedience due to its contents, and to certify in full the correct receipt thereof upon the Copy to be laid before you of the same. Glogau, the 6th of Aug. 1811. Royal Prussian Superior Provincial Court of Silesia. Fülleborn
