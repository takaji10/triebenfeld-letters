--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Order of the Royal Prussian Superior Court of Appeal in Berlin, arising from the suit brought on 30 July by the General Invalids' Fund, forbidding the Minister of State and Justice von Kircheisen, on his own liability, to pay out anything to Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld at Kontop near Grüneberg from the 50000 Rthl legacy due to the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen from the estate of Princess von Sacken, to which Triebenfeld had been directed for his claims; with like notices to Chief Medical Councillor Cosmar and to Triebenfeld, transmitted by the Silesian Superior Provincial Court at Glogau (Fülleborn) with a demand for certified receipt.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The Royal Minister of State and of Justice, Herr von Kircheisen, is hereby called upon, as executor of the testament of the deceased Frau Fürstin von Sacken, in the event that the assertion advanced by the General Invalids' Fund in its suit lodged on the 30th of last month before the Royal Chamber Court — namely that the Herr Fürst von Hohenlohe Ingelfingen would receive from the estate of the Fürstin von Sacken a legacy of 50000 Rthl, and that she had assigned this to the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld at Kontop near Grüneberg in satisfaction of the latter's claims against him — should prove to be founded, to pay out nothing to the said von Triebenfeld from this sum, upon his own liability. Berlin, the 1st of August 1811. Royal Prussian Chamber Court. [To] the Royal Minister of State and of Justice, Herr von Kircheisen. in simili: To the Royal Upper Medical Councillor Herr Cosmar, here. To the Councillor of War and Forests Herr von Triebenfeld at Kontop. No 1103. We transmit to you herewith a summons of the Royal Chamber Court at Berlin of the 1st of the current month in the matter of the General Invalids' Fund against you, with the command to render in every respect the obedience due to its contents, and to certify in full the due receipt thereof upon the Copy to be laid before you of the same. Glogau, the 6th of Aug. 1811. Royal Prussian Superior Provincial Court of Silesia. Fülleborn
