--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Defends himself at length against the suspicion, raised in Triebenfeld's letter of the 20th, of unfriendly conduct, and narrates the history of the Berlin creditor Eysenhardt (Eysach), settled in January 1807 through the Privy Councillor Major: the 6000 Rthlr instruction from Stettinius was ceded to him against 2500 Rthlr, the remaining 3500 Rthlr being lost when payment was suspended; Assessor Koppe has been instructed to pay Eysenhardt and retrieve the Alligl. Encloses 3000 Rthlr Courant, remarks on relations with Minister v. Stein, presses for a personal visit en route to Vienna via Dresden, and asks advice on the Wildegans matter.
