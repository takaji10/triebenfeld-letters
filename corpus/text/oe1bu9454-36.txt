--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Answering a reproachful letter, an explanation of what became of Triebenfeld's bond: in January 1807 the writer, pressed by the Berlin justice councillor Eysenhardt over an old debt, ceded it to him through privy councillor Meyer against a 6,000 Rthl payment order, only for Triebenfeld's suspension of payment to leave him short of the expected 3,500 Rthl. Assessor Koeppe has been instructed to redeem it for 2,500 Rthl. Encloses 3,000 Rthl Courant, regrets any coolness towards Minister von Stein, and urges a personal visit on the way to Vienna.
