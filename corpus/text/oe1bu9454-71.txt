--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Die Rochlitzsche Sache ist eingeleitet. Triebenfeld hofft, dass der Fürst nichts zahlen muss, und nennt sie eine Prellerei. Er habe Rochlitz 1804 als Generalbevollmächtigter abgesetzt, was Glenck bezeugen könne. Er bittet dringend um zwei bis drei unterschriebene und gesiegelte gedruckte Prozessvollmachten, damit Melzig die Sache betreiben und ausstehende Reste von an 18000 Rthl einfordern kann. Er erwartet Honrichs und will nach Sławięcice kommen, sobald die Sache in sicherem Geleise ist.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The Rochlitz matter has been started. Triebenfeld hopes the Prince will not have to pay anything, and calls it a swindle. He says he dismissed Rochlitz in 1804 as general attorney, which Glenck could confirm. He urgently asks for two or three printed powers of attorney for the lawsuit, signed and sealed, so that Melzig can pursue the matter and call in outstanding balances of close to 18,000 Rthl. He expects Honrichs and wants to come to Sławięcice as soon as the matter is safely on track.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
71

Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

The Rochlitz matter has now been set in train, as the enclosure shows, and Your Most Serene Highness will, as I confidently hope, have nothing to pay. This is a knavish swindle; the thief wanted to push it through in secret so as to defraud Your Most Serene Highness. Nothing of it had ever come before my eyes. That I dismissed the fellow in the year 1804 Glenck knows very well. He must bear witness to it as well. I gave him notice at that time in my capacity as general attorney, and dismissed him. Whether Your Serene Highness may perhaps afterwards, without my knowledge, have promised him anything is simply unknown to me, and neither do I credit the building councillor Glenck with having afterwards secretly given the rogue any assurance. I can swear with a true heart that I then removed Rochlitz from his post and called in the accounts, which he however refused to render to me.

May Your Serene Highness deign to send me at the very earliest two or three blank forms for powers of attorney in litigation - that is to say, merely three printed powers of attorney in litigation, signed and sealed - so that Melzig may prosecute the Rochlitz matter and may also call in the outstanding arrears for Your Serene Highness. They amount certainly to some 18,000 Rthl and are entirely liquid. Tomorrow or the day after I expect Honrichs

this matter is now of the utmost importance; once it is in the proper and secure track, then I shall come at once to Sławięcice — In deepest reverence I remain, until death, Your Most Serene Highness's The Polish matters will very much still I count especially Konotop

the 30th of March

1810 most faithful and obedient servant v Triebenfeld
