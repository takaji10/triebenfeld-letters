Unterthänigstes Pro Memoria
Aus das beigebogene Schreiben des Geheimen Raths Amelang werden Ewr Hochfürstliche Durchlaucht
zu ersehen geruhen, daß abermals eine neue Cabinets Ordre gegeben ist, daß man ganz das
LandRecht und alten Geſezzen entgegen jezt absolut Cour: zahlen muß wann auch gleich
die Valuta nur in Papieren gegeben worden.
dieser Schlag trift hart und erregt einen Verlust von 1500 Rthl in der vd Lahrschen Sache.
bezahlt muß nun nach der verſ. Cabinets Ordre | die nur allein zu Gunsten der Juden,
und Wucherer gegeben worden, absolut in baar Courant werden. Wo aber hernehmen?
Ich bin herunter und kan mich selbst nicht helfen; den noch jezt sizt ein Sequestor in meine
Kontopper Güter - ich muß von ferne stehen, und darf mich meinen eigenen Heerd
nicht nähern; Ja ich risquire alle Tage wegen die 3000 Rthl aufgehoben und aufs neue
durch den van der Lahr arretirt zu werden, welche Schmach ich aber mehr als den Todt fürchte
Ich habe um Unglük vorzubeugen in dieser Noth heute an. Amelang Nachmäus
Ewr Durchlaucht geschrieben
daß er sich in dieser Sache mit dem van des Lahr vergleichen solle, daß er
dem von der Lahr Ein tausend 4t baar Geld, und 350 Centner Eysen a 6 Rthl
bis Ende Aprill abgeliefert bieten solle.
Ich hoffe der Weiterer wird die offerte annehmen, ich schreibe desfals Nahmens Erer
Durchlaucht an den von der Lahr selbst.
Die 1000 Rthl will ich aufbringen und abführen, damit es Ewer Durchlaucht nicht zu schwer
fält. Höchſt dieſelben haben aber dan die Gnade und ſchicken mir 950 Leutner und
wann es sein kan 1000 Centz vor voll bis Gegen den 20ten Aprill hierher nach Kontop
ich beſorge gleich alles von hier nach Berlin, ſchicke an Michaelis die 600 Centp und

und man kan mit Muthe wie der beigebogene Bericht zeigt ein neues Leben und

stelligen, wie ein Vogel auf dem Dache und jezt stete Einkerkerung von dem

Ich hoffe indes zu Gott, daß Ewr Durchlaucht meine Bitte gewähren auch diesen in
höchster Noth gefaßten Entschluß approbiren wird, auch daß der van der Lahr
das Nahmens Ewr Durchlaucht gethane Anerbiete, annehmen wird.
Ich schicke daher dies Schreiben, da es durchlaucht in Ansehung Ihrer Pohlinischen Be¬
sitzungen einige Ruhe gewähren wird, und zugleich die dringende Bitte wegen
Abhelfung meiner Leiden in sich faßt, zu Expressen; haben höchst dieselben die

aber die beruhigende Nachricht, ob die 950 oder 1000 Centner Staab und reifen

damit ich mich nicht in den Wäldern verkriachen, sondern frey und muthvoll handeln

Französischen Gen Gouvernement für mich angewiesene Summa aus die Zago
romis Güter zurück und den sind die 1000 Leute Eysen wieder bezahlt - ich
hingegen habe schon viel verlohren, arrest und Millionen Ärger um die Geschichte
dulden müßen und bin in der damaligen Carriere dergestalt zurück gesezt daß
ich nie mehr redreisiren kan.
Der General v Zastrow, der sich dermalen hier auf seine Güter aufhält
empfiehlt sich Ewr Durchlaucht zu Gnaden. Er ist gewis Ewr durchlaucht
treußer Verehrer. Er in Ihrer Sache als redlicher Mann behauptet, daß
Sie dem N. bey Jena würklich geschlagen haben, nur daß Sie auf hohen Befehl den
Gen. B. verboten mit der Caval. vorzugehen, und alle dadurch sey alles vor
lohrn gegangen.
Schade nur daß dieser ehrliche Mann für diese Wahrheit im steten Scat wird
liegen müßen.
Wann ich Ewr Durchlaucht Nachricht erhalte um hier alles darnach orden
zu können, gehn ich gleich nach Wien und natürlich lege ich mich dem Hochstdieselben
im Schlawenschitz persönlich zu Füßen.
Kontop

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports, enclosing Privy Councillor Amelang's letter, a new Cabinet order compelling payment in cash Courant even where value was lent in paper, causing a 1500 Rthl loss in the van der Lahr affair; with a sequestrator in his Kontop estates and arrest threatened over the outstanding 3000 Rthl, Triebenfeld has offered van der Lahr 1000 Rthl plus 350 hundredweight of iron at 6 Rthl, and begs 950-1000 hundredweight delivered to his Royadler loading-place by 20 April, noting 163 Rthl Zagorowo legal costs and General von Zastrow's defence of the Prince's conduct at Jena; with a draft letter to van der Lahr.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most humble Pro Memoria. From the enclosed letter of Privy Councillor Amelang Your Most Serene Highness will deign to see that yet again a new Cabinet order has been given, that now, entirely contrary to the provincial law and the old statutes, one must absolutely pay in Courant even where the value was given only in paper.

This blow falls hard and occasions a loss of 1500 Rthl in the van der Lahr matter. Payment must now, under the aforesaid Cabinet order | which was given solely in favour of the Jews and usurers, absolutely be made in cash Courant. But where is it to be got from? I am brought low and cannot help myself; for even now a sequestrator sits in my Kontop estates - I must stand at a distance, and may not approach my own hearth; indeed, I risk every day being taken up on account of the 3000 Rthl and arrested anew by van der Lahr, which disgrace I fear more than death. To forestall misfortune in this distress I have today written to Amelang in the name [uncertain: Nachmäus] of Your Serene Highness, that he should come to terms with van der Lahr in this matter, that he should offer van der Lahr one thousand [uncertain: 4t] in cash and 350 hundredweight of iron at 6 Rthl to be delivered by the end of April.

I hope the usurer will accept the offer; I am writing on that account in the name of Your Serene Highness to van der Lahr himself.

The 1000 Rthl I will raise and remit, so that it does not fall too heavily upon Your Serene Highness. But may the same Most High then have the grace to send me 950 hundredweight, and if it can be, 1000 hundredweight in full, to arrive here at Kontop by about the 20th of April; I will see to everything at once from here to Berlin, send Michaelis the 600 hundredweight and van der Lahr as much as falls due to him. For the costs still amount to a great deal, and I have no small grounds for saying so, as now the Zagorowo people, who for the drawing up of all the powers of attorney, white [illegible] costs of Hawich, Hecker and of the justice-burgomaster Schnee of Neufeln with his actuary, have amounted to 163 Rthl in cash Courant. Let Your Serene Highness meanwhile send me the 1000 hundredweight of iron, and I shall bring everything into order, and one can with courage, as the enclosed report shows, begin a new life and course of conduct; but on the contrary, if Your [Serene Highness] cannot accomplish this, I must live like a bird on the roof and now fear constant imprisonment from

van der Lahr, and thereafter from the 1st of May onward also from Michaelis. I hope meanwhile to God that Your Serene Highness will grant my request and also approve this resolution taken in utmost distress, and likewise that van der Lahr will accept the offer made in the name of Your Serene Highness. I therefore send this letter, since in respect of your Polish possessions it will afford some peace of mind, and at the same time contains the urgent request for the relief of my sufferings, by express; may the same Most High have the singular grace to send me by the messenger your consent, but above all the reassuring news whether the 950 or 1000 hundredweight of bar and hoop iron can be here at my Royadler loading-place by the 20th of April, so that I need not creep away into the woods, but may act freely and with courage. Your Serene Highness loses little thereby, for you will indeed receive back the sum assigned to me by the French Government-General out of the Zagorowo estates, and then the 1000 hundredweight of iron is paid for again - whereas I have already lost much, have had to endure arrest and a million vexations over the business, and in my then career am so far set back that I can never make it good again.

General von Zastrow, who is at present staying here on his estates, commends himself to the favour of Your Serene Highness. He is assuredly a most faithful admirer of Your Serene Highness. He maintains in your cause, as an honest man, that you really did beat N. at Jena, only that by high command you were forbidden to let General B. advance with the cavalry, and that by all this everything was lost.

It is only a pity that this honest man will have to lie in perpetual [uncertain: Scat] for this truth.

When I receive news from Your Serene Highness so as to be able to order everything here accordingly, I shall go at once to Vienna, and naturally I lay myself personally at the feet of the same Most High at Schlawenschitz.

Kontop

the 7th of March 1809 von Triebenfeld

Your Right Honourable in Berlin

Right Honourable Sir,

Highly esteemed Mr Banker, Your Right Honourable has had the kindness, in my affairs, to lend the Councillor von Triebenfeld, against a bill of exchange made out to you by him and by General and M. von Zastrow, 5260 Rthl at par, which are promised to be repaid in Courant. Mr von Triebenfeld has already repaid you thereon 2260 Rthl on the capital and 153 Rthl in interest, and there are still 3000 Rthl and the interest thereon from July to be settled. It would run quite contrary to my manner of gratitude if I were to allow that Mr [uncertain: et Freib.] should suffer or have any unpleasantness on account of this matter. I therefore request you in friendship to release Mr von Triebenfeld from the obligation (ex nexu), soon to quash the suit against him, and to accept payment of the debt from me. I offer to pay you at once 1000 Rthl and the remainder by the end of April; and if you will let my request take full effect, then take from me (since cash Courant is very hard to come by) 350 hundredweight of good bar and white iron at 6 Rthl, which I most solemnly assure you I will send you at once from my ironworks. Privy Councillor Amelang is authorised by me to [uncertain: forward] this matter with you and to conclude it with you, and to give me the speediest news of it, so that I may then at once send in the 1000 Rthl and have the iron packed and forwarded by water. I shall regard your consent to this my request as a proof of your friendship, and for it shall always be, with true high esteem,

as in the blank form to His Your

Schlawenschitz, the 7th of March
