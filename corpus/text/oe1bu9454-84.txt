--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Zum Krotoszyn-Tausch berichtet v. Triebenfeld: Königl. Majestät hat Voss mit der Regelung beauftragt, der 4 Wochen für den Finalbericht erbat. Die Kammern sollen prüfen, ob die Revenuen gleichkommen. Wegen der Bedenken um Polajewo sollen aus der Starostei Adelnau, die an Krotoszyn grenzt, Güter im Wert des Mehrertrags gegeben werden. Er reist nach Pszczew, will wegen Broniewski sehen und in Wrocław die Sache des Prinzen George erledigen. Alle außer Voss seien wohlgesinnt.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
On the Krotoszyn exchange, von Triebenfeld reports that Your Royal Majesty has charged Voss with settling the matter, and that Voss asked for 4 weeks to produce the final report. The chambers are to examine whether the revenues are equal. Because of the doubts about Polajewo, estates to the value of the surplus yield are to be given from the district of Adelnau, which borders on Krotoszyn. He is travelling to Pszczew, intends to look into the Broniewski business, and will settle Prince George's affair in Wrocław. He says all but Voss are well disposed.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
84

Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Lord. Since His Majesty the King has ordered His Excellency Mr von Voss to arrange the Krotoszyn affair, the same has asked for 4 weeks, as the time within which the final report is to be submitted. The chambers are to investigate everything, in what manner the revenues come out equal. My plans are now already drafted, and at this very hour I am going from here to Pszczew; I shall also see what is to be done with Broniewski — for our revenues must absolutely remain as high, and since people are being so [uncertain: vare] about Polajewo, Your Serene Highness ought to be given from the Starostey of Adelnau, which borders hard upon Krotoszyn, as many estates as the higher value of our revenues amounts to — and hereby

more is gained than with Polajewo — for there too one must indeed reckon the matter at 4 per cent. — God grant that all goes well, indeed very well. Prince George will be in Wrocław next week — I mean to settle both his affair and the investigations concerning Your Serene Highness's estates during that time, so as to be back here at the end of the 4 weeks in question.

From Colonel von Köckritz a letter will just have come in — all mean it well, and apart from

Mr von Voss everyone is ready to anticipate Your Serene Highness's wishes — Mr Voss is governed by the councillor of war Nöldichen. From Russia strange news has come in here. — Everything there is in confusion because the new mistress rules the Emperor quite unrestrictedly — Repnin is said to be arrested; the Kurra[uncertain: h/k]ins are all gone; Zubow has had to come to Petersburg in haste, and Panie is said also to have asked for his discharge — the Empress, ill-used by Mr Paul, is shut up for 3 days; the

Grand Duke has lamented greatly and wept like a child — Siege, it is said, will depart, and only the secretary remain here — It looks strange — yet people believe everything will remain in statu quo. In 5 days I shall have the good fortune to lay myself personally at Your Serene Highness's feet. In deepest reverence I remain, until death, Your Most Serene Highness's. Berlin

the 20th of October.

1798.

faithful most humble and obedient servant von Triebenfeld
