--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports rumours that Austria, Russia and Prussia have settled the Saxon and Polish question, and pleads, having heard nothing since 13 January, that Triebenfeld press Prince Hardenberg before the Congress closes. Asks only for an advance of 112000 Rthl and immediate administration of the entailed estates, since the attorney Kurz will surrender the receipted debt instrument for 12000 Rthl cash plus an instruction on the hundred thousand, ending Cosmar's execution; also urges recovery of Trąbczyn and Kaemen from Mrs. Moszynska.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Right Honourable Sir,

Highly Honoured Sir, Councillor of War and Forests!

A few hours ago I received from a trusted friend the news that Austria, [uncertain: Russia] and Prussia have come to agreement concerning Saxony and Poland, and that the matter may now be very nearly concluded.

Since the 13th of January I have had no news from you; I live in great anxiety on that account. — After the several assurances on the part of His Serene Highness the Prince State Chancellor, I might count with the highest confidence upon rescue. But if the Congress is brought to an end without anything having been done for me, then I am irretrievably lost, and must perish in the greatest distress. Therefore exert everything you can, and lay my distress[?] before the heart of Prince Hardenberg, who has after all always been my friend. The report of the Brieg High Provincial Court was already sent in to His Serene Highness the Prince Chancellor under the 14th of last month — it is favourable to me and proves everything that has been said by me upon the matter. The Prince can now help me with every right.

I ask for nothing further — take this well to heart — than that His Serene Highness the Prince grant the advance of 112000 Rthl, and in return have all the entailed estates taken into administration at once. Then Cosmar's power is at an end. Only the day before yesterday the attorney Kurz gave this assurance, that as soon as he receives the 12000 Rthl in cash and a secure instruction for payment upon the hundred thousand Rthl, he will immediately deliver up the debt instrument receipted, and this is indeed all that is wanted if the entailed estates are once brought under [uncertain: assessment]. If the Prince grants only that, then I am already saved; the estates are of such consequence that the King will soon have the advance back again, each receives what is his, and I need no longer [uncertain: languish]. As regards Cosmar, the matter then settles itself; he withdraws at once from the entailed estates, his execution ceases, he must immediately render account and deposit in court all sums he holds behind him, for to that effect the report of the Brieg High Provincial Court points, and the matter rests upon law and equity. In substance you need only urge these things incidentally, since here everything must follow of itself. Only beg His Serene Highness for the 112000 Rthl, that he may take the trouble of this advance; then I am clear with the King and with the [uncertain: Curl] heirs, and am saved out of the claws of Cosmar.

Next I beg that the Trąbczyn and Kaemen matter now be taken up further. The King granted me the said estates by the instrument which you have there; I possessed the estates in peace for over 12 years, and the Poles took the same away by their own authority only 14 weeks after the Peace of Tilsit. Here now the Emperor would have to command that the estates be restored to me and that the revenues drawn from them be reimbursed by Mrs. Moszynska. Further I can add nothing more today, but only assure you, as ever, of my grateful friendship and high esteem, as Your Right Honourable

Schlawenschitz, the 29th of January

1815
