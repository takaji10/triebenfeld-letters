--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Eingabe an Staats Kanzler Hardenberg für den notleidenden Fürsten Hohenlohe, teils unleserlich. Medizinalrat Cosmar beherrsche als Testamentsvollstrecker der Fürstin Sacken die Majoratsgüter Sławięcice, Lasowice und Oppurg (79500 Rthl Ertrag). Er stütze sich auf einen Transact von 1811, zahle nichts und lege keine Rechnung. Die Curischen Erben wollen für 426000 Rthl 112000 nehmen. Erbeten werden ein Vorschuss von 112000 Rthl, Aufhebung des Transacts, Absetzung Cosmars und ein Bericht des Oberlandesgerichts Brzeg. Triebenfeld bietet 60000 Rthl Kaution.

Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord, I am to lay before Your Most Serene Highness the enclosed letter of the Prince of Hohenlohe most [text lost], and at the same time to beg that Most High Person for the most gracious permission to depict to Your Most Serene Highness the most pressing distress of the Prince, and therewith to lay at Your Serene Highness's feet a plan for his rescue and for the relief of his sufferings.

From of old the Prince of Ingelfingen was Your Most Serene Highness's most faithful adherent and admirer; this man, who has been brought to misfortune through no fault of his own but merely through his yielding to the influence of others upon his benevolent disposition, throws himself into Your Serene Highness's arms; he begs to be protected from the chicanery of the medical councillor Cosmar, which aims at nothing else than that the good Prince shall go hungry in his old age and perish in misery.

The Sławięcice, Lasowice and Oppurg estates amount in value to a fortune of more than two million Rthl, which, according to the instrument appended in copy under A, were confirmed as an entailed estate by His Majesty in 1799, and which today, in the most straitened of times, still yield 79500 Rthl of income, according to the statement appended under B. Of these considerable estates the Prince, as lord of the entailed estate, is to enjoy the revenues. But by the most inconceivable artifices the medical councillor Cosmar has contrived [uncertain: sumose] to install himself in the entailed estates as though in the bequest of the deceased Princess's affairs, so that he commands everything as though it were his own property, draws in all the revenues, and gives of it to a servant in name. He calls himself executor of the testament — but what could the Princess Sacken dispose of by way of death? She was of these estates, and that only in part, merely holder of a life interest. The Act A, sanctioned by His Majesty, determines everything that belongs to the aforesaid Princess, now married von Sacken, all the estates — after her [uncertain: marriage] she ceded her whole inheritance faithfully to her [text lost] wedded lord, the Prince of Hohenlohe, and the Prince founded out of it the trust entail which His Majesty [uncertain: Sancteousky]; so could the Princess Sacken also appoint an executor of the testament; over her ready cash bequest she might well make testament, but since she has for the greatest part bequeathed everything to her favourite, Prince Adolph, there vanishes every legal ground upon which she, and far less her [uncertain: authorised representative] Cosmar, might meddle in the affairs of the entailed estates; but in order to force his way into these affairs notwithstanding the legal barriers standing against it, to dispose at his own will, and to command the incomes without accountability, as though over his own property, the medical councillor Cosmar has undertaken the most audacious venture of a determined pettifogger. He threw in the far-reaching settlement here enclosed under C at a time when the Princess von Sacken, if indeed she was still living, could not possibly have surveyed the content and the consequences, since she lay at her last gasp, and in that [uncertain: pitiable] case was already so weakened in her vital powers that she signed the far-reaching document, instead of with her signature, only with 3 crosses. This settlement is the sum of all intrigues.
