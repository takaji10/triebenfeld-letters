--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Petition to Prince State Chancellor v. Hardenberg on the Fürst zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen's distress: the entailed Schlawenschitz, Lassowitz and Oppurg estates, confirmed as Fideicommiss in 1799 and worth over two million Rthl with 79500 revenue, are controlled by Cosmar as testamentary executor of the late Princess Sacken, who withholds accounts and over 43000 Rthl. Triebenfeld seeks annulment of the 1811 settlement, Cosmar's exclusion, an honest administrator, a report from the Brieg Upper Provincial Court, and a 112000 Rthl advance to settle the Lutz heirs' 426000 Rthl claim, offering 60000 security.
