--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Appeal to State Chancellor von Hardenberg on the Prince's behalf, denouncing the testamentary executor Cosmar for seizing control of the Schlawenschitz, Lassowitz and Oppurg entailed estates (worth over two million Rthl, yielding 79500 Rthl yearly), installing his under-age son as administrator, rendering no account, withholding over 43000 Rthl, and procuring the Princess von Sacken's settlement of 1811, signed with three crosses in her last illness, contrary to the entail confirmed in 1799. Requests an advance of 112000 Rthl to settle the Lutz heirs' claim of 426000 Rthl, annulment of the settlement, Cosmar's removal, and a report from the Brieg Higher Provincial Court; encloses documents A–E.
