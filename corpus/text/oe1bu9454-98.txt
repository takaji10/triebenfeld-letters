So quaal voll mich der hiesige Aufenthalt nach Gerade auch
immer wird, so will ich doch schon aushalten bis ich die Quest:

Glenck aber ſchicke ich eiligſt ab damit Ewer Hochfürſtliche
Durchlaucht jemanden haben dem Höchſt Sie dorten alle
Befehle erteilen können.
Wie hart hier der Kampf gewesen, wird Ewr Durchlaucht
der Glenck schildern; Gutes werden die Früchte des Sieges
desto Wannvoller und [C?]abewesen.
daß es der König Köckritz und Zastrow mit Ewr Durchlaucht
gut meinen beweiset die Einladung — es macht
Höchſt Ihnen dies die Reiſe ein wenig Mühſahm, aber
Die Ehre ist desto größer, und wann Ewer Durchlaucht
dorten den 14ten oder höchstens 15ten Abreisen, so könne
Höchstdieselben sehr füglich 3 bis 4 Tage hier bleiben —
Der Monarch muß keine guten Gründe haben, daß
Er Ewr Durchlaucht Tour hier über Berlin gelegt
wißen will. Grundlich weis ich nichts — indes Be¬
es wohl mit um den Prinzen Solms ſind —
Dieser hatt sich schon vor 5 Monath in Geheim mit
der Wittwe Princess Louise verheirathet
Der König ist darüber ſehr entrüſtet und dies trägt
zu seiner Unpäßlichkeit viel bey die Königen aber
stält noch immer aus einer Gemacht in die andere —

Über des Minister Hoyms Benehmen mündlich ein mehreres

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Triebenfeld bleibt in Berlin, bis die Sache geklärt ist, und schickt Glenck zum Fürsten voraus. Der König lädt den Fürsten nach Berlin ein; reist er am 14. oder 15. ab, kann er 3 bis 4 Tage bleiben. Vertraulich berichtet er von der geheimen Heirat des Prinzen Solms mit der verwitweten Prinzessin Louise, von Hoyms geheimem Gutachten, auf das sich Voss beruft, und rät, das Secularisations Geschäft nicht zu erwähnen, sondern mit Prinz George in Wien zu betreiben.

98

Most Serene Prince,

Most gracious Lord!

However full of torment my stay here becomes by degrees, I will nevertheless hold out until I see the Quest: matter set in order — but I am sending Glenck off with all haste, so that Your Most Serene Highness may have someone there to whom You may most highly give all Your commands.

How hard the struggle has been here, Glenck will describe to Your Serene Highness; good things will be the fruits of the victory, the more [uncertain: Wannvoller und Cabewesen].

That the King, Köckritz and Zastrow mean well by Your Serene Highness is proved by the invitation — it makes the journey a little toilsome for You, but the honour is the greater for it, and if Your Serene Highness departs from there on the 14th, or at the latest the 15th, then Highest the same may

most conveniently remain here 3 to 4 days — The Monarch must have no good grounds for wishing Your Serene Highness's route to be laid here by way of Berlin. I know nothing thoroughly — meanwhile [uncertain: Bees] are probably in it too, about Prince Solms — He married the widowed Princess Louise in secret five months ago already. The King is greatly incensed at it, and this contributes much to his indisposition; the Queen, however, keeps moving from one chamber to the other — Yesterday evening the Princess was stripped of the dignity of Royal Highness, her household was withdrawn, other arrangements made concerning the children, and now she is to depart in haste with her husband, who is being transferred as a major with the hussars to Ausbach — that this causes a mighty terror at court, Your Serene Highness can

readily imagine. And I believe it will be a relief to the Monarch if he can have You here for a couple of days.

The pregnancy of the [uncertain: Armez verwerten] Princess first gave the public much occasion for unspeakable [uncertain: Reiformements], and still no one knows the least thing about this matter which has actually been settled beforehand. Deign to say nothing about it there either.

Concerning Minister Hoym's conduct, more by word of mouth. On one occasion he spoke very well before the King in Köckritz's presence — but in secret, with his [illegible], he gave a contemptible opinion, upon which Mr von Voss for his part constantly relies. For that very reason I was glad that the King invited Your Serene Highness here so flatteringly — for then the B — will tremble again — because their conscience will torture them — Do not say anything to the Princess Sacken, Serene Highness, Your Serene Highness

nothing of it here, that I have disclosed to You the wicked tricks of Hoym and Wedell — And let nothing be noticed of it there either — As to the secularisation business, pray say nothing at all, Your Serene Highness — this is a mere farce — Prince George strongly advises against it — if anything can ever be done in the matter, we will do it in Vienna — I promise Your Serene Highness to work there with Prince George in its proper season just as here — for from our side nothing is being done — and if You can obtain something and enlarge Your realms, it is all one whether You obtain it from there or through action here — Settled it remains — if You have much to that end, the more compliments they will pay You.

Haugwitz is now playing a great part, but Schulemburg is being removed, because the latter always wants war. In deepest submission I remain, until death,

Your Most Serene Highness's

Berlin, the 9th of January

1799.

most humble and faithful servant

v. Triebenfeld.
