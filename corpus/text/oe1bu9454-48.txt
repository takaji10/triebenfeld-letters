--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports engaging justice-burgomaster Hawich of Zagorowo, newly appointed Ducal-Warsaw notary, with Chief Inspector Hertes, and executing thirteen special powers of attorney recorded by justice-burgomaster Schench of Neusatz: dismissal of the Dqbska over the Traperin estates; full lifting of the sequestration under the King of Saxony's three-year indult; collection and payment of interest; suit against Oppenheimer et Wolff in Kalisz over 72000 Rthl; removal and accounting of the sequestrator Honrichs and annulment of his land sales; arrears from v Niedzewiecki, the Olesnitz lessees (23000 Rthl) and the Radrimin heirs (over 10000 Rthl); boundary suits; and representation at the Warsaw Diet.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

I have had here the justice-burgomaster Hawich from Zagorowo, who is appointed as actual notary public of the Duchy of Warsaw, together with the Chief Inspector Hertes, and have negotiated with these men everything that tends to Your Serene Highness's advantage, and have issued the judicial special powers of attorney for that purpose. Accordingly I have, first, pressed for the dismissal of the Dqbska woman in the matter of the Trąbczyn estates, and have given power of attorney and instruction thereto.

Secondly, I have given the power of attorney for the complete lifting of the sequestration and have drafted the introductory application, the more so as a three-year general indult has been granted by the King of Saxony, and since in this matter everything depends upon absolute payment of the interest, I have thirdly given to the said Hawich the power of attorney to collect the interest and to pay the same, against receipt, to those creditors who already have legally binding judgments in their favour, but to remit the remainder to Your Serene Highness. Also I have fourthly given a power of attorney to bring suit against the Jew Oppenheimer et Wolff in Kalisz, and at once to press for the cancellation of the several sums entered amounting to over 72000 Rthl, in which matter a suit is already pending in Berlin. [uncertain: Fifthly] I have issued a power of attorney that the deceits of Honrichs, who had himself installed as sequestrator, be at once repudiated, and that he be compelled to render account ad Assam, that Hawich on the other hand should draw up the objections against him, and should compel him to make good the same as well as to pay the sums still outstanding from him. Likewise I have sixthly given a power of attorney to collect from von Niedzewiecki the [uncertain: outstanding] lease sum and to seek execution against him. Seventhly I have issued a power of attorney that all arbitrary transactions and

sales of the lands by Honrichs be at once annulled, and that the purchasers, if they do not submit to payment into the treasury, be evicted with recourse against Honrichs, because he, without being authorised, gave away part of the lands for nothing and appropriated to himself a little estate of several Hufe in that manner, and erected the buildings from the lordship's timber and bricks. Eighthly, I have given power of attorney for the collection of all arrears of purchase moneys, which are very considerable.

Ninthly, a power of attorney has been issued to compel the hereditary leaseholders of Olesnise to pay the entry moneys outstanding, inclusive of the canon, amounting to 23000 Rthl, or else to be evicted together with their dependents, so that the lands and buildings may be let out elsewhere. Also, tenthly, a power of attorney has been given to etc. Habesti for the prosecution of all boundary suits, and the necessary instructions for the same have been issued.

Eleventhly, that a power of attorney has been given to Hawich, that he may appear in the name of Your Serene Highness at the Imperial Diet at Warsaw and in all places, in order to maintain Your Most Exalted Highness's rights of possession and of property in their full force, the more so as every proprietor of an estate is now to appear in person and reside upon his estates, or else forfeit his possessions.

Twelfthly, besides this I have given to the said Hawich a power of attorney on my own part, together with the original decrees of the French General Government, in order to collect the [uncertain: hanttene] interest advanced by me to the Invalids' Fund, together with costs etc., and to remit the same to Your [uncertain: Kirchlauch] for the covering of the Lahr sum. Thirteenthly and finally, a further power of attorney has been drawn up for Hawich, in order to collect from the Radriminisch heirs the still outstanding hereditary-purchase moneys and the canon of several years, which amount to over 10000 Rthl, and to refute the unjust claims formerly drawn up by the deceased.

I have had all this taken down judicially by the justice-burgomaster Schench of Neusatz, in order to begin this new career right brilliantly; and since by virtue of the bond of 25000 Rthl I have a right before all creditors to have a word to say, I think this will indeed carry weight and bring about the most desired outcome. In the meantime, however, Your Serene Highness shall, according to my arrangement, faithfully receive the reports upon the progress of the matter from time to time. In deepest reverence I remain, until death,

Your Most Serene Highness's

most faithful and obedient servant

Kontop

the 7th of March 1809.

von Triebenfeld
