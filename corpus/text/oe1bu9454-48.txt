--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports a meeting with justice-burgomaster Hawich of Zagorowo, now a notary public of the Duchy of Warsaw, and Chief Inspector Hertes, at which thirteen special powers of attorney were made out: to lift the sequestration, collect and distribute interest to creditors holding judgments, sue Oppenheimer and Wolff at Kalisz over entries exceeding 72,000 Rthl, force the sequestrator Honrichs to account and undo his land sales, recover 23,000 Rthl from the Olesnica hereditary leaseholders, pursue boundary suits, and represent the Prince at the Warsaw Diet.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

I have had here with me the justice-burgomaster Hawich from Zagorowo, who is appointed as actual Notarius publicus of the Duchy of Warsaw, together with the Chief Inspector Hertes, and have negotiated with these men everything that tends to Your Serene Highness's advantage, and have had the judicial special powers of attorney made out for that purpose. Accordingly I have, first, pressed for the dismissal of the Dqbska woman in the matter of the Traperin estates, and have given power of attorney and instruction to that end.

Secondly, I have given the power of attorney for the complete lifting of the sequestration and drafted the introductory step, all the more since a three-year general indult has been granted by the King of Saxony, and since everything here turns upon absolute payment of the interest; therefore I have, thirdly, given the said Hawich the power of attorney to collect the interest, and to pay it against receipt to those creditors who already have legally binding judgments in their favour, but to remit the remainder to Your Serene Highness. I have also, fourthly, given a power of attorney to bring suit against the Jew Oppenheimer et Wolff in Kalisz, and at once to press for the cancellation of the several sums entered, amounting to over 72000 Rthl, in which matter a suit is already pending in Berlin as well. Fifthly, I have had a power of attorney made out that the deceits of Honrichs, who had himself installed as sequestrator, be at once repudiated, and that he be compelled to render account ad Assam, that Hawich in turn draw up the objections against him, and compel him to make restitution of the same, as well as to pay the sums still outstanding from him. Likewise I have, sixthly, given a power of attorney to collect from von Niedzewiecki the [uncertain: Usternde] lease sum and to seek execution against him. Seventhly, I have had a power of attorney made out that all high-handed dealings and

sales of the lands by Honrichs be at once annulled, and that the purchasers, if they do not submit to payment into the treasury, be evicted with recourse against Honrichs, since he, without being authorised, gave away the lands in part for nothing and appropriated to himself a little estate of several Hufen in that manner, and erected the buildings out of the lordship's timber and bricks. Eighthly, I have given power of attorney for the collection of all arrears of purchase money, which are very considerable.

Ninthly, a power of attorney has been made out to compel the Olesnica hereditary leaseholders to pay the entry monies outstanding, inclusive of the canon, amounting to 23000 Rthl, or else to be evicted with their dependents, so that the lands and buildings may be otherwise disposed of. Also, tenthly, a power of attorney has been given to etc. Habesti for the prosecution of all boundary suits, and the instructions necessary thereto have been issued.

Eleventhly, that a power of attorney has been given to Hawich, so that he may appear in Your Serene Highness's name at the Diet at Warsaw and in all places, in order to maintain Your Most Serene Highness's rights of possession and property in their due force, all the more since every estate owner must now himself appear, reside upon his estates, or else forfeit his possessions.

Twelfthly, besides this I have given the said Hawich a power of attorney on my own part, together with the original decrees of the French General Government, in order to collect the [uncertain: hanttene] interest advanced by me to the Invalids' Fund, together with costs etc., and to remit the same to Your [uncertain: Kirchlauch] for the covering of the Lahr sum. Thirteenthly and finally, yet another power of attorney has been issued to Hawich, to collect from the Radrimini heirs the still outstanding hereditary purchase monies and the canon of several years, which amount to over 10000 Rthl, and to refute the unjust claims formerly drawn up by the deceased.

I have had all this recorded judicially by the justice-burgomaster Schench from Neusatz, in order to begin this new career right brilliantly; and since, by virtue of the bond of 25000 Rthl, I have a right before all creditors to have a word in the matter, I think this will surely make an impression and bring about the most desired outcome. In the meantime, however, Your Serene Highness shall, by my arrangement, faithfully receive from time to time the reports on the progress of the affair. In deepest reverence I remain, until death,

Your Most Serene Highness's

faithful and most obedient servant

Kontop

the 7th of March 1809.

v Triebenfeld
