--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A royal grant by Friedrich Wilhelm adding to an earlier grant of 19 June 1797 the appurtenances of the Betsche, Zagórów and Wienietz estates: the settlements of Stołuń, Kuligowo, Szarcz, the bailiwick of Zielomyśl and Silna with their Hauländer holdings, the Pomher Hauländer holding and Heydemühle, the hamlet of Swięcin, and Konty, Szedlemin, Botkowo and Karczyn near Wienietz, in full hereditary property against a fixed canon and the share due to church foundations. Filed with it are a certified copy and a mortgage certificate entering the Prince's title to the lordship of Pszczew or Betsche in the mortgage book.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
We Friedrich Wilhelm, by the Grace of God King of Prussia etc., do hereby make known and declare, by virtue of this Our open letter, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors in the Crown, the Electorate and Our remaining lands, that out of the particular friendship and affection We bear towards the High-born Prince, Our amicably beloved uncle, Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, and likewise in order to give him a fresh proof of Our Royal gratitude for the distinguished and

profitable services rendered to Us and Our Royal House, We have found Ourselves moved to make over, grant and convey to His Lordship, by inheritance and in full property, the appurtenances belonging to the estates of Betsche, Zagórów and Wienietz already conferred upon him by the instrument of the 19th of June of this year, namely

1.) at Betsche, the settlements of Stołuń, Kuligowo, Szarcz, the bailiwick of Zielomyśl and Silna, together with the Hollander holdings

the Pomher Hollander holding and Heydemühle;

2.) at Zagórów the hamlet of Swięcin; and finally

3. near Wienietz, Konty, Szedlemin, Botkowo and Karczyn, against payment

1.) of the territorial-lordly dues hitherto rendered from the said settlements, Hollander holdings and parcels of ground, as a fixed and unalterable canon, and

2.) of the portion due therefrom to the spiritual corporations and foundations,

by inheritance and in full property.

We do so likewise hereby and by virtue of this present instrument, out of Royal power and plenitude, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors in the Crown, the Electorate and Our remaining lands, in such manner and to this effect: that Our above-mentioned amicably beloved uncle, the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, shall from henceforth and for all time have, hold and enjoy for himself and his

the above-named appurtenances belonging to the estates of Betsche, Zagórów and Wienietz, with all rights and privileges attaching thereto, as these have hitherto been exercised or may lawfully be exercised, with all lands thereto belonging, in fields, woods and meadows, and likewise in ponds and waters, according to the lawful boundaries and margins, and as everything has hitherto been used or may of right be used, from henceforth and for all time, for himself and his

descendants and heirs, as a true, well-acquired and unrestricted property, irrevocably from Us and Our descendants and successors, and possess and use the same; and that there shall be vested in him the power and authority to dispose thereof at his pleasure under the above-mentioned conditions, without hindrance from any man. We therefore command and enjoin all Our Supreme Provincial Boards, as also all other authorities

whom this concerns, herewith as graciously as earnestly, that they in no wise hinder or trouble either the Lordship of the oft-mentioned Lord Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen in the hereditary and proprietary possession thus granted to him of the above-named appurtenances belonging to the estates of Betsche, Zagórów and Wienietz, nor him or those to whom he may one day, by virtue of this Our letter of grant,

bequeath the same, or otherwise lawfully transfer it by other acts among the living or on account of death; but rather let His Lordship, and eventually also them, enjoy the same freely and undisturbed; that they themselves do nothing against it, nor occasion or permit such to be done by others, on pain of Our Most High displeasure and of unfailing

severe punishment. In witness whereof, under Our own most high hand and the appended Royal seal. Thus done and given in Our residence city of Berlin, the first of December after the birth of Christ Our Lord, in the one thousand seven hundred and ninety-seventh year, and of Our Royal reign in the first year.

Friedrich Wilhelm

Hoym. Reck.
