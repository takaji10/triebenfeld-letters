I. N. Mikołaj Kicki archidiakon gnieźnieński poświadcza przekazanie kościoła parafialnego w Zagórowie klasztorowi cystersów w Lądzie.
Or.: Warszawa, AGAD, dok. perg. 1930. Pergamin 290×422 - 409+39 mm. Z. N.: Na 3 gradusach krzyż wpisany w czworokąt, o ramionach zakończonych liliami wystającymi poza czworokąt. Na skrzyżowaniu ramion czteropłatkowa rozetka. U podstawy krzyża litera m. Na paskach pergaminowych 3 pieczęcie w żółtym wosku: 1. okrągła, o średnicy ok. 23 mm, z herbem Godziemba, napis nieczytelny (Mikołaja Kickiego); 2. w mandorlę, szerokości ok. 37 mm, z wyobrażeniem postaci stojącej pod baldachimem (?), napis nieczytelny, częściowo wykruszony (opata Gerarda?); 3. okrągła, o średnicy ok. 50 mm, z wyobrażeniem. Tronującej Madonny, napis nieczytelny (klasztoru lędzkiego). Na odwrocie informacje o treści i sygnatury (XV - XVIII w.).

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Notariatsinstrument: Mikołaj Kicki, Archidiakon von Gnesen, beurkundet, dass Paweł, Pfarrer von Zagórów, die Pfarrkirche von Zagórów, die der Papst dem Kloster Ląd inkorporiert hatte, dem Abt Gerard und dem Konvent abtritt; dafür nimmt das Kloster ihn auf Lebenszeit mit Kost, Kleidung und einem Diener auf. Ląd, 13. Juni 1414. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski VII, Nr. 733, nach dem Original. Ein Przybysław, Kirchendiener von Zagórów, ist Zeuge.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Notarial instrument: Mikołaj Kicki, archdeacon of Gniezno, attests that Paweł, rector of Zagórów, gives up the parish church of Zagórów, which the Pope had incorporated into the abbey of Ląd, to Abbot Gerard and the convent; in return the abbey takes him in for life with food, clothing and a servant. Ląd, 13 June 1414. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski VII, no. 733, from the original. A Przybysław, servant of the church of Zagórów, is a witness.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 733

Ląd, 13 June 1,414

Notarial instrument. Mikołaj Kicki, archdeacon of Gniezno, attests the handing over of the parish church at Zagórów to the Cistercian monastery at Ląd.

Original: Warsaw, AGAD, parchment document 1930. Parchment 290 × 422–409 + 39 mm. Notary's sign: on 3 steps a cross set in a square, its arms ending in lilies that stand out beyond the square. At the crossing of the arms a four-petalled rosette. At the foot of the cross the letter m. On parchment strips 3 seals in yellow wax: 1. round, about 23 mm across, with the Godziemba arms, legend illegible (of Mikołaj Kicki); 2. pointed oval, about 37 mm wide, with the image of a standing figure under a canopy (?), legend illegible, partly crumbled (of Abbot Gerard?); 3. round, about 50 mm across, with the image of the enthroned Madonna, legend illegible (of the monastery of Ląd). On the back notes on the content and shelfmarks (15th–18th century).

ᵃIn the name of the Lord, amen. We, Mikołaj, archdeacon and canon of the holy church of Gniezno¹, make | known by the tenor of these presents to all that, whereas the venerable and religious men, the brothers Lord Gerard, abbotᵇ, and the convent | of the monastery of Ląd of the Cistercian Order of the diocese of Gniezno² had procured that the parish church in Zagórów of the said diocese³ | be incorporated by the Apostolic See into the said monastery, for bearing the burdens of the hospitality of the said monastery, so that whenever that church fell vacant by the resignation or death of its rector for the time being, the lord abbot with his convent might freely take and lawfully keep bodily possession of that church, without asking the leave of the diocesan of the place or of anyone, and turn its fruits, revenues and incomes to the use of the said monastery, and have the church governed by a fit professed brother of that monastery, removable at the willᶜ of the lord abbot and convent whenever it seems good to them, as is set out more fully in the apostolic letters of the late Gregory the Twelfth⁴ and in the process of the executor appointed for this; in consideration therefore of this hospitality and these burdens the discreet man Lord Paweł, rector of the said church in Zagórów⁵, having special love for the said monastery and wishing that the said grace of incorporation should have its due effect, has of his own will and freely, not compelled nor led astray by fraud, given up and ceded the same church and its possession to the same lord abbot and convent, keeping for himself suitable food and clothing for the rest of his life. And the said Lord Gerard, abbot, with his convent, wishing to repay such great love to the same Lord Paweł with a benefit, has graciously taken the same Lord Paweł into the house of the said monastery for his fitting support. For his food he has assigned him the abbot's table, and for his clothing at each of the Ember seasons through the year a mark and a half, and also one tunic of the cloth usually given to the brothers of the convent, of eight ells, and also a fur, if he needs it, of lambskins, and also four pairs of shoes each year for the rest of his life, all to be faithfully supplied to the same Lord Paweł by the same Lord Gerard, abbot, and his successors and the convent; the same lord abbot shall also be bound to keep at his own cost a servant needed for the service of the said Lord Paweł, whose wages the same Lord Paweł will provide. And the same Lord Paweł has assigned and bequeathed to the said monastery, for the remedy of his soul, all and each of the foregoing things left after his death, in whatever goods or sums of money they may be. And we, Gerard, the said abbot of Ląd, with the brothers of our convent, promise in good faith to hold and keep all the foregoing expressed above to the same Lord Paweł, and to keep them we bind ourselves and our successors in good faith; and if, which God forbid, the foregoing should be broken by us or our successors, and the said Lord Paweł not be provided with the said food and clothing according to the needs of a priest's state, as is expressed above, then, lest the same Lord Paweł be seen to beg and suffer want to the shame and disgrace of the honour of the clergy, it shall be lawful for him to have recourse to taking the fruits, revenues and incomes of the same church and maintaining himself from them, in which fruits he is not to be disturbed in any way by us or our successors. And if he should be disturbed, then of our own will and freely we subject ourselves and our successors in this respect to the jurisdiction and coercion of the said Lord Mikołaj, archdeacon, and his successors, and we take upon ourselves the sentences of excommunication and interdict, in so far as he shall have pronounced them against us and the said monastery and the church of the monastery for the foregoing, and promise to obey them and keep the church interdict, renouncing in the foregoing the privileges of exemption of the said monastery and order granted by any Roman pontiffs or other ordinaries, and also the exceptions and appeals and the help of the laws and canons that belong or might in any way belong to us and our said successors, by which the said submission and coercion to be done by the said lord archdeacon might in any way be hindered, which we wish to avail us and our successors in nothing. In faith and testimony of all and each of the foregoing we have ordered these present letters or this present public instrument to be written and published by the notary written below and to be strengthened by the hanging of our seals and those of the said convent. Done and given in the monastery of Ląd in the year of the Lord one thousand four hundred and fourteen, in the seventh indiction, in the fifth year of the pontificate of the most holy father and our lord in Christ, Lord John the Twenty-third, Pope by divine providence, on Wednesday the thirteenth day of the month of June, at about the sixth hour, there being present the religious men, the lords: Jan, prior⁶, Feliks, rector of the parish church in Ląd⁷, Goblin, bursar⁸, Syfryd, cellarer⁹, and Gerard, brothers¹⁰ of the said convent of Ląd, and also the noble and discreet Trojan and Marcin, heirs of Kiki¹¹, and Przybysław, servant of the church of Zagórów¹², witnesses called and asked specially to the foregoing.

Notary's sign. And I, Maciej, son of the late Jan Linko, cleric of Gniezno, public notary by imperial authority¹³, since I was present together with the witnesses named above at the said giving up, promise and ordinance and at all and each of the foregoing while they were being done and made as is set out above, and saw and heard them done so, therefore, writing with my own hand, I have subscribed here and brought this into this public form, signing it with my usual and accustomed sign and name, asked and requested, in faith and testimony of all and each of the foregoing.
