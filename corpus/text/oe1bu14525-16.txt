--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A royal confirmation, issued under the South Prussian Government's seal at Posen, of the handing over of the lordship of Pszczew or Betsche in the district of Posen to Friedrich Ludwig, Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, as heritable property. The Treasury's fiscal officer Beyer ceded it on the grant of Friedrich Wilhelm of 19 June 1797 and the declaration of 1 December 1797; the Prince's agent, councillor of justice Melzer, accepted. Appurtenances named include Świechocin, Łowyń, Dormowo, Szarcz, the bailiwick of Zielomyśl and Silna, Hauländer settlements and Heyde Mühlen. A note records the title entered in the mortgage book Vol. VII, Fol. 1.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
We Friedrich Wilhelm, by the Grace of God King of Prussia etc.

Do hereby make known and give all and everyone, especially where it is needful, to know, that before Our South Prussian Government here the Chamber fiscal officer Beyer, in the name of the Treasury, appeared on the 22nd of March 1802, and to the reigning Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, upon the ground of the letter of grant of His Majesty Our Lord Father resting in God, King Friedrich Wilhelm the Second, of the 19th of June 1797, and of Our Declaration of the same of the 1st of December of the same year, did surrender, cede and yield up, to hereditary and proprietary right, the lordship of Pszczew or Betsche situate in the district of Posen, with its appurtenances Świechocin, Stoki, Łowyń, Głażewo, Dormowo, Stołuń, Kuligowo, Szarcz, the bailiwick of Zielomyśl and Silna, together with the Hollander settlements, the Pomcher Hollander settlement and Heyde Mühlen, nothing at all excepted therefrom, as they have lain and still lie within their bounds and borders from of old, as all previous possessors have held, enjoyed and used this lordship, or have been entitled to possess, to enjoy and to use it. Now whereas the reigning Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, through his likewise specially instructed mandatary, legitimated to the acts, Our district councillor of justice Melzer, has solemnly accepted the foregoing surrender of the lordship of Pszczew or Betsche, and has made most humble [continued]

petition for the civil delivery of possession thereof; therefore the same, with all its appurtenances, rights, seigneurial, usufructuary and other entitlements, as these stand described word for word above, nothing at all excepted therefrom, to all such rights, in every measure and intent as Our Treasury and all previous possessors ever had, enjoyed and used the same, or were entitled to possess, to enjoy and to use it, has been conveyed and delivered over to the reigning Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen and to his bodily heirs and successors, and has by Us, out of Our most high Royal sovereign supreme princely power and authority over the land, by virtue of this letter been confirmed in such manner: that he and all his future lawful heirs and successors shall have full right and power henceforth to possess, to enjoy and to use the said lordship of Pszczew or Betsche in the aforesaid manner, and also in turn to sell, to give away, to exchange and to pledge it, and to deal, do and dispose with it as with his well-acquired property; unhindered by all and everyone. Yet this without prejudice to Our Royal sovereign supreme princely rights over the land, entitlements, services and duties, nor otherwise to anyone

without prejudice. In witness whereof, under the customary signature of Our South Prussian Government and its greater seal. Given at Posen, the 29th of March 1802.

Götz

(L. S.)

Schirmer
