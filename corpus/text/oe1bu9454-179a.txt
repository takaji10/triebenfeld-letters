--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Copies of four enclosures: (A) a Kalisch Chamber revenue survey of Zagorowo and Trąbczyn certified by Mencke v. Hellen and Mündel, showing an unalterable net yield of 40260 Rthl 14 g. 11 d. from dues, hereditary canons from Olesnica and Szetlewek, and leases to Honrichs, Glinicki, Niedzewiecki and others, plus 4900 Morgen of reserved forest; (B) a schedule of the Prince's debts totalling 363933 Rthl, rising with the Courland debt to 698784; (C) Triebenfeld's draft memorial to the King seeking 698000 Rthl in Treasury notes secured as sole mortgage, with a 34000 Rthl amortisation fund; (D) the amortisation plan over 39½ years, and part of a power of attorney.

C. Draft of the memorial to His Majesty. Most Serene etc. etc.

Berlin, the 25th of April 1806.

If Your Royal Majesty should deign to favour with Your Most High attention and support a plan which, for a princely house whose head has consecrated his whole life to that same Most High service, fulfils several equally important purposes in an unimpeachable manner, and at the same time leaves no danger whatever to be apprehended for the Most High interest: then I may most humbly take comfort that the following representation will be thought worthy of a Most Gracious consideration.

It is known to Your Most High self with what exertion the Prince of Hohenlohe has been endeavouring for some time past to bring the affairs of his house into order; the just hope of doing so, entertained for many years and founded upon the decease of his uncle and the succession to him, has for the moment been frustrated anew by political events, for the damage he has suffered in the Empire amounts to 532 Rthl. One way out still remains open to him, founded upon the lordships of Zagorowo and Trąbczyn situated in South Prussia, and to submit this to Your Most High gracious decision is the purpose of this petition. Namely, it will be shown and proved specifically and in the fullest detail, by the mortgage and Chamber assessment records:

1. That the two lordships afford a net yield of 40260 Rthl. It is to this yield that I have succeeded in raising the said estates, under the direction of the Most High [uncertain: or] in the Domains Chamber at Kalisz. 16 new villages, and 500 families, might at the same time secure for the Prince, for this exertion, Your Most High approbation and an honourable remembrance in the province.

2. It will be shown specifically that all the liabilities of the Prince, without exception and including the intabulations upon these lordships, still amount to 698000 Rthl, and consequently that the interest at ... per cent amounts to ......... N.B.: at 4 per cent, 27920 Rthl.

At 3 per cent, 20940 Rthl yearly.

On the presumption of this proof, the Prince desires to obtain upon these estates a capital of 698000 Rthl in treasury notes, and stipulates:

1. The [uncertain: depuration] of the estates therewith, in such wise that the capital is secured as the first and sole mortgage;

2. The payment of interest at . . . . per cent.

N.B. It depends upon what [illegible]

G.C.V.B.[illegible] will say, whether 4 or 3 per cent.

3. The establishment of an amortisation fund out of the revenues, namely 34000 Rthl yearly for the payment of the interest and the amortisation of the capital.

whereby the capital will be entirely discharged in . . . . years, and there will remain to his descendants, as a perpetuation of the grace shown him by the bestowal of these estates, a clear entailed [uncertain: trust] property. To all measures directed to the strict execution of this condition, also in the event of his decease, the Prince submits himself without reserve; he will also 4. allow the delivery of the treasury notes to take place in successive instalments to be determined by the highest authority. Moreover this amount of treasury notes may yet be diminished, in so far as Your Royal Majesty may be pleased to let the registered charge of 50000 Rthl standing upon the estates for the Invalids' Fund continue to rest thereon. He desires the strict examination of this undertaking, which is as unimpeachable and in every respect entirely free of danger to the Most High interest as it is of the highest importance to the same; and thereafter he desires most earnestly that the execution may be hastened, since upon it, as has already been said, depends the entire regulation of the Prince's affairs, inasmuch as he is thereby set wholly clear of all foreign credit obligations and entirely in the clear.

I am ready to legitimate myself for this most humble representation by the judicial power of attorney of the Prince which I have in hand, and also to furnish personal surety with my own not inconsiderable fortune, both for the punctual payment of interest and for the yearly payment into the amortisation fund; which obligation I undertake the more gladly as it is known to me with the greatest certainty that, after the lapse of a few years, the revenues of the Prince from his principalities and entailed estates will guarantee a fixed income budget of 400000 Rthl.

I remain, until death, in deepest submission,

Your Royal Majesty's most humble etc. etc.

von Triebenfeld
