--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Copies of four enclosures: (A) a Kalisch Chamber revenue survey of Zagorowo and Trąbczyn certified by Mencke v. Hellen and Mündel, showing an unalterable net yield of 40260 Rthl 14 g. 11 d. from dues, hereditary canons from Olesnica and Szetlewek, and leases to Honrichs, Glinicki, Niedzewiecki and others, plus 4900 Morgen of reserved forest; (B) a schedule of the Prince's debts totalling 363933 Rthl, rising with the Courland debt to 698784; (C) Triebenfeld's draft memorial to the King seeking 698000 Rthl in Treasury notes secured as sole mortgage, with a 34000 Rthl amortisation fund; (D) the amortisation plan over 39½ years, and part of a power of attorney.

C. Draft of the memorial to His Majesty.

Most Serene of All, etc. etc. Berlin, the 25th of April 1806.

If Your Royal Majesty should deign to favour with Your Most High attention and support a plan which, for a Princely House whose head has dedicated his whole life to Your Most High self, fulfils in a blameless manner several equally important ends, and at the same time leaves no danger whatever to be apprehended for the Most High interest: then I may most humbly take comfort that the following representation will be deemed worthy of a Most Gracious consideration.

It is known to Your Most High self with what exertion the Prince of Hohenlohe has for some time been at pains to bring the affairs of his House into order; the just hope of doing so, long entertained, arising from the decease and the inheritance of his uncle, has for the moment been frustrated anew by political events, for the loss which he suffered in [uncertain: Weich] amounts to 532 Rthl. [text lost] One way out remains to him yet, founded upon the lordships of Zagorowo and Trąbczyn situated in South Prussia, and to submit this to Your Most High gracious decision is the purpose of this petition. Namely, it will be specifically and in the fullest detail documented and demonstrated from the mortgage and Chamber assessment records:

1. That the two lordships yield a net income of 40260 Rthl. It is to this yield that I have succeeded in raising the said estates, under the direction of Your Most High [illegible] in the Domain Chamber at Kalisz. 16 new villages, and 500 families, might well at the same time secure for the Prince, for this exertion, Your Most High approbation and an honourable remembrance in the province.

2. It will be specifically shown that the whole of the Prince's liabilities without exception, and including the intabulations upon these lordships, still amount to 698000 Rthl, and consequently the interest at ... per cent .......  NB: at 4 pct. 27920 Rthl; ad at 3 pct. 20940 Rthl yearly.

On the presupposition of this demonstration, the Prince desires to obtain upon these estates a capital of 698000 Rthl in Treasury notes, and stipulates:
1. The [uncertain: Depuration] of the estates therewith, in such wise that the capital shall stand as the first and sole mortgage;
2. The payment of interest at . . . . per cent. NB: this depends on what r[t] G.C.V.B.[illegible] will say, whether 4 or 3 pct.
3. The establishment of an amortisation fund out of the revenues, namely for the payment of the interest and the amortisation of the capital, 34000 Rthl yearly.

whereby the capital will be entirely discharged in . . . . years, and there will remain to his descendant, as a perpetuation of the grace shown him by the conferring of these estates, a clear entailed property. To all measures directed at the strict execution of this condition, even in the event of his decease, the Prince submits himself without reserve; he is also willing, 4., to let the delivery of the Treasury notes take place in successive instalments to be determined in the highest quarter. Further, this amount of Treasury notes may yet be reduced, in so far as Your Royal Majesty should be pleased to let the ingrossment of 50000 Rthl standing upon the estates for the Invalids' Fund remain thereon.

He desires the strict examination of this undertaking, which is as blameless and, in every respect, wholly free of danger for the Most High interest as it is of the highest importance to that same interest; but next to that he desires most ardently that the execution may be hastened, since upon it, as has already been said, the entire regulation of the Prince's affairs depends, inasmuch as he will thereby be set free of all foreign credit-nexus and entirely put in the clear.

I am ready to legitimate myself for this most humble representation by the judicial power of attorney of the Prince which I have in hand, and also to furnish personal surety, with my own not inconsiderable means, both for the punctual payment of interest and for the annual payment into the amortisation fund; which obligation I take upon myself the more gladly, as it is known to me with the greatest certainty that after the lapse of a few years the revenues of the Prince in his principalities and entailed estates will guarantee a fixed income establishment of 400000 Rthl.

I remain, until death, in deepest [uncertain: submission], Your Royal Majesty's most humble, etc. etc.

v. Triebenfeld
