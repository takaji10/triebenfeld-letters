1. from Oleśnica = 2000 Rthl.

C. Draft of the memorial to His Majesty.

Most Serene of All, etc.

Berlin, the 25th April 1806.

If Your Royal Majesty might be pleased to grace with Your Most High attention and support a plan which, for a princely house whose head has consecrated his whole life to that same Most High Person, fulfils in a blameless manner several equally weighty purposes, and at the same time leaves no danger whatever to be apprehended for the Most High interest: then I may most humbly take comfort that the following representation will be deemed worthy of a Most Gracious consideration.

It is known to that same Most High Person with what exertion the Prince of Hohenlohe has hitherto laboured to bring the affairs of his house into order; the just hope of doing so, entertained for many years and grounded in the decease of his uncle and the inheritance from him, has for the moment been frustrated anew by political events, for the damage he suffered in the [uncertain: Weich] amounts to 532/m Rthl. Only one way out remains to him still, founded upon the lordships of Zagórów and Trąbczyn situated in South Prussia, and to submit this to the Most High gracious decision is the purpose of this petition. There will namely be shown and proved specifically and in the fullest detail, by the mortgage and the Chamber's detaxation records:

1. that the two lordships afford a net yield of 40260 Rthl. It is to this yield that I have succeeded in raising the said estates, under the direction of the Most High [uncertain: der oder in] Domains Chamber at Kalisz. 16 new villages, and 500 families, should at the same time secure for the Prince, for this exertion, the Most High approbation and an honourable remembrance in the province.

2. it will be specifically set forth that the whole of the Prince's liabilities, without exception and including the intabulations upon these lordships, still amount to 698000 Rthl, and accordingly that the interest at ... per cent amounts to ......... NB at 4 pct. 27920 Rthl.

ad, at 3 pct. 20940 Rthl. annually.

2. the payment of interest at .... per cent. NB it depends on what Rthl

To all measures which [uncertain: zweckken: tend] to the strict execution of this condition, also in the event of decease, the Prince submits himself without reserve; he also will

4. allow the delivery of the treasury notes to take place in succession, in instalments to be determined in the highest quarter. Further, this amount of treasury notes may yet be reduced, in so far as Your Royal Majesty may be pleased to let the registered charge of 50000 Rthl standing upon the estates for the Invalids' Fund continue to stand thereon. He desires the strict examination of this undertaking, which is as blameless and, in every respect, as wholly free of danger to the Most High interest as it is of the highest importance to the same, but thereafter most ardently that its execution may be hastened, since, as has already been said, the entire regulation of the Prince's affairs depends thereon, inasmuch as he is by this means set free of all foreign credit ties and wholly put in the clear.

I am ready to establish my authority for this most humble representation by means of the Prince's judicial power of attorney which I have in hand, and also to give personal surety, with my own not inconsiderable fortune, both for the due payment of interest and for the annual payment into the amortisation fund; which obligation I undertake the more gladly in that it is known to me with the greatest certainty that, after the lapse of a few years, the Prince's income from his principalities and entailed estates will guarantee a fixed revenue establishment of 400000 Rthl.

I remain, until death, in deepest submission

Your Royal Majesty's most humble, etc.
