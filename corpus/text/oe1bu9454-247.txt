--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports from Vienna that Palen's case is settled favourably, that Privy Councillor Goerch has petitioned on behalf of the whole House of Hohenlohe (copy enclosed), and that Minister von Stumbolt promises support. Argues at length that Cosmar forged the Transact in fraudem tertii, extorting the dying old Princess's mark, and that his guardianship and the late Princess Sacken's claims on the entail must lapse; the Courland heirs could be satisfied with 112000 Rthl, other creditors with 12000 Rthl yearly. Notes the departure of the two Emperors and the King, and appends a damaged revenue and payments schedule totalling 79462 Rthl.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

Palen's fate too has been decided, and for us a more favourable lot has fallen than had been believed. For Your Serene Highness there is now nothing that need be begged for on the intervening side — [uncertain: Jagt] the imperial affairs will doubtless soon come up; of what the Privy Councillor Goerch submitted on behalf of the whole House of Hohenlohe a Copy follows here – His Excellency the Minister von Stumbolt has promised me to support Your Serene Highness's cause in particular. Upon the piece submitted before me through the Prince State Chancellor's office, of which I sent the Copy by the last post, His Serene Highness at the first report was graciously pleased to express himself in these words: The money is a trifle, it should be given, since so much can be obtained thereby; but with the judiciary we shall no doubt get much noise on account of the annulment of the settlement, all the more since the old Prince himself approved it. Meanwhile it is remarkable that the deceased F. Sachen disposed of the revenues of the entailed estates, and appointed an executor of the testament — the estates never belonged to her at all. I have once more set the whole matter out properly, and in particular that Cosmar, on account of the old [illegible] Princess, over the Courland loan which the former Princes of Hohenlohe co-signed, drove her to despair and brought her to the point that, on the brink of the grave, she signed with a cross the false settlement forged by Cosmar, and allowed it to happen that so many debts were entered therein, whereby all the other creditors were to be pushed back.

I maintain that the old Princess after her death could not have had the least share in

the revenues of the entailed estate, still less after her death[text lost] because with her death her right of life-tenure was to lapse, and[text lost] Cosmar as guardian of the minor pronounced, so this would cease [text lost] the Princes Adolph and Alexander were of age for the entailed estate, and since the former did not[text lost] received estates, but as the sole heir of the estate of the Princes Sacken[text lost] Cosmar has absolutely nothing to do with the entailed estates and must here[text lost] be dismissed — Meanwhile Prince Adolph would in a year and a day — all[text lost] in 2 to 3 years be of age for the entailed estate, whereupon his whole interference would also cease; the [text lost] would receive merely, as higher, an appanage of 1200 Rthl out of the entailed estates. All the apprehensions of the P. Sackin, which had induced her and even the Prince to sign the notorious settlement wheedled in fraudem tertii by Cosmar, would cease the moment the Courland heirs had been satisfied. And this could now be done with 112000 Rthl. Not only on this account, as was said in the earlier submission, had a composition already been made with the agent of the heirs of the estate, but agreement had also been reached with the other creditors to the effect that they would be content with 12000 Rthl yearly for as long as the Prince lives — To the question — but will the estates really yield so much that the 112000 Rthl and everything the deed of foundation specifies, and also the 12000 Rthl to the other creditors, can be paid? Answer: Certainly – I have laid at His Serene Highness's feet the demonstration of the yield and of the sums to be paid -

Well! my friend, it turns then upon their [uncertain: Oe] and security. The two Emperors and the King, with [uncertain: Pfar] Wilhelm, returned to Hungary last night. The King of Bavaria of Deresemberg remained here in the meantime with their hereditary prince, as did the King of Denmark. Grand Duke Constantine had meanwhile been viewing places outside the city - Respectfully I remain, until death,

Your Serene Highness's

Vienna, the 28th of October

1814.

humble and obedient servant

v. Triebenfeld
