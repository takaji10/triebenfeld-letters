w Warszawie dnia 5 Marca [18]12.
Kochany Bracie!
Pospieszam z doniesieniem, iż dla
różnych Interessów dopiero w dniu
15 m.b. z Warszawy będę mógł
wyiechać. Okoliczność względem
Trąbczyna podług powziętey pewney
wiadomości w dobrym znayduie
się położeniu, w czym przy powro
cie dokładniey obiacnie.
Dla krótkośći czasu ponieważ
okazya odchodzi nie mogę
więcey pisać.
Będąc zdrów
[k?]ony Bracie!
przyiaciela
B. Brzechsta

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A brief note from B. Brzechsta, written in haste as the post was leaving, announcing that various affairs will keep him in Warsaw until the 15th of the month. He reports, on the strength of reliable intelligence received, that the Trąbczyn matter stands well, and promises a fuller explanation in person on his return. Addressed to "Dear Brother"; the closing salutation is partly illegible.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
In Warsaw, the 5th of March [18]12. Dear Brother! I hasten to report that, owing to various affairs, I shall not be able to depart from Warsaw until the 15th of the present month. The matter concerning Trąbczyn, according to certain intelligence I have received, stands in a good position, which I shall explain more exactly upon my return. For shortness of time, since the opportunity [i.e. the post] is departing, I cannot write more. Keeping well, [uncertain: k]ony Brother! your friend B. Brzechsta
