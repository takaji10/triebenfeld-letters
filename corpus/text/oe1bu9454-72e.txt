w Warszawie dnia 5 Marca [18]12.
Kochany Bracie!
Pospieszam z doniesieniem, iż dla
różnych Interessów dopiero w dniu
15 m.b. z Warszawy będę mógł
wyiechać. Okoliczność względem
Trąbczyna podług powziętey pewney
wiadomości w dobrym znayduie
się położeniu, w czym przy powro
cie dokładniey obiacnie.
Dla krótkośći czasu ponieważ
okazya odchodzi nie mogę
więcey pisać.
Będąc zdrów
[k?]ony Bracie!
przyiaciela
B. Brzechsta

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A brief note from B. Brzechsta, writing as "brother", saying that business will keep him in Warsaw until the 15th of the month, and that on reliable information the Trąbczyn affair stands well, with fuller explanation promised on his return. Nothing further is given, the writer breaking off because the coach was leaving. A hasty few lines rather than a substantive report.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
In Warsaw, the 5th day of March [18]12. Dear Brother!

I hasten to report that, on account of various matters of business, I shall not be able to depart from Warsaw until the 15th of the current month. The affair concerning Trąbczyn, according to certain intelligence I have received, stands in good posture, which upon my return I shall explain more fully. For want of time, since the conveyance is departing, I cannot write more.

Be well

[uncertain: k]ony Brother!

friend

B. Brzechsta
