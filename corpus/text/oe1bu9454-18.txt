--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Written from a violent fever, rejects the instrument and power of attorney the Prince has had drawn up as needless, since Triebenfeld already holds a general power. Explains that all funds are under arrest and that, under the French General Government's decree, he alone may reclaim the 3121/3125 Rthl paid under French duress to Intendant Maluce and the Invalids' Fund. Demands a declaration assigning him 4219 Rthl from the arrears of Wrąbczyn, Alt Olesnica, Kopoyno, Skokum, Swiątniki, Adelow, Sophienthal and Neu Swięce, recoverable by komornik, and sends his forester to fetch it by the 15th or 16th.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince, Most gracious Prince and Lord! I lay in the most violent fever when Your Serene Highness's letter [uncertain: reached] me; the sight of your hand cheered my sum, but how great was my astonishment when I saw the instrument that had been drawn up, together with the power of attorney — who gave Your Serene Highness this counsel? W here what is to be recovered for Your Serene Highness, that would be done without any such instrument. I do after all hold a general power of attorney for that purpose. For you, unhappily, I can recover nothing, for everything is at this moment placed under arrest. Besides, you were indeed obliged to pay the interest to the Invalids' Fund. In Berlin they dragged me Collé; when the French held me under arrest I had to pay on behalf of Your Serene Highness, as your plenipotentiary. When I had been released from the arrest, I proved the unheard-of, unjust proceeding of the Intendant

Maluce and of the Invalids' Fund before the French General Direction — and this body then ordered that only the 3121 Rthl, together with costs and interest, were to be given back out of the estates. By virtue of this decree it is I who can demand the moneys back, not Your Serene Highness, for you are the party in default. — I claim, as to the interest, the interest and costs, which together amount to 4219 Rthl, and I require that the same be recovered for me out of the arrears and interest of the subjects, upon which the Invalids' Fund has now laid an arrest. Now, in order to facilitate this matter and not first to have to seek out lengthy judicial assistance, Your Serene Highness should, according to the counsel of the komornik, declare: that in accordance with the decree of the French General Government you are still owing to the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld the 3125 Rthl of interest taken from him by compulsion and arrest, which the Invalids' Fund received against receipt on the 14th of February 1808,

and which the French General Government has ordered to be [uncertain: remitted] to the said Triebenfeld out of the revenues of the Zagorow estate; and that the said von Triebenfeld has in this respect been satisfied neither as regards the capital advanced nor as regards the costs and interest. Since, then, the capital advanced, together with interest and costs, amounts to 4219 Rthl, you accordingly assign to Triebenfeld this sum, in the amount of 4219. —, out of the old and new interest payments which the inhabitants of Wrąbczyn, Alt Olesnica, Kopoyno, Skokum, Swiątniki, Adelow, Sophienthal and Neu Swięce have to pay, to be drawn thereupon, so that he may satisfy himself out of these funds. To that end you concede that this above-mentioned sum of 4219 Rthl may forthwith be recovered by a komornik, and consequently by the strictest execution, and be delivered into the hands of the said von Triebenfeld. This, then, the komornik has the Tribunal

approve, and then sees what he can recover or lay hold of thereupon ahead of the Invalids' Fund. This is the only means of rescue for obtaining some money here now, so as to keep the machine in motion. I am therefore sending my forester on horseback once again, and beg Your Serene Highness in the most pressing manner to have the instrument drawn up in the manner described above with all possible speed, and to convey it to me by the messenger. I remain, until death, Your Serene Highness's — if I do not have the instrument here by the 15th, or at the very latest early on the 16th, then everything comes to an end and collapses in a heap. Humble three, Blizanow, the 6th of July 1812, v Triebenfeld
