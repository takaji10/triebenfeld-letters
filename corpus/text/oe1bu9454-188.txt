auf die Güter, und besonders H. Weigel hier bezahlt - Vorzüglich ge¬
liegt es mir die bey Oppenheimer versezte Hypotheque von
200000 Rthl wieder Flott zu machen.
Die Ge Schlabrendorff werde ich in der Art zahlen, daß ich auf eine
Schweinersche Hypotheque 20000 Rthl vom Krieges Rath von Hellen
nehme welche ich an der Frau v Wulffen von Kontop herzahlen
wolte, die aber jezt noch stehen können.
Ist dies alles besorgt den sind Ewer Durchlaucht bald aufs rein,
besonders da man mit die 300000 Rthl Gu[s?]l Schuld warten um die
Intressen fortzahlen will.
Die verdammten kleinen SchuldPosten drüken nur am mehrsten den dieſen
Kaste Creditoren machen, um ihre bagatellen das mehreste Geschrey¬
Überhaupt bin ich diesmahl in einen Gedränge gewest wo ich Gott
hundertfach danken muß, wann das alles geebenet wird,

Gestern bin ich hier angekommen, und in dieser Stunde eile ich nach
Kalisz und Trąbczyn, um dort was möglich ist zusammen zu treiben

lich quittirt, und es bleibe für Ewr Durchlaucht 4000 Rthl die

die Johannis Pacht beträgt von den Pächtern allein 10000 Rthl die Bauer
und Erbkäufer Johanni Zinßen betragen an 4000 Rthl die Wrąbczyner
und Swięcier unßen an 1800 Rthl zahlen die Olesnicer restiren schon

Es kömt jetzt darauf an, ob Schenck eine Can: oder ehrlich flei¬
ßiger Kerl gewesen ist. Den er hatt den Auftrag beizutreiben
was möglich ist damit Ewr Durchlaucht auf Johann eine
erkleckliche Summa erhalten.
Grattenauer ist eine faule falsche Bestie - Er thut nichts,

und ſizt jezt fortwährend bey dem Minister, der ihm wie ich
so eben höhre einen Vorspann Paß zu einer Reise in Gebürge
und Ober schlesien gegeben hatt. Wie kan der schlechte Kerl

als factor dient? Der Bube solte schuldig halber arretirt

Ehrfurchts ersterbe als
Ewr Durchlaucht

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports success in Berlin: through friends, the Grand Chancellor will release some 176000 Rthl from the Justice Fund to pay off the pressing creditors, notably H. Weigel, while Triebenfeld hopes to refloat the 200000 Rthl mortgage with Oppenheimer and settle Schlabrendorff by taking 20000 Rthl on a Schweiner mortgage from War Councillor von Hellen. Small creditors clamour most. Betsche is settled, Eichaust receipted, and he lists some 102000 Rthl of arrears expected at Trąbczyn; denounces Grattenauer as idle and false.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Lord!

In Berlin it succeeded in the end better than I had believed, through the help of the friends G. v. K. CV B.; the Lord Grand Chancellor gives out of the Justice Fund some 176000 Rthl, and with that all the burdensome creditors upon the estates, and especially Mr. Weigel here, will be paid — It lies with me above all to set afloat again the mortgage of 200000 Rthl pledged with Oppenheimer.

The Schlabrendorff [claim] I shall pay in such a manner that I take 20000 Rthl upon a Schweiner mortgage from the Councillor of War von Hellen, which I had meant to pay over to Madame von Wulffen of Kontop, but which can now still stand over.

If all this is attended to, then Your Serene Highness will soon be in the clear, especially as they are willing to wait with the 300000 Rthl [uncertain: Gusl] debt so long as the interest continues to be paid.

The accursed small items of debt press the hardest, for these [uncertain: Kaste] creditors make the most outcry over their trifles. Altogether I have this time been in such a press that I must thank God a hundredfold if it is all smoothed out,

Yesterday I arrived here, and this very hour I hasten on to Kalisz and Trąbczyn, in order to drive together there what is possible, so that I may make over an order to pay a sum to Your Serene Highness at Türth. In Betsche I have put everything in order so far that Your Serene Highness need no longer be troubled by the creditors there. Echaust is fully satisfied, has given a receipt before the court, and there remains for Your Serene Highness 4000 Rthl which the deputies must pay on the 6th of July. These I think to take now already towards the 30000 Rthl. Now it depends on what I shall find at Trąbczyn. There the lessees still had 6000 Rthl to pay. 60000 Rthl were to come in as entry-money gulden; the Midsummer rent amounts, from the lessees alone, to 10000 Rthl; the peasants' and hereditary purchasers' Midsummer interest amounts to some 4000 Rthl; the Trąbczyn and Swięcier [text lost] pay some 1800 Rthl; the Olesnicer are already some 16000 Rthl in arrears; this amounts to some 102000 Rthl of outstanding arrears. It now depends upon whether Schenck has been a Can: or an honest, diligent fellow. For he has the commission to collect in what is possible, so that Your Serene Highness may receive a considerable sum at Midsummer.

Grattenauer is a lazy, false beast — He does nothing,

and now sits continually with the Minister, who, as I hear this very moment, has given him a pass for post-horses for a journey into the mountains and Upper Silesia. How can the wretched fellow draw a salary from Your Serene Highness when he serves the Minister as his agent? The knave ought to be arrested for debt; I have advanced him his salary for 1 1/2 years, and now that he has it he does nothing more. Little by little I give up the hope of finding an honest man any more.

God grant me that all goes happily and well at Trąbczyn. In deepest reverence I remain, until death,

Your Serene Highness's

Breslau, the 25th of June

1806.

faithful and most obedient servant

v. Triebenfeld
