--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Hereditary-quit-rent contract in thirteen paragraphs, concluded before the Trąbczyn Patrimonial Court between Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, general Bevollmächtigter of the Prince zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, and Princely Forest Inspector Carl Titz, conferring five Hufen Magdeburg of Trąbczyn demesne arable towards Podbiele, with orchard, in perpetuity: purchase-money 300 rt per Hufe, 1500 rt in two Johanni instalments, annual quit-rent 75 rt, plus royal taxes, the Ląd monastery competency, tavern obligation with penalties, tenth-Groschen laudemium on sale, and full renunciation of remission. Signed by Peter Friedrich August v. Triebenfeld and Titz.

Actum Trąbczyn, the 4th May 1805. Before the local Patrimonial Court there appeared in person,

1st, the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, as the known General Bevollmächtigter [general plenipotentiary] of His Serene Highness the reigning Prince zu Hohenlohe Ingelfingen, [uncertain: hereditary] court lord of the [uncertain: aforesaid] estates,

on the one part;

2nd, the local Princely Forest Inspector Carl Titz, on the other part.

The comparents are [uncertain: personally known to the Subforestis] and are found to be in a condition to execute judicial transactions validly.

The same do freely and after due deliberation declare to the protocol the following hereditary-quit-rent contract concluded between them, as an

irrevocable hereditary-quit-rent contract, in nothing to be altered.

§ I. Namely, His Most Serene Highness does confer upon the Forest Inspector Carl Titz, out of the local Trąbczyn demesne-farm arable, 5 Hufen, in words five Hufen Magdeburg [measure], and that [uncertain: below] the demesne-farm buildings, from the road which leads to Podbiele as far as the mill-ditch, including the orchard lying beside the stables, from now onward for everlasting times in hereditary quit-rent.

§ III. The Forest Inspector Titz binds himself, for the [uncertain: acquired] holding, from Johannis of this year onward, to a perpetual
