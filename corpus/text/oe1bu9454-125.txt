gekommen – Sie wollen im 4 Uhr bey Ewr Hoch¬
fürstliche Durchlaucht sein

ber werde ich Euer Durchlaucht Nachmittag

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A brief note announcing that the people from Olesnica have arrived with the monies and wish to attend the Prince at four o'clock; Triebenfeld reports having already negotiated something with them and promises a fuller account that afternoon, expressing confidence that the terms agreed will not displease His Serene Highness. No sums or names of the Olesnica party are given.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The people from Olesnica have arrived with the monies – they wish to be with Your Most Serene Highness at 4 o'clock. I have negotiated something with them here, upon which I shall make my report to Your Serene Highness this afternoon – I believe it will not be displeasing to Your Serene Highness. v. Triebenfeld. Breslau, the 28th of April 1804. To His Most Serene

Highness
