--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A brief note announcing that the men from Olesnica have arrived with the money and wish to wait on the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen at four o'clock. Triebenfeld reports having already negotiated something with them, the details of which he will lay before the Prince that afternoon, and expresses confidence that the terms he has obtained will meet with approval.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The people from Olesnica have arrived with the monies – they wish to be with Your Most Serene Highness at 4 o'clock. I have negotiated something with them here, upon which I shall make my report to Your Serene Highness this afternoon – I believe it will not be displeasing to Your Serene Highness. v. Triebenfeld. Breslau, the 28th of April 1804. To His Most Serene

Highness
