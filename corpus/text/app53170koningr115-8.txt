--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Bericht des Gerichtsboten Bartłomiej Szepczyński von Trąbczyn vom 26. Februar 1773, für Antoni Prusimski von Kolno, Starost von Niszczewice. Mit zwei Adligen hat er am Vortag die Trąbczyner Wälder und Wiesen besichtigt und gezählt: im Wald hinter dem Gehölz Lusnia einundfünfzig Stümpfe gefällter, zum Bauen tauglicher Kiefern, auf den Wiesen namens Kąty zweiunddreißig Erlenstümpfe. Nach seiner Angabe haben die Untertanen Chełmskis das Holz aus dem Wald abgefahren und Leute vom Gut Podbiele der Frau Zakrzewska die Erlen auf den Wiesen geschlagen.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A report of the court messenger Bartłomiej Szepczyński of Trąbczyn of 26 February 1773, for Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice. With two noblemen he inspected and counted, the day before, in the woods and meadows of Trąbczyn: in the wood beyond the Lusnia grove fifty-one stumps of felled pines fit for building, and on the meadows called Kąty thirty-two alder stumps. He states that Chełmski's subjects carted the timber out of the wood, and that men of the Podbiele estate of the lady Zakrzewska cut the alders on the meadows.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Done in Konin on the Friday after Ash Wednesday next, namely on the twenty-sixth day of the month of February in the year of the Lord 1773.

Judicial Site Inspection of Felled Trees — on behalf of the Right Honourable Prusimski

To the office and present records of the Konin Municipal Court, appearing in person, the Honest Bartłomiej Szepczyński of Trąbczyn, Royal Court Summoner, General, being sound, certified — because he, having with him two noblemen, namely the Honourables Piotr Makowski and Świętosław Bogusławski, although absent here from the court for the present, being nonetheless present at the time at the location of the below-written matter — on the day preceding today's act —

at the request and official requisition of the Right Honourable Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice, heir of the estates of Trąbczyn — to the Trąbczyn pine forests and the meadows pertaining to the same estates — descended personally and officially; and being there in such manner, saw and duly inspected and counted: namely, in the pine forest lying beyond the woodland called Lusnia, fifty-one stumps from a pine tree of value for building, felled; also, in the meadows pertaining to the estate of Trąbczyn, called Kąty, thirty-two alder trunks left [there], from a tree cut down.

Which aforementioned things — immediately at that time, by the subjects of the Right Honourable Chełmski, heir of the estates of Łukom, carried away from the pine forest beyond Lusnia; and then [by] men of the Podbiele estate of the Right Honourable Zakrzewska, cut down and intercepted from the meadows — asserting that he had duly seen, inspected, and counted [the same], he attested to the present office.
