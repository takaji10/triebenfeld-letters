Varsovie le 16 Nov. 1819
J’ai l’honneur de vous annoncer que M. Mitschke de Kalisz m’a envoyé les actes de votre procês contre Madame Miączyńska, les actes sont en ordres, il y manquent seulement les instrumens obligatoires, viz les certificats de l’hypothèque de la somme, que vous demandez par votre Action: Ces instrumens doivent, être presentès en original ou en copie duement atteste.
La partie contraire poursuit l’introduction de la cause devant la cour d’appel, moi je prolonge l’instruction mais enfin quand tous les termes seront epuisès, il faudra la finir viz soumettre la cause à la decision.
Pour l’instruction, je me sers de copies simples, que vous m’avez envoyé, mais quand il viendra à plaider il est indispensable de representer les originaux, ou les copies legalement attestées.
M. Tarczewski

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Warsaw, 16 Nov. 1819

I have the honour to inform you that Mr Mitschke of Kalisz has sent me the documents of your suit against Madame Miączyńska. The documents are in order; only the instruments of obligation are missing from them, viz. the certificates of the mortgage of the sum which you claim by your action. These instruments must be produced in the original or in a duly attested copy.

The opposing party is pressing for the case to be brought before the court of appeal; for my part I am prolonging the preliminary proceedings, but in the end, when all the time limits are exhausted, they will have to be concluded, viz. the case submitted for decision.

For the preliminary proceedings I am using the simple copies which you sent me, but when it comes to pleading it is indispensable to produce the originals, or legally attested copies.

M. Tarczewski
