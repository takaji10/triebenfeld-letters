--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A copy of a brief reply from the cabinet secretary Beyme, reporting that the King, having been shown the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen's letter of the 21st, has ordered that any answer be held back until the report called for from Minister of State von Voss comes in, an outcome the Prince had himself anticipated. Noted as received on 1 February 1799.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Copy. Most Serene Prince! Most gracious Prince and Lord! I do not fail to report most humbly to Your Most Serene Highness that His Majesty, upon the presentation of Your Most Serene Highness's letter of the 21st of this month, has been graciously pleased to command that the answering of the same, as Your Most Serene Highness yourself appeared to expect, shall remain deferred until the arrival of the report required from the Minister of State von Voss. I persist with the deepest reverence Your Most Serene Highness's Berlin, the 25th of January 1799. (ad 1 February 1799) most humble and obedient servant Beyme
