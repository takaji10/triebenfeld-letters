--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Beyme reports, in a copy, that His Majesty, on being presented with the Prince's letter of the 21st of the month, has ordered that the reply be postponed — as the Prince himself had anticipated — until the report demanded from Minister of State v. Voss arrives. A brief court communication in the most deferential terms, noted as received 1 February 1799, conveying only this deferral and no substantive decision.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Copy. Most Serene Prince! Most gracious Prince and Lord! I do not fail most humbly to report to Your Most Serene Highness that His Majesty, upon the presentation of Your Highness's letter of the 21st of this month, has been pleased to command that the answer to the same, as Your Most Serene Highness yourself seemed to expect, should be deferred until the arrival of the report required from the Minister of State v. Voss. I remain, with the deepest reverence, Your Most Serene Highness's Berlin, the 25th of January 1799. (ad 1. Febr 1799) most humble and obedient servant Beyme
