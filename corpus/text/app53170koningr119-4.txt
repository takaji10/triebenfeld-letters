Ex Parte JM Prusimski contra M Chełmski Relatio
Personaliter veniens Providus Bartholomaeus Szepczyński de Villa Trąmpczyn Ministerialis Regni Generalis Sanus Recognovit se Citt~ Litt~ Authen~ pro Judiciis Castren~ Callisien~ proxime in Castro Calissien~ Celebrand~ Qua ex Parte et ad Instantiam Illustris Magnifici Antonii in Kolno Prusimski Capitaneo Nieszczevicen~ Bonorum Trąmpczyna Haeredis, tum Generosorum Leonis Maszewski et Francisci Liberacki Aliorumque Actt~ In et Contra Magnificum Stanislaum Chełmski Thaesaurarium Vschoven~ Aliosque Cittos~ Copiae et essentiae futuro in Termino porrigendae Similem editt~ In Curia Bonorum Villae Łukom super mensa hippi~ praesente Citt~ Die haesterna Actum hornum praecedente posuisse et notificatt~ reliquisse

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Bericht des Gerichtsboten Bartłomiej Szepczyński von Trąbczyn vom 18. März 1783. Er hat eine Ladung vor das Burggericht Kalisz zugestellt, die Antoni Prusimski von Kolno, Starost von Niszczewice, mit Leon Maszewski, Franciszek Liberacki und anderen gegen Stanisław Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa, und andere erwirkt hat. Er hat sie am Vortag im Gutshof von Łukom in Gegenwart des Geladenen hinterlegt.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A report of the court messenger Bartłomiej Szepczyński of Trąbczyn of 18 March 1783. He has served a citation before the castle court of Kalisz that Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice, with Leon Maszewski, Franciszek Liberacki and others, obtained against Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, and others. He left it the day before at the manor of Łukom in the presence of the man cited.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
On behalf of His Honour Prusimski against the Right Honourable Chełmski — Report

Appearing in person, the Honest Bartłomiej Szepczyński of the village of Trąbczyn, General Royal Court Summoner, being sound, recognized that he had served authentic citation letters for the Kalisz Municipal Court sessions, for the matter to be heard at the Kalisz Municipal Court — because on behalf and at the instance of the Illustrious Right Honourable Antoni of Kolno Prusimski, Captain of Niszczewice, heir of the estates of Trąbczyn, and likewise of the Honourable Leon Maszewski and Franciszek Liberacki and others as plaintiffs, against the Right Honourable Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, and others as cited parties — copies and certified documents to be produced at the forthcoming session, similarly issued, at the manor house of the estates of the village of Łukom, at the table in the heated room, with the cited party present, on the preceding day, preceding the present act, set down and left notified.
