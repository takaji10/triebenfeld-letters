--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports that Minister von Voss has flatly forbidden any revision of the assessments, and weighs relying instead on the district councillor's certificate, since dismemberment will make the administration accounts fall away; a councillor is being sent to Zagorowo and Trąbczyn before consent is given. Sets out a plan to raise 130,000 Rthl: settlements with Meierowitz, Weigel, Schliefen and Honrichs, Radzinski's offer for Szetlewek, hereditary lease of Olesnica, and a three-year lease of the drink-selling rights at 4,000 Rthl yearly.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Lord!

Minister von Voss has, in the most despotic terms, forbidden any revision of the assessments; whatever names they may bear and to whomever they may belong, the Chamber is to reject them. Now I await only Amelang's answer - whether one will be content with the revision by the district councillor. Should this too be refused, then I must think of other means - and set light against despotism. The dismemberment has been submitted; the President is sending a councillor to Zagorowo, for Trąbczyn, who is to examine everything, whereupon the consent will then be granted. The revision concerning the dismemberment I shall know how to make use of. With Meierowitz I have concluded matters as the figure shows, and I am endeavouring to dislodge Honrichs - I think the affair will give Your Serene Highness pleasure: you get your 4000 Rthl back and profit yearly by 500 Rthl of canon, or 10000 Rthl in cash for it. Weigel receives, out of the eight thousand, above his 4000 Rthl

6000 Rthl, the Schliefen 6000 Rthl, and Honrichs — that makes 20000 Rthl of purchase money. Szetlewek Radzinski will not let slip. He offers, firstly, 18000 Rthl of entry money and the one ([uncertain: 113] of the 18000 I reckon off against his claims by 5; he must lay down 10000 Rthl in cash), some hereditary term of years 1500 Rthl, which together makes 48000 Rthl, and that is, God knows, a great deal for this wretched little place, which has only 1700 Morgen of surface area together with the peasant lands and in part quite poor soil - We are still disputing only over the pasture, for he wants to have free grazing with his cattle in the remaining woodland [uncertain: Epprech] - here too I would in the end fix upon a middle course.

Both [illegible] therefore already put the question of cash money at 14000 Rthl, without the canon - and Radzinski's claim is discharged and all the lawsuits lifted.

With the people of Olesnica I shall soon be finished — I now have important assistance - My opinion now is to have the fellows thrown out. To take the 10000 Rthl from the newer subjects and let them be settled, because they have already paid 10000 Rthl. Thereafter to come to terms with the old peasants, to bring together enough lands that the demesne farm is restored, in order then to grant Olesnica anew in hereditary lease after the mind of the Chamber

to take again another 8 to 10000 Rthl and then to fix the canon there - these together already make 44000 Rthl. For the propination rights I am still obtaining a lease, a three-year lease contract at 4000 Rthl yearly. Arrears outstanding /the people of Olesnica and others/ 2000 Rthl

That is 50000 Rthl. Takers, and people who fit into the dismemberment plan, are there in abundance, and hell would have to burst if I should not have 80000 Rthl here by the 1st of July. This is a little sum of 130000 Rthl, and with it all ways can be met until more comes in from the purchasers.

Should one in Berlin, then, not accept the certificate of the district councillor as sufficient, I shall not hesitate; for once I have dismembered, all the administration accounts in the assessments fall away accordingly - There remain pure [uncertain: rents] of interest; for that no revision is needed, no principles - for the Chamber has after all approved everything, and I attain the purpose better than by a heated revision - and need no Amelang or

any other fire-brand - the fellow behaves as though he had devoured Solomon's wisdom, preaches morality in his letters, counsels the greatest circumspection, and were. he fears that the world might learn that he and his games are arch-rogues - that Goldstücker has pledged the Glenck mortgage on Olesnica with Oppenheimer, over that the man lectures and writes me four pages full. For it had been promised that, if the loan from the Invalids' Fund were granted, the holder of that mortgage should concede priority; now the fellow has learned that Goldstücher has ceded the mortgage to Oppenheimer, whereupon the man asked Oppenheimer whether he would concede the priority, and the Jew has refused this, and now [uncertain: Arnden] makes an uproar as though the weal and woe of the world depended upon it. The Przespolewski affair will be brought to a good conclusion. With Glenck's stories may it please Your Serene Highness to spare me altogether, for I lose my senses over the man's conduct — because Sprengel claims to have found his 18 Morgen, you are to pay the fellow again – Sprengel and yet another such rascal have here again sued for 761 Rthl of old arrears; Chwalowski receives 800 Rthl, why, the dogs could have surveyed a whole principality with that. I remain, until death, as Your Serene Highness's

Kalisz, the 10th of March 1805.

faithfully yours

von Triebenfeld
