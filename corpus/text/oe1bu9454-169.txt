Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Lord, the Minister von Voss has, in the most despotic expressions, forbidden all revision of the valuations; whatever names they may bear and to whomsoever they may belong, the Chamber is to reject them. Now I await only Amelang's answer — whether they will be content with the revision by the Landrath. If this too is refused — then I must think of other means — and set light against despotism. The dismemberment is [uncertain: an ist] submitted; the President is sending a councillor to Zagórów [uncertain: um] Trąbczyn, who is to inspect everything, whereupon the consent will then be granted. The revision on account of the dismemberment I shall know how to turn to use — With Mr Meierowitz I have concluded, as the figure shows, and I am endeavouring to dislodge Honrichs — I think the matter will give Your Serene Highness pleasure: you recover your 4,000 Rthl and profit yearly by 500 Rthl canon, or 10,000 Rthl in cash for it. Weigelt receives out of the eight thousand, over and above his 4,000 Rthl,

6,000 Rthl, Schliefen, and Honrichs 6,000 Rthl; that makes 20,000 Rthl of purchase money. Szetlewek Radzinski will not let slip. He offers, first, 18,000 Rthl as entry money and the one (11[uncertain: 3] of the 18,000 — against this I set off his claims, 5; he must pay down 10,000 Rthl in cash), some hereditary term of years 1,500 Rthl, which together makes 48,000 Rthl. That is, God knows, a great deal for this wretched scrap, which has only 1,700 Morgen of surface area including the peasant lands, and soil in part quite bad — We are still at odds only over the pasture, for he expressly wants free grazing with his cattle in the woodland that remains — here too I should in the end fix upon a middle course.

Both [uncertain: facienden] therefore already ask cash money of 14,000 Rthl without the canon — and Radzinski's claim is extinguished and all the lawsuits are quashed.

With the Oleśnica people I shall soon be done — I now have important help — My opinion is now to have the fellows thrown out. To take the 10,000 Rthl from the newer subjects and let them be settled, because they have already paid 10,000 Rthl. Thereafter to come to terms with the old peasants, to bring together so much land that the demesne farm is restored, and then to let out Oleśnica anew, according to the mind of the Chamber,

on hereditary lease, and to take a further 8 to 10,000 Rthl and there fix the canon — these then together already make 44,000 Rthl. For the propination rights I obtain a further three-year lease contract at 4,000 Rthl yearly. Arrears outstanding / Oleśnica people and others / 2,000 Rthl. That is 50,000 Rthl.

Takers, and people who fit into the dismemberment plan, are here in abundance, and hell would have to burst if I were not to have 80,000 Rthl here by 1 [illegible] July. This is a [uncertain: little loan] of 130,000 Rthl, and with it all ways can be [uncertain: met] until more comes in from the purchasers.

Should they therefore in Berlin not accept the Landrath's certificate as sufficient, then I shall not delay; once I have dismembered, all the administration accounts fall away along with the valuations — There remain pure clear interest payments; for that no revision is needed, no principles — for the Chamber has after all approved everything, and I attain the purpose better than by a present revision — and need no Amelang or

other firebrand — the fellow behaves as though he had devoured Solomon's wisdom, preaches morality in his letters, counsels the greatest circumspection, and [uncertain: warns]. He fears the world might learn that he and his [uncertain: fellows in the game] are arch-rogues — that Goldstücker has pledged the Glenck mortgage on Oleśnica with Oppenheimer: over this the man makes an uproar and writes me four pages thick full of it. For it had been promised that if the loan were granted by the Invalids' Fund, the holder of that mortgage should concede priority; now the patron has learnt that Goldstücker has ceded the mortgage to Oppenheimer, and so the man asked Oppenheimer whether he would concede the priority, which the Jew refused, and so Amel[ang] makes an uproar as if the weal and woe of the world depended upon it. The Przespolewski matter will be well concluded. With Glenck's stories may Your Serene Highness be pleased to spare me altogether, for I lose my senses over the man's conduct — because Sprengel claims to have found his 18 Morgen, you are to pay the fellow again — Spreng and yet another such rascal have here again sued for 761 Rthl of old arrears; Chwalowski receives 800 Rthl — with that the dogs might indeed have [uncertain: surveyed] a principality. I remain, until death, Your Serene Highness's

Kalisz, the 10th of March 1805.

faithfully yours

von Triebenfeld

The busts have arrived and been delivered; they pleased greatly — My stay here lasts very long and is very costly, everyone wants to have something — God grant only Your Serene Highness health
