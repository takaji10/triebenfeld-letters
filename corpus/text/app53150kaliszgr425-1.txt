--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Vertagung in der Grenzsache zwischen Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice und Erbherrn von Trąbczyn, und Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Erbherrn von Łukomia, ausgestellt am 28. September 1776 und am 30. September in die Akten des Burggerichts Kalisz eingetragen; das Original ist in das Buch eingenäht. Fünf von den Ständen der Republik bestellte Kommissare erklären, sie könnten die Sache im jetzt fälligen Termin nicht durch ein Endurteil abschließen, weil einige von ihnen beim Reichstag in Warschau, andere beim Krontribunal in Piotrków zu tun haben. Der Termin geht auf die Vertagung im Dekret vom 13. September des Vorjahres zurück. Sie vertagen die Zusammenkunft auf den ersten Montag nach Dreikönig 1777 und erhalten den Parteien den letzten Termin zum Erscheinen vor dem Kommissionsgericht auf dem Grund von Łukomia. Es unterzeichnen und siegeln Stefan Leszczyc Zielonacki, Unterrichter des Kalischer Landgerichts, Maciej Leszczyc Mierzewski, Tomasz Radoński, Grenzvermesser von Kalisz, Stanisław Jabłkowski und Stanisław Otto-Trąmpczyński.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
An adjournment in the boundary cause between Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice and heir of Trąbczyn, and Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, heir of Łukomia, made on 28 September 1776 and entered in the records of the castle court at Kalisz on 30 September; the original is sewn into the book. Five commissioners appointed by the Estates of the Commonwealth declare that they cannot end the cause by a final judgment at the term now due, because some of them have business at the parliament in Warsaw and others at the Crown Tribunal in Piotrków. The term goes back to the adjournment in the decree of 13 September of the year before. They put the meeting off to the first Monday after Epiphany 1777 and keep for the parties the final term for appearing before the commission's court on the ground of Łukomia. Stefan Leszczyc Zielonacki, deputy judge of the Kalisz land court, Maciej Leszczyc Mierzewski, Tomasz Radoński, boundary surveyor of Kalisz, Stanisław Jabłkowski and Stanisław Otto-Trąmpczyński sign and seal.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Commissioners from the Estates of the Republic for the adjudication of the Case between the Honourable Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, Heir of the estates of Trąbczyn with their appurtenances, on the one part, and the Honourable Stanisław Ścibór Chełmski, Heir of the estates of Łukomia likewise with their appurtenances, on the other part, designated and expressed below in the signatures:

Since, both on account of the currently sitting Ordinary General Sejm in Warsaw and likewise on account of the sitting Crown Tribunal in Piotrków, some of us having obligations in Warsaw and others finding themselves at the said Tribunal with obligations, we cannot bring to a conclusion by final judicial resolution the Action between the aforementioned Parties, commenced at the present term falling from the adjournment made, for the reasons expressed therein, by the Decree issued between those same Parties on the Thirteenth Day of the Month of September of the preceding year — for such reason therefore, the term of the session adjourned to the present day by the above-mentioned Decree [we adjourn] to the First

Monday after the Feast of the Holy Three Kings in the coming year One Thousand Seven Hundred and Seventy-Seven, and we place, adjourn, and postpone it; and we preserve for the parties the peremptory term of appearing before the Commissarial Court, which was to hold its session at that time on the ground of the Łukom estates. By force of the present act. Done at our Residences, on the Twenty-Eighth Day of the Month of September in the Year of the Lord 1776.

Stefan Leszczyc Zielonacki, Deputy Judge of the Kalisz Land Court, Commissioner, by his own hand

Maciej Leszczyc Mierzewski, Commissioner, by his own hand

Tomasz Radoński, Boundary Surveyor of Kalisz, Commissioner, by his own hand

Stanisław Jabłkowski, Commissioner, by his own hand

Stanisław Otto-Trąbczyński, Commissioner, by his own hand

In the Kalisz Municipal Court, on the Monday after the Feast of Saint Michael the Archangel, namely on the 30th Day of the Month of September in the Year of the Lord 1776.

To the office and present records of the Kalisz Municipal Court, appearing in person, the Honourable Antoni Czyżewski, in the name of whoever has an interest therein, presented to the present office for enrollment and for entering into these records the Adjournment of the Boundary Commission between the hereditary estates of Trąbczyn and Łukom, in accordance with the intent of the Commissarial Decree between the Right Honourable Stanisław Chełmski, Heir of the estates of the village of Łukom, on the one part, and Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, on the other part, and others entering an appearance, to be conducted on the ground of those same estates of the village of Łukom — subscribed jointly below in the proper hands of the Illustrious Commissioners and secured with the seals of their arms, of the following tenor: /: Let it be inserted :/. The true original of this same Adjournment, enrolled in the manner written above, has been left here with the present records and sewn into the protocol.
