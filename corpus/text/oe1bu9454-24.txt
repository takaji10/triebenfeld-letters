zur Bezahlung der Intressen und Amortesirung des Capitals

Mit der vollkommesten Hochachtung bin ich

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Prince Hohenlohe's own appeal to a Privy Councillor to negotiate a single loan of 100,000 Rthl, secured by a first and sole mortgage on the colonised South Prussian lordships Zagorowo and Trombschen, with interest and amortisation payable yearly and a 5 pt. gratuity for the agents; power of attorney and documents promised. Appended are Triebenfeld's schedule of the 34 constituent places and a net yearly yield of 40247 Rthl 14 g 11 d certified by the Kalisch Chamber, and a list of debts (Schlabrendorff, Lichnowski, Oppenheimer and Wolff, the Invalids' Fund and others) totalling 366000.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Right Honourable Sir,

Highly Honoured Privy Councillor!

By the colonisation of my South Prussian lordships Zagorowo and Trombschen, carried out with Royal approval, a net yield from the same of 40 Rthl has been ascertained, and after exact examination has been confirmed by the Kalisz Chamber of War and Domains.

This should put me in a position to bring my so long entertained wish to fulfilment, and to arrange my debt affairs entirely. It depends, however, upon whether it is possible to obtain a sum of 100000 Rthl, namely as much as all my debts without exception amount to, in a single lot, against which I would then pledge the two aforenamed lordships, establish complete security upon them with a first and sole mortgage, and would also appoint a yearly sum for the payment of the interest and the amortisation of the capital, whereby this debt could be discharged in a few years.

I most respectfully request Your Right Honourable to undertake this business and, with your wide acquaintance, to negotiate a loan of this kind. The power of attorney necessary thereto I shall transmit by the first post in any form you please. I have likewise instructed my general attorney to deliver to you at once all documents needed for the prosecution of this business, and, for the men of affairs cooperating in these matters, to pay out without delay a d[illegible]ongratie of 5 per cent as soon as the matter is [uncertain: understood]. With the most complete esteem I am Your Right Honourable's

F. L. P. of Hohenlohe. Freyberg, the 25th of September

1806.
