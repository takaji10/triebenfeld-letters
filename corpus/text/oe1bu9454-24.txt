Mit der vollkommensten Hochachtung bin ich
Ewr Hochwohlgeborn

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Der Schreiber bittet einen Geheimen Rath, ein Anlehn von 400000 Rthl zur Tilgung sämtlicher Schulden zu vermitteln, gesichert durch erste Hypotheque auf Zagórów und Trąbczyn. Deren reiner Ertrag sei nach der Colonisirung von der Kalischen Kammer auf 40247 Rthl bestätigt. Vollmacht und Documente sollen folgen. Beiliegen ein Ortsverzeichnis beider Herrschaften (u. a. Trąbczyn, Szetlewek, Przybysław), eine von v Triebenfeld unterzeichnete Ertragsberechnung (Erbzinsen der Colonien 16781 Rthl) und eine Schuldenliste über 366000, darunter Prusimska circa 16000.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The writer asks a privy councillor to arrange a loan of 400000 Rthl to pay off all the debts, secured by a first mortgage on Zagórów and Trąbczyn. He says their net yield after the settlement has been confirmed by the Chamber of Kalisz at 40247 Rthl. A power of attorney and documents are to follow. Enclosed are a list of the places in both lordships (among them Trąbczyn, Szetlewek, Przybysław), a calculation of yield signed by v Triebenfeld (hereditary rents of the colonies 16781 Rthl) and a list of debts totalling 366000, including Prusimska at about 16000.

Right Honourable Sir,

Highly Honoured Sir, Privy Councillor!

Through the colonisation of my South Prussian lordships of Zagórów and Trąbczyn, carried out with Royal approval, a net yield of the same amounting to 40247 rt has been ascertained, and after careful examination has been confirmed by the Kalisz Chamber of War and Domains.

This might put me in a position to bring my so long sought wish to fulfilment, and to arrange my debt affairs entirely. It depends, however, on whether it is possible to obtain a sum of 400000 rt, namely as much as all my debts without exception amount to, in one single lot, against which I would then pledge the two aforenamed lordships, establish complete security thereupon with a first and sole mortgage, and would also fix a yearly sum for the payment of the interest and the amortisation of the capital, whereby this debt might be discharged in a few years.

I most humbly request Your Right Honourable to undertake this business and, with your extensive acquaintance, to negotiate a loan of this kind. The power of attorney necessary for the purpose I shall transmit by the first post in any form you please. I have also instructed my general attorney to deliver to the same at once all the documents needful for the prosecution of this business, and to pay out without delay, for the men of business [uncertain: Geschlußsmanner] concurring in these affairs, a [illegible]gratuity[illegible] of 5 per cent as soon as the matter [uncertain: versteht]. With the most perfect esteem I am Your Right Honourable's F[riedrich] L[udwig], P[rince] o[f] Hohenlohe. Freyberg, the 25th of September

1806.
