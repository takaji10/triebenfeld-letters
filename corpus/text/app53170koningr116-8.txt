--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Eintrag vom 16. Februar 1776. Antoni Lipiński und Nicolaus Ostrowski erscheinen mit dem Gerichtsboten Bartłomiej Szepczyński von Trąbczyn vor dem Burgamt Konin. Im Namen von Antoni Prusimski von Kolno, Starost von Niszczewice und Erbherr von Trąbczyn, verlangen sie die Herausgabe eines Kommissionsdekrets, das kraft der neuen Konstitution auf dem strittigen Grund von Trąbczyn und Łukom als Versäumnisdekret gegen Prusimski ergangen und angeblich dem Amt zur Eintragung eingereicht worden sei. Das Amt hat sein Protokoll durchgesehen: Das Dekret ist bis zum heutigen Tag nicht eingereicht worden; das Amt kann es nicht herausgeben und bescheinigt die Nachsuche. Die beiden haben unterschrieben.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
An entry of 16 February 1776. Antoni Lipiński and Nicolaus Ostrowski appear before the castle office at Konin with the court messenger Bartłomiej Szepczyński of Trąbczyn. In the name of Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice and heir of Trąbczyn, they ask to be given a commission decree that was pronounced under the new constitution on the disputed ground of Trąbczyn and Łukom in default of Prusimski and is claimed to have been brought in to the office for entry. The office has looked through its register: the decree has not been brought in up to this day; the office cannot give it out and certifies the search. Both men signed.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Done in Konin on the Friday next after Sexagesima Sunday, namely on the sixteenth day of the month of February in the Year of the Lord 1776.

On behalf of the Right Honourable Prusimski — Requisition of the Commissorial Decree

Coming in person before the present Municipal Court and Records of Konin, the Right Honourables Antonius Lipiński and Nicolaus Ostrowski, having with them the Honest Bartholomaeus Szepczyński of Trąbczyn, General Royal Court Summoner, being of sound mind, requisitioned and requested from the present Office that there be issued to them on behalf of the Right Honourable Antoni of Kolno Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, heir of the goods of Trąbczyn with its appurtenances, the Commissorial Decree — issued in contumacy of the Right Honourable Prusimski on the contested estate of the goods of Trąbczyn and Łukom by virtue of the New Constitution — as if submitted to the present Records in the manner of an Oblata. The present Municipal Court of Konin, consenting to this Requisition, was willing to issue the said claimed Boundary Field Commissorial Decree; but since, upon review of the Protocol of the Office, the said Decree has not been submitted to the present Records up to this point, it therefore cannot issue the aforesaid Decree, up to and exclusive of —

— the present day, as it is irretrievable here; and makes public attestation of the Requisition by these presents.

Antoni Lipiński, Mikołaj Ostroski
