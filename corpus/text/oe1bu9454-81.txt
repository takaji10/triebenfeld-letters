--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Michaelis kann trotz Drängens von Triebenfeld und Eysenhardt noch nichts Bestimmtes über den Gütertausch melden. Bei der südpreußischen Expedition ist nichts eingegangen, die Sache stecke also noch in der Korrespondenz zwischen Minister v. Voss und dem Geheimen Finanzrat Schulze, und entschieden sei noch nichts. Er erwartet die Vollmacht des Fürsten am kommenden Dienstag. Mit ihr will er sich legitimieren und bei Schulze eine zuverlässige Erkundigung einholen.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
81

Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord.

However much Mr von Triebenfeld and Eysenhardt press me to obtain for them definite news of the present state of the estate exchange, I am nevertheless not in a position to report anything definite to Your Most Serene Highness today. At the South Prussian registry with the expedition office, from which I have just returned, nothing has yet come in; this is a sign that the matter must still be stuck in the correspondence between Minister

v. Voss and the Privy Finance Councillor Schulze, and that nothing has yet been decreed. I do not think it expedient to trouble Schulze so often with my visits, since what he needs to know for the present I already told him the last time; I shall therefore, until

until the arrival of Your Serene Highness's power of attorney [uncertain: further/wait], and since I expect the same next Tuesday, endeavour without delay thereupon to prove my authority therewith and to obtain reliable information from Mr Schulze. I remain, until death, Your Most Serene Highness's Berlin

the 13th of October

1798 most humbly, faithfully and most obediently

Michaelis
