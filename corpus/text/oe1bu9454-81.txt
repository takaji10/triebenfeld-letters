--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports that no news of the proposed exchange of estates has yet reached the dispatch office registry, despite pressure from von Triebenfeld and Eysenhardt for definite information: the business is evidently still passing between Minister von Roth and Privy Councillor Schulze, with nothing yet decreed. The writer, Michaelis, thinks further visits to Schulze pointless for now and will wait for the Prince's power of attorney, expected the following Tuesday, before identifying himself and pressing for a reliable answer.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince,

Most gracious Prince and Lord.

However much Herr von Triebenfeld and Eysenhardt press me to procure them definite news of the present state of the exchange of estates, I am nevertheless not in a position to report anything definite to Your Most Serene Highness today. In the South [uncertain: Greustisch] registry with the dispatch office, from which I have this moment returned, nothing has yet come in; this is a sign that the matter must still be stuck in the correspondence between the Minister

von Roth and the Privy [uncertain: Einweg] Councillor Schulze, and that nothing has yet been decreed. I do not think it expedient to trouble the said Schulze so often with my visits, since what he needs to know for the present I have already told him the time before; I shall therefore, until

until the arrival of Your Serene Highness's power of attorney, and since I look forward to the same next Tuesday, I shall then endeavour without delay to authenticate myself therewith and to obtain a reliable enquiry from Herr Schulze. I remain, until death, Your Most Serene Highness's most humble, faithful and most obedient

Michaelis

Berlin

the 13th of October

1798
