--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Bericht des Gerichtsboten Jan Bekierski von Węgierce, eingetragen in Konin im März 1783. Er hat Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice, eine Ladung des Generalstarosten vom 10. März 1783 vor das Burggericht Kalisz zugestellt, auf Betreiben von Stanisław Chełmski und dessen Verwalter Ignacy Jaroszewski. Prusimski soll bestraft werden wegen eines Einfalls mit Leuten und Hunden in das Gut Łomowo, das zu Chełmskis Hauptgut Łukom gehört, und wegen dort verübter Gewalttaten, und den Schaden ersetzen. Der Bote hat die Ladung bei den Olędern von Trąbczyn im Haus eines Mathias hinterlegt, in Gegenwart von dessen Frau.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A report of the court messenger Jan Bekierski of Węgierce, entered at Konin in March 1783. He has served on Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, a citation of the Starost General of 10 March 1783 before the castle court of Kalisz, at the instance of Stanisław Chełmski and of his steward Ignacy Jaroszewski. Prusimski is to be punished for an inroad with men and dogs into the estate of Łomowo, which belongs to Chełmski's principal estate of Łukom, and for acts of violence done there, and is to make good the damage. The messenger left the citation among the Olęder settlers of Trąbczyn, at the house of one Mathias, in the presence of his wife.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
On behalf of the Right Honourable Chełmski, against His Honour Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — Report

To the office and present records of the Konin Municipal Court, appearing in person, the Honest Jan Bekierski of the village of Węgierce, General Royal Court Summoner, being sound, recognized that he had served authentic citation letters for the Kalisz Municipal Court sessions, of the underwritten tenor as follows:

Casimir Nałęcz Raczyński of Raczyno and Matoszyn, Starost General of Greater Poland — to you, the Illustrious Right Honourable Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, heir of the estates of Trąbczyn and others — from all your estates generally and from all sums of money of every kind, had and to be had, we command: that you appear in person and peremptorily at our next Kalisz Municipal Court sessions, next to be held at the Kalisz Municipal Court, at the instance of the Right Honourable Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, heir of the estates of Łukomia, Łomowa, and others, and of the Honourable Ignacy Jaroszewski, steward of those same estates,

as plaintiff — who, adhering to his manifestos made and to be made, cite you to see and hear: to be adjudged deserving of penalties in respect of the invasion of the estate of Łomowo, which belongs to the plaintiff's hereditary principal estate of Łukom, with a hunting party of men and dogs, and the perpetration therein of various violences of every kind, to be proven by full inquisition; and to be ordered to make good the damages; for which you are to be cited and respond judicially under full penalties.

Given in the Kalisz Municipal Court on 10 March 1783.

In the Hollandry pertaining to the principal estates of Trąbczyn, and in the tavern of the Honest Mathias [?]irtionius, at the table in the heated room, with his consort of the same [condition] present, on this present day, the contents of the present act set down and left notified.
