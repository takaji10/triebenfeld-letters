--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Triebenfeld berichtet nach beiliegenden Schreiben von Brzechffa und Thiele, dass Brzechffa erst am 15. aus Warschau abreisen kann und Geld nach Sławięcice mitbringen will. Brzechffa hat versprochen, die neue Veranlagung der Zagórów-Güter mit 5897 Rthl abzuwenden. Niedźwiecki verlor den Prozess und soll 2000 Rthl zahlen. Die Wrąbczyner Bauern drohen mit Appellation. Landunterrichter Piekarski, Rädelsführer in der Trąbczyner Sache, der die Zinsbeitreibung untersagt und die Kolonisten zu Klagen verleitet hatte, ist suspendiert.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Triebenfeld reports, on the basis of the enclosed letters from Brzechffa and Thiele, that Brzechffa cannot leave Warsaw until the 15th and intends to bring money with him to Sławięcice. He says Brzechffa has promised to avert the new assessment of the Zagórów estates at 5897 Rthl. Niedźwiecki, he reports, lost the lawsuit and is to pay 2000 Rthl, while the Wrąbczyn peasants are threatening to appeal. The district under-judge Piekarski, ringleader in the Trąbczyn affair, who had forbidden the collection of interest and induced the colonists to bring complaints, has been suspended.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince,

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

From the enclosed letters of the sub-prefect von Brzechffa and of the district councillor Thiele, Your Most Serene Highness will deign to gather that Mr von Brzechffa cannot depart from Warsaw before the 15th of this month. Everything is, as I know for certain from his house, prepared for the swiftest journey to Sławięcice after his return. As much money as he shall be able to gather together, he will bring with him. One thing, however, I fear: that Mr von Brzechffa, for the taking over of the Konin sub-prefecture, will have to expend some days' time beyond his journey. Meanwhile this will be of the most beneficial consequences for Your Serene Highness. [uncertain: Mann] has, at the new classification, laid 5897 Rthl upon the Zagórów estates. Mr von Brzechffa has given me his word of honour that he would [uncertain: redress] this and would so direct the matter that no more should be given than hitherto. His proof of effect is then great, and in this matter everything hangs

upon him — Zaborowski and Szymanowski have set this in train so knavishly; through a third party 500 # were demanded for those clean-handed men of business — where was I to get them from? Meanwhile I now hope, through the power and friendship of Mr von Brzechffa, [uncertain: ihm ergandt] to gain more than a loss than through those cut-purses.

The Wrąbczyn peasants have as yet lodged no appeal — but they threaten it — meanwhile this does no harm; the Tribunal's decree will certainly be confirmed in Warsaw. It is a matter of some few weeks' loss of time — the benefit will then be all the more profitable for Your Serene Highness.

The Oleśnica matter is [uncertain: ripe for judgment] and will be taken up as soon as Mr von Brzechffa returns.

With Hawich everything is settled — he receives the salary promised him at that time by [uncertain: Your] Serene Highness / yet only for a year and a half / at 1200 Rthl, which Mr von Brzechffa has promised to pay out to him; but he must give back the 9 Hufen of land together with all the buildings which Hecker, without being empowered thereto, had sold to him, without any compensation whatever — he has on that account already come to an agreement with Mr von Brzechffa and appeals no further

Meanwhile he now rages against Hecker, Honrichs and the former Trąbczyn tenant Ribbeck, who holds the 8 Hufen in Drzewce — he promises not only to dislodge the latter, but also to uncover the hidden knaveries of the two others — Niedźwiecki, instead of paying Your Serene Highness a further 6000 Rthl as he ought, made a demand for 8000 Rthl — the rogue has lost the suit; he is now wholly dismissed with his claim, and, since he has proved and sworn to 4000 Rthl of war costs, is to hand over a further 2000 Rthl to Your Serene Highness — he threatens to appeal to Warsaw and to drag the matter out. Mr von Brzechffa will perhaps, however, show him the way — meanwhile nothing is to be got from the villain, for he has nothing. At present our foremost endeavour is to bring in the arrears of interest — Mr von Brzechffa will have to be very effective in this, all the more since the old wicked lower land judge Piekarski, who was Your Serene Highness's greatest enemy, has been suspended. He is the chief ringleader in the Trąbczyn affair, in which Mr von Brzechffa [uncertain: promises] so much good. Up to this hour the wicked man has forbidden us all collection of interest and has protected the colonists and led them into bringing complaints, whereby everything was dragged out and many thousands might become irrecoverable.

The news of the young Princess has grieved me; I wish that she may soon be fully restored, and that Your Serene Highness may now at the earliest see your Most Serene sons about you. What lies within my powers I shall now [text lost] to come with haste to Sławięcice, in order to see the [uncertain: upright] father together with his good and exalted sons, and then to show Your Serene Highness the deepest reverence, in which I remain, until death, as Your Serene Highness may deign [uncertain: Your] Serene Highness to send the enclosed letter with speed by the long-distance post. There will be no war.

our quarter of the world now

resembles a madhouse —

Blizanow

12th March 1812.

faithful humble and obedient servant

v Triebenfeld
