--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports a letter of 23 November 1804 from Provincial Elder v. Lichnowski demanding 5572 Rthl 12 Groschen in interest arrears accrued from 4 May 1803 to 4 November 1804; expecting payment from the administered Trąbczyn, he obtained a mandate from the Kalisch Government setting a fourteen-day term, now expired, and threatens to apply for sequestration while professing goodwill and hoping for settlement at Christmas. The reply pleads unpaid tenants and v. Triebenfeld's ongoing regulation of affairs.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The provincial elder von Lichnowski 11. writes, dated 23 Nov. 1804, to His Serene Highness

that for a year and a half, from 4 May 1803 until 4 Nov. of this year, he has an arrears of interest of 5572 Rthl 12 Groschen to demand from His Serene Highness; he had believed that, since Trąbczyn was being administered, he would obtain his payment; he had therefore procured a mandate from the government at Kalisz, which imposes payment upon His Serene Highness within 14 days, but he is astonished that these have elapsed and that he has received nothing. He threatens to move for sequestration, wishes [uncertain: abwägerne] to spare His Serene Highness vexation, and desires to continue on good terms, and believes that Christmas would bring the means to satisfy him.

Reply.

the tenants had not paid — he would be satisfied, since Mr. von Triebenfeld was there putting everything in order and settling it

27 Nov. 1804
