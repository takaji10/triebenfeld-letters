--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Aktenauszug: Landesältester v. Lichnowski fordert mit Schreiben vom 23. Nov. 1804 vom Fürsten einen Zinsrückstand von 5572 Rthl 12 gg für anderthalb Jahre (4. Mai 1803 bis 4. Nov.). Ein Mandat der Kalischer Regierung setzte eine Zahlungsfrist von 14 Tagen, die ohne Zahlung verstrichen ist. Er droht mit einem Antrag auf Sequestration, wünscht aber gutes Einvernehmen. Die Antwort lautet, die Pächter hätten nicht gezahlt. Er werde befriedigt werden, da Herr v. Triebenfeld alles in Ordnung bringe.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Extract from the files: in a letter of 23 November 1804 the Senior Land Councillor von Lichnowski demands from the Prince arrears of interest of 5572 Rthl 12 gg for one and a half years (4 May 1803 to 4 November). An order of the Kalisz government set a payment deadline of 14 days, which has passed without payment. He threatens to apply for sequestration, but hopes for good relations. The reply is that the tenants have not paid; he will be satisfied, since Mr von Triebenfeld is putting everything in order.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
150. The provincial elder von Lichnowski writes, dated 23 November 1804, to His Serene Highness:

that for a year and a half, from 4 May 1803 to 4 November of the current year, he has an arrear of interest of 5572 Rthl 12 Groschen to demand of His Serene Highness; he had believed that, since Trąbczyn was [uncertain: administered], he would obtain his payment; he had therefore procured a mandate from the Kalisz government which imposes payment upon His Serene Highness within 14 days, but he is astonished that these have elapsed and that he has received nothing. He threatens an application for sequestration, but would gladly spare His Serene Highness vexation, and wishes to continue on good terms, and believes that Christmas would bring the means to satisfy [uncertain: him].

Reply.

the tenants had not paid — he would be satisfied, since Mr von Triebenfeld was putting everything there in order and setting it straight. — 27 November 1804
