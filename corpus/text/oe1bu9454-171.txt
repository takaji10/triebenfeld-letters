--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports on the Prince's health: the affliction of his foot is almost entirely healed, only slightly swollen and painless, and he is otherwise well. The Order of St Andrew pleased him, but the obliging conduct of the Emperor gratified him still more, and he is enchanted by the young monarch, who reportedly spoke flatteringly of him in Breslau. The Regiment Hohenlohe marched through Erfurt, the Prince riding out to meet it; the Duke of Weimar is expected next day.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Right Honourable Sir, Highly Honoured Sir Councillor of War and Forests,

Since I know that Your Right Honourable takes an interest in news of my most gracious Lord, I permit myself to convey to the same the assurance that His Serene Highness is almost entirely healed of the affliction on his foot, and is otherwise in perfectly good health; the foot is only very little more swollen, and causes no pain. His Serene Highness wishes to be most kindly commended [to you]; the Order of St Andrew has given pleasure, yet the obliging conduct of His Majesty the Emperor has produced still more gratification.

The Prince is quite enchanted by this young and obliging monarch. In Breslau too the Emperor is said to have spoken of His Serene Highness in the most flattering terms. Today the Regiment Hohenlohe marched through here; the Prince had ridden out to meet it. Tomorrow the Duke of Weimar is expected here. I commend myself to Your Right Honourable's favour, and have the honour to remain, in the most unbounded esteem,

Your Right Honourable's

Erfurt, 24 Nov. 1805

humble and obedient servant
A. Hahn
