Euer Hochwohlgebohren,

A. Hahn

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
News of the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen's health, sent by Hahn as a courtesy: the sore on his foot is almost healed and painless, though the foot remains a little swollen, and he is otherwise perfectly well. He sends his regards to Triebenfeld. The Order of St Andrew pleased him, but the Emperor's gracious behaviour pleased him more; he is enchanted by the young monarch, who is said to have spoken flatteringly of him in Breslau.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Right Honourable Sir, Highly Honoured Sir, Councillor of War and Forests. Since I know that news of my most gracious Lord is of interest to Your Right Honourable, I take the liberty of conveying to you the assurance that His Serene Highness is almost entirely healed of the [uncertain: Bibel] on his foot, and in all else perfectly well; the foot is only a little more swollen, and causes no pain. His Serene Highness wishes to be most kindly commended to you; the Order of St Andrew has given pleasure, yet the gracious conduct of His Majesty the Emperor has afforded still more gratification.

The Prince is quite enchanted by this young and gracious monarch. In Breslau too the Emperor is said to have spoken of His Serene Highness in the most flattering terms.
