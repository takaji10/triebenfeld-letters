Personaliter veniens Providus Stanislaus Stachowski de Villa Łukomie Ministerialis Regni Generalis sanus Recognovit se Citt~ Litt~ Authen~ pro Judiciis Castren~ Calissien~ per in Castro Calissien~ Celebran~ Qua ex Parte et ad Instantiam Magnifici Stanislai Chełmski Thaesaurarii Vschoven~ Aliorumque Actt~ In et contra Illustrem Magnificum Antonium In Kolno Prusimski Capitaneum Niesczevicen~ Aliosque Cittos~ Copiae et essentiae futuro in Termino porrigenda Similem editt~ praesen~ Matre familias Die horna Actu Contenta posuisse et notificatt~ reliquisse

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Bericht des Gerichtsboten Stanisław Stachowski von Łukomia, eingetragen in Konin im Mai 1783. Er hat eine Ladung vor das Burggericht Kalisz zugestellt, die Stanisław Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa, und andere gegen Antoni Prusimski von Kolno, Starost von Niszczewice, und andere erwirkt haben.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A report of the court messenger Stanisław Stachowski of Łukomia, entered at Konin in May 1783. He has served a citation before the castle court of Kalisz that Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, and others obtained against Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice, and others.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
On behalf of the Right Honourable Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, against His Honour Prusimski, Captain of Niszczewice — Report

Appearing in person, the Honest Stanisław Stachowski of the village of Łukomia, General Royal Court Summoner, being sound, recognized that he had served authentic citation letters for the Kalisz Municipal Court sessions, next to be held at the Kalisz Municipal Court — on behalf and at the instance of the Right Honourable Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, and others as plaintiffs, against the Illustrious Right Honourable Antoni of Kolno Prusimski, Captain of Niszczewice, and others as cited parties — copies and substantive documents to be produced at the forthcoming session, similarly issued, with the lady of the household present, on this present day, the contents of the present act set down and left notified.
