Durchlauchtigſter Fürſt
Gnädigſter Fürſt und Herr!
Was hier in Ewr Durchlaucht Güter gethan, und in der Folge zu erwarten ist
davon wird der Herr von Heuthausen Ewr Durchlaucht die Rapports und
Resultate vorlegen können.
Einen Anschlag habe ich entworfen und ihm habnnis auffertigen laßen - die
Außerheiten davon sind glänzend und dienen dazu ein vorteilhaftes Tauf
Geschäft abzuschließen, um mich als die 128000 Rthl als eine Leib-Leute
betrachtet werden könne.
Im Fall hier durchlaucht einen desfals mit dem Herrn v. Heuthausen und dem
Herrn Stallmeister Bally entworfenen Plan zu agniren geruhen sollen.
würde es darauf ankommen, daß der H. Weigel und der Herr v. Stöck
nach unſern Wünſchen in der Sache entrirten.
Der Plan ist eigentlich mit dem Gen. Gr: Wartenclaben auf Schurgart zu Bauschen
Schurgent ist für 207000 Rthl gekauft; Es hatte darauf 92000 Rthl Schulden
und es blieben reine 115000
Wann dagegen die Zagorower Güter mit einen Werth von 560000 Rthl angenommen
würden / den etwas muß auf Aufwandsezzung der Vorwerkner im Abzug gebracht
werden / so bliebe nach Abzug der auf Zagorowo stunden 237375 tℓ. Schuldig,
keine
322,625 Rthl davon wurden die 115000 Rthl welche an Schurgard
verbleiben, in Abzug zu bringen seyn, und Ewr Durchlaucht eine reinen Hypotheque
von 207625 Rthl an die Zagorower Güter behalten. Nach unseren Plan solte

nunso Stössel und dh Weigel Schurgast und die hiesige Hypotheque annehmen
wodurch Ewr Durchlaucht von einer Schulden Last um 322625 Rthl befreit würde,
dahin Stöckeln und Weigel zu disponiren, daß sie Schurgart und die Hypothec
nehmen ist eine Haupt Sache. Mit Gr Wartensleben würde ich die Sache
machen. Mit Stöchte aber gebe ich mich nicht ab-
solte indes die Schulden Maße von Stocheln und Weigeln noch viel höher als 322625 Rthl
sein, ſo würde ich brachte mit dem ältesten Sohn des Gr. Wastenleben auf
mein Blizanow zu handeln, und gerne und willig Ewr Durchkauft dies
Gut, welches ich dort einhandte, so wie eine Hypothique von 30000
auf Kontop mit geben, damit die alles beseitigen und Koschenten von
der Hälfte reine machen könte. Ewr Durchlaucht können dann das aus

destinirt ist, mit allen Rechte Bewer zurück fordern, mit diesen Ihnen

Gefalt der Plan Ewr Durchlaucht, so geruhen die gnädigster Dienst
nur Höchst Ihre Meinung durch einen weitenden Bothen wiße zu laßen
Schlaconein dann so fort nach Schlacornechitz, und gehe sogleich zum alten Wackung

mand von diesen Plan ein Werk erfährt, auch die Sache sehr schnell be¬

Mediginal Rath Gumperts in Unterhandlung steht.
Übrigens würde ich mit glühenden Eyfer bemüht sein noch vor der Übergabe der
Zagorow Gäter die beiden Prozeße mit Olemia und Weyberin zu gewinnen,

Was sonsten aus den Rechnungen der verschiedenen Beamte und Sequentors heraus
kommen wird, wird die Zeit lehren, daß inzwischen diese Sache untadelhaft

Die Gnädigster Fürst Reise Geld auf Kosten und zum Leben, und laßen ihm die

mein schönes Kontop zum Teufel ist —
Niemanden habe ich der eifschilft, was ich anwerbe, daß arbeiten soll, wird
Canaille, weil man nicht glaubt daß ein Fürst nicht spenden und auf

Trotz dem allen bleibe ich mit dem tiefsten Ehrerbietigkeit der treuesten
Anhänglichkeit und der tiefsten Hochachtung
Ewr Hochfürstliche Durchlaucht

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Proposes, with Herr von Heuthausen and Master of the Horse Bally, exchanging Schurgast (bought for 207000 Rthl, 92000 in debts) with General Count Wartensleben, valuing the Zagorow estates at 560000 Rthl against 237375 owing, leaving the Prince a clear mortgage of 207625; Stöckel and Weigel must be induced to take Schurgast and the mortgage. Offers his own Blizanow and a 30000 mortgage on Kontop; urges secrecy and haste against Medical Councillor Gumperts. Reports arrears at Betschen, uncollectable Olesnica debts of 35000 fl, the Wrąbczyn contract quashed, lawsuits with Olemia and Weyberin, and his own tenfold arrest and destitution.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince, Most gracious Prince and Lord! What has been done here on Your Serene Highness's estates, and what is to be expected hereafter — of that Herr von Heuthausen will be able to lay before Your Serene Highness the reports and results. I have drawn up a valuation and had him [uncertain: habnnis] draw it out fair — the exteriors of it are brilliant and serve to conclude an advantageous [uncertain: baptism/exchange] transaction, so that I may be regarded as the 128000 Rthl as a life-annuitant. In the event that Your Serene Highness should deign to approve a plan drawn up to that end with Herr v. Heuthausen and with the Master of the Horse Bally, it would depend upon Herr Weigel and Herr v. Stöck entering into the matter according to our wishes. The plan is properly to exchange Schurgart with Gen. Count Wartensleben. Schurgart has been bought for 207000 Rthl; there were 92000 Rthl of debts upon it, and there remained clear 115000. If, on the other hand, the Zagorow estates were taken at a value of 560000 Rthl / for something must be brought into deduction for the [uncertain: putting-up/expenditure] of the demesne farms /, then, after deduction of what stood upon Zagorowo, 237375 tℓ. owing, there would remain clear 322,625 Rthl, from which the 115000 Rthl which remain upon Schurgart would have to be brought into deduction, and Your Serene Highness would retain a clear mortgage of 207625 Rthl upon the Zagorow estates. According to our plan there should

now Stössel and Herr Weigel take over Schurgast and the mortgage here, whereby Your Serene Highness would be freed of a burden of debt of 322625 Rthl; to dispose Stöckel and Weigel so that they take Schurgart and the mortgage is the main thing. With Count Wartensleben I would settle the matter. But with [uncertain: Stöchte] I will have nothing to do — should meanwhile the mass of debts of Stockel and Weigel be much higher still than 322625 Rthl, then I would [uncertain: undertake] to treat with the eldest son of Count Wastenleben over my Blizanow, and would gladly and willingly make over to Your Serene Highness this estate, which I [illegible] there, together with a mortgage of 30000 upon Kontop, so that all might be cleared away and Koschenten made clear of the half. Your Serene Highness can then, with full right, demand back that out of the estate of the Princes Sacken which is destined for the purchase of an entailed estate, arrange with it your whole business of debts, and keep money in hand besides, but also appear with full right as lord of an entailed estate. If the plan pleases Your Serene Highness, then may you most graciously deign only to let your high opinion be known by a [uncertain: returning] messenger; [uncertain: Schlaconein] then at once to Schlacornechitz, and I go immediately to old [uncertain: Wackung haben] to conclude the matter. Only this I remark, that at present no one is to learn a word of this plan, and also that the matter must be pursued very quickly, because Wartenleben has for some time now been in negotiation with the

Medical Councillor Gumperts. For the rest, I would strive with burning zeal to win the two lawsuits with Olemia and Weyberin before the handing over of the Zagorow estates, so that Your Serene Highness might yet draw some 20 to 30000 Rthl from these. What may come out of the accounts of the several officials and sequestrators, time will teach; that in the meantime this business is executed irreproachably, Herr von Heuthausen can [text lost] Your Serene Highness in closer detail. The sum of the arrears will, as I can assure with certainty, be more than considerable, but a great many items will have to be regarded as [uncertain: inexigible], as for example is the story with Olesnica: the three Keels, against whom I then so honestly warned Your Serene Highness, owe 35000 fl. — from these 3 creatures you will never receive, most gracious Lord, a single [uncertain: Groschen], for what can a smith, a journeyman linen-weaver and a village innkeeper have? With Wrąbczyn it is the same case; fortunately the contract has now already been quashed in one instance — Prince, that was a recollection of the conversation which nearly drove me to madness — O my poor children! I have, after your manner, counted the window-panes — now back to business. In Betschen too there is much to do; there stand there some 20000 Rthl in arrears which are due to Your Serene Highness — I cannot go there further, for I have chiefly been with the Councillor of Justice Künsel in Posen, and have [uncertain: enlightened] the matters. Now a man of head and heart must pursue the affair in loco himself

— Heuthausen is, as I have come to know him, a man of head and heart, possesses the knowledge of the language required here, has the proper savoir-vivre to get on among the Poles; give him, most gracious Prince, travelling money for his expenses and for his living, and let him take charge of the matter, for as yet nothing is to be had here. Often I [uncertain: conceal] what I suffer; I show you, Serene Highness, [uncertain: figura] — [uncertain: Schwimmern] is on account of the Invalids' interest, with 9700 on account of 27000 Rthl of bank interest, on account of 4000 Rthl of the Epstein claims, when 3500 of old costs which I have here paid and stood surety for — on account of 4000 Rthl which the Schliefen still have to demand — laid under tenfold arrest. Nothing comes in here, I cannot save myself, my children [uncertain: suffer] with me; in short, I suffer and [uncertain: perish] — For what pains me most keenly is that my beautiful Kontop has gone to the devil — I have no one who [illegible]; whatever I engage that is to work turns rascal, because people will not believe that a prince should not spend and live at the devil's own rate — everyone wants at once to live so along with him — In spite of all this I remain, with the deepest reverence, of the most faithful [uncertain: attachment] and the deepest esteem, Your Most Serene Highness's — the star out of the north shines; more of it by word of mouth. Blizanow, the 21st 7br 1811. Faithful humble and obedient servant v Triebenfeld
