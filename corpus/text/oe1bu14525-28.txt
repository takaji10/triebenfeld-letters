--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Hypothekenschein der Regierung zu Poznań für Friedrich Ludwig, regierenden Fürsten zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, über das Gut Bątkowo im Kreis Radziejów mit dem Gut Szedlemin im Kreis Brześć. Beide gehörten dem Domkapitel zu Włocławek und kamen nach der Einziehung der geistlichen Güter durch die Verleihungsurkunde vom 1. Dezember 1797 an den Fürsten. Eingetragen sind die jährliche Kompetenz des Domkapitels, noch nicht beziffert, und das Verbot, die Güter ohne königliche Erlaubnis an Eingeborene des früheren Polen zu verkaufen; Schulden sind keine eingetragen.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A mortgage certificate of the Government at Poznań for Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, on the estate of Bątkowo in the Radziejów district with the estate of Szedlemin in the Brześć district. Both belonged to the cathedral chapter of Włocławek and came to the Prince, after the church estates were taken over by the state, by the deed of grant of 1 December 1797. Entered on them are the yearly allowance due to the chapter, not yet fixed in amount, and the ban on selling the estates to natives of former Poland without royal consent; no debts are entered.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The noble estate of Bątkowo, situated under the jurisdiction of the Royal South Prussian Government at Poznań in the Radziejewo district.

Together with the estate of Siedlemin situated in the Brześć district, the reigning Prince Friedrich Ludewig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen has received these, as formerly ecclesiastical estates which had belonged to the cathedral chapter at Włocławek, following the confiscation of all ecclesiastical estates in South Prussia, from His Majesty King Friedrich Wilhelm the Third, according to

the deed of grant dated Berlin, 1 December 1797, by heritable and proprietary grant; and title of possession has been entered for him on the ground of this deed of grant and of the letter of resignation made out on 10 June 1802, by decree of 19 July 1802.

Thereupon are entered

I. Perpetual burdens and restrictions upon the ownership or the disposition

1., the annual allowance due to the cathedral chapter at Włocławek out of these estates

annual allowance, which, because the fixed quantum has not been stated and certified, has been entered as an indeterminate sum on the ground of the deed of grant of His Majesty King Friedrich Wilhelm the Third of 1 December 1797 for the reigning Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, upon the entry of the title of possession in his name, ex officio by force of the decree of 19 July 1802.

2.) The sovereign prohibition, that

the estates which, having been confiscated from the ecclesiastical estates in South Prussia, have again been granted with noble rights may not be alienated to native-born inhabitants of the former Poland without express supreme royal consent; which prohibition has been entered against these formerly ecclesiastical estates upon the entry of the title of possession in the name of the reigning Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, ex officio by force of the decree of 19 July 1802,

and so has been done.

II. Judicially secured debts and other real obligations: none are entered. Apart from the foregoing items, nothing is entered against the estate of Bątkowo in the mortgage book, and for the rest the present mortgage certificate is hereby issued to the reigning Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen in the force of a recognition, on account of the title of possession entered for him. In witness whereof, under

the signature ordained for the Royal South Prussian Government and the greater seal. Given at Poznań, 26 August 1802. Götze

Dieterichs.

Fest. John.
