--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Heads of purchase for the noble estate of Witów in the Warta district of the Kalisz department, sold by forest inspector Christian Friedrich Rochlitz for the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen to Martin Honrichs and Walęty von Kwasiborski for 16,000 Rthl in three instalments at 5 per cent, with no warranty for 1016 składy claimed by the burgesses of Warta, a fallback one-year lease at 800 Rthl, and a 1000 Rthl penalty; appended is the Prince's cession of 8,000 Rthl to the Breslau banker Carl Ferdinand Weigel, attested by the St Vincent foundation justice office.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
E. W. v. Rösch — between the special authorised representative of His Most Serene Highness the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Forest Inspector Rochlitz, as seller on the one part, and Martin Honrichs and Valentin v. Kwasieborski, as purchasers on the other part, there has, with due deliberation, been agreed and concluded under today's date the following points of contract, for the purpose of a formal judicial contract of purchase and sale to be concluded hereafter concerning the noble estate of Witów.

The said Forest Inspector Rochlitz sells, in the name of His Most Serene Highness the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, the aforesaid his Lord and principal, the noble estate lying in the Royal South Prussian Kalisz Government Department and in its Warta district, namely the village and village mayor's holding of Witów with all appurtenant rights and privileges, nothing thereof excepted, just as the same was, pursuant to the Most High deed of donation of the most blessed King Frie-

-drich Wilhelm the Second, conferred upon Colonel von Schlieben, and by his widow and heirs, pursuant to the judicial contract of purchase of 22 October 1799, conveyed to His Most Serene Highness — to the said Martin Honrichs and Valentin v. Kwasieborski, and this for a purchase price deliberately agreed and fixed between the contracting parties of 16000 rt, in words sixteen thousand Reichsthaler Prussian Courant. 2: this purchase price the purchasers bind themselves to pay in solidum, that is to say, one for both and both for one, to His Most Serene Highness in the following instalments, namely at St John the Baptist of this year 4000 Rt, say four thousand Reichsthaler; at Christmas of this year 6000 rtl., say six thousand Reichsthaler; and at Midsummer 1804 the remainder with 6000 rt., say six thousand Reichsthaler; and until the arrears are fully paid, to pay interest on the same at 5 per cent.

3. His Most Serene Highness affords the purchasers warranty of title only for those parcels of land actually held in possession and to be handed over to them at the

delivery of possession. In respect of those 1016 składy of arable which may be evicted at the suit of the burgesses of Warta, and which are not in the possession of His Most Serene Highness, no warranty of title is afforded; rather it remains the purchasers' affair alone to vindicate the said fields at their own entire risk and cost, against which, however, His Most Serene Highness relinquishes all claims as against the purchasers, should they in fact recover these składy of arable against the burgesses of Warta, whether in whole or in part.

4. The handing over of this sold estate to the purchasers shall take place 14 days after Midsummer of this year, at which time, concurrently and against each other, the first instalment of the 4000 rtll. must be paid, since until this is done the handing over cannot take place.

5. At this handing over there shall be handed over as well such ground [text as transcribed: "Grund zuverloren"] and livestock and implements as are found upon the estate

and is specified in the judicial deed of transfer on the part of the tenant Knoblauch to von Bloch of Blotniz of 10 and 11 July of last year, excluding the surplus inventory in horses and oxen taken away at that time by Knoblauch pursuant to the deed of 11 July, which for that very reason are no longer to be found upon the estate and for that very reason cannot be handed over with it.

6. The seller promises to procure the consent to the purchasers' acquisition of this estate if possible at the latest by St John's of this year; should the same however not be forthcoming, then the present purchase falls away.

7. Should the final resolution regarding the said consent be delayed longer than 14 days after St John's of this year, in such wise that the handing over has already taken place and the first instalment of the purchase price has already been paid, but the resolution in the matter should then turn out

negative, then the contracting parties, to avoid all disputes concerning the revenues drawn, have agreed that the purchasers shall retain possession of this estate for one year, namely until 14 days after St John's Day 1804, by way of lease, but shall for that year pay to the seller a rent of 800 rtl., say eight hundred Reichsthaler, and shall likewise discharge all public dues concerning this estate, by whatever name they may be called, upon the assessment ex Propriet, without being permitted to bring anything thereof into account against the seller; and the purchasers, in this last event, renounce any and all remissions and other demands whatsoever, by whatever name they may be called, but bind themselves, subject to the most summary execution, to vacate the estate of Witów 14 days after St John's 1804 in good husbandry condition, and submit themselves in this

regard to the process of execution with all its legal consequences; it being however of course understood that in such event, upon their vacating the estate, the 4000 rtl. paid must be repaid to them by the seller, after deduction of the stipulated lease rent of 800 rt and of such public dues and costs as they could not show to have been paid at their departure.

8. The estate shall be handed over to the purchasers with the winter and summer sowing fully carried out, and the purchasers hereby declare that they accept the winter sowing as duly sown, and expect only the like in respect of the summer sowing.

9. The documents concerning this estate shall be delivered over to the purchasers immediately upon the arrival of the consent to their acquisition [uncertain: Frühige].

10.

10. Finally, the contracting parties renounce all objections and evasions running counter to these points of purchase, by whatever name they may be called, and in particular the purchasers renounce the plea of injury in and beyond the half; and they set, for the case of non-performance or non-fulfilment of this contract, a vadium or conventional penalty of 1000 rtll, say one thousand Reichsthaler, which the contravening party shall be bound to pay to the other, and nonetheless to perform the contract.

11. The costs of this contract both parties assume by halves; those of the rectification of the titulus possessionis in the purchasers' names, the latter alone.

Both parties have, this instrument of purchase points having been read by them themselves — which was explained to the co-purchaser v. Kwasiborski by his socius Honrichs in the Polish tongue — after prior

approval, executed the same with their own signatures manual.

Kalisz, 21 April 1803. At the signing, both parties observed to me that, in respect of the buildings standing upon the village mayor's holding, which the tenants there claim to have built themselves, no warranty of title is afforded to the purchasers by the seller; but that, in case the tenants' statement should prove correct, the purchasers must come to a separate settlement with them on that account.

Done as above. Christian Friedrich Rochlitz. Martin Honrichs.

Walęty v. Kwasiborski

Carl Anton Grimm
