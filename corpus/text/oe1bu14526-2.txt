--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Punctation of purchase in which Forest Inspector Christian Friedrich Rochlitz, acting for Fürst Friedrich Ludwig zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, sells the village and Scholtisey Witow in the Kalisch department, Warta district, to Martin Honrichs and Walęty v. Kwasiborski for 16,000 Reichsthaler in instalments, with no Eviction warranty for 1016 Skladen claimed by the burghership of Warta, a fallback one-year lease at 800 rtl., and a 1000 rtl. penalty; appended is the Fürst's cession of 8000 rtl. of the purchase money to banker Carl Ferdinand Weigel, attested at Breslau by the Justice Office of the Chapter of St. Vincenz.

E. W. v. Rösch. Between the Special Plenipotentiary of thy Most Serene Highness the reigning Lord Fürst zu Hohenlohe Ingelfingen, Herr Forest Inspector Rochlitz, as seller on the one part, and Herr Martin Honrichs and Herr Valentin v. Kwasieborski as purchasers on the other part, there is, for the purpose of a formal judicial contract of purchase and sale to be concluded hereafter concerning the noble estate of Witow, the following Punctation agreed upon and concluded under this day's date after mature deliberation.

The said Herr Forest Inspector Rochlitz sells, in the name of His Most Serene Highness the reigning Lord Fürst zu Hohenlohe Ingelfingen, the aforesaid, his Lord Principal's noble estate situated in the Royal South Prussian Kalisch Government Department and its Warta district, namely the village and Scholtisey Witow, with all rights and appurtenances thereto pertaining, nothing thereof excepted, in such wise as the same, pursuant to the Most High deed of donation [uncertain: Donations-Urkunde] of the most blessed King Frie-

-drich Wilhelm the Second, [uncertain: were granted to] Colonel v. Schlieben, and were conveyed by his widow and heirs, pursuant to the judicial contract of purchase of the 22nd of October 1799, to thy Most Serene Highness; unto the said Herr Martin Honrichs and Valentin v. Kwasieborski, and this indeed for a purchase price agreed and determined between the contracting parties after mature deliberation of 16000 rt, written out sixteen thousand Reichsthaler Prussian Courant.

2. This purchase price the purchasers bind themselves to pay in solidum, that is to say, one for both and both for one [uncertain], to His Most Serene Highness in the following instalments, namely at Johannes Baptista a. c. 4000 Rt, say four thousand Reichsthaler; at [uncertain: Weihnachten] a. c. 6000 rtl., say six thousand Reichsthaler; and at Johanni 1804 the remainder with 6000 rt., say six thousand Reichsthaler; and until the full payment of the arrears to pay interest upon the same at 5 per cent.

3. His Most Serene Highness grants to the purchasers Eviction warranty only for the parcels actually in possession and to be handed over to them at the

tradition. In respect of those 1016 Skladen of arable land which may be [uncertain: recovered] by the burghership of Warta, and which are not in the possession of thy Most Serene Highness, no warranty of Eviction is granted; rather it remains the purchasers' business alone to vindicate the arable in question at their own entire risk and cost, whereas, however, His Most Serene Highness renounces all claims against the purchasers should they in fact win these Skladen of arable, whether wholly or in part, against the burghership of Warta.

4. The delivery of this sold estate to the purchasers shall take place 14 days after Johanni a. c., at which same time and place the first instalment of the 4000 rtll. must be paid, since until this be done the delivery cannot take place.

5. At this delivery there shall be handed over together with it that [uncertain: ground stock] and cattle and implements which are found upon the estate

and is specified in the judicial deed of delivery [Uebergabe-Receß] on the part of the lessee Knoblauch to v. Bloch of Blotniz of the 10th and 11th of July a. p., exclusive of the plus Inventarii in horses and oxen taken away at that time by Knoblauch pursuant to the Receß of the 11th of July, which for that very reason are no longer to be found upon the estate and for that same reason cannot be handed over with it.

6. The Herr Seller promises to procure the consent to the acquisition of this estate for the purchasers, if possible at the latest by Johann a. c.; should the same, however, not be forthcoming, then the purchase in question falls to the ground.

7. Should the Final Resolution concerning the consent in question be delayed longer than 14 days beyond Johann a. c., in such wise that the delivery shall already have taken place and the first instalment of the purchase price shall already have been paid, but the resolution in question should then turn out in the negative

in the negative, then the contracting parties have, for the avoidance of all [uncertain: disputes] concerning the revenues drawn, agreed as follows: that the purchasers shall retain this estate in possession by way of lease for one year, namely until 14 days after St. Johanni 1804, but shall in return pay to the Herr Seller for this year a lease-sum of 800 rtl., say eight hundred Reichsthaler; likewise shall discharge all public dues concerning this estate, by whatever name they may be called, upon the [uncertain: Competenz ex Propriet], without being permitted to bring anything to the Herr Seller's account therefor; and the purchasers renounce, for this last case, each and every remission and other demand whatsoever, by whatever name they may be called, but bind themselves, upon the readiest execution, to vacate the estate of Witow 14 days after Johann 1804 in good husbandly condition, and submit themselves in this

behalf to the executive process with all its legal consequences; it being however understood of itself that in this case, upon the vacating of the estate, the 4000 rtl. paid must be repaid to them by the Herr Seller, after deduction of the [uncertain: stipulated] lease-pension of 800 rt and of those public dues and costs which they could not prove to have been paid at their departure.

8. [uncertain: Furthermore] the estate is handed over to the purchasers with the winter and summer sowing fully put in, and the purchasers hereby declare that they accept the winter sowing as duly sown, and only await the like in respect of the summer sowing.

9. The documents concerning this estate shall, immediately upon the arrival of the consent, be handed out to them for their [uncertain: purpose of acquisition].

Finally the contracting parties renounce all objections and evasions running counter to this Punctation of purchase, by whatever name they may be called, in particular the purchasers renounce the plea of injury in and above the half [laesio enormis]; and they set, for the case of non-observance or non-performance of this contract, a vadium or conventional penalty of 1000 rtll., say one thousand Reichsthaler, which the contravening party shall be bound to pay to the other, and nevertheless to perform the contract.

11. The costs of this contract both parties assume by halves; those of the rectification of the Titulus Possessionis in the purchasers' names, the latter alone.
