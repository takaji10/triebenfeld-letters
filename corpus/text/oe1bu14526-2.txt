--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Punctation of purchase for the noble estate, village and Scholtisey Witow in the Warta district of the Kalisch Department, sold by Forest-Inspector Christian Friedrich Rochlitz for Fürst Friedrich Ludwig zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen to Martin Honrichs and Walęty v. Kwasiborski for 16000 Reichsthaler in three instalments, with no eviction-warranty for 1016 Skladen claimed by the burghership of Warta, a fallback one-year lease at 800 rtl, and a 1000 rtl penalty; appended is the Fürst's cession of 8000 rtl to the Breslau banker Carl Ferdinand Weigel, attested by the St. Vincenz Justice-Office.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
E. W. v. Rösch. Between the special Bevollmächtigter of thine High Princely Serene Highness the reigning Lord Fürst zu Hohenlohe Ingelfingen, Herr Forest-Inspector Rochlitz, as seller of the one part, and Herr Martin Honrichs and Herr Valentin v. Kwasieborski, as purchasers of the other part, the following Punctation has, under today's date and after mature deliberation, been agreed and concluded for the purpose of a formal judicial contract of purchase and sale to be concluded hereafter concerning the noble estate Witow.

The said Herr Forest-Inspector Rochlitz sells, in the name of his High Princely Serene Highness the reigning Lord Fürst zu Hohenlohe Ingelfingen, of the Most Highly Mentioned his Lord Principal, the noble estate situated in the Royal South Prussian Kalisch Government Department and in its Warta district, namely the village and Scholtisey Witow with all appurtenant rights and privileges, nothing thereof excepted, just as the same was, pursuant to the Most High deed of donation [uncertain: DonctionsUmlünde] of the most blessed King Frie-

drich Wilhelm the Second, conferred upon Colonel v. Schlieben, and by his widow and heirs, pursuant to the judicial contract of purchase of the 22nd of October 1799, made over in property to thine High Princely Serene Highness; [sells it] to the said Herr Martin Honrichs and Valentin v. Kwasieborski, and that for a purchase price, well and deliberately agreed and determined between the contracting parties, of 16000 rt, write sixteen thousand Reichsthaler Prussian Courant.

2: This purchase price the purchasers bind themselves to pay in solidum, that is to say the one for both and both for the one, to his High Princely Serene Highness in the following instalments, namely at Johann Baptiste of the current year 4000 Rt, say four thousand Reichsthaler; at Christmas of the current year 6000 rtl., say six thousand Reichsthaler; and at Johanni 1804 the remainder with 6000 rt., say six thousand Reichsthaler; but until the complete payment of the arrears to pay interest thereon at 5 per cent.

3. His High Princely Serene Highness grants the purchasers eviction-warranty only for the parcels actually held in possession and to be handed over to them at the

delivery of possession. In respect of those 1016 Skladen of arable which may be evicted by the burghership of Warta, and which is not in the possession of thine High Princely Serene Highness, no eviction-warranty is given; rather it is the purchasers' affair alone to vindicate the said fields at their own entire risk and cost, against which, however, thine High Princely [Serene Highness] renounces all claims upon the purchasers should they in fact recover these Skladen of arable, whether wholly or in part, against the burghership of Warta.

4. The delivery of this sold estate to the purchasers shall take place 14 days after Johanni of the current year, at which time also, hand to hand [zug und zur], the first instalment of the 4000 rtll. must be paid, since before this is done the delivery cannot take place.

5. At this delivery there shall be handed over along with it that grain [uncertain: zuverloren] and the livestock and implements which are found upon the estate

and which is specified in the judicial deed of delivery on the part of the tenant Knoblauch to v. Bloch of Blotniz of the 10th and 11th of July of the past year, exclusive of the plus Inventarii in horses and oxen taken away at that time by Knoblauch according to the Recess of the 11th of July, which for that very reason are no longer to be found upon the estate and for that very reason cannot be handed over with it.

6. The Lord Seller promises to procure the consent to the purchasers' acquisition of this estate if possible at the latest by Johann of the current year; should the same not be forthcoming, however, the purchase in question falls to the ground.

7. Should the final resolution concerning the consent in question be delayed longer than 14 days after Johann of the current year, in such wise that the delivery has already taken place and the first instalment of the purchase price has already been paid, but the resolution in question should then turn out

unfavourably, then the contracting parties, in order to avoid all disputes concerning the revenues drawn, have agreed as follows: that the purchasers shall retain this estate in possession for one year, namely until 14 days after St. Johanni 1804, by way of lease, but shall for this year pay to the Lord Seller a lease sum of 800 rtl., say eight hundred Reichsthaler, and shall likewise discharge all public dues concerning this estate, by whatever name they may be called, upon their own account [Competenz ex Propriet], without being permitted to bring anything into account against the Lord Seller therefor; and the purchasers renounce, in this last case, any and every remission and other demand whatsoever, by whatever name they may be called, and bind themselves, under the readiest execution, to vacate the estate Witow 14 days after Johann 1804 in good husbandlike condition, and submit themselves in this

regard to the executive process with all its legal consequences; it being however understood of itself that in this case there must be repaid to them by the Lord Seller, upon the vacating of the estate, the 4000 rtl. paid, after deduction of the stipulated lease-rent of 800 rt and of such public dues and costs as they could not show to have been paid at their departure.

8. The estate shall be handed over to the purchasers with the winter [uncertain: Vater] and summer sowing fully put in, and the purchasers herewith declare that they accept the winter sowing as duly put in, and only expect the like in respect of the summer sowing.

9. The documents concerning this estate shall be extradited to the purchasers immediately upon the arrival of the consent to their acquisition [uncertain: Frühige].

10.

10. Finally the contracting parties renounce all objections and evasions running counter to this Punctation of purchase, by whatever name they may be called, and in particular the purchasers renounce the plea of injury in and beyond the half [laesio enormis], and set, for the case of non-performance or non-fulfilment of this contract, a vadium or conventional penalty of 1000 rtll., say [uncertain: sie] thousand Reichsthaler, which the contravening party shall be bound to pay to the other and nevertheless to fulfil the contract.

11. The costs of this contract both parties take upon themselves by halves; those of the rectification of the Titulus Possessionis in the purchasers' names, the latter alone.

Both parties have, after previous approval, this Punctation of purchase read out by themselves, which was explained to the co-purchaser v. Kwasiborski by his socius Honricht in the Polish tongue, after previous

approval, executed with their own-hand signatures.

Kalisch, the 21st of April 1803. — Upon the signature both parties remarked to me that in respect of the buildings standing upon the Scholtisey, which the tenants there claim to have built themselves, no eviction-warranty is given to the purchasers by the Lord Seller, but that, in case the tenants' statement should be correct, the purchasers must come to a separate settlement with them on that account.

Done as above.
