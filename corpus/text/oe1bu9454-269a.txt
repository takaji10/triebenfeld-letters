--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Copy of a plea on behalf of Prince August, forwarding a file of documents including the exchange with Minister von Kircheisen which the court refused to enter in its protocol. Citing medical councillor Cossnar's letter of 22 June 1810, Triebenfeld charges Cosmar with placing everything under arrest over the Kurz heirs' claim of 300,000 Rthl, which could be settled for 112,000 Rthl, only 12,000 in cash plus an instruction for 100,000; he pledges his property and liberty as security and urges administration of the entailed estates.

Copy

Most Serene Prince etc. In accordance with the commission of the Prince of Hohenlohe I am to present to Your Most Serene Highness the accompanying file of documents [uncertain: allerzubeißen]. It contains the correspondence with His Excellency Minister von Kircheisen, which the [uncertain: bringen] Q. L. court would not take up into the protocol kept, because it had not been commissioned to do so. Since, however, from the medical councillor Cossnar's letter of the 22nd of June 1810 under S: the most unmistakable [uncertain: Chirann] against the poor good Prince emerge, especially as Cormas himself admits that, on account of the claim of the Kurz heirs for 300000 Rthl, which the Prince alone undertook to contest, he has stinted the Prince in everything and laid it all under arrest, I hasten most humbly to lay the file of documents at Your Most Serene Highness's feet. Your Most Serene Highness will gather from it how highly necessary it is [uncertain: nur wischen] to satisfy the Kurz heirs. The heirs are quite certainly to be satisfied, according to the proofs held in hand, with 112000 Rthl; indeed only 12000 Rthl in cash is demanded at once, and for the 100000 Rthl a secure instruction to pay. - Should Your Most Serene Highness approve these requests and allow the sum to be advanced to the upright old Prince, while all the entailed estates are immediately administered on behalf of the treasury before they are given over, then an honest Prince is at once out of Cosmar's claws, and saved through Your Most Serene Highness's grace, upon which alone he counts. I bind myself in return, on forfeit of my whole property and my liberty, to deliver the debt [uncertain: Intravante] and above all the guarantee of His Majesty into Your Serene Highness's hands forthwith. The one thing that remains to be wished, however, is that the Monarch, if today or tomorrow the K. heirs should once more approach His Majesty concerning their satisfaction, should disclaim the matter, as has already happened, whereby everything will quickly be [uncertain: erricht], and the King's word remain sacred. Cosmar lies unmasked and trodden in the dust, particularly if he is held to render account with the utmost dispatch, all the more since Prince August is now recalling the power of attorney given to him.

Funds for the discharge of the 112000 Rthl as well as of the 35000 are present in abundance; indeed even the 18000 Rthl which Prince August is to draw yearly, and of which some 54000 Rthl already lie in store, can be [uncertain: verweint] applied to it out of hand, and thus the debt of 147/ discharged in a very short time. Should Your Most Serene Highness [uncertain: Er führen] this most humble request, then the good Prince, who all unguilty has already for 9 years endured the most [uncertain: anlaſaſten] sufferings, is saved through Your Most Serene Highness's grace, and every creditor set at rest. - In [uncertain: unbegragter] reverence I remain, until death,

Your Most Serene Highness's v. Triebenfeld

Vienna, the 24th of January

1815
