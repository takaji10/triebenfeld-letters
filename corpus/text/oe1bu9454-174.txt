--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Rechnet vor, dass ein Justitiarius, der auch den Posten eines Rendanten übernimmt, durch Ersparnisse so viel einbringe, wie Schenck erhält; die Sporteln würden auf wenigstens 1200 Rthl steigen. Bittet, Hahn nach Świniary zu schicken, um Amtmann Käufer und Förster Peltzold zu verwarnen. In Trąbczyn warten Kaufwillige auf etliche Huben Land gegen Barzahlung. Erwähnt die 1390 Rthl für Lichnowski und sendet Amelangs Brief zurück. Wiesolowski berichtet von russischen Truppen, zusammen über 300/m Mann.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
174 the actuary is always paid separately, and is brought in at every act, this deceit comes yearly to some 200 rt. The carriage-hire one could likewise reckon, together with the board — 200 rt, and consequently there is already a profit here. And if it be further assumed that he takes on as well the post of a cashier and all the other business, then here again at least some 600 rt will be saved, and in all as much gained as Schenck receives. Besides this the fees will rise in future and will amount to at least some 1200 rt; in this way the justiciary will cost nothing, since no advocate need be kept any longer, and there will still remain several hundred as the annual surplus.

Always shall I, wherever anything can be effected by my good will, strive with the most ardent zeal to further Your Serene Highness's advantage. But it is a misfortune for me that, while I labour here restlessly for Your Serene Highness, my own husbandry goes entirely to ruin — I cannot see to my estates, because I must be here. My people manage without restraint, and, since I never come there at all, they cheat me frightfully — Let Your Serene Highness therefore show me the grace to send Hahn to Świniary after all, and to have it said to my district administrator Käufer and to the forester Peltzold that Your Serene Highness were, as time would teach, interested in Świniary — There was talk that the officials managed badly

managed. — She had it intimated to him that we do not watch over the advantage of their lord and would prevent all detriment, that they should be dreadfully chastised.

I hope this will have its effect and that the fellows would not go on cheating quite so unobserved, for a prince is after all feared more than a knight errant wandering about. Has Your Serene Highness had the 1390 rt sent to Lichnowski? I have firmly promised it to the man.

Several people are waiting for me in Trąbczyn who wish to buy some Hufen of land and to pay at once in cash; as soon as I get out there I shall see to this, and then Your Serene Highness will have the money back directly — This would have been done already, but I cannot drive away from here and leave the most important matter of all standing half way.

The Amelang letter I send back — May Your Serene Highness be pleased to write to the patron after all.

"That you certainly reckoned upon the 100/m, with security so sufficient, for the 50/m rt which had been given would have been, but no luck — Triebenfeld had [reported] to you the difficulty on account of the revision of the assessments sooner than he notified; but you held this to be a mere whim and reckoned upon his savoir faire and great influence, that he would conduct the matter in such a way

as to leave one content, with security so great, with the revision by the district councillor, with the attestation and guarantee, the more so as within a short time much higher revenues, which the Chamber itself approves and which consist of pure interest throughout, would be ascertained."

That too will have its effect.

Of news one hears nothing at all here; I am here as though I sat in Siberia. Has Your Serene Highness anything, then communicate it to your servant.

My cousin Colonel von Wiesolowski is here from Russia; by his assurance, and for what he vouches, the [uncertain: Besitz] Emperor has a handsome army already upon the tree. In our region, that is at Kijow and as far as Odessa, there stand 80000 men — at Welna 60000 men, and just now there come forth further 20 [uncertain: Palch] [illegible]onasche Cossacks — each debt is 2000 März strong, that is 180000 men on this side alone. In Courland and other places considerable corps have likewise been drawn together, so that above 300/m men stand mobile there.
