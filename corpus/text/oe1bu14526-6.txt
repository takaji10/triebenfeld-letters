--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Hereditary-lease and purchase contract before the Hohenlohe Patrimonial Court at Trąbczyn granting the farmer Blazy Kukulski one Hufe of arable land, Magdeburg measure, at Osiny in perpetuity for 100 Reichsthaler purchase-money, already receipted, and 30 Reichsthaler yearly quit-rent payable half-yearly at Johanni and Christmas; Kukulski bears all public dues, chimney-money, forage and Vorspann, builds at his own cost, remains bound to manorial drink, mill and jurisdiction, owes a tenth-Groschen Laudemium on sale, and renounces all remissions. Filed with it are von Triebenfeld's general power of attorney of 19 February 1805 for Zagorowo, Wittow and Trąbczyn, its Guhrwitz attestation, and the engrossment.

The Serene [uncertain: Dürchlauch] von Hohenlohe Patrimonial Court hereby makes known that between the Herr Krieg es und derst Rath von Triebenfeld, as General Bevollmächtigter of His Serene Highness the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe Ingelfingen on the one part, and Blazy Kukulski on the other part, the following contract of sale and purchase concerning One Hufe of land, Magdeburg measure, situated by the estate of Osiny, has been concluded and published.

Actum Trąbczyn, the eighteenth of July, one thou-

sand eight hundred and

six.

Before the undersigned local Patrimonial Court there appeared in person

1st

1. the Herr Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, as General Bevollmächtigter of [uncertain: Er durch haupt] the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe Ingefingen, sole hereditary and jurisdictional lord, legitimated by the power of attorney here appended, on the one part, and

2. the farmer Blazy Kukulski from the princely estate of Osniy on the other part. The comparents are precisely known to the undersigned Justitiarius, and their capacity for the performance of judicial acts is subject to no doubt of mine. They declare freely and after due deliberation the following irrevocable [contract] concluded between them

and in no respect alterable hereditary-lease contract, which they hereby forthwith publish to the judicial protocol.

§. I. His Most Serene Highness namely grants to Blazy Kukulski

1 Hufe, in words One Hufe Magdeburg measure, the Hufe reckoned at thirty Morgen and each Morgen at one hundred and eighty square rods, in [uncertain: Kotikale Czazy w Kierzäwn] arable land situated by Osiny, in hereditary lease for perpetual times.

§. II. Huba Maydebuska: and this One Hufe Magdeburg measure has already been assigned to Blazy Kukulski and made over into natural possession

over to him, with which the same is entirely content.

§. III. Blazy Kukulski pays for this one Hufe Magdeburg measure as purchase-money 100 rtl., in words one hundred Reichsthaler, and since he has already discharged this purchase-money in full, he is hereby given receipt for it.

§. IV. Furthermore Blazy Kukulski hereby binds himself, for himself and [uncertain: keine] his successors in possession, to pay to the ground lordship from this one Hufe Magdeburg measure a yearly hereditary quit-rent of 30 rtl., in words thirty Reichsthaler, in cash current Courant; and this hereditary quit-rent shall now and

for the future be discharged in half-yearly instalments, namely the one at Midsummer (Johanni) and the other at Christmas, each time post numerando; and the first half-yearly hereditary quit-rent shall only be paid off at Christmas 1806.

§. V. The settlement and rendering of the Royal and other public dues falling upon this one Hufe, without any distinction whatever, as also the chimney-money, forage delivery and carting service (Vorspann), the hereditary lessee takes upon himself now and for perpetual times, without any deduction from the hereditary quit-rent, or demanding any reimbursement for it from the ground lordship.

In return, the hereditary lessee is now and for perpetual times entirely free of any and all manorial labour services, as also of hereditary subjection.

§. VI. The hereditary lessee must set up and build his establishment wholly at his own cost, and receives for this purpose neither now nor in future any building timber or other building assistance, and just as little any remission of the yearly hereditary quit-rent; however, all the timber standing on this Hufe remains without exception his property, and he may dispose of it without restriction.

§. VII. Outside his own holding the hereditary lessee has no right

to firewood or pasturage in the manorial woods and fields.

§. VIII. The hereditary lessee must always take his drink from the manorial brewery and distillery, or from the tavern.

If foreign drink is brought in by him or his people, the hereditary lessee incurs each time a penalty of Five Reichsthaler for every [uncertain: Tane] of beer and one Reichsthaler for every Garniec of brandy, which penalty is to be paid to the ground lordship as compensation.

§. IX. The hereditary lessee remains always subject to the jurisdiction of the ground lordship, as also to the milling compulsion in the manorial estates.

§. X. The hereditary lessee is entitled to dispose of this holding and to alienate it elsewhere at his pleasure.

The ground lordship, however, hereby reserves to itself once and for all the right of pre-emption; and if it does not wish to exercise the same, then the tenth Groschen of the purchase or exchange sum must be paid by the seller of the time to the ground lordship as a Laudemium.

On inheritances passing to children and other relatives no Laudemium is to be paid.

§. XI.

The hereditary lessee renounces entirely, for himself and his successors in possession, any and all remissions in cases of misfortune, whether these arise through fire from heaven, incendiar[y] malice of wicked people, hail-stroke, crop failure, cattle plague, war and pestilence, or in whatever manner it may [uncertain: mir] be; and in the event of such cases neither a bonification nor

remission of the hereditary quit-rent shall take place.

§. XII. [uncertain: Deiner] His Most Serene Highness consents that the hereditary lessee may, on the ground of this contract, have the title of possession to this one Hufe so acquired entered in his own name in the land and mortgage book.

§. XIII. All the costs pertaining to this contract the purchaser undertakes to settle.

§. XIV. So long as the hereditary lessee and his successors in possession shall fulfil all the obligations contained in this contract, His Most Serene Highness promises to maintain them in their rights and to defend them against every man's real claim. Should, however, the hereditary lessee or his successors in possession remain in arrears with the yearly hereditary quit-rent for longer than two years

or else lay waste this holding and thereby diminish the revenues of the ground lordship, the ground lordship shall be entitled to let out this holding elsewhere, and the hereditary lessee shall then forfeit the whole right, and the purchase-money paid in shall remain the property of the ground lordship.

Both parties promise to keep this contract firmly and inviolably; they renounce, having been judicially instructed as to the consequences, all exceptions available against it, in particular the objection of an injury suffered above or below the half, of cunning persuasion, fear, coercion, of matters otherwise agreed than written down, or not rightly understood; they requested that this contract be confirmed and engrossed, and after it had been

read aloud in the German and Polish languages, they have in token of approval subscribed and signed below in their own hands.

General Power of Attorney. I, the undersigned, hereby make known and confess that I do expressly empower the Royal Councillor of War and Forests, Herr Peter Friedrich August von Triebenfeld, Your Right Honourable, of Schweinern etc., to attend, in my name, both judicially and extrajudicially, to any and all affairs concerning the estates belonging to me in South Prussia in the Kalisch Department, namely Zagorowo, Wittow and Trąbczyn, whether they concern a dismemberment or purchase and sale, leasing or pledging, or by whatever other name they may go, and may have bearing and influence; to petition for, and respectively to notify, all approbations or orders of conduct necessary in the matter, as well as all other requisites appearing needful to him, both with the most laudable Chamber and with the most laudable

Government at Kalisch and with all other authorities; and to attend to all other necessary business for me; to settle accounts with the tenants and to conclude compositions, to pay moneys, to receive the like and give receipt for them; to issue to my officials and servants there such orders as he finds serviceable, to examine their conduct and performance of duty, and if in his judgement he should find such [uncertain: für angemeßen und mir nachteilig] fitting and prejudicial to me, to suspend them if need be; and in general to act unrestrictedly and freely before all authorities and in all affairs as he shall find good and most conformable to my interest. Whatever my said Bevollmächtigter shall do by virtue of this commission received, I hereby promise to approve and to hold so inviolably as if I had done it myself. At the same time I bind myself to indemnify my Herr Bevollmächtigter in every respect on account of this commission, and to make good to him all costs and outlays, pledging my present and future property so far as shall be required for this.
