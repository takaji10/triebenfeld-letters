--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Hereditary-lease contract before the Hohenlohe patrimonial court at Trąbczyn between War and Forests Councillor von Triebenfeld, general attorney of Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, and the farmer Blazy Kukulski of Osiny, granting one Hufe Magdeburg measure in perpetuity for 100 Rthl purchase money, already paid, and 30 Rthl annual hereditary rent in half-yearly instalments from Christmas 1806, with public dues, compulsory purchase of manorial drink, milling and jurisdiction, right of pre-emption and tenth-Groschen transfer fee. Filed with it are Triebenfeld's general power of attorney of 1805, attested at the Guhrwitz justice office, and the engrossment.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The Serene Highness of Hohenlohe's patrimonial court hereby makes known that between the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, as general attorney of His Serene Highness the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, on the one part, and Blazy Kukulski on the other part, the following contract of sale and purchase concerning one Hufe of land, Magdeburg measure, situated by the estate of Osiny, has been concluded and published.

Actum Trąbczyn, the eighteenth of July, one thou

sand eight hundred and

six.

Before the undersigned local patrimonial court there appeared in person

firstly

1. the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, as general attorney of His Serene Highness the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingefingen, sole hereditary lord and lord of the court, legitimated by the power of attorney [uncertain: appended] on the one part, and

2. the farmer Blazy Kukulski from the princely estate of Osniy on the other part. The parties appearing are exactly known to the undersigned justiciary, and their capacity to execute judicial acts is subject to no doubt. They declare freely and after due deliberation the following irrevocable

and in no respect alterable hereditary lease contract, which they herewith at once publish to the judicial protocol.

§ I. His Most Serene Highness grants, namely, to Blazy Kukulski

1 Hufe, in words one Hufe Magdeburg measure, the Hufe reckoned at thirty Morgen and each Morgen at one hundred and eighty square rods, at Kotikale Czazy w Kierzäwn, arable land situated by Osiny, in hereditary lease for perpetual times.

§ II. Huba Maydebuska — and this one Hufe Magdeburg measure has already been assigned to Blazy Kukulski and handed over into actual possession

with which he is fully content.

§ III. Blazy Kukulski pays for this one Hufe Magdeburg measure as purchase money 100 Rthl, in words one hundred Reichsthaler, and since he has already discharged this purchase money in full, he is hereby given a receipt for it.

§ IV. Moreover Blazy Kukulski hereby binds himself, for himself and his successors in possession, to pay to the manorial lordship (Dominium) from this one Hufe Magdeburg measure an annual hereditary rent of 30 Rthl, in words thirty Reichsthaler, in ready current Courant, and this hereditary rent shall now and

for the future be discharged in half-yearly instalments, namely the one at Midsummer and the other at Christmas, each time post numerando, and the first half-yearly hereditary rent shall not be paid until Christmas 1806.

§ V. The settlement and rendering of the royal and other public dues falling upon this one Hufe, without any distinction, likewise the chimney money, the delivery of forage and the provision of carting service, the hereditary lessee takes upon himself now and for perpetual times, without any deduction from the hereditary rent, or any demand upon the manorial lordship for reimbursement thereof.

In return the hereditary lessee is now and for perpetual times wholly free from any and every manorial labour service, as also from hereditary subjection.

§ VI. The hereditary lessee must set up and build his establishment entirely at his own cost, and receives for this purpose neither now nor in future any building timber or other building assistance, and just as little any remission of the annual hereditary rent; however, all the timber standing on this Hufe remains, without exception, his property, and he may dispose of it without restriction.

§ VII. Outside his own holding the hereditary lessee has no right

to firewood or pasturage in the manorial woods and fields.

§ VIII. The hereditary lessee must always take his drink from the manorial brewery and distillery or from the tavern.

If foreign drink be brought in by him or his people, the hereditary lessee incurs on each occasion a penalty of five Reichsthaler for every barrel of beer and one Reichsthaler for every garniec of brandy, which penalty shall be paid over to the manorial lordship by way of compensation.

§ IX. The hereditary lessee remains at all times subject to the jurisdiction of the manorial lordship, as also to the milling obligation within the manorial estates.

§ X. The hereditary lessee is entitled to dispose of this holding and to alienate it otherwise at his pleasure.

The manorial lordship reserves to itself, however, once and for all hereby the right of pre-emption, and if it does not wish to exercise the same, then the tenth Groschen of the purchase or exchange sum must be paid over to the manorial lordship by the seller of the day as a transfer fee (Laudemium).

In the case of inheritance by children and other relatives no transfer fee is paid.

§ XI.

The hereditary lessee renounces entirely, for himself and his successors in possession, any and every remission in cases of misfortune, be they caused by fire from heaven, arson by evil persons, hailstorm, crop failure, cattle plague, war and pestilence, or arise in whatever manner it may be; and in the event of such cases there shall be neither any allowance nor

remission of the hereditary rent.

§ XII. His Most Serene Highness consents that the hereditary lessee may, upon the ground of this contract, have the title of possession to this one Hufe so acquired entered in his own name in the land and mortgage book.

§ XIII. The purchaser undertakes to settle all the costs pertaining to this contract.

§ XIV. So long as the hereditary lessee and his successors in possession shall fulfil all the obligations contained in this contract, His Most Serene Highness promises to maintain them in their rights and to defend them against any man's real claim. Should, however, the hereditary lessee or his successors in possession remain in arrears with the annual hereditary rent for longer than two years,

or else lay this holding waste and thereby diminish the revenues of the manorial lordship, then the manorial lordship shall be entitled to put out this holding elsewhere, and the hereditary lessee thereupon forfeits his whole right, and the purchase moneys paid in remain the property of the manorial lordship.

Both parties promise to keep this contract firmly and inviolably; being judicially instructed as to the consequences, they renounce all evasions available against it, particularly the objection of an injury suffered above or below the half, of cunning persuasion, fear, coercion, of matters agreed otherwise than written down or not rightly understood; they moved that this contract be confirmed and an engrossment issued, and after it had been

read out in the German and Polish languages, they have in token of approval subscribed and signed below in their own hands.

General power of attorney. I, the undersigned, do hereby make known and confess that I hereby expressly empower the Royal Councillor of War and Forests, the Right Honourable Mr Peter Friedrich August von Triebenfeld of Schweinern etc., to attend, in my name, both judicially and extrajudicially, to any and every matter concerning the estates of Zagorowo, Witów and Trąbczyn belonging to me and situated in South Prussia in the department of Kalisz, whether they concern a dismemberment or purchase and sale, leasing or mortgaging, or by whatever other name they may go, and may have bearing and influence; to petition for all approbations or instructions needful in that regard, as also for all other requisites appearing to him needful, both at the most laudable Chamber and at the most laudable

government at Kalisz and before all other authorities, and respectively to make report, and to attend to all other requisite business for me, to come to terms with the tenants and to conclude compositions, to pay moneys, to receive the like and to give receipts therefor, to issue to my officials and servants there the orders serviceable to his purpose, to examine their conduct and their performance of duty, and should he by his own insight find the same fitting and prejudicial to me, to suspend them if need be, and in general to act unrestrictedly and freely before all authorities and in all matters as he shall find good and most conformable to my interest. Whatsoever my said authorised representative shall do by virtue of this commission received, I hereby promise to approve and to hold to inviolably in such wise as though I had done it myself. At the same time I bind myself also to indemnify my authorised representative in every quarter on account of this commission, and to make good to him all costs and outlays, pledging my present and future property so far as shall be required thereto.
