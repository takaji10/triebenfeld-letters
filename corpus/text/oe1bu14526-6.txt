--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Hereditary-lease contract before the Hohenlohe patrimonial court at Trąbczyn between War and Forests Councillor von Triebenfeld, general attorney of Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, and the farmer Blazy Kukulski of Osiny, granting one Hufe Magdeburg measure in perpetuity for 100 Rthl purchase money, already paid, and 30 Rthl annual hereditary rent in half-yearly instalments from Christmas 1806, with public dues, compulsory purchase of manorial drink, milling and jurisdiction, right of pre-emption and tenth-Groschen transfer fee. Filed with it are Triebenfeld's general power of attorney of 1805, attested at the Guhrwitz justice office, and the engrossment.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The Serene [uncertain: Dürchlauch] von Hohenlohe Patrimonial Court hereby gives notice that between the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, as General Bevollmächtigter of His Serene Highness the reigning Prince zu Hohenlohe Ingelfingen, of the one part, and Blazy Kukulski of the other part, concerning One Hufe of land, Magdeburg measure, situated by the estate of Osiny, the following contract of sale and purchase has been concluded and published.

Actum Trąbczyn, the eighteenth of July, one thou-

sand eight hundred and

six.

Before the undersigned local Patrimonial Court there appeared in person

firstly

1. the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, as General Bevollmächtigter of His [uncertain: durch haupt] the reigning Prince zu Hohenlohe Ingefingen, sole hereditary and jurisdictional lord, legitimated by the [uncertain: bediegende] power of attorney, of the one part; and

2. the farmer Blazy Kukulski from the Princely estate of Osniy, of the other part. The comparents are exactly known to the undersigned Justitiarius, and their capacity to perform judicial acts is subject to no doubt. They declare, freely and after due deliberation, the following irrevocable [contract] concluded between them

and in nothing to be altered hereditary-lease contract, which they hereby forthwith publish to the judicial protocol.

§. I. His Most Serene Highness namely grants to Blazy Kukulski

1 Hufe, in words One Hufe Magdeburg measure, the Hufe reckoned at thirty Morgen and each Morgen at one hundred and eighty square rods, [uncertain: an Kotikale Czazy w Kierzäwn] arable land situated by Osiny, in hereditary lease for perpetual times.

§. II. Huba Maydebuska, and this One Hufe Magdeburg measure has already been assigned to Blazy Kukulski and given over into natural possession

over, wherewith the same is fully content.

§. III. Blazy Kukulski pays for this one Hufe Magdeburg measure as purchase-money 100 Rthl, in words one hundred Reichsthaler, and since he has already discharged this purchase-money in full, he is hereby given receipt for it.

§. IV. Moreover Blazy Kukulski hereby binds himself, for himself and his successors in possession, to pay to the ground-lordship from this one Hufe Magdeburg measure an annual hereditary rent of 30 Rthl, in words thirty Reichsthaler, in ready current Courant; and this hereditary rent shall be, now and

for the future, discharged in half-yearly instalments, namely the one at Midsummer (Johanni) and the other at Christmas, each time post numerando; and the first half-yearly hereditary rent shall not be paid until Christmas 1806.

§. V. The settlement and rendering of the Royal and other public dues falling upon this one Hufe, without any distinction whatever, likewise the chimney-money, the delivery of forage and the provision of transport service (Vorspann), the hereditary lessee takes upon himself, now and for perpetual times, without any deduction from the hereditary rent, or demanding any reimbursement for it from the ground-lordship.

In return the hereditary lessee is, now and for perpetual times, wholly free of any and every manorial labour service, as likewise of hereditary subjection.

§. VI. The hereditary lessee must set up and build his establishment entirely at his own cost, and receives for this purpose neither now nor in future any building timber or other building assistance, and just as little any remission of the annual hereditary rent; however all the timber standing on this Hufe remains, without exception, his property, and he may dispose of it without restriction.

§. VII. Outside his own holding the hereditary lessee has no right

to firewood or pasturage in the manorial woods and fields.

§. VIII. The hereditary lessee must always take his drink from the manorial brewery and distillery or from the tavern.

If foreign drink is brought in by him or his people, then the hereditary lessee incurs each time a penalty of Five Reichsthaler for every barrel of beer and one Reichsthaler for every Garniec of brandy, which penalty is to be paid over to the ground-lordship by way of compensation.

§. IX. The hereditary lessee remains always subject to the jurisdiction of the ground-lordship, as likewise to the milling compulsion within the manorial estates.

§. X. The hereditary lessee is entitled to dispose of this holding and to alienate the same elsewhere at his pleasure.

The ground-lordship, however, hereby reserves to itself once and for all the right of pre-emption, and if it will not exercise the same, then the tenth Groschen of the purchase or exchange sum must be paid over by each successive seller to the ground-lordship as a Laudemium.

Upon inheritance by children and other relatives no Laudemium is to be rendered.

§. XI.

The hereditary lessee renounces, for himself and his successors in possession, entirely all and every remission in cases of misfortune, whether these arise through fire from heaven, incendiarism by evil people, hailstorm, crop failure, cattle plague, war and pestilence, or in whatever manner it may be; and in the event of such cases there shall be neither any bonification nor

remission of the hereditary rent take place.

§. XII. His Most Serene Highness consents that the hereditary lessee may have the title of possession to this One Hufe thus acquired entered in his own name in the Land and Mortgage Book on the ground of this contract.

§. XIII. The purchaser undertakes to settle all the costs pertaining to this contract.

§. XIV. So long as the hereditary lessee and his successors in possession fulfil all the obligations contained in this contract, His Most Serene Highness promises to maintain them in their rights and to defend them against any man's real claim. Should, however, the hereditary lessee or his successors in possession remain in arrears with the annual hereditary rent for longer than two years,

or else lay waste this holding and thereby diminish the revenues of the ground-lordship, then the ground-lordship shall be entitled to let out this holding elsewhere, and the hereditary lessee shall thereupon forfeit his whole right, and the purchase-monies paid in remain the property of the ground-lordship.

Both parties promise to hold this contract firmly and inviolably; having been judicially instructed as to the consequences, they renounce all evasions available against it, in particular the objection of an injury sustained above or below the half, of cunning persuasion, fear, compulsion, of matters otherwise agreed than written down, or not properly understood; they moved that this contract be confirmed and an engrossment issued, and, after the reading having taken place,

the reading having taken place in the German and Polish languages, they have, in token of approval, subscribed and signed below in their own hands.

General power of attorney. I, the undersigned, hereby make known and confess that I do hereby expressly empower the Royal Councillor of War and Forests, Herr Peter Friedrich August von Triebenfeld, Your Right Honourable, of Schweinern etc., to attend, in my name, both judicially and extra-judicially, to any and every affair touching the estates of Zagorowo, Wittow and Trąbczyn belonging to me, situated in South Prussia in the Kalisch Department, whether they concern a dismemberment or purchase and sale, leasing or pledging, or by whatever other name they may go, and may have bearing and influence; to petition for, and respectively to notify, all approbations or orders of conduct necessary in that regard, as also all other requirements appearing to him necessary, both with the most laudable Chamber and also with the most laudable

Government at Kalisch and with all other authorities, and to attend for me to all other requisite business, to come to an accounting with the tenants and to conclude compositions, to pay monies, to receive the like and to give receipts for them, to issue to my officials and servantry there such orders as are conducive to the purpose, to investigate their conduct and performance of duty, and, if in his discretion he should find such [conduct] fitting and prejudicial to me, to suspend them if need be, and generally to act without restriction and freely before all authorities and in all affairs as he shall find good and most conformable to my interest. Whatsoever my said Bevollmächtigter shall do by virtue of this commission received, I hereby promise to approve and to hold inviolably thereto in such manner as if I had done it myself. At the same time I also bind myself to indemnify my Bevollmächtigter in every respect on account of this commission, and [to make good] to him all costs and outlays, on the pledge of my present and future property, so far as shall be required for this purpose.
