--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A royal grant by Friedrich Wilhelm conveying to Friedrich Ludwig, Reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, sixteen formerly ecclesiastical villages, among them Betsche, Łowyń, Zagórów, Kopojno, Drzewce and Wrąbczyn, in hereditary and full ownership, subject only to the existing sovereign dues as a fixed unalterable payment and to the maintenance allowance owed to the ecclesiastical foundations. It replaces an earlier grant of the absconded former Starost von Prusimski's estates, which proved too heavily indebted, and orders all provincial boards not to obstruct his possession. Certified copy.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
We Friedrich Wilhelm, by the Grace of God King of Prussia, do by this Our open letter attest and declare, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors in the Crown, the Electorate and Our remaining lands, that, it having been submitted to Us in all humility in what manner Our intention — namely to give to the High-born Prince, Our friendly beloved uncle Friedrich Ludw., Reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, by the grant of the estates which had belonged to the absconded former Starost von Prusimski, a token of the friendship and goodwill We bear towards him, as also of Our Royal gratitude for the distinguished and beneficial services rendered to Us and to Our house — was not achieved entirely as We had willed it, because the said estates were burdened with many debts, We have most highly resolved to relinquish, to grant and to convey to His Lordship the hereinafter named formerly ecclesiastical estates, namely: the villages of Betsche, Świechocin, Stoki, Łowyń, Głażewo, Tarmowo, Zagórów, Skokum, Swiątniki, Kopojno, Olesnica, Drzewce, Grądzyń, Wrąbczyn, Wieniecz and Wolica, together with all their appurtenances, by whatsoever name they may be called, in hereditary and full ownership, against payment of

1) the sovereign dues hitherto discharged therefrom, as a fixed and unalterable canon, and

2) the maintenance allowance due therefrom to the ecclesiastical corporations or foundations.

We also do so hereby and by virtue of this present instrument, out of Our Royal power and plenitude, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors in the Crown, the Electorate, and Our remaining lands, in such manner and wise that Our aforementioned friendly beloved uncle, the Reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-

Ingelfingen shall from now on and for all eternity, for himself and his heirs and descendants, irrevocably have, possess and enjoy the abovenamed estates with all rights and entitlements attaching thereto, as these have hitherto been exercised or may in law still be exercised, and no less with all appurtenances belonging to him and other accessories, by whatsoever name they may be called, as the same have hitherto been used or may in law be used, and with all the lands appertaining thereto in fields, woods and meadows, in ponds and waters, as a true, well-acquired and unrestricted property from Us and Our descendants and successors; and that the power and authority shall likewise be vested in him to dispose thereof at his pleasure under the above-mentioned conditions, unhindered by any man. We do therefore command and enjoin all Our Superior Provincial Boards, as also all other authorities whom this concerns, hereby as graciously as earnestly, that they shall in no manner hinder nor obstruct the said Prince etc. in the hereditary and proprietary possession of the abovenamed estates, together with their appurtenances and accessories, granted to the same, nor those to whom he may one day bequeath the same or otherwise transfer them in lawful manner by other acts inter vivos aut mortis causa, but shall rather let His Lordship, and eventually them also, enjoy the same freely and peaceably; shall themselves do nothing against this, nor occasion or permit that such be done by others, on pain of Our Most High disfavour and unfailing severe reprimand.

reprimand.

In witness whereof etc. Given at Berlin, the 19th of June, 1797. Friedrich Wilhelm
