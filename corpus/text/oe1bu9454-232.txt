--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Triebenfeld, seit Wochen krank und ohne Geld für einen Arzt, berichtet von Schikanen um die Verpachtung; Brzechffa habe erklärt, zu Johanni zu zahlen. Mit Brzechffa will er nach Warszawa, um die Trąbczyner und Wrąbczyner Sache zu betreiben. Er bittet eilig um eine gerichtlich ausgestellte Erklärung, die ihm 3125 Rthl, mit Zinsen und Kosten 4219 Rthl, aus den Zinsen von Swięcia, Wrąbczyn, Alt Olesnica, Skokum, Sophienthal, Kopojno und Adelow anweist. Getreide, Vieh und Futter seien weggenommen, die Not sei groß.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Triebenfeld, ill for weeks and without money for a doctor, reports harassment over the leasing; he says Brzechffa has declared he will pay at Midsummer. He means to go to Warszawa with Brzechffa to pursue the Trąbczyn and Wrąbczyn business. He urgently asks for a declaration issued by a court assigning him 3125 Rthl, with interest and costs 4219 Rthl, out of the interest from Swięcia, Wrąbczyn, Alt Olesnica, Skokum, Sophienthal, Kopojno and Adelow. He says grain, livestock and fodder have been taken away and the hardship is great.

Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord, that Your Serene Highness has sent someone here who has satisfied himself as to the situation here and as to my misery, is well — I have lain very ill these many weeks, only the same already an [uncertain: Exquilete] — the daily eyes wear me out, not a Groschen of money is in the cash box, I cannot engage a doctor for myself and must yet perish in my sins [Hohenlohe: "Indeed!"]! Whether the lease will yet stand, God knows; people exhaust themselves in chicanery, and with [illegible] today to lease out the propination rights separately, in order to obtain old arrears from 1808 which the sequestrator has caused with 2000

d. Brzechffa has declared that he would pay at Midsummer, but continues [uncertain: in Catalirt]. I am therefore writing to those persons, as soon as the councillor of justice Kuntzel arrives, in the name of His Serene Highness, to the King of Saxony, and beg him that he might [have] the General Procurator Lipski, upon war, [come] hither to

now to fix and establish a norm between the manorial lordship (Dominium) and the subjects, and to draw the chicaners into a fiscal investigation. Count Kwilecki, who was with me here just now, is gathering [uncertain: Stiennen] in order to press for the dismissal of Szymanowski, who is our great friend; this may take 4 weeks of dragging about, and in the meantime I shall still have to suffer [illegible] want.

The Herrlichen cash office has now [moved] for execution upon the arrears of interest, but the Procurator will have the kindness to order that all the residues, as I direct them, be taken as day-money, levied by force, and the interest owing discharged. If that succeeds, then I come clear here, and at most 4000 Rthl. remain over, if the contract with Brzechffa holds — My and Brzechffa's plan is, once I have been [recovered], to go to Warsaw. To pursue the Trąbczyn business, likewise the Wrąbczyn and others, from the fief we [come] back and come to Sławięcice; in the meantime the suit with Meierowitz will also be

decided; should it turn out favourably, of which there is no doubt, then Your Serene Highness will obtain the 4000 Rthl., [illegible] through the [uncertain: Schläfen] it will be paid.

Your Serene Highness writes that the 300 Rthl. are to be procured for Mr Sommer. That is not possible until Brzechffa is in funds — for these many days there has not been a [uncertain: Denen] in the cash box — today 4 Rthl. came in, and these Mr Sommer has received. Everything here is at present laid under sequestration; I do not know whence I am to obtain a [illegible] to live on and to meet the costs. Now the Procurator [uncertain: Golynski] sends word through Chomanowski that I should press for the payment of the interest which at that time the heralichen cash office had taken from me in Berlin by arrest and French execution. For since I held a decree of the French General Government, and the Invalids' Fund was now seeking its satisfaction here, this claim had the priority — only Your Serene Highness would have to give a declaration — "that the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld must demand and receive the sum in question of 3125 Rthl., which with [illegible] [text lost] interest and costs amounts to 4219 Rthl., in which I not only hereby fully consent, but I hereby direct him to raise these 4219 Rthl. out of the interest"

of the villages of New and Old Swięcia, of the village of Wrąbczyn, of the village of Old Oleśnica, Skokum, the colony of Sophienthal, Kopojno, and the colony of Adelow, and to have them paid against receipt; I also consent that the said von Triebenfeld may have this money levied by execution through a komornik, without its first having to be applied for by judicial assistance." This, made out by His Serene Highness's courts, I beg may be sent to me by a [uncertain: rentencken] express with all possible speed, so that I may keep my foot in the [uncertain: Carrieren] and may have some money levied. For the rest, there is here a want that surpasses all conception; the marches through have consumed everything — grain, cattle, horses, sheep, and they have taken the [fodder], so that the people are reduced to grazing grass together with the beasts.

As regards the [uncertain: Brieges P(i/r)ecz] most graciously sent to me, it is not valid, for this sum is, according to the Konotop contract, determinately assigned and undertaken to be paid to him.

In deepest respect, I remain, until death,

Your Most Serene Highness's

Kopojno

the 16th of June 1811, most humble and obedient

von Triebenfeld
