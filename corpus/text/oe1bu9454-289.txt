--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Appeals to Cabinet Councillor Albrecht for a categorical royal decision on the Churisch heirs' claim of 300000 Rthl, now 426000 Rthl with interest, acknowledged by the late King in person and served by interest payments to 1806. The heirs, encouraged by fresh royal assurances in Vienna, have broken off a compromise settling at circa 112000 Rthl; Triebenfeld asks either payment with removal of Cosmar's unaccountable administration of the entailed estates, or disclaimer, and an advance repayable within four years, citing the Brieg Superior Provincial Court's confirming report.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Right Honourable Sir,

Highly honoured Privy Cabinet Councillor, it will certainly not be unknown to Your Right Honourable that the Churish heirs have a claim against the E. v. Hohenlohe of 300000 Rthl; the acknowledgement of debt concerning it was issued by the Prince and his divorced consort and signed by the Majesty of the late King as debtor in his own person, and the interest upon it was also paid out of the Royal [uncertain: lots] up to 1806. Upon this the Churish heirs believed they might ground their right to demand this sum, which today with the interest amounts to 426000 Rthl, from the treasury, and they commenced the suit against it. His Majesty thereupon, a year and a day ago, declared that he would have nothing to do with the matter, but rather renounced it entirely.

This moved the Prince (although the sum had first been negotiated for the Majesty of the late King) to enter upon a compromise with the Electoral heirs, according to which the latter would be content with circa 112000 Rthl and would give a receipt for the whole item of debt. During His Majesty's presence here, however, the same most highly gave the heiresses, quite contrary to the former declaration, the assurance that most highly he would have the matter laid before him upon his most high return to Berlin, and would then declare himself further.

On the ground of this most high decision, the councillor of justice Inten[text lost] in Berlin has intimated to the Cährish attorney Eberhard [uncertain: to bring][text lost] to break off at once all compromise negotiations, because the treasury would now[text lost] render payment in full and must render it. The princely[text lost] had sent me hither in order to conclude the matter and to request an advance[text lost] of 112 Rthl in accordance with the compromise, which was to be reimbursed again out of the estates in[text lost] 4 years. But now, since the[text lost] Tuhrish heirs have withdrawn their word upon the hope held out to them[text lost], I am declared unable to bring the matter to the desired[text lost] goal and to procure for His Majesty a capital of 426000 Rthl[text lost]. I would also fall silent in reverence, were it not that my princely client entreats me so dreadfully, for upon the [text lost] of this debt the medical councillor Cosmar administers all the entailed estates and gives no man any account of it, and already since ex little or nothing, renders no accounting, and nobody knows where the revenues of these considerable estates remain. I must therefore, in the name of my client, beg for a most high, most gracious categorical decision.
