--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Die Regierung zu Poznań teilt dem Fürsten zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen mit, dass auf sein Gesuch hin je 5000 Rthl für den Baron von der Marwitz, die Oberamtsrätin Michaelis und den Ökonomierat Glenck auf die Herrschaft Pszczew eingetragen und für jeden der drei Gläubiger ein Hypothekenschein verfügt worden ist. Die Scheine kann er nach vier Wochen gegen die Kosten im Archiv der Regierung abholen lassen.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The Government at Poznań informs the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen that, at his request, 5,000 Rthl each for Baron von der Marwitz, for Mrs Michaelis, wife or widow of a senior councillor, and for the estate councillor Glenck have been entered on the lordship of Pszczew, and a mortgage certificate ordered for each of the three creditors. He may have the certificates collected from the Government's archive after four weeks, on payment of the costs.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Poznań[uncertain] the 4th of August 1803.

By the Grace of God Friedrich Wilhelm, King of Prussia etc. etc.

Our Royal Grace and Friendship first of all, High-born Prince, especially dear Uncle, Friend and dear Faithful One! We hereby make known to Your Lordship that, in accordance with the petition of the 20th of last month, and on the ground of the original documents appended thereto, the entry of the 5,000 rttlr. for Baron von der Marwitz, of the 5,000 rttlr. for the wife of the Senior District Councillor Michaelis, and of the 5,000 rttlr. for the Councillor of Economy Glenck, in the mortgage book upon the lordship of Pszczew has been ordered, together with the issuing of a mortgage certificate in vim recognitionis for each of the three creditors, which Your Lordship may have received from the archive of Our Government after 4 weeks upon payment of the costs. We remain well disposed towards Your Lordship with Royal Grace and Friendship. Given at Poznań the 11th of July 1803. Royal South Prussian Government. Braudhan[uncertain]

To the Lord Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen.
