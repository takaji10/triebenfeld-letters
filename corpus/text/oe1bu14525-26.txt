--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Notifies the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen that, on his petition of the previous month and the original papers attached to it, three debts of 5,000 Rthl each, owed to Baron von der Marwitz, to the wife of Senior District Councillor Michaelis and to Councillor of Economy Glenck, have been ordered entered in the mortgage book against the lordship of Betsche, with a mortgage certificate acknowledging each creditor's claim to be collected from the government archive after four weeks.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Posen[uncertain] the 4th of August 1803.

By the Grace of God Friedrich Wilhelm, King of Prussia etc. etc.

Our Royal Grace and Friendship first of all, High-born Prince, especially dear Uncle, Friend and dear Faithful One! We hereby make known to Your Lordship that, in accordance with the petition of the 20th of last month, and on the ground of the original documents appended thereto, the entry of the 5000 rttlr. for Baron von der Marwitz, of the 5000 rttlr. for the wife of the Senior District Councillor Michaelis, and of the 5000 rttlr. for the Councillor of Economy Glenck, in the mortgage book upon the lordship of Betsche has been ordered, together with the issuing of a mortgage certificate in vim recognitionis for each of the three creditors, which Your Lordship may have received from the archive of Our Government after 4 weeks upon payment of the costs. We remain well disposed towards Your Lordship with Royal Grace and Friendship. Given at Posen the 11th of July 1803. Royal South Prussian Government. Braudhan[uncertain]

To the Lord Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen.
