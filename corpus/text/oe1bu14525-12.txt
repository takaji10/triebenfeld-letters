--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A mortgage certificate for the noble estate of Kolno in the Posen district, comprising the town of Kamionna and the market-town of Kolno, confirming the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen's registered title under a royal grant of 1796 after Anton von Prusimski's property was confiscated for joining the 1794 insurrection. It lists the charges standing against it: protests lodged for Anton von Broniewski over a disputed pledge-possession and claims of 79,795 Rthl, plus 250 Rthl and small annual dues owed to Kamionna church.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The noble estate of Kolno, situate under the jurisdiction of the Royal South Prussian Government at Posen and in the Posen district, consisting of the town of Kamionna or Kamionna and of the market-town of Kolno or Kulm, has been granted hereditarily and in full property to the reigning Prince, Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, by virtue of the deed of grant of His Majesty King Friedrich Wilhelm the Second of 9 August 1796 - the whole property of the former owner, Anton von Prusimski, having been confiscated, on account of his participation in the insurrection which broke out in South Prussia in the year 1794, by the legally binding judgment of confiscation of 27 June 1796, whereby this estate fell to the Treasury; and the title of possession has, for the same, upon the ground of this deed of grant and of the deed of resignation made out on 20 October 1800, and likewise

the title of possession of Anton von Prusimski, as sole male heir of his father Stephan von Prusimski, proved by the sworn depositions of two witnesses taken to the record on 15 October 1800, been accepted as duly established and entered by decree of 11 May 1801.

Thereupon there are entered

I. Perpetual charges and restrictions upon the property or upon the disposal thereof

1.) A protest, which has been entered on behalf of Anton von Broniewski, on account of the antichretic pledge-possession of this estate notified on 19 December 1796 but not acknowledged by the proprietor, by decree of 11 May 1801, with the remark that the claims of Anton von Broniewski under No. 1 of the judicially secured debts are specially noted by way of protest.

2.) 250 rt., or one thousand five hundred Polish gulden, which, as a perpetual charge resting upon the market-town of Kolno in favour of the parish church at Kamionna and bearing interest at 3 1/2 per cent, by virtue of the inscription of Johann von Prusimski in the municipal courts of Posen of the Monday after the feast of the Visitation of Mary 1673,

were notified on 28 December 1796 together with arrears of interest to an equal amount (ad alterum tantum), were however acknowledged by the proprietor to the record of 15 December 1800 only in respect of the capital and the running interest, and have therefore, with a note of this discrepancy, been entered by decree of 11 May 1801.

3.) An annual interest of four Polish marks, which must be paid yearly out of the estate of Kamionna to the church there, arising from an original capital of one hundred Polish gulden, and which, upon the notification made on 28 December 1796, upon the ground of the instrument in the acts of the general consistory of Posen of 26 June 1591, and in consequence of the acknowledgment of the proprietor to the record of 15 December 1800, has been entered by decree of 11 May 1801.

II. Judicially secured debts and other real obligations

1.) A protest, which has been entered on behalf of Anton von Broniewski, on account of various claims originally

arising out of an antichretic pledge contract between the former owner Anton von Prusimski and Joseph von Bienkowski, of the Monday before the Nativity of Mary 1768, notified on 20 December 1796 in a sum of 79,795 rt. 18 g. 8 4/5 pf., or four hundred and seventy-eight thousand, seven hundred and seventy-four Polish gulden, twenty and a half Groschen, with reservation of the costs of improvements provisionally stated at 21,666 rt. 16 gg., or one hundred and thirty thousand Polish gulden, which claims, the proprietor objecting thereto, have been entered by decree of 11 May 1801, with the remark that the alleged pledge-possession of this estate is specially noted by way of protest under No. 1 among the perpetual charges.

Apart from the foregoing items nothing is entered against the estate of Kolno in the mortgage book, and the present mortgage certificate is moreover issued to the reigning Prince

Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, herewith for his information. In witness whereof, under the signature appointed by the South Prussian Government here [text lost] and its seal. Given [text lost] the 29th of August 1803. Bormann

Fest. John.

Mortgage certificate, for his information, for the reigning Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, concerning the estate of Kolno situate in the Posen district. No. 1757.
