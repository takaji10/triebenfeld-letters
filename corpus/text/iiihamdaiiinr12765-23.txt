--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Die Witwe von Brehmer überreicht dem Ministerium ein rechtskräftiges Erkenntnis der ehemaligen südpreußischen Regierung zu Kalisz von 1806, wonach der Fürst zur Zahlung der 5000 Rthl nebst Zinsen verurteilt wurde, und das Testament ihres Bruders, des Majors von Seehausen. Sie bittet, sich wegen Kapital und Zinsen zu verwenden. Laut Verfügung des Ministeriums kann nach Lage der Umstände ein Mehreres nicht geschehen.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The widow von Brehmer submits to the ministry a legally binding judgment of the former South Prussian government at Kalisz of 1806, by which the Prince was ordered to pay the 5000 Rthl together with interest, and the will of her brother, Major von Seehausen. She asks the ministry to intercede on her behalf regarding the capital and the interest. According to the ministry's order, nothing further can be done as circumstances stand.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
To the High Royal Prussian Ministry of Foreign Affairs I submit, in answer to the resolution of the 29th of last month communicated to me, a legally binding judgment of the former South Prussian government at Kalisz etc., published the 24th of May 1806, by which the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen was condemned to pay the capital of 5000 rt together with interest; and I prove my title, by the will of my late brother the Major von Seehausen appended in copy, as his sole universal heiress and present owner of this capital. This will shows at the same time that a great part of this capital is designated for legacies; the legatees are just as needy as I am, and I therefore venture with confidence the most obedient request that

the High Ministry may be pleased most graciously to intercede in the matter of the capital together with interest. Sommerfeld, the 25th of July 1816. The widow of Colonel von Brehmer
