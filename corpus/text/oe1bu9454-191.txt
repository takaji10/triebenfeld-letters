--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Written from a sickbed after five weeks of bilious fever, an account of the summer's business: a schedule of debts totalling 311000 Rthl (Oppenheimer 110000, Lichnowski 40000, Grotowski 20000, Radzinski 24000 and others) against 184000 Rthl receivable; suits by Meyer Bernhard at Betsche; the Prusimska dowry execution at Trąbczyn contested at Kalisz; empty coffers, insolvent lessees and peasants reoccupying allotted ground; contracts with Honrichs, v. Zglinski and the Kopojno lessee cancelled or reduced to one year, resistance broken by knout-strokes; Tietz's claimed 300 Rthl pension queried; Glenck's lampoon and conspiracy deterring purchasers; supervision entrusted to v. Hallen, Honrichs and Cabanis; news from von Tesmar that 360000 Rthl may be had.

I must therefore, if, given the weakness that still presses upon me, I shall be able to recollect everything, begin with Berlin; from there I departed a few days before Midsummer with the assurance that I should receive so much out of the judicial fund as would enable me to pay Oppenheimer with 110000 Rthl and to get the mortgage book clear. So, as said overleaf, I set out from Berlin before Midsummer for Betsche, to whom the [uncertain: Rader] of the Jews, Meyer Bernhard, had brought 3 or 4 suits against Your Serene Highness, because nothing was yet known of the Royal approval concerning the Betsche affair.

At Betsche I communicated the news to the people, made up my accounts with them, paid Echaust likewise the old court costs, and after this the burgesses and residents still owed Your Serene Highness 4000 Rthl, which they must pay at Michaelmas. Then I learned, to my horror, that an execution decree had been sent from [uncertain: Posten] to Trąbczyn, by which (since Metzer has taken no notice of anything) the Prusimska's 10000 tl. bridal settlement together with interest and costs, thus altogether some 22000 Rthl, were to be paid.

I therefore hastened as fast as was possible to Kalisz and proved, by the documents procured for me by Rapacki, that Prussiemski had already paid part of that bridal settlement; by this I did indeed gain time, yet the matter hangs, and a year and a day may again pass before I bring it to a clear footing. This alone is certain, that not even those 16000 [uncertain: Fiden] entered in the [uncertain: tables] may be paid in full any longer, let alone the 22000 Rthl. From Kalisz I went to Trąbczyn, where I now confidently believed I should find some 30000 Rthl. But, o heavens, how I was startled when instead of money I found a thousand confusions, knaveries, and consequently empty coffers – the earlier ones all declared themselves insolvent – they made out great accounts on account of the exorbitant deliveries, cartage and quartering under the Russians and Prussians, so that the rents of 2 years would still have gone out on them.

The old peasants who had been put on rents had ceased, and had, in spite of all judicial contracts, approvals from the Chamber and from the Royal Majesty himself, again abandoned the grounds assigned to them, and, certainly in collusion with the leaseholders, so that these knaves might believe themselves entitled to make so much the greater account, had again

appropriated their former grounds and sown them, and thereby almost dissolved the demesne farms; indeed at [uncertain: the last] they even drove off the German colonists already spoken of, so that I could reckon neither on the stipulated entry monies from these nor still less on rents.
