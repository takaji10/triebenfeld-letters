--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Police burgomaster Nüchler of Pyzdry, the miller Luka of Białobrzeg and the marksman Reise of Wrąbczynek cancel their contract of 20 November 1798 with Glencke, the Prince of Hohenlohe's building and economy councillor, to recruit settlers and pay the purchase money for parcelling out Szetlewek in the Trąbczyn estates: von Brzezinski, holding it in mortgage tenure, has obstructed clearance. They ask 200 Rthl for travel costs, which Glencke offers subject to the Prince's approval, and settlers who paid earnest-money must look to the lordship.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Stamped sheet reserved. Actum Zagorowo, the 1st of July 1799. There appeared on this date before the local police magistracy:

1. The police burgomaster

H. Nüchler of Pyzdry

2. the miller Luka of Białobrzeg, and

3. The marksman Reise of Wrąbczynek, and they moved that the following be set down ad Protocollum: Under the 20th of November 1798 they had made a contract with the Most Princely Hohenlohe building and economy councillor, as general authorised representative, Mr. Glencke,

concerning the parcelling-out of the estate of Szetlewek situated in the Trąbczyn estates, wherein they had undertaken to procure, for the number of Hufen specified therein, subjects sufficient for the settlement of the same, and likewise to pay down the purchase money specified at Midsummer of this year.

the number of Hufen, subjects sufficient for the settlement of the same, and likewise to pay down the purchase money specified at Midsummer of this year.

All this they would also have duly performed, had not the present possessor von Brzezinski, who holds the estate of Szetlewek in mortgage-money tenure, raised certain difficulties concerning the clearing of it. They now saw very well that some considerable time might yet elapse before the difficulties with the aforesaid Raczinski could be removed; and therefore they wished to renounce the fulfilment of the said

contract, and hereby to declare it null and void, upon the condition that the travel costs etc. incurred by them in this business be made good to them with 200 Rthl, written out two hundred Reichsthaler; and that the people drawn hither, who have already laid down earnest-money, shall have no claims upon them, but shall hold themselves solely to the Most Princely manorial lordship (Dominium).

This foregoing submission was laid before the Most Princely Hohenlohe building and economy councillor Mr. Glenck, who was likewise in loco; and his declaration was required upon it.

Whereupon he then expressed himself in the following manner.

Although it was to all appearance a high demand for indemnification, he nevertheless offered to pay the same to the deponents upon the condition, namely, that the approbation of His Most Serene Highness could be obtained thereto.

Wherewith the same was closed and signed. Nüchler mpp xxx [uncertain: bedenket] Samuel Luck xxx Jacob Kriede. In witness whereof, under the signature and seal of the magistracy. Barck[illegible], police burgomaster.
