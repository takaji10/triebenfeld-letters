Actum in Conin in judicijs terrestribus particularibus districtus ejusdem feria secunda post Dominicam Septuagesimae proxima anno Domini millesimo septingentesimo vigesimo octavo idque ex limitatione feria secunda in crastino Festi Sancti Mathaei Apostoli et Evangelistae proxime praeteriti in anno nunc praeterlapso millesimo septingentesimo vigesimo septimo per id judicium facta legitime in diem hodiernam incidentibus et provenientibus
Inter Generosos Chełmskie et Generosum Prusimski Decretum Locationis.
In causa et actione judiciaria infrascripta pro anteriori judiciorum terrestrium particularium hic in Conin feria secunda post Festum Sanctissimae et Individuae Trinitatis proxima anno proxime praeterito 1727 celebratorum cadentia parti infrascriptae citatae per inferius nominandam actoream partem instituta et intentata non tamen ob farraginem pro tunc causarum decisa sed in regestris ad disjudicandum inter alias causas manente et pendente quam instigator judicii praesentis ejusque delatores Generosi Franciscus et Josephus Ścibor Chełmscy bonorum villarum Łukom Łomowo et aliorum eo pertinentium haeredes et possessores actores Generosis Christophoro Prusimski parenti principali tum et Antonio Prusimski filio atque nobilibus Niłecki Kałuzoski Jerzmanoski Cieszkoski Zdzęnicki Tomaszeski famulis alijsque nobilibus ad praesens de cognominibus ignotis in termino termini exprimendis tum et de bonis Trąpczyno subditis de nominibus ad praesens ignotis sed post qua innotuerint specificandis mandata ejusdem Generosus Prusimski exequentis comprincipalibus de personis bonisque ipsorum generaliter omnibus ac summis quibusvis pecuniis habitis et habendis in judicium praesens terrestre Calissiense terminosque terrestres particulares districtus Coninensis filiis quibus cum parentibus famulis et subditis cum dominis ipsorum ad inhaerendum protestationi suae contra eosdem factae sive faciendae instituerunt citatis pro paenis legibus Regni descriptis per ipsos vero succubitis et demeritis exeo quia is idem Generosus Prusimski non observata vicinitatis amicicia nec considerata imo magis postposita illa non contentus anterioribus damnis violentiis iniuriis et gravaminibus propter conservandam
in virtute vicinae commansionis amicitiam ad hoc usque tempus nondum ad acta publica regni oblata expressis eo praecipue quo tempore certo in termino termini exprimendo tegulas alias Szkudeł centum comburere et cuivis sufferre totiesque requisitus damna ex inde causata refundere noluit nunc recenter tempore in termino declarando boves quatuor laboratiles pro usu officinae vitriarij conservatos --- SUMMARY (German) ---
Dekret des Landgerichts in Konin aus der Sitzung, die am Montag nach Septuagesima 1728 begann, überschrieben „Decretum Locationis“. Die Brüder Franciszek und Józef Ścibor Chełmski, Erbherren von Łukom und Łomowo, haben Krzysztof Prusimski als Hauptbeklagten, seinen Sohn Antoni, sechs namentlich genannte adlige Dienstleute und ungenannte Untertanen von Trąbczyn geladen; die Klage war schon zur Sitzung nach Trinitatis 1727 erhoben und damals nicht entschieden worden. Sie werfen Prusimski vor, er habe ohne Rücksicht auf nachbarliche Freundschaft hundert Schock Schindeln, die auf ihrem Grund gefertigt worden waren, samt einer Hütte verbrennen lassen, wobei Kinder, die in der Hütte lebten, beinahe verbrannt wären; er habe vier Arbeitsochsen, die für die Glashütte gehalten wurden, bei einem bewaffneten Überfall wegnehmen lassen und Józef Chełmski nach dem Leben getrachtet; und er habe, während die Kläger am Krontribunal in Piotrków waren, seinen Sohn, die Dienstleute und Untertanen bewaffnet auf Grund geschickt, der seit alters zu Łukom gehöre, und dort Heu in nicht geringer Zahl von Fuhren wegnehmen lassen. Beide Seiten erscheinen vor Gericht. Das Gericht befindet, dass ein vereidigter Kommissar der Woiwodschaften Posen und Kalisz, Wojciech Biskupski, auf den beide sich geeinigt haben, mit je zwei Freunden beider Seiten am Montag nach dem Sonntag Misericordia auf den strittigen Grund gehen muss; sie sollen über das Eigentum am Grund, die Gewalttaten und die Schäden alte, kundige Zeugen unter Eid vernehmen und alle Ansprüche entscheiden. Berufung ist nur gegen das Endurteil zulässig. Beide Parteien haben eine Buße von vierzehn polnischen Mark zu zahlen, halb an die Gegenseite und halb an das Gericht; wer dem Dekret nicht nachkommt, verfällt der Verbannung.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A decree of the land court at Konin, of the sitting that opened on the Monday after Septuagesima 1728, headed "Decretum Locationis". The brothers Franciszek and Józef Ścibor Chełmski, heirs of Łukom and Łomowo, have cited Krzysztof Prusimski as principal defendant, his son Antoni, six noblemen in his service, who are named, and unnamed subjects of Trąbczyn; the suit had been brought at the sitting after Trinity 1727 and was not decided then. They charge that Prusimski, with no regard for neighbourly friendship, had a hundred threescore of shingles made on their ground burned together with a hut, so that children living in the hut were nearly burned; that he had four working oxen kept for the glassworks taken in an armed raid, and plotted against Józef Chełmski's life; and that, while the plaintiffs were at the Crown Tribunal at Piotrków, he sent his son, the retainers and the subjects in arms onto ground that belongs to Łukom from of old, and had hay taken there in no small number of cartloads. Both sides appear before the court. The court finds that a sworn commissioner of the provinces of Poznań and Kalisz, Wojciech Biskupski, on whom both have agreed, with two friends of each side, must go onto the disputed ground on the Monday after Misericordia Sunday; they are to examine old and knowledgeable witnesses on oath about the ownership of the ground, the acts of violence and the damage, and to decide all the claims. Appeal lies only from the final sentence. Both parties are to pay a penalty of fourteen Polish marks, half to the other side and half to the court; whoever does not comply with the decree falls under banishment.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Done in Konin, in the particular land courts of the same district, on the Monday nearest after the Sunday Septuagesima in the year of the Lord one thousand seven hundred and twenty-eight — this from the adjournment made by that same court on the Monday next after the Feast of Saint Matthew, Apostle and Evangelist, most recently passed, in the now-elapsed year one thousand seven hundred and twenty-seven — lawfully proceeding and arriving at the present day.

Between the Honourables Chełmski and the Honourable Prusimski — Decree of Location.

In the judicial cause and action described below, instituted and initiated — having fallen to the party cited below, at a sitting of the particular land courts here in Konin held on the Monday nearest after the feast of the Most Holy and Undivided Trinity in the most recently elapsed year 1727 — by the plaintiff party named below, but not then decided owing to the press of business at that time, and remaining and pending in the dockets awaiting adjudication among other causes: — the instigator of the present court and his informants, the Honourables Franciszek and Józef Ścibor Chełmscy, heirs and possessors of the goods of the villages of Łukom, Łomowo, and others belonging thereto, plaintiffs — have cited before the present Kalisz land court and the particular land terms of the district of Konin — the Honourables Krzysztof Prusimski as principal defendant, and further Antoni Prusimski his son, and the noblemen Niłecki, Kałuzoski, Jerzmanoski, Cieszkoski, Zdzęnicki, Tomaszeski, as retainers, and other noblemen whose surnames are at present unknown and are to be stated at the time of the session, and likewise the subjects of the goods of Trąpczyn whose names are at present unknown but are to be specified after they become known — the co-principals carrying out the orders of the same Honourable Prusimski — in respect of their persons and goods in general, and of any and all sums of money held and to be held — the sons with their parents, the retainers and subjects with their lords, to adhere to the protest made or to be made by [the plaintiffs] against the same — cited for the penalties prescribed by the laws of the Kingdom, which they have indeed incurred and merited, for the reason that the same Honourable Prusimski, having observed no neighbourly friendship and having given no consideration to it — nay, having set it altogether aside — not content with prior damages, acts of violence, injuries, and grievances — which, for the sake of preserving

the friendship that belongs to living as neighbours, have up to this time not yet been submitted to the public records of the Kingdom and set out — most particularly, at a certain time to be stated at the hearing of the cause, ordered shingles, otherwise called Szkudeł, a hundred threescore of them, prepared on the plaintiff's own estate and present there, together with a certain dwelling (gaza) to be burned by fire; and he was unwilling, despite being frequently called upon, to make good the damages caused thereby; and suffered the force of that same over-kindled fire to all but consume the children dwelling in that same dwelling (gaza) and endure it for anyone to suffer — and most recently, at a time to be declared at the session, ordered four plough-oxen kept for the use of the glassworks (officina vitraria) to be seized, without any pretext or claim, on the plaintiff's own estate, during his violent invasion of it in arms and in a band, to be detained by him for some time, to be deprived of their labour, and damage to be caused thereby — nay, on the occasion of a demand made by the Honourable Józef Chełmski, to scheme against his very life — not in the least satisfied with these things, and although he knew full well that the present plaintiffs had attended to their business at the ordinary general courts and at the tribunals of the Kingdom at Piotrków, and were residing there amid the various persons and causes there being pursued — he dared to instruct that same Honourable son of his and the above-mentioned retainers and subjects to commit additional violence upon [the plaintiffs]; and those so instructed were ordered to invade the plaintiffs' own estate, which belongs from antiquity to the goods of Łukom and has been in continuous use, with an armed force in groups, with sabres (frameis), with long and short firearms (bombardis) and other kinds of weaponry, forcibly and violently, and by such means — allegedly appropriating that same estate to themselves — to seize the hay gathered into carts from that same estate, in no small number of cartloads, to see to it that it be [uncertain: conveyed] to the goods of Trąbczyn, and thereby to cause the loss of livestock, and to carry out other acts of violence to be proven by full inquisition — through which they incurred penalties — for the decreeing of which penalties and for restitution of damages they are cited — the citation itself with its fuller copy in respect of the foregoing existing against them — the present Kalisz land court, in the particular land terms of the district of Konin, [examining] the controversies of both aforementioned parties — the plaintiff party of the Honourable Chełmski appearing personally through the Honourable Józef Chełmski in his own name and in the name of his brother, and the cited party of the Honourables Prusimski, husband and wife,

and of the other defendants appearing before it through the Honourable Konstanty Marszyński, and contesting judicially — having heard what was judicially done in the foregoing — deciding the term of the plaintiff party, instituted before the present court on account of the appropriation of the estate and of the violence and damages caused: since the cause of the estate is being ventilated before its own court, it finds that a commissarial descent of the Sworn Commissioner of the Palatinates of Poznań and Kalisz together with two arbitrators to be appointed from each side is necessary — decreeing that both parties together shall convey, at their mutual cost, the aforesaid Honourable Commissioner, namely the Honourable Albertus Biskupski, chosen by their mutual consent, and two arbitrators each, to and at the estate from which the controversy between the inheritances of the goods of Łukom and Trąmpczyn has arisen on the Monday nearest after the Sunday Misericordiae; and the said Honourable Commissioner and arbitrators shall inquire both into the ownership of the disputed estate and into the transgressions and damages caused as set out in the protests of both parties, shall conduct sworn inquiries (inquisitiones juratorias) in that name through sworn witnesses of advanced age well acquainted with the matter, and shall decide and adjudicate whatever is found by them among the parties according to justice and equity in all their claims — save for the right of appeal from the definitive sentence alone, reserved and safeguarded for the party that feels itself aggrieved, [such appeal] only to the present court, however — and both parties are to perform all the foregoing, [the default penalty being] a fine of fourteen Polish marks, payable by each party — half to the [opposing] party and half to the court — instantly and in the present [term] — under penalty of banishment and proscription from the Kingdom of Poland and its annexed dominions, which is hereby decreed by the present court and imposed upon the party contravening the present decree in the event of non-performance of the foregoing — and any General Royal Messenger present in [the relevant] place is appointed to proclaim and announce in the customary manner the penalty of the same banishment and proscription from the Kingdom of Poland and its annexed dominions, as hereby decreed and imposed in the manner aforesaid upon the party contravening the present decree, to the party complying with the present decree — and after the proclamation of the penalty, as well as after the proclamation of the same penalty of banishment and proscription from the Kingdom of Poland and its annexed dominions, the present cause together with its whole effect and the parties themselves are hereby remitted by the present court to the Magnificent

Captain of the place or to his competent captain's municipal court (officium capitaneale castrense), under the jurisdiction of which the party contravening the present decree — with his person and goods — is subject, for further and final execution in the foregoing to be carried out and determined in proper legal order — and the return of the exported citation having been acknowledged before the records of the present court and drawn from those same authentic records is herewith delivered to the plaintiff party; and the present court and its chancellery are hereby discharged in respect of that delivery.
