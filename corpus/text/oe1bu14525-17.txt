--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Eine königliche Schenkungsurkunde Friedrich Wilhelms, mit der dem regierenden Fürsten Friedrich Ludwig von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen die vormals geistlichen Dörfer Betsche, Świechocin, Stoki, Łowyń, Głażewo, Tarmowo, Zagórów, Skokum, Swiątniki, Kopojno, Olesnica, Drzewce, Grądzyn, Wrąbczyn, Winietz und Wolica samt Zubehör erblich und eigentümlich übertragen werden, gegen Zahlung des bisherigen landesherrlichen Kanons und der Kompetenz an die geistlichen Stiftungen; Anlass ist, dass die früher geschenkten Güter des entflohenen Starosten von Prusimski zu hoch verschuldet waren. Beigefügt sind die Beglaubigung der Abschrift durch von Steudener und die Eintragung des titulus possessionis für die Herrschaft Pszczew oder Betsche im Hypothekenbuch Vol. VII fol. 1.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A royal grant by Friedrich Wilhelm conveying to Friedrich Ludwig, Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, in heritable full property, sixteen formerly church villages including Pszczew, Łowyń, Zagórów, Kopojno, Drzewce and Wrąbczyn, with all rights, jurisdictions, woods, ponds and waters, subject only to the existing sovereign dues as a fixed unchangeable canon and the maintenance payments owed to the ecclesiastical corporations. Made because an earlier grant of the absconded starosta von Prusimski's debt-laden estates had fallen short. Filed with it are a certified copy made at Poznań and a note recording entry of the Prince's title to the lordship of Pszczew in the mortgage book.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
We Friedrich Wilhelm, by God's grace King of Prussia etc., do by this Our open letter attest and declare, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors to the Crown, the Electorate and Our remaining lands, that, whereas it has been laid before Us in all humility in what manner Our intention — to give the High-born Prince, Our friendly dear uncle Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, by the grant of the estates formerly belonging to the absconded former starosta von Prusimski, a token of the friendship and goodwill We bear towards him,

as also of Our Royal gratitude for the distinguished and beneficial services rendered to Us and to Our Royal house, has not been attained altogether as We had willed it, because the said estates were burdened with many debts, We have most highly resolved to leave over, to grant and to convey to His Lordship, heritably and in full property, the hereinafter named formerly ecclesiastical estates, namely the villages of Pszczew, Świechocin, Stoki, Łowyń, Głażewo, Tarmowo, Zagórów, Skokum, Swiątniki, Kopojno, Olesnica, Drzewce, Grądzyn, Wrąbczyn, Winietz and Wolica together with all their appurtenances, by whatever names they may be called, against payment:

1.) of the sovereign dues hitherto discharged therefrom, as a fixed and unal-

terable canon, and

2.) of the competence due therefrom to the ecclesiastical corporations or foundations.

We also do so hereby and by force of this present instrument, out of Royal power and plenitude, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors to the Crown, the Electorate and Our remaining lands, in such manner and wise that Our abovementioned friendly dear uncle, the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, shall have the abovenamed estates with all rights and jurisdictions attaching thereto, as the same have hitherto been exercised

or may lawfully be exercised, and no less with all their pertinences and other appurtenances, by whatever names they may be called, as the same have hitherto been enjoyed or may lawfully be enjoyed, and with all the lands belonging thereto, in fields, woods and meadows, likewise in ponds and waters, from now on for all eternity, for himself and his descendants and heirs, as a true, well-acquired and unrestricted property, irrevocably from Us and Our descendants and successors, to have,

to possess and to enjoy; and that there shall also be annexed to the same the power and authority to dispose thereof at pleasure under the conditions mentioned above, unhindered by anyone. We therefore command and enjoin all Our Supreme Provincial Collegia, as also all other authorities whom this concerns, hereby as graciously as earnestly, that they in no wise hinder nor obstruct the Lordship of the oft-mentioned Lord Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen in the heritable and proprietary possession, granted to the same, of the abovenamed estates together with their pertinences and appurtenances,

nor him or those to whom he may one day bequeath the same, or otherwise lawfully transfer them by other acts inter vivos aut mortis causa; but rather that they let His Lordship, and eventually them also, enjoy the same freely and quietly, themselves doing nothing against it, nor occasioning or permitting that such be done by others, on pain of Our most high displeasure and of unfailing severe punishment.

In witness whereof We have subscribed the present grant

instrument most highly with Our own hand and have caused Our greater Royal seal to be appended thereto. Thus done and given in Our Royal residence city of Berlin, the nineteenth day of the month of June after the birth of Christ Our Lord, in the one thousand seven hundred and ninety-seventh, and in the eleventh year of Our Royal reign.

(L. S.) F. Wilhelm. Hoym. Reck.
