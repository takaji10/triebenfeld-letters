--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports that the business is going well: a royal letter comes through Privy Councillor Beyhm, and Minister von Voss is instructed to report with all speed. Asks that Glen prepare thorough valuations matching the schedule and contracts, that the Prince send a power of attorney through Herr von Michaelis, and, more awkwardly, some pre-signed blank letter sheets, one being needed for a man engaged at Potsdam. A suitable loan is being arranged; Triebenfeld prefers to stay out of sight to avoid envy. Notes appointments: Burensprung as Land Master of Forests, Lindenau's new title.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Lord!

That everything is going according to wish, Your Most Serene Highness will deign to be convinced [uncertain: Fuh] from the letter which you receive today from His Majesty in the Privy Councillor Beyhm. To Minister von Voss the matter goes off today, that he is to report with the utmost dispatch – In this matter provision has now also been made that the business shall not be dragged along the long road. In the meantime it would now be very good if Glen were to draw up the valuations quite thoroughly, so that they conform to the schedule submitted and to the contracts mentioned therein. The contracts I have all here, only those of Tieten must [uncertain: ölzte] have there and keep in the best condition. Not before everything is in the clear (that is to say, what is to be done here) do I depart from here. A man in Potsdam – I have engaged, who

will be and remain active not only for now but also hereafter – but I must expend something upon it. Herr von Michaelis will write today for a power of attorney; this Your Most Serene [Highness] should send through him, only so that various things may be settled here on the spot without having to become long-drawn-out. I hope to God that the matter will now be settled quite according to wish, and even better than I at first believed – I shall assuredly attend to everything in the best manner with the most glowing zeal — I am at the same time also providing for a loan suited to the matter, so that one does not come into embarrassment and can keep one's word to the good King. Only this shall I always bear in mind, that the estates remain what they are, indeed that they shall ever become and remain the first jewel of all the property belonging to Your Serene Highness — and in this regard I shall only truly show myself effective at the finalisation of the business. Only for now I still wish to remain in

the background, so that envy may not, in consideration of my humble self, play Your Serene Highness a trick – The Minister, who here is truly in every respect quite well, commends himself most cordially to Your Serene Highness. One further matter I find necessary: let Your Serene Highness send me a couple of blank sheets for letters, such as I might have to write in haste to someone, that is, on the other side with a signature and above it

most devoted servant

H. I shall use such only in the most urgent cases, and so [uncertain: mippen] that Your Serene Highness will never be able to deny me your satisfaction, for one such is already necessary for the man in Potsdam. Tomorrow I go to His Serene Highness the Prince Sacken, to bring to the same the glad news of how far the matter has already advanced.

In deepest reverence I remain, until death, Your Most Serene Highness's

Herr von Burensprung, however, has become Land Master of Forests, and Wedel remains for the present what he is – In general, however, various changes are to be looked for. Lindenau has received the title of Excellency. Berlin, the 6th of October 1798

most humble and faithful servant

von Triebenfeld
