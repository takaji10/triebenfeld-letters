--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports progress on the affair in Berlin: a royal letter has come through Privy Councillor Beyhm and the matter goes to Minister v. Voss for speedy report. Urges thorough valuations conforming to the submitted Designation and the contracts, which Triebenfeld holds; notes an agent engaged at Potsdam, a power of attorney to be requested via Herr v. Michaelis, and a loan being arranged. Requests signed blank letter-sheets, and reports appointments: Burensprung as Land Forest Master, Lindenau's Excellency title.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince, Most gracious Lord! That everything is going according to wish, Your Most Serene Highness will [uncertain: Fuh] deign to be convinced from the letter which you receive today from His Majesty in the Privy Councillor Beyhm. The matter goes off today to Minister v. Voss, that he is to report with the utmost dispatch – In this matter provision has now also been made that the business shall not be dragged along the long road. In the meantime it would now be very well if [uncertain: Glen] were to draw up the valuations quite thoroughly, so that they conform with the submitted Designation and with the contracts mentioned therein. The contracts I have all here; only those of Tieten must [illegible] I have there and keep in the best condition. Until everything is in the clear (that is to say, what is to be done here), I shall not depart from here. A man in Potsdam – I have engaged, who

will be and remain active not only for now but also hereafter – but I must expend something upon it. Herr v. Michaelis will write today for a power of attorney; this Your Most Serene Highness may send through him, only so that various things may be settled here in loco without needing to become long-drawn-out. I hope to God that the matter will now be settled quite according to wish, and even better than I at first believed – I shall certainly attend to everything in the best manner with the most glowing zeal. — I am at the same time providing for a loan suited to the matter, so that one may not fall into embarrassment and may keep one's word to the good King. Only upon this shall I always be thinking, that the estates remain what they are, indeed that they shall become and remain always the first jewel of all the property belonging to Your Serene Highness — and in this regard I shall first show myself truly effective at the finalisation of the business. Only for now I still wish to remain in

the background, so that envy may not perhaps, on account of my humble self, play Your Serene Highness a trick – The Minister, who here is truly in every respect quite well, commends himself most cordially to Your Serene Highness. One further matter I find necessary: let Your Serene Highness send me a couple of blanks for letters, such as I might have to write to someone in haste; that is, on the other side with a signature and beneath it "most devoted servant H." I shall use such only in the most urgent cases, and in such a manner [uncertain: mippen] that Your Serene Highness can never withhold from me your satisfaction, for one is already necessary for the man in Potsdam. Tomorrow I go to His Serene Highness the Prince Sacken, to bring him the glad news how far the matter has already advanced. In deepest reverence I remain, until death, Your Most Serene Highness's — Herr v. Burensprung, however, has become Land Forest Master, and Wedel remains for the present what he is – In general, however, one meets with various changes. Lindenau has received the title of Excellency. Berlin, the 6th of October 1798, most humble and faithful servant v Triebenfeld
