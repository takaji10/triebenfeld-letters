auch wird er mit den Gl Etats Sachen ſprechen, daß man wegen

Ich habe hier einen alten Freund gefunden, der dem Strachwitz die Hölle
als das ſchönſte Paradies auszumahlen verſtaht, der ſoll Strachwitzen
alles voreiten — Es ist der Ober Amtmann Hegener aus Olla¬
bock - Er soll auch alles in Zagorowo revitiren - Er wird das
ohne Intresse thun den er ist ein edler Preuße und Ewr Durchlaucht
Verehrer
Ich würde jetzt mit der Trąbczyner Sache furiore vorschreiten auch

In Linsten Respect ersterben

Was macht Du für ein Hollo-Lahr schwört hoch und theuer er weis von

andereres Feindseeliges Wesen seinen Nahmen gemis braucht haben -

Es ist nur gut daß Köckritz mit Sack spricht, und daß ich da Ruhe

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports that General Köckritz has warned off von der Lahr, who has agreed to wait a year, and will press the treasury officials for patience over interest due to the Invalids' Fund. Triebenfeld will settle matters at Kalisz, then return to Breslau, and urges the Prince to push Goldstücher to complete an exchange transaction despite acute shortage of money. A senior district administrator, Hegener, is to prepare Strachwitz and inspect Zagorowo. A postscript quotes privy councillor Tismar and blames Flesch for the trouble.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

My judge has just returned from Berlin with the glad news that the two [of them], General Köckritz, has had von d. Lahr referred to rest [uncertain reading] and has most humbly declared himself willing to wait that year. He will also speak with the general treasury officials, so that patience shall be exercised regarding the interest payments to the Invalids' Fund. Now, since I am once in Poland, I shall set the affairs in Kalisz in the best train and then return to Breslau. To Goldstuckes and Flesch I am writing this very moment to bring the exchange transaction into the right track as far as possible — For the rest, the want of money is greater here than with us and I can promise nothing - Would to God the transaction came about. I have found an old friend here who knows how to paint hell to Strachwitz as the fairest paradise; he shall get everything ready for Strachwitz in advance — It is the senior district administrator Hegener from Olla bock - He is also to inspect everything in Zagorowo - He will do this without self-interest, for he is a noble Prussian and an admirer of Your Serene Highness. I would now proceed with the Trąbczyn matter with fury, and also set in train the Konojader[?] business, so that the whole forms one piece and

a good appearance is [uncertain: won] — Only let Your Serene Highness press upon Goldstücher that he bring the exchange transaction about. In [uncertain: utmost] respect I remain, until death, Your Serene Highness's. Ollaboch

the 31st of May

1810.

faithful, humble and obedient servant v Triebenfeld

just now I am reading a letter from the privy councillor Tismar, who is now the bosom friend of Köckritz - he says: What sort of hullabaloo are you making - Lahr swears high and dear that he knows nothing of it. He knows only too well that you are a well-to-do man - He has indeed convinced himself of it personally on your estates, in the case of a timber sale [uncertain: transacted] at a demesne farm. It must be that his attorney or some other hostile creature has misused his name - He is very angry at the dressing-down received from the General - So calm him down, etc.

I am now convinced to the highest degree of certainty that this was a piece of knavery by Flesch and his confederates, in order also to frighten [me] away from Breslau. But I shall find it out for certain, and then woe to the rogues- It is only good that Köckritz speaks with Sack, and that I thereby gain peace - The world sees after all that I still have good friends -
