Pro Parte M Prusimski Approbationis ad Cassan~ factae Oblata
Videatur hoc Loco Extractus Authentici ex Actis Castren~ Bidgosten~ Deprompti et Approbationem Termini ad Cassandam Condemnationem per Magnificum Antonium in Kolno Prusimski Capitaneum Nieszczeviensem Coram Actis Castrensibus Bidgostiensibus Recognitam in se complectentem Oblata ad Producta Porrecta.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Registervermerk vom Mai 1768: Für Antoni Prusimski von Kolno, Starost von Niszczewice, wird ein beglaubigter Auszug aus den Akten des Burggerichts Bromberg vorgelegt; er enthält die von ihm dort erklärte Anerkennung des Termins zur Aufhebung der Verurteilung.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A register note of May 1768: for Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice, an authentic extract from the records of the castle court at Bydgoszcz is brought in; it contains the approbation, declared by him there, of the term for quashing the condemnation.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
On behalf of the Right Honourable Prusimski — Oblata of the Approbation made for the Quashing

Let there be seen here the Oblata, submitted among the produced documents, of an authentic extract drawn from the records of the municipal court of Bydgoszcz, containing the approbation of the term for quashing the condemnation, acknowledged by the Right Honourable Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice, before the municipal court records of Bydgoszcz.
