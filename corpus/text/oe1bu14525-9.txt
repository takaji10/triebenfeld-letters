--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A royal grant by Friedrich Wilhelm conveying to his uncle Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, as unrestricted heritable property, sixteen formerly ecclesiastical villages including Betsche, Łowyń, Zagórów, Kopojno, Drzewce and Wrąbczyn with all appurtenances, subject only to the existing territorial dues as a fixed canon and the stipend owed to the ecclesiastical foundations. It replaces an earlier, debt-burdened grant of the absconded former Starosta von Prusimski's estates, and orders all authorities not to disturb his possession.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
We Friedrich Wilhelm, by the Grace of God King of Prussia etc., do hereby certify and declare by this Our open letter, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors in the Crown, the Electorate and Our remaining lands, that, after it had been most humbly laid before Us in what manner Our intention — namely to give to the High-Born Prince, Our friendly beloved uncle Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, by the grant of the estates formerly belonging to the absconded former Starosta von Prusimski, a token of the friendship and benevolence We bear towards him, and likewise of Our Royal gratitude for the distinguished and fruitful services rendered to Us and to Our Royal House — had not been attained entirely as We had willed, because the said estates had been burdened with many debts, We have most highly resolved to leave over, to grant and to convey to His Lordship, as heritable property of his own, the hereinafter named formerly ecclesiastical estates, namely the villages of Betsche, Świechocin, Stoki, Łowyń, Głażewo, Tarmowo, Zagórów, Skokum, Swiątniki, Kopojno, Olesnica, Drzewce, Grądzyń, Wrąbczyn, Wieniec and Wolica, together with all their appurtenances, whatever names these may bear, against the payment

1.) of the sovereign territorial dues hitherto discharged therefrom, as a fixed and unalterable canon, and

2.) of the stipend due therefrom to the ecclesiastical corporations or foundations.

We also do so hereby, and by virtue of this present, out of Royal power and plenitude, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors in the Crown, the Electorate and Our remaining lands, in such manner and to this effect: that Our above-mentioned friendly beloved uncle, the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, shall irrevocably have, possess and enjoy the above-named estates, with all rights and privileges attaching thereto, as these have hitherto been exercised or may lawfully be exercised, and no less with all their appurtenances (Portinentzien) and other accessories, whatever names these may bear, just as the same have hitherto been used or may lawfully be used, and with all the lands belonging thereto in fields, woodlands and meadows, likewise in ponds and waters, from henceforth for all eternity, for himself and his descendants and heirs, as a true, well-acquired and unrestricted property, from Us and Our descendants and successors; and that there shall likewise be attached to him the power and authority to dispose thereof at pleasure under the conditions mentioned above, without hindrance from any person. We therefore command and enjoin all Our supreme provincial collegia, as also all other authorities whom this concerns, herewith as graciously as earnestly, that they in no wise hinder or trouble either the oft-mentioned Lord Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen's Lordship in the heritable and proprietary possession granted to him of the above-named estates, together with their appurtenances and accessories, nor him or those

upon whom he may one day bequeath the same, or to whom he may otherwise lawfully transfer them by other acts inter vivos aut mortis causa, but rather shall let His Lordship, and eventually them also, enjoy the same freely and undisturbed; that they themselves do nothing to the contrary, nor occasion or permit such to be done by others, on pain of Our most high displeasure and of unfailing severe punishment. In witness whereof etc. Given at Berlin, 10 [uncertain: f.] June 1797.

Friedrich Willhelm

Deed of grant for the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, to the heritable and proprietary possession of the villages of Betsche, Świechocin, Stoki, Łowyń, Głażewo, Tarmowo, Zagórów, Skokum, Swiątniki, Kopojno, Olesnica, Drzewce, Grądzyń, Wrąbczyn, Wienietz and Wolica, together with appurtenances.
