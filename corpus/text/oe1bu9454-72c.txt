Hochwohlgebohrner Herr,
Insonders hochzuverehrender Herr Krieges und Forstrath
Den so eben vom Herrn Podprefecten o Brzechsta
durch den Advocaten v Wieckowski erhaltenen
Brief geben ich mir die Ehre Ew HochWohlgebohren

nen mittheilen und zugleich versichern wollen

Kalisz

gehorsamster Diener
der Kreis Rath
Thiel
ad 12. Merz 1812.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
District Councillor Thiel forwards to Triebenfeld an enclosed letter from Sub-prefect Herr v. Brzechsta, received via the advocate v. Wieckowski, reporting that Brzechsta's business will keep him in Warsaw until the 15th of the month, and that prospects concerning the Trąbczyner lordship are good. Written from Kalisz, with a note of receipt on 12 March 1812; otherwise consisting largely of formal assurances of service.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Right Honourable Sir, most particularly highly revered Sir, Councillor of War and Forests, The letter just now received from the Sub-prefect Herr v. Brzechsta by way of the advocate v Wieckowski I take the honour of communicating most obediently to Your Right Honourable in the enclosure, and from it Your Honour will be pleased to gather that Herr Brzechsta, on account of his business, does not intend to depart from Warsaw until the 15th of this month, and also that there is good prospect with respect to the Trąbczyner lordship; this I have wished to impart to Your Right Honourable, and at the same time to assure you that I shall never cease to call myself Your Right Honourable's Kalisz, the 8th of March 12. most obedient servant the District Councillor Thiel ad 12. March 1812.
