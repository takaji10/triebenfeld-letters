Hochwohlgebohrner Herr,
Insonders hochzuverehrender Herr Krieges und Forstrath
Den so eben vom Herrn Podprefecten o Brzechsta

Kalisz

gehorsamster Diener
der Kreis Rath
Thiel
ad 12. Merz 1812.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Thiel, a district councillor writing from Kalisz, forwards to Triebenfeld an enclosed letter from the sub-prefect Brzechsta, received through the advocate von Wieckowski. He points out two things from it: Brzechsta's business keeps him in Warsaw until the 15th of the month, and the prospects regarding the lordship of Trąbczyn look good. A short covering note, its value lying in the enclosure and in the encouraging word about Trąbczyn.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Right Honourable Sir, most particularly to be revered Councillor of War and Forests! The letter just now received from the sub-prefect o Brzechsta by way of the advocate von Wieckowski I take the honour of communicating to Your Right Honourable, most obediently, in the enclosure, and from it you will be pleased to gather [uncertain: to gather] that Mr Brzechsta, on account of his business, does not intend to depart from Warsaw until the 15th of this month, and also that there is good prospect with respect to the lordship of Trąbczyn; this I wished to impart to Your Right Honourable and at the same time to assure you that I shall never cease to call myself Your Right Honourable's

Kalisz

the 8th of March 12.

most obedient servant

the district councillor

Thiel

received 12 March 1812.
