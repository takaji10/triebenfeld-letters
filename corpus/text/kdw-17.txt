Dokument Nr 755
Gniezno, 16 kwietnia 1415
Starosta generalny Wielkopolski zaświadcza, że Święszek i Olbracht (?) bracia z Gulczewa sprzedali rolę na przedmieściu Gniezna klasztorowi cystersów z Lądu.
Kop.: Włocławek, Archiwum Diecezjalne, Cystersi - Ląd, rkps 3 (Liber privilegiorum monasterii z XVIII w.), s. 173.
In nomine Domini amen. Ad perpetuam rei memoriam. Nos Joannes de Sczecocini castellanus Lublinensis et capitaneus Maioris Poloniae generalis1 significamus tenore praesentium, quibus expedit, universis, praesentibus et futuris, praesentium notitiam habituris, quod ad nostram et aliorum eiusdem terrae nobilium venientes praesentiam Swanseck et Supracht fratres germani de Golczewo2, non compulsi, non coacti, nec aliquo errore devio seducti, freti amicorum suorum consiliis, sani mente et corpore existentes, aream eorum totam cum platea, sitam ad prope ecclesiam Sancti Laurentii in suburbio Gnesnensi, pro sedecim marcis communis monetae currentis, numeri Polonicalis, ad quamlibet marcam quadraginta octo grossos computando, venerabili viro domino Gerhardo abbati et conventui suo monasterii Landensis3 rite et rationabiliter in perpetuum vendiderunt cum omnibus utilitatibus, pertinentibus et distinctionibus in longum et latum quomodocunque ad eandam aream spectantibus ac cum omni iure et dominio, prout soli tenuerunt et habuerunt, nihil iuris pro se et pro suis penitus reservantes, coram nobis more solito in perpetuum resignarunt tenendam, habendam, utifruendam, commutandam, vendendam et in suos usus beneplacitos convertendam, prout Gerhardo praedicto et conventui suo monasterii Landensis melius et utilius videbitur expedire. Harum quibus sigillum nostrum praesentibus est appensum. Actum et datum in Gnesna feria tertia proxima post dominicam Misericordia Domini anno incarnationis eiusdem millesimo quadringentesimo quatuordecimoa, praesentibus nobilibus viris: Petro de Gunice4, Nicolao de Trambczyno5, Andrea de ibidem6, Pełka de Kuchary7, Joanne [deb] Swinary8, Nicolao de Grabienice9 et aliis pluribus fidedignis testibus ad praemissa vocatis specialiter et rogatis.
atak Kop., ale musi to być pomyłka kopisty (quarto - quinto), bowiem w 1414 r. Jan ze Szczekocin nie był jeszcze starostą Wlkp. Data 1415 zgodna z jego itinerarium - zob. dok. nast.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Jan von Szczekociny, Generalstarost von Großpolen, beurkundet, dass die Brüder Swanseck und Supracht von Gulczewo dem Abt Gerhard und dem Konvent von Ląd ihren Grund bei der Kirche St. Laurentius in der Gnesener Vorstadt für sechzehn Mark verkauft haben. Unter den Zeugen sind Mikołaj und Andrzej von Trąbczyn. Gnesen, 16. April 1415; die Abschrift hat 1414, nach den Herausgebern ein Schreibfehler. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski VII, Nr. 755.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Jan of Szczekociny, captain general of Greater Poland, attests that the brothers Swanseck and Supracht of Gulczewo have sold Abbot Gerhard and the convent of Ląd their plot by the church of St Lawrence in the suburb of Gniezno for sixteen marks. Among the witnesses are Mikołaj and Andrzej of Trąbczyn. Gniezno, 16 April 1415; the copy has 1414, a copyist's error in the editors' view. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski VII, no. 755.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 755

Gniezno, 16 April 1,415

The captain general of Greater Poland attests that Święszek and Olbracht (?), brothers of Gulczewo, sold a plot in the suburb of Gniezno to the Cistercian monastery of Ląd.

Copy: Włocławek, Diocesan Archive, Cistercians – Ląd, MS 3 (Liber privilegiorum monasterii, 18th century), p. 173.

In the name of the Lord, amen. For the perpetual memory of the matter. We, Jan of Szczekociny, castellan of Lublin and captain general of Greater Poland¹, make known by the tenor of these presents to all whom it concerns, present and to come, who will have knowledge of these presents, that, coming before us and other nobles of the same land, Swanseck and Supracht, brothers german of Gulczewo², not compelled, not forced, nor led astray by any error, relying on the counsel of their friends, being sound in mind and body, have duly and reasonably sold for ever to the venerable man Lord Gerhard, abbot, and his convent of the monastery of Ląd³ their whole plot with the square, lying near the church of St Lawrence in the suburb of Gniezno, for sixteen marks of common current money, of Polish reckoning, reckoning forty-eight groschen to each mark, with all its uses, appurtenances and divisions in length and breadth in any way belonging to that plot, and with all right and lordship as they alone held and had it, reserving nothing of right for themselves and theirs, and have resigned it before us in the usual way for ever, to be held, had, used and enjoyed, exchanged, sold and turned to their own pleasure, as shall seem better and more useful to the said Gerhard and his convent of the monastery of Ląd. In witness of these, to which our seal is hung. Done and given at Gniezno on the Tuesday next after Misericordia Domini Sunday, in the year of His incarnation one thousand four hundred and fourteenᵃ, in the presence of the noble men: Piotr of Gunice⁴, Mikołaj of Trąbczyn⁵, Andrzej of the same⁶, Pełka of Kuchary⁷, Jan [ofᵇ] Świniary⁸, Mikołaj of Grabienice⁹ and many other witnesses worthy of trust, called and asked specially to the foregoing.

ᵃSo the copy, but this must be a copyist's error (quarto – quinto), since in 1,414 Jan of Szczekociny was not yet captain of Greater Poland. The date 1,415 agrees with his itinerary; see the next document.

ᵇmissing in the copy.

¹Jan of Szczekociny, see above, no. 635/6.

²Gulczewo (Golczewo), parish of Marzenin, north of Września (Koz. 1, pp. 54, 89, 412; 3, pp. 560, 671; 4, p. 239). Świętosław (Święszek) occurs 1,404–1,447 (SHGWlkp.), later for many years burgrave and żupca at Gniezno, Pyzdry and Konin, 1,418–1,447 (GUrz., p. 171); compare also MPH V, p. 476. His brother Supracht, Czupracht, occurs only here; compare however Czuprachtowe Gulczewo (Koz., l.c.).

³Abbot Gerard, see above, no. 412/9.

⁴Piotr of Gonice, see above, no. 433/1.

⁵⁻⁶Trąbczyn, a parish village south-east of Zagórów (Koz. 5, p. 414). Mikołaj occurs from 1,402 (SHGWlkp.), later deputy standard-bearer at Gniezno 1,434–1,440 (GUrz. C 95). Andrzej occurs 1,414 (here) to 1,428 (SHGWlkp.).

⁷Kuchary Kościelne, a parish village north-west of Rychwał (Koz. 4, p. 432). Pełka, deputy standard-bearer at Konin 1,411–1,414, burgrave 1,414–1,416, then often an assessor of the court at Konin (GUrz. C 472, 363, 412).

⁸Świniary, parish of Kłecko, north-east of Kłecko (Koz. 1, p. 317; 5, p. 379). Jan occurs 1,396–1,426 (SHGWlkp.).

⁹Grabienice, a parish village north-west of Rychwał in the Konin land (Koz. 4, pp. 260–261). Mikołaj occurs 1,413–1,428 (SHGWlkp.).
