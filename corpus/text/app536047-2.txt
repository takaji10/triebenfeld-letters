--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Dekret des Kalischer Landgerichts in Konin, Eintrag Nr. 43 der Sitzung, die am 3. Mai 1784 begann. Parteien sind Michał Drogosław Skórzewski, Unterkämmerer von Posen und Erbherr von Biskupie oder Bystrzyca, und Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa und Erbherr von Łukomia und Łomowa, jeder zugleich Kläger und Geladener; mit ihnen geladen sind Franciszek Stadnicki, Starost von Ostrzeszów, samt seinen Kindern mit der verstorbenen Teresa, geborene Wężyk, und Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice und Erbherr von Trąbczyn. Prusimski wird die Verurteilung entgegengehalten, die Chełmski beim Ortstermin in Grab am 11. September 1780 wegen Zuwiderhandlung gegen das Tribunalsdekret erwirkt hat. Eine am Donnerstag nach Cantate 1783 in Pyzdry ausgestellte Vollmacht wird als ausreichend befunden; Prusimski weist sein Recht, gehört zu werden, nicht nach und unterwirft sich dem Gericht, das ihn gegen Zahlung einer halben Buße an die Gegenseite und einer Viertelbuße an das Gericht zulässt. Zur Sache: Weil die Parteien einander wegen der Abgrenzung von Biskupice oder Bystrzyca gegen Łomowa oder Łukom vor Gericht gebracht haben, hält das Gericht einen Ortstermin auf dem strittigen Grund für nötig, am 16. Juli des laufenden Jahres. Für die Grenze sollen die Parteien den Unterkämmerer des Kalischer Distrikts oder sein Feldamt gewinnen; für die Gewalttaten und Schäden sollen sie das Gericht selbst oder einen der Kämmerer beiziehen, der untersuchen, Eide abnehmen und den Ersatz festsetzen soll. Die Parteien haben bei Strafe der Verbannung zu gehorchen und auch die Anrainer zu laden.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A decree of the land court of Kalisz sitting at Konin, entry no. 43 of the sitting that opened on 3 May 1784. The parties are Michał Drogosław Skórzewski, sub-chamberlain of Poznań and heir of Biskupie or Bystrzyca, and Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa and heir of Łukomia and Łomowa, each both plaintiff and cited; cited with them are Franciszek Stadnicki, Starost of Ostrzeszów, with his children by the late Teresa, born Wężyk, and Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice and heir of Trąbczyn. Against Prusimski the condemnation is raised that Chełmski obtained at the sitting on the ground at Grab on 11 September 1780 for contravention of the Tribunal's decree. A power of attorney made at Pyzdry on the Thursday after Cantate Sunday 1783 is found sufficient; Prusimski does not show his right to be heard and submits to the court, which admits him on payment of half a penalty to the other side and a quarter to the court. On the cause itself: because the parties have brought each other before the court over the boundary between Biskupice or Bystrzyca and Łomowa or Łukom, the court finds a sitting on the disputed ground necessary, on 16 July of the current year. For the boundary the parties are to obtain the sub-chamberlain of the district of Kalisz or his field office; for the acts of violence and the damage they are to bring the court itself or one of the chamberlains, who is to inquire, take oaths and fix the compensation. The parties are to obey on pain of banishment and to cite the neighbours too.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
No. 43: Between the Illustrious Right Honourable Michał Drogosław Skórzewski, Sub-chamberlain of Poznań, Knight of the Orders of Saint Stanislaus and Saint Anne, heir of the estates of Biskupie or Bystrzyca, Plaintiff and Cited, through the Honourable Józef Stablewski; the Right Honourable Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, heir of the estates of Łukomia and Łomowa, Cited and Plaintiff, through the Honourable Konstanty Suchodolski; the Illustrious Right Honourable Franciszek, Count of Stadniki, Stadnicki, Starost of Ostrzeszów, Knight of the Order of Saint Stanislaus, [heir] of the estates of [uncertain: Królików], Biała and Lądek, and the successors of the late Right Honourable Teresa, born Wężyk, begotten with the same Illustrious Right Honourable Stadnicki, heirs and possessors of the above-written estates, Cited, through the Honourable Antoni Miroszewski; [and] the Illustrious Right Honourable Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, Knight of the Order of Saint Stanislaus, heir of the estates of Trąbczyn with appurtenances, Cited, through the Honourable Wojciech Żeromski —

regarding the objected condemnation, obtained and published at the condescension term held on the ground of the estates of the village of Grab on the eleventh day of the month of September in the year 1780, [uncertain: on the part] of the Right Honourable Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, against the Illustrious Right Honourable Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, to his advantage, for contravention of the Tribunal's Decree: the Court holds that the party objecting on behalf of the Right Honourable Chełmski is bound to produce a proxy in order to object the condemnation. And since a proxy made before the records of the Pyzdry Municipal Court on Thursday after Cantate Sunday in the year 1783 is laid down, a sufficient proxy being therefore found, the Court decrees that the same Illustrious Right Honourable Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, shall show his right of standing forthwith; and since he does not show it, but rather submits himself to the moderation of its judgement,

therefore, for the establishment of his right of standing, he shall pay: to the objecting party, with recovery from the same reserved, half of a capital bail; but to the Court half of a half bail, forthwith, under penalty of banishment, to be published at the close of the sessions. Meanwhile the Court declares that he has the right of standing, and orders the trial.

In the trial, deciding the terms of the parties, and their controversies having been heard: since the parties bring one another before the present Court for the delimitation of the estates of Biskupice or Bystrzyca from the estates of Łomowa or Łukom, the Court therefore finds that, for the delimitation of those estates, a condescension to the field, on the disputed ground between the aforesaid estates, is necessary, on and for the 16th day of the month of July next coming in the present year — ruling that the parties shall obtain for the above-written term the Illustrious Right Honourable Sub-chamberlain of the District of Kalisz, or his field office; but, as to the cause of the acts of violence and damages, shall bring for the same term its own Court, the absence of one or two notwithstanding, or one of the Honourable Boundary Surveyors [two words not fully read: "legitime praeven~"]. Which court, or he who comes to the condescension, shall inquire into the acts of violence and damages, and shall receive oaths; shall decree the compensation to be made, to whom, by whom and how much shall be due; and shall conduct inquisitions, fix terms, attach rigours, and shall decide, adjudge and moderate the present cause according to the contents of the parties' terms and manifests. Whose decision — as to the boundary cause, that of the Illustrious Right Honourable Sub-chamberlain; as to the cause of fact, that of its own Court or of the Honourable Boundary Surveyors — they shall be bound and obliged to obey and acquiesce in, under penalty of banishment, to be published upon the contravening party at that term, before the same [judges] of the condescension. The penalty of fourteen Polish marks having been paid — to be set off party against party, and to its own Court paid by halves by each party, under the usual rigour, to be extended from the register of penalties. For which condescension term the parties shall cite those whom the matter requires, and also the neighbours; those cited shall appear under the above-written penalty of banishment.
