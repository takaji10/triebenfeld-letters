Wohlgebohrner Herr
Insonders höchst zuverehrender Herr
Bau und Oeconom Rath
Der Müller Luka hat sich der
Entsagung des Contract haltens
bey mir geoffenbahret, und will
also zu diesem Ende bey Ew.
Wohlgebohren in Kalisch daß
nähere abmachen, Er hat
auch die qu. 25. Rthl richtig in Empfang
genommen.
Bey dieser Gelegenheit brachte
Er auch noch 2 Leuthe zur Annahme
einiges Landes mit zur Stelle diese
würden nun noch bis weiter hin
vertröstet, und ihnen versichert:
daß sobald, man nur mit
Tromczin und dem H. v. Szucki
ein Ende gemacht haben würde
Sie auch für allen Land in
Besitz
Besitz erhalten sollten, so lange
aber müsten Sie sich noch gedulden.
Wegen den Abstand des p. v. Szucki
haben doch Ew Wohlgebohren die Gütte
und dieses bey der Königl. Regierung
zu melden, und wenn auch noch zu
den 1000. Rthl welche die Gemeinde geben
will etwas Sr. Durchlaucht dazu
hergeben möchte, indem solches 10. Tage
wieder ersetzt würde, und alsdan
unter der Gemeinde Ruhe u Friede
seyn würde. Hochdieselben können
selbsten einsehen wie nunmehro
die Pikantri des v. Szucki auf
die Unterthanen durch das Angebot
der 1000. Rthl Abstand einer mehr zu
nimt; um die Armen Leuthe leiden
werden müßen.
Im übrigen bitten wir gantz gehor¬
samst uns durch den p. Luke nähern
Verhaltungs Befehle hochgeneigt zu
kommen zu laßen; Was wir sowohl
in ansichung derer noch etwa
kommenden Leuthen, welche mit Gelde
zu Land Besitz kommen dürften
als auch wegen den v. Szucki
sinnes
seines Abstandes zu thun und
zu laßen haben. Wir
werden schon unsere bestes
dabey thun.
Die wir übrigens die Ehre
haben Hochachtungsvoll zu
beharren.
Ew. Wohlgebohren
P. S.
der Tischler welcher ebenfals

Zagorowo

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Titz and Bancke report to the Building and Economy Councillor that the miller Luka has renounced his contract, will settle the details at Kalisch, and has received the 25 Rthl due; two men brought by him to take up land have been held off with promises until matters with Tromczin and Herr v. Szucki are concluded. They ask that the Royal Government be informed of v. Szucki's withdrawal-payment, and whether His Serene Highness might add to the commune's 1000 Rthl, and request instructions on further applicants; a joiner intended for Szetlewek has bought a house in Zagorowo instead.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Your Honour, especially most highly to be revered Sir, Councillor of Building and Economy. The miller Luka has disclosed to me his renunciation of the keeping of the contract, and accordingly wishes to settle the particulars to that end with Your Honour at Kalisch. He has also duly received the 25 Rthl in question.

On this occasion he further brought along with him 2 men to take up some land; these have now been put off with promises until further on, and assured: that as soon as an end should have been made with Tromczin and Herr v. Szucki, they too should be put in possession of all the land in

possession, but until then they would have to be patient. Concerning the withdrawal-payment of the said v. Szucki, may Your Honour have the goodness to report this to the Royal Government, and if perhaps, in addition to the 1000 Rthl which the commune is willing to give, His Serene Highness might contribute something further thereto, inasmuch as such would be made good again within 10 days, and thereafter there would be quiet and peace within the commune. Your Honour can yourself perceive how by now the piquancy of v. Szucki towards the subjects increases the more through the offer of the 1000 Rthl of withdrawal-payment; how the poor people will have to suffer.

For the rest we beg most obediently that Your Honour be most graciously pleased to let us have, through the said Luke, closer instructions as to conduct; as to what we have to do and to leave undone both in respect of any further people who may yet come who might come into possession of land with money, and also in the matter of v. Szucki's mind as to

his withdrawal. We shall for our part do our best in the matter.

Wherewith we for the rest have the honour, with high respect, to persist,

Your Honour's

P. S.

The joiner, who likewise wished to live in Szetlewek, out of desperation — being wholly consternated — has straightway bought a house in Zagorowo and means also to remain living here. Zagorowo,

the 16th April 1799. wholly most obedient servants Titz

Bancke
