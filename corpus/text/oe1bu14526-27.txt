--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Titz and Bancke report to a Councillor of Building and Economy that the miller Luka has given up the contract and will settle details at Kalisz, having received 25 Rthl. Two more men seeking land were put off until matters with Trąbczyn and von Szucki are closed. They ask that the Royal Government be told of von Szucki's withdrawal-payment, and that the Prince add to the 1,000 Rthl the community offers, since the offer is inflaming von Szucki's harassment of the subjects; they request instructions.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Your Honour, most particularly highly honoured Sir, Councillor of Building and Economy. The miller Luka has disclosed to me his renunciation of keeping to the contract, and accordingly wishes, to this end, to settle the particulars with Your Honour in Kalisz. He has also duly received the aforesaid 25 Rthl.

On this occasion he also brought along with him 2 more men to take up some land; these have now been put off with hopes until a later time, and assured that as soon as an end should have been made with Trąbczyn and Mr. von Szucki, they too would obtain possession of all the land in

possession, but until then they would have to be patient a while longer. Concerning the withdrawal-payment of Mr. von Szucki, may Your Honour have the kindness to report this to the Royal Government, and also, if something over and above the 1000 Rthl which the community is willing to give might be contributed toward it by His Serene Highness, inasmuch as the same would be made good again within 10 days, and thereafter there would be quiet and peace within the community. Your Honour can yourself perceive how the pointed provocation of von Szucki upon the subjects is now increasing ever more through the offer of the 1000 Rthl withdrawal-payment; so that the poor people will have to suffer for it.

For the rest we beg most obediently that Your Honour be graciously pleased to let us have, through the said Luke, closer instructions as to our conduct: what we are to do and to leave undone, both with regard to such further men as may yet come, who might come into possession of land with money, and also with regard to von Szucki's mind as to

his withdrawal. We shall assuredly do our best in the matter.

We who otherwise have the honour to remain, with high respect,

Your Honour's

P. S.

The joiner who likewise wished to dwell in Szetlewek has, out of desperation, being wholly consternated, at once bought a house in Zagorowo and intends also to remain living here. Zagorowo,

the 16th of April 1799. Most obedient servants Titz

Bancke
