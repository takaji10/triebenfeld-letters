Wohlgebohrner Herr
Insonders höchst zuverehrender Herr
Bau und Oeconom Rath
Der Müller Luka hat sich der
Entsagung des Contract haltens
bey mir geoffenbahret, und will
also zu diesem Ende bey Ew.
Wohlgebohren in Kalisch daß
nähere abmachen, Er hat
auch die qu. 25. Rthl richtig in Empfang
genommen.
Bey dieser Gelegenheit brachte
Er auch noch 2 Leuthe zur Annahme
einiges Landes mit zur Stelle diese
würden nun noch bis weiter hin
vertröstet, und ihnen versichert:
daß sobald, man nur mit
Trąbczyn und dem H. v. Szucki
ein Ende gemacht haben würde
Sie auch für allen Land in
Besitz
Besitz erhalten sollten, so lange
aber müsten Sie sich noch gedulden.
Wegen den Abstand des p. v. Szucki
haben doch Ew Wohlgebohren die Gütte
und dieses bey der Königl. Regierung
zu melden, und wenn auch noch zu
den 1000. Rthl welche die Gemeinde geben
will etwas Sr. Durchlaucht dazu
hergeben möchte, indem solches 10. Tage
wieder ersetzt würde, und alsdan
unter der Gemeinde Ruhe u Friede
seyn würde. Hochdieselben können
selbsten einsehen wie nunmehro
die Pikanterie des v. Szucki auf
die Unterthanen durch das Angebot
der 1000. Rthl Abstand immer mehr zu
nimt; um die Armen Leuthe leiden
werden müßen.
Im übrigen bitten wir gantz gehor¬
samst uns durch den p. Luke nähern
Verhaltungs Befehle hochgeneigt zu
kommen zu laßen; Was wir sowohl
in ansichung derer noch etwa
kommenden Leuthen, welche mit Gelde
zu Land Besitz kommen dürften
als auch wegen den v. Szucki
sinnes
seines Abstandes zu thun und
zu laßen haben. Wir
werden schon unsere bestes
dabey thun.
Der wir übrigens die Ehre
haben Hochachtungsvoll zu
beharren.
Ew. Wohlgebohren
P. S.
der Tischler welcher ebenfals
in Szetlewek wohnen wolte
hat aus Desperation in dem
Er sich gantz consternirt be¬
fand, so gleich ein Haus
in Zagorowo gekauft und
will auch hier wohnen bleiben.
Zagorowo
d. 16. Aprill 1799.
gantz gehorsamste Diener

Bancke

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Titz and Bancke report to the Building and Economy Councillor that the miller Luka has renounced the contract and will settle the particulars at Kalisch, having received his 25 Rthl; two men seeking land were put off until matters with Trąbczyn and Herr v. Szucki are concluded. They ask that the Royal Government be informed of Szucki's withdrawal-payment, that His Serene Highness supplement the community's 1000 Rthl, and for further instructions; a postscript notes the joiner intended for Szetlewek has bought a house in Zagorowo.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Your Honour, most particularly highly to be revered Sir, Councillor of Building and Economy,

The miller Luka has disclosed to me his renunciation of keeping to the contract, and accordingly wishes, to this end, to settle the particulars with Your Honour at Kalisch. He has also duly received the aforesaid 25. Rthl.

On this occasion he further brought along with him to the spot 2 men for the taking-up of some land; these were for now put off until further on, and assured that as soon as an end should have been made with Trąbczyn and with Herr v. Szucki they too should be put in possession of all the land [text lost]

possession; but until then they must yet have patience. Concerning the withdrawal-payment of the said v. Szucki, may Your Honour have the goodness to report this to the Royal Government, and, if in addition to the 1000. Rthl which the community is willing to give, something might be furnished thereto by His Serene Highness, inasmuch as the same would be made good again within 10. days, and thereafter there would be quiet and peace within the community. Your Honour can yourself perceive how by now the piquancy of v. Szucki towards the subjects is ever increasing through the offer of the 1000. Rthl withdrawal-payment; for which the poor people will have to suffer.

For the rest we most obediently beg that Your Honour be most graciously pleased to let further orders of conduct come to us by the said Luke: as to what we are to do and to leave undone, both in respect of such further people as may yet come who might come to possession of land with money, and also concerning v. Szucki's mind as to

his withdrawal-payment. We shall assuredly do our best therein.

We who for the rest have the honour to remain, with high esteem,

Your Honour's

P. S.

The joiner, who likewise wished to dwell in Szetlewek, out of desperation — finding himself wholly consternated — has straightway bought a house in Zagorowo and means to remain living here also.

Zagorowo, the 16th April 1799.

most obedient servants
Titz

Bancke
