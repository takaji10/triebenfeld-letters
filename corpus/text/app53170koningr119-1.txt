--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Bericht des Gerichtsboten Stanisław Stachowski von Łukomia, eingetragen in Konin im März 1783. Er hat eine Ladung des großpolnischen Generalstarosten Kazimierz Raczyński vom 10. März 1783 vor das Burggericht Kalisz zugestellt: an Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice und Erbherrn von Trąbczyn, und an dessen Verwalter Leon Maszewski, auf Betreiben von Stanisław Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa und Erbherr von Łukom, und dessen Verwalter Ignacy Jaroszewski. Maszewski sei in den Kiefernwald eingefallen, den die Linie des nachkommissarischen Dekrets auf der Karte samt einem Olęder dem Gut Łukom belassen habe, und habe Chełmskis Leuten, die dort nur das nötige Holz holten, Ochsen, Wagen und Äxte abgenommen. Genannte Leute aus Trąbczyn und Nowa Wieś werden mit der Ladung unter Arrest gestellt; auch Prusimski soll bestraft werden, falls die Gewalttaten auf seinen Befehl geschahen. Der Bote hat die Ladung im Vorwerk von Nowa Wieś hinterlegt.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A report of the court messenger Stanisław Stachowski of Łukomia, entered at Konin in March 1783. He has served a citation of the Starost General of Greater Poland, Kazimierz Raczyński, of 10 March 1783, before the castle court of Kalisz: on Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice and heir of Trąbczyn, and on his steward Leon Maszewski, at the instance of Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa and heir of Łukom, and of his steward Ignacy Jaroszewski. Maszewski is charged with breaking into the pine wood that the line of the post-commission decree on the map left, with one Olęder settler, to the estate of Łukom, and with taking oxen, carts and axes from Chełmski's people, who were fetching only the wood they needed. Named men of Trąbczyn and Nowa Wieś are put under arrest by the citation; Prusimski too is to be punished if the acts of violence were done at his order. The messenger left the citation at the farm at Nowa Wieś.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
On behalf of the Right Honourable Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, against His Honour Prusimski — Report

To the office and present records of the Konin Municipal Court, appearing in person, the Honest Stanisław Stachowski of the village of Łukomia, General Royal Court Summoner, being sound, recognized that he had served authentic citation letters for the Kalisz Municipal Court sessions, of the underwritten tenor as follows:

Casimir Nałęcz Raczyński of Raczyno and Matoszyn, Captain-General of Greater Poland — to you, the Illustrious Right Honourable Antoni Prusimski, Captain of Niszczewice, Knight of the Distinguished Order of Saint Stanislaus, heir of the estates of Trąbczyn and others pertaining thereto, and to the Honourable Leon Maszewski, steward of those same estates — from all your estates generally and from all sums of money of every kind, had and to be had, we command: that you appear in person and peremptorily at our own next Kalisz Municipal Court sessions, at the instance of the Right Honourable Ignacy Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, heir of the estates of Łukom and others, and of the Honourable Ignacy Jaroszewski, steward of those same estates, as plaintiff — who, adhering to his documents, both manifestos made and to be made, cite you to see and hear: you, or rather you, the Honourable Leon Maszewski — as an invader of the estates — with respect to the pine forest line, assigned by the post-commissorial decree concerning the adjudication of the boundary delimitation between the estates of Trąbczyn and the estates of Łukom as marked on the map, together with the Hollandry, hereditarily reserved and left as belonging to the estates of Łukom as the plaintiff's property, and held in the plaintiff's continuous peaceful possession up to the present act now of the erection of the boundary mounds carried out under the Tribunal decree; of the oxen, wagons and axes of the plaintiff's men entering that same pine forest only for the necessary extraction of timber, who were intercepted; in respect of the said interception and invasion of the pine forest together with the working men Thomas Fryzyer, Stanisław Lokaj, Jan Wochnicki — keepers of the Trąbczyn forest — Simon Jachowski — likewise keeper of the Nowa Wieś forest — Stanisław, Józef, and Grzegorz, coachmen, and Jan Mathias, court summoner, who by the present citation are arrested in your keeping: to be adjudged deserving of condign penalties; and that distinct penalties should also be extended to you, the Right Honourable Prusimski, if the same violent acts

are found to have been carried out on your orders; notwithstanding your manifesto and site inspection illegally procured by you — these being on the contrary annulled and held for nothing — for your action in the plaintiff's role to be adjudged incompetent against you; the stationing of men by you, pretended as null, to be removed; the presumed violences by you to be held as null; the production of the men to be ordered and an inquiry to be conducted; that you be cited and respond judicially.

Given in the Kalisz Municipal Court on 10 March 1783.

In the estates of Nowa Wieś and at the farmstead located there, at the seat in the heated room, in the presence of the revenue scribe, on this present day, the contents of the present act set down and left notified.
