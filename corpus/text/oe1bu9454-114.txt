--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A hurried, partly illegible note to the Councillor of War abusing an unnamed adversary as a rascal who has made endless difficulties for His Serene Highness and now, with nothing further to be squeezed out, wants the business closed; urges that the favourable moment be pressed and the matter settled so the recipient is no longer aggrieved, mentions a greeting from von Schlabrendorff, reports the writer's own affair neither well nor ill with notice given against Asch the elder, promises settlement within days including the large bill of exchange, and notes his recovering health.
