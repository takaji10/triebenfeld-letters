Actum in Conin Feria Sexta pridie Festi Sancti Thomae Apostoli Anno Domini 1771
Pro Parte Magnifici Chełmski Armorum Praesentatio
Ad Officium et Acta praesentia Castrensia Coninensia personaliter veniens Providus Nicolaus Krzepczyński de Villa Myszaków Ministerialis Regni Generalis Speciali Commissione Magnifici Stanislai Scibor Chełmski Bonorum Łukom Cum Attinentiis Haeredis presentavit Officio praesenti Arma Infrascripta et quidem Frameas Quatuor in Vaginis cum Cingulis coreis Item unam evaginatam Ferro Obductas Item Chastas Quatuor Spiculis ferreis super baculos Obsignatas Item Bombardas Longiores Tres et Sclopetum unum Orichalco obductum Plumbo et Minuto Pulvere Onustas. Quae praemissa praeter alia arma per Partem infrascriptam sublata In Borra Lusnie nuncupata Hollandis Superin[ae]dificata ad Bona Łukom nunc et ab antiquo Spectante Circa Violentam Magnifici Antonii a Kolno Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczeviensis Bonorum Trąmpczyn Contiguorum Haeredis Superinvasionem ac Armatis hominibus Licentiosis Gazae Tabernatoris Obsessionem Feria Secunda Immediate praeterita Arma eadem per Magnificum Chełmski Intercepta Is idem Ministerialis Suprascriptus esse asseruit Quae sic praesentata rursus ad se recepit et de receptis Officium suum praesentibus Quietatus est.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Eintrag vom 20. Dezember 1771. Der Gerichtsbote Nicolaus Krzepczyński von Myszaków legt im besonderen Auftrag von Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski dem Burgamt Konin Waffen vor: vier Säbel in Scheiden mit Riemen und einen blanken, vier Spieße mit eisernen Spitzen, drei lange Büchsen und eine mit Messing beschlagene Flinte, mit Blei und feinem Pulver geladen. Nach seiner Angabe hat Chełmski sie am vergangenen Montag im Wald Lusnia erbeutet, als Antoni Prusimski von Kolno, Starost von Niszczewice, dort gewaltsam einfiel und Bewaffnete das Haus des Schankwirts belagerten. Der Bote nimmt die Waffen wieder an sich.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
An entry of 20 December 1771. The court messenger Nicolaus Krzepczyński of Myszaków, by special commission of Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, lays weapons before the castle office at Konin: four sabres in sheaths with belts and one unsheathed, four spears with iron points, three long guns and one gun mounted with brass, loaded with lead and fine powder. He states that Chełmski took them on the Monday before in the Lusnia wood, when Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice, broke in there by force and armed men besieged the innkeeper's house. The messenger takes the weapons back.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Done in Konin on the Friday before the Feast of Saint Thomas the Apostle in the year of the Lord 1771.

On behalf of the Right Honourable Chełmski — Presentation of Weapons

To the office and present records of the Konin Municipal Court, appearing in person, the Honest Nicolaus Krzepczyński of the village of Myszaków, Royal Court Summoner, General, by special commission of the Right Honourable Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, heir of the estates of Łukom with their appurtenances, presented to the present office the below-listed weapons, namely: four swords in sheaths with leather belts; also one unsheathed [blade] fitted with iron; also four spears with iron tips fastened onto staffs; also three longer firearms and one firearm fitted with brass, loaded with lead and fine powder.

Which aforementioned things — besides other weapons taken away by the below-named party — in the pine forest called Lusnia, built upon by the olędry, belonging to the estates of Łukom now and from antiquity, during the violent incursion of the Right Honourable Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice, heir of the contiguous estates of Trąbczyn, and the besieging of the innkeeper's house by armed and lawless men, on the immediately preceding Monday — the same weapons having been seized by the Right Honourable Chełmski — the same above-named Court Summoner asserted to be so. Which, having been presented in this manner, he received back to himself, and was discharged from his office in respect of the received [weapons] in the presence of those present.
