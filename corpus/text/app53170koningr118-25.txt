--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Bericht des Gerichtsboten Mateusz Matuszkiewicz von Szetlewek, eingetragen in Konin 1782. Er hat Stanisław Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa und Erbherr von Łukom, und anderen eine Ladung vor das Burggericht Kalisz zugestellt, auf Betreiben von Antoni Prusimski von Kolno, Starost von Niszczewice: wegen der Strafen für verübte Gewalttaten samt der Festnahme von Leuten. Er hat sie im Gutshof von Łukom in Gegenwart des Geladenen hinterlegt.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A report of the court messenger Mateusz Matuszkiewicz of Szetlewek, entered at Konin in 1782. He has served on Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa and heir of Łukom, and on others a citation before the castle court of Kalisz, at the instance of Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice: for the penalties for acts of violence committed, with the seizing of people. He left it at the manor of Łukom in the presence of the man cited.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
On behalf of the Illustrious Prusimski, against the Illustrious Chełmski — a Report

Having come in person, the Honest Mateusz Matuszkiewicz of the village of Szetlewek, General Royal Process Server, sound [of mind], acknowledged that he had served authenticated Citation Letters for the sessions of the Kalisz Municipal Court next to be held in the Kalisz Castle — on behalf of and at the instance of the Illustrious Magnificent Antoni of Kolno Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, Knight of the Order of Saint Stanislaus, Heir of the estates of Trąbczyn and others, and other Plaintiffs — against the Illustrious Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, Heir of the estates of Łukom, and other Cited parties — for penalties on account of attempted violence with the arrest of people, [in Imo~] to be similarly carried out in the future, issued — in the manor of the estates of the village of Łukom, upon the small cupboard in the heated room, in the presence of the Cited party, on this very day — having placed [the citation] and having left notification.
