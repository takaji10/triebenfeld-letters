--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Pro Memoria mit Berechnung der Entschädigungsforderung des Fürsten Hohenlohe für Kamionna und Trąbczyn. Kamionna, 1796 nach Anton v. Prusimski verliehen und an Broniewski verpfändet, löste der Fürst 1804 per Vergleich mit 55000 Rthl ein; mit Prozesskosten und Zinsen soll die Prusimska 94550 Rthl baar erstatten. Trąbczyn wurde auf 316000 Rthl taxiert; von 138000 Rthl Schulden (Lichnowski, Weigel, Grotowski, Zerboni u.a.) bleiben 78000 Rthl. Die Souverains sollen 367164 Rthl in Gütern oder baar leisten, zusammen 461714 Rthl.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Memorandum with a calculation of the compensation claim of Prince Hohenlohe for Kamionna and Trąbczyn. Kamionna, granted in 1796 to Anton v. Prusimski and pledged to Broniewski, was redeemed by the Prince in 1804 by settlement for 55000 Rthl; with legal costs and interest, the Prusimska is to repay 94550 Rthl in cash. Trąbczyn was valued at 316000 Rthl; of 138000 Rthl of debts (Lichnowski, Weigel, Grotowski, Zerboni and others), 78000 Rthl remain. The sovereigns are to provide 367164 Rthl in estates or cash, 461714 Rthl in all.

The same Prusimski had pledged these estates to a certain Broniewski for more than 100000 rt., so that the Prince could never come into possession of them — the lawsuit pending on that account has [uncertain: given] the Prince over 6000 rt. In the first and second instance the Prince lost and was condemned to pay the pledge-money. Broniewski at last offered a settlement, and the Prince paid 55000 rt. in cash to Broniewski in 1804. — makes 61000 rt. Now on this, from 1804 to 1815, the interest is 33550 rt.
