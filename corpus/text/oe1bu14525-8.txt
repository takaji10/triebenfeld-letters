--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Eine Schenkungsurkunde Friedrich Wilhelms, mit der dem regierenden Fürsten Friedrich Ludwig von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen die vormals geistlichen Güter, nämlich die Dörfer Betsche, Świechocin, Stoki, Łowyń, Głażewo, Tarnowo, Zagórów, Skokum, Świątniki, Kopojno, Olesnica, Drzewce, Grądzyń, Wrąbczyn, Wienietz und Wolica mit allem Zubehör, zu erblichem Eigentum übertragen werden, gegen den bisherigen landesherrlichen Canon und die Abgabe an die geistlichen Korporationen. Begründet wird dies mit Diensten des Fürsten und damit, daß die früher verliehenen Güter des entwichenen Starosten von Prusimski zu hoch verschuldet waren. Alle Behörden werden zur Nichtbehinderung angewiesen. Beigefügt eine Beglaubigung des Regierungsauditors Richter.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A royal grant by Friedrich Wilhelm making over to his uncle Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, in heritable ownership, sixteen formerly church villages including Pszczew, Świechocin, Łowyń, Zagórów, Kopojno, Drzewce and Wrąbczyn with all appurtenances, subject only to the existing sovereign dues as a fixed canon and the allowance payable to the ecclesiastical foundations. It compensates for an earlier grant of the absconded starosta von Prusimski's heavily indebted estates, and orders all authorities not to obstruct possession. Certified copy.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
We Friedrich Willhelm, by the Grace of God King of Prussia etc., do hereby certify and declare by this Our open letter, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors in the Crown, in the Electorate, and in Our remaining lands, that, it having been laid before Us in all humility in what manner Our intention — namely to give the High-born Prince, Our friendly and dear uncle Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, by the grant of the estates which belonged to the absconded former starosta von Prusimski, a mark of Our friendship and goodwill borne towards him, as also of Our royal gratitude for the distinguished and profitable services rendered to Us and to Our royal house — was not attained wholly as We had willed it, because the said estates were burdened with many debts, We have most highly resolved to make over, grant and convey to Your Lordship, in heritable ownership, the hereafter-named formerly ecclesiastical estates, namely the villages of Pszczew, Świechocin, Stoki, Łowyń, Głażewo, Tarnowo, Zagórów, Skokum, Świątniki, Kopojno, Olesnica, Drzewce, Grądzyń, Wrąbczyn, Wieniec and Wolica, together with all their appurtenances, by whatsoever name they may be called, against payment

1.) of the sovereign dues hitherto discharged therefrom, as a fixed and unalterable canon; and

2.) of the allowance due therefrom to the ecclesiastical corporations or foundations.

We do also so hereby, and by virtue of this present, out of royal power and plenitude, for Ourselves and

Our descendants and successors in the Crown, in the Electorate and in Our remaining lands, in such manner and thus, that Our aforesaid friendly and dear uncle, the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, shall irrevocably have, possess and enjoy the above-named estates, with all the rights and prerogatives attaching thereto, as these have hitherto been exercised or may lawfully be exercised, and no less with all their pertinences and other appurtenances, by whatsoever name they may be called, just as the same have hitherto been used or may lawfully be used, and with all the lands belonging thereto, in fields, woodlands and meadows, and likewise in ponds and waters, from henceforth for all time, for himself and his descendants and heirs, as a true and well-acquired and unrestricted property from Us and Our descendants and successors; and that there shall likewise be vested in them the power and authority to dispose thereof at pleasure under the conditions above mentioned, unhindered by any man.

We therefore command and enjoin all Our High Provincial Boards, as also all other authorities whom this concerns, herewith as graciously as earnestly, that they in no wise hinder or impede the Lordship of the oft-mentioned Lord Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen in the heritable and proprietary possession granted to him of the above-named estates together with their pertinences and appurtenances, nor him or those to whom he may one day bequeath the same, or may otherwise lawfully transfer them by other acts inter vivos aut mortis causa; but rather that they let Your Lordship, and eventually also

them, freely and peaceably enjoy the same, themselves do nothing 1 against it, nor occasion or permit that such be done by others, on pain of Our most high displeasure and of unfailing severe punishment. In witness whereof etc. Given at Berlin, the 19th of June 1797. Friedrich Willhelm
