--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Der Schreiber hat das Tauschprojekt mit General v Schimmelpfennig aufgegeben und vermutet, dass die Verpachtung an Brzechffa gescheitert ist. Er hat die Besichtigung der Güter um Zagórów für ein neues Tauschprojekt erlaubt. Er will auch seine Praetensionen an die Trąbczyner Güter verkaufen und schickt wegen der Original-Schenkungsurkunde einen Expressen. Er fragt nach dem Erfolg von Brzechffas Fürsprache in Warschau im Trąbczyner Prozess und bittet um die 300 Rthl für Lieutenant Sommer.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The writer says he has given up the exchange project with General v Schimmelpfennig and suspects that the lease to Brzechffa has fallen through. He says he has permitted an inspection of the estates around Zagórów for a new exchange project. He also wants to sell his claims on the Trąbczyn estates and is sending a messenger about the original deed of gift. He asks what came of Brzechffa's intercession in Warsaw in the Trąbczyn lawsuit, and requests the 300 Rthl for Lieutenant Sommer.

In consequence of the news Your Right Honourable was last so kind as to communicate to me, I have, ever since receiving it, been looking forward from week to week — I might almost say from day to day — to Your Right Honourable's so ardently wished-for arrival with Mr von Brzechffa, and for a long time counted upon it with such certainty that on that account I gave up the whole exchange project with

General von Schimmelpfennig, in accordance with Your Right Honourable's expressed wish. I am convinced that the most compelling reasons may have kept me from Your Right Honourable's intention, just as, in view of Your Right Honourable's complete silence upon the matters, I cannot but believe that the leasing project with Mr von Brzechffa — which Your Right Honourable assured me had been concluded on condition of my subsequent approval — must have fallen through again through circumstances that have arisen. These conjectures, which force themselves upon me, joined with the need, ever more pressing with me, to come at last to a decisive resolution concerning my Polish possessions, are what have moved me to give a hearing to an exchange project only lately offered anew, and to grant my permission for an inspection of the Zagórów estates.
