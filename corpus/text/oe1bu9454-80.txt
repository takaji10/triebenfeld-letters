--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A short follow-up to a letter sent the previous day by Captain and Hahn: Minister Hoym has come back from Potsdam well satisfied, having heard several matters concerning the Prince discussed there, which Triebenfeld takes as proof of the monarch's regard and grounds for optimism. Berlin is full of talk of troop movements or war, which he disbelieves, trusting the King's principles. Nothing has yet been heard back from Herr von Voss.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Lord!

Presumably Your Most Serene Highness will already have received my letter, which I gave yesterday to Captain and Hahn. – Besides that letter, in which I said everything, I add only this further, that Minister Hoym has returned from Potsdam quite well pleased, where, according to the assurance of another man, various matters concerning Your Serene Highness were spoken of. It is herewith confirmed that the good Mo- truly esteems you, and in this regard I hope, indeed everything must go as one would wish. Today there is a great deal of talk here of marches or of war – I do not yet believe in it, for the Monarch holds too firmly to his principles, and maintains his honest good intentions. From His Excellency Herr von Voss nothing has yet come back. May Heaven govern his heart, that he may not act against the King's interest and against Your Serene Highness's peace of mind.

In deepest reverence I remain, until death,

Your Most Serene Highness's

Berlin, the 9th of October

1798.

most faithful servant

v. Triebenfeld
