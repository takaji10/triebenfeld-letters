--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Triebenfeld ergänzt sein Schreiben vom Vortag, das Hauptmann Hahn mitnahm. Minister Hoym sei zufrieden aus Potsdam zurückgekehrt, wo nach Aussage eines anderen Mannes mehrfach vom Fürsten die Rede gewesen sei. Dies bestätige, dass der König den Fürsten schätze, und er hofft auf einen guten Ausgang. In Berlin rede man von Märschen oder Krieg, er glaube aber noch nicht daran. Von Herrn von Voss sei noch nichts zurück.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Triebenfeld adds to his letter of the previous day, which Captain Hahn took with him. He says Minister Hoym has returned from Potsdam satisfied, where, according to another man, the Prince was spoken of several times. This, he says, confirms that the King holds the Prince in esteem, and he hopes for a good outcome. In Berlin there is talk of marches or war, but he says he does not yet believe it. Nothing has come back yet from Mr von Voss.

80

Most Serene Prince,

Most gracious Lord!

Your Most Serene Highness will presumably already have received my letter, which I yesterday gave to Captain and Hahn to carry. — Besides that letter, in which I have said everything, I add only this further: that Minister Hoym has returned from Potsdam very well pleased, where, according to the assurance of another man, various things were spoken of concerning Your Serene Highness. — It is hereby confirmed that the good Monarch truly esteems you, and in this respect I hope and trust all will and must go as one would wish. Today there is a great deal of talk here of marches or of war — I do not yet believe in it, for the Monarch holds too firmly to his principles, and maintains his honest good intentions. — From His Excellency Mr von Voss nothing has yet come back. May Heaven govern his heart, that he may not act against the King's interest and against Your Serene Highness's peace of mind.

In deepest veneration I remain, until death,

Your Most Serene Highness's

Berlin, the 9th of October

1798.

most faithful servant

v. Triebenfeld
