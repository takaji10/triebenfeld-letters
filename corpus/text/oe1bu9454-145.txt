--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Honrichs zahlt die 4000 Rthl nicht, obwohl alles gelöscht und mit Zustimmung des Pupillen Collegiums geregelt ist. Hoffrath Grimm, schwer krank, will ihn gerichtlich belangen, doch fehlen Contract und Vollmacht; der Contract liegt bei Rochlitz. Triebenfeld bittet, ihn dort eilig anzufordern und mit Vollmacht an Grimm zu schicken. In Trąbczyn werden, wie er hört, seinem Plan widersprechende Befehle gegeben: 100 Scheffel Korn wurden verliehen, viele Äcker besät. Nach einem Wagenbruch will er nach der Reparatur dorthin eilen.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Honrichs is not paying the 4000 Rthl, although everything has been cancelled and settled with the consent of the Wards' Board. Court Councillor Grimm, who is seriously ill, wants to sue him, but the contract and the power of attorney are missing; the contract is with Rochlitz. Triebenfeld asks that it be called in from there urgently and sent to Grimm with a power of attorney. At Trąbczyn, he hears, orders are being given that contradict his plan: 100 Scheffel of grain have been lent out and many fields sown. After a carriage broke he means to hurry there once it is repaired.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
145

Most Serene Prince,

Most gracious Lord!

Having broken my carriage and very nearly my neck along with it, I have nevertheless arrived here in Kalisz safe and sound, though somewhat shaken about. The greatest loss is the time I require for the repairing of my carriage, in order to be able to travel further with it. Court Councillor Grimm lies miserably ill; meanwhile he will work up [uncertain: verarbeiten] the matter for von Radzinski, for which, however, the contracts would be required.

With Honrichs things look bad — the man does not pay the 4000 rthl, notwithstanding that everything has been receipted, cancelled, and put in order with the consent of the Board of Wards.

Grimm wishes to compel him to payment at once by process of law — but he lacks the contract and a power of attorney — Rochlitz has the contract, and he has also had a power of attorney for the pursuit of this matter.

May Your Most Serene Highness be pleased to issue the order that the contract be demanded back from Rochlitz with the utmost haste and sent off, together with a power of attorney, to Mr Grimm. In Trąbczyn, I hear, orders are being given which run directly counter to my plan. Thus one of those in command has lent out 100 bushels of grain to somebody, whereof Your Serene Highness will never receive one of them [uncertain: einen denen], and a great part of the fields has likewise been sown. As soon as my carriage is mobile I shall hasten thither, in order then to report the rest to Your Serene Highness. In reverence I remain, until death,

Your Most Serene Highness's

Kalisz, the 14th of November

1804.

humble and obedient servant

v Triebenfeld
