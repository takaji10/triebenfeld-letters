Durchlauchtigster Fürst
Gnädigster Herr!
Nachdem ich den Wage und beinahe den Hals dazu gebrochen, bin ich,
obwohl etwas zerquatscht, dennoch wohlbehalten hier in Kalisz an
gelangt. Der gröste Verlust ist die Zeit, die ich zur reparien
meines Wagens bedarf, um damit weiter reisen zu können
Der Hoffrath Grimm liegt miserable kram; jedes wird
er dem v. Radzinski verarbeiten, wozu aber die Contracte
erfordert würden.
Mit dem Honrichs sieht es übel aus - der Mensch zahlt
die 4000 Rthl nicht, ohnerachtet alles quittirt gelöſcht und
mit Confentiment des kopillen Collegiums aufs reinen
gebracht ist.
Der Grimm will ihm sofort gerichtlich zur Zahlung an
halten – es fehlt ihm aber der Contract und eine voll
macht – den Contract hat Nochlitz, auch hat derselbe eine
Vollmacht zur Betreibung dieser Sache gehabt.

Geruhen Ewr Hochfürstliche Durchlaucht doch den Befehl zu er¬
teilen, daß der Contract auf das eiligste von Köckritz ab¬
gefordert und mittelst Vollmacht an H. Grimm abgeschickt
werde. In Trąbczin höhre ich giebt man andere Befehle
die meinen Plan gerade entgegen sind. So hat einer von
den Befehls habern 100 Scheffel Korn an jemand geborgt
wo Ewr Durchlaucht nie einen denen erhalten, auch hat
man einen großen Theil der Äcker besaet
Ich werde sobald mein Wagen Mobil ist, dahin erkan¬
um den Ewr Durchlaucht das weitere melden.
Ehrfurchtsvoll ersterbe
Ewr hochfürstliche Durchlaucht
Kalisz den 14ten Novbr
1804.
unterthäniger Diener
v Triebenfeld

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports a carriage accident on the road and his arrival at Kalisz shaken but unhurt, delayed until the vehicle is repaired. Court Councillor Grimm lies gravely ill but will prepare everything for von Radzinski if the contracts arrive; Honrichs still refuses to pay 4000 Rthl despite receipts and the Collegium's consent, and Grimm cannot sue without the contract, which is with Nochlitz. Asks that it be reclaimed from Köckritz with a power of attorney, and complains that orders at Trąbczin contradict his plan, 100 Scheffel of corn having been lent out and much land sown.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince, Most gracious Lord! After I had broken the carriage and very nearly my neck along with it, I have nevertheless arrived here at Kalisz safe and sound, though somewhat knocked about. The greatest loss is the time I require for the repair of my carriage, in order to be able to travel on with it. Court Councillor Grimm is lying miserably ill; he will work up everything for von Radzinski, for which, however, the contracts would be required. With Honrichs matters look ill — the man does not pay the 4000 Rthl, notwithstanding that everything has been receipted, struck off, and, with the consent of the [uncertain: kopillen — possibly Pupillen-Collegium, the board of wards] Collegium, brought into good order. Grimm means to hold him to payment forthwith by process of law — but he lacks the contract and a power of attorney — the contract is with Nochlitz, and he has likewise had a power of attorney for the prosecution of this affair.

May Your Most Serene Highness be pleased to issue the command that the contract be demanded back from Köckritz with the greatest despatch and sent off to Herr Grimm together with a power of attorney. At Trąbczin, I hear, other orders are being given which run directly counter to my plan. Thus one of the persons in command has lent out 100 Scheffel of corn to somebody, whereby Your Serene Highness will never get one of them back; a great part of the fields has also been sown. As soon as my carriage is mobile I shall [illegible] thither, in order to report the further particulars to Your Serene Highness. In deepest reverence I remain, until death, Your Most Serene Highness's, Kalisz, the 14th of November 1804, humble and obedient servant, v Triebenfeld
