Durchlauchtigster Fürst
Gnädigster Herr!
Nachdem ich den Wage und beinahe den Hals dazu gebrochen, bin ich,
obwohl etwas zerquatscht, dennoch wohlbehalten hier in Kalisz an
gelangt. Der gröste Verlust ist die Zeit, die ich zur reparien
meines Wagens bedarf, um damit weiter reisen zu können
Der Hoffrath Grimm liegt miserable kram; jedes wird
er dem v. Radzinski verarbeiten, wozu aber die Contracte
erfordert würden.
Mit dem Honrichs sieht es übel aus - der Mensch zahlt
die 4000 Rthl nicht, ohnerachtet alles quittirt gelöſcht und
mit Confentiment des kopillen Collegiums aufs reinen
gebracht ist.
Der Grimm will ihm sofort gerichtlich zur Zahlung an
halten – es fehlt ihm aber der Contract und eine voll
macht – den Contract hat Nochlitz, auch hat derselbe eine
Vollmacht zur Betreibung dieser Sache gehabt.

Geruhen Ewr Hochfürstliche Durchlaucht doch den Befehl zu er¬
teilen, daß der Contract auf das eiligste von Köckritz ab¬
gefordert und mittelst Vollmacht an H. Grimm abgeschickt
werde. In Trąbczin höhre ich giebt man andere Befehle
die meinen Plan gerade entgegen sind. So hat einer von
den Befehls habern 100 Scheffel Korn an jemand geborgt
wo Ewr Durchlaucht nie einen denen erhalten, auch hat
man einen großen Theil der Äcker besaet
Ich werde sobald mein Wagen Mobil ist, dahin erkan¬
um den Ewr Durchlaucht das weitere melden.
Ehrfurchtsvoll ersterbe
Ewr hochfürstliche Durchlaucht
Kalisz den 14ten Novbr
1804.
unterthäniger Diener
v Triebenfeld

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports arriving in Kalisz shaken after breaking his carriage, the repair of which delays his onward journey. Court Councillor Grimm lies gravely ill but will prepare work for von Radzinski if the contracts can be had. Honrichs refuses to pay the 4000 Rthl despite everything being receipted and cancelled; Grimm wishes to sue but lacks the contract, held by Nochlitz, and a power of attorney, both to be reclaimed urgently from Köckritz. At Trąbczyn contrary orders have been given: 100 bushels of grain lent out irrecoverably and much of the fields sown.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Lord!

After I had broken the carriage and very nearly my neck along with it, I have nevertheless arrived here in Kalisz safe and sound, though somewhat battered. The greatest loss is the time I require for the repair of my carriage, so as to be able to travel onward with it. Court Councillor Grimm is lying miserably ill; every [thing] he will work up for von Radzinski, for which, however, the contracts would be required.

With Honrichs matters look bad — the man does not pay the 4000 Rthl, notwithstanding that everything has been receipted, cancelled and set in order with the consent of the [uncertain: kopillen] College.

Grimm means to hold him to payment by legal process at once — but he lacks the contract and a power of attorney — the contract is with Nochlitz, and the same man has also had a power of attorney for the prosecution of this affair.

May it please Your Most Serene Highness to issue the command that the contract be demanded back from Köckritz with the utmost haste and sent off to Mr. Grimm together with a power of attorney. In Trąbczyn, I hear, other orders are being given which run directly counter to my plan. Thus one of the persons in command has lent out 100 bushels of grain to someone from whom Your Serene Highness will never receive any of it back, and a great part of the fields has also been sown. As soon as my carriage is mobile I shall [uncertain: set out] thither, in order to report the further particulars to Your Serene Highness. In deepest reverence I remain, until death,

Your Most Serene Highness's

Kalisz, the 14th of November

1804.

humble and obedient servant

v. Triebenfeld
