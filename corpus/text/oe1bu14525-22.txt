--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A grant by Friedrich Wilhelm, made because an earlier gift of the absconded former Starosta von Prusimski's debt-laden estates had fallen short, conveying to his uncle Friedrich Ludwig, Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, sixteen formerly ecclesiastical villages including Betsche, Łowyń, Zagórów, Kopojno and Wrąbczyn in full heritable ownership, subject only to a fixed sovereign canon and payments to the ecclesiastical foundations. Filed with it are a certified copy made for registration and a mortgage certificate for the town of Zagórów, showing no debts but a bar on sale to a native without consent.

We Friedrich Wilhelm, by God's Grace King of Prussia etc., Margrave of Brandenburg, Arch-Chamberlain and Elector of the Holy Roman Empire, sovereign and supreme Duke of Silesia, sovereign Prince of Orange, Neufchatel and Valangin, as also of the County of Glatz, Duke in Geldern, at Magdeburg, Cleve, Julich, Berg, Stettin, Pomerania, of the Cassubians and Wends, at Mecklenburg and Crossen, Burgrave of Nuremberg above and below the mountains, Prince of Halberstadt, Minden, Camin, Wenden, Schwerin, Ratzeburg, East Frisia and Meurs, Count of Hohenzollern, Ruppin, of the Mark Ravensberg, Hohenstein, Tecklenburg, Schwerin, Lingen, Bühren and Leerdam, Lord of Ravenstein, of the Lands of Rostock, Stargard, Limburg, Lauenburg, Bütow, Arlay and Breda etc., do by this Our open letter attest and declare, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors in the Crown, the Electorate and Our remaining Lands, that, whereas it has been most humbly laid before Us in what manner

Our intention of giving to the High-Born Prince, Our friendly beloved Uncle, Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, by the grant of the estates formerly belonging to the absconded former Starosta von Prusimski, a mark of Our friendship and goodwill borne toward him, as also of Our Royal gratitude for the distinguished and beneficial services rendered to Us and to Our Royal House, has not been achieved entirely as We had willed it, because the said estates were burdened with many debts; We have most highly resolved to leave over, to grant and to convey to Your Lordship, in heritable and proprietary right, the hereafter named formerly ecclesiastical estates, namely the villages of Betsche, Świechocin, Stoki, Łowyń, Głażewo, Tarmowo, Zagórów, Skokum, Swiątniki, Kopojno, Olesnica, Drzewce, Grądzyń, Wrąbczyn, Wienitz and Wolica, together with all their appurtenances, by whatever name they may be called, against payment

1., of the territorial-sovereign dues hitherto discharged therefrom,

of the territorial-sovereign dues, as a fixed and unalterable canon, and

2.) of the allowance due therefrom to the ecclesiastical corporations or foundations, in heritable and proprietary right.

This We also hereby do, and by force of this present instrument, out of Royal power and plenitude, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors in the Crown, the Electorate and Our remaining Lands, in such manner and to this effect, that Our aforesaid friendly beloved Uncle, the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, shall have the above-named estates with all rights attaching thereto

and prerogatives, as these have hitherto been exercised or may lawfully be exercised, and no less with all their pertinences and other appurtenances, by whatever name they may be called, as the same have hitherto been enjoyed, or may lawfully be enjoyed, and with all the lands belonging thereto, in fields, woods and meadows, likewise in ponds and waters, from henceforth for all eternity, for himself and his heirs and descendants, as a true, well-acquired and unrestricted proper-

ty, irrevocably from Us and Our descendants and successors, to hold, possess and enjoy; and that the power and authority shall also be attached thereto of disposing of the same at pleasure under the conditions mentioned above, unhindered by any man.

We therefore command and enjoin all Our supreme provincial colleges, as also all other authorities whom this concerns, herewith as graciously as earnestly, that they in no wise hinder or impede the Lordship of the much-mentioned Lord Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen in the heritable and proprietary possession of the above-named estates, together with their pertinences and appurtenances, granted to him, nor

him or those upon whom he may one day devolve the same by inheritance, or otherwise lawfully transfer it by other acts among the living or on account of death, but rather shall let Your Lordship, and eventually also them, enjoy the same freely and undisturbed, doing nothing themselves against it, nor occasioning or permitting that such be done by others, on pain of Our most high displeasure and unfailing severe punishment. In witness whereof We have signed the present instrument of grant with Our most high own hand, and caused Our greater Royal seal to be appended thereto.

Thus done and given in Our Residence City of Berlin, the nineteenth day of the month of June after the birth of Christ Our Lord, in the one thousand seven hundred and ninety-seventh, and of Our Royal reign in the eleventh year. Friedrich Willhelm. Hoym. Reck.
