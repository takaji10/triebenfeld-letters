zur Verabpachtung der Herrschaft Betsche an die dortige
Bürgerschaft und die übrigen ländlichen Einseßen, am
5ten Februar d. J. gewordenen Bekanntmachung, laßen
Wir Ewr. Liebden mit Rückgabe des eingereichten Erbpachts

höchsten Cabinets-Resolution vom 11ten v. M. an Unsere
Cammer ergangenen Rescripts Unsers SüdPreußl. Finanz
Departement vom 15ten v. M. hierdurch zum Bescheide
geben, daß die in Rede stehende Vererbpachtung ganz
unzuläßig befunden worden ist, und Ewr. Liebden der
dazu nachgesuchte Consens nicht ertheilt werden kann,
denn
1tens würden, wenn Ewr. Liebden dieser Consens nicht ver¬
sagt worden wäre, mehrere Grundherrschaften
ähnliche Anträge wagen, und dadurch die Besitzungen
des in hiesiger Provinz so zahlreichen Adels auf deßen Con¬
servation Bedacht genommen werden muß, vermindert

Hinderniß in Ewr. Liebden Vorhaben nicht schon entgegen stünde,

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Refusal by the Royal South Prussian War and Domains Chamber, on a Cabinet Resolution of 11 April and a Finance Department rescript of 15 April, of the Fürst zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen's petition of 31 December to grant the lordship of Betsche in hereditary lease to the town's burghers and rural inhabitants; the submitted hereditary-lease contract is returned. Grounds: the precedent would erode noble landholding, the sole considerable Betsche forest would be devastated, inhabitants already hold sufficient land, burghers should pursue urban trades, and most lack the necessary capital. Signed v Köller.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
By the Grace of God, Friedrich Wilhelm, King of Prussia. Our friendship, Royal grace and good will first of all, High-born Prince, especially Dear Friend and Dear Faithful One! Further to the notification issued to Your Liebden on the 5th of February of this year upon the petition presented on the 31st of December of last year for permission to lease out the lordship of Betsche to the burghership there and to the remaining rural residents, We hereby give Your Liebden, with the return of the submitted hereditary-lease contract, upon the ground of the Rescript of Our South Prussian Finance Department of the 15th of last month issued to Our Chamber in conformity with a Most High Cabinet Resolution of the 11th of last month, the following decision: that the hereditary-leasing in question has been found wholly inadmissible, and that the consent sought thereto cannot be granted to Your Liebden; for, firstly, if this consent had not been refused to Your Liebden, several landed lordships would venture similar applications, and thereby the possessions of the nobility so numerous in this province — upon whose conservation regard must be had — would be diminished, and the opportunities for the acquisition of estates would be taken from it. Even were, however, this considerable obstacle not already to stand in the way of Your Liebden's design, the aforementioned leasing-out could not be approved,

because, secondly, the appropriate management of the Betsche forest — which, since this forest is the only considerable one in that region, deserves every regard — could not take place, inasmuch as, if the free use of it were left to the hereditary lessees, even the best supervision could not entirely prevent devastation; thirdly, there is here also still to be weighed the circumstance that, if Your Liebden's application were deferred to, the lands to be taken in hereditary lease by the residents could not even be managed in an orderly manner, since every resident, and likewise every burgher of the town of Betsche, possesses as much land as he needs for an orderly husbandry; and beyond that, in respect of the burghers, it is to be seen to that they apply themselves more to the [uncertain: pursuit of urban trades] than to that of agriculture, the greater part of the residents being moreover too [uncertain: without means] to be able to raise the sums of money required for the management. Are Your Liebden, with friendship, Royal grace and good will, well disposed toward.

Posen, the 18th of May 1805. Royal South Prussian Chamber of War and Domains. [uncertain: G] v Köller

To

The Reigning Prince

of Hohenlohe Ingelfingen

Liebden, Lieutenant-General

of Infantry and Governor
