--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A covering note forwarding the enclosed Wittowes deeds to Herr Criminal-Rath in Grimm, with the urgent request that the recipient speak to him in person about the circumstance. The writer explains that the files had long been searched for in the Trąbczyn registry, where he himself believed them to be, but that they turned up among his wife's papers when he moved from his Breslau lodging to Wilhelmsruh, she having received them during his absence and set them aside for safekeeping.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
I permit myself an urgent request, together with the enclosed files, upon their delivery to the criminal-court councillor in Grimm, that you yourself might speak with him concerning the following circumstance.

These Witów files have long been sought in the registry at Trąbczyn, where I myself supposed and maintained [that they would be lying]. They were, however, upon my moving out

that they would be lying.

from my lodgings at Breslau to Wilhelmsruh, found among the papers of my wife, because these files were received by my wife at a time when I was absent from Breslau, and were laid for safe keeping among her belongings, where the papers are never read, and thus it was forgotten to hand them over to me afterwards.
