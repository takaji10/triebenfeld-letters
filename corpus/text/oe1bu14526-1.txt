--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A covering note sending on the long-missing Witów files, with an urgent request that the recipient speak in person to the criminal-court councillor in Grimm about the matter. The writer explains that the papers had been searched for in vain in the registry at Trąbczyn, where he himself believed them to be, and only came to light on his move from his Breslau lodgings to Wilhelmsruh, among his wife's belongings, she having received them in his absence and forgotten to pass them on.
