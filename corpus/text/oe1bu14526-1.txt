Eine inständige Bitte erlaube ich mir mit den anliegenden
Acten bey derselben Behändigung an den H. Criminal in
Grimm, daß Sie selbst mit ihm über folgenden Umstand

Es werden diesen Wittowes Acten schon lange in der Trąbczyner
Registratur gesucht, wo ich selbst gemeint und behaubtet habe.
Es sind aber solche beym Ausziehen
daß sie liegen würden.
von meinem Logis zu Breſslau nach Wilhelmsruh unter den
Pappieren meiner Frau gefunden worden, weil diese Acten in
einer Zeit wo ich von Breschlau abwesend gewesen bin von meiner
Frau in Empfang genommen, und zur sichern Verwahrung unter
ihre Sachen gelegt worden sind, wo die Pappiere nie gelesen
werden, und somit mir nachher zuzustellen vergeßen worden
sind

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A covering note forwarding the enclosed Wittowes deeds to Herr Criminal-Rath in Grimm, with the urgent request that the recipient speak to him in person about the circumstance. The writer explains that the files had long been searched for in the Trąbczyn registry, where he himself believed them to be, but that they turned up among his wife's papers when he moved from his Breslau lodging to Wilhelmsruh, she having received them during his absence and set them aside for safekeeping.

I take the liberty of adding a pressing request to the enclosed [uncertain: deeds], upon their delivery to Herr Criminal[-Rath] in Grimm, namely that you might yourself speak with him concerning the following circumstance.

These Wittowes files have long been sought in the Trąbczyn registry, where I myself supposed and maintained [them to be]. But they were, upon my moving out

from my lodging at Breſslau to Wilhelmsruh, found among my wife's papers, because these deeds were received by my wife at a time when I was absent from Breschlau, and were laid among her things for safe keeping, where the papers are never read, and so it was forgotten to deliver them to me afterwards.
