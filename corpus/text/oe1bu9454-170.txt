--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Hahn berichtet, er sei auf Befehl des Fürsten nach Triebenfelds Erbgut Świniary geritten, um mit dem Amtmann Käufer und dem Förster Bezold zu reden. Der Förster fragte, wer ihn beim Fürsten angeschwärzt habe, und versicherte, alle Kräfte für das herrschaftliche Interesse einzusetzen. Den Amtmann traf Hahn in Breslau. Dieser erwiderte dasselbe, verwies auf beste Zeugnisse und seinen guten Namen und sieht einer Untersuchung seiner Handlungen ruhig entgegen.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---

Right Honourable Sir, Highly Honoured Sir, Councillor of War and Forests! In accordance with the command of His Serene Highness the Prince, I rode last Saturday to Your Right Honourable's hereditary estate Świniary, in order to speak there with the district administrator Käufer and the forester bezold. The former I did not find there, for he had ridden early to Wrocław, and had not yet returned from there. To the latter I set out the charge I had to lay upon him entirely in the manner in which it had been written to me by [text lost]; he did not appear taken aback by it, but rather enquired who might have placed him in an unfavourable light with His Serene Highness, whereupon I said to him that I did not know this myself, and that I could only admonish him to the strictest probity and attentiveness in his service, since he saw that with a contrary manner of proceeding one was never secure, and that such conduct would one day be severely punished. He assured [uncertain: me]

that he exerted all his powers for the lordship's interest, and was not in a position to do more. I then rode back, and sought out the district administrator here, whom I did indeed find, and who answered me to approximately the same effect, only with the addition that he had the best testimonials from the most trustworthy [illegible], and that his good name was worth much to him, which in his present situation he certainly did not deserve to lose. Of this he would give proofs, and he could look forward calmly to an investigation of his actions. Your Right Honourable, I report all this likewise upon His Serene Highness's command, and permit myself only to add further that the Wüsten go off today by the carriage post. In the most perfect veneration:

Your Right Honourable's

Wrocław, the 19th of March

1805

humble and obedient servant

A. Hahn
