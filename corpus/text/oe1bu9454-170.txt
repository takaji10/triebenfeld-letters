--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports, on the Prince's orders, on confronting two estate servants with complaints against them: the forester Bezold, met at ErtheSchweiner, who showed no alarm and asked who had blackened him with the Prince, insisting he did all he could for the lordship; and the district steward Käufer, found in Breslau, who claimed testimonials from creditable men, valued his good name, and said he could face any investigation calmly. Notes the Wüsten leave by carrying post.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Right Honourable Sir, Highly Honoured Sir Councillor of War and Forests! In accordance with the command of His Serene Highness the Prince, I rode last Saturday to Your Right Honourable's ErtheSchweiner, in order to speak there with the district steward Käufer and the forester bezold. The former I did not find, for he had ridden early to Breslau, and had not yet returned from there. To the latter I set forth the charge I had against him entirely in the manner in which it had been written to me; he did not appear dismayed at it, but rather enquired who might have placed him in an ill light with His Serene Highness, whereupon I told him that I did not know this myself, that I could only admonish him to the strictest uprightness and attentiveness in his service, since he saw that with a contrary manner of conduct one was never safe, and that such would one day be severely punished. He assured me

that he exerted all his powers for the interest of the lordship, and was not in a position to do more. I then rode back, and sought out the district steward here, whom I did indeed find, and who answered me roughly the same, only with the addition that he had the best testimonials from the most creditable men, and that his good name was worth much to him, which in his present situation he certainly did not deserve to lose. Of this he would give proofs, and could calmly face an investigation of his actions. Your Right Honourable, I report all this likewise by command of His Serene Highness, and permit myself only to add further that today the Wüsten depart by the carrying post. In most perfect reverence:

Your Right Honourable's

Breslau, the 19th of March

1805

humble and obedient servant

A. Hahn
