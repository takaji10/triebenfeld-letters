--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Ein Hypothekenschein für das freie Allodialgut Szetlewek im Kreis Konin bestätigt Fürst Friedrich Ludwig von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen den Besitztitel aus der Schenkungsurkunde Friedrich Wilhelms II. vom 9. August 1796; den Wert gibt er mit 24000 Reichsthaler an. Eingetragen sind das Verbot, das Gut an Landeseingesessene zu verkaufen, ein zinsloses Darlehen des Ignatz von Radzinski über 8333 Rthl 8 ggl (50000 poln. Gulden) als Sicherheit für Pachtgelder, sowie eine Protestation Jacob von Przespolewskis über 30000 poln. Gulden aus einer Schuldverschreibung des Vorbesitzers von Prusimski.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A mortgage certificate confirming the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen's title to the free allodial estate of Szetlewek in the Konin district, held in full ownership under a grant of 9 August 1796 from Friedrich Wilhelm II and valued by him at 24,000 Reichsthaler. It records a bar on sale to natives of the land without consent to alienate, an interest-free loan of 8,333 Rthl 8 Groschen (50,000 Polish florins) from Ignatz von Radzinski securing lease moneys, and a protest by Jacob von Przespolewski over 30,000 Polish florins claimed from a former possessor, von Prusimski.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The free allodial estate of Szetlewek, situated in the province of South Prussia and in its Konin district, was received by the present possessor, the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Friedrich Ludwig, Serene Highness, in heredity and in full ownership by grant of His Majesty the most blessed King Friedrich Wilhelm II, according to the deed of donation of 9 August 1796, and he has declared the value of the same at 24,000 Reichsthaler, written out Twenty-Four Thousand Reichsthaler. Entered by decree of 2 July 1799.

Thereupon are entered:

I. Onera perpetua. Restrictions upon the ownership or upon the disposition. The restriction upon the ownership, that these estates may not be sold to any native of the land, unless the first or one of the succeeding possessors shall have obtained the consent to alienation to natives of the land, in which case an unconditional power of alienation to persons capable of possession takes effect for the future, has been entered upon the ground of the Court Rescript of 24 February 1800, by decree of

27 May 1800.

Judicially secured debts and other real obligations.

1. 8,333 Rthl. 8 Groschen, Eight Thousand Three Hundred Thirty-Three Reichsthaler 8 Groschen, or 50,000 Polish florins in specie thalers, were lent by Ignatz von Radzinski to the possessor without interest, as security for the lease moneys to be paid for Szetlewek and to cover any deteriorations that may arise; they have been entered for the creditor von Radzinski upon the ground of the settlement concluded with him on 5 September 1799 and confirmed on the 17th of the same, §. 5, by decree of 22 October 1799.

A protestation pro salvando iure et loco for Jacob von Przespolewski, on account of a demand of 30,000 Polish florins ex inscriptione of the former possessor von Prusimski of 25 June 1789, together with arrears of interest at 5 per cent since Midsummer 1790, which has been entered by decree of 8 April 1800 here and at the same time upon Trąbczyn.

Besides the foregoing items nothing is entered upon the estate of Szetlewek in the mortgage book, and for the rest

this mortgage certificate is hereby issued to His Serene Highness the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Friedrich Ludwig, in vim recognitionis, on account of the titulus possessionis entered for the same upon the ground of the deed of donation of 9 August 1796.

In witness whereof, under the greater seal of the Royal South Prussian Government and the customary signature. Given at Kalisz, 22 July 1800.

Renk[illegible]

Von Rangeler[uncertain: Rangeler] Government Councillor
