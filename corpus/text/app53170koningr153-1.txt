Actum in fundo bonorum Trąmpczyn locoque angularitatis penes tres scopulos angulares visibiles et quidem primum bonorum Trąmpczyn minus, seu Nowa Wieś Illustrissimi Magnifici Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczeviensis Ordinis Sancti Stanislai Equitis, alterum Łukom Magnifici Ścibor Stanislai Chełmski Thesaurarii Vschovensis, tertium denique villae collateralis Drzewce Venerabilis Conventus Landensis Ordinis Cistersiensis haereditariorum, in borra pinatica circa vias Pyzdrensem, et aliam de Zagurowo et Drzewce ad Chocz seu Kalisz tendentem per decretum commissionis vi sanciti à Statibus Regni emanatum anno 1775to die tertia mensis 8bris constitutos et de terra erectos decreto tribunalis regni Petricoviensis feria 4ta post Festum Sanctae Agnethis Virginis et Martyris proxima scilicet die vigesima tertia mensis Januarii anno nunc currenti 1782do post commissoriali, tum subalterno inferius de actu specificando actum praesentem constituenti approbatos, feria 5ta post Festum Sanctae Catharinae Virginis et Martyris die scilicet vigesima octava mensis 9bris anno Domini 1782do.

In inductione, cujus citationes partium et quidem Illustrissimi Magnifici Antonii in Kolno Prusimski Capitanei Niszczeviensis Ordinis Sancti Stanislai Equitis, contra Magnificum Stanislaum Chełmski Thesaurarium Vschoviensis atque Joannem Orławski Commissarium Granicialem Terrae Dobrinensis nec non Illustrissimum et Reverendissimum Benedictum Lubstowski Ordinis Cistersiensis Conventus Landensis abbatem, totumque continuum cassatis quibusvis actibus praejudiciosis pro determinatione scopularum juxta lineam per ante actum judicii in mappa appositam condescensioneque in ordine erectionis praemissorum scopulorum asignanda, tum actuum praejudiciorum restrictione, legitimorum vero pro inviolabilibus sententiatione paenarum pro procurata discrepanti sententia super Magnifico Chełmski districtarum super Conventum Landensis ratione manifestationis ad acta contra decretum post commissoriale illatae, extensione, in Conin die 18 mensis 7bris.
Ex adverso Magnifici Stanislai Chełmski Thesaurarii Vschoviensis contra Illustrissimum Magnificum Antonium Prusimski Capitaneum Niszczeviensis, tum Magnificum Jacobum Gorski Comissarium Granicialem Iunivladislaviensium cassato actu officii campestri Iunivladislaviae Hollandoque uno juxta resolutionem decreti anteacti judicii sui in fundo bonorum Łukom relicto pro ob[s?]ignatione parietis inter bona Łukom
Trąmpczyn comportationeque manifestationis cujusdam acsi coram actis Castrensis Posnaniensis contra decretum officii Castrensis Gnesiensis factae paenarum tam super Illustrissimi Magnifici Prusimski quam et officio campestri extensione, processus illegaliter obtine[ri] cassatione, in Conin feria 2da post Festum Exaltationis Sanctae Crucis.
Alteram ejusdem essentiae in Castrensis Radziejoviensis omnes anno praesenti recognitas, decidendo controversiis parte ut pote Illustrissimus Magnificus Prusimski Capitaneus Niszczeviensis Ordinis Sancti Stanislai Equitis circa decretum anteacti judicii sui feria 4ta post Festum Sanctae Agnethis Virginis et Martyris anno praesenti inter se et Magnificum Chełmski latum sipationem scopulorum parietalium inter bona Łukom Trąmpczyn juxta lineam in mappa appositam, à diamethro scopulorum angularium ad diamethrum scopulorum acialium determinans, deoccupationemque Hollandorum per Magnificum Chełmski traditorum pro re sui praeter unum Magnifico Chełmski relictum assignans vimque inviolabilitatis ex nexu constitutionis 1776ti habens sese [con]sistentis in virtuteque praeinserti decreti deoccupationem ___ sexta Martii in fundo Hollandorum testante actu officii condescensoriali subsecutam dislimitationem vero praemissarum haereditatis post excisionem lineae juxta lineam in mappa compositam verificationemque ejusdem per officii condescensorialia commissaria Junivladislaviae et Dobrinensis die 27ma Maii anno praesenti ob discrepantiam horumce officiorum ex re acsi in existens in fundo Hollandi Magnifico Chełmski decreto quo supra per lineam in mappa appositam adjudicati, ac exinde difformitatis mappae non effectuatam esse inferentis [exi]stentiam tamen hujusce Hollandi seu fundum per combustionem ejusdem visione, ex requisitione sui

È converto Magnifici Chełmski Thesaurarii Vschoviensis decreto prae allegato anteacto judicii sui Hollandum unum pro re sui per lineam in mappa appositam ad judicatum
ejusque deoccupationem pro re sui determinatam sibi habuisse inferentis hunc verò circa actum dislimitationis perficiendi ad tenorem decreti superius expuncti rectilinii in fundo à parte sui irreperibilem per actus discrepantes officiorum campestrium demonstrantis, et hoc in puncto remissionem per officiorum cmle Dobrinensis ob difformitatem mappae ad judicium suum legitime factam esse explicationem visionem verò combustarum gasarum Hollandi repositam nihil evincere ex re quod nexu altius memorati decreti Hollandus pro re sui nulla facta de praemissa combustione mentione adjudicatus sit exposcentis, executionemque totius dicti decreti relicto non nisi à parte suis uno Hollando exposcentis, tum paenas respectu manifestationis acsi in actis Castrensis Posnaniensis factae circa decreta campestria pro Illustrissimum Magnificum Prusimski repositae connotatae in actis verò iisdem attestantibusque requisitionibusque non existentis tum distinctas ratione causate discrepantiae tam super Illustrissimo Magnifico Prusimski, quam et officiali meliorationem liniae ultra regulam decreti tribunalii injungente, irrogari postulantis, conventum denique Landensis uti à manifestatione praemissa contra decretum facta per recessum coram actis judicii sui die decima sexta mensis praesenti per generosum Ignatium Gzowski nomine ejusdem conventus facta resilientem immunem declarari, paenalitatemque per Illustrissimum Magnificum Prusimski hoc in puncto praetensam removeri concludentis, exauditis et intellectis.

Quo itaque decreto actoratum primum et originalem limitum lectis multiplicibus inquisitionibusque in variis condescensionum terminis, tum et commissionibusque utriusque eductis disjudicando, angularitatem horumce bonorum Trąmpczyn et Łukom cum bonis Drzewce.
Iam verò acialitatem eorundem bonorum cum bonis Biskupice determinatam, et in mappa demonstratam esse, hocque in puncto decretum commissoriale per commissarios ex parte Illustrissimi Magnifici Prusimski conductos prolatum approbatum extitisse, ab hac igitur angularitate bonorum Drzewce uti termino à quo, parietem praedictorum bonorum Trąmpczyn et Łukom usque ad acialitatem cum bonis Biskupice uti terminum ad quem per lineam rectam per idem judicium anteactum in mappa factam, manibusque Illustrissimi Praesidentis et Mareschalii subscriptam constitutum extasse, in ordineque erigendorum scopulorum parietalium tum demolitionis scopulorum per commissarios Magnifici Chełmski erectorum tam angularium quam et parietalium.
Jam verò Illustrissimus Magnificus Prusimski parietalium tantum, etenim scopuli aciales et angulares
per decretum commissoriale anni 1775ti per commissarios Illustrissimi Magnifici Prusimski prolatum erecti approbati extiterant nihilque eosdem tangendo, condescensionem judiciorum seu officiorum campestrium assignatam fuisse, eisdem judiciis seu officiis scopulos parietales juxta terram in mappa positam erigere demandasse, in successivo verò actoratu deoccupationis Hollandorum per Magnificum Chełmski modo traditione preoccupationem distinctam cond[itional]um officii Castrensis Gnesniensis adinventam fuisse, ac deoccupationem praemissam horumce Hollandorum per idem officium pro parte Illustrissimi Magnifici Prusimski peragendam praeter unum Hollandum à parte Magnifici Chełmski in mappa p[er] lineam demonstratum injunctam esse, virtuteque decreti ejusdem judicii sui hanc deoccupationem Hollandorum praeter unum Hollandum ingenuum Martinum Hunt, qui pro re Magnifici Chełmski acsi per revolutionem judicii sui anteacti adjudicatus extiterat, pro re Illustrissimi Magnifici Prusimski Capitanei Niszczeviensis per officium Castrensis Gnesniensis notariatus testante actu in fundo Hollandorum feria 4ta post Dominicam Oculi Quadragesimae proxima nempe die 6[?] mensis Martii anno praesenti prolato actis Castrensis Coninensis die decima octava mensis et anni ejusdem oblatuato peractam esse.
In termino verò condescensionis graniciali in borra pinatica penes scopulos tres, duos videlicet haereditatum Trąmpczyn et Łukom, tertium verò hareditatis Drzewce collateralis robore commissionis anni 1775ti erectos feria 2da post Festum Sanctissimae et Individuae Trinitatis proxima nempe die 27ma mensis Maii
anno praesenti agitatae per idem decretum assignatae officia commissorialia campestria nempe Iunivladislaviae et Dobrinensis à partibus conducta praescriptum altedicti decreti judicii sui exequendo lineam rectam juxta positam in mappa per judicium suum geomethrae jurato à diamethro scopulorum angularium bonorum Trąpczyn, Łukom, et Drzewce, usque ad diamethrum scopulorum acialium eorundem bonorum cum bonis Biskupice in ordine erectionis scopulorum parietalium bona hacce Trąmpczyn seu Trąmpczynek, sive Nowa Wieś dicta à bonis Łukom distinguentium perficere demandasse, eandemque lineam rectam juxta praescriptum decreti tribunalis geomethram juratum perfecisse, et per officia commissorialia campestria condescensorialia revisam, ac confrontatam fuisse.
Sed quia post lineam hanc per geomethram factam Hollandusque idem ingenuus Martinus Hunt Magnifico Chełmski per officium notariatus Castrensis Gnesniensis relictus, à parte dextra scilicet in fundo ipsi adjudicato non extiterat hac in occurrentia officia condescensorialia secum discrepasse utpote officium comitiale Dobrinensis causam hanc per sui actum in Pyzdry die vigesima prima mensis Junii oblatuatum praesens ad judicium remisisse.
Iam verò Iunivladislavienses testante actu ejus actis Castrensis Coninensis die octava mensis Junii oblatuato meliorationem lineae per geomethram peractae et quod sit conformiter cum linea in mappa apposita facta per utraque officia declaratae, ultra praescriptum decreti tribunali imo contrariando lineae decisivae praemeditasse, et geomethrae hanc meliorationem ita ut
locus in quo Hollandus Joannis tempore commissionis anni 1775ti et conficiendae ejusdem mappae extiterat, ante quatuor annos combustus, à parte dextra pro re Magnifici Chełmski relinquatur intimasse, verum eandem meliorationem lineae Magnificum Chełmski denegasse, hacque in occurrentia causam hanc suum ad judicium devenisse, quocirca quam v[erò] magnificus Chełmski bonorum Łukom cum attinentibus haereditates, decreto suprascripto judicii sui anteacti sese tutand[ae?] ea ratione quod unus Hollandus per idem decretum officium condescensoriali ad deoccupationem designato, ipse relinqui demandatus et tanquam adjudicatus extitisse videtur, parietem bonorum suorum Łukom cum bonis Trąmpczyn ita formandum ut dictus Hollandus à parte dextra lineae parietalis existat in fundoque ejus proprio consistat exoptat habitoque respectu ejusdem Hollandi pro re sui decisa elucidationem seu quodam modo meliorationem regulae decreti anteacti judicii sui exposcit, erroneamque mappae deliniationem objicit.
Cum tamen constat, geomethras juratos circa opera delineationis mapparum solummodo ductum praesentium signaque quaevis in ductibusque reperibilia ac alia principaliora eisdem ductibusque propinquiora, tum ea quae decreta expresse praecipiunt arte geometrica dimensurare, et in mappis accurate exprimere obligatos esse. gasas Hollandorum inter ductus praesentium multum sibi contrarios intermedie positas remotasque geographicae tantum adnotasse, dum verò mappa haec per ipsum confecta, quo ad ductus praesentium signaque principaliora indisputabilis coram anteacto judicio per Illustrissimum Magnificum Prusimski reposita extiterat, hocce judicium anteactum lites inter partes sopiendo adinventis et constitutis signis cardinalibusque, angularitationem acialitationem indubitatam demonstrantibusque, ab angularitate et acialitate nempe à diamethro ad diamethrum lineam granicialem parietalem hacce in mappa apposuisse eandemque lineam in fundo quam rectissime per geomethram juratum ad erectionem scopulorum comparandam executioni judiciorum vel officiorum Succamerarialium praecommisisse. Iam verò circumspecto Hollando à parte dextra lineae appositae geographicae tantum adnotato in supposito quod idem Hollandus haereditatis bonorum Łukom per lineam in fundo perficiendam cedet, fine abbreviationis processuque constituto in vim calculi quarto et praecidente actum campestrem termino deoccupatorio Hollandorum praefixo ne dein alter calculus ex possessione occupatorum eorundem Hollandorum oriatur, hunc Hollandum officio Castrensis Gnesniensis ad deoccupationem designato pro re Magnifici Chełmski relinquendum demandasse, non tamen eundem Hollandum tanquam pro termino per quem reputasse.

immobilis suo in loco existit resolutioque per idem decretum tribunalium Hollandi nullo modo causae graniciali per factam lineam et determinatas angularitates et acialitates finaliter determinatae obesse valet.
Idemque Hollandus nullo alio iure ad Magnificum Chełmski spectare dignoscitur, regulam ejusdem decreti omnino adimplendam et observandam praecipit.
Cassatisque utrisque actibus per officia comitalia campestria prolatis, supra expunctis in ordine executionis toties dicti decreti anteacti judicii sui postcommissorialis, necessariam esse condescensionem Illustrissimorum Magnificorum Succamerariorum, vel eorundem Camerariorum, praevio etiam conducendo geomethra jurato, Gnesniensis, modo Vschovensis, vel Brestensis Cujaviae, aut Plocensis, sit Siradensis, vel Vielunensis, aut Ravensis sive Gostinensis, modo Łomzensis, aut Cernensis vel Varsaviensis, in sex ab actu praesenti decreti septimanis in fundum bonorum Trąmpczyn controversum, locumque suprascriptae angularitatis conducendorum ad invenit.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Dekret des Feldgerichts von Franciszek Ksawery Jasiński, Grenzkämmerer der Woiwodschaft Gnesen, begonnen am 28. November 1782 auf dem Grund von Trąbczyn bei den drei Eckhügeln von Trąbczyn Minus oder Nowa Wieś, Łukom und Drzewce. Es rückt das Dekret des Krontribunals in Petrikau vom 17. Oktober 1782 zwischen Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice, und Stanisław Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa, im Wortlaut ein; geladen waren auch die Grenzkämmerer Jakub Górski von Inowrocław und Alojzy Orłowski vom Land Dobrzyń sowie Abt Lubstowski und der Konvent von Ląd. Das Tribunal hält fest: Die von den Ständen eingesetzte Kommission hatte den Grenzstreit durch zwei einander widersprechende, in Abwesenheit ergangene Dekrete entschieden; nach dem Gesetz von 1776 kamen beide Parteien vor das Tribunal, das am 23. Januar 1782 endgültig entschied. Jenes Dekret bestimmte den Eckpunkt von Trąbczyn und Łukom mit Drzewce und den Endpunkt mit Biskupice so, wie die Karte sie zeigt, und bestätigte darin das Dekret der von Prusimski beigezogenen Kommissare; dazwischen zog es auf der Karte eine gerade Linie, vom Präsidenten und vom Marschall unterschrieben. Die Olęder, die Chełmski in Besitz genommen hatte, waren Prusimski zurückzugeben, bis auf einen, Martin Hunt, der Chełmski belassen wurde; das geschah am 6. März und wurde am 18. März in Konin eingetragen. Beim Ortstermin am 27. Mai zog der vereidigte Geometer die Linie; Hunt lag aber nicht auf der Chełmski zugesprochenen rechten Seite, und die beiden Feldämter gingen auseinander: Das von Dobrzyń verwies die Sache an das Tribunal zurück, das von Inowrocław wollte die Linie so verbessern, dass die Stelle einer vor vier Jahren abgebrannten Olęder-Stelle rechts bliebe, was Chełmski ablehnte. Das Tribunal entscheidet: Ein vereidigter Geometer hat nur Grenzzüge und Grenzzeichen zu vermessen, Häuser trägt er bloß der Lage nach ein; das Dekret vom 23. Januar ist unabänderlich und zu erfüllen, die Akten beider Feldämter werden aufgehoben. Binnen sechs Wochen soll ein Unterkämmerer oder Kämmerer mit einem vereidigten Geometer auf dem Grund erscheinen, die Linie von den drei Eckhügeln bis zum Endpunkt mit Biskupice ziehen lassen und nur Wandhügel errichten; was rechts der Linie liegt, gehört zu Łukom, was links liegt, zu Trąbczyn oder Nowa Wieś; die 1775 von Chełmskis Kommissaren errichteten Hügel sind alle, die von Prusimskis Kommissaren errichteten Wandhügel ebenfalls zu schleifen. Weil Martin Hunt nicht rechts der Linie sitzt, hat Chełmski ihn binnen sechs Wochen vor dem Kommissar der großpolnischen Woiwodschaft in Prusimskis Besitz zu übergeben, bei Strafe der Verbannung. Chełmski soll Prusimski die schon zuerkannte Summe für die von den Olędern bezogenen Einkünfte samt Strafgeldern am 20. Januar 1783 in Posen zahlen. Górski, den Prusimski beigezogen hatte, wird wegen der eigenmächtigen Verbesserung der Linie zu einer Woche Turmhaft in der Burg Radziejów und zu einer Geldbuße an Chełmski verurteilt; Orłowski, den Chełmski beigezogen hatte, wegen der unbegründeten Rückverweisung zu einer Geldbuße an Prusimski. Der Konvent von Ląd wird von der geforderten Strafe freigesprochen: Sein Protest in Posen vom 26. Juni war nur vorsorglich und ist am 16. Oktober zurückgenommen worden. Der Vollzug: Am 29. November erschien Prusimski, Chełmski blieb aus; Prusimski legte die Karte mit der vom Präsidenten Sebastian Sierakowski und vom Marschall Ksawery Kęszycki unterschriebenen Linie vor, und der vereidigte Geometer Benedykt Woronowski, Kanoniker von Łęczyca, stellte die Linie mit Stangen her. Am 30. November, am 2. und am 3. Dezember ließ das Gericht entlang der Linie einundvierzig Wandhügel aufwerfen und im Sumpf namens Stawisko einunddreißig Pfähle aus Erle und Eiche einschlagen; am 2. Dezember erschien Chełmski persönlich. Der letzte Hügel steht zwölfeinhalb Ruten vor den Endhügeln von Trąbczyn, Łukom und Biskupice, die unberührt blieben; jeder Hügel misst sechs Ellen im Geviert und enthält als Zeichen fünf Steine in Kreuzform, Ziegelstücke, Glasscherben und Kohle. Danach ließ das Gericht die älteren Hügel schleifen und für nichtig erklären; Jasiński hat unterschrieben. Ein Vermerk hält fest, dass Leon Maszewski das Dekret am 7. Dezember 1782 dem Burgamt Konin vorgelegt hat und das Original in die Akten eingeheftet wurde.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The decree of the field court of Franciszek Ksawery Jasiński, boundary chamberlain of the palatinate of Gniezno, begun on 28 November 1782 on the ground of Trąbczyn at the three corner mounds of Trąbczyn Minus or Nowa Wieś, Łukom and Drzewce. It copies in full the decree of the Crown Tribunal at Piotrków of 17 October 1782 between Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, and Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa; the boundary chamberlains Jakub Górski of Inowrocław and Alojzy Orłowski of the land of Dobrzyń, and Abbot Lubstowski and the convent of Ląd, were cited too. The Tribunal records: the commission appointed by the Estates had decided the boundary cause by two decrees that contradicted each other and were given in default; under the law of 1776 both parties came before the Tribunal, which gave its final decree on 23 January 1782. That decree fixed the corner of Trąbczyn and Łukom with Drzewce and the end point with Biskupice as the map shows them, confirming on that point the decree of the commissioners engaged by Prusimski; between the two it drew a straight line on the map, signed by the President and the Marshal. The Olęder settlers whom Chełmski had taken into possession were to be given back to Prusimski, all but one, Martin Hunt, who was left to Chełmski; this was done on 6 March and entered at Konin on 18 March. At the sitting on the ground on 27 May the sworn surveyor drew the line; but Hunt did not lie on the right-hand side adjudged to Chełmski, and the two field offices disagreed: that of Dobrzyń sent the cause back to the Tribunal, that of Inowrocław meant to correct the line so that the site of an Olęder holding burnt down four years before should stay on the right, which Chełmski refused. The Tribunal decides: a sworn surveyor has to measure only boundary lines and boundary signs, and marks houses merely by their position; the decree of 23 January cannot be changed and is to be carried out, and the acts of both field offices are quashed. Within six weeks a sub-chamberlain or chamberlain is to come to the ground with a sworn surveyor, have the line drawn from the three corner mounds to the end point with Biskupice, and raise wall mounds only; what lies to the right of the line belongs to Łukom, what lies to the left to Trąbczyn or Nowa Wieś; the mounds raised in 1775 by Chełmski's commissioners are all to be levelled, and the wall mounds raised by Prusimski's commissioners as well. Because Martin Hunt does not sit to the right of the line, Chełmski is to hand him over into Prusimski's possession within six weeks before the commissioner of the palatinate of Greater Poland, on pain of banishment. Chełmski is to pay Prusimski the sum already adjudged for the revenue drawn from the Olęder settlers, with penalties, on 20 January 1783 at Poznań. Górski, whom Prusimski had engaged, is sentenced for correcting the line on his own authority to a week in the tower of Radziejów castle and a money penalty to Chełmski; Orłowski, whom Chełmski had engaged, for sending the cause back without ground, to a money penalty to Prusimski. The convent of Ląd is freed from the penalty claimed: its protest at Poznań of 26 June was only a precaution and was withdrawn on 16 October. The execution: on 29 November Prusimski appeared and Chełmski stayed away; Prusimski produced the map with the line signed by the President, Sebastian Sierakowski, and the Marshal, Ksawery Kęszycki, and the sworn surveyor Benedykt Woronowski, canon of Łęczyca, set out the line with poles. On 30 November and on 2 and 3 December the court had forty-one wall mounds thrown up along the line and thirty-one stakes of alder and oak driven into the marsh called Stawisko; on 2 December Chełmski appeared in person. The last mound stands twelve and a half perches short of the end mounds of Trąbczyn, Łukom and Biskupice, which were left untouched; each mound measures six cubits square and holds as signs five stones in the form of a cross, pieces of brick, broken glass and charcoal. The court then had the older mounds levelled and declared void; Jasiński signed. A note records that Leon Maszewski brought the decree to the castle office at Konin on 7 December 1782 and that the original was sewn into the records.

The court of the present field office of the Gniezno Voivodeship — having declared that the appearance of the Right Honourable Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, is to be noted should he arrive, but his present absence not being attended to — fulfilling the rule prescribed by the forenamed Tribunal decree, first of all compels the same Most Honourable Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice, to the immediate repositing of the map drawn up at the act of the 1775 commission, ratified by the post-commissarial Tribunal decree; and the Most Honourable Prusimski immediately reposited the said map with the line drawn by the Tribunal court beginning from the corner mounds of the estates of Trąbczyn, Łukomia, and Drzewce, extending to the axial mounds of those same estates of Trąbczyn and Łukom and Biskupice, and on one side signed by the hand of the Most Honourable and Most Reverend Sebastian Sierakowski, Count, Canon of Kraków Cathedral, Knight of Saint Stanisław, President; and on the other by the hand of the Most Honourable Ksawery Nałęcz Kęszycki, Castellan

of Gniezno, Marshal of the Crown Tribunal, as aforesaid subscribed; the court, having found satisfaction on this point, proceeding by the tenor of the Tribunal decree to the geometer engaged by the Most Honourable Prusimski, namely the Most Illustrious and Most Reverend Benedykt Woronowski, Canon of Łęczyca, by whom the above-mentioned map was drawn:

to form the straightest possible line in accordance with the prescription of the post-commissarial Tribunal decree — the present court intimates.

The aforesaid Most Illustrious and Most Reverend geometer formed the line — also prepared in the previous field inspection act, which had not been effected on account of the discrepancy of the field offices, by the driving of rods into the ground — and filled in certain missing rods in the cut line. The court of the present field office descended to the review of the said line and its confrontation with the reposited map; having reviewed it, since it is brought to that court's knowledge that the said line from the corner junction to the axial junction has been formed in the straightest possible manner in accordance with the intent of the post-commissarial Tribunal decree:

Therefore, executing the intent of the said Piotrków Crown Tribunal decree at the present time, it declares that it proceeds to the raising or erection of mounds — and in the boggy places to the driving-in of stakes — in the line designated by the Tribunal court. Finally, evening approaching that same day, it ordered its act to be proclaimed to the following day; and likewise on the 30th of November — namely Saturday, that being the very

feast of Saint Andrew the Apostle — having resumed its jurisdiction; and the Most Honourable and Most Reverend Benedykt Lubstowski, Abbot, and also the Reverend Ląd Convent of the Sacred Cistercian Order, having been cited, appearing in person through the Religious and Very Reverend Paweł Wybieralski, Prior, in his own name and in the name of the Most Honourable Abbot and the Reverend Convent; but the Right Honourable Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, still absent — the court of the field office, executing the sentence delivered the previous day, commenced the erection of mounds.

Beginning indeed from the centre of the corner mounds, in the act of specification, [with] the straight line formed, at a distance of ten pręty — each [mound] comprising eight and a half łokcie — [they erected] the first wall mound, or guardian of the corner junction.

[Section 4 — Mound-Erection Record]

Day 1: 30 November 1782

The 1st [wall mound] — from which, proceeding along the same line, extended geometrically toward the south, at a distance of 20 pręty, a similar 2nd; after it at a similar distance:

The 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th — from which [i.e., from the 8th mound], entering into the boggy place called stawisko, densely overgrown with various trees: the cut line at a distance of ten pręty on the edge of that same marshy ground, in place of a mound, [he drove] alder stake the 1st; from which at a similar distance of ten pręty, oak stake the 2nd; then again oak:

[Translator's note: Lat. palum quaercinum alterum demum quercinum — "oak stake the 2nd; then [also] oak." Demum functions as a continuative particle. The full sequence of stake types is: 1 alder, 2 oak, 3–5 alder, 6 oak, 7–9 alder, 10–11 oak, 12–15 alder, 16 oak, 17 alder, 18 oak (Day 1); 19 oak, 20 alder, 21 oak, 22 alder, 23 oak, 24 alder, 25 oak, 26 alder, 27 oak, 28–31 alder at marsh edge (Day 2).]

The 3rd, 4th, 5th — again alder; the 6th — again oak; the 7th, 8th, 9th — then alder; the 10th and 11th — oak; the 12th, 13th, 14th, 15th — alder; the 16th — oak; the 17th — alder;

the 18th — oak.

The 18th having been driven in, the court of the present office ordered its jurisdiction to be proclaimed for the 2nd of December.

Day 2: 2 December 1782

On that day arriving: oak stake the 19th; from which, in a higher place, or ground and estate accustomed to be ploughed, in place of a stake, wall mound the 9th; from which again in a boggy place at a similar distance, alder stake

the 20th; from which oak the 21st; again alder the 22nd; then oak the 23rd; after it alder the 24th; oak the 25th; likewise alder the 26th; the 27th — oak;

the 28th, 29th, 30th, 31st — at the edge of the muddy ground, alder — all notched in four directions, he caused to be driven in.

[Translator's note: Lat. omnes in quatuor partes obsecatos — "all notched in four directions." Stakes were notched in four directions as a standard practice to distinguish them as legal boundary markers.]

From the 31st stake, leaving those same boggy places and entering into the high pine forest, and crossing the road from the village of Łumowo — as the people say — running to the Trąbczyn olędry, at a distance of ten pręty from the last-mentioned stake, in the same line, not deviating in anything whatsoever: again wall mound the 10th; from which at a distance of twenty pręty, the 11th, the 12th, the 13th — around the hilly place; after it, on the other side of that same hilly place, the 14th — and on that same day the Right Honourable Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, appearing personally, in whose presence the court of the present office [erected] the 15th, 16th, then the 17th and 18th — at the equal distance described above — where, evening approaching, it ordered its act likewise to be proclaimed to the following day, namely

the 3rd of December, by the general Court Summoner of the realm.

Day 3: 3 December 1782

And again on that same following day, jurisdiction having been resumed, proceeding to the continuation of the erection of mounds — in this same line and at a similar distance: the 19th, 20th, 21st, 22nd, 23rd, 24th, 25th, 26th, 27th, 28th, 29th, 30th, 31st, 32nd, 33rd, 34th, 35th, 36th, 37th, 38th, 39th, 40th — at the equal distance set out above; from which at a distance of fifteen pręty, in a place showing signs of moisture, the 41st — this being at a distance of twelve and a half pręty from the centre of the axial mounds of those same estates of Trąbczyn, Łukom, and Biskupice — those [axial mounds] being left untouched —

all in the high pine forest, each comprising six square łokcie, newly raised from the ground with the placement of metallic signs — namely into each one: five stones in the sign of the Holy Cross, pieces of burned brick, broken glass, and charcoal.

And [the court] made known that whatever remains on the right side of that line and the recently erected mounds — all of that is to serve the estate of Łukom; and whatever on the left side — all of that [is to serve] the estate of Trąbczyn or Nowa Wieś; and determined these boundaries as to endure in perpetuity.

Demolition of Prior Mounds

And so, after the foregoing had been completed, it proceeded to the demolition of the mounds — both those erected in 1775 by the commissioners engaged on the part of Chełmski, and also the distinct wall mounds erected by the commissioners

of the Most Honourable Prusimski — in accordance with the prescription of the Piotrków Crown Tribunal decree by whose authority the present act is carried out; and first of all ordered the demolition of the mounds erected as above by the commissioners engaged on the part of the Right Honourable Chełmski — both corner mounds and axial mounds and also wall mounds — and likewise those erected on the part of the Most Honourable Prusimski by the commissioners: the wall mounds only; and ordered the stakes to be extracted from the ground, and the metallic signs [placed in the mounds] in the customary manner to be extracted from those same mounds; and declared the said mounds, levelled to the ground, as null and void.

And having fulfilled everything committed to it by the Tribunal decree enrolled above, it concluded the force of the present act, no one contradicting.

Franciszek Ksawery Rawicz Jasiński of Jasień, Boundary Surveyor of the Gniezno Voivodeship. By his own hand.

In Konin, Saturday on the vigil of the Feast of the Immaculate Conception of the Most Blessed Virgin Mary, namely the 7th of December in the year of the Lord 1782.
