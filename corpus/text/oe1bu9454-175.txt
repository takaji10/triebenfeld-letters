Hochwohlgebohrner Herr
Hochzuverehrender Herr Kriegs- und Forst Rath!
Ich habe dem Befehl Euer Hochwohlgebohren die anlie¬
genden Piecen zu übersenden, wovon das eine
Original und das zweite eine Abschrifft ist.
Sr Durchlaucht bitten ergebenst hierüber
dern Meinung zuschreiben, und was in die¬
sen Sachen zu thun ist, besonders draengen
die Girard und Haupt, und stünden Sr. durchlaucht
mit Ihrer Lasserwelche durch die Reiße sehr
erschöpft wurde, nicht etwas im bloßen, so wür¬
den Höchst Sie sogleich die erforderliche Summa
schiken, um diesen Herrn zu befriedigen

Ich empfehle mich zu fernerer Gewogenheit
und beharre in treuer Verehrung
Euer Hochwohlgebohren
Breslau d 2ten August
1805.
unterthäniger Diener
A. Huhn

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Writing on behalf of His Serene Highness, A. Huhn forwards two enclosures, one an original and one a copy, and asks Triebenfeld to set down his opinion on the matters and on what should be done. The creditors Girard and Haupt are pressing especially hard; the Prince would remit the sum needed to satisfy them at once, but his cash-box has been much depleted by his recent journey.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Right Honourable Sir, Most Highly Revered Councillor of War and Forests! I have, by command of Your Right Honourable, to transmit the enclosed pieces, of which the one is an original and the second a Copy. His Serene Highness most humbly requests that you write your opinion upon these matters, and what is to be done in these affairs; the Girard and Haupt press particularly, and were His Serene Highness not somewhat short with his cash-box, which was much exhausted by the journey, His Highness would at once send the requisite sum in order to satisfy these gentlemen.

I commend myself to your further favour and persist in faithful veneration, Your Right Honourable's — Breslau, the 2nd of August 1805. Humble and obedient servant, A. Huhn
