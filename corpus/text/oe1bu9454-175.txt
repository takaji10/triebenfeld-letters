Hochwohlgebohrner Herr
Hochzuverehrender Herr Kriegs- und Forst Rath!
Ich habe dem Befehl Euer Hochwohlgebohren die anlie¬
genden Piecen zu übersenden, wovon das eine
Original und das zweite eine Abschrifft ist.
Sr Durchlaucht bitten ergebenst hierüber
dern Meinung zuschreiben, und was in die¬
sen Sachen zu thun ist, besonders draengen
die Girard und Haupt, und stünden Sr. durchlaucht
mit Ihrer Lasserwelche durch die Reiße sehr
erschöpft wurde, nicht etwas im bloßen, so wür¬
den Höchst Sie sogleich die erforderliche Summa
schiken, um diesen Herrn zu befriedigen

Ich empfehle mich zu fernerer Gewogenheit
und beharre in treuer Verehrung
Euer Hochwohlgebohren
Breslau d 2ten August
1805.
unterthäniger Diener
A. Huhn

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Writing on behalf of the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, A. Huhn forwards to Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld two enclosed papers, one an original and one a copy, and asks him to write his opinion on them and on what should be done. The creditors Girard and Haupt are pressing hard for payment; the Prince would settle with them at once but for his cash being depleted by his recent journey.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Right Honourable Sir, Highly Honoured Sir Councillor of War and Forests! I have, by the command of Your Right Honourable, [uncertain: to transmit] the enclosed pieces, of which the one is an original and the second a copy. His Serene Highness most submissively requests that you write your opinion upon these, and what is to be done in these matters; the Girard and Haupt are pressing especially hard, and if His Serene Highness were not somewhat short in his cash-box, which was greatly exhausted by the journey, he would at once send the requisite sum in order to satisfy these gentlemen.

I commend myself to your further favour and persist in faithful reverence, Your Right Honourable's. Breslau, the 2nd of August 1805.

humble and obedient servant

A. Huhn
