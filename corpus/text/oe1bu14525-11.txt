--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Nachtrag zur Schenkung vom 19. Juni: Friedrich Wilhelm überträgt seinem Oheim Friedrich Ludewig, regierendem Fürsten von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, erblich und eigentümlich die Zubehörungen der Güter Betsche, Zagórów und Wienietz – bei Betsche die Dörfer Stołuń, Kuligowo, Szarcz, die Vogtei Zielomyśl und Silna mit den Hauländereien, der Pomherr-Hauländerei und Heidemühle, bei Zagórów das Dörfchen Swięcin, bei Wienietz Konty, Szedlemin, Batkowo und Karczyn. Bedingung ist die Zahlung der bisherigen landesherrlichen Abgaben als unveränderlicher Kanon sowie der Kompetenz für geistliche Stiftungen. Beigefügt die Beglaubigung des Geheimen Südpreußischen Archivs (Beugnitz).

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A royal grant by Friedrich Wilhelm, given as a certified copy from a South Prussian archive, adding to the estates of Pszczew, Zagórów and Wienietz already conferred on his uncle Friedrich Ludwig, Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, in June: at Pszczew the villages of Stołuń, Kuligowo, Szarcz, the bailiwick of Zielomyśl and Silna with their Hauländer holdings and Heidemühle; at Zagórów, Swięcin; near Wienietz, Konty, Szedlemin, Bątkowo and Karczyn. Held heritably and as property, subject to a fixed unalterable rent equal to the sovereign dues hitherto paid and to church dues, with authorities forbidden to obstruct him.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
We Friedrich Wilhelm, by the Grace of God King of Prussia etc., do hereby certify and declare, and by force of this Our open letter, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors in the Crown, the Electorate and Our remaining lands, that We, out of the particular friendship and affection We bear toward the High-Born Prince, Our kindly beloved Uncle Friedrich Ludewig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, as also in order to give him a fresh proof of Our Royal gratitude for the distinguished and beneficial services rendered to Us and to Our Royal House, have found Ourselves moved to make over, grant and convey to His Lordship, in heritable and proprietary form, the appurtenances belonging to those estates already conferred upon him by the instrument of the 19th of June of this year, namely Pszczew, Zagórów and Wieniec, to wit:

1. at Pszczew, the villages of Stołuń, Kuligowo, Szarcz, the bailiwick of Zielomyśl and Silna, together with the Hollander holdings, the Pomherr Hollander holding and Heidemühle;

2. at Zagórów, the little village of Swięcia; and finally

3. near Wieniec, Konty, Szedlemin, Bątkowo and Karczyn — against payment:

1. of the sovereign dues hitherto discharged by the said villages, Hollander holdings and parcels of land, as a fixed

and unalterable canon; and

2. of the competence due therefrom to the ecclesiastical corporations and foundations.

This We also do hereby and by force of these presents, out of Royal power and plenitude, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors in the Crown, the Electorate and Our remaining lands, in such manner and wise that Our above-mentioned kindly beloved Uncle, the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, shall irrevocably have, possess and enjoy the above-named appurtenances belonging to the estates of Pszczew, Zagórów and Wieniec, with all rights and prerogatives attaching thereto, as the same have hitherto been exercised or may lawfully be exercised, with all lands pertaining thereto, in fields, woodlands and meadows, likewise in ponds and waters, according to the lawful boundaries and bounds, just as all has hitherto been used or may by right be used, from now on for everlasting time, for himself and his descendants and heirs, as a true, well-acquired and unrestricted property, from Us and Our descendants and successors; and that there shall likewise be conferred upon him the

power and authority to dispose thereof at his pleasure under the above-mentioned conditions, unhindered by any man. We therefore command and enjoin all Our Supreme Provincial Colleges, as also all other authorities whom this concerns, herewith as graciously as earnestly, that they in no wise hinder or impede either the Lordship of the oft-mentioned Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen in the heritable and proprietary possession thus conferred upon him of the above-named appurtenances belonging to the estates of Pszczew, Zagórów and Wieniec, nor him or those upon whom he may one day pass the same by inheritance, by force of this Our letter of grant, or may otherwise lawfully transfer them by other acts inter vivos aut mortis causa; but rather that they let His Lordship, and eventually also them, enjoy the same freely and quietly, doing nothing against it themselves, nor causing or permitting such to be done by others, on pain of Our supreme displeasure and of unfailing severe punishment. In witness whereof etc. Berlin, the 1st of December 1797.

Friedrich Wilhelm

Letter of grant for the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, for the heritable and proprietary possession of the appurtenances belonging to the estates of Pszczew, Zagórów and Wieniec.
