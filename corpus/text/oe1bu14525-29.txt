--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Abschrift eines Schreibens Johann Wilhelm Glencks, des Bau- und Ökonomierats des Fürsten zu Hohenlohe, an den König: Dem königlichen Befehl gemäß, den Besitztitel der fürstlichen Güter Kopojno, Zagórów, Drzewce und anderer zu berichtigen, habe er alles getan, um die Urkunden beizubringen. Er hofft, das bisherige Hindernis sei behoben und der Befehl könne binnen vier Wochen ausgeführt werden, und bittet, alle strengen Verfügungen aufzuschieben. Der Befehl, auf den er antwortet, ist auf den 18. November datiert, sein Schreiben auf den 3. November.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A copy of a letter of Johann Wilhelm Glenck, the Prince of Hohenlohe's councillor for building and estate management, to the King: in obedience to the King's order to put in order the title to the Prince's estates of Kopojno, Zagórów, Drzewce and others, he has done everything needed to supply the documents. He hopes that the obstacle so far is removed and the order can be carried out within four weeks, and asks that all severe measures be held off. The order he answers is dated 18 November, his letter 3 November.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Copy

Most Serene and Most Mighty King! Most gracious King and Lord!

In most humble obedience to Your Royal Majesty's most high command of 18 November, according to which the title of possession (titulus possessionis) to the Princely Hohenlohe estates of Kopojno, Zagórów, Drzewce, etc. etc. is to be rectified, I have done everything required for the production of the documents, and I hope that the impediment which has hitherto prevailed is removed, and that Your Majesty's most high commands may be most exactly carried out within 4 weeks; wherefore I most humbly beg that all severe measures against this be held back, and in deepest reverence I remain, until death, Your Royal Majesty's most humble Johann Wilhelm Glenck, Princely Hohenlohe Councillor of Building and Economy.

Wrocław, the 3rd of November 1800
