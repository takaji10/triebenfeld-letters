--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports a fresh revolt of the Zagorowo peasants against the separation of the interior grounds, now settled, though the resulting absence from Berlin lost the hoped-for fund when the King and Triebenfeld's patron departed. Councillor of justice Bucholz has instead arranged 80000 Rthl from von Bredow's capital, secured on Kontop (30000), Schwammern (20000) and Trąbczyn (30000), to pay Countess Schlabrendorff 25000, Weigel 25000, Hahn 5000 for Koschentin, the Prince 5000 cash and Triebenfeld 30000 for the Wulffs against a mortgage; von Tismar meanwhile negotiates 360000 Rthl with Nathusius of Magdeburg.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince, Most gracious Lord! An unforeseen occurrence made it necessary that, after Your Serene Highness's departure from Berlin, I had to hasten very quickly to Zagorowo, because the peasants had once again raised an insurrection and would not permit the separation of the lands in the interior. This matter is now indeed settled and entirely [uncertain: arranged]; meanwhile the King, however much I hastened back hither, had departed before I returned, and since my patron was, as is natural, gone away with him, he/I could no longer reckon upon this fund. I had therefore to strike out upon other paths in order to obtain the money for Weigel, for the Countess Schlabrendorff, and for Your Serene Highness. This has now only today been assured through the Justice Councillor Bucholz. This man manages the affairs of a certain von Bredow, who has 80000 Rthl lying with the Seehandlung here, which he has ordered to be placed elsewhere (since the Minister v Stein, His Excellency, would now give only 3 pl.). Herr Bucholz has arranged the matter thus: Upon Kontop there are given, against cession of 2 mortgages, 30000 Rthl; upon Schwammern 20000; upon Trąbczin, for the Grotowski mortgage which Weigel holds, 30000. Of this there are to be paid — being 80000 Rthl: To the Countess Schlabrendorff the 25000 still coming to her; to Weigel the 25000 still due to him; to Hahn, as assistance in Koschentin, 5000; to Your Serene Highness in cash 5000. I take for myself, for payment of the Wulffs, 30000 — 80000 Rthl. In return,

in return Your Serene Highness will then give me a note or mortgage for 30000 Rthl, being as much as I raise upon my own estates for Your Highness's behalf! Next to this, the Privy Councillor v Tismar is pursuing with the wealthy house of Nathusius at Magdeburg a negotiation, in which he thinks first of a capital of 360000 Rthl upon the whole of the South Prussian estates. With this all debts are then to be paid, and the mortgage of 250000 Rthl refused with Oppenheimer at 90000 fl is in particular to be redeemed and [uncertain: cancelled]. When all this has been seen to and brought into order, then I myself [text lost] to Your Serene Highness and bring you the moneys or good orders to pay. With the sale of the lands it goes somewhat slowly, on account of the warlike events; meanwhile Honrichs writes to me yesterday that once again several purchasers had come forward who would buy at the newer fixed prices. Once this matter is properly under way at the new higher prices, then — since all hindrances are now removed — much money will be brought together, and an end will be made of all cares. May God grant Your Serene Highness the most lasting health, and bestow upon you (when it comes to blows) victory. This is the one most heartfelt wish of Your Most Serene Highness's
Berlin, the 28th of Septbr.  faithful and most obedient servant
1806.  v Triebenfeld
