--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Triebenfeld hat einen neuen Bauernaufstand in Zagórów gegen die Separation der Gründe beigelegt. Über Justiz Rath Bucholz sind ihm 80000 Rthl eines von Bredow zugesichert: 30000 Rthl auf Konotop, 20000 auf Świniary, 30000 auf Trąbczyn für die Grotowskische Hypothek Weigels. Auszuzahlen sind 25000 an Gräfin Schlabrendorff, 25000 an Weigel, 5000 an Hahn für Koszęcin und 5000 bar an den Fürsten. Tismar verhandelt mit Nathusius in Magdeburg über 360000 Rthl auf die südpreußischen Güter.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Triebenfeld says he has settled a new peasant uprising in Zagórów against the separation of the lands. Through Councillor of Justice Bucholz, 80000 Rthl from a von Bredow have been promised to him: 30000 Rthl on Konotop, 20000 on Świniary, 30000 on Trąbczyn for Weigel's Grotowski mortgage. To be paid out are 25000 to Countess Schlabrendorff, 25000 to Weigel, 5000 to Hahn for Koszęcin and 5000 in cash to the Prince. Tismar is negotiating with Nathusius in Magdeburg over 360000 Rthl on the South Prussian estates.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
25

Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Lord!

An unforeseen event made it necessary that, after Your Serene Highness's departure from Berlin, I should hasten with all speed to Zagórów, because the peasants had once again stirred up a rising and would not permit the separation of the lands in the interior. This matter is now indeed settled and entirely arranged; however, much as I hurried back here again, the King had departed before I returned, and since my patron had naturally gone off with him, I could no longer reckon upon that fund. I was therefore obliged to take other paths in order to obtain the money for Weigel, for the Countess Schlabrendorff and for Your Serene Highness. This has only now, today, been assured through the councillor of justice Bucholz. This man conducts the affairs of a certain von Bredow, who has 80000 Rthl lying with the Maritime Trading Company here, which he has ordered to be placed elsewhere / since the Minister von Stein, His Excellency, would now give only 3 per cent /.

Mr Bucholz has turned the matter thus: Upon Konotop, against cession of the two mortgages, there will be given 30000 Rthl. Upon Świniary the 20000. Upon Trąbczyn, for the Grotowski mortgage which Weigel holds, 30000. Of this there are to be paid, being = 80000 Rthl: To the Countess Schlabrendorff the 25000 still coming to her; To Weigel the 25000 still due to him.

To Hahn, as assistance in Koszęcin, 5000.

To Your Serene Highness in cash 5000.

I take for myself, for the payment of the Wulffen debt, 30000

in return for which Your Serene Highness will then give me a note or mortgage for 30000 Rthl, as being the amount I am raising on my own estates for Your Highness! Next to this, the privy councillor von Tesmar is pursuing a negotiation with the rich house of Nathusius at Magdeburg, in which he hopes to obtain a capital of 360000 Rthl upon the whole of the South Prussian estates. With this all the debts are to be paid, and the mortgage of 250000 Rthl [uncertain: refused] with Oppenheimer for 98000 Rthl is in particular to be redeemed and cancelled. When all this shall have been seen to and brought to a clear footing, then I myself to Your Serene Highness and bring you the moneys or good orders to pay. With the sale of the lands things go somewhat slowly on account of the warlike events; meanwhile Honrichs writes to me yesterday that several purchasers had once again come forward who would buy at the newly fixed prices. Once this business at the new higher prices is properly under way, then, since all the hindrances are now removed, much money will be brought together, and an end will be made of all cares.

May God grant Your Serene Highness the most lasting health and bestow upon you (when it comes to blows, victory! This is the sole and most heartfelt wish of Your Most Serene Highness's

Berlin, the 28th of September

1806.

faithful and most obedient servant

v Triebenfeld
