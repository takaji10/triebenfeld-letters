--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports a fresh peasant revolt at Zagorowo against the division of interior lands, now settled, and the loss of a hoped-for source of funds when the King and Triebenfeld's patron left Berlin. Instead the councillor of justice Bucholz has arranged 80,000 Rthl from von Bredow's capital, secured on Kontop, Schwammern and Trąbczyn, to pay Countess Schlabrendorff and Weigel 25,000 each, Hahn 5,000, the Prince 5,000 in cash and Triebenfeld 30,000 for the Wulffs, against a mortgage to him. Mentions von Tismar's projected 360,000 Rthl loan and slow land sales.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Lord!

An unforeseen occurrence made it necessary that, after Your Serene Highness's departure from Berlin, I had to hasten very swiftly to Zagorowo, because the peasants had once again stirred up a revolt and would not allow the separation of the grounds in the interior. This matter has now indeed been settled and entirely arranged; meanwhile, however much I hastened back here again, the King had departed before I returned, and since my patron, as was natural, had gone off with him, he could not count any longer upon that fund. I therefore had to take other courses in order to obtain the money for Weigel, for the Countess Schlabrendorff, and for Your Serene Highness. This has only now, today, been assured through the councillor of justice Bucholz. This man conducts the affairs of a certain von Bredow who has 80000 Rthl lying with the maritime trading company here, which he has ordered to be placed elsewhere / since the Minister von Stein, His Excellency, now wishes to give only 3 per cent /.

Herr Bucholz has [uncertain: arranged] the matter thus: upon Kontop, against cession of two mortgages, there are to be given 30000 Rthl; upon Schwammern 20000; upon Trąbczyn, for the Grotowski mortgage which Weigel holds, 30000. Of this there are to be paid, being 80000 Rthl: to the Countess Schlabrendorff the 25000 still coming to her; to Weigel the 25000 still due to him.

To Hahn, as assistance in Koschentin, 5000.

To Your Serene Highness in cash, 5000.

I take for myself, for the payment of the Wulffs, 30000 — 80000 Rthl — whereas

whereas Your Serene Highness will then give me a certificate or mortgage for 30000 Rthl, as being the amount which I am raising on my own estates on Her/His Highness's behalf! Next to this, the privy councillor von Tismar is pursuing, together with the wealthy house of Nathusius at Magdeburg, a negotiation in which he intends, in the first place, a capital of 360000 Rthl upon the whole of the South Prussian estates. With this all the debts are to be paid, and the mortgage of 250000 Rthl refused with Oppenheimer at 90000 fl is to be redeemed and cancelled in particular; and when all this has been attended to and brought to a clear footing, then I myself [will come] to Your Serene Highness and bring you the monies or good orders to pay. With the sale of the grounds matters go somewhat slowly amid the warlike events; in the meantime Honrichs writes to me yesterday that once again several purchasers had come forward who would buy at the newly fixed prices. Once this business is properly under way on account of the new higher prices, then, since all obstacles are now removed, much money will be brought together and all cares will be brought to an end.

May God grant Your Serene Highness the most lasting health and give you (when it comes to blows) victory. This is the one most heartfelt wish of Your Most Serene Highness's

Berlin, the 28th of September

most faithful and obedient servant

1806.

von Triebenfeld
