P. M.
In dem Tableaux was Glenck Ewr Hochfürstliche Durchlaucht eben
alle Revenuen der S. P. Güter gegeben hat steht
Swięcia 1800 Rthl jährliche Zinsen und 10000 Rthl Capital.
Bey heut genndigter Reviion der Taxatoren trägt Suvicia
nur 918 Rthl Zinsen jährlich – der Verstoß ist zu künfig¬
und das ſchöne Gut ist beym Himmel verſchleudert.
Es hat 3792 Morgen und hätte eben so viel Tausend rt.
Mannen bringen müßen. Es ist aber für 10000 Rthl Capital
und 918 Rthl Zinsen, und folglich für 28360 Rthl wegegeben worden,
wodurch ein offenbarer Verlust von 47340 Rthl Ewr Durch
laucht erwächſt.
Wann hierzu der Olesnicer Verlust mit 48000 Rthl gegen den
jezigen Verkauf genommen wird so verliere Ewr Durchlaucht an diese
95340 Rthl — und eben
beide, Güter Netto
solcher Ausfall entsteht gezt bey der Taxe.
Trąbczyn
den 2ten Xbr 1804.
v Triebenfeld

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Corrects Glenck's revenue tableaux for the South Prussian estates: Swięcia is entered at 1800 Rthl annual interest on 10000 Rthl capital, but the assessors' revision completed that day yields only 918 Rthl, so the 3792-Morgen property has effectively been alienated for 28360 Rthl, a loss of 47340 Rthl. Added to the Olesnicer loss of 48000 Rthl against the present sale, the Fürst stands to lose 95340 Rthl.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
P. M. In the tableaux which Glenck has just given Your Most Serene Highness of all the revenues of the S. P. estates, Swięcia stands at 1800 Rthl annual interest and 10000 Rthl capital. Upon the revision of the assessors concluded today, Swięcia yields only 918 Rthl interest yearly – the error is [uncertain: to be reckoned for the future] and the fine estate has, by heaven, been squandered away. It has 3792 Morgen and must have brought in just as many thousand rt. [uncertain: Mannen]. But it has been given away for 10000 Rthl capital and 918 Rthl interest, and consequently for 28360 Rthl, whereby a manifest loss of 47340 Rthl accrues to Your Serene Highness. If to this the Olesnicer loss of 48000 Rthl be taken against the present sale, then Your Serene Highness loses on these 95340 Rthl — and precisely both, estates net such a shortfall arises now upon the assessment. Trąbczyn, the 2nd of December 1804. v Triebenfeld
