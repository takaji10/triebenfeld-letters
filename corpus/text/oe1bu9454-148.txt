alle Revenuen der S. P. Güter gegeben hat steht
Swięcia 1800 Rthl jährliche Zinsen und 10000 Rthl Capital.
Bey heut genndigter Reviion der Taxatoren trägt Suvicia
nur 918 Rthl Zinsen jährlich – der Verstoß ist zu künfig¬
und das ſchöne Gut ist beym Himmel verſchleudert.
Es hat 3792 Morgen und hätte eben so viel Tausend rt.
Mannen bringen müßen. Es ist aber für 10000 Rthl Capital
und 918 Rthl Zinsen, und folglich für 28360 Rthl wegegeben worden,
wodurch ein offenbarer Verlust von 47340 Rthl Ewr Durch
laucht erwächſt.
Wann hierzu der Olesnicer Verlust mit 48000 Rthl gegen den
jezigen Verkauf genommen wird so verliere Ewr Durchlaucht an diese
95340 Rthl — und eben
beide, Güter Netto
solcher Ausfall entsteht gezt bey der Taxe.
Trąbczyn
den 2ten Xbr 1804.
v Triebenfeld

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Challenges Glenck's revenue table for the estates: Swięcia is entered there at 1,800 Rthl yearly interest on 10,000 Rthl capital, but a revision by assessors completed that day yields only 918 Rthl. Triebenfeld argues the 3,792 Morgen property has been given away far below worth, a loss of 47,340 Rthl, which with a 48,000 Rthl loss on Olesnica makes 95,340 Rthl against the Prince. A short, indignant note, unfinished at the foot.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
P. M.

In the table which Glenck has just given Your Most Serene Highness of all the revenues of the S. P. estates, Swięcia stands at 1800 Rthl annual interest and 10000 Rthl capital. Upon the revision of the assessors concluded today, Suvicia yields only 918 Rthl interest yearly – the error is [uncertain: to be reckoned in future] and the fine estate has, by heaven, been squandered. It has 3792 Morgen and ought to have brought in just as many thousand Rthl in [uncertain: men/rents]. It has, however, been given away for 10000 Rthl capital and 918 Rthl interest, and consequently for 28360 Rthl, whereby a manifest loss of 47340 Rthl accrues to Your Serene Highness.

If to this the Olesnica loss of 48000 Rthl be reckoned against the present sale, then Your Serene Highness loses on these 95340 Rthl — and just

both, estates net such a shortfall arises now upon the assessment. Trąbczyn

the 2nd December 1804.

v Triebenfeld
