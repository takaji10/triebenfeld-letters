--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Eine Schenkungsurkunde Friedrich Wilhelms, mit der Friedrich Ludwig, regierender Fürst von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Generallieutenant der Infanterie und Gouverneur von Breslau, für langjährige Dienste die Stadt Kamionna und das Städtchen Kulm im Posener Kreis, die Dörfer Trąbczyn, Szetlewek, Nowa wies, Łazy, Alt- und Neu-Laske, Osiny und Hauland Trąbczyński im Koniner Kreis sowie Brzyce in Kujawien samt Zubehör erblich und eigentümlich erhält, vorbehalten die landesherrlichen Steuern und öffentlichen Abgaben; Regierungen und Kriegs- und Domänenkammern wird jede Behinderung untersagt. Als beglaubigte Abschrift, bestätigt von Sonnabend, Auditeur des Regiments.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A royal grant by Friedrich Wilhelm, King of Prussia, conveying South Prussian estates in hereditary and full ownership to his uncle Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Lieutenant General and Governor of Wrocław, in return for long service: the town of Kamionna and market town of Kulm in the Poznań district, villages including Trąbczyn, Szetlewek, Nowa wies, Łazy, Old and New Laske, Osiny and Hauland Trąbczyński in the Konin district, and Brzyce in Cujavia. Sovereign taxes and public dues are reserved, and all governments and chambers are forbidden to obstruct his possession or later transfers. Certified at Wrocław in 1802 by Sonnabend, the regiment's judge advocate.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
We Friedrich Willhelm, by the Grace of God King of Prussia, etc.

Do hereby make known and declare, by means of this Our open letter, for Ourselves and Our successors to the Crown of the Electorate and Our remaining lands, and herewith graciously give every man whom it may concern to know, that, as We are, out of Our inborn favour and clemency, most inclined to acknowledge and to requite the good and fruitful services rendered to Us and to Our Royal House by particular marks of Our Royal benevolence, We have weighed in Our Royal mind the important, distinguished and useful services rendered to Us and to Our Royal House for a considerable number of years by the high-born Prince, Our friendly dear uncle, Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Our Lieutenant General of Infantry, chief of a regiment of foot, Governor of the capital city of Wrocław, Knight of Our Orders, etc., and have resolved, in order to give the said Prince a mark of the recognition of his excellent merits and of Our Royal gratitude, to confer and convey upon the same the hereinafter named estates, namely the town of Kamionna, or Kamionna, and the market town of Kulm, in the Poznań district, and the village of Brzyce together with its appurtenances in Cujavia, with all their belongings, by whatsoever name they may be called, hereditarily and in full ownership, and

to convey.

We also do so herewith, and by virtue of this, out of Royal power and plenitude, for Ourselves and Our successors and heirs to the Crown of the Electorate and Our remaining lands, in such manner and to this effect, that Our aforesaid friendly dear uncle, the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, shall have, possess and enjoy the above-named estates, with all rights and privileges attaching thereto, as these have hitherto been exercised or may lawfully be exercised, and no less with all their appurtenances and other belongings, by whatsoever name they may be called, as the same have hitherto been used or may lawfully be used, as a well-acquired and unrestricted property — yet under the reservation of the sovereign taxes and other public dues to be laid upon them, as upon the other estates of Our South Prussian vassals — for himself and his successors and heirs, from now on for all eternity, irrevocably from Us and Our successors; and that the power shall also be accorded to the same to dispose thereof at his pleasure, unhindered by any man. We therefore command and enjoin all Our South Prussian governments and Chambers of War and Domains, as also all other authorities whom this concerns, herewith

as graciously as earnestly, that they shall in no wise hinder or impede the oft-mentioned Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen in the hereditary and proprietary possession, conferred upon him, of the above-named possessions and estates together with their appurtenances and belongings, nor him or those to whom he may one day, by virtue and in force of this Our letter of donation, bequeath the same or otherwise lawfully transfer it by other acts among the living or by reason of death; but rather shall let him, and eventually also them, enjoy the same freely and undisturbed, shall themselves do nothing against it, nor occasion or permit such to be done by others, on pain of Our All-Highest displeasure and of unfailing severe punishment.

In witness whereof We have signed the present letter of donation with Our own All-Highest hand. Friedrich Willhelm

Deed of donation, for the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, to the hereditary and proprietary possession of the town of Kamionna, or Kamionna, and of the market town of Kulm in the Poznań district, of the villages of Trąbczyn, Szetlewek, Nowa wies, Łazy, Old and New Laske, Osiny, Hauland Trąbczyński, in the Konin district, and of the village of Brzyce together with appurtenances in Cujavia. That the foregoing copy is in complete agreement with the All-Highest executed

original, is herewith attested under the seal of the court, in faith thereof. Wrocław, the 18th of February 1802. Sonnabend, judge advocate of the Princely Hohenlohe Infantry Regiment.
