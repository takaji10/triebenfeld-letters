--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Triebenfeld berichtet nach schwerer Krankheit, dass niemand zahlt und 31 Prozesse schweben. Die Olesnicer, Niedźwiecki aus Kopojno und Hawich aus Drzewce sollen hinausgesetzt, 3 Vorwerke wieder eingerichtet werden. Napoleons Erklärung zur Tilgung der Schulden an Preußen in 40 Jahren verwirrt die Kolonisten; angebotenen Zinsnachlass nehmen sie nicht an. Die Trąbczyner Sache verwies das Tribunal nach Poniatowskis Schreiben an die höhere Magistratur. Die alten Hauländer bieten sofort 40000 Rthl. Anschlag Zagórów: 22140 Rthl Jahresertrag, 7140 Rthl Überschuss ab Johanni.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Triebenfeld reports, after a serious illness, that no one is paying and that 31 lawsuits are pending. He says the people of Oleśnica, Niedźwiecki of Kopojno and Hawich of Drzewce should be turned out and 3 demesne farms set up again. Napoleon's declaration on paying off the debts to Prussia over 40 years is confusing the colonists, who will not accept the reduction of interest offered them. After Poniatowski's letter the tribunal referred the Trąbczyn matter to the higher authority. The old Hollanders offer 40,000 Rthl at once. Valuation of Zagórów: 22,140 Rthl annual yield, 7,140 Rthl surplus from Midsummer.

Most Serene Prince,

Most gracious Lord!

A severe illness, the like of which I have not yet had, has hindered me from writing to Your Serene Highness sooner — now I am so far restored that I can think and act again. The frightful amount of vexation which I must [uncertain: swallow] here daily would surely hinder me from ever becoming well again. Still nobody here pays; 31 lawsuits are at present pending on that account, and now I hope that some will be compelled to pay by execution and distraint, and also that the Olesnicer will be thrown out. Likewise Niedźwiecki is to be thrown out of Kopojno and Hawich out of Drzewce, and then 3 demesne farms are to be established again, because the Germans not only pay nothing at all but go to the devil. — There are still to be paid some 8,657 Rthl. of soldiers' money or taxes and dues, 7,500 Rthl. of interest to the Invalids' Fund, and out of 27,000 Rthl. other interest and costs — [uncertain: hardly] shall I take in enough to [uncertain: cover] this.

According to the new edicts every colonist drawn into the country is to receive 6 free years, and upon this the people now rest themselves — Meanwhile all this must be [settled] between now and (NB the [uncertain: Podacken] were otherwise remitted in order to preserve the estates, but on account of the interest which I have guaranteed to pay, all my estates are being sequestrated — how am I to live thereby —? and where are 34,000 Rthl. to be [illegible] at once_

Easter, if the estates are not to be lost.

I have promised the colonists that if for 3 years they [uncertain: prangt] pay the half, I would obtain a remission of the interest; but still the villains refuse. Your Serene Highness would have gained after 1 1/2 years and money would nevertheless have come in. Everything was already well disposed, but the more recent declaration of Mr Napoleon, that the people are to have so many free years that they shall pay the sums owing to Prussia over 40 years in such a manner that on the capital only 3½ per cent interest and 2½ per cent toward amortisation of the capital must be paid, has anew confused all heads, so that nothing good is to be hoped for the present — even the Trąbczyn affair lies still. The Prince Poniatowski has written in secret to the tribunal not to give judgment in the matter at all, but that if the tribunal may not award the estates to the Dąbska woman, it should refer the matter to the higher magistracy — this latter the tribunal has done, after the bitterest debates — the higher magistracy is now the King of and Napoleon — the estates were, according to the Peace of Tilsit,

torn from Your Serene Highness, and must absolutely be restored to you. Salz is now working the matter out. The subjects all wish to have Your Serene Highness as their lord; the old Hollanders offer at once to lay down 40,000 Rthl. upon the contract concluded with them, and therefore I above all seek every means to get the estates back. The valuation of the Zagórów estates I would transmit to Your Serene Highness as soon as I can work again. It comes out at 22,140 Rthl. annual yield — but that can only be reckoned from Midsummer, and when all arrears are paid and all interference of the Government is lifted.

The shortfall arises from the reduced interest, for there I reckon back 1/3 in the first 6 years; by my calculation the interest according to the amortisation quantum amounts to 11,000 Rthl. yearly.

Taxes etc. and salaries about 4,000 Rthl. = 15,000

Revenues 22,140

There remain to Your Serene Highness 7,140 Rthl.

Meanwhile, after some years, through the absorption of the capital a further 5,000 Rthl. accrue again — and likewise the 1/3 of interest with 4,250 Rthl.

Then the yield will be 16,390 Rthl. The 7,140 Rthl can, however, as said, only be reckoned from Midsummer —

What may be saved out of the arrears of interest and the entry moneys I cannot yet determine — that will first be ascertained by a district commission. I shall do whatever is at all possible. For the rest it goes sadly with me: I am ill, lie here in a peasant's hut and freeze, and in Silesia they have sequestrated my [uncertain: Świniary] because I am here at [text lost]. The vexation will yet bring me my death, and the Princes' [uncertain: Sacken] will live; may no year be better for Your Serene Highness than the old one —

In deepest respect I remain, until death,

Your Most Serene Highness's
