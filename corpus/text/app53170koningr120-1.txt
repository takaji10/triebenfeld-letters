Actum in Conin Die 14 Mensis Martii Anno Domini 1785
Laboriosus Andraeas Kinos de Trąmpczyn Obduxit
Personaliter veniens Laboriosus Andraeas Kinos de Bonis Villae Trąmpczyn Inhaerendo Juri Communi Commonstravit Officio praesenti Contusiones Infrascriptas ut pote Oculum dextrum in circum Sanguine obortum sub Oculo Eodem Corpus Intumefactum et lividum Item Oculum Sinistrum Sanguine per medium Obortum et lividum Item totum Vultus Intumefactum Item Humeros dextros intumefactos Quae praemissa asseruit Sibi Idem Obducens per Generosum Martinum Sarzyński in Via publica Domo, ad Oppidum Pleszew discedenti in Villa Obory viam praecludens Sabbatho proximo praeterito vi et violenter pugnis et baculo illata et inflicta esse

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Eintrag vom 14. März 1785 im Buch des Burggerichts Konin. Andrzej Kinos, ein Untertan aus dem Dorf Trąbczyn, zeigt dem Amt seine Verletzungen: das rechte Auge ringsum blutunterlaufen, darunter geschwollen und blau, das linke Auge blutunterlaufen und blau, das ganze Gesicht geschwollen, die rechte Schulter geschwollen. Er gibt an, Marcin Sarzyński habe sie ihm am vergangenen Samstag mit Fäusten und einem Stock zugefügt, als er ihm im Dorf Obory auf öffentlicher Straße den Weg zur Stadt Pleszew versperrte.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
An entry of 14 March 1785 in the book of the castle court at Konin. Andrzej Kinos, a subject from the village of Trąbczyn, shows the office his injuries: the right eye suffused with blood all round and swollen and livid beneath, the left eye suffused with blood and livid, the whole face swollen, the right shoulder swollen. He states that Marcin Sarzyński inflicted them on him with fists and a stick on the Saturday before, barring his way on the public road in the village of Obory as he was going to the town of Pleszew.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Done at Konin on 14 March in the Year of the Lord 1785

The Hard-working Andreas Kinos of Trąbczyn filed an obduction

Coming personally, Laboriosus Andreas Kinos, from the goods of the village of Trąbczyn, adhering to the common law, showed to the present office the bruises written below, namely: the right eye suffused with blood all around; under the same eye the flesh swollen and livid; likewise the left eye suffused with blood through the middle and livid; likewise the entire face swollen; likewise the right shoulders swollen. Which aforementioned injuries he asserted, himself the same one filing this report, to have been inflicted and visited upon him by force and violence with fists and a stick by Right Honourable Martinus Sarzyński on the public road, while departing from home to the town of Pleszew, cutting off his path in the village of Obory, on the most recent past Saturday.
