Von Gottes Gnaden Friedrich Wilhelm Koenig von Preussen
Unsere gnädige Gruß zuvor, Ehrenvester Rath lieber Ge¬
treuer! Wir lassen Euch auf Eure Vorstellung vom 13. huj.
m. hierdurch bekannt machen, daß die Resignation der Güter
Kolno und Kaemen von dem Fisco bereits geleistet wor¬
den ist. Zur Resignation der Betscher Güter steht
Terminus auf den 22 März c. an. Von den in Cujaviens
belegenen, dem regierenden Herrn Fürsten zu Hohenlohe In¬
gelfingen verliehenen Güter ist die Resignation des
Fisci noch gar nicht nachgesucht worden, und es wird er
wartet, daß die Berichtigung des Tituli possess. dieser
Güter durch einen recipirten Justiz Commissar ohne Remo¬
nern Anstand bewürkt werde. Sind Euch mit
Gnaden gewogen. Gegeben Posen de 22. Febru 1802
Königl. Südpreuß. Regierung.
Götz.
An
den Fürstl. Hohenlohschen Bau und
Oeconomie Rath Glenk
zu Breslau

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Replying to a representation of the 13th, the Royal South Prussian Government at Posen informs Glenck, building and economy councillor to the Prince of Hohenlohe at Breslau, of the state of the title transfers: the Treasury has already resigned Kolno and Kamionna, a term for resigning the Betsche estates is set for 22 March, while for the Cujavian estates granted to the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen no resignation has yet been sought, and the title is to be put right promptly through an approved judicial commissary.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
By the Grace of God, Friedrich Wilhelm, King of Prussia. Our gracious greeting first: Worthy Councillor, dear and faithful subject! We hereby make known to you, upon your representation of the 13th of this month, that the resignation of the estates of Kolno and Kamionna has already been performed by the Treasury (Fiscus). For the resignation of the Betsche estates a term is appointed for the 22nd of March of this year. As regards the estates situate in Cujavia which have been conferred upon the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, the resignation of the Treasury has not yet been applied for at all, and it is expected that the rectification of the title of possession of these estates be effected by an admitted judicial commissary without [uncertain: Remonern] delay. We remain graciously disposed towards you. Given at Posen, the 22nd of February 1802. Royal South Prussian Government.

Götz.

To the Princely Hohenlohe Building and Economy Councillor Glenck at Breslau
