--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Entwurf eines Schreibens an Schöler: Mit Bezug auf das Schreiben vom 29. April teilt das Ministerium mit, dass die hypothekarischen Gläubiger Gefahr laufen, ihre Kapitalien zu verlieren, da der Fürst Trąbczyn deterioriert und mit Schulden belastet hat; Schöler möge dies zur Sprache bringen. Er soll Nachricht geben, falls der Kaiser dem Fürsten eine Entschädigung gewährt, damit die Gläubiger ihre Gerechtsame verfolgen können.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Draft of a letter to Schöler: with reference to the letter of 29 April, the ministry reports that the mortgage creditors are in danger of losing their capital, since the Prince has let Trąbczyn deteriorate and has burdened it with debts; Schöler is asked to raise this. He is to send word if the Emperor grants the Prince compensation, so that the creditors can pursue their claims.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
4,277.

To the Royal Major General and Envoy at the Imperial Russian Court, Mr von Schöler, Your Right Honourable

at St Petersburg

[uncertain: Hg.] 3rd, fair copy made.

With reference to my letter of 29 April of this year concerning the intercession for the Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen with His Majesty the Emperor of Russia, to procure some

compensation for the estates confiscated from him in the Kalisz department, I [uncertain: find myself moved] most respectfully to communicate to Your Right Honourable the information [illegible] received later, that, since the Prince of Hohenlohe has let the estates of Trąbczyn with appurtenances [uncertain: deteriorate] and has burdened them with debts, the mortgage creditors run the risk of losing their capital, leaving it to your discretion to bring this circumstance into the discussion, should you support the Prince's claim for compensation. At the same time I also most respectfully request [uncertain: Your] Right Honourable, in case His Majesty the Emperor should deign to grant the Prince some compensation, to send word, so that the said creditors may be moved to pursue their rights further.

Berlin, 22 June 1816.

Ministry of Foreign Affairs, 3rd Section.

Hoffm[ann].

B[alan].

1 Jul[illegible]
