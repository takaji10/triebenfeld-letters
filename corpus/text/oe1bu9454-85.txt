--- SUMMARY (English) ---
King Friedrich Wilhelm refuses, in a copy of a royal letter, the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen's proposal of 29 February to exchange his South Prussian estates for the domain offices of Krotoszyn and Polajewo: on the minister von Voss's report, Krotoszyn alone yields 49,043 Rthl, so the State would lose some 17,400 Rthl a year. He notes Councillor of War von Triebenfeld received 26,000 Rthl in 1795 for surrendering the lease, and that he had already ordered such proposals rejected.

High-born Prince!

Friendly dear [uncertain: Geim]!

From the report of the State Minister v. [uncertain: Miricht] v. Voß, come in this day upon Your Lordship's obliging letter of the 29th of February of this year, wherein you propose to me the exchange of your South Prussian estates against the domain offices Krotoszyn and Polajewo, I have seen that the office of Krotoszyn alone already now, if only those appurtenances are added which were not brought into the valuation at the present leasing, yields a revenue of 49043 Rthl, which moreover, upon a new and correct valuation, can still be considerably increased, because the Councillor of War v. Triebenfeld received, merely for the surrender of the lease effected in the year 95, a compensation of 26,000 Rthl. I should therefore, without boasting of this advantage, and without taking into account that the superfluous woodland permits very considerable and advantageous clearings, already in the present state lose yearly 17400 Rthl against the stated yield of your estates and the capital sums to be paid in upon them. Given this [uncertain: length] of the matter, which has certainly been as little known to Your Lordship as to me, but which is confirmed by the fact that even your estates, valued at a yearly yield of only 650 Rthl,

estates, after roughly 26,000 [text lost] were expended thereon for improvements, already now yield 40,500 Rthl of yearly income, you will yourself perceive that the State cannot enter at all upon the proposed exchange. I had, [uncertain: instructed] by no example, already on the 19th of July of this year charged the State Minister v. Voß to reject all proposals of this kind out of hand. Accordingly it is out of [uncertain: sincere] high regard for Your Lordship that I have entered upon your later proposal, and only the evident proof of the disadvantage arising therefrom for the interest of the State restrains me from approving it. I am therefore also assured that you will not mistake herein the sentiments of the most sincere friendship and high regard with which I remain

Your Lordship's

well-disposed [uncertain: Grün]

Friedrich Wilhelm.

Berlin

the 4th of December

1798.

of Hohenlohe Ingelfingen
