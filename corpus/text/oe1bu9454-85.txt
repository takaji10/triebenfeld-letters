--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Copy of a royal rescript from Friedrich Wilhelm refusing the Prince's proposal, forwarded through Etats-Minister von Voß, to exchange his South-Prussian estates for the Domain Offices Krotoszyn and Polajewo. Krotoszyn alone yields 49,043 Reichsthaler and could yield more on fresh valuation, von Triebenfeld having received 26,000 for ceding the 1795 lease; the exchange would cost the State some 17,400 yearly. Voß had been instructed on 19 July to reject such offers outright.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Copy. High-born Prince! Kindly dear Cousin! From the report of the Minister of State von Voß, come in this day upon Your Beloved's obliging letter of the 29th of February of this year, wherein you propose to me the exchange of your South-Prussian estates in exchange for the Domain Offices Krotoszyn and Polajewo, I have seen that the Office of Krotoszyn alone, even now, if only the appurtenances not brought into the valuation at the present leasing be added, yields a a revenue of 49043 Reichl., which moreover, upon a new and correct valuation, may still be considerably increased, because the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld has received, merely for the cession of the lease effected in the year 95, a [uncertain: benen] settlement of 26,000 Richt. I should therefore, without boasting of this advantage, and without taking into consideration that the superfluous woodland admits of very substantial and advantageous clearings, lose yearly, already according to the present state of things, 17400 Rich. against the stated yield of your estates and the capital sums to be advanced upon them. In this length of the matter, which has certainly been as little known to Your Beloved as to me, but which is confirmed by this, that even your estates, valued at only a yearly yield of §. 650 Rthl,

estates, after about 26,000 Rthl has been expended upon them in improvements, already now yield 40,500 Rthl of annual income, you will yourself perceive that the State cannot possibly enter upon the proposed exchange. I had, [text lost] taught by [illegible] example, already under the 19th of July of this year instructed the Etats-Minister von Voß to refuse all such proposals out of hand. Accordingly, it is out of true high regard for Your Beloved that I have entered upon your later proposal, and it is only the evident proof of the disadvantage arising therefrom for the interest of the State that withholds me from approving it. I am therefore also assured that in this you will not mistake the sentiments of the most sincere friendship and high regard with which I remain Your Beloved's well-disposed friend Friedrich Wilhelm. Berlin, the 4th of December 1798. To My Cousin the Prince of Hohenlohe Ingelfingen at Breslau
