--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Der König lehnt den vom Fürsten beantragten Tausch seiner südpreußischen Güter gegen die Domänenämter Krotoszyn und Polajewo ab. Laut Bericht des Ministers v. Voss könne Krotoszyn allein schon 49043 Rthl Revenue gewähren, wenn die bei der jetzigen Verpachtung nicht veranschlagten Pertinenzien hinzukommen; v. Triebenfeld habe für die Abtretung der Pacht einen Abstand von 26,000 Rthl erhalten. Der Staat verlöre jährlich 17400 Rthl. Die auf 8650 Rthl veranschlagten Güter des Fürsten brächten nach etwa 26,000 Rthl Meliorationen jetzt 40,500 Rthl.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The King refuses the exchange, applied for by the Prince, of his South Prussian estates for the domain offices of Krotoszyn and Polajewo. According to the report of Minister v. Voss, Krotoszyn alone could yield 49043 Rthl in revenue once the appurtenances not valued in the present lease are added; he says v. Triebenfeld received 26,000 Rthl in compensation for giving up the lease. The state would lose 17400 Rthl a year. The Prince's estates, valued at 8650 Rthl, now bring in 40,500 Rthl after improvements of about 26,000 Rthl.

High-born Prince!

Kindly beloved Uncle!

From the report of the Minister of State v. Voss, received this day upon Your Lordship's obliging letter of the 29th of February of this year, in which you propose to me the exchange of your South Prussian estates for the domain offices of Krotoszyn and Polajewo, I have perceived that the office of Krotoszyn alone, already now, if only those appurtenances be added which were not brought into the valuation at the present leasing, affords a a revenue of 49043 Reichsthaler, which however upon a new and correct valuation may be raised still considerably, because the Councillor of War v. Triebenfeld received only the relinquishment of the lease, effected in the year 95, a [uncertain: benen] compensation of 26,000 Reichsthaler. I should therefore, without boasting of this advantage, and without taking into account that the superabundant woodland admits of very considerable and advantageous clearances, already according to the present condition lose 17400 Reichsthaler yearly as against the stated yield of your estates and the capital sums to be advanced upon them. In this situation of the matter, which has assuredly been as little known to Your Lordship as to me, but which is confirmed by the fact that even your estates, valued at a yearly yield of only 8650 Rthl.,

estates, after approximately 26,000 Reichsthaler had been expended therein upon improvements, now already afford a yearly yield of 40,500 Rthl., you will yourself perceive that the State can by no means enter into the proposed exchange. I had, instructed by [uncertain: durch ich nicht] example, already under the 19th of July of this year directed the Minister of State v. Voß to reject all such proposals out of hand. Accordingly it is out of [uncertain: wehren] high esteem for Your Lordship that I have entered upon your later proposal, and only the evident proof of the disadvantage arising therefrom for the interest of the State holds me back from approving it. I am therefore also assured that you will not mistake herein the sentiments of the most sincere friendship and high esteem with which I remain

Your Lordship's

Berlin

1798.

the 4th of December

[uncertain: gutwilliger Grün]

Friedrich Wilhelm.

To my Cousin the Prince

of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen

at Wrocław
