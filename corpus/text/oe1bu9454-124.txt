--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Encloses a draft reply to Kunckel for the Fürst's approval, and asks that fair copies of the two letters be made by another hand: Triebenfeld is "as though crushed", his wife being buried that day, and he has withdrawn with the children to his brother-in-law's, promising a fuller exposition once he has collected himself. Notes that he has to hand a map of Kopoyno, Skokum and Swiątniki.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Your Most Serene Highness, I herewith submit the reply to the aforesaid Kunckel; may Your Highness be pleased to look it over, whether it accords with Your Highness's will and intention. At the same time I beg that someone be permitted to make fair copies of the two letters, for I am as though crushed, and, since my good wife is being laid to rest today, I have withdrawn with the children to my brother-in-law's. In my letter which I send along with Kunkel's, I shall set everything out thoroughly still — I must first try to collect myself somewhat. A map of Kopoyno Skokum and Swiątniki I have here. v Triebenfeld. Breslau, the 24th of April 1804. To His Most Serene Highness.
