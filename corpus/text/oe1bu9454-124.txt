überreiche ich anliegend die Beantwortung

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Encloses a draft reply to Kunckel for the Prince's approval, asking that fair copies of the two letters be made by someone else: the writer is crushed by grief, his wife being buried that day, and he has withdrawn with the children to his brother-in-law's house. He promises to set out the whole matter thoroughly in his own accompanying letter once he has collected himself, and notes that he holds a map of Kopojno, Skokum and Swiątniki.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
To Your Most Serene Highness I herewith submit the reply to the aforesaid Kunckel; may Your Highness be pleased to look it over as to whether it accords with Your Highness's will and meaning. At the same time I beg that someone be permitted to make fair copies of the two letters, for I am as though crushed, and, since my good wife is being laid to rest today, I have withdrawn with the children to my brother-in-law's.

In my letter, which I am sending along with Kunkel's, I shall set out everything thoroughly once more — I must first seek to collect myself somewhat. A map of Kopojno, Skokum and Swiątniki I have here. v Triebenfeld

Breslau, 24th April 1804

To His Most Serene

Highness
