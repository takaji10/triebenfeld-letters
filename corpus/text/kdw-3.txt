# 1243-05-04  Gniezno

Dokument Nr 238
Premisl dux Polonie 1243 Mai. 4, in ecclesia hospitalis in Gnezna; tradit una cum fratre suo duce Boleslao hospitale in Gniezno, amplissime bonis dotatum, sub regimen sancti Sepulchri domus Miechoviensis.
Poznań. Königl. Staats Arch. Fascicul. Mączniki, B. IV. (Kopia z XVIII w. sporządzona z oryginału opatrzonego pieczęcią) - Nakielski, Miechovia.
In nomine sancte et individue Trinitatis amen. Cunctis liquet mortalibus qua volubilitate temporum iagtantur, quod longi tractus temporis pariunt antiquitatem, antiquitas oblivionem, oblivio vero rerum actarum exinanicionem; quam ob rem necesse est ut res digna memorie scripto debeat commendari, quo canescens antiquitas crebrius valeat renovari. Ego igitur Premisl Dei gracia dux Polonie cum fratre meo minori Bolezlao et cum dilecta matre nostra domina Hadwiga notum facimus tam presentibus quam futuris presens scriptum intuentibus, quod ad honorem Dei, in memoriam et remedium animarum nostrarum, consencientibus venerabili patre nostro domino Fulcone archiepiscopo Gneznensi et suo Capitulo, consilio quoque baronum nostrorum, dedimus hospitale nostrum Gneznense sancto Sepulcro, sub regimine et gubernacione domini Henrici prepositi domus Mechoviensis suorumque successorum iure perpetuo possidendum: cui iam dictus prepositus per virum honestum et providum, rectorem industrium, tenebitur providere, ne elemosine predicto hospitali collate negligantur vel inutiliter dispensentur. Si vero talis, quod absit, magister esset, qui nec Deo nec domui sue utilis comprobaretur, ad nostram peticionem et domini archiepiscopi nec non et Capituli sui amoveatur: si tamen canonice convictus fuerit et comprobatus, etiam alius magister idoneus a preposito domus supra nominate substituatur, qui favorem et honorem quantum Deus permiserit sciat conservare. In hospitali autem superius memorato, sicut iam diximus ob remedium animarum predecessorum successorumque nostrorum et nostrarum, cum in statum bonum fuerit restauratum, infirmi cuncti elemosinarum indigentes, non raptores, non violenti, debent pro facultate domus recreari. Aliqui eciam scolares pauperes ibidem tenebuntur confoveri in expensis, non in vestimentis, ex voluntate magistri hospitalis. Et quia peccatis nostris exigentibus, modernis in temporibus frigescente charitatis in terris igniculo, plures inveniuntur invasores domorum spiritualium quam adiutores seu defensores, nos in his que ad laicalem manum pertinent, dominus autem archiepiscopus in spiritualibus cum suo Capitulo, tanquam veri heredes volumus esse defensores. Actum anno gracie M.CC.XLIII in ecclesia predicti hospitalis in Gnezna in die beati Floriani, presentibus hiis testibus: domino Iohanne decano, Christiano custode, Andrea, Thoma, Stephano cancellario ducis, Vincencio et Petro fratre eius, Isaia et Petro canonicis Gneznensibus et pluribus aliis canonicis et vicariis, et hiis baronibus: Bogumilo, Dirsicraio, palatinis Polonie; Cecirado castellano Gneznensi, Thoma castellano Poznaniensi, Domarato iudice et aliis pluribus militibus; fratribus Predicatoribus Georgio et Petro, Artungo, quibus mediantibus hec facta dignoscuntur. Hee autem sunt ville hospitalis istius: Zdechova cum suis ascriptis, Leccovo, Chemelino, Pawlowo cum lacu nomine Prenevo, et Preseca, Cymellowo, Ugasd, taberne in Clech, et Ielemno cum pleno lacu et molendino et cum castoribus libere capiendi, Stasin eciam cum lacu nomine Cubuchowo et cum suis omnibus utilitatibus, Lozine, Conicowo, et molendinum et pomerium et unum agrum circa pomerium et circa hospitale. Hec sunt ville que excepte sunt in unam viciniam, que libere sunt: Kurrino, Preseka, Iezesani, Zdechowa, Ugasd: ita quod venatores cuiuslibet officii non recipiantur, nec canes nostros pascant, neque podwodam dent, ab omni expedicione et castrorum municione, et ab omnibus aliis exaccionibus et solucionibus eas totaliter reddimus absolutas.
Hospitale Scti Sepulcri, tak nazw. Bożogrobców. Mechoviensis, de Miechów. Zdechova, Zdziechowa. Leccovo, Lachowo. Chemelino, Imielno. Pawlowo, Pawłowo. Preseca, Przysieka. Cymellowo, Cielimowo. Ugasd, Ujazd. Clech, Kłeck. Ielemno cum pleno lacu et molendino, niegdyś pewnie osada nad rz. Wełna w pobliżu miasta Kłecko, dziś znikła. Stasin, Lozine, nie odgadnione. Conicowo, Konikowo. Kurrino, Koreczno. Iezesani, Jezierzany.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Herzog Przemysł von Polen gibt mit seinem Bruder Bolesław und ihrer Mutter Jadwiga das Spital von Gnesen den Kanonikern vom Heiligen Grab in Miechów unter ihrem Propst Heinrich, mit Zustimmung des Erzbischofs Fulko; das Spital soll Kranke und arme Schüler versorgen. Die Dörfer des Spitals werden aufgezählt und von Lasten befreit. Gnesen, in der Spitalkirche, 4. Mai 1243. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski I, Nr. 238. Keines der Güter um Trąbczyn kommt darin vor.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Duke Przemysł of Poland, with his brother Bolesław and their mother Jadwiga, gives the hospital of Gniezno to the canons of the Holy Sepulchre at Miechów under their provost Henryk, with the consent of Archbishop Fulko; the hospital is to care for the sick and keep poor scholars. The hospital's villages are listed and freed of dues. Gniezno, in the hospital church, 4 May 1243. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski I, no. 238. None of the estates around Trąbczyn occurs in it.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 238

Przemysł, Duke of Poland, 4 May 1,243, in the church of the hospital at Gniezno; together with his brother Duke Bolesław hands over the hospital at Gniezno, most amply endowed with goods, to the rule of the Holy Sepulchre of the house of Miechów.

Poznań. Royal State Archive. Fasciculus Mączniki, B. IV. (A copy made in the 18th century from the original, which bore a seal.) Nakielski, Miechovia.

In the name of the holy and undivided Trinity, amen. It is plain to all mortals how they are tossed by the turning of the times: that a long stretch of time gives birth to age, age to forgetting, and forgetting to the undoing of things done; for which reason it is needful that a thing worthy of memory be committed to writing, so that the greying past may be the more often renewed. I therefore, Przemysł, by the grace of God Duke of Poland, with my younger brother Bolesław and with our beloved mother Lady Jadwiga, make known to those present and to come who look on the present writing that, to the honour of God, in memory and for the remedy of our souls, with the consent of our venerable father Lord Fulko, Archbishop of Gniezno, and of his Chapter, and on the advice of our barons, we have given our hospital of Gniezno to the Holy Sepulchre, to be possessed by perpetual right under the rule and government of Lord Henryk, provost of the house of Miechów, and of his successors: for which the said provost shall be bound to provide through an honest and prudent man, a diligent rector, so that the alms given to the said hospital are not neglected or spent to no purpose. But if there should be, which God forbid, such a master as proved useful neither to God nor to his house, he shall be removed at our request and that of the Lord Archbishop and of his Chapter, if he has been canonically convicted and proved so, and another fit master shall be put in his place by the provost of the house named above, who will know how to keep favour and honour as far as God allows. In the hospital named above, as we have said, for the remedy of the souls of our forebears and successors and of our own, once it has been restored to a good state, all the sick in need of alms, not robbers, not men of violence, are to be cared for as far as the house can. Some poor scholars too shall be kept there at its cost, in food, not in clothing, at the will of the master of the hospital. And because, as our sins deserve, in these modern times when the little fire of charity grows cold on earth, there are found more invaders of spiritual houses than helpers or defenders, we wish to be true heirs and defenders in what belongs to the lay hand, and the Lord Archbishop with his Chapter in spiritual things. Done in the year of grace 1,243 in the church of the said hospital at Gniezno on the feast of St Florian, in the presence of these witnesses: Lord Jan the dean, Krystian the custodian, Andrzej, Tomasz, Stefan the duke's chancellor, Wincenty and his brother Piotr, Izajasz and Piotr, canons of Gniezno, and many other canons and vicars; and these barons: Bogumił and Dzierżykraj, palatines of Poland; Cieciert, castellan of Gniezno; Tomasz, castellan of Poznań; Domarat the judge, and many other knights; the Dominican friars Jerzy and Piotr, and Artung, by whose mediation this is known to have been done. These are the villages of this hospital: Zdziechowa with those assigned to it, Lachowo, Imielno, Pawłowo with the lake called Prenevo, and Przysieka, Cielimowo, Ujazd, inns at Kłecko, and Ielemno with the whole lake and a mill and with beavers to be taken freely, Stasin too with the lake called Cubuchowo and with all its uses, Lozine, Konikowo, and a mill and an orchard and a field by the orchard and by the hospital. These are the villages excepted into one neighbourhood, which are free: Koreczno, Przysieka, Jezierzany, Zdziechowa, Ujazd; so that the huntsmen of any office are not to be received there, nor are they to feed our dogs or give carts, and we make them wholly free of every military expedition and the building of castles and of all other exactions and payments.

Hospitale Scti Sepulcri, the so-called Bożogrobcy (canons of the Holy Sepulchre). Mechoviensis, of Miechów. Zdechova, Zdziechowa. Leccovo, Lachowo. Chemelino, Imielno. Pawlowo, Pawłowo. Preseca, Przysieka. Cymellowo, Cielimowo. Ugasd, Ujazd. Clech, Kłeck. Ielemno cum pleno lacu et molendino, once probably a settlement on the river Wełna near the town of Kłecko, today vanished. Stasin, Lozine, not identified. Conicowo, Konikowo. Kurrino, Koreczno. Iezesani, Jezierzany.
