--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Protest von Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Erbherr von Łukomia und Myszakowo, gegen Jakub Górski, Grenzkämmerer von Inowrocław, vom 15. Juni 1782, von Chełmski unterschrieben. Vollziehende Ämter hätten Dekrete des Krontribunals zu vollziehen, nicht auszulegen. Górski habe beim Ortstermin auf dem Grund von Trąbczyn die Regel des Tribunalsdekrets überschritten, sei vom Feldamt des Landes Dobrzyń abgewichen und habe sich in die Auslegung des Dekrets eingelassen. Er habe Chełmski beschrieben, als hätte dieser sich widersetzt, seine Amtshandlung ohne Zustimmung der Parteien auf unbestimmte Zeit vertagt und seine Akten wegen militärischer Hilfe an das Kriegsdepartement des Immerwährenden Rates geschickt, statt die Sache an das Tribunal zu verweisen. Chełmski erklärt Górskis Handlung für nichtig. Darunter ein Vermerk vom 16. August: Piotr Gosławski, Bevollmächtigter Prusimskis, hat vor Zeugen wegen des Platzes nachgefragt, für den keine Angaben und keine Abschrift geliefert worden waren.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A protest of Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, heir of Łukomia and Myszakowo, against Jakub Górski, boundary chamberlain of Inowrocław, of 15 June 1782, signed by Chełmski. Executive offices, he says, are to carry out the decrees of the Crown Tribunal, not to interpret them. At the sitting on the ground of Trąbczyn Górski went beyond the rule of the Tribunal's decree, departed from the field office of the land of Dobrzyń, and took upon himself to interpret the decree. He described Chełmski as though he had resisted, put off his proceedings without the parties' consent to an unnamed time, and sent his acts to the War Department of the Permanent Council for military help, instead of remitting the cause to the Tribunal. Chełmski declares Górski's act null. Under it is a note of 16 August: Piotr Gosławski, Prusimski's plenipotentiary, inquired before witnesses about the space, for which no information and no copy had been supplied.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Done in Konin, on Saturday, on the very day of the Feast of Saints Vitus and Modestus, Martyrs, namely the 15th of June, in the Year of the Lord 1782.

The Illustrious Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, against the Illustrious Gorski, Boundary Surveyor of Inowrocław — a Manifest is lodged

Having come in person to the present Office and Records of the Konin Municipal Court, the Illustrious Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Heir of the estates of Łukomia [and] Myszakowo with appurtenances, safeguarding the integrity of his estates and substance — lodges this Manifest in the present Records, on account of the matters below-written, against the Illustrious Jakub Gorski, Boundary Surveyor of Inowrocław — and this for the following reason: Because the same Illustrious Jakub Gorski, presuming that everything is permitted to his Office, as is apparent from his Field Acts oblated to the present Records — although he knew that Executive Offices executing each individual Decree of the Most Serene Crown Tribunal are obligated not to interpret but to execute, by the chain of laws and especially the Constitution of the Year 1768 — but the same [Gorski], exceeding the prescribed rule of the Condescension for Field Courts and Offices in the settlement of the estate of Trąbczyn as prescribed by the Decree of the Most Serene Crown Tribunal, and inconveniently diverging from the Field Office likewise of the Dobrzyń Land — in the execution not only of the Tribunal Decree but also of the Acts of the Gniezno Municipal Court emanating from the same and similarly oblated to the present Records — thrust himself into [the matter of] the evacuation of the Olędry of the Illustrious Magnificent Prusimski;

and with his illegal object circumscribed the Manifesting party as if [Chełmski] had placed himself rebelliously at the field act; and nullly and unduly, contrary to the meaning of the Statute and without the consenting parties, postponed his Act until some unknown time; declaring, beyond the presumed scope of the above-mentioned Tribunal Decree and by his own private notifications, that he would descend to the site again — instead of [properly] remitting the matter to the Crown Tribunal on account of the impossibility [of execution] according to the Line on the Map, which is incompatible with the actual site locations, made by the Most Serene Tribunal — without accounting for the Olędry adjudicated to [Chełmski] and the Manifesting party; and for military forces remitted his Acts to the Military Department of the Permanent Council — and thereby also transgressed against [the sanctions of] the Tribunal, and in his [further] proceedings greatly injured the Manifesting party, and caused litigation expenses.

Therefore in fact the Manifesting party, on account of all the above-mentioned matters, against the same [Gorski], regarding the nullity of his Act, in the most solemn legal form, manifests — offering to pursue [the matter] with the same [Gorski] regarding the actually conducted Office, for penalties and the nullity of the inserted Act, by law — and to vindicate by law, through legal mediation, the harm and injuries caused to him, where it shall come by right — the present Manifest's improvement, diminution, or augmentation to be made as the business opportunity requires, reserved to himself with full force, and the right guarded.

Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski

At the requisition of the party, namely the Honourable Piotr Gosławski, as the bonded Plenipotentiary of the Illustrious Magnificent Antoni of Kolno Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, acting in the name of the same [person as] his Principal — in the presence of the Honourables Tomasz Reczkowski and Świętosław Bogusławski, and also the Honest Mateusz Matuszkiewicz of Szetlewek, General Royal Process Server — for this interval, on account of information not given for enrollment and the copy not submitted — the requisition was made on the 16th day of the month of August.

Petrus Gosławski

Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski
