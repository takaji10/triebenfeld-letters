--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Protest vom 7. Dezember 1782, auf Polnisch, von Leon Maszewski, Verwalter von Trąbczyn, auf Befehl seines Herrn Antoni Prusimski von Kolno, Starost von Niszczewice, der selbst nicht vor den Büchern erscheinen konnte, gegen Stanisław Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa, dessen Diener Jaroszewski und andere; Maszewski hat im Namen seines Herrn unterschrieben. Chełmski wisse aus den Dekreten des Tribunals und des Ortstermins dieses Jahres, dass die Linie von der Ecke von Trąbczyn, Łukom und Drzewce bis zur Ecke mit Biskupice, vom Tribunal in die Karte gezogen und vom Geometer in Chełmskis Gegenwart im Gelände ausgehauen und abgepflockt, Trąbczyn von Łukomia scheidet. Dennoch habe er seine Leute aus Łomów und Łukom, die der Protest mit Namen nennt, mit Schlitten, Pferden, Ochsen und Äxten in den Kiefernwald links der Linie geschickt, der zu Trąbczyn gehört; sie hätten eine große Zahl Kiefern gefällt und nach Łukomia gebracht. Chełmskis Leute seien außerdem in Trąbczyn eingefallen, hätten Leute aus Trąbczyn auf der Straße angegriffen und andere Gewalttaten verübt.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A protest of 7 December 1782, in Polish, by Leon Maszewski, steward of Trąbczyn, at the order of his lord Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice, who could not come before the books himself, against Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, his servant Jaroszewski and others; Maszewski signed in his lord's name. Chełmski knows from the decrees of the Tribunal and of this year's sitting on the ground that the line from the corner of Trąbczyn, Łukom and Drzewce to the corner with Biskupice, drawn on the map by the Tribunal and cut and staked on the ground by the surveyor in Chełmski's own presence, divides Trąbczyn from Łukomia. Yet he sent his people from Łomów and Łukom, whom the protest names, with sledges, horses, oxen and axes into the pine wood on the left of the line, which belongs to Trąbczyn; they felled a great number of pines and took them to Łukomia. Chełmski's people also rode into Trąbczyn, attacked people of Trąbczyn on the road and committed other acts of violence.

The Honourable Maszewski, in the name of the Illustrious Prusimski, against the Illustrious Chełmski — a Manifest is lodged

Having come in person to the present Office and Books of the Konin Municipal Court, the Honourable Leon Maszewski, Estate Manager of the hereditary estates of Trąbczyn with appurtenances of His Worship the Right Honourable Antoni of Kolno Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — by the order of the same, the Most Honourable, his Lord, who at this time [W/U?x?ią[uncertain: g]

was unable to attend to official matters — solemnly lodges a Complaint against the Right Honourable Pan Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of the Wschowa Land, Lord and Heir of the estates of Łukomia and others, and also the Honourable Jaroszewski, [uncertain: s]ervant of his, and other perpetrators of the below-described and above-mentioned actions — in the following manner: that the Right Honourable Pan Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, although sufficiently informed by the Tribunal Decrees — first the post-commissorial [decree], the second ordering the execution of that same post-commissorial, and also the field [acts] that followed in the present year — that the Line from the corner markers of Trąbczyn, Łukom, and Drzewce to the corner of those same estates with Biskupice, drawn on the Map by the Most Serene Tribunal, and on the ground [...] and cleared by the Geometer in the presence of the Right Honourable Pan Chełmski himself, Treasurer of Wschowa, according to the prescription of the Tribunal Decree during the condescension, and marked with stakes — separates the [estates] of Trąbczyn from the [estates] of Łukomia — nevertheless, transgressing not only the above-mentioned Decrees but also the Law established in the Year One Thousand Seven Hundred and Sixty-Eight, the same Right Honourable Pan Chełmski, already after the last Tribunal Decree, in the very [Sam...z?iem] of the two condescensions commencing by its force, [sent] his people, namely: of the Honourables: Ignacy Jaroszewski, Weynerski Clerk; of the Honest and of the Industrious: Stanisław Augustyński, half-farmstead holder from Łomów; Woysko, cottager; Michał Płachetka; Ignacy Korzalik; Szymon Wyterek; Jan Wyterek; Łukasz Wyterek; Antoni Waziak; Wawrzyn also called Old Wach, father of [Antoni] Wasiak; Piotr Motylek; Wrzycka, wool-carder; Grzegorz Nowaczyk; Franciszek Wyterek; Stanisław Wyterek and Jakub, cottagers; Antoni Nowaczyk; Paweł Wałęsa; Kazimierz Płocha; Stanisław Wyterek and Wojciech, half-farmstead holder from the village of Łomów; Andrzej and Szymon, farmhands; Wojciech Forczpan; Jan the Watchman from Łukom; and other some fifteen with [Torami] from the wastelands belonging to the estates of Łukom — with sleighs, [uncertain: horses], oxen, axes, and other tools — fell upon the pine forest on the left side of the line directly cleared, belonging to the estates of Trąbczyn, who, having fallen upon [it], cut down a great number of pines and carried them to the estates of Łukomia — as the judicial site inspection made about this testifies — and moreover, [uncertain: na] the same people of the Right Honourable Pan Chełmski invaded the [lands of the] Trąbczyn estate, broke into the manor domain and [uncertain: u/o]

violently took away [goods/things], accosted people from Trąbczyn on the road, and committed other acts of violence as evidenced by testimony. In which matter, the Right Honourable Starost of Niszczewice being greatly wronged, [he] ordered the present Complaint to be lodged as a Manifest in the present Books — the right of improvement of this manifest being reserved.

Leon Maszewski, in the name of my lord
