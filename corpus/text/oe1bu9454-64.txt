--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports the Queen's death at Strelitz and the King's illness, declares the Collorina affair void, and urges recovery of 100,000 Rthl and the papers held by Köckritz. Encloses a copy of a scheme for exchanging the Zagorowo estates against von Knobelsdorff's Bischdorff and Mollna, with valuations, Landschaft assessments and claims by Weigel (190,000 Rthl) and Stössel (180,000 Rthl). A postscript records Chancellor Hardenberg's promise of help over 112,000 Rthl and begs repeatedly for 300 Rthl in gold.
