--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Triebenfeld meldet den Tod der Königin zu Strelitz und die schwere Krankheit des Königs. Die Collonnasche Sache sei nun ein Unding, der Fürst solle seine 100000 Rthl sichern. Wegen der Zinsen des Invaliden Capitals droht ihm die Sequestration seiner Güter. Beigefügt ist ein Vorschlag an einen Freund: Knobelsdorffs Güter Biskupice und Molna zu 700000 Rthl gegen die Zagórówer Güter und Quittierung der Forderungen Weigelts und Stössels. Im Nachtrag bittet Triebenfeld dringend um 300 Rthl in Gold.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Triebenfeld reports the death of the Queen at Strelitz and the King's serious illness. He says the Collonna affair is now an absurdity and the Prince should secure his 100000 Rthl. Because of the interest on the invalids' capital, he says sequestration of his estates is threatened. Enclosed is a proposal to a friend: Knobelsdorff's estates Biskupice and Molna at 700000 Rthl in exchange for the Zagórów estates and the discharge of the claims of Weigelt and Stössel. In a postscript Triebenfeld urgently asks for 300 Rthl in Gold.

64 This very moment I receive from friend F. in Berlin the news that the Queen died at Strelitz on the 19th, early, at 9 o'clock; also that the King is very ill, and that the most grievous consequences are feared. Although I offered to send these matters to Exp. [and] recommend all haste, Your Serene Highness will nevertheless probably have the news sooner. Copy

Dear honest friend, herewith I convey to you the valuations, and a copy of the Landschaft assessment of the Biskupice and Molna estates. The latter are, as the figure shows me, sound, so that anyone must find the demand of Mr von Knobelsdorff fair. The debts intabulated upon the estates consist of mortgage bonds and small mortgages. Together they amount to 330000 rt, and since only 75000 rt are callable after the expiry of the moratorium, they can, with your means, my upright friend, [not] become burdensome to you. Everything depends on how you are able to handle Mr von Stössel. He is hard pressed and, as I know, is offering his papers at 50 per cent; I believe you should buy the whole 180000 rt from him for 90/m rt, whereby he must still concede sound terms of payment. To draw this gentleman in is no sin, for he treated the good Prince, both of them, abominably in the Koszęcin transaction. Meanwhile I leave that to your wisdom.

For the rest, the matter now stands thus: Biskupice yields, according to the Landschaft assessment, annually 20462 rt 12 gg; Molna 10951 rt 18 gg. The royal yield is slight; the [uncertain: Schauß] taken at only 12; hence the 60000 rt can very properly be pledged, which makes a further 3000 rt. Consequently annual revenue 34414 rt.

The interest amounts, to the Landschaft, at 4 1/4 per cent = 10264 rt; to the mortgage creditors = 3750 rt; to Stössel, whose 100000 capital I still assume after all = 5000 rt; for Mr Weigelt's, Councillor [C. R. R. N.], capital of 190000 rt = 9500 rt; = 28514 rt; and accordingly there already remains to you, dear friend, a surplus of 5900 rt. With your interest, which after all derives from interest upon interest and God knows what, you have, dear old friend, when you are called hereditary lord and lord of the court of the above-named lordships, an annual clear revenue of 16000 rt — and what will you, as a rich man, not further make of the ironworks?

I wish you all good fortune and success therein, and another twenty years of life besides, in which I shall often rejoice to have been the intermediary who served you and the good Prince in a sound manner. With particular respect, etc.

v. Triebenfeld
