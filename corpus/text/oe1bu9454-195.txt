--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Awaiting Count Strachwitz, expected in Breslau on the 23rd, before proceeding; reports a vexatious interview with Herr Flesch, who rails at Goldstücker for naming the Count and urges secrecy, suggesting a swindle and an unknown lawsuit attached to the business. The properties are said to be the Oderish estates near Leobschütz, valued at 900000 Rthl., Zackraw excepted. Adds rumours of war with France, Austria and the Turks, French contribution demands, Hardenberg's return, Prince Wilhelm's popularity, Koloma's testament given to Legation Councillor Henckel, and a two-year extension of the moratorium.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

At this moment I can report nothing of consequence to Your Most Serene Highness, because Count Strachwitz is not yet here.

Only today did I manage, with vexation and displeasure, to get to speak with Mr. Flesch. He does nothing but rail at Goldstücker for treating the matter as so easy a thing, and for having named the Count outright - He urges all secrecy and caution - Why, I do not know - To speak frankly, it seems that a swindle underlies it all, and so far as I can tell there is a lawsuit, which I do not know of, that is to be settled along with the business.

For the rest, I must await Count Strachwitz here - he is expected to arrive in Breslau on the 23rd - so as to be able to take further measures in the matter. So much I now know, that the estates in question are said to be the Oder estates situated near Leobschütz, and that their value is given as 900000 Rthl.

Zackraw does not belong to them; the Count means to keep that for himself - Count Steine it is not, for that belongs to the younger Strachwitz. Today, if my fever abates, I shall speak with Mr. von Massow. Meanwhile I know this much, that Massow is not to blame for the purloined lawsuit etc. Mr. Michel has made the outcry. My Mr. Schade, who is his day-and-bosom friend, is working on him today.

I shall most obediently report the results - the war between France, Austria and the Turks is here held for a settled certainty, and just as much is a breach with Russia believed in.

Our good King is in a favourable strait. He is absolutely required to pay the contribution quickly, or the French will move into our states - Count Schneidniz arrived here from Paris the day before yesterday with this news. Others will have it that Napoleon insists on an accession to the war against the Turks, for which we are to furnish 20000 men forthwith, and against which he will let ten million etc. of contribution be dropped.

Hardenberg is coming forward again, so they say here, and will play a great role - Dohna [uncertain: Dąmbska] will probably go out - He has already left over 4000 matters lying overturned; everything is stirred up against him - the whole system seems to be tottering; the little Prince out of, of, and in the mire is more hated here than

9. There is an ardent wish for Your Serene Highness's return. Prince Wilhelm has won all hearts and is idolised. For the rest, my Whode recounted here yesterday in the presence of several persons that the late Koloma is said to have given the testament in question to the councillor of legation Henckel - but since this is not a man of law, it has no validity; had he handed it over to Fenckenstein, then it would have had the proper form - I could not question him about it at the moment - today I shall hear whence he has this -

All letters and things I have delivered.

In deepest reverence I remain, until death,

Your Most Serene Highness's

the moratorium has been extended

by 2 years

Breslau, the 22nd of May

1810.

faithful most humble and obedient servant

v. Triebenfeld
