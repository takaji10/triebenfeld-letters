--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports that Herr von Knobelsdorff has visited and inspected the place, is pleased with it, and is judged an entirely truthful man; Triebenfeld is meanwhile trying to put the accounts and affairs into order so the whole can be seen at a glance, complaining of great confusion and stubborn people. The Trąbczyn lawsuit, with a hearing pending since the 24th, may take another fortnight before the tribunal's decree; winning it would end all distress. Asks to be left sole direction.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

Herr von Knobelsdorff was here and has satisfied himself as to the place. It pleases him, and I am therefore endeavouring now to bring everything into good order, so as to arrange it all in such a way that the matter can be surveyed at a glance. The confusion is meanwhile very great, the people stubborn, who are heard from the Hawich; often I could go out of my mind. With God I hope to set everything to rights; the suit concerning Trąbczyn will come to a good end, the hearing date has already been pending since the 24th, and still the matter is not concluded, and it may easily be another 14 days before the decree is read out by the tribunal. If I win here, then all distress shall be at an end.

Herr von Knobelsdorff I hold to be an exceedingly upright man who states what he has only in accordance with the truth, and this shall also be done from this side (if Your Serene Highness leaves the direction to me alone [uncertain: to fetch]) - until my last breath I remain Your Serene Highness's

Klein Öhringen, the 29th of November

1810

faithful servant

v Triebenfeld
