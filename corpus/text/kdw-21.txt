Buda, 22 listopada 1440
Król Władysław III zapisuje 200 grzywien na wsiach Adamierz, Borzykowo i Wrąbczynek Stanisławowi Czarnemu.
Kop.: Warszawa, AGAD, Metryka koronna, t. 109, s. 8-9. Oblata 27 kwietnia 1570 (wg Or.).
Wzmianka: MRPS V, nr 4154 (wg Kop.).
Wladislaus Dei gratia Hungariae, Poloniae, Dalmaciae, Croatiae etc. rex Lithuaniaeque princeps supremus et haeres Russiae etc. significamus tenore praesentium, quibus expedit universis, quod nos consideratis fidelibus servitiis dilecti nobis nobilis Stanislai Czarni de Pietrzikow1 a diu nobis et praesertim in adap(ta)tione coronationis nostrae foelicis Regni huius nostri Hungariae fideliter et constanter exhibitis et in futurum per eum diligentius exhibendis, sibi horum gratia ducentas marcas numeri et monetae [s. 9] Polonicalium, XLVIII g(rossos) in marcam quamlibet computando, in et super villis nostris Niedomirz2, Borzikowo3 et Wrączino districtus Pizdrensis4, quas sibi de manibus nobilis Bartassiia Plomikowski5 liberare indulsimus, b-cdamus, donamus
inscribimusque praesentibus, largimur per ipsum cum omnibus utilitatibus, fructibus, censibus, redditibus, proventibus, agris, pratis, campis, sylvis, mericis, omnibus piscinis, piscaturis, aquis, molendinis, emolumentis, mellificiis, emolimentisd, aucupationibus, attinentiis et obventionibus universis habendas, tenedas, utifruendas et pacifice possidendas ita diu, quousque sibi vel posteris praefatae ducentae marcae per nos vel successores nostros ex integro persolvantur. Quibus exsolutis dictarum villarum possessio ad nos et successores nostros pleno iure devolvetur. Iuribus et stationibus nostris regalibus semper salvis. --- SUMMARY (German) ---
König Władysław III. schreibt Stanisław Czarny von Pietrzyków für seine Dienste bei der Krönung in Ungarn weitere 200 Mark auf die Dörfer Adamierz, Borzykowo und Wrąbczynek, die er aus der Hand des Bartosz Płomikowski auslösen darf. Buda, 22. November 1440. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski X, Nr. 1532, aus der Kronmetrik.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
King Władysław III charges a further 200 marks for Stanisław Czarny of Pietrzyków, for his services at the coronation in Hungary, on the villages of Adamierz, Borzykowo and Wrąbczynek, which he is allowed to redeem from the hands of Bartosz Płomikowski. Buda, 22 November 1440. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski X, no. 1532, from the Crown Register.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 1532

Buda, 22 November 1440

King Władysław III charges 200 marks on the villages of Adamierz, Borzykowo and Wrąbczynek in favour of Stanisław Czarny.

Copy: Warsaw, AGAD, Metryka koronna, vol. 109, pp. 8–9. Entered on 27 April 1570 (from the original).

Mention: MRPS V, no. 4154 (from the copy).

We, Władysław, by the grace of God King of Hungary, Poland, Dalmatia, Croatia, etc., supreme prince of Lithuania and heir of Ruthenia, etc., make known by the tenor of these presents to all whom it concerns that, having regard to the faithful services of the noble Stanisław Czarny of Pietrzyków¹, beloved by us, shown to us faithfully and constantly for a long time and especially in the preparation of the happy coronation of this our Kingdom of Hungary, and to be shown by him still more diligently in future, we give him for their sake two hundred marks of Polish reckoning and money [p. 9], reckoning 48 g(roschen) to each mark, in and upon our villages of Adamierz², Borzykowo³ and Wrąbczynek, of the district of Pyzdry⁴, which we have allowed him to free from the hands of the noble Bartosz Płomikowskiᵃ ⁵, ᵇ⁻ᶜwe give, donate

and inscribe by these presents, we bestow them on him with all the uses, fruits, rents, revenues, incomes, fields, meadows, plains, woods, heaths, all ponds, fisheries, waters, mills, emoluments, beehives, emolumentsᵈ, fowling, appurtenances and incomes whatsoever, to be had, held, used and peacefully possessed for so long until the said two hundred marks are paid to him or his descendants in full by us or our successors. When they are paid, the possession of the said villages shall return to us and our successors with full right. Our royal rights and hospitality dues always saved. In witness of these letters, to which our seal is hung below. Given at Buda on the Tuesday after the feast of St Elizabeth, in the year of the Lord 1440⁶.

The report of the generous Andrzej of Tęczyn⁷.
