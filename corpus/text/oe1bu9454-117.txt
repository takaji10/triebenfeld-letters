--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports progress on the 30,000 Rthl due to von Grotowski: Weigel, backed by a guarantee, confirms the money and interest lie ready with him, but the bank will not discount until Grotowski executes a deed of cession in court, a draft of which Court Councillor Ebersbach has prepared. Asks the Prince to forward Weigel's enclosed letter by express, and a further letter to Grotowski's agent at Kalisz halting the forced sale. Notes the deaths of Captain von Müllern and Morgenbesser, urges the Prince to spare his health, and passes on news of Nelson off Toulon.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

Herr Weigel, after I had first of all furnished him with a guarantee, is zealously endeavouring to smooth the matter over entirely. But until the rights have been ceded (jura cessa) by von Grotowski, the bank will not discount[.] I have therefore urged upon Herr Weigel that he should write at once to Herr von Grotowski and give him the assurance that the 30000 Rthl and the interest as well are lying with him. But that, before the monies could be paid, Herr von Grotowski must most speedily execute the instrument of cession for the 30000 in court there, after which the monies shall at once be remitted to him. In order now to fail in nothing here that may serve to bring the affair about most swiftly, I drove to the Court Councillor Ebersbach and had him draft the instrument as Grotowski must execute it, which Herr Weigel is despatching in the enclosed letter to von Grotowski for speedy judicial execution.

I hasten to convey this letter enclosed to Your Most Serene Highness [uncertain: derstest] by the orderly just now departing. May Your Highness be pleased to send it on directly to Herr von Grotowski by express, so that he may execute the instrument of cession, whereupon I shall then finalise the matter at once. The sudden death of the honest Captain von Müllern has shaken me deeply

He was truly a good man and a genuinely faithful adherent of Your Most Serene Highness.

But may Your Serene Highness be pleased to take this sudden death to heart – in these critical times think of Your own preservation, and do not storm too hard upon your health – such rapid marches make even the most iron-firm man falter — Morgenbeßer likewise died yesterday evening at 7 o'clock – Now, Most gracious Prince and Lord, I appeal to your heart[.] That for the rest the gallant Nelson caught the French as they were trying to slip away from Toulon and unite with the Brest fleet, took ships of 74 cannon together with many boats, and sank several, Your Most Serene Highness will doubtless already know. In Vienna this news has made a quite extraordinary sensation, the people rejoiced aloud, and it has been let to be noticed everywhere all too plainly that the Court and the nation take England's part. That [uncertain: Sidney] Smith burned Flushing and also the little fleet that was there is indeed reported from Vienna, yet it is said withal that the official notices on this are still wanting. With Bernhardi I have arranged everything according to Your Most Serene Highness's will – he wrote by yesterday's post to Herr Stösseln and is from now on honestly endeavouring to lead the water onto the wheel, so as to set the springs in motion whereby the good cause will swiftly be brought to its goal.

In deepest reverence I remain, until death,

Your Most Serene Highness's

Breslau

29. Febr. 1804. faithful and most obedient servant

v Triebenfeld

P.S. I have further prevailed upon Weigel – that he must approach Grotowski, so that Grotowski shall at once send along with it a letter to his mandatary at Kalisz – wherein he informs him that he is satisfied, and that the latter is to report this immediately to the Government and to tell it that there is now no further question of any [uncertain: compulsory auction]. If Grotowski sends this letter along, I shall send it on at once by express to Kalisz, so that before the 22nd of this month everything is settled.

In order to convince Your Most Serene Highness that everything has been arranged in the best way, Weigel's letter follows under flying seal – may Your Highness be pleased to close it and send it with all haste to Grotowski, so that he may swiftly execute the cession and send the instrument here. Until Weigel gave this letter and engaged himself therein that the monies already lie ready with him, I did not hand over the money – only now am I letting it be conveyed to him.
