# oe1bu9454-209  209  letter
# 1810-10-16  Breslau
# /documents/oe1bu9454/209/

Durchlauchtigster Fürst
gnädigster Fürst und Herr!
Die Anweisung ist eingegangen das Geld werde ich erheben und sofort
abreisen. Von Zagorowo aus melde ich Ewr Durchlaucht sogleich alles
durch einen reitenden Bothen der den H. v. Knobelsdorff dorthin bringen
soll. Die Post will fort ich muß schließen.
In tiefsten Respect verharren
Ewr Hochfürstliche Durchlaucht
unterthänigster Diener
v Triebenfeld
Breslau
16ten 8br.
1810.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A hurried note, written as the post was leaving: the payment order has arrived, and Triebenfeld will collect the money and depart immediately. He promises to report everything from Zagorowo at once by mounted messenger, who is also to bring Mr. von Knobelsdorff there. Nothing further is given on the sum involved or the purpose of the payment.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince, Most gracious Prince and Lord! The order to pay has come in; I shall collect the money and set out at once. From Zagorowo I shall report everything to Your Serene Highness immediately by a mounted messenger, who is to bring Mr. von Knobelsdorff thither. The post is about to go; I must close. In deepest reverence there remains Your Most Serene Highness's most humble and obedient servant, v. Triebenfeld

Breslau

16th October

1810.
