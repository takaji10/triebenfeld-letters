--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Bericht des Gerichtsboten Bartłomiej Szepczyński von Trąbczyn vom 15. April 1778. Er hat drei Ausfertigungen einer königlichen Ladung vor das Krontribunal in Petrikau zugestellt, ausgestellt in Petrikau am Montag nach Laetare 1778: an Stanisław Chełmski, an den Richter Franciszek Wierusz Walknowski, an den Unterrichter Stefan Zielonacki, zugleich Richter des Ortstermins, und an den Notar Xawery Mikorski. Auf Betreiben Prusimskis soll die Sache an das Tribunal verwiesen und das Dekret vom 20. Oktober des Vorjahres aufgehoben werden; sie lasse sich nicht entscheiden ohne die Urkunden, die Prusimski von den Erben der Witwe seines Onkels, Katarzyna Prusimska, geborene Rozdrażewska, zurückfordert. Erweise sich daraus die Forderung von Tracholz und Chełmski als unrechtmäßig, soll die Übertragung aufgehoben werden; ergebe sich vielmehr eine Schuld von Tracholz gegenüber dem Onkel, soll Chełmski sie samt Zinsen zahlen. Die Ausfertigungen wurden in Trąbczyn, in Konin im Haus des Bürgers Paulszewicz, wo die Kanzlei des Landgerichts ihre Akten hat, und im Gutshof von Łukom hinterlegt.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A report of the court messenger Bartłomiej Szepczyński of Trąbczyn of 15 April 1778. He has served three copies of a royal citation before the Crown Tribunal at Piotrków, dated at Piotrków on the Monday after Laetare Sunday 1778: on Stanisław Chełmski, on the judge Franciszek Wierusz Walknowski, on the sub-judge Stefan Zielonacki, who was also judge at the sitting on the ground, and on the notary Xawery Mikorski. At Prusimski's instance the cause is to be sent to the Tribunal and the decree of 20 October of the year before lifted; it cannot be decided, he says, without the papers that Prusimski is claiming back from the heirs of his uncle's widow, Katarzyna Prusimska, born Rozdrażewska. If the papers show the claim of Tracholz and Chełmski to be unlawful, the transfer is to be quashed; if they show instead a debt of Tracholz to the uncle, Chełmski is to pay it with interest. The copies were left at Trąbczyn, at Konin in the house of the townsman Paulszewicz, where the chancery of the land court keeps its records, and at the manor of Łukom.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
On behalf of the Honourable Prusimski against the Land Court and the Condescension Court Office — to the Tribunal — Relatio

To the office and present records of the Konin Municipal Court, appearing in person, the Honest Bartłomiej Szepczyński of the village of Trąbczyn, Royal Court Summoner, General, sound of health, acknowledged the authenticated citation letters, one and the same in substance, issued for the ordinary general sessions of the Crown Tribunal of the Realm at Piotrków as described below, of the following tenor:

Stanislaus Augustus, by the Grace of God King of Poland, Grand Duke of Lithuania, of Russia, of Prussia, of Masovia, of Samogitia, of Kuiavia, of Podolia, of Podlachia, of Livonia, of Smolensk, of Seversk, of Chernigov — to you, the Honourables: Stanisław Chełmski, hereditary owner of the estates of Łukomia; and Franciszek Wierusz Walknowski, Judge; Stefan Zielonacki, Deputy Judge and also judge at the condescension term of the cause described below; and Xawery Mikorski, Notary of the Kalisz Land Court — judges in the cause described below — regarding your persons and all claims and amounts of yours, we command that you appear in person and peremptorily before us or our Crown Tribunal of the Realm at Piotrków, whenever the present cause shall fall to be called and proclaimed for adjudication from the register and inscription belonging to it — at the instance of the Honourable Antoni of Kolno Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, hereditary owner of the estates of Trąbczyn with appurtenances, plaintiff;

who, adhering to the prosecutions — namely the manifest and prosecution term — previously lodged; and to this subaltern manifest made or to be made; and to the laws of the Realm; and to the prior decrees and the Commission designated by the Republic in this cause that have followed; and to the reasons and those same decrees set out in the prosecutions — cites you; or rather, reiterating those same prosecutions and staying the adjudication of the present cause in your court as having been prosecuted — cites you to see and hear the foregoing:

notwithstanding the remission of the present cause by the Honourable Sub-judge of the Kalisz Land Court to the full Kalisz Land Court as prosecuted — and himself, being one of the number of that court — improperly made from the condescension term at Trąbczyn and enrolled in the authenticated records; which remission ought to have been made not to the Kalisz Land Court but to the Tribunal by the finality of the law, as had been sought by the plaintiff:

[for] this cause — for the resolution of your decree expressed below — to be remitted to the Tribunal, by virtue both of the prior and of the present [citations] issued against you and also against the Honourable Condescension Court Judge; and also by virtue of the original decrees and the Commission of the Republic that followed together with the successors of the widow of the plaintiff's uncle; and there, having reviewed your decree of the enjoined condescension to the estates of Trąbczyn issued between the Honourable Chełmski and the plaintiff in the preceding year, on the Monday after the Feast of Saint Hedwig, Widow and Chosen of God, namely 20 October — which is contrary to your prior decree of remission of this cause to the condescension together with the successors of the widow of the plaintiff's uncle, [a remission made] together with the late Honest Jan Tracholz, the legal assignor of the Honourable Chełmski; and what is more, to his cause against the successors of that same widow of the plaintiff's uncle, instituted after her death — the same prior remission decree neither reviewed nor resolved, and likewise not having a term available from the Honourable Chełmski; and with the decrees and the Commission designated by the Estates of the Realm in this cause which also obstruct and do not admit the Kalisz Land Court forum and your decision not being attended to — unable to be reviewed, having been issued prejudicially and also without being ordered; nay rather, with the communication of documents interrupted in so long-standing a cause, begun thirty years ago after the barren decease both of the plaintiff's uncle and of his widow, and having its beginning according to the prescription of the King's new statute, which ought entirely to be decreed — having been issued:

himself the plaintiff to be freed from its bond and rigour; and this cause — which cannot be decided in any way without the documents to be recovered from the Rozdrażewski successors — to be remitted together with the cause and proceedings against the successors of the widow of the plaintiff's uncle, namely Katarzyna of Rozdrażewo Prusimska, regarding the documents to be delivered both for this cause and for the general estate of the plaintiff's uncle — left after the death of the uncle with his surviving consort, and after the death of the widow passing to her successors, and vindicated through a process by the plaintiff to the present day — and for which final decision has been ordered through the ultimate Commission of the Republic and which can be brought to execution by the Tribunal alone — by the authority both of that Commission and of the prior Kalisz Land Court decree of remission to the condescension term — not only by reason of the connected and concatenated nature of the matter, but also because the matter demands the production and delivery to him by the Rozdrażewski successors and the advancing of the present cause's documents — to be remitted; and there for that same cause to be decided and determined; and that if the illegal law and pretension of the Honest Jan Tracholz and likewise of the Honourable Chełmski, his acquirer, shall appear from the documents to be received from the Rozdrażewski successors — then thereafter the plaintiff, with the transfer of the Honourable Chełmski annulled

and his illegal right [annulled], to be freed from this legal demand; and by reason of the improper [proceedings] and a mere vexation and the entanglement of the plaintiff's boundary cause and discrepant commissarial decree, which sought right — and likewise by reason of the improper vexation, and also regarding the costs of litigation unjustly caused to the plaintiff — that the same [defendants] be punished with condign penalties, or that the litigation costs be decreed to be made good to the plaintiff; and if moreover a greater pretension and sums contracted by the late uncle of the plaintiff from the late Honest Tracholz and owed to him shall emerge from the documents to be produced and received from the Rozdrażewski successors — then that the same Honourable Chełmski be bound to payment of those sums, as the acquirer of his right, on behalf of the plaintiff, together with provisions; and nonetheless that penalties for the prejudicial decrees procured by the party and issued by the court be extended both against the party and against the court; and in the remainder all things that shall be required by law, justice, the requirements of the present cause and its documents — to be decided, decreed, and sentenced against you. Be prepared to appear at this term of litigation and respond judicially. Given at Piotrków on the Monday next after Laetare Sunday in the Year of the Lord 1778.

The first of those — against the Honourable Condescension Court Judge — at the estate of Trąbczyn, on the table of the heated room, with the mother of the household present, on the Thursday after Laetare Sunday; the second — in the town of Konin, at the house of the Honest Paulszewicz, where the chancellery of the Kalisz Land Court keeping its records at Konin is situated, on the table of the heated room, with the mother of the household present; the third — at the manor court of the estates of the village of Łukom, on the table of the atrium, with a maiden present — all on today's day, as contained in the act, he placed distinctly and, having notified, left.
