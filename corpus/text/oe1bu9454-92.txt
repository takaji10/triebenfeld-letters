--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Michaelis reports from Berlin that the information the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen had requested on the last post-day has been secured: Councillor of Works Glenck handed over on Sunday the receipts of the Gleissenbergs and of Schneider, leaving the writer fully able to answer the General Auditoriate on the dispositions communicated to the Prince. He expects Councillor of War von Triebenfeld to report separately that same day on the current state of the Krotoszyn affair.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince, Most gracious Prince and Lord, The information which Your Most Serene Highness requested of me by last post-day I have obtained in this wise, that Herr Baurath Glenck delivered to me on [uncertain: Donntag — Sunday/Monday?] the receipts of the Gleissenbergen and of Schneider, whereby I feel myself fully put in a position to answer the General Auditoriat upon the dispositions communicated to Your Most Serene Highness. I do not doubt that Herr Kriegsrath v. Triebenfeld will this day give Your Most Serene Highness intelligence of the present posture of the Krotoszyn tausp. affair, and in deepest reverence I remain, until death, Your Most Serene Highness's Berlin, the 18th December 1798. most humbly, faithfully obed. samſtes Michaelis
