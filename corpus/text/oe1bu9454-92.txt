--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Michaelis reports that the information the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen asked for on the last post day has been obtained: Councillor of Works Glenck handed over receipts from the Gleissenbergs and from Schneider, which put him in a position to answer the General Auditoriate on the orders communicated to the Prince. He expects Councillor of War von Triebenfeld to report separately the same day on the state of the Krotoszyn business. A short covering note from a third party, not Triebenfeld.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince,

Most gracious Prince and Lord, The information which Your Most Serene Highness requested of me on the last post-day I have obtained in this way, that the Sir Councillor of Works Glenck delivered to me on Sunday the receipts of the Gleissenbergs and of Schneider, whereby I feel myself fully placed in a position to answer the General Auditoriate upon its dispositions communicated to Your Most Serene Highness.

I do not doubt that the Sir councillor of war von Triebenfeld will give Your Most Serene Highness news today of the present situation of the Krotoszyn [uncertain: tausp.] affair, and in deepest reverence I remain, until death, Your Most Serene Highness's Berlin

the 18th of December

1798.

most humbly, faithfully [illegible]

most obedient

Michaelis
