# 1346-11-13  Pyzdry

Dokument Nr 1253
Dobeslaus iudex terrarum Gneznens. et Kalisiens. 1346 Nov. 13, in Pysdri; ecclesiae Gneznensi hereditates Stropiszyn et Pępocin contra petitiones Andreae heredis de Trąbczyn eiusque cognatorum adiudicat.
Gniezno. Archiv. Capituli Metropol. Lib. privil. XV sec. fol. 93 v.
Nos Dobeslaus iudex terrarum Gneznensis et Kalisiensis notum facimus universis et recognoscimus per presentes, quod in Pisdri in die sancti Briccii, anno Domini m.ccc.xlvi, presentibus Nicolao capitaneo Polonie, Maczkone Poznaniensi et Nicolao Kalisiensi palatinis, Thomislao Poznaniensi, Sbiluto Gneznensi, Ianussio Kalisiensi castellanis et aliis fide dignis, de ipsorum consilio et mandato adiudicavimus domino Kelczoni custodi, et ecclesie Gneznensi hereditates Troppessino et Panpycze perpetuo possidendas, absolventes ipsum seu ecclesiam Gneznensem ab instancia seu peticione Andree de Trampczino heredis et omnium cognatorum suorum, qui coram nobis ipsum statuentes, comiserunt sibi, suo et ipsorum nomine, facultatem litigandi pro hereditatibus eisdem; cuius allegacionibus et partis adverse hinc inde auditis, super huiusmodi hereditate sibi et omni sue cognacioni perpetuum silencium imposuimus, predicto domino Kelczoni et ecclesie Gneznensi presentes nostras litteras, nostro sigillo roboratas, super adiudicacione huiusmodi concedentes. Datum et actum in Pysdri in die sancti Briccii, anno Domini m.ccc.xlvi, presentibus testibus superius nominatis.
Troppessino, Stropiszyn. Panpycze, in margine dodano: sive Kopydłowo, pewnie Pępocin; Kopydłowo odległe od ostatniego o 5 mil. Trampczino, Trąbczyn.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Dobiesław, Richter der Länder Gnesen und Kalisch, spricht der Kirche von Gnesen die Güter Stropiszyn und Pępocin zu, gegen die Ansprüche Andrzejs, Erben von Trąbczyn, und seiner Verwandtschaft, denen ewiges Schweigen auferlegt wird. Pyzdry, 13. November 1346, am selben Tag wie das vorige Urteil. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski II, Nr. 1253.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Dobiesław, judge of the lands of Gniezno and Kalisz, adjudges the estates of Stropiszyn and Pępocin to the church of Gniezno against the claims of Andrzej, heir of Trąbczyn, and his kin, who are put to perpetual silence. Pyzdry, 13 November 1346, the same day as the judgment before. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski II, no. 1253.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 1253

Dobiesław, judge of the lands of Gniezno and Kalisz, 13 November 1,346, at Pyzdry; adjudges to the church of Gniezno the estates of Stropiszyn and Pępocin against the claims of Andrzej, heir of Trąbczyn, and his kin.

Gniezno. Archive of the Metropolitan Chapter. Liber privilegiorum of the 15th century, fol. 93v.

We, Dobiesław, judge of the lands of Gniezno and Kalisz, make known to all and acknowledge by these presents that at Pyzdry on the feast of St Brice, in the year of the Lord 1,346, in the presence of Mikołaj, captain of Poland, Maćko of Poznań and Mikołaj of Kalisz, palatines, Tomisław of Poznań, Zbylut of Gniezno and Janusz of Kalisz, castellans, and others worthy of trust, on their counsel and command we adjudged to Lord Kiełcz the custodian and to the church of Gniezno the estates of Stropiszyn and Panpycze to be possessed for ever, absolving him and the church of Gniezno from the suit and claim of Andrzej, heir of Trąbczyn, and of all his kin, who, setting him up before us, entrusted to him, in his name and theirs, the power to go to law for those estates; having heard his pleadings and those of the opposing party on both sides, we imposed perpetual silence concerning that estate on him and all his kin, granting to the said Lord Kiełcz and the church of Gniezno these our present letters, strengthened with our seal, concerning this judgment. Given and done at Pyzdry on the feast of St Brice, in the year of the Lord 1,346, in the presence of the witnesses named above.

Troppessino, Stropiszyn. Panpycze, added in the margin: sive Kopydłowo, probably Pępocin; Kopydłowo lies 5 miles from the latter. Trampczino, Trąbczyn.
