--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A short covering note from the Prince's secretary Hahn, sending on an enclosed letter at the Prince's command: the Prince was setting off for church with his father and the courier left within the hour, so he could not write himself. Hahn reports that the old Prince is in very good health for his age and much attached to his master, and confirms that the return to Breslau is fixed for the 25th.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Right Honourable Sir, Highly Honoured Sir Councillor of War and Forests! At the very moment when His Serene Highness the Prince is driving to church with the old gentleman, he commands me to send to Your Right Honourable, with his most high compliments, the enclosed letter. He would gladly have written himself, had not the ordonnance been departing within the hour.

The old Prince finds himself, for his age, very well, and shows exceedingly much [uncertain: attachment] for my most gracious Lord. I wish from the heart that Your

Right Honourable may find yourself in right good health; I commend myself to your continued favour and have the honour to persist in most perfect veneration: Your Right Honourable's, Öhringen, the 7th of July 1805.

humble and obedient servant A: Hahn — the return to Breslau is fixed, without fail, for the 25th!
