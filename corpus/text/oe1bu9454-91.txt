--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Beyme thanks the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen for his information on the proposed exchange of estates, welcomes the King's order that the further investigation be carried out, and pledges to pursue the matter as conscientiously as before, leaving nothing untried that might reconcile the Prince's wishes, his own master's willingness to accommodate them, and the interest of the State. A short letter of acknowledgement and assurance, with no details of the estates or terms.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince.

Most gracious Lord!

To Your Most Serene Highness I [give] most humble [thanks] for the information which Your Highness has been pleased to impart to me in the matter of the exchange of estates. It is withal an infinite joy to me that His Majesty has ordered the further investigation that was proposed, and I most humbly assure Your Highness that in the further pursuit of this matter I shall observe my duties just as conscientiously as hitherto, and shall accordingly leave no avenue untried which may be capable of reconciling the wishes of Your Most Serene Highness, the inclination of my Lord to meet them, and the interest of the State with one

another. To this assurance I add the protestation of the most boundless reverence, wherewith I remain, my whole life long, Your Most Serene Highness's most humble and obedient servant Beyme

Berlin

the 17th of December

1798.
