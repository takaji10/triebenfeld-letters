Ex Parte M Chełmski contra M Prusimski Citationis Relatio
Videatur hoc loco Citationis ex Parte Magnifici Stanislai Chełmski In et Contra Magnificos Antonium Prusimski tum Successores Magnificae olim de Rozdrażewskie Prusimska pro Judiciis Terrestre Calissien~ hic Celebran~ editt~ Relatio in Productis.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Registervermerk im Buch des Burggerichts Konin, Januar 1768: Der Bericht über die Ladung, die Stanisław Chełmski gegen Antoni Prusimski und die Rechtsnachfolger der verstorbenen Prusimska, geborene Rozdrażewska, vor das Kalischer Landgericht erwirkt hat, liegt bei den vorgelegten Schriftstücken.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A register note in the book of the castle court at Konin, January 1768: the report of the citation that Stanisław Chełmski obtained against Antoni Prusimski and the successors of the late Prusimska, born Rozdrażewska, before the Kalisz land court is among the documents produced.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
On the part of the Right Honourable Chełmski against the Right Honourable Prusimski — Report of a Citation

Let there be seen here, among the produced documents, the report of the citation issued on the part of the Right Honourable Stanisław Chełmski against the Right Honourables Antoni Prusimski and the successors of the late Right Honourable Prusimska, born Rozdrażewska, for the Kalisz land courts to be held here.
