Hochwohlgebohrner Herr
Hochzurerehrender Herr Kriegs- und ForstRath!
Noch fande ich eben einige chartes blauetes von
Sr Durchlaucht unterschrieben, unter meinen
Papieren, die ich in der Eilrigbreslau
eingepakt habe, und die für Euer Hochwohlge¬
behrem bestimmt waren. Dieſelben werden
mir dis Versehen verzeihen, wem ich denensel¬
ben sage, daß ich die leztern Nächte vor der
Abreiße keine Ruhe hatte, indem der Cammer¬
Director Graf aus Behringen nach dort ankand,
und nun alles was das Heheelohischen angrenge aus¬

gemacht werden sollte, ehe die Reiße ange¬
treten würde, die übrigen Chartes blanches
habe ich an die gnädigen Frauenlein von
Triebenfeld geſchikt.
Ich habe die Ehre mit vollkommenster Verehrung
zu beharren.
Euer Hochwohlgebohren
Dreßden d 8ten Nov:
1805.
N.S. Sr. Durchl. haben mich noch vor der Abreiße
zum Expeditions Kathernannt!!
unterthäniger Diener
A: Hahn

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Hahn apologises for having mislaid, among his papers, chartes blanches signed by His Serene Highness that were meant for Triebenfeld, explaining that in the last nights before departure he had no rest, the Chamber Director Count from Behringen having arrived and all Hoheelohisch business needing settlement before the journey. The remaining chartes blanches he has sent to the young ladies von Triebenfeld. A postscript notes his appointment, before the departure, as Expeditions-Kathern.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Right Honourable Sir, Highly Honoured Sir Councillor of War and Forests! I have just now found among my papers some chartes blanches signed by His Serene Highness, which I had packed up in the [uncertain: Eilrigbreslau] and which were intended for Your Right Honourable. The same will forgive me this oversight when I tell him that in the last nights before the departure I had no rest, since the Chamber Director Count from Behringen arrived there, and now everything that concerned the Hoheelohisch matters was to be [continued overleaf]

done before the journey should be undertaken. The remaining Chartes blanches I have sent to the gracious young ladies von Triebenfeld. I have the honour to remain, with the most perfect veneration, Your Right Honourable's Dresden, the 8th of Nov: 1805. N.S. His Serene Highness appointed me, still before the departure, Expeditions-[uncertain: Kathern]!! humble and obedient servant A: Hahn
