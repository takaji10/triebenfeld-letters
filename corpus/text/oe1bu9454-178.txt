Hochwohlgebohrner Herr
Hochzurerehrender Herr Kriegs- und ForstRath!
Noch fande ich eben einige chartes blauetes von
Sr Durchlaucht unterschrieben, unter meinen
Papieren, die ich in der Eilrigbreslau
eingepakt habe, und die für Euer Hochwohlge¬
bohren bestimmt waren. Dieſelben werden
mir dis Versehen verzeihen, wem ich denensel¬
ben sage, daß ich die leztern Nächte vor der
Abreiße keine Ruhe hatte, indem der Cammer¬
Director Graf aus Behringen nach dort ankand,
und nun alles was das Hohenlohischen angrenge aus¬

gemacht werden sollte, ehe die Reiße ange¬
treten würde, die übrigen Chartes blanches
habe ich an die gnädigen Frauenlein von
Triebenfeld geſchikt.
Ich habe die Ehre mit vollkommenster Verehrung
zu beharren.
Euer Hochwohlgebohren
Dreßden d 8ten Nov:
1805.
N.S. Sr. Durchl. haben mich noch vor der Abreiße
zum Expeditions Rath ernannt
unterthäniger Diener
A: Hahn

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Hahn apologises for having packed up, among his own papers at Breslau, several sheets signed in blank by the Prince that were meant for Triebenfeld; he pleads the sleepless nights before his departure, when Chamber Director Count arrived from Behringen and all the Hohenlohe business had to be settled before the journey. The rest of the blank signed sheets have gone to the young ladies von Triebenfeld. A postscript notes his appointment as Councillor of Expeditions.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Right Honourable Sir, Highly Honoured Sir, Councillor of War and Forests! I have just now found a few blank signed sheets (chartes blanches), signed by His Serene Highness, among my papers, which I packed up in the haste [uncertain: at Breslau], and which were intended for Your Right Honourable. You will forgive me this oversight when I tell you that in the last nights before my departure I had no rest, inasmuch as the Chamber Director Count arrived there from Behringen, and now everything that concerned the Hohenlohe affairs was to be

attended to before the journey was undertaken. The remaining blank signed sheets (chartes blanches) I have sent to the gracious young ladies von Triebenfeld.

I have the honour to remain, with the most perfect veneration,

Your Right Honourable's. Dresden, 8th Nov: 1805.

P.S. His Serene Highness, before the departure, further appointed me Councillor of Expeditions. Humble and obedient servant A: Hahn
