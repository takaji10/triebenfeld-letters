Extractus Commissionis ad Bona Trąpczyn designat[o?] Oblata
Videatur hoc Loco Extractus Sancitae Constitutionis in Ordine demandatae ad Fundum Bonorum Trąmpczyn fine Dislimitationis Bonorum Eorundem cum Bonis Łukom a Statibus Reipublicae Commissionis et Actis Curiae Regiae Varsavie~ deprompti Oblata in Productis.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Registervermerk im Buch des Burggerichts Konin, August 1775: Vorgelegt wird ein Auszug der Konstitution, mit der die Stände der Republik eine Kommission auf den Grund von Trąbczyn angeordnet haben, um dieses Gut gegen Łukom abzugrenzen; der Auszug ist den Akten des königlichen Hofes in Warschau entnommen und liegt bei den vorgelegten Schriftstücken.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A register note in the book of the castle court at Konin, August 1775: an extract is brought in of the constitution by which the Estates of the Republic ordered a commission to the ground of Trąbczyn, to mark that estate off from Łukom; the extract was drawn from the records of the royal court at Warsaw and is among the documents produced.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Extract of the Commission Assigned to the Estate of Trąbczyn — Oblata

Let there be seen here the Extract of the enacted Constitution, ordered in respect of the estate of the goods of Trąbczyn for the purpose of the re-delimitation of the same goods with the goods of Łukom by the Estates of the Republic — drawn from the Acts of the Commission and of the Royal Court of Warsaw — enrolled among the Produced Documents.
