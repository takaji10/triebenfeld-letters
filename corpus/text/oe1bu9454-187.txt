--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Die Vollmachten und Blanquets sind eingegangen. Der Gros Kanzler soll 90000 Rthl aus dem Justiz Fond geben, womit Weigel, die Schlabrendorff und Lichnowski bezahlt werden; der Fürst soll die gewünschten 30000 Rthl erhalten. Der Hypothekenschein der Güter Zagórów und Trąbczyn ist so belastet, dass man nicht weiter borgen will. Triebenfeld will Oppenheimer loswerden, um die Hypothek von 250000 Rthl zurückzubekommen. Er will nach Pszczew wegen der Klage Meyer Bernhards, dann nach Kalisz und Trąbczyn, um Geld einzutreiben.

187

Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

The powers of attorney and the blank forms have arrived; of the first I at once made the necessary use, and I am now assured of the best success — through the mediation of the worthy General and of the other gentleman — the Lord Grand Chancellor will give 90000 rt out of the Justice Fund — out of which I shall pay the 3 most restive creditors — that is, Weigel, the Schlabrendorff lady and Lichnowski.

The last named must return to me 20000 rt against a Świniary mortgage which I pledge to him; 10000 rt I must contrive to raise in like manner, and then Your Serene Highness will receive the desired 30000 rt.

It is a bad thing that the mortgage certificate of the Zagórów and Trąbczyn estates is so motley; nobody will lend any further upon it — what is given is against Jura Ca[uncertain: u]sa. In time, however, it will all be manageable.

My single endeavour is to be rid, and soon, of Oppenheimer, who last year promised to give 160000 rt and who worked upon Amelang

only, so that I may get back the mortgage of 250000 rt.

The scoundrel himself had a claim upon Oleśnica of 20000 rt; he undertook to pay the Invalids' Fund 50000 rtl, to Lichnowski 36000 rt.

to Meyer Bernhard 7600.

That is 113600 rt.

Of all these he has paid only 32000 rt to Lichnowchen, together with the money which Your Serene Highness received at Fürth, and notwithstanding this the fellow reckons the whole brokerage to himself at 11000 rt.

Once I am clear of Weigel and the other, I shall apply everything to making this [illegible] pay what he has really given, and shall at once procure the above-named mortgage back, which is thereby annulled eo ipso, whereby the matter then takes on quite another shape.

Today I go to Pszczew, and there to set everything in order, in particular to stop the mouth of that wretched Jew Meyer Bernhard. The fellow has brought suit against Your Serene Highness, notwithstanding that I had made it known to the knave that His Majesty had approved the Pszczew

lease and had removed all difficulties in such a manner that Your Serene Highness was released from the obligation (ex nexu).

From Pszczew I go to Kalisz and Trąbczyn, arrange everything there, see what money I can gather in at that place, and hasten back here with it in order to smooth out the principal business. Eighteen days from today I expect to be in Berlin again, and if all now goes as wished, I shall then perhaps myself bring to Öhringen the sum desired by Your Serene Highness.

The constellations, moreover, are now very evil, and it is much wished here that Your Serene Highness might be quite soon entirely restored to health, and that you would take your return journey by way of Berlin. Through my long stay here I was lately very nearly placed in a favourable situation. The Głogów government had ordered that the 30000 rt of the von Wulffen lady which stand upon Konotop be paid. The mandates had been left lying in Wrocław, so that I knew nothing of it until they fell upon my neck here. In the meantime the upright General at once helped here; he gave me in cash what I needed, and the Lord Grand Chancellor had 200000 rt paid upon the one mortgage, and so these two honest men provided me — Now I have full quiet and can attend to all Your Serene Highness's affairs.

With unbounded reverence I remain, as Your Serene Highness's

Berlin, the 17th of June

1806.

faithful, humble and obedient servant

v Triebenfeld
