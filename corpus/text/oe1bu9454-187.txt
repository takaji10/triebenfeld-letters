--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports that the powers of attorney and blank forms have arrived and been used: the Grand Chancellor will advance 90000 Rthl from the Justice Fund to satisfy the three most restive creditors, Weigel, the Schlabrendorff woman and Lichnowski, of whom the last is to return 20000 Rthl on a Schweiner mortgage, so that the Prince may have his 30000 Rthl. Complains of the motley Zagorow and Trąbczin mortgage certificate, of Oppenheimer's unpaid 113600 Rthl, and of Meyer Bernhard's suit despite the approved Betsch lease; travels via Beteche, Kalisz and Trąbczyn, with the von Wulffen claim of 30000 Rthl on Kontoppt averted by the General and Chancellor.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince, Most gracious Prince and Lord! The powers of attorney and the blank forms have arrived; of the former I have at once made the necessary use, and I am now assured of the best success — through the mediation of the worthy General and of the other gentleman — the Lord Grand Chancellor will give 90000 Rthl out of the Justice Fund — out of this I shall pay the 3 most restive creditors — that is, Weigel, the Schlabrendorff woman, and Lichnowski. The last-named must return to me 20000 Rthl upon a Schweiner mortgage which I pledge to him; 10000 Rthl I must contrive to raise in some such way, and then Your Serene Highness will receive the desired 30000 Rthl. It is a bad business that the mortgage certificate of the Zagorow and Trąbczin estates is so motley; no one will lend further upon it — what is given is against jura caisa. Still, everything will let itself be arranged little by little. My one endeavour is only to be rid soon of Oppenheimer, who last year promised to give 160000 Rthl and worked upon Amelang,

so that I may recover the mortgage of 250000 Rthl. The scoundrel had claims of his own upon Olesnica: 20000 fl. He undertook to pay the Invalids' Fund 50000 Rthl, Lichnowski 36000, Meyer Bernhard 7600. That makes 113600 Rthl. Of all these he has paid only 32000 Rthl to Lignowchen, together with the money which Your Serene Highness received at Fürth, and notwithstanding this the fellow reckons the whole Pro[uncertain: x]enitirum[uncertain] at 11000 Rthl. Once I am clear of Weigel and of the other man, I shall apply everything to paying this Gmür what he has actually given, and shall at once procure the return of the above-named mortgage, which is thereby eo ipso annulled, whereby the affair will then take on quite another shape. Today I go to Beteche, and there to put everything in order, in particular to stop the mouth of the wretched Jew, Meyer Bernhard. The fellow has brought suit against Your Serene Highness, notwithstanding that I had made it known to the villain that His Majesty had approved the Betsch

leasing-out, and had removed all difficulties in such fashion that Your Serene Highness were released from the obligation ex nexu. From Betschen I go to Kalisz and Trąbczyn, arrange everything there, see what money I can gather together in that quarter, and hasten hither with it in order to level out the principal matter here. Eighteen days from today I think to be in Berlin again, and if all now goes as wished, then I shall perhaps myself bring to Öhringen the sum desired by Your Serene Highness. The constellations, for the rest, are now very evil, and it is much wished here that Your Serene Highness might only be quite soon fully restored to health, and that you would take your return journey by way of Berlin. Through my long stay here I was lately very nearly placed in a favourable [uncertain: Lutuation]. The Glogau Government had ordered that the von Wulffen woman be paid the 30000 Rthl which stand upon Kontoppt. The mandates had been left lying in Breslau, so that I knew nothing of it, until they came down upon my neck here. In the meanwhile the upright General helped at once — he gave me in cash what I needed, and the Lord Grand Chancellor had 200000 Rthl paid upon one mortgage, and thus these two honest men furnished me — Now I have complete quiet and can attend to all Your Serene Highness's affairs. With unbounded veneration I remain, as Your Serene Highness's Berlin, the 17th of June 1806. faithful, humble and obedient servant v Triebenfeld
