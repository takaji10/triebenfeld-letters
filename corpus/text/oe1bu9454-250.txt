--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Humboldt, writing from Vienna, acknowledges Triebenfeld's letter of 3 November and confirms that he has seen the memorial submitted to Prince von Hardenberg, a copy of which he returns enclosed, promising to support its contents so far as his own official sphere allows. A brief, non-committal reply, filed as an enclosure to Triebenfeld's letter of 17 November, evidencing his lobbying of Prussian ministers at the Congress of Vienna.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Enclosure to the letter of the 17th of November. I have had the honour of receiving Your Right Honourable's obliging letter of the 3rd of this month, and through it have obtained knowledge of the memorial submitted to His Serene Highness the Prince von Hardenberg. I have the honour of returning a copy of the same in the enclosure, and shall not fail [uncertain: not have failed] to take an interest in its contents so far as it lies within my sphere of action. Receive herewith, Your Right Honourable, the assurances of my most perfect esteem.

Vienna, the 5th of November 1814.

Humboldt.

To the Sir

Herr von Triebenfeld
