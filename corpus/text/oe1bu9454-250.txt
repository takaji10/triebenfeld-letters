--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Humboldt, writing from Vienna and enclosed with Triebenfeld's letter of 17 November, acknowledges Triebenfeld's letter of the 3rd and confirms he has learned of the memorial submitted to Prince von Hardenberg. He returns a copy of the memorial and promises to take up its contents so far as his own sphere of action permits, closing with assurances of esteem.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Enclosure to the letter of the 17th of 9br. I have had the honour of receiving Your Right Honourable's obliging letter of the 3rd of this month, and have through it gained knowledge of the memorial delivered to His Serene Highness the Prince von Hardenberg. I have the honour to return a copy of the same in the enclosure, and shall not fail to interest myself in its contents so far as it lies within my sphere of action. Receive herewith, Your Right Honourable, the assurances of my most perfect esteem. Vienna, the 5th of November 1814. Humboldt. To Herr von Triebenfeld
