--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Protest von Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa, Erbherr von Łukomia, Łomów, Imielno und Bukowo, eingetragen in Konin im April 1778 und von ihm unterschrieben. Er beruft sich auf das Felddekret, das die von seiner Seite beigezogenen Kommissare 1775 zwischen Łukom und Trąbczyn erlassen haben: gestützt auf Urkunden und namentlich auf die Besichtigung von 1592, am letzten Grenzhügel mit benachbarten Zeugen durch Eid bekräftigt und rechtzeitig zu den Akten der eigenen Woiwodschaft eingereicht. Dagegen sei die Handlung, die die von Prusimski beigezogenen Kommissare später, nach der Grenzziehung und nach Errichtung der Hügel, privat und mit größter Gunst abgefasst hätten, unrechtmäßig. Prusimski habe sie weder zu den Akten der eigenen Woiwodschaft eingereicht noch ihre Auflagen befolgt und sie damit selbst als ungültig behandelt. Damit sein Schweigen nicht als Anerkennung gelte, erklärt Chełmski sie für ungültig und kündigt an, ihre Aufhebung zu betreiben.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A protest of Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, heir of Łukomia, Łomów, Imielno and Bukowo, entered at Konin in April 1778 and signed by him. He relies on the field decree that the commissioners brought in on his side gave in 1775 between Łukom and Trąbczyn: resting on documents and in particular on the inspection of 1592, confirmed by oath at the last boundary mound with neighbouring witnesses, and brought in good time to the records of its own province. Against it, he says, the act that the commissioners engaged by Prusimski drew up later, after the boundary had been drawn and the mounds raised, privately and with the greatest favour, is unlawful. Prusimski neither brought it to the records of his own province nor carried out what it prescribed, and so himself treated it as invalid. So that his silence should not pass for acceptance, Chełmski declares it invalid and announces that he will seek to have it quashed.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
To the office and present records of the Konin Municipal Court, appearing in person, the Honourable Stanisław Scibor Chełmski, Thesaurarius [Treasurer] of Wschowa, hereditary owner of the estates of Łukomia, Łomów, Imielno, and Bukowo and others pertaining thereto — although he knows very well the field decree issued by the commissioners procured on behalf of the petitioning party in the year 1775 between the estates of Łukom with appurtenances, his own, the appearing party's, on one part, and the estates of Trąbczyn with appurtenances of the hereditary [estates] of the Honourable Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, on the other part — all solemnities having been duly fulfilled, following the appearance of the collateral parties, from the recognitions of the corner and axial [mounds] thereof, on the basis of documents and notably the judicial site inspection of the year 1592 conducted at the instance of the sole former heir of the estates of Trąbczyn, from sworn inquiries both previously and at that time expedited — having been made; authorised by the oath performed at the last mound with neighbouring witnesses; and submitted for oblata to the records of the proper palatinate in due time — had truly and legitimately intervened —

he now [manifests that] a certain field act drawn up by the commissioners engaged by the Honourable Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — privately and with the greatest favour, beyond the evidence of the matter and the documents — later, already after the aforesaid delimitation, after the mounds were erected and the oath sworn by the appearing party — drawn up by persons related to the Honourable Starost of Niszczewice — is illegal; and that the Honourable Antoni Prusimski had neither caused it to be submitted to the records of his own palatinate, nor had observed the rigours prescribed by the same illegal act — and that thus Prusimski himself alone had acknowledged that same act, which followed in his favour, as invalid;

lest, however, by his silence he appear to accept the illegality of the aforesaid act that thus followed on behalf of the Honourable Prusimski — therefore, to meet a future contrary interpretation, and standing firm around the decree properly and lawfully established in due time — he manifests the want of substance and invalidity for any judgment claimed in favour of the Honourable Prusimski from the procured field act and others of similar kind that had intervened — offering to seek the cassation of it;

with the fuller improvement of the present manifest in form being entirely reserved to himself.

Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski
