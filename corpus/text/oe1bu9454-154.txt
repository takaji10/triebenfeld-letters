[aufge?]gen den Intressen auf 6 Wochen aufgehoben.

Versicherung auch aufgehoben mit künftiger Post werde

Arrest ist ja aufgehoben. Übrigens hat die Dębinska

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Triebenfeld meldet, die Anleihe sei in gutem Gang und liege einem Mann zur Prüfung vor. Gegen Wollenhaupts Gerede von einer Sequestration stellt er, Lichnowski habe den Arrest auf 6 Wochen aufgehoben, der Arrest wegen Magnuski sei laut Grimm ebenfalls aufgehoben. Przespolewski habe keine 30000 Rthl zu fordern, die Dębinska habe nur Arrest gegen Auszahlungen an Magnuski gelegt. Blomberg drohe in einem Brief vom 25. Dezember, ohne Erfüllung seiner Forderungen werde aus der Dismembration nichts. Triebenfeld hat ihn scharf zurechtgewiesen.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Triebenfeld reports that the loan is going well and is with a man for examination. Against Wollenhaupt's talk of a sequestration he says that Lichnowski has lifted the attachment for 6 weeks, and that according to Grimm the attachment on account of Magnuski has likewise been lifted. He says Przespolewski has no claim to 30000 Rthl, and that Dębinska has only laid an attachment against payments to Magnuski. He says Blomberg threatens, in a letter of 25 December, that without the fulfilment of his demands nothing will come of the dismemberment. Triebenfeld has sharply rebuked him.

Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Lord, The matter concerning the loan is in the best train; God grant that no new impediments arise. It lies for examination with a man who is otherwise a good one.

Everything that Your Serene Highness has been pleased to charge me with under the 13th I shall faithfully attend to.

As for the rest, what Wollenhaupt has prattled about a sequestration is a pure [uncertain: position]; on the contrary, Lichnowski has lifted the attachment [text lost]gainst the interest for 6 weeks. The attachment on account of Magnuski has, according to Grimm's assurance, likewise been lifted; with the next post I shall in the meantime write to him. I do not, for my part, rightly understand the whole business: Przespolewski surely has no 30000 Rthl to claim, and the attachment procured by Magnuski is surely lifted. For the rest, the Dębinska woman has only laid an attachment again, to the effect that nothing is to be paid out to Magnuski. — Nor will that be done in any case. Blomberg has written a letter under the 25th of December, consequently earlier than the one that was answered to him by express,

to me, which has followed me hither by the post; this letter is of such a content — that Blomberg has behaved infamously and must sit at least 10 years in the fortress. No robber who throws to the ground the wanderer in the wilderness stripped of all defence can make such base demands so wholly unabashed as this dishonoured man does. In dry words he says today [uncertain: thereupon] — that if all this were not punctually given him, then nothing should come of the dismemberment — but if he received what he demands, he would himself manage the business — Moreover, he means to go on skirmishing with me until one of them [uncertain: the devil takes]. Here, however, he meets his master, for today he already receives the full [uncertain: broadside] — I have given him notice that if he should so much as presume to speak another word, or to meddle in the business without my knowledge, I should know how to chastise him — for with a villain, which he has stamped himself to be, I would have no pity, and woe to him if he now stirred himself — He will surely be very meek.

In [uncertain: deepest] submission I remain, until death,

Your Serene Highness's

Berlin, the 15th of January

1805

most faithful servant

v Triebenfeld
