--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports negotiations with justice-burgomaster Hawich of Zagorowo, now a Ducal Warsaw notarius publicus, and Chief Inspector Hecker, and lists thirteen powers of attorney taken down judicially by justice-burgomaster Schenk of Neusatz: instruction of the Döbske over the Trąbczyn estates; full lifting of the sequestration under the King of Saxony's three-year indult; collection of interest for creditors holding judgments; suit against the Jews Oppenheimer et Wolf in Kalisz to cancel entries of over 72000 Rthl; removal of the sequestrator Honrichs and annulment of his sales; recovery of purchase arrears, 32000 Rthl from Olesnica leaseholders and over 10000 Rthl from the Niedzewiecki heirs; boundary suits; representation at the Warsaw Diet; and recovery of interest advanced to the Invalids' Fund towards the Larisch sum, Triebenfeld citing his 250000 Rtl bonds as priority.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince, Most gracious Prince and Lord! I have had here the justice-burgomaster Hawich from Zagorowo, who is appointed as actual Ducal Warsaw notarius publ:, together with the Chief Inspector Hecker, and with these men I have negotiated everything that tends to Your Serene Highness's advantage, and have for that purpose issued the judicial special powers of attorney. I have furthermore, firstly, pressed for the instruction [Anweisung] of the Döbske concerning the Trąbczyn estates, and have given power of attorney and instruction for it. Secondly, I have given the power of attorney for the entire lifting of the sequestration, and drafted the introductory step, the more so since a 3-year general indult has been granted by the King of Saxony, and since in this matter everything turns upon the absolute payment of the interest; so I have, thirdly, given Hawich the power of attorney to collect the interest, and to pay the same, against receipt, to those creditors who already have judgments in force in their favour, but to deliver the remainder to Your Serene Highness. Also I have, [uncertain: fourthly], given a power of attorney to bring suit against the Jews Oppenheimer et Wolf in Kalisz, and at once to press for the cancellation of the several sums entered, amounting to over 72000 Rthl, in which regard a suit is also already [uncertain: pending] in Berlin; [uncertain: fifthly] I have issued a power of attorney that the fraudulent Honrichs, who had himself installed as sequestrator, be at once removed and compelled to lay his accounts ad Acta, that Hawich on the other side draw up the objections against him, and [text unclear: that he be pressed] for the restitution of the same as well as for the payment of the sums still outstanding from him. Likewise I have, [uncertain: sixthly], given a power of attorney to collect from Niedzewiecki the outstanding [uncertain: Hochta] sums and to apply for execution against the same. [uncertain: Seventhly] I have issued a power of attorney that all high-handed transactions and sales of the lands by Honrichs be forthwith annulled, and that the purchasers, if they do not submit to payment into the treasury, be evicted [exmittirt] with recourse against Honrichs, because he, without being authorised, has in part given the lands away for nothing, and has appropriated to himself in this manner a little estate of several Hufe, and has erected the buildings from the lordship's timber and brick,

Eighthly, I have given power of attorney for the collection of all arrears of purchase-monies, which are very considerable. Ninthly, a power of attorney has been issued to compel the Olesnica hereditary leaseholders to pay the arrears of [uncertain: Linstands-] monies, inclusive of the canon, amounting to 32000 Rthl, or else to be [uncertain: evicted (German reads "committirt", i.e. committed; context requires "exmittirt", evicted)] with their following, so that the lands and buildings may be let out to others, far [text unclear]. Also, tenthly, a power of attorney has been given to Herr Hawich for the prosecution of all boundary suits, and the necessary instructions for it have been imparted to him. Eleventhly, Hawich has been given a power of attorney that he may appear in Your Serene Highness's name at the Diet at Warsaw and in all places, in order to maintain the same's rights of possession and property in their due force, the more so as every landed proprietor is now to appear in person, to reside upon his estates, or else to forfeit his possessions. Twelfthly, I have besides this given Herr Hawich, on my own part, a power of attorney together with the original decrees of the French General Government, in order to recover the interest advanced by me to the Invalids' Fund together with costs, and to deliver the same to Your Serene Highness towards the covering of the Larisch sum. Thirteenthly, and finally, yet another power of attorney has been drawn up for Hawich, to recover from the Niedzewiecki heirs the still outstanding [uncertain: Tobkanſts-] monies for several years' canon, which amount to more than 10000 Rthl, and to refute the unjust claims formerly framed by the deceased. I have had all this taken down judicially by the justice-burgomaster Schenk of Neusatz, in order to begin this new career right brilliantly; and since, by virtue of the bonds of 250000 Rtℓ., I have a right before all creditors to have a word to say, I think this must surely make an impression and bring about the wished-for outcome. In the meantime, however, Your Serene Highness shall, according to my arrangement, faithfully receive from time to time the reports upon the progress of the affair. In deepest reverence I remain, until death, Your Most Serene Highness's faithful and most obedient servant v Triebenfeld
