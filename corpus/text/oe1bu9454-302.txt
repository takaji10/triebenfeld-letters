--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Triebenfeld berichtet, er habe Hawich und Ober-Inspektor Hecker bei sich gehabt und dreizehn gerichtliche Vollmachten ausgestellt: wegen der Dąbska und der Trąbczyner Güter, zur Aufhebung der Sequestration, zur Klage gegen Oppenheimer et Wolf in Kalisz auf Löschung von über 72000 Rthl, zur Absetzung des Sequesters Honrichs und Annullierung seiner eigenmächtigen Grundverkäufe, gegen Niedźwiecki wegen rückständiger Pacht, gegen die Olesnicer Erbpächter und zur Vertretung auf dem Reichstag in Warszawa. Seine Obligationen über 250000 Rthl gäben ihm Mitspracherecht.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Triebenfeld reports that he had Hawich and Chief Inspector Hecker with him and has issued thirteen court powers of attorney: concerning the Dąbska and the Trąbczyn estates; for the lifting of the sequestration; for a suit against Oppenheimer et Wolf in Kalisz for the cancellation of over 72000 Rthl; for the removal of the sequestrator Honrichs and the annulment of his unauthorised land sales; against Niedźwiecki over arrears of rent; against the Oleśnica hereditary tenants; and for representation at the Diet in Warszawa. He says his bonds for 250000 Rthl give him a say.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

I have had here with me the justice-burgomaster Hawich from Zagórów, who has been appointed as actual Duca[l?] [illegible] Warsaw notary public, together with the Chief Inspector Hecker, and with these men I have transacted everything that tends to Your Serene Highness's advantage, and have issued the judicial special powers of attorney for it. Beyond this I have, firstly, pressed for the instruction of the Dąbska concerning the Trąbczyn estates, and have given a power of attorney and instruction to that end.

Secondly, I have given the power of attorney for the complete lifting of the sequestration, and have drafted the opening step, the more so since a three-year general indult has been granted by the King of Saxony, and since everything here turns upon absolute payment of the interest; so I have, thirdly, given Hawich the power of attorney to collect the interest, and to pay it, against receipt, to those creditors who already have judgments in force in their favour, but to deliver the remainder to Your Serene Highness, b given. Also I have, fourthly, given a power of attorney to bring suit against the Jew Oppenheimer et Wolf in Kalisz, and at once to press for the cancellation of the several sums entered, amounting to over 72000 rt., in which matter a suit is already pending in Berlin; fifthly, I have issued a power of attorney that the fraudulent Honrichs, who had himself installed as sequester, be forthwith removed and compelled to lay his accounts ad Assam, that Hawich on the other hand draw up the objections against him and compel him to make good the same, as also to pay the sums still outstanding from him. Likewise I have, sixthly, given a power of attorney to collect from Niedźwiecki the outstanding lease sums and to apply for execution against him. Seventhly, I have issued a power of attorney that all high-handed dealings and sales of the grounds by Honrichs be forthwith annulled, and that the purchasers, if they will not submit to paying into the treasury, be evicted with recourse against Honrichs, because he, without being authorised, gave away part of the grounds for nothing, and in this manner appropriated to himself a little estate of several Hufe, and put up the buildings from the lordship's timber and brick,

Eighthly, I have given a power of attorney for the collection of all arrears of purchase-money, which are very considerable.

Ninthly, a power of attorney has been issued to compel the Oleśnica hereditary lessees to pay the arrears of entry-money, inclusive of the canon, amounting to 32000 rt., or else, with all their following, to be evicted, so that the grounds and buildings may be let out afresh to others. Also, tenthly, a power of attorney has been given to Mr Hawich for the prosecution of all boundary suits, and the instructions necessary thereto have been imparted to him. Eleventhly, a power of attorney has been given to Hawich that he may appear in Your Serene Highness's name at the Diet at Warsaw and in all places, in order to maintain Your Highness's rights of possession and property in their due force, the more so as every landowner is now to appear in person, to reside upon his estates, or else to forfeit his possessions. Twelfthly, besides this I have on my own part given Mr Hawich a power of attorney, together with the original decrees of the French General Government, in order to collect the interest advanced by me to the Invalids' Fund, together with costs, and to remit the same to Your Serene Highness in covering of the Larisch sum.

Thirteenthly, and lastly, a further power of attorney has been made out to Hawich, also, for the Niedźwiecki heirs, to collect the still outstanding hereditary purchase-monies for several years' canon, which amount to over 10000 rt., and to refute the unjust claims formerly framed by the deceased. I have had all this taken down judicially by the justice-burgomaster Schenck from Neusatz, in order to begin this new career right brilliantly; and since, by virtue of the bonds of 250000 rt., I have a right before all the creditors to have a word to say, I think this must surely make an impression and bring about the desired outcome. In the meantime, however, Your Serene Highness shall, by my arrangement, faithfully receive the reports on the progress of the matter from time to time.

In deepest reverence I remain, until death,

Your Most Serene Highness's

faithful and most obedient servant

v Triebenfeld
