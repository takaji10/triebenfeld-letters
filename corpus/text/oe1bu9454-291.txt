--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports the Cabinet Order of 27 April, sent to Kircheisen, commanding prompt payment of the 100000 Rthl to Prince August, notified to him on 6 May. The compensation for the Polish estates, worth 300000 Rthl, is settled through Minister Humboldt but awaits the Emperor, despite efforts by Stein, Woronzow and Nesselrode; Stein threatens to pursue it at Heilbronn. Cosmar is ordered to deposit the monies on pain of arrest; the military affair remains withheld by the Prince Chancellor. Notes the King's departure, Koppe's Hanoverian appointment, and requests money.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince etc.: just now I have again had in my hands the Cabinet Order of the 27th of April, according to which the King has commanded the promptest payment of the 100000 Rthl to Prince August with the utmost dispatch, and has sent it to Kircheisen; on the 6th of May this was made known to Prince August, which he absolutely must have had long since; to this matter [uncertain: nothing whatever stands in the way any longer], and hereby everything is won, unless it be that God had created the good Prince August as a scourge to his father and had struck him with blindness. The compensation matter concerning the affairs of the Polish estates is now decided through the mediation of Minister Humboldt; the how, the when and the where now depends upon the Emperor. Minister Stein has already taken all imaginable pains, and Woronzow and Nesselrode too have knocked at the door, but as yet the Emperor has determined nothing. Stein says: if it does not succeed here, then I shall manage it at Heilbronn. The matter is too important and cannot be let go; 300000 Rthl is no child's play. With regard to your affair with Cosmar it is commanded that he shall absolutely and at once deposit your monies, failing which he is at once threatened with arrest, and a Chief Superintendent is to be appointed; the matter lies only with the Chancellor,

for signature. As to the further question whether you or the King are to pay the 30000 Rthl, that will be found out in time, if only they no longer sniff out the Hlr. from us — pardon me. Concerning your military affair nothing whatever has yet been said. The Prince Chancellor reserves that whole journal to himself, and therefore nothing is to be learnt or to be [uncertain: divulged/betrayed]. The King departs in the night from the 25th to the 26th; he goes still to Berlin and thence directly to Heilbronn; the Prince Chancellor follows him soon. I [uncertain: will] then come to you to see to what remains. Saved you are and will be. The King of Würtemberg plays a great part; the mediatised are laid down in salt — be it then that the times change, for the Crown Prince of Würtemberg is marrying the Princess of Oldenburg; these are singular times. Koppe has been placed in the Hanoverian service; I shall learn from Minister Stein where. Write for Your Serene Highness, so as not to [uncertain: burden] me further, for I depart when Hardenberg and Stein depart; but it would be a sad thing if you sent me no money, as [uncertain: I did] through Adolph Both, for I should not know how I am to get on here. Forever I remain Your Serene Highness's, Wien, the 24th of May 1815, [uncertain: when] obedient servant v Triebenfeld
