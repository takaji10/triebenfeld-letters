--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A report on business at the Congress: a royal cabinet order of 27 April directs immediate payment of the 100,000 Rthl to Prince August, and compensation for the Polish estates, some 300,000 Rthl, has been settled through Minister Humboldt but awaits the Emperor, with Stein, Vorontsov and Nesselrode pressing and Stein threatening to pursue it at Heilbronn. Cosmar is ordered to deposit the Prince's monies on pain of arrest. Nothing yet on the military affair, and Triebenfeld asks urgently for money to leave Vienna.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

p.p.

Just now I have again had in my hands the cabinet order of the 27th of April, according to which the King has commanded the promptest payment of the 100000 Rthl to Prince August with the utmost dispatch, and has sent it to Kircheisen; on the 6th of May this was made known to [uncertain: Prince] August, which he Probsolut must have had long since; nothing whatever stands in the way of this matter any longer, and hereby all is won, unless God had created the good Prince August as a hostage to his father and struck him with blindness. The matter of compensation concerning the affairs of the Polish estates has now been decided through the mediation of Minister Humboldt; the how, the whether and the where now depend upon the Emperor. Minister Stein has already given himself every conceivable trouble, and Worowzow and Nesselrode have also knocked at the door, but the Emperor has as yet determined nothing. Stein says: if it does not go here, then I shall succeed with it at Heilbronn. The matter is too important and cannot be let go of; 300000 Rthl is no child's play. With regard to your affair with Cosmar it is ordered that he shall Absolutely and at once deposit your monies, failing which he is at once threatened with arrest and a chief overseer is to be appointed; the matter lies only with the Chancellor

for signature. As for the rest, whether you or the King are to pay the 30000 Rthl will come to light in due course, if they do not scent out the [illegible] from us any more — pardon me.

Concerning your military affair nothing at all has yet been said. The Prince Chancellor ascribes all of that Journal to himself, and therefore nothing is to be learnt or betrayed. The King departs in the night from the 25th to the 26th; he is going still to Berlin and from there straight to Heilbronn; the Prince Chancellor will soon follow him. I shall then [uncertain: come] to you in order to see to the rest; you are saved and will be saved. The King of Württemberg plays a great part; the mediatised are laid in the stocks, unless the times should change, for the Crown Prince of Württemberg is marrying the Princess of Oldenburg — these are peculiar times. Koppe has been appointed in the Hanoverian service; I shall learn from Minister Stein where. Write for Your Serene Highness's sake, so as not to [uncertain: increase] me, for I depart when Hardenberg and Stein depart. But it would be a sad thing if you sent me no money, as I do through Adolph Both, for I should not know how I am to get away from here. I remain forever Your Serene Highness's, Vienna, the 24th of May 1815

when obedient servant

v. Triebenfeld
