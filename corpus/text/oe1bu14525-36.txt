--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A petition to the King, on behalf of the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, pressing for the Treasury to resign title to the whole of the princely estates in Cujavia and Betsche as quickly as possible. It recalls that a Royal Government at Posen had earlier demanded that the title of possession to the Hohenlohe estates be put right as urgent, and that the Prince accordingly submitted the missing deeds of donation in the original. Signed by J. Wilhelm Glenck, princely Hohenlohe Councillor of Building and Economy.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene, Most Mighty King, most gracious King and Lord!

Already a considerable time ago a Royal Government at Posen demanded from His Serene Highness the rectification of the titulus possessionis of the princely Hohenlohe estates as necessary and urgent, whereupon His Highness the Prince sent in to Your Majesty the deeds of donation wanting for this purpose in the original. As much, then, as His Serene Highness has been pressed in this matter to the speedy rectification of the titulus possessionis, so earnestly does he since look [uncertain: forward] to the conclusion of this affair, and I have now been charged in the name of His Serene Highness my Lord to beg Your Majesty most humbly and most urgently for the most speedy resignation by the Treasury (Fiscus) of the whole of the princely estates in Cujavia and Betsche. I remain, until death, in deepest reverence

Your Royal Majesty's

Betsche, the 13th of February 1802.

most humble and obedient

J. Wilhelm Glenck, princely Hohenlohe Councillor of Building and Economy
