--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A brief note from Brzechsta to a correspondent addressed as "Dear Brother," reporting that various affairs will delay his departure from Warsaw until after the 15th of the month, and that reliable intelligence indicates the Trąbczyn matter stands quite well, details of which he will convey in person. The writer breaks off for want of time, the conveyance being about to leave, and sends greetings.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Warsaw, the 5th of March 1812.

Dear Brother, I hasten to inform you that, on account of various pieces of business, I shall not be able to depart from Warsaw before the 15th of this month. The matter concerning Trąbczyn stands, according to reliable information obtained, quite well, about which I shall tell you more by word of mouth. For shortness of time, as the conveyance is leaving, I cannot write you more. Farewell

to the friend and both.

Brzechsta
