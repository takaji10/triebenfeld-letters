--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A brief note from Brzechsta, writing as "dear brother", reporting that business will keep him in Warsaw until the 15th of the month, and that on reliable information the Trąbczyn affair stands quite well, details to follow in person. Written in haste as the conveyance was leaving, it gives no particulars and closes with greetings to the friend and to both.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Warsaw, the 5th of March 1812.

Dear Brother, I hasten to inform you that, on account of various pieces of business, I shall not be able to depart from Warsaw before the 15th of this month. The matter concerning Trąbczyn stands, according to reliable information obtained, quite well, about which I shall tell you more by word of mouth. For shortness of time, as the conveyance is leaving, I cannot write you more. Farewell

to the friend and both.

Brzechsta
