--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A brief note from Brzechsta to a correspondent addressed as "Dear Brother," reporting that various affairs will delay his departure from Warsaw until after the 15th of the month, and that reliable intelligence indicates the Trąbczyn matter stands quite well, details of which he will convey in person. The writer breaks off for want of time, the conveyance being about to leave, and sends greetings.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Warsaw, the 5th of March 1812. Dear Brother, I hasten to inform thee that on account of various affairs I shall not be able to set out from Warsaw before the 15th of this month. The matter concerning Trąbczyn stands, according to reliable intelligence obtained, quite well, whereof I shall say more to thee by word of mouth. For shortness of time, since the conveyance is departing, I cannot write thee more. Farewell to the friend and both. Brzechsta
