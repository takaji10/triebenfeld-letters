--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Brzechffa teilt Triebenfeld mit, dass er wegen verschiedener Geschäfte erst am 15. des Monats aus Warschau abreisen kann. Nach eingezogenen zuverlässigen Nachrichten steht die Sache wegen Trąbczyn recht gut. Näheres will er mündlich berichten. Wegen der abgehenden Gelegenheit schreibt er nur kurz.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Brzechffa informs Triebenfeld that because of various pieces of business he cannot leave Warsaw until the 15th of the month. He says that, according to reliable information he has gathered, the matter concerning Trąbczyn stands quite well. He will report the details in person. Because the post is about to leave, he writes only briefly.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Warsaw, the 5th of March 1812.

Dear Brother, I hasten to inform you that, on account of various matters of business, I shall not be able to set out from Warsaw until the 15th of this month. The affair concerning Trąbczyn stands, according to the reliable intelligence I have gathered, quite well, of which I shall tell you more by word of mouth. For shortness of time, as the conveyance is departing, I cannot write you more. Farewell

to the friend and brothe[r]

Brzechffa
