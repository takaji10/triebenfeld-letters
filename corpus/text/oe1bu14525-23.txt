--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Gerichtliche Taxe des dem regierenden Fürsten Friederich Ludewig von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen gehörenden Gutes Szetlewek im Kreis Konin, Pfarrei Rzgow: 1724 M 22 R Fläche nach der Vermessung des Landmessers Sprengel von 1798, Acker, Wiesen, Kiefernwald, Teiche, dazu Vorwerk, Kmieci, Chalupnici, Komornice, Schenke, Schmiede und Wassermühle. Verpachtet 1799 bis 1805 an Ignatz von Radzinski für 7800 fl, tatsächlich 10.865 fl. Wert 34.803 Rthl 15 gg, nach Abzug des Inventars 31.171 Rthl 23 gg 4 d. Beigefügt Taxen für Brauerei, Brennerei und Ziegelei.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A judicial valuation of the noble estate of Szetlewek in the Konin district, parish of Rzgow, belonging to the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, issued in provable form by the Kalisz government. It records the 1798 survey by Sprengel at 1,724 Morgen, graded arable, meadow, pasture, gardens, woods and ponds, the peasant holdings, mill, tavern and smithy, a lease to Ignatz von Radzinski to 1805, and a gross value of 34,803 Rthl, reduced to 31,171 Rthl 23 Groschen after deducting the lessee's inventory, with separate brewery, distillery and brickworks yields.

We Friedrich Wilhelm, by the Grace of God King of Prussia etc., do hereby make known and give to be understood that of the noble estate of Szetlewek in the Konin district a judicial valuation has been taken up, which reads as follows:

Instrumentum taxationis of the estate of Szetlewek, belonging to the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Friederich Ludewig, Serene Highness, situate in the Province of South Prussia, in the Department of the Royal Government at Kalisz, in its Konin district and the parish of Rzgow.

Is, in the rectified capital value, in the sum of 31,171 rt. 23 gg. 4 d., or 187,031 fl. 25 g. Polish, correct in the calculation.

Cretius, Calculator to the Government.

The estate of Szetlewek, situate in the Province of South Prussia in the Department of the Royal Government at Kalisz, in its Konin district and the parish of Rzgow, lies 1 1/4 miles from Zagórów, 3 miles from Konin, 2 miles from Pyzdry, 2 miles from Slupce, 5 miles from Kalisz, and 1 mile from the river Wartha. It borders towards the east upon Podbiele and Grabienice, has a corner[illegible]boundary with Grabienice, Swiątniki and Kurow; towards the south upon Kurow and Biskupice; towards the west upon Łukom; and towards the north upon Trąbczyn. At present there are no boundary suits; however, when in the year 1799 a judicial re-delivery of possession was undertaken, several witnesses pointed out patches of arable which had successively been torn away by the neighbours, which the manorial lordship (Dominium) intends to vindicate, but which have not here been brought further into the account. In the year 1798 this estate was surveyed by the surveyor Sprengel

and the surface content thereof ascertained at 1724 M. 2 R. For the purposes of this valuation the parcels have been graded for quality, with the assistance of the surveyor Chwalowski and two sworn [uncertain: Bautners], and according to the grading register they comprise

I. the manorial parcels

1. In arable: a. of the 1st [uncertain: Ex.] soil, whose constituent parts consist in loam and good earth, where in the 1st crop-course wheat, in the 2nd crop-course barley, in the 3rd crop-course rye and in the 4th crop-course oats may be sown: 239 M 39 R

b. of the 2nd class: soil which has less loam, good earth, and few sandy parts, where in the 1st and 3rd crop-course rye, in the 2nd crop-course barley and in the 4th crop-course oats may be sown: 277 M 140 R

c. of the 3rd class: soil which consists of earth and sand, where in the 1st and 3rd crop-course rye and in the 2nd and 4th crop-course oats can be sown: 40 M 78 R = 557 M 77 R

The holdings, the farm buildings, the livestock of their own, the number of souls, and the dues and services of the subjects appear from the table taken up in the year 1799 and annexed to this instrument of valuation. The holdings belong to the manorial lordship and are left to the residents merely for cultivation. The lordship must build the subjects their houses; they, on the other hand, keep them in repair, just as they also build their barns and stables out of their own means, though they receive timber for the purpose from the lordship free of charge. They have also received firewood from the adjoining wood of the estate of Trąbczyn, since the latter estate and Szetlewek have all along had one and the same hereditary lord, although these estates have different folios in the mortgage book. The fields are, between the

lordship and the subjects, separated; the pasture, however, is held in common. With pasture and timber rights, or with other servitudes, this estate is not burdened. At present Szetlewek is leased to Ignatz von Radzinski from the year 1799 to 1805 for an annual rent of 7800 fl.
