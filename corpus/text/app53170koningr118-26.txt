Actum in Conin Sabbatho Invigilia Festi Immaculatae Conceptionis Beatissimae Mariae Virginis Die Scilicet Septima Mensis Decembris Anno Domini 1782
Pro parte JM Prusimski Visionis Borram Excissa~ Relatio
Ad Officium et Acta praesenti Castrensia Coninensia personaliter veniens Providus Bartholomaeus Szepczyński de Trąmpczyno Ministerialis Regnis Generalis sanus Recognovit Quia Ipse habitis Secum Duobus Nobilibus videlicet Generosis Adamo Milewski et Joanne Wilgiewicz hic Licet ad praesens absentibus In loco tamen negotii Infrascripti pro tunc praesentibus. Ad Instantiam et officiosam Requisitionem Illustris Magnifici Antonii in Kolno Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen~ Insignis Sancti Stanislai Equitis Bonorum Trąmpczyna cum attinen~ haeredis, Die Quarta mensis et Anni Currentium cum Suprascriptis Nobilibus in Borram ad Bona Trąmpczyn Spectantem Condescenderat. Ibidemque taliter existens affatus est se debite vidisse et Connumerasse, ut pote Sosien w Boru własnym do Dóbr Trąmpczyna nalezącym polewey Stronie wyciętey Linij, przez Dekret Trybunalski y ziazdowey widoczney y Kołkami wziemi Utkmiętemi oznaczoney będącym Dwiescie dwadziescia i Jednę Budynkowych przez Ludzi z Dóbr Łukomia Łomowa y Innych WJmc Pana Stanisława Chełmskiego Skarbnika Wschovykiego[?] Dziedzicznych Dnia Dwudziestego Osmego Listopada, Roku biezącego, pod Czas Aktu Kondescensyi Dwoch przez Siebie Zeznawaiącego przywoływanych sciętych, które Drzewo czyli Moce do uspomnionych Dóbr pozabierane procz Siodmiu kloców odsiemnych y Gałęzi[?] na mieysce lezązych[?] Quae praemissa Is Idem Miniterialis cum suprascriptis Nobilibus Die ut supra vidisse Connumerasse, et prout Eadem de Jussu et Mandato Magnifici Chełmski Thesaurarii Vschoven~ per acta sint[?] attestatus est de quo hanc suam Officio praesenti Relationem fecit, peteris Eandem ase[?] Suscipi quod obtinuit.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Bericht des Gerichtsboten Bartłomiej Szepczyński von Trąbczyn vom 7. Dezember 1782, für Antoni Prusimski von Kolno, Starost von Niszczewice. Mit zwei Adligen, Adam Milewski und Jan Wilgiewicz, war er am 4. des laufenden Monats im Trąbczyner Kiefernwald. Links der Linie, die nach dem Dekret des Tribunals und des Ortstermins ausgehauen und mit Pflöcken bezeichnet ist, zählte er 221 gefällte Baukiefern, nach seiner Angabe am 28. November des laufenden Jahres von Leuten aus Łukomia, Łomów und anderen Gütern Chełmskis geschlagen und auf dessen Güter fortgeschafft, bis auf sieben Klötze. Nach seiner Angabe geschah es auf Befehl Chełmskis.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A report of the court messenger Bartłomiej Szepczyński of Trąbczyn of 7 December 1782, for Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice. With two noblemen, Adam Milewski and Jan Wilgiewicz, he was in the pine wood of Trąbczyn on the 4th of the current month. On the left of the line that was cut and staked under the decree of the Tribunal and of the sitting on the ground he counted 221 felled building pines, cut by his account on 28 November of the current year by people from Łukomia, Łomów and other estates of Chełmski's and carried off to those estates, all but seven logs. He states that it was done at Chełmski's order.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Done in Konin, on Saturday, the Eve of the Feast of the Immaculate Conception of the Most Blessed Virgin Mary, namely the 7th day of the month of December, in the Year of the Lord 1782.

For the part of the Illustrious Prusimski — a Report of the Judicial Site Inspection of the Felled Pine Forest

Having come in person to the present Office and Records of the Konin Municipal Court, the Honest Bartłomiej Szepczyński of Trąbczyn, General Royal Process Server, sound [of mind], acknowledged: that he, having with him two Nobles, namely the Honourables Adam Milewski and Jan Wilgiewicz, although absent here from the court at present, but present at the time at the location of the below-written matter — at the instance and official requisition of the Illustrious Magnificent Antoni of Kolno Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, Knight of the Order of Saint Stanislaus, Heir of the estates of Trąbczyn with appurtenances — on the 4th day of the present month and year, had descended with the above-mentioned Nobles into the pine forest belonging to the estate of Trąbczyn; and being there in such manner, stated that he had duly seen and counted:

namely pines in the pine forest itself belonging to the estates of Trąbczyn, on the left side of the cut line, visible by the Tribunal Decree and the field [session], and marked by stakes driven into the ground — two hundred and twenty-one construction-grade [trees], cut down by the people of the estates of Łukomia, Łomowa, and other hereditary [estates] of the Right Honourable Pan Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of [uncertain: Wschowa], on the 28th day of November of the present year, during the Act of the Condescension of the two [sessions] called by the deponent himself — cut down; which timber was taken away from the aforementioned estates, except for seven [odsiemnych] logs and [uncertain: branches] lying in place.

The same Process Server, with the above-mentioned Nobles, saw and counted the above-mentioned things on the day as above, and attested that the same were done by the order and mandate of the Illustrious Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, through [the court's] acts — of which he made this his report to the present Office, requesting that the same be received from him, which he obtained.
