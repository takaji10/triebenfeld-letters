--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports that Amelang has finally settled matters far enough to issue an instruction on the brothers Kuh, drawn against monies from the Invalids' Fund held as a negotiation-obligation, amounting to 36,755 Rthl 7 gg, which is forwarded without delay; asks that the enclosed letter to the Messieurs Kuh be delivered at once. The Hangsche and Eysenhardtsche affairs are to be put in order next, after which he will travel to Kalisz to pursue the 100,000 Rthl.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord, With the most unspeakable trouble the honest Ame lang has at last so far buried the matters that he has given, from the Ban quis leve, an instruction to pay to the brothers Kuh, before the monies from the Invalids' Fund, which properly consist in a negotiating obligation / to the amount of 36755 Rthl 7 gg., have been received. I wish, without the very least delay, most obediently to transmit the same to Your Most Serene Highness. Tomorrow everything will be arranged, and likewise the Hang matter as well as the Eysenhardt matter will be brought into order, and when I am finished with that I shall hasten back to go to Kalisz and to see to the further steps for obtaining the 100000 Rthl. In deepest reverence I remain, until death, Your Most Serene Highness — may Your Serene Highness deign to have the letter to the gentlemen Kuh delivered at once — most faithfully obedient Serv[ant] How true it is, indeed, that [someone] is in a position to hinder the dismemberment? I defy anyone. Berlin, the 31st of January 1805.

v Triebenfeld
