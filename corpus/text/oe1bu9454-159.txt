--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports that Amelang has finally settled matters far enough to issue an instruction on the brothers Kuh, drawn against monies from the Invalids' Fund held as a negotiation-obligation, amounting to 36,755 Rthl 7 gg, which is forwarded without delay; asks that the enclosed letter to the Messieurs Kuh be delivered at once. The Hangsche and Eysenhardtsche affairs are to be put in order next, after which he will travel to Kalisz to pursue the 100,000 Rthl.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince, Most gracious Prince and Lord, With the most unspeakable toil the honest Amelang has at last laid the affairs to rest so far that he has given, from the [uncertain: banking house / Banquier], an instruction upon the brothers Kuh, before the monies from the Invalids' Fund, which properly consist in a negotiation-obligation, / to the amount of 36755 Rthl 7 gg. which he has received. I [uncertain: wish] to convey the same, without the very least delay, most obediently to Your Most Serene Highness. Tomorrow everything will be arranged, and the Hangsche affair as well as the Eysenhardtsche one put in order, and when I have done with that I shall hasten back to go to Kalisz and to see to what further is needed for the obtaining of the 100000 Rthl. In deepest reverence I remain, until death, Your Most Serene Highness's — may Your Serene Highness graciously cause the letter to the Messieurs Kuh to be delivered at once — faithful and most obedient serv[ant]. How true it is: who indeed is in a position to hinder the dismemberment? I defy anyone. Berlin, the 31st of January 1805. v Triebenfeld
