--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Destitute and crippled by gout, Triebenfeld renews his plea for a few hundred in coin for his children, Lombardini having withheld 150 Rthl, and sets out an account of the 4300 Rthl received during his Berlin arrest against borrowings from v. d. Lahr (5140 Rthl, 1600 Rthl outstanding), Cote (9000 Rthl) and Blochsräff and Holzapfel (3500 Rthl), leaving some 9200 Rthl. He resents talk that he plunders the Prince, reports 591 Rthl 18 Groschen paid and 185 Rthl of Glogau court dues due, lists seven pending suits, and recommends criminal-court councillor Stenger to vindicate Zagorowo, Trąbczyn, Betsche and Witów, with Seifert as clerk at 250-300 Rthl and Honrichs to be called to account.
