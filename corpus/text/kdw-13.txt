Dokument Nr 2014
Dobrogostius s. Gneznens. ecclesie archiepiscopus 1399 Oct. 13, in stuba murata monasterii Landensis; decimam manipularem de villa Trąbczyn, monasterio Ląd contra petitionem Iohannis plebani de Trąbczyn adiudicat.
Włocławek. Lib. privilegior. monasterii Ląd, pag. 181.
In nomine Domini amen. Nos Dobrogostius divina et apostolice Sedis gratia sancte Gneznensis ecclesie archiepiscopus, notum facimus quibus expedit universis presencium notitiam habituris, quod cum inter venerabilem virum dominum Gerardum abbatem totumque conventum monasterii Landensis Ordinis Cisterciensis nostre Gnesnensis diocesis, actores, ab una, et discretum virum Iohannem rectorem ecclesie in Trapczyno, reum, parte ab altera, de et super decima manipulari campestri ibidem in Trambczyno exorta fuisset coram nobis materia questionis: nos igitur predictis partibus coram nobis personaliter comparentibus, auditisque ipsarum partium propositis et inductis probationibus quibus partes predicte uti solebant, communicato iuris peritorum consilio et de ipsarum parcium consensu, voluntate et assensu, simpliciter et de plano, sine strepitu et figura iudicii amputatis omnium litium anfractibus, Christi nomine invocato, ipsum solum pre oculis habentes et eum metuentes, pro tribunali sedentes per hanc nostram sententiam definitivam pronunciamus, decernimus et declaramus in his scriptis: decimam manipularem campestrem in Trambczyno, ad dominum abbatem Gerardum et conventum predictum pertinuisse et pertinere, spectasse et spectare, Iohannique plebano de Trambczyno predicto, super eadem decima perpetuum silentium imponentes. In cuius rei testimonium et evidentiam pleniorem, presentem nostram sentenciam per Iohannem, notarium publicum nostrumque et cause huiusmodi scribam, scribi et publicari nostrique sigilli appensione iussimus communiri. Actum et datum anno Domini M.CCC.XCIX, Indictione septima, pontificatus sanctissimi in Christo patris et domini, domini Bonifacii digna Dei providentia Pape noni anno decimo, die Lune tredecima mensis Octobris, in stuba murata monasterii Landensis, presentibus honorabilibus viris, dominis Nicolao Strozberg preposito, Iaroslao archidiacono Gneznensis, et Matthia rectore de Blandowo ecclesiarum, testibus ad premissa.
Et ego Iohannes Alberti de Wielathow clericus Gneznensis diocesis, publicus imperiali autoritate notarius ipsiusque reverendissimi patris domini archiepiscopi et cause huiusmodi scriba, quia omnibus et singulis premissis, dum sic ut premittitur (agebantur), una cum prenominatis testibus presens fui eaque omnia et singula sic fieri vidi et audivi, meque, aliis arduis occupato negotiis, per alium fidelem notarium scribi feci et in hanc publicam formam redigi, de mandatoque dicti domini archiepiscopi cum sui appensione sigilli me hinc subscripsi, signoque et nomine meis solitis consignavi in fidem et testimonium premissorum.
Landensis, de Ląd. Trambczyno, Trąbczyn. Blandowo, Błędów. Wielathow, Wielatowo.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Erzbischof Dobrogost von Gnesen entscheidet im Streit zwischen dem Kloster Ląd und Jan, dem Pfarrer von Trąbczyn: der Garbenzehnte der Felder des Dorfes Trąbczyn gehört dem Kloster. In der Steinstube des Klosters Ląd, 13. Oktober 1399. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski III, Nr. 2014, aus dem Privilegienbuch von Ląd.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Archbishop Dobrogost of Gniezno decides the dispute between the abbey of Ląd and Jan, parish priest of Trąbczyn: the sheaf tithe of the fields of the village of Trąbczyn belongs to the abbey. In the stone room of the abbey of Ląd, 13 October 1399. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski III, no. 2014, from the Liber privilegiorum of Ląd.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 2014

Dobrogost, Archbishop of the holy church of Gniezno, 13 October 1,399, in the stone room of the monastery of Ląd; adjudges the sheaf tithe of the village of Trąbczyn to the monastery of Ląd against the claim of Jan, parish priest of Trąbczyn.

Włocławek. Liber privilegiorum of the monastery of Ląd, p. 181.

In the name of the Lord, amen. We, Dobrogost, by the grace of God and of the Apostolic See Archbishop of the holy church of Gniezno, make known to all whom it concerns who will have knowledge of these presents that, whereas a matter of dispute had arisen before us between the venerable man Lord Gerard, abbot, and the whole convent of the monastery of Ląd of the Cistercian Order, of our diocese of Gniezno, plaintiffs, on the one part, and the discreet man Jan, rector of the church in Trąbczyn, defendant, on the other, concerning the sheaf tithe of the fields there in Trąbczyn: we therefore, the said parties appearing in person before us, having heard the propositions of the parties and the proofs brought forward which the said parties were wont to use, having taken the counsel of men learned in the law and with the consent, will and assent of the parties themselves, simply and plainly, without the noise and form of a trial, all the windings of lawsuits cut off, having invoked the name of Christ and having Him alone before our eyes and fearing Him, sitting as a tribunal, by this our definitive sentence pronounce, decree and declare in writing: that the sheaf tithe of the fields in Trąbczyn has belonged and belongs, has pertained and pertains, to Lord Abbot Gerard and the said convent, imposing perpetual silence on Jan, the said parish priest of Trąbczyn, concerning that tithe. In testimony and fuller evidence of which we have ordered the present sentence of ours to be written and published by Jan, public notary, our own and the scribe of this cause, and to be strengthened by the hanging of our seal. Done and given in the year of the Lord 1,399, in the seventh indiction, in the tenth year of the pontificate of the most holy father and lord in Christ, Lord Boniface the Ninth, Pope by the worthy providence of God, on Monday the thirteenth day of the month of October, in the stone room of the monastery of Ląd, in the presence of the honourable men, the lords Mikołaj Strozberg, provost, Jarosław, archdeacon of Gniezno, and Maciej, rector of the church of Błędów, witnesses to the foregoing.

And I, Jan son of Wojciech of Wielatowo, cleric of the diocese of Gniezno, public notary by imperial authority and scribe of the most reverend father the Lord Archbishop and of this cause, since I was present together with the witnesses named above at all and each of the foregoing while they were (being done) as is set out above, and saw and heard all and each of them being done so, and, being busy with other arduous affairs, had them written by another faithful notary and brought into this public form, at the command of the said Lord Archbishop with the hanging of his seal I have subscribed here and signed it with my usual sign and name, in faith and testimony of the foregoing.

Landensis, of Ląd. Trambczyno, Trąbczyn. Blandowo, Błędów. Wielathow, Wielatowo.
