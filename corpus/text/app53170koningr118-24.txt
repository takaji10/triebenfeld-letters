Actum in Conin Sabbatho ante Festum Sancti Michaelis Archangeli proximo Die scilicet 29 Mensis 7bris Anno Domini 1782
Ex parte Venerabilis Conventus Landensem contra JM Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen~ Relatio
Videatur hoc loco Relatio ex parte Venerabilis Conventus Landensis contra J Magnificum Antonium Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen~ pro Judicium Ordine~ Generalis Tribunalis Regni Petricovien~ prout Copia extat in productis

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Registervermerk vom September 1782: Bericht über die Ladung, die der Konvent von Ląd gegen Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice, vor das Krontribunal in Petrikau erwirkt hat; eine Abschrift liegt bei den vorgelegten Schriftstücken.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A register note of September 1782: the report of the citation that the convent of Ląd obtained against Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, before the Crown Tribunal at Piotrków; a copy is among the documents produced.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Done in Konin, on Saturday, next before the Feast of Saint Michael the Archangel, namely the 29th day of the month of September, in the Year of the Lord 1782.

On behalf of the Venerable Ląd Convent, against the Illustrious Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — a Report

Let there be seen at this place a Report on behalf of the Venerable Ląd Convent, against the Illustrious Magnificent Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — for the Ordinary General Sessions of the Crown Tribunal of the Kingdom at Piotrków — as the copy stands in the submitted documents.
