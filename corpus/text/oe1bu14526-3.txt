--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Confirms and puts on record a sale, made at Birnbaum in June 1797, of the free village mayor's holding at Dormowe near Birnbaum by Joseph von Jankowski of Ninino to Captain and district tax collector Andreas Ernst Gneust for 2,000 Rthl. cash, with buildings, fields, meadows, fisheries, implements and sowing. Eleven clauses set the annual dues to the Betsche manor, rights to graze 250 sheep, to brew for household use, and to free firewood and building timber. The purchaser bears all costs; Prince Friedrich Ludwig confirms, reserving examination of his entitlements against the old land registers. The deed was read to Jankowski in Polish by a sworn interpreter.
