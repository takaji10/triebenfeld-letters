--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Court-office confirmation from Betsche of the sale, for 2000 Reichsthaler Prussian Courant paid in cash, of the free village-headman's estate No. 1 at Dormowe near Birnbaum by Joseph von Jankowski of Ninino to Captain and District Tax Receiver Andreas Ernst Gneust, with the contract of 24 June 1797 recited in full: annual onera in Tynphe, Szostawa, oats and church dues, rights to 250 manorial sheep, free brewing and Betsche timber. Recognised at Birnbaum, published at Betsche with Polish interpretation by Senator Hintsch, and confirmed by Friedrich Ludwig Fürst zu Hohenlohe with reservation of his claims.

Actum Betsche, the 6th of May 1799.
