--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Confirmation by the Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen Court Office at Betsche of the sale of the free schulz-holding No. 1 at Dormowe near Birnbaum by Joseph von Jankowski of Ninino to Captain and District Tax Receiver Andreas Ernst Gneust for 2,000 Reichsthaler cash, with the enclosed contract of 24 June 1797 recognised at Birnbaum, its schedule of onera (travelling-money, ground-rent, oats, fox-money, church dues), rights to 250 manorial sheep, brewing and Betsche timber, the publication protocol with Polish interpretation by Senator Hintsch, and the Fürst's reserved confirmation.

Melich] We, the Princely Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen Court Office at Betsche, do hereby attest and testify that between Mr Joseph von Jankowski as seller of the one part and Mr Captain and District Tax Receiver Andreas Ernst Gneust as purchaser of the other part, the following deed of sale, recognised before the Magistracy at Birnbaum on the 24th of June 1797, was solemnly published before Us on the 6th of May last, and the whole of the proceedings run word for word thus:

Between the Right Honourable Mr Joseph von Jankowski of [illegible]inino, seller,

and the Honourable Mr Captain and District Tax Receiver Andreas Erenst Gneust, purchaser, there has been, subject to higher confirmation, agreed and concluded the following house contract concerning the free schulz-holding belonging to the former and situated in Dormowe not far from Birnbaum, together with appurtenances, after negotiations previously conducted thereupon; the nearer determinations whereof are confirmed by the present deed of sale drawn up thereon and executed by both

of the participating Gentlemen by their own-hand signatures and seals

seals, executed. Namely, there sells, for himself and his heirs and heirs-at-law,

I. the Mr Joseph von Jankowski named at the outset, his free schulz-holding situated in Dormowe and hitherto possessed by him in property, together with all dwelling and [uncertain: work] buildings belonging thereto, fields, [uncertain: furrows] meadows, [uncertain: ponds] and pond-sites, fisheries, cattle pastures, pleasure and fruit gardens, waters, watercourses, marshes, all as the same lie within their bounds and boundary-stones, together with the farming and ploughing implements belonging to the holding, all according to the fuller content of the house-valuation appended to these house-grounds, together with all that is therein and [unparseable] fixed by nail, wall, [uncertain: church] and root, no less all rights and privileges, also usages, burdens and encumbrances, nothing whatsoever excepted therefrom, and entirely in the measure in which the Mr Seller purchased the said free schulz-holding, by privilege of the 19th of June 1787, from Mr Florian Gelanski and his sister Jadwiga Galanska, in heritable property,

II. to the last-mentioned Mr Captain and District Tax

Receiver Andreas Ernst Gneust, in heritable property, for 2000 rt., in words two thousand Reichsthaler Prussian Courant, as a whole, fixed and this day cash-paid purchase sum, for the due receipt whereof the Mr Seller does also acquit the Mr Purchaser in the best and most legally binding form.

III. All costs of this transaction of sale, among which belong also the 10 per cent, or withdrawal-monies, customary in former times, the Mr Purchaser bears alone, and on this account he shall render the Mr Seller no accounting.

IV. The yearly onera consist, according to the aforementioned privilege,

a., in 25 Tynphe travelling-money to the Betsche manor

b., in 5 Szostaki ground-rent

c., in 5 quarters of oats

d., in 1 Tynph fox-money

e., in 2 [uncertain: Rappamten] and

f., the customary church dues, which the Mr Purchaser assumes like his predecessor and has vowed to render.

50 head with free pasturage upon the Dormowe ground. Should he however wish to keep more, then for each head kept above this number a [uncertain: Büttchen] of the Silesian [uncertain: Heinige], one Silbergroschen, shall be paid to the Betsche manor.

VI. He may brew beer freely for his household use, but sell or give away nothing thereof. Failing which he will have to pay the penalty imposed upon him by the Betsche manor.

VII. The Mr Purchaser has firewood free from the Betsche forest for his whole household, servants and livestock, also for brewing; likewise the needful building timber, yet not otherwise than upon the instruction of the Commissarius or forester appointed thereover. Just as those oaks outside his tree-gardens which stand upon the fields or grounds of this free holding are to be used by the Mr Purchaser in the same manner as by the other free schulzen of the Betsche key-district.

VIII. The holding cannot as yet be given quite definitely as to the fields, in respect of the number of Hufen. It is however sold as it lies within its [uncertain: bounds] and borders, in buildings, fields, gardens and meadows etc., also with everything that the present lessee Mr Hertzog received pro Inventario in cattle and ploughing implements, and which is moreover to be separately [uncertain: appended], also with complete winter and summer sowing.

X. The Mr Seller now consents to the corresponding rectification of the Titulus possessionis in the name of the Mr Purchaser in the mortgage book to be there kept for the purpose. Finally, and

At today's term for the publication of the deed of sale concluded extrajudicially on the 24th of June 1797 between Joseph von Jankowski, lessee at Ninino[uncertain], and the District Tax Receiver Andreas Ernst Gneust at Birnbaum, concerning the free schulz-holding situated under No. 1 at Dormowe, and recognised sub eodem dato before the Justice Magistracy at Birnbaum, there appeared:

1, the seller Joseph von Jankowski

2. the purchaser District Tax Receiver Gneust; and since the former was not known to the court, he was identified by the leaseholder Eichaust as the person for whom he gave himself out. To the seller von Jankowski, not being master of the German tongue, the aforesaid deed of sale of the 24th of June 1797 was thereupon read by the sworn interpreter Senator

Hintsch, slowly and distinctly in the Polish tongue, but to the purchaser in the German tongue, and both declared once more deliberately before the court

how they hereby once again approve this deed of sale in all its points and will faithfully hold to it for ever. In particular the seller von Jankowski declared that he hereby resigns the sold parcel to the purchaser District Tax Receiver Gneust for himself and his heirs, and renounces all further claims for ever; likewise that he acknowledges himself fully satisfied in respect of the purchase price, and therefore approves that the Titulus possessionis be rectified for the purchaser at the mortgage book upon the ground of the deed of sale.

Wherewith this protocol of publication was

closed, and after the reading aloud and the interpretation had taken place was approved throughout by the comparents and, in evidence thereof, signed by their own hand in Actis: Joseph Jankowski

Andreas Ernst Gneust

Eichaust

Schleich

Thus done, Betsche, the 6th of May 1799. Princely Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen Court Office here. Schenck
