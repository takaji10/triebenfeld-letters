--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A damaged report, chiefly political gossip: a valuation has arrived at Gosztiszyn but no covering letter; Zerboni, having drafted an opinion on the organisation of the Grand Duchy of Posen with his secretary Schmidt and been refused a counter-signature by Prince Radziwill, declared himself the governing lord and has been packed off to Stettin. Also rumours from Petersburg of the Senate rejecting the Polish constitution. Triebenfeld plans journeys to Betsche and on to press the indemnification business.
