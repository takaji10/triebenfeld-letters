--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Damaged and lacunose report noting that a valuation has been delivered at Gosztiszyn, to be fetched and used. At length recounts the quarrel over the opinion on the organisation of the Grand Duchy of Posen: Zerboni, drafting it with his secretary Schmidt, was refused co-signature by Prince Radziwill and disparaged him, whereupon Radziwill complained to the King and Zerboni was sent to Stettin. Adds rumours from Petersburg of the Senate rejecting the Polish constitution, and plans travel to Betsche and Sterbui for the indemnification business.
