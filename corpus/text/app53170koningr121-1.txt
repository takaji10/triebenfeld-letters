--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Eintrag vom 9. Juni 1788 im Buch des Burggerichts Konin. Ludwik Żurawski, im Dienst von Antoni Prusimski von Kolno, Starost von Niszczewice und Erbherr von Trąbczyn, führt dem Amt ein Gespann vor: einen Wagen, vorn mit unbeschlagenen und hinten mit beschlagenen Rädern, zwei Ochsen, eine Kuh und eine kastanienbraune Stute. Das Gespann gehört Marcin Gryndel, einem Bauern aus Łomowo, einem Gut von Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa, das zu dessen Hauptgut Łukom gehört. Gryndel war in den Kiefernwald gefahren, der zu Prusimskis Erbgut Trąbczyn gehört und nie strittig war, und fuhr von dort schon einen gefällten, zum Bauen tauglichen Baum nach Hause; der Trąbczyner Waldhüter hielt ihn im Wald an. Nach der Vorführung überlässt Żurawski Tiere und Wagen bei der Kanzlei zur Herausgabe an Gryndel, der sie sofort verlangt; darüber ist am selben Tag eine Quittung eingetragen.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
An entry of 9 June 1788 in the book of the castle court at Konin. Ludwik Żurawski, in the service of Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice and heir of Trąbczyn, brings a team before the office: a cart with unshod front wheels and iron-shod rear wheels, two oxen, a cow and a chestnut mare. The team belongs to Marcin Gryndel, a peasant of Łomowo, an estate of Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, which belongs to his principal estate of Łukom. Gryndel had driven into the pine wood that belongs to Prusimski's hereditary estate of Trąbczyn and was never in dispute, and was already carting home from it a felled tree fit for building; the Trąbczyn forest keeper stopped him in the wood. After showing them, Żurawski leaves the animals and the cart at the chancery to be given back to Gryndel, who asks for them at once; a receipt for this was entered the same day.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Done in Konin, on 9 June in the Year of the Lord 1788.

On behalf of the Most Honourable Prusimski — Presentation of Livestock and a Horse with a Cart joined to the same.

Coming in person to the present Municipal Court Office and Records of Konin, the Honourable Ludovicus Żurawski, serving the Most Honourable Antoni in Kolno Prusimski, Starost [district administrator] of Niszczewice, hereditary owner of the estate of Trąbczyn, and adhering to the common law, presented to the said Office a cart — with two front wheels, bare, that is without an iron plate placed on top, that is with bare front wheels; but with the rear wheels having an iron plate placed on top, that is with rear wheels shod with iron — together with two rear oxen: one of yellowish coat, that is of pale coat; and the other of mixed colour, that is of mottled, overly-white coat; and also a cow of striped coat, that is of dun mottled coat; and a chestnut mare harnessed to the above-mentioned cart with its cart stanchions — the oxen and cow in a yoke, that is in yokes, the mare in reins, that is in traces, all pulling the same cart — [all found on] the Peasant Martin Gryndel from the estate of Łomowo of the Right Honourable Scibor Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, hereditary owner of the said estate, belonging to the more principal hereditary estates of Łukom of this Right Honourable Scibor Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa; the serf [found in] the pine forest belonging to the estate of Trąbczyn of the above-mentioned Most Honourable

Antoni in Kolno Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, Knight of the Order of Saint Stanislaus, and belonging and pertaining to his hereditary estates, and not subject to any dispute regarding the hereditary estate at any time; while [the serf] was riding into the said pine forest and already transporting from it a cut tree — suitable and fit for buildings — to the aforementioned estate of Łomowo, to his own home; [all of the foregoing] intercepted in this very pine forest by the Guardian of the Forests of Trąbczyn, and by reason of the presentation that followed in the manner set forth above, driven and brought to the present Municipal Chancellery of Konin. Which oxen, cow, and mare, with the cart harnessed to the same, standing before the Chancellery, [he] presented. The same presenter, the Honourable Ludovicus Żurawski, left the said [animals] at the present Municipal Chancellery of Konin for the purpose of the extradition of the same to the above-mentioned Peasant Gryndel, requesting the immediate extradition of these following the aforementioned Presentation — (as the aforesaid Quietance of Extradition pertaining to the matter of his Chancellery Office, drawn up in the Act on this day, having followed, sets forth more fully) — and left, and leaves [them], by these presents.
