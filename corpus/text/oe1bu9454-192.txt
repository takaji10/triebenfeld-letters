Mein lieber Freund!

Pächter zum Teufel gejagt. Wann mir Gott oder Teufeln und
höllenbrände von Menschen Querstreiche machen daß ich vor Argus schier
crepiren muß den hört alle herrlichkeit auf¬
Glenck seyt hier hatt sich mit die Olesnicer Hollenbrände zusammen
gethan dazu einen gewißen Kramplich Bergemann und Rybbeh ingagirt die

zurük es ist zu toll wie mirs geht. Hilft sich indes meine Natur
und ich bin wieder gesund den soll die Buben alle der Teufel hohlen.
Die Pächter sind wie vor gesagt alle zum Teufel und von
Weinachten und Johann ab muß alles so verstehen, daß nur Gelder

hier elend liege kan ich nicht in Berlin würken, sobald ich mich in etwas
erhohle komme ich nach Breslau und gehn gleich mit meine Töchter nach Berlin

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Writing from his sickbed in the fifth week of illness, Triebenfeld reports 360000 Rthl promised him in Berlin, the pacification of stirred-up peasants and the dismissal of tenants. Glenck has allied with the Olesnica agitators, engaging Kramplich, Bergemann and Rybbeh, and circulated a lampoon among prospective colonist purchasers. Weigel and Lichnowski are to be paid from the Justice Fund and cede their mortgage. The Prince is still to receive 190000 Rthl cash and 40000 in perpetuity; receipts from Swięcia, Olesnica and Szetlewek are itemised, and the Giese judgment is criticised.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
My dear friend! There are lying in Berlin 360000 Rthl which have been promised to me. Only I lie here and have been keeping to my bed now in the 5th week; I have vexed myself here quite to ruin. Meanwhile I have now brought everything here into order and entirely quieted the peasants who had been stirred up by arch-rogues, and also driven the tenants to the devil. When God or devils and hell-brands of men play me crosswise tricks, so that I must all but perish of vexation — there all splendour comes to an end. Glenck has been here and has made common cause with the Olesnica hell-brands, and has engaged thereto a certain Kramplich, Bergemann and Rybbeh; the [text lost] pack of hell stirs the people up and turns the purchasers away; it is too mad, how it goes with me. If meanwhile my constitution helps itself and I am well again, then the devil shall take all the rascals. The tenants are, as said before, all [uncertain: to the bottom of the devil], and from Christmas and St. John's on everything must be so understood, that only moneys may be raised, and that nothing further must arise for expenditures. Herr Weigel will be paid; for that I had, since I receive from the Justice Fund the money for Weigel and Lichnowski; these must cede their mortgage, and receive their money; I have already said this to the witnesses at Weigel's too. But while I lie here in misery I cannot work in Berlin; as soon as I recover somewhat I shall come to Breslau and go at once with my daughters to Berlin.

From here I can as yet draw nothing, but from Michaelmas on much money should come in little by little. It would have been easy here to obtain moneys, if I had been willing to give the parcels away for a third of the price, but God preserve me from that. — Now the Prince still receives 190000 Rthl in cash and 40000 in perpetuity, that is, the annual revenues. The Prince has already received 10000 Rthl from Swięcia, 10000 Rthl from Olesnica, 12000 Rthl from Szetlewek, and 9000 Rthl have been given for grounds, all of which has gone off for the costs and interest, and also upon old debts. The peasants' revolution and the Plenck rascalities have had their use — I have indeed had to give much back, but now it is also the better and more amply set out for it. Enough, all will go well — if only I am well again first — writing comes very hard to me. So this yet, that I am unremittingly Your v Triebenfeld. Trąbczyn, the 12th of Aug. 1806. Upon the whole Prochenkamr affair I must suspend my judgment, for I do not know it. — As for me, there is wanting a man who knows the locality entirely, and somewhat much money — the Stöz: praise is suspect to me, well evaded — the Pro Memoria of the Koſchenkin officials give sufficient [text lost] friend and serve direction. The enclosed lampoon Glenck has made upon me and had it distributed among the colonists who are minded to purchase; the original I have sent to His Serene Highness — It is drawn up and dealt out by Glenck's own lofty hand.

To the Olesnica [uncertain: Schluzbuben] he has of course given attestations and helped to support their cause, and for this they have promised him to sell him the Voigtey. Yesterday the judgment came down upon the fellows: 1. Giese may not evict the fellows; 2. he shall discharge the debts encumbering Olesnica; 3. he shall apply for the consent; 4. when that has been given, have the hereditary lease entered in the mortgage book; 6. but Giese is refused, in that the Prince may not hand over the estates to him before consent has hitherto been issued. That is a sentence at which the dogs might well howl. I shall now at once apply to the authority for the consent; then the resolution will be that such [uncertain: Curiellen] may not possess noble estates, and the Chamber will throw them out; then all the feud has an end.
