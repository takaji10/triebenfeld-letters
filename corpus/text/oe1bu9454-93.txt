--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Encloses a royal decision obtained on the Prince's latest petition, with Minister von Struensee laying the files before the King and Colonel von Köckritz, praised as the Prince's only real advocate at court, presenting a memorandum. In it Triebenfeld offers, should Polajewo obstruct matters, to lease the estates for 30 years, building six peasant farms yearly and clearing waste land at his own cost, paying 20,000 Rthl rent and making good any shortfall. He will next pursue the 7,000 Rthl for the Niedernhall business, and advises courteous but plain speaking to the departing minister.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Lord!

To Your Most Serene Highness I transmit herewith the All-Highest letter which has issued upon the last petition. I hope that the same will have answered Your Highness's wishes. This very day the Minister von Struensee had to lay all the files before His Majesty; it now remains to be awaited whether everything will be duly gone into.

The truly upright Colonel von K., who commends himself most respectfully to Your Serene Highness, promises the most desirable outcome of the matter.

H. C. H. Beym is indeed mightily preoccupied; still, he is too upright and open a man, and therefore I fear nothing from that side.

The honest councillor of war von Cöln has set the matter out quite well, as Your Serene Highness will gather from the enclosure.

I have given this letter (which will remain no more than a plan, since I received it too late), together with the Pro Memoria, to the Colonel von Köch:, and he has had the kindness to lay it before His Majesty today. In this Pro Memoria, however, I have made the offer on my own part that, if Polajewo should stand in the way of the matter, I would take these estates on lease for 30 years — at the same time building six peasant farms every year out of my own means — clearing all waste grounds at my own cost — paying 20000 Rthl of rent annually — and, at the end of the lease years, making good whatever of the yield of 20000 Rthl could not be shown. This heavy battery, it is thought, would at one stroke [uncertain: elementiren] all the friendly pieces, and since one already stood on the enemy's flank and had pressed him away from the main [uncertain: April], the victory would infallibly remain with Your Serene Highness. For the present I mean only to work a few days on the matter of the 7000 Rthl, so that the Niedernhall affair may be happily concluded.

The Minister has departed; he has received a snuffbox

worth several thousand [uncertain: vlld], with which a very fine letter was enclosed.

For the rest, neither Your Serene Highness nor I owe him any thanks — the good man — has done nothing for anyone except – but may Your Serene Highness have the grace to meet him handsomely, to believe everything — yet tell him plainly that You see one wishes to vex You, and in secret [illegible] that You would endure this no longer, but would from now on say straight out what You thought and open the King's eyes directly. I have suffered much these days; my openness and straightforwardness [illegible] many a villain does shy from it. Colonel von Köckritz is a worthy, upright man; he alone has disposed the truly good King in Your Serene Highness's favour – and has explained matters to the honest cabinet councillor B. more uprightly than all the others — In deepest reverence I remain, until death, Your Most Serene Highness's

Berlin, 18th December 1798.

Most loyally and humbly Your

von Triebenfeld
