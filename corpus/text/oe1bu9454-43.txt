Durchlauchtigster Fürst
Gnädigster Fürst und Herr!
Nach ein so eben vom Geheimen Rath Amelang aus Berlin erhaltenes Urtel hat man
dem Lahr, ganz dem Landrecht entgegen, baar Geld statt Papiere wie er sie
gegeben zu erkannt, und es sollen die ihm noch kommenden 3000 Rthl in Kling-Convent bezahlt
werden. Im Fall ich selbige nicht zahlen oder deponiren kan, soll ich, da es ein
Wechsel Prozeß ist arretirt werden. Amelang hat zwar appellirt, wo rette ich
mich aber einstweilen? Ich bin durch diese Affaire wahrlich sehr unglücklich, und ich muß,
wan diesen Kummer nicht bald Gränzen gesezt wird, noch völlig zu Grunde gehen.
Ich habe durch ein ganzes Jahr gearbeitet, Gesundheit vermögen und Credit dabey
verlohren. Mehrere Vorteile habe ich auch schon dadurch für Ewr Durchlaucht errungen.
Was ist aber mein Lohn? Haß, Verfolgung, und Gefängnis am Ende
Mein Innern empört sich und das Herzbarst mich, wan ich nur diesen Gedanken denke
Ich bitte und beschwöre Ewr Durchlaucht bezahlen, was heilig ist die Sache zu beher,
zigen und schnell Rath zu schaffen, damit die Gelder baar oder im Staats Papieren
Nach Grevenitz und Amelangs Versicherung wird der
deponirt werden.
Prozeß in Appellations Senat gewis gewonnen. Der Lahr muß Pappiere nehmen
wodurch an 1500 Rthl Circa gerettet werden.
Ewr Durchlaucht verliehren in dieser Sache an Ende nichts als die Kosten, die sich durch
dies Jahr zwar sehr gehäuft inzwischen aber den Weg gebahrt haben, worauf
alles am erwünschten Ziel gebracht werden kan.
das Geld für Lahr erhalten Sie laut Befehl des Franz: Gen. Gouvernements

dem Justitiarius Honrichs zu mir her beschieden, und werde das nötige mit ihm verabreiben

der Prozeiß mit Oppenheimer ist im besten Gange und da kan man mit Papieren
wegkommen und gewis die Hälfte ersparen.
Erbarmen sich Ewr Durchlaucht nur nun auch meiner und schaffen schnellen Rath
zur deponirung der 3000 Rthl entweder in baaren Gelde oder in so viel

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports Amelang's news that Lahr has won judgment for 3000 Rthl in hard coin rather than papers, exposing Triebenfeld to arrest under bill-of-exchange law; begs the Prince to provide cash or state papers for the deposit, since appeal to the Senate of Appeals should save some 1500 Rthl. Notes the Oppenheimer suit going well, the French General Government's repayment and lifting of the sequestration once interest on circa 10000 Rthl is paid, Honrichs summoned, and pleads for 600 hundredweight of iron promised to Michaelis.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

According to a judgment just now received from Privy Councillor Amelang in Berlin, Lahr has been awarded, quite contrary to the territorial law, cash money instead of the papers as he gave them, and the 3000 Rthl still coming to him are to be paid in hard coin. In the event that I cannot pay or deposit the same, I am to be arrested, since it is a bill-of-exchange suit. Amelang has indeed appealed, but where do I find rescue in the meantime? I am truly most unhappy through this affair, and if bounds are not soon set to this distress I must go utterly to ruin. I have laboured a whole year through, and lost health, means and credit in it. I have also already won several advantages for Your Serene Highness thereby. But what is my reward? Hatred, persecution, and prison at the end. My inward parts revolt and my heart bursts in me when I do but think this thought. I beg and implore Your Serene Highness to pay, as is sacred, to take the matter to heart and to provide counsel swiftly, so that the monies may be deposited in cash or in state papers. suit will certainly be won in the Senate of Appeals. Lahr must take papers, whereby some 1500 Rthl circa will be saved.

Your Serene Highness in the end loses nothing in this matter but the costs, which have indeed mounted up very much through this year, but have meanwhile paved the way by which everything can be brought to the desired end.

The money for Lahr you will receive back by order of the French General Government, and the sequestration will be lifted forthwith once the interest on this sum, circa 10000 Rthl, has been paid. To that end I have summoned the justiciary Honrichs to come to me, and shall arrange the necessary with him

The suit with Oppenheimer is going in the best train, and there one can get off with papers and certainly save the half. Have mercy now also on me, Your Serene Highness, and provide swift counsel for the depositing of the 3000 Rthl, either in cash money or in so much state paper at the rate of exchange.

It is only a great good fortune that I am away from Berlin, and had I not concluded a contract with the merchant Michaelis for 600 hundredweight of iron and received 2000 Rthl from him, so as to buy myself free to some degree, I should now already be sitting in the Hausvogtey in Berlin. In this regard I beseech Your Serene Highness most imploringly to give the 600 hundredweight of iron without fail, so that I may keep my word to this man and not come to harm here as well. If Michaelis receives 300 hundredweight of the iron by the end of April and 300 hundredweight by about Midsummer Day, then great reliance may thereafter be placed on this man. The distress and the dread that I may be sought out here too by Lahr — presses me to the ground; I can say nothing further now than: rescue me speedily, Your Serene Highness, and do not let me be utterly ruined and branded before the world for my faithful devotion, for the sake of Your cause. I remain, until death,

Your Most Serene Highness — Do send Your letters, Your Serene Highness, by all means by express messenger to Professor Rhode at Breslau; he then sends them on here again by express — Only rescue me.

Kontop

30th January

1809

most faithful servant

v. Triebenfeld
