# oe1bu9454-283  283  letter
# 1815  Wien
# /letters/oe1bu9454/283/

Monseigneur,
Il suffit que Votre Altesse Serenissime daigne me donner des ordres, pour
que je mʼempresse de Lui obeïr selon mes facultés, je ne suis pas assés
presomptueux pour oser croire quʼil soit possible que mon intervention
ajoute un prix à une lettre qui vient de la part de Vôtre Altesse
Serenissime ; Pour executer neanmoins Vos volontés Monseigneur, je remettrai
incessament au Prince Chancellier dʼEtat la lettre qui lui est addressée
je saisirai cette occasion pour lui mettre sous les yeux les desirs que Vôtre
Altesse Serenissime a daignée me temoigner. Trop heureux si en executant
les ordres du Prince Chancellier dʼetat je pouvois être lʼorgane dont il ſe
servit pour remplir les volontés de Vôtre Altesse Serenissime. Ce nʼest
quʼici à Vienne que jʼai appris à connoitre Mr. de Triebenfeld qui

mʼetoit entierement inconnu jusquʼà present. Il lui suffisoit du reste
de me parler de Vôtre Altesse Serenissime pour que je pretasse la
plus grande attention à tout ce quʼil avoit à me dire.
Daignéz agréer Monseigneur lʼexpression du très profond respect
avec lequel jʼai lʼhonneur dʼêtre
Monseigneur
de Votre Altesse Serenissime
le très humble & tres soumis
serviteur. Beugelin
Vienne le 26 Mars 1815.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Undertakes, at the Fürst's request, to hand the enclosed letter to the Prince Chancellor of State (Hardenberg) without delay and to put the Fürst's wishes before him personally, professing that his own intervention can add nothing to a letter from the Fürst and hoping to serve as the Chancellor's instrument in satisfying them. Notes that he made Mr. de Triebenfeld's acquaintance only at Vienna, having been previously unknown to him.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Monseigneur, It is enough that Your Serene Highness should deign to give me orders, for me to hasten to obey Him according to my faculties; I am not so presumptuous as to dare believe it possible that my intervention should add any value to a letter which comes from the part of Your Serene Highness. Nevertheless, in order to execute Your wishes, Monseigneur, I shall without delay deliver to the Prince Chancellor of State the letter which is addressed to him; I shall seize this occasion to lay before his eyes the desires which Your Serene Highness has deigned to signify to me. Too happy should I be if, in executing the orders of the Prince Chancellor of State, I might be the organ of which he made use to fulfil the wishes of Your Serene Highness. It is only here at Vienna that I have come to know Mr. de Triebenfeld, who

was until now entirely unknown to me. It sufficed him, moreover, to speak to me of Your Serene Highness for me to lend the greatest attention to all that he had to say to me. Deign to accept, Monseigneur, the expression of the very profound respect with which I have the honour to be, Monseigneur, Your Serene Highness's very humble and very submissive servant. [uncertain: Bequelin] Vienna, the 26 March 1815.
