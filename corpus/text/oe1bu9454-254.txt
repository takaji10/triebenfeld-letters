--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Complains of long silence and want of funds while lobbying in Vienna, where the Prince Chancellor is inaccessible and the attendants who really decide must be entertained nightly at 20–30 Rthl; he asked 1400 Rthl for four or five weeks but has had only 640, owes Barbe 94 ducats, and demands 100 ducats more. Lists six benefits promised: recovery of the Polish estates, shedding Leixner and Meyer Bernhard, discharge of the Curish debt of 461000 Rthl, and release from the Koschentin bonds. Enclosed receipts for monies from Referendarius Bally; begs 200 Rthl for his starving children; asks letters be sent via Chief Post Director Mann in Vienna and the Koschentin sale kept secret from creditors.

It is very sad that I must always wait so long for an answer and for support. Since everything goes so exceedingly happily through the honest en endeavour, I have no wish to break off, but rather to bring everything to the desired goal – that this cannot be done here in these times with empty hands is surely easy enough to estimate; daily I must be together with the men who actually take effect, for the Prince Chancellor is too seldom to be seen and in truth has no time to occupy himself with matters of this kind. Only these good men who are always about him alone must take effect and direct everything — no evening passes on which they do not visit me – 20-30 Rthl go the way, I do not know where they get to – One says that upon good news a good draught tastes well, and truly I give it to him gladly; men of that sort deserve it of him – I believed I should be here 4 - 5 weeks, and for that I demanded 1400 Rthl. Now I have already been sitting here going on 12 [uncertain: weeks] and in that long time I have received 640 Rthl – today I am already 94 ducats in debt to Barbe. Now if you also send 100 ducats, what remains to me, for I must hand over his money to Barbe – What I have received then amounts to 940 Rthl, and that certainly and truly does not suffice for so raging a length of time. My affairs stand badly there; I am abandoning my children and my own, for since everything is once going happily, I now wish also to bring your affairs quite to the desired goal – and to rescue you entirely, as has always been my wish – whereafter I may with every right hope in turn for rescue from you, most gracious Prince – It is no small matter what I have already done here and still
