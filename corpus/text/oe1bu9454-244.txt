--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Reports from the Congress: the great Vienna manoeuvre, regiments given to Grand Duke Constantin and others, the King of Wurtemberg driving in an eight-horse Oriental turn-out, and the King of Prussia toasting the soldiery from the balcony amid unprecedented Austro-Prussian cordiality. Notes the Estates' deputation to Metternich seeking the Emperor as their head, talk of Saxony, and Bar. v. Hum.'s helpful exertions; promises the Prince quiet at Schlawenschitz and his enemy's fall, under strict secrecy; asks about Hardenberg's report on Cosmar, the Brieg Superior Provincial Court and annulling the 1811 settlement; awaits instructions. Holland's Sovereign Prince takes the royal title.
