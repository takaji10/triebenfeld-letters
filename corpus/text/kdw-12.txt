Dokument Nr 283
Poznań, 31 stycznia 1384
Mikołaj z Wenecji sędzia kaliski przysądza Wrąbczyn(ek) klasztorowi Cystersów w Lądzie.
Or.: Kraków, WAP. D. Dep. 344. Pergamin 229 - 219 × 130 - 126 + 23 mm. Trzy dziury na prawym marginesie, dół wystrzępiony. Nacięcia po pasku do przywieszenia pieczęci. Na odwrocie informacje o treści i sygnatury (XIV - XVIII w.).
In nomine Domini amen. Nos Nicolaus iudex Kalisiensis1 notum facimus, quibus expedit, universis, | quod Poznanie die dominico ante Purificacionem Marie Virginis gloriose sub anno Domini milessimo | trecentessimo octuagesimo quarto, venientes ad nostram presenciam Petrko heres de Lang2 eciam | ex parte fratris ipsius uterini parte ex una, et religiosus vir Rudgerus claviger et procurator fratrum et conventus mo- nasterii de Land3 parte ex altera, in presencia reverendi in Christo patris domini Johannis ducis Opoliensis et episcopi Poznaniensis4 et strennuorum militum ac nobilium: Peregrini capitanei Polonie5, Dirscone castellano Santocensi6, Franczkone pincerna7, Stephano subpincerna Poznaniensibus8 ac honorabilium virorum: Alberti archidiaconi Poznaniensis9, Nicolai cantoris Poznaniensis10, in termino ipsis peremptorie signato pro hereditate dicta Vronczino11 coram nobis iuridice litigabant. Nos vero perpendentes bene causas ipsorum ex informacione dominorum iam scriptorum et aliorum terre seniorum, predicto Rudgeri clavigeris nomine tocius conventus monasterii supradicti adiudicavimus dare investituram ville supradicte et presentibus adiudicavimus, non removentes Petrkonem nec fratrem ipsius agere contra villam iuridice memoratam. Ita tamen, quod iure possint attemptare villam supradictam, nullam violen- ciam monasterio memorato facientes. In cuius rei testimonium sigillum nostrum presentibus duximus appendendum. Datum et actum Poznanie die, anno, loco quibus supra.

6Dziersław, Dzierżek, Grochola z Ostroroga, Nałęcz, kasztelan santocki 1383 - 1387 (Spisy wlkp.).

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Mikołaj von Wenecja, Richter von Kalisch, spricht in einem Streit zwischen Piotrko, Erben von Łęg, und Rudger, dem Schlüsselträger des Klosters Ląd, dem Kloster die Investitur des Gutes „Vronczino“ zu; Piotrko und sein Bruder dürfen es auf dem Rechtsweg weiter fordern. Nach den Herausgebern ist Wrąbczyn oder Wrąbczynek gemeint. Posen, 31. Januar 1384. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski VI, Nr. 283, nach dem Original.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Mikołaj of Wenecja, judge of Kalisz, in a suit between Piotrko, heir of Łęg, and Rudger, key-keeper of the abbey of Ląd, adjudges to the abbey the investiture of the estate of "Vronczino"; Piotrko and his brother may still pursue it at law. The editors take it to be Wrąbczyn or Wrąbczynek. Poznań, 31 January 1384. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski VI, no. 283, from the original.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 283

Poznań, 31 January 1384

Mikołaj of Wenecja, judge of Kalisz, adjudges Wrąbczyn(ek) to the Cistercian monastery at Ląd.

Original: Kraków, WAP. D. Dep. 344. Parchment 229–219 × 130–126 + 23 mm. Three holes in the right margin, the bottom frayed. Slits from the strip for hanging the seal. On the back notes on the content and shelfmarks (14th–18th century).

In the name of the Lord, amen. We, Mikołaj, judge of Kalisz¹, make known to all whom it concerns | that at Poznań on the Sunday before the Purification of the glorious Virgin Mary, in the year of the Lord one thousand | three hundred and eighty-four, there came before us Piotrko, heir of Łęg², also | on behalf of his uterine brother, on the one part, and the religious man Rudger, key-keeper and procurator of the brothers and convent of the mo- nastery of Ląd³, on the other, in the presence of the reverend father in Christ Lord Jan, Duke of Opole and Bishop of Poznań⁴, and of the strenuous knights and nobles: Pielgrzym, captain of Poland⁵, Dzierżek, castellan of Santok⁶, Franczek the cupbearer⁷, Stefan the under-cupbearer of Poznań⁸, and of the honourable men: Wojciech, archdeacon of Poznań⁹, Mikołaj, cantor of Poznań¹⁰; and on the peremptory term set for them they went to law before us for the estate called Vronczino¹¹. And we, weighing their causes well on the information of the lords already written and of other elders of the land, adjudged that the said Rudger the key-keeper, in the name of the whole convent of the said monastery, should be given the investiture of the said village, and have adjudged it by these presents, not removing Piotrko or his brother from acting at law against the said village. Provided that they may try for the said village by law, doing no violence to the said monastery. In witness of which we have had our seal hung on these presents. Given and done at Poznań on the day, in the year and at the place above.

¹Mikołaj of Wenecja, see above, no. 228/23.

²Piotr of Łęg near Śrem), most probably the same as the later steward of Kalisz 1392, then deputy judge of Poznań 1393–1398 (Spisy wlkp.; SHGWlkp.). His brother not identified.

³The key-keeper of Ląd, Rudger, not otherwise known.

⁴Jan Kropidło, see above, no. 282/1.

⁵Peregryn, Pielgrzym, of Węgleszyn (in the land of Sandomierz), of the Jelita arms, under-chamberlain of Sandomierz 1379–1400, captain of Greater Poland 1383–1387 (PSB XXV, pp. 602–603).

⁶Dziersław, Dzierżek, Grochola of Ostroróg, of the Nałęcz arms, castellan of Santok 1383–1387 (Spisy wlkp.).

⁷Franciszek of Cerekwica, see above, no. 190/1.

⁸Stefan the under-cupbearer occurs only here; most probably, though, this is the steward of Poznań 1376–1399, Stefan of Trląg and Głogowiec (see above, no. 258/2).

⁹Wojciech of Będlewo, see above, no. 227/17.

¹⁰Mikołaj, cantor of Poznań, confirmed 1380–1384 (here), probably the deputy of (or the same as) Mikołaj Strosberg (see above, no. 250/4).

¹¹Wrąbczyn, just west of Zagórów, or Wrąbczynek (west of Wrąbczyn): in both these settlements, called Wronczyn (Little and Great), the monastery of Ląd later had its shares.
