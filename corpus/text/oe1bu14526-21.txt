--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Punctation in twelve paragraphs between Peter Friederich August v. Triebenfeld, acting as General Mandatarius for the Fürst zu Hohenlohe, and Franz Meierowitz for the sale of the estate Wittow in the Warta district with all appurtenances, in Pausch und Bogen and without warranty: 20,000 Reichsthaler Courant (8,000 at George, 12,000 at Johanny) plus a perpetual annual canon of 500 Rthl, redeemable by 10,000 Rthl capital. Conveyance awaits annulment of the sale to Honrichs and a royal concession to hold noble estates; costs shared, with 6,000 Rthl owed the widowed Frau Obristen von Schliesen possibly left standing at priority. Witnessed by Friedrich Willhelm Eger.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Between the Royal Councillor of War and Forests, also General Plenipotentiary of His Most Serene Highness the reigning Prince and Lord of Hohenlohe, Herr Peter Friederich August v. Triebenfeld, and Herr Franz Meierowicz, the following Punctation has been agreed upon and drawn up.

§ 1. Herr von Triebenfeld undertakes, as General Mandatarius of the Herr Fürst zu Hohenlohe, to convey the estate Wittow, situated in the Warta district, with all its inhabitants and all appurtenances, taverns, lands, Scholtzerey, waters, buildings and inventory, with all rights and usufructs, by whatever name they may be called, nothing thereof excepted, just as the same was purchased from the widowed Frau Obristen von Schliesen, to Herr Meierowitz as heritable and absolute property; as soon as the sale with the Honrichs — who is neither capable of possession nor, still less, solvent — shall have been annulled, for which purpose the requisite proceedings have already been instituted before the competent authority.

§ 2. The sale is effected in Pausch und Bogen, and upon the conclusion of the contract no further warranty shall be

rendered by the seller.

§ 3. Herr Meierowitz shall pay at George, that is on the 23rd of April of this year, for the abovenamed estate a cash purchase price of Twenty Thousand Reichsthaler in Courant, namely at George, that is on the 23rd of April of this year, Eight Thousand Reichsthaler, and at the coming Johanny of this year, Twelve Thousand Reichsthaler.

§ 4. Besides this purchase money stipulated and fixed in § 3, the purchaser Herr Meierowitz shall pay to His Serene Highness the seller, or wherever the same shall most highly direct [Anweisung], yearly and indeed in perpetuity, a canon of Five Hundred Rthl in Courant.

§ 5. This canon shall be paid in half-yearly instalments post numerando to the purchaser, under pain of the compulsory measures laid down in the provincial law.

§ 6. Should, however, the purchaser be minded, and find it convenient to his interest, to pay Ten Thousand Rthl of capital in place of the canon of Five Hundred Rthl fixed in § 4, in order to discharge himself of this burden of debt — which, as a purchase residuum not subject to being called in, is to be entered upon this estate Wittow as a first mortgage — then this shall be conceded on

the part of the seller; only, if the purchaser wishes to redeem the canon by payment of the Ten Thousand Rthl, a half-yearly notice must first be given.

§ 7. Although it is fixed in § 3 that Meierowitz will pay Eight Thousand Reichsthaler on the 23rd of April of this year and Twelve Thousand Reichsthaler at Johanni of this year, it is here further agreed that, in case the Frau Obristen von Schliesen should wish to let the Six Thousand Reichsthaler still due to her according to the mortgage book stand further outstanding, His Serene Highness will permit this to be done and, with the canon of Five Hundred Thaler to be rendered yearly, will stand behind and grant the Frau Obristen v. Schliefen the priority.

§ 8. The Eight Thousand Thaler mentioned in § 3, however, must be promptly paid on the 23rd of April of this year, to which the purchaser binds himself in the most earnest manner.

§ 9. The drawing up and execution of a judicial contract concerning the estate Witttow shall take place as soon as the purchase with the Honrichs has been annulled. The complete taking-over from Honrichs, however, and the delivery to the purchaser cannot, howsoever the matter may proceed, take place earlier than Johanny of this year, because otherwise, with

the payments of interest, many entanglements might arise.

§ 10. Since the purchaser cannot at present demonstrate his capacity to possess noble estates, the seller undertakes to contribute everything toward obtaining the concession to possess the estate Wittow, as soon as the purchase with Honrichsen is annulled, and to intercede on that account himself with His Majesty the King. All costs, however, for the procuring of the concession the purchaser bears alone.

§ 11. All the costs arising in purchases of this kind, by whatever name they may be called, both parties bear by halves.

§ 12. Both parties bind themselves to the maintenance of this Punctation, renouncing all objections of whatever name.

Done at Kalisch, the 9th of March 1805. Peter Friedrich August v. Triebenfeld. Franz Maierowitz. Friedrich Willhelm Eger as witness. [uncertain: Bezuer] qua Testis
