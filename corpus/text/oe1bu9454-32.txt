--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Triebenfeld kann keine neue Nachweisung schicken; in der vor 2 Jahren übersandten seien nur die Trąbczyner Revenuen abzuziehen. Die Einkünfte der Herrschaft Zagórów beziffert er auf 27700 Rthl jährlich, die Schulden auf 148,800 Rthl, sodass 20260 Rthl blieben. Oppenheimer hat die 50000 Rthl an die Invaliden Casse nicht bezahlt; Triebenfeld hat ihn verklagt. Die Trąbczyner Güter hält er nicht für verloren. Er hatte 200000 Rthl aus Holland in Aussicht und bittet um baldige Nachricht über Aussichten des Fürsten.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Triebenfeld says he cannot send a new statement; in the one sent two years ago only the Trąbczyn revenues need to be deducted. He puts the income of the lordship of Zagórów at 27,700 Rthl a year and the debts at 148,800 Rthl, so that 20,260 Rthl would remain. Oppenheimer has not paid the 50,000 Rthl to the Invalids' Fund; Triebenfeld has sued him. He does not consider the Trąbczyn estates lost. He had had 200,000 Rthl from Holland in prospect and asks for early news of the Prince's prospects.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince,

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

To Your Most Serene Highness's most honoured letter of the 23th of last month I reply most obediently, that it is at present [text lost] possible for me to convey from here to the same, in haste, any other statement than that which Your Princely Serene Highness has already had for 2 years. In this one only the Trąbczyn revenues are to be deducted, and there then results the sum of yield of the Zagórów estates itself. So far as I can recall, the lordship of Zagórów will amount to

1.) the propination rights 5,000 Rthl annually

2.) interest from the old subjects according to the contract of [uncertain: Ges] 2,000 Rthl

3.) Oleśnica, where there are now some 19,000 Rthl first, according to the hereditary lease contract 2,000 Rthl

4) Wrąbczyn interest according to the hereditary lease contract 1,000 Rthl

5.) Emilien[hain], Augustinow, Alexandrow, Adolphsberg, by contract 3,000 Rthl (interest [text lost] likewise)

6.) Klein Ingelfingen and Grądzyń interest according to the hereditary lease contract 2,000 Rthl

7.) Swięcia already bears interest according to the hereditary lease contract 1,800 Rthl

8.) Alt Drzewice, which is not yet built up, but interest 1,800 Rthl

9.) Swiątniki likewise 1,800 Rthl

10.) Sophienthal bears interest according to the hereditary lease contract 1,000 Rthl

11.) Klein Schrazberg, Friedrichsfelde, Fürstenfelde etc. likewise 2,000 Rthl

12.) Kopojno and Adelow according to the lease and hereditary lease contract 2,000 Rthl

13.) Zagórów according to the instrument 300 Rthl

14.) jurisdictional dues, transfer fees (Laudemium), hunting and small usages, on average 2,000 Rthl

= 27,700 Rthl. All taxes and costs are borne, according to the hereditary lease contracts, by the hereditary lessees, and therefore nothing may be deducted from the revenues.

Debts encumber the considerable estates according to the mortgage certificate and the judicial instrument between me and Oppenheimer, only in actual effect

1.) Lichnowski, which have been transferred to Oppenheimer 32,300 Rthl

2.) Meyer Bernhard 5,600 Rthl

3.) for Glenck likewise 20,000 Rthl

4.) the Invalids' Fund, which the Jew Oppenheimer took over and has not paid. 50,000 Rthl

5.) costs which the Jew; paid 1,400 Rthl

6.) To Bernhardi likewise 4,500 Rthl

7.) To Your Serene Highness per further band, do 15,000 Rthl

8.) To sundry items, as costs to Your Serene Highness through not the 20,000 Rthl. Sum 148,800 Rthl. This amounts, in annual interest reckoned at 5 per cent, to 7,440 Rthl. Accordingly there must remain annually to Your Serene Highness's treasury 20,260 Rthl

Hereupon a mortgage of 250,000 Rthl has been pledged to the Jew Oppenheimer, whereby the Jew stipulated for himself 7 per cent brokerage according to the judicial instrument annexed hereto, which of the sum, with the 50/m Rthl to the Invalids' Fund, would come to 160,000 Rthl in question; but since the Jew has again paid neither the 50,000 Rthl to the Invalids' Fund nor still less the interest, then they are not to claim the 7 per cent, and the 11,200 Rthl fall away and to Your Serene Highness's benefit.

I have now sued his Jew on this account, and I hope to finalise the matter yet during my presence here.

It is therefore, as Your Serene Highness may be pleased to satisfy yourself, the actual sum of 148,800 Rthl — which would have to be discharged. Thereupon the mortgage now encumbered to the amount of 250,000 Rthl is wholly and altogether free, and all the [uncertain: unschende] debts entirely discharged. The mortgage certificates annexed hereto in copy, attested by the court, do indeed at the beginning still state the canons and competences; however, as already said, the hereditary lessees have entirely taken these over, and they will never again fall as a burden upon Your Serene Highness. Finally I note further that to Ein dem Flauche Eröhn considerable sums must still be paid, namely marriage and hereditary lease monies which remained in arrears after the war, unless it be that the various general attorneys have already appropriated a part thereof to themselves. For my part I have in 2 years collected nothing from there, have seen no man from there, and despite messengers sent many times from die Haaren I have not been able to obtain the least intelligence — because they had now entered into correspondence with Your Serene Highness yourself, I have without wurren Vorte paid out of my own pocket express courier monies, small capital sums and lawsuit costs, and also considerable interest, and have had them paid through Professor Rhode; but at present I am [uncertain: möchte].

As for the rest, so far as concerns the Trąbczyn estates, such are, according to my first conviction, not lost. I myself cannot for the present go onto the estates, for reasons; but I shall, with the French authorities and in all places, be heard more than Your Serene Highness may thank for it. As concerns the Kamionna affair, Your Serene Highness has answered just as I did; however it seems to me that

the period for issue fixed in the contract had also already expired. In conclusion I must further state here that I was on the point of obtaining a capital of 200,000 Rthl upon the mortgage out of Holland, through a couple of Müllers now staying here. But if Your Serene Highness now has closer prospects and certainty there of drawing such a capital, then be pleased to write to us upon it at the very earliest, so that I may spare myself unnecessary costs and vexation here. With unbounded veneration and the deepest respect I remain Your Serene Highness's

Berlin

the 3rd of April 1808

faithful, most humble and obedient servant

v Triebenfeld
