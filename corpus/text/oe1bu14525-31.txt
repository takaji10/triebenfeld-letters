--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Aufforderung der Königlich Südpreußischen Regierung an Friedrich Ludwig, Fürst von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen: da er das Gut Trąbczyn mit Zubehör vom Fiskus erworben hat, muss der Besitztitel zuerst im Hypothekenbuch eingetragen werden. Dazu soll der Fürst binnen vier Wochen eine vidimirte Abschrift des Urteils einreichen, mit dem das gesamte Vermögen des Anton von Prusimski dem Fiskus zugesprochen wurde, dazu eine Bescheinigung des zuständigen Gerichts über die Rechtskraft des Urteils.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A royal rescript from the South Prussian government at Kalisz calls on the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, having bought the estate of Trąbczyn and its appurtenances from the Treasury, to have his title entered in the mortgage book. Within four weeks he is to produce a certified copy of the judgment awarding the whole property of Anton von Prusimski to the Treasury, with a court attestation that the judgment has taken legal effect.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
By the Grace of God, Friedrich Wilhelm, King of Prussia etc. etc.

Our gracious greeting, Royal favour, and all else that is dear and good which We are able to bestow, first of all!

High-born Prince, kindly beloved cousin, Since Your Lordship has acquired the estate of Trąbczyn with its appurtenances from the Treasury, the title of possession must first of all be entered upon the same in the mortgage book. We therefore hereby request of Your Lordship that to this end you submit, within 4 weeks, a certified copy of the judgment by which the entire property of Anton von Prusimski was adjudged to the Treasury, together with an attestation of the competent court that the judgment has entered into legal force. We remain well disposed toward Your Lordship with Royal favour and friendship. Given at Kalisz, the 28th of December 1798.

Royal South Prussian Government, Meuser[illegible]

To the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen

H. A. R. Lucas Schleyer

No. 3459
