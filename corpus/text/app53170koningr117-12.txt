M Nożalski in vim Diligentae Manifestatur
Ad Officium et Acta praesentia Castrensia Coninensia personaliter veniens Magnificus Martinus Nosalski Thesaurarida Czerniechovic~ Bonorum Rzgów cum Attinen~ haeres ac Possessor prospiciendo Indemnitati Suae ne quomodocunque per depositionem Testimoniorum ex Laboriosis Bartholomeo Szepczyński et Matthaeo Wąchnicki in Causa cum Ilustrissimi Magnifico Prusimski Capitaneo Nieszczevicen~ in Juditis Juribus Terrestribus Palatinatinus Calissien~ in Conin promota praejudiciosum sibi inferre videatur hanc Actis praesentibus in vim Diligentiae infert querimoniam protestaturque sequenti methodo Etenim Supra praefati Laboriosi Bartholomaeus Szepczyński et Matthaeus Wąchnicki Mandata Generosi Thoma Joachimowicz Exequentes ausi sunt ac praesumpserunt hominibus Sui Ma
manifestantis de Sylvis Magnifici Chełmski haereditariis in Trahibus arbores vehentibus in via publica iter praepedire. Eosque Concutere ac Converberare ad bonaque Trąpczyn cum Trahibus adducere Secures violenti modo apphendere Arboris in Foresta Curiali Trąpczynen~ iisdem Eicere exindeque in Complicitatem Facti intrare, his itaque peractis violentiis omnino paenas in se trahere non Erubuerunt, Quare Manifest~ns contra Eosdem violentiarum Executores quam Solennissime Manifestatur, offerendo se praemissas violentias per Ipsosmet peractas omnimoda Inquisitione deducturum a perhibendoque hac in Causa Testimonio relegaturum Complicitatemque delicti Seorsivis punctis probaturum velle, Salvis Salvandis.
Martinus Nosalski

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Protest von Marcin Nosalski, Erbherr und Besitzer von Rzgów, eingetragen in Konin am 1. Mai 1779 und von ihm unterschrieben. Er legt ihn vorsorglich ein, damit ihm die Aussagen von Bartłomiej Szepczyński und Mateusz Wąchnicki in seiner Sache mit Prusimski vor dem Kalischer Landgericht in Konin nicht schaden. Die beiden hätten auf Befehl von Tomasz Joachimowicz seine Leute, die auf Schlitten Bäume aus den Erbwäldern Chełmskis fuhren, auf öffentlicher Straße angehalten, geschlagen, mit den Schlitten nach Trąbczyn gebracht und ihnen die Äxte mit Gewalt abgenommen. Nosalski protestiert gegen sie als Urheber der Gewalt, bietet an, die Gewalttaten durch Zeugenverhör zu beweisen, und will sie vom Zeugnis in dieser Sache ausschließen lassen.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A protest of Marcin Nosalski, heir and possessor of Rzgów, entered at Konin on 1 May 1779 and signed by him. He enters it as a precaution, so that the testimony of Bartłomiej Szepczyński and Mateusz Wąchnicki in his cause with Prusimski before the Kalisz land court at Konin shall not harm him. The two, he says, on the orders of Tomasz Joachimowicz, stopped his people on the public road as they were carting trees on sledges from Chełmski's hereditary woods, beat them, took them with the sledges to Trąbczyn and seized their axes by force. Nosalski protests against them as the authors of the violence, offers to prove the acts of violence by an inquiry of witnesses, and means to have them shut out from giving testimony in this cause.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The Honourable Nosalski — manifests by way of diligence

To the office and present records of the Konin Municipal Court, appearing in person, the Honourable Marcin Nosalski, son of the Treasurer of Czernihów, hereditary owner and possessor of the estates of Rzgów with appurtenances — looking to the indemnity of his estate, lest any prejudice be brought against him in any way by means of testimony to be given by the labouring men Bartłomiej Szepczyński and Mateusz Wąchnicki in the cause promoted in the land court proceedings of the Kalisz Palatinate at Konin against the Most Honourable Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — lodges against the present records, by way of diligence, the following complaint and protests in the following manner:

That the aforesaid labouring men Bartłomiej Szepczyński and Mateusz Wąchnicki, executing the orders of the Honourable Tomasz Joachimowicz, dared and presumed [to act] against the people of his

estate —

obstructing [them] on the public road as they were carting trees on sledges from the hereditary woodland of the Honourable Chełmski; striking and beating those same [people]; bringing them to the estates of Trąbczyn together with the sledges; confiscating their axes by force; charging them with the felling of trees in the Trąbczyn manor forest; and drawing them into complicity in the deed thus constructed — and having committed all these acts of violence, they did not blush to draw the penalties wholly upon themselves.

Wherefore the manifestant, manifesting most solemnly against those same perpetrators of violence, offers to establish the aforesaid acts of violence committed by them in person through a full sworn inquiry, to have them shut out from giving testimony in this cause, and to prove their complicity in the offence in separate points; with all things preserved that ought to be preserved.

Martinus Nosalski
