Auf verschiedene von Sr. Majestät erhaltene Befehle, vacante
Gratial- und confiscirte Insurgenten Güther zum Besten verkauft
voller Officiers in Vorschlag zu bringen, habe ich anliegenden
Bericht vom 14ten dieses erstattet, und Allerhöchstdieselben haben
sämmtliche Anträge nach der unter ganz ergebenster Zurü¬
Erbittung accludirten Cabinets Ordre vom 23ten dieses zu geneh¬
migen befunden.
Da der Inhalt der bisher entworfenen Donations-Ur¬
kunden mit denjenigen, welche nunmehr zu expediren sind,
ganz gleichlautend seyn kann, indem nur die Nahmen der
begnädigten Persohnen, und die ihnen zugetheilten Güter den
wesentlichen Unterschied machen, so habe nicht erst besondere
Concepte zu den diesfälligen Donations Briefen entworfen,
sondern stelle Euer Excellenz ganz ergebenst anheim, ob
dieselben solche in der Lehns Canzley expediren, und mir
solche mittelst gemeinschaftlichen Berichts zur Mitzeichnung
und weitern Beförderung an Sr. Majestät zu zusen¬
den belieben wollen. Ich bemerke dabey mir noch, daß
bey den Güthern der Beysatz: cum appertinentia, oder
Zubehör, stets wird inserirt werden müßen, weil es in

sodann bey künftiger Tradition alle diejenigen Güther
dazu gerechnet werden, welche der vorige Gratial Besitzer
oder Privat-Eigenthümer in dem oder jenem Creiße
beseßen hat, und welche nach diesem Maasstabe von Sr.
Majestät an die jetzigen Percipienten transferirt
werden sollen. Da jeder von ihnen die Behändigung
des Donations- Instruments und den Verreich der Gü¬
ther bald wünschten; so darf Euer Excellenz um so
mehr ganz ergebenst ersuchen, die Ausfertigung der
Urkunden möglichst beschleunigen zu laßen.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Akte zur Schenkung konfiszierter und vakanter Güter in Südpreußen an Offiziere. Hoym schlägt vor, dem Fürsten von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen die dem Insurgenten Anton von Prusimski konfiszierten Güter zu verleihen: Kamionna, Kolno, Trąbczyn, Szetlewek, Nowawies, Lazy, Alt- und Neu-Laske, Osiny, das Hauland Trąbczyński und Brzyce. Die Kabinettsordre genehmigt dies sowie Güter für von Sanitz, Blücher, von Dolffs, von Pontanus, von Holtzendorff und von Larisch. Enthalten sind der Entwurf der Schenkungsurkunde für den Fürsten und Hoyms Wunsch nach der Klausel „cum appertinentia“.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The file on the donation of confiscated and vacant estates in South Prussia to officers. Hoym proposes granting the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen the estates confiscated from the insurgent Anton von Prusimski: Kamionna, Kolno, Trąbczyn, Szetlewek, Nowawies, Łazy, Old and New Laski, Osiny, the Trąbczyn Hauland and Brzyce. The cabinet order approves this, and estates for von Sanitz, Blücher, von Dolffs, von Pontanus, von Holtzendorff and von Larisch. It contains the draft of the donation charter for the Prince and Hoym's request for the clause 'cum appertinentia'.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
6 Aug. 1796. Let the necessary be despatched in the manner proposed. The 8th of Aug. 1796.

etc. conclusum eodem.

Mellentz[?] Upon various orders received from His Majesty, to bring forward proposals of vacant estates granted by royal favour (Gratial-Güter) and of confiscated insurgents' estates for the benefit of sold-off full officers, I have rendered the enclosed report of the 14th of this month, and His Most High Majesty has been pleased to approve all the proposals in accordance with the cabinet order of the 23rd of this month, which is here enclosed with the most humble request for its return.

Since the content of the donation charters hitherto drafted may be entirely identical with those which are now to be despatched, inasmuch as only the names of the persons favoured, and the estates assigned to them, make the essential difference, I have not first drawn up particular drafts for the donation letters in question, but leave it most humbly to Your Excellency whether you will be pleased to have them despatched in the Fiefs Chancery, and to send them to me by way of a joint report for my counter-signature and further conveyance to His Majesty. I note besides that with the estates the addition: cum appertinentia, or appurtenances, will always have to be inserted, because, in the absence of any system of mortgage registration in Suprensten, it is too difficult to obtain speedy and thorough information of the relations of the estates belonging together, and otherwise the beneficiaries would easily suffer a disadvantage contrary to His Majesty's intention, if in future, through strict specifications in the donation charters, appurtenances which belong to the principal estates were to remain not delivered over to them.

Thus, for example, there are reckoned to the Duninow estates the villages of Duninow, Tolenczna, Szadow, Nowawies, and Krzement

and designated merely by the expression: the Duninow estates granted by royal favour, of 1500 rt. in yield. But if the donation charter is framed in general terms, then upon the future delivery of possession all those estates may be reckoned to it which the former holder by royal favour or private proprietor possessed in this or that district, and which by that measure are to be transferred by His Majesty to the present recipients. Since each of them would wish soon to have the donation instrument put into his hands and the estates delivered over, I may all the more humbly request Your Excellency to have the issue of the charters expedited as far as possible. Wrocław, the 30th of July 1796. Hoym

To His Excellency the Royal Actual Privy Minister of State and Justice, Baron von der Reck, at Berlin.

Lieutenant General of Infantry, Chief of a Regiment of Foot, Governor of the capital city of Wrocław We Friedrich Wilhelm by the Grace of God King of Prussia (tot. tit.) do declare and make known by means of this Our open letter, for Ourselves and Our successors in the Crown, the Electorate and Our remaining lands, and hereby graciously give to know to each and every one whom it concerns: that, as We are, out of inborn favour and clemency, much inclined to acknowledge and requite the good and profitable services rendered to Us and to Our Royal House by particular marks of Our Royal good will, We have considered the important, distinguished and useful services rendered for many years past to Us and to Our Royal House in Our Royal Heimath by the High-Born Prince, Our kindly beloved uncle, Friedrich Ludewig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Our (Inseratur der Charakter des Fürsten im Königl. Dienst), Knight of Our Orders etc., and have resolved, in order to give to the said Lord Prince a mark of the recognition of his excellent merits and of Our Royal gratitude, to bestow and convey upon him, heritably and in property, the following estates which have fallen in to Our Most High disposal, namely: the town of Kamionna or Kamionna, and the market town of Kulm, in the district of Poznań; the villages of Trąbczyn, Szetlewek, Nowawies, Lazy, Old and New Laske, Oszny, Hauland Trąbczyński, in the district of Konin; and the village of Brzyce, together with appurtenances, in Kuyavia; with all their belongings, by whatever name they may be called, heritably and

in property.

This We also do herewith and by virtue of these presents, out of Royal power and plenitude, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors in the Crown, the Electorate and Our remaining lands, in such wise and manner that Our aforesaid kindly beloved uncle, the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, shall from now on for eternal times have, possess and enjoy the above-named estates, with all rights and privileges attaching thereto, as such have hitherto been exercised or may lawfully be exercised, no less than with all their appurtenances and other belongings, by whatever name they may be called, just as the same have hitherto been used or may of right be used, as a well-acquired and unencumbered property — yet subject to the reservation of the sovereign taxes and other public dues to be laid upon them like other estates of Our South Prussian [uncertain: territories] — for himself and his descendants and heirs, irrevocably from Us and Our successors, and the power shall likewise be vested in him to dispose thereof at his pleasure, unhindered by any man. We therefore command and enjoin all Our South Prussian Governments and Chambers of War and Domains, as also all other authorities whom it concerns, herewith as graciously as earnestly, that they shall in no way hinder or impede either the [uncertain: aforementioned] Lord Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen in the heritable and proprietary possession, granted to him, of the above-named holdings and estates together with their appurtenances and belongings, nor him or those upon whom, by virtue and in force of this Our letter of donation, he may bequeath the same therein or otherwise lawfully convey it by other acts inter vivos or mortis causa, but shall rather let him, and eventually also them, enjoy the same freely and peaceably, doing nothing themselves against it, nor occasioning or permitting that such be done by others, upon pain of Our Most High disfavour and unfailing severe punishment. In witness whereof We have caused this present letter of donation to be signed with Our own Most High hand and Our Royal great seal to be appended thereto. Thus done at Berlin, 9 August 1796.

Ad contrasignandum

Goldbeck
