--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A royal deed of gift by which Friedrich Wilhelm grants Friedrich Ludwig, Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Lieutenant-General and Governor of Breslau, heritable and full property in estates fallen to the Crown: the town of Kamionna and market-town of Kulm in the Posen district; the villages of Trąbczyn, Szetlewek, Nowa wies, Łazy, Old and New Laske, Osiny and Hauland Trąbczyński in the Konin district; and Brzyce in Cujavia, reserving sovereign taxes, with governments and chambers forbidden to obstruct him. Certified at Kalisz and entered in the Konin mortgage book for Szetlewek.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Certified Copy

We Friedrich Wilhelm, by God's Grace King of Prussia etc., do by means of this Our open letter, for Ourselves and Our successors to the Crown of the Electorate and Our remaining lands, attest and declare, and hereby graciously give to know to each and every one whom the same concerns: that, inasmuch as We are by inborn favour and clemency much inclined to acknowledge and requite the good and profitable services rendered to Us and to Our Royal House by particular marks of Our Royal benevolence, We have [considered] the services rendered by the High-Born Prince, Our

affectionately dear Uncle Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Our Lieutenant-General of Infantry, Chief of a Regiment of Foot, Governor of the capital city of Breslau, Knight of Our Orders etc., the important, distinguished and useful services rendered for a considerable number of years to Us and to Our Royal House, weighed in Our Royal mind; and that, in order to give the said Lord Prince a mark of the recognition of his excellent merits and of Our Royal gratitude, We have resolved to confer and convey to him, heritably and in full property, the below-named estates which have fallen to Our All-Highest disposition, namely:

Kamionna or Kamionna, the town, with the market-town of Kulm

of the Posen district; the villages of Trąbczyn, Szetlewek, Nowa wies, Łazy, Old and New Laske, Osiny, Hauland Trąbczyński of the Konin district; and the village of Brzyce together with its appurtenances in Cujavia, with all their belongings, by whatever names they may be called.

This We also hereby do, and by force of this present instrument, out of Royal power and plenitude, for Ourselves and Our successors and heirs to the Crown of the Electorate and Our remaining lands, in such manner and to the effect that the above-mentioned Our affectionately dear Uncle, the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, shall have the above-named estates with all rights and jurisdictions attach-

ing to them as the same have hitherto been exercised, or may lawfully be exercised, and no less with all their appurtenances and other belongings, by whatever names they may be called, in such manner as the same have hitherto been enjoyed or may lawfully be enjoyed, as a well-acquired and unrestricted property - yet subject to the reservation of the sovereign taxes and other public dues to be laid upon them in like manner as upon the other estates of Our South Prussian vassals - for himself and his successors and heirs, from now on unto eternal times, irrevocably from Us and Our successors, to

have, hold and enjoy; and that there shall likewise be vested in him the power to dispose thereof at his pleasure, unhindered by any man.

We accordingly command and enjoin all Our South Prussian governments and Chambers of War and Domains, as also all other authorities whom the same concerns, herewith as graciously as earnestly, that they in no wise hinder or trouble either the aforementioned Lord Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen in the heritable and proprietary possession, conferred upon him, of the above-named holdings and estates together with their appurtenances and belongings, nor him or those to whom he may in time, by virtue and in force of this

Our deed of donation, bequeath the same, or otherwise lawfully transfer it by other acts inter vivos aut mortis causa; but rather shall let him, and eventually also them, enjoy the same freely and quietly, doing nothing themselves against it, nor occasioning or permitting that such be done by others, on pain of Our All-Highest displeasure and unfailing severe punishment.

In witness whereof We have signed the present deed of donation with Our own most high hand, and have caused Our

1.

Royal great seal to be appended thereto.

So done and given in Our residence city of Berlin, the ninth day of the month of August after the birth of Christ our Lord in the one thousand seven hundred and ninety-sixth, and in the tenth year of Our Royal reign.

Friedrich Wilhelm

Hoym. Goldbeck.
