# 1803-04-08  Breslau
# /letters/oe1bu14526/20/

Nachdem ich heute dato die Vorwerks Wirtschafts
Gebäude zu Swięcia nebst darzu gehörigen Aekkern
Wiesen Waldungen pp an den Johann Christian Birlack
aus Zilenzig, Johan Götzinger aus Brennenhofs Fleis
Joh. Siebert aus Zamaika bey Sonnenburg und an
Friedrich Götzinger Gastwirth zu Posen Erbpachtsweise
überlaßen, hiervon aber das eigentliche herrschaftliche Wohn¬
haus zu Swięcia ausgezogen habe. So überlaße und
schenke ich hiermit dieses herrschaftliche Wohnhaus zu
Swięcia an den Friedrich Götzinger Gastwirth zu Posen
als eine Erkenntlichkeit für die bey diesem Erbpachts Ge¬
schäfte gehabte Mühwaltung und Reisen erb und eigenthüm¬
lich um da mit nach seinen Gefallen schalten und walten
und gebohren zu können, und weil derselbe dieses Haus bey
der übernommenen Brauerey Erbpacht zum Schank besonder¬
gut brauchen kann.
Breslau den 8ten Aprill 1803.
Friedrich Ludwig Fürst zu Hohenlohe

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A deed of gift confirming that, the Vorwegs farm buildings at Swięcia with their fields, meadows and woodlands having that day been let in hereditary lease to Johann Christian Birlack of Zilenzig, Johan Götzinger of Brennenhofs Fleis, Joh. Siebert of Zamaika near Sonnenburg and Friedrich Götzinger, innkeeper at Posen, the manorial dwelling-house at Swięcia excepted from that lease is bestowed on Friedrich Götzinger as heritable absolute property, in recognition of his trouble and journeys and for use as a tap with his brewery lease. Signed by Friedrich Ludwig Fürst zu Hohenlohe.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Whereas I have this day made over by way of hereditary lease the Vorwegs [uncertain: Vorwegs] farm buildings at Swięcia together with the appurtenant fields, meadows, woodlands etc. to Johann Christian Birlack of Zilenzig, Johan Götzinger of Brennenhofs Fleis, Joh. Siebert of Zamaika near Sonnenburg, and to Friedrich Götzinger, innkeeper at Posen, but have excepted therefrom the actual manorial dwelling-house at Swięcia: I therefore herewith make over and bestow this manorial dwelling-house at Swięcia upon Friedrich Götzinger, innkeeper at Posen, as a token of recognition for the pains and journeys he has had in the matter of this hereditary-lease business, as heritable and absolute property, so that he may deal and dispose with it and [uncertain: gebohren — "bear/be born"; sense unclear, perhaps for "gebahren" (to conduct oneself) or "gebieten" (to command)] according to his pleasure, and because he can put this house to particularly good use for the tap in connexion with the brewery hereditary lease he has taken over.

Breslau, the 8th of April 1803.

Friedrich Ludwig Fürst zu Hohenlohe
