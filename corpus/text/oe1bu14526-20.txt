Nachdem ich heute dato die Vorwerks Wirtschafts
Gebäude zu Swięcia nebst darzu gehörigen Aekkern
Wiesen Waldungen pp an den Johann Christian Birlack
aus Zilenzig, Johan Götzinger aus Brennenhofs Fleis
Joh. Siebert aus Zamaika bey Sonnenburg und an
Friedrich Götzinger Gastwirth zu Posen Erbpachtsweise
überlaßen, hiervon aber das eigentliche herrschaftliche Wohn¬
haus zu Swięcia ausgezogen habe. So überlaße und
schenke ich hiermit dieses herrschaftliche Wohnhaus zu
Swięcia an den Friedrich Götzinger Gastwirth zu Posen
als eine Erkenntlichkeit für die bey diesem Erbpachts Ge¬
schäfte gehabte Mühwaltung und Reisen erb und eigenthüm¬
lich um da mit nach seinen Gefallen schalten und walten
und gebahren zu können, und weil derselbe dieses Haus bey
der übernommenen Brauerey Erbpacht zum Schank besonder¬
gut brauchen kann.
Breslau den 8ten Aprill 1803.
Friedrich Ludwig Fürst zu Hohenlohe

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A grant by Friedrich Ludwig, Prince of Hohenlohe, of the manorial dwelling house at Swięcia to Friedrich Götzinger, innkeeper at Posen, in full heritable ownership. The house had been expressly excepted from a hereditary lease of the Swięcia demesne farm buildings, fields, meadows and woodlands made the same day to Götzinger with Johann Christian Birlack of Zilenzig, Johan Götzinger and Joh. Siebert. It is given for Götzinger's trouble and travel in arranging the lease, and because he can use it as a tap-house under the brewery lease he has taken on.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Whereas I have this day made over by way of hereditary lease the demesne-farm working buildings at Swięcia, together with the fields, meadows, woodlands etc. belonging thereto, to Johann Christian Birlack of Zilenzig, Johan Götzinger of Brennenhofs Fleis, Joh. Siebert of Zamaika near Sonnenburg, and to Friedrich Götzinger, innkeeper at Posen, but have excepted therefrom the actual manorial dwelling house at Swięcia: I hereby make over and give this manorial dwelling house at Swięcia to Friedrich Götzinger, innkeeper at Posen, as a token of acknowledgement for the pains and journeys he has had in this hereditary-lease business, heritably and in full ownership, so that he may deal, dispose and act with it at his pleasure, and because he can put this house to particularly good use as a tap-house under the brewery hereditary lease he has taken over.

Breslau, the 8th of April 1803.

Friedrich Ludwig, Prince of Hohenlohe
