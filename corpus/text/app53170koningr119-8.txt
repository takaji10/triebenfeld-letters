Actum in Conin Feria Quarta post Festum Exaltationis Sanctae Crucia proxima Die Scilicet Decima Quinta Mensas Septembris Anno Domini 1784
Pro parte JM Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen~ requisitio
Ad officum et Acta praesenti Castrensia Coninensia personaliter veniens Generosus Leo Maszewski Bonorum Tractus Trąmpczynen~ Commissarius Nomine et Ex speciali Commisso Illustris Magnifici Antonii de Kolno Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen~ Eorundem Bonorum haeredis Domini sui Requisivit ab Officio praesenti extradique Petiit Relationem edittae Citationis pro Judiciis Ordinariis Generalibus Tribunalis Regni Petricovien~ ex parte Magnifici Stanislai Scibor Chełmski Thesaurarii Vschoven~ ad bona Trąmpczyn Feria Quinta post Festum Nativitis Beatissimae Virginis Maria Anno fluenti per Ministerialem exportatae utrum in Actis praesentibus recognitt~ reperitur vel ne cui requisitioni officium praesens annuendo Reviso Protocollo Relationum a Feria Quinta proxime praeterita. De inexistentia praefatae Relationis, publicam facit fidem
Leon Maszewski Imieniem JW Prusimskiego

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Eintrag vom 15. September 1784. Leon Maszewski, Kommissar der Trąbczyner Güter, fragt im Namen von Antoni Prusimski von Kolno, Starost von Niszczewice, ob der Bericht über eine Ladung vor das Krontribunal in Petrikau, die Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa, erwirkt hat und die am Donnerstag nach Mariä Geburt des laufenden Jahres nach Trąbczyn gebracht wurde, vor dem Amt erklärt worden ist. Das Amt hat sein Register durchgesehen und bescheinigt, dass ein solcher Bericht nicht vorliegt. Maszewski hat im Namen Prusimskis unterschrieben.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
An entry of 15 September 1784. Leon Maszewski, commissioner of the Trąbczyn estates, asks in the name of Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice, whether the report of a citation before the Crown Tribunal at Piotrków, obtained by Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, and taken to Trąbczyn on the Thursday after the Nativity of the Virgin of the current year, has been declared before the office. The office has looked through its register and certifies that there is no such report. Maszewski signed in Prusimski's name.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Done in Konin on Wednesday next after the Feast of the Exaltation of the Holy Cross, being 15 September 1784.

On behalf of His Honour Prusimski, Captain of Niszczewice — Requisition

To the office and present records of the Konin Municipal Court, appearing in person, the Honourable Leon Maszewski, Commissioner of the Trąbczyn estate district, in the name and by special commission of the Illustrious Right Honourable Antoni of Kolno Prusimski, Captain of Niszczewice, heir of those same estates, his principal — requested of the present office and petitioned for the extraction of the relatio of the citation issued for the Ordinary General Sessions of the Piotrków Crown Tribunal, on behalf of the Right Honourable Stanisław Scibor Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, to the estates of Trąbczyn, exported by a court summoner on Thursday after the Feast of the Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary in the current year — whether it is found recognized in the present records or not.

To which requisition the present office, granting it and having reviewed the Protocol of Relationes from the Thursday last past, makes public certification of the non-existence of the aforesaid relatio.

Leon Maszewski, in the name of the Most Honourable Prusimski
