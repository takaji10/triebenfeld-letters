--- SUMMARY (English) ---
An official extract from the Kalisz mortgage register sets out the charges on Zagórów, granted to Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen in 1797 and registered in 1801: an annual allowance of 88 Rthl 5 Groschen 9 d to the Kalisz War and Domains Treasury, treasury and hereditary-lessee protests over wood and pasture rights, and a 250,000 Rthl loan to von Triebenfeld, portions of it pledged or ceded to the General Invalids' Fund and to the Oppenheim and Wolff banking houses, plus an 8,000 Rthl claim by the Seher-Thoss heirs.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Official Extract

[The?] town of Zagórów, situated in the province of South Prussia (now, however, in the Duchy of Warsaw) and in the district of Konin thereof, was conveyed to the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Friedrich Ludwig, Serene Highness, by His Majesty King Friedrich Wilhelm II, by the instrument of grant of Berlin, 19 June 1797, in hereditary and proprietary right, and the title of possession was registered for him pursuant to the decree of 31 July 1801. Thereupon there are registered:

I. Perpetual charges and restrictions upon the property or upon the disposition thereof.

1., is cancelled.

2., 88 Rthl 5 Groschen 9 d, eighty-eight Rthl 5 Groschen 9 d Prussian Courant, are paid annually out of this estate as an allowance to the Kalisz War and Domains Treasury,

which has been registered ex officio on the strength of the application of the War and Domains Chamber at Kalisz, dated Kalisz, 18 November 1802, by virtue of the decree of 14 December 1802.

3., A protestation on behalf of the Chamber's treasury (pro fisco Camerae) concerning the entitlement already vested in it, on the strength of the confiscation of the ecclesiastical estates which has taken place, to demand the right to building and firewood from the forests belonging to this estate and the payment of the surplus cash forest allowance, has been registered upon the application of the treasury's mandatary by virtue of the decree of 25 January 1803.

4., A protestation on behalf of Johann Giese, Johann Bagans and Friedrich Gietzinger concerning the right stipulated to them in the hereditary-lease contract concluded with the proprietor on 22 February 1804 over the estate of Olesnica,

namely to enjoy free windfall and gathered wood, and yearly 4 days of free digging of pine-stumps in the Zagórów forests; also that, should the lordship find it necessary to assign a certain district in addition, this district may not be under 330 Morgen, and that those hereditary lessees shall be entitled to pasture their sheep in this district: this has been provisionally noted upon the application of those named at the outset, by virtue of the decree of 18 December 1804.

II. Judicially secured debts and other real obligations

1., is cancelled. is

2., 250000 Rthl, two hundred and fifty thousand Reichsthaler in Prussian Courant, at 5 per cent annual interest, and after the lapse of one year subject to a 6 months' notice of termination open to both parties, have, as a loan under the bond of the proprietor dated Breslau, 1 June 1814, been for

the Councillor of War and Forests Peter Friedrich August von Triebenfeld, by virtue of the decree of 5 June 1804, in consequence of the debtor's petition to that effect, registered against the estates of the town of Zagórów, Kopojno, Swiątniki, Skokum, Drzewce, Olesnica, Wrąbczyn, Grądzyń, which are at the same time liable for costs and damages.

To No. 2. 50,000 Rthl, fifty thousand Reichsthaler out of the sum of 250,000 Rthl registered above under No. 2, the holder thereof, the royal Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, has pledged to the Royal General Invalids' Fund, and eventually ceded in full property, on account of a loan of like amount received from the same at 5 per cent annual interest payable in half-yearly instalments at Berlin free of postage, subject to a mutual 6 months' notice of termination, to the said General Invalids' Fund, in accordance with the instrument issued thereon under date Berlin, 28 January

1805, which has been registered by virtue of the decree of 5 March 1803, with the remark that priority over the residue has been constituted for this loan of the 50000 Rthl.

To No. 2. 52,100 Rthl, fifty-two thousand one hundred Reichsthaler out of the principal sum of the 250,000 registered above under No. 2, together with interest, the holder thereof, the royal Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, has ceded judicially on 14 June 1805 to the bankers Mendel Oppenheim and Wolff in Berlin, with full effect and with priority of cession over the residue, which has been registered by virtue of the decree of 9 July 1805. To No. 2. 30,000 Rthl, say thirty thousand Reichsthaler out of the principal sum of the 250,000 Rthl registered above, together with interest, the holder thereof, the royal Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, has to the banker Wolff

Oppenheim in Königsberg in Prussia ceded judicially with full effect on 15 June 1805, which has been registered by virtue of the decree of 9 July 1805.

To No. 2. 30,000 Rthl, say thirty thousand Rthl out of the principal sum of the 250,000 Rthl registered above, together with interest, the holder thereof, the royal Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, has ceded judicially with full effect to the banker Wolff Oppenheim in Königsberg in Prussia on 15 June 1805, which has been registered by virtue of the decree of 9 July 1805.

To No. 2. 30,000 Rthl, say thirty thousand Reichsthaler out of the principal sum of the 250,000 Rthl registered above, together with interest, the holder thereof, the royal Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, has to the bankers Mendel Oppenheim and Wolff in Berlin with full

effect ceded judicially on 15 June 1805, which has been registered by virtue of the decree of 9 July 1805.

With the three sums of 30,000 Rthl intabulated above, together 90,000 Rthl, there was at the same time ceded in the instrument of cession the priority over the residue belonging to von Triebenfeld to the amount of 57,900 Rthl, and these are liable among themselves with equal right. To No. 2. 32,300 Rthl, that is, thirty-two thousand three hundred Rthl Courant out of the above sum of the 250,000 Rthl, the holder thereof, the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, has ceded with full effect to the bankers Mendel Oppenheim and Wolff in Berlin, with priority over the residue as to the 25,600 Rthl, by the instrument executed on 8 September 1805 before a notary and witnesses, which by virtue of the decree of the 10th of the same month

22 Feb. [uncertain: gr.] has been registered.

3., A protestation concerning a capital of 8000 Rthl, that is, eight thousand Reichsthaler, together with interest at 5 per cent from Christmas 1803, on behalf of the heirs of the Count von Seher-Thoss, [uncertain: Oberhof mundschmuck (possibly Oberhofmundschenk, Chief Cupbearer of the Court)], has been registered, upon the application of the Royal Breslau Board of Wards of 14 Feb. 1806, with reference to the settlement dated Breslau, 25 January 1804, between the proprietor, the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Friedrich Ludwig, Serene Highness, and the special authorised representative of the said Count Seher-Thoss, by virtue of the decree of 4 March 1806, against the estates of Trąbczyn, Szetlewek, Zagórów, Kopojno and Drzewce, also Swięcia with their appurtenances. I hereby certify the agreement of this extract with the mortgage book remaining from the former government, with the addition that the above state of the mortgage of the estate of Zagórów has in no respect been altered down to the present day. Given at Kalisz, on the eighteenth day of September in the year one thousand eight hundred and eleven. Conservator of Mortgages of the Department of Kalisz
