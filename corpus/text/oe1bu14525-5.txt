--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A royal grant by Friedrich Wilhelm, replacing an earlier gift that had failed because the estates of the absconded former starosta von Prusimski proved heavily indebted: the King instead conveys to the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, hereditarily and in full property, sixteen formerly ecclesiastical villages including Pszczew, Łowyń, Zagórów, Kopojno and Wrąbczyn, subject only to a fixed unalterable canon of sovereign dues and the maintenance owed to the ecclesiastical bodies. Provincial authorities are ordered not to obstruct possession. Certified copy of 1803.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
We Friedrich Wilhelm, by the Grace of God King of Prussia etc., do hereby attest and declare by this Our open letter, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors to the Crown, to the Electorate, and to Our remaining lands, that whereas it has been most humbly laid before Us in what manner Our intention — namely, to give to the High-born Prince, Our kindly beloved uncle, Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, by the grant of the estates that had belonged to the absconded former starosta von Prusimski, a mark of the friendship and goodwill We bear towards him, as also of Our Royal gratitude for the distinguished and beneficial services rendered to Us and to Our Royal House — has not been attained entirely as We had willed it, because the said estates were burdened with many debts, We have most highly resolved to make over, grant and convey to His Lordship the hereinafter-named formerly ecclesiastical estates, namely the villages of Pszczew, Świechocin, Stoki, Łowyń, Głażewo, Tarnowo, Zagórów, Skokum, Swiątniki, Kopojno, Olesnica, Drzewce, Grądzyń, Wrąbczyn, Wienitz and Wolica, together with all their appurtenances, by whatever name they may be called, against payment of,

1.) the sovereign dues hitherto discharged therefrom, as a fixed and unalterable canon, and

2, the competence due therefrom to the ecclesiastical corporations or foundations.

to be left over to him hereditarily and in full property, granted and conveyed.

We do so likewise hereby and by virtue of this present, out of Royal power and plenitude, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors to the Crown, to the Electorate and to Our remaining lands, in such wise and so that Our aforesaid kindly beloved uncle, the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, shall from now on and for all time, for Himself and His descendants and heirs, irrevocably have, possess and enjoy the above-named estates with all rights and liberties attaching thereto, as these have hitherto been exercised or may lawfully be exercised, and no less with all their pertinences and other appurtenances, by whatever name they may be called, as the same have hitherto been used or may lawfully be used, and with all the lands belonging thereto, in fields, woods and meadows, likewise in ponds and waters, as a true, well-acquired and unrestricted property, from Us and Our descendants and successors; and that there shall further be conferred upon the same the power and authority to dispose thereof at pleasure under the above-mentioned conditions, unhindered by any man.

We therefore command and enjoin all Our Supreme Provincial Colleges, as also all other authorities whom this concerns, herewith as graciously as earnestly, that they in no wise hinder or impede the Lordship of the oft-mentioned Lord Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen in the hereditary and proprietary possession of the above-named estates granted to Him, together with their pertinences and appurtenances, nor him or those upon whom He may one day bequeath the same, or may otherwise lawfully transfer them by other acts inter vivos aut mortis causa, but rather shall let His Lordship, and eventually them also, enjoy the same freely and quietly, shall themselves do nothing against it, nor cause or permit that such be done by others, on pain of Our Most High disfavour and unfailing severe punishment. In witness whereof etc. Given at Berlin, the 19th of June 1797. Deed of Grant.

Friedrich Wilhelm

for the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, to the hereditary and proprietary possession of the villages of Pszczew, Świechocin, Stoki, Łowyń, Głażewo, Tarnowo, Zagórów, Skokum, Swiątniki, Kopojno, Olesnica, Drzewce, Grądzyń, Wrąbczyn, Wienietz and Wolica, together with appurtenances. Hoym. Reck.
