--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Replying to Triebenfeld's letter of 1 January after consulting Herr v Weithausen from Cosel and Bolli, the Prince accepts in principle Herr v Brzechsta's leasehold offer for the Zagorow estates, insisting on half-yearly rent paid prenumerando, cash security in hard coin, repair of the three demesne farms, the lessee bearing all burdens, and clauses barring actions for remission or recourse; hopes for a higher bid. Reports no progress with General v Schimmelpfennig, awaits the old Baron v Stössel, urges haste, asks 300 Rthl for L. Sommer, and requests game and the promised dog.

the lease offer of Mr. von Brzechsta deserves my attention, and since Your Right Honourable's counsel and [uncertain: opinion] carry great weight with me in all cases, I shall therefore, inasmuch as I honour your insight, take it into consideration with your [illegible] with preference; it is to be hoped that one will not wish to stop at the first bid; concerning the putting-in-repair of the 3 demesne farms we shall doubtless come to agreement; the half-yearly payment of the lease rent seems to me advisable and such must naturally follow in advance; some cash security seems to me necessary for proper assurance, and I ask Your Right Honourable to make the [uncertain: necessary] arrangements upon this point: That the lease contract be concluded in such a way that with all caution all suits for remission and recourse be forestalled lies in the nature of the matter; the security offered out of the whole estate of the lessee may be very good, but in the present times almost only hard ringing coin is a [uncertain: secure] pledge; that the bearing of all burdens on the part of the lessee [uncertain: is his] I find natural; to fix a sum for withdrawal against a possible sale during the term of the lease I myself find [uncertain: proper], and I shall let myself be found as fair as possible in this at the conclusion of the contract; in the contract everything hereupon would then have to be [uncertain: precisely] determined; this and any other conditions tending toward a sale of the estates would then emerge more particularly in the negotiations here

After these my expressions I believe that Your Right Honourable may [uncertain: reach agreement] with your friend, whereupon I shall gladly offer my hand to the conclusion of the lease; herein, in my opinion, the term of the lease, the amount of the rent and the necessary conditions would also first have to be quite exactly determined; herein it follows of itself, to be sure, that Mr. von Brzechsta would have to bring several thousand Rthl with him, in order to be able to conclude everything at once. With the Mr. General von Schimmelpfennig I have not advanced a single step further in the matter of our estate transaction, which delay has arisen partly through his severely suffered illness, partly through the not so desirably advanced negotiation of his sons in Berlin concerning the capital of their lady mother. Since I received the news yesterday, however, that the General is now himself again taking up his affairs, I shall send to him and learn his decision more closely; I confess that any decent sale of the Zagorowo estates will always be the agreeable thing to me, therefore I will here also so act that I may not have to reproach myself with any reproach of a duty of omission toward myself; but since your friend in leasing is always to be covered by the stipulation of indemnification, I hope Your Right Honourable will let nothing hinder you from coming here with him as soon as possible, since for several reasons I must particularly wish that on the arrival of my children I may also be able to show that with the Zagorowo estates, through Your Right Honourable's exertion and preparatory work, a possibly useful [uncertain: portion] has been drawn; [uncertain: after] we can hold it for this: political occurrences, and the pressing situation in which you find yourself on account of the dues to be rendered from the estates, make haste in the undertaking advisable, in which I would not hinder you, Your Right Honourable's project of sale to Mr. Weigel and Baron von Stössel.
