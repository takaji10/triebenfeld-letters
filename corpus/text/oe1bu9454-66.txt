--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Triebenfeld erwartet, dass die falschen Denuncianten 6 Jahre Festungsstrafe erhalten und die Aufwiegler ausgewiesen werden. Honrichs' Forderung an Hecker übersteigt dessen Caution dreifach. Bitten der Collonisten und Bauern um Zinsnachlass seien abzuweisen. Für Garczynski, Zakrzewski und Brzechffa lässt er drei Böcke schießen. Die Schiffer Kulawy und Neumann halten etliche 70 Centner Eisen wegen 243 Rthl 14 sg Frachtlohn fest; er bittet um die Quittungen. Der Trąbczyner Prozess wird viel kosten. Coste würde 2000 Centner Schieneisen mit 8000 Rthl bezahlen.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Triebenfeld says he expects the false informers to be given six years' imprisonment in a fortress and the agitators to be expelled. Honrichs' claim against Hecker, he says, is three times the latter's security. Requests from the colonists and peasants for a reduction of interest should be refused. For Garczynski, Zakrzewski and Brzechffa he is having three bucks shot. The boatmen Kulawy and Neumann are holding back some seventy odd Centner of iron over 243 Rthl 14 sg of freight charges; he asks for the receipts. The Trąbczyn lawsuit will cost a great deal. Coste, he says, would pay 8,000 Rthl for 2,000 Centner of bar iron.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
66 The verdict will now, as I am written, turn out thus: that the false denunciators are to be sentenced to 6 years' fortress imprisonment — the instigators, however, [uncertain: flogged out] and banished from the country.

As for Hecker I shall nevertheless do everything possible for his father's sake, who is Senior Consistorial Councillor in Berlin, but Honrichs is making a claim against him which exceeds his paper caution 3 times over; on Your Serene Highness's side too he is being held to account, for it was through him that the administration was introduced. If for the rest the colonists and old peasants there should beg for a remission of the interest for the future — for at present Niemann pays anything, because Honrichs is not permitted to take anything but also will not let anyone else take anything par force — then one must not listen to it. If the fellows have nothing, then for the time being nothing can be taken from them; but upon a sale one has the advantage that all the arrears can nevertheless be reckoned in. And a prefect in Poland has an extensive power — if Brzechffa buys, he would torment the poor creatures soon enough — he can in turn spare them in respect of public dues and burdens — consequently Your Most Serene Highness need for the present pay no heed to the whimperings, and must concede the fellows nothing; they pay nothing in any case and have long given nothing. I am having

three [uncertain: roe] bucks shot here, of which Honrichs is to take one along for the Chief Prefect Garczynski, one for the President of the High Tribunal Zakrzewski, and one for the sub-prefect Brzechffa; that, I think, will work more than money, for the fellows have everything in their hands and still cut their pipes as they please — If things go as wished, then Honrichs gets the treasury back at New Year, the administration will be wholly lifted, and then we shall receive much money — One has now full power there over the subjects, and therefore this class of people all wish the Prussian government back, but meanwhile one can [uncertain: pain] them — So much for the Polish affairs — Whether the King will really come to Berlin is still doubted by many; if he really does come, then he will go to Paris too — for only a couple of days ago somebody wrote to me: if it goes back to Berlin, then it goes to Paris as well, for where should Louise find the strength to refuse the invitation, not to see the place where the finest fashions in the world beckon — In the meantime I believe nothing at all of the promises that are said to have been made to our good King. They will no doubt leave us lying in the [uncertain: Seat], and our wretched

[uncertain: existence] overlooked as a point on the map of Europe, as at Tilsit and Schönbrunn — A couple of days ago it was said that we should get Hanover back, a piece of Hesse, Brunswick, Magdeburg and the Halberstadt territory. But for this a greater faith is required than that which Frederick the Only ascribed to the prelate of Scharnewand when he was to have the mountain near Cont cleared away for him.

Here they will absolutely have it that Napoleon is suffering from dropsy of the chest and that his travel passport already lies with Pluto for signature on the [uncertain: Tischlinge]. Were that so, then something might yet be hoped for, especially if among us people no longer merely organised but acted with energy. Something must after all be afoot: the Poles were to be on the march back, to move into winter quarters and their garrisons on the 11th, they were already as far as below Konin, Kalisz and those parts — but on the 8th came a counter-order that they must go back at that instant as far as Galicia, because the Russians were occupying Galicia and pressing back the Poles stationed there. Honrichs says that since then everything is full; the [uncertain: Müßen] can, in order to appropriate the province of Warsaw to themselves — As soon as I have finished here I shall come at once to Sławięcice, and then I shall stay there a long while — I will lease out everything, provided only Coste [illegible] and I first have a free hand again.

That Princess Emilie may be right happy I wish from the bottom of my heart — She is the crown of the female sex, if there is anything I have seen. I beg for the continuance of Your Highness's most gracious goodwill and remain, until death, in deepest respect,

Your Most Serene Highness's

Kulawy and Neumann have as yet received only 173 Rthl 6 Groschen in coin from the syndic Horne at Opole — the Centner is contracted at 10 Groschen coin as far as Bojadła, which makes for 1000 Centner 416 Rthl. 16 Groschen coin; the men therefore still have to receive in freight money — 243 Rthl. 14 sg. coin, and in addition the possible costs at Wrocław, which however they have incurred wrongfully, for Kulawy and Neumann [uncertain: freighted] the iron again by way of Schuster of [uncertain: Neasal], by the names of Macholy, Christ Liebs, Vogel — and these are holding the iron back because they have not received their freight. Your Serene Highness promised to have the freight money settled; be so gracious, then, as to send the receipts with all haste, so that the boatmen Kulawy and Neumann are entirely satisfied as to the freight money, for then I get the 74 Centner of iron back and can help myself with it — Money is [uncertain: scarce] — I must weekly lay out money for postage, messengers' wages, advances in lawsuits, in Your Serene Highness's affairs, and am myself in want. There one receives nothing in it, because everything is still under arrest — I may let nothing lie idle, and therefore I contrive counsel where possible — the arrangement in Poland is now mad — for a lawsuit, for the power of attorney, where the matter amounts to 1000 Rthl., a stamped sheet of 10 Rthl. must be [uncertain: taken] — so too must the advances be paid straight away, in like measure, to the so-called [uncertain: burgraves]. The Trąbczyn lawsuit, the Niedźwiecki one, the Ostarszewski one, etc., will still cost frightful money; the two last alone now cost about 320 Rthl. For the Trąbczyn one the heart only [uncertain: trembles] — but it must be done in order to save the estates — the woman sits in Warsaw and whores; perhaps one outstrips the [uncertain: Curence] and swift snow comes to its goal. Do send the receipts, so that I can get the iron afloat and can determine the costs —

Should it not be possible that Your Most Serene Highness could have 2000 Centner of rail iron delivered here soon? For it 8000 Rthl in hard Courant would be paid at once.

The general contractor Coste would remit the monies immediately. I should think that if you gave instruction for the iron from the Wrocław depot to be delivered here, and had as much brought from there to Wrocław again, then, if we still had 14 days of good weather, the thing could be settled — I expect Coste at any hour; he is to give an instruction to pay, which I will send off at once by express courier — If it is accepted, good — if not, then the instruction comes back — Coste in return for it cheap grain out of Poland
