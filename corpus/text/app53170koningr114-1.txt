Actum in Conin Feria Quarta post Festa Solemnia Sacri Paschałis proxima Anno Domini 1767
Pro Parte Magnifico Prusimski Aggeris Visio
Ad Officium et Acta praesentia Castrensia Coninensia personaliter veniens Providus Bartholomeus Szepczyński de Villa Trąmpczyn Ministerialis Regalis Sanus existens Recognovit quia ipse habitis Secum Duobus Nobilibus Videlicet Generosis Stanislao Lipiński et Josepho Rudkowski hic Licet absentibus
In loco tamen Infrascripti Negotii pro tunc praesente ad Instantiam et Officiosam Requisitionem: Magnifici Antonii de Kolno Prusimski Bonorum Villae Trąmpczyn Cum Attinentiis haeredis Sabbatho Magno non pridem praeterito In haereditatem Bonorum Trąmpczyn Condescenderat Ibidemque taliter juxta Formam Juris et Consuetudinis existens Vidit et Debite Conspexit Groblą niedawno w końcu Stawiska Usypaną przez której usypanie Woda z Stawiska tego płynąca przez Dziedziny Trąmpczynskie Wstecz Się cofnęła y Olendrów w Lesiech Trąmpczyńskich Osiadłych z Rolmi Ku Uprawienim Wymierzwionemi y Łąkamy zalała Quae praemissa per Homines Vicinae Haereditatis Villae Łukom ex Mandato Eorun[dem?] Bonorum Haeredis his temporibus facta esse Asseruit de Quo Is idem Ministerialis Regalis Suprascriptus hanc Sui Relationem fecit facitque praesentem.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Bericht des Gerichtsboten Bartłomiej Szepczyński aus dem Dorf Trąbczyn, am 22. April 1767 in die Akten des Burggerichts Konin eingetragen. Auf Verlangen von Antoni Prusimski von Kolno, Erbherrn von Trąbczyn, ist er am Karsamstag mit zwei Adligen, Stanisław Lipiński und Józef Rudkowski, auf das Gut gegangen und hat einen Damm besichtigt, der vor kurzem am Ende des Teichgrunds aufgeschüttet wurde. Durch den Damm staute sich das Wasser, das aus dem Teichgrund durch die Trąbczyner Fluren fließt, zurück und überschwemmte die Olęder, die in den Trąbczyner Wäldern angesiedelt sind, samt ihren gedüngten Äckern und ihren Wiesen. Nach seiner Angabe haben das Leute des Nachbarguts Łukom auf Befehl von dessen Erbherrn getan.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The report of the court messenger Bartłomiej Szepczyński of the village of Trąbczyn, entered in the records of the castle court at Konin on 22 April 1767. At the request of Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, heir of Trąbczyn, he went onto the estate on Holy Saturday with two noblemen, Stanisław Lipiński and Józef Rudkowski, and inspected an embankment lately raised at the end of the pond-ground. Because of the embankment the water that flows from the pond-ground through the Trąbczyn lands backed up and flooded the Olęder settlers living in the Trąbczyn woods, with their manured fields and their meadows. He states that this was done by people of the neighbouring estate of Łukom on the order of its heir.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Done at Konin on the Wednesday next after the Solemn Feasts of Holy Easter in the year of the Lord 1767.

Judicial Site Inspection of the Embankment on behalf of the Right Honourable Prusimski

Coming in person to the office and present records of the Konin Municipal Court, the Honest Bartłomiej Szepczyński of the village of Trąbczyn, Royal Court Summoner, being of sound health, declared that he, having taken with him two noblemen, namely the Honourables Stanisław Lipiński and Józef Rudkowski, though absent here from the court,

being nonetheless present at the time at the location of the below-written matter — at the instance and official requisition of the Right Honourable Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, heir of the estates of the village of Trąbczyn with its appurtenances — on the Holy Saturday not long past had descended upon the hereditary estates of Trąbczyn, and being there in such manner according to the form of law and custom, saw and duly inspected:

an embankment recently raised at the end of the stawisko, by the raising of which the water flowing from that stawisko through the Trąbczyn estates had turned back, and had flooded the olędry settled in the Trąbczyn woodlands, together with their fields manured for cultivation and their meadows;

which aforementioned things he asserted had been done in these times by the people of the neighbouring hereditary estate of the village of Łukom, on the mandate of the heir of those estates;

concerning which the same above-written Royal Court Summoner made and makes this his present report.
