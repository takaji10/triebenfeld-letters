--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A 1766 privilege of Theodor, Prince Czartoryski, Bishop of Posen, granted at Dolsk and approved by the cathedral chapter in 1767, selling Simeon and Apollonia Rządkowski and their heirs a waste Hufe of arable at Głazewo in the Betsche key, with a manor-built house and barn, for 30 ducats, against 130 Polish gulden yearly in two instalments, poll-tax and winter money, four days of labour service and clearing of the Obsko meadow, with beer from the Betsche brewhouse. Given in German, in the Latin and Polish original extracted from the chapter archive, and with a sworn translator's certificate.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Capitulum Ecclesiae Cathedralis

Posnaniensis.

We make known by these presents to all and each whom it concerns: that We, upon the petition of the party whom it concerns, have ordered the privilege written out below, in which the Actus and the year are contained, and which was approved in the General Chapter of St Martin in the year 1767, to be taken out from the book of the Privilegia preserved in our Archive, where it was found, and to be issued in Forma probanti. The content whereof is as follows: Theodor of Klewan and Zukow, Prince of Czartorinski, by the grace of God and of the Holy Apostolic See Bishop of Posen and Warsaw, make known to all together and to each one to whose knowledge this shall come now and in future, that the noble Simeon and Apollonia Rząkowski, the married couple, have come before Us supplicando (have applied), that We might be pleased to grant over and to sell to them one Hufe of waste arable land lying in the village of Głazewo belonging to Our Betsche key, together with the dwelling house and barn built at the cost of the manor, against an annual rent according to the custom of the other inhabitants of this village. Since We defer to this their petition, which is directed to the increase of our certain income, We have resolved to grant over and to sell this same Hufe of arable together with the dwelling and the barn to those same persons and to their Successores, as by the present privilege, in consideration of the costs expended upon the aforementioned buildings, We do grant over and sell for thirty ducats in specie, say 30 ducats. From which manly Hufe of arable these same other

von Rząkowski married couple and their Successores bind themselves to pay 130 fl. Polish in two instalments, namely on the feast of St John the Baptist 65 fl. and on the feast of the Holy Three Kings the poll-tax, and to deliver the Hibern (money for the soldiers in winter) punctually to the commune according to custom; they shall be likewise bound to be sent, together with the commune, to the compulsory labour on the Głazewo demesne farm four times in the year, and likewise to the clearing of the meadow called Obsko, which has been added to this demesne farm by right. For every two quarters of barley, old Neustceter measure, one tun of beer from the Betsche brewhouse shall be allowed. We promise hereupon, in Our name and in the name of Our most laudable Successores the Bishops of Posen, that We shall not remove the aforementioned noble von Rządkowski married couple from the use and quiet possession, nor their Successores either, of this Hufe of arable, nor shall We permit anyone whatsoever the power to remove them, but rather shall maintain them unshaken under the present privilege, and Our aforesaid Successores shall likewise maintain them. Reserving the rights of the Church and of the Republic. Whereupon We have signed with Our own hand for greater validity, and have ordered it to be confirmed with the seal. Given at Dolsk the 26th of September Anno Domini 1766. Theodor, Prince von Czartoryski, Bishop in Posen (L. S.) Ginlily ./. Andreas Miskowski D. C. P. President Capituli (L. S. Capitularis.)

Capitulum Ecclesie Cathedralis Posnaniensis. We signify by the tenor of these presents to all and each whom it concerns, that We, at the petition of the party whom it concerns, have ordered the privilege written out below, concerning the act and year contained therein and approved in the General Chapter of St Martin in the year of Our Lord one thousand seven hundred and sixty-seven, found in the book of Privileges of the Cathedral Church of Posen preserved in our Archive, to be transumed and to be issued in probative form; the tenor whereof follows, of this kind: Theodor of Klewań and Żuków, Prince Czartorski, by the grace of God and of the Holy Apostolic See Bishop of Posen and Warsaw. To all in common and to each severally, to whom it shall belong to know of this now and hereafter, We make known that the noble Simeon and Apollonia Rządkowcy, married couple, have supplicated Us that We might be pleased to let out and sell to them a waste holding of arable in the village of Głazewo, belonging to our key of Pszczew, together with the dwelling building and barn erected at manorial cost, at a rent differing [uncertain: in accordance with] the other inhabitants of that same village. We, inclining to such a petition inasmuch as it tends to the increase of our certain revenue, have resolved to let out and sell that same holding of arable together with the dwelling and the barn to them and to their successors, as by the present privilege, in respect of the said cost of the said buildings, We do let out and sell for red gulden in specie, thirty, say # 30; from which holding of arable those noble Rządkowski and their successors shall be obliged to pay one hundred and thirty Polish gulden in two instalments, that is, on the feast of St John the Baptist sixty-five gulden, on the feast of the Holy Three Kings likewise sixty-five, and to render the poll-tax and the Hiberna punctually to the commune according to custom. They shall be bound to send, together with the commune, to the compulsory labour on the Głazewo demesne farm four times in the year, and likewise to the clearing of the meadow called Obska, joined by right to that same demesne farm. For every two quar-

-ters of barley of the old Zwowecki measure, one barrel of beer from the Pszczew brewery is to be permitted. We promise by this Our present, and in the name of Our Most Illustrious successors the Bishops of Posen, that We shall not remove the said noble Rządkowski and their successors from the use and quiet possession of that same holding of arable, nor shall We permit to anyone whomsoever the power of removing them; on the contrary, We shall preserve them inviolate under the present privilege, and Our Most Illustrious successors shall preserve them. Preserving entire the rights of the Church and of the Republic. Whereupon, for the greater weight, having subscribed with Our own hand, We have ordered it to be confirmed with Our seal. Given at Dolsk on the twenty-sixth day of September in the Year of Our Lord one thousand seven hundred and sixty-six. Theodor, Prince Czartonski, Bishop of Posen (L. S.) Gentilitio.
