--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Chapter transumpt from the Posen cathedral archive's Book of Privileges, approved in the General Capitulum of St Martin in 1767, reproducing a privilege of Theodor Prince Czartoryski, Bishop of Posen, granting Simeon and Apollonia Rząkowski and their Successores one Hufe of waste arable at Głazewo in the Betsche key, with dwelling-house and barn, sold for thirty ducats in specie against 130 fl. po. yearly in two instalments of 65 fl., head-money and Hibernen, four annual labour services at the Głazewo demesne, clearing of the Obsko meadow, and beer from the Betsche brewhouse.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Capitulum Ecclesiae Cathedralis

Posnaniensis.

We make known by these presents to all and each whom it concerns: that We, upon the petition of the party whom it concerns, have commanded the privilege written below, in which the Actus and the year are contained, and which was approved in the General Capitulum of St. Martin in the year 1767, to be taken out, having been found in the Book of the Privilegia preserved in Our archive, and to be issued in Forma probanti. The content whereof is as follows: Theodor of Klewan and Zukow, Prince of Czartorinski, by the grace of God and of the Holy Apostolic See Bishop of Posen and Warsaw, make known to all together and to each one to whose knowledge this shall come now and in the future, that the noble Simeon and Apollonia Rząkowski, the married couple, have come before Us supplicando (have applied), that We might be pleased to make over and to sell to them one Hufe of waste arable lying in the village of Głazewo belonging to Our Betsche key, together with the dwelling-house and barn built at the cost of the manor, against an annual rent according to the custom of the other inhabitants of this village. Since We defer to this their petition, which is directed to the increase of Our certain revenue, We have resolved to make over and to sell this same Hufe of arable together with the dwelling and the barn to the same persons and to their Successores, as We do, by the present privilege, in consideration of the costs expended upon the aforesaid buildings, make over and sell for thirty ducats in specie, say 30 ducats. Of which manly Hufe of arable these same other

v. Rząkowski married couple and their Successores oblige themselves to pay 130 fl. po. in two instalments, namely 65 fl. at the Feast of John the Baptist and at the Feast of the Holy Three Kings the head-money and the Hibernen (money for the soldiers in winter), and to render these punctually to the commune according to custom: they shall likewise be bound to send [labourers] to the demesne farm of Głazewo four times in the year for the requisitioned labour, and likewise, together with the commune, to the clearing of the meadow called Obsko, which has by right been added to this demesne farm. For every two quarters of barley, old Neustadt measure, one tun of beer from the Betsche brewhouse shall be allowed. We promise hereafter, in Our own name and in the name of Our most honourable Successores the Bishops of Posen, that We shall not remove the aforesaid von Rząkowski married couple, nor likewise their Successores, from the use and the quiet possession of this Hufe of arable, nor yet permit to anyone the power to remove the same — We do not [permit it] — but rather shall maintain them undisturbed in the present privilege, and that Our most honoured Successores shall so maintain them also. Reserving the rights of the Church and of the Republic. Whereupon We have subscribed with Our own hand and, for the greater validity, have commanded it to be confirmed with the seal. Given at Dolsk, the 26th of September, Anno Domini 1766. Theodor Prince v. Czartoryski, Bishop in Posen (L. S.) Ginlily ./. Andreas Miskowski D. C. P., President Capituli (L. S. Capitularis.)

(L. S.)
