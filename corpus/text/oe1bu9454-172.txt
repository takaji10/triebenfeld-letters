--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

Since yesterday I have been here in Trąbczyn in order to press forward the dismemberment business with Eyster. There are already a great many people here, but since many more were expected, I have postponed the principal appointed day until next Monday, when I then certainly intend to conclude the contracts over all the parcels of ground. President von Goldbeck is now making himself out noble and gallant; he has written to Minister Voss for eight days' leave in order to go to Breslau and to be able to arrange his domestic affairs; on that occasion he says to Herr von Vor: In view of the district councillor's protocols submitted to Your [uncertain: Epl.] concerning the dismemberment business of the Prince of Hohenlohe, I must still most humbly observe that the matter has already progressed too far and could not be reversed. Upon closer examination I have found that the Chamber has already consented to the dismemberment and has furnished to the district councillor von Blomberg the principles pertaining thereto under the 2nd of September of the present year. Both in these and also in the latter report the departmental councillor, councillor of war zur Höllen,

has incurred unpardonable errors and compromised the collegiate board; against this, the princely manorial lordship (Dominium) has conducted itself better and has done more than was required by the Chamber at that time, so that only in few cases has there been any acting contrary to the norm.

In the name of the collegiate board I beg Your pardon, but at the same time I pledge that, if You should be pleased to grant the consent to the dismemberment of the princely estates, the gross errors of the departmental councillor shall be rectified so far as possible, etc. etc.

That is after all everything an honest man can do, and with his approval I am now to proceed briskly and dismember everything at once; for even if His Excellency von Voss should wish to make difficulties, the Chamber must keep its word and press for the consent. This part has been played masterfully through all the acts. With the people of Olesnica I think I shall soon be finished, for today Mona Kunckel was fetched away by the mounted district officer and, through the mediation of Schencis, placed in custody for a longer period. Now it will be easy play with the 3 knaves.

On account of Szetlewek I shall have a warm day tomorrow; God grant good fortune as in other cases.

Today the Winnich heirs have again brought suit and demand a further 1300 Rthl of arrears in surveying costs. It is truly frightful how matters have gone on here: the revenues are constantly used up and not a soul has been paid.

By this conveyance may Your Serene Highness be pleased to have the 6 tables and 30 fruit trees carried to the President. I have instructed the man that he is indeed to say that he brings it all from me. — For the rest, may God the All-Good only grant that Amelang does good business, for then all wishes are attained. In deepest reverence I remain, until death, Your Most Serene Highness's Trąbczyn, the 28th of March

1805.

faithful and most humble and obedient servant von Triebenfeld
