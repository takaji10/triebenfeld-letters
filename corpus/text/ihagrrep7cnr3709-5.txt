--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Der Fürst von Hohenlohe schreibt dem Großkanzler: Die minderjährige Michalina von Prusimska hat bei der Regierung zu Posen gegen ihn auf Herausgabe der ihm vom König geschenkten Brzycer Güter geklagt; die Sache soll nun zum Spruch vorgelegt werden. Da das Pupillenkollegium zu Posen den Prozess im Namen der Klägerin betreibt und seine Mitglieder zugleich Mitglieder der Regierung sind, also seine Richter wären, bittet er, das Urteil der Regierung zu Kalisch zu übertragen und ihr die Akten zuzuschicken. Die empfangende Behörde vermerkte darauf den 2. Dezember 1800.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The Prince of Hohenlohe writes to the Grand Chancellor: the minor Michalina von Prusimska has sued him before the Government at Poznań for the recovery of the Brzyce estates, which the King gave him; the case is now to be laid before the court for judgment. Since the guardianship board at Poznań conducts the suit in the plaintiff's name and its members are also members of the Government, and so would be his judges, he asks that the judgment be entrusted to the Government at Kalisz and the files sent there. The receiving office marked it with the date 2 December 1800.

Right Honourable Sir!

Most Highly Honoured Lord Grand Chancellor!

The minor Michalina von Prusimska has brought a suit against me before the Government [text lost] Poznań for the recovery of the Brzyce estates given to me by His [text lost] Majesty. The case has now been prepared and is to be laid before the court for judgment [text lost]. Since the guardianship board at Poznań is conducting this lawsuit in the name of the minor plaintiff, but the members of the guardianship board are at the same time members of the Government, and would consequently be my judges in the case, I humbly ask Your Excellency: "both for these and for other reasons, which depend solely [text lost] on my individual conviction, graciously to give the order that the Government at Kalisz should draw up the judgment in this case, and that to this end the files
