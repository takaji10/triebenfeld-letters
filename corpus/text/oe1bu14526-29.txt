ne Erbzinsguth an seine Halbschwester die

Vorkaufsrecht zustehet, so bitten wir höchst¬

unterthänigste

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A court office at Betsche notifies the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen that Sigismund von Rzadkowski has sold his hereditary quit-rent holding at Głazewo to his half-sister Susanna, née Treben, enclosing a copy of the sale deed of 28 September and its papers. The contract has been approved subject to the princely rights, and the Prince is asked to declare within a set number of weeks whether he will exercise his right of pre-emption; silence will count as waiving it.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince,

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

From the deed of sale of the 28th of September of the current year, appended here in Copy, and from its enclosures, Your Most Serene Highness will most graciously be pleased to gather that Sigismund von Rzadkowski has sold the hereditary quit-rent estate situated in Głazewo to his half-sister Susanna, née Treben. Since now the right of pre-emption belongs to Your Most Serene Highness, we beg the same most highly (the contract has been approved under reservation of the princely rights) most graciously to declare, within S. weeks, whether the same most highly will make use of the right of pre-emption; because, after the expiry of this term, it is assumed in accordance with the laws that the same most highly relinquishes this right for the present case.

In which we persist with deepest reverence, Your Serene Highness's

most humble and obedient

The Court Office of this place

Metzig

Neumann

Betsche

the 10th of April 1805
