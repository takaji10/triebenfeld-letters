--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Das fürstliche Gerichtsamt zu Pszczew meldet dem Fürsten zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, dass Sigismund von Rzadkowski sein Erbzinsgut in Głażewo an seine Halbschwester Susanna geborene Treben verkauft hat; der Vertrag ist unter Vorbehalt der fürstlichen Rechte genehmigt. Da dem Fürsten das Vorkaufsrecht zusteht, soll er sich binnen einer Frist erklären, ob er es ausüben will; sonst gilt es nach dem Gesetz für diesen Fall als aufgegeben.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The Prince's court office at Pszczew reports to the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen that Sigismund von Rzadkowski has sold his hereditary-rent holding at Głażewo to his half-sister Susanna, born Treben; the contract has been approved without prejudice to the Prince's rights. Since the Prince holds the right of pre-emption, he is to declare within a set time whether he will use it; otherwise by law he is taken to have waived it in this case.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene Prince,

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

From the deed of sale of the 28th of September of the current year, appended here in Copy, and from its enclosures, Your Most Serene Highness will most graciously be pleased to gather that Sigismund von Rzadkowski has sold the hereditary quit-rent estate situated in Głażewo to his half-sister Susanna, née Treben. Since now the right of pre-emption belongs to Your Most Serene Highness, we beg the same most highly (the contract has been approved under reservation of the princely rights) most graciously to declare, within S. weeks, whether the same most highly will make use of the right of pre-emption; because, after the expiry of this term, it is assumed in accordance with the laws that the same most highly relinquishes this right for the present case.

In which we persist with deepest reverence, Your Serene Highness's

most humble and obedient

The Court Office of this place

Metzig

Neumann

Pszczew

the 10th of April 1805
