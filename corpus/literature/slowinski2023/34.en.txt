I would most like one who comes with a farm. What grieves me most is that you had to become a soldier and are not yet married." He is also very grateful that Bruno wrote a letter to Kadzia. She is at once different after she has read the letter. I tried to tell her this – but with words one cannot always achieve what one would like. Nor was it simple and pleasant. What the brothers tried to talk about and write to their sister about, we do not know. At the end, the good advice of the elder one. He is sending a parcel of food. "And may it arrive safely and taste good to you, and serve your health. Above all you should not go hungry. From the bread and bacon I sent today one can cut two slices and put a piece of bacon in the middle, then hide it in your pocket and nibble at it from time to time. I only fear – for I know your comradeship and your good heart – that you will immediately share everything and keep nothing for yourself. I do that too – but everything has its limits in the world."

Bruno writes from Innsbruck on 30 May that they are finishing their training in the Alps and the next day set off for Lapland. (In Innsbruck was stationed the 2nd Mountain Rifle Division formed on 1 April 1938 – the 2. Gebirgs-Division, and with this formation the war fortunes of the soldier Mielke will be linked). At the Sunday mass many high-ranking officers appeared, and he was also allowed to invite a few of his comrades, *Kameraden*, to the church.
