Preserved in the hiding place at Łazińsk is an interesting example of various forms of a peculiar sapping of the morale of the German home front. It is a planted fake of a document appointing its addressee as a reproducer, who is to "service 9 women and 18 girls" in order to raise the birth statistics. (Probably in the area it was known that Gustav Adolf had for some time been looking around for a candidate for a wife). The authors note that this honorable task cannot be protested against by the wife and will not be recognized as grounds for divorce.

The "official paper" is an example of fictitious, misleading information and directives produced in the underground and distributed among the Germans. A special unit in the information and propaganda directorate of the AK (Home Army) was occupied with this. It must be added that officials of the SS Main Office of Race and Settlement (RuSHA), responsible for the purity and propagation of the Germanic race, carried out a kind of selection among the slaves sent to labor in Germany, qualifying selected men as so-called race men (rasowcy).
