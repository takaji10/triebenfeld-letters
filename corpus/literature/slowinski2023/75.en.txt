# Słowiński (2023), p. 75
# translation by the editor's files

> You are hereby recognized as a person of particular productivity, and in connection with this we resolve to confer upon you the honorary office of fulfilling the German duty. It is noted that your wife has no right to divorce on account of this duty of yours, since every woman must accept this small inconvenience as a consequence of the war. A district is assigned to you, which will be specified more precisely, comprising 9 women and 18 girls. If you should prove incapable of performing the task assigned to you, then you are obliged to report this within 3 days, citing the file reference given above and naming an able man as your substitute. If, however, you are able to take on, in addition to your district, yet another district, then you will be appointed an officer-reproducer and will receive, in addition to the reproduction premium, also the Order for Copulation, First Class, with red ribbon. You will receive the list of ladies whom you are to visit shortly. You are asked to commence your blessed activity at once, and the success after 9 months is to be reported here on a form which will be sent separately.
