# Słowiński (2023), p. 25
# translation by the editor's files

 The Bible – in keeping with tradition – was a wedding gift for Samuel Julius Mielke and Natalie Tonn in the year 1892. The volume contains, before the title page, pages for keeping a family chronicle with handwritten entries by the recipients, which I write about further on. Among the books there are two collections of sermons, *Rozważania na każdy dzień roku* [Reflections for Every Day of the Year], a New Testament in a pocket edition, and other mystical, religious works. There are guides to married and sexual life (*Liebe und Ehe. Ein Buch Für Eheleute* or *Das Liebes und Geschlechtsleben des Menschen*) as well as songbooks for festive occasions. There is also *Schlag nach über England* – a guide to England and the British Empire for a mere 50 pfennigs… It is difficult to enumerate all these titles in detail[^8].

It is worth returning once more to the political part of the library collection and devoting a few words to Goebbels's biographer. Wilfrid Alfred Karl Bade (born 1906) belonged to the very active figures in Nazi propaganda. He rose to the position of government counsel in the Ministry of Public Enlightenment and Propaganda of the Third Reich. He was one of the leading lecturers and "creative interpreters" of the thought and ideas of the führer and his superior Goebbels. Besides the biography of Goebbels first published in 1933, he also produced such works as: *Die SA erobert Berlin* [The SA Conquers Berlin], *Geschichte des Dritten Reich 4 Bünde* [History of the Third Reich in 4 Volumes], *Das Hohelied vom Dritten Reich* [The Song of Songs of the Third Reich], or *Tod Und Leben. Verse des Krieges* [Death and Life. Verses of the War]. Bade died in December 1945 in Russian captivity in Kaunas. Yet more important than the author of the biography is its subject – Paul Joseph Goebbels himself.
