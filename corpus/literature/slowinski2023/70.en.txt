"If only dear God, with his grace, would be close to our people, so that everything ends well and so that mankind may again know peace." On Sunday 8 August he sends a letter which he writes in some religious rapture, at 11 o'clock, in the blessed hour of the services. "In spirit I see the churches in all lands, I see in them those who through the blood and salvation of Christ have become my siblings." And he sees himself in the pulpit – in the most splendid of all official vestments, proclaiming the gospel. Instead of greetings he asks them to read out Psalm 23.

psalm 23

*The Lord is my shepherd*…

*Psalm of David*.  
*The Lord is my shepherd*,  
*I shall lack nothing*.  
*In green pastures he pastures me*.  
*He leads me beside still waters*.  
*He refreshes my soul*.  
*He leads me in the paths of righteousness*  
*For the sake of his name*.  
*Even though I walk through a dark valley*,  
*I will fear no evil, for You are with me*,  
*Your rod and Your staff comfort me*.  
*You set a table before me in the presence of my enemies*,  
*You anoint my head with oil, my cup overflows*.  
*Goodness and mercy shall accompany me*  
*All the days of my life*.  
*And I shall dwell in the house of the Lord for long days*[^15].



[^15]: Psalm 23, quotation added – M.S.
