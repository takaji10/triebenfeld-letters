# Słowiński (2023), p. 68
# translation by the editor's files

 War. He asks how the "total war" has affected their lives. Whether his brother wants to enlist in the army – though he is so needed in Łazińsk, and besides he has a wedding in the plan. (Soon the Wehrmacht itself will decide about this, without asking Gustav's opinion.) At the end Bruno writes: "Two weeks ago we listened to the speech of the Führer. He spoke of how decisive times our nation and fatherland are living through. On this special spring day, when we have happily survived the winter and countless dangers, we should be joyful and grateful that God, through our Führer, is preserving us from the threatening evil, and how this strengthens us, soldiers, that we do not bear our sacrifices in vain, do not suffer in vain and – die in vain."

Still, in many letters from the coming months written by the soldier Bruno we read with a certain astonishment that he believes more in the successive assurances of the leader and the assorted retinue of politicians than in what he sees and must do at the front. How dangerous is the force of propaganda, which falls on well-prepared ideological ground. In what a different tone he writes a letter six days later. He already knows that he will not get leave for Easter; too many with longer service are in the queue ahead of him. "I already long for the quiet, for the blessed hours, and I remember the time I was able to experience in your house, dear brother. Łazińsk, Zagórów, also Anielewo, Białobłoty, Maślaki, Konary, Grodziec – all of these are blessed places!" So he will not be at Anni's wedding, and Gustav's plans, too, may be thwarted. They may call him up into the army. Which would be fatal! Evidently the Lord, who is in the heavens, has so ordained all this. Yet he believes that "He, the faithful Saviour, will never leave me alone, and at Easter again – as every day – he will comfort me with his nearness! This spiritual strengthening is what I especially need now. For soon the decisive attacks must, here in the east, gain in strength, and then various thoughts come. It will be as God wills." He advises his loved ones to listen to and proclaim the holy word in the time of Easter. That of which he now lacks so much.
