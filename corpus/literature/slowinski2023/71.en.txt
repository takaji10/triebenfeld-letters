# Słowiński (2023), p. 71
# translation by the editor's files

> *In You, Lord, I place my trust*  
> (Ps 71:1)

The September (16 September 1943) dating of the letter carries a new annotation: "in the field". It is above all an extensive text on the occasion of Gustav's 44th birthday. We read a long disquisition on the virtues of 36-year-old Elza – the girl to whom he wants to draw his brother's attention. He knows her only from a friend's account, but what decides her merits (in accordance with Bruno's system of values, which we have already come to know) is the fact that she is pure in faith, character and virtue, that is, she possesses all those most beautiful values which qualify her to be his wife. "Of course you must judge for yourself whether she pleases you […] but you must not set your judgments and demands too high – more important than outward beauty, after all, remains inner worth". As for children, despite her age, she may yet give birth to a few, and surely he does not want a large brood…? The letter also brings thanks to the family for the birthday wishes sent and assurances of prayers to God for intercession on his behalf. He writes all this in the field and explains where such an address comes from. "Being in good health, I am presently helping with road construction. It is not easy to work physically 9–10 hours with pick and shovel, but everything will pass; I am grateful that the food is good (better than last year) and that I can work for Germany, and not like a prisoner of war in Russia – as has happened to many".
