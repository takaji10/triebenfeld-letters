The congregations Sompolno, Babiak, Przedecz and Chodecz belonged at that time to the Płock superintendency. It was a wide-reaching territory over which the Kalisch diocese extended. The total number of the affiliated parishioners amounted within it to 68,581, of them 32,928 of the male sex and 35,653 of the female. "In 198 schools 4753 boys and 4049 girls — 8802 children of both sexes — were instructed by 204 teachers and 1 teacher"[^p13-13]

Superintendent von Modl was, although not entirely free of rationalistic influences, an active and energetic administrator of the diocese entrusted to him[^p13-14] The visitations of the congregations lay very near his heart. Thus he visited, for example, in the year 1829 nine parishes, in which he always took care to promote the religious-ecclesiastical life in them and to convey appropriate impulses. As he himself attested, at the visitations he wished to ascertain how the pastors "sought to make themselves worthy" as preachers, liturgists, catechists, shepherds of souls "in the vineyard of the Lord," whether the "cura animarum" was their chief task. Of the church councils he wrote: "Our church colleges have lost the significance of the presbyteries . . . and have become merely ordinary stewards of the church's property." He therefore called the attention of the church elders all the more to the fact that "their sphere is a greater one than that of mere stewards of the church's property . . ., that the promotion of the salvation of souls" must also lie near to them. Unfortunately, he said, he had "heard only a few voices in the church colleges which expressed a pious wish directed to the salvation of the congregation's souls . . ." On the school question Sup. von Modl expressed himself in a report to the general consistory to the effect that "our congregations have almost no properly organized schools of their own at all . . . Can we, few excepted, call the institutions that exist under cantors etc. schools? May we designate the prayer-readers and teachers in our colonies and Hauländereien with the name 'schoolmaster'? . . . If our Zion is not to collapse, it is high time to hasten to the aid of the congregation of the Lord by taking more Christian care of its youth."
