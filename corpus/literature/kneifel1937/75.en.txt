# Kneifel (1937), p. 75
# translation by Claude

On June 27, 1926 the congregation celebrated, after many years, again a mission festival, in which the pastors: Consistorial Councilor Loth, Friedenberg, Erich Buse, Gross and Doberstein took part.

On the basis of the bequest of January 9, 1797 the congregation possesses: 1 Morgen of land and 2 Morgen of meadow (at Stare-Grądy) for the pastor and 1 Morgen of land for the cantor. In the year 1927 the parish acquired, at Pastor Ulbrich's suggestion, a further 9.5 Morgen of land for 10,000 zlotys. This land was bought in order that the local pastor might have his own team of horses for the visiting of the congregation. In 1929 the organ was repaired and a congregational library established. On September 9, 1934 the parish received back from the gmina Szymanowice the property of the cantorate of Obory (prayer hall, schoolhouse, farm buildings and 15 Morgen of land), which in the year 1919 the "dozór szkolny" (school board) had taken over. The lawsuit instituted against this was won by the evangelical congregation. It is the third cantorate (Orlina Wielka, Obory and Gadow) that has become finally the property of the congregation of Grodziec. Concerning Białobłoty (12 Morgen) and Wojciechów (5 Morgen) like lawsuits have been instituted. For the recovery of these cantorates, as also for the development of the whole congregational system, Pastor Ulbrich acquired enduring merit.

The religious-moral state of the parish is satisfactory. Church attendance is good, Bibles and devotional books are everywhere present, family prayers are held in many households.

In the congregation there still prevail particular customs and usages. Thus, for example, the local pastor performs all burials, even those of children. The confirmation takes place here always on Maundy Thursday. On Christmas eve the children recite the Christmas gospel.

Year of foundation: 1796.

Souls: 7000[^p75-9]. 1834: about 2500.

Church language: German.

Pastors.

David Bergemann, 1797–1807.

Johann Christian Gottlieb Rissmann, 1807–1815.

Johann Stegmann, 1815–1831.

Joseph Spleszynski, 1832–1839.

Karl Friedrich Orive, 1841–1876.

Karl Eduard Erdmann, 1878–1892.

August Loth, 1894–1898.

[^p75-9]: 9) The "Rocznik Ewangelicki 1925" gives for 1923 only 5700 souls. According to the number of births that is much too low. — In the vicinity of the church at Grodziec there dwells pretty nearly the half of the congregation. The other half is distributed among the 5 cantorates of the parish.
