Müller received, whose parish congregation Petrikau now came to lie in the newly organized diocese of Petrikau, the call to be superintendent of this church district. To the Kalisch superintendency there belonged, according to this new arrangement, the congregations treated here and the filial Panki, which afterward was absorbed into the parish of Czenstochau. In the year 1902 the pastorate of the Kalisch diocese elected, in place of the retiring Sup. Müller, Pastor Eduard Ignatius Boerner of Zduńska Wola as superintendent, which office he filled until his death in 1910. From 1910 to 1914 the diocese was administered by Superintendent Rudolf Buse of Grodziec, who directed his attention especially to the raising up of the cantorates. To this end he held cantors' conferences in his congregation and thereby spurred other pastors on to give more attention to the cantorate system, until then treated rather stepmotherly, and to hold like gatherings, from which manifold impulses and influences went out to the cantorates. Since Sup. Buse was banished to Russia at the outbreak of the war in 1914, where he died in 1917, Pastor Eduard Wende of Kalisch first represented him, then was entrusted wholly with the direction of the diocese.

In the years 1906–1914, as also after the recovery of Polish statehood, Gen.-Sup. Bursche visited the congregations of the Kalisch diocese repeatedly. In the detailed, clear visitation protocols composed by himself, which are to be reckoned an important contribution to the history of the diocese, the whole life of the parishes mirrors itself in a definite present situation.

The Kalisch diocese embraces a large part of the Lodz voivodeship — the counties of Koło, Konin, Turek, Kalisch, Sieradz, Łask, Wieluń — and a smaller part of the Warsaw voivodeship — the counties of Kutno, Łęczyca, Włocławek and Nieszawa. The number of evangelical affiliated parishioners in the diocese amounts to 65,200 souls; that of the German-evangelical schools — avowedly evangelical-confessional schools are not permitted by law — is subject to constant fluctuations[^p15-17]

According to the new church law, which will presumably come into force in the course of this year by decree of the president of the state, 16 parish and 5 filial congregations are to be included in the Kalisch diocese (instead of the hitherto existing 19 parishes and 4 filials).

[^p15-17]: 17) The author takes the school system into detailed consideration in the presentation of the individual congregations. As far as the German schools are concerned it is legally and factually quite confused, because it depends too much on the discretion of the respective school inspectors.
