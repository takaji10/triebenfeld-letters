# Kneifel (1937), p. 172
# translation by Claude

Janowo (formerly Butnowskie Holendry). Cantor Edmund Schmidt, about 15 evangelical children. Catholic teacher.

Dąbrowa. Cantor Karl Thebus, about 15 evangelical children. Catholic teacher.

Patrzykow. Cantor Albert Wolff, about 55 evangelical children.

Borowo. Cantor Robert Frost, about 22 evangelical children.

Brzeźno. Cantor Robert Frost, about 22 evangelical children.

Głodno. Cantor Robert Frost, about 20 evangelical children.

Town of Slesin, cantor Karl Thebus, about 18 evangelical children.

Konin. In the two general elementary schools with 7 classes, 42 evangelical children in all, to whom the local pastor gives instruction in religion.

Children's service.

The children's service at Konin takes place the whole year through, namely on Sundays at 9 o'clock in the morning, in the Polish and the German language.

Societies of the congregation.

Youth-league singing choirs: Konin, founded 1929, 13 members; Święcia; Bielawy; Borowo; Dąbrowa; Węglewskie Holendry; Brzezińskie Holendry.

Brass choirs: Święcia, founded 1910; Nowy Czarków, founded 1922; Węglewskie Holendry, founded 1928; Bielawy, founded 1929.

Youth league at Konin: founded 1929, 34 members.

Ladies' committee at Konin, founded 1928; about 20 ladies. Directress Mrs. Kakoschke. Purpose: care for the evangelical poor and needy.

Home for the aged: 2 inmates.

Periodicals 1935.

"Friedensbote" 10 copies; "Neue Evangelische Kirchenzeitung" 10 copies; "Głos Ewangelicki" 3 copies; "Zwiastun Ewangeliczny" 10 copies.

Converts.

In the year 1929: 2 persons.

Economic matters.

Landholding: 15 Morgen, 10 Morgen of arable land and 5 Morgen of meadow.

Number of Morgen in the congregation about 10,000.

Contributors 1907: 466; 1914: 465; 1930: 518.

Size of the budget 1929: 12,271 zlotys.

Amount of the church contribution: 1908: 6 kopecks per Morgen; in the town of Konin the highest contribution 15 rubles. Otherwise 1 ruble, 3 rubles, 5 rubles; from lesser craftsmen 50 kopecks. 1930: 60 groschen per Morgen and 5 zlotys family money. In Konin individual taxation.

Salaries 1931: pastor: 500 zlotys monthly (and 200 zlotys monthly from the filial Maślaki. Cantor: 200 zlotys monthly. Sexton: 70 zlotys monthly.
