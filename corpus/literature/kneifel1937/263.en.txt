Pastor Eugen Biedermann of Zagórów visited in the year 1864 Peisern 4 times a year, Lissewo 3 times, Ciemierów 3 times, Wierzchy 2 times and Wrąbczynkowska Kolonia 1 time. During the administration of the filial congregation by Pastor Haeßner of Stawiszyn, the filial members desired that Pastor Julius Binner of Miłosław (province of Posen) should represent the administrator in case of sudden illness. The Warsaw consistory, to which Pastor Haeßner turned in this matter, declared itself on July 20, 1873 in agreement with this representation.

The house of prayer at Wierzchy, which lies on the evangelical cemetery there, dates from the year 1803. In spite of the many renovations it is very dilapidated. Already in 1906 there was the intention here to build a new house of prayer. The building site was to be bought from the married couple Martin and Auguste Schwartz. Even the supreme permission for the building of the house of prayer was granted. Nevertheless no hand was laid to the work.

Before the World War of 1914/18 the religious life in the filial was very weakly developed. Much contributed to this the circumstance that the Evangelicals here dwelt for the most part near the former German-Russian frontier, whereby many fell into temptation to occupy themselves with frontier smuggling. Money easily earned in dishonest ways led many to drunkenness and other vices. After the shifting of the frontier a certain improvement in religious-moral respect has set in.

The cantor's dwelling at Peisern was for many years officially under seizure. The filial members thereupon, with the help of the consistory, instituted a lawsuit, which they also won. They thereupon renovated prayer hall and dwelling and called to be their cantor Gustav Adam, who is also teacher in the general elementary school with 7 classes at Peisern.

The Evangelicals of Ostrowska Kolonia, belonging to the cantorate of Wierzchy, are attached to the congregation of Grodziec, where they also pay their church contribution and enter all acts of the civil register.

The cantorate members of Wrąbczynek, a part of the cantorate congregation of Wrąbczynkowska Kolonia, have been included in the parish of Zagórów.

The filial of Lissewo-Pyzdry has no civil register office and therefore also no filial cantors. Concerning the place where the civil registers are to be kept, the filial members can still come to no agreement.
