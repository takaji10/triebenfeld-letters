# Łukasiewicz (1997), p. 40
# translation by Claude

In consequence in South Prussia there followed a considerable rise in the prices of landed estates[^48], which we can also easily observe in the "Black Register". This rise however had in part a speculative and conjunctural character, so that it is hard to regard the enormous sums then paid for landed estates as reflecting their real value, that is, such as followed from the capitalisation of the estate at the accepted rate of 5%. In other words, one could earn more by trading in estates than in the farm produce coming from those estates, in spite of the good conjuncture in the sale of the grain harvest. Treated speculatively were above all the estates bought up by people from the circle of Minister Karl Georg Hoym. The "Black Register" itself, moreover, speaks of it. Many a buyer did not even see the estates, and put them up for sale straight after the purchase.
