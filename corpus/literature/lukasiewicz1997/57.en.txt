Notes to Tabela 1:

[^t1-1]: Where the „Czarny Rejestr" gives only the purchase value, we have completed it with the values according to the tax valuation. After the joining of the estates given at the Poznań department, the real value of the estates in the Kalisz department would come to about 3.5 million thalers, whereas H. Held writes that the revenues from these estates in February 1799 were 247 000 thalers, which would suggest that the value of those estates was about 5 million thalers. It is possible that Held was right, and the discrepancy would point to the efficient cause in the shape of the grain conjuncture; see Das schwarze Register, s. 20.

[^t1-2]: Here included also the side donations in other departments: Hrabia Lüttichau: the Poznań department — 84 000, the Kalisz — 26 000; Prince Hohenlohe: — 77 250, — 12 000; merchant Treskow: — 73 325, — 12 500.

[^t1-3]: „the husbandmen who know agree" (these and the following annotations come from the „Czarny Rejestr").

[^t1-4]: Only the estates of the Poznań department. In the Kalisz only Kuchary — one of the 8 estates valued at 26 000 thalers was sold for 90 000 thalers.

[^t1-5]: Cf. C. Grünhagen, Die Südpreußische, s. 287.

[^t1-6]: „for 50 000 of income yearly". The „Czarny Rejestr" gives the value of the estate 800 000, and beside it the income 50 000 thalers.

[^t1-7]: According to C. Grünhagen 1079 thalers of contribution (that is 89 772 thalers of value), and then he gives that the official value of the estate 47 200 thalers, op. cit., s. 287.

[^t1-8]: According to C. Grünhagen the income from the lustration 600 thalers, op. cit., s. 252.

[^t1-9]: For Lubrzec alone Count Kwilecki of Dobrojewo gave 12 000 thalers. The value of all the estates completed by Cölln.

[^t1-10]: According to C. Grünhagen 32 000, op. cit., s. 288.

[^t1-11]: „but they are worth more".

[^t1-12]: In the printed version 2650.

[^t1-13]: Completed by F. Cölln.

[^t1-14]: According to C. Grünhagen 56 180, in accord with the tax valuation, which assessed the net revenue at 2809 thalers, op. cit., s. 283.

[^t1-15]: W. Smoleński's edition gives wrongly 100 000.

[^t1-16]: „He sold them to the landrat Potworowski for 60000 but they are worth 100 000 (Potworowski in the district of Kościan)".

[^t1-17]: According to C. Grünhagen 934 thalers of net revenue, op. cit., s. 251; meanwhile in the tax return there stands a tax valuation similar to Grünhagen's — 467 thalers, but the table is described expressly as a compilation of the estates paying 24% contribution, while Eisenberg moreover paid 513 thalers of Kompetenzgeld, which however gives together 980 thalers.

[^t1-18]: In the table the nominal value of the double donataries is given together. Their Poznań estates were separated only in the summary.

[^t1-19]: Those named in the case of Treskow, Lüttichau and Hohenlohe are the joined value of their estates in all the departments. As Held gave no such assessment for the Poznań department itself, we completed these assessments from the tax returns and estimation, like these items of the „Czarny Rejestr" for the same department, where only the purchase value of the estate was given. This gives therefore a value of the estates somewhat deflated in relation to Held's intention, which might be assessed at 3 million thalers. The value of all the items in the table of the Poznań department is 4 million.

[^t1-20]: For that only two of the three estates were sold; according to the tax valuation 28 620 thalers, C. Grünhagen, op. cit., s. 285-286.

[^t1-21]: According to C. Grünhagen the revenues from Kłobuck were 8259 thalers, from Kroszyc 1784 thalers, which gives 10 043 thalers and answers the sale value of the estate, s. 250.

[^t1-22]: C. Grünhagen: „er wolle dafür 1000 Friedrichsdor zahlen", that is 5000 thalers, op. cit., s. 289.

[^t1-23]: C. Grünhagen gives the tax 777 thalers (that is 64 646 thalers), which Triebenfeld voluntarily raised for himself to 1000 thalers (83 200 thalers), op. cit., s. 295.

[^t1-24]: Of the 20 estates the first two in 1801 were valued at 82 300 thalers, Canon — 13 398 thalers, from which would follow an enormous value of the estate. H. Held did not explain that this is the contribution + Kompetenzgeld; the contribution alone comes to 6699 thalers, from which follows the value of the estate 557 356 thalers, C. Grünhagen, op. cit., s. 289.

[^t1-25]: The confirmation of the lease from the Polish times.

[^t1-26]: Because of the lack of assessments of the real value of the estates of Treskow and Hohenlohe, the nominal value of their estates in the Kalisz department has been transferred here, which probably slightly deflates the whole sum for this department.

[^t1-27]: In accord with the assessment from the lustration, where the income was assessed at 545 thalers, C. Grünhagen, op. cit., s. 252.

[^t1-28]: According to C. Grünhagen the incomes according to the Polish lustration, 3485 thalers, which gives the value 69 7001, op. cit., s. 251.
