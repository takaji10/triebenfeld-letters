Lastly, it is quite possible that false birthplaces were often given without ill will, simply from embarrassment. This happens especially often, since the clerk only in the rarest cases asks to be shown a paper.

The places of origin given here are not to be taken as complete. The intention is to close all the searches at a common year, about 1870. For guidance we give the years of the marriage and burial books searched so far, since the baptism books only in the rarest case give places of origin: Władysławow 1778 - 1833, Grodziec 1798 - 1842, Konin 1826 - 1845, Turek 1837 - 1851, Sompolno 1840 - 1865, Koło 1843 - 1862, Zagórow 1843 - 1867. Immigrants from the Mark also streamed in great numbers into the forest region south of the Warthe. There are many records of this from the parishes of Stawiszyn, Turek and Prażuchy. A summary article on the immigration from the Mark is soon to appear.
