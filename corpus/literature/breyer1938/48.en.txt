# breyer1938  48  en
# Breyer (1938), p. 48
# translation by Claude

The statistical figures on the German inhabitants of the Warthe marshes are taken from the official publications of the Polish statistical office for the census year 1921[^22]. Regrettably, no announcements at all have so far been made on the Polish census of 1931 that would concern the results for single villages or communes. By private inquiries, the German population has not been subject to any strong swings in the last decade and a half. In some German communes, on the contrary, a gratifying growth of population is to be recorded, so that we believe we are not mistaken if we put the German share of the population of the Warthe marshes of central Poland at some 10,000 souls.

In the figures of 1921 we have everywhere set Evangelical equal to German. We know from our own observation that in the area of the Warthe marshes there can be no question at all of a visible de-Germanisation.

[^22]: Skorowidz miejscowości Rzeczypospolitej Polskiej (Gazetteer of the places of the Polish Republic), vol. II, Łódź voivodeship, Warsaw 1925.
