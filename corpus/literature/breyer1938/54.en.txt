# translation by Claude

> *§ 16*

> *In the waters lying within the bounds of the takers of the hereditary lease, fishing and the taking of crayfish are also allowed them; but they must not presume to fish or take crayfish outside their bounds; rather the lordship reserves this to itself, indeed even within the boundary towards Spicimierz and the old Rzeczysko; and should it suit the manor to build a mill or lay out a fishery, the manor reserves to itself the place at the heath mill as far as the boundary of the church mill by the Warta river at the bare hill; and should the takers of the hereditary lease presume to fish or take crayfish outside their bounds, a fine of fifteen Thaler shall be paid to the lordship for each case of contravention.*

> *§ 17*

> *The innkeeper is indeed allowed to keep his own oats and roughage in the inn. On the other hand, no drink other than the lord's Borzewice drink, beer as well as brandy, shall be served in it or in the colony at all, on pain of confiscation of the drink and ten Thaler in money for each case of contravention; whereas if, against expectation, there should be no drink of the lord's, an order shall be given by the manor for procuring other drink.*

> *§ 18*

> *The takers of the hereditary lease are bound to keep the bounds of the ten Hufen given in hereditary lease, and to secure them they shall be allowed to dam them, mark their bounds, and also furnish them with water bridges; but they must take care not to do harm to the lord's meadows, or even to graze them off.*

> *§ 19*

> *As the ten Hufen assigned to the hereditary takers are deeded to them as true property, so they are also entitled to sell them and to dispose of them in any other lawful way. In case of a sale they are bound to notify the lordship of it and to obtain the due*
