# Chodyła (2015), p. 73
# translation by Claude

In 1789 the Ciążeńskie or Zarzeczne Olędry were created in the district of Pyzdry, in the parish of Ciążeń, in the Ciążeń key of the mensal estates of the bishopric of Poznań. According to the revision of this key of 1793, 4 Olęders lived there: 3 with Polish names and 1 with a German name, Catholics; they were founded by a charter of 25 June 1789, not yet approved by the chapter, of Antoni Onufry Okęcki, ordinary bishop of Poznań[^123] (Ill. 23-24).

Before 1789 the Olęder settlement of Poroże Małe arose in the district of Kalisz, in the parish and on the estate of Przespolew Kościelny. It was a small settlement, with only 2 hearths in the years 1789-1790[^124], probably tied administratively to the Przespolewskie Ol., later, it seems, enlarged, and inhabited by Lutheran Olęders, as is shown, indirectly, by the survival of a Protestant cemetery in it.

23\. Part of the revision of the Ciążeń key of the mensal estates of the bishops of Poznań of 1793, with information on Bishop Antoni O. Okęcki's foundation charter for the Ciążeńskie or Zarzeczne Ol. of 1789 and with a list of their settlers by name. AAP. CP 152 fols. 23-24v.

24\. Antoni Onufry Okęcki (1729-1793), bishop of Poznań (1780-1793), founder of the Ciążeńskie Ol. in 1789.
