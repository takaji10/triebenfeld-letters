# Chodyła (2015), p. 42
# translation by Claude

*to the whole community of the Białobłockie Hollędry now already settled, granting to each from his entering and paying the gruntgield...* The settlers then took up the Królików forests, which they were to clear within 7 free years. In this charter the founder also laid on the Olęders, who were of mixed confession, Catholics and dissenters (Protestants), the obligation to pay fees for baptisms, weddings and funerals to the parish priest of the parish church at Królików[^65] (Ill. 9).
