# Chodyła (2015), p. 109
# translation by Claude

*...who make the contract in their own name and that of others...* (joined later by the honest Antoni Morawski and Bartłomiej Masłowski, who had earlier lived on the Turek estates), who were, however, Protestant Lutherans[^216].

In the Kuszyńskie or Jaszczurowo Olędry, in turn, also in the district of Kalisz, 7 colonists settled, 4 with German and 3 with Polish names, Protestant Lutherans. They took up "the forest or deserta" (empty land) Jaszczurowo, belonging to Kosmów. The settlement agreement was first made with the founder in 1776 by two representatives of the colonists, Michał Gesse and Krzysztof Malus (Mikosz?), and then in the years 1776-1781 3 more settlers were added to it: Marcin Makosz, Paweł Dysza and Piotr Jering[^217].

In the years 1776 and 1781 Wrąbczynek and the Wrąbczynkowskie Ol. were founded. In Wrąbczynek at least 4 settlers settled (3 with German names and 1 with a Polish one), Protestant Lutherans. The settlers of the Wrąbczynkowskie Ol., Jakub Krysa/Kriese (sołtys), Wojciech Kurtz and Krzysztof Szultz (assessors), were settled on overgrown, probably wooded, land, since the second *konotacja* contained the information: *Since the Rąbczynek Holendry, their free years for the rumunek being out, have today received the law under which they are to remain in their dues, so that these too, settled together with them, are not raised to higher dues than the Holędrzy, they are ascribed an equalisation with those, so that they* [belong] *to the dues set out here*.

In the *asekuracja* of 20 XI 1781 of S. Dobrski, plenipotentiary of Turno, starost of Pietrzyków, besides confirming that the labour services had been done, the Olęders are promised that they will obtain the royal law (that is the king's confirmation of the foundation charter issued by the starost-tenant) once they have paid a contribution for the purpose, and it is stated *that the Germans, who have been measured out on the lord's demesne farm, will be bound to pay the remaining half of the grungielt*[^218]. In the Wrąbczyńskie Ol. 3 settlers settled, with German and Polish names, Protestant Lutherans. *Settled on the Wrąpczyn wood, three new inhabitants give fl.* [silver złoty] *32*; according to the charter of 1776, *and also the Rąbczyn Holendry, who hold half of the tenth* [9½] *huba cleared from the deserta in the forest, with Bogusław Szeyder, sołtys, Jakub Wróbel and Michał Szpryngier, assessors*[^219].

In the charter for the Gadowskie St. and Nw. Ol., set up in 1777 and then before and in 1784 in the district of Konin, 3 settlers with German names are named: Walenty Ginter (probably the same as the Olęder who had settled earlier, in the years 1767-1770, in Wielołęka), Bogusław Gryger and Jonat Zamer, the sołtys and assessors; but others with German and Polish names, Lutherans and Catholics, also lived there[^220].
