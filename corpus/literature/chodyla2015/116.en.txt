# Chodyła (2015), p. 116
# translation by Claude

Under a law of 1775, emphyteutic charters were to be issued on stamped paper, with a fiscal fee of 1 grosz. If this condition was not met, the official validity of the charter could be legally questioned, even by its issuer himself. This was what happened in the Pyzdrskie Ol., and it became the cause of a long lawsuit between their inhabitants and the magistrate of the town of Pyzdry, which had perhaps deliberately issued the settlers a charter on ordinary paper[^252].

Be that as it may, the ownership of the land assigned to the Olęders was not full but usufructuary and divided, since its legal owner was still the lord, who, however, had no right to remove an Olęder from it as long as he used it in accordance with the provisions of the charter. The Olęder was bound to take such care of the land as if it were his property, and could dispose of it freely as long as he paid the lord the dues owed and paid the entry money mentioned. In Greater Poland this was paid only once, on obtaining the right of perpetual possession of the land, in a lump sum, exceptionally in instalments. It was a payment for the land allotted[^253]. For this reason the peasants – Olęders who had paid the entry money also obtained freedom of alienation: of transferring the ownership of the land by gift to their natural heirs, and by transactions of sale and purchase to other persons, on condition of obtaining the manor's consent and paying the lord a fee (*wadium*) of one grosz in ten of the sale price of the farm. This was stated expressly in the charters for: the Pyzdrskie Ol. (freedom to sell farms with the magistrate's consent, on payment of a *vadium* of one grosz in ten), Wrąbczynek and the Wrąbczynkowskie Ol. (the Olęders may sell, give or exchange the lands they have paid entry money for, cleared and built on, after informing the manor), the Lubieńskie Ol., the Orlińskie D. and M. Ol., the Cekowskie St. Ol. (the buildings and live stock belong to the farmers, who may sell them *...should any of them not like it in this country*), the Cekowskie Nw. Ol. [^252]: In the complaint of the Olęders Mateusz Konieczny, sołtys, and Józef Baranowski and Jakub Bończyk, assessors and plenipotentiaries of the commune, against the magistrate of the town of Pyzdry, there is the information that the magistrate, taking advantage of the fact that they, "..not being enlightened, as unlearned little peasants", had received from the town council an improperly written foundation charter, since it was not on stamped paper, and for that reason the chancery of the castle court of Pyzdry would not accept it for entry (*oblata*), gave them, in place of the old charter they had handed in, one on stamped paper, but with the signatures of its own members and without the signatures of the commonalty [the guild elders], and demanded a second payment for it. Now, they said, it wanted to abolish this law and take it away from them by deceit, spreading it about that this was because of the objection of the commonalty, which "knew everything", although they had already paid the town the entry money for the holdings of land they had received, acquiring a perpetual rent tenure of them. To this end it informed the Olęders that it had once obtained a decree of the starost's court abolishing this foundation charter, which had, after all, earlier been confirmed by the king. Besides, attempts were made in various other ways to drive the Olęders out of their farms, among others by cutting the deeds of sale of farms out of the sołtys's record and taking from them the original law kept in that record, and by a raid of citizens of Pyzdry with 15 horses, acts of violence committed under the pretext of taking recruits, and other abuses. Accordingly, the Olęders declared they would pursue the matter before the Referendary Court. That proceeding, which ended in a just judgment, is the subject of other records (a royal letter of safe-conduct for the Olęders, complaints of the Olęders, powers of attorney for the court from the Olęder commune, summonses, decrees, correspondence and others, of 1790), with their call numbers, titles and the pages of the edition of the Ks. RKor., in: Z. Chodyła, *Osadnictwo olęderskie w Wielkopolsce (1597-1793)*, Annex I/5.

[^253]: W. Rusiński, *Osady tzw „ olędrów”*, p. 44.
