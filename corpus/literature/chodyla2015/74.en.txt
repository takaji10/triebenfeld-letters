# Chodyła (2015), p. 74
# translation by Claude

In 1790 the settlement of Świdle Nowa Kolonia, or the Świdle Olędry, was founded in the district of Kalisz, in the parish and on the noble estate of Przespolew(o). Its founder was Ignacy Zaremba, son of the starost of Prośniatów, civil and military commissary for order of Kalisz, holder of this estate. According to the table of the grain levy from the self-tilling hearths of the district of Kalisz of 1791, the Świdle Ol. were "*newly set up in the year 1790*", and they then had 3 "self-tilling" hearths[^125].

Summing up and supplementing the detailed information given above on the distribution, time, causes, conditions and organisation of the founding of the 58 Olęder settlements in the Pyzdry Forest in the years 1746-1793, the following more general conclusions can be drawn, which, to make them easier to read, we set out in several points. 1) Of the 58 Olęder settlements founded in the years 1746-1793 in the Pyzdry Forest in 3 districts of the voivodeship of Kalisz, in 21 parishes (4 of which lay on the border of two districts), 5 were founded in the district of Pyzdry, in 4 parishes: Ciążeń (1), Pyzdry (1), Szymanowice (2) and Zagórów (2); 28 in the district of Konin, in 8 parishes: Dzierzbin, whose centre lay in the district of Kalisz (6), Grochowy (1), Grodziec (5), Królików (2), partly lying in both of the last two parishes (4), Lisiec Wielki (1), Trąbczyn (3), Sławsk (1), Zagórów (5); and 25 in the district of Kalisz, in 10 parishes: Brudzew (1), Chocz (2), Dzierzbin (2), Kościelec (5), Kosmów (5), Lipe (2), Przespolew Kościelny (3), Rzegocin (2), Szymanowice (2), Zbiersk (1). Of these 58 Olęder settlements, 7 were founded on royal estates (1 in the starosty of Pyzdry and 2 in the starosty of Pietrzyków in the district of Pyzdry, 1 in the starosty and district of Konin, 2 in the lease of Ceków in the district of Kalisz, 1 on the estates of the royal town of Pyzdry), 3 on church estates (1 on secular church estates, in the Ciążeń key of the latifundium of the bishopric of Poznań, in the district of Pyzdry, and 2 on the monastic estates of the convent of the Order of St Clare, the Franciscan nuns, in Kalisz, in the district of Konin), and the majority, 48 settlements, on noble estates (25 of them in the district of Konin and 23 in the district of Kalisz), mostly on such estates as Gadów, Grodziec-Królików and Trąbczyn, Łukom and Łomowo in the district of Konin, and Dzierzbin, Chocz, Kosmów and Lipe in the district of Kalisz. Of these, two villages (the Kazimierskie Stare and Nowe Olędry) in the parish and on the estate of Chocz, which formed the endowment of a noble foundation of the chapter of the collegiate church of Chocz (created in 1620 on the condition that it be presided over by a clergyman of the Lipski family, with the dignity of a mitred prelate), are sometimes classed in the sources as villages that were church property[^126]2) The date of origin of more than half (31) of the 58 Olęder settlements that arose in the Pyzdry Forest in the eighteenth century is fixed exactly by their foundation charters; for the rest it can be established exactly or approximately from the first mentions in other kinds of sources, such as inspections, inventories, court decrees and tax tables. The chronology of their founding, certain and hypothetical, falls within the periodisation we have adopted for the history of Olęder settlement
