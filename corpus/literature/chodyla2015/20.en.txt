This was done, however, at second hand, from the typescript of the master's thesis of Mirosława Jasińska on the Olęder settlements on the royal estates of the district of Pyzdry in the second half of the eighteenth century and from the article of S. Błaszczyk presenting the folk culture of the district of Kalisz, and, in the light of field research, from the studies of the architects I. F. Tłoczek, Piotr Szwiec and Radosław Barek[^32].

Written sources (in originals and copies) concerning above all the founding of the Olęder settlements and their internal administrative and judicial organisation, together with parts of cartographic sources showing where the Olęders were and portraits of their founders, are reproduced in the text of this study, with an explanation of each there and in the list of illustrations.

At the end of this study, besides the conclusions that follow from the account of these questions, we show very briefly, in outline, chiefly from the literature (and above all from the studies of A. Breyer mentioned above, from the cartographic sources and from the communes' development plans), some of the most important facts of the later history of rural settlement (Olęder settlement included) in the Pyzdry Forest, in the period from 1794 to recent years. The range of subjects and of time, the large holdings and different character of the sources and their scattering over many different archives mean that the history of this settlement in the years 1794-1864, and the later fortunes of the Olęder settlements, require a separate study.

In its construction the study consists of an introduction; six separate chapters, which present the questions named above in an essentially scholarly manner and are furnished with notes, bibliographical and sometimes on points of substance, numbered throughout; a conclusion; a *Glossary of Old Polish terms* that appear often in the text and are not understood today and need explaining; and a list of illustrations.
