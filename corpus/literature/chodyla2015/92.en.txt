we leave aside 2 breweries, 6 inns and 2 craftsmen's hearths, hearths with 1 farmer decidedly predominated, found in all 15 Olęder villages, 636 in all. Hearths with 2 farmers, 37 in all, were found in only 5 settlements, garden hearths with land and garden, 16 in number, in 3 settlements, and those without land and garden, 2 in number, in 1 settlement. Unfortunately there are no data on the structure of the other 43 Olęder villages. It may be supposed, however, among other things from the social structure of the inhabitants, that not only in these 16 settlements but also in most of the Olęder settlements that concern us, hearths-farms with one farmer predominated, that is of one or half a włóka (full and half peasant farms, *kmiece*, *rolnicze* and *półkmiece*, *półrolnicze*), and only a very small part of them were smaller ones, with two farmers, and smallholders' ones with land and garden, and rather rarely lodgers' ones.

According to the tables of the grain levy (in rye and oats) for the army of 1791 for the districts of Pyzdry, Konin and Kalisz, there were 662 "self-tilling", that is farming, hearths in the 58 Olęder villages of the Pyzdry Forest. A comparison of the data on the farming hearths of 1791 with the information on the total numbers of hearths of 1789 shows that in all there were 1.662% more farming hearths two years later. Since, however, the numbers of hearths for 34 settlements are estimates, the totals of hearths of both categories need correcting. We do this by raising the total of 662 farming hearths by 6.34%, that is by 42 hearths, since that was the difference between farming hearths and hearths in general shown by the complete statistical data surviving for a representative sample of rural settlements of the districts of Kalisz, Pyzdry and Kościan. As a result of this addition we obtain 704 hearths. By adding this sum of 42 hearths to the 649 hearths in general (since most of the estimated numbers in the first column of the table repeat the number of farming hearths as a minimum), we obtain the number of 691 hearths in general, which should be taken as more realistic for the year 1789. By 1791 their number, in keeping with a slightly higher annual rate of natural increase[^187], could have grown by at least 1%, that is by 6 persons, that is to about 699 persons in all. The literature shows, however, that more than 98% of the inhabitants of the Olęder settlements were occupied in farming. This will be discussed further below. So probably almost the same, or a similar, percentage of the hearths in the 58 Olęder settlements founded in the Pyzdry Forest were farming hearths (farms) in 1789. For the farms of innkeepers and teachers, which were the main source of their holders' living, were not counted as farming hearths.

The settlement charters of the Olęder settlements in the Pyzdry Forest contain, in principle, no information on their layout and the character of their buildings. Only in the foundation charters cited above, for the Orlińskie D. and M. Ol. of 1784 and the Białobłockie Ol. of 1787, is it said that their inhabitants are "*...to build properly and on a line*"[^188], that is to create a village in the form of a street village.
