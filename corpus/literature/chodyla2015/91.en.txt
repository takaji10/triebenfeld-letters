The analysis of the whole of this table shows that in the 58 Olęder settlements founded in the Pyzdry Forest there were in the years 1789-1790, approximately, 651 hearths in all. So on average there would have been 11.224 hearths to a settlement. For the greater part of these settlements the number of farms showed a certain constancy compared with the initial state of the period of their founding, shown above; for the smaller part, however, it varied, most often upwards, because further settlers were brought in, or because of the increasingly varied social and economic structure of the villages, through the division of farms by inheritance.

There was a wide range in the number of farms among the Olęder villages. It can be grasped directly for the 38 settlements given separately. Among them there were: very small settlements, of 2-5 hearths – 9; small ones, of 6-10 hearths – 11; middling ones, of 11-15 hearths – 5; larger than middling, of 16-20 hearths – 8; large, of 21-25 hearths, only 2; and very large, of 26 hearths, only 1. It is impossible to give the proportions by number of hearths for the settlements for which these were given in the tables, directly or indirectly, but jointly. The number of their hearths ranged from 6 to 33, so that on average one of them would have had from 3 to 16.5 hearths.
