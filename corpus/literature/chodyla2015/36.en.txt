and the Olęders, personally free peasants, 9 in number (8 of them with German names and 1 with a Polish name), Lutherans, settled in Wielołęka *by the right of free purchase* (as is shown by the allotment of half a huba to the *szkolny* [teacher] and by the mention of an agreement with the parish priest of Grodziec about the fees for weddings and funerals). The settlement arose *on the lands of the empty village of Wielołęka... now overgrown with woods and forests*. The foundation agreement was made with the founder on behalf of the Olęders by Jan Czarneski and Jan Kimel[^55] (Ill. 7). The inventory of the properties of Grodziec, Królików and others of 1779 shows that 15 farmers lived in the Wielołęckie Ol.
