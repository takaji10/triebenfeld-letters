Labour services were of secondary importance in the Old Polish Olęder settlements of the Pyzdry Forest. They are indeed named in the foundation charters of many of them, on a yearly basis per huba (or per half-huba), in the form of manual work, varying in kind and in the number of days, chiefly seasonal (the so-called *tłoki*), and of journeys (carting, *podwody*), but often with the possibility of commuting them into money at a set rate.

Labour services are known for: the Ciążeńskie Ol. (6 manual *tłoki* at harvest), Wrąbczynek and the Wrąbczynkowskie Ol. (*to plough 3 morgen, to cart dung 2 days and at harvest to work 3 manual days*)[^305]. Then in: the Białobłockie Ol. (*for digging ditches 4 days or 2 zł during the free years, for the harvest 4 days during the free years or 2 zł, with the scythe 1 day or 1 zł, for mud or dung 1 day or 2 zł 15 gr, for raking 2 days or 1 zł, 1 day for cutting a square sążeń of wood, or ploughing 3 morgen in place of the journey*), in the Borowieckie St. and Nw. Ol. and the Konarskie Ol. (*for digging ditches 6 days or 3 zł, 8 days at harvest, for the scythe 6 days or 6 zł, for raking 2 days or 1 zł, to cut 1 square sążeń for the manor*), the Gadowskie St. and Nw. Ol. (*at harvest 4 days, "szarwark* [road labour] *for the pasture, for which a barrel of beer from the manor for the 2 days"*), the Lubieńskie Ol. (*2 tłoki at the manor's order*), Wielołęka (24 days of manual service; for each day not worked, 1 zł with the rent)[^306]. Further in: the Cekowskie St. Ol. (3 days of labour service) and the Cekowskie Nw. Ol. (formerly 3 days, later commuted to *2 capons or 3 larger hens*), the Dębniałki and Piskory Ol. (*8 days of tłoka at harvest from ½ huba and ploughing the fields once in winter and once in summer, carting 2 loads of wood at Easter and Christmas*)[^307].

A significant burden on the inhabitants of the Olęder settlements in the Pyzdry Forest owed to the manor were the journeys (carting), found, however, only in some villages. Thus in: the Pyzdrskie Ol. (journeys to Leszno and Toruń), Wrąbczynek and the Wrąbczynkowskie Ol. (the journey to Poznań or Wschowa, *and when it is not needed, 10 zł*)[^308].
