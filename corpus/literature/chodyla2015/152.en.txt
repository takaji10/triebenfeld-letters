# List of illustrations

1\. The Olęder settlements in the Pyzdry Forest founded in the years 1746-1792, on a part of the *Mapa szczegulna*[!] *Województwa Kaliskiego* of Karol de Perthées [n.d. [1791?] and about 1801, at a scale of 1:225,000.

2\. The Olęder settlements in the Pyzdry Forest founded in the years 1746-1792, on a part of the map *Special Karte von Südpreussen* (...) of David Gilly of 1802 and 1803, at a scale of 1:150,000.

3\. The Olęder settlements in the Pyzdry Forest founded in the years 1746-1792, on a part of the *Topograficzna Karta Królestwa Polskiego* (the map of the Quartermaster General's staff of the Polish Army), [Warsaw], prepared before 1830, published in 1843 dated 1839, column I, section II.

4\. (1-2) Parts of the decree of the boundary commission court of 1776 telling of the founding of the Trąbczyńskie St. Ol. and the Luśnie Ol. by Paweł and Katarzyna née Rozdrażewska Prusimski in 1746. APP, Konin Gr. 145, fols. 681 and 692.

5\. Michał Drogosław Skórzewski, chamberlain of Poznań, starost-tenant of Pyzdry, founder of the Lisewskie Ol. after 1765. J. Łojko, *Civitas Pyzdry. Dzieje miasta do roku 1793*, Pyzdry 2007.

6\. (1-3) Parts of the emphyteutic-perpetual foundation charter for the Zbierskie Ol. of 11 XI 1761, issued by Stanisław Dzik Kożuchowski, land judge of Wieluń, owner of the Zbiersk estate. APP Kal. Gr. 448, fols. 254-255v (obl. 25 VII 1783).

7\. (1-2) Parts of the foundation charter for Wielołęka as an Olęder settlement, issued on 6 XI 1770 by Jan Wężyk, castellan of Konary, of Sieradz, in Polish, an authenticated extract from the records of the Civil Tribunal of the Government of Kalisz of 19 IX/1 X 1841, a typescript copy. APP, SAB. 789.

8\. Part of the foundation charter in German of Józef Wężyk, castellan of Konary, of Sieradz, for Konary/the Konarskie Ol. of 6 IX 1770. APP, SAB 696.

9\. Part of the joint second emphyteutic-perpetual foundation charter for the Konarskie Ol. and the Borowieckie St. and Nw. Ol. in the district of Konin, founded in the years 1770-1772, issued on 25 VIII 1785 by Count Franciszek Stadnicki, starost of Ostrzeszów, owner of the estates of Grójec (Grodziec), Królików and Biskupice. APP. Kalisz Gr. 476 (1789), fols. 421-422v.

10\. (1-2) Parts of the emphyteutic-perpetual foundation charter for the community of the Białobłockie Olędry "now already settled", of 17 X 1787 and 1792, of Count Franciszek Stadnicki, SAB 626 (no. 8, extract of the *oblata* of 29 VI 1788 from the castle-court book of Kalisz). APP, SAB 626.

11\. Part of the joint second emphyteutic-perpetual foundation charter for the Grądy St. and Nw., founded before and in 1772 and in 1791, in the district of Konin, issued on 6 II 1791 by Count Franciszek Stadnicki, starost of Ostrzeszów, owner of the estates of Grójec (Grodziec), Królików and Biskupice. APP, Kalisz. Gr. 482, fols. 77-77v and 89-89v, obl. 4 III 1791.

12\. (1-2) Parts of the emphyteutic-perpetual foundation charter for the Cekowskie Nw. Ol., issued on 28 X 1780 by Krzysztof Celiński, APP, Kal. Gr. 450, fols. 27-27v and 29-29v; obl. 27 III 1783 by the honest Bogusław Szwander, Olęder of the Ceków estate.

13\. (1-3) Parts of the emphyteutic-perpetual foundation charter for the Lubieńskie Ol., issued on 10 XI 1777 by Anna Chlebowska, superior of the convent of the Order of St Clare (the Franciscan nuns) in Kalisz. APP, Kalisz. Gr. 429 (1778), fols. 153v-154v.

14\. (1-4) Emphyteutic-perpetual foundation charter for the Pyzdrskie Ol., issued on 24 IV 1778 by the magistrate of the town of Pyzdry. APP, Pyzdry Gr. 82 (1780), fols. 36-39, the same in: Konin Gr. 118 (1780), fols. 158-159v (obl. 16 X 1780 by the honest Jakub Bończyk, sołtys, and Józef Baranowski, assessor.

15\. The Rev. Kazimierz Lipski, mitred provost of Chocz, founder of the Kazimierskie Stare Ol. (1782) and the Kazimierskie Nowe Ol. (1790) in the district of Kalisz. Chocz. Portrait of about 1790 in the former provost's palace. Katalog Zabytków Sztuki w Polsce. Vol. V, no. 19, p. 5, fig. 4.

16\. Part of the emphyteutic-perpetual foundation charter for the Dzierzbińskie-Zamęty and Zdanowiec Ol., issued on 25 VI 1783 by Kazimierz Poraj Biernacki, *wojski* of the district of Ostrzeszów, owner of the estates of Siąszyce and Dzierzbin and of the property of Sul[i]szew. APP, Kalisz. Gr. 448, fols. 216v-218.
