In 22 of the 25 Olęder villages of the district of Kalisz, with a total population of 1,368 persons, 309 were men, then 304 sons under 15, then 78 sons aged 15 and over, then 282 women, and 395 daughters. So in the 25 Olęder settlements of the districts of Pyzdry and Kalisz, of a total of 1,817 persons of their population, 397 were men, 412 sons under 15, 116 sons aged 15 and over, 371 women and 521 daughters. Among these five categories of the demographic composition of the population the category of sons aged 15 and over was undoubtedly understated, probably because of the threat of conscription into the army. Perhaps some of them were counted among the sons under 15, whose number seems somewhat too high, especially given the high mortality of children under 5 in former times. In a somewhat simplified form (without dividing the boys into age groups) the demographic composition of the population of 20 of the 28 Olęder settlements in the district of Konin can be grasped; according to the lists of 1790-1791 it is given in the records of the visitation of the parishes of the deanery of Konin already mentioned above. In these lists the population of the settlements was divided into four categories: men, women, boys and girls. Adding up the data in this source for the 20 Olęder settlements that concern us, it can be said that of the total of 1,337 inhabitants (in this case probably covering only the Olęder farmers with the members of their families, leaving out the farm servants and servants and other categories of landless people) men numbered 427 persons, women 428, boys 227 and girls 255. Summing up the information above on the numerical relation of the four demographic categories of the population of 45 Olęder settlements in the three districts of Pyzdry, Konin and Kalisz, leaving aside the differences of age among the boys, it can be said that of their 3,124 inhabitants for whom such a division is known, 824 persons were men, 598 women, 956 boys and 756 daughters (girls and unmarried women of various ages).

While the size of the population of each, and of all, of the 58 Olęder villages in the Pyzdry Forest, and the demographic structure of most of them, 45, can be established exactly or by estimate, it is harder to give exact statistics of their social, property and occupational structure. It can be grasped in the detailed census tables for only 25 of them (3 in the district of Pyzdry and 22 in the district of Kalisz). There were in all in them 1,518 farmers, that is Olęders proper with the members of their families, farmers and rent-paying cottagers, who can in principle be regarded as one and the same social and occupational category, 50 lodgers, 7 craftsmen, 13 innkeepers and tapsters, 102 farm servants and servants, 68 *luźni* (landless people) and 59 "other" persons. The village community of the settlements, as far as the Olęder farmers were concerned, was internally differentiated in property already at the founding, according to the size of the holdings received, and still more so a dozen or several dozen years after it, when farms were sometimes divided into smaller ones through inheritance. The differences of property were also tied to the financial means of the settlers themselves, in the form of the size of the entry money they had to, and could, pay for the holdings of land of a given size that they received for perpetual use.
