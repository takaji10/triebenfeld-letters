The death of his beloved wife affected him so painfully that from then on his strength failed more and more. Struck by paralysis, having prepared for death with the great piety that never left him in his life, he fell asleep in peace, surrounded by his children, on 31 October 1869 at Pawłowo, and was buried at Trąbczyn. Of his three children, his daughter **Stanisława**, born 1848, died 1860, went before him to the grave. Two remained:

† Countess **Wanda**, born 5 December 1849, married to Gustaw **Potworowski** of the Dębno arms, son of Gustaw, founder and long-time president of the Polish parliamentary group in Berlin, and of Klementyna née Chłapowska, and grandson of Andrzej and of Ludwika née Żychlińska of the Szeliga arms, heir of the estates of Gola and Stary Gostyń, member for Krobia in the Berlin parliament; she died in the flower of her age and beauty on 9 April 1875 in Berlin, and was laid in the burial chapel of the Potworowskis at Stary Gostyń. Without issue.

Count **Władysław** [13th generation], born 31 January 1852, heir of the Pawłowo estates by his father, and of the Trąbczyn estates by his uncle Włodzimierz, *lieutenant* of the reserve of the guard cuirassier regiment in Berlin; having gone through the Sophien-Realschule in Berlin and the agricultural academy at Freiberg, he took possession of the Pawłowo estates, which he sold a few years ago, and moved to the Kingdom of Poland. Since 1877 a member of the Berlin Geographical Society in Berlin ("Gesselschaft [for Gesellschaft] für Erdkunde"), further of the Society of the Friends of Learning in Poznań, and of the heraldic societies "Herold" in Berlin and "Adler" in Vienna; he married, on 7 August 1883, in the court church at Dresden, Helena **Potocka** of the Piława arms, daughter of Bolesław, lord of Będlewo, Dakowy, Wojnowice etc., and of the late Helena née Countess Kwilecka. The witnesses of the marriage were: Prince Antoni Sułkowski, holder of the entail of Rydzyna; Count Franciszek Kwilecki; Gustaw Potworowski; and Count Maksymilian Mielżyński. — Of this marriage:

Countess **Elżbieta** Marya Helena Felicya Józefa, born 5 October 1884 at Wrocław.

2\. Count **Włodzimierz** [12th generation], *officer of 1831*, heir of the Trąbczyn estate, which he bought from his brother Mieczysław after his return from exile; born 29 July 1811, died 18 December 1879 at Trąbczyn and buried there. Unmarried. The Poznań papers gave him the following notice:

"The late Włodzimierz, son of a powerful family known throughout the country, did not, it is true, take a prominent part in public life; but as a citizen of rare uprightness of character, of steady and conscientious work, and of great love of his native soil and its tradition, he was everywhere held up as a model to the younger generations. Brought up with great care in his parents' house and in the then famous school of the Piarists at Żoliborz, at seventeen he went to Kraków to complete his studies at the Jagiellonian University. The events of 1830 broke off the course of his further studies and condemned Count Włodzimierz, with his elder brother Count Aleksander, to a stay of many years abroad.
