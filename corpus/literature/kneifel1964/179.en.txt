In the first decade of the 20th century the remodeling of the church and tower was carried out in the parish of Konin. The total costs amounted to 29 631 rubles 50 kopecks. In Maslaki, the filial of Konin, Pastor Henkel of Konin likewise wished to build a massive house of God. The disagreements in the filial congregation over the building site — some wished Maslaki, others again Kleczew — as well as the outbreak of the First World War thwarted the realization of the project.
