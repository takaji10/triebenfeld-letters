From 1930 to 1939 Pastor Viktor Maczewski administered the congregation of Sobiesenki. In the years 1928—1939 Pastor Robert Badke was active in Konin. Under the new church law of 1936 the filial of Maslaki was made independent as a parish congregation. In Zdunska-Wola, where from 1925 to 1945 the industrious and purposeful Pastor Dr. theol. Georg Lehmann labored, church life took a gratifying upswing 4 Ö ). The willingness to sacrifice in the congregation was great. Thus on May 1, 1933 Mrs. Florentine Schultz gave her property (house, plot and 10 Morgen of land) to the parish. On December 7 and 8, 1930 Zdunska-Wola celebrated its 100th anniversary of founding. On November 1, 1928 the 100th jubilee of the church congregation took place in Turek. In Sompolno Pastor Philipp Kreutz served from 1924 on, who became known as a religious poet. In 1935 he issued the volume of poems "Zeit und Ewigkeit". On November 7, 1931, under the leadership of General Superintendent Bursche, the founding of the filial of Ludwikowo took place. From 1923 to 1945 Pastor Alexander Groß was active in Zagör, who undertook the building of the parsonage and completed it in 1938. Pastor Groß, who stood close to the Christliche Gemeinschaft in Lodz, made the religious care of the congregation members very much his concern.
