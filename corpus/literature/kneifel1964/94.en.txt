In the town schools, by contrast, the situation with regard to the training of the teachers looked gratifying. Pay here was more orderly and secure. Similar conditions in the field of schooling prevailed at that time also in Germany.

With the development of the school system there went hand in hand also that of the congregations. In the year 1795 the Lutherans settled in and around Kalisch and immigrated there obtained the "concession for the establishment of their own evangelical church system46.

Already since 1775 Kalisch had had several Evangelicals, who were cared for by the pastors of Ostrowo and then of Stawiszyn. "They held public worship, for which a spacious room was assigned in the town hall."13)

As their local pastor they called Karl Michael Christian Sienell, who in the first two years held worship in the so-called shoemakers' benches. On the basis of the breve of 27 August 1797, Pope Pius VI, thanks to the efforts of the Prussian government, granted to the Kalisch Protestants the Jesuit church standing empty there for almost 25 years, built by Archbishop Karnkowski toward the end of the 16th century, "cum pleno iure patronatus". On the first day of Pentecost 1798 Pastor Sienell consecrated it as an evangelical house of worship. In the church there remained as before various monuments and memorial tablets, among them also the relief monument which shows Primate Karnkowski before the cross of Christ in an attitude of prayer.

As a further congregational foundation followed Grodziec. On 12, 13 and 14 July 1796 the representatives of the Holland villages of Borowiec, Konary, Grunde, Wieloleka, Orlina, Gadow and Bialobloty concluded with Count Stadnicki at Grodziec a contract concerning the formation of the Grodziec parish. Stadnicki reserved for himself and his descendants the right of patronage, in particular in the filling of the pastorate. The first pastor of the congregation became David Bergemann, born in Fraustadt14)

In the years 1797—1803 the church was built in the form of a "Prussian wall" 4 4 (half-timbered or frame construction of beams with bricks in between), together with the parsonage and cantor's house as well as all the farm buildings. In the same year as in Grodziec, the parish of Babiak organized itself. Bonaventura von Raczynski, the landowner of Ozorzyn, supported the efforts of the German Lutherans with regard to the founding of a parish, whose definitive establishment was set for 23 November 1796. A small wooden church was built and a bell procured from Thorn. In 1808 the evangelical elementary school at Babiak was founded. The pastorate was taken over by Pastor Johann Gottlieb Maske, formerly in Bialowiezyn near Lipno, who died in 1808 after severe illness.

By decree of the Prussian chamber at Plozk of 24 September 1799 the seat of the parish of Bialowiezyn was transferred to Lipno. In the year 1800 the house of worship in Ossowka was built. In the same year the congregation of Kowal was founded. It extended over the territory of the later parishes of Chodecz and Przedecz. In the absence of a church, the building of which in Kowal despite manifold efforts nonetheless did not come about, the parishioners gathered for worship in private houses.

The efforts for the establishment of the parish of Dombie date from 1799. At that time a commission appeared here, whose members included, among others, War Councillor von Colomb and Tax Councillor von Koehlichen.
