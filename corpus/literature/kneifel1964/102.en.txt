The Evangelicals of Sobiesenki had a house of worship, whose consecration took place on 13 April 1796. For worship the pastors of Stawiszyn traveled through the region. In the year 1808 the estate owner Wezyk of Iwanowice enabled the colonists settled there the formation of the parish and the erection of a small wooden church near his manor farm, whereupon Pastor Gottfried Felke was soon called to Iwanowice. In the year 1812 he went to Adelnau, and Iwanowice received in the person of August Friedrich Haase a new pastor. In 1817 the house of worship near Iwanowice was destroyed by a lightning stroke. On 17 March 1819 the government commission confirmed the project of the "establishment of the church system in Sobiesenki". Between Sobiesenki and Iwanowice there prevailed a strained relationship. In the first place Pastor Haupt officiated since 1819, and in the second Pastor Haase. In 1822 Pastor Haupt took over the Wengrow pastorate, while Pastor Haase moved to Sobiesenki, to which the parish seat was transferred.

In 1809 the Lutherans living in Lask and in the villages of Chorzeszöw, Anielin, Rembow, Erywangrod, Mierzqczka, Zabloty, Bocianichy and Okup joined together into the parish of Lask and called as their pastor Johann Karl Ulrich, who came from Salzwedel in the Neumark (died 1816). As house of worship they received from Peter Gzolchanski, the owner of the town of Lask, the unused Roman Catholic chapel of the Holy Spirit (hospital church). From 1818 Pastor Friedrich Christoph Rüdiger, born in Schawoine near Trebnitz, labored here, who in 1824, because of his difficult economic situation, took over the pastorate in Prazuchy. For over 100 years — from 1824 to December 1927 — Lask had no resident pastors any more and was a filial of Zdunska-Wola. In the years 1834/40 and 1855/59 it was temporarily also administered from Pabianice.

The combining of the Evangelicals of German and Polish nationality settled in Wielun and its environs into an independent Lutheran congregation was energetically pursued by the elders (Sarchenältesten) Christoph Lübke and Friedrich Gutt.

In 1820 the new parish was formed and to it was assigned the former Roman Catholic church of the Bernardine convent, built in 1612 in the round-arched style. As parsonage the Evangelicals received the former house of the nuns' confessor. As first pastor Samuel Gottlieb Sachs was installed on 28 March 1824. At the same time the solemn consecration of the church also took place.

Since 1790 the small town of Slesin, 20 kilometers from Konin, with the preaching places of Butter-Holl (Maslaki), Szyszyner-Holl. and Grabina was a filial of Witkowo, province of Posen, and was visited by Pastor Johann Gottlieb Bothe. In 1811 the congregation of Slesin organized itself, with August Bernhard Christian Drechsler as pastor, who, however, already gave up the pastorate in 1815. In 1815 the parish of Slesin again lapsed. Until 1817 Pastor Röhl from Witkowo (Bothe's successor) regularly visited the evangelical localities in the environs of Slesin. In the year 1824 the Evangelicals at Konin bought the former Roman Catholic Holy Spirit church, a house of worship built of wood in 1801.
