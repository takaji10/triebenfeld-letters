# Kneifel (1964), p. 123
# translation by Claude

Administratively the Augsburg church was divided into the dioceses of Warschau, Kalisch, Plock, and Augustowo. From 1901 there were five dioceses, the fifth being that of Petrikau (Piotrköw Tryb.). Superintendent of the Warschau diocese was General Superintendent Ludwig, who had held this office already since 1838; superintendent of the Kalisch diocese Ernst Johann August von Modi; superintendent of the Plock diocese Ignatius Karl Wilhelm Boerner, active in the same capacity since 1836; superintendent of the diocese of Augustowo since 1851 David Bergemann, pastor at Marjampol.

The diocese of Warschau comprised the congregations: the St. Trinitatis in Warschau, Wengrow with the filial Sadolei, Lublin with the filial Konskawola, Kielce with the filials Dombrowa and Przedborz, Radom, Pilica, Stara-Iwiczna, Nowydwör, Radzymin, Wiskitki, Lowicz with the filial Kutno, Dow, Brzeziny, and Rawa with the filial Blendow. In all there were 14 parishes and six filial congregations.

The diocese of Kalisch included: Kalisch, Sobiesenki, Wielun with the filial Panki, Belchatow with the filial Pozdzienice, Prazuchy, Stawiszyn with the filial Jözef 6w, Grodziec, Zagörow with the filial Lissewo-Pyzdry, Konin with the filial Maslaki, Wladyslawow, Dombie, Turek with the filial Kolo, Alexandrow with the filial Huta Bardzinska, Konstantynow with the filial Poddembice, Pabianice, Zdunska-Wola with the filial Lask, Tomaschow with the filial Wielka-Wola, Piotrköw with the filial Pilica, Kleszczöw with the filials Dziepölc and Czejstochowa. Thus 19 parishes and twelve filial congregations were included in it.
