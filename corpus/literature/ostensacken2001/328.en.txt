I authorise Herr von Cosmar further, over the estates of Bellin and Hülseburg, to conclude every contract and settlement that may be necessary and expedient. (…) All sums which Herr von Cosmar will promise to the creditors of the two Sacken estates I will pay out of my means and from my fortune. (…) I will in general hold fast to the whole settlement which Herr Cosmar will conclude with these creditors as a settlement gone out from myself and fulfil it especially in respect of the payments to be made - I only fix hereby as one, indeed as the single provision, that the quantum which Herr Cosmar will grant the hypothecary creditors of Bellin and Hülseburg for the time to 1 January 1808 in complete interest, and all personal creditors of the two von Sacken spouses in capital and interest, may not exceed the sum of 60,000 thaler Courant, so that I thus enter for these hypothecary creditors and the personal creditors resp. in respect of the interest and capitals of the latter only up to the sum of 60/m thousandths. Among the interests that I will pay the hypothecary creditors are the sums which I have already paid to Herr Graf von Hardenberg and to Herr Baron von Hammerstein. (…) Everything that Herr von Cosmar according to this, for me, even if under his name, will receive and negotiate, shall be recognised as binding, by me and my revenues as norma, and as an act received for me, and faithfully fulfilled: and for the protection of Herr Cosmar and my heirs I fix hereby further that this paper shall count not merely as a power of attorney issued by me but as a codicil belonging to my testamentary disposition, even if it is not found in my estate, and that the sums to be granted the creditors shall be paid as legacies ordered by me for the benefit of my lady daughter out of my fortune, and that they shall be borne out of my revenues even then, if my lady daughter should depart this life before me or such a case should occur that she is excluded altogether from my inheritance in accordance with my free disposition or receives only the compulsory portion or still less. I have read this power of attorney and codicil myself and signed it in my own hand, and had it sealed with my seal of justice.

Berlin, 6 November 1807

Christiane Charlotte Sophie, dowager princess of Sacken

née von Dieskau
