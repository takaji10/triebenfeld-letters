# Seidel (2018), p. 38
# translation by Claude

*[illustration: photograph of a street corner building, the former "Alte Kelter" in Ingelfingen]*

The "Old Winepress" (though historically the second one, since a first press had stood opposite the church) at the beginning of Mariannenstraße was rebuilt after the "Outer Winepress" was completed in 1787. This produced an inn and, under the same roof adjoining it, six craftsmen's workshops with their associated dwellings.
