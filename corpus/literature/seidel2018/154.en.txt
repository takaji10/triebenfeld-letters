# Seidel (2018), p. 154
# translation by Claude

> *placed under strict French guard and arrested. A fate I never deserved and which has given my civic existence a very bad turn. I was sent here from Memel at the end of December with important commissions, and I was already close to the goal of my wishes — soon to be able to depart again — when, without my suspecting it, two French gendarmes were sent to my lodgings with the order that they should keep me under strict watch and arrest until I, as General Plenipotentiary of the Prince of Hohenlohe, should have paid all the outstanding interest owed to the Invalid Fund alone, 5,000 reichsthalers. Shock and shame at this incident nearly drove me to the desperate resolve — the outstanding interest for 2 years amounts to 5,000 reichsthalers, where was I to get this amid the terrible want of money, all the more since there is no royal treasury here, and all revenues are in the hands of the French authorities. I proved with all the [jurists] that I was not the debtor. I showed the legal instrument by which the banker Oppenheimer had already, three years ago, undertaken to pay this remainder to the Invalid Fund at a discount of 7%, that he had had it entered under his own name, and that he was the one who must pay. I further proved that Your Serene Highness had not collected a single [penny] from the estates for two years, and that the Poles were hiding behind the order given by Napoleon not to pay here. But none of this counted for anything [...] and I had to sit until a remedy was found. I had 2,000 net on me; the upright Lieutenant-General and Minister von Zastrow stood surety for 5,000 net in paper money, which we then converted into specie and received 2,875 reichsthalers for, though we must repay 5,000 reichsthalers in specie for it by 1 June — before the last farthing is paid, I shall not be free of the execution nor released from my post. Meanwhile, through this affair, not only my honor, standing, and credit have been diminished, but the highest interests have been so*
