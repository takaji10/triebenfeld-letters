### 3. List of Friedrich Ludwig's Estates

*[illustration: reproduction of a handwritten list of properties/estates, numbered 1–34, with place names]*

One of the great lists of estates. The place names frequently refer to Hohenlohe or to the princely family: Sophienthal, Ludwigslust, Friedrichsfelde, Augustinow, Emilienhayn, Adolphsberg, Alexandrow, Klein Ingelfingen, Klein Öhringen, Klein Neuenstein, Klein Schrozberg.
