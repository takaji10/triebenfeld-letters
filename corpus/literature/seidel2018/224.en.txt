# Seidel (2018), p. 224
# translation by Claude

The great differences between the two personalities, FL and Massenbach, cannot be overlooked. One might ask why FL, of higher rank, did not simply part ways with Massenbach. Perhaps their shared origin played a certain role for both of them: small dominions in southern Germany, from which they had set out into the world (at first, admittedly, represented by the figure of Frederick II); perhaps they both spoke with a South German dialect or accent (one small indication of this might be that FL wrote, in letters, of "Scheitnich" — or dictated it so; the place near Breslau is called Scheitnig — although "ch" at the end also occurs quite occasionally in printed form. FL always signed his letters to the children with "Vatter," that is, with two "t"s, as suits the dialect — the children, for their part, addressed him as "Vater." The expression "Hüttensach" for mining activities belongs here as well). Both were, as "Franconians," somehow outsiders — there were parallels here, and these may have bound them together.
