# Seidel (2018), p. 184
# translation by Claude

August thus committed himself financially, taking over the remaining debts from the purchase of the Bytkow estate. The outstanding salaries were settled either by him or by Sophie Christiane.

Sophie Christiane Louise of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen returned to Ingelfingen on 20 April 1818. She died in 1831 and found her final resting place in the Princes' Vault between her parents. The castle at Slaventzitz continued to be used by the family.
