# Seidel (2018), p. 197
# translation by Claude

after FL's death, the estates of Betsche, Zagorow, Trobeczyn, Borkowo, and Wieniec were quickly sold once more (thus Fischer,[^144] though according to other sources[^145] FL had already begun this himself beforehand, as for instance in 1815 with the property at Alt-Scheidnigg as well). The area around Zagorow now belonged to Congress Poland, that is, to the Tsarist Empire of Russia, which did not make Triebenfeld's task on FL's behalf any easier, since he was indeed present in Vienna. He had tried to obtain compensation for the estates of FL's that had been lost there. For Bytkow and Slaventzitz, both in Silesia, protection existed through the entail (Fideikommiss) secured by the Princess Sacken. Koschentin was in the end taken over by Adolf.
