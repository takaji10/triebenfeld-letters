# Seidel (2018), p. 14
# translation by Claude

*[illustration: portrait of Christian Kraft zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, an elderly man in a powdered wig and dark coat; painter and year unknown]*

Christian Kraft zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen. (Painter and year unknown)

*[illustration: hand-drawn map of the Hohenlohe territories in northern Württemberg, showing Mergentheim, Weikersheim, Dörzbach, Schöntal, Neuestadt, Öhringen, Langenburg, Schwäbisch Hall, Rothenburg and (Massenbach), with Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen's territory shaded dark]*

The territory of all the Hohenlohe principalities in northern Württemberg, with the names of a few better-known places. Rothenburg and, to the south of it, Hohenlohe-Schillingsfürst already lie in Bavaria. Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen is shaded dark. – Massenbach is indicated here only because of its relative proximity (on the person of Christian von Massenbach, see further below).
