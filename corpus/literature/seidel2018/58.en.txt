### 5.3 Family Letters II (FL to his young children, 1798–1806)

Letters from Marianne to the children perhaps did not exist at all before 1810. She was constantly together with them until the year of separation, 1799. Only more than ten years later does a correspondence begin with August, who is by then an adult and himself Prince of Hohenlohe-Öhringen (see p. 88f.).

With the letters from FL that follow, I also attempt to narrow down somewhat the date of the mother's departure from Breslau. FL wrote a short but informative letter to his twelve-year-old daughter Adele on 13 February 1799 from Öhringen to Breslau, in his own hand.[^38] He thanks her for the good wishes and also for the drawings, "with which you gladdened me on my birthday [31 January]." He asks her to tell Mama that he has not yet received any further letters from Breslau

> *than the ones I have answered by the packet addressed to her. I shall remain here until the 15th of this [month], then go to Ingelfingen and stay there. I am here overwhelmed with grace and kindness. In Ingelfingen several visits await me, among which the young gentleman son will probably also be. V. Stetten is still in Asbach, will probably return these days. If the weather with you is as dry and moderately cold as it is here, do not forget to take vigorous exercise. As to the fulfillment of your assurance that you will conduct yourself well, I remain the more confident of it, since you yourself are now able to see the benefit of it. Of my true love you may be assured [...]. Keep your love for your faithful father [signature of Friedrich Ludwig].*

From Ingelfingen FL still had to deal with the purchase of the Mainz share in the town of Niedernhall. That was to take up time.
