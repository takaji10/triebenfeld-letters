> *here, since danger is present in delay, nothing further is required than*
>
> - *first: that the [...] or [Arrestführer] state his claim and what it consists of*
> - *second: that circumstances from which a present danger in delay may be inferred be demonstrated or definitely [ergebe] and the means of proof thereof be stated*
> - *third: that he immediately furnish a [...] surety both to cover the [...] and the arrest on account of the disadvantage that might arise to the same from the seizure.*
>
> *From the third requirement, concerning the furnishing of surety, the [fiscus] and thus also the General Invalids' Fund, which has Dura fisci, is exempt pursuant to § 33 Part. Title 29 of the Court Ordinance. It thus depends only on the presence of the other requirements for an application for arrest. It is further shown by the autograph letter of v.
