# Seidel (2018), p. 181
# translation by Claude

> *— moreover, comme il n'a jamais peche par la Beaute, so he had little to lose there for me, and I find that he has gained in the jour [...] and in point of amiability – to be sure, the costume which he had carried over from Switzerland to our North, and in which he assured me he had presented himself to you – was as strikingly hideous as possible – yet he still knows how to appreciate beauty and good taste as before – and gave me proof of his adoring reverence for your Louise – let Emilie tell you the particulars of this.*  
> *Adieu, good August! Angelique and Auguste commend themselves to your love, as I do to your heartfelt remembrance,*  
> *your mother who loves you dearly, Marianne*  
> *I ask that you give the enclosure to Emilie.*

Marianne had celebrated her birthday (6 October); she was now 54 years old. The place name "Bovden" may refer to Bovenden in southern Lower Saxony (formerly the Herrschaft Plesse, she calls it "our Baroness"), where parts of the family had met and from where she had traveled home via Hanover. Not a word about her marriage or her husband. She was proud of her daughters.

The choice of words expressing her love, to virtually everyone, ("heartfelt," "most heartfelt," also "longing," repeatedly used), appears rather stereotyped. – Is this Biedermeier style or specific to Marianne? She surely still had something to make up for or work through (the separation of 1799); it is fairly certain that she suffered from this her whole long life; the document "A Word of Trust" (see p. 83f.) she had indeed deposited, but who knows of it? – To assess the separation nowadays, after more than 200 years, as an emancipatory act by a woman may seem tempting; but for this too we have no basis.
