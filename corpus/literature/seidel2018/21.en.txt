### 2.2 Marriage and Family

FL married late — Marianne, Countess of Hoym zu Droyßig, seventeen years his junior. None other than Frederick II, King of Prussia, had drawn Friedrich Ludwig's attention to this very wealthy young lady (certainly already aware of the financial problems already besetting his protégé). The couple was regarded as glamorous. Frederick II then also became godfather to the first son, August.

Marianne, Countess of Hoym zu Droyssig, born in Mainz on 6 October 1763, died on 20 April 1840 at Marienhof in Mecklenburg. Her parents had moved temporarily to Mainz, and afterward also to Pyrmont, for political reasons. Her father died as early as 1769. The family's home was in Saxony.

The children of Friedrich Ludwig and his wife Marianne, née Countess Hoym, were:

- August, born on 27 November 1784 in Breslau, died on 15 February 1853 (godfather was Frederick II); married in 1811 to Louise of Württemberg (niece of the King).
- Adelheid (Adele), born on 20 January 1787 in Breslau, died in 1858; married in 1812 to Ludwig zu Hohenlohe-Kirchberg.
- Luise Amalie (Emilie), born on 20 November 1788 in Breslau, died in 1859; married in 1810 to Albrecht zu Erbach-Fürstenau.
- Wolfgang Ludwig, born on 18 December 1789 in Breslau, died on 9 February 1790.
- Auguste, born on 16 November 1793 in Walluf (Rheingau), died on 8 July 1821; married in 1816 to Karl von Hessen-Philippsthal-Barchfeld.
- Ludwig Karl, born on 16 November 1794 in Ingelfingen, died on 14 December 1794.
