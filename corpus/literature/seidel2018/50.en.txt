The King had understood, and granted a salary increase of 5000 florins. FL's letter is one of the very few, and a very early, documents in which he himself addresses his financial situation.

In Bayreuth he met the Prussian reformer Karl August von Hardenberg, who served there in a civil capacity at the court of the Prussian province of Brandenburg-Ansbach-Bayreuth for some years from 1791. The two remained on friendly terms in the further course of political business as well, and met, for instance, also in Ingelfingen.

### 4.2 Duties after the Peace of Basel

The Peace of Basel had come about in early 1795 with von Hardenberg as Prussia's chief negotiator, without the participation of Austria (that is, of the imperial army and the troops of the south German imperial estates allied with it under General Clairfait – note, only his name is French)!

A demarcation line (also called a neutralization line) through southern Germany had been agreed upon, intended as far as possible to prevent warlike undertakings.
