# Seidel (2018), p. 125
# translation by Claude

> *collapse, are now to be torn down. There now remains for me only the new construction of a barn in Lechina, and then we would have the farm buildings in this department fairly well in order. In the matter of the works, this is already the case. For the new fitting-out of the Jakobswalde wire mill, the iron castings are now being formed in the local blast furnace. The whole is being carried out at little cost, just as you saw it done in model form at Korb's in Jakobswalde. The parsonage there will only be fully finished and occupied by winter. You will see from this, my dear son, that one has not been altogether idle. The clearing in the forests is proceeding well, and charcoal-burning of this cordwood will now begin. The damage from the last great storm, which felled over 500 cords in the forests, actually did no essential harm, since correspondingly less will now be cut in the ordinary felling. We are presently occupied here with, upon the reduction of the portion of field bordering the forest road that is to go to the peasants and is already partly measured out, establishing a regulation whereby the forest is given a natural boundary [...] and gains a useful increment. Mr. Sillg [...] is being accommodating in this matter, and since after the survey these peasants are given even more field than is their due, this good arrangement will probably begin without great difficulty. Korb let me have your letter [conveying] your opinion regarding the memoranda of J.K. Hofrichter. I see from it that I, and the matter, have been misunderstood on your side. Mr. Hofrichter is the commissioner for our Lassowitz estates as well as for Landsberg. He has, however, nothing whatever to do with the Slaventzitz estates. What he says regarding the gardens is his expert opinion.
