# Seidel (2018), p. 248
# translation by Claude

> *the interest thereon, to be attached by decree.*  
> *Berlin, 30 July 1811*  
> *Seelmann*
>
> *His Excellency the Royal Minister of State and Justice, Herr von Kircheisen, is hereby called upon, in the event that the claim asserted by the General Invalids' Fund in its suit filed on the 30th [...] [M.] before the Royal Kammergericht — that His Highness the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen received from the estate of the Princess von Sacken a legacy of 80,000 florins and has assigned the same to the War and Forestry Councillor von Triebenfeld of Kontopp near Grünberg toward the settlement of the latter's claims against him — should prove well founded, to pay out nothing of this sum to von Triebenfeld on his own authority.*  
> *Berlin, 1 August 1811*  
> *Royal Prussian Kammergericht*
>
> *To His Excellency the Royal Minister of State and Justice*  
> *Herr von Kircheisen, in simili*  
> *To the Royal Chief Councillor of Medicine, Herr Cosmar, here*  
> *To the War and Forestry Councillor Herr von Triebenfeld*  
> *at Contopp*
>
> *No. 1103. We herewith transmit to you a summons of the Royal Kammergericht of Berlin of the [1st] instant, in the matter of the General Invalids' Fund against you, to the end that you give due obedience to its contents in every respect and duly certify proper receipt thereof upon the copy to be presented to you for that purpose.*  
> *Glogau, 6 August 1811*  
> *Royal Prussian Superior Court of Silesia*  
> *Fülleborn*
