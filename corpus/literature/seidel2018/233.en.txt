He would report to FL whenever he had heard something of Auguste and of Adelheid (Adele), or had met them. Adolph and Alexander grew up in Öhringen and in Stuttgart; they too visited their father (and also their mother).

The exchange with August was especially intensive — and had to be. He was, after all, the successor. Of him FL could say, toward the end of his life, as his sister reported, that he (August) had "come to love him (FL) quite dearly after all" — FL had only decided on marriage at the age of 37, and after its end he had then seized the chance, even now, in his good relations with his children from the failed marriage and at the age of over 55, to become a good family man after all.
