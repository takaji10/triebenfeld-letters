### 7.2 "A Word of Trust"

This concerns a document of central importance to Marianne, which she composed on 12 October 1811 for particular purposes and wished to have kept separately. Since it deals with the separation, the Transact, and the divorce papers, it is reproduced here in full — this document was found by Gerlinde Kraus in the archive in Upper Silesia.[^64]

> *A word of trust to my two sons, Prince August von Hohenlohe Oehringen and Prince Adolph von Hohenlohe, and for the present in his stead to His Excellency, the Lord Minister of Justice von Kircheisen, Lord Privy Justice Councillor von Sack, and Lord Chief Councillor of Medicine Cosmar. To my son, Prince August von Hohenlohe, and to the aforementioned gentlemen guardians of my remaining children, I take the liberty, together with the enclosed declaration concerning the will of my late, dear mother, to speak yet a word of trust.
