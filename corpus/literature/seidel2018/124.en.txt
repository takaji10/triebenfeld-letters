# Seidel (2018), p. 124
# translation by Claude

> *weeks longer. Her [Ehrenpfort] she left behind here ill, but she is now better again; the local brewer's daughter accompanied her. I passed on to Sophie the good news you gave me of your state of health. From Auguste I had letters from Landshuth of the 4th of this month, and learned also that they had passed through to Schmiedeberg by evening. They must, if they still delay in order to look about the district of Warmbrunn, nevertheless have arrived in Herleshausen between the 10th and 12th. I hope, therefore, that they will have found her brother Wilhelm together with his wife, who traveled with him from Altona to [H.], he too having gone to Bückeburg at the time Prince Eugene was there; the birth of the Hereditary Prince will have caused great joy there, though the Elector of Hesse, who had long hoped for the reversion of the fief of the county of [S.], may not have wished for it quite so ardently. General von Welzien, formerly my adjutant and who recently paid me a visit, has been appointed the new Commandant at Cosel. His neighborhood is agreeable to me. His post as Inspector of the Landwehr is to go to Colonel Thiele, whom you know from Gera, where he served on my General Staff. The protocol of our last conference here, signed by you, I am filing away, and your will in this matter will be followed, as I have already directed. The harvest is still under way and is turning out excellently of every kind. These past days I inspected the buildings at Burawa and found the [tenancy] building, along with the other installations, nearly finished. The sawmill, which had to be rebuilt, was completed and is already cutting. The following day I was in Lechine, where I found the new granary floor finished, as well as the new sheepfold and shepherd's house, which are already occupied.
