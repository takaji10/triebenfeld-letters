### 11.3 The Castle at Slaventzitz as a Retirement Seat

The castle, originally built by the Piast dynasty, FL had taken over from his wife's (Hoym) family holdings. His mother-in-law, Princess Sacken, had made attempts to secure parts of the inheritance (for example, the attempt to convert it into a so-called Fideikommiss, which made it impossible for creditors to seize it). This was the case with Slaventzitz. It was here that the letters now went, here that he received visits from former staff members and from his children. – Marianne, by then married and become Countess von der Osten-Sacken and living at Bellin Castle, was always kept informed about their visits to FL and events there, through the lively exchange of letters and through visits to her. Slaventzitz was thus his residence for old age and retirement. This says nothing — an unfortunately recurring theme — about the financial situation, which gave rise to manifold deliberations at Öhringen.[^88]

### 11.4 Friedrich Ludwig's Dreams Before and After His Time as a Military Commander

Before attempting to grasp his everyday life there, it seems appropriate to look once more at his life thus far and his dreams. He had achieved a good deal: one need only recall his Ingelfingen, with its settlement of crafts and its educational opportunities for the population — this was a lasting success. His military career and his diplomatic activity between the Peace of Basel of 1795 and 1805, a period without many armed conflicts, was likewise a success. As Governor of Breslau he had found recognition even from the Prussian King.
