# Seidel (2018), p. 194
# translation by Claude

> *sojourn of 6 weeks, to the regret of both sides, since she had become [...] attached, and she herself is a most agreeable companion and especially very musical. A short while ago we very nearly lost good old Ohlnhausen, who lay dangerously ill with an inflammation of the abdomen, but has fully recovered./With most filial reverence and love I remain, dearest Father/your most obedient son/August[^142]*

Note: There are also letters from the daughters to their father FL. They extend up to the year 1817.[^143] For reasons of space, they had to be omitted here.
