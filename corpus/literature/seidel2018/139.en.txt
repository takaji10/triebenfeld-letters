Glenck had once written to FL that he had also put private money into the Weißbach saline. That was not all: in the last schedules of debts that Triebenfeld had drawn up for FL (see appendix, p. 239), a loan from Glenck to FL also appears! And, one can scarcely believe it, between 1823 and 1828 Glenck himself once fell into a financial bind, so that he had to ask Goethe for 2000 thalers in order to be able to continue the boreholes! – Who, faced with this, does not think – as with FL – of entrepreneurial spirit and the making of debts!

A great breakthrough was represented by Glenck's discoveries of rock salt in Basel-Land. They became a foundation of the chemical industry now established there – and also of the wealth he ultimately achieved. Such wealth was attained only by FL's grandson, in Upper Silesia.
