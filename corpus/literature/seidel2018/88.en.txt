## 8 Family Letters III (FL to August, 1800–1805)[^65]

A single letter has a letterhead. It was written, in French, in Breslau on 10 January 1803. The writer (probably not FL himself, who mostly dictated and probably did not hold the pencil) has drawn a building at the top right of the letter sheet. It is — most likely — the House of the Double Eagle at the north-east corner of the Rynek (market square); no connection to FL emerges from a historical explanation posted in the entrance there.
