# Seidel (2018), 266-u2
# translation by Claude

At the beginning of the "long 19th century," Europe was profoundly shaped and forever changed: the French Revolution, the rise and fall of Napoleon, early industrialization, and the reordering of the continent. In the midst of these turbulent events stood Friedrich Ludwig, Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen (b. 1746; d. 1818). As a general in Prussian service, he experienced the wartime confrontations at first hand; he had to watch as "his" Hohenlohe was absorbed into the new Kingdom of Württemberg, and over the course of his life he amassed an immense mountain of debt through his entrepreneurial activities.

In this book, Hans-Joachim Seidel examines the Prince's dramatic biography and, through numerous contemporary documents and excurses, offers for the first time an insight into the personal circumstances and life of the Hohenlohe prince.
