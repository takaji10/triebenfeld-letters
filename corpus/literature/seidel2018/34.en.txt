## 3 Friedrich Ludwig as Urban Renewer

### 3.1 Settlement of Trade and Construction of the Mariannenvorstadt

How did it actually come about that, in dreamy Hohenlohe, a local ruler hit upon the idea of resolutely pursuing economic progress as a path of development for his small realm? How did FL arrive at the idea of settling trade and industry — comparable to the founding of an industrial park today? He had, quite simply, seen a great deal, out there in the world, where his military service to the Prussian rulers had taken him. His idol was Frederick II, who, when he was not waging war, concerned himself a great deal with improving the economic condition and living circumstances of his subjects. To this belongs his acquaintance with Karl August von Hardenberg, and also his marriage to Marianne, Countess von Hoym zu Droyßig, who was native to Saxony. More will be said of this later. — It does not diminish his merit to point out that his uncle in Öhringen, Prince Ludwig Friedrich Karl, had undertaken something similar there with the Karlsvorstadt.

Friedrich Ludwig announced his intentions in a "Proclamation" in the year 1782:

> *It is the principal object of this most gracious Prince to make the new inhabitants of this suburb happy, to which end His Highness shall most graciously endeavor to bring about all that is possible, whereby the branch of trade and industry may be brought to the fullest flourishing.[^18]*
