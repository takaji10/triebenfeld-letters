### Michalinów Trąbczyński, Łazińsk, Huta Trąbczyńska. Inhabitants List

The village of Michalinów Trąbczyński lies to the south of Trąbczyn and is recorded in the Catholic church register of Trąbczyn as a locality already around the year 1800. Even then many German families lived there. This Michalinów is often confused by mistake with the village bearing the same name, Michalinów (Emilienheim), and causes many problems in the search for ancestors from the early years before the year 1843, because the two localities belonged to two different Catholic churches.

For this reason the names carry additional designations: Michalinów Trąbczyński, the one discussed here, and Michalinów Oleśnicki (= Emilienheim).

Below is a list of the former inhabitants of the locality, state as of the year 1945, which was compiled by Mr. Leonard Kuske of 28857 Syke. Thanks are also due to Mrs. Regina Schiewe for her general help in assembling the facts.
