 by Wiesława and Przemysław Kowalski, the film *Ludzie z żelaznych domów* was being made, telling the story of the Hauländers who inhabited the lands of the Pyzdry Forest for 200 years. One of its protagonists is Mr Erich Kuhn – the untiring initiator and organiser of the meetings of the former Protestant inhabitants of these lands in Zagórów. On Saturday, 18 August 2018, on the three disused Evangelical-Augsburg cemeteries in the gmina of Zagórów, ecumenical prayers took place, led by the Rev. Waldemar Wunsz of the Evangelical-Augsburg Parish in Konin and the Rev. Jan Walczak of the parish of St Stanislaus Bishop and Martyr in Trąbczyn, with the participation of invited guests and the inhabitants of the surrounding villages. On the cemeteries in Michalinów Trąbczyński, Łazińsk Pierwszy and Łazińsk Huta, stones bearing plaques informing about the character of the place were set in the course of the summer. The founder and initiator of the placing of the plaques was Erich Kuhn. On the initiative of the Frydhof Association, thorough clearing work had already been carried out in the year 2017 on the cemeteries in Michalinów and at the Huta, thanks to which the memory of the deceased was restored. Help came from the inhabitants of the villages, from the Society of Friends of the Zagórów Land, from the pupils of the Gimnazjum in Trąbczyn, and from Jan Kloc – a Zagórów councillor, who with his own hands performed most of the heaviest work on all three cemeteries. The cemetery at Łazińsk Pierwszy is at present in private hands. A patch of ground was cleared there for the setting up of a cross, a stone and a symbolic lapidarium. The meeting ended in the hall of the Volunteer Fire Brigade (OSP) of Trąbczyn, where the guests could admire a collection of miniatures of works made by Jan Kloc, and likewise an exhibition, also his own work, connected with the centenary of the recovery of independence. On 19 August 2018, the traditional service took place. The musical setting was provided by the choir Cantabile of Zagórów, which for many years had been cooperating with the parish in Konin. In the following year, on 18 August, in the Evangelical church in Zagórów, an ecumenical service took place, combined with the unveiling of a plaque commemorating the former Protestant inhabitants.
