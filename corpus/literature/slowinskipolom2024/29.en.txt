# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 29
# translation by the editor's files

And fate – governing itself by its own singular and unpredictable logic – caused a collection of letters, documents and books, found in the house of the cantor Gustav Adolf Mielke of Łazińsk, to fall into our hands, revealing the picture of a family utterly intoxicated by Nazism, fervent, but also naïve in their blindness, admirers of the Führer. For the writer of these words it was not an easy read, but this work had to be done… Thus came into being the text "Listy żołnierza Wehrmachtu odkryte w ścianie domu kantora".

It is difficult to define the character of this volume, on account of the variety of the material placed in it. It is not a monographic study, but rather a book, which in its composition recalls the old domestic *Silva Rerum* (Las rzeczy). In such books there were written down accounts of important events, notes from journeys, diaries, receipts and household recipes, as well as legends preserved in memory. So too can our texts be read. From the first to the last page, or the chosen items, which have been grouped in three parts: Historia i pamięć, Wspomnienia i refleksje, Świadectwa. If we have succeeded in preserving in memory and telling even a small fragment of this two-hundred-year history, then perhaps it will be easier to understand.

That the work undertaken was recognized by the Zagórów community as needed is testified by many rather unusual incidents, which accompanied us. On the website of the Zagórów House of Culture there appeared a communiqué with a request for information or for the delivery of surviving documents, photographs, testimonies and writings. The announcement met with a quick response, and very interesting, source materials reached the House of Culture. The Society of Friends of the Zagórów Land announced a collection among internet users, to support the budget of the publisher.

An altogether extraordinary event, however, was the concert of Daniel Słowiński and friends, organized by Rev. Waldemar Wunsz on 23 September 2023 in the Evangelical church in Zagórów, combined with a collection of money for the needs of the publication. The church was filled with listeners, who rewarded the artists and the organizer with a standing ovation.
