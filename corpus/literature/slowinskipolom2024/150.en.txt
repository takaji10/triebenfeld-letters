He writes the letter on the way to the front "on the march" on 10 June 1942. "By God's grace we arrived safely in Finland. The journey by ship passed without problems. The sea was calm and majestic. […] We travelled for two days in Finland by train and found ourselves near a little town close to the Arctic Circle. Now we are to undertake a long march which is to last many weeks". You must understand that for reasons of security we cannot state anything precisely. If – God grant – victory is achieved, then I shall describe and tell you everything in detail". And further about his worries in service. "Some tasks are very difficult – e.g. the marching, for I have always been a stay-at-home and have not grown used to it. But since everyone wants to accomplish it, I too shall not give up.

I shall do everything within my power. Apart from my health and my life I can give nothing else. I shall keep my oath. […] May God grant victory". He cannot give the field post number, for they have not yet reached their destination.

A letter recounting the company's arrival at its place has happily survived.
