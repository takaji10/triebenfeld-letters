# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 214
# translation by the editor's files

 the Jewish cheder, a triumphal gate entwined with laurel wreaths. At the top of the gate there was an inscription: "We Welcome the Reverend Shepherd". As it happened, the Father Bishop's entry into the town fell at the time when the cattle were returning from pasture. Beside the triumphal gate a crowd had gathered, headed by the mayor of the town and the town councillors, the parish priest, and a good number of local priests. Next to the mayor and the parish priest stood also, with bread and salt on a tray, the local rabbi, and beside him the Evangelical pastor. Behind their backs, in ranks, this time in agreement, stood two rival bands – the fire brigade's and that of the "Sokół" Gymnastic Society. Further along the road were drawn up ranks of scouts, Sokół members, riflemen and firemen, and a little ahead, on either side of the Słupca road, schoolchildren with little flags. To meet the bishop there set out a mounted unit hastily mobilized from among the local youth, splendidly adorned with ribbons. In short – a parade such as the town had not seen for a long time. Unfortunately, one mistake was made, for the whole assembly was located on the route along which the cattle returned from pasture, and the cattle, being cattle, did not consider it necessary to behave any differently than on any other day: it liked to gambol about, nibble something on the way, and relieve itself wherever it pleased. "Mr Matuszak" sensed the scandal hanging in the air, and wanting to curb the more aggressive cows, moved on foot to the head of the frisky herd. The cows slowed their pace, astonished by a crowd of people such as they had never encountered before, and our cowherd, with a dignified countenance, passed under the triumphal gate with the inscription: "We Welcome the Reverend Shepherd", bowed to the mayor, the councillors, the priests and the other dignitaries of the town gathered there, took the bread from the astonished rabbi's hands and uttered with dignity the following words:

– Thank you all most heartily for this solemn welcome. At least once the town has recognized my work. God reward you for it.

A small commotion ensued, and the cowherd, with his herd and his loaf of bread, escorted by the laughter of the gathered crowd, marched off down the sand-strewn streets of the little town.
