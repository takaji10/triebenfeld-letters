# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 106
# translation by the editor's files

##### Michalinów Oleśnicki, the cantors' school

In the settlement there functioned a one-class school with German as the language of instruction. It was housed in the house of prayer. It had approximately 60 pupils, who were under the care of a teacher named Julius Berendt. After the Second World War a primary school for the children of Michalinów was organized in the building. The building is the property of the Evangelical-Augsburg Parish in Konin; at present there are apartments there rented by private individuals.

##### Michalinów Trąbczyński, the German denominational school

We know very little about this school. Nearly 70 pupils attended it. The language of instruction was German, and from 1923 the teacher was Friedrich Klingbeil. The function of cantor was performed by the farmer Paul Schulz, who lived near the cemetery. The building in which it was housed has survived to this day – it is now in private hands.

##### Wrąbczynkowska Kolonia (Wrąbczynkowskie Holendry)

The teacher Olga Ritterówna, who kept the chronicle of the Public School in Wrąbczynkowskie Holendry in the school year 1938/1939, notes:

From time immemorial there has existed in the Colony of Wrąbczynkowo, also called the Wrąbczynkowskie Holendry, a cantorate settlement […] comprising in a single parcel 10 morgi and 71 rods, of which 8 morgi are arable land. On this settlement there stood, and still stand to this day, the following buildings:

- the cantor's house, thatched with straw, consisting half of a schoolroom and half of a dwelling for the cantor;
- a house of clay and boards, roofed with tiles, in which there is one great hall of prayer;
- a barn of beams;
- a farm building of clay, for pigsties and a cowshed.
