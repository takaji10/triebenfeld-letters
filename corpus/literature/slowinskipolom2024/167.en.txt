# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 167
# translation by the editor's files

 This drawing is a motif often exploited in propaganda and war publications. An oak on a hillock, helmets on the grave of the fallen and cranes returning to their native nests.



"Death for the fatherland is worthy of eternal honour". This is a quotation from the writer, poet and officer of the Prussian army Ewald Christian von Kleist, who died of wounds received in the battle of Kunowice.
