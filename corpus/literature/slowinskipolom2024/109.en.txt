After the outbreak of the war, Olga Richterówna left Wrąbczynkowskie Holendry; on 16 November 1939 Edmund Weiss was appointed headmaster of the school, and the former headmistress Maria Gawrońska, with the consent of the occupiers, tried to organize instruction in the school. German was to be the official language of instruction, and Polish – an additional subject. "Naturally this existed only on paper – none of the teachers knew German well enough, nor did anyone feel like teaching it"[^48]. On 1 March 1940 all the Polish teachers were dismissed, and the Polish children left the walls of the school.
