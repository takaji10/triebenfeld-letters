#### “…a Polish officer and a Hitler woman”.

##### Bronisława Wrzesińska, née Szumańska, remembers

My uncle Czesław married a German woman already during the occupation. Slogans, it was said, began to appear on the walls: “An equal married an equal – a Polish officer and a Hitler woman”. She was called Nela Milicz; she came from Zagórów. She worked at the bank, and whatever funds she could, she saved for the Poles. Unfortunately, my mother's dowry she did not manage to save. Auntie Nela had a camera and liked taking photographs. Thanks to this I have family keepsakes from those times. After the war, in 1949, auntie had a case in court in which it was demonstrated that she had acted to Poland's benefit, and she escaped punishment.

#### The Home Army billet in our house

From February 1940 the Home Army billet for the Słupca-Zagórów District was housed in our home[^90]. Meetings were held here, oaths were taken by the newly admitted, and documents were kept.
