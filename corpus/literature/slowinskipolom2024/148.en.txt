# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 148
# translation by the editor's files

 to this is added the worry where you will be in a few weeks. […] Apart from you I have no one. I have no friends at all, our girls themselves need support, and father… you know after all – he sleeps and eats, and reads all the time. But he is well. Without you I am rather lonely and would best like to leave here too – to where you are and where you expose yourself to dangers". If only he got a leave, he would set off immediately for Klagenfurt.


At present he is feverishly busy putting affairs in order. A few days ago they (the year-classes 1899 to 1895) received a summons to register for the duty of defence. "So on 28 April we were at the Commune Office and registered ourselves. I, Mundt, Grunwald Heinrich, Schmechel, Stolp and many others. This means that we too may be called up to the army in the coming days". In the closing sentences he writes that he does not understand those people who are "happy" when they try to take our faith from us. "I am a Christian and in recent days this has been made felt to me again and again". He could not bear it if some young man tried to arouse disgust for it or harass them – and then misfortune would happen. "I know – you can stomach something like that. But it cannot be tolerated for too long". (This fragment is precisely the contemptuous, indeed aggressive attitude of the adherents of the Nazi ideology towards the Evangelicals, generally Christians faithful to the Church, signalled outright).

After an account of the farm work and the neighbourly help of Stelter, Gustav declares to his brother that this year he has decided to marry. There is, however, one problem: "That Stell behaved earlier as if she did not want to – but now she does everything in that direction. Yet I cannot decide on her, because I believe she is such that discord might arise through her among my remaining siblings, and I do not want that at any price. Another will yet be found. I would most gladly like one with a farm. It grieves me most that you had to become a soldier and are not yet married". He is also very grateful that Bruno wrote a letter to Kadzia.
