We shall also find such accounts as appreciate the behavior of the evangelical neighbors – because thanks to them the war was survived, or their Polish neighbors were saved. It is hard to agree with the thought that the citizens of Poland of German nationality, even those absorbed in the propaganda demonstrating the superiority of German culture over Slavic culture, or asserting the superiority of the Aryan race over others – expected or anticipated actions as dramatic and criminal as those undertaken by Hitler's Germany during the occupation. Eloquent is the behavior during the war of the Zagórów pastor Aleksander Gross, who, according to oral tradition, walked about Zagórów and, meeting the inhabitants, repeated: "Forgive me, forgive me for what the Germans are doing…".
