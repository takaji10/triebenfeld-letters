He cannot count on leave at Christmas. The long queue of those with longer service still stands before him. Perhaps they will allow him in January, "which may mean a long cold journey, but others too travel in winter alive there and back, so I suppose I shall endure it too". He is constantly corresponding with candidates for wife. At present with two: one – a neighbour from Konary – pretty and intelligent, but not a believer; the other *Volksdeutsche* (an ethnic German). The first one began writing to him from Wallensen. A little lacking in intelligence. "But one thing is certain for me: I shall not marry before I find a believing girl". She must guarantee a deep communion – especially since he is to become a pastor in the future.

Apart from that all is well – also from the soldierly point of view. When he compares the lot of the fighting comrades in the south or his own past winter, he is so far 50 per cent better off and it is finer. But the beautiful time may end any day. "So our commander said recently – the front from the Arctic Ocean [Eismeer] to Italy can at any moment become open". As a Christmas present he sends the family the splendid little book received from Hamburg, *Der Weg des Glaubens* [The Way of Faith]. "Write often". Their commander knew what he was saying. Already in January 1944 the resolute advance of the Red Army began, whose aim was to lift the blockade of Leningrad – which took place on 27 January 1944. The Russian attacks forced Army Group North to retreat. They withdrew to the region of Lake Peipus (now on the border of Estonia and Russia). Bruno Mielke's unit belonged to that grouping. In the south, at the same time, there began the advance which in the following five months will bring the liberation of Ukraine,
Volhynia and the Crimea. Still at the end of 1943, in the days from 28 November to 1 December, there takes place in Tehran the meeting of the leaders of the great coalition, Churchill, Roosevelt and Stalin.
