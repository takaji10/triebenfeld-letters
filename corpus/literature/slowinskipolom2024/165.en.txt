He will not be able to come to the wedding and in this situation does not want to either. He knows that Hans loves her, that he is kind to the family and to him, and this is dreadful to him. He has only the hope that his sister does not share this severe prohibition and that they will be able to talk about everything. At the end he writes: "this is my request and wish for you – that you remain in your faith, which is clear and dear to you, steadfast and faithful. You might yet bring Hans to the faith. But that is not simple".

These most zealous cares for the state of his sister's soul and the attitude of her fiancé must have been repeated, for there has come to light a letter of Anna in which she presents her position, firmly grounded in reality. "I am sorry that I cause you restless hours, but believe me that there is no need for you to worry on my account. I can neither prove to you nor explain it, but I know that your cares are unnecessary, and they – on the other hand – unsettle me. If I were to leave Hans on your account or on yours, then after all I would gain nothing, since from his side no danger threatens me, if one may call it so. He loves me far too much to want to force me into anything I do not want. Am I to wait for a pastor who perhaps more than once was insincere? I do not want, like my sisters, to be 40 years old or more and then to suffer hysterical fits, because overloaded nerves lose hope. Those whom I have known were little different from Hans. He is at least loyal and worthy of trust. And in no case is he an unbeliever".

She reassures Bruno that there is nothing disquieting in the matter. On the other hand she considers it disadvantageous if he keeps brooding over it, for "at night and in a foreign land everything looks different". "We are all touched by sentimentality which we ourselves do not wish to see and do not wish to admit to. I believe we camouflage ourselves well". Anna, in turn, warns her brother not to fall into a peculiar religious mysticism. This Bruno certainly did not expect from his sister...
