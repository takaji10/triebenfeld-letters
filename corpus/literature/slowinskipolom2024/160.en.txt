# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 160
# translation by the editor's files

In this letter there is also a description of the order of the day in force, or rather of the whole day and night. "We now have 6 hours of sentry duty within 24 hours – that can still be endured. Yes, here, dear life functions quite differently, also the times of meals. We eat dinner at 6 p.m., supper at 3 in the night, breakfast at 13 in the afternoon. Why so? Because at night we must be more on the alert and watchful than in the daytime. Whereas when day comes, there is nothing to fear, and then begins our time of sleeping". And the most important, joyful news. After a mere eight months of service, as of 1 December 1942, he was promoted to senior private. Captain Lebech with Lieutenant Hensler made their way in person to his sentry post and, in the face of the enemy, read out the order of promotion for exemplary service and a deep sense of duty.

To the soldiers present at the post the captain said that they should be proud to have such a comrade. "He would be pleased if we had only such soldiers". Curious that before reading out the order the commander asked Mielke to which class of nationality he belonged. The answer came that to the Volksliste. After a moment's reflection and pondering, the captain read out the order of promotion. Already in this same letter the senior private drifted into dreams about the chances of further promotions… "This I want to include in my prayer: that God grant victory to our dear fatherland and thereby peace to Europe and bread, that God grant my dear siblings a happy, blessed marriage; that God give me the strength to fulfil my soldierly duty in blessing and with honour".

Two letters written in December to Bruno were found in the cache. They probably reached him after the New Year – at that time a letter took a minimum of fifteen days to reach the Eastern Front. On 12 December Anna Steinbart writes from Konary, the woman standing in for Mielke in his work at the school. "Dear comrade in work in the grey uniform.
