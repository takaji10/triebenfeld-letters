### The Parish of Zagórów[^95]

Compiled by Günter Hennings[^95]

The settlement of the Zagórów area by Evangelical colonists began as early as the middle of the 18th century. Łazińsk became the largest kantorate of the later gmina and comprised 150 inhabitants. Besides Łazińsk, the following were also established: Tarszewo (Friedrichsfelde), Chruściki (Adelshof), Michalinów (Emilienheim), Olchowo (Sophiental), Marly Las (Ludwigslust), Anielewo (Ingelfingen) and Augustynów (Augustinenhof). Around the year 1800 clothmakers also settled in Zagórów.

The immigrants remained at first under the care of pastors from Grodziec, Konin and Żychlin.

On 29 July 1840 the Evangelical Christians resolved to establish a filial in Zagórów and to elect a church college. However, the final establishment of the filial did not take place until 1843. Services were at first held in rented premises, and from the year 1850 in the house at 54 Konińska Street. The filial was tended by Pastor Orive. In the year 1856 Zagórów became independent, and Pastor Winkler became the first local incumbent. A year later the parish acquired two small buildings as a parsonage. After only four years of activity Pastor Winkler left the parish, and his successors Eugen Biermann (1860-1865), Karl Eduard Erdmann (1876-1878) and Bernard Erdmann (1898-1903) served here only briefly as well.
