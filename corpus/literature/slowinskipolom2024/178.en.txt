He knows her only from the account of an acquaintance, but what decides her worth (in accordance with Bruno's system of values, which we have already come to know) is the fact that she is pure in faith, character and virtue, that is, she possesses all those most beautiful values which qualify her to be his wife. "Of course you must judge for yourself whether she pleases you […] but you must not set your judgements and requirements too high – more important than outward beauty remains, after all, inner worth". As for children – despite her age – she may still give birth to a few, and surely he does not want a large brood…? The letter also brings thanks to the family for the birthday wishes sent and the assurances of prayers to God for intercession on his behalf. He writes all this in the field and explains where such an address comes from. "Being in good health, I am at present helping with the building of roads. It is not easy to work physically 9-10 hours with pick and spade, but everything will pass; I am grateful that the food is good (better than last year) and that I can work for Germany and not like a prisoner of war in Russia – as has befallen many".

Where does this mood come from? Bad news after the heavy defeat at Kursk comes not only from the Eastern Front. On 10 July the British and Americans land in Sicily. On 25 July the Grand Fascist Council carries out a surprising coup, depriving Benito Mussolini of power. A few days later – on 3 September – Italy (the government of Marshal Badoglio) capitulates! On that same day the Allied troops land in southern Italy. The arrested Duce is freed by German commandos on Hitler's orders. And this event arouses the admiration of Bruno Mielke. "The fidelity of our Führer pleases me especially. This matter in Italy will become at least clear, and certainly further steps will soon be taken". Under the Germans' umbrella Mussolini creates in northern Italy the puppet Republic of Salò. This is another letter in which Mielke allows himself a commentary on broader events concerning the war and politics.
