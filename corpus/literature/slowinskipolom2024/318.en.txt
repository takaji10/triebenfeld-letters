# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 318
# translation by the editor's files

 Mr Kuhn's ancestors, without having to force one's way through a thicket of bushes, dry branches and rubbish, it was precisely Erich Kuhn who initiated a collection of funds for placing, on three cemeteries important to him, memorial stones commemorating the former inhabitants of those villages resting there. Thanks to his initiative, in August 2018 stones with inscriptions in Polish and German stating that these are Evangelical cemeteries, where the inhabitants of these lands have long rested, were erected in Michalinów Trąbczyński, Łazińsk Pierwszy and Łazińsk Huta. The greatest and most welcome support is rendered to us precisely by the units of the Volunteer Fire Brigades, which have a numerous workforce marked by physical vigour, but often also heavy equipment so very necessary in many cases. Some stone or concrete blocks weigh several hundred kilograms, and to restore them to their former place truly enormous strength is needed. Without the support of the firefighters, on many cemeteries what we managed to accomplish together would have been impossible. The local fire brigades are truly a help beyond all measure. It has not yet happened that firefighters, when asked – as long as they have no other duties – did not hurry to help.


It also gladdens one – and this should be emphasised – that despite the daily rush, the municipal authorities very often find the time to bend over the problem of caring for a disused Evangelical cemetery and, within their means, take action aimed at protecting such a place. In many cases our work became the efficient cause of actions that the municipality undertook after us. An example here may be the Krzymów municipality, with the wójt Danuta Mazur at its head, who joined in the work of the association while still a municipal councillor and has continued it steadily. But the Zagórów municipality and its representatives also have considerable merits in this field.
