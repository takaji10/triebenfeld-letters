# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 312
# translation by the editor's files

From the post-war years, that is from 1945, often up to the present day, the Hauländer cemeteries – or, to put it another way, the Evangelical cemeteries – were the target of attacks by the local population, the local authorities, and eventually plain hooliganism. It is not for us to judge the motives of human actions we do not understand, not for us to mete out punishment, so we usually do not wonder why it happened that way. What we do wonder is what we can do, and we set to work. For several years the two groups mentioned travelled across the territory of the former Konin voivodeship, explored Hauländer settlements, and found and discovered Evangelical cemeteries independently of each other. In the area of Koło and the Koło and Turek counties, the enthusiasts of the heritage of the Hauländers chiefly gathered around Urszula Karolczak, while in Konin and the Konin and Słupca counties it was the writer of these words – Damian Kruczkowski. We were not formal leaders of these groups. Rather it was that, knowing of our interests, people who in some way had at heart the preservation of the material traces of the former inhabitants of the lands on which we worked would turn to us for help, and often simply formed ad hoc groups together with us, which tidied up and restored successive cemeteries, thereby restoring the memory of the Evangelical inhabitants of a given locality, and thus restoring the cemetery to the local communities. These two centres of the fight for memory existed, as I have said, independently of each other for several years, trying with lesser or greater success to preserve as much as possible of the historical heritage of the region of the former Konin voivodeship.

Today probably none of us remembers exactly when these two groups met each other, but it is certain that together we worked through the whole of 2016, acting as an informal group of "grobersi," as we were somewhat ironically called. We worked entirely on a voluntary basis, as we continue to do, often buying the necessary equipment, fuel and materials with our own money.
