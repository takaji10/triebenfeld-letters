# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 42
# translation by the editor's files

In 1923 Aleksander Gross became pastor in Zagórów (he remained so until his death in 1945). In the same year the parish received one bell, in place of the previous one – requisitioned during the First World War by the occupation authorities. In the years 1926-1927 the organ was rebuilt. The clock for the church tower, for the sum of 5000 złoty, was in turn endowed in 1927 by the married couple Luiza and Ferdynand Krüger of Olchowo. In this way they wished to commemorate their son, who had gone missing during the war in 1920.

The parish was visited, among others, on 22 August 1877 by superintendent Teichmann of the Diocese of Kalisz, on 10 August 1884 by general superintendent Everth, on 10 May 1903 by general superintendent Manitius, on 23 May 1909 and on 6-7 July 1925 by bishop Juliusz Bursche. The church officials supervised and passed judgement on the expansion and the activity of the parish.

The construction of the parsonage was resumed in 1931, it was roofed over in 1934, and four years later the new building was solemnly consecrated. This was an important event for the whole community of the faithful of the Evangelical-Augsburg parish in Zagórów. Pastoral work in the parish concentrated above all in the cantorates, where the cantors led Bible meetings, choir rehearsals and rehearsals of the trombone ensemble. As a rule, once a month the pastor came to the cantorate to administer to the faithful the sacrament of communion. On the more important feast days, the parishioners from the cantorates made their way to the church in Zagórów.

On 1-11 September 1927, pastor Gross organised an evangelization in the parish, in which the following pastors took part: Leffler of Gębin, Paweł Otto of Łódź, and the evangelist Mark of Lwówek. In that year the choir was reactivated and a youth circle was founded. In October 1933 the solemn observance of the 50th anniversary of the consecration of the church took place. Apart from Zagórów, the largest Lutheran parish was the parish at Łazińsk, numbering before 1937 more than 150 Protestant owners of farms living there to the number of 115, plus 15 farmers from Huta Trąbczyńska, 10 from Trąbczyn B, 5 from Trąbczyn C and about 12 farmers from Trąbczyn D.
