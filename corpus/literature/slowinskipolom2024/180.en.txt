Bruno's letters of November come already from Finland. Addressed in Parkkino (the region of Petsamo) in the far north. The field post number 31 059 indicates that Bruno served in the 136th Mountain Ranger Regiment. He sends his sister the money saved from his pay as a present on the occasion of her approaching birthday. In the parcel there is also a jar, into which he put a few little bunches of berries found between the snow and the ice. On 8 November, when he writes these words, the 10th anniversary of his mother's death falls. He was at a Catholic field mass. "There, too, one can hear a worthwhile word". Later, as was his custom, he went into the forest to pray and to read the Gospel of St Matthew appointed for that Sunday: "For many are called, but few are chosen" (Mt 22,14)[^61]. These are great and grave words, which he passes on to his sister for another, new year of life.

A few days later he writes to his brother. He knows that thanks to his letters to the sisters and the father Gustav is up to date with the news from Finland, but he prefers it when they write to one another directly. Above all the news of the next deferral rejoices him. He considers that Gustav can do more for the community on the spot than as a recruit. That he is a teacher would have no significance at the beginning of service. Like Bruno he would be a "Barras" and treated accordingly. He himself recalls that time as very hard, and everyone must pass through it. He survived that hard time; now he already has the status of an older soldier, and his high education serves him well. "From the commander I received appropriate functions; above all I give lessons (twice a week) at the level of primary school and teach all those who are weak in the German language". He also runs a lending library (of 500! volumes).



[^61]: The Gospel according to St Matthew, quotation added – M.S.
