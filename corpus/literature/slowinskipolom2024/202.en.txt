# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 202
# translation by the editor's files

 speak up. The Matelskis' dwelling, abandoned today, and the aforementioned bread oven intrigue me more and more… Fear of the Poles' revenge accompanied Janina Breede for a long time yet. When Dr Sławek settled in Zagórów, he was assigned the house and surgery of the German doctor. Driven out of her own home, Breede took shelter for a time with her sister in Łódź. The sons left for Germany, where at first they hired themselves out to work in a carpentry shop. They worked hard; after a few years they managed to improve their living conditions and gain an education. But Breede did not go to the West. She stayed. Mrs Halina relates:

– She was so afraid of the Poles, of the people of Zagórów, that when they came to her and her sister in Łódź, fleeing, she jumped out of a first-floor window.

What, then, prompted her to return to the little town? I do not know. But she returned. Perhaps she had hopes of regaining the house, calling to mind the good relations with the Sławek family? She must have been bitterly disappointed, for the new occupants would not even let her into her former home. Cieślewiczowa recalls:

– Up there, in the attic, lived Dr Sławek's mother. I myself heard more than once how Breede called out: "Wanda, Wanda, come to me! For I cannot go in there, he will not let me in…".

Someone else told me that the desperate woman would sometimes climb onto the roof and cling to the chimney; she had to be taken off it by force. The more incidents of this kind there were, the more firmly the conviction began to seep into the consciousness of the inhabitants of Zagórów that the Doctor's wife had "gone off her head", yet few pitied her. Mad, then mad – said some – she had it coming… Mad? Was she really, though? Perhaps, at first, feigning madness secured her a measure of inviolability? Despised and pushed away by the majority, but with time increasingly regarded as a harmless madwoman, she slowly ceased to be associated with the dangerous "German woman".
