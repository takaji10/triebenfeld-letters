#### Kantorate schools and cantors' schools in the parish of Zagórów

At the outbreak of the Second World War, the Evangelical-Augsburg parish in Zagórów administered the following kantorate schools:

Olchowo: cantor and teacher from 1924, Ferdinand Homt.

Tarszewo: cantor Adolf Münch.

Michalinów, gmina Oleśnica: cantor Ludwig Schulz.

Michalinów, gmina Trąbczyn: cantor Paul Schulz (farmer).

Łazińsk: cantor and teacher Gustav Adolf Mielke, from 1925.

Anielewo: cantor and teacher Bruno Mielke.

Schools for children of the Evangelical confession operated in the following localities:

Zagórów, Łazińsk, Olchowo, Anielewo, Michalinów Oleśnicki, Michalinów Trąbczyński, Wrąbczynkowska Kolonia (today: Wrąbczynkowskie Holendry).

The information we possess about them is, unfortunately, incomplete and at the present time largely impossible to supplement.

The fundamental document on which I base my study is the book by E. Kneifel entitled *Die evangelisch-augsburgischen Gemeinden der Kalischer Diözese* [The Evangelical-Augsburg Parishes of the Kalisz Diocese] of 1937, which constitutes the most reliable and most extensive source of knowledge on the subject that interests us. An interesting source also proved to be the Chronicle of the Primary School on Łazińsk, written down by the school's first post-war headmaster, Józef Magryta, and the Chronicle of the Public School in Wrąbczynkowskie Holendry – notes kept by Olga Ritterówna, M. Wróblewski, Maria Gawrońska and Konrad Kryszak. The rest of the information comes from the recollections of former parishioners, former and present inhabitants of Zagórów and its surroundings. In the protocol of the visitation of the superintendent of the Kalisz diocese, Pastor Teichmann, of 22 August 1877, we find the information that under the administration of the parish of Zagórów there is one elementary school with some sixty children
