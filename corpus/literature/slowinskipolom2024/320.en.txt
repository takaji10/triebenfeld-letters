We always appear where our knowledge and experience can be helpful, where we can inspire and motivate work for the saving of memory. The Stowarzyszenie FRYDHOF also has two publications to its credit: the first, published in 2018 at its own expense, "We Restore the Memory of the Hauländers in the Warta Valley," and the second, published in 2019 with the participation of funds from the Fundacja Santander, "Hauländers around Konin". In 2017, in turn, FRYDHOF organised a promotional meeting for the diaries of Rabbi Jehoszua Mosze Aronson, "The Scroll of the House of Bondage," published by the Muzeum Okręgowe in Konin, a publication over which the late and much-missed dr Łucja Pawlicka-Nowak, who died in 2020, worked for years. The association does not focus solely on Evangelical cemeteries. We have already cooperated three times in tidying up the Jewish cemetery in Rychwał. In this action we assisted the initiators of the undertaking: the Towarzystwo Przyjaciół Ziemi Zagórowskiej, the Towarzystwo Drugie Pokolenie-Potomkowie Ocalałych z Holokaustu and the Gmina Żydowska w Poznaniu. In the book *Poniemieckie* by Karolina Kuszyk, published by Wydawnictwo Czarne in 2020 and gathering excellent reviews, one can find a mention of the work of our association. Although the object of our particular care is the Evangelical heritage, we also undertake actions at Catholic cemeteries. In 2019 we managed to restore the forgotten and crumbling tomb located at the cemetery in Konin-Morzysław, in which rest participants in the struggles for Poland's independence: the Wielkopolska insurgent Major Stanisław Zieliński, his wife, a Home Army courier, Genowefa Zielińska, and her brother-in-law Major Stanisław Bobola, a member of the Home Army, participant in the First and Second World War, decorated with the Cross of Merit and the order of Virtuti Militari.
