Tactical insignia of the 2. Gebirgs-Division.

Bruno writes on 30 May from Innsbruck that they are finishing their training in the Alps and the next day set out for Lapland. (In Innsbruck was stationed the 2nd Mountain Division – 2. Gebirgs-Division – formed on 1 April 1938, and with this formation the war fortunes of the soldier Mielke will be bound up). At the Sunday service many high-ranking officers appeared, and he was also allowed to invite a few *Kameraden* to the church. (Perhaps such ostentatious conduct on the part of the officer corps was connected with the fact that the 2nd Mountain Division was formed from the earlier Austrian 6th Division, in which there were not so many adherents of the new "Germanic religion"). From the newspapers he learns that larger combat operations on the Murmansk front can be expected only in the middle of June. He assumes that they will not go straight to the front line, but will certainly take the place of those who go to the first line. He thinks that the time of an important decision "on the matter of Russia" is approaching. The next fragment, worth recording in full, is a kind of testament which he sends to the family:

"Dear Brother, there is one thing I should like to suggest to you, as man to man: let us not deceive ourselves and let us look the truth in the eye. It is clear (for me and for you) that there now comes for me a stage more or less threatening to life. (That the English have attacked German ships and will go on doing so is probably certain).
