# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 218
# translation by the editor's files

Erich Kuhn, 2014

> *The father's home is a true paradise for the Heavenly Father.*  

> *Though you roamed the whole world, you would find no more beautiful one*…[^78]

#### Łazińsk – my family home…

I was born in Łazińsk in 1932. Nothing is left of our homestead on Łazińsk Drugi… Our house was made of iron ore. We had eight hectares of land. When my parents married, they received dowries from their parents. Before his wedding my father had also worked abroad. With the money they had saved they bought this land. My father, besides working on the farm, also did bricklaying and carpentry. He built houses and made furniture. That is mainly what we lived on. We had no horses, maybe two or three cows and some other animals… sheep, goats, poultry. Mama's name was Emma and Papa's was Adolf. Mama did all sorts of work in the house and on the farm, and she could also sew. She sewed everything for us. When I was five, I went to live with my father's mother, Loisa Kuhn née Fandrich, also in Łazińsk, not far from our house. My parents sent me there because there were rather a lot of us at home, nine children. They could not cope.

At Grandma's I had it very good. I was given sweets… I remember the Easter nests filled with sweets. At home things were not so rich. My older brothers worked far more in the field than I did. I, as the little one, gathered potatoes with them. We weeded the field together. Those were our basic tasks.



[^78]: This is a fragment of a song performed by the Zagórów choir "Quarta" during the annual reunion of members of the former Evangelical-Augsburg parish in Zagórów in the year 2014. To the meeting, combined with a solemn service, there came from Germany a large group of former Hauländers (settlers of Dutch law) originating from the Zagórów area, among them Erich Kuhn, who organized the group and led it to his family's homeland.
