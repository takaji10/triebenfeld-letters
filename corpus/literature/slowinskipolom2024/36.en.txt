In all, 3245 such settlers lived in the vicinity of Zagórów at that time.

The arriving settlers cleared the forest, turning it into arable land, founded villages and built chapels – houses of prayer – with a school room and a dwelling for the cantor. The oldest cantorate in our area arose at Łazińsk (a new house of prayer was built in 1838 for 300 persons); further cantorates were built later in Olchowo, Michalinów Trąbczyński, Michalinów Oleśnicki, Anielewo and Tarszewo. In 1849 there existed a cantorate at Tarszewo, lying at a distance of five versts from Zagórów, and it numbered 150 of the faithful.
