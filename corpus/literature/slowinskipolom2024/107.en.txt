All these buildings were maintained from the contributions of the faithful. During the First World War the cantors' school was transformed into a public elementary school for children of both confessions. In 1928 it was decided to build a new school building, and the former seat was converted into dwellings for the teachers. The German settlers were rather reluctant about this idea, wishing to preserve in the old building a separate one-class elementary public school with German as the language of instruction. They even submitted a petition to the school board, declaring categorically that until their application was considered favorably, they did not intend to send their children to the five-class public school[^45]. On 10 September 1931 the construction was completed and the new school building was solemnly consecrated. The five classes (8 divisions) were attended by 313 pupils, and they were taught by 4 teachers: Maria Gawrońska, Konrad Kryszak, Stanisław Trębaczkiewicz and J. Mikołajczykówna.
