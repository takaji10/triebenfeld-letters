We shall endeavour to name separately all the donors and sponsors thanks to whom the printing of the book will be possible.

This book is the result of a joint effort. Special thanks for the cooperation and the commitment go to all the authors. However, I regard it as my duty to single out in particular the contribution of Katarzyna Połom, Łukasz Parus, Damian Kruczkowski and Günter Hennings, as well as the contribution of the translators in the translations from the German language, and the commitment of Jacek Kin, who produced the greater part of the translations and made available a number of important materials. Without their outstanding achievement the publication could not have come into being.

> *Mirosław Słowiński*  
> *Translated by Jacek Kin*
