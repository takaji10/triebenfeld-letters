# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 245
# translation by the editor's files

Through all the years of the Evangelical Senior Home's existence, its residents took an active part, as far as possible, in meetings, cultural and parish events; they also visited the Zagórów Social Welfare Home and its inmates on the occasion of holidays, parties and artistic reviews. The closeness of the large market square, the park, the shops and the traditional Wednesday market made it easy for the residents of the senior home to go out for a walk, to do their shopping and to keep in constant contact with the inhabitants of Zagórów and with visitors. The Protestant seniors were also often visited, as witnessed by the numerous, cordial entries preserved in the chronicle. Constant care over the seniors was given by Rev. Andrzej Mendrok together with his wife Rozwita (usually called in the chronicle the 'Pastor's wife') and Mr and Mrs Erika and Artur Fandrich. On the first and third Sunday of each month the residents attended services held in the church standing a few steps from the senior home, which was a great convenience for elderly people. As the years passed, the oldest residents declined in health and departed – as it is beautifully put in the chronicle – to the Lord, and others came in their place. Among them were also young people, students of theology on diaconal placements, who appeared among the seniors and grew close to them. Among them was the catechist employed in the parish, Mrs Katarzyna Cieślak, today Rev. Katarzyna Kowalska, one of nine women priests in Poland[^84].

I had the pleasure of meeting Rev. Katarzyna Kowalska and her husband Rev. Eneasz Kowalski in person, during their stay in Zagórów. Today the two of them serve as clergy in a completely different part of Poland, but I managed to get in touch with the former resident of the EDS and ask about her recollections of the time when she lived in Zagórów (the period from 1 September 2006 to January 2008). We had a truly interesting conversation, full of positive energy, during which I learned many facts 


[^84]: On 16 October 2021, the Synod of the Evangelical-Augsburg Church in Poland voted by secret ballot in favour of introducing the ordination of women as priests. On 7 May 2022, Bishop Jerzy Samiec ordained nine deaconesses as priests in the Lutheran Church.
