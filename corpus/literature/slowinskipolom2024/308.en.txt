# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 308
# translation by the editor's files

> Reichsstelle für Familienzuwach  
> Reich Office for Family Increase  
> Hauptstelle Berlin  
> Head Office Berlin Berlin, 15.5.1943  
> Concerns: The fight against ruin (of the nation)  
> File reference I.A.4711
>
> Dear Sir!
>
> Since, as a result of the war, a certain number of men have fallen, there now rests upon those still living the duty of attending to the women and girls, so that the birth rate may be considerably raised.
>
> Henceforth you are deemed a person of particular productivity, and we resolve accordingly to confer upon you the honorary office of fulfilling the German duty. It is pointed out that your wife has no right to divorce on account of this duty of yours, since every woman must accept this small inconvenience as a consequence of the war. There is assigned to you a district, which will be specified more closely, comprising 9 women and 18 girls. If you should turn out to be incapable of performing the task assigned to you, you are then obliged to report this within 3 days, quoting the file reference mentioned above, and naming an efficient man as your deputy. If, however, you are able to take over, in addition to your district, one more district, you will then be appointed a reproduction officer and will receive, besides the reproduction premium, also the Order for Copulation, First Class, with red ribbon. You will receive the list of the ladies whom you are to visit shortly. Your blessed activity you will please begin at once, and the success, after 9 months, is to be reported here on a form which will be sent separately. In order to preserve your efficiency, the offices responsible for nutrition have been instructed to issue to the gentlemen concerned additional food cards for persons performing heavy work, and in the case of 2 districts, for persons performing very heavy work, and you are therefore asked to contact the nutrition office responsible for you.
>
> Reich Office for Population Increase  
> signed: Hüpfauf
