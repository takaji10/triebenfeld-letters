After the split of the Church in the sixteenth century as a result of the Reformation, the office of cantor continued to function in both confessions, with the functions of cantors in the Catholic Church gradually being taken over by organists.

#### Cantors in the Polish lands

In the Lutheran Church the role of this office took on ever greater significance in the socio-cultural and educational sphere, especially with regard to the migrating population. For at the beginning of the nineteenth century there came an intensified influx of German settlers into the territories of the Kingdom of Poland, and among the inhabitants of the newly founded colonies there was a clear need for spiritual leadership.

#### "Where the pastor cannot go, he will send the cantor"

Unfortunately, not everywhere and not in every circumstance could the pastor reach all the faithful awaiting him. This concerned rural areas in particular, and often the centers in which the pastor was to exercise his ministry lay several dozen kilometers apart. Covering such a route on foot, by bicycle or even by horse-drawn cart was certainly not among the easiest of tasks; it cost a good deal of time, and in bad weather conditions it was no small feat.
