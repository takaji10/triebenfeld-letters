Should we succeed in preserving and telling even a small fragment of this two-hundred-year history, it may perhaps be easier to understand it. That the work we undertook proved necessary to the community of the town of Zagórów is borne witness to by the numerous, rather unusual events that accompanied us.

A notice appeared on the website of the Cultural Centre in Zagórów asking people to make information, or documents, photographs, testimonies and certificates in their possession, accessible to the editorial committee. This appeal met with a quick response, and very interesting source materials were made available to the Cultural Centre. The Gesellschaft der Freunde der Gemeinde Zagórów announced a collection among internet users in order to support the project financially.

A completely exceptional event, by contrast, was the concert of the musician Daniel Słowiński and his friends, organised by Pastor Waldemar Wunsz on 23 September 2023 in the Evangelical church in Zagórów, which was combined with a collection for the publication of the book. The church was well attended by listeners, who after the concert gave the artists and the organiser an enormous round of applause. The money collected by the Gesellschaft and during the concert supplements the funds designated for this purpose by the mayor of the town of Zagórów, Roman Kultermann.

Erich Kuhn, too, continued to show great interest in our work, and he likewise supported us financially. We highly value the fact that Dr. Zbigniew Chodyła, a merited researcher and connoisseur of the history of the German settlers (the so-called Hauländers) in Wielkopolska, stood by our side with scholarly advice in the realisation of our publication.
