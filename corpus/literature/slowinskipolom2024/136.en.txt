# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 136
# translation by the editor's files

It is the end of 1944. As the east wind blows, one can hear the approaching front, the thunder of artillery salvoes. Despite the assurances coming from Berlin radio, it was known that the entry of the Russians was a matter of weeks. We do not know which member of the Mielke family was at home at that time (from various indirect pieces of information one may conjecture that it was one of the elder sisters, Leokadia). Preparing the cache in the gap between the walls required time, technical skill and ingenuity. The hiding place would surely never have been discovered had it not been for the decision to replace the doors. Most probably the objects being hidden were lowered in from above, from the attic, and then the whole gap was carefully plugged.

The packed collection consisted to a great extent of letters from the front written by the younger son of Julius Samuel Mielke – Bruno. But there were also letters and cards written to Bruno by the father, the brother Gustav, the sisters Leokadia (Kadzia), Anni and Olga. Besides this, many loose letters (63 in all), 43 postcards and 18 documents from the years 1936-1939, such as, for example, the permit of the Konin starosta of 26 September 1939 (!) for Gustav Adolf Mielke to move freely around Konin county. From the reading one may infer that the siblings exchanged photographs, including those from the front. None of the photographs mentioned was in the cache. More interesting is the question of how the letters of the father and the siblings sent to the Eastern Front came to be among these documents? Did Bruno leave them in Łazińsk while on leave? The surviving Book of Baptisms (*Taufe*) covers the period from 1912 to 1918 in Łazińsk. It was kept by the then cantor E. Cellmer.

The collection of books whose preservation was attempted may be helpful in describing the family that lived in the kantorate. The titles alone say a great deal. *Biographie Joseph Goebbels* by Wilfrid Bade, Lübeck 1938, *Die Nationalversammlung von Potsdam. Deutschlands große Tage. 21 bis 23 März 1933* by Hans Wendt, Berlin 1933 [The National Assembly in Potsdam. Germany's Great Days], *Deutsche Geschichte im 19. Jahrhundert* by Heinrich von Treitschke with an introduction by Alfred Rosenberg, Safari – Berlin 1937 [A History of Germany in the Nineteenth Century], *Der deutsche Aufbruch 9.11.1918*
