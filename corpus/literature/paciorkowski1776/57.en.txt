The morrow arriving, namely the Tuesday after the feast of [name] in the present year, his jurisdiction having been reassumed and proclaimed by the court messenger of the Kingdom; in proceeding, the resolution of the corner point and the particular corner point being suspended, the court proceeded to the review of the boundary paths of both parties, and indeed first of the originally plaintiff's party, the Right Honourable [names], heir of the estates of the village of [name]. The Right Honourable [names], heir of the estates of the village of [name], ^[*The plaintiff commenced his boundary path.*] therefore began and commenced his boundary path from the convergence of three estates and the place of the founded jurisdiction, proceeding from the quarter of the winter west (or likewise the summer west may be written, as may be the case) toward the quarter of the summer east, for half a furlong only to the small river called [name]; which small river he took as the natural boundary, and proceeding along that same small river for more or less ten furlongs, using it as the natural boundary, he reached the upper end of the mere belonging to the estates of the village of [name], hereditary property of the Honourable [names], cited party; and taking the midpoint of that same mere as his boundary wall, he led the court to the water sluice, commonly called *upust* [T.N. 1]; alleging that one half of that same mere had from antiquity belonged, and ought at present to belong, to his estate of the village of [name].
