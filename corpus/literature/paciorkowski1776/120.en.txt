# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 120
# translation by Claude

### *What has once been decreed by the commissioners in the delimitation of estates shall not be renewed.*

^[*Herburt, ibid.*] In order that what has once been decreed by Our commissioners (which We grant with respect to the delimitation of Our royal and noble estates) shall no longer be renewed, We enact: that after the delimitation of Our royal and noble estates, Our Captains shall do no injury in the plots of the nobility; and Our subjects shall have no usufruct in the delimited plots, neither bee-keeping nor any other advantage.

### *Whatever the commissioners shall have done shall stand. No reviewers shall be appointed.*

^[*Herburt, fol. 97.*] We shall appoint Our commissioners to make the boundaries; and their judgment We shall not obstruct by Our inhibitions; We shall designate neither assessors nor commissioners, nor a reviewer, after the boundaries have been made. But whatever Our Commissioners ^[*Commissioners shall make the boundaries between the royal estates notwithstanding the contumacy and opposition of the possessor.*] shall have decreed, that shall be ratified and firm. Moreover, when Our commissioners convene at the place of the boundaries — whether Our Captain or leaseholder has gone out or not, whether he has produced the elderly witnesses or not — the commissioners themselves shall nonetheless pursue and conclude those same boundaries: the opposition and likewise the absence of Our Captains, and of those who seek remedy on account of injuries to the boundaries by way of motion and appeal,
