Likewise, with respect to all injuries arising from the plots and estates mentioned: they shall be required to respond to the injured party as is appropriate in the land court and municipal court; and likewise when by way of appeals and remissions such cases shall fall to the Crown Tribunal, they shall be adjudicated by all the secular judges and by six ecclesiastical persons delegated to all cases by the ecclesiastical estate according to the constitution, from the side of the hereditary estates. However, if an ecclesiastical person should be found among the co-parties in such a case: such cases shall be adjudicated by the secular court alone. Likewise cases concerning such estates as began to be adjudicated *cum Personis sæcularibus* and thereafter *lite pendente* fell to ecclesiastical persons, whether by succession or upon the acquisition of a right — the secular court shall adjudicate in the manner described below, *ex regestro causarum sæcularium, fine rejectione*: excepting those estates which have been or shall be attached to the Church *per commutationem* or by the sale of ecclesiastical estates, which are subject to the ancient prerogatives of the Church. The *onera publica æquivalentia* remain upon the commuted and sold estates. Other cases — both ecclesiastical and secular — *in sæculari* shall be conducted according to the constitution of 1578.
