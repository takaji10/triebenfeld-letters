# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 90
# translation by Claude

^[*Royal mandate to the Right Honourable Captain.*] The series of the Royal Mandate of His Sacred Royal Majesty given to the same Right Honourable Captain is as follows: AUGUSTUS THE THIRD, by the Grace of God King of Poland, Grand Duke of Lithuania, Ruthenia, Prussia, Masovia, Samogitia, Kiev, Volhynia, Podolia, Podlachia, Livonia, Smolensk, Severia, and Chernigov, and likewise Hereditary Duke of Saxony, Prince and Elector.

To the Right Honourable or Honourable [names] [names], Captain or leaseholder of Our estates, sincerely and faithfully beloved by Us. We have designated certain of Our commissioners to make and establish boundaries between Our royal estates of the town or village of [name] of the leaseholding of Your Fidelity on the one part, and the hereditary estates of the village of [name] of the Honourable or Noble [name] on the other part. Wherefore We require Your Fidelity, that as soon as it shall have been summoned by the summons letters of the aforesaid Our commissioners, it appear before them at the aforesaid place of the differences, be present at the delimitation of the estates, and keep and observe firmly whatever they shall decree in that matter. Let it take care, however, that no detriment be done to Our royal estates by that delimitation. For Our grace. *Given* etc. The service return of which mandate, acknowledged for service in the estates of [name] before the municipal records of [name] on the [day] of [name] in the year of the Lord [names], by the court messenger of the Kingdom, the Honest [names] of the village of [name], together with two noblemen [names].
