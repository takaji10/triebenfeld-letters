### *On the Authority of Officials at a Site Inspection in Masovia.*

^[*Masovian Exceptions 1576, fol. 937, vol. 2.*] When a Boundary Chamberlain or court messenger fixes a certain day for the parties for the conduct of a site inspection at the estate in accordance with the summons, and the fixing of that day has been recorded in the land or municipal records — if any party should not attend at that appointed day of the named inspection, nor appear before that official at the place and session of the inspection: such a party shall, from such a removal of that aforesaid official made before the court at the next sitting, be condemned and adjudged to the loss of the matter concerning which that summons was brought and the inspection was to take place. And if the official himself should fail to come on that appointed and recorded day for the conduct of that inspection, *intercedente legali impedimento*: then another day for the conduct of that inspection, the same Sub-Chamberlain's office shall fix at the next land sittings and shall be required to come to it — since no one should or may lose through the office.
