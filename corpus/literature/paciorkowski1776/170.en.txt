# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 170
# translation by Claude

from a final sentence, or in a case not admitted, by grievance, shall then go to the Tribunal. Which Tribunal, if from any legitimate considerations it should not be able to approve the Sub-Chamberlain's decree at the table, shall then refer the matter to a second field hearing on the plot, and shall designate another Sub-Chamberlain and not a Boundary Chamberlain; and if at such a second field hearing conducted on the plot the first boundary decree should be approved, then that decision shall *transire in rem plene iudicatam*; and We will that the party wishing to disturb such decrees be punished *pænis convulsionis Decretorum*.

Cases not having *delineationem Mappæ & exequitationem Ductuum*, even if begun before the Tribunal, shall be adjudicated first and foremost before the land court *cum decernenda Condescensione ad Campum*. The party raising a boundary question concerning a plot ^[*Acts of violence are prohibited in disputed places under penalties.*] possessed by a neighbour shall in no way violently take possession of the plots or forests claimed, nor commit any damage, *sub pæna siedzenia Wieży czterech Niedziel & mille marcarum ex Regestro Terminorum Tactorum vindicandarum & responsione* for such damage and violence, *etiam ante decisionem Causæ Granicialis extendendæ*. And the party possessing the plot or forest in dispute shall upon its adjudication to the other party be required to surrender it *una cum calculo seu bonificatione*.
