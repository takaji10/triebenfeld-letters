If the convent wins the boundary path, then the Prior of that convent shall be required to confirm it with six noble witnesses, and not with religious.

By the same method, if a delimitation and confirmation of the boundary path of abbatial estates follows, if the abbot together with his convent wins the boundary path.

### *Estates Situated on the Borders of the Kingdom.*

If a noble heir of terrestrial estates is situated in the vicinity of royal estates and suffers injury from the leaseholder of those estates, and the boundary wall of both estates, terrestrial and royal alike, meets the boundaries of a foreign province, and he wishes to delimit his estates from the royal estates and from the foreign province, then the nobleman shall be obliged first to procure commissioners for the delimitation of those estates at the General Diet of the Kingdom, so that commissioners may be appointed by constitution, and thereafter from the royal court of that province the Commonwealth of the Kingdom must obtain commissioners for the purpose of attempting those boundaries.

### *Furthermore, What is the Corner Point and the Particular Corner Point? They are to be understood as follows.*

The corner point belongs to that estate which takes for itself the beginning of the boundary wall, and the collateral angle. The particular corner point, however, ^[*At the convergence of 3 or 4 estates.*] and the corner mound of the particular kind is so called for that estate, because it determines the boundary wall
