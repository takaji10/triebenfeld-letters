He wills and declares those boundaries to have the force of perpetual validity, with the judicial declaration that whatever lies to the right of the plots, fields, meadows, and forests toward the west shall belong entirely to the estate and village of [name]; and whatever lies to the left shall belong entirely to the estate of the village of [name], so he declares. Both parties shall, however, have free and common pasture in the forests and barren plots for all time, without damage to the sown fields and meadows, as they were from antiquity.
