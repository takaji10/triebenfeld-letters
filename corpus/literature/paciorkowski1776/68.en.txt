(Which having been read, and weighed and examined in all their circumstances with the mature deliberation of his court, and the documents of the parties having been reviewed, favouring no party, having only GOD and justice before his eyes; both parties' boundary paths having been rejected as containing the greatest obliqueness, confirmed neither by documentary evidence nor by real mounds or boundary markers found in the boundary path — he directs the boundary path to the parties from the thing presented to the eyes, at the arbitration of his court, so that perjuries may not occur; and the court proceeded to the erection of corner mounds, or the renewal of two of them, and the new erection of the third, at the application of the collateral party. (The circumscription of the mounds erected shall be written from the foregoing act, yet without the confirmation, since no party has won the boundary path.)
