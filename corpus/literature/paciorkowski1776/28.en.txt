# translation by Claude

the same convergence elsewhere, and the true mounds situated according to the boundary act of the year [name]. ^[*The plaintiff counter-protests against both, as well as regarding collusion.*] The Honourable [names], principal plaintiff, counter-protesting against both — both the Honourable [names], principal respondent, and the Honourable [names], collaterally summoned — because they, having entered into collusion with one another, proceeding to the avulsion of the hereditary estate of the plaintiff who protests, wrongfully deny that same corner point and the true and legitimate mounds demonstrated to the court by the plaintiff himself who protests (which he offers to prove by documentary evidence in the further course of the case). Having noted the protests of the parties, the Sub-Chamberlain's court orders the parties to register their appearance. The parties having appeared — the plaintiff's party with the Honourable [names], heir of the estates of the village of [name], in person; the cited party with the Honourable [names], heir of the estates of the village of [name], likewise in person; the Honourable [names], heir of the estates of the village of [name], collaterally summoned, likewise in person — having appeared before his court and contested the matter judicially between themselves; the contested submissions having been heard, he finds them bound to satisfy the decree of the land court in the matter of the summons of both the cited party and the collateral heir for the present session to the field.
