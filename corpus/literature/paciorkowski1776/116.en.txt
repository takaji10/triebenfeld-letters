# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 116
# translation by Claude

### *On Royal Commissions and Commissioners, for the Delimitation of Royal and Terrestrial Estates.*

Formerly two dignitaries and a Sub-Chamberlain were given for commissions on boundaries.

### *But henceforth five commissioners shall be appointed.*

^[*Herburt, fol. 95.*] Likewise, since injuries and oppressions, and sometimes even acts of killing, were committed between Our registered and unregistered estates and those of Our neighbouring subjects; and since many difficulties arose in the delimitation of the boundaries, with Our Captains or leaseholders oppressing Our neighbouring subjects under cover of Our royal name and Our estates, or obstructing the delimitation of the boundaries — therefore, from the virtue and royal benevolence innate in Us, for the sake of all justice and peace for Our subjects in this matter, We promise: that to all Our subjects of whatever rank and condition, seeking boundaries from the side of Our estates, We and Our successors, according to the ancient statutes, for the discernment of equity, shall appoint and shall cause to be appointed the commissioners of that land and district in which the estates to be delimited are situated,
