which We approve by the present constitution — therefore, providing in this for the needs of the noble estate of the Palatinate of Sandomierz, since access to the land records is not always open, We order that all boundary acts and divisions in general from each district shall be separately extracted *specificè in unum Volumen* and thereafter all together from all the districts delivered to the records of the Honourable Sub-Chamberlain of Sandomierz as boundary records, and kept in proper order by the said Sub-Chamberlain's notary together with the others. And so that those boundary acts and divisions from each district may be properly extracted and delivered to the Sub-Chamberlain's office: the Honourable Land Notary of the Palatinate of Sandomierz shall be required to see to this with diligence *sub quadrupla pæna contra negligentes Officiales sancita & sub amissione Officij sui*. And in the future all acts of perpetuities, as mentioned above, shall no longer be submitted to the land or municipal records, but to the Sub-Chamberlain's records of that palatinate.
