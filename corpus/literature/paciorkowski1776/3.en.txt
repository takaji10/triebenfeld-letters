### *The Original Terrestrial Process.*

First, when the plaintiff wishes to delimit his estate with a neighbouring estate, and to resolve the differences that have arisen in respect of the boundaries, he is obliged, in the legal form described below, to summon by formal citation to the land court both the principal party — from whose side and estate the plaintiff seeks the separation and delimitation of his own estates — and also the collateral heirs: the one as the starting point of the boundary path, as the point from which it begins, and the other as the determining point of the boundary path, as the end point, that is, the party whose boundary wall the boundaries are directed toward.
