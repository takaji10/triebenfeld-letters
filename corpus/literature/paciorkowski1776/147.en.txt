### *On the Sub-Chamberlain in Masovia.*

^[*Masovian Exceptions, Constitution 1576, fol. 947, vol. 2.*] The land Sub-Chamberlain, according to the ancient custom observed in the Palatinate of Masovia, shall himself and through his Boundary Chamberlain conduct the site inspection and how it shall be conducted, and similarly the cases concerning boundaries, and shall bring them before the land court. And so that the contests which the parties shall raise before him or his Boundary Chamberlain may also be raised and presented before the court in his presence, and likewise the title deeds that the parties have between themselves be shown. And the court, according to the ancient custom and his testimony, and from the contests of the parties and their presentations or title deeds, as it shall understand according to the common law — shall have the power to adjudge both the right to proceed and the proof. And this has been preserved from of old, because the court shall make a legal decree from the contest, and the land notary shall write down the contest sufficiently; the common law therefore has not and could not permit the Sub-Chamberlain or his Boundary Chamberlain to have records. Appeal, however, shall be free to the party according to the ancient custom.
