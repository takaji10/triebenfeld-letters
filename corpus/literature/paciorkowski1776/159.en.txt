# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 159
# translation by Claude

Land Deputies — all condemnations, and any courts and *actus quosvis Juridicos* obtained during the present and future Sejms up to the relation sitting, both before the Tribunal and *in quibusvis subselliis in præjudicium & aggravationem* of both the Right Honourable Senators and the Honourable Land Deputies and their spouses, *etiam in causis dotis & inscriptionum* — We annul and destroy, wishing that the tribunals of all courts shall in everything conform to this law. That same security shall also cover the Inspectors of Our estates.
