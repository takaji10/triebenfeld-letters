# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 158
# translation by Claude

### *Security of the Lord Senators and Land Deputies.*

^[*Constitution 1659, fol. 591, vol. 4.*] Reassuming in full the constitutions of the most recent Sejms concerning the security of the lord senators, Crown officials, commissioners, deputies to foreign lords, and land deputies, and their wards — by the authority of the present convention We declare that all decrees and condemnations that shall have stood against any of the lord senators and land deputies elected to the Sejm, whether by contest or in contumacy, at whatever election sitting, in whatever court of the greater or lesser tribunal, and that shall be standing until the relation sittings — all such decrees and condemnations We annul. After the relation sitting, however, within two weeks each of the lord senators and deputies shall be required to conduct himself *in quocunque Judicio* at the instance of any person. And so that there may be no doubt about this in the future, by the authority of the present convention We declare this law to be a perpetual law. Which shall also serve for deputies to foreign lords, residents, or commissioners *post expeditam functionem*.
