The following day arriving, namely the [day] of [names], the sworn inquiries of the parties — both principal acting parties regarding both the corner point and the entire general boundary path, and that of the collateral heir regarding his particular corner point alone and the convergence of three estates at the place where he had acknowledged it to the originally cited party — having been concluded. The Sub-Chamberlain's court, after the completion of those same sworn inquiries and the introduction of the matter, descended to the reading thereof; which having been read and weighed and examined in all their circumstances — since the court finds, both from those same sworn inquiries and from the documentary evidence of the plaintiff's party, namely the boundaries of the year [name] drawn from the Sub-Chamberlain's records, that the same corner point demonstrated by the plaintiff's party, beside the paved public road running from the city of [name] to the town of [name], is true and legitimate; adjudging therefore that same place for the erection of corner mounds and the particular corner mound for the Honourable [names], collaterally summoned, and the more probable boundary path of the plaintiff's party, as supported by mounds and boundary markers found therein; conducted in a straight line from the north, from the starting point, through the intermediate points, to the end point situated toward the southern quarter, judicially acknowledged by the Honourable [names], collaterally summoned, and agreeing with the cited party in the same.
