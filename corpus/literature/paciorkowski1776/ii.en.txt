# PREFACE

### *To the Benevolent Reader.*

Among the various branches of knowledge that appear most necessary for common human use, I have judged jurisprudence to be of the greatest utility, in particular in our Kingdom of Poland; and since, in order to acquire a thorough command of it, a great many volumes of laws must be studied, I thought it worthwhile to present to you, Benevolent Reader, this work which I have compiled in summary form over many years with great labour. I wished to call it the *Rule of the Field Boundary Process*, not because I might wish to instruct in this matter the Illustrious Right Honourable Sub-Chamberlains (*Succamerarii*), chosen from among thousands and proven and approved in all branches of learning, nor their Right Honourable Boundary Chamberlains, since I am more than fully aware that they have a perfect command of both the laws and the limits of estates — but so that the studious youth of jurisprudence might have a readier means by which to arrive more quickly at a mastery of this branch of knowledge. For one can plainly see that our young men spend several years in academies of jurisprudence, yet return to their own homes with little or no competence in this kind of knowledge, and involve themselves in the clamour of the law — not without great inconvenience to the judges — in the land courts, municipal courts, and Tribunal courts alike.
