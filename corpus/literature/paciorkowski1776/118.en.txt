# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 118
# translation by Claude

We shall designate commissioners for the making of those same boundaries; none, however, other than those who shall have possessions in those lands where the boundaries are to be made.

### *The Captains shall defend the royal estates until the delimitation.*

^[*Herburt, fol. 49.*] We enact: that henceforth the Prefects of Our castles and cities shall not obtain commissions for the fixing of boundaries, but noblemen alone; ^[*A nobleman who obstructs a commission shall be liable.*] yet so that nevertheless it shall be lawful for Our Prefects or Captains (as has been received by ancient custom and is provided by statute) to defend Our estates until the declaration of the boundaries.

### *The Captain or leaseholder of the royal estates shall go out to the delimitation of the estates, and shall provide for the commissioners if he holds free estates.*

^[*Herburt, fol. 96.*] We shall command Our Captain or leaseholder of Our estates to go out and descend together with those same commissioners to delimit those same estates, and to provide for the commissioners at his expense — in particular with respect to those estates which We hold free and without encumbrance.

### *The session of the commission for delimiting the estates: two weeks.*
