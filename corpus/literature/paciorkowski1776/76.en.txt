# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 76
# translation by Claude

the same Very Reverend Parish Priest, in satisfaction of the decree of his court, swore a bodily oath before the image of the Crucified Lord, the solemnities of oaths being observed, together with six noble witnesses [names], in the formula described above, before the face of his court, with the party present — he therefore wills and declares that those same boundaries shall have the force of perpetual validity; which boundaries let no one dare to violate or annihilate, under a penalty of two thousand Polish marks to be decreed by the competent court, by virtue of the present decree.

It is to be noted here: if the cited party had interposed a motion against the swearing of the oath, it must be admitted as at the first session of the field hearing, from the decree of the land court as the court of first instance, to the Crown Tribunal court; and a delineation of a map shall be made by the Sub-Chamberlain's office, with a true and real representation of those same estates, and of the collateral estates and the boundary paths of both parties, and of the mounds raised — both corner and wall mounds — so that the Tribunal court may be able to cognise the straighter and more probable boundary path and subscribe to it; faith shall be given to it in that same court, if the delineation of that same map is made by a sworn person. This falls not only to the surveyor but is also the duty of the Sub-Chamberlain's office; they must have experience in this.
