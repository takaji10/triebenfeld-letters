And the Sub-Chamberlains, standard-bearers, military judges, and that office shall, for the effective conclusion of such decrees, send their sufficient sentences, with the sufficient contests and presentations explained, to the Tribunal — for the Poles in Polish writing, and for the Volhynians in Ruthenian writing. For which such session the parties shall be required to appear in an equal retinue — that is, of thirty horses only, counting among them servants and friends, without any firearms, only with customary weapons — that is, with sabre, sword, cutlass, and rapier — under a fine of five hundred marks: for which the respondent shall give account and shall have a complicated session before the Tribunal among the official cases. And where there should be an adjacency of an estate of His Royal Majesty leased by anyone: then by the customary method, according to the law, such differences shall be delimited by commissioners.
