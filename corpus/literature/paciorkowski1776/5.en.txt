# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 5
# translation by Claude

### *First Uniform Citation against Both Collateral Heirs.*

The royal title is written from the above, down to the words: and appear peremptorily, at the instance of [name], plaintiff, who cites or rather summons you, and this for the following reason: That you demonstrate your corner point or your own particular corner point, meeting the plaintiff's boundary wall, who wishes to distinguish and delimit his estates and inheritance from the estates of [name], and that you attempt the same delimitation. Be therefore present at the session to comply, and to respond judicially to the foregoing matters. *Given etc. etc.*

Upon these first citations, issued both against the principal party and against the collateral heirs, a penalty of contumacy must be obtained in the land court. Or, with the consent of the parties, the matter can be done even at the first session (for no injury is done to one who consents): a referral to the field to the Right Honourable Sub-Chamberlain for the making of the boundaries.

### *Second Land Court Citation against the Principal Party and Collateral Heirs.*

The above is written, with this addition: who cites you for the second time, after the penalty of contumacy has been obtained. *Summons etc. etc.*
