### *The commissioners, having received an oath from the elderly witnesses on both sides, shall make the delimitation.*

^[*Herburt, ibid.*] Which commissioners, after they have gone out, must summon the elderly witnesses from both sides; from whom, having received an oath, they shall proceed to the delimitation according to their recognition and testimony. If, however, those same elderly witnesses are in disagreement, then the commissioners according to their consciences, reconciling the disagreements of the elderly witnesses according to their own private judgment, shall pursue and conclude the delimitation itself. Where, moreover, the commissioners themselves should disagree, We will that the defined authority and power to conclude shall rest with the sentence of those who are in agreement by a majority of voices — the opposition of Our officials, namely the Captain or leaseholder, notwithstanding.
