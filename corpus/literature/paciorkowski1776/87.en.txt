# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 87
# translation by Claude

of [name] and the villages belonging to it, leased to the Right Honourable [names], Palatine of [name], [names], Our Captain, on the one part; and the hereditary estates of the village of [name] of the Honourable [names], on the other part. Which matter We, desiring it to be settled and composed, have thought it fitting to commit to your Sincerity and Fidelity, in whose good faith, integrity, and skill in conducting affairs We have confidence and knowledge; as We commit it by these presents. Enjoining and commanding the same, that by mutually consulting together on a time and day suitable for that purpose, and fixing it among themselves, and having summoned the parties whose interest it concerns by their summons letters to their presence, they convene at the place of the differences; and there, having heard and listened to the submissions and responses of those same parties, and received the sufficient testimony of elderly witnesses of good repute, by means of their bodily oath if necessary, and having fully cognised and examined the thing itself presented to the eyes and the position and truth of that place — favouring neither party, but having only GOD and His justice before their eyes — let them delimit and distinguish the said estates, and mark them with mounds and metal markers, in the manner customary in such matters; being diligent, however, to provide that by this delimitation Our royal estates be in no way diminished.
