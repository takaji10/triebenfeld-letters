In the prosecution of the case, he decrees both parties bound, before all else, to satisfy that same Tribunal decree in the matter of the production of documents pertaining to the present case; decreeing that those same parties shall both produce before his court all documents in their possession, with a prior summary thereof drawn up, and that each party shall communicate to the other and confirm the real production thereof, immediately, under penalty of loss of the case. And since both parties have produced those same documents and present themselves for the sworn confirmation of the real production thereof, those same parties being found admissible, the court messenger is added for the pronouncing of the oath formula; and since both parties, before the face of his court, before the image of the Crucified Lord Jesus Christ, with the court messenger reading the formula to them in that oath formula — that all the documents pertaining to the present case deposited in court they have truly produced, and nothing to the damage and prejudice of the party have they retained by them nor concealed, so help them GOD and the Holy Passion of JESUS Christ — swore; (but if the parties remit the oath to one another, which is lawful for the parties, then it shall be written:) and since the parties have remitted this oath of real production to one another, he therefore finds that the decree of his court has been satisfied, and
