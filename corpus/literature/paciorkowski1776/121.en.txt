### *On commissioners unjustly cited, and on other courts.*

^[*Herburt, ibid.*] Whoever should cite the commissioners, or any court or office, on account of any process — and such person shall be determined to have cited them unjustly — shall be required to give twenty-eight marks to each separately as a penalty. Likewise to the party, if the same should be cited.

### *Letters of the commissioners issued for the delimitation need no confirmation.*

^[*Herburt, fol. 271.*] Induced by the petitions of Our subjects, We permit: that it shall be in the discretion of Our subjects to petition and obtain from Us a confirmation of the letters of boundaries issued by five commissioners ^[*A completed commissarial act requires no approval.*] deputed by Us according to the tenor of the statute. Which letters, in which Our commission and mandate shall have been inserted and the seals of the commissioners affixed, shall obtain authority and perpetual force, even if no confirmation of Ours intervenes thereupon.
