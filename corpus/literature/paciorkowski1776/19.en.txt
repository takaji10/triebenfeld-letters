and specifically and expressly entered into the records, so that in future the circumscription and delineation contained in the documents may correspond to the signs that have been marked, and so that the heirs may also, all the more easily, as the marks fade with age over fifty years — in the course of which especially the renewal of boundaries and investigation of boundaries is accustomed to be made — restore those same boundaries by mutual consent in a shorter time, after the lapse of any decade, in the presence of the Sub-Chamberlain's office, and renew the mounds broken down, trodden flat, and reduced to nothing, after the manner of the prior act. And any heir of estates, if he observes this, will leave peaceful tranquillity to himself and his successors. And first, because boundary paths are concluded in various ways — from the limits and starting point to the limits and end point — the following are to be generally observed: ^[*What is to be observed in all matters in a boundary case.*] east, west, south, north, or the eastern, western, southern, northern (also called the boreal and aquiline) quarter; for one proceeds, or is led, from the east, summer or winter, toward the west, from the south toward the north, and conversely, as shall seem good and pleasing first to the plaintiff as leading party; or also between the solar quarters, the southern and western, eastern and northern, or conversely; therefore from where the beginning commences and in what direction the steps are taken must be specifically noted.
