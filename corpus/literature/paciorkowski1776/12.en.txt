When the accessory matters have thus been resolved, after the registered appearance, the court shall proceed, by its sentence proclaimed to the parties, to the review of the boundary path, first of the plaintiff's party and then of the respondent's party; and it shall consider carefully and diligently the places and positions, the mounds if they are credible, and the boundary markers demonstrated by the parties from the starting point, through the intermediate points, to the end point, and proved by the documents deposited in the boundary path, noting faithfully from which quarter of the sun it begins and toward which it is directed, whether in a straight line or not, or obliquely, setting aside partiality, together with the protests and counter-protests declared by the parties, and shall inscribe these into the decree; and it shall likewise inscribe the documents deposited by the parties in the court, with the act and the day of session.

After the review of the boundary paths, before all else, regarding the corner point, if it is invisible and confirmed by no documents, ^[*The corner point and the particular corner point are established first.*] the court shall conduct sworn inquiries, and each party — not only the principal acting parties but also each collateral heir for its own part — shall call witnesses; when these have been completed, the court shall proceed to the other particular corner point and perform the like, and shall thereafter descend to the reading of the sworn inquiries and shall decide finally.
