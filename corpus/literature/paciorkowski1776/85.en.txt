Finally, ^[*Between royal and terrestrial estates no amicable settlement is granted.*] whereas differences occasioned by the boundaries and limits between estates on both sides terrestrial may be resolved and settled by amicable composition, those between royal and terrestrial estates cannot be concluded and resolved by the same method and by amicable means, for the reason that they are estates of the Commonwealth, which cannot, without the consent of all the orders of the Kingdom, be alienated, nor in any part diminished, severed, or made to suffer detriment.
