# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 31
# translation by Claude

heir of the estates of the village of [name], through the Honourable [names], his attorney registered in the municipal records of [name] on the [day] of [name] in the present year, appearing before his court and judicially contesting the matter between themselves; the contested submissions having been heard, at the application of the collaterally summoned party, the Honourable [names], or his attorney — since a boundary case cannot be decided without the judicial acknowledgement of the collateral heir, nor can a corner point be established — therefore adhering both to the statute of the Kingdom and to the constitution of the year 1611 enacted on postponements, grants and concedes the principal postponement of eight weeks, in which session the party shall be required to prove, by a document or attestation of the Illustrious Right Honourable Marshal of the Crown Tribunal, under penalty of loss of the case. With the Honourable [names], originally cited, he suspends the present case for judgment until that same session of eight weeks, preserving for the parties the peremptory session of appearance by virtue of the present decree.
