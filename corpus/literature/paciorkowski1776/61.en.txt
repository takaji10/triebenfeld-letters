The following day arriving, namely the [day] of [name] in the present year, the same sworn inquiries having been concluded, he orders the parties in the present case to respond directly. In the direct response, he descended to the reading of the sworn inquiries drawn out by all parties — both the principal acting parties and the collateral heir, the Honourable [names] — confirmed on oath and deposited in his court. Which having been read and weighed and examined in all their circumstances, ^[*Decree of adjudication of the boundary path.*] resolving the suspension of the corner point made above: although the collaterally summoned party acknowledged judicially the corner point or particular corner point, as being at the convergence of three estates, near the boggy place called *Biały Łąg* and beside the paved road running from the town of [name] to the village of [name] — yet since the cited party who is also plaintiff proves that same corner point, and the entire boundary path taken along that same public road, by no authentic documents and no sworn inquiries at the present session, the court therefore, the entire boundary path of the cited party having been rejected, and the more probable boundary path of the Right Honourable [names], plaintiff, having been found, assigns that same small river or torrent as the perpetual boundary and boundary wall to those same estates; and the court proceeded to the erection of corner mounds at the place of the founded jurisdiction.
