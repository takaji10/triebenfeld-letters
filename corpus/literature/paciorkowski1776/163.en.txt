# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 163
# translation by Claude

to me for inscription by the Sub-Chamberlain's office — changing nothing, adding nothing — as well as the contests of the parties; having no regard in this matter to anyone, whether poor or rich, friend or enemy. And all the records given over to me by the Honourable Sub-Chamberlain of Sandomierz or his office, I shall keep faithfully. So help me Lord GOD, and the Passion of Christ the Lord. Every Boundary Chamberlain of that palatinate shall therefore, after the conduct of the boundaries, be required to send the act of those same boundaries, properly written up, into the hands of the said Sub-Chamberlain's notary, *sub amissione Officij sui*, within the course of six weeks, and to enter it in the records of his district. And since the Honourable Krzysztof of Tęczyn Ossoliński, the present Sub-Chamberlain of Sandomierz, *ex nunc* releases the revenue from the Sandomierz customs for the provision of that Sub-Chamberlain's notary — We therefore approve this for that same notary for all time. In this same manner the Palatinate of Cracow receives the act and the Sub-Chamberlain's notary. And since the Palatinate of Sandomierz shall at the next sitting, which shall be fixed and conducted immediately after the Sejm in that same palatinate, designate a certain tax for the transcription of the land records of the Palatinate of Sandomierz, and shall there draw up a more sufficient ordinance concerning this matter,
