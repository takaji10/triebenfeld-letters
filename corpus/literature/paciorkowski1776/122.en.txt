# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 122
# translation by Claude

### *How royal estates that have not been delimited are to be defended, and how Captains are to be proceeded against in law for injuries.*

^[*Herburt, fol. 48.*] Likewise We enact: that leaseholders of the estates of Our Royal Table, ^[*The land court is assigned as the forum for the Captain in matters of boundaries.*] if they suffer injury from neighbours or others, shall be required to stand before the land court, after they have been summoned on account of the injury suffered. Even if they drive cattle or livestock to royal lordships or courts on account of a difference of boundaries, or on account of the destruction of crops — they shall be required to give to suretyship for a decree of the lords, according to the custom of the land long observed. If they do otherwise, they shall pay penalties like other nobles; which penalties may be sought and exacted from them, even in money, if any they shall have inscribed in the royal estates.
