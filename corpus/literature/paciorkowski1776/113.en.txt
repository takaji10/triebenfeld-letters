# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 113
# translation by Claude

of the year 1588. The party protests the grievance; the court counter-protests with the party that, the oath having been sworn — since the plaintiff's party having won the boundary path, in satisfaction of positive law and the decree of his court, before the face of his court, after the cited party withdrew from attempting the oath, with the court messenger reading the formula, on bended knee, at the final particular corner mound before the image of the Crucified Christ the Lord, and the witnesses with two fingers raised to heaven — he considers that same party having won the boundary path to have satisfied the decree of his court; and wills and declares by the authority of the present act that those same boundaries shall have the force of perpetual validity. And whatever is the property of the estate of the village of [name] from the original corner point toward the south, on the left side, shall belong entirely to the estate of the aforesaid village of [name]; and whatever is on the right side, likewise all entirely, shall for all time belong and pertain to the aforesaid estates of the village of [name] of His Sacred Royal Majesty. Which the above-named court messenger [names] was ordered to proclaim and announce. As regards, however, the claims of the plaintiff's party in respect of revenues wrongfully received from the plots appropriated by the Right Honourable Captain, and of acts of violence perpetrated, the commissarial court refers the present case, as not falling within the competence of his court, to the competent forum for final adjudication. By virtue of the present decree.
