# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 95
# translation by Claude

weighed carefully and diligently; the decision of the corner point or particular corner point ^[*The decision of the corner point duly suspended.*] of the aforesaid estates being suspended — as not having been acknowledged, indeed denied, both by the Honourable [names], collateral heir, and by the Right Honourable Captain — ^[*The court proceeded to the review of the boundary paths.*] the court proceeded to the review of the boundary paths to be demonstrated by the parties. The sun at last declining toward the west, he ordered the commissarial act to be adjourned to the morrow by the court messenger of the Kingdom, the Honest [names], apparitor of his court. The morrow arriving, namely the Tuesday after the feast of [names] in the present year, the commissarial jurisdiction having been reassumed and proclaimed by the aforesaid court messenger, he proceeded to the review of the plaintiff's party's boundary path first, and declared that it should follow the same. And first the plaintiff's party commenced its boundary path ^[*The plaintiff's party's boundary path.*] from the place of the founded jurisdiction and from those same three corner mounds, from the west toward the east, as straight as possible; and demonstrated to the court a hillock resembling a mound, fifty paces from the corner mounds, at the edge of a dense pine forest grown with various kinds of trees; against which demonstration of the mound, both the Right Honourable Captain and his subjects protested, denying it to be a true mound but a hillock built by ants; and in verification thereof requested its opening, granting which application as allowable by law,
