If, however, the Most Illustrious Bishop wishes to delimit his episcopal estates, whether as plaintiff or as respondent, with any neighbouring nobleman or with the leaseholder of royal estates, and if he wins the boundary path, then not the bishop but two canons deputed from the chapter are required to confirm the won boundary path with six noble witnesses.

It is to be observed that the Most Illustrious Bishop cannot on his own raise a question in respect of boundaries, except together with his cathedral chapter, and likewise respond as respondent.

If, however, the leaseholder of royal estates, together with the community (without whose appearance the case cannot be decided), wins the boundary path, then not the leaseholder but six subjects from the community shall be required to confirm the same at the final mound according to the intent of the law.

### *As Regards the Estates of His Most Serene Highness the Archbishop of Gniezno, First Prince and Primate of the Kingdom.*

He may advance the right of path through his commissioners, as he is accustomed to advance it before every court and office of the Kingdom, yet with the assistance of the Most Illustrious Cathedral Chapter and of two canons appointed to the boundary case.
