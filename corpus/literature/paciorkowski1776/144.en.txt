### *When Minors Reach the Years of Discretion, They May Proceed for Injuries Suffered; Prescription Not Obstructing Them.*

^[*Herburt, ibid.*] Wishing to succour and provide for children, especially those in need, We enact: that if an injury shall have been done to any persons by someone following the death of their father — on coming to the years of discretion, they may proceed at law against those who committed the injury, and the exception of prescription cannot be opposed to them, unless the lawful age be then fully completed in them. And if within the due time they shall have neglected to proceed for the injuries once done to them, then the exception of prescription stands against them on account of the negligence alleged.
