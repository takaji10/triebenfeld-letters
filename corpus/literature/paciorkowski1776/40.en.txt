# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 40
# translation by Claude

upon the death of the Honourable [names], former heir of the village of [name], the hereditary estate of the present plaintiff, the session had lapsed; and thereafter, with hostilities breaking out and ravaging our lands for almost twenty years, there was neither time nor place for vindicating the injury. Proceeding further in the same field toward the south, continuing his boundary path as straight as possible, the court reached three particular corner mounds, clearly notable, and at that place concluded and determined his boundary path. In connection with that determination, he reproduced the service return of the summons letters issued on the part of the plaintiff against the Honourable [names], heir of the estates of the village of [name], collaterally summoned, for the demonstration and acknowledgement of the corner point or particular corner point existing at this place, acknowledged before the municipal records of [name] on the [day] of [name] in the present year by the court messenger of the Kingdom, the Honest [names] of the village of [name], authentic and sworn, the tenor of which is as follows: (The summons letters are to be inserted here.) By virtue of which summons the Honourable [names], heir of the aforesaid estates of the village of [name], appearing in person, judicially acknowledged that same corner point or particular corner point as true, as situated at the convergence of three estates. After which acknowledgement by the collateral heir and determination of the plaintiff's party's boundary path, the cited party, namely the Honourable [names], solemnly protests against the whole boundary path of the plaintiff's party, as not having been continued and determined from the starting point,
