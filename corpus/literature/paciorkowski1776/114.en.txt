### *As Regards the Delimitation of Terrestrial Estates with Royal Ones, in the Contumacy of the Leaseholder.*

All the formalities pertaining to the boundary case having been completed beforehand, and the summons letters and mandate having also been issued to the Captain or leaseholder of the royal estates and to the community of subjects, as well as to the collateral heirs, according to the form set out in the original commissarial act; and the service return of the court messenger acknowledged in the proper district once served.

If the Captain or leaseholder of the royal estates fails to appear at the session assigned by the Right Honourable Commissioners by their summons letters, then the commissarial court in its full number, according to the intent of the law, together with the Sub-Chamberlain or his office, the jurisdiction having been founded and the act enrolled at the place where the plaintiff's party has stationed them, shall proceed notwithstanding the contumacy of the Captain or leaseholder of the estates and of the community of subjects of that Captaincy; and shall, in the customary legal form, order the plaintiff's party to conduct and demonstrate its boundary path before the court, with prior sworn inquiries also in verification of that boundary path; and shall adjudicate the case justly and equitably and determine the boundaries, even notwithstanding any collateral heir's
