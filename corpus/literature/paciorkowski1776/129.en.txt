### *Provision for boundary commissions.*

^[*Constitution 1598, fol. 1456, vol. 2.*] Inclining ourselves to the ancient laws, We annul all summonses to the Sejm or to the court, issued against the commissioners and the party for the delimitation of all Our estates of whatever kind from terrestrial estates, issued by leaseholders or by Our Instigator; declaring that a commissarial act, conducted by the full number of commissioners according to the laws, is not to be called into any doubt nor impugned, according to the ancient law.
