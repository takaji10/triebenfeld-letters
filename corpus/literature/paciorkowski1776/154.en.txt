# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 154
# translation by Claude

### *On Sub-Chamberlain's Surrogacies.*

^[*Constitution 1601, fol. 1503, vol. 2.*] Since it is provided by law that Sub-Chamberlains shall sub-delegate surrogates to their places in their own cases — those surrogates, however, must be for that one act only; and it is necessary that they be sworn, as the Sub-Chamberlains are; and it is difficult to persuade persons of good judgment and honourable reputation to this. We therefore enact: that each Sub-Chamberlain, when he has his own case, shall be required to bring out the nearest Sub-Chamberlain or his Boundary Chamberlain, or a sworn judicial official of his land or district, for such acts; who as a sworn official shall conduct the boundary act, and having subscribed himself upon his summons letters, shall be required under the penalty enacted for commissioners to arrive at the appointed time. This is to be understood for future times.
