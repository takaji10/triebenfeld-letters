# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 112
# translation by Claude

of the aforesaid mounds, confirm, with six witnesses of equal rank, according to the intent of the statute, the won boundary path at the final particular corner mound, in that oath formula: that by his boundary path he took nothing of the land and estate of the above-named estates and has not alienated anything from the same, so help him GOD and the Holy Passion of JESUS Christ.

And first he ordered the corner mound for the estate of the village of [name] of the plaintiff to be renewed and raised toward the east; a second for the royal estates of the village of [name] toward the west; and a third for the collateral estate and village of [name] toward the north; with the placing in each of them of metal markers, namely charcoal, brick, and iron and glass slag. Proceeding from those mounds by a straight path toward the south, the first wall mound was raised sixty paces from the corner mounds; (and so the erection of the wall mounds shall be written, and of the final particular corner mounds, with sufficient description of the places, the distance of each mound from the next, and their number up to the determination thereof.)

The boundaries having been duly and legitimately determined and the mounds raised, the court messenger is added immediately for the pronouncing of the oath formula for the party that has won the boundary path. The cited party — the Right Honourable Captain — moved to the Tribunal courts of the Kingdom at Piotrków; not admitted, adhering to the law and the constitution
