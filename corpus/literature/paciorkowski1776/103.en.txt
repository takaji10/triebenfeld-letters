# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 103
# translation by Claude

for the royal estates toward the west, and for the Honourable [names], collateral heir, and his estate of the village of [name] toward the north; with the placing of metal markers in each of them, namely charcoal, brick, and iron slag, and glass bottles filled with millet, with a description in parchment of the present act by which the delimitation of those estates is made, with the year and act, so that it may remain for posterity in the event of any question arising. And from that same corner point, toward the south a first wall mound was raised, eighty paces from the corner point; (and so the boundary path and the erection of the mounds shall be written out and accurately described, so that the description thereof corresponds with the position of the places); and the court reached the particular corner mounds or corner mounds situated in the southern quarter, pursuant to the acknowledgement of the collateral heir, and ordered those same mounds to be renewed with the placing of metal markers, as placed at the original corner point. The boundaries having been duly and legitimately determined, the Illustrious and Right Honourable Commissioners by their authority order the parties to keep those same boundaries and observe them inviolably, and will and declare that they shall have the force of perpetual and inviolable validity; and decree in the present that whatever is on the right side of the hereditary estates from the original corner point to the determination of the particular corner point shall belong entirely to the estate of the village of [name].
