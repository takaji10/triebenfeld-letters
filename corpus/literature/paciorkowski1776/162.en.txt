### *A Vault for the Sub-Chamberlain's Records in Sandomierz; On the Records and the Boundary Notary of the Palatinate of Sandomierz.*

^[*Constitution 1631, fol. 698, vol. 3.*] Considering the security of the boundary records of the inhabitants of the Palatinate of Sandomierz, so that they may not be kept in private houses as has been the case up to now — for this reason We designate a vault in Our castle of Sandomierz for the keeping of the Sandomierz boundary records; which vault the Right Honourable Starosta of that place shall show. And so that access for everyone needing extracts from those records may be the easier — the Honourable Sub-Chamberlain of Sandomierz shall be required to provide from his Sub-Chamberlain's office a notary resident in that same palatinate. Which notary, before he takes those boundary records into his administration, shall be required to swear an oath at the land sittings of Sandomierz in these words: I, [name], swear to Almighty GOD, One in the Holy Trinity, that I shall faithfully enter into the boundary records the boundary acts presented
