If, however, the heir of terrestrial estates wins the boundary path, then not his subjects but the nobleman himself, together with six witnesses of equal rank, shall be required to confirm the won boundary path in the oath formula prescribed by the statute of the Kingdom.

Moreover, when both parties are wanting in documentary proof ^[*The commissioners make the boundaries in the absence of boundary markers.*] and visible boundary markers, or only doubtful ones are standing, then in such a case, both parties' boundary paths having been rejected, the commissarial court shall determine the aforesaid boundaries from the thing presented to the eyes and by the moderation of its court, on equitable and just grounds, the oath barred to the parties.

^[*Prosecutorial letters against the commissarial act are void and of no effect.*] Furthermore, any citations and summonses to any court of the Kingdom whatsoever — whether on account of a motion or appeal not admitted, whether in accessory matters or in the final sentence, or for the purpose of quashing or relieving the commissarial act, issued on whatever legal pretext and ground — are void and of no effect, once the commissarial act has been determined in full and complete form, in the customary legal form and with all prior solemnities necessary for that purpose.
