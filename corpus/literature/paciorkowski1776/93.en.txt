# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 93
# translation by Claude

that as soon as You shall have been summoned by the summons letters of the aforesaid Our commissioners, You appear before them at the place of the differences, attempt the boundary wall of Your estates, and where it is to be acknowledged, acknowledge it. Whatever, moreover, they shall decree in that matter, keep and observe it firmly and inviolably. For Our grace. *Given at Warsaw* etc. (It is to be noted that if there are several collateral heirs on the other boundary of the boundary wall, they are likewise to be summoned by summons letters and mandate.)

After which enrolment, the commissarial court caused the parties to be called to appear. Those having been called, the Honourable [names], heir of the estates of the village of [name], personally arriving at that place, ^[*The collateral heir protests, not acknowledging for the plaintiff the convergence of three estates and the corner point at that place.*] not acknowledging there the true convergence of three estates, but elsewhere, where he offers to demonstrate it; and the mounds which the plaintiff's party takes as corner mounds for itself are not true mounds but hillocks — and on that account reproduced before his commissarial court the boundaries or official act drawn from the Sub-Chamberlain's records of [name], of the act of [name] in the year [name], from which it appears that the corner point or particular corner point of those estates existed in another place, and on that account protested against the same Honourable [names], plaintiff, regarding the improper founding of his court, as being on his own property, subject to no dispute, proceeding only to the avulsion of the hereditary estate of the protestant
