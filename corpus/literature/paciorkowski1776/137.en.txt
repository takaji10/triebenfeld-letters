The reviewers, both parties' boundary paths having been examined and reviewed, with due regard to the acknowledgement of the collateral heirs, the metal markers and real mounds having been examined, but above all a careful investigation having been made of continuous use and lordship, and of the testimony of elderly witnesses — shall adjudge the right of path to that party which has proved peaceful possession, demonstrated boundary markers, and relies on the acknowledgement of the collateral heirs; and shall moderate the matter and business of the boundaries of the acknowledged boundary path — both corner and particular corner mounds, and wall mounds — the oath being barred to the leaseholders themselves, as is customary in such matters of ambiguous and doubtful boundary paths of both parties, by their revisory sentence. From which, if an appeal shall have been made, and at the session arising from the appeal His Sacred Royal Majesty shall have approved the reviewers' sentence, those boundaries are always valid and acquire the force of perpetual firmness. As regards acts of violence, if any occurred, the forum is assigned in the court of His Sacred Royal Majesty after the court as for leaseholders of the royal leasehold.
