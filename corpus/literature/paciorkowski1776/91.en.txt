# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 91

Thereafter were issued the summons letters of the Right Honourable Commissioners [names] against the Honourable [name], heir of the estates of the village of [name], collateral heir; the acknowledged service return of those same served letters by the same court messenger, before the municipal records of [name], together with the mandate of His Sacred Royal Majesty; ^[*Summons letters to the Honourable collateral heir.*] the tenor of which follows in this form: Done at the castle of [name], on the [day] of [name] in the year [name] etc. (Written from the authentic extract of the service return.) We etc., commissioners designated and deputed by His Sacred Royal Majesty to perform the matters written below. To you, the Honourable [names], heir of the estates of the village of [name], collateral heir: we give notice that the Commission Letters of His Sacred Royal Majesty have been presented to us, by which His Majesty has seen fit to commit and command us to compose the differences occasioned by the boundaries between the royal estates of [name] of the leaseholding of the Right Honourable [names], Captain of [name], on the one part, and the hereditary estates of the village of [name] of the Honourable [name], plaintiff, on the other part; to distinguish them by mounds and metal markers customarily used in such matters, and to mark and delimit them.
