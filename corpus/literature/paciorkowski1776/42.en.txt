# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 42
# translation by Claude

session, at the call of the court messenger, the Honourable [names], collaterally summoned, appearing in person, judicially acknowledged to the cited party that the true corner point was situated at that place, and firmly alleged it. The plaintiff's party, namely the Honourable [names], protests against both the originally cited party and the collateral heir, regarding the improper demonstration of allegedly corner mounds and the acknowledgement thereof by the collateral heir, as proved by no documents; in connection with which protest made before his court, the plaintiff's party requested the opening of those corner mounds, granting the application therefor, he ordered those same mounds to be opened in the presence of all parties; in which, when opened, nothing was found. From those mounds or hillocks, proceeding from the north toward the south, the cited party demonstrated to the court a hillock resembling a mound, alleging it to be the true first wall mound, standing eighty-five paces from the opened corner mounds, under the very forest called *Nart*, whose opening the same cited leading party requested. Granting the application of that same party, he opened the same mound, in which, when opened, nothing was found save rock, that is, earth congealed in the manner of brick; against which mound the Honourable [names], plaintiff, protested regarding the unlawful demonstration of that hillock as a wall mound. And because that pine forest from antiquity belongs to his estate of the village of [name].
