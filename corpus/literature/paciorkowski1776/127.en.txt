The Starosta, like the nobleman, shall have no more of a retinue than thirty persons with friends and servants; and subjects at most twenty, for the showing of markers and the erection of mounds. And so that perjuries may not occur with offence to GOD, the commissioners are first to examine the markers shown from both sides by an elderly witness, and consider the matter among themselves according to their conscience; and if either party should show legitimate markers and probabilities: they shall require that party to prove it through elderly witnesses. And if both sides show uncertain markers, then without taking oaths from both sides, on account of the risk of perjury, they are to make the boundaries according to their conscience, inclining toward the statute and *juxta æquitatem*. And since some Starostas and leaseholders of Our estates are accustomed to exceed the measure in the defence of the estates: We therefore declare the prior statutes made in this matter as follows — that defence is not to be understood as the ploughing up of another's labour, namely crops, buildings, and any work that shall have stood for a year without the Starosta's obstruction; but only as the enjoyment of Our estates, or the bringing of an action after the year to the land court. And if a court, Starosta, or leaseholder should do anyone injury or harm beyond this, the forum for that shall be in the land court where those royal estates are situated.
