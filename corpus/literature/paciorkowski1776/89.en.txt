Wherefore We command You and the community of the subjects of the aforesaid village of [name], by royal authority and Our own commissarial authority which We exercise in this matter, that You and the true community of the subjects, with Your assistance as leaseholder, appear personally, legitimately, and peremptorily before Us and Our commissarial court, on the [day] next after the feast of [name], next and immediately to come, at the aforesaid place of the differences, where We shall have been situated and stationed by the plaintiff's party, at the instance of the aforesaid Honourable [names], heir of the village of [name], plaintiff; and that You judicially attempt the matter of the delimitation of the aforesaid estates, and respond to whatever by law shall be due. Certifying You that whether You appear or not at the fixed session, We shall nonetheless proceed to those matters that are incumbent upon Us from the prescription of the laws. Your contumacy and that of the subjects of the aforesaid royal estates subject to Your possession, and Your opposition, notwithstanding. Given at Our places of residence on the [day] next, in the year of the Lord [names].
