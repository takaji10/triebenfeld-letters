### *Soldiers Exempt from Courts.*

^[*Constitution 1609, fol. 1681, vol. 2.*] Guarding against those dangers that might fall upon the Crown and Our adjacent domains from various sides, and which could not be repelled without soldiers — We therefore, for all those who on any side, wherever the need of the Commonwealth indicates, shall have been directed by the Crown Hetmans and of the Grand Duchy of Lithuania — reassuming in full the constitution of the reign of King Stefan in the year 1578, We exempt them from all courts in general, *exceptis Causis ad Inscriptiones, Expulsionis, Violentiæ & Rei judicatæ*.
