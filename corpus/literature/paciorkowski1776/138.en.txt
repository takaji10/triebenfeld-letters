### *Soldiers' exemptions.*

^[*Constitution 1673, fol. 105, vol. 5.*] We reassume the prior constitutions on exemptions in soldiers' cases, which the Tribunals and other courts are to observe in the cases in which they are required to proceed according to law, according to the ancient law.

### *Exemptions.*

^[*Constitution 1676, fol. 363, vol. 5.*] The exemptions of Our Right Honourable Hetmans, as they are permitted by the ancient law — so that they may have their force in every court *in personalibus* only: We declare. If, however, condemnations should stand against soldiers' persons remaining in camp *in Personalibus Terminis* before any present cap-tribunal courts anywhere: such condemnations likewise, which are contrary to law, We annul by the authority of the present convention, the principal matter restored *in integrum*.
