# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 108
# translation by Claude

the court orders the registration of appearance for those same parties, (written according to the declaration of the Honourable counsels); appearing before his court and judicially contesting the matter between themselves.

The Royal commissarial court of His Sacred Royal Majesty, ^[*In trial.*] finding the appearance of the parties sufficient, orders those same parties to proceed to trial in the present case. In the trial, the contested submissions of the parties having been heard and weighed carefully and diligently: the decision of the corner point or particular corner point ^[*The decision of the corner point being suspended, the court proceeded to the review of the boundary paths.*] of the aforesaid estates being suspended, the court proceeded to the review of the boundary paths to be demonstrated by the parties. The sun at last declining toward the west: (the adjournment to the morrow shall be written.) The morrow arriving, namely the [day] of [name] in the present year, the commissarial jurisdiction having been reassumed and proclaimed by the aforesaid court messenger, he proceeded to the review of the plaintiff's party's boundary path and declared that it should follow the same. And first the plaintiff's party commenced its boundary path. (The boundary path shall be described accurately from where it begins, with a clear expression of the position of the places, and whatever the party shall demonstrate in the boundary path, together with the protests and counter-protests declared by the parties in the boundary path, and the documents, if any were produced in proof thereof.)

It reached the place called [names], at which it demonstrated ^[*Determination of the plaintiff's party's boundary path.*] the particular corner mounds determining the boundary wall of its estate
