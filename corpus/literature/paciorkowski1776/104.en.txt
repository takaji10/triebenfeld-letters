# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 104
# translation by Claude

Terrestrial estate; and whatever is on the left side and left hand, similarly entirely, shall for all time belong and pertain to the aforesaid estates of the village of [name] of His Sacred Royal Majesty; this also being provided and safeguarded: that the heirs and leaseholders of the aforesaid estates now delimited shall not deny one another mutual pasture in their respective estates (being careful, however, of damage), but shall maintain it among themselves, and their successors for all perpetual times. Which the above-named court messenger was ordered to proclaim and announce, by virtue of the present decree.

### *Commissarial Decree on Serious Contested Matters of the Parties, with the Oath Enjoined upon the Party That Has Won the Boundary Path.*

Done in the pine forest or forest grown with various kinds of trees, called [name], at the place of the differences between the estates of the village of [name], hereditary property of the Honourable [names], on the one part, and the estates of His Sacred Royal Majesty of the village of [name] belonging to the Captaincy of [name], presently leased to the Right Honourable [name], on the other part; near three particular corner mounds or corner mounds of the aforesaid estates and the third of the village of [name], hereditary property of the Honourable [names], collaterally summoned; on the Monday next after the feast of [names], in the year of the Lord [names].
