# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 69
# translation by Claude

a wall mound of the estate of the village of [name] of the Honourable [names], and the estates of the villages of [name] of the Honourable [names], plaintiff, and of the village of [name] of the Honourable [names], respondent, hereditary estates of both villages meeting wall-to-wall, having from time immemorial been of one estate and lordship, erected in approximately the middle of the boundary wall; on the [day] of [name] in the year [name].

Before the Honourable [names] of Sieradz, and [names] of Łęczyca, Boundary Chamberlains, by virtue of the decree of the Crown Tribunal of Piotrków, issued on the [day] of [name] in the year [name] between the parties named below, in the matter of the delimitation of estates never previously delimited, whereby etc. (The protests, if any intervene, and the appearance, shall be written from the foregoing acts in accessory matters; and the review of the boundary paths down to this place.) Which having been read and weighed and examined in all their circumstances — since it has been proved before his court that the above-named estates of the village of [name] and the village of [name] had from time immemorial existed as one lordship and estate, never delimited, and had now been disposed into distinct hands and persons by terrestrial dispositions or instruments of resignation by the former heir — the court therefore, both parties' boundary paths having been rejected and indeed adjudged away from them, directs its boundary path from the thing presented to the eyes, according to its conscience, the oath barred to the parties so that perjuries may not occur, at the arbitration of its court; and setting aside that same wall mound demonstrated at the place of the founded jurisdiction, the court proceeded toward the east to the third mound in order standing
