And the Sub-Chamberlain shall swear as follows: ^[*The oath formula of the Right Honourable Sub-Chamberlain.*] I, [name], swear to the Lord GOD that I will judge justly, according to the Lord GOD, the written law, and the justice of the parties' contests, listen carefully to those contests and receive them; likewise consider the boundary markers and their probabilities, and faithfully describe them; and likewise keep my records and those of my predecessors faithfully, and enter matters in them; and at which court I shall have no regard for any person — neither friend nor enemy, rich nor poor, stranger nor kinsman — showing no favour or disfavour to anyone in that my court; and taking no gift from anyone with respect to that court. So help me Lord God, and this Holy Gospel.

### *On the Relaxation and Annulment of Banishment.*

^[*Same year, fol. 1220, vol. 2.*] If anyone being under banishment should reconcile himself with the opposing party and receive from that party itself a release from that banishment: then such a person shall be given a relaxation of banishment from Our Chancery, which he shall obtain at the latest within twelve weeks after the release; and during that time he shall have standing before every court, and may register any recognitions.
