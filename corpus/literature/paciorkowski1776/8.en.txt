# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 8
# translation by Claude

Royal authority and Our own, in which capacity We exercise Sub-Chamberlain's authority in this matter, that you appear personally and peremptorily before Us, or before Our Sub-Chamberlain's Field Boundary Office, at the place of the differences between the hereditary estates of the village of [name] of the undersigned plaintiff on the one part, and your likewise hereditary estates of the village of [name] on the other part, at such place namely as the said plaintiff shall appoint for Us or Our office, on the Monday next after the feast of [name], next and immediately to come, at the instance of the Honourable [name], heir of the said estates of [name], plaintiff, who, adhering to the decree of the Land Court of [name], issued on the [day] of [name] in the year [name], between himself the plaintiff on the one part and you on the other part, cites or rather summons you for the delimitation of the estates and the erection of mounds where there are none, and for the renewal of the old ones where they are found, for the purpose of testing the matter in that case; therefore you are cited to see and hear the right of path adjudged to him the plaintiff by the sentence of Our court as the more probable, and to be admitted to swear an oath at the final mound according to the intent of the statute. Be therefore present at the session to comply, and to respond judicially to the foregoing matters. *Given etc.*

The court messenger shall acknowledge the service return of these summons letters before the proper court where the estates are situated.
