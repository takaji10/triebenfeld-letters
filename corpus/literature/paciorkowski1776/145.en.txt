## MOUNDS.
### *The Penalty and Satisfaction for Destroyed Mounds.*

^[*Herburt, fol. 447.*] If anyone shall have destroyed or violently annihilated — in part or in whole — any mounds erected, or other metal markers: such a violator shall pay to the party proceeding against him three marks of current money for every particular corner mound. For other mounds destroyed and all metal markers destroyed and annihilated, he shall pay six *skots*. And the same violator shall nonetheless be required to bring the Sub-Chamberlain's office to the place of the violated boundaries at his own expense, and to restore and newly erect those same boundaries destroyed by him — which he shall be required to do, after notification by the court messenger and noblemen on behalf of the party that suffered such injury, within eight weeks: under a penalty of fourteen marks succumbing to the party and the same to the court.
