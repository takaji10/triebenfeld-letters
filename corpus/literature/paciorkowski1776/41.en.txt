The plaintiff's party's boundary path having been concluded and determined, the Sub-Chamberlain's court ^[*The cited party's boundary path.*] proceeded to the review of the cited party's boundary path. The sun declining toward the west, he ordered the present act to be adjourned to the morrow by the above-named court messenger, the apparitor of his court.
