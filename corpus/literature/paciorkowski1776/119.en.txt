Moreover, Our aforesaid commissioners, two weeks before they convene together for the making of the boundaries, shall inform Our Captain or leaseholder ^[*The mandate shall be served in the presence of two noblemen.*] of the time and place of their convening through the court messenger and two noblemen, and also through their own letters and Our mandate.

### *On the honour and security of the commissioners.*

^[*Herburt, ibid.*] If any commoner, in the matter and court of the boundaries, ^[*A violator of the commissarial act shall be referred to the Captain of that place for due penalties.*] or on any other occasion, should inveigh against the commissioners in words and dishonour them — such a person shall be punished with imprisonment, or otherwise with a sitting of half a year. The execution of that penalty the Captain of that place shall carry out, under the penalty prescribed in the statute against those who should neglect to carry out its execution. Should any person, even a commoner, seize and brandish arms — even if he wounded no one — he shall pay the penalty with his neck; which the Captain shall likewise execute. Nobility, however, for such excesses shall be punished according to the statute.
