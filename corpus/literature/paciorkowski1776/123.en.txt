### *When the boundaries have been made, the Captains shall not commit injury.*

^[*Herburt, ibid.*] So that what is defined once by Our commissioners appointed for the delimitation of Our royal and noble estates may no longer be called into dispute: We enact that after the delimitation of Our royal and noble estates, Our Captains or officials shall not commit injury in the noble plots. And Our subjects too shall have no use of such delimited plots — neither in bee-keeping nor on any account.
