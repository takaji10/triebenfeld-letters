And where he has no residence in the Crown, proceedings shall be taken against him according to the procedure agreed at this Sejm against those without possessions; and if he has a sum inscribed in the leasehold, the legal process and execution shall be extended to that sum. And Starostas and leaseholders shall be free to summon anyone before the land court for injuries and losses that occur on the royal plot, both in Our benefit and that of the Commonwealth and in their own; and where oaths are prescribed for them by law, they are to discharge them both in Our name and in their own, saving the appeal to the Tribunal, where such cases are to be determined without remission of the cause. The subjects of Our villages too shall be able to summon before the land court for their own injuries *cum assistentia Capitaneorum vel Tenutariorum*. Likewise those from Our towns without assistance, according to custom. And where boundaries do not exist, the judges shall be required to send out a commission and conclude the boundaries for the nobleman without interruption.
