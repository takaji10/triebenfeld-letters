# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 98
# translation by Claude

^[*Determination of the plaintiff's party's boundary path.*] of the village of [name], partly with the small river taken wall-fashion as a natural boundary, confirmed by no documents and demonstrated by no real mounds. The Honourable [names] counter-protests against the aforesaid protest, having duly and legitimately continued his boundary path, and offers and declares that he will prove the same both by documents in the course of the case and by sworn inquiries. At which place the same plaintiff's party requested of the court of the Illustrious and Right Honourable Commissioners that the Honourable [names], heir of the village of [name], as the final collateral heir of the determined boundary path, be called to the court, pursuant to the commissarial summons letters issued at his instance against the same, of the following tenor: (Similar and conforming summons letters as against the first collateral heir at the commencement of the boundary path shall be inserted, with the name and surname and estates changed, and likewise by virtue of the royal mandate directed to that same cited heir for that purpose; a similar mandate as above.) To which application the Illustrious and Right Honourable Commissioners ordered the aforesaid Honourable [names], heir of the village of [name], as the final collateral heir, to be called to their court; ^[*Acknowledgement of the corner point or particular corner point by the final collateral heir.*] who, called by the aforesaid court messenger, appeared in person at that place, and there at that same place called *Ostra Górka*, acknowledged the convergence of the boundary walls of three estates, or the true particular corner point, of the village of [name], hereditary property of the Honourable plaintiff, and of the village of [name] of the royal estates, and of his own third collateral estate;
