# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 135
# translation by Claude

### *Soldiers' Exemptions.*

^[*Constitution 1627, fol. 544, vol. 3.*] Reassuming the ancient constitutions on soldiers' exemptions, with the consent of the Sejm We enact and provide: that exemptions from both Our Chanceries of both Nations, as well as from Our Hetmans, shall be accepted before all courts — excepting the cases expressly specified by the constitutions of the years 1611 and 1622.

## ON REVIEWS
### *Between Estates on Both Sides Royal, and on Reviewers Assigned by His Sacred Royal Majesty.*

Reviews, in place of commissions, between estates on both sides royal but of different possession and leasehold of Captains or leaseholders, are customary when a difference and dispute over a plot arises between leaseholders.
