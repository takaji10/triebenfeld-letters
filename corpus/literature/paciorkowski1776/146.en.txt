### *What Penalty Is Paid by One Who Erects Mounds Without Authority, and How He Gives Satisfaction.*

^[*Herburt, ibid.*] If anyone on another's soil or estate, without the Sub-Chamberlain and his office, shall of his own accord, voluntarily, and rashly erect and make boundary markers — such a person shall likewise pay three marks for every particular corner mound, and six *skots* for others (as above). And nonetheless all the mounds and metal markers so unlawfully made shall be formally destroyed, removed, and annihilated by the Sub-Chamberlain's office: so that in future no prejudice may come to the injured party from any trace of them — which shall be done under a penalty of fourteen marks to the party and the same to the court. And proceedings shall similarly be taken against such a person, in each eight weeks: as against the violent destroyer of boundaries as described above.
