# Paciorkowski (1776), p. i
# translation by Claude

This work, entitled *Rule of the Field Boundary Process of the Kingdom of Poland, etc.*, with an addition of Laws and Constitutions of the Kingdom, compiled and written with great labour by the Honourable Master MARTINO PACIORKOWSKI, Sworn Sub-Delegate of the Municipal Court Office of Opoczno, I have examined and read with care, and therein I have recognised all things compiled and arranged for the common benefit of this Kingdom in the matter of delimitation disputes, which arise so frequently, through the singularly praiseworthy efforts of the Author for the public good; and I have found nothing contrary to the Orthodox Roman Faith. I therefore grant permission for so useful a work to be committed to the press and to see the light of day. Given at Cracow, 15th May, A.D. 1749.

MATHIAS ZIĘTKIEWICZ, Doctor and Professor of Sacred Theology, Canon of the Cathedral of Cracow,
Censor of Books for the Diocese of Cracow.
