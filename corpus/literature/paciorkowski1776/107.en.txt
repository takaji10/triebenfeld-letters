# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 107
# translation by Claude

The commissarial court caused the parties to be called to appear. Those having been called, the Honourable [names], heir of the estates of the village of [name], appearing in person at that place, ^[*Acknowledgement of the corner point by the collateral heir.*] acknowledged there the true convergence of the aforesaid estates and the real corner mounds of the aforesaid parties, and his own collateral heir's particular corner mound at the determination of the boundary wall. At which place the same Right Honourable Captain protested, in his own name and in the name of the community ^[*The Captain protests against the collateral heir.*] of the royal village of [name], regarding the improper acknowledgement of the corner point by the aforesaid Honourable [name], collateral heir, alleging that they are not true mounds but hillocks, not situated in the proper legal form and in the customary triangle. Both the Honourable [names], original plaintiff, and the collateral heir, the Honourable [names], counter-protested ^[*Both the plaintiff and the collateral heir counter-protest.*] against the same Right Honourable Captain, regarding his non-acknowledgement of the true corner point and convergence of three estates — denied by words alone, not by documents — and in verification thereof the plaintiff's party reproduced the boundary act conducted by the Royal Commissioners, by which it is proved that the true corner point and real mounds exist at that place according to the description of that same act; and on the basis thereof offers to demonstrate the boundaries and boundary wall of the aforesaid estates as far as his particular corner point.
