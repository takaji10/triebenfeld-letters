# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 73
# translation by Claude

the tenor follows and is as follows: [names], Sub-Chamberlain of the Palatinate of Sieradz, etc. (The service return of those same letters, drawn from the records, acknowledged both against the cited party and against the collateral heirs, is to be inserted.) At today's session, the Sub-Chamberlain's office subscribed below founded its official jurisdiction, in the presence of the Honourable and Noble [names] and others of various rank and condition assembled for this act, and ordered the court messenger of the Kingdom, the Honest [names] of the village of [name], authentic and sworn, to proclaim and publish the same with security of the present act, and caused the parties to be called to appear. The parties having appeared — and indeed the plaintiff's party with the Very Reverend [names], Parish Priest of the Parish Church of [name], with the consent of His Most Serene Highness the Archbishop of Gniezno, Primate of the Kingdom, as the local ordinary, given in person for the delimitation of those same estates of the village of [name] belonging to the church, and with the Honourable [names], heir of the estates of the village of [name], likewise in person; and the Honourable [names], collaterally summoned, likewise in person — (if any of the collateral heirs enters a protest, it shall be written at that place before the appearance, after the founded jurisdiction) — the parties having appeared and judicially contested the matter between themselves; the contested submissions of those same parties having been heard; the resolution of the corner point being suspended, the court proceeded to the review of the boundary paths demonstrated by both parties.
