**Translator's Note**

[T.N. 1] *Włóka Chełmińska* (Chełmno *włóka*, Latin *mansus Culmensis*): a unit of land area specific to Prussian law, equal to approximately 16.8 hectares or about 41.5 acres. Used as the threshold measure for whether appeals are permitted in Prussian boundary commissions.

### *The Forum of Persons of Noble Estate with Ecclesiastical Persons in Real Civil Cases of the Crown and the Grand Duchy of Lithuania.*

^[*Constitution 1635, fol. 857, vol. 3.*] And so that, as in criminal cases on account of the forum, We might bring the ecclesiastical estate together with the noble estate to a settlement also in real civil cases: We enact that as regards real cases — on account of simple civil, terrestrial, and boundary injuries — the forum of every nobleman with an ecclesiastical person shall be, by ordinary process, before the land court and in the field; and such cases shall be conducted on appeal before the Tribunal *coram Judicio Composito*, like other ecclesiastical cases. And if any ecclesiastical person were found to be such as to hold the common law in contempt and to allow banishment to be obtained against him: the *loci Ordinarius* shall execute the same upon his person and upon all his estates generally — his own but not the terrestrial ones — until real satisfaction is given to the injured party. The people of the Greek faith, however, as in the first constitution in the matter of the forum *in Personalibus*, remain in their ancient customs.
