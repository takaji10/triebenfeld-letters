### *On royal commissions and commissioners for boundaries.*

^[*Constitution 1588, fol. 1210, vol. 2.*] Royal commissions for boundaries shall always be given from Our Chancery without any difficulty to whoever has need of them. And the commissioners, together with the Sub-Chamberlain or Boundary Chamberlain of that land, may be appointed not only from that but also from another closer palatinate, even if they have no residence in that palatinate or land. Which commissioners, when they subscribe themselves upon the summons letters, are required to arrive at the session and bring that commission to execution by making the boundaries. And if they cannot be concluded in one day, they are to conclude them on the following days without departing, so that the commission shall not lapse for the nobleman.
