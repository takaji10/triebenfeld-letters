From that decree, both the originally cited party and the collateral heir, the Honourable [names], moved to the Crown Tribunal courts of the Kingdom at Piotrków. Not admitted; the party protests the grievance; the court counter-protests with the party that it inflicted no grievance; and after the motion interposed and not admitted, proceeded to the erection of those same mounds; and first raised the first corner mound for the estate of the village of [name] of the Right Honourable plaintiff, toward the southern quarter; a second for the cited party and its estates of the village of [name], toward the north; and a third toward the west, for the estate of the village of [name] of the Honourable [name], collateral heir; and from those same mounds, on the bank of that same small river or torrent, a first wall mound was raised, half a furlong from the corner mounds; in which mounds — both corner mounds and wall mound — boundary markers were placed, namely charcoal, brick, and iron slag, and fragments of glass. From which wall mound, by that same small river or torrent, the boundary wall extends through the middle of the mere and the water sluice, commonly called *upust*.
