# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 133
# translation by Claude

### *Provision for boundary commissions.*

^[*Constitution 1651, fol. 712, vol. 4.*] Although the security and authority of boundary commissions is sufficiently safeguarded by the common law, there are nevertheless those who, in contravention of the public laws, attempt to invalidate such commissions conducted according to the prescription of the law by various means — by mandates issued to the Sejm and to the court. Therefore, providing against this now and in the future, and reassuming the constitutions of the years 1550, 1567, 1588, 1598, and all other statutes and constitutions on the authority of commissions, by the authority of the present Sejm: We provide that every person who should wish to invalidate such legitimately conducted commissions, whether by mandates or by other means, shall be punished by a fine of five thousand marks, to be recovered before the Crown Tribunal *inter causas Officij peremptorie*.

### *Reassumption of the constitutions on commissions.*

^[*Constitution 1703, fol. 111, vol. 6.*] Reassuming the prior constitutions concerning commissions, all commissions and decrees issued by them conducted to this day between Our estates and terrestrial ones, according to the statutes and ancient laws, We preserve inviolably and confirm. Which constitution is also to serve the Grand Duchy of Lithuania
