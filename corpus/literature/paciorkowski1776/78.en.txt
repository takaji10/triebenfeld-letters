# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 78
# translation by Claude

one almost to the final ruin of his fortune through a dispute over boundaries lasting several years — we have made between ourselves such a plan of the boundary path and boundary wall between our estates and villages, from corner to corner. (*The boundary path agreed upon shall be described explicitly, by what places it runs, even if not in a straight line, for in such boundaries the straight line is rarely observed.*) For the purpose of which delimitation we are obliged to request His Grace the Sub-Chamberlain of our Palatinate, and to bring him to the described place, at the latest within four weeks; which boundaries marked by mounds by His Grace the Sub-Chamberlain, together with our successors, we bind and shall be obliged to keep and observe for ever, under a pledge of two thousand marks — in case of contravention, we designate the forum of the Land Court of Sieradz or of the Crown Tribunal for the rendering of account — and to this we subscribe with our own hands under our customary seals. Given at [name], in the year [name].

At which session, by virtue of the aforesaid amicable settlement, at the application and official request of the parties to the settlement, at the disputed place, the jurisdiction having been founded there, and proclaimed by the court messenger in the presence of both the principal acting parties and the collateral heirs and other persons of various rank and condition assembled for this act,
