But because it often happens that the neighbours of the sold estate, whose boundaries likewise touch upon it, postpone troubling the buyer within the said three years and three months — until the seller and intercessor escapes through the prescription of the sale; after which has elapsed, they do not scruple first to disturb the buyer over the boundaries, even though they were present at the sale — for this reason we wish and enact: that when any estate ^[*Prescription of three years in respect of boundaries with the new possessor of the estates.*] is shown to the buyer by the seller, the neighbours of that estate shall be summoned by the buyer with the court messenger, to be present at that showing. If they shall have been unwilling to come, summoned thus by the court messenger, and within three years and three months shall not have raised a question of boundaries against the possessor, then the possessor shall be admitted to proof of his purchase with witnesses, and shall be assisted by the prescription of so long a time.
