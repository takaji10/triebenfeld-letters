who, called by the abovementioned court messenger, appeared in person and there at that same place called [name] acknowledged the convergence of the boundary walls of three estates, or the true particular corner point, of the aforesaid plaintiff's estate of the village of [name] and of the royal estates, and of his third collateral estate; and proved it by the boundaries of the year [names].

The plaintiff's party's boundary path having been reviewed and faithfully noted by the commissarial court, it proceeded to the review of the Right Honourable Captain's boundary path. And first he commenced his boundary path (it shall be written in conformity with the position of the places, after the manner of the original act, as far as the determination thereof).

And thus continuing his boundary path further, the Right Honourable Captain reached by the same boundary wall the convergence of three estates, with the collaterally summoned party present and acknowledging the same, ^[*The plaintiff protests the nullity of the entire boundary path of the Right Honourable Captain.*] and determined his boundary path. Upon the conclusion thereof, the plaintiff's party protests the nullity and invalidity of the entire boundary path, as having been continued through the proper plots of the village of [name] and not determined through the intermediate points. Both boundary paths of both parties having thus been concluded and reviewed, the present act is limited as the sun declines toward the west (the adjournment to the morrow shall be written). The morrow arriving, namely the [day] of [name] in the year [name], the jurisdiction having been reassumed etc., ^[*In the prosecution a sworn inquiry is enjoined.*] he ordered the parties to proceed in the case.
