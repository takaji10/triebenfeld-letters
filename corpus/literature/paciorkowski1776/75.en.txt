# Paciorkowski (1776), p. 75
# translation by Claude

Parish Priest of [name], and the cited party's having been rejected, the same having been adjudged to the said Very Reverend Parish Priest, the court proceeded to the erection of mounds — both corner and wall mounds — and to the renewal of the ancient ones. And first, at the place of the founded jurisdiction, it renewed the ancient corner mound for the estate of the village of [name] belonging to the church and raised it up; renewed a second likewise for the estate of the village of [name] of the cited party; and a third for the estate of the village of [name] of the collaterally summoned party; and marked all three of them with metal markers, namely glass, charcoal, brick, and iron slag. Proceeding from those corner mounds from the north toward the south, a wall mound was raised sixty paces from the corner mounds. (The further erection of mounds shall be written out, both of the wall mounds and of the other corner point; the positions of the places and the distance of each mound from the next shall be observed.) All the corner and wall mounds having thus been raised, the court decrees that the said Very Reverend Parish Priest shall confirm the same in that oath formula, adhering to the statute of the Kingdom, at the final mound with six noble witnesses — that by his boundary path he has taken nothing of the estate and land of the aforesaid hereditary village of [name] of the cited party and appropriated it to his own village and ecclesiastical estate.
