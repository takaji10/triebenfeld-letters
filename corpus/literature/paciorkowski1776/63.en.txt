Right up to the particular corner mounds or corner mounds of the estates of [names], which the court ordered to be renewed and raised up, with the placing of metal markers in each mound, as it placed in the prior ones. Those corner and particular corner mounds having been erected and renewed, the Sub-Chamberlain's court orders the party that has won the boundary path, adhering to the statute of the Kingdom, to swear an oath with six witnesses of equal rank, and this immediately; the court messenger is added for the pronouncing of the oath formula. The cited party moved to the Crown Tribunal courts of the Kingdom at Piotrków; not admitted, on the ground that final adjudication of the present case, every appeal cut off, was pre-committed by the Tribunal decree. Which oath the Right Honourable [names], plaintiff, together with six witnesses of equal rank, namely the Honourable [names], on bended knees before the image of the Crucified JESUS Christ the Lord — the witnesses with two fingers raised to heaven — swore a bodily oath at his final particular corner mound, with the court messenger reading the formula, before the face of his court, the party not being present but having withdrawn rashly; he therefore declares that those same boundaries shall have the force of perpetual validity. Which boundaries let no one dare to violate or annihilate, under a penalty of two thousand marks to be decreed from now by the competent court, by virtue of the present decree.
