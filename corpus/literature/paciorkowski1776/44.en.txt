Finally, continuing its further boundary path and inclining obliquely to the left toward the quarter between the south and the winter east, the court was led to the middle of the fields where it alleged the mounds had been ploughed up by the above-named Honourable [names], plaintiff — which allegation it proves by no contestation of the case. The Honourable [names], plaintiff, protesting against the leading party regarding the improper conduct of his court through his own plots and fields, subject to no dispute since antiquity, and also regarding the trampling of grain by its boundary path, and because those plots and fields have from antiquity been manorial, belonging to the village and estate of the protestant himself; continuing further through the fields by the same boundary path, diverting itself this way and that, the leading party reached that corner point and those three corner or particular corner mounds where the originally plaintiff's party had concluded its boundary path; there also the cited party determined its boundary path. The plaintiff's party protests the nullity and invalidity of the entire boundary path, as not having been conducted from the starting point through the intermediate points, containing as it does the greatest obliqueness, and proved by no documents throughout its course.
