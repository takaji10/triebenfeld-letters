And thereafter, in place of a summons, the summons letters ^[*The summons letters and mandate must precede the session by two weeks.*] of the lord commissioners, both to the Captain and to the collateral heir, for a certain fixed session and place, shall be issued and served by the court messenger and two noblemen two weeks before the session, together with the royal mandates; the service return of the same, once served, the same court messenger must acknowledge before the records of his own district; and the commissarial act itself must be concluded within the space of one year and six weeks.

Furthermore, that in the court and act of a commission, by virtue of the laws and constitutions of the Kingdom contained in this book, no postponement is granted, on the ground that it is an act of a single occasion; except that if the matter cannot be concluded in one day, it may be transferred to the next immediately following day and continued and executed on the morrow.

Moreover, no appeal from that commissarial court ^[*In the commissarial act no postponement is granted. No appeal is granted.*] and its decree lies to any higher court of the Kingdom, on the ground that it is an absolute court and has, by virtue of the laws of the Kingdom (its conscience alone obligated and burdened thereto), the absolute faculty and power of judging and distinguishing estates from royal ones.
