### *On the Renewal of Boundaries.*

^[*Herburt, fol. 948.*] When one person summons another to renew boundaries with him and erect mounds through the old boundary markers between their estates — which markers and ancient boundary wall or mounds he would name in the summons and undertake to confirm by oath — such a person shall by law have the priority of presentation and proof through elderly witnesses from those same markers.
