**Translator's Note**

[T.N. 1] *Page 115x* interpolates a diagram page between pp. 115 and 116. The text on this page describes in prose the analogy between a boundary system and a building with walls and corners, and provides two brief captions for a diagram of a river serving as a natural boundary alongside a new course of the same river that is not counted as the boundary. The diagram itself is not reproduced in the Markdown source; the captions are translated as they appear.
