# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 105
# translation by Claude

Before the Illustrious Right Honourable [names], Castellan of [name], and the Right Honourable [names], Boundary Chamberlain of the Palatinate (the other several commissioners are to be given their proper titles, those who subscribed themselves upon the summons letters of the parties, now present), commissioners designated and deputed by His Sacred Royal Majesty to decide, finally conclude, and determine the differences occasioned by the boundaries between the estates described above; by virtue of the Commission Letters issued under the title of His Sacred Royal Majesty, sealed with the seal of the greater or lesser Chancery of the Kingdom, sound, intact, and unimpaired, and bearing no mark of suspicion; the tenor of which, word for word, follows and is as follows: AUGUSTUS THE THIRD, by the Grace of God King of Poland (this instrument is to be inserted to the end). Those Commission Letters of His Sacred Royal Majesty having been read out, at today's session assigned to the parties by Us, or by our summons letters as commissioners from His Sacred Royal Majesty, the commissarial jurisdiction having been founded and proclaimed with all security of the present act by the court messenger of the Kingdom, the Honest [name] of the village of [name], authentic and sworn, in the presence of the Honourable and Noble [names] and others of various rank and condition assembled for this act; the tenor of the summons letters issued both to the Right Honourable Captain or leaseholder of the royal estates
