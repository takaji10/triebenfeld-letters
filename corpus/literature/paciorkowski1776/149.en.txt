Which witnesses the plaintiff shall name and enter before the land court at the nearest sitting after the judgment, in the manner with an oath formula; and at the place of the differences with those witnesses of his passage from corner to corner through the places and boundary markers, as mentioned above — he shall be required to swear at the sitting appointed to the parties by the official before that same official. Which act in its entirety the aforesaid official shall be required to declare before the land court of the mounds and boundary markers made or circled, and to enter them in the land records. And where one party should have a privilege, or any letters, or any deeds concerning the boundaries or certain boundary places — namely certain boundary features — that party shall have priority in presenting and supporting those boundaries and places, according to its title deeds and ancient observance.
