### *Delimitation of the Inhabitants of the Palatinates of Ruthenia, Bełz, Podolia, and Volhynia Among Themselves.*

^[*Constitution 1601, fol. 1505, vol. 2.*] We enact that the inhabitants of the palatinates of Ruthenia, Bełz, Podolia, and Volhynia — between whose hereditary estates the matter concerns not jurisdiction or forum with respect to the estates, but only the delimitation itself and the settlement of the plots — since such differences requiring settlement only in the matter of use itself, having no controversy over jurisdiction and forum, require delimitation: therefore, without waiting for a principal commission according to the ancient laws and constitutions, an inhabitant of any of those palatinates who requires boundaries from the adjacent inhabitants of another palatinate of the above-named palatinates shall bring out both Sub-Chamberlain's offices — both that of his own district and that of the district from which the differences arise — as well as the standard-bearer and the military judge of both districts.
