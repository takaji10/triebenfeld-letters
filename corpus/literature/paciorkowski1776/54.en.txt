At which place, the Honourable [names], originally cited and plaintiff, heir of the estates of the village of [name], protests against the Right Honourable [names], heir of the estates of the village of [name], regarding the improper founding of the jurisdiction and the situating of the field hearing court of the Sub-Chamberlain's office, as being on his own hereditary property of the village of [name] and not at the convergence of three estates. The Right Honourable [names], heir of the aforesaid estates of the village of [name], counter-protests against the Honourable [names] regarding the wrongful denial of the jurisdiction founded at that place, and of the convergence of three estates thereat; in proof of which he reproduced before his court an official inspection carried out by the court messenger and two noblemen, acknowledged before the municipal records of [name] on the [day] of [name] in the year [name], on account of the destroyed mounds at the place of the founded jurisdiction, by the subjects of the hereditary village of [name] of the cited party; the same Honourable [names] counter-protesting that although the Right Honourable [names] reproduces before the court an official inspection for proof of the convergence of three estates, as though at the place of the founded jurisdiction, nevertheless that same inspection ought not to bear upon the place where the jurisdiction is founded, but upon the place which the cited party offers to demonstrate in its boundary path.
