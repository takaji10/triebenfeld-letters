# Rostworowski (1918), p. 44
# translation by Claude

of 24 grosze and the March instalment of the chimney tax are to be paid — and after reading in the matter the opinion of the late Director of the Treasury Małachowski — it was decided by a resolution in actis that this whole question, as likewise that of the following instalments of the tax of 24 grosze and the chimney tax, should, once the project has been drawn up, be referred again to His Excellency the Director of the Interior as Deputy for the Treasury.

Likewise the memorial of Michalina née Prusimska Dąbska was referred for an opinion to His Excellency the Director of Justice, with the recommendation that, with regard to the confiscation of the estates of Citizens by the past Government, he submit a project of general rules in this matter.

The lists of candidates for the Administrative Chamber of Warsaw, submitted by the 4 Directors, were read. Likewise the list submitted by the Director of Justice of Justices of the Peace for the Departments of Płock, Kalisz, Poznań and Warsaw, of Justices of the Land and of the Court of Appeal of the Department of Warsaw, was read. The Director of Justice was instructed to submit another list, with two candidates placed upon each seat.

His Excellency the President was asked to give a reply to Governor Gouvion on his letter recommending the Messieurs Jędrzejewicz, with the assurance that the Prince Director of War would be instructed to do full justice to the Governor's intercession, as far as circumstances allow.

The sitting was announced for after dinner.

Stanisław Małachowski, President.
