SITTING 58.

On 18 February, after dinner.

Present His Excellencies President Małachowski, Gutakowski, Wybicki, Działyński, Bieliński, Sobolewski.

The memorials of Mr. Podgurski, asking that it be permitted him to deliver the requisitioned forage not to Łowicz but to Kalisz or Częstochowa; of Wojciech Maryański, asking for an office; and of the Magistrate of Mszczonów, asking leave to establish a magazine of forage and cattle in the town of Mszczonów — were referred to His Excellency the Director of the Interior; the project of Amtmanowicz Kossowski concerning the tithes was ordered to be laid aside ad acta.
