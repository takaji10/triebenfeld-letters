# breyer1935mutterland  5  en
# Breyer (1935b), p. 5
# translation by Claude

German farmers of the neighbouring marsh areas also took over the Dutch way of farming. So the name "Holländer" passed to them in the legal and technical sense.

According to the Thorn church books, a small number of settlers from eastern Pomerania, from the neighbourhood of Christenberg, Schifelbein, Kolberg and Latzken, also lived in the new Vistula villages.

A comparison of the present *family names* with those of the old Thorn church books convinces us of the likeness of the names; a proof that the Vistula Germans have long been settled. Names such as Leuschner, Heise, Milke, Kersten, Gollnig, Moltzahn, Netzel, Kröning, Raths and so on bear witness to this. After 1700 the description "Holländer" disappears from the Thorn church books; it is replaced by farmers' names from Slonske, Woluschewo and so on.

About 1740 settlement in the lowland villages began to quicken. One after another numerous villages arose, up to the mouth of the river Wieprz, 100 km south of Warsaw. Of the origin of these settlers we receive reliable information from the church books of the Lutheran congregations of *Gombin, Iłow, Plozk* and *Neuhof*. Most came from the surplus population of the old settlements between Thorn and Wlozlawek. But settlers from the lowland villages *below* Thorn, Bromberg and Graudenz also took part in the clearing of the Vistula meadows; some even came from East Prussia.

Going through five years of the registers of the congregation of *Gombin*, founded in 1832, gave 45 statements of origin for the lowland villages. Of these West Prussia had 30, the old Vistula villages of central Poland 15; in detail, the neighbourhood of Thorn 12, Slonske 9, Graudenz 5, Schwetz 4, Bogpomoż and Dembe 3, Bromberg 2, and Fordon, Neuenburg, Straßburg, Riesenburg, Mewe, Mehlsack, Braunsberg and Driesen on the Warthe one family each.

The church books of the Lutheran parish of *Iłow*, founded in 1775, contain for the period 1779 to 1840 the following 80 places of origin, which we list going up the Vistula: Danzig Werder, Baldrum near Marienwerder, Neuenburg, Montau 1 settler each, Graudenz 4, the Kulm lowland 2, Bromberg, Flötenau 1 each; from Neidenburg, Amt Schoden, Bergbruch, Neuenort, Altretz 1 settler each, Altendorf (3), the neighbourhood of Thorn (11), Nischewke 19, Deutsch-Glinne 9, Slonske 9, Neu-Ciechocinek (2), Woluschewo and Rybit (1), Bogpomoż 5, Witoschiner Lengde, Liciszewo near Lipno 1 family each.
