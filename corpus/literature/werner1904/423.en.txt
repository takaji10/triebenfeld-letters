On 9 November 1879 the congregation kept its centenary jubilee, on which occasion the pastor Schramm published "Nachrichten über die evangelische Kirche in Wreschen" (Notices on the evangelical church in Wreschen).

After the parishes of Miloslaw (see there) and Neßla-Hauland (see there) were branched off, the parish comprises the following localities: Wreschen, Bardo, Biależyce, Biechowo, Bieganowo, Bierzglin, Bierzglinek, Chocicza, Chwalibogowo, Gozdowo, Grzymisławice, Gr. and Kl. The filial Wilhelmsau belonging to it, set up on 1 June 1892, to which nine other localities belong, counts 336 Evangelicals. In Wilhelmsau stands a Bethaus erected by the Royal Settlement Commission, which was dedicated on 3 June 1892 by the General Superintendent D. Hesekiel.

The pastorate has been held by:

1. Martin August Marggraff, formerly rector in Schmiegel, came here in 1779 and went in 1783 to Schwarzenau (see there).

2. Jonathan Benjamin Krause, born in Liegnitz, was sent here by the Consistory in 1783 as general substitute and died here in 1787.

3. Christian Gottfried Rohrmann, born in Fraustadt, died here as general substitute in 1795.

4. Samuel Gottlieb Schrödt, born in Wollstein, was rector in Schwerzen and was called here as pastor in 1795. He died on 15 November 1818.

5. Johann Gottlob Tiesler, born at Storchnest, became second master and later rector in Rawitsch and was called here in 1819. He died on 18 April 1835.
