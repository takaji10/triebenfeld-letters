# The Government at Poznań in the Prussian state handbook, 1800 and 1801

Source: *Handbuch über den Königlich Preußischen Hof und Staat*, Berlin
(Decker), the volumes for 1800 and 1801, section Justiz-Departement, "IV.
Südpreußen", "A. Südpreußische Regierung zu Posen". Read on 2026-10-04 from the
Internet Archive's scans of the Google Books copies:

- 1800: https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_qI9KAAAAcAAJ
- 1801: https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_oRpKAAAAcAAJ

Only the machine-read text was consulted, not the page images. It is badly
garbled in places; names are given below as far as they can be made out, and a
name the text mangles is marked. Found by Claude, not supplied by the editor.

## What it settles

Working reading, in English: the Poznań court had a first president (von
Steudener) and a vice-president. Count Danckelmann was vice-president in the
1800 volume; by the 1801 volume von Götze holds that post, which agrees with
the Grand Chancellor's draft of 7 December 1800 (I. HA GR, Rep. 7 C, Nr. 3709,
document 11) that Danckelmann had "lately" moved to the Government at Kalisz.
That draft calls him "President der Regierung zu Posen".

The signatures under the court's three reports in Nr. 3709 (documents 1, 3,
12) are all in these lists: Danckelman, v. Götz(e), Hering, Fromme,
Diederichs, Dannenberg, Schwarz, Bormann, v. Fischer, Hoening, Leuchert,
Richter, Dühring (Düring / Döhring), Herford.
