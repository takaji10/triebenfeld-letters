# The repurchase of Trąbczyn in 1896 (the town history sign at Trąbczyn)

Source: a **town history sign (information board) at Trąbczyn**, as the
editor reported it in chat on 2026-11-13. The sign's own wording was not
supplied, and the editor has not yet found the primary source it rests on.
Cited by the owners' family tree (`reference/estate_tree.yml`, source
`sign1896`, on Tadeusz Miączyński).

## The editor's report, as written

> The Trąbczyn estate was later repurchased in 1896 by Tadeusz Francisczek
> Władysław Miączyński (1857-1923), who was a cousin of Władysław
> Miączyński and grandson of Michalina Miączyńska. This information comes
> from a town history sign in Trąbczyn, and I have not yet been able to find
> the primary source of that information - but it should be added to the
> project/genealogy as knowledge.

("Francisczek" as typed; the name is Franciszek.)

## Against the edition's sources

- **The person.** Minakowska's database (dw.51608, saved 2026-11-12):
  Tadeusz Franciszek Władysław hr. Miączyński, born 9 III 1857, died 15 IX
  1923 at Będlewo, son of Witold Michał hr. Miączyński (1823-1880), who was a
  son of Stanisław Adam Miączyński and Michalina née Prusimska. So he was
  Michalina's grandson and a first cousin of Władysław Miączyński
  (1852-, son of Aleksander), the heir of 1879 and seller of 1892, as the
  sign says.
- **The sale before it.** Słowo of 4 January 1892 has Trąbczyn sold by
  Count Miączyński to Countess Władysławowa Wielopolska (Maria née
  Plater-Zyberk) for 225,000 rubles; the Łódź liquidation file has
  "Countess Maria Wielopolska" owner in June 1892 (APŁ 39/98/0/-/6603,
  doc. 4). The sign's repurchase would end her ownership after four years.
- **A witness in the edition.** Albert Breyer's notebook of July 1936
  (APP 53/968/0/-/669, doc. 2, page 5194_a2): "Parzeliert Włodzimierow vor
  25 Jahre vom Gute Trambczyn, Besitzer hiess: Graf Miączyński" -
  Włodzimierów parcelled from the Trąbczyn estate about 1911, when its
  owner was a Count Miączyński. No forename is given; it fits the sign.
- **Not found**: the act of the purchase of 1896 (the Trąbczyn mortgage
  book, a notarial act, or a press notice like Słowo's of 1892).
  `docs/MISSING_SOURCES.md` lists it.
