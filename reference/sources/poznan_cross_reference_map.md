# Cross-Reference Map — Trąbczyn / Łukomia Boundary Dispute

*This map covers four dimensions: (A) explicit cross-citations between corpus documents; (B) named persons appearing across multiple documents; (C) recurring physical features; (D) decrees and proceedings referenced in the corpus but not yet in it — the gap map for future archival work.*

*Document numbers refer to the Chronological Document Register. All geographical terms follow the established glossary conventions: **pine forest** [*bór*], **woodland** [*las*], ***stawisko***, **embankment** [*grobla*], **corner mound** [*kopiec narożny*], **wall mound** [*kopiec ścienny*], **field inspection** [*kondestensja*], **judicial site inspection** [*wizja*].*

---

## A. Cross-Citations Between Corpus Documents

This section records every instance where one corpus document explicitly invokes, refers to, or adjudicates the content of another.

---

### Document 10 (1776) citing earlier corpus documents

Document 10 is the most reference-dense document in the corpus. Its adjudication section explicitly resolves matters arising from the following:

**→ Document 3 (1728):** The sworn inquiry of 1728 concerning the *stawisko* is specifically identified at f. 660 of document 10 as one of the instruments the commission orders to be submitted. At the close of the decree (f. 715), the commission seals "one [sworn inquiry] of 1728 concerning the *stawisko*" among the four sworn inquiries delivered to Prusimski. Document 3 itself (53/6/0/-/40, ff. 368, 372–373v) is the **Decree of Location** that ordered this inquiry — appointing Albertus Biskupski and two arbitrators per side to descend to the disputed ground on Monday after the Sunday *Misericordiae* 1728 and conduct sworn inquiries into both ownership and the alleged transgressions. The inquiry's actual findings (what the 1776 commission read and then sealed back to Prusimski) are a separate document from the Decree of Location and have not been located: Inquisitiones [protocollon] 1724–1779 (Kalisz Land Court) has a gap covering 1728 (surviving material begins c. 1751), and 53/6/0/-/40 has no further 1728 entries after ff. 372–373v (the volume jumps from 1728 to 1766). Given the Decree of Location frames this as arbitration "chosen by mutual consent" with appeal reserved only from a definitive sentence, the findings were most likely never separately enrolled in any court protocol, and — if they survive at all — would be among Prusimski/Hohenlohe family papers rather than the APP Kalisz/Konin court series.

**→ Document 9 (1771 Kalisz delivery of possession):** Explicitly identified in document 10 at ff. 712v–714 as the delivery of possession "made in the Lusnia woodland in 1771 on Monday after the Feast of All Saints by the Honourable Wawrzyniec Wstowski, Commissioner of the Poznań and Kalisz voivodeships, on behalf and in favour of the Honourable Stanisław Chełmski, heir of the Łukomia estate — filed in the Konin municipal court on Monday after Saint Andrew the Apostle of the same year." The commission finds it unlawful and annuls it in full.

**→ Documents 6 and 7 (1767 wizja records):** The *stawisko* and *embankment* inspected in those documents appear as a boundary landmark in Prusimski's boundary line (Section 4, ff. 667v–680). The sworn inquiry concerning the *stawisko* submitted to the 1776 commission (sealed at f. 715) is likely the record arising from or related to the 1767 inspections, though the 1728 sworn inquiry is separately identified.

The five Tribunal and condescension decrees cited in document 9 (1761, 1766, 1767, 1768, 1770) are all adjudicated individually in document 10's closing section (ff. 710–714). See the Gap Map (Section D) for detail.

---

### Document 13 (1782) citing earlier corpus documents

**→ Document 10 (1776):** The corner mounds from which the 1782 field execution proceeds are identified in Section 1 as those "established and raised from the ground in the pine forest near the road to Pyzdry... by commission decree sanctioned by force of law by the Estates of the Realm, issued in 1775 on the 3rd of October." This is the 1776 commission decree. The 1782 Piotrków Tribunal decree (Section 2) approves the post-commissarial proceedings flowing from that decree.

**→ Document 11 (1780):** Section 1's reference to the contravention "die undecima mensis 7bris anno 1780... in fundo bonorum Grab" (11 September 1780, Monday after the Feast of the Nativity of the BVM) is the same condescension and contravention recorded from the Konin land court's own side in document 11. Document 13 §2c states that, as a consequence, Prusimski was ordered to pay fourfold fourteen Polish marks (half to Chełmski, half to the court); document 11 records a penalty in the same proceeding, though expressed differently ("two [marks?], by half" — the figures are not straightforwardly reconcilable and the discrepancy has not been resolved).

---

### Document 11 (1780) citing earlier proceedings

**→ Document 10 (1776), inferred:** Document 11 states that Chełmski obtained "to his advantage, a contravention of the Decree of the Tribunal" arising from the Grab condescension of 11 September 1780. The Tribunal decree being contravened is not named in document 11 itself. Given the chronology, this is most likely the 1776 commission decree (document 10) or a post-commissorial decree flowing from it — but this is inference, not stated directly in document 11, and should not be treated as established.

---

### Document 12 (1781–1782) citing and cross-referencing other corpus documents

**→ Document 11 (1780):** The "Term of the Condescension in the settlement/estates of Grab," reported repeatedly in document 12 (ff. 398, 495v, 519, Nov 1781 – Jun 1782) as "being conducted, upcoming," is the same proceeding whose underlying 1780 contravention is recorded in document 11.

**→ Document 10 (1776):** Gorski's manifest (ff. 523–530v) describes his attempted execution of "the Crown Tribunal Post-Commissorial Decree at Piotrków... the 23rd day of the month of January in the present year [1782]... after the boundary cause was decided from the map and two Commissorial Decrees issued discrepantly and contrary to each other were resolved" — i.e. the post-commissorial sequel to document 10's 1775/76 commission.

**→ Document 13 (1782):** Two enrollment notices at f. 674v cross-reference material recorded in full in document 13: "Enrollment of a Condescensorial Boundary Decree between the estates of Trąbczyn and Łukom... with the description of the boundary mounds" corresponds to document 13 §§3–4, and "Delivery of the Honest Martin Hunt, Olęder of Trąbczyn... by the Illustrious Józef Łukaszewicz" corresponds to document 13 §6. Separately, the 7 December 1782 inspection of 221 felled pines (f. 673) describes felling "on the 28th day of November... during the Act of the Condescension of the two sessions" — the same field execution as document 13 §4 (30 November – 3 December 1782).

### Document 9 (1771 Kalisz) citing earlier proceedings

**→ Tribunal and field decrees (not in corpus):** Document 9 cites five instruments as the legal foundation for the delivery of possession. All five are in the Gap Map (Section D).

### Document 8 (1771 Gr. 115) citing earlier corpus documents

**→ Document 9 (1771 Kalisz):** Section 1 of document 8 is an *oblata* cross-reference to the delivery of possession instrument that constitutes document 9. The two documents record different stages of the same act: document 9 is the delivery instrument itself; document 8 Section 1 is its enrollment note in the Konin Municipal Court.

**→ A prior *manifest* (not in corpus):** Section 5 (the protest) cites a "previously lodged *manifest*" acknowledged as the legal basis of Chełmski's possession of the Lusnia *olędry*. This manifest is not yet in the corpus; it is likely one of the instruments enrolled at Konin municipal court in connection with the 1766–1768 Tribunal proceedings. See the Gap Map.

---

### Document 5 (c. 1762–1765) citing ongoing proceedings

**→ Ongoing Crown Tribunal case:** Document 5 refers to a boundary case "being heard undecided before the Crown Tribunal," which is the proceeding that produces the chain of Tribunal decrees cited in document 9 (1761 condemnation → 1766 Bydgoszcz → 1767 Poznań → 1768 Bydgoszcz → 1770 condescension). Document 5 thus sits immediately before that chain begins.

### Document 10 (1776) citing non-corpus documents submitted in evidence

The following documents were submitted to the 1776 commission as evidence and are explicitly described in Sections 3–5, but are not in the corpus:

- A 1443 compromise between the heir of Trąbczyn and the Ląd Convent concerning demarcation of Trąbczyn Wielki and Mały with the Convent villages, attested in the Konin land court records, filed in the Poznań municipal court on Friday after Saint Michael 1761.

- A *manifest* of 1733 (Friday after Low Sunday) in the Konin municipal court, concerning corner mound damage in the Grądzeń boundary with the Ląd Convent.
- Two 1644 default decrees (Monday after the Octave of Corpus Christi) in the Konin land court: one against Jerzy Chełmski and others, co-heirs of Łukomia, Imielno, and Łomowo, concerning demarcation from Trąbczyn; one against the Ląd Convent concerning demarcation of Drzewce from Trąbczyn.
- Two 1644 motions decrees (Monday after Saint Hedwig) in the Konin land court, concerning the demarcation of the same estates between the same parties.
- A copy of the 1581 boundaries between Szetlew and Szetlewek, submitted by Maci Jaroszewski.
- A copy of the 1693 partition among the Trąbczyński family of Trąbczyn, Trąbczyn Mały or Nowa Wieś, Osiny, Łazy and others, from the Pyzdry municipal court, submitted by Urszula Fulwarska.
- A compromise decree of 1761 (Thursday after Sunday *Misericordiae*) between Chełmski and the Ląd Convent, filed in the Kalisz municipal court on Monday after Sunday *Cantate*.
- A judicial site inspection of 1592 (cited in Prusimski's boundary line argument as describing the position of the corner mounds relative to the Pyzdry road).
- Three prior field decrees of 1759 and 1760 — see the Gap Map.

## C. Recurring Physical Features

Each feature is identified with the documents that describe it, its role in those documents, and the consistent terminology to be used.

---

### The *stawisko* and *embankment* (*grobla*)

The water-management infrastructure at the junction of Łukomia and Trąmpczyn is the single most persistent physical feature in the corpus.

| Doc | Date | Description |
|-----|------|-------------|
| 1 | 1589 | Thomas Łukomski's pond (constructed with other neighbours) floods Trąbczyn, Nowa Wieś, and Osiny, killing standing timber of oak, beech, and ash |
| 6 | Apr 1767 | A newly raised *embankment* at the end of the *stawisko*, built by Łukom people on their heir's mandate, turns back water and floods Trąmpczyn *olędry*, fields, and meadows |
| 7 | Aug 1767 | The same *embankment* inspected again; raised "some years earlier" by the heirs of Łukomia, now repaired; the pond waters cause overflows onto the grounds and meadows of four Trąmpczyn *olędry* |
| 8 §3 | Dec 1771 | The *embankment* dug up by more than thirty Trąmpczyn subjects in Prusimski's presence: fourteen structural components cut with axes — enclosure elements, posts, sluice gates, channels — demolished; the water and fish let out |
| 10 | 1776 | The *stawisko* appears as a boundary landmark in Prusimski's boundary line (Section 4, ff. 667v–680); the sworn inquiry of 1728 concerning the *stawisko* is among the four sealed and delivered to Prusimski; a dam (*tama*) is also mentioned in the boundary description |
| 13 | 1782 | The *stawisko* appears as a terrain feature in the mound-erection record (Section 4); alder and oak stakes are driven through the marshy section at the *stawisko* in place of mounds |

**Terminology:** *stawisko* always retained in Polish. The adjacent active pond is *staw* (= **pond**). The structure built across it is the *grobla* (= **embankment**). Its regulated outlets are *stawidła* (= **sluice gates**).

### The three corner mounds (Trąbczyn / Łukomia / Drzewce junction)

| Doc | Date | Description |
|-----|------|-------------|
| 10 | 1776 | The starting point of the commission: three mounds in a triangle, of differing visibility (the first clear, the second less so, the third damaged), lying between the roads described in the act, in the pine forest near the Pyzdry road and the Zagórów–Drzewce–Kalisz road; their position is tested against the judicial site inspection of 1592 and against the three prior field decrees of 1759 and 1760; confirmed by the commission as the genuine convergence of Łukomia, Drzewce, and Trąbczyn (not Bukowe, Łukomia, and Drzewce, as Chełmski had argued at the three prior field inspections) |
| 13 | 1782 | The starting point (*terminus a quo*) of the wall boundary line; described as the three corner mounds established in 1775 and approved by the post-commissarial Piotrków Tribunal decree of 23 January 1782 |

**Terminology:** These are **corner mounds** (*kopce narożne*); in the 1782 document each is a *scopulus angularis*. The junction they mark is the **corner junction** (*angularitas*). The wall boundary proceeds from the **guardian of the corner junction** (the first wall mound in the series).

### Group 1: The three pre-commission field decrees (high priority)

All three are cited in Prusimski's boundary line argument in document 10 (ff. 226–229) as the basis for the acknowledged corner mounds at the three prior field inspections (*kondestensje*).

**Field Decree 1 (1759)**
- Adjudicated by two Sieradz Boundary Surveyors
- Filed in the Pyzdry municipal court on Thursday after the Sunday called *Cantate* in 1759
- Subject: the boundary between Łukomia, Drzewce, and Trąbczyn at the corner mounds

**Field Decree 2 (1760)**
- Made in the field on Monday after the Sunday called *Exaudi* 1760
- By the Łęczyca Boundary Surveyor
- In the Drzewce boundary with Trąbczyn
- Filed in the Konin municipal court on Friday, the very Feast of Saint Anthony of Padua (13 June) 1760
- Subject: the corner mound and Drzewce–Trąbczyn boundary

**Field Decree 3 (1760–1761)**
- Made in the field on Monday after Saint Bartholomew (24 August) 1760
- By the Łęczyca and Gostyń Boundary Surveyors jointly
- Filed in the Konin municipal court on Monday after the Sunday called *Jubilate* 1761
- Subject: as above; from the same set of three field sessions

*Note:* Document 10 also references an associated Piotrków Tribunal decree of Thursday after Saint Martin 1760, which preceded and authorised at least Field Decree 2.

### Group 2: The Tribunal condescension and penalty chain (high priority)

All five are cited in document 9 (1771 Kalisz) as the legal foundation for the delivery of possession, and all five are individually adjudicated in document 10 (1776).

**Condemnation of 1761 (Monday after Saint Bartholomew)**
- Default judgement obtained by both Chełmski brothers against the late Antoni Prusimski (uncle)
- Filed in the Konin Municipal Court on Wednesday after Saint Matthew 1761
- Document 10 finds it unlawfully obtained (past the nephew who was already of full age) and overturns it

**Bydgoszcz Tribunal Condescension Decree of 1766 (Tuesday before Saint Giles)**
- Issued at Bydgoszcz in the ordinary general sessions of the Crown Tribunal
- Decreed as the primary matter: demarcation of the estates of Łukom and Trąpczyn
- Document 10 finds Prusimski contravened it but releases him from the full penalty, as the decree was subsequently superseded

**Poznań Tribunal Decree of 1767 (Thursday after the Exaltation of the Holy Cross)**
- Issued in the ordinary general sessions of the Crown Tribunal at Poznań
- Document 10 notes this decree was "overturned by the Bydgoszcz Tribunal decree of Monday after the Visitation of the Blessed Virgin Mary of 1768" and adjudges a half-double pledge to Chełmski for the related default judgement

**Bydgoszcz Tribunal Condescension Decree of 1768 (Monday after the Visitation of the Blessed Virgin Mary)**
- Issued at Bydgoszcz in the ordinary general sessions
- The last and decisive Tribunal condescension decree in this chain
- Document 10 finds Prusimski contravened this decree, and orders him to pay three starost's pledges to Chełmski

**Condescension Session of 1770 (Monday after Sunday *Cantate*)**
- On-site condescension session; result enrolled at the Kalisz Municipal Court on Monday after the Feast of the Eleven Thousand Holy Virgins 1770
- Related default judgement filed at Kalisz municipal court on the same date
- Document 10 resolves this contravention and default judgement together with the 1768 Bydgoszcz decree
