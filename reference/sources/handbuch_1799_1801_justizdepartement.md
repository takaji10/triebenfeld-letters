## Findings

**1. "Massow".** "Hr. v. Massow" (the handbook gives no forenames; Julius Eberhard is the name the literature gives the minister, not this source) is attested in all three volumes as "wirkl. Geh. Staats- und Justizminister" (appointed to the Staats-Rath 26 March 1798), head of the Lutheran spiritual department and, in the Justice Department, the minister who "hat das Special-Departement von den Provinzen Südpreufsen und Neu-Ostpreufsen". That fits a note of October 1799 in a South Prussian suit that speaks of a collection Goldbeck sent to the writer: in 1799 South Prussia lay with Massow, not with Goldbeck, who had East and West Prussia, Middle March, Uckermark and Prignitz. The other Massows in the volumes are a court marshal, a forest master, a provincial director and an estate owner; none is in the Justice Department. Uncertain: the Handbuch gives offices, not signatures, so the match rests on the signature reading "Massow" and on the department; the Handbuch does not itself show that Massow wrote the note.

**2. "Willenz".** No name in the three volumes reads "Willenz" or "Wilenz". Candidates:

- v. Scheibler, Geh. Ober-Justiz- und Tribunalsrat: the vortragender Rat at Massow's department for South and New East Prussian justice matters. Fits the office of "councillor who drafted the department's decisions" on a South Prussian matter; the letters do not resemble "Willenz".
- Wilcke / Wilke (Geh. Sekretär, Neu-Ostpreuß. department of the General-Directorium; also Geh. Oberrevisions- und Kammergerichtsrat; Syndicus): letters close at the start (Wil-c/k-e); wrong bodies (finance, court), and no "-nz".
- Wilkens (Geh. Kriegsrat, General-Controlle; Criminalrat; Commerz-Agent): letters moderately close; wrong department.
- Wernitz (Geh. Sekretär / Kriegsrat of the Geheime Staatskanzlei, expedient for Silesia; Wernitz jun. at the archive for East, New East and West Prussia): shares initial W and the ending "-nitz/-enz", has "r" where "Willenz" has "ll"; the Silesia desk, not South Prussia; the Staatskanzlei executes Justice papers.
- Kunowski (expedition of South Prussia) and Poll (extradent for South Prussia), Staatskanzlei: the clerks for South Prussian papers; letters do not fit.

No printed name fits both the letters and the office. By office alone the one councillor in the South Prussian department is v. Scheibler; by letters the nearest are Wilcke/Wilke and Wernitz. Uncertain: the reading of the signature itself (to be compared with the manuscript for Scheibler, Wilcke, Wernitz); whether an "ita conclusum" signer was a Rat or a chancery secretary; whether the signer was a junior official not printed in the Handbuch. The 1798 volume was not read.
