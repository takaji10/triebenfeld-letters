# Who's Who

### People named in the letters (and the letter 303 register), von Triebenfeld / Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen correspondence

## Scope and method

Every entry below is a person who actually appears in the letters corpus (`von_Triebenfeld_Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen_cleaned.txt`, including letter 303, the liquidation register) — using the canonical spellings already settled in `phase2_proper_noun_report.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`. Nobody who appears only in the 2018 Seidel biography and not in the letters is included here, per instruction.

For each person, identity is sourced one of two ways, and every entry says which:

- **Book-confirmed** — found in *Friedrich Ludwig, Fürst zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen* (2018, Seidel), cross-checked against letter context (dates, nearby names, described role) for a plausible match. Per standing project policy, the book's own *proper-noun spellings* are not treated as authoritative (its author made his own editorial choices there) — but its biographical and factual content is treated as reliable secondary-source material, which is what's being cited here.
- **Externally confirmed** — a real, documented historical figure, confirmed independently during Phase 2 (Wikipedia / Deutsche Biographie), not via the book.
- **Context-inferred** — no book or external match found. A role is inferred from how the letters (or the register) describe the person — a title, a profession, a relationship to someone else. **This is always flagged as uncertain**, not stated as fact, per instruction.
- **Role unclear** — neither the book, external record, nor letter context gives anything usable. Said plainly rather than invented.

Occurrence counts are approximate (regex hits on the canonical root, so include inflected forms). Distinct-people notes flag anywhere Phase 2 explicitly resolved a similar-looking name as a *different* person — so this document doesn't reintroduce a confusion the project already settled.
