### **Orlina Duża Lutheran Cemetery**

Graves: 57, plus a handful without identification

The cemetery at Orlina Duża is the largest of the cemeteries in the area, and also includes a low stone wall that surrounds the property. There are signs of upkeep, along with a contemporary wooden cross that stands in the cemetery. Many headstones are well-preserved and still standing upright. There appeared to be a separate section for infants. Notably, there is a larger unique carved stone gravestone for the Jursch family, which mentions a birth of Gustav Jursch in Pleschen (Pleszew), located 20 kilometres south. Gustav Jursch appears to be the oldest person found in the survey of cemeteries, with a birth year from the 18th century (1797). The three headstones of the Jesse family are also unique in that they appear to be recently repainted*—*the letters are painted gold, and the background of the nameplates is white, and a layer of glass has been added to cover the face of the nameplates. The headstones also have inscriptions on the backside, similarly painted and protected with glass.

The original cantor’s house still stands and is located across the road from the cemetery; its original function indicated by a metal cross affixed to its roof. The building appears to be inhabited, and is constructed of bog iron masonry and features carved wooden doors which may be original to the building.
