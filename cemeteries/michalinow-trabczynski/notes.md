# Michalinów Trąbczyński Lutheran Cemetery

From the editor's survey of the Evangelical cemeteries of the
Trąbczyn/Zagórów area (`reference/cemeteries/source/cemeteries.md`), one
visit in 2023 (the editor's word). The account is the survey's own
paragraph, kept as written. The German account is Claude's.

Headstones: 81 rows read (two stones carry two names, the survey's "&":
Munk Bronislawa/Emanuel and Zache Julius/Hermann, the names and their dates
in the survey's order); the survey counts 83 graves plus some without
nameplates. The asterisked numbers are the survey's genealogical
identifiers (the editor, 2026-10-26: kept) — Amandus Celmer (604), Natalie
Zelmer (316), the (315) inside the stone's own inscription. The survey's
figure references ("Fig. 28", "Fig. 29") are left off the page (the
editor, the same day).

The place: the register's `michalinow-oledry`, the gazetteer's
Michalinów Trąbczyński (the entry records the name's history).
