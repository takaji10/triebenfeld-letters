# Łazińsk Pierwszy Lutheran Cemetery

From the editor's survey of the Evangelical cemeteries of the
Trąbczyn/Zagórów area (`reference/cemeteries/source/cemeteries.md`), one
visit in 2023 (the editor's word). The account is the survey's own
paragraph, kept as written; its footnote marker for the sign's source moved
to the cemetery.yml sources list. The German account is Claude's.

Headstones: five rows read; the survey counts 7 graves. The asterisked
number after Eduard Zellmer's name is the survey's genealogical identifier
(the editor, 2026-10-26: kept); the survey's "(Fig. 25)" reference is left
off the page (the editor, the same day).

The place: the survey's Łazińsk Pierwszy. The gazetteer carries the two
Łazińsk villages in one entry (`lazinskie-oledry`) until the editor's split
into Pierwszy and Drugi is approved; the page links that entry's item on
the Places page meanwhile.
