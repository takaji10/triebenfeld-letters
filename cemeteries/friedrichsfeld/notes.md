# Friedrichsfeld

From the editor's survey of the Evangelical cemeteries of the
Trąbczyn/Zagórów area (`reference/cemeteries/source/cemeteries.md`), one
visit in 2023 (the editor's word). The account is the survey's own
paragraph, kept as written. The German account is Claude's.

Headstones: two rows read; the survey counts fewer than five graves.

The place: the gazetteer's `tarszewo` — the colony founded as Kolonia
Friedrichsfeld by Friedrich Ludwig, Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, and
renamed Tarszewo in 1891. The AGOFF article series names a "Der
evangelische Friedhof in Tarszewo" and a "...in Chruściki" (Adelhof) —
whether the edition wants them is open (NEEDS_CONFIRMATION, "The
cemeteries").
