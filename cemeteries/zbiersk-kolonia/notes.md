# Zbiersk Kolonia

From the survey of the Evangelical cemeteries of the
Trąbczyn/Zagórów area (`reference/cemeteries/source/cemeteries.md`), one
visit in 2023 (the editor's word). The account is the survey's own
paragraph, kept as written. The German account is Claude's.

Headstones: eight rows read (the first stone names no person — the
"Ruhestate der Familie Heinrich"); the survey counts about ten graves and
allows there may be more.

The place: the gazetteer's own entry since 2026-10-26 (`zbiersk-kolonia`,
drafted from this survey at the editor's word). The survey's own account
says why the cemetery is in it: Zelmers from the Trąbczyn area moved to
Zbiersk Kolonia and Łyczyn.
