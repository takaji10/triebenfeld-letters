# Zbiersk Kolonia

From the editor's survey of the Evangelical cemeteries of the
Trąbczyn/Zagórów area (`reference/cemeteries/source/cemeteries.md`), one
visit in 2023 (the editor's word). The account is the survey's own
paragraph, kept as written. The German account is Claude's.

Headstones: eight rows read (the first stone names no person — the
"Ruhestate der Familie Heinrich"); the survey counts about ten graves and
allows there may be more.

The place: the gazetteer does not carry Zbiersk Kolonia yet; the entry is
drafted from this survey and waits on the editor's eye
(NEEDS_CONFIRMATION, "The cemeteries"). The page names the place without a
link meanwhile. The survey's own account says why the cemetery is in it:
Zelmers from the Trąbczyn area moved to Zbiersk Kolonia and Łyczyn.
