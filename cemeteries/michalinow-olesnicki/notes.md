# Michalinów gm. Oleśnica

From the editor's survey of the Evangelical cemeteries of the
Trąbczyn/Zagórów area (`reference/cemeteries/source/cemeteries.md`), one
visit in 2023 (the editor's word). The account is the survey's own
paragraph, kept as written. The German account is Claude's.

Headstones: none read — the survey found no descriptive nameplates, so the
page has no table.

The place: the register's `michalinow-olesnicki`, kept distinct from
Michalinów Olędry (Michalinów Trąbczyński) and from Oleśnica; the survey's
"gm. Oleśnica" is the commune's name. The gazetteer carries the place.
