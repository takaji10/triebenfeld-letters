# Wrąbczynkowskie Holendry Lutheran Cemetery

From the survey of the Evangelical cemeteries of the
Trąbczyn/Zagórów area (`reference/cemeteries/source/cemeteries.md`), one
visit in 2023 (the editor's word). The account is the survey's own
paragraph, kept as written. The German account is Claude's.

Headstones: twenty rows read; the survey counts 36 graves. Rudolf
Celmer's asterisked number (313) is the survey's genealogical identifier
(the editor, 2026-10-26: kept); the survey's "(Fig. 31)" reference is left
off the page (the editor, the same day).

The place: the gazetteer's `wrabczynkowskie-holendry` (the colony Szczepan
Turna established in 1781).
