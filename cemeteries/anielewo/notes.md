# Anielewo (Ingelfingen)

From the survey of the Evangelical cemeteries of the
Trąbczyn/Zagórów area (`reference/cemeteries/source/cemeteries.md`), one
visit in 2023 (the editor's word). The account is the survey's own
paragraph, kept as written. The German account is Claude's.

Headstones: ten rows read; the survey counts 11 identifiable graves and a
few unidentified.

The place: the gazetteer's `anielewo` — the village founded as Kolonia
Ingelfingen by Friedrich Ludwig, Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, and
renamed Anielewo in 1891.
