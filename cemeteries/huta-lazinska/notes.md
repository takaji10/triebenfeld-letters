# Huta Łazińska Lutheran Cemetery

From the survey of the Evangelical cemeteries of the
Trąbczyn/Zagórów area (`reference/cemeteries/source/cemeteries.md`), one
visit in 2023 (the editor's word). The account is the survey's own
paragraph, kept as written. The German account is Claude's.

Headstones: eleven rows read; the survey counts 15 graves. Rosalie's row
carries the survey's footnote: the reading comes from the AGOFF article
"Der evangelische Friedhof in Łazińsk Drugi" (Jürgen Frantz, 2016;
members-only — docs/MISSING_SOURCES.md), and the survey notes the stone was
not found when visiting in 2023.

The place: the gazetteer's `huta-trabczynska`, which records "Also known as
Huta Łazińska".
