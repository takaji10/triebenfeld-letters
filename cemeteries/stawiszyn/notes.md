# Stawiszyn

From the editor's survey of the Evangelical cemeteries of the
Trąbczyn/Zagórów area (`reference/cemeteries/source/cemeteries.md`), one
visit in 2023 (the editor's word). The account is the survey's own
paragraph, kept as written. The German account is Claude's.

Headstones: three rows read — only the three Zelmer graves were recorded;
the stones' own spellings ("unzre", "unzer") stand as read. The survey
notes the cemetery's use continued after World War II.

The place: the gazetteer does not carry Stawiszyn yet; the entry is drafted
from this survey and waits on the editor's eye (NEEDS_CONFIRMATION, "The
cemeteries"). The page names the place without a link meanwhile.
