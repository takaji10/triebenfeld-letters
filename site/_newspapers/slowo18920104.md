<div class="lit-en" lang="en" markdown="1">

Sales of estates. The Tomisławice estate, in the powiat of Turek, 17  
włókas of land, was bought from Mr Repphan by Mr Lutostański for 31,000  
rs. The Trąbczyn estate, in the powiat of Słupca, 150 włókas of land, was  
bought from Count Miączyński by Countess Władysławowa Wielopolska for  
225,000 rs.
