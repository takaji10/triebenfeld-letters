<div class="lit-en" lang="en" markdown="1">

Hitlerized colonists

The neighbourhood of Zagórów — that is, the rural communes of Oleśnica,  
Trąbczyn and Szymanowice — is inhabited by a fair number of German  
colonists. The colonies they live in once bore German names, e.g.  
Emilienhain, Ludwigslust and so on. Since Hitler took power in Germany,  
something, it would seem, has turned over in this population's heads. For  
example, when conducting business at the commune office they make  
themselves heard not in Polish but in German. Then again, the children of  
these colonists who attend school answer in German when they are greeted  
with the words "dzień dobry" (good day). Recently an incident occurred: on  
the arrival of the school inspector at one of the schools, where an  
Evangelical taught, not one of the German colonists' children came to  
school. The teacher of that school was dismissed from his post after the  
incident.
