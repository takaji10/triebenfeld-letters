Twenty-nine entries on thirty-seven pages of the register of the municipal court of Konin for 1780 to 1782, kept in the State Archive in Poznań (Archiwum Państwowe w Poznaniu). They carry the quarrel between Antoni Prusimski of Trąbczyn and Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski of Łukomia to its decision by the Crown Tribunal and to the attempt to mark the boundary on the ground, and they show two other suits of Prusimski's in the same years. They are in Latin, three of them in Polish, and are twenty-nine documents. <!-- context -->

### Historical background

Since 1775 two boundary decrees had stood against each other: that of the parliament's commission, for Prusimski, and that of the commissioners Chełmski had brought, for Chełmski: <a href="{{ '/sources/app53170koningr145/' | relative_url }}">53/17/0/-/Konin Gr.145</a>, <a href="{{ '/sources/app53170koningr116/' | relative_url }}">53/17/0/-/Konin Gr.116</a>. In 1778 each side was still protesting against the other's: <a href="{{ '/sources/app53170koningr117/' | relative_url }}">53/17/0/-/Konin Gr.117</a>. The entries here show how it ended. The Crown Tribunal at Piotrków decided the cause "from the map" and ruled on the two decrees, and sent an officer to raise the boundary mounds along a line it had drawn on that map. The Tribunal's decree itself is not included. <!-- context -->

Most entries of a register like this one are one-line notes that a paper was brought in and lies among the papers produced; here sixteen of the twenty-nine are. The papers themselves were kept apart and are not in the book. <!-- context -->

### Contents

**The boundary: the Tribunal's line on the ground, 1782.** On 18 March 1782 a decree of a sitting on the ground, about the Olęder settlement belonging to Trąbczyn, was brought in <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/11/' | relative_url }}">11</a>]</span>. In June the boundary chamberlain of Inowrocław, Jakub Górski, protested: he had come, engaged by Prusimski, to carry out the Tribunal's decree of 23 January between Trąbczyn Minus or Nowa Wieś and Łukom and to raise the mounds along the line drawn on the map. Chełmski in person pushed away the man with the surveyor's chain, kicked the chain off the line and threatened, and Górski stopped to ask the War Department for soldiers <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/17/' | relative_url }}">17</a>]</span>. Chełmski answered on 15 June that an executive officer has to carry out a decree of the Tribunal and not interpret it, that Górski had gone beyond it and had wrongly described him as resisting, and that his act was null <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/19/' | relative_url }}">19</a>]</span>. Chełmski had also announced a protest against the Tribunal's decree itself, which was never written <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/15/' | relative_url }}">15</a>]</span>, and cited Prusimski and Górski before the Tribunal <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/21/' | relative_url }}">21</a>]</span>; Prusimski cited Chełmski, the chamberlain of the land of Dobrzyń and the convent of Ląd <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/22/' | relative_url }}">22</a>]</span>. In December the boundary decree of the sitting on the ground, with the description of the mounds, was brought in <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/28/' | relative_url }}">28</a>]</span>, and so was the delivery of an Olęder settler of Trąbczyn, Martin Hunt <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/29/' | relative_url }}">29</a>]</span>. An inspection of a place on Trąbczyn's ground had been brought in for Prusimski in June <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/18/' | relative_url }}">18</a>]</span>.

**Wood cut across the new line, December 1782.** Prusimski had Chełmski cited before the municipal court of Kalisz for acts of violence <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/25/' | relative_url }}">25</a>]</span>. On 7 December his messenger reported 221 building pines felled on the Trąbczyn side of the line, by his account on 28 November, by Chełmski's people <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/26/' | relative_url }}">26</a>]</span>, and the Trąbczyn steward Leon Maszewski protested in Polish, naming the men of Łomów and Łukom who had come with sledges and axes <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/27/' | relative_url }}">27</a>]</span>.

**The convent of Ląd.** Prusimski had charged the Cistercians of Ląd, owners of Drzewce, with colluding with Chełmski at the boundary commission. On 22 November 1781 their administrator, Father Tesselin Malechowski, protested in Polish that the convent had acknowledged the meeting point of the three grounds as Chełmski showed it for the reasons in the commission's decree, and offered an oath <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/8/' | relative_url }}">8</a>]</span>. In January 1782 the abbot, Benedykt Lubstowski, and the convent cited Prusimski before the Tribunal to be cleared of the charge, relying on the inspection of 1592 <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/10/' | relative_url }}">10</a>]</span>; a further citation followed in September <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/24/' | relative_url }}">24</a>]</span>.

**The Tracholz debt and Grab.** The claim that Chełmski had bought from the merchant Tracholz was now to be heard at a sitting on the ground at Grab, the estate of the late Katarzyna Prusimska. Citations to it were entered for the land court judge Stefan Zielonacki and for Chełmski <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span> <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span> <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/9/' | relative_url }}">9</a>]</span>, and for Prusimski <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/16/' | relative_url }}">16</a>]</span>. One is given in full: Prusimski and the Bogdańskis, husband and wife, she the heiress of Katarzyna Prusimska, are to appear on 3 June 1782, and four subjects from Nowa Wieś are to be produced as witnesses <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/14/' | relative_url }}">14</a>]</span>. In August Prusimski's plenipotentiary asked the office whether a report of a citation of Chełmski's had been declared; none had <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/20/' | relative_url }}">20</a>]</span>.

**The succession to Prusimski's uncles.** Prusimski cited Kazimierz Zakrzewski and his sons, and the land court, before the Tribunal in October 1781 <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/4/' | relative_url }}">4</a>]</span> and again in April 1782 <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/12/' | relative_url }}">12</a>]</span>, and his plenipotentiary Piotr Gosławski protested against a decree of the land court that had refused him a delay <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/13/' | relative_url }}">13</a>]</span>. The citation of 21 September 1782 is given in full. It names those who claimed a share: the children and grandchildren of Marianna, Wiktoria and Konstancja Prusimska. And it sets out the plaintiff's own descent: son of Stefan Prusimski and Wiktoria Malczewska, grandson of Krzysztof Prusimski and Teresa Golińska, and sole successor of his uncles Antoni and Paweł, who died childless. He relies on a Tribunal decree of 1738 and argues that daughters whom their father has dowered can claim nothing from their brothers <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/23/' | relative_url }}">23</a>]</span>.

**Other citations and a protest.** Notes record a protest by Prusimski against Chełmski <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/3/' | relative_url }}">3</a>]</span>, two citations of Prusimski by Chełmski <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/5/' | relative_url }}">5</a>]</span> <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/7/' | relative_url }}">7</a>]</span>, and one by the former and the present owner of Biskupice <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr118/6/' | relative_url }}">6</a>]</span>.

**Notes on the entries.** Fifteen entries have no dated heading of a sitting above them and are dated from the headings before and after, some only to the year; each says so. One heading gives a weekday and a day of the month that do not agree, and the weekday is followed. One note is unfinished, and one entry is a heading with nothing written under it. <!-- context -->

Antoni Prusimski appears here as "Ostroróg Prusimski" and as knight of the Order of St Stanislaus. The citation of September 1782 is the only document that names his parents and grandparents. <!-- context -->

### Transcription

The transcription is the editor's own. The Latin abbreviations are written out. One note on a photographed page that the editor's text lacked was transcribed from the scan by Claude. Claude then compared the whole transcription with the scans and corrected it where the page is plain.

### Translation and summaries

The English is the editor's own translation; Claude changed it where the corrected text required it. Claude wrote the summaries, and a separate pass checked every statement against the document; a statement the document does not support was cut or weakened.
