After the reform of 1864 made the peasants of the Kingdom of Poland owners of the land they farmed, officials drew up a liquidation table for every estate, fixing the land each farmstead received and the rights it kept in the landowner's forest. The file that the peasant-affairs office at Kalisz kept on the liquidation tables of the Trąbczyn estate shows how those decisions were argued over. The edition gives the file's index and five of the papers it lists: the plan of the village of Trąbczyn of 1865, two petitions of the owner, Count Włodzimierz Miączyński, against the commissioners' decisions, and two petitions of the colonists of Łazińsk for their old rights in the forest. <!-- context -->

### Historical background

The ukases of 19 February (2 March) 1864 made the peasants of the Kingdom of Poland owners of the land they farmed, and kept for them their servitudes, the rights of pasture, wood and litter in the manor's land. Commissioners for peasant affairs, each over a district, settled who was entitled to what, and appeals went to the commission at Kalisz and to the Constituent Committee and its Central Commission in Warsaw. The liquidation table of each village recorded the result; the table of the Łazińsk colony, approved in 1868, is in <a href="{{ '/sources/apl399806603/' | relative_url }}">39/98/0/-/6603</a>; Daniel and Wilhelm Hohm, who sign and petition here, are probably the Daniel and Wilhelm Холмъ among its colonists. <!-- context -->

### Contents

The index, in Russian, lists 145 papers with their dates and numbers, the number of their leaves and the folio where each begins <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/apl399801333/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. Its first papers are of 1864 and 1865: petitions of the peasants of Trąbczyn, Nowawies and Marianton and of Count Miączyński, the resolutions of the Commissioner of the 5th District of 13/25 May 1865 in copies and translations, the drafts of the commission's minutes and of its letters to the military district chiefs of Konin and Kalisz. From 1867 come the orders of the Central Commission and the reports of the commissioners and of the Słupca district commission; the last papers, of 1869 and 1870, are written in a second hand <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/apl399801333/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

The plan of the village of Trąbczyn, drawn on 15 May 1865, shows the gardens and buildings of the peasants and of the manor; it is given as an image <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/apl399801333/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>.

On 23 July 1865 Miączyński asked the commission to annul Baron von Rosen's decision granting Krystian Lippeldt, of a neighbouring village, pasture for 2 horses and 7 head of horned cattle on the folwark Włodzimirów, at three hollows called "smugi" on the boundary of Emilienhejm. Lippeldt had grazed there by written leave, under a contract that ended on 1 November 1863 and a new agreement of 1 May 1864, which he had given up; the owner held that a stranger who had not used the pasture at the time of the ukase had no right to it <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/apl399801333/4/' | relative_url }}">4</a>]</span>. On 10/22 August 1865 he appealed against the decision of the Commissioner of the 5th District of 13/25 May 1865, which ordered six farmsteads of Nowawies, of 5 Morgen each, returned to the peasants who had held them. He wrote that those peasants had left of their own will before 1856, that he had settled new peasants on land from the folwark, and that the old land was now under the folwark's crop rotation <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/apl399801333/3/' | relative_url }}">3</a>]</span>.

The colonists of Łazińsk wrote to the Constituent Committee on 6/18 December 1867 for the recognition of their servitudes of pasture in the Trąbczyn forest, litter and stumps, and asked for an impartial official <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/apl399801333/5/' | relative_url }}">5</a>]</span>. On 10/22 August 1868 they wrote again: under Prince Hohenlohe's privilege of 1805 they might graze in the Studzienka forest section, rake litter, and use 6 Morgen of clay land; the owner had taken these from them since 1838, and had brought false witnesses before Commissioner Władimirow <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/apl399801333/6/' | relative_url }}">6</a>]</span>.

The index gives these papers at its numbers 30, 59, 75, 102 and 112; the editor photographed them from the file <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/apl399801333/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. The resolution the commission wrote in the margin of the petition of 1865, in Russian, is only partly read <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/apl399801333/3/' | relative_url }}">3</a>]</span>.
