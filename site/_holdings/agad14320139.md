Two annual reports for 1882 in German, kept in the Central Archives of Historical Records in Warsaw (Archiwum Główne Akt Dawnych) among the papers of the consistory of the Evangelical-Augsburg Church in Warsaw. Pastor E. Erdmann of Zagórów wrote them on the form the consistory sent to every parish of its district: one on the parish of Zagórów, one on its filial Lissewo-Pyzdry. <!-- context -->

### Historical background

The Evangelical-Augsburg Church in the Kingdom of Poland, under the Russian emperor, was governed by a consistory at Warsaw, divided since 1849 into dioceses under superintendents, with a General Superintendent at their head. Kneifel's history of the church lists the parishes of the Kalisch diocese, among them "Zagörow with the filial Lissewo-Pyzdry", and names Ignatius Karl Wilhelm Boerner as superintendent of the Płock diocese (Kneifel 1964, p. 123). <!-- context -->

Each year the parishes answered the consistory's questions on their church buildings, schools, church life, morals, confirmations and collections. The German colonists on the Trąbczyn and Zagórów estates, whose schools and prayer houses appear in the reports, are the settlers of the Olęder colonies: <a href="{{ '/sources/app539680669/' | relative_url }}">53/968/0/-/669</a>. <!-- context -->

### Contents

Both reports answer an order of the consistory of 30 October, No. 267, and follow the same headings: church buildings (I, a), church life (I, b), remarkable events (II), confirmands (III), candidates in the congregation (IV) and collections (V) <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>, <a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>. The second report repeats the first almost word for word in its later sections.

**Document 1, the parish of Zagórów.** The congregation has a brick-built church that still lacks a baptismal font, organ and bells; the pastor's house and the cantor's house are very small and low <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. Besides the religion school at Zagórów there are seven more schools with prayer houses, one of them an elementary school at Michalinowo; the eight schools have about 240 children <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. The congregation has eight cemeteries <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. The communicants are given as 726 men and 871 women, 1,597 in all <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. The mission collection of 10 roubles 71 kopecks was passed on to Superintendent Pastor Manitius at Warsaw <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. The report names no Baptists, Irvingians or other sects, no special vices and no conversions to other confessions, no remarkable events, 67 confirmands (36 boys and 31 girls) and no candidates in the congregation <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. For other purposes 4 roubles 61 kopecks was collected for grammar-school pupils intending to study theology and 2 roubles 36 kopecks for the widows' and orphans' fund, sent to Superintendent von Boerner <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

**Document 2, the filial Lissewo-Pyzdry.** The filial has neither a church nor a sexton's house; divine service at Pyzdry is held in a rented room <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>. It has five schools with prayer houses, with about 120 children, and five cemeteries, fenced and with crosses <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>. The communicants are 326 men and 408 women, 734 in all, 131 more than in 1881 <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>. The Evangelicals of Pyzdry would like a school of their own but are too poor, the town counting only nine families <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>; the colonies have their own teachers and prayer houses <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>. The mission collection was 3 roubles 40 kopecks; there were 23 confirmands (13 boys and 10 girls) <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>. For students of theology 1 rouble 76 kopecks was collected <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>.
