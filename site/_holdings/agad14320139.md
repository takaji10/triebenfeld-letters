Every year the parishes of the Evangelical-Augsburg (Lutheran) Church in the Kingdom of Poland answered a set of questions from the church's consistory in Warsaw. For 1882 Pastor E. Erdmann of Zagórów answered them for his parish and for its filial congregation of Lissewo-Pyzdry: the churches, schools, cemeteries and congregations of the German colonists around Trąbczyn. <!-- context -->

### Historical background

The Evangelical-Augsburg Church in the Kingdom of Poland, under the Russian emperor, was governed by a consistory at Warsaw, divided since 1849 into dioceses under superintendents, with a General Superintendent at their head. Zagórów, with its filial at Lissewo-Pyzdry, belonged to the diocese of Kalisz (Kneifel 1964, p. 123). <!-- context -->

Each year the parishes answered the consistory's questions on their church buildings, schools, church life, morals, confirmations and collections. The German colonists on the Trąbczyn and Zagórów estates, whose schools and prayer houses appear in the reports, are the settlers of the Olęder colonies: <a href="{{ '/sources/app539680669/' | relative_url }}">53/968/0/-/669</a>. <!-- context -->

 Besides the school at Zagórów, where the children were taught their religion, there are seven more schools with prayer houses
**Document 1, the parish of Zagórów.** The congregation has a brick-built church that still lacks a baptismal font, organ and bells; the pastor's house and the cantor's house are very small and low <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. Besides the religion school at Zagórów there are seven more schools with prayer houses, one of them an elementary school at Michalinowo; the eight schools have about 240 children <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. The congregation has eight cemeteries <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. 726 men and 871 women took communion, 1,597 in all <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. The collection for missions, 10 roubles 71 kopecks, went to Superintendent Pastor Manitius at Warsaw <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. No Baptists, Irvingians or other sects had appeared, no one had left for another church, and nothing remarkable had happened; 36 boys and 31 girls were confirmed <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. For other purposes 4 roubles 61 kopecks was collected for grammar-school pupils intending to study theology and 2 roubles 36 kopecks for the widows' and orphans' fund, sent to Superintendent von Boerner <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

**Document 2, the filial Lissewo-Pyzdry.** The filial has neither a church nor a sexton's house; divine service at Pyzdry is held in a rented room <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>. It has five schools with prayer houses, with about 120 children, and five cemeteries, fenced and with crosses <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>. The communicants are 326 men and 408 women, 734 in all, 131 more than in 1881 <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>. The Evangelicals of Pyzdry would like a school of their own but are too poor, the town counting only nine families <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>; the colonies have their own teachers and prayer houses <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>. The collection for missions was 3 roubles 40 kopecks; 13 boys and 10 girls were confirmed <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>. For students of theology 1 rouble 76 kopecks was collected <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/agad14320139/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>.
