In 1806 Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, through his agent Peter Friedrich von Triebenfeld, let a parcel of the Trąbczyn forest to the settler Christoph Erbet in hereditary lease. Five years later, under the Duchy of Warsaw, the file went to the peace court at Konin to make Erbet pay the costs still owed on it. <!-- context -->

### Historical background

Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen held the Trąbczyn estates in South Prussia by a royal grant of 1796. As lord of the estates he maintained a patrimonial court, which sat at Mariantów and recorded contracts made with the settlers. His general agent, Peter Friedrich von Triebenfeld, let parcels of forest and waste land in hereditary lease: the lessee paid a sum on entry and a fixed annual rent, and held the land heritably. After 1807 the district belonged to the Duchy of Warsaw, and its courts worked in Polish. <!-- context -->

### Contents

On 31 March 1806, before the estate's patrimonial court, Triebenfeld granted Christoph Erbet of the Trąbczyn Hauland one Hufe, Magdeburg measure, in the Trąbczyn forest for 100 Rthl entry money and an annual rent of 30 Rthl <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app5371057/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. Because the land was wholly uncleared, he was to pay no rent for the first three years; the first payment, of 15 Rthl, fell due at Christmas 1809. The court had the contract drawn up for both parties in May 1806 <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app5371057/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

In 1811 an official, Hawich, handed the file to the peace court of the Konin district, the lowest civil court of the Duchy, and asked that Erbet be made to pay 9 Rthl 18 dgl in costs <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app5371057/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.
