In October 1777 the land court at Konin gave two decrees in the quarrel between Antoni Prusimski of Trąbczyn and Stanisław Chełmski of Łukom. The first orders Prusimski to enter at Konin, within four weeks, the boundary decree of 1775 given for him; the second sends a claim for money, which Chełmski had bought from the merchant Tracholz, to a hearing on the ground of Trąbczyn. The reading is made from a microfilm and is uncertain in places. <!-- context -->

### Historical background

The commission that fixed the boundary between Trąbczyn and Łukomia ruled for Antoni Prusimski, and its decree was entered in the court records at Brześć Kujawski, in another province, in September 1776: <a href="{{ '/sources/app53170koningr145/' | relative_url }}">53/17/0/-/Konin Gr.145</a>. A judgment took its public force from being entered in the books of a court, and by law in the province where the land lay. Stanisław Chełmski of Łukomia, who had lost, went looking for the entry. A court office searched its books on request and certified what it found. One of his searches survives: at Kalisz, on 23 November 1776, nothing was found: <a href="{{ '/sources/app53150kaliszgr424/' | relative_url }}">53/15/0/-/Kalisz Gr.424</a>. <!-- context -->

The copy of the commission's decree that survives is the one in the book of the municipal court of Konin. This decree of the land court is the likely reason it is there. <!-- context -->

The land court at Konin was a court of the nobility. The starost or his deputy, as the king's lieutenant, had the leading part in it, with the judge, the sub-judge, the voivode, the chamberlain and the standard-bearer. The court travelled round the palatinate and held its sittings in the district; the terms at which it sat were called "roczki". It was competent in every suit in which a nobleman was the defendant, except those assigned to other courts, such as the dietine courts or the royal court, and except uncontested business. In 1792 its competence passed to a new land court, the "sąd ziemiański". <!-- context -->

### Contents

**The claim.** At the land court's sitting at Konin on 20 October 1777 Stanisław Chełmski, heir of Łukomia, demanded that Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, produce the "so-called boundary act" between Łukom and Trąbczyn that the commissioners appointed by parliament had given on his side. Searches made for Chełmski at the court offices of Kalisz in November 1776, Kościan in January 1777 and Konin in October 1777 had found that it was not registered in its own province <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app536046/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

**The answer.** Prusimski answered with a search of his own, made at the municipal court of Brześć in Kuyavia on 10 October 1777 and entered at Konin on 16 October. It shows that the commission's decree, between the estates of Trąbczyn Minus or Nowa Wieś, Łukom and Drzewce, was entered at Brześć in September 1776 <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app536046/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

**The decree.** Rights, settlements and decrees must be brought in for entry in their own province. The court therefore orders Prusimski to bring the decree to the municipal court records at Konin within four weeks, on pain of outlawry <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app536046/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

**The debt bought from Tracholz.** In a second suit between the same parties Chełmski sued under a transfer acknowledged at the municipal court of Pyzdry in 1768, by which the merchant Jan Tracholz made over to him sums he claimed from the late Paweł Prusimski, and demanded the contract for the sale of the pine wood of Trąbczyn. The court is shown that a decree of its own of 1766, between Tracholz and Antoni Prusimski, had sent the cause to a hearing on the ground, and that this was never held. It suspended the matter and fixed a hearing of the court on the ground of Trąbczyn for the Monday after Laetare Sunday 1778, at which Prusimski was to produce the contract <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app536046/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>. The summons to that hearing names the Monday after Invocavit Sunday, three weeks earlier: <a href="{{ '/sources/app53170koningr117/' | relative_url }}">53/17/0/-/Konin Gr.117</a>. <!-- context -->

The court's deputy judge, Stefan Leszczyc Zielonacki, was one of the five commissioners who had adjourned the boundary cause in September 1776: <a href="{{ '/sources/app53150kaliszgr425/' | relative_url }}">53/15/0/-/Kalisz Gr.425</a>. <!-- context -->
