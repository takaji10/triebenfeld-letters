One entry on two pages of a book of decrees of the land court at Konin for the years 1773 to 1777, kept in the State Archive in Poznań (Archiwum Państwowe w Poznaniu). It is a decree of the sitting of October 1777 on where the boundary decree of the commission had to be entered. It is in Latin and is one document. The reading is made from a microfilm and is uncertain in places. <!-- context -->

### Historical background

The commission that fixed the boundary between Trąbczyn and Łukomia ruled for Antoni Prusimski, and its decree was entered in the court records at Brześć Kujawski, in another province, in September 1776: <a href="{{ '/sources/app53170koningr145/' | relative_url }}">53/17/0/-/Konin Gr.145</a>. A judgment took its public force from being entered in the books of a court, and by law in the province where the land lay. Stanisław Chełmski of Łukomia, who had lost, went looking for the entry. A castle office searched its books on request and certified what it found; such a search is a "requisitio". One of his searches survives: at Kalisz, on 23 November 1776, nothing was found: <a href="{{ '/sources/app53150kaliszgr424/' | relative_url }}">53/15/0/-/Kalisz Gr.424</a>. <!-- context -->

The copy of the commission's decree that survives is the one in the book of the castle court of Konin. This decree of the land court is the likely reason it is there. <!-- context -->

### Contents

**The claim.** Stanisław Chełmski, heir of Łukomia, plaintiff, through Melchior Krzyzanowski, against Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, cited and present in person. Chełmski demands that Prusimski produce a certain act, "as though a boundary act", given between the estates of Łukom and Trąbczyn by commissioners appointed by the Estates and brought in by the Starost. He lays down three searches for it: at the castle court of Kalisz on the Saturday after the Presentation of the Virgin 1776, made by Antoni Mierzejewski in his name; at Kościan on 2 January, made by Antoni Smoliński; and at Konin in October of the current year, made by himself. By them he proves that the act was not in the records of its own province <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app536046/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

**The answer.** Prusimski, to free himself from the demand, lays down a search of his own, made at the castle court of Brześć in Kuyavia on 10 October 1777 and entered at Konin on 16 October. It shows that the commission's decree, between the estates of Trąbczyn Minus or Nowa Wieś, Łukom and Drzewce, was entered at Brześć in September 1776 <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app536046/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

**The decree.** Rights, settlements and decrees must be brought in for entry in their own province. The court therefore orders Prusimski to bring the decree to the castle records at Konin within four weeks, on pain of outlawry <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app536046/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

### Form and language

The entry is no. 16 of its sitting. It begins halfway down leaf 182 verso and fills the page; for want of room the clerk wrote its end at the foot of the other side of the same leaf, under entry no. 15, and put a direction to it at the foot of the page. The Latin is in a small hand that abbreviates nearly every word, and the images are frames of a black and white microfilm. The transcription writes the abbreviations out where the word is sure and marks four places where it is not. <!-- context -->

The entry has no date of its own. The heading of the sitting, which the editor transcribed from a page that is not among the images, reads: "Actum in Judiciis Terrestribus Palatinatus Calissiensis Districtualibus Feria Secunda post Festum Sanctae Hedvigis Viduae Electae Dei videlicet Die 20 mensis Octobris Anno 1777 in Conin celebratis", before Franciszek Wierusz Walknowski, judge, Stefan Leszczyc Zielonacki, deputy judge, and Franciszek Ksawery Mikorski, notary, of the land of Kalisz. The deputy judge is one of the five commissioners who had adjourned the boundary cause in September 1776: <a href="{{ '/sources/app53150kaliszgr425/' | relative_url }}">53/15/0/-/Kalisz Gr.425</a>. <!-- context -->

### Scans

The folder has two frames of a black and white microfilm, each showing two facing pages. Both were cut at the fold. The entry begins on the left page of the second frame and ends at the foot of the right page of the first; those two pages are shown.

### Transcription

The editor transcribed the entry from the film and supplied it by paragraph, with about thirty marks of doubt in it: the hand is small and abbreviates nearly every word. The editor's file also has the heading of the sitting, from a page not among the images, and the entry that follows, no. 17, which is not translated and is not given here.

### Check against the scans

An AI model (Claude) read the whole entry against the film, enlarged, and corrected ten passages. The film shows the words but not every ending, so the document is marked as a rough transcription. Three corrections change the sense: the opening is the usual formula of a decree; it is Chełmski who lays down three searches and proves that the act was not in the records of its own province; and what Prusimski lays down is a search for the decree, made at Brześć Kujawski on 10 October 1777. The clerk's direction to the other page was added. Four places stay marked as not surely read. Each change is logged with its reason.

### Division and dating

The entry is one document. It has no date of its own. The heading of its sitting gives 20 October 1777 as the day the sitting opened; the decree mentions a search made later that month, so only the year is claimed.

### Translation

The editor's English had been made from the first reading and marked ten places as unresolved. Claude revised it throughout to follow the corrected reading, keeping the editor's terms. The editor's notes, each on a word then unread, are not used.

### Summary

The summary was written in German from the Latin and translated into English. Each of its seven statements was then checked against the document. One was weakened, because the day of one search is not surely read; the rest held.
