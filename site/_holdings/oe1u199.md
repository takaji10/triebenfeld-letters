A charter of Friedrich Wilhelm III, King of Prussia, issued at Berlin on 19 December 1805. It confirms the family entail founded by Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen (1746-1818), a Prussian general, in his will of 7 October 1805. The holding consists of this one document, on fifteen pages, and is kept in the Hohenlohe-Zentralarchiv Neuenstein. <!-- context -->

### Historical background

Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen owned estates and ironworks in Silesia and, by a royal grant of 1796, estates in South Prussia, the province formed from Prussia's share of the partitions of Poland. An entail bound the named properties to a fixed order of succession: they passed undivided to one heir and could not be sold or divided. The charter gives the foundation the King's confirmation as sovereign. <!-- context -->

### Contents

The charter opens with the King's full title and recites an extract of the will, certified by a court at Wrocław <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/oe1u199/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. The extract places under entail the houses of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen in Wrocław and the park at Szczytniki; the estate of Bytków; the lordship of Koszęcin with Boronów, Olszyna, Harbultowitz and Cieszowa; and the South Prussian lordships of Zagórów, Trąbczyn, Pszczew, Wieniec and Bątkowo <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/oe1u199/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. A further clause determines the succession if the male line ends: the third daughter, Auguste, receives the South Prussian estates, Adelheid the Silesian ironworks, and Emilie Koszęcin <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/oe1u199/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>. The charter closes with the ratification, the King's reservations and an order to the authorities <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/oe1u199/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.
