The quarrel between Antoni Prusimski of Trąbczyn and Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski of Łukomia went on into the later 1780s. In 1785 a subject of the Trąbczyn estate showed the court the injuries of a beating on the road; in 1786 Chełmski pressed on with the suit over the debt he had bought from the merchant Tracholz, begun nine years before. <!-- context -->

### Historical background

Chełmski had bought from the merchant Jan Tracholz a claim that went back to a contract of 1744 for oak timber with Paweł Prusimski, Antoni's uncle, and had pursued it since 1777 through the land court, sittings on the ground and the Crown Tribunal: <a href="{{ '/sources/app53170koningr117/' | relative_url }}">53/17/0/-/Konin Gr.117</a>, <a href="{{ '/sources/app53170koningr118/' | relative_url }}">53/17/0/-/Konin Gr.118</a>. Prusimski had always answered that the papers were not in his hands. In 1786 Chełmski said that the contract itself had stayed with Prusimski. <!-- context -->

A person who had been beaten could come to the court office and have his injuries seen and recorded, as proof for a later suit. <!-- context -->

### Contents

**The beating of Andrzej Kinos.** On 14 March 1785 Andrzej Kinos of Trąbczyn showed the office a bloodshot and swollen right eye, a bloodshot left eye, a swollen face and a swollen right shoulder, and stated that Marcin Sarzyński had beaten him with fists and a stick the Saturday before, barring his way on the public road at Obory as he went to Pleszew <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr120/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

**Chełmski's summons of October 1786.** Chełmski summoned Prusimski before the Crown Tribunal at Piotrków. Prusimski was to swear to and produce the contract for the sale of an oak wood made with Tracholz, which Chełmski said had stayed with him; to be punished for having had the case moved from the judge sitting on the ground to the full land court and on to the Tribunal; and the land court's orders for a sitting on the ground were to be carried out <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr120/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>.
