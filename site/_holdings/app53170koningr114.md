Water was one of the weapons in the quarrel between Trąbczyn and Łukom. In 1767 a court messenger reported twice to the municipal court at Konin, for Antoni Prusimski of Trąbczyn, that the people of Łukom had raised an embankment at the pond on the boundary, and that the water held back by the embankment was flooding the fields and meadows of the Olęder settlers in the woods of Trąbczyn. <!-- context -->

### Historical background

By 1767 Trąbczyn belonged to Antoni Prusimski, later Starost of Niszczewice. In its woods, towards the boundary with Łukom, he and his predecessors had settled Olęder farmers. On that boundary lay the pond-ground (stawisko), the bed of a former pond, which both estates claimed. An embankment across its outlet held the water back onto whoever lay upstream. The commission of 1775 judged the pond-ground to be Trąbczyn's and awarded Prusimski damages for the flooding: <a href="{{ '/sources/app53170koningr145/' | relative_url }}">53/17/0/-/Konin Gr.145</a>. <!-- context -->

The municipal court and office of Konin was the starost's court for the nobility of the district. Its office, open every day, registered the business they brought to it, sales, debts, the reports of the court's messengers and their suits, and what was entered in its books was accepted as proof. <!-- context -->

### Contents

**April 1767.** On 22 April the court messenger Bartłomiej Szepczyński of Trąbczyn declared at Konin that on Holy Saturday, at the request of Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, heir of Trąbczyn, he had gone onto the estate with two noblemen, Stanisław Lipiński and Józef Rudkowski. He saw an embankment lately raised at the end of the pond-ground. Because of it the water that runs from the pond-ground through the Trąbczyn lands had backed up and flooded the Olęder settlers living in the Trąbczyn woods, with their manured fields and their meadows. He stated that people of the neighbouring estate of Łukom had done this on the order of its heir <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr114/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

**August 1767.** On 6 August he reported again. On the Friday after Saint James, with the noblemen Józef Gołembiewski and Rudkowski, he had seen the embankment in the wood on the pond-ground, by the boundary of the Łukom lands but raised on Trąbczyn ground. The water dammed in the pond spilled onto the fields and meadows of the Trąbczyn Olęder settlers, so that the meadows of four of them lay under water and could not be mown. He and the noblemen declared that the embankment had been raised some years before by the heirs of Łukomia and had now been repaired <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app53170koningr114/2/' | relative_url }}">2</a>]</span>.
