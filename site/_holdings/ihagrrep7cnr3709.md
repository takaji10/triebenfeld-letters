A file of the Prussian central administration for South Prussia, kept in the Geheimes Staatsarchiv Preußischer Kulturbesitz, Berlin. According to its cover it concerns the complaints of Michalina von Prusimska in her case against Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen (1746-1818), and runs from 18 June 1800 to 29 January 1802. The file has not yet been transcribed. Its pages are shown as scans only. <!-- context -->

### Historical background

In 1796 the King of Prussia granted Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen estates in South Prussia that had been confiscated from the Starost Prusimski after the uprising of 1794. Michalina Prusimska was his daughter. In 1807, after Prussia had ceded the province, a Polish commission transferred the estates to her. <!-- context -->

### Contents

The contents have not been read. From the scans the file appears to hold a petition to the King of June 1800, royal orders of June and July 1800 to the government at Poznań, reports of that government, a signed letter of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen dated Wrocław, 30 November 1800, drafts of the ministry of December 1800, and a petition in French of January 1802 signed by Prusimska. A description of the case will follow once the text has been transcribed and read. <!-- context -->

### Scans

The archive photographed the file as 22 images: a cover and 21 openings of two facing pages. Two blank openings were left out on the editor's instruction. Fifteen openings with one blank side were cut at the fold to the written side, at a line the editor placed or approved. An opening with writing on both sides was left whole and counts as one page.

### Transcription

None yet. Each page carries the note "(not transcribed)" beside its scan. The editor will supply the transcriptions.
