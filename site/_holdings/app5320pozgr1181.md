The brothers Antoni and Paweł Prusimski died without children, and their estates had to be divided between their nephew Antoni Prusimski, the owner of Trąbczyn, and the heirs of their three sisters. In March 1784 the castellany court of Poznań sat at the manor of Trąbczyn to settle the division for good, going back through the family's divisions, debts and title deeds to the early seventeenth century. The record breaks off in mid-sentence, with nothing after it in the volume <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app5320pozgr1181/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

On one side stood Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, starost of Niszczewice, knight of the Order of St Stanisław, the brothers' nephew and heir; on the other the Zakrzewski, Tomicki, Taczanowski, Kołaczkowski and Tworowicz families, descended from the three daughters of Krzysztof Prusimski and Teresa Golińska. The Cistercian abbey of Ląd was summoned too: the family had deposited 3,500 Hungarian ducats and its jewels and silver in the abbey's treasury, and the abbey was to produce the receipt for them <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app5320pozgr1181/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

First, Ignacy Zakrzewski had sworn at Konin in December 1783 that his side had produced all the documents the Crown Tribunal had ordered in 1782 <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app5320pozgr1181/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

Then the court went through the history of the inheritance. In 1740 the brothers had divided their father's estates into three shares: Kolno and Kamionna, valued at 250,000 Polish złoty; Trąbczyn with its villages; and Wierzbno and Brzezno, each share with the debts charged on it. The court traced the family's property from a bond of 1700 and the death of Ludwik Prusimski, childless, at Kamionna in 1708, through the dowries paid to the three daughters and their receipts, back to the gifts to churches charged on their mother's estates since 1616 <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app5320pozgr1181/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

Then it decided. The mother's estate, the Golińska inheritance, worth 98,379 złoty 5 groszy clear of debts, was to be shared equally by all six children, three sons and three daughters, at 16,396 złoty 15 groszy each. The father's estates went to the starost as his uncles' heir: Paweł's share at 154,436 złoty 24½ groszy and Antoni's at 154,936 złoty 24½ groszy, each less its part of the mother's inheritance. Two old debts, the widow Nierzychowska's 30,000, settled in 1740, and Puchalski's 10,000, presumed paid, were not counted into the estate, so the sisters' heirs could claim no share of them. The Radoliński family's claim to 43,000 złoty on Trąbczyn rested on a letter of 1736 that the other side said Andrzej Radoliński had not written; the case had been sent to the court at Gniezno and had stood undecided since 1777, and the court left the claim at the 18,000 listed until that court decided it <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app5320pozgr1181/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

Last, the court turned to what the brothers had given away outside the division of 1740: the 40,000 złoty secured in 1745 on Peczyska and Dubno for Paweł's widow, Katarzyna, born Rozdrażewska; the sitting held at her estate of Grab on 3 June 1782 with Ludwik Bogdański, chamberlain of Kalisz, and his wife Teresa, born Rozdrażewska, her heiress; and, where the record breaks off, Ewa Potocka, born Maniewska, widow of the lieutenant-general Joachim Potocki <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app5320pozgr1181/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

The same inheritance and the same neighbours run through the Konin court books of these years, <a href="{{ '/sources/app53170koningr115/' | relative_url }}">53/17/0/-/Konin Gr.115</a>, <a href="{{ '/sources/app53170koningr116/' | relative_url }}">53/17/0/-/Konin Gr.116</a>, <a href="{{ '/sources/app53170koningr117/' | relative_url }}">53/17/0/-/Konin Gr.117</a>, <a href="{{ '/sources/app53170koningr118/' | relative_url }}">53/17/0/-/Konin Gr.118</a> and <a href="{{ '/sources/app53170koningr153/' | relative_url }}">53/17/0/-/Konin Gr.153</a>, and through the copy-book of the abbey of Ląd, <a href="{{ '/sources/app5314550221/' | relative_url }}">53/1455/0/2/21</a>. <!-- context -->
