In 1820 the elected heads of the old Olęder colony on the Trąbczyn estate engaged Samuel Adolph as the teacher of the colony's own school, giving him a dwelling, land and grain, and fees for teaching the children, for confirmations and for funerals. <!-- context -->

### Historical background

The estate's settlers were Olęders, colonists who held their land by contract with the owner; their villages kept German schoolmasters, and the schoolmaster's pay came from the village board and the parents, not from the manor. The historian Breyer visited the colonies a century later and wrote down what the settlers remembered of their schools and their teachers: <a href="{{ '/sources/app539680669/' | relative_url }}">53/968/0/-/669</a>. <!-- context -->

After 1815 the estate lay in the Kingdom of Poland, under the Russian emperor, and belonged to Michalina Prusimska, who had recovered it in 1807 and was later Countess Miączyńska: <a href="{{ '/sources/agad1174016/' | relative_url }}">1/174/0/1/6</a>. <!-- context -->

### Contents

Tropiczyner Holland is the Olęder colony later called Łazińsk, the Trąbczyńskie Stare Olędry. On 14 March 1820 its village board, the headman (Schulze) Johann George Lindner and his two assessors, Christoph Rimpel and Samuel Gellert, took on Samuel Adolph, a teacher from Borwiezer Holland, who had offered to take over their school; they had examined him and found him fit <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app539680782/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

The colony gave him a free dwelling, three Morgen of land and some meadow for the school, the land tilled for him by the community, and five Viertel of grain and half a Viertel of seed. The parents paid 3 groschen for a child learning from the ABC to reading and 6 for writing and reckoning; every child confirmed gave him a present; he was paid for burials, more for an adult than for a child; every householder gave him an offering at Christmas, Easter and Whitsun; and on his rounds of the colony he was given 6 groschen at New Year and 3 on Maundy Thursday, in Polish money <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app539680782/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.

Adolph bound himself to keep the Sunday school and teach the children as an honest man; the board engaged him for one year and bound itself to keep its side <span class="cite">[<a href="{{ '/documents/app539680782/1/' | relative_url }}">1</a>]</span>.
