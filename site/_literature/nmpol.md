Not a work read whole but a citation file: the 23 entries of the Nazwy miejscowe Polski that the gazetteer cites, one per heading with its volume and page, in the corrected transcription of 2026-10-27. Each entry carries the dictionary's own wording – the settlement's status, its administrative seat, its distance from the market town, its houses and inhabitants, the dated forms of its name, and the etymological note where the dictionary gives one. The companion english.md is a summary of each entry, not a translation. The dictionaries themselves are not republished here; their texts stay whole in the private place-name repository.
