<h2 class="lit-loc"><a href="#p-159">p. 159</a></h2>
<div class="lit-orig" lang="la" markdown="1">

LXXVII.

STANISLAUS LOCHOWSKI, Nobilis Minoris Poloniae, isque Castri Opocznensis in Satrapia Sandomiriensi Notarius, VLADISLAO IV regnante, iuris disciplina in Polonia maxime enituit. Scriptis hisce testantibus:

I.) REGULÆ IURIS, ET LOCI COMMUNES FORENSES. Quorum de Iure et Praxi Communi tritus et versatus in Foro Usus: et Allegatio. Auctore STANISLAO LOCHOWSKI Castri Opoczn. Notario.

POSNANIÆ, e Typographeo ALBERTI REGULI, 1636, et 1642, 12. Ibidem, sine Typographi indicio, 1643, pari forma. CRACOVIÆ, ex Officina FRANCISCI CÆSARII, 1644: filiique eius cognominis, 1686, 1691, et 1701, 8o. Eademque in Urbe, Typis Haeredum FRANCISCI CÆSARII Junioris, 1737, forma similis.

Cracoviensem editionem anni 1701, Litteratores Lipsienses in ACTIS ERUDITORUM, MENSIS MAII, MDCCII. p. 197 - 199, merita cum laude recensuerunt. Eandemque Princeps Iosephus Alexander IABLONOWSKI, Palatinus Novogrodensis, in MUSEO POLONO, p. 145, indicavit. Cui ceteræ editiones, reliquaque LOCHOWSKII opera haud innotuerunt.

II.) PROCESSUS IURIS LIMITANEI, REGNI POLONIÆ. Auctore STANISLAO LOCHOWSKI Castren. Opoczen. Notario. CRACOVIÆ, in Officina Typographica FRANCISCI CÆSARII. Anno Domini 1641. 4.

</div>
<div class="lit-en" lang="en" markdown="1">

LXXVII.

STANISLAUS LOCHOWSKI, a nobleman of Lesser Poland and notary of the castle of Opoczno in the satrapy of Sandomierz, distinguished himself above all in the discipline of law in Poland in the reign of Władysław IV, as these writings of his bear witness:

I.) RULES OF LAW, AND THE COMMON FORENSIC PLACES. On whose law and common practice he was experienced and versed in their use in the forum: and the Allegatio. By Stanisław Lochowski, notary of the castle of Opoczno.

Posnania, at the press of Albertus Regulus, 1636 and 1642, duodecimo. The same, without the printer's name, 1643, in the same format. Cracow, from the press of Franciscus Caesarius, 1644: and of his son of the same name, 1686, 1691 and 1701, octavo. And in the same city, at the press of the heirs of Franciscus Caesarius Junior, 1737, in a similar format.

The Cracow edition of the year 1701 the learned men of Leipzig reviewed, with deserved praise, in the Acta Eruditorum, in the month of May 1702, pp. 197-199. The same edition Prince Joseph Alexander Jabłonowski, Palatine of Nowogródek, pointed out in the Museo Polono, p. 145. Beside it, the other editions, and the rest of Lochowski's works, never became known.

II.) THE BOUNDARY PROCESS OF THE LAW OF THE KINGDOM OF POLAND. By Stanisław Lochowski, notary of the castle of Opoczno. Cracow, in the printing shop of Franciscus Caesarius. In the year of the Lord 1641. Quarto.

<section class="lit-passage" id="p-160">
<h2 class="lit-loc"><a href="#p-160">p. 160</a></h2>
<div class="lit-orig" lang="la" markdown="1">

Librum hunc perutilem, Nobilibusque Polonis pernecessarium: sed inventu olim in Polonia ipsa difficillimum; aetate nostra MARTINUS PACIORKOWSKI bis curavit recudendum. Qui tamen, oppresso LOCHOWSKII auctoris veri nomine, temere atque insolenter opus sibi ipsi tribuit, inscriptione eiusmodi praefixa:

REGULA PROCESSUS GRANICIALIS CAMPESTRIS, REGNI POLONIÆ. Secundum Praxim moderni sæculi, ad dislimitanda Bona Regalia, Spiritualia, et Terrestria.

Ad usum publicum, cum Additamento Legum et Constitutionum Regni.

Auctore MARTINO PACIORKOWSKI, Officii Castrensis Opocznensis Subdelegato jurato. Typis Clari Montis CZENSTOCHOVIENSIS. 1749. 4. Lublinique, typis S. R. M. Collegii Soc. Jesu. 1760. eadem forma.

III.) DE CORRECTURA IURIUM, ET PROCESSUS IUDICIARII TERRESTRIS COMPENDIO: Brevis Discursus. Auctore STANISLAO LOCHOWSKI Castren. Opoczen. Notario. CRACOVIÆ, in Officina Typographica FRANCISCI CÆSARII. Anno Domini 1641. 4. Liber summe rarus.

IV.) COMITIORUM regni Poloniæ generalium FORMA, VIS, ET EFFECTUS. CRACOVIÆ, in Officina Typographica FRANCISCI CÆSARII, Anno a partu Virginis 1644. 4.

Libellus rarissimus. Praemissa ad CHRISTOPHORUM SULOWSKI, Ravensem Palatinum, Epistolam: auctor ipse, STANISLAUS LOCHOWSKI, Castri Opocznensis Notarius, Miedzyborze xi mensis Ianuarii, MDCXLIIII. subscripsit.

</div>
<div class="lit-en" lang="en" markdown="1">

This book, most useful, and most necessary for the nobles of Poland, but once so hard to come by in Poland itself, in our own age MARTINUS PACIORKOWSKI twice had printed again. Yet he, suppressing the true author Lochowski's name, rashly and insolently claimed the work for himself, prefixing an inscription of this kind:

RULE OF THE FIELD BOUNDARY PROCESS OF THE KINGDOM OF POLAND, ACCORDING TO THE PRACTICE OF THE PRESENT AGE, FOR THE DELIMITATION OF ROYAL, ECCLESIASTICAL AND TERRESTRIAL ESTATES.

FOR PUBLIC USE, WITH AN ADDITION OF THE LAWS AND CONSTITUTIONS OF THE KINGDOM.

BY MARTINUS PACIORKOWSKI, SWORN SUB-DELEGATE OF THE MUNICIPAL COURT OFFICE OF OPOCZNO. AT THE PRESS OF JASNA GÓRA, CZĘSTOCHOWA. 1749. QUARTO. AND AT LUBLIN, AT THE PRESS OF THE COLLEGE OF THE SOCIETY OF JESUS OF HIS SACRED ROYAL MAJESTY. 1760. IN THE SAME FORMAT.

[T.N. 1] Janocki quotes the title of the first edition of 1749. The edition's copy is the second edition of 1776, whose title page adds "Editio secunda ab ipso authore aucta" (second edition, enlarged by the author himself) and omits Janocki's "Ad usum publicum, cum Additamento Legum et Constitutionum Regni" as a separate line; the edition's English renders "Terrestria" as "noble estates". Janocki's dates for the two editions are 1749 and 1760; the edition's copy is dated 1776.

III.) ON THE CORRECTION OF THE LAWS, AND A COMPENDIUM OF THE TERRESTRIAL JUDICIAL PROCESS: A BRIEF DISCOURSE. By Stanisław Lochowski, notary of the castle of Opoczno. Cracow, in the printing shop of Franciscus Caesarius. In the year of the Lord 1641. Quarto. A book exceedingly rare.

IV.) THE FORM, FORCE AND EFFECT OF THE GENERAL ASSEMBLIES OF THE KINGDOM OF POLAND. Cracow, in the printing shop of Franciscus Caesarius, in the year from the Virgin's childbirth 1644. Quarto.

A most rare little book. The letter prefixed to it, to CHRISTOPHORUS SULOWSKI, Palatine of Rawa: the author himself, STANISLAUS LOCHOWSKI, notary of the castle of Opoczno, subscribed it at Miedzyborze on the eleventh day of the month of January, 1644.

</div>
