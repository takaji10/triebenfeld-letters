### Zmiany przestrzeni wsi w drugiej połowie XIX i na początku XX wieku na przykładzie wybranych map ze zbiorów Muzeum Narodowego Rolnictwa i Przemysłu Rolno-Spożywczego w Szreniawie

**Wojciech Mielewczyk**

Muzeum Narodowe Rolnictwa i Przemysłu Rolno-Spożywczego, Szreniawa

E-mail: w.mielewczyk@muzeum-szreniawa.pl

**Zarys treści:** Od połowy XIX stulecia aż po połowę XX wieku na ziemiach polskich na wsiach zachodziły zasadnicze zmiany społeczno-gospodarcze. Związane one były przede wszystkim z procesami uwłaszczeniowymi chłopów, ale także z prowadzonymi pracami melioracyjnymi, z komasacją gruntów rolnych, a także karczunkiem lasów pod nowe uprawy i tworzeniem nowych osad. Działania te powodowały szybkie i częste zmiany w przestrzeni wiejskiej.

Przemiany zachodzące na wsi znajdowały swoje odzwierciedlenie i udokumentowanie na opracowywanych współcześnie mapach wielkoskalowych, z których wiele znajduje się w Narodowym Muzeum Rolnictwa w Szreniawie. Mapy przedstawiają zmiany w użytkowaniu ziemi i zabudowie, tworzone nowe kolonie, a także przebiegi rowów odwadniających. Zmiany te zostały przedstawione graficznie, także przy użyciu odpowiedniej palety kolorów. Innym sposobem na zaznaczenie zmian było umieszczenie odpowiednich adnotacji zamieszczanych w wybranych częściach mapy. W wielu przypadkach zmiany są nanoszone odręcznie ołówkiem. Przedstawione mapy stanowią cenne źródło historycznych zmian w badaniach przestrzeni wiejskiej w XIX i XX wieku.

**Słowa kluczowe:** mapy wielkoskalowe, wieś, przestrzeń wiejska, reforma rolna, źródło historyczne

### 1. Przemiany na wsi w XIX wieku

### 1.1. Zabór pruski

W odniesieniu do terenów wiejskich wiek XIX oraz pierwsza połowa XX stulecia, były bogate w wydarzenia skutkujące zmianami w przestrzeni wsi na ziemiach polskich. Związane one były przede wszystkim z reformami agrarnymi wprowadzonymi przez władze państw zaborczych. Największe przekształcenia miały miejsce na wsiach w zaborze pruskim.

W wyniku przeprowadzonych w latach 1823–1865 reform uwłaszczeniowych i regulacyjnych ziemię nadano ogółem 88 918 gospodarstwom chłopskim

### Changes in rural space in the second half of the 19th and early 20th centuries as exemplified by selected maps from the collections of the National Museum of Agriculture and Agro-Food Industry in Szreniawa

**Wojciech Mielewczyk**

The National Museum of Agriculture and Agricultural-Food Industry, Szreniawa

E-mail: w.mielewczyk@muzeum-szreniawa.pl

**Summary of the contents:** From the mid-19th century until the mid-20th century fundamental socio-economic changes took place in the villages of the Polish lands. They were bound up above all with the processes of the enfranchisement of the peasants, but also with the drainage works carried out, with the consolidation of agricultural lands, and with the clearing of forests for new crops and the founding of new settlements. These actions caused quick and frequent changes in the rural space.

The changes taking place in the countryside found their reflection and their documentation on the large-scale maps drawn at the time, of which many are held in the National Museum of Agriculture in Szreniawa. The maps show the changes in the use of the land and in the buildings, the new colonies being created, and the courses of the drainage ditches. These changes were presented graphically, also with the use of a suitable palette of colours. Another way of marking the changes was to place suitable annotations in chosen parts of the map. In many cases the changes are entered by hand in pencil. The maps presented are a precious source of the historical changes in the study of the rural space in the 19th and 20th centuries.

**Key words:** large-scale maps, village, rural space, land reform, historical source

### 1. The changes in the countryside in the 19th century

### 1.1. The Prussian partition

With regard to the rural areas, the 19th century and the first half of the 20th were rich in events resulting in changes in the space of the village in the Polish lands. They were bound up above all with the agrarian reforms introduced by the authorities of the partitioning states. The greatest transformations took place in the villages of the Prussian partition.

The second period of changes in the countryside of the Prussian partition fell at the end of the 19th and the beginning of the 20th century. ### 1.2. Zabór rosyjski

W zaborze rosyjskim uwłaszczenie chłopów nastąpiło później i miało inny przebieg niż w zaborze pruskim. ### 1.2. The Russian partition

In the Russian partition the enfranchisement of the peasants came later and took a different course than in the Prussian one. The enfranchisement ukase of the tsarist authorities issued on 2 March 1864, "On the ordering of the peasants", gave the peasants as their property the land they used. This grant was not joined with the merging of the arable plots belonging to the individual holdings, the abolishing of the servitudes and the separation of the peasants' lands from the manorial ones. An example of a map with the lands of the enfranchised peasants marked on it is the map of the estates of Trąbczyn (today the gmina of Zagórów, the powiat of Słupca, the voivodeship of Wielkopolska) (fig. 4)<sup class="lit-fn"><a href="#n-en-8">8</a></sup>.

*Fig. 4. A fragment of the map of the estates of Trąbczyn of 1861 at the scale of 1:15 000 with the field of the enfranchised peasants marked. Reduced. Shelfmark AN 489/5*

In the face of the great fragmentation of the fields belonging to the individual peasant holdings, in the second half of the 19th century in the territory of the Kingdom of Poland there began to develop a consolidation from below, carried out by those interested themselves. By the outbreak of the First World War it covered an area of about 2.5 million ha.

### 2. The changes in the countryside in the interwar period

After the regaining of independence, among the most important matters requiring regulation in the countryside were: the carrying-out of the land reform, that is the parcellation of the great landed property and the granting of land to landless and smallholding peasants, and also the liquidation of the chessboard of lands and the carrying-out of melioration.

In the years of the Second Republic 2,654 thousand ha of land were parcelled in all, that is about 7% of all the agricultural lands in Poland. Thus the surface of the lands held in the possession of the great landed property diminished by more or less a third; the majority of these areas passed into peasant hands<sup class="lit-fn"><a href="#n-en-11">11</a></sup>.

The act introduced the right of excluding from consolidation the lands under buildings; for the moving of the buildings every owner had to give consent. On the other hand in the case of the consolidation of the lands of dwarf holdings the parcellation of the estate neighbouring on the village had to be carried out, to the end of making the holdings full-sized.

Of the about 12 million ha of lands requiring consolidation in the interwar period 5,423 thousand ha were merged, belonging to over 920 thousand holdings, chiefly in the central voivodeships<sup class="lit-fn"><a href="#n-en-18">18</a></sup>. The consolidation of the peasants' plots carried out was bound not only with the liquidation of the mid-field balks, but also with the change of the course of the access roads to the fields, and not seldom also with the removals of the peasants' holdings. The economic effects of the consolidation works were significant; as a result of the liquidation of the balks there came an increase of the area under cultivation of 3–9%.

### 3. Zakończenie

Od połowy XIX stulecia aż po połowę XX w. na ziemiach polskich na wsiach zachodziły zasadnicze zmiany społeczno-gospodarcze. Związane one były przede wszystkim z procesami uwłaszczeniowymi chłopów, ale także z prowadzonymi pracami melioracyjnymi, z komasacją gruntów rolnych, a także karczunkiem lasów pod nowe uprawy i tworzeniem nowych osad. Działania te powodowały szybkie i częste zmiany w przestrzeni wiejskiej.

Mapa, jako obraz powierzchni Ziemi, przedstawia wybrane obiekty i zjawiska w określonej przestrzeni geograficznej, a przede wszystkim w określonym czasie. Jednak wskutek nieustannych zmian mapy, zwłaszcza wielkoskalowe, prezentujące najdokładniej stan środowiska naturalnego i kulturowego, ulegają szybkiej dezaktualizacji. Powodowało to z jednej strony potrzebę częstego rysowania nowych map, ale z drugiej strony dla oszczędności czasu nanoszono adnotacje lub poprawki na mapy już istniejące. Ten sposób aktualizacji map wsi stosowany był już w drugiej połowie XIX w., czego przykłady można znaleźć na omówionych tu mapach. Jednak w przypadku map rysowanych ręcznie naniesione aktualizacje nie zawsze można odróżnić od pierwotnego zapisu: przy rysowaniu map stosowano różne style kaligrafii, a przy dopiskach nie umieszczano datacji. W tej sytuacji trudno jest ustalić czas zaistniałych zmian w przestrzeni wiejskiej. Dla uzyskania wiarygodnej informacji w tej kwestii konieczne byłoby sięgnięcie do innych źródeł: aktów nadania gruntów, adnotacji w księgach wieczystych i związanych z nimi akt oraz dokumentów katastralnych. Niemniej jednak mapy te pozostają bogatym źródłem historycznym do dziejów poszczególnych wsi, często, z braku innych dokumentów, jedynym.

Besides, consolidation made possible the introduction of more perfect ways of husbandry, owing to which the value of the land rose considerably, by at least 30%<sup class="lit-fn"><a href="#n-en-19">19</a></sup>. The results of the merging action were however short-lived — as a result of family and inheritance divisions there followed in a short time a renewed fragmenting of the holdings.

Examples of maps showing the consolidation of lands in the year 1930 in eastern Wielkopolska are the maps of the village of Błonawy (the gmina of Sompolno, the powiat of Konin, the voivodeship of Wielkopolska) (fig. 13) and of Lisewo (the gmina of Pyzdry, the powiat of Września, the voivodeship of Wielkopolska) (fig. 14).

### 3. Conclusion

From the mid-19th century until the mid-20th century fundamental socio-economic changes took place in the villages of the Polish lands. They were bound up above all with the processes of the enfranchisement of the peasants, but also with the drainage works carried out, with the consolidation of agricultural lands, and with the clearing of forests for new crops and the founding of new settlements. These actions caused quick and frequent changes in the rural space.

A map, as a picture of the surface of the Earth, presents chosen objects and phenomena in a defined geographical space, and above all in a defined time. Yet owing to the unceasing changes the maps, above all the large-scale ones, presenting most exactly the state of the natural and the cultural environment, quickly become out of date. This caused, on the one hand, the need to draw new maps often, but on the other hand, to save time, annotations or corrections were entered on maps already existing. This way of updating the maps of villages was already in use in the second half of the 19th century, and examples of it can be found on the maps discussed here. Yet in the case of maps drawn by hand the updates entered cannot always be told apart from the original record: in the drawing of maps various styles of hand were used, and in the added notes no date was placed. In this situation it is hard to fix the time of the changes that took place in the rural space. To obtain reliable information in this matter it would be necessary to reach for other sources: the acts of the granting of lands, the annotations in the land registers and the acts belonging to them, and the cadastral documents. Nevertheless these maps remain a rich historical source for the history of the individual villages, often, for the lack of other documents, the only one.

Muzeum Narodowe Rolnictwa i Przemysłu Rolno-Spożywczego, Szreniawa

E-mail: w.mielewczyk@muzeum-szreniawa.pl

**Summary:** From the mid-nineteenth century up to half of the twentieth century in polish villages became fundamental socio-economic changes. They were connected mainly with the process of enfranchisement of peasants, parceling out of large estates, as well as with the making melioration of agricultural land, the regrouping of fields and grubbing of forests for new fields, installing of new farms and colonies. As a result of these actions followed quick and significant changes in the rural space.

These processes are shown on great-scale maps of villages, many of which are located in the National Museum of Agriculture in Szreniawa. The maps presents the changes in the land and building the village, creating new colonies of settlement, as well as course of drainage ditches. These changes were presented graphically, also by using the appropriate color palette. Another way of marking changes was to place the appropriate annotations written on certain parts of the map . In many cases, changes are handwritten pencil drawing.

The presented maps make up to valuable historical source to research changes in the rural space in nineteenth and twentieth century’s.
