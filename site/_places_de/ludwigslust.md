<div class="place-desc" lang="en" markdown="1">

*Dated forms of the name:* *Mały Las*; *Kolonia Ludwigslust*.

Hamlet south of and belonging to Olchowo. The German colony *Kolonia Ludwigslust* was likely integrated into the smaller Mały Las settlement immediately south in 1891 as part of efforts by the Polish authorities to remove traces of previous Prussian occupation. A hand-drawn map from the 1990s by descendants/former residents of Olchowo listed them as two separate places (Map 26, Tab. 26). First appeared on Messtischblatt 1940. The settlement map found in the Lutheran church in Zagórów included a list of settlers, along with the text: “These are the names of the families who lived here as the last generation. The numbers are randomly assigned and are not identical to the official house numbers.” The Słownik geograficzny records it as a colony, powiat słupecki: until 1891 named *Ludwigslust*, afterwards in gmina Emilienheim (the 1891 renamings of the Łukom estate colonies).[^1]

[^1]: Słownik geograficzny, t. XVI, p. 298
