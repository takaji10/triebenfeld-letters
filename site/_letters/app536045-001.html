The heading of the sitting

Actum in Judiciis Terrestribus Palatinatus Calissiensis Districtualibus Feria Secunda post Festum Sanctissimae et Individuae Trinitatis proxima Anno Domini Millesimo Septingentesimo Sexagesimo Tertio in Conin in propria Cadentia Celebratis

Actum in Judiciis Terrestribus Palatinatus Calissiensis Districtualibus Feria Secunda post Festum Sanctissimae et Individuae Trinitatis proxima Anno Domini Millesimo Septingentesimo Sexagesimo Tertio in Conin in propria Cadentia Celebratis

Done at the district land courts of the palatinate of Kalisz, held at Konin in their own term on the Monday next after the feast of the Most Holy and Undivided Trinity in the year of the Lord one thousand seven hundred and sixty-three.

[p. 653v]
