# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 23
# translation by Claude

The penalty for the destruction of mounds between estates ^[*The penalty for the destruction of mounds between terrestrial estates.*] on both sides terrestrial: for each corner or particular corner mound, adhering to the statute of the Kingdom, three marks; for each wall mound, six *scuta*, reckoning the *scuta* at two groschen — with the summoning of the field Sub-Chamberlain's office for the restoration at the expense of the destroyer himself. (In the present day, however, larger penalties are extended according to the precedents of the decrees of the Crown Tribunal, having regard to the greater value of money.) ^[*A similar penalty against those who erect mounds without authority.*] Similarly against those who erect mounds without authority and out of turn, with the formal demolition and removal of the unlawfully erected mounds as a penalty.

The penalty, however, against leaseholders of royal estates ^[*The penalty between royal and terrestrial estates.*] who destroy the boundaries between royal and terrestrial estates: one hundred marks, with restoration at the expense of the destroyer through the engagement of commissioners. But against royal subjects who demolish boundaries: fifty marks, with the restoration thereof at the expense of the leaseholder of the royal estates, according to the intent of the constitution of the year 1578.

The penalty against a commissioner absent from the act of the commission, ^[*A penalty of 100 marks for a commissioner who is absent.*] after having signed the summons letters, is one hundred marks — saving the lawful impediment of illness.
