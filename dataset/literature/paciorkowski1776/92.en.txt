# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 92
# translation by Claude

stationed by the aforesaid plaintiff's party, at the instance of the above-named heir [name], plaintiff; and that You judicially attempt this matter of the delimitation of the aforesaid estates, and acknowledge Your boundary wall from Your hereditary estates of the village of [name] and Your corner point or particular corner point existing at the convergence of three estates, and respond to whatever by law shall be due. Certifying You that whether You appear at the fixed session or not, We shall nonetheless proceed to those matters that are incumbent upon Us from Our office and the prescription of the laws, against You — Your absence, contumacy, and unlawful opposition notwithstanding. *Given at Our place of residence on the [day]* etc.

Likewise the Royal Mandate of His Sacred Royal Majesty given to the same cited collateral heir of the village of [name], of the following tenor: AUGUSTUS THE THIRD etc. The title shall be written from the foregoing act.

To the Honourable [names], heir of the estates of the village of [name], faithfully beloved by Us. Honourable, faithfully beloved by Us. We have designated certain of Our commissioners to make boundaries between Our royal estates of the town of [name] and the villages belonging to it, presently leased to the Right Honourable or Honourable [names] and [name], Noble [names], and his hereditary estates on the other part. Since, however, the above-named hereditary estates of [name] of Your Fidelity are said to lie adjacent to those same estates to be delimited, We therefore command Your Fidelity
