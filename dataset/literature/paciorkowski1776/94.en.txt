# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 94
# translation by Claude

^[*The plaintiff counter-protests regarding the improper repulsion.*] The Honourable [names], plaintiff, counter-protested against the Honourable [name] regarding his improper repulsion of both the plaintiff himself and the commissarial court from the place of the founded jurisdiction, alleging that the boundary act which the collateral party produces does not bear upon this place of the founded jurisdiction at all, but upon another corner point and boundary wall of that same hereditary village; regarding which improper demonstration he solemnly protested. ^[*The Captain likewise protests, not acknowledging the corner point at the place of the founded jurisdiction.*] The Right Honourable Captain likewise protests, not acknowledging at the place of the founded jurisdiction the true corner point, nor the convergence of the three aforesaid estates, but elsewhere, where he offers and declares he will demonstrate it in his boundary path. Having noted the protests and counter-protests of the parties, he orders the registration of appearance for those same parties. The plaintiff's party with ^[*Appearance.*] the Honourable [names] in person; the Right Honourable [names], Captain of [name], and the Honest [names], advocate, and the whole community of the settlers of the aforesaid royal village, cited, in the assistance of their master, in person; the Honourable [names], heir of the collateral estates of the village of [name], likewise in person; appearing before his court and judicially contesting the matter between themselves. The Royal commissarial court of His Sacred Royal Majesty, finding the appearance of the parties sufficient, orders those same parties to proceed to trial in the present case.
