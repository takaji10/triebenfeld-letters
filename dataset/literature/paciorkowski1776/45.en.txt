# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 45
# translation by Claude

alleged collusion with the collateral heir, the Honourable [names], who proceeded with no collusion with the protestant, but only according to the knowledge received from elderly residents of his village, demonstrated to the Sub-Chamberlain's court his particular corner point and the determination of the boundary wall at the convergence of three estates, and from that point acknowledged to the protestant the corner mound.

The boundary paths of both parties having been concluded and carefully and diligently reviewed, for more sufficient proof of the boundary paths the court considers it fitting that a sworn inquiry be conducted by both the principal acting parties and the collateral heir, the Honourable [names], regarding that same particular corner point; enjoining that the said parties, having first drawn up interrogatories and communicated them to one another, party to party, shall conduct it immediately under penalty of loss of the case; and orders the parties to swear an oath on the impartiality of the witnesses; the court messenger is added for the pronouncing of the oath formula, and this immediately.
