# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 153
# translation by Claude

required to allege ^[*A wrongly obtained process is to be proved.*] *male obtentum* without bringing a separate action therefor. And in such a measure a free appeal to the Tribunal shall be permitted. And when the Tribunal shall recognise that process to be good and shall have imposed banishment on the party defeated in law: no annulment shall be given to such a person any longer. Nevertheless after such banishment, the party defeated in law shall have twelve weeks' free time for a settlement *cum jure vincente*; and may make deeds for the settlement of that case, may dispose of and sell its estates, and satisfy creditors *authoritate præsentis Constitutionis mediante* — otherwise if it is not willing, then the Starosta shall already be required to carry out execution against that person under banishment. And if the banishment was obtained *in contumaciam*: then at the summons of that Starosta and the opposing party by the person under banishment before the Tribunal, he may annul that banishment by his oath, even if he has no annulment *jure victus*. And where from anyone in any case banishment has been lifted by *per definitivam sententiam*: there no relaxation from Our Chancery shall be needed. Likewise where banishment has been imposed on anyone *in Contumaciam* in any case: immediately ^[*A discharge is granted on account of ignorance of the process.*] upon swearing *super ignorantiam illius Bannitionis*, he shall have standing *in illo Judicio*, and thereafter may remove that banishment from himself in the manner described above.
