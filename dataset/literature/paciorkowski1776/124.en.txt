### *We leave in force the statute concerning the erection of boundaries between Our estate and the terrestrial estates.*

^[*Constitution 1562, fol. 621, vol. 2.*] And any Starosta who destroys the boundaries: he shall be required to erect them again by the feast of Saint JOHN the Baptist next to come, under pain of deprivation of the office of the Captaincy or of the leasehold of the castle; and they shall be kept firmly with mounds in perpetuity. And where anyone should be summoned in respect of them at the instance of the Instigator of Our office from the denunciation of the Captain or leaseholder, We exempt all persons from such summonses.
