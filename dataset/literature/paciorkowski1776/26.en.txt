But because provision has been made in the new statute that, before the proof of the sentence — that is, before the decree — a party may improve its contested claim or case: this is to be understood as follows: that this same improvement of the contested matter must be made once, at the peremptory session, that is, at the time when the judge is already receiving the matter for deliberation, or for examination; for if a party were required to improve its case several times, and the judge were likewise required to deliberate upon the same several times, then a great delay of justice would arise and be permitted.

**Translator's Note**

[T.N. 1] *Odsypisko*: a Polish legal term for land deposited by the natural action of a river on one bank at the expense of the other. It denotes the accreted land itself, and the legal rule that such natural accretion belongs to the heir of the bank where it is deposited. The term has no established single-word English equivalent and is retained in italics.
