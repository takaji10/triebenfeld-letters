# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 30
# translation by Claude

Before the Right Honourable [names], General Sub-Chamberlain of the Palatinate of [names], in the presence of the Honourable and Noble [names] and others of various rank and condition assembled for this act. At the today's session, preserved by the decree of deliberation of his court, the jurisdiction having been founded and proclaimed with security of the present act by the court messenger of the Kingdom, the Honest [names] of the village of [name], authentic and sworn — the tenor of which decree of deliberation, submitted by oblata to the Sub-Chamberlain's records, follows and is as follows: (The foregoing decree of deliberation is to be inserted here.) He caused the parties to be called to appear. At which place the Honourable [names], heir of the estates of the village of [name], originally cited, protests regarding the improper founding of the jurisdiction of his court at this place, as being on the property of the protestant himself, subject to no dispute, and not at the convergence of three estates, alleging those same mounds to be unreal. The plaintiff's party counter-protests against the foregoing, that the Sub-Chamberlain's court has situated itself at a proper place, alleging the mounds to be true and legitimate, not mere hillocks, and situated at the convergence of three estates.
