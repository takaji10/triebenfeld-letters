Upon such a summons and notification the respondent shall be required to appear before the plaintiff's party and lawfully dispute over the ownership of the plot and the differences; and the absence of one or two of the mentioned officials shall not obstruct this — indeed, those who assemble at that time shall be required to conduct that act. There, for the weightier proofs, an oath shall be adjudged to whichever party by the Sub-Chamberlains or their offices, together with the other dignitaries — that is, the standard-bearers and military judges — yet preserving in this a free appeal to the Tribunal from such a decree, if any party should consider itself injured by such a decree.
