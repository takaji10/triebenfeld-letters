The sworn inquiries having been produced and concluded, ^[*Final decree.*] he descended to the reading thereof. Since it is clearly apparent both from those same sworn inquiries and from the documents of the plaintiff's party — namely the boundary act of the year [name] drawn from the records of the Kingdom's Chancery — that the Honourable plaintiff, on the basis of that same act conducted in the customary legal form by the Right Honourable Commissioners, commenced his boundary path from the starting point — namely from the particular corner point of the estate of the village of [name] of the Honourable [names], collaterally summoned — from the true and legitimate mounds and the convergence of three estates; and continued the same, namely through the intermediate points, with only three wall mounds, true, real, and visible (as appeared from their opening, proved them); and determined the same to the end point, according to the acknowledgement of the collateral heir. Whereas the Right Honourable Captain had commenced with the greatest obliqueness, not from the true convergence of three estates, and had conducted the same through the plaintiff's party's own fields and plots, and had not determined it to the end point either.
