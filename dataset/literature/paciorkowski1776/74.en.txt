# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 74
# translation by Claude

And indeed first the Very Reverend Parish Priest commenced his boundary path from the place of the founded jurisdiction. (The boundary path shall be written according to the position of the places and what is found therein; whatever the leading party has shown, whether it proceeded in a straight line or obliquely, shall be noted in conformity with the form of the boundary path noted above.) And likewise that of the cited party. The boundary paths having thus been concluded, the court finds that a sworn inquiry is necessary for more sufficient proof from both parties, etc. More shall be written from the foregoing act down to this place. Which having been read, and weighed and examined in all their circumstances with the mature deliberation of his court — since it has been proved, both from those same sworn inquiries and from the documentary evidence deposited in court by the Very Reverend Parish Priest (the party's documents establishing the matter, with the act and day of session, shall be written) — that that same boundary path is true and legitimate, as having been directed by the Very Reverend Parish Priest and concluded from the starting point, through the intermediate points, to the end point, demonstrated to the court by a straight path; whereas the cited party had not commenced from the convergence of three estates, had demonstrated no mounds at all in the intermediate points, had directed its boundary path with the greatest obliqueness, and had not determined it to the end point; the Sub-Chamberlain's court therefore, having weighed those same boundary paths on both sides, finding the more probable boundary path to be that of the Very Reverend
