Which oath the Honourable [names] together with six witnesses of equal rank, [names], with the court messenger reading the formula to them as described above, according to the intent of the statute before the face of his court and with the party present — the Honourable [names], plaintiff, before the image of the Crucified Lord; and the aforesaid witnesses, with two fingers raised to heaven and on bended knee, at the final particular corner mound swore; therefore the court wills and declares that those same boundaries shall have the force of perpetual validity, and ordered the court messenger to proclaim and declare this with a loud and sonorous voice in the presence of all assembled for this act. Which boundaries let no one dare violate or annihilate in any way, under a penalty of two thousand marks to be decreed by the competent court. (If the parties shall grant one another mutual common pasture, it shall be written:) And since both parties mutually grant to one another common pasture in the pine forests and deserted open country of their estates, according to ancient usage, the court therefore decrees that both parties shall enjoy those same freely granted mutual common pastures, yet without damage.
