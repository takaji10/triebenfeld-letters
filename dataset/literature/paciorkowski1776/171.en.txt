# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 171
# translation by Claude

Boundary Chamberlains shall be possessioned residents ^[*Sub-Chamberlains must be residents of the palatinate.*] in the palatinates or land, *sub amissione Officij*; and in the Palatinates of Kiev, Volhynia, Podolia, Bracław, and Bełz, on account of the remoteness of the districts, We permit the Honourable Sub-Chamberlain to have three Boundary Chamberlains in each district.

And since the Convocation Sejm drew up a penalty for officials ^[*On official reports at field hearings, fol. 219.*] who write dissenting opinions at field hearings, and the rigour of that penalty *metum incutere* seems to the judges — wishing sometimes to avoid an unjust sentence and the risk of responsibility in the promotion by *Activiorem Partem* of the case before the Tribunal — We therefore, clarifying the Convocation constitution, will it to be understood as follows: that if from indifferent proofs and evidence such cases conducted by *per aliter sentientes Officiales* should be written in disagreement, and before the Tribunal from those two sentences one *ex potiori iustitia* should prove to be more just — then in that case the sentence of the other official shall not carry *pænalitatem* for the one writing the dissent; but the one writing the dissent shall be subject to punishment only in the case where his sentence should prove manifestly favourable *contra Documenta* or *aliter obloquentibus Inquisitionibus*, or contrary to the Rule of the Tribunal decree.
