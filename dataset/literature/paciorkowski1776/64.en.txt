# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 64
# translation by Claude

The boundary act between the above-named parties having been concluded and determined by the Sub-Chamberlain's offices of Sieradz and Łęczyca, and the municipal court offices named below — of Radom and Opoczno — engaged by the parties on today's day, namely the [day] of [name] in the present year; the official jurisdiction having been reassumed and proclaimed by the court messenger of the Kingdom, the Honest [names], in the presence of the Honourable [names] — wishing to proceed to the execution of the Tribunal decree of the act written above. But since both parties, namely the Right Honourable [names] and the Honourable [names], staying further proceedings in law on account of various claims and acts of violence arising from the disputed estate, overcome by the persuasion of their friends, and reconciling neighbourly friendship between themselves, came to a composition; he therefore, approving that same amicable settlement, considers claims to be compensated against claims and acts of violence against acts of violence; annuls, voids, and cancels the protests, declarations, and the entire and complete process, and declares that nothing shall be to the detriment of the good name and unblemished honour of either party, by virtue of the present decree.

If, however, the parties have not entered into an amicable settlement, then the municipal court offices shall settle, cognise, and determine the account of revenues from the plots wrongfully held and delimited and all claims, shall order compensation and restitution thereof to whom and from whom it shall be due,
