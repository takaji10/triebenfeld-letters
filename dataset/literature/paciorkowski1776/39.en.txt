And proceeding further from the same toward the same southern quarter, the court reached fields sown with various kinds of grain, where the leading party presented to the court furrows running lengthwise toward the village of the cited party, and to the left toward the village of the plaintiff, ploughed differently; in which fields the same leading party alleged that five mounds, one after the other in a straight line, had stood for some ten years past, but had now been ploughed up and reduced to nothing by the former heirs of the village of the respondent, proceeding to the avulsion of the hereditary estate of the plaintiff.
