The Honourable [names], plaintiff, protested against the unlawful demonstration by the Right Honourable Captain and his subjects — both of the hillocks and of the incisions freshly made not long ago by his subjects; regarding which unlawful act, he produced a protest and an inspection by the court messenger together with noblemen [names] of those same incisions, before the municipal records of [name] in the year [name], and proves it at the present session.

The Right Honourable Captain counter-protesting that those same incisions were made from antiquity, and the mounds are truly real, because antiquity was not in the habit of placing metal markers. Having thus noted the protests, he led the court to the boundary of that same forest, and beneath the very forest, toward the fields and plots of the estate of the village of [name], at the convergence of three estates, ^[*Determination of the Right Honourable Captain's boundary path.*] with the collaterally summoned party present and acknowledging the same, determined his boundary path. The plaintiff's party protests the nullity and invalidity of the entire boundary path, as having been conducted obliquely, neither through the intermediate points nor to the end point.
