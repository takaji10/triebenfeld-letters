In the prosecution of the case, since in the present case various doubts and complexities intervene, therefore, adhering to positive law, the Sub-Chamberlain's court, lest through its own hastiness any party suffer detriment to its fortune (since the present boundary case has the force of perpetuity), deliberates for eight weeks, preserving for the parties at that place a peremptory session of appearance, together with the collaterally summoned party, by virtue of the present act.

### *Decree on Serious Contested Matters, Resolving the Deliberation.*

Done at the field place of the differences between the hereditary estates of the villages of [name], of the Honourable [names], plaintiff, on the one part, and of the Honourable [names], principal respondent, on the other part, near three notable corner mounds (or not particularly notable ones), one of the said plaintiff's village of [name], the second of the aforesaid principal respondent's village of [name], the third of the village of [name] of the Honourable [names], collaterally summoned, standing by a paved public road running from the city of [name] to the town of [name], on the Monday next after the Sunday of [name], in the year of the Lord [names].
