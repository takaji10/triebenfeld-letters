Crossing that road, proceeding in a straight line toward the south, exiting the same pine forest, a mound was raised under the very edge of the pine forest on the boundary of the fields called *Długie Pole*, belonging from antiquity to the estate of the village of [name] of the plaintiff, the seventh in number, eighty paces from the sixth; and from that same seventh mound toward the south, proceeding as straight as possible through those same fields and open country, he ordered a ditch to be raised with a plough prepared for that purpose, extending to the particular corner mounds; which, at the application of the parties — both the principal acting parties and the collateral heir — as being subject to no dispute, he ordered to be renewed and raised up; and marked them all with metal markers, both all the wall mounds and the particular corner mounds, with charcoal, brick, and iron slag placed separately in each of them. After which erection of mounds — both corner and particular corner mounds, and wall mounds — the Sub-Chamberlain's court decrees that the plaintiff's party ^[*The confirmation of the boundary path enjoined.*] that has won the boundary path shall confirm it as the seventh with witnesses of equal rank, adhering to the statute of the Kingdom, at his final particular corner mound, in that oath formula: that by his boundary path he has taken no land or estate from the respondent, nor has alienated any from that hereditary estate; and this immediately — the court messenger is added for the pronouncing of the oath formula.
