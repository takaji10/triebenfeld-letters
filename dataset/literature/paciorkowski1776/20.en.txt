Then there are to be observed and accurately noted in the records, if they occur or intervene in the boundary tract: streams, rivulets, brooks, rivers, waterways, watercourses; springs, torrents, lakes, ponds, cisterns, marshes, wellsprings, channels, runnels, fords, mud, mire, banks, shores, margins, the edge of a mere. Moreover roads, incidental, royal, paved, public, both those running straight and in a line and those running crosswise, leading, conducting, or going from village to village, from town to town, and conversely; crossroads, three-way junctions, divergence of roads, footpaths among them.
