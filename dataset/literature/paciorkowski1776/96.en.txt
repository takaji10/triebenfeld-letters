Proceeding further toward the same eastern quarter, through that same pine forest, the court reached a place called *Czarny Smug*, at which place it demonstrated to the court a hillock in the manner of a mound, having no circle or roundness; in which, when opened at the application of the party, nothing was found. Continuing its boundary path further toward the same quarter, and crossing with difficulty the boggy and waterlogged places called *Białe Ługi*, it demonstrated to the court, in a more elevated place, an oblong hillock, not resembling a mound at all; and likewise, when the same was opened, no metal markers were found. And proceeding further through the same pine forest with the path cleared, curving and not in a straight line toward the winter east, inclining this way and that, it demonstrated to the court a hillock of no small height, taking it as a wall mound; regarding which demonstration the Right Honourable Captain protested regarding the unlawful display, asserting that it had grown from the roots of a felled tree; the party having requested the opening, the roots of the felled tree and part of a rotted trunk were found therein.
