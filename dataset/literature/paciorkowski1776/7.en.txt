### *Decree of Referral to the Field.*

The present land court of [name], resolving the citation of the plaintiff's party issued to its court, and having heard the contested submissions of the parties — since the plaintiff's party acts in the present session not only in respect of the acts of violence committed, but also for the delimitation of the estates of [name] — therefore, adhering to the constitution of the year 1611, and suspending the resolution of the case of fact, refers the present case, in respect of the boundaries, for final adjudication, together with the collateral heirs entering appearance, to the field, to the Right Honourable Sub-Chamberlain of the district of [name], or his office; upon summons letters to be issued by the Right Honourable Sub-Chamberlain to both the respondent's party and the collateral heirs; before which Right Honourable Sub-Chamberlain or his office the parties shall be obliged to appear and to prosecute the case, under the penalty of banishment to be proclaimed at that session by virtue of the present decree.
