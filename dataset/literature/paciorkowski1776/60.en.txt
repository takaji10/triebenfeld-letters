# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 60
# translation by Claude

the Honourable [names], heir of the estates of the village of [name], collaterally summoned, appearing in person, acknowledged the true convergence of three estates — of one collateral heir's estate and of the two litigating parties — to be there, alleging that at this place, and not at the place from which the plaintiff's party commenced its boundary path, the mounds had been destroyed — not by the subjects of the cited party, but of the Right Honourable plaintiff. (A protest regarding the nullity of the entire boundary path, and likewise against the collateral heir, shall be written.)

Both boundary paths of the parties having been concluded and judicially determined, the court considers, for better information to itself and more sufficient proof from the parties, that a sworn inquiry shall be conducted — by elderly witnesses of good repute and free from any charge of crime — by the parties, both the principal acting parties regarding the entire boundary path and the collateral heir regarding the corner point and the convergence of three estates at the place acknowledged by him; decreeing that those same parties shall conduct the aforesaid sworn inquiry, with prior interrogatories drawn up and communicated to one another, immediately; and the court messenger is added for the pronouncing of the oath formula regarding the impartiality of the witnesses. Which oath, since the parties — both the principal acting parties and the collateral heir — with the court messenger reading the formula to them, before the face of his court, before the image of the Crucified Lord, on bended knees, in the customary formula, swore a bodily oath;
