### *The Election of the Land Court and the Sub-Chamberlain's Court, and the Sub-Chamberlain's Oath.*

^[*Constitution 1588, fol. 1211, vol. 2.*] The election for the election of officials of the land court — after the departure or death of any of them — according to the ancient custom, the Palatine, and in his absence the senior Castellan of that palatinate or land, shall fix at the latest within six weeks at the customary place. And if the Palatine should not fix that sitting within six weeks, then the Castellan shall be required to do so.
