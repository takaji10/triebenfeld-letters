A postponement shall also be given for the principal ^[*Ibid., fol. 79.*] likewise through ^[*A postponement for the principal.*] a possessory deputy sent with full authority, if personal sessions fall in different courts — that is, if an oath has arisen for someone, he is required to discharge it first in the case that involves the greater sum, and that one shall be postponed as the lesser to the next sittings; and thereafter he shall prove this to the one to whom the postponement was given by the signatory letters of that court, that he truly had a case under oath there, and a proceeding from which that case arose — so that the plaintiff may not be mocked and damaged in vain *sub amissione causæ*.

Adding to this: if in injuries and land differences ^[*Ibid., fol. 79.*] that arise between neighbours ^[*A boundary case cannot be decided without cognisance of it.*] there are no boundaries made, and one summons another either to escape injuries or wishing himself peace — then the boundary case shall first be adjudicated, and then the injuries considered. However, without delay the boundaries are to be continued; otherwise, if the party shall not pursue them, the actions brought for injuries and losses shall have free recourse to the law, without any opposition or legal impediment.

### *The suspension of cases is required to run for senators, commissioners, and deputies.*
