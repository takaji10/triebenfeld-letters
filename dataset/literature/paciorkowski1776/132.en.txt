### *On reviews.*

^[*Constitution 1647, fol. 125, vol. 4.*] Since complaints reach Us from the land deputies that leaseholders of Our estates are conducting reviews *inter Bona ab utrinque Nostra cum præjudicio fundi Terrestris per Commissionem antea non dislimitati* — We therefore, inclining ourselves to the ancient law concerning commissions made for the delimitation of Our estates with terrestrial ones, declare that Our estates are to be delimited from terrestrial ones by commissioners alone and not by reviewers; without prejudice as between Our estates *ab utrinque*, which have been and are to be in the future conducted by Our secretaries and not by commissioners. And such reviews, whether conducted in anticipation or after the commission, both before this constitution and in the future, cannot prejudice the terrestrial estate or the commission.
