Against that entire boundary path, ^[*The Right Honourable Captain protests the nullity of the entire boundary path.*] and regarding the nullity thereof, the Right Honourable Captain protested, as not having been continued and determined in a straight path, in accordance with the purport of the boundary act of the year written above, but diverging this way and that obliquely, through the sown fields and plots.
