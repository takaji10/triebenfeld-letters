# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 168
# translation by Claude

Volhynian — and their districts and lands, those needing boundary proceedings may bring out together with the Sub-Chamberlains or their offices not only the standard-bearers and the land office but also other land officials together with the Sub-Chamberlains or their offices — that is, stewards of the table, cup-bearers, under-stewards, military judges, sword-bearers, treasurers — provided they are from whatever district or land of those palatinates; then sufficiently and fully such a delimitation may be conducted, provided there are not fewer than three from one side and likewise from the other side. Adding to this: when the summons from the plaintiff's commissioners reaches the cited party and shall have been acknowledged in the municipal court *ubi bona Citati ratione quorum res est consistunt*, the cited party shall be required *pro tempore & loco* assigned by the summons of the plaintiff's commissioners to bring out his own commissioners from his own side; and for him an equal number shall likewise be sufficient as for the plaintiff. And if by the appointed time from the plaintiff's commissioners the cited party shall not have brought out his commissioners, and either shall have gone out himself or shall not have gone out (in which only true illness, *juxta Statutum in Termino sequenti comprobanda*, can excuse him) — then the commissioners brought out by the plaintiff shall be able to delimit on their own and erect the mounds; and this We enact to be ratified and firm.
