**Translator's Note**

[T.N. 1] *Upust*: a Polish term for a water sluice or mill-gate — the opening or device by which water is released from a mill pond or mere. The term is retained in italics and the Latin gloss (*vulgo upust*) indicates Paciorkowski himself treated it as a vernacular word requiring explanation within the Latin text.
