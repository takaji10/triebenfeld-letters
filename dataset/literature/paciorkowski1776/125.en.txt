and which shall in the future be made, even where Our steward was not present at their making, who in respect of such boundaries has and can have no interest in the future according to the law and ancient observance — all shall be firmly kept by the Starostas and leaseholders of Our estates and by Our subjects for ever; which We confirm by this constitution, and according to the old laws of the Crown, according to which also in Masovia commissioners are and shall be appointed, the confirmations of those commissioners shall be issued from Our Chancery, to whoever shall have need of it. And those noblemen who are summoned on behalf of the Starostas in the name of Our Instigator, for any injuries or losses that might be contained within those boundaries — all of them We exempt from such summonses.

### *On destroyed boundaries.*

^[*Constitution 1578, fol. 977, vol. 2.*] Boundaries that have been juridically concluded by Our commissioners between Our estates and those of the nobility — let neither Our Starostas and leaseholders nor the subjects in Our estates dare to violate or destroy them. And if any have been destroyed since the constitution of the year one thousand five hundred and sixty-two, they shall erect them again at their own expense through the commissioners, before the feast of Saint JOHN the Baptist next to come, having paid the party one hundred marks
