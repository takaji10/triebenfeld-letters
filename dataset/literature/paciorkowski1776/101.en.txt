# Paciorkowski (1776) [= Lochowski 1641], p. 101
# translation by Claude

to adjourn the same. The morrow arriving, namely the [day] of [name] in the year [name], the jurisdiction having been reassumed from the abovementioned limiting incident, ^[*In the prosecution.*] he ordered the parties to proceed in the case. In the prosecution of the case, he finds ^[*A sworn inquiry enjoined.*] that a sworn inquiry is necessary for more sufficient proof of the boundary paths; decreeing that both parties, and likewise the collateral heir, the Honourable [names], standing in the position of an uncertain corner point — and indeed the principal acting parties in respect both of the corner point and of the general boundary path; and the Honourable [names], collateral heir, as regards his corner point or particular corner point only, where the true convergence of three estates may be found — shall conduct that sworn inquiry and call witnesses, with prior interrogatories drawn up and communicated to one another, and this immediately; and the court messenger is added for the pronouncing of the oath formula regarding the impartiality of the witnesses. Which oath, since the parties — both the principal acting parties and the collateral heir — before the face of his court, before the image of the Crucified Lord, with the court messenger reading the formula to them, swore; he therefore proceeded to conduct that same sworn inquiry, and orders the parties to call witnesses — first for the plaintiff's party and then for the cited party. They called, produced, and swore. The sworn inquiries having been produced and concluded, he descended to the reading thereof. Which having been read and weighed and examined in all their circumstances with the mature deliberation
