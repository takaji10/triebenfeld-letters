In the first decade of the 20th century the church and tower alteration was carried out in the parish of Konin. The total costs amounted to 29 631 rubles 50 kopecks. In Maslaki, the filial of Konin, Pastor Henkel of Konin also wished to build a solid house of God. The disagreements in the filial congregation over the building site — some wished Maslaki, others in turn Kleczew — as well as the outbreak of the first World War, frustrated the realization of the project.
