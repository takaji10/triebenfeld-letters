Prussian times in the Lodz region 15 German-evangelical elementary schools. The village teachers were not pedagogically trained forces; for the most part they still practiced a side occupation. In the town schools, by contrast, matters looked more pleasing with respect to the training of the teachers. The pay was here more orderly and secured. Similar conditions in the field of the school prevailed at that time also in Germany.

With the expansion of the school system went hand-in-hand also that of the church congregations. In the year 1795 the Lutherans settled in and around Kalisch and those who had immigrated obtained the „Konzession zur Errichtung eines eigenen evangelischen Kirchensystems46 ("concession for the establishment of an own evangelical church system").

Already since 1775 Kalisch had several Evangelicals, whom the pastors of Ostrowo and then of Stawiszyn cared for. „They held public divine service, for which a spacious room was assigned in the town hall."13)

As their local pastor they called Karl Michael Christian Sienell, who in the first two years held divine service in the so-called cobbler's benches. On the basis of the breve of August 27, 1797 Pope Pius VI., thanks to the efforts of the Prussian government, left to the Protestants of Kalisch the Jesuit church standing empty there for almost 25 years, built by Archbishop Karnkowski toward the end of the 16th century, „cum pleno iure patronatus" (with full right of patronage). On the first Whitsun holiday of 1798 Pastor Sienell consecrated it as an evangelical house of God. In the church there remained, as before, various monuments and memorial tablets, among others also the relief monument that shows Primas Karnkowski before the cross of Christ in an attitude of prayer.

As further congregation foundation followed Grodziec. On July 12, 13 and 14, 1796 the representatives of the Holländer villages Borowiec, Konary, Grunde, Wieloleka, Orlina, Gadow and Bialobloty concluded with Count Stadnicki of Grodziec a contract on the formation of the Grodziec parish. Stadnicki reserved for himself and his descendants the right of patronage, especially in the filling of the pastoral office. First pastor of the congregation became David Bergemann, born in Fraustadt14)

In the years 1797—1803 one erected the church in the form of a „preußischen Mauer 4 4 (half-timber or frame construction of beams and bricks set between), the parsonage and cantorate house as well as all the farm buildings. In the same year as in Grodziec, the parish of Babiak organized itself. Bonaventura von Raczynski, the lord of the manor of Ozorzyn, supported the efforts of the German Lutherans with respect to the founding of a parish, whose definitive establishment was set for November 23, 1796. A small wooden church was built and a bell procured from Thorn. In 1808 the evangelical elementary school of Babiak was founded. The pastoral office was taken over by Pastor Johann Gottlieb Maske, formerly in Bialowiezyn near Lipno, who died in 1808 after severe suffering.

By decree of the Prussian chamber at Plozk of September 24, 1799 the seat of the parish of Bialowiezyn was transferred to Lipno. In the year 1800 one built the prayer house in Ossowka. In the same year the congregation of Kowal was founded. It extended over the territory of the later parishes of Chodecz and Przedecz. For lack of a church — the building of which in Kowal, despite manifold efforts, nevertheless did not come about — the parishioners gathered for divine services in private houses.

The efforts for the establishment of the parish of Dombie date from 1799. At that time a commission appeared here, whose members were, among others, War Councillor von Colomb and Tax Councillor von Koehlichen.
