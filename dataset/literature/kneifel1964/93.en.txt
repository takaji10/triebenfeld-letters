### 2. Expansion of the School and Church System

In the year 1803 Samuel Gottlieb Linde (1771—1847), born in Thorn, arranger of the Polish vocabulary (dictionary in six volumes; completed 1814), was called by Minister Voss to the establishment and direction of a lyceum in Warschau. Linde acquired merits for the institution, which was housed in the Saxon Palace and whose outfitting cost almost 5000 thaler[^p93-12]

From 1814 to 1819 he was preses of the Warschau church collegium and had a counting of the congregation carried out in 1814, which yielded almost 8000 souls. As excellent Lutheran town schools of Central Poland from that period are named: Warschau, Kalisch and Peisern. — In the country German-evangelical schools also gradually formed. Thus in 1795 in the Weichsel lowland in Wölka-Turzynska and Chinow; further around 1798 in Borki with 38 pupils, Troszyn with 37, Sady 22, Kazun 20 and Secymin 24. Likewise Lutheran schools in Famuiki and Myszory were mentioned.

In the Dobrin land the number of German elementary schools and cantorates for the year 1807 is given as 29. Around 1800 there already existed in Kujawien about 42 elementary schools, of which some were endowed with 15 Morgen of tax- and rent-free land. There were, however, also individual poorly established schools, like that in the „Wüstung Smol-nik" ("deserted settlement Smol-nik"), where the cantor's main trade was tailor and who lived in the shepherd's house. The school and cantorate congregation was far too small and too poor to erect a schoolhouse from its own means. In the Warthebruch one counted in the year 1800 about ten elementary schools. At the same time — according to a report composed by Pastor Grimm in Stawiszyn for the Posen consistory — 15 German-evangelical schools are named in the Kalisch land. Of these, as already existing in 1797: Zamenty, Zakrzyn, Przespolew, Czachulec, Grodziec and Mycielin. In the Gostynin land, around the turn of the 18th century, according to the information of the Hower pastor Witthold, there were already eleven reasonably well established German elementary schools. After the „General-Tabelle von dem Zustand aller evangelischen Schulen... im Jahre 1798", which the üower pastor Witthold likewise compiled, there existed in the Lodz region the schools in Slowik, Groß-Bruzyca, Klein-Bruzyca, Radogoszcz, Kaly, Dombrowa-Holl., Swendow, Glogowiec, Mileszki and Domaradzyn.
