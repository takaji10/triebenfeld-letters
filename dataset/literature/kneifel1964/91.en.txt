In all these colonies mainly Swabians were settled» Besides, in Neusulzfeld in the first decades of the 19th century families immigrated from the Baden country, from Bavaria (Palatinate), from Hesse, Prussia, Saxony, from the Posen country and in part from Lorraine. To Königsbach came Swabians, to Grünberg also Alsatians. The first settlers of Effingshausen came from Baden and Saxony. In Königsbach Swabian ways and speech have been preserved longest and purest. In Hochwald Swabians and Alsatians settled. The settling of the colonists in Hochwald took place as in the other royal villages. In the woodlands paths were cut, then sites surveyed and given out, in the form of hereditary lease deeds (Erbzinsverschreibung), to the individual families. The building of dwelling and farm buildings, the acquisition of cattle, seed and implements, the war and domain chambers attended to. Let it be noted that in the early period the colonists had to suffer severely under the plague of wolves.

With the state colonization private colonization kept step, and that through Polish Catholic lords of the manor. Near Lask there were founded around 1800 on the lands of the estate of Wojslawice the settlements Hahnenfelde and Zamlynie. In the forests of the estates of Bedon the lady of the manor Tuchecka settled in 1805 on 30 hides 29 German farmers. In the year 1805 the village Żakowice near Brzeziny arose. To the efforts of the Prussian recruiting commissar von Nothardt goes back the establishment, in 1801, of the four Swabian villages on the waste and brush-grown soils of the domain office of Leszno Wola: Alt-Ilvesheim, Neu-Ilvesheim, Ludwigsburg and Schwiningen. Most of the colonists came here from Ilvesheim in Franconia. Near Warschau one laid out the village of Szopy Niemieckie; in the domain office of Potycz the Swabian settlement Kanstatt (Ka,ty). Around the year 1800 Count Felix von Lubienski founded in the forests of his estate of Guzow eleven German colonies: Josefhof, Marienfeld, Heinrichsdorf, Felixdorf, Teklin, Zader-Buden, Babsche-Buden, Moritzin, Bieganow, Anto-niew and Franciszkow. At Kawa, south of the railway station of Skierniewice,there was laid out around 1800 the settlement of Strobow. Around 1802 there arose the Swabian villages of Erdmanns weiler (Kochanow) and Birkenfeld (Brzozow). In New East Prussia, in the region of Plozk, Wyszogrod, Plonsk, Lomza, Bialystok and in the extreme north of the province, 32 colonies in all were founded. On more than 1500 magdeb. hides one settled about 600 families with approximately 3500 persons, of whom 82 families with nearly 480 persons came from the land itself, while all the rest stemmed above all from Württemberg and Mecklenburg °). Thus in the year 1797 there were settled, on 247 hides, 149 families with a total of 913 souls in the first colony of Schröttersdorf (Maszewo). Soon afterward there followed the villages Güntersruhm, Agnesenau (Biele-Brzeznica), Luisenhuld (Cieszkowo) and Königsdorf (Wiciejewo). Into the period around the turn of the 18th century falls the foundation of the linguistic island of Königshuld (Paproc Duza), colonized with Mecklenburg immigrants. To it belonged the villages Königshuld, Luisenau, Wilhelmsdorf and Mecklenburg. Around 1800, east of Sierpc, the colony of Sokolowy Ka,t owes its origin to private initiative. Simultaneously with the rural colonization the urban one also went forward.
