From 1930 to 1939 there administered the congregation Sobiesenki Pastor Viktor Maczew-ski. In the years 1928—1939 there was active in Konin Pastor Robert Badke. After the new church law 1936 the filial Maslaki was made independent to a parish congregation. In Zdunska-Wola, where from 1925 to 1945 there worked the diligent and purposeful Pastor Dr. theol. Georg Lehmann, the church life took a gratifying upswing 4 Ö ). The willingness of sacrifice in the congregation was great. Thus gifted on 1 May 1933 Mrs. Florentine Schultz her property (house, lot and 10 Morgen of land) to the parish. On 7 and 8 December 1930 there celebrated Zdunska-Wola its 100th founding jubilee. On 1 November 1928 there took place in Turek the 100th jubilee of the church congregation. In Sompolno there officiated from 1924 Pastor Philipp Kreutz, who has become known as a religious poet. In 1935 he issued the volume of poems „Zeit und Ewigkeit". On 7 November 1931 there took place under the leadership of General Superintendent Bursche the founding of the filial Ludwikowo. From 1923 to 1945 there was active in Zagörow Pastor Alexander Groß, who took in hand the building of the parsonage and completed it in 1938. The religious care of the congregation members made very much his concern Pastor Groß, who stands near to the Christian Gemeinschaft in Lodz.
