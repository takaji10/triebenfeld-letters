# Kneifel (1964), p. 256
# translation by Claude

[…]

Pastor Adolf Doberstein wrote in the "Luthererbe in Polen" of March 13, 1938 on the "numerical relationship between the German and Polish congregational members in the Evangelical-Augsburgian church of Poland". On the basis of the Rocz. Ew. 1925 (Evang. Yearbook 1925) he calculated the soul-count of all congregations in all dioceses. For the yearbook contains from page 108 to 168 more detailed information on the soul-count of the individual congregations and filials, the baptisms in German and Polish, the confirmands and communicants in both languages, among other things. The last year underlying the data was 1923, whereby "the soul-count — as Pastor Doberstein explained — rests only on an approximate assumption". For all congregations of the Warsaw diocese the yearbook gives the following figures: soul-count: 55 000, confirmands: 1540, of them German 1131, Polish 409. For all parishes of the Kalisch diocese (not taken into account was the purely German evangelical congregation of Zagorow): soul-count: 59 000, confirmands: 1645, of them German 1488, Polish 157. After the addition of the parish of Zagorow (on the basis of my book "Die evangelisch-augsburgischen Gemeinden der Kalischer Diözese", 1937, pp. 255/58) and the reckoning in of the population increase of five percent in the years 1924 to 1934 (soul-count 1934: 3800, confirmands: average figure for 1923: 70), the soul-count in all parishes of the Kalisch diocese was: 62 610, confirmands: 1715, of them German 1558, Polish 157.
