# Kneifel (1964), p. 256
# translation by Claude

[…]

Pastor Adolf Doberstein wrote in the "Luthererbe in Polen" of 13. March 1938 on the "Zahlenverhältnis zwischen den deutschen und polnischen Gemeindegliedern in der evangelisch-augsburgischen Kirche Polens". On the basis of the Rocz. Ew. 1925 (Evang. Yearbook 1925) he calculated the soul-count of all the congregations in all dioceses. For the yearbook contains from page 108 to 168 detailed data on the soul-count of the individual congregations and filials, the baptisms in the German and Polish language, the confirmands and communicants in both languages and much else. The last year underlying the data was 1923, whereby "the soul-count — as Pastor Doberstein expounded — rests only on an approximate assumption". For all the congregations of the Warsaw diocese the yearbook names the following figures: soul-count: 55 000, confirmands: 1540, of them German 1131, Polish 409. For all the parishes of the Kalisch diocese (not taken into account was the purely German evangelical congregation of Zagorow): soul-count: 59 000, confirmands: 1645, of them German 1488, Polish 157. After the supplementation of the parish of Zagorow (on the basis of my book "Die evangelisch-augsburgischen Gemeinden der Kalischer Diözese", 1937, pp. 255/58) and the reckoning of the population increase of five percent in the years 1924 to 1934 (soul-count 1934: 3800, confirmands: average figure for 1923: 70), the soul-count in the Kalisch diocese in all the parishes was: 62 610, confirmands: 1715, of them German 1558, Polish 157.
