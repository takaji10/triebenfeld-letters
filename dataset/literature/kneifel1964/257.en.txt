From these individual figures results the following statistical total picture of the evangelical-augsburgian church: soul-count in all (including Zagöröw): 420 610, confirmands: 10 793, of them German 9141, Polish 1652.

On the basis of this compilation Pastor D ob erst ein concludes: "For the answering of the question of the numerical ratio between German and Polish in our church, the ratio of the confirmands who are confirmed in the German language to the Polish confirmands is presumably the decisive one. For here it should show most clearly who counts himself with the one or the other linguistically conditioned part of our church". He lets himself be heard further: "These figures, which speak an audible language for the German and the Polish side, would absolutely have to be heard in the dispute between the two parts".
