The foundation stone of the building of the Lutheran church in Zagórów was laid on 8 May 1878. On the first Advent Sunday 1882 the dedication of the house of God took place. In 1896 a house was bought here for the parsonage. Pastor Teichmann from Turek, administrator of the filial of Kolo, built in the years 1882/83 the Lutheran church at Kolo, which was dedicated on 15 November 1883. For the church building especially strove: Count Alexander von Kreutz from Koscielec, the church wardens August Widt (died 1886) and Karl Fleischer (died 1891). In the superintendency of Augustowo, namely in the filial of Wladyslawow, a massive church was erected in 1861.
