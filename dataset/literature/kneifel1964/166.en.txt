The foundation stone for the building of the Lutheran church in Zagórów was laid on 8 May 1878. On the 1st Advent Sunday 1882 the dedication of the house of God took place. In 1896 a house was bought here for the parsonage. Pastor Teichmann of Turek, administrator of the filial of Kolo, built the Lutheran church at Kolo in the years 1882/83; it was dedicated on 15 November 1883. Those who especially promoted the church building were: Count Alexander von Kreutz of Koscielec and the church wardens August Widt (died 1886) and Karl Fleischer (died 1891). In the superintendency of Augustow, namely in the filial of Wladyslawow, a massive church was erected in 1861.
