The Evangelicals of Sobiesenki had a prayer house, the consecration of which took place on April 13, 1796. For divine service the pastors of Stawiszyn traveled through the region. In the year 1808 the estate owner Wezyk of Iwanowice made possible for the colonists settled there the formation of the parish and the erection of a small wooden church near his manor farm, whereupon Pastor Gottfried Felke was soon called to Iwanowice. In the year 1812 he went to Adelnau, and Iwanowice received in the person of August Friedrich Haase a new pastor. In 1817 the prayer house near Iwanowice was destroyed by lightning. On March 17, 1819 the Government Commission confirmed the project of the „Einrichtung des Kirchensystems in Sobiesenki" ("establishment of the church system in Sobiesenki"). Between Sobiesenki and Iwanowice there prevailed a strained relationship. In the first place Pastor Haupt held office from 1819, and in the second Pastor Haase. In 1822 Pastor Haupt assumed the Wengrow pastorate, while Pastor Haase moved to Sobiesenki, whither the parish seat was transferred.

In 1809 the Lutherans dwelling in Lask and in the villages Chorzeszöw, Anielin, Rembow, Erywangrod, Mierzqczka, Zabloty, Bocianichy and Okup joined together into the parish of Lask and called as their pastor Johann Karl Ulrich (died 1816), who came from Salzwedel in the Neumark. As house of God they received from Peter Gzolchanski, the owner of the town of Lask, the unused Roman-Catholic chapel of the Holy Ghost (hospital church). From 1818 Pastor Friedrich Christoph Rüdiger, born in Schawoine near Trebnitz, worked here; he in 1824, because of his difficult economic situation, took over the pastorate in Prazuchy. For over 100 years — from 1824 to December 1927 — Lask had no pastors resident on the spot and became a filial of Zdunska-Wola. In the years 1834/40 and 1855/59 it was administered for a time also from Pabianice.

The gathering of the Evangelicals of German and Polish stock settled in Wielun and its surroundings into an independent Lutheran congregation was energetically pursued by the Sarchen elders Christoph Lübke and Friedrich Gutt.

In 1820 the new parish was formed and the former Roman-Catholic church of the Bernardine convent, built in 1612 in the round-arch style, was assigned to it. As parsonage the Evangelicals received the former house of the nuns' confessor. As first pastor Samuel Gottlieb Sachs was installed on March 28, 1824. At the same time the solemn consecration of the church also took place.

Since 1790 the little town of Slesin, 20 kilometers from Konin, with the preaching stations Butter-Holl (Maslaki), Szyszyner-Holl. and Grabina was a filial of Witkowo, province of Posen, and was visited by Pastor Johann Gottlieb Bothe. In 1811 the congregation of Slesin organized itself with August Bernhard Christian Drechsler as pastor, who however already in 1815 gave up the pastorate. In 1815 the parish of Slesin again lapsed. Until 1817 Pastor Röhl from Witkowo (Bothe's successor) regularly visited the evangelical localities in the surroundings of Slesin. In the year 1824 the Evangelicals in Konin bought the former Roman-Catholic Holy-Ghost church, a house of God built of wood in 1801.
