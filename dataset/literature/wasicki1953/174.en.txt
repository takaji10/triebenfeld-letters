Of the 400 families who came to the Prince of Orange's estates, that is about 2000 persons, there remained in South Prussia 79 families, that is barely 400 persons[^84]. We can now see that the arguments of some authors, such as Komornicki, do not correspond to the actual state of affairs. For according to Komornicki's data 2500 colonists in all were to have settled on the Prince of Orange's estates by 1800[^85]. The author, however, does not give their later fortunes.
