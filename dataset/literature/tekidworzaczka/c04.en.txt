# Nr. 1123 IV (1692)
Castellany and land courts > Poznań > Inscriptions > 17th/18th century
14143 (Nr. 1123 IV) 1692
Jan Trąmpczyński, son of Władysław Tr[ąmpczyński], records to his wife Teresa Prusimska, daughter of Jan of Kolno Br. [so printed; the decree of 1775 names him Prusimski] by the late Teresa Gajewska, 6,000 złp. of dowry and as much again of dower, on half his estates (f. 73)
