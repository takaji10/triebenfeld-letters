# Nr. 182A (1658)
sw.34858 Teki Dworzaczka, Regests: Księgi grodzkie poznańskie (the castellany court books of Poznań), Relacje series, 17th century: 2731 (Nr. 182A) 1658: Ewa Gorecka, widow of the late Mikołaj Lubowiecki, holding her dower and life interest, and Stanisław, Jan, Franciszek, Katarzyna, Dorota, Urszula, Jadwiga and Zofia L[ubowiecki], her children, minors, against Andrzej Przyjemski, crown pantler, and Katarzyna of Rozdrażew, spouses; [and against] Jan and Teresa, born of the late Teresa Grudzińska, [and] Konstancja Maria, born of the late Wiktoria Opalińska of Bnin, of the second marriage, the son and daughters of the late Krzysztof of Kolno Prusimski (f. 244)
