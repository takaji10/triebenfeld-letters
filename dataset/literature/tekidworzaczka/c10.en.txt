# Nr. 1176 (1720)
sw.250025 Teki Dworzaczka, Regests: Księgi grodzkie poznańskie (the castellany court books of Poznań), Inskrypcje series, 17th-18th century: 14988 (Nr. 1176) 1720: Krzysztof Prusimski, son of Jan of Kolno Pr[usimski] by the late Teresa Gajewska, by the brothers' divisions of 1700 between him and his full brothers Sebastian and the late Ludwik, written under a penalty of 700,000 złp., and by the second division after the childless death of Ludwik, written at Kamionna on 23 July 1708 under a penalty of 223,000 złp., sold the whole estates of Goraj, Słodzona, Nowiny, the manor Wiejce, Nowe Olędry otherwise Stary Krobielów, Rozenthal otherwise Rozy, Marienwald formerly Gołgowa Krynica, Krobielewko, Piła Krobielewska and the mill there, in the district of Poznań, from the first divisions, and Popowo and Debrzno from the second, after the late Ludwik, to his full brother Sebastian P[rusimski] the younger for 350,000 złp. (f. 24v); and Sebastian P[rusimski] sold the estates of Trąpczyn, Nowa Wieś, Łazy and Osiny in the district of Konin, and Wierzbno and Brzezno, Żerniki, Kunino, Świątniki and Jaryszki in the district of Poznań, which had fallen to him by the first divisions, [and] the town of Kamionna and Kolno by the second, to Krzysztof P[rusimski], his elder brother, for 350,000 złp. (f. 26)
