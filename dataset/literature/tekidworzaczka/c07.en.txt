# Nr. 68 (1673)
sw.36203 Teki Dworzaczka, Regests: Księgi grodzkie wschowskie (the castellany court books of Wschowa), part 1: 3556 (Nr. 68) 1673: Jan of Kolno Prusimski, son of Krzysztof P[rusimski] by the late Teresa Grudzińska, gives a quittance to Apolinara Opalińska of Bnin, widow of the late Wojciech Gajewski of Błociszewo, castellan of Rogoźno, and to Łukasz and Franciszek Gaj[ewski], the castellan's sons, for 12,000 zł, the rest of the 40,000 zł of dowry for his wife Teresa G[ajewska], the castellan of Rogoźno's daughter (f. 720v)
