The newly founded villages often had the word Kolonia [colony] in their names, e.g. Kolonia Łazińskie, Kolonia Huta, which came into being on the parcelled estates of the Trąbczyn property (the gmina of Zagórów, the powiat of Słupca, the voivodeship of Wielkopolska). Several new colonies appear on the map of the village of Grabienice (the gmina of Rzgów, the powiat of Konin, the voivodeship of Wielkopolska) of the year 1873 (Kolonia Huta, Kolonia Ludwikowo, Kolonia Wojciechowo and Kolonia Boberfeldowo) (fig. 8). Also in the estates of the Umień property (the gmina of Olszówka, the powiat of Koło, the voivodeship of Wielkopolska) several colonies came into being (Kolonia Banachów, Kolonia Hilarów and Kolonia Szczepanów), which were made visible on the map of the year 1845 (fig. 9).
