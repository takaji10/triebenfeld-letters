The lands belonging to colonists were as a rule marked on the maps in orange, as against the manorial lands marked in red and the peasants' painted in yellow[^10]. On the maps presented as a rule the whole area taken by the colony is marked. Not always, however, were the individual arable plots or the buildings marked (fig. 10).
