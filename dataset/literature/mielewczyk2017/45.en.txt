# 1.2. The Russian partition

In the Russian partition the enfranchisement of the peasants came later and took a different course than in the Prussian one. The enfranchisement ukase of the tsarist authorities issued on 2 March 1864, "On the ordering of the peasants", gave the peasants as their property the land they used. This grant was not joined with the merging of the arable plots belonging to the individual holdings, the abolishing of the servitudes and the separation of the peasants' lands from the manorial ones. An example of a map with the lands of the enfranchised peasants marked on it is the map of the estates of Trąbczyn (today the gmina of Zagórów, the powiat of Słupca, the voivodeship of Wielkopolska) (fig. 4)[^8].

*Fig. 4. A fragment of the map of the estates of Trąbczyn of 1861 at the scale of 1:15 000 with the field of the enfranchised peasants marked. Reduced. Shelfmark AN 489/5*

In the face of the great fragmentation of the fields belonging to the individual peasant holdings, in the second half of the 19th century in the territory of the Kingdom of Poland there began to develop a consolidation from below, carried out by those interested themselves. By the outbreak of the First World War it covered an area of about 2.5 million ha.

[^8]: Examples of maps showing the fields of enfranchised peasants were presented in: W. Mielewczyk, Wykorzystanie map do badania zmian przestrzeni społeczno-ekonomicznej wsi… [The use of maps in the study of the changes of the socio-economic space of the village…], pp. 109–110.
