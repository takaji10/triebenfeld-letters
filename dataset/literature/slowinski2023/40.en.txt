# Słowiński (2023), p. 40
# translation by the editor's files

 the profusion of July letters. Father, sister and brother, independently of one another, send mail full of heartening words. Brother Gustav writes (22 July 1942): "Today I was again in Zagórów with Kadzia [sister Leokadia]. Even if in Zagórów one sees and hears more, still nothing could distract my thoughts, and the whole time I thought of you. […] How many hundreds of times the question arises how you are getting on and why it was precisely you who were transferred so far away. […] Sister Anna with her fiancé Hans are at home at Łazińsk. Hans has two weeks of leave. In general many Łazińsk people come on leave often, mainly those who are stationed in Germany or in France." It pains him that his brother is so far away that leave is out of the question. On the southern front it goes from victory to victory, so Bruno, crowned by this victory, may soon return home. He also has a question and at the same time a request. "Have you not yet had the opportunity to have a photograph taken of yourself in your soldier's uniform? In a helmet and with the other things. We – and I most of all – would be very glad."

Two letters of the father – Samuel Julius Mielke – have survived. The one of 20 April was written on the führer's birthday and is dedicated above all to him. "May the Lord God preserve him in good health for a long time, until he defeats all evil and we can sing with him the great Alleluia of peace." He is also worried by the fact that many of the local people avoid the church. In the second letter (28 July 1942) we read: "Dear Bruno, beloved son of my heart! May these lines find you in the best of health and in true soldierly courage. I would most gladly be there with you, to help you carry your burdens! But the distance is too great. Talk there with your commanders. I could be used as a translator of the Russian language and take upon myself the hardships of the journey." Then come news of the much delayed, wet harvest. The letter ends with the words: "The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God and the communion of the Holy Spirit be with us all. Amen. Jesus works miracles. *Heil Hitler*!"
