# Słowiński (2023), p. 42
# translation by the editor's files

In the letters from sister Olga and brother-in-law Erwin there are great thanks for the monetary gifts and for the fact that with his own body he protects them and their beloved homeland from the evil enemy. One of the letters begins with an exalted supplication for Christ's help, through His wounds, passion and death, in the sudden need in which the brother found himself. The sister believes she has the right to such words, because she and her husband want not only to wear the former *hakenkreuz* (swastika) but also to cherish it in their religion. "May God Almighty send down upon you grace and mercy. Our single most ardent wish is to see you alive and well when this evil war is over." She briefly describes the christening ceremony of the little son, whom they named Adolf. "The holy baptism was modest, at home." Unfortunately they did not receive permission to slaughter a pig, but at the church the pastor arranged everything nicely and delivered a beautiful speech. The godparents were Gustav and Leokadia. We liked it very much. She ends with the information that she packed a bit of bacon and black bread. Well baked.

Sister Anna also tries hard to support her brother. "When at the cinema I watch the newsreels and the conquerors of the mountains advance, I always think that you too are there. And then I am so proud of you. I know that being at the front is not among the pleasures, but I would set off at once if I were a man. After all, it must be a heartening feeling, to protect the Fatherland so that it does not bend before the wild hordes, and its inhabitants before maltreatment. We shall always be grateful to you for that […] we feel with our heart everything that you physically have to endure." And further: "Actually it is senseless of me to write this to you. You have never complained and you are certainly in this respect an exemplary soldier. You were taught obedience and were never spoilt. You also have a good heart, full of enthusiasm. That is really the only thing that is needed in order to accept everything with joy."
