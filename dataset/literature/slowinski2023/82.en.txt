(The letter reached Łazińsk on 23 March 1944, from field post 31 059E – the number assigned to the 136th regiment of the 2nd Mountain Rifle Division, 10th company).

The letter written before Easter, 12 March 1944, reaches Łazińsk as early as 17 March! Within five days the mail travels from the distant Finnish north to the village hidden in the forests of Podzagórze. He writes on a Sunday in the penitential season. "How once more God's kindness crowned me, in that He granted me (us) a free and peaceful Sunday, so that in its holy morning hours I might read His holy gospel and then find a quiet place in the sunny, snowy solitude and join the invisible community of prayer in heaven and on earth". Further on he thanks the Lord that He has brought such wonderful, dear people into his life: his parents, his brother, his sisters. "The cross of Golgotha in this exceptional time allows us – sinners – to lay down all our burdens and to become pure and without blemish". This is an important and strong sentence. Soldier Mielke grants himself full absolution.

From everyday life he can report that he is healthy, that he enjoys a good appetite, and he encloses stamps for packages in which he hopes to find "pączken" (pączki – doughnuts). The Russians are not conducting any major operations. For several days they had a combat alert, sleeping in their uniforms and with full equipment, but the comrades at the front managed without their help. Although he knows that in the fifth year of the war there may be hardships and one must husband what one has, if it were possible, he asks that five-fingered gloves be sent to him. He has two-fingered mittens – so his hands do not freeze – but the multi-fingered ones are more useful.
