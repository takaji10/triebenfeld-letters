 – pretty and intelligent, but not a believer; the second a *Volksdeutsche* (ethnic German). The first began writing to him from Wallensen. Somewhat unintelligently. "But one thing is certain for me: I will not marry before I find a believing girl". He must have the guarantee of a deep communion – especially since in the future he is to become a pastor.

Apart from that, everything is well – also in the military respect. When he compares the lot of his comrades fighting in the south or with his own last winter, things are so far 50 percent better and more beautiful. But the beautiful time can end any day. "Thus our commander said recently – the front from the Arctic Ocean [Eismeer] to Italy can at any moment become open". As a Christmas present he sends to the family the splendid little book *Der Weg des Glaubens* [The Way of Faith] received from Hamburg. "Write often". Their commander knew what he was talking about. As early as January 1944 the determined offensive of the Red Army set out, whose aim was the lifting of the blockade of Leningrad – which took place on 27 January 1944. The Russian attacks forced Army Group North into retreat. They withdrew to the region of Lake Peipus (now on the border of Estonia and Russia). Bruno Mielke's unit belonged to this formation. In the south, at the same time, an offensive set out which over the following five months would bring the liberation of Ukraine, Volhynia and the Crimea. Still at the end of 1943, in the days from 28 November to 1 December, there takes place in Tehran the meeting of the leaders of the great coalition – Churchill, Roosevelt and Stalin.

The next letter that has survived bears the date of 2 March 1944. Bruno reassures his loved ones that they need not be afraid needlessly. "After a heavy week on duty we have, thanks to God's graciousness, quiet days so far. Every day is like a feast, like a present. And if the Lord sends heavy days again, then He will also graciously help to bear their burden. Only to hold out until Easter, and we shall have the winter behind us.
