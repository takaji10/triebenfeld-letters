 the multitude of thoughts that constantly accompany him as he dreams of the longed-for marriage. He asks how his brother is getting on with Erika, whether sisters Anna and Kadzia will be getting married this year. They are not to wait with that until his arrival on leave. That is all. About his frontline service he writes one sentence. "Things go very differently for me." For wartime conditions he receives quite a lot of letters from potential candidates. But the post brings more disappointments than hopes in this matter. The twenty-three-year-old young lady from Trzemeszno thanks him for Bruno's photograph, on which he looks younger than in reality. On matters of religion she states outright: "As for my faith and community, I will tell you, Sir, this very clearly. As I already wrote to you earlier, Sir, I am a Seventh-day Adventist. The greatest privilege is that I sanctify the day which our Saviour also sanctified when he walked the ends of the earth, that is, Sunday. We call ourselves Adventists because we await the second coming of Christ and we keep the seventh day." This declaration cuts short the further acquaintance.

In turn, there reached Bruno from Lipice a letter from Ursula – the leader of a B.D.M. troop (Bund Deutscher Mädel – the League of German Girls). It is the female section of the Nazi youth organisation Hitlerjugend. She is disappointed that the two previous letters she sent came back to her. She encloses them in the envelope again as proof. The girl writes that she has returned to her old path. Earlier her love for Bruno blinded her! What does that mean today, when she reflects on it? That it is the only path for her: "I believe in the almighty God, who will not allow the downfall of Germany. Every faith that is directed against my beloved fatherland I reject from myself. Therefore I do not need any intermediary, those who so often see only their little 'church' and do not perceive the highest goal for the Church, which should be Germany. Our Führer never intended to separate the Church from the state – quite the contrary! In the year 1933 services were established as an obligation for all the organisations of the party.
