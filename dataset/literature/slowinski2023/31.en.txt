The autumn months of 1941 were a terrible time in this land. From September to November, the liquidation of the Zagórów ghetto lasted. Jews were transported in trucks to the forests near Kazimierz Biskupi (the Wygoda area), in which they were suffocated with exhaust fumes. Those who were brought in alive were shot and thrown into pits with lime. Letters from this period are few, and they are the last before Bruno Mielke's enlistment into the Wehrmacht. Whether there were more of them, and if so, what became of them, we do not know. In any case, of the tragedy taking place in those days in Zagórów we find not the slightest mention.
