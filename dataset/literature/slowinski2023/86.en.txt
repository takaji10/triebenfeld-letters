Christians are people guided by the principles of mercy, love of neighbor, penitents... It is the religion of weak people, whose canon of laws cannot apply to a true Germanic man. As one could read outright in the letter of one of the girls to Bruno Mielke quoted above, the Church acts against the Reich and the Führer. The realities of everyday life in the Nazi world forced Christians into conduct that allowed them to survive. But only that? For example, the organization Deutsche Christen [German Christians] was founded – who accepted the suggestions of Nazi ideologues about the chosen character of the German nation. There were also found experts on the Bible proving that Christ was an Aryan...

In Gustav's letter of 1 May 1942 one can find the "fruit" of these fairly widely proclaimed teachings. He writes about the times of Christ: "Should wise people then have lived at all and stopped God, and said: What are You doing, choose the Savior from the Germanic people, not from among the Jews. But it pleased God thus." In May 1942 in Zagórów and the vicinity there was no longer a single Jew. But Gustav Adolf looks further with worry and, on one of those occasions, encodes the question of what the authorities intend to undertake g. J. – an abbreviation of the words *gegen Juden* – against the Jews[^24]. "The scale of irrationalism, brutality, servility and cultism can be seen in the poetry of the brown poets" – writes Hubert Orłowski[^25]. In presenting the leader, words are chosen from the New Testament; Hitler is God's anointed, chosen by God or other, vaguely defined powers. He is the savior of the German nation.
