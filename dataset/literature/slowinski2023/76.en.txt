Bruno's letters from November already come from Finland. Addressed to Parkkino (the Petsamo region) in the far north. The field post number 31 059 indicates that Bruno served in the 136th Mountain Rifle Regiment. He sends his sister money saved from his pay as a present on the occasion of her approaching birthday. In the package there is also a jar, into which he placed several little bunches of berries found between the snow and the ice. On 8 November, when he writes these words, it is the 10th anniversary of Mama's death. He was at a Catholic field mass. "There, too, one can hear a worthwhile word". Later, as was his custom, he went into the forest to pray and to read the Gospel of St. Matthew appointed for that Sunday: "For many are called, but few are chosen" (Mt 22:14)[^17]. Great and solemn words, which he passes on to his sister for another, new year of life.

A few days later he writes to his brother. He knows that thanks to his letters to his sisters and father, Gustav is up to date with news from Finland, but he prefers that they write to each other directly. What gladdens him above all is the news of another deferment. He believes that Gustav can accomplish more for the common good on the spot than as a recruit. The fact that he is a teacher would have no significance at the beginning of service. Just as Bruno will be a "Barras" and treated accordingly. He himself remembers that time as very hard, and everyone must go through it. He survived that difficult time; now
