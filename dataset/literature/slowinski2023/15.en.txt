At the meeting, at which the professor's wife took part – Professor Alicja Pihan-Kijasowa, I heard words of strong encouragement to publish the article as an independent print publication, independently of placing it in a collective work. To my surprise, the professor indicated persons and a publishing house with whom he had already spoken earlier, encouraging them to take an interest in my work. I therefore have a particular reason to thank the venerable professor cordially for this unexpected and quite non-standard mobilization and support. I am grateful to Katarzyna Połom, to Łukasz Parus, to Fr. Waldemar Wunsz, and to the sons Maciej and Piotr for their valuable remarks and support during the long period of work on the emerging text. I thank very much Messrs Zdzisław Jacaszek and Damian Kruczkowski, thanks to whom the editorial work was set in motion. In this way "Listy żołnierza Wehrmachtu…" ["The Wehrmacht soldier's letters…"] reach the Reader's hands in the form of a small booklet with an extended analysis and appropriate illustrations.
