# Słowiński (2023), p. 97
# translation by the editor's files

 of the war in the year 1945 the inhabitants of Łazińsk met the same fate as all Germans, namely the loss of land, flight, expulsions, deportations, dispersal. It rarely happens that former neighbours meet anywhere and share memories. In contrast to the former inhabitants of Łazińsk[^30], the inhabitants of the neighbouring Orlina maintain contact to this day. Here it is Mrs Marga Todt, a former teacher from the school in [nearby] Orlina, who cares about maintaining the bond. Every year at the beginning of May she organizes the "Orlina Meetings" (Orlińskie Spotkania) at her home in Mühlheim an der Ruhr.

[^30]: Łazińsk and Zagórów were visited regularly by Erich Kuhn, born at Łazińsk, a descendant of one of the oldest families that founded the village in 1746. For his merits for the cooperation between Poles and Germans, in 2017 he received from the state authorities of Germany the "Medal Zasługi za pracę na rzecz Porozumienia Międzynarodowego" [Medal of Merit for Work towards International Understanding].

    The village was also visited by, among others, Eugenia Plitt née Schmechel; her descendants came to these lands around the year 1778 from eastern Brandenburg.

[^31]: From the year 1914 under German occupation – note added by M.S.
