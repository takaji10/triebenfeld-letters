 In this way they will best arm themselves for the times of drought that may come. Nothing happens without the will and permission of the Lord. These are decisive and grave months; one must be able to sacrifice one's own wishes.

This is the first letter in which Mielke allows himself a certain generalisation of the situation on his sector of the front. Until now these were "narrow plans", showing the individual threats and dangers menacing him directly. Now he says: prepare yourselves for a hard time, for things do not look good. Regardless of what he had in mind and what his knowledge really was, in saying that the fighting was entering its decisive phase he was not mistaken. The next letter preserved in the hiding place is dated Eastern Front, 27 July 1943. At the beginning of the month – on 5 July – the German attack on the Kursk salient begins. The struggle lasted until the end of August. The Russians knew the enemy's plans, codenamed "Cytadela", well and adopted the defensive strategy proposed by Żukow. When the forces of the attacking armies, bogging down in the mud and dying on the mines and under the murderous artillery fire, were exhausted, they launched a counterattack. It was the greatest tank battle in history. The lost battle meant that the German command was no longer able, until the end of the war, to seize the strategic initiative on the Eastern Front. "Things are relatively calm with us" – he writes. "Confirmation of the fact that the Russians are using all their forces in the south in order to win back the central areas again. Here it is often gloomy and misty. Then it is dangerous, because the Russians gladly make raids then, but we are already on the alert and our machines do not fail."

Bruno Mielke's thoughts run to Łazińsk, where people can gather the grain from the fields in peace and run across the stubble fields. Here, on the depopulated and harsh north, one feels nothing of that by which the people in the village are living now. And the harvests are important for the war and for the nation.
