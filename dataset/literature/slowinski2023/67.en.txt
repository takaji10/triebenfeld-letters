# Słowiński (2023), p. 67
# translation by the editor's files

"Death for the fatherland is worthy of eternal honour." This is a quotation from the writer, poet and officer of the Prussian army Ewald Christian von Kleist, who died of the wounds received in the battle of Kunowice.

This drawing is a motif often exploited in propaganda and war publications. An oak on a hillock, helmets on the grave of the fallen, and cranes returning to their native nests.
