# Słowiński (2023), p. 64
# translation by the editor's files

 of February it was not yet known how great a significance the lost battles on the Wołga and the Don had. The southern front also failed to achieve its intended objectives (the capture of the oil sources) and Grupa Armii A had to withdraw from the Kaukaz. All the signs in heaven and on earth, however, said that something important had happened.







In this sense, Bruno's marriage efforts, his wedding plans, can be regarded as a kind of defensive reaction, an escape from the black everyday reality, a peculiar repression of what was reaching him. For Bruno Mielke was no fool. Two weeks later he sends a letter which is already considerably more open. "For a week now we have been here in the reserve and we lead the barrack life of soldiers. Today is the first Sunday – to my and everyone's joy, without duty. One can truly meditate spiritually and clerically. Spiritually is my thinking and my writing of letters to you. Clerically I am, as before, still lonely and in the desert. When the hours of holding the service come, I always feel the pull. Today, too, I was able to do it. […] I went into the snow-covered mountains and found again an altar for prayer… How good it is then for a poor, lonely soul, if it finds itself near the one who said: 'I am with you through all the days'". But he immediately qualifies: "I cannot tell everyone about this. Most would consider me a dreamer." But for him this is blissful and beatifying dreaming, a real happiness which most pitiable people do not know. "Dreaming" is one of the milder terms he could have heard. Many comrades steeped in the new faith often coarsely mocked the humble adherents of the religion of the weak, the eternal penitents, as Christians were contemptuously called. Hence that search for a solitude. It is a kind of escape, of hiding oneself, in order to devote oneself to prayer in peace.
