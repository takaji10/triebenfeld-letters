A broad search in Polish and German sources available on the internet and inquiries about the further fate of Bruno Mielke yielded no results. The letter of 14 May 1944 is the last material trace he left behind.

"You walk in darkness, and your ignorance is like the cloak of night"[^27].

In the last year of the war, the 2. Gebirgs Division is transferred from Norway to Denmark. It later took part in heavy fighting against the Western Allies near Trier and Philippsburg – Heilbronn. Part of the division ended the war in Württemberg, while the remaining units capitulated in the Tyrol[^28].
