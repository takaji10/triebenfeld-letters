### Łazińsk – A Contemporary Note

At the present time, the former Łazińsk, whose name often underwent various "modifications", exists within the boundaries of the Zagórów commune, Słupca county, in the form of two villages.

**Łazińsk Pierwszy**

The village was founded in 1746. The initiators of its founding were Andrzej and Paweł Prusińscy. To this day it is a model example of an Olęder village that has preserved its original spatial layout: farm buildings, a mosaic of fields, forests, meadows, canals and field roads. There are numerous wooden, clay and bog-iron-ore buildings. In the years 1975-1998 the locality administratively belonged to the Konin voivodeship.

On the territory of the village there are the remains of an Evangelical cemetery with scant remains of gravestones.

The village numbers 159 inhabitants.

> Data of the Office of the Town and Commune of Zagórów dated 23.10.2023.

**Łazińsk Drugi (formerly: Holendry Łazińskie, Holendry Stara Huta)**

A village in Poland situated in the Greater Poland Voivodeship, in Słupca county, in the Zagórów commune.

The village was founded as an Olęder settlement. At the beginning of the 18th century a glassworks based on the local resources of glass sand operated in the village. In 1943 the Hitlerites opened a bog-iron-ore mine here. The village has a dispersed character.
