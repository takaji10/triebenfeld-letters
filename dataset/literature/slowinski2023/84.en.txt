# Słowiński (2023), p. 84
# translation by the editor's files

Bruno has luck with his commanders, but no luck with girls. He writes that he has "fallen for" Anna Steinbart and now pins his hopes on Elizabeth Kaschuba, who is trying to creep into his heart. "Not bad-looking, educated, an excellent housekeeper." (Where did he get that information?) Of course – as always in his case – the matter of checking her religiousness and faith remains… He urges his brother not to dawdle with his advances to Erika, because this indecisiveness may cost him her. As a corporal, in place of the single chevron previously worn on his sleeve V has a double chevron. "So nothing special" – he concludes. Happily, a letter dated 4 May 1944 has survived. It came by airmail. (Field post number 48 341 refers to the 67th Medical Company of the 2nd Mountain Division). Here we learn that Bruno Mielke was on leave in Łazińsk. He could thus have left behind part of the letters that had reached him at the front.

He writes to his brother Gustav. Great joy for him is the news that at Easter his brother was deemed worthy of the Lord's Supper[^22], that he overcame himself to show strong fidelity to his God. That he did so after a long time, broke through his inhibition and opened himself to "forgiving grace." He had long been expecting such a letter and thanks him for the trust and candor shown to him. "It gladdens me also because I can well put myself in your situation and understand you and your needs." There was a time when his brother's stance – the departure from the Church – aroused in him great, depressing worry. [^22]: The Lord's Supper. In the tradition of the Evangelical Churches, the Eucharist – the Lord's Supper is, next to Holy Baptism, a Sacrament. In accordance with the words of its institution by Jesus Christ (Mt 26,26-28; Lk 22,19-20; 1Cor 11,23-26) and with the exposition of the Books of Concord, the Lord's Supper is the true body and the true blood of Jesus Christ truly present, and it is administered to believers under the form of bread and wine. Therefore the faithful receive the Eucharist under both kinds: bread and wine.
