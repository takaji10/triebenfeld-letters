# Żychliński (1891), p. 225
# translation by Claude

Countess **Michalina**, born 1848, wife of Ambroży **Zaborowski**, senator of the Kingdom of Poland, actual councillor of state and imperial-royal chamberlain, heir of the estate of Pińczyce.

Count **Józef** (13th generation), born 29 June 1854, heir of the estate of Brzeź, married in 1880 Marya Józefa Felicya **Kołłupajło** of the Bożawola arms, born 18 September 1858, daughter of the late Ferdynand and of Julia née Tołłoczko of the Pobóg arms (daughter of Julian Tołłoczko, marshal of Wołkowysk), granddaughter of Ignacy Kołłupajło, chamberlain of Wołkowysk, and of Marya née Kołłupajło, heiress of the estates of Greater and Lesser Święcica, Tereszki and Krasnybród in the Wołkowysk district. Of this marriage a son:

Count **Witold** Józef Ferdynand (14th generation), born 30 May 1881.

Count **Tadeusz** (13th generation), born 9 March 1857, heir of the estate of Wielkie, married Marya **Miączyńska** of the line at Gumowo, daughter of Władysław and of Aniela Gniazdowska.[^1]

Count **Jerzy** (13th generation), born 21 August 1860.

Countess **Marya**, born 26 September 1862, married on 20 May 1885 at Warsaw Władysław **Karski** of the Jastrzębiec arms, born 30 September 1859, heir of estates in the Radom country, son of Wincenty and of Konstancya née Wodzińska, and nephew of the late Stanisław, gentleman of the bedchamber of the Russian court, councillor of the committee of the Land Credit Society, married to Princess Jadwiga Czetwertyńska. The marriage was blessed by the Most Reverend Father Popiel, Archbishop of Warsaw.

[^1]: Our information on Count Witold's sons goes to 1885; we therefore do not know whether they have more children. Author's note.
