# breyer1935mutterland  8  en
# Breyer (1935b), p. 8
# translation by Claude

Silesian dialect, the way of farming (the growing of hops), the customs, the dress, the way the houses are built, the hymn books, mostly of Züllichau and Breslau, and the family names give a convincing pointer to Lower Silesia, in part to the Warthe marshes [Wartebruch]. Special importance in this case, too, falls to the registers of the Lutheran congregations of Zagorow, Grodziec, Stawiszyn, Turek and Władysławow, which contain statements of origin, though not always in the number one would wish. Some of the writers of the church books gave no information at all on the place of origin for years. Sometimes, too, in the death entries, the relatives did not know the place of birth; this was mostly the case with retired farmers (Ausgedinger).

Going through the marriage and death registers of the congregation of *Zagorow* from 1843 to 1847 gave 18 place names, divided as follows: Wollstein county 1, the western Warthe marshes, Lower Silesia 1, the Silesian dialect area west of Posen, in Posen county, 2; the Netze marshes 2, the counties east of Posen 2, from the rest of the Reich 5.

From the church books of the Lutheran congregation of *Grodziec* we take for the period 1797 to 1826 137 place names: the neighbourhood of Neutomischel county 36, Meseritz 3, Wollstein 5; the western Warthe marshes 20, the Oder marshes 3; Lower Silesia 18, the Silesian dialect area in Posen county 16, the Pomeranian dialect area in the same county 8, the Netze district 15, Pomerania 8, the rest of the Reich 5.

For the colonists of the congregation of *Stawischin* we found for the period 1780 to 1830 70 statements of place; of these Neutomischel county had 16, Meseritz 1, Wollstein 3; Lower Silesia 7, the Silesian dialect area in Posen county 5, the Pomeranian one in the same county 5; the western Warthe marshes 17, the Netze district 8, the rest of the Reich 8.

The church books of the congregation of *Turek* supply for 1837 to 1847 42 statements of place: Neutomischel county 6, Lower Silesia 14, Silesian dialect areas in Posen county 3, the Pomeranian area east of Posen 7, the Netze marshes 10, the Reich 2.

For *Władysławow*, the mother congregation of the Kalisz country, we found for 1775 to 1800 the following 15 statements of place: Neutomischel county 2, Lower Silesia 5, the Silesian dialect area in Posen county 3, the eastern, Pomeranian part of the same county 2, the Netze marshes 1, from Pomerania 2.
