# breyer1935mutterland  13  en
# Breyer (1935b), p. 13
# translation by Claude

Particular credit for the growth of the Lutheran congregation of Warsaw was earned by Pastor Karl Heinrich Wilhelm *Schmidt*, in office from 1790 to 1813, who was born in Karge. For many years he was guardian of the German Lutheran school in Warsaw and president of the Consistory. His greatest merit, however, is that in 1803 he published the *first hymn book* for the Lutheran church in the Kingdom of Poland, whose improved edition appeared only 40 years later. From the Polish national hero Th. Kościuszko Pastor Schmidt received a letter in his own hand for exemplary care of the sick and truly patriotic feeling, which he had shown in the war year 1795.

The later superintendent and pastor of the Warsaw congregation, Karl *Lauber*, whom the South Prussian consistory installed as preacher in 1803, came from Schmiegel; he was the eldest son of the pastor there. The first pastor of the congregation of Michalke, founded in 1779, Albert *Bocianowski*, was born in Lötzen in East Prussia. The first pastor of the congregation of Neuhof, founded in 1775, Gottfried *Leske*, came from Rastenburg in East Prussia; he was also active as a writer. The pastor of the congregation of Stawischin, Karl *Callmann*, was born in Bojanowo and died in Stawischin in 1794.

In the Prussian period eleven new Lutheran congregations arose in central Poland. In the mother congregation of the Vistula lowland, in Iłow, Pastor *Wittold*, born in Schadewalde near Marienburg, held office. In the congregation of Grodziec [Grodzierc] the first pastor, *Bergemann*, came from Fraustadt, where his father was a cloth manufacturer. Pastor *Herzberg* in Kalisch came from Schweidnitz. Pastor *Grimm* in Stawiszyn was born in Großstrelitz in Silesia.

In the time of the Duchy of Warsaw there immigrated: Pastor *Gieser* in Babiak, born at Wronke; Pastor *Hewelke*, later superintendent of the Płock diocese, an uncommonly able organiser of the parishes under him. On his gravestone is to be read:

> *"Death is not death, only the ennobling of mortal nature. Resting place of Johann Karl Hewelke, pastor of the Lutheran congregation at Plozk, superintendent of the Plozk diocese, born at Thorn in the year 1771, died at Plozk 1836."*

The long-serving pastor and president of the Reformed consistory in Warsaw, the court preacher Karl *Diehl*, was born in Lissa in 1765. The deserving pastor of Władysławów, the mother congregation in the Kalisz country, Karl Gottlieb *Bartsch*, who held office in that congregation from 1810 to 1852, came from Michelsdorf in Silesia.
