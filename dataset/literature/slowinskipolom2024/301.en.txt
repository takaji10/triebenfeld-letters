### Protocol of the meeting of the communal/parish assembly of the Cantors' Council of Łazińsk, held on 26 November 1928

The agenda included:

Construction of a small church

Acquisition of funds for the construction

Election of the building committee

1\) After long deliberation the undersigned resolved to build a small church on the territory of the Cantors' Council area.

2\) The collection of funds for this construction was approved in the form of a tax amounting to 7 złotych per day and 10 zł per citizen.

3\) The building committee consists of the following gentlemen, whose names are: Magnat Schuller, Schuch Gustaw, Brunsch Lenard, August Grunwald, Gnater Seer, Wilhelm Moritz, August Schonfisch, Osdaldt Mirundt, Gustaw Mielke, teacher. It was then decided that, when purchasing material for the construction, not only the members of the committee would be present, but also two or three members of the communal/parish community.

Further steps will be decided at special meetings.

Chairman: August Grunwald

Deputy: Schuch Gustav

Minutes taker: Gottlieb Karinnger Haar
