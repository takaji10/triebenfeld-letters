#### Thou hast given me the shield of thy salvation (Ps 18,36)

The short letter which Bruno writes on Sunday 20 December 1942 (it reaches Łazińsk on 5 January) announces the sending of a longer description of how he spent the Christmas feast and contains only one account: "During the hours of worship from 9 to 11 I stood sentry at my post, which is the more dangerous one (today we had to make the change). The main post consists of an oblong stone bunker. There I really ought to have been. But 'by mistake' I went to the side bunker. While I was just there giving myself to prayer, a shell of the Russian artillery struck the main post, broke a hole in the wall and threw many stones inside. What would have happened to me had I been just there?". And he ends: "Let God be praised and glorified".

To the account of the first day of the Feast Mielke sits just before midnight, straight after coming off the post on which between 21.00 and 23.00 he had stood with a good comrade Schwartz. He honestly admits that he had feared these Feasts and the onset of longing. But it was not so bad. In the forenoon the captain and the divisional chaplain came on a visit. They even came up to his post, and the chaplain was so kind as to ask about the family and gave him a bar of chocolate, wishing him a Happy New Year.
