# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 177
# translation by the editor's files

On Sunday 8 August he sends a letter written in some religious exaltation, at eleven o'clock, in the blessed hour of the services. "In my spirit I see in all countries the temples, I see in them those who through the blood and salvation of Christ have become my siblings". And he sees himself in the pulpit – in the most splendid of all official vestments, proclaiming the gospel. Instead of greetings he asks them to read Psalm 23.

Psalm 23  
*The Lord is my shepherd*…

*A Psalm of David.*  
*The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want*.  
*He maketh me to lie down in green pastures*.  
*He leadeth me beside the still waters*.  
*He restoreth my soul*.  
*He leadeth me in the paths of righteousness*  
*for his name's sake*.  
*Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death*,  
*I will fear no evil: for thou art with me*,  
*thy rod and thy staff they comfort me*.  
*Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies*,  
*thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over*.  
*Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me*  
*all the days of my life*.  
*and I will dwell in the house of the Lord for ever*[^60].
