# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 54
# translation by the editor's files

 Tadeusz Jelinek of the Evangelical-Reformed Parish in Żychlin; the starosta (county head) of Słupca, Jacek Bartkowiak; the mayor of Zagórów, Roman Kulterman; the councillors of Zagórów with their chairman Ryszard Sikorski; and many other invited guests. In the full church, the hymns sung by the male choir Quarta of Zagórów rang out beautifully. The group of former Protestant inhabitants was represented by Erich Kuhn, born at Łazińsk, of Detmold in Germany.

On 15 August 2021, after the pandemic break, the parishioners returned to meetings with the participation of guests from Germany. During the service, the hymn *Amazing Grace* was played on the saxophone by Jakub Wunsz, while Alan Woźniak accompanied him on the piano. After the service, the parish picnic took place, as usual. The collected offerings were handed over to aid the persons affected by the torrential rains and floods in Germany. On Sunday, 21 August 2022, the next Polish-German service took place in Zagórów, as is traditional. Taking part in it were inhabitants of Germany whose roots reach back to the vicinity of Zagórów. Our guests also included parishioners from Żychlin with the Rev. Tadeusz Jelinek. During the service, there performed the ecumenical choir operating at the parish in Konin. The third Sunday of August, the 20th day of the month – such was the date of the next meeting of former and present parishioners and their friends, at the service and the parish picnic in 2023. Among others, we remembered the late Edith Kuhn, the wife of Erich Kuhn. During the service, Daniel Słowiński played and sang. The cooperation with him bore fruit in the organisation of an extraordinary concert, the proceeds of which were destined to help in bringing out a book devoted to the history of the parish. This one-of-a-kind musical event took place on 23 September. At Daniel Słowiński's side there performed: Joanna Słowińska, Zuzanna Wilczyńska, Agnieszka Witkiewicz and Wojciech Nowakowski. The moving concert, maintained in an ecumenical spirit, gathered many listeners, who favoured the coming into being of the publication commemorating the history of the Protestants of Zagórów. The proceeds of the collection and of the sale of the ceramic fund-raising bricks made by Kinga Kujawska were credited to the account destined for the publishing work. We believe that many beautiful moments still lie before us. The Evangelical-Augsburg Parish
