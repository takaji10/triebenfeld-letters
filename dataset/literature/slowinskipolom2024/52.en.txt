# translation by the editor's files

 the choir of the Evangelical parish in Detmold under the direction of Mr Johann Pöld, established not only fruitful cooperation but also personal friendships, organising joint concerts in Zagórów, Konin and Detmold. In 2001, the choir Cantabile went off on a several-day tour together with their German friends and had the opportunity to give several unforgettable concerts. The crowning of this musical adventure was the recording of a joint CD on 28 April 2001. The performances of the choir Cantabile accompany most of the August services, which are at the same time reunions of former and present parishioners and of their descendants.


On 18 August 2002, the inauguration of the 120th anniversary of the consecration of the Evangelical-Augsburg church and of the sixtieth anniversary of confirmation took place. This beautiful ceremony will long remain in the memory of the parishioners. In the years 2003-2013, the tradition of the August services is being continued[^24]. Every year a numerous delegation comes to Zagórów from Detmold to pray together with the local Protestants and, after the service, to take part in a parish picnic, to talk, to reminisce and to plan further meetings. In August 2008, the church building was fitted with a new electronic clock and bells, whose sound carries to this day through the little streets of the town and over the meadows along the Warta. In the morning, the carillon plays the hymn *Kiedy ranne wstają zorze*; in the evening, one hears the melody of the hymn *Wszystkie nasze dzienne sprawy*.

On 24 August 2014, the traditional service took place with the participation of former Protestant inhabitants of Zagórów and its surroundings. Taking part in it were guests from the partner parish in Detmold, parishioners of the Reformed parish of Żychlin, and parishioners of the Konin parish. The sermon for the Polish-German community present at the service was preached by pastor Burkhard Krebber of Detmold.
