Further on there is already news of the much delayed, wet harvest. The letter ends with the words: "The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God and the communion of the Holy Ghost be with us all. Amen. Jesus works miracles. *Heil Hitler*!".

In the letters from sister Olga and brother-in-law Erwin there are great thanks for the monetary gifts and for the fact that with his own body he shields them and their beloved homeland from the evil enemy. One of the letters opens with an exalted appeal for the help of Christ, through His wounds, passion and death, in that sudden need into which the brother has fallen. The sister believes she has the right to such words, because she and her husband want not only to wear the former *hakenkreuz* (swastika) but also to cultivate it in religion. "May God Almighty send down upon you grace and mercy. Our one most ardent wish is to see you alive and in health when this evil war is over". She briefly describes the christening of the little son, to whom they gave the name Adolf. "The holy baptism was modest, at home". Unfortunately they did not receive permission to kill a pig, but in the church the pastor arranged everything nicely and delivered a beautiful speech. The godparents were Gustav and Leokadia. "We liked it very much". She ends with the information that she has packed a bit of bacon and black bread.
Well baked.

Sister Anna, too, strives to support her brother strongly. "When at the cinema I watch the newsreels and the conquerors of mountains advance, I always think that you too are there. And then I am so proud of you. I know that being at the front does not belong to pleasures, but I would set off at once were I a man. After all, it must be an uplifting feeling, to defend the Fatherland, so that it does not bend before the wild hordes, and its inhabitants from mistreatment. We shall always be grateful to you for it […] we feel with our hearts everything that you physically must endure". And further: "In truth it is senseless for me to write this to you.
