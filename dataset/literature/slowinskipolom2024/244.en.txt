# translation by the editor's files

 of the local Parish Council, the Mayor of Zagórów Wiesław Radniecki, the Head of the Lądek Commune Jerzy Kaszna, the Commune Secretary Mrs Alina Kmieć, the Director of the PDPS in Zagórów, Mr Marian Parus, the Parish Council from Konin, the Fire Brigade Band from Zagórów and many fellow believers from neighbouring parishes, performed the act of its dedication".

The first residents moved into the senior home even before its official opening. They were:

- Aldegunda Kinast b. 20.09.1926 in Bielawy, moved in on 9.06.1995.
- Marta Deitner b. 11.11.1922 in Stara Paprotnia, moved in on 8.06.1995.
- Edward Szwarc b. 4.09.1926 in Wrąbczynkowskie Holendry, moved in on 8.06.1995.
- Helena Wegner b. 6.01.1920 in the village of Dąbie, came to the senior home in July 1995.
- Genowefa Dalke b. 11.10.1911 in Linden, took up residence in the senior home on 1.10.1996.
- Erna Woltman b. 7.07.1923 in Bielawy – at the senior home in Zagórów since 21.10.1995.
- Lidia Zach b. 11.11.1912 in the village of Młynek, moved in on 15.10.1996.

Initially the residents of the senior home were looked after by Mrs Ilona Bürger; soon afterwards, from 12 June 1995, social care was taken over by Mrs Erika Fandrich, supported by her husband Artur. In January 1997 support was also received from the Municipal Social Welfare Centre (Mrs Kazimiera Marczak); nursing care was provided by Mrs Gabriela Okseniuk, and medical care by the physician Mariusz Eicke. The bookkeeping at the care home was kept by Mrs Teresa Kierzek, and the centre was run by Rev. Andrzej Mendrok.
