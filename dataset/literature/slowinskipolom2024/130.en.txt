After a fortunate departure from Poland, already after the end of the war, he reached Canada, where he founded and ran until his retirement a large bakery in Midland in Ontario, which was then taken over by his son. Arnulf and his wife Lydia had three sons. A few years ago a tornado severely damaged the bakery's buildings, and the enormous costs of repair meant that Gary Beutler had to turn to something entirely different. It was Arnulf who, right after the war, began the search for his brother Alfred, of whom the family had had no news from the moment he was taken to the front. Arnulf Beutler died at the age of 71 in 1994. His wife Lydia, born in 1926, lives in Canada to this day. The penultimate child of Adolf and Emilia was Arwid Beutler, born in Dąbrowica in 1925. In him, as in the next member of the Beutler clan, there awoke the need for a change of place, a change of surroundings. He left the family home at the age of fourteen; he stayed in Germany, the USA, then again in Germany and once more in the USA, where in Florida he was a pilot. He then worked in an aircraft factory, and there, as the result of an unfortunate accident, he lost one eye. Apparently unable to warm a lasting place for himself anywhere, he emigrated to Canada, where he settled near his brother Arnulf and where, not far from his sister-in-law Lydia, he lived until his death in February 2020. When he left the family home, he had no contact with the family — above all because the war had scattered them across the world, and he himself, too, travelled a great deal after it, seeking for himself a place on earth. Gertruda and he found each other through their sister-in-law Lydia on 2 January 2014, after 75 years. Arwid telephoned Gertruda and, after dozens of years, they spoke for the first time by phone. Enormous emotions accompanied it. From that time on the siblings spoke with each other regularly.

The youngest child of the farmer Adolf Beutler of Dąbrowica was Edmund Beutler, born, like his siblings, precisely on that beautifully situated farm in Dąbrowica, not far from the bank of the Warta, in 1929. In that same year Ludwik Beutler, Adolf's father, died; he had been spending the last years of his life with his son Ewald in Koło.
