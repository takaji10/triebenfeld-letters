# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 330
# translation by the editor's files

**On 23 September 2023, in the Evangelical-Augsburg Church** in Zagórów, an extraordinary concert took place: **Music and the Sacred**.

The originators of the event were Pastor Waldemar Wunsz and Daniel Słowiński, and the main aim was to support the printing of the book *The Broken Link. Evangelicals of the Zagórów Land*.

Performing: Daniel Słowiński and friends – Zuzanna Wilczyńska, Agnieszka Witkiewicz, Joanna Słowińska and Wojciech Nowakowski.

The patrons of the concert were the Fundacja Alana Woźniak Per Musicam and the MGOK in Zagórów.

Especially for this occasion Kinga Kujawska fired commemorative ceramic tiles with the image of the Zagórów Evangelical church.

The audience that filled the church thanked the performers with long standing ovations.

The donations collected in the collection box from the participants of the concert boosted the publishing budget by the sum of 3200 zł.
