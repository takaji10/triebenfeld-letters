##### The denominational school in the village of Anielewo

In this locality there existed a one-class school with German as the language of instruction, attended by a fairly numerous group of 60–70 children. The teacher at the institution was the cantor Bruno Mielke – the brother of Gustav, the teacher and headmaster of the school on Łazińsk. Shortly before the outbreak of the Second World War, Bruno Mielke was serving as a teacher in the locality of Maślaki near Wilczyn. It is difficult to determine whether he worked at both schools at once, or whether someone else, whose name we shall never know, replaced him in Anielewo. After the start of the military operations of the Third Reich, the young cantor, who dreamed of completing the seminary and of a pastoral ministry, was called up to serve in the Wehrmacht.

##### The cantors' school in Olchowo

In the beautifully situated settlement of Olchowo there operated a one-class school with German as the language of instruction. More than 40 children attended it. From 1924 the post of teacher was held by the then cantor, Ferdinand Homt. Today not even a trace remains of the school building; a small rise below the forest recalls its former location. Olchowo at present is a locality almost entirely depopulated; most of the buildings left by the pre-war inhabitants have fallen into ruin and been dismantled down to the last plank.
