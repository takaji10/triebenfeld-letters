### The Doctor's wife: the splendour and fall of Janina Breede


Katarzyna Połom



Janina Breede at the age of 38

They say that wherever she appeared, she instantly drew attention. There was something about her – whether in her bearing or in her gaze – that made it impossible to pass her by with indifference. Attractive, with a tall yet graceful figure, she would stroll the streets of Zagórów, and the heels of her elegant shoes beat out a measured rhythm of footsteps. That distinctive sound echoed among the buildings of the pre-war little town.

Her outfits – as if straight out of a fashion magazine – cut by a skilled tailor's hand, fit perfectly.

– Oh, look, there goes Mrs Breede… – people would say, following the woman with their eyes, and in those voices admiration was sometimes mixed with a certain measure of envy.

The wife of the popular doctor Hugo Breede kept her head high, carefully coiffed and topped with a fashionable little hat. Her hand, clad in a glove suited to the season, gripped the handle of her handbag. Right beside her strutted a stately manservant, carrying his mistress's purchases. The elegant lady was recognised and greeted from afar. She would bow slightly; she was famous for her impeccable manners. Among the people captured in a photograph taken in 1938 to commemorate the ceremony of the consecration of the rectory of the Lutheran church in Zagórów, I recognise her unmistakably: she sits second from the right in the front row, at her husband's side. Hugo Breede is quite a distinctive figure: a stout, rather bald man with protruding ears, in round spectacles on a nose of considerable size, and with a moustache fashionable in the 1930s. I note that the Doctor's wife is one of only four women in this picture.
