# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 275
# translation by the editor's files

 the sun was shining – a beautiful sight. At noon the next day everyone drove off home.

#### I danced with the pastor, too

We were also at the wedding at Genia Najman's. She asked only me and a friend. I made all the cakes for her and baked them together with her. First we were at the wedding in the German church, we offered our congratulations. Then Genia sent two relatives to fetch me from home; supper was already being served. Mrs Nowak, Kazimiera, was there too, it seems with her father. I came in and bowed in every direction. The pastor was sitting at the table and at once asked me to sit beside him. I introduced myself. He served me food and did not stir a step from me. They played forfeits, and I bustled about so that I wouldn't have a forfeit, and the pastor kept stopping me so that I wouldn't manage it. And at last I ended up with one! I danced with the pastor, too. He was very handsome, merry, witty, kind, and I think he rather took a fancy to me as well. And when I was to walk home from the wedding, he said he would see me home. But since Dryzner and several Germans were walking that way, I thanked him and took my leave. On the way we also saw Mrs Nowak home.

#### We went to German weddings and funerals

The Helman, the cooper, from whom we bought shavings for kindling next door, wanted to take Grandma as godmother, but the pastor did not agree to it. But we did go to German weddings and funerals. The pastor always gave a speech over the grave, in Polish too when there were many Poles at the funeral.
