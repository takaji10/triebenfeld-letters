At the invitation of the Fundacja Pałac Stolec and the owners of the palace there, in April 2017 we tidied up, together with the inhabitants of the village of Stolec in the West Pomeranian voivodeship, the local Evangelical cemetery. In the course of the work we managed to uncover and restore a dozen or so cast-iron gravestone crosses.

Twice already, in 2017 and 2020, we took part in renovation work at the Evangelical cemetery in Gostków near Wałbrzych. We appeared there at the invitation of the owners of the cemetery, a mother and daughter. Mrs Halina Bryk (who, by the way, trace their lineage from Ląd) and Angelika Babula, two fantastic women, several years ago entered the tender announced by their municipality and bought the cemetery grounds so that a potential buyer would not raze them to the ground. The women established the Fundacja Anna z Gostkowa, which is restoring to its former glory the resting place of the Evangelical inhabitants of Gostków and its surroundings. In 2019 representatives of the Fundacja Anna came to support our activities at the Evangelical cemetery in Święcie in the Rychwał municipality. We began a cooperation with the Towarzystwo Kulturalne TEODOR from Zaniemyśl, which strives for the preservation and renovation of the local Evangelical cemetery. Overall care was taken by the voivodeship conservator of monuments from Poznań.

The activities of the Stowarzyszenie FRYDHOF are not limited solely to physical work at cemeteries; one of our missions is also education. We try to respond to invitations from local communities or groups interested in our work. Behind us are many meetings in schools in Konin, the Konin county, Koło, the Koło county, the Słupca county – among others in Zagórów. I give lectures regularly on history as part of the Third Age University which operates at the Państwowa Wyższa Szkoła Zawodowa in Konin, in the course of which I often speak about the history of the Evangelicals. In 2017 and 2018 we had the pleasure of speaking about our activities at the Centrum Dialogu im.
