##### 2. Further Construction Work and Pastoral Activity

In the territorially vast and numerically large Zagórów parish, construction and repair work went on constantly. A few years after the construction, precisely in 1890, a mighty hurricane tore off the sheet-metal roofing of the roof and the tower. Repair work was necessary, at a cost of 760 roubles.

In 1898 a new house of prayer was built in the cantorate of Chruściki, connected with the Zagórów parish. In the same year the parish also suffered a loss – the house of prayer in Michalinów burned down. In Zagórów itself, the parishioners at their Assembly of 7 March 1913 resolved to begin as quickly as possible the construction of a parsonage, a cantor's house (cantorate) and a school (on a plot purchased from Jan Hilman for 311 roubles). For this purpose a building committee was appointed, composed of August Driesner, Wilhelm Neumann, Paweł Rentz, Ferdynard Schulz, Christian Münch, Friedrich Schütz, August Kintzel, Juliusz Schneider, August Gibel, Daniel Gliege, Edward Kuhn and Heinrich Kinast.

On 16 October 1913 the foundation stone was laid for the construction of the parsonage, the cantor's house and the school. Unfortunately, the construction work was interrupted by the outbreak of the First World War. Work was resumed only in 1931.
