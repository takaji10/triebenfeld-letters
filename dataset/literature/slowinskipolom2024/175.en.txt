Whether the brother wants to enlist – though he is so needed in Łazińsk, and there is the wedding in the plan too. (Soon the Wehrmacht itself will decide about that, without asking Gustav's opinion). At the end Bruno writes: "Two weeks ago we listened to the speech of the Führer. He spoke of how decisive a time our nation and fatherland are living through. On this special spring day, when we have happily survived the winter and the countless dangers, we ought to rejoice and be thankful that God, through our Führer, preserves us from the threatening evil, and how it strengthens us soldiers that we do not bear our sacrifices in vain, do not suffer in vain and – die in vain".

In many of the letters written in the coming months by the soldier Bruno we read with a certain astonishment that he believes more in the successive assurances of the leader and of the various conduct of politicians than in what he sees and must do at the front. How dangerous is the force of propaganda falling on ground ideologically well prepared. In quite a different tone is the letter written six days later. He already knows that he will not get leave for Easter; too many of those with longer service stand in the queue before him. "I already long for the quiet, for the blessed hours, and I recall the time I was able to live through in your house, dear brother. Łazińsk, Zagórów, also Anielewo, Białobłoty, Maślaki, Konary, Grodziec – all these are blessed places!". He will therefore not be at Anni's wedding, and Gustav's plans too may come to nothing. They may call him up to the army. What would be fatal! Evidently the Lord, who is in the heavens, so ordained everything. He believes, however, that "He, the faithful Saviour, will never leave me alone, and at Easter too – as every day – will comfort me with His nearness! This spiritual strengthening is particularly necessary for me now. For soon the decisive attacks must here, in the east, gather strength, and then various thoughts come. It will be as God wills". He advises his loved ones to hear and proclaim the holy word at the time of Passover. That which he now so lacks. In this way they will best arm themselves for the times of drought that may come.
