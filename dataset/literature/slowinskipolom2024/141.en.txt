If one leaves documents important to the family in a house one is forced to abandon, then presumably somewhere at the bottom of the soul a little flame of hope burns that perhaps this is only a painful moment, that soon we shall return to the family nest and to the land that fed us for decades. But – and this question also arises – does the protecting of the writings of Rosenberg, Goebbels or other authors like them not testify that the terrible defeat which the expellees and all of Germany are experiencing has taught them nothing? Are they unable, or do they not wish, to abandon the idea of "their" great Third Reich?
