# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 11
# translation by the editor's files

Zagórów and the surrounding villages are, throughout that entire century, territories of the Russian-Prussian borderland. Not far away, along the Prosna, which flows into the Warta beyond Pyzdry, and further north on the eastern side of Strzałkowo, ran the border of these states. Across the green border, spirits and vodka were smuggled, and even whole herds of cattle and geese were driven over. The latter, on their short little legs, could cover as much as 60 kilometres. My grandfather, who as a young lad took part in these "operations", told how before setting out the geese were "shod", by being driven through warm tar and scattered chaff or fine sand. From Prussia there came mainly medicines, machines and other products of a developed industry. In nearby Słupca various companies were registered, which served as a cover for this widely practised contraband. This unlicensed trade undoubtedly had a positive influence on the economic revival of the region. It was also an important contributing cause of so widespread a raising of geese on the meadows of the Warta valley. This white bird is the traditional chief delicacy of the German festive cuisine…

In Zagórów the best testimony to the settling of the newcomers and their ever higher material status were the two houses of worship handed over to the faithful in that same year 1882: the synagogue on Średnia Street and the Evangelical-Augsburg church on Konińska Street. The revolution of 1905 in Russia awakens in the Poles the hope of a change of fate. In the last months of the year, after the masses in the church, patriotic demonstrations take place. Under banners with the White Eagle, the introduction of the Polish language in the schools and offices was demanded. The effect was the founding in 1906 of the Elementary School, in which teaching was in Polish. The Volunteer Fire Brigade, organized in 1899 by Dr Konstanty Lidmanowski, becomes an important centre of patriotic upbringing and the seed of many cultural initiatives.
