It was no longer only clearing up, hauling away fallen trees and branches, cutting out shrubs and mowing the grass that lay within the bounds of our capabilities; thanks to the numbers and strength of our group it also became possible to lift the heavy stone blocks scattered about, of which the gravestones had been built, and to glue together the ones that had been smashed. More and more often we thought about formally constituting the group, so that, being more credible and transparent, we could apply for grants and subsidies for our activity. On this matter we were unanimous. And our group has been made up, from the very beginning until today, of various people representing almost the entire cross-section of Polish society.

From the very beginning of the functioning of both groups, and later as one, we have cooperated closely with the Evangelical-Augsburg parish of the Holy Spirit in Konin, under whose jurisdiction are now found all the Evangelical cemeteries of the area of the former Konin voivodeship, and with the Evangelical parishes in Koło, Turek and Zagórów. Pastor Waldemar Wunsz was engaged in our activities from the beginning, contributing greatly to them and working with us. The pastor is now a member of the association's board and belongs to the group of founding members. At the end of 2016 we jointly made the decision to create an association, which should make many matters easier for us, not least those connected with seeking financial resources or with obtaining the necessary consents and permits for successive works.

On 13 January 2017 we were entered in the KRS, and from that date the formal period of the existence of the Stowarzyszenie FRYDHOF is counted. Urszula Karolczak became president of the association, Damian Kruczkowski deputy, Radosław Grzelak secretary, Przemysław Tomczyk treasurer, and Waldemar Wunsz a member of the board, and this composition of the board remains to this day. Our activity is focused mainly on Evangelical cemeteries, since there are the most of them – more than 200. It is on the Evangelical cemeteries, such as those in Zagórów itself and its close vicinity within the municipality, that the most has been preserved, to speak in scholarly terms, of the above-ground layer of the cemetery, that is the gravestones, which we try to save.
