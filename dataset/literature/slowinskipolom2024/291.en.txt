#### Population and administration

1815-1918 the village belongs to the Russian partition (Congress Poland)

1921 Official name: Łazińska Kolonia

- gmina Trąbczyn, Słupca county,
- with 900 inhabitants,
- of whom 571 of German nationality — 757 of Evangelical denomination,
- 144 of Polish nationality — 138 of Catholic denomination, 5 of Jewish nationality

1933 Łazińska Kolonia, gmina Trąbczyn, Konin county

- post office, Evangelical parish of Zagórów,
- District Court (Amtsgericht) of Słupca,
- Catholic parish of Trąbczyn

1940-1945 Łazińsk, administrative district (Amtsbezirk) and post office of Drommin (Trąbczyn), Konin county, government region of Inowrocław (Hohensalza)

\*

At the present time the former Łazińsk, whose name was often subject to various "modifications", exists within the gmina of Zagórów, Słupca county, in the form of two villages.  
Łazińsk Pierwszy – the village has 159 inhabitants.  
Łazińsk Drugi – the village has 82 inhabitants[^94].



[^94]: Data of the Office of the Town and Municipality of Zagórów as of 23.10.2023.
