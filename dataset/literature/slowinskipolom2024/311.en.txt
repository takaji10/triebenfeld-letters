### FRYDHOF is not just a cemetery.


Damian Kruczkowski

#### The idea, local heritage, the fight for memory: on the association's activities

How did it come about that we founded an association, and what were the beginnings of the Stowarzyszenie FRYDHOF? We always have a bit of trouble answering that question, because we simply are. It happened. And it is. Something in the nature of an accord of thoughts, views, desires and actions must surely have come to pass. Above all, a desire to work to save things from oblivion. That, too, is how we most often present it. First there was a community of views, actions and motives among individuals acting on their own; then a group came together; and finally that group brought into being an association: the Wielkopolskie Stowarzyszenie na rzecz Ratowania Pamięci FRYDHOF. But before we begin to describe the history of the association itself, it is worth saying something about the motives by which the Stowarzyszenie FRYDHOF is guided. It is precisely this that we speak of when we answer the question: why do you do it? What unites us, namely, is a deep refusal to accept transience and forgetting, as well as the effacement and devastation of the last traces left behind by people – whole social groups – who long ago departed into history.

That refusal, that incomprehension of certain human behaviours leading to the effacement of traces and the denial of historical facts, and even a certain kind of anger directed at the process of forgetting, meant that in several places of so-called eastern Wielkopolska there appeared two groups of enthusiasts to whom the historical heritage of the Hauländer settlers became dear – putting it in confessional terms, the historical heritage of the Evangelicals of eastern Wielkopolska. In many localities formerly inhabited by the settlers, this heritage is already only the cemeteries, which are the last traces of these people's presence.
