### Reproduction Officer

We are publishing a document discovered in the kantorate in Łazińsk, a copy of which we reproduce in full. It is a rare example of a forgery produced by an underground cell specializing in distributing among the Germans fictitious, misleading information and directives.

Header: Reichsstelle für Familienzuwach. (Reich Office for Family Increase) Berlin 15.5.1943. The matter "concerns" a citizen of the Reich. One must add, however, that the German offices caring for the purity of the race carried out a kind of selection among the slave laborers directed to work in Germany, qualifying the chosen men as so-called race breeders.
