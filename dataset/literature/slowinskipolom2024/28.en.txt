# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 28
# translation by the editor's files

 of the town and gmina of Zagórów is far from complete. If one leafs through the pages of the internet records, then in fact we have an insight only into the times of the Second World War. The scholarly literature is exceptionally modest. I thought, therefore, that we would never come closer to sketching a fuller picture of the history of the town and of this land, if we did not try to learn something more about those who lived here two hundred and fifty years ago and whose traces time is erasing. Despite the whole tragic period of the years 1939–1945, one must remember that this is only a part of the history of this land and of its many inhabitants, which one should attempt to tell.

This is that broken link in the chain of mutual connections, forming a kind of code of life and the picture of those generations. Of that strange community, living at once together and apart. Yet one must try. Perhaps we shall be able to gather into a coherent whole this scattered history. In August 2020 I met with Łukasz Parus – the director of the House of Culture in Zagórów – and shared with him the observations described above. He caught fire at the idea and, what is important, knew well whom to invite to cooperate. Nor did the idea have to be urged upon Rev. Waldemar Wunsz, the pastor of the Evangelical-Augsburg parish in Konin, in whose territory the Evangelical church of Zagórów lies. The inaugural meeting took place on 10 September 2020. And so the idea of undertaking work on the preparation of a publication about the history of the Evangelical community (Hauländers and non-Hauländers, Germans, Poles) in the Zagórów land began to materialize. A few weeks later, at a meeting in the Gęsi Puch Guest House, the former cantorate house, the editorial committee was constituted, which undertook to bring the whole matter to a successful conclusion. After nearly three years of work there had been assembled a rich body of material in the form of studies, articles, interviews, memoirs (here the precious record of Wiesława Kowalska's conversations with Erich Kuhn, and the story of Eugenia Plitt recorded by Günter Hennings), documents and photographs, which we hand over to the readers in the form of the prepared book. We strove, as far as lay in our power, to realize the main task that guided us – that is, to look into the depth of history, into the tragic years of the war of 1939–1945 and into the most recent times.
