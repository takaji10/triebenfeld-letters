### Settlement history of the Zagórów-Trąbczyn region

#### Settlement history of the Greater Zagórów-Trąbczyn area (Siedlungsgeschichte Großraum Zagorow-Trabczyn)[^96]

#### ZAGÓRÓW (1943-1945 HINTERBERG)

- Year 2014 – Zagorow is a town and county seat in Powiat Slupecki, Wojewodschaft Großpolen in Poland.  
  The year 2014 – Zagórów is a town in Słupca county, in the Greater Poland Voivodeship.
- The town lies on the River Warthe about 30 km west of Konin. Until the Second World War the town was surrounded by many small German settlements.  
  The town is situated on the Warta river, about 30 km west of Konin. Until the Second World War the town was surrounded by many small German settlements.
- In the year 1145 German Cistercian monks founded the monastery of Lad (Lond) on the Warthe. Around this monastery the monks promoted the establishment of small settlements, since they received dues from the peasants. In 1553 the German monks left the territory and fled with the charters to the monastery of Heinrichau in Silesia, because a Polish government took over in Lad.  
  In the year 1145 the Cistercians founded a monastery in Ląd on the Warta. The monks supported the founding of small settlements around this monastery, since they received tributes from the peasants. In the year 1553 the German monks left this area and moved, together with the documents, to the Heinrichau monastery in Silesia, because a Polish administration came to power in Ląd.
