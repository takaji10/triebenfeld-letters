In the so-called cantor's house, from the end of the Second World War until the first half of the 1960s, there was located the Municipal Kindergarten in Zagórów, and after it, until the mid-1980s, the Municipal Centre of Culture together with the “Ruch” Club and the Municipal Library. The ground floor of the parsonage, meanwhile, until the mid-1960s was the seat of the Municipal National Council, and afterwards it was leased to the Municipal Kindergarten in Zagórów, moved there from the cantor's house (I attended the kindergarten both in the cantor's house and at the parsonage; I remember those years well and recall them with nostalgia). The first floor of the parsonage was inhabited by the Bürger family. Mrs Ilona Bürger for many years was the caretaker of the local Evangelical church, tending with care the sanctuary entrusted to her keeping[^21].

In the year 1968, a reorganisation was carried out in the Evangelical-Augsburg Church, as a result of which the Zagórów parish lost its legal personality and became a branch of the Evangelical-Augsburg Parish in Konin. After the Rev. Karol Świtalski, parish priest of the Evangelical-Augsburg Parish in Konin, the ministry was exercised by the Rev. Rudolf Gajda, and at the turn of the 1960s and 1970s – by the Rev. Henryk Schneider. From 1976 the parish priest of the Konin parish was the Rev. Councillor Andrzej Mendrok, who was at the same time parish priest-administrator of the branch congregation in Zagórów[^22]. Thanks to his initiative and his devotion, in the 1980s the church was successfully renovated and the building regained at least some of its former charm.
