# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 184
# translation by the editor's files

Every day is like a feast, like a present. And if the Lord sends heavy days again, He will also graciously help to bear their weight. Only to hold out until Easter, and we shall have the winter behind us. Perhaps next one we shall already have peace. But since that hour has not yet come, we wish to continue faithfully performing our duty, in order to help bring about victory". On the seventh of March the writing was interrupted by an alarm. "We must set out, hard days will come again". (The letter reached Łazińsk on 23 March 1944, from field post 31 059E – the number assigned to the 136th regiment of the 2nd Mountain Division, 10th company).

The letter written before Easter, on 12 March 1944, reaches Łazińsk already on 17 March! Within five days the post arrives from the distant Finnish north to the village hidden in the forests below Zagórów. He writes on a Sunday in the Passiontide. "How again the kindness of God crowned me, that He granted me (us) a free and quiet Sunday, so that in its holy forenoon hours I could read His holy gospel and then find a quiet place in the sunny, snowy solitude and join the invisible community of prayer in heaven and on earth". Further on he thanks the Lord that He caused such wonderful dear people to appear in his life: father, mother, brother, sisters. "It is the cross of Golgotha that in this special time allows us – sinners – to lay down all burdens and become clean and without blemish". This is an important and strong sentence. The soldier Mielke grants himself full absolution.

From everyday life he can report that he is healthy, enjoys a good appetite and sends up stamps for parcels in which he hopes to find "pączken" (pączki). Although he knows that in the fifth year of the war there may be difficulties and one must husband what one has, if it should be possible he asks them to send five-fingered gloves. He has two-fingered ones – so his hands do not freeze – but the many-fingered ones are more useful.
