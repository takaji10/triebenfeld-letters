# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 272
# translation by the editor's files

 Julius Lim in the parish cemetery in Lądek. The son of Genowefa and Reinhold Dalke, Jan, is a sailor by education. During my meeting with him I looked through a family album, turned the pages, often gazed at the face of Grandma's teacher looking out of the photographs. Suddenly I froze. In Mr Jan's album I found a small photograph I knew well from childhood. An identical one is in Grandma's album. Two men are standing on a jetty; on the right one can see part of a boat with the name Chłopi. One of the men is the then bosun Jan Dalke, and the other… my grandfather, Walenty Powała.


Grandpa sailed barges on the Warta. So it must have been. The stories of my family and of Genowefa Dalke's family perhaps intertwined precisely so that this tale could come into being and commemorate Mrs Dolkowa.
