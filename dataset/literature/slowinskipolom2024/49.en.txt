It was precisely on the initiative of pastor Andrzej Mendrok that, on 15-16 August 1992, the second gathering of the parishioners of the Evangelical-Augsburg Parish took place. The 110th anniversary of the consecration of the church in Zagórów and the 50th anniversary of confirmation were then celebrated. During the momentous ceremonies, a memorial tablet in honour of pastor Aleksander Gross, who had been parish priest of the Zagórów parish for over twenty years (1923-1945), was unveiled in the church. Apart from the local Protestants, the gathering was attended by the faithful who had once belonged to the community and now live beyond the borders of Poland. There came about 90 persons from Germany and two persons from the USA. Through the efforts of the parish priest, the Rev. Councillor Mendrok, the Evangelical Seniors' Home in Zagórów was opened on 16 June 1995. The act of consecration was performed by the superior of the Pomeranian-Wielkopolska Diocese, the Rev. bishop Michał Warczyński. In June 2000, the celebrations of the fifth anniversary of the Evangelical Seniors' Home took place. In 2002, it was inhabited by seven residents, among them Edward Szwarc, a former farmer from Stanisławów. He was often seen at the Evangelical cemetery in Zagórów, where he tended the graves with devotion. Nine years later, on 30 November 2009, with the death of the last resident, the EDS ended its activity[^23]. After 2000, a few families of German origin lived in Zagórów itself, and several dozen in the territory of the whole gmina – in their decisive majority Protestants. With each year, this number alas grows smaller: the older ones pass away, the young set out into the world. For those who remain, services are held on the first and third Sunday of each month at 12.00.

On 9 June 2001, during the celebrations of the jubilee of the ordination of the Rev. Councillor Andrzej Mendrok in Konin, the municipal authorities of Zagórów were represented by the mayor, Wiesław Radniecki, and the deputy mayor, Włodzimierz Chmielewski. During the ceremonies, there performed the choir Cantabile under the direction of Aleksandra Koronkowska, MA.
