### The Evangelical Senior Home in Zagórów and Its Residents in the Recollections of Rev. Katarzyna Kowalska


Recorded by Katarzyna Połom

The chronicle of the senior home records that in the pre-war cantor's house next to the evangelical church, a kindergarten was organised after 1945. Later the public library and the community centre found their seat in it. After it was vacated by the above-mentioned institutions, the building was designated for lease – shops operated in it, while two rooms facing the garden were occupied as a dwelling by the sexton, Mr Edward Rydel. With the passage of time the buildings fell into decay and required ever greater intervention. Before it was decided to carry out a major renovation, Mr Edward Kuhn convinced the Parish Council in Detmold of the need to take solitary, elderly, less well-off Protestants under care and to use the said building for this purpose. The parish in Konin accepted this idea, and thanks to joint financing of the project by both parishes and by individuals, a major renovation of the building was carried out in 1994-1995.

When all the construction work had come to an end, the time arrived for the solemn dedication of the building. In the notes from the chronicle we can read:
