In a letter to the dean of the Evangelical-Theological Faculty of the University of Vienna he writes: "Dear Mister Dean! The time of the autumn semester will soon come. I would gladly begin my studies this autumn (for my intention of coming to Vienna has become a certainty). But unfortunately the Wehrmacht and the war will probably claim me. For the moment I have not yet been called up, but I have recently received a summons to be in readiness, so that I expect the order to report any day. As soon as I am released from military service after the end of the war, I shall want – should God preserve me in life and health – to lay down my office and come to Vienna. If, according to God's will, I were to fall in the war, then my brother will inform you of it". This letter, dated 10 October 1941, was written by Bruno Mielke in Konary, in the commune of Grodziec, where he worked as a teacher. Earlier, from 1940, there survives a document signed by him as teacher and lower school commissioner in Maślaki, in the commune of Wilczyn. It is a letter to the Konin starosta documenting the need to build new school buildings in Maślaki and Lipnica. The inhabitants – I quote: "with the wójt at their head consider it obvious to build a school worthy of the New Germany and are ready to give financial help".

It may be worth mentioning at this point that both Maślaki and Konary were, from the end of the eighteenth century, localities that came into being as a result of contracts concluded by the local Polish landowners with German settlers of the Protestant faith. Over more than a hundred years these localities became important regional centres of the Evangelical community. Earlier, Bruno Mielke had for five years been helping Aleksander Gross, the parish priest of the Evangelical-Augsburg parish in Zagórów, and had worked as a teacher and cantor in nearby Anielewo.
