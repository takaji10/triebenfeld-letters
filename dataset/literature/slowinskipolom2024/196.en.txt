# translation by the editor's files

Dr Hugo Breede, Janina Breede's husband.


Photo from the archive of Bogdan Grzonkowski.

She has an upright figure, her legs gracefully crossed at the ankles, and in her hands, folded on her knees, she holds gloves. She looks into the lens and smiles faintly; she appears somewhat weary. Here she is exactly my age – she is 39. Over her shoulders she has thrown an impressive stole of natural fur.

– She and my mother-in-law had identical furs, – recalls Mrs Halina Cieślewicz, whom one day I take in for a good grilling. – As soon as my father-in-law returned from America, he gave his wife such a gift. He travelled to Warsaw especially for that fur. Barely a few days had passed, and Mrs Breede had one just the same!

Not only in matters of fashion did she like to keep up with the times – I think she liked to stand out, to dazzle, to shine in company. It is possible she had grown used to this as early as childhood, being, after all, a daughter of one of the finest Zagórów families. She came into the world in 1899 as Janina Franciszka Stawicka, daughter of Michał and Joanna née Synoracki. Her father held the office of mayor of Zagórów in the years 1919–1921. There is no doubt that young Janina received a careful upbringing at home, and later on – having first completed the appropriate schools – she herself began work as a teacher. Brilliant, elegant, not bad-looking – she was an excellent match and must certainly have aroused the interest of men. Little wonder that young Hugo Breede, a medical student staying in Zagórów on an internship (earlier as an interpreter with the War Commandant), lost his head over her and proposed even before finishing his studies. Their wedding took place on 29 July 1922 in the Catholic church under the invocation of Sts. Peter and Paul. Janina was of the Catholic faith, her chosen one was an Evangelical. In accordance with the prevailing custom they raised their two sons in this way, Georg and Hugo: one in the Catholic faith, the other as an Evangelical. At first the newlyweds lived in the in-laws' house at Kościelna Street 12. There too the young doctor ran his medical practice.
