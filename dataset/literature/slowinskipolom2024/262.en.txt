# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 262
# translation by the editor's files

I mentioned that Ida Disterheft took up residence in the Ślesin facility in 1983. The life of this woman cannot have been easy; her fate was certainly not positively affected by the fact that she bore the same surname as the aforementioned mayor, who enjoyed no good repute. The harshness of her lot may be underscored by the fact that only in 1977 did she receive an identity card. Earlier it had presumably not been necessary for her, and perhaps she did not even know that she could apply for one, for her entire adult life – as she herself wrote in one of the applications I managed to obtain – she lived and worked for farmers in the nearby villages. She went into service after the death of her father, who was the last surviving member of the family in Poland. In the post-war years the sisters left for Germany, and Ida was left alone without any means of support. The people I managed to reach, who still remember her from the times when she worked for the local farmers, recall that she was called Lida. Ida alias Lida lives in memory as a tall woman with a slightly red face (perhaps acne or a disease of the blood vessels), who wore long, sleeked-down hair. What lodged in the memory of her contemporaries was an element of her attire – Ida always wore long dresses.

These are mainly the recollections of Mrs Wanda Rydlichowska (née Kin) of Szetlewek in the municipality of Zagórów, at whose parents' home Ida worked in the 1950s. Mrs Wanda tried to recall Ida and the details of her life, but she herself was a little girl when Ida worked for her parents. Mrs Wanda did say, however, that when she was preparing for the conversation with me, she had the impression that she could see Ida before her, so deeply had Ida's appearance lodged in her memory. Mrs Rydlichowska mentioned that in her opinion Ida was a person with an intellectual disability, as her behaviour and the way she spoke would indicate, but those were times when nobody particularly concerned themselves with it, diagnosed it, or provided rehabilitation. Wanda Rydlichowska recounted an incident that might point to Ida's mental state.
