In turn, a letter from Lipice reached Bruno from Ursula – the leader of a B.D.M. troop (Bund Deutscher Mädel – League of German Girls). This is the female section of the Nazi youth organisation Hitlerjugend. She is disappointed that the two previous letters she sent came back to her. She encloses them again in the envelope as proof. The girl writes that she has returned to her old path. Earlier her love for Bruno had blinded her! What does that mean today, when she ponders it? That it is for her the only path: "I believe in the almighty God, who will not allow the fall of Germany. Every faith that is directed against my beloved fatherland I reject from myself. Therefore I need no intermediary, those who so often see only their little "church" and do not perceive the highest aim for the Church, which ought to be Germany. Our Führer never intended to separate the Church from the state – quite the contrary! In the year 1933 services were instituted as an obligation for all organisations of the party. But the Church rejected that". Nor was it able to lead the German nation spiritually, and therefore the German youth cannot be entrusted to it. She judges such conduct of the hierarchy as acting together with the enemy states against the Third Reich. When victory has at last been achieved, she believes, the affairs with the Church will finally be set in order, for it cannot be that the Church acts against the state. Then too she will take decided steps. It will be either – or. And although she will not try to persuade her parents, she at least hopes that they will show understanding for her stance. Naturally she considers that "…among Christians faithful to their Church there are a few who stand up in defence of Germany and the Führer, but these are exceptions – among them are you.
