# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 293
# translation by the editor's files

 was administered first by the pastor from Stawiszyn (1865-1876), then from Grodziec and from Konin.

During the tenure of Karl Eduard Erdmann, on 8 May 1878 the foundation stone was laid for the construction of the Evangelical church in Zagórów.

The construction of the church lasted four years and cost over 13,000 rubles. During Advent of the year 1882 the consecration took place. In the year 1883 the organ was ordered, and in 1884 two bells. The contribution of Pastor Erdmann, who from the year 1887 cared for the parish from Grodziec and died in the year 1892, was honored with a marble memorial tablet in the church. In the year 1898 the house of prayer in Adelshof was built, and in the year 1917, after four years of construction, the kantorate and school building in Zagórów was completed.

As the last pastor, Alexander Gross held the office in the years 1923-1945, born on 5.5.1898 in Łódź, who was closely bound to the Christian parish.

In his time the parish acquired a new bell in place of the bell requisitioned in the First World War, and a clock for the tower, which was funded by the Krüger couple in the year 1927.

The kantortes of Łazińsk and Ciemierów received back through the courts, in the year 1935, the property that had been taken from them. In the year 1938 the parish house was consecrated, which cost 40,000 złotys. In the year 1945 Pastor Gross had to leave Zagórów, together with several hundred other Germans, and was deported to Stalino. There he worked in a quarry and in a lime works. He fell ill and died in the middle of November 1945 in the infirmary in Finolny.

After the war Zagórów was for a long time under the care of Pastor Świtalski, today Bensheim.

Around the year 1980 services were held here once a month by Pastor Andrzej Mendrok from Konin. In this period Ewald Riedel lived in the parish house. He was the church warden (Kirchenvorsteher), at the same time the sexton, and that at the age of 80.
