### The Beutlers: Two Centuries in the History of a Family


Damian Kruczkowski

In telling the story of the Evangelical, Hauländer families of the Konin region, including the areas of the Zagórów municipality, it is necessary to speak of particular families and particular individuals descended from them. One of the many families and figures worth mentioning — because in the last few years of her life she was one of the main sources of knowledge about the settlers of the Konin and Zagórów area — is the Beutler family and Gertruda Schwemmer, who came from it. Mrs Schwemmer was a carrier of the experience and knowledge of several generations of the Beutler family, who settled, were born, worked, founded families and died within the borders of present-day Poland. Gertruda Adelheid Schwemmer née Beutler was a contemporary of independent Poland. She was born on 26 December 1918 and died in Berlin on 11 February 2018. I first met Mrs Gertruda Schwemmer in Zagórów, in the Evangelical church, during a Polish-German service attended by parishioners led by Fr Waldemar Wunsz, parish priest of the Evangelical-Augsburg Parish in Konin, and parishioners from Detmold led by their pastor. This is already a tradition of many years' standing, begun by Erich Kuhn, born in Łazińsk near Zagórów — his ancestors came to these lands as settlers nearly two centuries ago. Mrs Gertruda Schwemmer had snow-white hair and bright eyes looking out from behind her glasses. Her small figure gave her person a kind of ethereality, airiness — one could say fragility. That is how I came to know her. The gaze of her bright eyes, despite her age, was very lively, curious about the world, about events and people.
