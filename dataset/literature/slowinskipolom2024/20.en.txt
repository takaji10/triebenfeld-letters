Contempt for the human being, for the former neighbour, knows no bounds. The first Zagórów Jew to be killed is the well-known butcher Jakub Szafraniecki. Disterheft's gang and the soldiers organize for themselves an entertainment, setting fire to the synagogue. Later – since not everything burned down – they drive the Jews to demolish the remaining scorched walls. These are organized, methodical actions, designed to break these people and deprive them of every basis of existence. These actions were, however, a zealous implementation of the policy of the Third Reich. The local people and Disterheft's men had their own scores to settle here. The new mayor is said to have invited to his office the unsuspecting members of the POW who had taken part in the recapture and sinking of the barge in 1918, under the pretext of carrying out some repairs. All six were arrested and, together with four others detained for other reasons, taken away to the not distant forest near Grabina. There they were brutally murdered with axes, entrenching tools and spades. They were buried in that forest in a common grave. Zagórów never forgot this crime, and it had a fundamental influence on the attitude towards the Germans – long after the end of the war. It happened, after all, in a terrain in which the majority of the inhabitants were German settlers and their descendants, and it is not possible that they did not know of this crime, while some took part in it outright. Disterheft himself made a flourishing business out of the office of mayor. Rich Jews, well-to-do Poles, if they wanted to get something done, had to pay dearly. Most gladly in gold, in valuables. Despite the ransom paid, the matter did not always get moving, and the victim still remained in the ghetto or in the investigative custody.

How deeply this grim figure lodged itself in the memory of the inhabitants is testified by the tales of Disterheft's end. In one of them his eyes were gouged out and he was killed; in another his wife and daughter were raped and he himself was shot; in a further one he escaped safe and sound and then hid somewhere in America.
