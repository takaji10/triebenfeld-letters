# Słowiński and Połom (2024), p. 209
# translation by the editor's files

I never had the opportunity to meet Janina Breede – I came into the world almost 10 years after her death. After many years I came to know the wider story. Being aware that human memories are always subjective, I tried to listen attentively to every account and, from the information conveyed, to put together a portrait as close to the real one as possible. But who the Doctor's wife Breede really was, probably only she herself knew.
