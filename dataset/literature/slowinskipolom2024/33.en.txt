### The Protestants of the Zagórów Land (16th to 21st Centuries)[^11]


Bogdan Grzonkowski[^11] Text prepared and made ready for print by Katarzyna Połom

#### I. The Reformation Era



Drawing for the design of the stained-glass window The Coat of Arms of Zagórów, by Andrzej Żurek


A plaque on the wall of the Evangelical church in Zagórów commemorating the inhabitants of the Protestant faith who settled on the Zagórów land from the mid-18th century onward.


Photo from the archive: “Zagórów miejsce z historią”

Protestantism reached the vicinity of Zagórów, as it did the whole of Wielkopolska, in the years 1520–1525. In the second half of the sixteenth century the territory of Konin county, together with the estates of the Cistercian monastery in Ląd on the Warta, to which the little town and part of the surrounding villages belonged, had been seized by the Reformation, and the missionary centre of the religious “novelties” was precisely the Cistercian monastery, from which the clergy carried them to the surrounding localities.

The Evangelical nobility reduced the extent of corvée labour, the threshing quotas and other burdens of serfdom, winning adherents of the new faith 


[^11]: Bogdan Grzonkowski (born 1958 – died 2009) – a historian by education and vocation, a figure of great merit in the study and promotion of knowledge of the history of the Zagórów land. One of the fruits of his many years of searching and work was the text "Historia ewangelicyzmu w Zagórowie" [The History of Protestantism in Zagórów], written in 2002. It was published in part, in the form of articles, in the local press. The author's intention was to write a book on the history of the Zagórów Lutheran parish. His tragic death brutally thwarted these plans. Bogdan Grzonkowski was my teacher and mentor. It was he who instilled in me the bacillus of delving into history and of respect for the land on which I grew up. When word came of the beginning of work on "Zerwane ogniwo" [The Broken Link], Barbara Leśniewska, Bogdan Grzonkowski's sister, contacted me and handed into my keeping all the materials left behind by her late brother. Among them was, inter alia, the typescript of the "Historia ewangelicyzmu w Zagórowie". She also kindly consented to the article being worked up and used in the publication being prepared. I made minor corrections, supplemented the history of the Zagórów church after the year 2003, and gave the text a new title, footnotes and a bibliography. In one of our numerous conversations Bogdan Grzonkowski mentioned that perhaps one day we would write a book together… In a certain sense that succeeded.
