#### The Oak

On the site of our family home on Łazińsk Drugi I planted in October 2015 a little oak tree. On a wooden board I wrote the initials of my siblings' names: Gustav, Bruno (they were killed), Helmut, Erich, Erhard, Irka (she bathed in the ditch), Horst, Harry (the youngest brother), Frieda (the youngest sister).

Before I planted the oak I had a few sleepless nights. I did not know whether it would be received well by the Poles who live here now. My siblings are glad that I come to my home region, that I do not let the memory of the family home die… But the war changed so much… I would not want anyone in Poland to judge these actions of mine badly, to see something bad in my intentions. It turned out that I need not have worried. The family who now live on the site of our farmstead not only received me very kindly, but also took care of my little oak, watering it and protecting it from wild animals. Such moments heal old wounds. The way I feel it, this oak is a joy not only for me but also for its Polish caretakers. For after all, where there is a border, there is also a road.

> *Interview conducted and edited by Wiesława Kowalska*,

*Pyzdry – Detmold, 2014–2021*
