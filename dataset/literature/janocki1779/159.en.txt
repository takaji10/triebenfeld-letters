# Janocki (1779), p. 159
# translation by Claude

LXXVII.

STANISLAUS LOCHOWSKI, a nobleman of Lesser Poland and notary of the castle of Opoczno in the satrapy of Sandomierz, distinguished himself above all in the discipline of law in Poland in the reign of Władysław IV, as these writings of his bear witness:

I.) RULES OF LAW, AND THE COMMON FORENSIC PLACES. On whose law and common practice he was experienced and versed in their use in the forum: and the Allegatio. By Stanisław Lochowski, notary of the castle of Opoczno.

Posnania, at the press of Albertus Regulus, 1636 and 1642, duodecimo. The same, without the printer's name, 1643, in the same format. Cracow, from the press of Franciscus Caesarius, 1644: and of his son of the same name, 1686, 1691 and 1701, octavo. And in the same city, at the press of the heirs of Franciscus Caesarius Junior, 1737, in a similar format.

The Cracow edition of the year 1701 the learned men of Leipzig reviewed, with deserved praise, in the Acta Eruditorum, in the month of May 1702, pp. 197-199. The same edition Prince Joseph Alexander Jabłonowski, Palatine of Nowogródek, pointed out in the Museo Polono, p. 145. Beside it, the other editions, and the rest of Lochowski's works, never became known.

II.) THE BOUNDARY PROCESS OF THE LAW OF THE KINGDOM OF POLAND. By Stanisław Lochowski, notary of the castle of Opoczno. Cracow, in the printing shop of Franciscus Caesarius. In the year of the Lord 1641. Quarto.
