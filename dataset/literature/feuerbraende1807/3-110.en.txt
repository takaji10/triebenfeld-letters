# Neue Feuerbrände (1807), part 3, p. 110
# translation by Claude

with the best intention to lay the foregoing to the hearts of the Right Worshipful Magistrates named below, so that such precautions may be taken by them as shall, so far as possible, keep away from our province those ruinous consequences which those countries have felt, and still feel, that the force of arms and an unexampled fortune of war[^24] subjected to these invincible conquerors.

Glogau, the 26th of February 1807.

v. Tschirschky.
