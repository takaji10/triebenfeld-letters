Names that come into question here are: Niemiecko-Czeskie and Polsko-Czeskie, and probably also the Albertowskie and Amtskasnerowskie Ol. It is possible that the last two names became established from the surnames of the locators or of the sołtyses of the villages, but this supposition cannot be established with complete certainty. Frequently the same localities appear under two or more names; in such[^21-1] cases we adopted the name that recurs most frequently in the sources. If the settlements bore two names at the same time, both were given in the enumeration (e.g. Błonie Ol., or Źdżar, etc.).
