Wol. Leg. — Volumina Legum.

VO 78, 83, 84, 87 — Visitatio generalis in archi-diaconatu
Srzemensis decanatuum Srzemensis, Borcensis, Neomestensis et
Costensis... 1778. Call mark: A. V. no. 2. — Visitatio Generalia
Decanatuum Crobensis, Smiglensis, Wschovensis et Cosminensis...
Anno 1777-mo inchoata... 178 expedita. Call mark: A. V. no. 25. —
Visitatio generalis in Archi-Diaconatu Posnaniensi Decanatuum
Kostrzynensis, Srodensis, Pyzdrensis, Rogoznensis, Obornicensis et
Bucoviensis... in... Anno 1777-mo inchoata... 1784-to expedita.
Call mark: A. V. no. 26. — Visitatio Generalis Trium Decanatuum
Zbąszynensis, Grodecensis et Lwowecensis... Anno 1780... ad 1787...
expedita. Call mark: A. V. no. 28. The protocols of these
visitations, known under the name of the Okęcki visitations
(carried out under the episcopate of Andrzej Młodziejowski, later
of Onufry Okęcki), have been partly published by the Theological
Department of the Poznań Society of the Friends of Learning
(Series I, fascicles 1–3, 1917–8). They are kept in the
Archdiocesan Archives in Poznań.
