The general economic situation and the standard of living of the Olęders

Judge Stenger, who wrote his remarks on the Olęders on the basis of personal observation of the settlements in the neighbourhood of Trzciel, Nowy Tomyśl and Zbąszyń, states that in these Olęder settlements a relative prosperity prevailed: “On the whole the Olęder likes to represent himself as quite poor and suffering misery; taking it all in all, he is not rich, but by no means poor”[^109-6]. The same is stated by Holsche: “These people here and there are doing quite well”[^109-7]. The author of the brochure “Uber Ursprung u. Verbreitung” (p. 33) follows the opinion of the writers mentioned above, adding, however, a proviso: “It is true that the unfavourable fact that many of these Olęder settlements were founded on ground too light, sandy, forest soil, and received no meadows, often hindered them in attaining prosperity”. The scanty source materials that have survived with regard to this question speak for the truth of the opinions cited above. On the whole the economic position of the Olęders, despite the poor soil, was satisfactory, but there existed differences in the economic situation of the individual settlements. It seems that the Olęders of the neighbourhood of Trzciel and Nowy Tomyśl were best situated; the soil there reached a medium quality, and the obligations were on the whole low.
