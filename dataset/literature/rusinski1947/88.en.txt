# rusinski1947  88  en
# Rusiński (1947), p. 88
# translation by Claude

2. Payments. Besides the money rents the Olęders paid various dues. They differed from the rents in this, that they were paid only in certain circumstances (e.g. upon the sale of a farm), whereas the rents were paid always, in a fixed and defined amount. On the border between rents and dues stood the already discussed payment for the pasture servitudes, paid for each individual sheep. The proper dues paid by the Olęders may be divided into 2 groups: dues of an economic character and administrative dues. To the first category of dues belonged the okupne (for land, for buildings), then the eventual market dues. The amount of the okupne fluctuated within very wide limits and it is difficult to give here any average figure. In 2 settlements founded at the close of the seventeenth century the okupne came, for the huba, to 40 thalers, reckoning the thaler at 15 szóstaks (== 120 zł),[^88-1] and 100 thalers at the rate of 5 zł to the thaler (== 500 zł).[^88-2] At the beginning of the eighteenth century there was paid for the huba: 150 zł (in 1701),[^88-3] 30 zł (in 1707),[^88-4] 10 zł (in 1709),[^88-5] 100 tymfs (in 1712).[^88-6] In connexion with the fall in the value of money the sum of the okupne rose nominally, reaching about 1730 exceptionally even 600 zł.[^88-7] On the other hand, in the same period we have to record okupne in the amount of 6 zł,[^88-8] and somewhat later (in 1752) 1 red złoty from the huba.[^88-9] For a longer time the amount of the okupne settles down at approximately 100 zł from the huba. In the last quarter of the eighteenth century the okupne is higher. For the huba there is paid: 900 zł (in 1771 — arable lands with buildings),[^88-10] 24 zł (in 1773),[^88-11] 30 red zł. (in 1775),[^88-12] 900 zł (arable lands with buildings — in 1776 and 1781),[^88-13] 10 red zł. (in 1783),[^88-14] 1,800 zł and 1,500 zł (arable lands with buildings — in 1785),[^88-15] 150 zł (in 1786),[^88-16] 20 red zł. (in 1787).[^88-17]
