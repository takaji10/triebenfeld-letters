# Rusiński (1947), p. 13
# translation by Claude

After the founding of the settlements mentioned there follows a period of temporary pause. Colonising activity, this time already on a broad scale, revives at the close of the XVII century and continues with unabated force until the partitions.[^13-1] Great clusters of settlements form: on the Noteć along the Czarnków–Wieleń line, on the Warta in the neighbourhood of Międzychód, and to the east of the Obra line. On the border of the former voivodeships of Poznań, Kalisz 1 Gniezno we have two further great clusters of settlements: the first on the Warta along the Śrem–Pyzdry stretch, and the second, stretching in a broad belt along the line Czerniejewo–Murowana Goślina–Rogoźno–Chodzież. All the settlements lie in the former counties of Kościan and Poznań; in Wałcz county and in the Wschowa land Olęder settlements are entirely absent. The distribution of the settlements is shown on the map appended at the end. In a number of cases the drawing of the boundaries of the individual counties on it presented great difficulty. The boundaries drawn on the map are of a provisional character; the Poznań Atlas Commission is working on their exact reconstruction.

In the brochure Uber Ursprung und Verbreitung des Deuischtums im Grossherzogihum Posen, published in 1849 (p. 34), the number of Olęder settlements in the territory of Wielkopolska was put at 400. This figure was repeated 1 by the later authors as well. In recent times, in the sphere of the statistics of settlements, independent attempts have been made by Hładyłowicz and Maas. The former, in his work on the changes of the landscape and the development of settlement in Wielkopolska from the XIV to the XIX century, reckoned for Kościan county 64, and for Poznań county 133 localities. The author writes that these are settlements of the XVI and XVII centuries, which, however, must be explained as an error, perhaps a printer's error, since for example in Kościan county the settlements date, in all the instances known to us, from the XVIII century, and it was that century the author undoubtedly had in mind. The above figures do not reach the actual number of settlements. These differences nevertheless become smaller if we take into account that a part of the settlements was classed by Hładyłowicz among settlements of other types. To entirely different results as to the number of settlements Maas came in his work Die Posener Hauländereien (DWZP, 34), supplemented in 1941 by the article: Weitere Posener Hauländereien (DWZW, 2). This author, as will be said later, arrived at the conviction that at least 75% of the inhabitants of the Olęder settlements belonged to the German nationality 1. In the interest, therefore, of that nationality he made every effort to increase the number of the settlements as far as possible. To this end, independently of his long and detailed disquisitions on which settlements should, and which should not, be regarded as Olęder, he noted down from various lists of localities and maps all the settlements with the addition “olędry”, or of equivalent designations
