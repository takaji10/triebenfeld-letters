# Rusiński (1947), p. 149
# translation by Claude

The carting of grain, over a distance of at most six miles, they are to perform once a year; provided, however, that no more than four korcy of the new measure, that is eight wierts, may ever be loaded on to one waggon. If the manor has no need of this, then instead of the cartage over six miles he is to do a day's ploughing and break up and plough eight rods. Each of the leaseholders is to perform one road cartage of at most six miles, and if this is not needed, he is to go out ploughing for one day, eight rods. The Czerwonak Olęders are freed from the grain cartage and from the rod-ploughing of the cartage duty; in return they are to go out ploughing two days a year, eight rods each. For the brewing of beer at the Kicin brewhouse they are to take turns, prepare and cart the wood for it, and carry out the malt and bring it back. All manner of help in building — that is, in bringing timber, stone, brick, lime, clay and sand, in raising buildings and in daubing — they owe in turn. Church dues, as well as taxes and instalments of whatever kind, now or in future, each is to hand over to the sołtys, and he is to deliver them where they are due.

They are to keep the ditches (so that the water has its fall), the dams by the mills and the public roads in the best possible condition and repair them when called upon to do so by the sołtys. As to the laying waste of the Kicin forests, they shall not only not cut there themselves (apart from on their own morgs), but the seizure of what they detect being felled shall be their duty.

So that everyone may know and understand all this, when the community has assembled twice a year at the village court, that is on St Martin's day and on St Wojciech's day, at the sołtys's at Czerwonak, it is to be read out aloud and freely.

Since it is the honour of our leaseholders of the Kicin estate complex that is at stake, the last point concerns theft, namely that if there should appear the least sign not only of actual theft but of complicity with a thief, such a person, as an enemy of the common good, is to be expelled without delay; his estate having been sold, the injured party is to be compensated as the case shows, ten grzywnas are to be paid into the community chest, and the remainder given to the culprit.

So that these our present provisions may carry the greater weight, we append them with our own hand together with the impression of the seal. Given at Kicin on the second day of February of the year one thousand seven hundred and eighty-one.

Józef Bończa Miaskowski, Provost of Poznań Cathedral, mpp.
