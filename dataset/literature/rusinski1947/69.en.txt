The number and size of the farms changed continually[^69-1] as a result of their division by inheritance, and possibly also through partial sale.[^69-2] A change in the area of the farms was brought about likewise by the unlawful ploughing-in of the lord's lands. This was, as far as the Olęders were concerned, an abuse commonly practised, which the manor tried in vain to combat. The surveys of the Prussian period showed that the area of almost every farm represented a size with a fraction.[^69-3] In the Prussian classification records we meet with copies of a series of deeds of sale of lands long since ploughed in unlawfully and used by the Olęders.[^69-4] There occurred cases in which the Olęders enlarged the area of their farms at the cost of the public roads. [^69-2]: An example may serve the Ol. Folsztyn. According to the lustration of the Wieleń estate in 1630 there lived there 32 farmers upon 30 włókas. The lustration of 1653 already lists 38 farmers, of whom 2 sat upon 1½ włókas, 14 upon 1 włóka, 5 upon ¾ of a włóka, 12 upon ½ of a włóka and 1 upon ¼ of a włóka; altogether there fall 27 włókas. The diminution by 3 włókas resulted probably from the lands having been measured out with a larger rod. In 1773 (Kl. XIII, 7, fol. 122) Folsztyn numbered 50 farmers, of whom 1 sat upon one włóka, 2 upon ½ of a włóka, 42 upon ½ of a wł., 8 upon ⅜ of a wł., 3 upon ½ of a wł.; the settlement numbered altogether 27½ włókas. Finally, according to the lustration drawn up about 1800 (AM. Wieleń, C. 250, a copy of the lustration of the Wieleń estate deposited in the process records of 1800), there were in Folsztyn likewise 56 farmers, of whom 2 possessed whole włókas, 1 — ¼ of a wł., 44 — ½ of a wł., — ⅗ of a wł., 13 — ½ of a wł., which makes altogether 27⅝ włókas. As the comparison shows, in the meantime the average area of the farm had diminished by half, from 50 to 25 morgs. In reality the area of the farms in Folsztyn somewhat exceeded the round figures given, for in the period from the founding of the settlement to 1773 the individual farmers had ploughed in for themselves considerable pieces of land, sometimes reaching 15 morgs. Altogether the original area of the settlement, amounting to 27 włókas with a fraction, grew in this way by more than 4½ włókas.
