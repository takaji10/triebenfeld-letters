# Rusiński (1947), p. 20
# translation by Claude

The names of the settlements. All the names of the Olęder settlements can, according to their immediate source, be divided into two groups: the first group consists of names inherited from settlements existing previously.[^20-1] This was the case when an Olęder settlement was located on the site of a depopulated village?. In cases where Olęder rights were granted to[^20-2] a village that had existed earlier, the previous name of the settlement was kept.[^20-3][^20-4]

In the second group, comprising the names of new settlements, one can in turn distinguish: a) names derived from the topographical names of the terrain on which the settlements were located, e.g. Dziewczastruga (previously a stream), Orłowiec, Wymyślanka (previously a wood of that same name) 5; b) leasehold names with the addition “Olędry”, formed from the localities on whose ground the settlements were founded$; c) characteristic names, e.g.[^20-5] Wielkie Olędry, Nowe Olędry, Pólsko-Czeskie, Niemiecko-Czeskie Olędry.

All the new names were either bestowed on the settlements by the owner — which, moreover, happened exceedingly rarely 7 — or they arose[^20-6]

The following qualification must, however, be made here: the term “Olędry” is used in the sources beside the proper name of a settlement in a twofold sense — to denote the character of the locality (like the terms: folwark, colony, etc.) and as a component part of the settlement's name. On account of the old orthography, however (the word “Olędry” always written with a capital letter), no distinction can be made here. In giving the term “Olędry” with the names of settlements we were guided by considerations of a purely external kind; we gave it in those cases in which it figured beside the name of the village in the majority of the sources. 7 Even in those few cases in which the owner assigned a name to the settlement in the location charter, a different name often later prevailed. The cases known to us of the bestowal of a name by the owner relate to the following settlements: Cegielskie Ol. (in the charter the name Trzcielskie Ol. was given), Gębickie Ol. (in the charter Mielęcin), Marienwald (Marigenwalde), Nowe Ol. (part of Krobielewo, part Baba), Róża Ol. (Rosenthal), Ujskie Ol. (Olędrowo). Further, names such as Marianowo (in the parish of Zbąszyń and in the parish of Wieleń), Filipowo, Folsztyn, Herbardowo, Józefowo, Krystynka, Wyssogotowo, Zakrzewo undoubtedly took their origin from the owner.
