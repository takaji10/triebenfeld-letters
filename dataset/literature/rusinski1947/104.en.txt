G. Church obligations

Olęder settlements, from the moment of their foundation, entered within the parochial administration of the Catholic church. In connection with this, for the most part[^104-4], on a par with the other parishioners, the Olęders were obliged to pay certain dues to the parish church. Catholics and evangelicals alike were made liable to these dues. The latter had, in return, just like the Catholic population, a right to the religious ministrations of the parish priests[^104-5]. The church dues paid by the Olęders can be divided into fixed dues and dues for the individual religious services. The fixed dues were the tithe and the meszne[^104-6]. Usually either the tithe or the meszne was paid; exceptionally a certain sum was paid on account of both dues at the same time[^104-7]. Both the tithe and the meszne were paid either in grain[^104-8] or in money[^104-9]. The sources
