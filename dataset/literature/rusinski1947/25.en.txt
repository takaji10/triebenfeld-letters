As in the case of Kościan county, in the appropriate columns of the enumeration of settlements (Appendix I b) there have been entered: 1. the hearths of the individual settlements of the county on the basis of Tp. 89 or else 90 (marked with an asterisk); 2. the hearths of the settlements for which Tp. 89 or else 90 is lacking; 3. the hearths of the settlements seized by Prussia in the first partition, according to Goldbeck or the Prussian classifications. The number of hearths shown in the first two columns is 2,254. After multiplying this number by the number of persons falling in Kościan county to 1 Olęder hearth (i.e. 6.05), the sum of the Olęder population in the area of Poznań county remaining with Poland will be expressed by the figure of 13,637 souls.

For the sake of comparison we shall estimate by the same method the remaining rural population of this part of the county. The total number of non-Olęder rural hearths here amounts to 12,462.[^25-1] Applying the average number of persons per 1 hearth (i.e. 6.41) calculated for Kościan county, the rural population without the Olęders will be expressed by the figure of 79,881 persons, and with the Olęders it makes 93,518 persons. In the Wschowa land there were no Olęder settlements. The rural population of the Wschowa land in the population table of 1789 (TW) was reckoned at 10,556 persons, and after adding the estimated 5% for the szlachta and the clergy we obtain roundly 10,900 persons. Wałcz county, where there were likewise no Olęder settlements, was, moreover, seized in its entirety by Prussia in the first partition.

The results of the calculations made so far can be summed up in the following table:

[^25-1]: i.e. those given in Tp. 89:
    10,703 noble hearths
    2,834 clerical hearths
    952 royal hearths
    14,489 hearths
    less 16 hearths counted twice in Tp. 89 (the Psarskie Ol.),
    less 84 hearths of the Sowinice estate belonging to Kościan,
    together 14,389 hearths.
    There are added 42 hearths of the parish of Iwno omitted in Tp. 89,
    together 14,431 hearths. As we see from the column of hearths not covered by Tp. 89, placed in Appendix I, this last source is incomplete for Poznań county. It must be supposed that the omissions in Tp. 89, with which we came up in calculating the Olęder hearths, occurred also in regard to the remaining rural hearths. Since, however, within the frame of the present study it is not possible to check the hearths of each individual locality of the county, we must rest content with increasing the non-Olęder rural hearths in the same proportion as for the Olęders, i.e. in the proportion 38 : 2216. To the previously given figure of 14431 hearths there will thus be added a further 247 hearths, which makes altogether 14678 hearths. After deducting the 2216 hearths falling to the Olęder settlements, we obtain finally 12,462 hearths.
