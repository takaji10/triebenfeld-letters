For an enumeration of the Olęder settlements to have any value at all, it is necessary, having first established suitable criteria, to carry out, in relation to each individual locality of the territory under investigation, examinations aimed at determining whether a given settlement belongs to the Olęder type. We gave such criteria in the preceding chapter. On their basis there was compiled the list, given in the appendix, of the Olęder settlements existing in the territory of the former voivodeship of Poznań 1 For example, the Rajewskie or Wyszyńskie Ol., wrongly given already, by the way, by W. Schulz (Die zw. d. Ostsiedlung). By that last name were designated collectively the settlements belonging to the Wyszyny estate, namely the Raczyńskie, Filipowo and Józefowo Ol., at times also the Huta Ol. Besides other sources this is shown exactly by MGd. In just the same way there never existed the Kicińskie Ol. (= Kliny, Czerwonak and Janikowo Ol.), Ćmak Ol., Dronniki Ol., Koninko Ol., Smolarnia, Trocken Hld. (by the Kamińskie Ol.) % An example here may be Chojno near Wronki. In reality this is an old Polish contract village; it never had anything in common with the Olęder settlements, and its marking on MG as olędry occurred through an obvious mistake (cf. AGH court of Wronki, Chojno estate, Generalia fol. 38). The author classes as Olęders a whole series of such villages, among others: Chudopsice, Grzebienisko, Jaroszewo, Jezierze, Krobielewko (Klein Krebbel), Samołęż, Stajkowo, Sławienko, and further several rent villages located under German law (the so-called sołtys villages); we shall mention here, for instance, Gościejewo, Podlesie, Turowskie Ol. This last settlement is marked wrongly as olędry in some sources; 3 settlers sat here under individual, separate charters (of the years 1771, 1778 and 1781), and entirely separate obligations bound them as well. (cf. AGH court of Pniewy, Turowo estate, vol. I, fol. 188–9). As olędry there figure also with Maas folwarks (e.g. Mokre or Mokrz), mill settlements

(e.g. Mniszek), forest lodges (e.g. Tarnówko), and finally the colonies of the Prussian period (e.g. Birkenhorst (a settlement that developed from the older suburb of Trzciel), Julianka, Władysławowo (a settlement that came into being in the first half of the XIX century from the forest lodge of that same name), Lubs Gross and Klein, Schwaben Hld., Siewert Hld. (on MG 1 settler), Trzydomy near Chodzież (this hamlet is absent from the classification records of 1773), probably also Wełna Ol. (for the first time on MG and MGd I — a settler), and the settlement of Schwarzlug (sometimes described as a forest lodge (Fórsterei)); this settlement could not be located; near the Trzciel Hamr there lay separate Ol. Czarne (Scharne). Such questions of the author as: “are they Olęders?” (e.g. Stróżewo), or: “since when Olęders?” (e.g. Piotrowo), “where?” (e.g. Fiechoren, or else: Bożny, Bronisz, Kościelec, Buckow Hld.) must be regarded as pointless, since the settlements called in doubt were never Olęder, or (in the third case) did not exist. Besides this, the author gathered a mass of erroneous data concerning the settlements from other sources, e.g. from the Słownik Geograficzny K. P. (Sowinki, Stodólsko, Włościejewki, Różyńskie Ol. etc.), from the studies of Goldmann (Glinno or Glinki, and probably Boruja as well), Hampel (Bukowiec), and even of W. Schulz (Jędrzejewskie Ol.). Equally wrongly does the author infer the founding of the Nowe Jastrzębskie and Chojnickie Ol. from an article on the Zbąszyń Olęders in the newspaper Posener Tageblatt of 18 March 1934. We quote only examples relating to the former voivodeship of Poznań, for it is not possible within the frame of the present study to enumerate all the errors committed by the author. The author made no effort to present the true state of affairs; he did not even examine the fundamental source for knowing the numbers of the settlements, namely the original (large) map of Gilly, kept in the state collections in Berlin.
