The Olęder settlements are also distinguished by the character of their communal self-government. The administration and jurisdiction in the commune[^10-1] are exercised by a sołtys and by ławniks elected annually. There is thus no hereditary sołtys[^10-2] nominated by the lord, permanently endowed with certain privileges, as was the case in the colonisation under German law. Among the members of the commune there exists a far-reaching coexistence and co-operation. Equality and solidarity, both in relation to the lord and in their mutual communal relations, thus constitute the essence of Olęder settlement in the territory of the former voivodeship of Poznań 1. The rent-charge, as the legal-economic basis of the Olęders' relation to the manor, is in many cases a valuable indication when establishing the character of settlements, but it does not possess the significance of a decisive criterion. For especially in the XVIII century non-Olęder rent villages are in the area of the former voivodeship of Poznań a fairly frequent phenomenon. The same applies to leasehold for years; this form of holding land, at first characteristic of the Olęders, is completely absent from the later Olęder settlements; in its place we have the perpetual lease proper also to the other types of younger settlements.

The soundness of the distinctions made above is confirmed by the terminology of the sources. The settlements classed as Olęder on the basis of the considerations presented here are also designated in the sources, as a rule, as Olęder; conversely, the settlements whose source terminology is uncertain usually show the absence of one or other of the elements we have adduced as characterising this settlement. These settlements will be discussed at greater length in the following chapter.

There remains to be considered the question whether the essence of Olęder settlement in the area of the former voivodeship of Poznań remained unchanged throughout the whole time of the settlements' existence, or whether in the course of the years it underwent a certain evolution. The sources show that such an evolution did exist
