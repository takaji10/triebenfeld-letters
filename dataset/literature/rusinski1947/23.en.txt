As regards the number of hearths in the individual settlements, Tod. 89 and Tp. 89 are in complete agreement with each other. It must further be noted that the tables of hearths cover all strata of the population. In the appropriate columns of the list of settlements (Appendix I) there are set out in turn: I. the number of hearths of the individual Olęder settlements according to Tp. 89 (an asterisk marks those hearths which are given collectively in Tp. 89; they were then usually estimated according to Tp. 90, and in the case of mixed settlements on the basis of the number of Olęder farmers sitting in the settlement); 2. the number of hearths for settlements not covered by Tp. 89;[^23-1] 3. the population of the settlements according to Tol. Omitted were the figures given in Tol. for the Jaromierskie Nowe and Stare Ol. and for Ujście Stare, as evidently incomplete. On summation we obtain for the settlements covered by Tol.: 2144 hearths and 12967 souls. One hearth thus corresponds to 6.05 persons. By this average we calculate the population of the settlements for which Tol. is lacking: 334 hearths*? × 6.05 = 2020 souls.[^23-2] The Olęder population of Kościan county in 1789 amounted in all, therefore, to 14,987, in round figures 15,000 persons. It is harder to answer the question what proportion of the population of Kościan county the Olęder population constituted. For that purpose one would have to calculate the entire population of the county. Such calculations, however, go beyond the frame of the present study. In order to answer the question posed above at least in part, we shall attempt — leaving aside the question of the urban population — to calculate the ratio of the Olęders to the remaining rural population. According to Tol. the population of the rural settlements of Kościan county was 97,557 persons.[^23-3] Tod. 89 gives for these same settlements 15,831 hearths. In the total population according to Tol. the szlachta and the clergy are missing.[^23-4] Not having any more exact data for the calculation of these two strata of the population, we shall adopt as the starting point the calculations of Holsche 5[^23-5] for the Poznań department of 1800.
