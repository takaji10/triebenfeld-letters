According to Stamm[^68-1] the Chełmno ell, probably identical with the old or German ell mentioned in the sources, was somewhat shorter, measuring 57.6 cm.[^68-2] If we take this last ell as our basis, the area of a włóka measured with the rod of 7½ ells will come to 16.80 ha; of 8 ells — 19.10 ha; of 8½ ells — 21.60 ha; of 9 ells — 24.20 ha.

Besides the Chełmno włóka other measures were sporadically employed. In the neighbourhood of Międzychód the land was measured in ślads.[^68-3] From the mention in the charter for the Ol. Marienwald defining the area of the settlement as “seventeen ślads, 13 morgs, 107 różgas, all in Chełmno ells and measure” we may infer that they were identical with the włóka (huba).[^68-4] It was otherwise in the starostwo of Rogoźno. Here too the lands were reckoned in ślads, but these amounted to 2 włókas, that is 60 morgs.[^68-5] Likewise in the neighbouring Wieleń estate the włóka was reckoned at 50 or even 60[^68-6] morgs of normal size.

The size of the farms in the Olęder settlements underwent considerable changes in the course of the colonization period. In the earlier settlements the norm was the włóka; alongside this, farms of an area of 1½ włókas were also created. More rarely do we meet with farms of other sizes: 2 włókas, ¼, ½ of a włóka.[^68-7] False is the assertion of W. Schulz that at the founding the size of all the farms was equal; one of the charters lays down, for example, that of the land “each of them (i.e. the Olęders) in that forest shall be bound to have one or two włókas”,[^68-8] while in another settlement 2 Olęders are settled upon one ślad, 2 upon 1½ of a ślad, 3 upon 1½ of a ślad.[^68-9] The lustration of the Wieleń estate of 1653 records that at Marianowo an Olęder sits upon 2 włókas, 6 upon 1 włóka and 5 upon ½ of a włóka. It is out of the question that so unequal a division of the lands in the settlement could have come about in the course of 50 years (from 1630 the population of the settlement increased by only 2 farmers).[9] An unequal endowment with land,[^68-10]
