# Rusiński (1947), p. 17
# translation by Claude

— absent. TV — the Burzykowo cottage, folw., inn, d. 3. (własn. szlach.). From this it is evident that the marking on MG and MGd is erroneous. This is probably a newly founded folwark, or else an individual farm. The absence of sources from the Polish period and the name “cottage” give rise even to the doubt whether at that time this was not some abandoned building, inhabited only occasionally.[^17-1]

Two special situations required a separate decision: when an old settlement received Olęder rights, and when single-homestead settlements were called olędry. In the first case, despite the absence of a new location in the material sense, there arose, in the legal sense, a new Olęder settlement, and from then on it must be assigned to such unconditionally. Single-homestead settlements, however — whose number is, moreover, small 1 and which are usually treated unclearly in the sources — do not belong to the Olęder settlements. For here the coming into being of the conditions deciding their classing among the Olęder settlement is not possible. These are usually villages located on the principles of German law, and they acquired the name of olędry in some sources because they arose in the early modern period together with the Olęders 1, either in the surroundings of Olęder settlements, or else they were settled by settlers coming from those settlements. On the basis of the sources the following single-homestead settlements can be eliminated:

In Kościan county: 1. Katrynki,[^17-2] a folwark, today Katarzynki in the parish of Dłużyna; 2. Szklana Huta,[^17-3] a folwark in the parish of Bukowiec; 3. The settlement of Wymysłowo already mentioned.

In Poznań county: I. Budy, a folwark (2 1/5 hubas); 2. The already-mentioned Burzykowo; 3. Czerwieniec, a pitch kiln, or else a folwark, in the parish of Międzychód; 4. Kamień,[^17-5] in the parish of Międzychód; 5. Krebs,[^17-4] parish of Skwierzyna; 6. Kurzastopa,[^17-6] in the parish of Sieraków ] gosp.; 7. Nadziepozna

[^17-3]: Comparison of the sources: MG — Vw. Glashiitte; TV — Huta (Glashiitte); Tol. d. 3.
