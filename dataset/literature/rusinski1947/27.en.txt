B. The religious composition of the Olęder population

We possess no source statistics from which the religious composition of the population of the Olęder settlements could be reconstructed; certain general conclusions can be drawn only on the basis of the loose, chance mentions and data found in the sources. The question of the religious affiliation of the Olęders had already been discussed by German authors; a few, contrary to the obvious, tried to maintain that the whole Olęder population was Evangelical,1 but the majority admitted,[^27-1] that a certain percentage of the Olęders belonged to the Roman Catholic confession. The question therefore reduces to establishing what part of the population of the Olęder settlements was made up of Catholic settlers. The sources show that the population of the settlements founded in clerical estates on the territory of the Poznań voivodeship was always Catholic. In the Kościan county there existed 3 such settlements[^27-2] with a population of 401 souls. In the Poznań county 17 settlements belonged to church property.[^27-3] Their population, on estimation from the number of hearths, will come to 1,252 persons. From chance mentions we learn that in the Kościan county alone, besides the foregoing, wholly Catholic were Ol. Charłub,[^27-4] Ol. Chromiec,[^27-5] Ol. Kiączyńskie 6, Ol. Radoszkowskie 7, Ol. Terespotockie,[^27-6] Ol. Troszczyńskie,[^27-7][^27-8] Ol. Włościejewskie 19 and in part Ol. Łęskie l. Similar[^27-9][^27-10][^27-11]
