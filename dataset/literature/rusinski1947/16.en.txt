This year was adopted as the basis of the calculations for the reason that it is precisely from that time that there date a series of important sources, already mentioned in the introduction, which make it possible to carry out fairly exact calculations of the population of the settlements. According to the above list the number of Olęder settlements is: in Kościan county 111, in Poznań county 156 settlements whose Olęder character appears clearly in the sources, 11 settlements of transitional type, and 4 settlements whose belonging to the Olęder settlements could not, on account of insufficient, uncertain or contradictory source tradition, be established with greater probability. In sum we obtain 282 localities. The known dates of location of the settlements have been given in the appropriate column of the list. Question marks in brackets at some of the dates mean that in those cases the date of the charter was adopted as the date of location. In qualifying the settlements a rather broad interpretation was adopted. Thus all those settlements were classed as Olęder about whose organisation (of decisive significance, as is known) there were no data, provided only that the sources unanimously described them as olędry.
