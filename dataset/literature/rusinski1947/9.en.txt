I. The criterion of nationality, essential in relation to the settler-Dutchmen of Prussia in the first period of colonisation, loses its significance in the area of the former voivodeship of Poznań. Of the Dutch nationality of the settlers, especially in the later period, it is difficult here to speak at all; instead of it we have the German and the Polish nationality 1.[^9-1] In order to emphasise that the ethnic affiliation of the settlers is not the factor deciding the essence of “Olęder” settlement, and also in agreement with the terminology of the sources, in the further course of the study the term olędrzy will be used to denote the settlers, and olędry to denote the settlements.

2. Another characteristic — settlement on low-lying ground and the preparation[^9-2] of the soil for tillage by way of drainage — likewise does not adequately characterise the Olęder settlements in the territory of north-western Wielkopolska. The majority of these settlements arose on dry, wooded sites, and the improvement of the land took place not through drainage but through clearing?.

3. What remain are distinctions of a legal character. Of these, personal freedom is the privilege of almost all the settlers located in the XVI–XVIII centuries, and consequently provides no basis for separating the Olęder settlements from the other, younger settlements. The essence of the Olęders' distinctness must be sought in certain principles of the so-called Olęder law, which have been preserved in their social organisation. This concerns in the first place the settlers' relation to the lord. As in the territory of Prussia, it is regulated by a charter which the lord issues on the basis of an agreement with the whole commune, or with a deputy acting in its name. The manorial obligations are fixed per unit of land; every settler pays them in strict proportion to the area of his farm. Often the whole commune answers solidarily for their fulfilment by the appointed term. A privileged position vis-à-vis the lord is held only by the locator 1 the innkeeper.
