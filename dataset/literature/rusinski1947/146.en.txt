First

Establishment of the village office and its duties. Having assembled on the feast of the Three Kings, the communities of the Kicin estate complex, in the village of Czerwonak and at the former sołtys's, are to elect from among the farmers of that village, the most long-standing rent-payers, a sołtys, whose term of office is to last for [printed: from] three years; and if he should prove remiss in anything or wanting in vigilance, and in particular should transgress this present law, and just complaints against him should come from the communities to the manor, then, with the permission received from the manor, they may proceed within the three years to the election of another sołtys. On the same feast the communities are also to elect two ławniks in that village, one from the Czerwonak Olęders, the other from the Kliny Olęders; one of them must at least know Polish and German [printed: 'po niemieckich', recte: 'po niemiecku']; and if within those three years one of these officials dies or sells up, the elder of those remaining is to summon the community at any time to elect another. The duty of these two ławniks will be to come to Czerwonak to the sołtys on every court day and then, together with the sołtys, to hear the complaints that a neighbour may have against another from whichever community, and to punish the guilty party according to the articles of the law written out below. At the same time it will also belong to this office to see to the public roads, the dams and the ditches, to give one another notice in good time of any meadow tax or dues, likewise to collect the taxes from the communities and to pay them over to whom they are due, to take the receipts for what has been paid and to keep them in the chest of the community treasury.

If, however, some new case should call them out beyond those court days — whether by order of the manor or by summons of the sołtys — the ławniks must appear at once and deal with the matter for which they have been summoned. If this village office (into which it should diligently inquire) sees that any of the rent-payers is slothful in the management of his holding or is letting his buildings go to ruin, then, after a triple [warning], it is to assess his huba and his buildings and see to finding a buyer.
