# Władysław Rusiński, *Osady tzw. „olędrów" w dawnym województwie poznańskim* — English translation

> **Translator's note.** This is a translation of the monograph
> (Poznań 1939, Kraków 1947). Conventions, agreed with the editor:
> British spelling; the ethnic term **Olęder(s)** (Pol. *olędrzy*,
> German *Hauländer*) is retained and glossed at first use; archival
> sigla (AW., Rel. Posn., Tp. 89 …), archive call marks and
> bibliographic titles are kept in their original form; numbers
> follow the print exactly, with Polish thousand/decimal separators
> converted to the English convention (13.737 → 13,737; 6,05 → 6.05)
> and no value altered — including the print's own internal
> discrepancies; measures are glossed at first use; quotations from
> archival Polish are translated, the original wording being given
> only where the author discusses it; quotations in German keep the
> original with a translation; the tables of ANEKS I are not
> translated in this edition. The bracketed `[p. N]` markers are the
> print's own pagination.
>
> **Terms used.** *Olęder(s)* — one of the Dutch-origin farming
> colonists and their settlements (Pol. *olędrzy/olędry*, German
> *Hauländer*); "Ol. X" = an Olęder settlement named X. *huba*
> (pl. *hubas*) — a full hide, the basic holding unit (cf. German
> *Hufe*). *włóka* (pl. *włókas*) — the field measure (in this book
> the 30-morg włóka). *morg* (pl. *morgs*) — a land measure. *dym* —
> hearth, the fiscal unit (the hearth tax = *podymne*). *tymf* — the
> debased Polish coin of the late 17th and early 18th century.
> *folwark* — manor farm. *sołtys* — village headman. *ławnik* —
> lay assessor (juror). *wolnizna* — a rent-free period granted at
> settlement. *wilkierz* — a village by-law. *oblaty* — enrolments
> of documents in the court books. *wieś zaciężna* — a contract
> village settled under Polish law. *chałupnik* — a cottage dweller
> without land. *komornik* — a lodger. *zagrodnik* — a smallholder
> with a garden plot. *starostwo* — a royal domain held by a
> starosta. *królewszczyzny* — royal estates. *majętność* — a
> private estate complex. *serwituty* — servitudes (rights of use).
> *szarwark* — corvée labour. *robocizna* — labour due. *przymus
> mlewa* — the milling monopoly. *propinacja* — the propination
> (liquor) monopoly. *pogłówne* — poll tax. *hyberna* — the winter
> levy. *korzec/garniec* — dry measures (pl. *korcy/garnce*).
