“It seems, however, that this right of pasturage was not granted at the location of the settlement, but only acquired through old usage.” He also cites further lawsuits with the Olęders, however already from Prussian times. —

Schmidt takes the same road[^98-1]. As an example of the development of conditions in Wielkopolska he cites the settlements of Nowe Dwory and Folsztyn in the Wieleń estate. According to him the rent in the first settlement was raised from 750 zł in 1677 to 3187 zł in 1773[^98-2], and in Folsztyn in the year

599 zł 12 gr even to 6588 zł. This last figure would be weighty proof if it were true. The author, however, probably by mistake, cited, instead of the amount of the rent, the sum of the settlement's gross income, calculated by the Prussian officials for fiscal purposes[^98-3]. This sum has nothing in common with the Olęders' rent, though it is 2½ times greater. The rent amounted to 2627 zł. Schmidt also failed to mention that in the meantime the Olęders in Nowe Dwory ploughed in for themselves 5 2/5, and in Folsztyn 4 3/5, włókas[^98-4], nor that in Nowe Dwory the włóka contained 60 morgs[^98-5], while in Folsztyn 50 morgs. After taking this into account there falls per włóka: in Nowe Dwory 88½ zł, and in Folsztyn 82 zł 23 gr. On the normal 30-morg włóka we shall obtain respectively:

in the first case 44¼ zł, in the second 49 zł 18 gr. Meanwhile the author states on the following page that the average rent per włóka at that time had settled by itself at 70 to 80 zł. Similar results we shall obtain when we compare the real value of the rents given above. At the beginning of the seventeenth century, when there was assigned to the Olęders the rent still binding in 1677, at the rate of 25 and 22 zł per huba, the silver content in the złoty amounted to 20, a little later 17; meanwhile in the złotys current at the moment of the first partition it amounted to only 3 g[^98-6]. The value of money thus fell 6-fold, while the rent was raised 3–4 times. On the other hand the author quite rightly emphasises that various fees were unlawfully, contrary to the charter, imposed on the Wieleń Olęders, such as solne, stróżowe, popielne, karczunkowe (Reuthergeld), amounting to 3% of the proper rent.
