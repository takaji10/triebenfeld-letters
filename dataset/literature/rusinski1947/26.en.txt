The figures given include the population of all the settlements of Poznań county listed in Appendix I b, that is, also the settlements of transitional and of uncertain character. If we leave these settlements aside, there remain 2,109 Olęder hearths, and the population of the settlements taken into account will be 12,759 persons. The total sum of the rural population of the county (within the borders after the first partition) will rise to the figure of 93,569.[^26-1] The population of the Olęder settlements will then amount to 13.6% of that figure.

For the part of Poznań county taken by Prussia in the first partition we possess population lists of 1773, contained in the Prussian classification records, and a census of hearths published in 1789 by Goldbeck on the basis of materials of 1783 and later. On the whole the number of hearths in the Olęder settlements showed no tendency to abrupt changes; we shall therefore not depart far from reality,[^26-2] if we accept Goldbeck's data as depicting the state of 1789. The last column of Appendix I b shows 514 Olęder hearths beyond the cordon. After multiplying them by the coefficient of 6.05 established previously[^26-3] we obtain 3,122 persons. For lack of sufficient data, and because of the difficulty of determining the county affiliation of certain localities, it is not possible here to estimate the remaining rural population of this part of the county.

In the territory of Wielkopolska, apart from the counties of Kościan and Poznań, only the counties of Gniezno and Pyzdry had a more substantial number of Olęder settlements. Probably, therefore, for Wielkopolska as a whole the proportion of the Olęders to the total rural population will fall below 10%.
