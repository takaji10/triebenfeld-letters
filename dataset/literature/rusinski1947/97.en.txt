The fact that in the domain of Podstolice the Prussian officials applied flogging to the Olęders does not prove that it was so in all the domains, but it shows to what errors the method of generalising from extreme cases leads. Brandenburger[^97-2] and Koerth[^97-3] approach the question in a similar way. The reasoning of the latter author proceeds by strange paths. He reproaches the lords with acting contrary to the provisions of the charters, and then, as an example, cites a dispute with the lord over pasture servitudes, adding, however: in the king's name he orders the petitions of the communities to be examined favourably and categorically forbids the complainants to be beaten. Evidently, however, the position of the settlers did not much improve, for on 22 May 1782 the Brzekiniec Olęders send a new letter (fol. 130) to the king of the following tenor: “Nachdem Ihro Majestät aus besonderer Gnadenbezeigung gerichtet haben, uns allergnädigst bey unserer alten Gerechtigkeit, so wie ehemals unter Königlich Pohlnischer Regierung genossen, zu schützen und unverändert dabei zu erhalten; wir aber demohngeachtet nach der Maassgabe des Conducteurs Eichmann, welcher unsere Felder vor zwey Jahren gemessen, in unsern Abgaben sehr erhöhet worden, indem gedachter Conducteur aus unsern Gründen weit mehr gemessen als wir nach altem Königlich Pohlnischen Maasse wirklich besitzen. Da doch unsere Gründe vor wenig Jahren noch durch die ehemalige Pohlnische Obrigkeit aufs allergenauste gemessen wurden und zwar jeden Morgen zu 300 Ruthen, jede Ruthe zu 8 Ellen, jede Elle zu 24 Zoll, die Baustelle wurde aus einem Morgen, darzu Graben, Wege und Triften Zinsfrey gelassen. ["Whereas His Majesty, out of a special mark of grace, has been pleased most graciously to protect us in our old rights, such as we formerly enjoyed under the Royal Polish government, and to preserve them unchanged; we nevertheless, in accordance with the measure of the conductor Eichmann, who measured our fields two years ago, have been very heavily raised in our dues, inasmuch as the said conductor measured far more out of our lands than we actually possess according to the old Royal Polish measure. And yet our lands were still measured a few years ago by the former Polish authority with the utmost exactness, namely every Morgen at three hundred rods, every rod at eight ells, every ell at twenty-four inches, and the building site was released from one Morgen rent-free, together with the ditches, roads and driftways."]

Darnachst wurde uns zu Pohlnischen Zeiten gestattet freyes Bau-Holz, sowohl zu Reparaturen als auch zu gantz neuem Bau von der Königlichen Heide zu holen, welches uns auch bisher von Ihro Majestät unserm allergnädigsten Könige zwar noch nicht ist gänzlich abgeschlagen, dennoch aber bis dato noch keines erhalten haben, so höchstnöthig es auch bey vielen unserer Einsassen, deren Gebäude alt und baufällig ist, zu gebrauchen wären. Wir sind überzeugt, dass Ihro Majestät, unser allergnädigster König und Herr, uns Landes-Väterlicher Hulde geruhen werden, diese unsere Bitte, so wir denenselben in tiefster Ehrfurcht und Veneration übergeben, allergnädigst von uns auch annehmen werden, und uns in obbemeldten Puncten laut unserm Privilegio bey unsern alten Rechten und Schützen behalten werden, welche uns, seitdem unsere Gründe durch obbemeldten Conducteur gemessen worden, dessen Maassgabe wir unmöglich vor richtig annehmen und erkennen können, sind gesindert und unsere Abgaben erhöhet worden, unangesehen das unsere Gründe schlecht und im gantzen hiesigen Amte den sandigsten und untragbarsten Boden gaben, wenn wir bey Ihro Majestät unserm allergnädigsten Könige die hohe Gnade erhalten, werden wir uns verpflichtet halten dero Leutseligkeit und Gnadenbezeigung lebenslang zu rühmen, und dabei unveränderlich zeigen das wir seyn Eur. Königl. Majestät allerunterthänigste Knechte. Bracknitzer Holl., d. 22-ten May 1782”. ["After that, in Polish times, we were permitted to fetch free building timber, both for repairs and for entirely new building, from the Royal Heath; this has indeed not yet been entirely refused to us by His Majesty our most gracious King, but up to the present we have received none of it, however necessary it would be for use by many of our inhabitants, whose buildings are old and dilapidated. We are convinced that His Majesty, our most gracious King and Lord, will deign to show us a fatherly grace, will most graciously accept this our petition, which we submit to the same in deepest reverence and veneration, and will keep us, in the points named above, according to our charter, in our old rights and protections, which — since our lands have been measured by the aforesaid conductor, whose measure we can by no means accept and recognise as correct — have been altered, and our dues raised, notwithstanding that our lands are poor and give, in this whole Amt here, the sandiest and most unprofitable soil. If we retain the high grace of His Majesty our most gracious King, we shall hold ourselves obliged to praise your gracious kindness and mark of grace all our lives long, and therein to show unchangeably that we are Your Royal Majesty's most humble servants. Bracknitzer Holl., dated the twenty-second of May, seventeen eighty-two"]

["...by virtue of three charters, which mean more than one thing but nevertheless refer only to two and a half Hufen, and were never taxed higher even in Polish times, but have now been fixed by the former Commission of Classification at twenty-five Gulden"]. The lawsuits of the Olęders with the State treasury drag on for several dozen years, into the nineteenth century (G. 1–12).
