also mention the kolędas[^105-1]. Incidental dues were collected for christenings, weddings, visitations, burials[^105-2]. These were the so-called iura stolae [fees for the priest's ministrations][^105-3]. The evangelical Olęders were often obliged to pay these dues also when they made use of the ministrations of evangelical clergymen[^105-4]. They were then evidently treated as compensation for the loss of church revenue. Nor was this anything exceptional; in Prussia the same was done towards Catholics[^105-5]. In many cases, when the Olęders paid the fixed dues, no dues were collected for the individual services. Most often, however, all the hitherto mentioned dues were combined into one, a certain fixed lump-sum due being established. As one established by agreement with the lord or the settlers it was sometimes called a composita[^105-6], although sometimes the composita concerned only the meszne[^105-7]. Exceptionally, for the designation of church obligations, we meet the term Gottespfennig[^105-8]. The Olęders paid the fixed dues either per unit of land, or else the whole community paid a collective due. [^105-5]: Kośc., fol. 71 and 73: The parish priest of Trzcianka in a letter of 14 February 1773 to the Prussian minister of finance writes as follows: „Die mehresten Protestanten in dieser Gegend sind vor Jahren Einkómlinge aus Deutschland gewesen, die sich auf catholischen Platzen niederge- lassen und mithin denen Juribus Stolae freywillig uniterworfen, daher es auch gekommen, als durch ihre Ausbreitung sich die Catholiken verminderten und sie durch Schwedischen Schutz Kirchen und Prediger erhielten, dass sie dennoch dennen catholischen Pfarrern einen gewissen Canon oder so genannte Compositam wie noch in Polen wo evangelische Kirchen und Prediger sind, beobachtet wird. Dass dieses kein unrechtmassiges Verfahren, wird durch die Gewohnhcit in protestantischen Lindern bewiesen, wo die ankommende Catholicken, wenn sie sich auch vermehren und catholische Priester haben, dennoch dem alten Stamm, denen protestantischen Predigern die Jura Stolae erlegen miissen, wie in Berlin, Stettin und in anderen Liindern zu schen”. ["The greater number of Protestants in this region were years ago immigrants from Germany, who settled on Catholic ground and therefore voluntarily submitted themselves to the Jura Stolae; whence it came about that, as through their spread the Catholics diminished and they obtained churches and preachers under Swedish protection, they nevertheless observed towards the Catholic parish priests a certain canon, or so-called composita, as is still observed in Poland where evangelical churches and preachers exist. That this is no unlawful procedure is proved by the custom in Protestant lands, where the Catholics arriving there, even if they increase and have Catholic priests, must nevertheless pay the Jura Stolae to the old stock, to the Protestant preachers, as is to be seen in Berlin, Stettin and other lands.”] In reply (fol. 73) the Prussian authorities argue as follows: „Der Satz, dass in protestantischen Landen die darin befindliche catholische Gemeinden, dennoch die Jura Stolae denen protestantischen Predigern erlegen massen, hier keine Anwen- dung findet, weil dort die catholische Religion blos gedułdct wird”. ["The proposition that in Protestant lands the Catholic communities dwelling therein must nevertheless pay the Jura Stolae to the Protestant preachers finds no application here, because there the Catholic religion is merely tolerated”."]
