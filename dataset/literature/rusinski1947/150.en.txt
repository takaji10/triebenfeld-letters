# Rusiński (1947), p. 150
# translation by Claude

The foregoing extract from the Chwaliszewo burgher books has been written out and collated with those books, that is with the enrolment, and found to agree in every respect; the Contentious Department of the Court of Peace of the City of Poznań, which has oversight of the archive, hereby certifies this and signs in its own hand with the seal of the court affixed, with the added remark that the crossing-out of two lines, written by mistake from another case, was done by the court itself.

Poznań, on the 26th of the month, in the year 1810. (signed) Wincenty Mierzyński, Podsędek of Poznań (L. S.) [locus sigilli]. Certified as authentic by signature:

M. Kamiński P. D. i L. M. O. B. Certified as agreeing with the original (signed) Betkowski
