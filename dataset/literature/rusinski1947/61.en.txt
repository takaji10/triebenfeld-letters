[^61-1]: That there were more innkeepers than the Tol. shows may be inferred from the number of hearths given for the taverns in the Tol. 89. It is beyond doubt that for all the innkeepers agricultural farms (very often exempt from rent) were the basis of subsistence. Part of the innkeepers was presumably entered in the Tol. in the column “farmers”. We have estimated their number by multiplying the surplus of tavern hearths in the Tol. 89 by the coefficient of 5, expressing the number of persons falling to an innkeeper's family according to Tab. III.
[^61-2]: In all, there stood in the settlements 1 water mill and 8 windmills. The water mill standing in the Sroczewskie Olęders existed already before the founding of the Olęders. The Tol. gives only the families of 2 millers. The families of the remaining owners of mills, 7 in number, given in the Tol. 89, we have estimated at 42 persons, reckoning 6 persons to a miller's hearth. Their principal occupation was without doubt agriculture.
[^61-3]: The persons assigned to this group form the crew of the hammer mill standing in the Borujskie Olęders. This hammer existed already before the founding of the Olęders; it was not embraced by the founding charter. It may nevertheless be presumed that its workers were made up of inhabitants of the settlement. Source information is here lacking.
[^61-4]: There must have been more village schoolmasters than the 1 family recorded in the Tol., as may be inferred from various incidental mentions. They are presumably contained in the columns “farmers”, “lodgers” and “craftsmen”. The figure of persons belonging to the teachers' families, entered in the column “incidentally employed in the given occupation”, has been estimated on the basis of the results of Table III. For the 94 settlements of Kościan county there fell, proportionally, 36 teachers (together with the readers — cf. Tab. III), of whom 1 had already been reckoned, while the rest, reckoning 4 persons to a teacher or reader with his family, made in all 140 souls.
