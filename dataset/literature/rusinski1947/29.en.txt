C. The nationality of the Olęder population

Determining the ethnic affiliation of the Olęders presents very great difficulties. Direct source information in this respect is almost entirely lacking.[^29-2] For contemporaries this question had no significance whatever in view of the complete equality of rights enjoyed by both nationalities. Indirect indications are supplied to us by the surnames of settlers given from time to time.

The national character of the Olęder settlement on the territory of the former Poznań voivodeship was not uniform throughout the whole duration of the colonization. It cannot admit of any doubt that the first settlers were authentic Hollenders.[^29-3] They were settled in the oldest settlement on the territory that interests us, namely in the Olęders of Ujście (Olędrowie), and in the four oldest settlements of the Wieleń estate: Folsztyn, Herbardowo, Marianowo and Nowe Dwory. We possess in this respect a series of certain, irrefutable source data:

I. All the settlements mentioned are located on the waterlogged Noteć lowland. As in Prussia, the Holendrzy were brought in for the purpose of draining the lands assigned to them.
