# Rusiński (1947), p. 41
# translation by Claude

As regards content the charters show a great kinship. This comes of the fact that in issuing them the pattern of the charters of earlier settlements was followed. There is no lack, however, of very original documents, at times marked by a deep social and moral thought. One of such documents, the collective charter for the Olęders of the Kicin estate complex, we quote in full in the appendix. To confirm the force of the charter, the lords frequently bound themselves to enrol it in the proper grod books. This set a precedent for the Olęders, who themselves too began to submit their charters for enrolment. A number of charters contain additional confirmations by later owners of the settlement. At times such later confirmation became the occasion for an increase in the duties of the Olęders. In almost every charter the lord bound himself, in the event of the destruction or loss of the original, to issue free of any cost a counterpart with identical wording.[^41-1] b. The legal dependence of the Olęders on the lord could manifest itself in a threefold manner: as personal, judicial[^41-2] or tenurial dependence. In relation to the corvée population this dependence was complete; one distinguishes here accordingly personal, judicial and tenurial subjection.[^41-3] Personal subjection[^41-4] of the Olęder farmers did not in general exist. Almost all the charters stressed this unanimously. Less clear[^41-5] presented itself the question of the personal dependence of the Olęders in cases where a village already in existence was transferred to Olęder law. Thus, for example, the charter for Ol. Chorzępowa, which arose in this way in the Sieraków estate, allowed them to sell their farms, and therefore probably also to leave the estate, and defined their duties in the same way as the duties of other Olęders. But the charter lacks any declaration confirming their personal freedom, and the inventory of the estate, later by 6 years, classes part of the farmers as subjects.5 Entirely lacking, on the other hand, are data for the remaining settlements of this type.
