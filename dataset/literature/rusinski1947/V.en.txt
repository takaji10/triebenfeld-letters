# rusinski1947  V  en
# Rusiński (1947), p. V
# translation by Claude

LIST OF ABBREVIATIONS

The abbreviations used in the study are set in italics in the print.

A. Sources

(Sources not further specified were located, or are still located,
before the outbreak of the war in the State Archives in Poznań).

AC. 1739–40 — Acta Causarum of 1739–40, the Archdiocesan Archives
in Poznań.

AGH — land and mortgage records (Akta gruntowe i hipoteczne).

AM — town records (Akta miejskie).

AW — village records (Akta wiejskie).

Dom. — Prussian records concerning the state domains, classed under
the call mark: Domeny.

G — Johann Friedrich Goldbeck, Vollständige Topographie des
Königreichs Preussen. Zweiter Theil, welcher die Topographie von
Westpreussen enthält. Marienwerder 1789.

GE — Allgemeine Geographische Ephemeriden. Verfasset von einer
Gesellschaft Gelehrten und herausgegeben von A. C. Gaspari u.
F. J. Bertuch. Achter Band. Weimar 1801.

Decr. Posn. — the court books of the Poznań grod (castle court).

Inser. Valc. — the grod books of Wałcz (Inscriptiones Valcenses).

Kat. I, II — Kataster der der Westpreussischen Kontribution
unterliegenden Ortschaften im Regierungs-Bezirk Bromberg, vols
I–II, call mark: WPZ. Kam. Dep. Bydgoszcz. XII. A. 1.

Kl. — the Prussian classification records (Klassifikationsanschläge)
of the years 1773–4, call marks: WPZ. Kam. Dep. Bydgoszcz,
XII. 6 I–V, XII. 7, XII. 13.

Kl. M. — the Prussian classification records of 1795 concerning the
estates of the Order of the Knights of Malta, call mark:
SPZ. C. I. 14.

Kośc. — records concerning religious affairs in the Prussian
partition after the First Partition, call mark: WPZ. Kam. Dep.
Bydgoszcz II. B.

Landr. — records of the former Prussian landrats' offices
(Landratury), classed under the call mark: Landratury.

Lustr. pruska 1664 — the lustration of the royal domains of Royal
Prussia, published as vol. 32 of the Fontes of the Toruń Scientific
Society under the title: Opis królewszczyzn w województwach
chełmińskim, pomorskim i malborskim w r. 1664 (A description of the
royal domains in the chełmno, pomorskie and malbork voivodeships).
Toruń 1938.

MG — Gilly, Special Karte von Südpreussen (a reduction at the scale
of 1:115,200). Berlin 1802 and 1803.

MGd — the Map of South Prussia of the years 1796–1805 at the scale
of 1:57,600 (the large Gilly).

MP — Mappa szczegulna Woiewództwa Poznańskiego zrządzona... przez
Karola de Perthees... 1791 (A particular map of the Poznań
voivodeship, made... by Karol de Perthees).
