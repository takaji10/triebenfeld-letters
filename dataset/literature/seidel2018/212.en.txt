### 22.2 Massenbach as FL's Quartermaster-General and the Events of 1806

Von Massenbach had already been near FL during the Rhine campaigns of 1792–94, and then, at the latest since 1805, he served directly on his staff as Colonel ("Obrist") and Quartermaster-General. He was thus FL's most important military collaborator and, in addition to the aides-de-camp, also a constant conversational partner. The correspondence, too, is extensive.[^152] And this was probably often quite strenuous, since they were very different personalities: one can hardly imagine how they endured the years 1805/06 together.
