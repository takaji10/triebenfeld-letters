# translation by Claude

> *most deeply. You know well, my Friend, that my heart knows how to value such conduct — so mine rejoices, in a burning wish to be able to be of use to you, to prove all its gratitude, its feelings, and its desire to be good for something to you.*

The letter then became quite concrete after all: she found the plans he had submitted very well considered, but did not know to whom she should turn:

> *I await your commands in this matter. Your plan for your return is the one that comes closest to my wishes [...]. May God keep you in your good resolve to return in the course of this month. I confess to you honestly that I believe you would do well to hold firm on every point of this plan.*

Where was FL? Presumably in Ansbach-Bayreuth, or even Ingelfingen. Marianne was

> *convinced that people wish to see you pressed toward your own rule. Persons who occupy themselves with envying you and doing you harm seize upon this motive to prove that you are little satisfied with what you have. [...] For now everyone is saying that you are aspiring to the government of Ansbach; it is even claimed in Silesia [...].*

One may conclude from this that his further career was a topic of discussion in Berlin. What plans, then, was he himself pursuing? In the end this proved of no consequence, however, for he was very soon obliged to march against France in the War of the Coalition under the supreme command of the Duke of Brunswick.
