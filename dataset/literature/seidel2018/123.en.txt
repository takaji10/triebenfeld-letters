### 11.6 Friedrich Ludwig Again as Renewer of a Territory

We have additional knowledge of FL's activities through letters he wrote to his son and successor in Öhringen, August. These included, among other things, the difficulties arising from the restrictions imposed by the King of Württemberg upon the former rulers of the annexed territories.

One letter to August in particular seems well suited to illustrate his evidently continuing drive to create things around his new residence, the castle at Slaventzitz. It concerns agriculture and the exploitation of mineral resources (the further development of coal and ore mining). The letter bears the date 17 August — without a year. It dates from 1814, since his son Adolph is visiting FL that summer, being only 17 years old at the time. The letter, as stated, is addressed to August:[^95]

> *Dearest son! I dictate this letter from the bath, which suits me well; already half the cure is completed, and I [get] no other addition to the residues. [He evidently has health problems that are being treated with a bath cure at home]. Adolph goes today with the Forest Master by way of Lassowitz to Landsberg; from the former office J.B. Kunorsky and Court Councillor Hahn from Koschentin are coming today as well; they were there for 3 days, and Kunorsky made the journey there, accompanied by Baildon and Korb, by way of Bytkow and Tarnowitz; Bytkow is said to have made a great and lasting impression on him, as Korb told me. Everything now seems so well prepared for its acquisition that it is hoped the bidding date can be set for the 28th of this month. As the works overseer told me, Kunorsky intends to be present himself. I do not know whether he will find the time for it, since he still wanted to go on to Brieg. From my sister I receive good news from Carlsruhe, and all are well there; she will probably remain a few*
