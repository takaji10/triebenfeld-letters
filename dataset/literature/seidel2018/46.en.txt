## 4 Friedrich Ludwig's Career as a Prussian General

### 4.1 Stations in the Military up to the Peace of Basel in 1795

Even early on FL was drawn to a military career. At the age of 13 he received the promise of a captaincy in the Franconian Circle Regiment, part of the Imperial troops. He took up the post at 17. After that he was rarely at Ingelfingen Castle (as would also later be the case when he was reigning Prince). Military duties came first, and there were armed conflicts in abundance. He made his first campaign with this regiment. With the Imperial troops under General Kleefeld, FL rose to the rank of senior major (Obristwachtmeister). He received the necessary military knowledge from him and also acquired it himself.

One episode concerned the capture of a Prussian officer by ordinary soldiers, who intended to shoot this prisoner — it was Major von Zastrow. FL intervened, gave each of the soldiers involved a small sum of money, and von Zastrow was set free — and he was never to forget this of FL. He made a career directly at the court in Berlin and years later was able to assist FL with various wishes. For example, when it came to the promotion of officers, for which FL had petitioned the King. In that same year, 1766, at the age of 20, he entered the service of the King of Prussia, Frederick II, as a Major.

After the end of the Seven Years' War in 1763, FL was sent traveling, and among other places stayed for a long time in Paris. Frederick II was his first patron, and it was him he wished to emulate. It is reported that he came to Potsdam
