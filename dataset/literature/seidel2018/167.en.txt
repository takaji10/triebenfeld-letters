### 16.3 Kahlert as Sender of Literary Works and Other Items

Kahlert did not merely inform FL about new publications concerning the events of the war. He also supplied him, in his new provincial solitude, with "literary products" on at least somewhat different subjects. On one occasion he simply copied out a long journal article by hand and sent it to him. FL also asked him directly for "entertaining reading." Kahlert procured the books for him, making use for this purpose also of the Breslau "Streitische Lending Library," which was located near the house "Prince Hohenlohe." The reading lists in letters from the last two years indeed contain "entertaining reading," but also more demanding historical works:

- Charakteristik Friedrich II., installments 1–3.
- Vie privée, politique et militaire de Prince Henri de Prusse.
- Über König Friedrich II. Öffentliche Vorlesungen, gehalten zu Berlin von Adam Müller.
- Anekdoten von König Friedrich II., issues 2–6.
- D. Tralles Erzählung seiner mit König Friedrich dem Großen, der Kaiserin Maria Theresia und der Herzogin Louise Dorothea von Sachsen-Gotha gehaltenen Unterredungen.
- Friedrich des Zweiten Briefwechsel mit seinen Geschwistern, Dukan de Gander.

Another compilation contained:

- Memoires de M. le Baron de Besenval. Parts I and II.
- Mon sejour aupres M. de Voltaire.
- Lebensbeschreibung des Grafen Schwerin.
- Memoires du Prince Eugene de Savoie.
- Heinrich von Bülow.
- Annalou Elisabeth Landgräfin von Hessen.
- Memoiren des Herzogs von Lückinfarn.
