Under these circumstances, and since it appears from the mortgage certificate communicated to the General Invalids' Fund by the Glogau Superior Court by letter of 21 Sept. 1810, and likewise enclosed here sub petito retardationis, that the estate of Kontopp near Grüneberg in Silesia, belonging to von Triebenfeld, is encumbered with debt to almost the full amount of its purchase [...] in the year 1800, and further, as this letter of the Glogau [...] Superior Court shows, that the estate [Sch.], belonging to von Triebenfeld and situated in the district of the Breslau Superior Court, is likewise burdened with considerable debts, the General Invalids' Fund is, as regards its claim to the capital sum of 50,000 [...] and the interest thereon in arrears, which by now, as noted, up to the end of July of this year already amounts to 9,375 [...], entirely without security. Meanwhile, however, this Fund has learned that His Serene Highness the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen has received, from the estate of the late Princess von der Osten-Sacken who died here, the sum of 80,000 [...] as a legacy, and that he will in turn pay out this sum to von Triebenfeld in satisfaction of the sum of money owed him; and I have been instructed in this matter, according to the enclosed original, on the basis of the order — enclosed in copy — of the Royal Privy State Councillor and Chief of the General Police Department in the Ministry of the Interior, dated the 28th of this month, contained in the letter of the 29th [...], to lay attachment forthwith, at the [Royal Prussian] Kammergericht, upon the aforesaid 80,000 [...], in coverage of the loan of the 50,000 [...] and of the arrears thereon amounting, from 1 Nov. 1807 to the end of July 1811, to 9,375 [...], together with the interest accruing thereafter.
