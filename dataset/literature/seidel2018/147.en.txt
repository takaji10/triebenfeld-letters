# Seidel (2018), p. 147
# translation by Claude

FL's contact with the King on the matter of "estates in South Prussia" continued, for in a royal letter of 4 December 1798 these are again the subject. It concerned price negotiations, not donations, and the name Triebenfeld came up as well. This fits with a remark by FL in a letter of 26 March 1799 to his fifteen-year-old son August in Berlin.[^116] He added in his own hand that the son should send a "deposit" straightaway to War Councillor Triebenfeld. Whether the "deposit" is an indication of a payment? At the end of 1799, in the written agreement ("Transact," see chap. 7.1) made necessary by Marianne's separation from FL, quite considerable possessions in South Prussia are listed.
