### 19.5 Letter from his son Adolph

> *Dear Father!/It was with the most heartfelt regret that I learned through Lombardini that your health, so precious to me, has still not improved; it would make me very happy, and I might perhaps be able to entertain you – to be permitted to help shorten the time for you. Rest assured, my dearest Father, that I feel happy only in the fulfilment of my duties toward you. I have therefore instructed Lombardini to inform me, should you permit it, that I may keep you company. Prince and Prince Wilhelm, as well as Prince Blücher, who show me much favor, inquire diligently after you and send you their best regards./In faithful love I remain your obedient son Adolph H./Berlin, 8th Febr. 1818[^141]*

### 19.6 Letter from the Son August of 22 January 1818

> *Dear Father! Your forthcoming birthday gives me the occasion, so gladdening to my heart, to express toward you those filial wishes and feelings which I constantly hold for you. May God grant you, dearest Father, health and contentment in full measure, and grant me the happiness of always possessing your paternal love and your goodwill.*
