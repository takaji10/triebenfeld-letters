### 20.2 The End of Marianne's Life

Marianne died on 20 April 1840 in Mecklenburg, where she had lived for nearly 40 years, at the Marienhof built by the family in 1837 near the Bellin manor house where she had previously resided. Its manor building has since been much altered, unlike the entrance area of the manor courtyard, which still represents the scale of the complex.

Prince August, her firstborn son and by then already resident at Slaventzitz, had met with his mother in Berlin only shortly before. To what extent regular contact otherwise existed is unknown. Her two daughters, Angelika and Auguste, had already been married in Mecklenburg for more than 15 years by the time of her death, and there were presumably no longer any relations with their father, Count Sacken (but once again, take note: there are no corresponding documents in the Hohenlohe Archive). Marianne was interred in the vault of the church in Bellin.

August invited to his home in Upper Silesia a family gathering of the Hohenlohes, the joint children of Marianne and FL, for a family reunion. Of the siblings, however, Auguste and Alexander had already died in 1816 and 1829 respectively.

Memorial services were held on 1 and 4 June, the first in the Protestant church of Jakobswalde, presumably for the general public, the second in Laskowitz, conducted by a pastor from Budkowitz.
