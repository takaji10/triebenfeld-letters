He also named reading matter for the Landgravine Auguste (FL's daughter) as well as for Princess Sophie (FL's sister, who had stood by his side since October 1816).

FL must have been pleased by the news that his former aides-de-camp Pirch and Blumenstein — now bearing the rank of general — intended to visit him. Blumenstein had an inspection tour to southern Silesia ahead of him.

The next letter names the following books:

- Geschäfte Gustavs Waso, König von Schweden von Archenhol.
- Zimmernmanns Verhältnisse mit der Kaiserin Catharina II.
- Peter der Große von Claudius Reisen durch das Innere von Afrika.
- Das Privatleben des Königs von Preußen Friedrich II. u. Voltaire.

The letter of 1 January 1818 begins as follows:

> *As I cannot have the good fortune on this day to offer Your Serene Highness my most respectful congratulations on the new year in person, I beg gracious permission at least to do so in writing, and at the same time to commend myself to the continuance of Your favor.*

It again concerned reading matter for Princess Sophie, who had sent books back to him, "only I did not receive the box with the books until yesterday evening, so I was not in a position to obtain others in exchange on this day, since the library is closed on the bridge days between holidays."

Besides politics and literature, health was almost always a topic as well (particular remedies were requested), on one occasion also "health porcelain," which was unfortunately not available in Breslau. Kahlert asked whether he might order the available "blue porcelain." Also mentioned are spices and baked goods from "Redlich" in Breslau, which he sent to him.
