### 10.3 An Unnamed Prussian Officer (1831)

There are then also quite different voices to be heard:

> *Above all we must remember the noble, truly princely bearing and that proud composure of the warrior which never left him for a single moment - The only question that remained was: whether he was not equal to the greater upheavals of the later age, or whether he merely succumbed to circumstances, since fortune had abandoned him.
