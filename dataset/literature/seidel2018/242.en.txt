# Seidel (2018), p. 242
# translation by Claude

> *that he be compelled to make good the same, as well as to pay the sums still outstanding from him. Likewise I have*

- *sixth: given a power of attorney to collect from v. Riednewiecki the outstanding lease payments and to seek execution against him.*
- *seventh: I have issued a power of attorney that all unauthorized negotiations and sales of land by Henrichs be annulled forthwith, and that the purchasers, unless they consent to payment at the treasury, be evicted with recourse against Henrichs, [...]. He has, without being authorized, given away portions of the land for nothing and has himself thereby appropriated a little estate out of our estates and erected the buildings from lordly timber and bricks.*
- *eighth: I have given power of attorney for the collection of all outstanding purchase moneys, which are very considerable.*
- *ninth: a power of attorney is issued to compel the [Oleniher] hereditary leaseholders to pay the inclusion of the [...] outstanding entrance moneys amounting to 23,000 Rth, or else be evicted along with their dependents, so that the land and buildings may be otherwise [...].*
- *tenth: a power of attorney given to P. Habich for the pursuit of all boundary lawsuits, and the necessary instructions given to him.*
- *eleventh: that Habich has been given a power of attorney so that he may appear in Your Serene Highness's name at the [...] diet at Warsaw and in all places, in order to secure His Highness's rights of possession and property in his holdings, all the more so as every estate owner must now appear entirely in person, reside upon his estates, or else forfeit his possessions.*
