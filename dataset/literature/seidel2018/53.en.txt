## 5 Family Letters from the Archive as Sources

### 5.1 Preliminary Remark

A brief note on the letters as a whole: the Neuenstein archive contains numerous letters written by FL and Marianne to members of the family. The majority of those composed by FL are addressed to the firstborn son August, in the period between 1798 and 1817.

What is found in an archive is often a mixture of chance and the documentary interest of the persons concerned, or of the archivists themselves. In our case there is the peculiarity that "occupation-related" documents of FL, the high-ranking military officer, were sorted out and taken over by the Prussian state after his death in Upper Silesia. Occasionally there are copies or preliminary drafts of these in the archives of the Kalliope network (a union catalogue of German archival finding aids) that could be used. – On the whole, however, the state of the sources is quite excellent and also extensive. A picture of the two persons, FL and Marianne, can indeed be gained from the letters. (I hope, in any case, that readers of my selection will find this to be so.) Where a letter yields quite essential elements for the outward progress of the biography, the information has been integrated into the running text. The selected "family letters" are reproduced in five bundles (Chapters Five, Eight, 17, and 19), with attention paid to their chronological context.
