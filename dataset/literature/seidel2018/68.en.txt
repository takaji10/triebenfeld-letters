# seidel2018  68  en
# Seidel (2018), p. 68
# translation by Claude

> *enduring residence town remains perceptible to the good people of Ingelfingen. [...] I continue to bear my name of Ingelfingen, since it is under this name that I have become particularly well known in the world.*[^45]

In substance he neither wishes nor, presumably, is able to reverse the matter; it remains at "affording security, [...] preserving prosperity, [...] and also improving it."

For the former residence, the facts remain: everything will now proceed from Öhringen. On one point the Prince can meet the wishes of the people of Ingelfingen. In their reply they had asked that the Prince release the rooms in the town hall that Count Christian Kraft had occupied when he moved to Ingelfingen (about 100 years earlier). These rooms, they said, were needed for the many tasks now to be handled in the town hall. FL agrees to the repurchase at the price then paid, 380 florins.

In the summer of 1806 the moment came when the obligation to take over Öhringen was solemnly carried out before the public. His son August had fetched him from Gera, and together they traveled from Ingelfingen via Künzelsau to Öhringen. There the reception had been festively prepared. FL received the key to the town from the magistrate.

Only a short time later, everything changed. He had his firstborn, August, declared of age and transferred the princely title to him. The latter was carried out on 23 August. He thereby resigned as sovereign lord. The dissolution of the small states called "mediatization," through the "Confederation of the Rhine Act" dated 12 July 1806, then also affected Hohenlohe. Hereditary Prince August had already foreseen this in January 1806: "My opinion is that sooner or later it will certainly happen. — If only lasting peace could be bought for our poor German fatherland through the abolition of the small states!"[^46] How reasonable that sounds! — Incidentally: along with the princely title, August also took over his father's considerable household debts and private liabilities!
