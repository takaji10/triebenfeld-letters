## List of Illustrations

For many of the illustrations, which I largely wished to take over from the cited literature, the image rights are held by the House of Hohenlohe, represented by Kraft, Prince of Hohenlohe-Öhringen. Upon my request I received permission for reproduction without hesitation – thank you! The same holds for the HZAN itself, for which its director, Dr. Schludi, granted the corresponding permission. The captions to the paintings contain only partial information on the painter and date – these could not be reliably ascertained. All sources of illustrations are listed here with reference to the page numbers in the book:
