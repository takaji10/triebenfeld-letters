### 11.5 On FL's Activities During His Time in Upper Silesia

His primary residence there was of course Breslau, with the summer house in Alt-Scheitnig. There FL had worked as Governor, as already described.

After 1806, as a prisoner in Öhringen, he had only limited opportunities to supervise or control his Upper Silesian estates, which he had not, after all, lost. He must therefore have had confidants "on the ground" there. In 1809 he went there again, more precisely: to Slaventzitz.

Here too, requests and information reached him from Öhringen, for instance through Privy Councillor Hermann[^91] — why, exactly? FL was, after all, responsible for nothing there. The Senior Collegii of the Hohenlohes was the Prince of Kirchberg. Nevertheless, the matters often concerned money, but also the road-building (!) from Künzelsau toward Mergentheim, and occasionally family news was included as well. The conditions in Hohenlohe therefore did not remain hidden from him.
