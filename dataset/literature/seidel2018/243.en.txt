*I have had all this taken down at law through the Justice-Burgomaster Scheuch [...], so as to begin this my career quite brilliantly; and since, by virtue of the obligation of 250,000 Rth, I have a right among all the creditors to have a say in the matter, so [...] this will surely command respect — and the desired outcome be brought about. In the meantime, Your Serene Highness shall, at my direction, receive faithfully from time to time reports on the progress of the affair. In deepest reverence I remain Your Most Serene Highness's most faithfully obedient servant [signature Triebenfeld]*
