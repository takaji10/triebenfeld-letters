# seidel2018  180  en
# Seidel (2018), p. 180
# translation by Claude

> *little trio warmly for me and commend me most kindly to their mother – I learned of the danger in which she found herself – hers and your two [children] besides, my good August – just as I certainly feel with you, deeply, faithfully and honestly, your joys and everything that concerns you – distance will never weaken the devotion which a mother who loves you inexpressibly has dedicated to you forever [...] the moments of happiness were only too short for me – and those of separation and pain followed only too closely upon one another – moreover, 'on m'a fait mourir a petit feu' – for first the parting from my so dearly beloved Adele – then the separation in Cassel from Auguste – but especially from my Emilie, your Albert, whom I also love infinitely – and from the dear children – moved me more than I can describe, – I remained there another day and a half, whose finding again did my heart indescribable good: His outward appearance, which must please less biased eyes than those of a mother – united with the heartfelt feeling, with the great love and attachment which he shows me at once and too vividly for him in my heart – so that the separation from him was not very painful to me – although the hope of knowing him closer to me in future, seeing at last fulfilled a wish ever dear to my heart, ought to have eased the parting for me. – Now I am back here again – everything bleak and empty, I might say horribly empty in this house where but a few days ago only glad, happy hours passed for me in the circle of my loved ones – I remain until Monday in this solitude, because I must await the post. Then I hasten by way of Hanover home, where I await with longing letters and news from you, my beloved ones! I almost forgot to tell you that I saw Mr. Gustaw in Doberan a few days after he left you in Canstatt – I rejoiced doubly at this because he told me of you and your Louise. His change I noticed less, because I had seen him more often*
