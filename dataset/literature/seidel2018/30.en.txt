# Seidel (2018), p. 30
# translation by Claude

> *to be taken, it has therefore been humbly requested herein.*

On closer examination it emerges here that the claim was valid and that no grounds were found for discontinuing the payment. The "Sillerin" herself is to produce documentary proof. This is presumably not possible for her – that is sometimes how reluctant authorities proceed. August accordingly notes on the first page of the statement:

> *In the matter of Caroline Siller's affair, I have consulted with my Lord Father and have been authorized by him to repeat here in writing his repeatedly made verbal declaration, namely: that if the said Caroline should produce the consent of Prince Friedrich Carl to the deduction from his annual apanage, the Court Chamber will resume payment of the said pension of 200 florins to her.*

At any rate, he has discussed it with FL. That this was possible was almost a matter of chance: as already noted, FL had only very recently arrived on the scene. Whether the written consent of Prince Friedrich Karl ever actually existed is another question.
