## Acknowledgements

I am especially indebted, in the course of this research, to Mr. Wörner (a member of staff at the HZAN), who showed great interest and became my local point of contact; he contributed to the scope of the book with several finds of his own.

The two translations of letters written in French were kindly made by Monika Kautenburger and Monika Radermacher. Dr. Litz of the Ulm City Archive assisted in deciphering individual words in the manuscripts — even the two of us together could not decipher everything. Herrmann Schumm gave invaluable comments on a first rough draft of the manuscript. My thanks go to all of them, and I include Monika Scheller, who for many months endured my devotion to this subject.

The publisher, Dr. Ulrich Klemm of Klemm und Oelschläger Verlag (Ulm), was kind enough to take this book into his programme as well. Johannes Klemm edited the manuscript.

Hans-Joachim Seidel
