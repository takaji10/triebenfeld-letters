## 19 Letters That Still Reached FL Before His Death (1818)

Only a few of these selected letters referred to a serious illness, present or past. Of particular interest are those from his nephew Eduard and his sons Alexander and Adolph, since few testimonies from them survive. They report on their everyday lives.

### 19.1 Sender: Frederick William III

King Frederick William III responded to the customary New Year's greeting, but also included an additional personal message; some are addressed to FL, since his birthday is, or had been, on 31 January. For several years, as may be inferred from the King's responses, FL had regularly sent greetings to Frederick William at the turn of the year, which were likewise reciprocated. In 1818 this looked as follows (no salutation):

> *From Your Serene Highness's letter of the 1st of this month, I have had the pleasure of learning of the good wishes which you express for me on the occasion of the new year's beginning. I recognize therein your devoted and loyal sentiments, and it shall give me joy if the future also proves fortunate for you. With regard to the appointment of your son, I refer Myself and my letter of [...], which will by now already have reached Your Serene Highness.*  
> *Berlin, 16 January 1818*  
> *Frederick William*[^137]

The remark "the appointment of your son" refers to Alexander, who, after his military training in Stuttgart, had transferred to Prussia.
