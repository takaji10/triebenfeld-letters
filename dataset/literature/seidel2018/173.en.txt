# seidel2018  173  en
# Seidel (2018), p. 173
# translation by Claude

This letter was furnished by its addressee, Cosmar, on 24 July 1811, with an addition, and forwarded to "Your Serene Highness" August.

What can we glean from this letter?

- On the one hand, there was a meeting at Marianne's residence in Berlin with August, Marianne's husband Sacken, and Cosmar, at which a quarrel evidently arose between Sacken and August; the "misunderstanding" in Marianne's own phrasing. It was probably about the will and financial matters.
- Von Sacken, in Marianne's view, wished to draw somewhat closer to August.
- August had an appointment with the King, presumably that of Württemberg.
- August's situation is "delicat" and complicated with regard to consideration for his father.
- At least in the remark that she had previously regarded the "relationship in which August stands" as too good, one must think of the economic situation.
- The true reason for the quarrel must have been the utterance of something she did not wish to have said, and which probably had to do with the effects of the will.
- Her greatest fear is that she might lose August's love.

The background to the fear of difficult circumstances for August could be that, around this time, following the incorporation of Hohenlohe, the Kingdom of Württemberg "took into administration" the local "Kameralverwaltung" (financial administration) of Hohenlohe-Öhringen,[^134] presumably on account of the burden of debt that August had had to take on there.
