### 6. Transcribed list of measures initiated or commissioned (from Triebenfeld in a letter to FL of 7 March 1809, which he sent him from Kontopp):

> *Most Serene Prince, most gracious Prince and Lord*  
> *I have had here the Justice-Burgomaster Hawich of Zagorow, who is engaged as an actual Ducal Warsaw Notary [...], together with the Chief Inspector Heitzer, and with these men have negotiated everything that is to Your Serene Highness's benefit [...] and for this purpose issued the special power of attorney at law. I have accordingly*

- *first: pressed for the dismissal of [D.] regarding the Trabeziner estates and given power and instruction thereto*
- *second. Given power for the complete lifting of the sequestration and drafted the initiative, all the more so as a 3-year general [indult] has been granted by the King of Saxony, and since this depends on the absolute payment of the interest; so I have*
- *third: given P. Habich power to collect the interest, and to pay it to the creditors who already have legally valid [judgments] before them, against receipt, but to remit the remainder to Your Serene Highness. I have also*
- *fourth: given a power of attorney to sue the Jew Oppenheimer et Wolff in Kalisch, and to press at once for the cancellation of the several sums entered in excess of 72,000 Rth, concerning which a lawsuit is already pending in Berlin.*
- *fifth: I have issued a power of attorney that the swindler Henrichs, who must let himself be [...] as sequestrator, be dismissed forthwith and compelled to render account and [...], that Hawich in turn draw up the objections against him, and*
