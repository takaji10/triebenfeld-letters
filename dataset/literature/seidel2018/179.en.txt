Another letter to August of 17 October 1817:

> *My dearly beloved August!*  
> *With the most heartfelt feelings of love and gratitude I bring you the expression of the same for your loving letter, and your kind remembrance of my birthday. The manner in which this was celebrated this year bound me, indeed, with beautiful ties to life and let me find in it a great worth never before found. – Good August – only you were missing, for the days to have been lived through in perfect happiness. – Emilie will however testify to you how your remembrance and the heartfelt wish for your presence were ever close to us – my longing to see you again and to introduce Angelika and Auguste to you is indescribably great – A pure feeling, which is founded, indeed, more on trust in your love than on an excess of modesty, lets me hope that you too would share our joy in our midst. – I tell you nothing, my good August! of our Baroness here in Bovden, nothing of my feeling upon seeing my children again; Emilie will surely take charge of the [organization] of the celebration, and the [...] cannot be described in words. So let your own heart paint for you this, for me, so beautiful, so blessed moment: – Thank God that Emilie's health permitted her to enjoy it as well – her children are my whole passion, and truly dear, lovable little creatures. Emilie made me the gift of the beautiful painting of your Mathilde, who through this magnetism a distance has already stolen herself so into my heart that I cannot at all detach myself from the lovely little figure – I take you at your word, good August – and beg you indeed to keep your promise, and to send me still the paintings of the two brothers of this little angel; – kiss this dear*
