*Thirdly*: further children of FL, raised by the "foster mother" (Pflanzmutter) Beata Kreiselmayer. – This story chronologically belongs at the end of the book, since it was only brought before the House of Hohenlohe-Öhringen after FL's death in Upper Silesia in 1818; on account of its substantive connection it is included here.

The document dates from September 1818, according to which the aforementioned woman, bearing the memorable designation "Pflanzmutter" (i.e., a foster mother who raises children entrusted to her), appears before the Court Chamber in Öhringen with a girl now 17 years old. She now comes, as expressly noted, from the Rothenburg region. She reports having raised three children of His Serene Highness FL, besides the daughter two sons, who (at some unspecified earlier point) had already been handed over to the Cadet House in Berlin. The mother of the girl, she says, had been "a distinguished lady whose name she could no longer recall." This woman had at first also occasionally visited the child. His Serene Highness, as the father, had sent money; she names – without further particulars – six ducats. She was also supposed to come to Slaventzitz to settle further matters. Now she had learned with dismay of his death. The girl now needed protection and provision, and lodging too would have to be purchased.

Her statements are not doubted, and the petition is evidently treated seriously. This matter must again be dealt with
