# Seidel (2018), p. 177
# translation by Claude

> [sic] and of your continuing love for me. – Through your sisters I received news of you, and followed you with faithful remembrance and heartfelt sympathy on all your ways; I rejoiced heartily at your good relations with the King of W. – and even more at the joy of the reunion in the [home] of your own. – on the beautiful moment that led you into the arms of your Louise and your angel children – I wish you happiness and rejoice in your happiness, good August! – Forgive me my egotism, good dear August. – yet with every thought of reunion the longed-for moment also stands before me, of one day enjoying this same joy myself, and with it seeing the fulfillment of my most heartfelt wish confirmed – too vividly before my eyes for me not finally to seek to bring the reality nearer to myself – Good August! Would this not be possible on your return journey? – I await from you a sketch of a plan, the working-out of which would be my finest care and joy, especially if I might have the happiness of making the acquaintance of your Louise and your angel children. – How happy my two girls would feel to see you again, and what joy for me to be able to present them to you now. I enjoy the pleasure of seeing Adolph often. He pursues his Wilhelminian career with great zeal and punctuality, and thereby earns praise and esteem from his superiors – the Uhlan uniform suits him very well – and in that respect he cuts a very fine figure – to be sure his appointment as Lieutenant is not brilliant – but since the royal princes must begin with that – nothing more can be said about it; he will certainly not remain long in the subaltern ranks, and I know from his commanding officer himself that he will very soon be proposed for Cavalry Captain – meanwhile his existence here is so agreeable that it can well be borne with patience for a little while longer – he knows that I am writing to you, and charges me with many heartfelt greetings to you – I am to convey to you very many compliments
