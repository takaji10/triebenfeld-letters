# Seidel (2018), p. 254
# translation by Claude

Additional research was carried out via the Kalliope network of German-language archives. Here, under the keyword "Friedrich Ludwig zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen", a number of further items came to light. Upon corresponding request I received the documents by post; almost all of them I was able to use. Thanks are due to the following institutions:

**Kantonsbibliothek St. Gallen Vadiana (Robert Alther Collection):**
- 27 July 1807, letter from FL, whose addressee must be Glenck.

**Saxon State Library Dresden (letters from FL):**
- 25 August 1800, addressed to Frederick William III, Mscr. Dresden, U.207.
- 20 November 1805 (Erfurt), to General Tauentzien, Mscr. Dresden n. 308.
- 22 November 1805 (Erfurt), to C.A. Böttiger, Mscr. Dresden 37.4 vol. 51, 123a.

**Leipzig University Library (letters from FL):**
- 18 January 1792 (Wallau), Slg. Nebauer/H/H-K/H281.
- 10 June 1795 (Frankfurt), to the Landgrave of Hesse, as above /H282.
- 17 July 1798 (Ingelfingen), Slg. Kestner/II/A/III 714/Nr. 1 Mappe 714 Bl.1.

**Freies Deutsches Hochstift Frankfurt:**
- 14 February 1795 (Groß-Gerau), letter to Charles Augustus of Saxe-Weimar-Eisenach.
- 6 February 1795, letter from Frederick William II to FL.
- 6 February 1795 (Berlin), letter from Zastrow to FL, Sign. 24854.

**Stadtgeschichtliches Museum Leipzig:**
- 17 June 1787, to Adam Oeser, Sign. A/2014/1440.
