# Seidel (2018), p. 189
# translation by Claude

> *Theoretical Philosophy with Prof. Sigwart, who was formerly tutor to Gustav von Langenburg. His delivery is somewhat monotonous, but otherwise what he presents is excellent. Herr von [Forstner] has now also been appointed here as a professor, after a new cameralistic faculty was opened, in which he too managed to find a post. He has already begun lecturing and has met with complete approval, only his manner of expression is far too high-flown. The Catholic faculty is now also here, under the Landvogt of Ellwangen, [Herr] von Heller, who was formerly in Öhringen; his son is now also studying here and sometimes comes to celebrate with us. The son of Privy Councillor von Braun is likewise here. Yesterday I received a letter from Adele, who wrote to me that Louise in Öhringen is very ill, which grieves me greatly for August's sake, who was just at Fürstenau for the wedding of Sister Albrecht's. Would you have the graciousness to pass on the enclosed little letter to my aunt; and so, repeating my wishes for your welfare, I remain, with reverence, your obedient [nephew/Eduard von Hohenlohe[^139]*

One could verify this at the Archive of the University of Tübingen. Riding from 9 to 10 o'clock! A career from tutor in Langenburg (Prof. Sigwart) to professor of philosophy seems noteworthy. Eduard had contact with the studying sons of court officials. The mention of an illness of Louise, August's wife, attests to how close the family exchange of news was. August wrote more precisely about this in his (last) letter to his father; see p. 192f.
