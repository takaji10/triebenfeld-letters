### 19.3 Letter from His Nephew Eduard

Eduard von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen (1801–1830) was the son of Georg von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen (1757–1803), FL's younger brother. Eduard had lost his parents early; his mother, née Baroness von Klüchzner, also died in 1807. He then grew up in Öhringen. A letter from the ten-year-old, in French, to "Mon très cher Oncle," written in pen on lined paper in a careful hand, has survived. The uncle was already living in Slaventzitz at that time, but the boy knew of him and this was not the first time he had written — chiefly about his progress in French. There must have been at least an occasional correspondence, for FL had presumably asked him at the end of 1817 to report on his studies.
