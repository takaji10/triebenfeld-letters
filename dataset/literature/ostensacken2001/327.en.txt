Appendix 10

Clarification of the debt relations of the daughter Marianne countess of Osten-Sacken

Beside the debt obligations that Marianne countess of Osten-Sacken had signed for her first husband, the prince Friedrich Ludwig zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, she had also, in her second marriage with the count Friedrich Ludwig von der Osten-Sacken, got into a debt situation which she could hardly still manage without her mother's help. Her mother therefore attempted to reach a settlement with the creditors. For this purpose she had charged her confidant, the medical councillor Cosmar, to travel to Güstrow, to examine the situation there. The agreements reached there were taken up into her codicil of 6 November 1807:[^p327-8]

Since my lady daughter, the countess von Sacken, is so deeply entangled in debts that the opening of bankruptcy over her fortune is to be feared, I hereby charge the chief medical councillor Cosmar to betake himself to Güstrow and to regulate the debt relation of my lady daughter and her husband, the count von Sacken, as well as is possible, to come to an agreement with their creditors over their satisfaction resp. treatment according to his best judgement and conscientiousness, to examine and fix the amount of their claims, to determine whether they are satisfied entirely and completely or whether only a part is paid and in what terms the creditors are to be discharged.
