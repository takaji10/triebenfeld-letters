Despite the strenuous efforts of Minister Hoym, the collection of the fines proceeded very sluggishly; in addition, some had to be partly remitted and a few even entirely. By 1 June 1798 a total of 1,707,168 Polish zloty, that is 284,528 thalers, had been collected from the whole of South Prussia[^492]. In all, 1,195,506 Polish zloty, that is 199,251 thalers, flowed into the Prussian treasuries in the Poznań department. As appears from numerous receipts found in the files of the State Archive in Poznań[^493], those sentenced paid the fine imposed on them in instalments. The largest sums, that is, those amounting to 2,000 thalers and more, were paid by: Bieńkowski Sebastian — 5,000 thalers, the heirs of Bolesza — 8,000 thalers, Gliszczyński Antoni — 7,000 t (remitted — 1,000 t); Gurowski Władysław — 6,000 t; Gorzeński Nicefor — 4,000 t (remitted — 6,000 t); Jaraczewski Hieronim — 3,000 t; Koszutski Józef — 4,500 t (remitted — 1,500 t), Gen.
