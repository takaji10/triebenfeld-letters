# Muszyńska-Zygmańska (1947), p. 54
# translation by Claude (machine translation, checked and corrected in a bilingual QA pass, 2026)

It is the report of Lieutenant v. Milkau sent to General Manstein at Leszno [^142]. Lt. Milkau set out, by order of the commandant of Poznań, Diether, on 10 August into the Kalisz region with half a depot battalion from Frankenberg's regiment and 1 cannon, to cover the magazines at Koło and Konin. On 23 August it was reported to him at Koło that the insurgents, whose gathering had long been rumoured, were planning a simultaneous attack on Koło and Konin with a force of 800 men. From Konin, on the following day, Lt. v. Prittwitz, who was stationed there as garrison, informed him that the insurgents had likewise gathered in large numbers in the neighbourhood of Miłosław and intended on the next day to go to Słupca with a detachment of 400 armed men, as a result of which communication with Poznań would be cut. Shortly before that he had also received news from Kalisz of the appearance of insurgents at the gates of that town and of their firing on the guard. On 25 August Lt. Milkau arrived at Konin to cover the considerable magazine in that town. He learned then that a detachment of 300-400 insurgents had entered Stawiszyn[^143]. He remained at readiness through the nights, and his pickets were constantly alarmed. On 27 August the outposts of the insurgents appeared before Konin at a distance of half a mile. Milkau sent a patrol against them, but the insurgents attacked it. They killed 1 Prussian and took two prisoner. On the same day a Prussian detachment of 23 men escorting 25 prisoners arrived at Konin from Łęczyca. Insurgents attacked it on the way, between Koło and Konin, but achieved no success. On 29 August 200 armed men burst into the suburb of Konin and took the cash box from the salt depot there.
