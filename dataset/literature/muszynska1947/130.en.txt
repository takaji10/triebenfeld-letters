# Muszyńska-Zygmańska (1947), p. 130
# translation by Claude (machine translation, checked and corrected in a bilingual QA pass, 2026)

Insurgents also still held out in the neighbourhood of Gniezno. According to the *Südpreussische Zeitung*[^409] small detachments of them were still roaming there at the end of November. On the 10th of that month a single insurgent, armed with 4 pistols and a broadsword, fell between Trzemeszno and Wilatowo[^410] upon two couriers dispatched from Gniezno and destroyed them completely[^411].

Throughout November the insurgents were active around Wieluń and Sieradz, despite the defeat that Colonel Dossow had inflicted on them there at the beginning of that month, and also in the Kalisz region, reaching as far as Częstochowa and Rawa. Their bands numbered for the most part 50 or 100 men. By day they hid in the forests, and by night they left their hiding places and carried out raids to the detriment of the Prussian government.

In the Sieradz region especially their "escapades" caused much trouble. On the night of 8 to 9 November the insurgents burst into the customs office at Panke(?) in the Krzepice district[^412] and took the cash box from it. They were commanded by Major Pągowski and Captain of Cavalry Karski. The Prussian lieutenant von Lüttwitz, hastening to help from Kłobuck[^413] with 20 cuirassiers of Görtz's regiment and as many infantrymen of Lichnowski's regiment, was attacked by an insurgent detachment consisting of 300 foot and 300 horse. Lüttwitz could not withstand such superiority and was taken prisoner together with 21 soldiers. On 9 November Generals Lipski and Stokowski took up position at Krzepice with the intention of gathering more insurgents and attacking the weakly garrisoned Częstochowa[^414]. It went no further than a plan, however. At about this time the insurgents came to Kłobuck, removed the Prussian emblems and soon departed. According to news of 23 November, given in the *Südpreussische Zeitung*, there stood near Wieluń and in the Ostrzeszów district 2,000 insurgents, supplied with two guns.
