## Kopojno [Słownik geograficzny, t. IV, p. 382]

Village (on the Czarna Struga) and folwark (on the Warta); gm. Emilienheim, par. Zagórów; 20 versts from Słupca. Village 65 houses / 791 inhabitants, folwark 9 / 102; in 1827 52 / 480
