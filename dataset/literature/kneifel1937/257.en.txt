# Kneifel (1937), p. 257
# translation by Claude

Kolonia. At Dolany the Evangelicals bury their dead in the Roman Catholic cemetery there, namely in a special part of it assigned to them even before the World War of 1914/18.

Schools.

Zagórów. General elementary school with 7 classes, about 20 evangelical children. Instruction in religion is given by the local pastor. — In 1835 August Gabler was teacher at the evangelical school in Zagórów.

Łazińsk, one-class, German language of instruction, over 160 children; teacher Gustav Adolf Mielke.

Anielewo, one-class, German language of instruction, with 60–70 children; teacher Bruno Mielke.

Olchowo, one-class, German language of instruction, over 40 children. Teacher Ferdinand Homt.

Michalinów gm. Oleśnica, one-class, German language of instruction, about 60 children, teacher Julius Berendt.

Michalinów gm. Trąbczyn, one-class, German language of instruction, about 70 children, teacher Friedrich Klingbeil, since 1923.

Wrąbczynkowska Kolonia, German language of instruction, about 70 children. Teacher A. Bauer, since 1928.

Societies.

Mixed singing choir at Zagórów; about 30 singers. Director cantor Ferdinand Homt.

Youth league Zagórów, about 40 members, since 1927. Director the local pastor.

Children's service, only in the summer half of the year, about 50 children, no group system.

Periodicals.

1930. "Friedensbote" 20 copies; "Wach auf" 100 copies; "Zwiastun Ewang." 1 copy. — 1936 "Friedensbote" 6 copies; "Wach auf" 100 copies; "Jugendfreude" 5 copies; "Jugendsieg" 8 copies; "Neues Leben" 5 copies.

Sects. 1 New-Apostolic family.

Converts.

In the period 1925–1930, 1 person (from the Greek Orthodox church).

Economic matters.

Number of contributors 1930: 560; 1935: 550.

Size of the budget 1930: 10,200 zlotys; 1935: 7480 zlotys.

Amount of the church contribution 1930: 1 zloty per Morgen; 1935: 50 gr. and 3 zlotys family money.

Number of Morgen in the congregation: 6500.

Salaries 1930. Pastor 500 zlotys monthly, 1934: 400 zlotys.

Cantor 140 zlotys monthly, 1934: 110 zlotys.
