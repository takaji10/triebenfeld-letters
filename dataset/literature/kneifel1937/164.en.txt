To ascertain this was all the more necessary, since through this uncertainty the congregation was bewildered. Superintendent Ernst Wilhelm Drechsler of Rogasen warned the Evangelicals in and about Slesin, in the letter of March 31, 1810, "not to let themselves be led astray by the candidate Radke to Slesin nor to be held back from their lawful parish (Witkowo). The candidate has no authority to attach parishes and equally little authority to baptize and to marry or even to administer the sacra; since he has done this hitherto without all permission of the secular and spiritual superiors, he will be called to account therefor . . ." On August 30, 1810 there sojourned at Slesin Superintendent Ephraim Gottfried Stechebahr of Posen, who recorded in protocol that without the evangelical colonies about Slesin the maintenance of the evangelical-Lutheran preacher at Witkowo could not be met. Since Pastor Bothe of Witkowo had likewise raised objection to the establishment of the Slesin congregation, the prefect of the Posen department called upon him on March 13, 1811 to present documents or copies of them, in order to prove that the evangelical villages in the Slesin region were legally attached to the parish of Witkowo. He was unable to furnish this proof. In the same year Pastor August Bernhard Christian Drechsler came to Slesin, son of Sup. Drechsler of Rogasen4). From his period of office date the "Tauf-Buch der Sleszyner Evangelischen Parochie, angefangen den 1. April 1811" (Baptismal book of the Sleszin evangelical parish, begun April 1, 1811), and the "Todten-Kirchenbuch der Evangelischen Gemeine zu Slesin" (Death register of the evangelical congregation of Slesin), which are preserved in the Konin parish archive. As the "Tauf-Buch" reports, many Evangelicals had their churchly acts performed also in the neighboring Roman Catholic congregations of Lubstów, Kazimierz, Skulsk and Wilczyn. "The disorder — so it further reports — was very great, because the Slesin parish was not yet regulated". This never took place either.

Since Pastor Bothe, notwithstanding the calling of P. Drechsler to Slesin, continued to visit individual outlying localities, the provisional consistory U. A. C. of the Posen department instructed him to desist from this in the future. In the year 1815 Pastor Drechsler left Slesin and went to Obornik, probably moved thereto by the changed conditions of the frontier. After his departure Pastor Karl Gottfried Röhl — Pastor Bothe's successor at Witkowo — received from Walichnowski, the owner of Slesin, the permission to visit this place, as also Szyszyner-Hol. and other colonies.
