# Kneifel (1937), p. 253
# translation by Claude

On August 22, 1877 Pastor Teichmann, superintendent of the Kalisch diocese, visited Zagórów. In the visitation protocol he noted that the parish possessed only 1 elementary school with 60 children at Michalinów and 7 cantorate schools with approximately 230 pupils. He mentioned also that in the congregation the Neu-Strelitz catechism was to be introduced. Of the parish buildings, which Sup. Teichmann inspected, he let himself say only that they were in an unwelcome state.

The foundation stone of the building of the evangelical church at Zagórów was laid on May 8, 1878 by the local pastor Karl Eduard Erdmann. For the building the congregation members were to raise within 4 years a sum of 8000 rubles, while 3000 rubles, as already mentioned, were granted by the government. Members of the building committee were: Alexander Arndt, Wilhelm Schmechel, Wilhelm Münch, Wilhelm Kuck, Martin Ziegeldorf and Emanuel Munk; of the church council (elected on July 5, 1882): Christian Schultz, Karl Pfeifer, Heinrich Zelmer, Alexander Arndt and Johann Schütz. The technical supervision of the building was in the hands of the county building inspector Przanowski of Słupca. All the work at the building was undertaken by the master mason Gustav Jursch of Orlina Wielka for the fee of 3000 rubles. The town of Zagórów presented for the church building 110 trunks of building timber from the town forest. The building of the house of God cost 13,131 rubles 78 kopecks. In the late autumn of 1882 the church was already erected. On July 1, 1883 the church council ordered of the firm Schlag und Söhne in Schweidnitz an organ. The congregational meeting of June 29, 1884 resolved also to tax itself 25 kopecks per Morgen for the purchase of two bells. The consecration of the new church took place, according to oral tradition (the records report nothing more definite concerning it), on the first Advent Sunday 1882. In the beginning of October 1883 there took place the protocolary handing over of the church to the congregation.

Until his death († 1892) Pastor Karl Eduard Erdmann-Grodziec cared for the Zagórów congregation, which, mindful of his merits for the parish (the building of the church), honored his memory by the placing of a corresponding marble tablet in the house of God.

The administration of Zagórów the consistory then entrusted to Pastor Henkel of Konin, whereupon from the middle of July 1898 until the beginning of November 1903 Pastor Bernhard Erdmann, formerly vicar at Zgierz, labored here as settled shepherd of souls.

In the year 1890 the sheet-metal roof of the church and of the tower was torn off by the storm wind. The church council expended 760 rubles for the new roofing.
