# Kneifel (1937), p. 78
# translation by Claude

3. Rychwał (Reichwald), 6 evangelical children attend here the 7-class elementary school.

4. Zarzew, two-class, 19 evangelical, 86 Catholic. Two Catholic teachers.

5. Obory, two-class, head: Augustyniak (Catholic), second teacher Otto Schwalbe (evangelical).

Societies.

Singing society at Grodziec, mixed choir, 16–18 members.

Brass choirs: Grodziec, Wojciechowo, Gadow, Białobłoty, Orlina Wielka.

Evang.-Luth. youth league at Grodziec.

At the Sunday children's service at Grodziec 60–80 children take part.

Converts.

From 1924 to 1930: 3 persons.

Within the parish there belong to the sect represented here, the "Evangelische Gemeinschaft" (direction of the preacher Wecke-Vandsburg), 13 families, which are distributed over 6 villages. Further, there are still "Evangelische Christen" at Gadow, where until now 3 women and 1 man have been baptized.

Economic matters.

Contributors. 1879: 787; 1930: 1116; 1934:

Size of the budget. 1929: 12,500 zlotys;

Amount of the church contribution. In the vicinity of Grodziec 1930: 90 groschen per Morgen, in the cantorates 85 groschen;

Property of the congregation: 10.5 Morgen of land and 2 Morgen of meadow for the pastor; 1 Morgen of cantor's land.

Number of Morgen in the congregation: 12,000.

Salaries: 1930. Pastor: 700 zlotys monthly; cantor: 110 zlotys monthly; sexton: 55 zlotys monthly; congregational clerk 50 zlotys monthly: grave-treader 15 zlotys quarterly. 1935. Pastor: 450 zlotys per month, cantor: 130 zlotys; sexton: 45 zlotys.

Social structure.

Within the parish there are no evangelical estate owners; it consists chiefly of small farmers (with 7.5 Morgen of land each) and agricultural laborers, the so-called Sachsengänger (seasonal workers in Saxony).
