# Kneifel (1937), p. 180
# translation by Claude

Lipnica, cantor Leonhard Schmidt.

Brzozogaj, cantor Erwin Benoist.

In the village of Świętno, where there is no cantorate, the farmer Emil Primas officiates in place of the cantor.

Cemeteries.

Maślaki, Nowyświat, Karolkowo, Bolesławowo, Genowefa, Kamionki, Doły, Lipnica, Brzozogaj, Kochowo, Korwin, Czartówek.

Schools.

1858: 1. Maślaki (Butterholland), elementary school, teacher Karl Ludwig Fiedler.

2. Brzozogaj (Birkenholland), cantorate school, teacher Andreas Mikołajewski.

3. Borowiec, cantorate school, teacher Michael Huf7).

4. Genowefa, cantorate school, teacher Heinrich Fiedler.

5. Doły, cantorate school, teacher Christian Zander.

6. Stogi, cantorate school, teacher Gottlieb Witzke.

7. Skrzynka Wielka (Groß-Lade), cantorate school, teacher Ferdinand Gutsche.

8. Bolesławowo, cantorate school, teacher Christoph Möller.

9. Kochowo, cantorate school, teacher Karl Heinrich Fiedler.

10. Lipnica, cantorate school, teacher Friedrich Reich.

1935.

Schools with Polish as language of instruction, visited by evangelical children.

1. Dębowiec, one-class, mixed, 26 evangelical children, Catholic teacher. Instruction in religion evangelical given by cantor Greger-Maślaki.

2. Świętno, 11 evangelical children, Catholic teacher; no evangelical instruction in religion.

3. Wola Spławiecka, 40 children, Catholic teacher; instruction in religion given by cantor Greger.

4. Rostoka, 18 children, Catholic teacher; instruction in religion given by cantor Schinzel-Doły.

5. Doły, 25 children, Catholic teacher. Instruction in religion K. Schinzel-Doły.

6. Genowefa, 28 children, Catholic teacher; instruction in religion — cantor Alexander Kanwischer.

7. Lipnica, 20. children, Catholic teacher. Instruction in religion K. Schmidt.

8. Brzozogaj, 30 children, Catholic teacher. Instruction in religion cantor Erwin Benoist.

In the filial there is at present no evangelical teacher; in 1858 there were 10 evangelical teachers active here. — At Maślaki there exists no school any more. The schoolhouse of Maślaki the Evangelicals lost, according to oral tradition, through the fault of the teacher of that time.

[^p180-7]: 7) In Borowiec there dwells at present no evangelical farmer any more. earlier (1858) there was here even a cantorate school.

At Karólkowo the colonists emigrated.
