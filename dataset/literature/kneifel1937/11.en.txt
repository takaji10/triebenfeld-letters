The latter comprised the parishes Zduny, Adelnau, Ostrowo, Krotoszyn, Koźmin, Rosterschütz (Władysławów) and Kempen. As senior of the Kalisch church district Pastor Christoph Dienegott von Geißler of Zduny was called, while Pastor Hellwig of Rawitsch was assigned the oversight of the church district of Bojanowo as co-senior. After Pastor Geißler's death — "old and weak he died in the first days of October 1795 in the 73rd year of his life"[^p11-9] — on February 25, 1796 Pastor Paul Immanuel Matthiä became senior of the Kalisch church district. The year 1797 brought a "new departmental organization in church and school matters." Thereby the congregations of Adelnau, Ostrowo, Kempen, Pleschen, Kalisch, Stawiszyn, Grodziec and Rosterschütz were incorporated into the Kalisch church district. In 1798 the South Prussian consistory at Petrikau was transferred to Kalisch. "The consistory at Kalisch consists of the provincial governor, a governmental director, five governmental councilors and one consistorial councilor . . . With the Lutheran consistorial affairs the Lutheran school system also is joined . . . Moreover in every county an inspector is appointed, who is called the county senior"[^p11-10] Consistorial councilor of the Kalisch departmental consistory, which was subordinate to the Posen higher consistory, and inspector (superintendent) of the evangelical-Lutheran churches and schools, as well as first pastor of the Kalisch congregation, became Pastor Karl Friedrich Herzberg (†1812)[^p11-11] After him, in the archival records, Pastor Karl Michael Christian Sienell of Kalisch is repeatedly mentioned as Kalisch superintendent, "senior of the evangelical clergy" (†1820).

As early as 1807/8 Pastor Karl Diehl, the Reformed consistorial president in Warsaw, was striving for the union of the Evangelicals of both confessions in a common consistory. From him comes the "Project of a constitution for the evangelical general consistory of the Duchy of Warsaw." His endeavors met at first with success. "With fullest conviction Karl Herzberg, superintendent of the Lutheran church in the Kalisch department, declared himself, after consultation held, for the project, and did so at the same time also in the name of the preachers placed under his supervision."
