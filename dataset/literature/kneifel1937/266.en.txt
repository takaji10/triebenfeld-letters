# Kneifel (1937), p. 266
# translation by Claude

Cemeteries.

Pyzdry, Lissewo, Ciemierów, Wierzchy and Wrąbczynkowska Kolonia.

Children's service. At Peisern 20 children take part in the children's service.

Church periodicals 1934: "Friedensbote", about 10 copies.

Sects: none.

Economic matters.

Number of Morgen in the filial: 2500.

Amount of the church contribution 1935: 40 groschen per Morgen.

No special budget.

Salaries 1935: the pastor-administrator receives 100 zlotys monthly (counted within the budget of the Zagórów congregation). At the establishment of the filial there was fixed in 1842, for the visiting of the pastor, 90 rubles yearly. In 1873 it was 65 rubles (besides income for acts of office).

Numerical development of the filial.

The filial of Lissewo-Pyzdry and the parish congregation of Zagórów have a common civil register office. In the year 1896 there were baptized in the filial 52 children; 48 persons died. The Holy Supper was received by 738 filial members, of them 370 men and 368 women (among them also 4 home communions). In 1864 there were 709 communicants, 300 men and 409 women; confirmands 41, of them 11 boys and 30 girls. 1918: 222 male and 322 female communicants, in all 541 (also among them 1 home communion). 1933: the number of communicants counted jointly with Zagórów. 1934: 41 confirmands.

Social structure.

1 estate owner, Adolf Lauf, 10 hides of land; about 30 laborer families; the rest small farmers (on the average 15 Morgen).

Localities of the filial.

Town of Pyzdry, county Konin.

Gmina Dłusk, county Konin: Dłusk, Lissewo, Zamość, Pyzderska Kolonia (1st part), Łupnica, Ciemierów, Tarnowo, Janowo, Młynik (small mill), Tomicki Lasek, Wrąbczynkowska Kolonia, Białobrzeg, Adamierz, Tarnowiecki Lasek;

gmina Trąbczyn, county Konin: Koszelewska Łąka, Pyzderska Kolonia (2nd part);

gmina Szymanowice, county Konin: Wierzchy, Śnietnia, Ostrowska Kolonia.
