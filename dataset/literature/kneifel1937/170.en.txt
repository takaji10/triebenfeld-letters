# Kneifel (1937), p. 170
# translation by Claude

vent Sunday 1929 was joined with Pastor Badke's installation and with the centenary celebration of the congregation. At the same time there took place the consecration of a memorial tablet in honor of the late Pastor Karl Henkel.

On January 29, 1930 the congregation won the lawsuit against the gmina Gosławice concerning the cantorate property of Bielawy (7 Morgen of land, cantorate house and farm buildings). On the occasion of the 400-year Bible jubilee in 1934 an altar Bible was procured "by a collection."
