1888: Karl Fibich, Ernst Paul Michel, Heinrich Arndt, Ferdinand Milbradt, August Petschke, Christian Zarse.

1896: Ernst Paul Michel, Ferdinand Milbradt, Heinrich Arndt, Christian Zarse, August Petschke.

1914: Adolf Laube, Konrad Maurin, Julius Milbradt, Friedrich Zarse, Ludwig Reymond, Friedrich Wilhelm Arnholz.

1925: Karl Kunde, Adolf Mikołajewski, Andreas Busse, Ludwig Fritz, H. Deckert, J. Laube, F. Milbradt, R. Schnitzer.

1930: Otto Meitzer, Hugo Zaremba, Ludwig Fritz, Adolf Mikołajewski, Hermann Hennig, Adolf Hein, Robert Wenckowski, Andreas Busse, Julius Polej, Julius Fiedler.

Churches: Konin.

Houses of prayer:

1. Węglewskie Holendry
2. Nowy Czarków

separate houses of prayer.

The other prayer halls, which are united with the schools, are claimed as property by the civil communes. In Węglewskie Holendry the land on which the house of prayer stands was bought in the year 1927. In Nowy Czarków it was built on the school land.

Cantorates.

1. Święcia;  5. Dąbrowa;
2. Węglewskie Holendry;  6. Nowy Czarków;
3. Bielawy;  7. Borowo;
4. Pontnowskie Holendry;  8. Brzezińskie Holendry.

Cemeteries.

1. Konin; 2. Święcia; 3. Węglewskie Holendry; 4. Bielawy; 5. Pontnowskie Holendry; 6. Brzezińskie Holendry; 7. Borowo; 8. Nowy Czarków; 9. Dąbrowa; 10. Lutnia; 11. Żdżary.

The cemetery at Żdżary is used jointly also by the evangelical-Reformed congregation of Żychlin.

Schools with German as language of instruction.

Węglewskie Holendry, 70–80 children. Teacher Adam Hein, since 1925/6; in 1936 the teacher was Alwin Kufeldt. The only German-evangelical school in the commune.

Schools with Polish as language of instruction.

Święcia. Teacher Alexander Batke, about 70 evangelical children (second teacher Catholic).

Bielawy. Cantor Robert Baumgart, about 40 evangelical children.
