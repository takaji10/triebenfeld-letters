On August 21, 1840 the general consistory at Warsaw accordingly announced to the gubernial government at Kalisch that "the boundaries of the Kleczew filial must extend over all those localities which in their situation lie nearer to Kleczew than to Sompolno, Babiak, Konin, Koło, Grodziec, Pyzdry and Zagórów".

Against the choice of Kleczew as filial town Walichnowski, the owner of Slesin, turned. In a letter of the year 1840 to Pastor Hinz at Konin he reported that certain "eldest ones from the evangelical villages" had petitioned him, with reference to the resolutions of the former Prussian government, to build a church at Slesin, offering him at the same time "their help or voluntary contributions". On October 27, 1840 Walichnowski wrote to the general consistory that the establishment of the filial, "in consideration of the convenience of the colonists," would be more suitable at Slesin than at Kleczew. He declared also that he wished to present the whole building material for the building of the house of prayer. In consequence the consistory requested Sup. Modl on December 30 of that year for an opinion in the matter. Already on January 16, 1841 Sup. Modl announced to the consistory that neither Kleczew nor Slesin, but Maślaki alone, was suitable as filial town, because it possessed a numerous evangelical population and lay in the center of the evangelical colonies. In this sense the consistory also decided. On January 13, 1842 the first church council of the filial was elected, namely Peter Kitzmann, Martin Kintop, Gottlieb Kuk, Daniel Zorn and Johann Lehmann. In the same year the filial congregation of Maślaki was formed, whose number of souls at that time (according to the register of the evangelical population of January 13, 1842) amounted to 1584, divided into 305 families; of these 4 families, or 14 souls, were of the Reformed confession. The civil register office has existed here since 1843.

The school building at Maślaki, which had only one room with a chamber, "which served at the same time as dwelling room for the cantor and as room for the school youth," was in the year 1843 in a forlorn state. In 1851 it was rebuilt and enlarged. On July 2, 1850 Pastor Hinz installed as first elementary teacher of Maślaki Ludwig Fiedler, whom he "admonished in a fitting address to the zealous fulfillment of his duties and handed to him, in the presence of the congregation assembled in the little church there, the document of appointment". In 1868 the elementary school here was again transformed into a cantorate school. In the month of March 1870 the county master builder of Słupca and the lord of the manor of Kopydłowo, Julian Kitzmann, inspected the evangelical school, in order to reach a decision concerning its further use.
