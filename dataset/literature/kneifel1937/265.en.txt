# Kneifel (1937), p. 265
# translation by Claude

Houses of prayer.

At Peisern, Wierzchy and Wrąbczynkowska Kolonia separate houses of prayer. On the other hand at Lissewo and Ciemierów prayer hall and school in one building, but in separated rooms.

Cantorates.

Pyzdry 1909: cantor Friedrich Klingbeil; 1934: cantor Gustav Adam, teacher in the general elementary school with 7 classes at Pyzdry.

Lissewo, cantor-farmer Gustav Wolter of Ciemierów.

Ciemierów, cantor and teacher Heinrich Elgert.

Wierzchy, cantor and teacher Eduard Steinke.

Wrąbczynkowska Kolonia, cantor and teacher Bruno Bauer.

Schools.

Lissewo 1845: cantorate school, 31 evangelical children. Teacher and cantor Christian Wilhelm Friedrich (a native of Breslau), here already in 1839. The cantorate school endowed with 4 Morgen of arable land (Magdeburg measure).

1853: cantor and teacher Georg Ernst Adam. "The house of prayer, in which are the school and the teacher's dwelling, is of brick".

1857/8: teacher and cantor Adam Ernesti.

1868: " " " Julius Wiediger, 22 evangelical children.

1906: " " " Raphael Fiege.

1913: " " " Ernst Dekert.

The school came to an end a few years ago (teacheress Schielke). The evangelical children of Lissewo attend the school at Ciemierów.

Ciemierów 1843: cantor and teacher Ludwig Gleisner (born at Landsberg in Pr.), 48 evangelical children.

1854: cantor and teacher Karl Ferdinand Wiediger, 47 evangelical children. 1934: cantor and teacher Heinrich Elgert.

Wierzchy 1872: "The school and prayer house of timber, thatched with straw, in good condition". 1901 teacher and cantor Julius Wiediger (dismissed for dishonorable conduct). 1904: cantor and teacher Friedrich Klingbeil. 1934: teacher and cantor Eduard Steinke. Two-class mixed school; 50 evangelical and 20 Catholic school children.

Wrąbczynkowska Kolonia 1857/58: elementary school. Teacher and cantor Rudolf Haefke. 1867: still the same teacher, 31 children, of them 17 evangelical. The school exists no more.

Koszelewska Łąka 1858: cantorate school, teacher Michael Jeß. 1862: teacher and cantor Julius Wiediger, 15 evangelical children. 1870: teacher Karl Lehmann; 1871: teacher Heinrich Schwarzbach. The school came to an end because of the small number of children.

At Peisern 20 evangelical children attended in 1934 the general elementary school with 7 classes.
