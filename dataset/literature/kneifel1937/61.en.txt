# Kneifel (1937), p. 61
# translation by Claude

From December 28, 1902 Eduard Wende labored at Kalisch as vicar; after Pastor Haberkant's death, in the month of April 1906, he was called by the congregation to be its shepherd of souls and was installed by Gen.-Sup. Bursche on the first Whitsun holiday of that year, with the assistance of Pastor Manitius-Lodz.

The new local pastor introduced Passion services, increased the number of Polish services, and in the year 1903 held for the first time the confirmation in the Polish language for children who had no command of German. In 1906 the singing choir received its first officially confirmed society statute and was registered as the "Kalischer Gesangverein". On January 1, 1907 there took place the detachment of the cantorate congregation of Koźminek from Kalisch and its attachment to Prażuchy. The proximity of the cantorate of Prażuchy determined this measure. In the session of the church council on October 30, 1907 the thought arose of erecting an evangelical home for the aged at Kalisch. At the basis of the thought, "res sacra miser" (a sacred thing is the wretched man), lay the wish: "No Evangelical may beg." In spite of all hindrances (especially also concerning the building permit) the home for the aged could be erected, consecrated and handed over to its purpose "after two years and three days." "On that day (November 16, 1909) namely, in quiet seclusion from our town, on the so-called 'Piskorzewie,' it was consecrated, and there was opened the new place of the love of God and of one's neighbor . . ." The consecration was performed by Pastor Wende with the participation of Pastor Sachs of Stawiszyn[^p61-12] The building site for the home for the aged was presented by Mrs. Johanna Kunig. The costs of the building amounted to 26,550 rubles 68 kopecks and were met almost entirely by voluntary gifts. The congregation pledged itself to provide for the maintenance of the home. Thus, for example, in the year 1910 the costs of maintenance amounted to 1529 rubles 76 kopecks. In the year 1907 there arose at Kalisch the "Christliche Gemeinschaft" (Christian Community), whose founders are to be regarded as the manufacturer Hugo Müller-Kalisch and Ludwig Hausmann-Rokicie. In the period 1912–1914 the Community preacher Blümke labored here.

The congregational meeting that took place on December 1, 1912 resolved to sell the old parsonage for 20,000 rubles and to erect a new parsonage at the Niecałastr. No. 8, in the immediate vicinity of the beautiful park of Kalisch. This resolution soon became deed, for already on September 8, 1913 the new parsonage could be consecrated. Besides the local pastor there appeared for the celebration the pastors: Edmund Bursche-Łowicz, Buse-Grodziec, Sachs-Turek and Mergel-Sobiesęki. The total costs of the building (including the site) amounted to 81,750 rubles. For this purpose notable gifts came in, among others from the former manufacturer of Kalisch
