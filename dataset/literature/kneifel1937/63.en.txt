This will we never forget, you great son of this earth!"14) Since 1915 Pastor Wende has been superintendent of the Kalisch diocese. He is also the author of the Polish sermon collections "Sursum Corda" and "Lux in tenebris"15).

Since 1929 the "Christliche Gemeinschaft" (Christian Community) at Kalisch has had a hall of its own at the Marjańska-Str. No. 4, for the factory premises placed at its disposal by the firm of Müller proved much too small. Since the work of the Community required constant care, in 1929 the preacher Alfred Hoffert was appointed here. In the Community there exists a youth league of the E. C., a Sunday school and a singing choir16).

On Sunday, December 4, 1932, the Kalisch singing society celebrated its 75-year jubilee, which was joined with the consecration of the first banner. At that time the society counted 35 active and 90 passive members as well as 3 honorary members, namely Pastor Eduard Wende, Johann Gustav Hammermeister and Alexander Schaub. Chairmen of the singing society from its founding to the present were: Ernst Schröter, Wilhelm Feiler, Julius Betting, Julius Kunig, Adolf Stenzel, Alexander Schaub, Gustav Hempel. As conductors there were active in the course of the years: Ernst Schröter, Loy, Müller, Hammermeister, Padyga, Horky, Madlo, Ziebart, Hill, Cepelli, Bielecki, Emil Ismer. To the former chairman of the society Schaub belongs the merit of having acquired a plot of ground for the building of a society home. This plot is to be sold and a smaller, more suitable one acquired in its place. At present the premises of the singing society are at the Dobrzecka-Str. 21, in the Hempel hall.
