# Kneifel (1937), p. 65
# translation by Claude

Chancery officials ("Kirchenschreiber" [church scribes]).

Godlewski, 1836; Ernst Heinrich Schröter, 1846–1856; Michael Kitzmann, 1861; Karl Kleindienst, 1862; Oswald Bothner, 1863; Wilhelm Vogt, 1878–1880; Miss Wehr, since 1918.

Sextons.

Johann Benjamin Fischbach, 1796–1822; at present Walenty Cierpka, since 1922.

Church councils.

1795: Samuel Krause, G. Hoffmann, Rother.

1817: the printer Mehwald, the apothecary Schach, Johann Handke.

1818: David Schnerr, Thomas Vollert, Friedrich Welke, Samuel Scheche, Gottlieb Schröer, Johann Handke.

1824: Assessor Gerhardt, Thomas Vollert, Wilhelm Eiselin, Leopold Thomas, Baumert.

1836: the cloth manufacturer Karl Scholtz, the merchant Heinrich Buhle, Karl Miram, Traugott Görne, Samuel Wüstehube.

1850: Samuel Wüstehube, the printer Hindemith, Eichhorn, Neugebauer, Szliwe.

1860: the cloth manufacturer August Repphan, born at Birnbaum (52 years of age (f); Robert Pusch, hotel owner, born at Brieg in Silesia; the merchant Eduard Vetter, born at Bojanowo; the house owner Johann Szliwe, born at Kalisch, Ephraim Linke, born at Lissa, "possessor of several mills on the Tyniec and house owner"; Wilhelm Hindemith, printer, born at Jauer in Prussia.

1870: August Repphan, Ephraim Linke, Wilhelm Hindemith, Robert Pusch, Ludwig Scholtze, Johann Szliwe,

1878: Emil Repphan, August Linke, Theodor Esse, Wilhelm Fulde, August Biedermann, August Bertelmann.

1900: Emil Repphan, B. Felde, R. Weigt, E. Tahn, J. Handke.

1914: E. Fulde, R. Fibiger, E. Rathe, Adolf Stenzel, Albert Knoch.

1930: the manufacturer Hugo Müller, Gustav Hempel, Alexander Ulrych, E. Rathe, E. Scharfenberg, Viktor Scholtz, R. Stilter.

1936: Hugo Müller, Gustav Hempel, Emil Scharfenberg, Viktor Scholtz, Rafael Stiller, Alexander Ulrych.

Church at Kalisch; prayer hall at Opatówek. Cemeteries: Kalisch, Opatówek, Dębe.

Societies.

"Kalischer Evangelischer Gesangverein", founded 1857.

Singing choir (within the "Christliche Gemeinschaft");

Evangelical youth society, founded 1916 (German lectures also); youth league of the E. C.

Sunday school (in the Christliche Gemeinschaft); welfare committee at the home for the aged.

Christliche Gemeinschaft. Preacher Alfred Hoffert.
