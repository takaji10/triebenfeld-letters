According to the new church law Maślaki was raised to an independent congregation.

STATISTICS.

Year of foundation of the filial: 1842.

Souls: in 1842 there were here 1584 souls (in 305 families), of them 14 souls of the Reformed confession; in 1849 the filial counted 320 families with in all 1644 souls, besides 4 families with 19 souls of the Reformed confession. 1935: 2000 souls.

Church language: German.

Administrators of the filial.

Justus Hermann Otto Hinz-Konin, 1843–1882.

Karl Ferdinand Seelig-Sompolno, 1882–1884.

Karl Henkel-Konin, 1884–1915.

Alexander Bierschenk-Sompolno, 1915–1916.

Schnorr-Konin, 1916–1917 (pastoral administrator from the Reich).

Adolf Löffler-Konin, 1917–1927.

Philipp Kreutz-Sompolno, 1927–1928.

Robert Gustav Badke-Konin, since 1928.

Filial cantors: Julian Nebermann (born 1819 at Kołaczkowo in the Posen land), 1843–1850; Karl Ludwig Fiedler, 1850; Adolf Münch; Adam; Jesse; Philipp Nikolai, 1929/30; Ernst, 1931/32; Nikolaus Weber, 1932/33; Karl August Greger, 1934.

Sexton: Eduard Lutzer.

Church councils.

1842: Peter Kitzmann, Martin Kintop, Gottlieb Kuk, Daniel Zorn and Johann Lehmann.

1880: Julian Kitzmann, Friedrich Kuk, Heinrich Marcinkowski, Christoph Penno.

1900: Friedrich Jeske, Gottlieb Lutzer, Andreas Krüger, Ludwig Kakoschke.

1906: Wilhelm Scholtz, Gottlieb Lutzer, Emil Kaschube, Friedrich Penno.

1935: Adolf Penno, Adolf Henkelmann, Edwin Fiedler, Ludwig Martin, Adolf Tielmann, Richard Jeske and Friedrich Krüger.

Filial church at Maślaki.

Cantorates.

Bolesławowo, cantor Edmund Steinke.

Genowefa, cantor Alexander Kanwischer.

Kamionki, cantor Bruno Schinzel.

Doły, cantor Bruno Schinzel.
