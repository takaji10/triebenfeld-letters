# Kneifel (1937), p. 178
# translation by Claude

In the year 1918 the farmer Emil Kaschube of Neu-Kopydłowo was appointed to the Polish council of state as representative of the German colonists.

In the time of the administration of Pastor Löffler-Konin there was built at Maślaki in 1921 the congregational house (chancellery, confirmands' hall, 3 rooms and kitchen for the cantor). The building site was bought from Wilhelm Hein. In the same year the house of prayer at Brzozogaj (Birkenholland) was set on fire by the hand of villainy. Since the members of the cantorate were left without religious care, the churchly life suffered greatly thereby. In the year 1926 the cantorate built, thanks to the direction of Pastor Adolf Löffler and the help of the church elder Ludwig Martin, a new house of prayer (prayer hall, school room and cantor's dwelling). In November 1926 Pastor Löffler consecrated it. In 1930 the brass choir at Brzozogaj was founded, in 1934 the youth league. The religious-ecclesiastical life here received a gratifying impulse through the appointment of the cantor Erwin Benoist, who has come out of the work of helper and youth leader at the Lodz congregation of St. Trinity. "In devotions, Bible hours, house assemblies, youth and children's hours the Word of God is proclaimed for the awakening and deepening of the religious life"6). From November 10 to 13, 1929 there took place at Brzozogaj, under the direction of Pastor Badke-Konin, the first cantors' conference in the filial.

On Ascension Day of the next year Gen.-Sup. Dr. Bursche visited Maślaki and on this occasion inducted into their office the newly elected filial elders. In place of the organ, become unusable, the filial congregation procured a new organ harmonium, which was consecrated on July 31, 1932; in 1935 the premises of the congregational house were fenced in; further, the cemetery here is to be enlarged.

In the course of the year 1934 the lawsuit between the cantorate of Genowefa and the gmina Sławoszewek came to its conclusion. In 1921 the gmina named expropriated from the cantorate congregation of Genowefa the schoolhouse with three Morgen of land. In spite of all the efforts of the evangelical population, which even produced the deed of purchase found in the evangelical chancellery at Konin, nothing could at first be attained. In 1932 the cantorate again entered upon the path of litigation. The Kalisch district court decided in favor of the congregation of Genowefa, awarding to it the expropriated property as well as 1500 zlotys compensation. The gmina thereupon turned to the Posen court of appeal, yet without success. House and land Genowefa took again into possession; on the other hand the gmina long refused to pay the compensation.
