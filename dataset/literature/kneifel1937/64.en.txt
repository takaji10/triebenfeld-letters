# Kneifel (1937), p. 64
# translation by Claude

Kalisch singing society on the occasion of its 75 years of existence.17) On November 25, 1934 the "Evangelische Greisenheim" (home for the aged) (at the Litewska-Str.) celebrated its 25-year jubilee. In the past quarter-century it sheltered 129 aged persons. The direction of the home lies in the hands of a ladies' committee, the constant supervision in those of a sister of the Warsaw diaconate "Tabita"18).

Since 1936 in Dębe, where about 15 evangelical families dwell (of them 3 German-speaking), a reading service and a children's service in the Polish language are held once a month (Soencke). In Opatówek, where approximately 22 evangelical families are settled, a pastoral service and a children's service take place once a month.

The acts of office in the congregation are performed for the most part in the Polish language.
