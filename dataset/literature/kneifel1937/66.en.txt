# Kneifel (1937), p. 66
# translation by Claude

Converts.

From 1845 to 1929 in all 62 persons went over to the evangelical church; of them in the period 1920–1929, 8 persons; 1934: 1; 1935: 1.

Economic matters.

Number of contributors 1935: 280; size of the budget 1935: 21,311 zlotys; amount of the church contribution: individual taxation: the minimum contribution 3 zt.; the highest 1000 zt. yearly.

Salaries 1936: pastor 600 zlotys monthly; cantor 80 zt.; chancery clerk 80 zt.; sexton 55 zt.; gravedigger 45 zt.

Social structure: manufacturers 13; estate owners 4; house owners 92; the professions 27; officials 64; craftsmen 100; laborers 99, farmers 24.
