# Kneifel (1937), p. 256
# translation by Claude

Congregational cantors: August Gabler, August Rasch, Johann Ferdinand Hampel, Karl August Rottke, Adolf Wahren, Schultz, Witke, Samuel Hein, Karl Eduard Thim, Adolf Petznik, Emil Kunitzer, Adolf Krüger, Karl Gustav Welke, 1899–1909; Johann Milner, 1909–1917; Behnke, 1917–1920; Arno Tschadek, 1921–1922; Ferdinand Homt, since 1924.

Sextons: Samuel Jahns, Wilhelm Pfennig (1864), Gottlieb Pfennig († 1899), Gottlieb Wolff, the widow Eichhorst, Eduard Schultz, Eduard Krüger, since 1926.

Church councils.

1840: Johann Abramowski, Matthäus Luther, Johann Friedrich Rasch, Johann Eichhorst, Ludwig Neumann, Gottlieb Schnitzer.

1854: Karl Seydel, Friedrich Schnitzer, Friedrich Rasch, Christian Schütz.

1860: Karl Militz, Christian Schultz, Daniel Horn, Friedrich Steinbarth, Gottlieb Strauß.

1875: Karl Pfeiffer, Wilhelm Rentz, Christian Schultz, Heinrich Zelmer, Gottfried Schnitzer.

1900: Emanuel Munk, Christian Wolter, Karl Schultz, Alexander Arndt, Friedrich Wegner.

1914: August Grunwald, Julius Schneider, Gustav Stark, Ferdinand Krüger, Karl Schmechel.

1925: August Driesner, Alexander Schreiber, Wilhelm Heilmann, Julius Zache, Gottlieb Gliege, Theophil Runge.

1934: Gottlieb Gliege, Eduard Rittammer, Adolf Beutler, Adolf Rau, Reinhold Wegner, Robert Litwin, Julius Zache, Reinhold Reiser, August Pfeifer.

Church: Zagórów.

Houses of prayer.

At Olchowo and Michalinów, gm. Trąbczyn, the prayer hall serves at the same time as school room; on the other hand at Łazińsk, Michalinów gm. Oleśnica and Anielewo school and prayer hall are in one building, but separated.

Cantorates.

Olchowo. Cantor and teacher since 1924 Ferdinand Homt.

Tarszewo. Cantor Adolf Münch.

Michalinów gm. Oleśnica. Cantor Ludwig Schulz.

Michalinów gm. Trąbczyn. Cantor Paul Schulz (farmer).

Łazińsk, cantor and teacher Gustav Adolf Mielke, since 1925.

Anielewo. Cantor and teacher Bruno Mielke.

Cemeteries.

Zagórów, Olchowo, Tarszewo, Chruściki, Michalinów gm. Oleśnica, Michalinów gm. Trąbczyn, Łazińsk, Anielewo and Wrąbczynkowska
