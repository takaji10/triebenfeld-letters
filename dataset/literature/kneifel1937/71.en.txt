Within a period of 8 years I shall furnish all the roof tiles (computed according to the cost estimate at 10,426 zt.) and all the building timber (estimated at 4938 zlotys)." Yet this under the condition that the congregation must work up the material he was to furnish at its own cost and haul it to the building site, which he himself would assign to it.

The hope set upon the help of the authorities was fulfilled only in part. In the letter of March 2, 1842 the governmental commission announced that it confirmed the plan and the cost estimate for the new building of the church, and declared its readiness to contribute the missing sum from the evangelical fund.

The church building took on ever more tangible shape. On August 14, 1843 the county building office at Konin resolved to carry out the building by administrative procedure, the more so as no building contractor showed himself inclined to undertake it. Alexander Pawłowski, the estate steward of Grodziec, began in 1846, as plenipotentiary of the count, the building of the house of God. It was erected, against the wish of many congregation members, on a hill of sand. The solemn laying of the foundation stone of the church took place in the presence of Sup. Modl on August 9, 1846. Soon the shell of the building (up to the roof) was finished. Then there came unexpectedly an interruption of the work, partly because the lordship would not fulfill its obligations, partly also because the energetic estate steward Pawłowski had left Grodziec. Pastor Orive, notwithstanding all difficulties, did not remain idle. He negotiated with the county building office, with Count Bieliński, took steps with Sup. Modl-Kalisch and with the consistory, in order only to push the building forward.

Meanwhile the old dilapidated church threatened to collapse. Therefore the authorities saw themselves compelled to order the sealing of the church. This took place on October 24, 1850. By this measure the holding of divine services met with difficulties, for the uncompleted new house of God could not yet be used. In consequence the church council of Grodziec petitioned the county office at Konin to have the old church repaired so far until the new one should be built. The county office, however, did not consent to this. It is to be assumed that the divine services took place in the open air for lack of a suitable room. At Whitsuntide 1852 the congregation celebrated its first divine service in the church, not yet fitted out within. It did so reluctantly, in the fear that the further building might thereby be hindered. Sup. Modl sought to dispel these misgivings. In the letter of May 26, 1852 he encouraged "his dear parish children at Grodziec" not to despair but to trust in the success of the work begun
