# Kneifel (1937), p. 181
# translation by Claude

Societies.

Singing choir at Maślaki, mixed, 12 members, director the filial cantor Greger.

Brass choirs: Maślaki, Bolesławowo, Jarotki. At Brzozogaj there was formerly also a brass choir.

Youth league at Brzozogaj, director cantor Erwin Benoist.

At the children's service at Maślaki about 80 children take part; in the cantorates also children's services take place.

Sects. In and near Kleczew there dwell 3 Baptist families.

Economic matters.

Size of the budget 1843: 161 rubles 90 kopecks; 1901: 248 rubles 69 kopecks; 1916: 620 rubles 52 kopecks.

Contributors 1935: 296.

Amount of the church contribution: at Maślaki 80 groschen, in the cantorates 40 groschen.

Number of Morgen in the filial: over 5000.

Salaries.

Pastor-administrator: 150 zlotys monthly; cantor 120 zlotys monthly.

Social structure.

1 estate owner (Adolf Lutzer-Kopydłowo). 390 Morgen of land; the rest — farmers.
