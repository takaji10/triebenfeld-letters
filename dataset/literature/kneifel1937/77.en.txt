# Kneifel (1937), p. 77
# translation by Claude

1884: Johann Samuel Pfeiffer; Gottlieb Henschel; Friedrich Moritz; Samuel Kanwischer.

1909: Gottlieb Henschel; Gottlieb Steinbrenner; Wilhelm Reschke; Wilhelm Janott.

1925: Karl Kindler; August Richter; Christian Fenger; Gottfried Steinbrenner; Wilhelm Kanwischer; Theodor Brudler.

1934: Christian Fenger, August Heider, Heinrich Kanwischer, Karl Schmechel, Heinrich Jahns, Erdmann Gebauer.

Churches.

Grodziec.

Houses of prayer.

Obory, Wojciechowo — separate houses of prayer; Białobłoty and Gadow — prayer hall and school room separated, but in one house; in Orlina Wielka prayer hall and cantor's dwelling in one building.

Cantorates.

Obory, Białobłoty, Orlina Wielka, Gadow and Wojciechowo. In Zarzew there was formerly also a cantorate.

Cemeteries.

Grodziec, Stare Grądy, Nowe Grądy, Konary, Stary Borowiec, Wielołęka, Obory, Białobłoty, Orlina Wielka, Wojciechowo, Gadow and Zarzew.

Schools with German as language of instruction13).

1. Stare Grądy, one-class, about 80 children. Teacher Lerch, since 1928.

2. Konary, one-class, 70–80 children. Teacher Miss Berndt, since 1926/27.

3. Stary Borowiec, one-class, toward 120 children. Teacher Edmund Herrmann, since 1928.

4. Nowy Borowiec, one-cl., about 50 children. Teacher Badke, since 1929.

5. Ciświca Stara i Nowa, one-cl., 60 children. Teacher Adam, since 1929.

6. Orlina Wielka, one-cl., 120 children. Teacher Loepold Schmidt.

7. Białobłoty, one-cl., 120 children, teacher Adam (†), since 1929.

Schools with Polish as language of instruction, visited by evangelical children.

1. Grodziec, 5-class, 12 evangelical and 60 Catholic children. Head since 1909 the teacher and congregational cantor Karl Welke; in 1928 there were here 20 evangelical, 80 Roman Catholic and 20 Jewish children.

2. Wielołęka, two-class, 60 evangelical children. Catholic teacher Dobrochowski.

[^p77-13]: 13) The school conditions are subject to constant fluctuations.
