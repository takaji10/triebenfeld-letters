In all the costs of the interior fitting out, the renovation and the furnishing of the church (altar, pulpit, benches and the like) amounted to 10,022 rubles 30 kopecks.12) The total costs of the tower building, the remodeling and interior fitting out, the renovation and adornment of the house of God amounted to 29,631 rubles 50 kopecks.

On May 3, 1915 Pastor Karl Henkel died of the consequences of a long throat ailment. His death was sincerely mourned both by his congregation members and by those of other faith. In the evangelical cemetery at Konin he found his last rest.

The temporary administration was assumed by Pastor Sachs of Turek and after him by the pastor Schnorr from the Reich, who laid down the pastorate in 1917 in order again to care for his home congregation of Feuchtwangen in Bavaria. He was followed by Pastor Adolf Löffler from 1917 to 1927.

To Pastor Löffler there fell the hard task of protecting the evangelical population of the country from the excesses of the local authorities. It was especially the cantorates that he had to defend. While the German-evangelical country population gave him its full confidence, the Polish-speaking Evangelicals of Konin, together with the local authorities, stood opposed to him.13) This cleavage led to unpleasantness and conditioned also Pastor Löffler's transfer to Chodecz.

After Pastor Krempin-Koło had temporarily cared for the congregation of Konin and Pastor Kreutz-Sompolno for the filial Maślaki, the consistory appointed on April 1, 1928 Pastor Robert Gustav Badke administrator (Verweser) of the parish and filial congregation. He unfolded his activity: "1. in the direction of the completion of the restoration of the church begun in Pastor Henkel's time and 2. in the direction of the work of building up the congregation by the deepening of pastoral care"14). In the year 1929 the church and the parsonage were renovated, the organ put in repair, for which about 12,000 zlotys were expended. The electric lighting plant in the church was presented by August Walter of Konin (it cost 4000 zlotys). In the parish as also in the filial Maślaki Pastor Badke instituted in the year 1928 mission festivals, gathered the confirmed youth, founded singing and brass choirs, held cantors' conferences in 1929 and 1930.
