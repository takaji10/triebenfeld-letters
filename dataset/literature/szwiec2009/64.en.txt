2.2.1. The roofs of farm buildings

The single-storey farm buildings have only pitched roofs with two symmetrical slopes, of collar-beam construction, more rarely on posts. The rafters were fixed with tenons and mostly rest on the ceiling beams, which means that there are as many rafters as beams. The outermost trusses are mostly set flush with the gable wall. In buildings of timber with boarded gables, the ends of the wall-plate beams and the wall beneath them were sometimes covered by a horizontal board set at a slant.

Buildings with walls of timber and of clay were roofed with straw or reed, brick ones with tiles of various kinds.
