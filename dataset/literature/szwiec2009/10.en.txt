sample design proposals. The aim is to create guidelines that may become suggestions for those who make regional architecture. It matters that the work should show regional architecture in a broad visual and scientific (physiographic and historical) context.

Publishing the project will let the report reach a suitably wide circle of people, some of whom have, in varying degrees, an influence on the relation between building and landscape. It may change attitudes to their own heritage, lead them to appreciate the qualities of the region’s architecture and its particular character, and to see the physiographic relations in areas transformed by settlement.

In the longer term it may help to save the valuable architectural fabric that exists, understood not only as houses and buildings but also as the relations of the landscape. The research carried out made it possible to document the technique of building “houses of iron”, that is, of bog iron ore, found in this region.

Given the progressive decay and disappearance of regional elements in architecture and landscape, it may be supposed that any further delay in carrying out wide-ranging research in this area may affect the survival of many culturally valuable elements of regional architecture.

Rescue work on existing historic buildings is possible even today. The LEADER+ programme[^1] allows to a large extent the co-financing of the repair of roofs and elevations made in traditional technologies that are now dying out, such as roofing with straw or reed, or outer walls – elevations made of bog iron ore. With funds obtained in this way a building can also be modernised by replacing its damaged window and door joinery with new joinery of the traditional proportions and divisions.

The conclusions of the interdisciplinary research are necessary for a full understanding of the relations of the landscape and are needed for the design of new undertakings. The project is addressed both to conservators, planners and architects and to the local community. It is needed because it will also allow the regional features of the man-made landscape of this district to be given their due place in the national heritage.

[^1]: LEADER+ is a current Community initiative, that is, a programme of non-repayable aid from the European Union to specified bodies. Local authorities, non-governmental organisations and businesses receive aid for the projects of regional development they submit in the member states.

the building of a network of the EU’s rural areas – both those receiving LEADER+ aid and those not receiving it. In general all the rural areas of the European Union may use the LEADER+ initiative. The beneficiaries of the aid are above all “Local Action Groups”, that is, partnerships of public and private partners undertaking together innovative actions connected with the development of a rural area.
