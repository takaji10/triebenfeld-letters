Jarantów, Jarantów Kolonia, Józefów, Kolonia Obory, Królewiny, Lipe, Nowa Kaźmierka, Nowolipsk, Nowy Olesiec, Orlina, Ruda Komorska, Ruda Wieczyńska, Stara Kaźmierka, Trąbczyn, Walga, Wierzchy, Wrąbczynek, Wrąbczynkowskie Holendry, Wronów, Zapowiednia.

Each place was first studied in the field in general terms. At this stage buildings were chosen for detailed study, which was then carried out.

In studying each village the following questions were taken into account: the village’s position in relation to the neighbouring towns, the plan of the settlement, its internal network of tracks and its relation to the road network, the original division of the plots, the layout of the buildings on the homestead, the existing greenery, that is, trees, crops, farmyard planting, orchards and gardens, and woods among the fields, and the natural and altered network of waters.

The research on the architectural fabric of town and village covered the spatial structure of the region, which is made up of its urban and architectural composition and of spatial relations and their mutual influence, in the context of the semantics of space in the broad sense.

1.4. The results expected.

The project aims to gather and preserve knowledge of the regional features of the existing architecture, of its relation to the landscape and of their mutual influence.

The scientific aim of the project is to carry out research on the architectural fabric of town and village, covering urban and architectural composition, spatial relations and their mutual influence, in the context of the semantics of space in the broad sense.
