# Breyer (1938), p. 56
# translation by Claude

> *§ 25*

> *both contracting parties renounce all exceptions and excuses that may arise in this contract, chiefly of injury, of overhaste, of persuasion and whatever other names they may have; rather they approve the contract and [ask that it be] issued to them in forma probandi [probandis]. They signed the present protocol of agreement after it was read out and ratified [Retihabilierung].*
