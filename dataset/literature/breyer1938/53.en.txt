# Breyer (1938), p. 53
# translation by Claude

> *§ 12*

> *For building the houses and the farm buildings belonging to them, the holders of the hereditary lease have already been assigned the places needed. Chiefly they shall build in the lowland towards the church mill, on the right side of Borszewice, on the Warta river, or on the Lesznik, or on their shares, as they think fit; but the building in the lowland shall extend only to the part of the river where one crosses, and to the so-called old Rzeczyska beside the little ponds on the side of Lesznik as far as towards Spicimierz. The inn itself, however, shall be built on the road by the church mill.*

> *§ 13*

> *For building the houses and farm buildings, free wood as much as they need, be it building timber, saw logs, rafter wood, lath poles and the like, is given to the hereditary takers from the lord's forest, namely from the Krempki Brod, as the wood stands in it. From the patch assigned they are entitled to take the wood of that kind, but also bound to cut it down one after the other. From other pieces of the lord's forest, however, they are not entitled to cut building timber.*

> *§ 14*

> *The hereditary holders are likewise allowed to take firewood from the lord's forests belonging to Borszewice. As long as they have firewood from their own Hufen they are bound to take it from these. After these are cleared, the forest by Spicimierz, from the boundary mounds to the Borzewice peasants' meadows, is assigned to them for their need of firewood. Should there later be no firewood in it, the hereditary lord binds himself to assign another piece of forest in its place; for this free cutting of firewood each holder is bound to pay the lord ten Gulden cull. a year at Martinmas, it being understood of course that if there is no firewood at all in the lord's forests the due of ten Gulden also ceases.*
