It happens that in a very high flood the whole field area is covered by the waters, or as the marsh farmer says, "by the bare water", and traffic can be kept up only by boat. The members of the cantorate then come to the service by boat; funerals likewise go by water. All this vividly recalls the roads of the Spreewald. In some respects economic life in the Warthe marshes resembles that of the Vistula lowland. On the whole, however, the Warthe marshes are a landscape rich in peculiarities of their own, which stands out markedly from the area of settlement of central Poland. The "Brücher" are loath to marry up to the "Höhe" (the height), the Silesian or Pomeranian villages on the upland. They cling with all their heart to their green home. It should be stressed that the marsh dwellers of Brandenburg (Mark) stock in particular are distinguished by a sober and straightforward mind. Here we have never found sects, which cannot be said of the Pomeranians in the Leśnik language island. The Pomeranian farmer is more open to religious enthusiasm.
