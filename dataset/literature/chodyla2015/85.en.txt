In the noble Dębniałki and Piskory Olędry the settlers "*... who ... have settled and will settle*" received holdings of 1 and ½ huba *according to the settlers' wish* (h. = 300 rods), and in the second charter *free pasture in the forests and pastures, and the use of the pond at Piskory*"[^163]. In the Table of the tax of the offering of 10 or 20 grosz of 1789 the Piskory Olędry were entered in two ways. First as those which, from *separate* Olęders, *whose years of freedom end from 1791 and who will begin to pay in that year*. Then with the information that (...) *in the year 1792 again 11 szczególni [individual] Olęders will end their freedom and begin to pay...* This source also records that one Olęder (probably belonging to the Piskory Ol.) lived in the village of Lipe[^164]. In 1791 there were 9 *self-tilling* hearths, that is farms, in the Dębniałki Ol., and 16 in the Piskory Ol.[^165].

In the noble Dzierzbińskie, or Zdanowiec and Zamęty, Olędry, also founded in 1783, the settlers first received under separate contracts holdings of one włóka (the włóka equalled 30 morgen, the morgen 30x10 rods measured with the rod of 8 ells), *into which włóka the buildings, meadows and gardens are to be included* [counted], and also the small ditches, but not the large ditches. 7½ morgen were granted for the *bakałarze* (teachers), free of rent and labour, both on the Zdanowiec and on the Zamęty; 15 morgen in each for the innkeepers, which the Olęders were *to win at their own cost* [that is, to clear and build on]; and for a common cemetery for the dissenters [Protestants] a plot of 14 x 14 rods[^166]. With 37 *self-tilling* hearths in 1791[^167], the combined area of the two villages can be estimated at as many włóki, and with the farms of the innkeepers, the teacher and the cemetery at about 39 włóki.

In the Kosmowskie Ol. the holdings were most probably of one huba. The village also received in the foundation charter of 1775 ½ huba each for the school and the innkeeper. In 1784 2 more settlers obtained a foundation charter to build on half-hubas in Kosmów under Olęder law, *like the other Kosmowskie Olędry*[^168]. With 16 hearths in 1789 the area of this settlement could be estimated at a maximum of 15 hubas (włóki), and with the two farms in Kosmów at 16[^169].
