Under the servitudes of pasture the inhabitants – farmers in the Olęder settlements could graze cattle or pigs on the lord's land, on condition of making a certain payment or giving a due in grain. In the Dębniałki Ol. and the Piskory Ol. the settlers received in a separate charter pasture in the forests and the use of the pond at Piskory, in return for each of the 16 farmers ploughing within the year 10, and in all 150, *laski* (rods) of the manor's ploughland on the Bilawy "*for fallow, spring crop and strip*"[^260].

The lords' occasional economic help for the Olęders took the form of material and non-material benefits, temporary or long-term, which they received from them. During their free years they could use free of charge, freely or to a set degree and extent, every kind of building and firewood on the land taken up for their farms. In the Cekowskie Nw. Ol. they obtained in the foundation charter help in firewood and building timber on their own huba, and after fires they were to receive timber for rebuilding from the Ceków forest; they could also dig clay on land set aside for it[^261]. In the Stropieszyńskie, or Radzany and Gostynie, Ol. they were granted timber for building a school[^262]. In the Orlińskie D. and M. Ol., and also in the Wierzchy Ol., they obtained help in the building timber cleared from their holding[^263]. In the Grądy St. and Nw. "*Every Olęder, before selling off the timber, is to build himself properly and in line with the others within four years...*"[^264].

The regular and joint payment of the dues to the lord, the state and the Church, the carrying out of other administrative and judicial tasks, and the keeping of the peace in the Olęder settlements of the Pyzdry Forest were ensured, as in other Olęder villages, by an elected self-government. It consisted of the sołtys and the assessors, elected by the commune assembly of all the farmers (sometimes called the *gmin* or *gromada*). In administrative matters the sołtys and the assessors were, as was said above, to see that the settlers built as quickly as possible, properly and on a line (Orlińskie D. and M. Ol.[^265]), and to watch that *...every discipline and obedience of the said Dutch communes* was kept (Gadowskie St. and Nw. and probably the Smuszewskie Ol.)[^266].

The existence of a sołtys, and often also of assessors, is clearly confirmed in the foundation charters or other records in 21 of the 58 Olęder settlements that concern us. Namely: in the district of Pyzdry in the Ciążeńskie Ol. (sołtys), Wrąbczynek (sołtys and assessors) and the Wrąbczynkowskie Ol. (sołtys and assessors); in the district of Konin in the Białobłockie Ol. (sołtys and assessors), the Gadowskie St. and Nw. Ol. (sołtys and assessors), the Grądy St. and Nw. Ol., Konary Ol. (sołtys), the Orlińskie D. and M. Ol.
