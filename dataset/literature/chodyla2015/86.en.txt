In the second foundation charter of the same day, month and year, issued for 6 settlers, who probably also received holdings, though probably not of one but of half a huba, there is the founder's reservation: *...having on the manor land of the said village* [Mycielin] *a part of the ground, that is thickets, dense with various trees, called krzewina* (...)*, since the... Holendry... undertake to settle the said ground called Rusiny...;* (...) *and should there not be enough ploughland in the Rusiny for the sixth Holender, then I allow the sixth Holender to rudować* [clear]... *ploughland for himself on the other part of my manor land called Kozale* [Kazala]. According to the first charter the Olęders were to settle from the boundaries of Korzeniew, and according to the second from the boundaries of Słuszków. In both charters the amount of the entry money per morgen was set at 8 zł. The second charter also contained the provision that *the szkolny and the innkeeper will each receive 1 quarter of a huba from what "will be left over" from the hubas cleared by the Olęders*[^170]. From the detailed tables of hearths and population of 1789 it is known that then *the Holendry settled last year on the new colony have 6 hearths*, and also that there were in this settlement 25 *farmers, Holendry settled last year*; in 1791 there were 9 "self-tilling" hearths in the Mycielińskie St. and Nw. Ol., and five years later, in 1796, there were already 17 houses there[^171]. So, as a result of further settling, the area of the whole village would have grown again.

In the Oborskie Stare Ol., that is Wronów, and the Nowe Oborskie Olędry, there were farms of half a huba[^172]. According to the list of hearths of 1789 there were 27 of them, among them: 1 "*inn hearth*", *16 "hearths of rent-paying and entry-fine Holędrzy" and 10 "hearths of contract Holędrzy and lodgers*"[^173].

In the Plewińskie (Stare) and Nowe, or Kuźnickie, Olędry, founded before 1783 and in 1793, there were 19 farms (14 of them "*newly settled on the Kuźnica by Plewnie*" and 5 "*By the Old Hollendry, the New Hollendry*"). The holdings differed, of 1, ½ and ¼ huba; the village also received ¼ huba for the *szkolny* and ½ huba for the inn[^174].

In the Przespolewskie Olędry, that is a part of Przespolew Pański and of Poroże Wielkie, according to the foundation charter of 1772, at least 3 individual holdings were assigned at first, with ½ h. for the innkeeper and 1/2 h. for a school, free of the manor's dues and services, and a plot for a cemetery, which the Olęders were to enclose with a fence; the *grundgeld* for a holding (probably of a huba) was to be 8 Polish złoty[^175]. The inventory of a part of the Przespolewskie Ol. of 1785 named 22 Olęders, 19 of them with holdings of half a huba and three (the innkeeper among them) of 1 huba[^176]. In 1789, as was said above, 37 "self-tilling" hearths, that is farms, in all were recorded in the Przespolewskie Ol.
