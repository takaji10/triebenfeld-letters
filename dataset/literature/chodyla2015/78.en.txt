# translation by Claude

*– Zdanowiec and Zamęty, Grądy, Luśnie, Orlińskie (Orlina) Duże and Małe, Piskory, Poroże Małe, Wierzchy – Olędry; of marshes and bogs, such as: Białebłota Olędry;* names of industrial sites, such as: *Stara Huta Grójeckie and Stara Huta Zagórowskie – (Olędry)*; 3) characteristic, formed independently of the naming of old settlements, that is, among others, personal names, derived from the first names and surnames of the founders and members of their families, such as: *Kazimierskie (Stare and Nowe) Olędry.*

There were also combinations of two types of name, namely possessive and topographical, such as: *Ciążeńskie* or *Zarzeczne, (Kosmowskie)* or *Szadokierskie (Szadykierz), Kuszyńskie* or *Jaszczurowo, Lisewskie* or *Olsz/Olszowiec, Obory Stare* or *Wronów, Przespolewskie-Przespolewa (Pańskiego) and Poroża Wielkiego, Mycielińskie* or *Kazala Stara and Nowa, Stropieszyńskie* or *Radzany and Gostynie – Olędry.*

Some settlements, on the other hand, bore names inherited from settlements long deserted (overgrown with forest and bushes), or from settlements that had changed their legal situation, that is had been turned from labour-service villages into Olęder ones.

These were names that were:

1\. topographical, such as: *Bukowa/-e, Gliny, Imielno, Konary, Łazy, Lubień, Świdle Nowe, Wielołęka, Wrąbczynek*

2\. possessive, such as: *Myszakówek Olędry (written with or without the element Olędry).*

The names of these settlements were also often written in both forms, interchangeably, as *Konary* or *Konarskie*, *Lubień* or *Lubieńskie – Olędry.*

Because of the changes in the meaning and function of the term *(H)olędry*, the names of Olęder settlements containing this element, but not only these, were transformed in the nineteenth and twentieth centuries. This happened above all by dropping the element *Olędry (Olendry/Holendry)*. It also happened that in the names of Olęder settlements the distinguishing elements *Kolonia* or *Pustkowie* were used in place of *(H)olędry*, *(H)olendry* (in such forms as *Świdle Nowa Kolonia*, *Pyzdrskie Pustkowie* for the *Pyzdrskie Ol.*), applied to the Olęder villages because of their scattered spatial form and their small size. Several other factors also affected the transformation of the names of Olęder settlements, namely: 1) the persistence of the earlier tendency in the evolution of naming, connected with the settlers' own interchangeable use of the names of settlements, and with their changing forms in official records and lists of places (soon after their founding); 2) the parcelling and consolidation of land as a result of the regulation and the enfranchisement reforms, which brought back names used earlier, which in the case of the *Pyzdrskie Ol.* meant that one collective name was replaced by nine names of scattered settlements that had probably been moved to Olęder law together (*Baraniec, Glinianki, Dolne and Górne Grądy, Królewiny, Kruszyny, Tłoczyzna, Trzcianki, Zapowiednia*); 3) decisions of the administrative authorities at various levels, following a tendency to shorten and simplify the written forms of place names, as a result of which the adjectival elements of the names of Olęder settlements were changed into noun forms, for example *Borowieckie St. and Nowe Ol.* into *Borowiec Stary and Nowy*.
