The innkeepers in both settlements also obtained half a huba each free of rent, and land of an area not more closely stated was also assigned for the school (the teacher) common to both villages and for the cemetery[^133]. According to the survey of the crown estates of the district of Pyzdry of 1789, there were 14 farmers in Wrąbczynek with 11 hubas, and in the Wrąbczynkowskie Ol. 17 farmers with 9½ hubas of ploughland and 17½ morgen of meadows; there was also a little school there, newly built[^134], on the holding mentioned, assigned to the teacher.

Of the 28 Olęder settlements in the district of Konin, by contrast, the total area can be established from written sources for only 16.

In the Białobłockie or Królikowskie Olędry the holdings, of a number not exactly known, were probably of at least half a włóka. According to the foundation charter of 1787, ½ huba was assigned for a school common to the communities of Orlina and Białobłoty[^135]. In 1791 there were probably about 26 farms here[^136].

In the Borowieckie (Stare) Olędry, according to the inventory of the properties of Grodziec and Królików of 1779, 27 farmers lived on holdings of ½ (15), ¾ (8) and 1 (4) huba, with stated buildings, which allows the area of the whole village (with the school land) to be put at 18 hubas[^137]. The charter of 1785, common to the Konarskie and also to the Borowieckie – not only the Stare but also the Nowe – Olędry, did not state the number and area of the farms then existing and created there. It granted *to the whole community of the Borowieckie Hollendry, settled long ago, which has not yet paid the gruntgielt [entry money], and which it is reserved and left to set and receive at a suitable time" – ½ włóka for a school for both communities, of Borowiec and of Konary... gratis* [free of charge][^138]. In 1789 there were probably as many farms in both settlements as hearths in general, that is 21, of which about 10, it seems, in the Borowieckie Nowe Ol.; in 1791, 24 farming hearths were recorded in both villages, and later, in 1796, as many as 60 houses in all[^139].

In the Gadowskie Nw. and St. Olędry the dissenters received for a school and a cemetery, according to the foundation charter of 1784, 1/4 huba, that is 7½ morgen, free of rent. The settlers' holdings were of 1 or ½ włóka (the włóka, otherwise the huba, equalled 30 morgen, the morgen 30x10 rods measured with the Polish rod of 8 ells). The sołtys received in addition half a quarter of a huba free of dues.
