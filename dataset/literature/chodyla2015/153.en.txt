# Chodyła (2015), p. 153
# translation by Claude

17\. (1-2) Parts of the emphyteutic foundation charter for the Plewińskie (Stare) and the Nowe, otherwise Kuźnickie, Olędry, issued on 1 IX 1793 by Antoni Kawiecki, owner of the estates of Kuszyn and Plewnie. APP, Kal. Gr. 488, fols. 26v-28.

18\. Wawrzyniec Antoni Łodzia Czarnecki, *stolnik* of Inowłódz, deputy castle starost of Łęczyca (d. 1783), founder, together with his wife Ewa née Nowowiejska, of the villages of Dębniałki Ol. and Piskory Ol. (1783), in the district of Kalisz. Katalog Zabytków Sztuki w Polsce, Vol. V, no. 6, p. 6, fig. 106.

19\. (1-2) Part of the joint emphyteutic-perpetual foundation charter for two settlements, Dębniałki/the Bolimowskie or Brudzewskie and the Piskory or Lipeckie Olędry, issued on 17 VII 1783 by Wawrzyniec Antoni Łodzia, *stolnik* of the district of Inowłódz, and Ewa, the married couple Czarnecki, owners of estates in the district of Brzeziny in the voivodeship of Łęczyca and in the district and voivodeship of Kalisz. APP, Kalisz. Gr. 482, fols. 1-2v; obl. 7 I 1791 (Ryc. 30 Dębniałki i Piskory Ol.jpg).

20\. Information on the Siąszyckie Ol., founded about 1786, in the *manifestacja* (protest) of Leon Bi(e)lski, plenipotentiary of Kazimierz Biernacki, *stolnik* of Piotrków, owner of the estate of Siąszyce, against Onufry Gałczyński, a *towarzysz* of the National Cavalry, of 1790. APP, Konin. Gr. 122 (1790), fols. 275v-276.

21\. (1-2) Parts of the first emphyteutic-perpetual foundation charter for the Mycielińskie St. and Nw. – Kazala St. and Nw. Ol., issued by Maciej Łuczycki, owner of the estate of Mycielin, on 30 III 1787. APP, Kalisz. Gr. 472 (1788), fols. 50-50v, 53; obl. 22 I 1788.

22\. (1-2) One of the two first emphyteutic-perpetual foundation charters for the Stropieszyńskie-Radzany and Gostynie Ol., issued on 6 1787 by Ignacy Skarbek Kozietulski, treasurer of the land of Czersk, owner of the village and demesne farm of Stropieszyn. APP, Kalisz Gr. 466 (1787), fols. 335-336v; obl. 7 XII 1787 by the honest Michał Gultz, Olęder.

23\. Part of the revision of the Ciążeń key of the mensal estates of the bishops of Poznań of 1793, with information on Bishop Antoni O. Okęcki's foundation charter for the Ciążeńskie or Zarzeczne Ol. of 1789 and with a list of their settlers by name. AAP. CP 152 fols. 23-24v.

24\. Antoni Onufry Okęcki (1729-1793), bishop of Poznań (1780-1793), founder of the Ciążeńskie Ol. in 1789.

25\. Part of the village book of the Dzierzbińskie Olędry in Polish and German of the years 1792-1913, with a register, in Polish, of the Olęders who paid their rent, of 14 XI 1792, APP, SAB 803.

The other illustrations (scans) of archival documents on the history of Olęder settlement in the Pyzdry Forest, which for lack of space could not be included in this publication, can be found on the website www.puszczapyzdrska.com, under the tab: archival documents.
