# 1. Distribution, chronology, causes, conditions and organisation of the founding of the Olęder settlements in the Pyzdry Forest, and their naming

As has already been said above, 58 Olęder settlements were founded in the years 1746-1793, that is within almost half a century, in the area of the Pyzdry Forest, within 21 parishes and 3 former districts – those of Pyzdry, Konin and Kalisz – in the voivodeship of Kalisz, under several types of feudal landownership. The whole network of these settlements is shown on a map published in our day, Puszcza Pyzdrska (1:55,000), available in tourist bookshops. For times nearer to their founding it is shown, with varying accuracy, together with the Olęder settlements founded after 1793 and against the whole picture of settlement, by the Polish and Prussian maps described above. Parts of three of them, the *Mapa szczególna województwa kaliskiego* of Karol de Perthées, the map of South Prussia of David Gilly, and the *Karta topograficzna Królestwa Polskiego* prepared by the Quartermaster General's staff of the Polish Army, with the network of Olęder settlements in various parts of the Pyzdry Forest, are reproduced below (Ill. 1-3)[^33].
