# Chodyła (2015), p. 48
# translation by Claude

In the years 1775 and 1784, in the same district, on the noble estate of Kosmów, in the parish of that name, the Kosmowskie or Szadokierskie Olędry were founded. Their founder was Antoni Kawiecki, owner of the estates of Kosmów [Kuszyn and Gostynie]. The settlement agreement (foundation charter) of 1775 was made between him and two colonists settled in the Kosmów forest called Szadykierz, with Polish names, Marcin Kapliński and Jan Droga/Droże, *...who make the contract in their own name and that of others..*., acting as the locators, that is the establishers, of this village. In this agreement ...*all who, together with those making the contract, wish to come and build were given* 7 free years. The colonists were Protestant Lutherans, as is confirmed by the mention in the charter of an agreement with the parish priest of Kosmów about their obligations to him[^75]. Two further settlers, "honest", that is personally free, also with Polish names, were settled under Olęder law by a foundation charter of 1784, receiving the same free years[^76].

Next, in the years 1776-1781, in the same district, on the same estates and in the same parish, and by the same founder, Antoni Kawiecki, owner of the estates of Kuszyn, Kosmów and Plewnie, the settlement of the Kuszyńskie or Jaszczurowo Olędry was founded. 7 colonists (4 with German and 3 with Polish names), Lutherans, settled there. They took up *the forest or deserta* (empty land) Jaszczurowo, belonging to Kosmów, and received 7 free years. They first made the settlement agreement in 1776 with the founder
