The literature on the Olęder settlements that concern us is modest. Most of the Olęder settlements founded in the districts of Pyzdry and Kalisz are recorded by K. J. Hładyłowicz in his study of the changes of the landscape and the development of settlement in Greater Poland from the fourteenth to the nineteenth century, who, however, leaves out the district of Konin on principle[^10]. Some of the settlements of this kind founded in the district of Konin in the eighteenth century on noble estates are described by B. Szczepański in his article[^11]. The results of Szczepański's research on Olęder settlement on noble estates, and of J. Wąsicki's on state settlement in the period of South Prussia (1793-1806)[^12], were put on a map, with a commentary, by the geographer Stanisława Zajchowska[^13]. The Olęder settlements in the southern part of the Pyzdry Forest, within the district of Kalisz, were taken into account by W. Rusiński in his article on the settlement structure and the population of that district[^14]. The development of settlement in the Pyzdry Forest, Olęder settlement included, was set in the wider context of its natural environment by I. Tłoczek in his contribution (though concentrating chiefly on the Pyzdrskie Ol., which belonged to the town of Pyzdry)[^15]. The history of settlement in the Pyzdry Forest from the Middle Ages to 1945 (Olęder settlement included), though above all in the eighteenth century, was shown very briefly by Z. Chodyła in one of the sketches in a popular, brochure-style publication entitled "Puszcza Pyzdrska. Olęderskie dziedzictwo" (The Pyzdry Forest. The Olęder heritage).
