# Chodyła (2015), p. 53
# translation by Claude

In 1777, and then before and in 1784, the Gadowskie Stare and Nowe [Ol.] (today Gadowskie Holendry) were set up in the district of Konin, in the parish of Dzierzbin. Their founders were Piotr Sokół Gałczyński and Agnieszka née Sieroszewska Gałczyńska, his widow, life holder of the estates of Gadów, Smuszew and others with their appurtenances. She justified the founding of the settlement on economic grounds in her foundation charter issued at Gadów on 1 November 1784: *Since the extent and abundance of the woods on the estates of Gadów with their appurtenances... bring no use, neither now nor later, taking the example practised in this country until now in setting up Holędrzy, on hereditary estates and on estates held for life, seeing the need to strengthen the revenues* [income] *of Gadów, weakened by various accidents to the land, and wishing to keep their condition at its best for my most beloved children in my lifetime* [that is, to keep these estates in the best possible condition for the children], *and also to hand* [pass] *on to them the intrats of these estates increased later* [the profits of these estates increased later], *I have resolved ... to give emphyteutic law to the Gadowskie Holendry, to free people subject to no subjection, brought in while my husband was still alive... and coming in during my possession ... according to separate contracts* [agreements]. This charter names 3 settlers with German names, the sołtys and the assessors, but there were also others with German and Polish names, Protestant Lutherans and Catholics. They were settled in the forest called "Zielony Las" (the Green Wood). Its text also shows that they settled gradually and received free years fixed individually, set earlier by separate contracts[^86]. According to a protest of 23 VIII 1787 against A. Gałczyńska, lodged by Bogumił Leszczyc Dobrzycki, royal chamberlain, owner of the estate of Rychwał and of the *deserta* of Sokołowo with their appurtenances, which bordered on the Gadów estates, *A. Gałczyńska settled some dozen Olęders on the Rychwał forests and the deserta* [empty land] *of Sokołowo, which she had seized by force and unlawfully, having cut down the trees and sold them, and besides she is carrying off by force the hay from meadows she has appropriated*[^87]. In the inspection of the Gadów estates of 10 June 1780, A. Gałczyńska argued that the Olęders had been settled on land whose belonging to Gadów had not been questioned for centuries, and in the answers to the court's interrogatories (questions) of 3 XII 1787, which were also put to the inhabitants of the Gadowskie Ol., there was, among other information, also this, that they had been settled 10 years before[^88].

After 1777 and before 1784 the Smuszewskie Ol. arose, in the same district and parish.
