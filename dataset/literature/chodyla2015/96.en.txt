# 3. The population of the Olęder villages in the Pyzdry Forest and its demographic, social and occupational, confessional and ethnic structure, and the origin of the settlers

In keeping with the differences in the size of the 58 Olęder villages in the Pyzdry Forest by the number of hearths in the years 1789-1791, their population too differed at this time. Only for some of them do we know, from foundation charters or other sources, the numbers of the settler farmers (the "heads" of families) at the time of their founding or in the following years. For most of them (50) these numbers, with their structure, can be established at one time on the basis of the data of the detailed tables of population of 1789 and the record of the visitation of the deanery of Konin of 1790-1791, and for the remaining 8 settlements (1 in the district of Kalisz and 7 in the district of Konin) approximately, by multiplying the previously estimated numbers of their hearths by the population index we have established, 6.05 persons per hearth[^196].

The population of the Olęder settlements in the Pyzdry Forest in the years 1789-1791, within the three former districts of Pyzdry, Konin and Kalisz and in alphabetical order, is shown in Table 3, prepared in three parts, a, b and c. Parts 3a and 3b show respectively the numbers and, for the most part, the demographic structure (by sex and to some extent by age) and the social and occupational structure of the inhabitants of 5 Olęder settlements in the district of Pyzdry and 25 settlements in the district of Kalisz, mostly on the basis of the data of the detailed population tables drawn up at the census of 1789, and for a few settlements for which there are no source data, only their total populations, estimated.
