"...and should the dissenters wish to keep their rites in the church of their religion, they shall be free to do everything; nevertheless they promise to give the contribution now settled and agreed to the Catholic Church voluntarily, without any quarrel or dispute*"[^324]. In the Grądy St. and Nw., as in the Borowieckie St. and Nw. Ol. belonging to the same owner, the Catholics were joined to the nearby parish church at Grodziec and were to pay it the same fees for baptism and funeral until their amount was fixed by the higher church authority, and the *dyssidenci* [Protestants] of the same commune were to hold their weddings and baptisms in this church *"pod sztrofem"* [under penalty of a fine][^325]. In the Lubieńskie Ol., which were church property, the inhabitants – Catholics – were to pay the parish church at Dzierbin every year for the administration of the sacraments[^326], that is probably to pay the so-called *kompozyta*, a one-off lump-sum payment for baptisms and weddings, and probably for other kinds of dues as well (*meszne*, tithe). In the Cekowskie St. Ol. an agreement on the tithe was to be made with the parish priest through the manor; in the Dębniałki and Piskory Ol. the dissenters were exempt from dues, and the Catholics were to pay *jura stolae*; in the Dzierzbińskie Ol. – Zamęty and Zdanowiec Ol. the Olęders were by agreement to give *a wiertel of rye per włóka and pay for a wedding 4 zł, for the funeral of an old person 1 zł, for a baptism 1 zł, for the three banns 18 gr*; in the Kosmowskie Ol., the Kuszyńskie Ol. and the Plewińskie St. Ol. they were to render dues according to a future agreement with the parish priest of Kosmów, while in the Plewińskie Nw. Ol. the Olęders of the same Evangelical-Augsburg confession were to give the same payment as the inhabitants of the Plewińskie St. Ol.[^327], not stated more closely, however. In the Oborskie St. and Nw. Ol. the inhabitants were bound to give the parish priest at Żegocin *1 ducat every year*, and in the Przespolewskie Ol. they were to pay the *jura stolae* fees to the Catholic parish priest at Przespolew Kościelny[^328]. The first foundation charter for the Stropieszyńskie or Radzany and Gostynie Ol. of 1787 laid down: "*Since by a recent law freedom of worship has been reserved for all people of the Protestant* [sic!] *religion, therefore, as regards confession and burial, they shall be free to go to their own church, and the manor will give them protection in this, on the foundation of the said law, against all impeachments of the clergy (should there be any)*".
