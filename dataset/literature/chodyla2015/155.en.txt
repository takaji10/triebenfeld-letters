# Chodyła (2015), p. 155
# translation by Claude

Zbigniew Chodyła – Doctor of Humanities in history, author of

150 scholarly publications (7 of them in French, German and English, in foreign and Polish journals and publications), among them 2 books, treatises, articles, reviews, and subject and biographical entries (among others in the *Encyklopedia Historii Gospodarczej Polski do 1945 r.* [Encyclopaedia of the economic history of Poland to 1945], the encyclopaedia *Britannica. Edycja Polska*, and the *Polski Słownik Biograficzny*); editor of source publications, editor and co-editor of 3 books chiefly in the field of the social and economic history of Poland in the sixteenth to eighteenth centuries, on, among other things, the history of rural settlement (especially Olęder settlement), towns, prices and price lists, various questions of the history of Greater Poland, and the life and work of a dozen or so historical figures.
