# Chodyła (2015), p. 47
# translation by Claude

About 1771 the Gliny Olędry were founded in the district of Konin, in the parish of Dzierzbin (whose centre lay in the district of Kalisz), on the estate of Biała (Panieńska) of the convent of the Order of St Clare (the Franciscan nuns) of Kalisz[^73]. The inhabitants of this village were probably Catholics and Poles.

Not much later, in 1774 and 1780, the Cekowskie Nowe [Ol.] or Prażuchy were set up in the district of Kalisz, on the royal estates, in the lease of Ceków. The founders of this settlement were first Arnold Byszewski, colonel of the Front Guard, later major general of the Crown army, and Krzysztof Celiński, holder [from or after 1775] *by the right he acquired, both of life tenure and of emphyteusis for fifty years, which... serves him by inheritance*. At least 8 settlers (6 with German and 2 with Polish names), Lutherans, settled there. They took up *the ground in Ceków beyond the pond called Prażuchy*, which was undoubtedly wooded, since in the contract (foundation charter) of 1780 they received 7 free years *for clearing the ground and the meadows*. This contract was made with four *honest free men of the Dutch estate*, named by their surnames, who (...) *had settled earlier under the contract of the former holder, the Most Illustrious and Magnificent His Honour Pan Arnold Byszewski, major general in the Crown army, and also with other men of the Dutch estate, whoever signs this contract*. The list of Olęders included in the charter shows that the founding of the village was still going on in the years 1780-1782, and that some farms changed owners later, in 1785 and 1787[^74] (Ill. 12).

12\. (1) Parts of the emphyteutic-perpetual foundation charter for the Cekowskie Nw. Ol., issued on 28 X 1780 by Krzysztof Celiński, APP, Kal. Gr. 450, fols. 27-27v and 29-29v; obl. 27 III 1783 by the honest Bogusław Szwander, Olęder of the Ceków estate.

[^73]: See APP, Kalisz. Gr. 414 (1771), fols. 124v and 141-142.

[^74]: APP, Kal. Gr. 450 (1783), fols. 27-27v and 29-29v (emphyteutic-perpetual foundation charter issued at Stropieszyn on 28 X 1780 by Krzysztof Celiński; obl. 27 III 1783 by the honest Bogusław Szwander, Olęder of the Ceków estate); this foundation charter is mentioned in: APP, Tab. 20, Kalisz (Survey of the starosties and crown estates of the voivodeship of Kalisz of 1789), p. 23; cf. also the Cekowskie St. Ol.
