# Chodyła (2015), p. 104
# translation by Claude

Tab. 3c shows that the population of 20 of the 28 Olęder settlements in the district of Konin, 1,337 persons in all, was made up demographically of 654 males and 683 females. Adults numbered 782 persons, and children and young people (described as boys and girls without division by age) 482 persons. By confession, the 1,337 inhabitants of 20 of the 28 settlements were as follows: there were 6 settlements with an exclusively dissenting population, 385 in all (Lutheran, and perhaps to a small degree Reformed), 4 with a Catholic population, 210 in all, and 10 settlements with a mixed population, Catholic and Protestant (almost exclusively Lutheran), with 365 Catholics and 377 dissenters in all. So in all these settlements there would have lived 575 Catholics and 762 dissenters in all, that is Protestants – Lutherans, and perhaps in two or three of these settlements also Reformed. The former would thus have made up 43.01% and the latter 56.99% of their whole population. In the remaining 8 settlements of the district of Konin, for which the demographic and confessional composition is not known from the sources, there lived, according to estimates made in the same way as for such settlements in the districts of Pyzdry and Kalisz, 522 persons. In all, 1,859 persons would have lived in all 28 Olęder villages of this district.

Summing up the results of the calculations given in Tab. 3a, b, c, it must be said that about 3,815 persons in all lived in the 58 Olęder settlements in the Pyzdry Forest in the years 1789–1790, of whom 469 in 5 settlements in the district of Pyzdry, 1,457 in 25 settlements in the district of Kalisz, and 1,859 in 28 settlements in the district of Konin. The statistical average number of inhabitants per settlement would have been 65.77 persons. In the 45 settlements for which the demographic structure of their inhabitants is known there lived 3,154 persons, 1,707 (50.32%) of them male and 1,685 (49.68%) female. Of these 3,154 persons, 449 (234 male and 215 female) lived in 3 settlements of the district of Pyzdry, 1,368 persons (691 male and 677 female) lived in 22 settlements of the district of Kalisz, and 1,337 persons (654 male and 683 female) lived in 20 settlements of the district of Konin. For 13 settlements (2 in the district of Pyzdry, 8 in the district of Konin and 3 in the district of Kalisz) the structure by sex is not shown in the sources that survive and are available to us. The demographic structure of 25 Olęder settlements (3 in the district of Pyzdry and 22 in the district of Kalisz) looked different again by the criteria adopted in the census of 1789, with the population divided into men, married men and widowers; sons (that is boys of pre-conscription age, under 15, and those subject to the conscription register, aged 15 and over); *białogłowy* (married women and widows); and daughters, that is girls, in the sense, it seems, not only of persons young in age but of unmarried women in general, from infancy to old age.
