The settlement charters for the Olęders limited the circle of buyers of farms to people of equal condition (that is above all personally free peasants): in the Lubieńskie Ol. (a farm may be sold after informing the manor, *without reducing the sowing on the huba and without ruining or carrying away the buildings...*, on payment of one grosz in ten to the manor, *...to one equal in his estate...*, to burghers, but not to nobles)[^255], and in the Zbierskie Ol. (they may sell the lands to people of equal condition, give, donate or exchange them *without any claim of the lord*)[^256]. Transactions in farms, with the sellers and buyers and the conditions and prices of the sales, are confirmed by their entries in the village (sołtys) books of the Pyzdrskie and the Dzierzbińskie – Zdanowiec and Zamęty Ol.[^257]

The right of inheritance in the emphyteutic settlements in the Olęder villages of the Pyzdry Forest was probably held – as in the Olęder settlements in the districts of Poznań, Kościan and Gniezno, for which the relevant data survive in the foundation charters – by all the natural heirs of a deceased farmer, women included (in an order set by the degree of kinship). If an Olęder had no offspring or nearest relatives (brother and sister), the farm he tilled, with all its equipment, was to return to the disposal of the manor.[^258]

In the foundation charters surviving for 37 of the 58 Olęder settlements founded in the Pyzdry Forest, with the exception of those for 2 settlements, the principle of the Olęders' solidarity and their collective responsibility for the dues to the manor is not stressed. In the charter for the Stropieszyńskie, or Radzany and Gostynie, Ol., this principle is expressed in general terms by the stress on the punctual payment of the rents and *other dues*[^259]. For a dozen or so of the other settlements, however, it appears implicitly in statements that speak of the joint payment of the manor's dues through the sołtys, or the sołtys and the assessors, who were made responsible for their collective payment, which will be discussed in detail below.
