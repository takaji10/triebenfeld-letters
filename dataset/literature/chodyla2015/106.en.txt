This follows from the statistical data for 22 Olęder villages (2 in the district of Pyzdry and 20 in the district of Konin), already given above in Tab. 3a and 3c, contained in the records of the church visitation of the deanery of Konin of 1790-1791, and also from the direct and indirect information on this question in the foundation charters of these settlements and other sources concerning them, especially such as inspections, inventories and revisions, and sometimes also the census tables of 1789, which have partly been given above and will partly be presented below.

Thus it is known that in the three oldest settlements, neighbours of one another, in the district of Konin – the Trąbczyńskie St. and Nw. Ol. and the Luśnie Ol. (founded in 1746 and 1754) – Catholics and Lutherans settled, the latter predominating. This follows from the names given in the commission decree, Kinos and Meller, and from the list of population of 1790-1791 in the record of the visitation of the deanery of Konin[^197]. In the Łazy Ol., probably also founded about 1754, and certainly before 1789, only Catholics lived in 1790[^198].

In the Przespolewskie Ol. in the district of Kalisz at least several German settlers, Lutherans, settled before 1750 and in 1772, 3 of them known by name: Marcin Schmarschel, Fryderyk Jakobeck, Marcin Lehmann[^199] The decree of the commission court of 1779 on the tradition (judicial handing over) of a part of the Przespolew estate in payment of a debt of its owner, Tadeusz Zaremba, to the creditor Tomasz Moraczewski names 3 Olęders living in a part of this settlement, Poroże Wielkie: Marcin Smaża, Andrzej Szuldz and Frydrych Jakopczyk[^200]; two of them probably the same as those named above in the charter of 1772. The inventory of (a part of) the Przespolewskie Ol. of 1785 names 22 Olęders with names that are (except perhaps for one Polish one) undoubtedly German. Here they are, with their first names, in the forms written in this source: Krystian Lekner, Krystian Werner, Mateusz Bruchajzer, Wawrzyniec Fagen, Balcer Gotfryd, Krystian Szulc, Józef Kubs, Kr[z]ysztof Mager, Henryk Draier, Adam Walkier, Krystjan Macanka, Jan Raych, Wojciech Walle, Jan Walkier, Bernard Caydler, Gotfryd Daubyc, Jan Perszel, Jan Caydler, Wojciech Frank, Kasper Waber, Marcin Leyman, Bogusław Geerszling[^201]. Marcin Schmarschel and Fryderyk Jakobeck/Jakubek/Jakobczyk, named above, are not among them.

In the Lisewskie Ol. (Olsz), in the district of Pyzdry, founded after 1751 and before 1765, the existing Polish and Catholic population probably lived, though in one or two cases also, it seems, German people[^202]. The names and first names of the inhabitants of the Lisewskie Ol. are given only in the registers of the parish of Szymanowice. As living in this settlement there were first entered there, under the year 1775, in turn: Weronika Pankowska, Wojciech Panek, and Stanisław and Maria Krzywiński.
