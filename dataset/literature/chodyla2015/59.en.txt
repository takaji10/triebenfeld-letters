# Chodyła (2015), p. 59
# translation by Claude

Kazimierz Poraj Biernacki, *wojski* of the district of Ostrzeszów, owner of the estate of Siąszyce, which was set down in one joint collective emphyteutic-perpetual foundation charter, issued at Kalisz on 25 June 1783, Dzierzbin and the property of Sul[i]szew. The founder justified their creation by saying that he did so *having so many woods beyond his need*, and at the request of the Olęders. The recipients of this charter were Marcin Szynwald, Jan Fitz and other settlers not named, mostly, if not exclusively, Lutherans.

They were given the woods called Zdanowiec and Zamęty to settle. The first settlers received holdings of one włóka each. The two villages formed separate communes, with separate sołtyses and assessors. The charter stated that: *They are allowed freedom for as many years as they have agreed in their separate contracts, which will be entered in the wilkierz or inventory, making them free of all dues and labour during these free years*[^98] (Ill. 16). From this last piece of information it can be inferred that settlers had settled in this settlement under individual agreements earlier, that is before 1783. Later, further settlers were brought into both villages and farms were divided through inheritance, since rent in the years 1791-1792 and taxes in the years 1789-1795 were paid, or were to be paid, from different numbers of hubas, as is shown by the entries in a village book common to both settlements[^99].

Also before 1783 the Plewińskie (Stare) Olędry were founded in the district of Kalisz, in the parish of Kosmów, and in 1793 the Plewińskie Nowe or Kuźnickie Olędry. Their founder was Antoni Kawiecki, owner of the estates of Kuszyn, Kosmów and Plewnie. The first, also called Stara Huta, whose foundation charter is unknown to us, are recorded in the years 1783-1784, and in 1789 they had 7-8 hearths[^100]. From the foundation charter issued by A. Kawiecki for the Plewińskie Nowe Ol. at the manor of Kuszyn on 1 September 1793 it is known that their locators were the "honest", that is personally free, Bogusław Werner and Paweł Pus, who made the foundation contract in their own name and that of others who *...wish to come and build* with them. 19 settlers settled in this village, 14 of them *newly settled on the Kuźnica by Plewnie* and 5 "*By the Old Hollendry, the New Hollendry*", 14 with German and 5 with Polish names, Protestant Lutherans. The place of the founding was *the Plewnie forest called the Kuźnica, belonging to Plewnie*. The settlers received 4 free years for clearing it and setting up their farms[^101] (Ill. 17).
