Given this state of knowledge about the earliest history of Olęder settlement in the Pyzdry Forest, the sources of this article are, in the main, archival (most of them written in Polish, but a large part also, or only, in German, and in Latin), in the form of: 1) settlement charters (in fact contracts), which tell above all of the time, causes and conditions of the founding, the legal constitution, the social and economic constitution, the internal organisation of the settlements and the dues their inhabitants owed to the manor, the Church and the state; 2) inventories and inspections; 3) commune (sołtys) books and the village ordinance (*wilkierz*); 4) other, various records (secular as well as church ones, and among the latter the registers of the parish of Szymanowice of the second half of the eighteenth century and the visitation of the deanery of Konin of 1790-1791), cited below in the notes, which for lack of space we do not discuss further here. The most important of them survive in the series of the municipal court books (*księgi grodzkie*), especially those of Pyzdry, Konin and Kalisz[^23], but also in a valuable collection of originals and copies of such documents and records called the *Papers of Albert Breyer* (*Spuścizna Alberta Breyera*)[^24], kept in the State Archive in Poznań (cited below as APP). For questions of the same kind, but also others, concerning above all the number and structure of the farms, the population and the social and economic life of the Olęder settlements, of fundamental importance are: the surveys of the crown estates of 1789[^25], the tables of hearths, of hearth tax and of population and the tax of the offering of 10 and 20 groszy of the years 1789-1790, and the so-called grain levy (*zsypka zbożowa*) of 1791[^26]. Because of the gaps in these sources, however, full findings on particular questions are possible only for a part of these settlements.
