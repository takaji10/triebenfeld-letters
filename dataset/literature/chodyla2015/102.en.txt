The table shows that in the 25 Olęder settlements founded in the district of Kalisz, within the Pyzdry Forest, 1,457 persons lived in the years 1789-1790. So the average population per settlement was 62.18 persons.

Of the total of 1,368 inhabitants in the 22 settlements for which their demographic and social and occupational structure can be established, 691 persons were male and 677 female. By age and marital status, 591 persons were adults, counted among husbands and wives and widowers and widows. On the other hand, 772 persons can be regarded as children and young people, 382 of them male and 395 female. These figures are open to doubt, however, because of the number of sons over 15 (78), undoubtedly understated in order to protect them from recruitment into the army, and also because the age limit is not stated for the daughters. By social and occupational structure, of the 1,368 inhabitants of these 23 villages 1,210 were counted as farmers, 41 as lodgers, 3 as craftsmen, 13 as innkeepers and tapsters, 81 as farm servants and servants, 15 as *luźni* (landless labourers), and 5 in the category of other persons.

The number and demographic structure of the inhabitants of the Olęder settlements within the Pyzdry Forest in the district of Konin are shown in Tab. 3c.
