# Chodyła (2015), p. 40
# translation by Claude

They included, first, Konary or the Konarskie Olędry, which their owner, Józef Wężyk, already mentioned, founded on the Grodziec estate by a charter that was in effect shared with, and in any case identical in content to, that of the Wielołęka Ol., issued on 6 September 1770. Lutheran Olęders settled there, as is shown by the mention in this charter of an agreement with the parish priest of Grodziec about the fees for weddings and funerals[^60] (Ill. 8). The founding of this settlement was formally confirmed and extended by Count Franciszek Stadnicki by his charter, shared by them and the Borowieckie Ol., issued in Polish at the castle of Grójec (Grodziec) on 25 August 1785. The settlement was founded in the Grójec forests, which were a part of the Pyzdry Forest. The settlers were granted 7 free years *...to those now already settled, and these* [the Grójec forests] *...granting to each from his entering and paying the gruntgielt..."*. According to this charter the settlers were Protestants and Catholics[^61]. This last piece of information can, however, apply only to the Borowieckie Ol., since in Konary, as information from another contemporary source cited below shows, only Lutherans lived.

Probably in 1770 the Grójeckie or Grodzieckie Olędry (today a part of Grodziec, or perhaps Janowo?) were founded on the Grodziec and Królików estates, by an unknown charter, also issued by Józef Wężyk. The settlers were placed in the forest. This follows from the inspection of the estates of Grodziec and Królików with their appurtenances of 1771, made at the request of Helena née Jordan, widow of Józef Wężyk, *pani oprawna* (that is, holding a life settlement on this property), in which they are described as: *the Hollendry of Grudziec, newly settled on ten hubas, who are only now clearing [them] for building themselves*[^62].

Probably in the same year 1770 the Białobłockie (Białe, Białogłowy, otherwise Królikowskie) Olędry arose on the Królików estate, founded by an unknown charter issued by the owner of this estate, Józef Wężyk, named above[^63]. Their existence in 1775 is confirmed by a deed of purchase and sale of land, dated 10 March 1775 and signed by the sołtys and the assessors of this commune[^64]. They were re-founded and enlarged by a charter of 17 October 1787, issued by his son-in-law and heir, the Count Franciszek Stadnicki already mentioned. He did so *to multiply the intrats* [income] *from an increased population, moved by the request of free incomers wishing to settle in the Królików forests..*. [and at the] *request of the Olęders now already settled and of free incomers wishing to settle in the Królików forests*. He granted them *a right that is to serve in perpetuity*
