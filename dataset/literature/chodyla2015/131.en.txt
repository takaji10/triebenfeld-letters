# Chodyła (2015), p. 131
# translation by Claude

*for light...*" through the sołtys, by way of the manor[^329]. In the Zbierskie Olędry the Lutheran inhabitants were to pay *the parish priest at Zbiersk the kompozyta, that is the one-off lump-sum payment for baptisms, but probably also for meszne and tithe, in an amount to be agreed, in the fixing of which, and in the making of an agreement on the matter with the parish priest, the founder of the settlement declared he would help them*[^330].

[^329]: Cf. nn. 113-114.
