# chodyla2015  84  en
# Chodyła (2015), p. 84
# translation by Claude

In the Orlińskie Małe and Duże Olędry, according to the foundation charter of 1784, 20 settler farmers were settled, 18 of whom had a whole huba each and 2 only ½ huba each, which allows the area of the village at that time to be put at 19 hubas. They received for clearing in the forest *the hubas beyond Otnie in the place by the name of Orla* and the Królików woods called *Orlina*[^152]. In 1791, together with the Białobłockie Ol., they had 49 *self-tilling* hearths[^153], that is farms.

In the Trąbczyńskie Stare Olędry, according to the inspection of the Trąbczyn estate with the villages of Osiny, Łazy, Nowa Wieś, Olędry and Huta of 1754, made at the request of Katarzyna Prusimska, there were: – *15 Olęders, the sixteenth huba empty; some have built well, others* [!] *have only huts in the ground*. From this information it may be supposed that the village had 16 hubas in all, one of them not settled. In 1789 the Trąbczyńskie – Luźne Stare and Nowe Ol. had 10 hearths in all, and in 1791, after the founding of the latter had been completed, 31 farming hearths[^154].

In Wielołęka, according to the inventory of the properties of Grodziec, Królików and others of 1779 already named, there lived 15 farmers, an innkeeper and a teacher, partly still clearing their holdings: of an area not stated (2), on parts of a huba (5), on ¼ huba (3), on ½ huba (4) and on 1 huba (1), with stated buildings, which allows the area of the whole village to be estimated at about 6 hubas (włóki)[^155] (after the re-founding and enlargement of this village by the charter of 1791[^156]). The area of the village undoubtedly grew too, since in 1789 it had only 13 hearths in all, and in 1791 there were already 24 *self-tilling* hearths[^157], that is farms.

Finally, of the 25 Olęder settlements in the district of Kalisz, the total area is known from the foundation charters, or from inventories and inspections, for only 18.

In the royal Cekowskie Stare Ol., founded in 1768, the farmers received farms of 19 morgen, and 15 morgen were assigned to the school besides[^158]. With 5 farmers, according to the inventory of 1774, the area of the whole village could thus be put at 110 morgen[^159]. In 1791 the Cekowskie St. Ol. had 9 *self-tilling* hearths[^160], that is farms.

In the Cekowskie Nowe Olędry, or Prażuchy, also royal, founded in the years 1774 and 1780, the farmers' holdings differed. The settlers, 12 in number, took into hereditary possession in the years 1774 – 1787 respectively 1 huba, 1 h. and 9 morgen, 1/2 h. and 5 morgen, 1 h., 1½ h., ¾ h., 1¼ h., 1 h., 3/4 h., ¾ h., 1 h. and 2 h., paying 7½ złoty of entry money, grungielt, per huba. The whole village also obtained ½ h.
