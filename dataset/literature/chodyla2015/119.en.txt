The sołtys and the assessors were on the whole elected every year by the commune assembly and confirmed by the manor, though there were sometimes departures from this rule, on the whole short-lived. In Wrąbczynek and the Wrąbczynkowskie Ol. the sołtys and the assessors were elected, with the manor's consent, by the commune assemblies (meetings of the Olęder farmers) of both villages; in the Pyzdrskie Ol., according to the foundation charter of 1778, the farmers were to elect from among themselves every 3 years, on the first Monday after St Adalbert (23 IV), a sołtys and 2 assessors, confirmed by the magistrate; in the Gadowskie St. and Nw. Ol. and the Grądy St. and Nw. Ol. the sołtys and two assessors were elected by the *gromada*, that is the *gmin*, in the Stropieszyńskie or Gostynie and Radzany Ol. by the commune assembly, and the manor had to confirm them for a year[^268]. In the Orlińskie M. and D. Ol. the sołtys Bogusław Krol, who had permission for "*...free clearing and rudowanie with his co-samsiads* [!]..." [neighbours], was given Michał Arent to help him[^269], probably as an assessor, which suggests seeing the two of them as the locators of one of these settlements.

In Konary Ol., in turn, the sołtys was elected from 3 candidates put forward by the community[^270]; the two of them not elected to the office probably then served as assessors. In the Dzierzbińskie – Zamęty and Zdanowiec Ol. there were separate sołtyses and assessors, elected every year on 11 XI; in the Kosmowskie Ol., the Plewińskie St. and Nw. Ol., the Stropieszyńskie or Radzany and Gostynie Ol. and the Wierzchy Ol. the sołtys was also elected every year and approved by the manor; in the last of these, in the first year, the sołtys Daniel was chosen by the manor (probably because he was the locator), and the assessors by the commune, that is by the commune assembly made up of the farmers[^271].

For his work the sołtys often received an additional endowment in land or other remuneration. Thus in Wrąbczynek, for his labours for the commune, the sołtys received an *ostrów* [allotment, island, part] *of five morgen in each field* [that is the spring, the winter and the fallow field]; in the Gadowskie St. and Nw. Ol. and the Grądy St. and Nw. Ol. half a quarter of a huba in addition, free of dues[^272].

As the commune court, the sołtys and the assessors were to keep internal peace and order in the settlements and to settle quarrels and disputes. They judged by Olęder law, whose terms were set by the foundation charters of the individual settlements and, for some of them, also by the *wilkierze* (village ordinances). The *wilkierze* were collections of legal rules issued formally by the feudal owners – the founders of the settlements or their successors – but in fact formulated by the commune assembly
