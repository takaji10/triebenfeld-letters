# Held (1801), p. 70
# translation by Claude

All that I could do to preserve my rights was to extract the recognition of the court office at Freyhahn[^13], enclosed herewith in the original, concerning the protest made against the validity of the aforesaid agreement, and to make known the whole occurrence of 13 August 1792 to the Minister von *Struensee* as head of the Society *qua* lessor, and also to request his assistance, to which he expressly bound himself under the terms of § 5 of the lease contract.
