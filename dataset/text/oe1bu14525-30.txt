--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Die Regierung zu Kalisz setzt Glenck, dem Bau- und Ökonomierat des Fürsten zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen in Wrocław, in der Hypothekensache des Guts Swięcia eine Frist von 14 Tagen für das Hypothekenwesen dieses Guts und der dazugehörigen Güter Kopojno, Drzewce und Zagórów, mit der Ankündigung, dass dann unausbleiblich der Fiskus gegen den Fürsten tätig werden müsse.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The Government at Kalisz, in the mortgage business of the estate of Swięcia, sets Glenck, the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen's councillor for building and estate management at Wrocław, a term of 14 days for the mortgage records of that estate and of the estates belonging with it, Kopojno, Drzewce and Zagórów, with the warning that the fisc would then without fail have to be set in motion against the Prince.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
By the Grace of God, Friedrich Wilhelm, King of Prussia. Our gracious greeting first: Honourable, dear and faithful subject. In the mortgage matter of the estate Swięcia, it is hereby made known to you that if within 14 days the mortgage affairs of this estate and of the remaining estates belonging thereto, Kopojno, Drzewce and Zagórów, are not [uncertain: unv] put in order, then the Treasury must without fail be moved to proceed against your principal, the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Your Lordship; whereby you are to govern yourself accordingly. We remain graciously disposed towards you. Given Kalisz, the 18th of November 1800. Royal South Prussian Government. Meuser [uncertain: Meuser]

To the Princely Hohenlohe Building and Economy Councillor Glenck at Wrocław
