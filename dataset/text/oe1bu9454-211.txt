--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Herr von Knobelsdorff hat die Örtlichkeit besichtigt, sie gefällt ihm. Triebenfeld will nun alles so ordnen, dass man die Sache sofort überblicken kann. Er klagt über große Verwirrung und halsstarrige Leute, die von Hawich gehört werden. Vom Prozess um Trąbczyn erwartet er ein gutes Ende: Der Termin steht seit dem 24. an, bis zur Verlesung des Dekrets des Tribunals kann es noch 14 Tage dauern. Gewinnt er, soll alle Not enden.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Mr von Knobelsdorff has inspected the place and likes it. Triebenfeld now means to arrange everything so that the matter can be taken in at a glance. He complains of great confusion and of stubborn people, who are being heard by von Hawich. From the lawsuit over Trąbczyn he expects a good outcome: the hearing has been under way since the 24th, and it may take another 14 days until the Tribunal's decree is read out. If he wins, he says, all the hardship should come to an end.

211

Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

Mr von Knobelsdorff was here and has satisfied himself as to the premises. It pleases him, and I am therefore endeavouring now to bring everything into order, so as to arrange it all such that one can survey the matter in an instant. Meanwhile the confusion is very great, and the people stubborn who are heard by Hawich; often I could go out of my mind. With God's help I hope to set everything aside. The lawsuit concerning Trąbczyn will come to a good end; the hearing has stood open since the 24th, the matter is still not concluded, and it may easily be another 14 days before the decree is read out by the tribunal. If I win here, then all distress shall be at an end.
