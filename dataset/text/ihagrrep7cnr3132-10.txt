--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Haugwitz' Konzept seiner Antwort an Goldbeck, Berlin, 30. Mai 1803. Er dankt Goldbeck für sein Schreiben vom 11. Mai mit der Nachricht von der Rückkehr Prusimskis und Xaver von Jezierskis, gibt die originale Kabinettsordre mit den von ihm mitgezeichneten Konzepten und Reinschriften zurück und fügt hinzu, dass er mit Voss' Mitzeichnung der Kammer zu Posen bereits aufgegeben hat, die beiden nunmehr den Huldigungseid ablegen zu lassen. Unterzeichnet Haug[witz]. Ein Vermerk am Kopf lautet "d. 6. ten Juny" (der Rest nicht gelesen).

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Haugwitz's draft of his reply to Goldbeck, Berlin, 30 May 1803. He thanks Goldbeck for his letter of 11 May with the news of the return of Prusimski and Xaver von Jezierski, returns the original cabinet order with the drafts and fair copies he has countersigned, and adds that, with von Voss's countersignature, he has already instructed the Chamber at Poznań to have the two now take the oath of allegiance. Signed Haug[witz]. A note at the head reads "d. 6. ten Juny" (the rest not read).

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
To His Royal Grand Chancellor, Minister of State, v. Goldbeck, Excellency.

[Signature M?]

d. 6th June both c. alltis ins.

I thank Your Excellency most kindly for the news given me in your most esteemed letter of the 11th of this month of the return of v. Prusimski and Xaver von Jezierski. With the original cabinet order kindly communicated to me I return to you

herewith the drafts and fair copies countersigned by me, and add most obediently that, with the countersignature of His Royal Minister of State etc. v. Voss, Excellency, I have also already instructed the Chamber at Poznań to have these subjects now take the oath of allegiance.

Berlin, 30 May 1803

Haug[witz]
