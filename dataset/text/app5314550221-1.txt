Privilegium Nicolai Haeredis super granitiis seu limites Villae Makownica 14.
In Nomine Domini Amen. Ad perpetuam rei memoriam Nos Nicolaus haeres de Venacia Judex terrae Calisiensis generalis Notum esse Volumus Universis et singulis praesentibus et futuris praesentium Notitiam habituris: Quod matura deliberatione sano sufficientique super eo habito Consilio cupientes Omnipotentis suffragio[?] praesumpto Animae Nostrae et Animabus Affinum Nostrorum remedium consequi salutare, Haereditatem Nostram videlicet Makownicam pro Monasterio Nostro Landensi Coram praesentia Serenissimi Principis et Domini Domini Vladislai Dei Gratia Incliti Regis Poloniae etc. antea resignatam et literis suis patentibus in perpetuum Confirmatam, quam quidem Haereditatem volentes in suis metis et granitiis plenius fore cognitam et Manifestam. Ipsam justa terrae Nostrae Consuetudinem recepto Ministeriali gadibus[?] seu Cumulis Alias kopcami in vulgari, a granitiis Malenino Villae, usque ad granities haereditatis Ruchocino nuncupatae effodere vulgariter Odsuć[?] mandavimus et limitationem facere: Sicque Medietas Virgulti Kobielnica dicti, quae fluit sive vadit Samica in vulgari ad praedictam haereditatem Makownicam perpetualiter pertinebit. Insuper adjungimus tanquam finali pro testamento, praemissis, quod post obitum Nostrum praedictum Monasterium et totus Conventus ipsius Fratrum Ordinis Cisterc. de Substantia omnium bonorum Nostrorum ibidem in praenominata Haereditate Contentorum et postmo[?] continendorum Scilicet Apum, peccorum, Ovium Equarum pecudum et generaliter Omnium rerum tam Mobilium, quam immobilium habebunt et perpetuis temporibus pacifice possidebunt In cujus rei testimonium ne per posteros cassari videal praesentes Literas Nostras firmiter conscribi et Sigilli nostri munimine roboravimus Actum in Landa Dominica infra Corporis Christi Octavam sub Anno eodem 1399. Praesentibus Discreto Viro Jacussio Plebano in Czaten[?] et Nobilibus Viris Dominis Joanne Siemenci[?] Pechone Czarkovien. Castellan. Nicolao Retch[?] Burgrabio Pyzdrensi, Vito[?] Lukomski Jaschone ibidem et aliis plurimis fide dignis.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Abschrift (Nr. 14 des Kopialbuchs, aus dem 18. Jahrhundert) einer lateinischen Urkunde, gegeben in Ląd am Sonntag in der Oktav von Fronleichnam 1399 (1. Juni). Mikołaj, Erbherr von Venacia und Generalrichter des Landes Kalisch, lässt sein Gut Makownica, das er zuvor vor König Władysław dem Kloster Ląd aufgelassen hatte und das der König mit offenem Brief bestätigt hatte, nach dem Brauch des Landes mit einem Gerichtsboten durch Grenzhügel (kopce) abgrenzen. Die Grenzlinie reicht von den Grenzen des Dorfes Malenin bis zu denen des Erbguts Ruchocin. Die Hälfte des Gebüschs Kobielnica soll für immer zu Makownica gehören. Dazu bestimmt er als letzten Willen, dass das Kloster und sein Konvent nach seinem Tod seine ganze Habe in Makownica erhalten: Bienen, Vieh, Schafe, Stuten und alle beweglichen und unbeweglichen Dinge. Zeugen sind der Pfarrer Jakusz, der Kastellan von Czarnków, der Burggraf von Pyzdry und andere.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A copy (no. 14 of the eighteenth-century copy-book) of a Latin charter given at Ląd on the Sunday within the octave of Corpus Christi 1399 (1 June). Mikołaj, heir of Venacia and general judge of the land of Kalisz, has his estate of Makownica, which he had earlier resigned to the monastery of Ląd before King Władysław and which the king had confirmed by letters patent, marked out by boundary mounds (kopce) with a court summoner, by the custom of the land. The line runs from the boundaries of the village of Malenin to those of the heritage of Ruchocin. Half of the thicket of Kobielnica is to belong to Makownica for ever. He adds, as his last will, that after his death the monastery and its convent are to have all his goods in Makownica: bees, cattle, sheep, mares and all things movable and immovable. The witnesses are the parish priest Jakusz, the castellan of Czarnków, the burgrave of Pyzdry and others.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Privilege of Mikołaj, heir, on the boundaries or limits of the village of Makownica. 14.

In the name of the Lord, Amen. For the perpetual memory of the matter. We, Mikołaj, heir of Venacia, general judge of the land of Kalisz, wish it to be known to all and each, present and to come, who will have knowledge of these presents: that, after mature deliberation and sound and sufficient counsel taken on it, desiring, with the help of the Almighty [uncertain: suffragio] first secured, to obtain a saving remedy for our soul and for the souls of our kin, and wishing our heritage, namely Makownica, which had earlier been resigned to our monastery of Ląd in the presence of the Most Serene Prince and Lord, Lord Władysław, by the grace of God illustrious King of Poland etc., and confirmed in perpetuity by his letters patent, to be more fully known and manifest in its bounds and boundaries, we ordered it, according to the custom of our land, a court summoner having been taken, to be marked with [uncertain: gadibus] or mounds, otherwise kopce in the vernacular, from the boundaries of the village of Malenin up to the boundaries of the heritage called Ruchocin, to dig them, in the vernacular [uncertain: odsuć], and to make the boundary: and so half of the thicket called Kobielnica, which flows or runs, Samica in the vernacular, shall belong in perpetuity to the said heritage of Makownica. We add besides to the foregoing, as a final disposition for a testament: that after our death the said monastery and the whole convent of its brothers of the Cistercian Order shall have, and shall possess peacefully for all time, the substance of all our goods contained there in the aforenamed heritage and [uncertain: postmo] to be contained there, namely the bees, cattle, sheep, mares, beasts, and generally all things, movable as well as immovable. In witness of which, lest it be annulled by those who come after, we have ordered these our letters to be written firmly and have strengthened them with the protection of our seal. Done at Ląd on the Sunday within the octave of Corpus Christi, in the same year 1,399. Present: the discreet man Jakusz, parish priest at [uncertain: Czaten], and the noble men, the lords Jan [uncertain: Siemenci] Pech, castellan of Czarnków, Mikołaj [uncertain: Retch], burgrave of Pyzdry, [uncertain: Vito] Lukomski Jaszko of the same place, and very many others worthy of trust.
