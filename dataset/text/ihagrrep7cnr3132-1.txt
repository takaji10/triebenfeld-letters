--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Der Großkanzler Goldbeck an den Kabinettsminister Grafen von Haugwitz, Berlin, 19. Februar 1803, eingegangen am 23. Februar. Der König hat ihnen beiden das Originalgesuch eines ehemaligen Starosten von Prusimski und eines Xaver Jezierski um Erlaubnis zur Rückkehr in ihr Vaterland zum Bericht übersandt. Da Goldbeck die Einsicht in die Akten über ihre Vergehen und ihr Vermögen für nötig hält, hat er ein Reskript an die Regierung zu Posen ausfertigen lassen, das er in Konzept und Reinschrift beilegt; er bittet Haugwitz, es mitzuzeichnen, die Reinschrift abgehen zu lassen und das Konzept an die Geheime Etatskanzlei zurückzugeben. Unterzeichnet Goldbeck.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The Grand Chancellor Goldbeck to the cabinet minister Count von Haugwitz, Berlin, 19 February 1803, received 23 February. The King has sent them both, for a report, the original petition of a former Starost von Prusimski and of one Xaver Jezierski for leave to return to their fatherland. Since Goldbeck thinks it necessary to see the files of the proceedings on their offences and their property, he has had a rescript to the Government at Poznań dispatched, which he encloses in draft and fair copy; he asks Haugwitz to countersign it, forward the fair copy and return the draft to the Privy State Chancery. Signed Goldbeck.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
South Prussia
No. 5.
received 23 February 1803
19 Feb. 1803.

His Majesty the King has been pleased to remit to Your Excellency and to me, for a report, the enclosed original petition of a former Starost von Prusimski and of one Xaver Jezierski for leave to return to their fatherland. As I consider it necessary for our full information to see the files of the proceedings on their offences and their property, I have had the rescript to the Government at Poznań, enclosed in draft and fair copy, dispatched, and most obediently ask Your Excellency (factum) kindly to countersign it, to forward the fair copy for dispatch, and to be so good as to have the draft returned to the Privy State Chancery.

Berlin, 19 February 1803.

Goldbeck

To His Royal Actual Privy State, War and Cabinet Minister and directing Minister of Finance, Count von Haugwitz, Excellency
