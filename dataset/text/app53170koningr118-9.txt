Ex Parte M Chełmski et Aliorum contra JM Prusimski pro T~ Condnis~ Relatio
Videatur hoc loco Relatio ex Parte Magnificorum Stephani Zielonacki Judicis Terrestris Calissiensis et Stanislai Chełmski Thaesaurarii Vschoven~ contra Illustrem Magnificum Antonium Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicencem pro Termino Condescensionis in Fundo Bonorum Grab agitt~ inciden~ prout Copia in productis

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Registervermerk vom 4. Dezember 1781: Bericht über die Ladung, die Stefan Zielonacki, Richter des Kalischer Landgerichts, und Stanisław Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa, gegen Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice, zum Ortstermin auf dem Grund des Gutes Grab erwirkt haben.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A register note of 4 December 1781: the report of the citation that Stefan Zielonacki, judge of the Kalisz land court, and Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, obtained against Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, for the sitting on the ground of the estate of Grab.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
On behalf of the Illustrious Chełmski and others, against the Illustrious Prusimski — Report for the Term of the Condescension

Let there be seen at this place a Report on behalf of the Illustrious Stefan Zielonacki, Judge of the Kalisz Land Court, and Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, against the Illustrious Magnificent Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — for the Term of the Condescension in the settlement of the estates of Grab, being conducted, upcoming — as the copy in the submitted documents [attests].
