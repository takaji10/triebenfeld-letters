# 1363-03-16  Ląd

Dokument Nr 1490
Wyrzbentha castellanus Poznaniens. n. n. capitaneus Polonie general. 1363 Mart. 16, in Land; protestatur, Stasconem una cum consorte ipsius Iachna tertiam partem hereditatis Wrąbczyn monasterio Ląd vendidisse.
Pergam. orygin. Skrawek pergamin. od przywieszenia pieczęci. Warszawa. Zbiory Rusieckich. Diplomata.
Nos Wyrzbentha castellanus Poznaniensis nec non capitaneus Polonie generalis, universis et singulis tam modernis quam futuris ad quos presens scriptum fuerit devolutum publice profitemur declarantes: quod accedentes ad nostram aliorumque terre Polonie nobilium presenciam nobilis vir Stasco una cum sua consorte reverenda domina Iachna, heredes de magno Wronczino, non compulsi, non coacti nec alico vicio aut errore prorsus seducti, sed maturo consilio suorum amicorum super eo habito, reverendo in Christo patri domino Iohanni divina miseracione abbati de Land nec non predicto cenobio sancte Marie et cenobitis ibidem Ordinis Cysterciensis, terciam partem hereditatis magnum Wronczino vulgariter nuncupate, cum omnibus et singulis utilitatibus, redditibus et proventibus nec non iurisdicionibus, quas habent aut habere per quempiam poterint in futuro, pro septuaginta marcis grossorum paratorum vendiderunt, contulerunt, ascripserunt et resignaverunt iure hereditario tytuloque proprietatis perpetuis temporibus quiete et pacifice possidendam, tenendam, habendam, vendendam, commutandam et ad usus beneplacitos convertendam, secundum quod prefato domino abbati suoque monasterio predicto melius ac utilius videbitur expedire; in cuius rei testimonium, signaculum nostri sigilli presentibus est appensum. Actum et datum in Land quinta feria post dominicam qua Letare in ecclesia decantatur, sub anno Incarnacionis Domini m.ccc sexagesimo tercio, presentibus hiis testibus: domino Lasczone subvenatore Landensi, domino Nicolao de Wysoczsko, Andrea de Dreczkowo, Lascarzone de Slawosewo, Pyotrkone de Stralcowicze, Iohanne de Beganowo heredibus, et aliis multis fidedignis ad hoc specialiter rogatis.
Magnum Wronczino, Wrąbczyn. Land, Ląd. Wysoczsko, Wysokie. Dreczkowo, Drzeczkowo. Slawosewo, Sławoszew. Stralcowicze, Strzałkowo. Beganowo, Bieganowo.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Derselbe Wyrzbenta beurkundet, dass Stasco mit seiner Ehefrau, der Dame Iachna, Erben von Groß-Wrąbczyn, aus freiem Willen dem Abt Jan von Ląd und der Abtei ihren dritten Teil des Gutes mit allen Nutzungen, Einkünften und Gerichtsbarkeiten für 70 Mark fertiger Groschen verkauft haben. Ląd, 16. März 1363, am Donnerstag nach Laetare. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski III, Nr. 1490.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
At Ląd, on 16 March 1363, the Thursday after Laetare, Wyrzbenta, castellan of Poznań and general captain of Poland, attests that Stasco, with his wife the lady Iachna, heirs of great Wrąbczyn, of their free will sold to Abbot Jan of Ląd and the abbey their third part of the estate, with all its uses, revenues and jurisdictions, for 70 marks of ready groschen.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 1490

Wyrzbenta, castellan of Poznań and the same general captain of Poland, 16 March 1363, at Ląd; attests that Stasco, together with his consort Iachna, sold the third part of the estate of Wrąbczyn to the monastery of Ląd.

Original on parchment. A scrap of parchment from the hanging of the seal. Warsaw. The Rusiecki collection. Diplomata.

We, Wyrzbenta, castellan of Poznań and also general captain of Poland, declare publicly and make known to all and singular, present as much as to come, to whom the present document shall have descended, that there came before us and the other nobles of the land of Poland the noble man Stasco, together with his consort the reverend lady Iachna, heirs of great Wronczino; not compelled, not forced, nor led astray by any vice or error whatsoever, but by the mature counsel of their friends had over this, to the reverend father in Christ Lord Jan, by divine mercy abbot of Ląd, and to the said abbey of St Mary and the monasteries there of the Cistercian Order, they sold, gave, assigned and resigned the third part of the estate commonly called great Wronczino, with all and singular the uses, the revenues and the incomes, and also the jurisdictions which they have or shall be able to have from anyone in the future, for seventy marks of ready groschen, by hereditary right and the title of ownership, to be possessed, held, had, sold, exchanged and turned to whatever uses for all time quietly and peaceably, as it shall seem better and more useful to the said lord abbot and his monastery aforesaid. In testimony of this matter the impress of our seal is hung upon these presents. Done and given at Ląd on the fifth day after the Sunday on which Laetare is sung in the church, in the year of the Lord's Incarnation 1363, in the presence of these witnesses: the Lord Lasczone, under-huntsman of Ląd, the Lord Mikołaj of Wysoczsko, Andrzej of Dreczkowo, Laskarz of Sławoszew, Piotrko of Strzałkowo, Jan of Bieganowo, heirs, and many others worthy of trust, specially asked for this.

Magnum Wronczino, Wrąbczyn. Land, Ląd. Wysoczsko, Wysokie. Dreczkowo, Drzeczkowo. Slawosewo, Sławoszew. Stralcowicze, Strzałkowo. Beganowo, Bieganowo.
