--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Protest von Jakub Pomian Górski, Grenzkämmerer der Woiwodschaft Inowrocław, gegen Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa und Erbherr von Łukom, eingetragen in Konin im Juni 1782 und von ihm unterschrieben. Górski war, von Prusimski beigezogen, zum Ortstermin zwischen Trąbczyn Minus oder Nowa Wieś und Łukom erschienen, den das Krontribunal in Petrikau mit seinem Dekret vom 23. Januar des laufenden Jahres angesetzt hatte, um Grenzhügel zu errichten; das Tribunal hatte den Grenzstreit nach der Karte entschieden und über zwei einander widersprechende Kommissionsdekrete befunden. Als er das Dekret nach der vom Tribunal in die Karte eingetragenen Linie vollziehen wollte, widersetzte sich Chełmski persönlich mit seinem Gefolge, stieß den Mann fort, der die Messkette zog, trat die Kette von der Linie, drohte und erklärte, er lasse eine weitere Berichtigung der Linie nicht zu. Górski brach die Amtshandlung ab, um beim Kriegsdepartement militärische Hilfe zu erwirken.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A protest of Jakub Pomian Górski, boundary chamberlain of the palatinate of Inowrocław, against Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa and heir of Łukom, entered at Konin in June 1782 and signed by him. Górski, engaged by Prusimski, had come to the sitting on the ground between Trąbczyn Minus or Nowa Wieś and Łukom that the Crown Tribunal at Piotrków fixed by its decree of 23 January of the current year for the raising of boundary mounds; the Tribunal had decided the boundary cause from the map and ruled on two commission decrees that contradicted each other. When he set about carrying out the decree along the line the Tribunal had drawn on the map, Chełmski in person, with his followers, resisted, pushed away the man who was drawing the surveyor's chain, kicked the chain off the line, made threats, and declared that he would allow no further correction of the line. Górski broke off his proceedings in order to obtain military help from the War Department.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The Illustrious Górski, Boundary Surveyor of Inowrocław, against the Illustrious Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa — a Manifest is lodged

Having come in person to the present Office and Records of the Konin Municipal Court, the Illustrious Jakub Górski, Boundary Surveyor of the Inowrocław Palatinate, adhering to the new law concerning the effectuation of decrees in settled matters, and also insisting upon the other laws enacted concerning judicial security and authority, and looking to judicial security — solemnly, before the same Office and its Records, in and against the Illustrious Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, Heir of the estates of Łukom with appurtenances — a Manifest is lodged, for this reason: Because the Manifesting [party], for the Term of the Field Condescension between the estates of Trąbczyn minor or Nowa Wieś, of the Illustrious Magnificent Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, Knight of the Order of Saint Stanislaus, and [the estates of] Łukom, the hereditary [property] of the above-mentioned Illustrious Chełmski — [said Term] assigned by the Crown Tribunal Post-Commissorial Decree at Piotrków, on Wednesday after the Feast of Saint Agnes, Virgin and Martyr, namely the 23rd day of the month of January in the present year, between the above-mentioned Parties, after the boundary cause was decided from the map and two Commissorial Decrees issued discrepantly and contrary to each other were resolved, in the matter of the erection of boundary mounds — having been obtained [at the instance of] the Illustrious Magnificent Prusimski: when he descended to the site for the Term of that Condescension, wishing to bring to effect [the decree] according to the prescription of that same Tribunal Decree and the line placed on the map by the Tribunal — that Tribunal Decree, being a Post-Commissorial [decree], not subject to any interpretation or relief pursuant to the new law of the Year 1776, enacted concerning Post-Commissorial Causes and Decrees — and intending to complete and effectuate the other contents according to the rule of the same Tribunal Decree and the line placed and subscribed on the map by the Tribunal:

the Illustrious Chełmski, Heir of the estates of Łukomia, personally himself with his retinue, rebelliously obstructed the further effectuation of the Post-Commissorial Tribunal Decree of the last instance and also the judicial [process] hedged about by so many laws — and pushed away the man drawing the geometric chain, and kicked it out of the line with his foot — and began to threaten — and also, responding to the judicial interrogation of his Office, declared himself judicially as not admitting the further laying of the Line to be carried out by the Geometer and his Office.

For which reason his Office, avoiding any danger, this rebellious obstruction notwithstanding, was obliged to suspend itself from any further act and from the execution and effectuation of the above-mentioned Tribunal Decree — and suspended [proceedings] for the purpose of obtaining military [assistance] from the Illustrious War Department.

Therefore his Manifesting [party], having recourse to the Laws of the Kingdom with respect to all the above-mentioned matters and notably this kind of rebellious obstruction and denial, manifests again and again — saving all that is to be saved by law.

Jakub Pomian Górski, Boundary Surveyor of the Inowrocław Palatinate
