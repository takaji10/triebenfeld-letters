Privilegium sive Commutatio Villae parvum Wronczyno et Molendini in Suburbio Pyzdrensi siti, cum Casimiro Rege Poloniae, pro Sylva Czernietz, in qua locata est[?] posthac Villa Drzewce 25.
In Nomine Domini. Amen. Ne res vel facta Mortalium quae fiunt et Ordinantur in tempore, per decursum temporis transeant et vacillent, necessarium est: ut Literarum praesidiis et Testium annotatione ad perpetuam memoriam reducantur. Igitur noverit tam praesens aetas quam futura. Quod Nos Casimirus Dei Gratia Poloniae Rex, nec non Terrarum Cracoviae, Sandomiriae, Siradiae, Lanciciae, Cujaviae, Pomeraniae, Russiaeque Dominus et Haeres de matura deliberatione, et Consilio communi Baronum Nostrorum ad hoc accedente, permutationem Bonorum nostrorum Regalium, Videlicet Sylvarum Desertarum prout inferius expresse describuntur, Cum Venerabili ac Religioso Viro Fratre Joanne Abbate et Conventu Fratrum Monasterii Landen. Ordinis Cisterciensis Gnesnensis Districtus fecimus in hunc modum. Quod Haereditatem ipsius Monasterii videlicet parvum Wronczyno circa Civitatem Pyzdry, et Molendinum sub Castro Pyzdrensi etiam dicti Monasterii Landen. cum omnibus et singulis Censibus, fructibus, utilitatibus, et pertinentiis Universis ad ipsam Haereditatem Wronczyno et Molendinum praedictum quomodolibet ab Antiquo spectantibus, prout in suis limitibus circumquaque sunt distincti, pro Nobis et Regno Nostro modo et Nomine Commutationis receptis, appropriatis, et perpetuo incorporatis; Quam Haereditatem Wronczyno cum Molendino supradicto Dominus Abbas praedictus suo et Conventus Sui Nomine per modum Commutationis ut permittit Nobis et Regno Nostro tenendam perpetuo et habendam a Se et dicto Monasterio Suo Landensi libere et voluntarie cum Omni Jure et Dominio resignavit. In recompensam vero Haereditatis et Molendini praedictorum Eidem Domino Abbati Ejusque Monasterio et Conventui praedictis modo et Commutationis more Sylvam Nostram in parte Czirnetz vulgariter nominatam in Districtu Coninen. situatam, ad culturam Agrorum valentem Sexaginta Mansos Franconicos in sua debita mensura continentem Nostra authoritate et per Nuntios Nostros Speciales mensuratam, et circumquaque ab omni parte ab omnibus Haereditatibus tam Nostris Regalibus quam quorumlibet Nobilium circumadjacentium Suis certis limitibus qui Kopce vulgariter nominantur limitatam debite et circumdatam, cum omnibus utilitatibus et fructibus Universis quae in ipsa Sylva sunt, aut quomodolibet esse poterunt in futurum, damus, donamus, ascribimus, appropriamus, et tenore praesentium perpetuis temporibus cum omni Jure et Dominio prout ad Nos
pertinebat
pertinebat pro Ipsorum utilitate possidendam Nostra sub tuitione incorporamus. Dantes Abbati et Conventui praedictis in Sylva Sexaginta Mansorum praedictorum Villas quotcunque poterint Jure Polonico sive Theutonico prout ipsis utilius videbitur expedire locandi plenam et omnimodam potestatem. Quasquidem Villas in dicta Sylva locatas, omni libertate qua gaudent a solutionibus Nostris Regalibus quibuscunque, aliae Possessiones dicti Monasterii Landen. omnino gaudere volumus et potiri de Nostra Gratia Speciali prout in Antiquis Privilegiis super ipsa libertate Possessionum Ejus Monasterii confectis, plenius continetur. Si vero Idem Abbas et Conventus hujusmodi Sexaginta Mansos nollent ad plenum aut non possent collocare, quantamque partem eorundem Nomine Gaji sive Sylvae dimiserint, Venatione Cervorum et Aprorum pro nostra Regia deductione reservata in Eisdem, alias omnes Utilitates fructus et proventus videlicet in pascuis, spatiis, et Venationibus ferarum quorumlibet sive Avium, ac etiam nominatim Utilitates Omnes et singulas per Nos in praedictis borris haberi consvetas, et prout ad Nos pertinebant, et etiam soli sub Nostra potestate Regia tenuimus omnia praemissa Abbas et Conventus supradicti Monasterii Landen. exnunc sibi per Nos ascripta et incorporata perpetuis temporibus tenebunt pacifice et quiete possidebunt, Cum requisitione Nostra Speciali praemissa vendendi, donandi, commutandi ac pro Sua Suique Monasterii Landen. saepedicti utilitate quomodolibet poterint convertendi. --- SUMMARY (German) ---
Abschrift (Nr. 25 des Kopialbuchs) einer lateinischen Urkunde König Kasimirs von Polen, gegeben in Pyzdry am Dienstag nach der Oktav von Epiphanias 1362 (18. Januar). Mit dem Rat seiner Barone tauscht der König mit Abt Jan und dem Konvent der Zisterzienser von Ląd: Das Kloster überlässt dem König und dem Reich sein Erbgut Klein-Wronczyn bei der Stadt Pyzdry und seine Mühle unter der Burg Pyzdry. Dafür gibt der König dem Kloster auf ewig einen Teil seines Waldes, genannt Czirnetz, im Bezirk Konin, zur Rodung geeignet und sechzig fränkische Hufen groß, von seinen Boten vermessen und mit Grenzhügeln (kopce) umgrenzt. Das Kloster darf dort so viele Dörfer nach polnischem oder deutschem Recht anlegen, wie es will, und diese sollen von allen königlichen Abgaben so frei sein wie die übrigen Güter des Klosters. Was es als Wald stehen lässt, gehört ihm mit allen Nutzungen; nur die Jagd auf Hirsche und Wildschweine behält sich der König vor. Nach der Überschrift des Abschreibers wurde in diesem Wald später das Dorf Drzewce angelegt. Unter den Zeugen sind Albert, Woiwode von Brześć, Wyrzbięta, Kastellan von Posen, und Florian, Kanzler von Łęczyca.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A copy (no. 25 of the copy-book) of a Latin charter of King Kazimierz of Poland, given at Pyzdry on the Tuesday after the octave of the Epiphany 1362 (18 January). With the counsel of his barons the king makes an exchange with Abbot Jan and the Cistercian convent of Ląd: the monastery gives the king and the kingdom its heritage of Little Wronczyn near the town of Pyzdry and its mill below the castle of Pyzdry. In return the king gives the monastery for ever a part of his forest called Czirnetz, in the district of Konin, fit for clearing and sixty Franconian mansi in size, measured by his messengers and bounded by boundary mounds (kopce). The monastery may found there as many villages as it wishes, under Polish or German law, and they are to be as free of all royal payments as the monastery's other estates. What it leaves as forest is its own with all profits; only the hunting of deer and boar the king keeps for himself. By the copyist's heading, the village of Drzewce was later founded in this forest. Among the witnesses are Albert, voivode of Brześć, Wyrzbięta, castellan of Poznań, and Florian, chancellor of Łęczyca.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Privilege, or exchange, of the village of Little Wronczyn and of the mill in the suburb of Pyzdry with Kazimierz, King of Poland, for the forest Czernietz, in which the village of Drzewce [uncertain: est] was afterwards founded. 25.

In the name of the Lord. Amen. Lest the affairs or deeds of mortals that are done and ordained in time pass away and waver with the course of time, it is necessary that they be brought to perpetual memory by the safeguard of letters and the noting of witnesses. Therefore let the present age, as well as the future, know: that we, Kazimierz, by the grace of God King of Poland, and also lord and heir of the lands of Kraków, Sandomierz, Sieradz, Łęczyca, Kuyavia, Pomerania and Ruthenia, after mature deliberation, and with the common counsel of our barons joining in it, have made an exchange of our royal goods, namely of the deserted forests described expressly below, with the venerable and religious man Brother Jan, abbot, and the convent of the brothers of the monastery of Ląd, of the Cistercian Order, of the Gniezno district, in this manner. The heritage of that monastery, namely Little Wronczyn near the town of Pyzdry, and the mill below the castle of Pyzdry, also of the said monastery of Ląd, with all and singular rents, fruits, profits and appurtenances belonging in any way of old to the said heritage of Wronczyn and the said mill, as they are marked off all round in their boundaries, being received, appropriated and incorporated in perpetuity for us and our kingdom by way and in the name of exchange: which heritage of Wronczyn with the mill above-said the lord abbot aforesaid, in his own name and that of his convent, by way of exchange, freely and willingly resigned from himself and his said monastery of Ląd to us and our kingdom, to hold and have in perpetuity, as he is permitted, with all right and dominion. In recompense for the said heritage and mill, we give, donate, ascribe and appropriate to the same lord abbot and his monastery and convent aforesaid, in the manner and by way of exchange, our forest in the part commonly called Czirnetz, situated in the district of Konin, fit for the cultivation of fields, containing sixty Franconian mansi in their due measure, measured by our authority and by our special messengers, and duly bounded and surrounded all round on every side, from all heritages, our royal ones as well as those of any nobles lying around, by its certain boundaries, which are commonly called kopce, with all profits and fruits whatever that are in that forest or may in any way be in it in future; and by the tenor of these presents, for all time, with all right and dominion, as it

belonged to us, we incorporate it under our protection, to be possessed for their benefit. We give the abbot and convent aforesaid full and complete power to found in the forest of the said sixty mansi as many villages as they can, under Polish or German law, as shall seem to them more useful and expedient. And we will that the villages founded in the said forest shall fully enjoy and possess, by our special grace, every freedom from our royal payments whatever which the other possessions of the said monastery of Ląd enjoy, as is more fully contained in the ancient privileges made on the freedom of the possessions of that monastery. But if the same abbot and convent should be unwilling or unable to settle these sixty mansi to the full, then in whatever part of them they leave as a grove or forest, the hunting of deer and boar being reserved in it for our royal pleasure, all other profits, fruits and revenues, namely in pastures, spaces, and the hunting of any wild beasts or birds, and also by name all and singular profits customarily had by us in the said forests, as they belonged to us and as we alone held them under our royal power: all the foregoing the abbot and convent of the above-said monastery of Ląd shall hold for all time, as from now ascribed and incorporated to them by us, and shall possess peacefully and quietly, with our special requisition made beforehand, to sell, give, exchange and turn them in any way to the use of themselves and their oft-said monastery of Ląd. In witness of which our seal is hung on these presents. Done at Pyzdry on the Tuesday after the octave of the Epiphany of the Lord, in the year of the same 1,362. Present these witnesses: Albert, voivode of Brześć; Wyrzbięta, castellan of Poznań and captain of Poland; Janusz of [uncertain: Bechovien.] and Dobiesław of Kruszwica, castellans; Adam of Kalisz and Trojan of Brześć, sub-chamberlains; and Florian, chancellor of Łęczyca, by whose mandate the present privilege [uncertain: transmitti]; and very many other witnesses worthy of trust.
