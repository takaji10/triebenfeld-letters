Dokument Nr 191
1411. die 30. mensis Novembris, in Ląd.
Gerhardus abbas monasterii Landensis cum Petro sculteto de Zagórowo Nowe alias de Drzewiec, scultetiam in Kownaty scultetia in Drzewiec commutat.
In nomine Domini amen. Ne res geste in tempore simul cum pretericione temporis euanescant, expedit, vt quilibet tractatus, qui deberet in posterum tractari, litterarum apice ac fidedigna testium subscripcione firmiter roboretur. Hinc est, quod nos frater Gerhardus abbas in Landa, ordinis Cysterciensis, Gneznensis diocesis, scire volumus quoslibet inspectores seu auditores tam posteros, quam modernos, quod communi consensu nostrorum seniorum prehabito, permutacionem fecimus et presencium testimonio permutamus iure, modo et forma, quibus moris et consuetudinis est permutari, cum prouido viro Petro sculteto in Nouo Sagorowo,1) alias Drzewycz nuncupato, suisque successoribus legittimis, dantes eidem Petro sculteto pretaxato successoribusque legitimis suis mansum cum medio in Cownathi2) villa nostra situatis(s) cum scultecia eiusdem ville nostre Cownathi antedicte, pro suo altero medio manso et suis pertinencijs omnibus et singulis cum scultecia in Drzewycz predicta villa nostra situatis, super quibus idem Petrus scultetus prefatus, suique successores legitimi plane et omnimode abrenunciant, nullum sibi ius in eadem hereditate et scultecia penitus reseruantes, omni dolo et fraude exclusis, talibus tamen sub condicionibus infrascriptis. Primo quidem, quod eidem Petro sculteto sepefato vendidimus ac pro presenti vendimus mansum cum medio in Cownathi villa nostra prefixa, pro quibus ipse nobis duodecim marcas latorum grossorum, XLVIII grossos pro qualibet marca computando, dedit, persoluit totaliter et assignauit, sic tamen, quod idem scultetus suique successores legitimi ibidem in Cownathi villa nostra sepefata tres mansos habebunt libere, pacifice et possidebunt. Insuper scultetus a data presencium libertatem habebit viginti quatuor annos duraturam, simili modo et kmethones a tempore, quo aduenerint, computando, viginti annos libertatem se nouerint dubio procul modo habituros. Eciam eidem sculteto nostro dedimus et assignauimus, ac per presentes damus et assignamus vnum iuger arabile exceptis tribus mansis pretaxatis, super quod eundem licenciamus ortulanum siue ortulanos, tabernatorem, secundum quod sibi placitum fuerit, collocandum. Item idem scultetus pistrinum, fabrum, sutorem et maccellum supra suam hereditatem, vbicumque sibi visum fuerit, edificare et construere poterit, nostra de licencia, consensu et voluntate. Eciam prefatus scultetus suique posteri ad omnia seruicia nobis obligantur, quibus alij nostri sculteti seruire sunt astricti, ad que seruicia vnum equum de tribus marcis latorum grossorum tam ipse, quam sui posteri habebunt et seruabunt; ac eciam ipse suique successores tercium denarium de qualibet re iudicata habebunt, eademque libertate gaudebunt, qua sculteti in alijs nostris villis constituti, frui hactenus consueuerunt. Insuper kmethones in dicta nostra villa residentes, de quolibet manso post decursum eorum libertatum singulis annis ad festum sancti Martini marcam latorum grossorum dabunt, assignabunt totaliter et soluent. Eciam predicti nostri kmethones ad festum Assumpcionis beate Marie nobis per duos pullos et ad festum Pasche per mediam sexagenam ouorum quolibet anno dare sint astricti; scultetus vero in magno iudicio, quod secundum communem consuetudinem terre anno quolibet obseruatur, nobis sex scotos, kmethones vero mediam marcam soluere, dare et assignare prorsus sunt astricti. Simili modo et kmethones in dicta villa nostra de quolibet manso nobis quolibet anno tribus vicibus ac qualibet vice vnum iuger arare tenebuntur. Insuper vt omnis disceptacio inposterum super seruicio et libertate kmethonum funditus subtrahatur, ad omnia, ad que kmethones militum sunt astricti, kmethones nostre ville memorate nobis modo simili obligantur. In quorum omnium testimonium et robur perpetue firmitatis, presentem litteram cum nostro ac nostri conuentus sigillis fecimus muniendam. Datum in monasterio nostro Landa, sub anno Domini M° CCCC° undecimo, in die s. Andree apostoli. Presentibus discretis et religiosis viris, fratribus: Felice priore, Philippo antiquo abbate, Iohanne suppriore, Goblino bursario, Syffrido celerario ac alijs pluribus fidedignis ad hoc vocatis specialiter et rogatis in testimonium premissorum omnium et singulorum.
Z kolońskiego kopiarza przywileji lądzkicb, karta 29 N. 64 i karta 36 N. 77.
1)Zagorów niegdy miasteczko, dziś wieś paraf. w pow. słupeckim.
2)Kownaty, wieś w pow. słupeckim, par. Wilczyn.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Abt Gerhard von Ląd tauscht mit Piotr, Schulzen in Nowe Zagórowo, auch Drzewiec genannt, das Schulzenamt in Drzewiec gegen das in Kownaty, und verkauft ihm dort anderthalb Hufen für zwölf Mark; das Dokument setzt die Freijahre, Zinsen und Dienste des Schulzen und der Bauern von Kownaty fest. Ląd, 30. November 1411. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski V, Nr. 191, aus dem Kölner Kopialbuch von Ląd.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Abbot Gerhard of Ląd exchanges with Piotr, schultheiss in Nowe Zagórowo, also called Drzewiec, the schultheiss's office in Drzewiec for that in Kownaty, and sells him a mansus and a half there for twelve marks; the document sets the free years, rents and services of the schultheiss and the peasants of Kownaty. Ląd, 30 November 1411. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski V, no. 191, from the Cologne copy-book of Ląd.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 191

1411, 30 November, at Ląd.

Gerhard, abbot of the monastery of Ląd, exchanges with Piotr, schultheiss of Nowe Zagórowo alias Drzewiec, the schultheiss's office in Kownaty for the schultheiss's office in Drzewiec.

In the name of the Lord, amen. Lest things done in time vanish together with the passing of time, it is expedient that every transaction which is to be dealt with hereafter be firmly strengthened by the point of letters and the trustworthy subscription of witnesses. Hence it is that we, Brother Gerhard, abbot in Ląd, of the Cistercian Order, of the diocese of Gniezno, wish all who see or hear this, those to come as well as those of now, to know that, the common consent of our elders having first been had, we have made an exchange, and by the witness of these presents do exchange, by the right, manner and form in which it is the custom to exchange, with the provident man Piotr, schultheiss in Nowe Zagórowo,¹⁾ called alias Drzewiec, and his lawful successors: giving to the said Piotr the schultheiss and his lawful successors a mansus and a half in Kownaty,²⁾ our village, situated there, with the schultheiss's office of that our village of Kownaty, for his other half mansus and all its appurtenances with the schultheiss's office in Drzewiec, our said village, over which the same Piotr the said schultheiss and his lawful successors renounce plainly and wholly, reserving to themselves no right at all in that estate and schultheiss's office, all deceit and fraud excluded; yet on these conditions written below. First, that we have sold and do now sell to the said Piotr the schultheiss a mansus and a half in Kownaty, our said village, for which he has given, paid in full and assigned to us twelve marks of broad groschen, reckoning 48 groschen to each mark, so however that the same schultheiss and his lawful successors shall have and possess three mansi there in Kownaty, our said village, freely and peacefully. Further, from the date of these presents the schultheiss shall have a freedom lasting twenty-four years, and likewise the peasants, reckoning from the time they come, shall know without doubt that they will have twenty years of freedom. We have also given and assigned, and by these presents give and assign, to our said schultheiss one arable iugerum beyond the three said mansi, on which we license him to settle a gardener or gardeners and an innkeeper as he pleases. Item the same schultheiss may build and set up a bakery, a smith, a shoemaker and a butcher's stall on his estate wherever it seems good to him, with our leave, consent and will. The said schultheiss and his descendants are also bound to us to all the services to which our other schultheisses are bound to serve, for which services both he and his descendants shall have and keep one horse worth three marks of broad groschen; and he and his successors shall have the third penny of every matter judged, and enjoy the same freedom as the schultheisses set up in our other villages have been wont to enjoy until now. Moreover the peasants living in our said village shall, after their freedoms have run out, give, assign and pay in full every year on the feast of St Martin a mark of broad groschen from each mansus. Our said peasants are also bound to give us every year two chickens each at the feast of the Assumption of the Blessed Mary and half a threescore of eggs each at the feast of Easter; and at the great court, which is held every year according to the common custom of the land, the schultheiss is bound to pay, give and assign to us six scots, and the peasants half a mark. Likewise the peasants in our said village shall be bound to plough for us from each mansus three times a year, one iugerum each time. And so that all dispute hereafter over the service and freedom of the peasants may be utterly removed, the peasants of our said village are bound to us in the same way to all things to which the peasants of knights are bound. In testimony of all which and for the strength of lasting firmness, we have had the present letter fortified with our seal and that of our convent. Given in our monastery of Ląd, in the year of the Lord 1411, on the day of St Andrew the Apostle. In the presence of the discreet and religious men, the brothers: Feliks, prior; Filip, the old abbot; Jan, subprior; Goblin, bursar; Syfryd, cellarer; and many others worthy of trust, called and asked specially for this in testimony of all and each of the foregoing.

From the Cologne copy-book of the privileges of Ląd, fol. 29, no. 64, and fol. 36, no. 77.

¹⁾Zagorów, once a small town, today a parish village in the district of Słupca.

²⁾Kownaty, a village in the district of Słupca, parish of Wilczyn.
