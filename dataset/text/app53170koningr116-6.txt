Actum in Conin Feria Tertia Ante Festum Sancti Francisci Confessoris proxima Videlicet Die Tertio Mensis Octobris Anno Domini 1775.
Ex Parte H Trąmpczyn Ductus Granitialis Visio

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Überschrift eines Eintrags vom 3. Oktober 1775: für den Erbherrn von Trąbczyn, Besichtigung des Grenzzugs. Der Eintrag selbst wurde nicht geschrieben.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The heading of an entry of 3 October 1775: for the heir of Trąbczyn, an inspection of the boundary line. The entry itself was not written.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Done in Konin on the Tuesday next before the Feast of Saint Francis the Confessor, namely on the third day of the month of October in the Year of the Lord 1775.

On behalf of the Heir of Trąbczyn — Boundary Survey Inspection
