--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Protest von Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Erbherrn von Łukom und Besitzer der Olęder von Lusnia, gegen Antoni Prusimski von Kolno, Starost von Niszczewice, dessen Hofleute und Untertanen, eingetragen in Konin im März 1773 und von Chełmski unterschrieben. Prusimski habe, obwohl er wisse, dass der Besitz der Olęder von Lusnia Chełmski rechtlich zuerkannt sei, seinen Verwalter Lipiński und seinen Kammerdiener Marzęcki mit anderen das Schankhaus und die Olęder überfallen lassen; sie hätten dort Gewalttaten verübt.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A protest of Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, heir of Łukom and possessor of the Olęder settlers of Lusnia, against Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice, his household and his subjects, entered at Konin in March 1773 and signed by Chełmski. Prusimski, although he knows that possession of the Lusnia settlers has been adjudged to Chełmski at law, let his steward Lipiński and his chamber servant Marzęcki, with others, ride upon the tavern and the settlers; they committed acts of violence there.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The Right Honourable Chełmski against the Right Honourable Prusimski — Protest

To the office and present records of the Konin Municipal Court, appearing in person, the Right Honourable Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, heir of the estates of Łukom with their appurtenances, possessor by force of his rights of the olędry called Lusnia, lodged this protest to the present records against the Right Honourable Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice, heir of the estates of the Trąbczyn tract, and likewise against his household servants and subjects — for this reason: that the same Right Honourable Prusimski, proceeding in subversion of the laws, although he well knows that possession of the Lusnia olędry has been acknowledged by law to him, the protester, nonetheless — whether by his special mandate or by his connivance — permitted the Honourables Lipiński, [his] steward, and Marzęcki, his chamber attendant, together with those recruited to them — Kazimierz, farm servant of the miller of Drzęzgi, Filip Wąchnicki, and others — to ride upon and attack the tavern and the olędry; which same violent attackers, having set aside the safety cautiously secured for the settlers dwelling there, did not scruple to carry out and perpetrate various transgressions and acts of violence — in respect of which unlawful conduct the same Right Honourable Chełmski hereby protests.

Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, by his own hand
