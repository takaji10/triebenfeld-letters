Dokument Nr 678
Secundus Premisl dux Maioris Polonie 1291 Oct. 21, in Gnezna; roborat commutationem hereditatum monasterii Ląd, vocatarum Głowiew, Sławsk et Chocicza, cum hereditate comitis Sendyvoii subcamerarii, dicta Kopojno, quam amplissimis munit privilegiis.
Pergam. orygin. Na grubym sznurze nici jedwabn., zielon. przywieszona pieczęć Przemysława księcia; (pieczęć XXVI).
Bibl. XX. Czartoryskich. Diplomata. - Rzyszczew. Muczkow. Cod. diplomatic. Poloniae I, Nr. 77; z innego oryginału, ma na końcu: Data per Iasconem notarium curie nostre anno Domini M.CC. nonagesimo primo in Gnezna, in die XI millium Virginum.
In nomine Domini amen. Quoniam humane memorie innata oblivio, actuum temporalium constituta dirigit in errorem que non fuerint litterali apice aut testium subscripcione firmiter roborata, nos secundus Premisl Dei gracia dux Maioris Polonie universis Christi fidelibus tam presentibus quam futuris volumus esse notum, quod venerabilis abbas dominus Gerhardus de Lenda et eius conventus cum dilecto subcamerario nostro comite Sendyvoio pro commodo domus sue commutacionem fecerunt nostro gracioso favore et consensu mediante, donantes ipsi tres hereditates, videlicet Glovevo, Slavsco et Chociczha pro hereditate dicta Copaino, quam idem Sendivoius pro suo patrimonio, scilicet Gorcino, a nobis commutatam possedit hereditarie liberam et exemptam ab omni iure Polonico quocunque nomine censeatur, filiis dicti Sendivoii, scilicet Iancone et Martino, ad quos spectabat ius hereditarium, dictam hereditatem coram nobis resignantibus et a iure hereditario cedentibus, abbate vero prefato cum suis fratribus prelibatam hereditatem Copayno intrantibus hereditarie possidendam. Nos autem affectum quem circa domum Lendensem semper habuimus augere cupientes, volumus sepedictam hereditatem cum incolis eiusdem, propriis seu liberis, exemptam fore et liberam, ut prius erat cum a dicto Sendyvoio possideretur, ab omni prevod, a povoz, a stroza, a dan, a naraz, a podvorove, a targove, a mostne, a castrorum edificio seu poncium quorumlibet, a recepcione seu custodia vel conductu castariorum (sic), castores enim domino abbati ac suis fratribus venandos perpetuo contulimus. Insuper a vicinia que opole vulgariter dicitur, a recepcione subvenatoris aut venatorum qualiumcunque, a custodia vel solucione herodiorum quod socol populariter nuncupatur, ab omni exactione seu collectione quod povolove dicitur, a vecturis et expedicionibus seu omnibus iuribus in Polonia constitutis, quibuscumque nominibus censeantur. Iudiciorum nichilominus omnium, ut est de contencione, percussione, membrorum mutilacione, seu eciam capitis perplexione, ut maiorem circa Deum in tremendo iudicio fiduciam habeamus, predictis fratribus per suum iudicem liberam auctoritatem contulimus exequendi, nulli castellanorum aut iudicum sive citandi sive iudicandi in ipsa hereditate ius aliquod reservantes. Et ut hoc factum nostrum in honorem Dei sancteque Marie semper virginis robur perpetue firmitatis obtineat, presentem paginam sigilli nostri munimine roboramus. Huius rei testes sunt: Philippus archidiaconus Gneznensis, Berwoldus castellanus Kalisiensis, Abraham thezaurarius Gneznensis. Acta sunt hec anno Domini MCC nonagesimo primo in Gnezna, in die Virginum Undecim milium.
Lenda, Ląd. Glovevo, Głowiew. Slavsco, Sławsk. Chociczha, Chocicza. Copaino, Kopojno. Gorcino, Górczyn.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Herzog Przemysł II. von Großpolen bestätigt den Tausch der Güter des Klosters Ląd, Głowiew, Sławsk und Chocicza, gegen das Gut Kopojno des Grafen Sędziwój, Unterkämmerers, und stattet Kopojno mit den weitesten Freiheiten aus. Gnesen, 21. Oktober 1291. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski II, Nr. 678, nach dem Original.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Duke Przemysł II of Greater Poland confirms the exchange of the abbey of Ląd's estates of Głowiew, Sławsk and Chocicza for Kopojno, the estate of Count Sędziwój the under-chamberlain, and endows Kopojno with the widest liberties. Gniezno, 21 October 1291. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski II, no. 678, from the original.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 678

Przemysł the Second, Duke of Greater Poland, 21 October 1291, at Gniezno; confirms the exchange of the estates of the monastery of Ląd called Głowiew, Sławsk and Chocicza for the estate of Count Sędziwój the under-chamberlain called Kopojno, which he endows with the most ample privileges.

Original on parchment. On a thick cord of green silk thread hangs the seal of Duke Przemysł (seal XXVI).

Library of the Princes Czartoryski. Diplomata. Rzyszczewski and Muczkowski, Codex diplomaticus Poloniae I, no. 77; from another original, which has at the end: Data per Iasconem notarium curie nostre anno Domini M.CC. nonagesimo primo in Gnezna, in die XI millium Virginum.

In the name of the Lord, amen. Since the forgetfulness inborn in human memory leads into error the settlements of temporal acts that have not been firmly strengthened by the point of letters or the subscription of witnesses, we, Przemysł the Second, by the grace of God Duke of Greater Poland, wish it to be known to all the faithful of Christ, present as well as to come, that the venerable abbot Lord Gerhard of Ląd and his convent made an exchange with our beloved under-chamberlain Count Sędziwój for the good of their house, with our gracious favour and consent, giving him three estates, namely Głowiew, Sławsk and Chocicza, for the estate called Kopojno, which the same Sędziwój had held by inheritance, exchanged with us for his patrimony, namely Górczyn, free and exempt from every Polish right by whatever name it is called; the sons of the said Sędziwój, namely Janko and Marcin, to whom the hereditary right belonged, resigning the said estate before us and yielding up the hereditary right, and the said abbot with his brothers entering the said estate of Kopojno to possess it by inheritance. And we, desiring to increase the affection which we have always had for the house of Ląd, will that the said estate with its inhabitants, their own or free, shall be exempt and free, as it was before when it was held by the said Sędziwój: from every conveyance, from carting, from watch, from tribute, from the naraz, from the podworowe, from market and bridge tolls, from the building of castles or of any bridges, from receiving, guarding or escorting beaver-keepers (sic), for we have given the beavers to the lord abbot and his brothers to hunt for ever. Moreover from the neighbourhood which is called in the common tongue opole, from receiving the under-huntsman or huntsmen of any kind, from the guarding or payment of falcons, which is popularly called socol, from every exaction or levy which is called povolove, from carting and military expeditions, and from all the rights established in Poland by whatever names they are called. We have given the said brothers, through their own judge, free authority to carry out all judgements, such as of strife, striking, maiming of limbs, or even beheading, so that we may have the greater trust towards God at the dreadful judgement, reserving to no castellan or judge any right to summon or judge in that estate. And that this our deed, to the honour of God and of holy Mary ever Virgin, may obtain the strength of lasting firmness, we strengthen the present page with the protection of our seal. Witnesses of this are: Filip, archdeacon of Gniezno; Berwold, castellan of Kalisz; Abraham, treasurer of Gniezno. These things were done in the year of the Lord 1291 at Gniezno, on the day of the Eleven Thousand Virgins.

Lenda, Ląd. Glovevo, Głowiew. Slavsco, Sławsk. Chociczha, Chocicza. Copaino, Kopojno. Gorcino, Górczyn.
