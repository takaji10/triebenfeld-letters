--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Die Regierung zu Posen berichtet auf das Reskript vom 7., eingegangen am 16. Dezember: In der Sache des Vormunds der Michalina von Prusimska gegen den Fürsten von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen ist bereits am 9. Dezember in erster Instanz erkannt worden, weil die Kabinettsordre vom 1. Juli die Beschleunigung besonders empfohlen hatte. Wird ein Rechtsmittel eingelegt, will sie die Akten an den Oberappellationssenat des Kammergerichts zur Entscheidung in zweiter Instanz einsenden. Die empfangende Behörde verfügt am 31. Dezember 1800, der Regierung zu Warschau mitzuteilen, dass das Reskript vom 7. Dezember keine Anwendung finde.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The Government at Poznań reports on the rescript of the 7th, received on 16 December: in the case of Michalina von Prusimska's guardian against the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, judgment in the first instance was already given on 9 December, because the cabinet order of 1 July had especially urged speed. If an appeal is lodged, it will send the files to the Senate of Higher Appeal of the Kammergericht for decision in the second instance. The receiving office decrees on 31 December 1800 that the Government at Warsaw be told the rescript of 7 December does not apply.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
In reply to the most gracious rescript of the 7th, received the 16th of this month, in the case of the guardian of Michalina von Prusimska against the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, we do not fail hereby most humbly to report that judgment has already been given in this case in the first instance, on the 9th of this month, since the most exalted cabinet order of 1 July of this year, of which we sent a copy to Your Royal Majesty's court with our report of the 10th of the same month, especially enjoined on us the hastening of this case. Should a legal remedy be lodged against the judgment drawn up by us, we shall not fail dutifully to comply with the high instruction cited, and, once the preparation of the case is complete, to send the files to the Superior Appeal Senate of the Kammergericht for judgment in the second instance.

Poznań, 18 Dec. 1800.

The Government
