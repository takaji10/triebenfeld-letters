--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Johann Wilhelm Glenck, Bau- und Ökonomierat des Fürsten zu Hohenlohe, schreibt dem König: Die Regierung zu Poznań habe die Berichtigung des Besitztitels der fürstlichen Güter als nötig und eilig gefordert, und der Fürst habe die fehlenden Schenkungsurkunden im Original eingeschickt. Im Namen des Fürsten bittet er inständig um die schleunigste Abtretung aller fürstlichen Güter in Kujawien und bei Pszczew durch den Fiskus.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Johann Wilhelm Glenck, the Prince of Hohenlohe's councillor for building and estate management, writes to the King: the Government at Poznań, he says, demanded as necessary and urgent that the title to the Prince's estates be put in order, and the Prince sent in the missing deeds of gift in the original. In the Prince's name he asks urgently for the speediest formal surrender by the fisc of all the Prince's estates in Cujavia and at Pszczew.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene, Most Mighty King, most gracious King and Lord!

Already a considerable time ago a Royal Government at Poznań demanded from His Serene Highness the rectification of the titulus possessionis of the princely Hohenlohe estates as necessary and urgent, whereupon His Highness the Prince sent in to Your Majesty the deeds of donation wanting for this purpose in the original. As much, then, as His Serene Highness has been pressed in this matter to the speedy rectification of the titulus possessionis, so earnestly does he since look [uncertain: forward] to the conclusion of this affair, and I have now been charged in the name of His Serene Highness my Lord to beg Your Majesty most humbly and most urgently for the most speedy resignation by the Treasury (Fiscus) of the whole of the princely estates in Cujavia and Pszczew. I remain, until death, in deepest reverence

Your Royal Majesty's

Pszczew, the 13th of February 1802.

most humble and obedient

J. Wilhelm Glenck, princely Hohenlohe Councillor of Building and Economy
