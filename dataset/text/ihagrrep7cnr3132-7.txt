--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Eine Abschrift des Reskripts im Namen des Königs an die südpreußische Regierung zu Posen, gefolgt auf derselben Seite von der Resolution im Namen des Königs an die beiden Supplikanten, beide Berlin, 11. Mai 1803, jeweils unterzeichnet Goldbeck und Haugwitz. Das Reskript gibt die 6 Bände Akten in der Untersuchungssache gegen den ehemaligen Starosten Anton v. Prusimski und den ehemaligen Kanonikus Xaver von Jezierski zurück, die die Regierung mit ihrem Bericht vom 24. März eingeschickt hatte, und übersendet eine Abschrift der Kabinettsordre vom 10. Mai, durch die beiden die Erlaubnis zur Rückkehr in ihr Vaterland erteilt und die Vermögenskonfiskation samt ihrer neunjährigen Verbannung für eine hinreichende Bestrafung ihrer Teilnahme an der Insurrektion erachtet wird. Die Resolution, an den ehemaligen Starosten Anton v. Prusimski und Xaver von Jezierski zu Dresden gerichtet, antwortet auf ihre Immediatvorstellung vom 27. Januar desselben Jahres. Ihnen wird die Rückkehr in ihr Vaterland bewilligt, in der Voraussetzung, dass sie sich dort als [getreue?] Untertanen betragen; die rechtskräftig erkannte Vermögenskonfiskation bleibt unabänderlich bestehen, und Jezierski bleibt seiner geistlichen Ämter und Pfründen verlustig; sonst haben sie keine weitere Untersuchung oder Bestrafung wegen ihrer Teilnahme an der Insurrektion zu befürchten. An die Regierung zu Posen ist am selben Tag das Nötige ergangen.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A copy of the rescript in the King's name to the South Prussian Government at Poznań, followed on the same page by the resolution in the King's name to the two petitioners, both Berlin, 11 May 1803, each signed Goldbeck and Haugwitz. The rescript returns the 6 volumes of files in the inquiry against the former Starost Anton v. Prusimski and the former canon Xaver von Jezierski, which the Government had sent in with its report of 24 March, and sends a copy of the cabinet order of 10 May, by which both are granted leave to return to their fatherland, the confiscation of their property together with their nine years' banishment being deemed sufficient punishment for their part in the insurrection. The resolution, addressed to the former Starost Anton v. Prusimski and Xaver von Jezierski at Dresden, answers their immediate representation of 27 January of that year. They are granted leave to return to their fatherland, on the assumption that they will conduct themselves there as [loyal?] subjects; the confiscation of their property decreed with final effect stands unalterably, and Jezierski remains deprived of his clerical offices and benefices; otherwise they need fear no further inquiry or punishment for their part in the insurrection. The necessary has been ordered to the Government at Poznań the same day.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Copy.

Friedrich Wilhelm etc.

Our etc. In returning to you the 6 vol. of files in the matter of the inquiry against the former Starost Anton v. Prusimski and the former canon Xaver von Jezierski, sent in with your report of the 24th of March of this year, we at the same time send you, in the copy enclosed, the cabinet order issued on the 10th of last month, from which you will see that both have been granted the leave they asked for to return to their fatherland, and that the confiscation of property decreed against them, together with their 9 years' banishment, has been deemed a sufficient punishment for their part in the insurrection. [We] are etc. Berlin, 11 May 1803.

Auzl etc. special command.

Goldbeck Haugwitz

To the South Prussian Government at Poznań

His Royal Majesty of Prussia, our most gracious Lord, hereby lets Anton v. Prusimski and Xaver von Jezierski know, on their immediate representation of 27 Jan. of this year, that the leave they asked for to return to their fatherland has been granted, on the assumption that they will conduct themselves there in future as loyal[?] subjects; that the confiscation of property decreed against them with final effect remains, however, unalterably in force, and that von

Jezierski must remain deprived of his clerical offices and benefices; but that otherwise they need fear no further inquiry or punishment for their part in the insurrection, on which the necessary has been ordered to the Government at Poznań this day.

Berlin, 11 May 1803.

Auzl.[?] etc. special command.

Goldbeck Haugwitz

To the former Starost Anton v. Prusimski and Xaver von Jezierski at Dresden.
