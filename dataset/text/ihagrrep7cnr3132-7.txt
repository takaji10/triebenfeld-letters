--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Eine Abschrift des Reskripts im Namen des Königs an die südpreußische Regierung zu Posen, Berlin, 11. Mai 1803, unterzeichnet Goldbeck und Haugwitz. Es gibt die 6 Bände Akten in der Untersuchungssache gegen den ehemaligen Starosten Anton v. Prusimski und den ehemaligen Kanonikus Xaver von Jezierski zurück, die die Regierung mit ihrem Bericht vom 24. März eingeschickt hatte, und übersendet eine Abschrift der Kabinettsordre vom 10. Mai, durch die beiden die Erlaubnis zur Rückkehr in ihr Vaterland erteilt und die Vermögenskonfiskation samt ihrer neunjährigen Verbannung für eine hinreichende Bestrafung ihrer Teilnahme an der Insurrektion erachtet wird.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A copy of the rescript in the King's name to the South Prussian Government at Poznań, Berlin, 11 May 1803, signed Goldbeck and Haugwitz. It returns the 6 volumes of files in the inquiry against the former Starost Anton v. Prusimski and the former canon Xaver von Jezierski, which the Government had sent in with its report of 24 March, and sends a copy of the cabinet order of 10 May, by which both are granted leave to return to their fatherland, the confiscation of their property together with their nine years' banishment being deemed sufficient punishment for their part in the insurrection.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Copy.

Friedrich Wilhelm etc.

Our etc. In returning to you the 6 vol. of files in the matter of the inquiry against the former Starost Anton v. Prusimski and the former canon Xaver von Jezierski, sent in with your report of the 24th of March of this year, we at the same time send you, in the copy enclosed, the cabinet order issued on the 10th of last month, from which you will see that both have been granted the leave they asked for to return to their fatherland, and that the confiscation of property decreed against them, together with their 9 years' banishment, has been deemed a sufficient punishment for their part in the insurrection. Sind etc. Berlin, 11 May 1803.

Auzl etc. special command.

Goldbeck Haugwitz

To the South Prussian Government at Poznań
