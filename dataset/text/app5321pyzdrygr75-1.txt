--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Eintrag Nr. 39 des Registers des Burggerichts und -amts Pyzdry, lateinisch, mit einer polnischen Schlussformel: Der Kaufmann Jan Tracholz aus Witkowo erklärt vor dem Gericht, dass er an Stanisław Chełmski, der ihn dafür bezahlt, seine eigenen Geldforderungen an den verstorbenen Paweł Prusimski abtritt. Die erste Summe, 1053 Imperialen, 13 Groschen und (wie es scheint) 6 Fenige, betrifft Bäume aus den Kiefernwäldern von Trąbczyn, die er aufgrund eines schriftlichen Vertrags gekauft hatte und die Prusimski ihm gewaltsam wegnahm und an einen Kaufmann Simon aus Stettin verkaufte. Die zweite, 1513 Gulden, hatte er teils für die Herstellung von Schnittware (neun Stapel) aufgewendet, die Pauls leiblicher Bruder Antoni Prusimski ebenfalls wegnahm, teils zu Pauls Nutzen ausgelegt. Mit den Summen gehen die Rechte daran, die Zinsen und das von Tracholz gegen die Prusimski-Nachfolger eingeleitete Verfahren über. Tracholz unterschreibt mit „gehaltener Hand“. Der Eintrag trägt auf seinen Seiten kein eigenes Datum.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Entry no. 39 of the register of the grod court and office of Pyzdry, in Latin with a Polish closing formula: the merchant Jan Tracholz of Witkowo declares before the court that he makes over to Stanisław Chełmski, who pays him for them, his own monetary claims against the late Paweł Prusimski. The first sum, 1,053 imperials, 13 groschen and (it seems) 6 pfennigs, concerns trees from the pine forests of Trąbczyn that he had bought under a written contract and which Prusimski seized from him by force and sold to a merchant Simon of Szczecin. The second, 1,513 florins, he had spent partly on the making of sawn goods (nine stacks), which Paweł's full brother Antoni Prusimski likewise seized, and partly laid out for Paweł's benefit. With the sums pass the rights to them, the interest, and the proceedings Tracholz had begun against the Prusimski successors. Tracholz signs "by his held hand". The entry carries no date of its own on its pages.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
No. 39: The Excellent Jan Tracholz, merchant and inhabitant of Witkowo, sound in mind and body, openly, withdrawing, etc., acknowledged that he himself, wholly and entirely, his own monetary sums, one of them being 1,053 imperials, 13 groschen and 6 pfennigs[?], owed through the late Right Honourable Paweł Prusimski, for trees bought by the present acknowledger, the honourable Tracholz, in the pine forests and woods of Trąbczyn by means of a contract written out for the said late Right Honourable Prusimski for the purchase of those same trees [...], worked out from the honourable acknowledger Tracholz's own funds and care (although the same late Right Honourable Prusimski had once received from the same acknowledger full satisfaction according to the tenor of the aforesaid contract), the trees having been seized from the same acknowledger by force in the said pine forests and woods of Trąbczyn by this late Right Honourable Prusimski and sold by the same Right Honourable Prusimski to the honourable Simon, merchant of Szczecin; received in this amount (as the written reversal of the same honourable Simon, merchant of Szczecin, commonly called an *Auscuk* [Auszug], attests);

and then a second sum, of 1,513 florins, spent by the same honourable Tracholz, the present acknowledger, partly on the making of sawn goods, that is, *Klapek*, nine stacks, commonly called *Rynek*, likewise seized by the late Right Honourable Antoni Prusimski, full brother of the same late Right Honourable Paweł Prusimski, by reason of what followed upon the pine forests and woods in controversy and inquiry between them, seized in this pine forest; spent by the acknowledger, the honourable Tracholz, and partly laid out for the benefit and interest of the said late Right Honourable Paweł Prusimski, set out in a separate[?] register written by this acknowledger, and received for these sawn goods by this late Right Honourable Antoni Prusimski and disposed of for [...] and for his own benefit; and other sums, [shown] by writings and documents, handwritten ones and registers,

and also explained by various annotations, called [...]; these sums belonging to the acknowledger by right of legacy, reserving and excepting nothing from the aforesaid sums for himself and his successors thereafter, he has ceded, conferred, transposed, transferred and made over (as he cedes, confers, transposes, transfers and makes over) into the person of the Right Honourable Stanisław Chełmski, as one paying these sums to the acknowledger by his own right[?] and fully satisfying him for the foregoing, and to his successors, with full, total and entire right, dominion, ownership and competence, together with any and all rights of claiming that relate to these sums and set these sums out, together[?] with the interest arising from the same principal sums and with[?] the legal process instituted by the acknowledger against the successors of the Prusimskis; and by the present act [...] and conceding [...], this his personal acknowledgement freely assenting to the foregoing.

Jan Tracholz, by his held hand

On the transfer of his right in the person of M. Chełmski
