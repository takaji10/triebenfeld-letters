--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Protest von Piotr Gosławski als Bevollmächtigtem von Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice, gegen ein Dekret des Kalischer Landgerichts, eingetragen in Konin 1782 und von ihm unterschrieben. Vor dem Landgericht in Konin habe sein Auftraggeber am 15. Oktober des Vorjahres ein Zeugnis von Franciszek Ksawery Kęszycki, Marschall des Krontribunals, vom 13. Oktober vorgelegt und um Aufschub gebeten. Das Landgericht habe das Zeugnis nicht beachtet und Aufschub und Antrag nicht zugelassen; der Vertreter habe sich, ohne Urkunden, zurückziehen müssen. Gosławski protestiert gegen das Landgericht und die Gegenpartei und kündigt an, die Aufhebung des Dekrets und der erwirkten Verurteilung zu betreiben.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A protest of Piotr Gosławski, as plenipotentiary of Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, against a decree of the Kalisz land court, entered at Konin in 1782 and signed by him. Before the land court at Konin on 15 October of the year before, his principal laid down a certificate of Franciszek Ksawery Kęszycki, Marshal of the Crown Tribunal, of 13 October, and asked for a delay. The land court disregarded the certificate and admitted neither the delay nor the motion; the representative, without the papers, had to withdraw. Gosławski protests against the land court and the other party and announces that he will seek to have the decree and the condemnation obtained under it quashed.

The Plenipotentiary of the Illustrious Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, against a Decree of the Kalisz Land Court — a Manifest is lodged

Having come in person to the present Office and Records of the Konin Municipal Court, the Honourable Piotr Gosławski, in the name of the Illustrious Magnificent Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice (for whom he provides surety), acting as Plenipotentiary, lodges a solemn Complaint in the present Records — for the following reason: that although his Manifesting Principal, in the Land Courts of the Kalisz Palatinate sitting by district in Konin, on Monday, on the very day of the Feast of Saint Hedwig, Widow, Elect, namely the 15th day of the month of October in the past year, when [the court] was held, had appeared through his Plenipotentiary and had produced an attestation issued by the Illustrious Magnificent Franciszek Ksawery Nałęcz Kęszycki, Marshal of the Crown Tribunal, on the 13th day of October in the past year, and had sought a delay to be granted to him for [establishing] the principal matter — nonetheless the Kalisz Land Court, paying no attention whatsoever

to the above-mentioned attestation, had ordered [him] to prove the principal matter; and when the Appearing [party], on behalf of the Manifesting [party], had cited [the existence of] a term before the Crown Tribunal at Piotrków issued against the Illustrious Chełmski, as proof of the cause in the Tribunal Court, [se se [?]erte[uncertain: r]nem] — that the cause, from the Records of the Palatinates of Greater Poland to be promoted to the Tribunal Courts, is judged in favour of the Illustrious Zakrzewskis and others — not heedfully noting for himself the [Kingdom's] Expulsions even at the time of the Register of the [Rlocen~] Palatinate, judging itself, from which [time] his Manifesting Principal had obtained a judgment in the Crown Tribunal Court at Piotrków — [the Land Court] had not admitted the delay and motion interposed by his Appearing [party]. The Appearing [party] of the Manifesting Principal, therefore, lacking documents, was compelled to withdraw from the appearance.

Therefore the Manifesting [party], in the name of the same person as above, against the Kalisz Land Court and the party presenting the above-mentioned grievances, manifests again and again — offering, on behalf of his Principal, regarding the matters promised by law of the Acts, both the cassation of the Decree ordering [its production] and [redress for] the coercively obtained Condemnation — wishing then to request that penalties for the above-mentioned interests be extended — saving all that is to be saved.

Petrus Gosławski, as [illegible]
