--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Abschrift einer Urkunde der Südpreußischen Regierung in Kalisch vom 5. März 1805, im Namen des Königs ausgestellt. Vor dem Regierungsrat Stosch als Kommissar ist Triebenfeld am 27. Februar 1805 in Kalisch erschienen und hat seine Schuld- und Verpfändungsurkunde vom 28. Januar über 50.000 Taler aus der General-Invalidenkasse nochmals anerkannt, ebenso das unter der Obligation stehende Anerkennungsprotokoll; der Regierungsrat von Lichnowski bezeugte seine Identität. Darunter steht der Vermerk des Ingrossators Beda vom 13. März 1805: Aufgrund eines Dekrets vom 5. des Monats sind die 50.000 Taler, zu 5 Prozent verzinslich und nach sechsmonatiger Kündigung zahlbar, im Hypothekenbuch des Koniner Kreises auf Zagórów, Drzewce und Kopojno für die Invalidenkasse eingetragen worden.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A copy of a certificate of the South Prussian Government at Kalisz of 5 March 1805, issued in the King's name. Before the Government Councillor Stosch as commissary, Triebenfeld appeared at Kalisz on 27 February 1805 and once more acknowledged his deed of debt and pledge of 28 January for 50,000 Thaler from the General Invalids' Fund, and likewise the protocol of acknowledgement standing under the bond; the Government Councillor von Lichnowski vouched for his identity. Below it stands the note of the Ingrossator Beda of 13 March 1805: on the ground of a decree of the 5th of the month the 50,000 Thaler, bearing 5 per cent interest and payable after six months' notice, have been entered for the Invalids' Fund in the mortgage book of the Konin district upon Zagórów, Drzewce and Kopojno.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
We Friedrich Wilhelm, by the Grace of God King of Prussia etc., make known and hereby give to be understood: that coram Commissario, Our Government Councillor Stosch, the Councillor of War and Forests Peter Friedrich August von Triebenfeld appeared in person and once more acknowledged the instrument of debt and pledge made out under the 28 January of the current year to the General Invalids' Fund for 50,000. Rthl, as the following protocol states:

Actum Kalisz, the 27th of February 1805.

There presented himself before the undersigned commissaries the Royal Councillor of War and Forests Peter Friedrich August von Triebenfeld, who is identified by the Government Councillor von Lichnowski, also present, and says.

He hereby once more approves the instrument of bond and respective cession made out under the 28th of January of this year for the sum of 50,000. Rthl, in words fifty thousand Reichsthaler, which sum he has received as a loan out of the General Invalids' Fund on the part of the Royal Supreme War College. He remarked also that the protocol of acknowledgement standing under this bond was made out by him, and that he likewise once more approves the same according to its whole content

Since there was now nothing further to remark, the protocol was, after being read out and

approved, closed and subscribed

signed: Peter Friedrich August von Triebenfeld

signed von Lichnowski as witness of identity.

a. u. s.

signed: Stosch qua Commissary.

signed: von Zelislawski

In witness whereof, under the greater seal of the South Prussian Government and signature. Given at Kalisz, the 5th of March 1805. (L. S.)

signed Schiller
