Ex Parte MM Gałecki Capitanei Bidgostien~ Skorzewski Succamerarius Posnan~ contra Illustrem Magnificum Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen~ Relatio
Videatur hoc loco Relatio ex Parte Illustris Magnifici Ignatii Gałecki Capitanei Bidgostien~ anteacti et Illustris Magnifici Michaelis Drogosław Skorzewski Succamerariuu Posnaniensis Praesentanei Bonorum Biskupice Haearedum contra Illustrem Magnificum Antonium Prusimski Capitaneum Nieszczevicencem pro Judiciis Orind~ Generalibus Tribunalis Regni Petricovien~ prout Copia in productis

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Registervermerk von 1781: Bericht über die Ladung, die Ignacy Gałecki, Starost von Bromberg, und Michał Drogosław Skórzewski, Unterkämmerer von Posen, der frühere und der jetzige Erbherr von Biskupice, gegen Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice, vor das Krontribunal in Petrikau erwirkt haben.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A register note of 1781: the report of the citation that Ignacy Gałecki, Starost of Bydgoszcz, and Michał Drogosław Skórzewski, sub-chamberlain of Poznań, the former and the present heir of Biskupice, obtained against Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, before the Crown Tribunal at Piotrków.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
On behalf of the Illustrious Gałecki, Starost of Bydgoszcz, and Skorzewski, Sub-chamberlain of Poznań, against the Illustrious Magnificent Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — a Report

Let there be seen at this place a Report on behalf of the Illustrious Magnificent Ignacy Gałecki, Starost of Bydgoszcz, the former heir, and the Illustrious Magnificent Michał Drogosław Skorzewski, Sub-chamberlain of Poznań, the present heir of the estate of Biskupice — against the Illustrious Magnificent Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — for the Ordinary General Sessions of the Crown Tribunal of the Kingdom at Piotrków — as the copy in the submitted documents [attests].
