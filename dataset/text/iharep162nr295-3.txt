--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Abschrift des Hypothekenscheins über die Stadt Zagórów im Koniner Kreis, den die Südpreußische Regierung in Kalisch am 13. März 1805 der General-Invalidenkasse erteilt (Nr. 1179). Der Fürst zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen besitzt das Gut durch die Verleihungsurkunde König Friedrich Wilhelms II. vom 19. Juni 1797; sein Besitztitel ist durch Dekret vom 31. Juli 1801 eingetragen. Als dauernde Lasten stehen darauf: eine jährliche Abgabe von 88 Reichstalern 5 Groschen 9 Pfennig an die Kalischer Kriegs- und Domänenkasse; ein Vorbehalt des Fiskus wegen Bau- und Brennholz aus den Forsten des Gutes nach der Einziehung der geistlichen Güter; und ein Vorbehalt für Johann Giese, Johann Bagans und Friedrich Gietzinger wegen der Holz- und Weiderechte, die ihnen ihr Erbpachtvertrag über Oleśnica vom 22. Februar 1804 in den Zagórówer Forsten gibt. Als Schulden stehen darauf: ein Vorbehalt für den Landesältesten von Lichnowski in Brieg wegen einer schon auf Trąbczyn eingetragenen Forderung von 32.300 Reichstalern; 250.000 Reichstaler für Triebenfeld aus der Schuldverschreibung des Fürsten vom 1. Juni 1804; und davon 50.000 Reichstaler, die Triebenfeld der Invalidenkasse verpfändet und abgetreten hat, mit Vorrang vor dem Rest. Die letzte Zeile beginnt wie dieser Schein und bricht ab.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A copy of the mortgage certificate for the town of Zagórów in the Konin district, which the South Prussian Government at Kalisz issues to the General Invalids' Fund on 13 March 1805 (No. 1179). The Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen holds the estate by the deed of grant of King Friedrich Wilhelm II of 19 June 1797; his title of possession was registered by a decree of 31 July 1801. As perpetual charges there stand on it: a yearly due of 88 Reichsthaler 5 Groschen 9 Pfennig to the Kalisz Treasury of War and Domains; a caveat of the Treasury for building timber and firewood from the estate's forests, after the confiscation of the ecclesiastical estates; and a caveat for Johann Giese, Johann Bagans and Friedrich Gietzinger for the rights of wood and pasture that their hereditary lease of Oleśnica of 22 February 1804 gives them in the Zagórów forests. As debts there stand on it: a caveat for the Provincial Elder von Lichnowski at Brzeg for a claim of 32,300 Reichsthaler already registered on Trąbczyn; 250,000 Reichsthaler for Triebenfeld under the Prince's bond of 1 June 1804; and 50,000 Reichsthaler of these which Triebenfeld has pledged and ceded to the Invalids' Fund, with priority over the remainder. The last line opens like this certificate and breaks off.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
(6 Groschen stamp)

The town of Zagórów, situated in the province of South Prussia and its Konin district, was made over to the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Friedrich Ludwig, His Serene Highness, by His Majesty King Friedrich Wilhelm the IInd, by the deed of grant dated Berlin, the 19th of June 1797, with hereditary and proprietary rights, and the title of possession (titulus possessionis) has been registered for the same ex decreto of the 31st of July 1801.

Thereupon are registered

I. onera perpetua, restrictions of the property or of disposition.

1., — is struck out.

2., 88. Rthl. 5. Groschen 9. Pfennig, i. e. Eighty-eight Rthl. 5 Groschen 9 Pfennig

Prussian Courant, are paid yearly out of this estate as a due to the Kalisz Treasury of War and Domains; which has been registered ex officio, on the ground of the application of the Chamber of War and Domains at Kalisz dated Kalisz, the 18th of November 1802, by virtue of a decree of the 14th of December 1802.

3. A protestation pro fisco camerae, on account of the right belonging to it, on the ground of the confiscation of the ecclesiastical estates that has taken place, to demand the right of building timber and firewood in the forests belonging to this estate and the payment of the surplus forest due in cash, has been registered on the application of the mandatary of the Treasury by virtue of a decree of 25. January 1803.

4. A protestation for Johann Giese, Johann Bagans and Friedrich Gietzinger, on account of the right stipulated to them in the hereditary-lease contract concluded with the owner under the 22nd of February 1804 concerning the estate of Olesnica, to enjoy free gathered and fallen wood and yearly

4. days' free digging of pine stumps in the Zagórów forests; and that, should the lordship find it necessary to assign a certain district for this, this district may not be under 330. Morgen, and that those hereditary lessees shall be entitled to pasture their sheep in this district, has been provisionally noted on the application of those named at the beginning, by virtue of a decree of the 18th of December 1804.

II. Judicially secured debts and other real obligations

1., A protestation pro salvando jure et loco for the Provincial Elder von Lichnowski at Brzeg, on account of a claim belonging to him against the possessor, the Prince of Hohenlohe, and already registered upon Trąbczyn, of 32,300. Rthl, in words two and Thirty thousand three hundred Rthl, bearing interest at 5. per cent; has been registered ex decreto of the 24th of January 1804

2., 250,000. Rthl, i.e. Two hundred and fifty thousand Rthl in Prussian Courant, at 5. per cent yearly interest, and after the lapse of one year subject to a term of 6. months' notice free to both parties, have been registered as a loan, from the owner's bond,

dated Wrocław, the 1st of June 1804, for the Councillor of War and Forests Peter Friedrich August von Triebenfeld, by virtue of a decree of the 5th of June 1804, in consequence of the debtor's application to that effect, upon the estates of the town of Zagórów, Kopojno, Świątniki, Skokum, Drzewce, Olesnica, Wrąbczyn, Grądzyń, which are liable at the same time for costs and damages.

ad Nº 2., 50,000. Rthl, i.e. fifty thousand Rthl of the sum of 250,000. Rthl registered above sub No 2. have been pledged, and eventualiter ceded in property, by its holder, the Royal Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld, to the Royal General Invalids' Fund, on account of a loan of the same amount received from the same at 5 per cent yearly interest, payable free of cost at Berlin in half-yearly instalments, and under a term of 6. months' notice on either side, according to the deed made out thereon dated Berlin, the 28th of January 1805, which has been registered by virtue of a decree of the 5th of March 1805, with the remark that for this loan of the 50,000 Rthl priority prae residuo has been

constituted.

Besides the foregoing items nothing is registered upon the estate of Zagórów in the mortgage book, and for the rest this mortgage certificate is hereby issued to the Royal General Invalids' Fund in vim recognitionis, on account of the 50,000. Rthl sub-registered for the same ex documento de 28. January of the current year upon the said estate Rubr. III. ad No. 2. In witness whereof, under the greater seal of the Royal South Prussian Government and the customary signature:

Given at Kalisz, the 13th of March 1805. signed: Danckelmann.
