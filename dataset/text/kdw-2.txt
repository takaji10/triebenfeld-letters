Dokument Nr 222
Fulco Gneznens. ecclesie archiepiscop. 1240 (sic) Sept. 30, s. l.; confert monasterio de Ląd decimas capellarum s. Petri et s. Andreae.
Poznań. Königl. Staats Arch. Ląd, Lib. privil. B, Nr. 38.
In nomine Domini amen. Cum ante tribunal Christi stare debeamus in die novissimo, sive bonum sive malum prout in corpore gesserimus recepturi, opportet ut ad eterne vite patriam per opera misericordie nobis semitam preparemus. Igitur nos Fulco Dei gracia sancte Gneznensis ecclesie archiepiscopus notum esse volumus universis presentem paginam inspecturis, quod cum illustris princeps dominus Vladislaus filius Odonis dux Polonie apud cenobium Landense pridie Remigii cum suis baronibus negocia terre ingruencia disponeret, accidit ut divinitus instinetus varias angarias abbatis fratrumque inibi Deo serviencium pietatis oculo consideraret, et locum castri quod dicebatur Lenda cum suis adiacenciis eis tribuens, capellas sancti Andree sanctique Petri, prout sui patronatus fuerant, devocione intentissima ipsorum ascriberet potestati. Cumque contingeret nos vocacione principis eiusdem huiusmodi colloquio una cum venerabili fratre nostro et coepiscopo domino Paulo Poznaniensi interesse, precibus predicti principis ac devotissima deprecacionum baronum instancia pulsati, abbatis fratrumque suorum paupertate miserabili medullitus compuncti, decimas dictarum capellarum Deo beateque Marie semper virgini pro remedio anime nostre ac successorum nostrorum usibus fratrum contulimus, prout nunc sunt ac in posterum esse poterunt perpetuo possidendas, nullam utilitatem excipientes que de nova ipsarum ampliacione poterit exoriri. Ut autem nostra tam devota donacio a nullo successorum nostrorum poterit irritari, nominetenus expressimus hereditates in quibus prelibate decime cum omnibus utilitatibus sunt cessure: de Zagorovo, Skokome, Kopanino, Trampczino, Szetlevo, Lukome, Wronczino; que decime quocunque iure excollantur, sive ville dividantur sive transponantur vel alio nomine nuncupentur, totum quod iuris nostri esse poterit, libenti animo fratrum nostrorum usibus condonamus. Igitur ut hec omnia rata et in perpetuum inconcussa permaneant, presens scriptum nostri et Capituli sigillorum appensione dilectissimique fratris nostri domini Pauli supranominati episcopi qui hiis interfuit communimus, fidelium nichilominus virorum attestacione roborantes quorum hec sunt nomina: Gotpoldus maior prepositus, Hugo decanus, Nicolaus custos, Venceslaus scolasticus, et quam plures alii fidedigni. Datum et actum anno Domini M.CC.XL(sic),II Kalendas Octobris, Indiccione XII.
Landensis, de Ląd. Zagurow, Skokom, dziś tak samo. Kopanino, późniéj Kopasino, dziś Kopojno. Trąbczyn, Szetlewo, Łukomie, dziś tak samo. Wronczino, dziś Wrąbczyn.

Erzbischof Fulko von Gnesen gibt dem Kloster Ląd die Zehnten der Kapellen St. Peter und St. Andreas, darunter die aus Zagórów, Skokum, Kopojno, Trąbczyn, Szetlew, Łukom und Wrąbczyn, auch wenn die Dörfer geteilt, verlegt oder anders genannt werden. Ohne Ort, 30. September 1240; die Herausgeber setzen „sic“ zum Jahr. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski I, Nr. 222, aus dem Privilegienbuch B von Ląd.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Archbishop Fulko of Gniezno gives the abbey of Ląd the tithes of the chapels of St Peter and St Andrew, among them those from Zagórów, Skokum, Kopojno, Trąbczyn, Szetlew, Łukom and Wrąbczyn, even if the villages are divided, moved or called by another name. No place, 30 September 1240; the editors mark the year "sic". Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski I, no. 222, from the Liber privilegiorum B of Ląd.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 222

Fulko, Archbishop of the church of Gniezno, 1240 (sic), 30 September, without place; gives the monastery of Ląd the tithes of the chapels of St Peter and St Andrew.

Poznań. Royal State Archive. Ląd, Liber privilegiorum B, no. 38.

In the name of the Lord, amen. Since we must stand before the judgement seat of Christ on the last day, to receive good or evil according to what we have done in the body, it is fitting that we prepare ourselves a path to the homeland of eternal life by works of mercy. Therefore we, Fulko, by the grace of God Archbishop of the holy church of Gniezno, wish it to be known to all who will look on this page that, when the illustrious prince Lord Władysław, son of Odo, Duke of Poland, was settling the pressing business of the land with his barons at the monastery of Ląd on the day before St Remigius, it happened that, moved by God, he considered with the eye of piety the many burdens of the abbot and of the brothers serving God there, and, giving them the site of the castle that was called Lenda with its appurtenances, he assigned with most earnest devotion to their authority the chapels of St Andrew and St Peter, which had been of his patronage. And since it happened that we, called by the same prince, were present at this assembly together with our venerable brother and fellow bishop Lord Paweł of Poznań, moved by the prayers of the said prince and the most devoted entreaties of the barons, and pierced to the heart by the wretched poverty of the abbot and his brothers, we have given the tithes of the said chapels to God and to the blessed Mary ever Virgin, for the remedy of our soul and of our successors, for the use of the brothers, to be possessed for ever as they now are and as they may be in future, excepting no profit that may arise from their enlargement. And so that our devout gift may not be undone by any of our successors, we have named the estates in which the said tithes, with all their profits, are to fall to them: from Zagórów, Skokum, Kopojno, Trąbczyn, Szetlew, Łukom and Wrąbczyn; which tithes, by whatever right they are raised, whether the villages are divided or moved or called by another name, we give gladly, whatever of our right there may be, for the use of our brothers. And that all this may remain ratified and unshaken for ever, we strengthen the present writing by hanging our seal and that of the Chapter and that of our most beloved brother Lord Paweł, the bishop named above, who was present, and confirm it also by the witness of faithful men, whose names are these: Gotpold, the greater provost; Hugo, the dean; Mikołaj, the custodian; Wacław, the scholastic; and very many others worthy of trust. Given and done in the year of the Lord 1240 (sic), on the second day before the Kalends of October, in the twelfth indiction.

Landensis, of Ląd. Zagurow, Skokom, the same today. Kopanino, later Kopasino, today Kopojno. Trąbczyn, Szetlewo, Łukomie, the same today. Wronczino, today Wrąbczyn.
