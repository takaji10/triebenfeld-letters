Dokument Nr 1739
Preczslaus palatinus Kalisiens. ceterique iudices 1377 Mai. 27, Gnezdne; coram Ludovico rege protestantur, villas Wrąbczynek et Adamierz cum molendino prope civitatem Pyzdry sito, ab antiquo ad monasterium Ląd pertinere.
Pergam. orygin. Na skrawkach pergamin. przywieszono 6 małych okrągłych pieczęci. Pierwsza znikła; druga zupełnie zatarta; trzecia przedstawia na tarczy trójkątnéj godło rodu Porajów, napis: S. Iohannis d.Nancowo; czwarta przedstawia na tarczy trójkątnéj godło rodu Zarębów, napis: ...crol...; piąta ma wycisk odłupany; szósta przedstawia na trójkątnéj tarczy godło rodu Doliwów, napis zatarty.
Warszawa. Zbiory Rusieckich. Diplomata.
Magnifico principi Lodoyco Ungarie ac Polonie regi, Preczslaus palatinus, Thomislaus iudex, Iohannes castellanus, Nicolaus subcamerarius Kalisienses, Laurencius castellanus Poznaniensis et Ianusius castellanus Behoviensis humilima servicia ad quevis beneplacita mandatorum. Coram vestra regia maiestate ac universis et singulis presentibus recognoscimus publice profitentes: quod Parvum Wronczino cum Nadamirza villa adiacenti sita prope Pysdri, cum omnibus utilitatibus, videlicet agris, campis, pratis, aquis, silvis, borris et lacu dicto Prziborowo, nec non molendino sito sub civitate Pysdrensi, est de fundo monasterii Landensis Ordinis Cysterciensis: et protestamur palam et publice, quod eadem villa, videlicet Parvum Wronczino cum sua adiacenti, ab antiquo cum pleno et toto dominio ac omnibus iuribus, proventibus, fructibus et obvencionibus universis, semper spectavit, prout et nunc spectat, ad dictum monasterium Landense tamquam ipsius verus fundus. In evidenciam autem nostre protestacionis huiusmodi maiorem, sigillis nostris presentes fecimus communiri. Actum et datum Gnezdne in vigilia Corporis Christi, anno Domini millesimo ccc.lxx. septimo, nobis tunc ad terminos generales ibidem congregatis.
Behoviensis, de Biechowo. Parvum Wronczino, Wrąbczynek. Nadamirza, Adamierz. Lacus Prziborowo, dziś bagno na północ Wrąbczynka leżące. Landensis, de Ląd.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Przecław, Wojewode von Kalisch, und die anderen Richter erklären vor König Ludwig, dass die Dörfer Wrąbczynek und Adamierz mit der Mühle bei der Stadt Pyzdry seit alters dem Kloster Ląd gehören. Gnesen, 27. Mai 1377. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski III, Nr. 1739, nach dem Original mit sechs Siegeln.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Przecław, palatine of Kalisz, and the other judges declare before King Louis that the villages of Wrąbczynek and Adamierz, with the mill near the town of Pyzdry, have belonged to the abbey of Ląd from of old. Gniezno, 27 May 1377. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski III, no. 1739, from the original with six seals.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 1739

Przecław, palatine of Kalisz, and the other judges, 27 May 1377, at Gniezno; declare before King Louis that the villages of Wrąbczynek and Adamierz, with the mill lying near the town of Pyzdry, have belonged to the monastery of Ląd from of old.

Original on parchment. On strips of parchment 6 small round seals are hung. The first is lost; the second is wholly worn; the third shows on a triangular shield the arms of the Poraj family, legend: S. Iohannis d. Nancowo; the fourth shows on a triangular shield the arms of the Zaremba family, legend: ...crol...; the fifth has its impression chipped; the sixth shows on a triangular shield the arms of the Doliwa family, legend worn.

Warsaw. Collections of the Rusiecki family. Diplomata.

To the magnificent prince Louis, King of Hungary and of Poland, Przecław the palatine, Tomisław the judge, Jan the castellan, Mikołaj the under-chamberlain of Kalisz, Wawrzyniec, castellan of Poznań, and Janusz, castellan of Biechowo, their most humble service at every pleasure of his commands. Before your royal majesty and all and each who are present we acknowledge, publicly professing: that little Wrąbczyn with the village of Nadamirza adjoining it, lying near Pyzdry, with all its uses, namely fields, plains, meadows, waters, woods, forests and the lake called Prziborowo, and also the mill lying below the town of Pyzdry, is of the property of the monastery of Ląd of the Cistercian Order; and we declare openly and publicly that this same village, namely little Wrąbczyn with its adjoining village, has belonged from of old, with full and entire lordship and all rights, revenues, fruits and incomes whatsoever, always to the said monastery of Ląd as its true property, as it does now. And in greater evidence of this our declaration we have had the present letters strengthened with our seals. Done and given at Gniezno on the eve of Corpus Christi, in the year of the Lord one thousand three hundred and seventy-seven, we being then gathered there for the general courts.

Behoviensis, of Biechowo. Parvum Wronczino, Wrąbczynek. Nadamirza, Adamierz. Lacus Prziborowo, today a marsh lying north of Wrąbczynek. Landensis, of Ląd.
