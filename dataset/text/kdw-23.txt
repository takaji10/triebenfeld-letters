Dokument Nr 535
Secundus Premizl dux Polonie 1284 Febr. 18, in Kalis; concedit comiti Nicolao thezaurario de Ląd, ut hereditatem Trąbczyn iure Theutonico locare valeat.
Pergam. orygin., w którego dolnym założonym brzegu dwie dziurki od przywieszenia pieczęci. Warszawa. Zbiory Rusieckich. Diplomata. - Rzyszczew. Muczkow. Cod. dipl. Poloniae I, Nr. 64.
In nomine Domini amen. Dum vivit littera vivit et actio commissa littere, cuius assercio nutrit memoriam et roborata suscipit incrementum. Igitur nos secundus Premizl Dei gracia dux Polonie notum facimus universis tam presentibus quam futuris presentem paginam inspecturis, quod intuentes fidele servicium et utile dilecti militis nostri comitis Nicolay thezaurarii Lendensis, et eciam statum terre nostre cupientes in melius reformare, super sua hereditate que Tropcssino wlgariter nuncupatur sibi liberum dedimus arbitrium locandi ius Theutonicum, eo iure, quo omnes civitates et ville per districtum nostri dominii possidentur. Ita videlicet, ut eadem hereditas ab omnibus solucionibus Polonicis quibus nostrum potitur dominium perpetuo sit exempta: a naraz, a povoz, a prevod, a stroza, a podvorove, a castri citacione, ab opole, a bove, a vacca, a cifo mellis, et ab aliis omnibus solucionibus quibuscunque nominibus censeantur. Ut autem melius dicta hereditas informetur, incole eiusdem hereditatis poradlne solvere non teneantur. Ut autem hec nostra donacio perpetuo inviolabilis perseveret, presentem paginam nostri sigilli munimine duximus confirmandam presentibus hiis testibus: comite Precslao castellano Gneznensi, comite Andrea iudice Gneznensi, comite Sanzivoyo castellano Rudensi. Datum in Kalis anno gracie Domini MCCLXXXIIII, Indictione XII, feria sexta proxima ante dominicam Esto michi, per manus Thilonis notarii curie nostre.
Lendensis, de Ląd. Tropcssino, Trąbczyn. Rudensis, de Ruda.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Herzog Przemysł II. von Polen gibt Graf Mikołaj, Schatzmeister von Ląd, freie Gewalt, sein Gut Tropcssino (Trąbczyn) nach deutschem Recht auszusetzen, wie es allen Städten und Dörfern seines Gebiets zusteht, und befreit das Gut auf ewig von allen polnischen Abgaben: dem naraz, dem povoz, dem prevod, der stroza, dem podvorove, der Burgladung, dem opole, vom Ochsen und von der Kuh, vom Honigtopf; die Siedler sollen die poradlne nicht zahlen. Kalisz, 18. Februar 1284, durch die Hand des Hofnotars Thilo. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski I, Nr. 535, nach dem Original aus der Sammlung Rusiecki.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Duke Przemysł II of Poland gives Count Mikołaj, treasurer of Ląd, free authority to locate his estate of Tropcssino (Trąbczyn) under German law, as all the towns and villages of his district are held, and frees the estate for ever from all the Polish payments: the naraz, the povoz, the prevod, the stroza, the podvorove, the castle's summons, the opole, the ox and the cow, the cifo of honey; the settlers are not to pay the poradlne. Kalisz, 18 February 1284, by the hand of the court notary Thilo. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski I, no. 535, from the original in the Rusiecki collection.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 535

Przemysł the Second, Duke of Poland, 18 February 1284, at Kalisz; allows Count Mikołaj, treasurer of Ląd, to locate his estate of Trąbczyn under German law.

Original on parchment, in whose lower folded edge are two holes for the hanging of the seal. Warsaw. The Rusiecki collection. Diplomata. — Rzyszczew. Muczkowski, Codex diplomaticus Poloniae I, no. 64.

In the name of the Lord, amen. While the letter lives, the deed committed to the letter lives, whose assertion nourishes memory and, strengthened, takes increase. Therefore we, Przemysł the Second, by the grace of God Duke of Poland, make known to all, both present and to come, who examine this page, that, having regard to the faithful service and the usefulness of our beloved knight Count Mikołaj, treasurer of Ląd, and wishing also to reform the state of our land for the better, we have given him free authority to locate his estate, which is commonly called Tropcssino, under German law, by the same right by which all the towns and villages through the district of our lordship are held. Namely so, that the same estate be for ever exempt from all the Polish payments by which our lordship is won: from the naraz, the povoz, the prevod, the stroza, the podvorove, the castle's summons, the opole, the ox, the cow, the cifo of honey, and from all other payments under whatever names they are counted. And that the said estate may be shaped the better, the settlers of the same estate are not to pay the poradlne. And that this our gift may persevere inviolate for ever, we have had the present page confirmed with the protection of our seal, in the presence of these witnesses: Count Przecław, castellan of Gniezno, Count Andrzej, judge of Gniezno, Count Sędziwój, castellan of Ruda. Given at Kalisz in the year of the grace of the Lord 1284, in the twelfth indiction, on the Friday next before the Sunday Esto michi, by the hand of Thilo, notary of our court.

Lendensis, of Ląd. Tropcssino, Trąbczyn. Rudensis, of Ruda.
