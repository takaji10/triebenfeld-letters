Videatur hoc Loco Copia Manifestationis Certa ex parte Illustris Magnifici Prusimski Contra Magnificos Nosalskie facta prout patet Copia in Productis
Antoni Prusimski Star: Nieszczewski

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Registervermerk von 1779: Eine Abschrift des Protests, den Prusimski gegen die Nosalski eingelegt hat, liegt bei den vorgelegten Schriftstücken. Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice, hat unterschrieben.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A register note of 1779: a copy of the protest that Prusimski made against the Nosalskis is among the documents produced. Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, signed.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Let there be seen in this place a certain copy of a manifest made on behalf of the Most Honourable Prusimski against the Honourables Nosalski, as is apparent from the copy in the produced documents.

Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice
