--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Abschrift (Nr. 16 des Kopialbuchs, überschrieben „Confirmatio Gradziem Regis Casimiri“, die Bestätigung von Grądzyń durch König Kasimir) einer lateinischen Urkunde König Kasimirs von Polen, gegeben in Gnesen am Dienstag vor dem Fest des heiligen Laurentius; das Jahr ist überschrieben und als 1447 gelesen, wie eine spätere Hand es am Rand vermerkt hat, und die genannten Zeugen hatten ihre Ämter in diesem Jahr inne (8. August 1447). Der Abt Maciej von Ląd hat dem König vorgetragen, er habe mit den Brüdern Mikołaj, Jan und Warsz von Koscielec, Erben, und ihrem Stiefsohn einen Kaufvertrag über drei Hufen Ackerland im Dorf Łukomia im Bezirk Konin und den Wald Grądzyń geschlossen, mit weiteren Wäldern, Wiesen, Feldern, Heiden, Fischteichen, Bienenstöcken und Gewässern, ohne dass die Verkäufer sich ein Recht daran vorbehielten, und hat um seine Zustimmung gebeten. Der König stimmt zu und bestätigt den Vertrag auf ewig: um Himmlisches für Irdisches zu erwerben und den Gottesdienst im Kloster zu mehren, vor allem aber, weil dem Kloster anderswo die Wälder fehlen, mit denen es wiederhergestellt werden könnte, und damit es bei seinen Besuchen dort besser versorgt sei. Unter den Zeugen sind der Bischof Andrzej, der Woiwode von Posen Łukasz von Górka und der Kastellan von Sandomierz Przedbor von Koniecpol; gegeben durch den Kanzler Jan von Koniecpol. Der Text beginnt am Fuß von S. 14 und endet auf S. 15, die unter seinem Ende abgeschnitten ist.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A copy (no. 16 of the copy-book, headed "Confirmatio Gradziem Regis Casimiri", the confirmation of Grądzyń by King Kazimierz) of a Latin charter of King Kazimierz of Poland, given at Gniezno on the Tuesday before the feast of St Lawrence; the year is written over and read as 1447, as a later hand noted it in the margin, and the witnesses named held their offices in that year (8 August 1447). Abbot Maciej of Ląd has told the king that he made a contract of purchase with the brothers Mikołaj, Jan and Warsz of Koscielec, heirs, and their stepson, for three mansi of fields in the village of Łukomia in the district of Konin and the forest of Grądzyń, with further forests, meadows, fields, heaths, fishponds, beehives and waters, the sellers reserving no right in them, and has asked for his consent. The king consents and confirms the contract for ever: to buy heavenly things for earthly ones and to increase divine worship in the monastery, but above all because the monastery lacks forests elsewhere with which it might be restored, and so that it may be better provided when he visits. Among the witnesses are Bishop Andrzej, Łukasz of Górka, voivode of Poznań, and Przedbor of Koniecpol, castellan of Sandomierz; given by the chancellor, Jan of Koniecpol. The text begins at the foot of p. 14 and ends on p. 15, which is cut below its end.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Confirmation of Grądzyń by King Kazimierz. 16.

In the name of the Lord, Amen. For the perpetual memory of the matter. We, Kazimierz, by the grace of God King of Poland, and also of the lands of Kraków, Sandomierz, Sieradz, Łęczyca and Kuyavia, Grand Duke of Lithuania, lord and heir of Pomerania and Prussia etc., make known by the tenor of these presents to all whom it concerns, present and to come, who will have knowledge of them, how, coming to our presence and that of our prelates and barons

the venerable and religious Maciej, abbot of the monastery of Ląd, our devout and beloved, made known to us by his statement that he had made a contract of purchase with the noble Mikołaj, Jan and Warsz, brothers german of Koscielec, heirs, and with their stepson, for three mansi or łany of fields lying in the village of Łukomia in the district of Konin, and for the forest called Grądzyń, and also other forests and woods, meadows, fields, heaths, fishponds, beehives, waters and their courses, and all other profits, uses and revenues belonging in any way of old to the said three mansi or łany, as the heirs written above have possessed them until now, reserving to themselves and their successors no right of property or dominion in them; and humbly and devoutly besought our majesty with due instance that we might give our gracious consent and assent to the said contract of purchase. We, then, wishing to buy heavenly things for earthly ones and to enlarge divine worship in that venerable monastery of Ląd, and especially considering the lack of forests and woods, with which that monastery might be restored in its [uncertain: Casanis] and old ruins, since it lacks them elsewhere, namely forests and woods; and so that, for our happy arrival when we happen to come down there, the necessaries might be had the better: graciously inclined to the prayers of the said abbot, we have given our gracious will to the said contract of purchase of the said three łany, forests, woods and all other appurtenances, made, as is touched on above, by the abbot himself and his convent; we have assented, and by these presents assent, decreeing that it shall have the strength of perpetual validity. In witness of which our seal is hung on these presents. Done at Gniezno on the Tuesday before the feast of St Lawrence the Martyr, in the year of the Lord [uncertain: 1,447]. Present: the Reverend Father, Lord Andrzej, bishop, and the magnificent and noble Łukasz of Górka, of Poznań, and Marcin of [uncertain: Rasko], of Kalisz, voivodes; [uncertain: Dobrogosto] of Szamotuły, of Poznań, Przedbor of Koniecpol, of Sandomierz, and Piotr of Kurów, of Sącz, castellans; and many other witnesses worthy of trust to the present. Given by the hands of the magnificent Jan of Koniecpol, chancellor, and Piotr of [uncertain: Tczenaczmy], vice-chancellor, our sincerely beloved.
