--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Drei Entwürfe königlicher Reskripte vom 7. Dezember 1800, einer nach dem anderen. Der erste, an die Regierung zu Posen: Die meisten ihrer Räte sind Mitglieder des dortigen Vormundschaftskollegiums, das die Klage des Vormunds der Michalina von Prusimska gegen den Fürsten von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen auf Herausgabe der Brzycer Güter veranlasst hat und leitet. Deshalb wird festgesetzt, dass die Sache in erster Instanz vom Plenum der Regierung zu Warschau und in zweiter vom Oberappellationssenat des Kammergerichts entschieden wird. Beide Kollegien sind am selben Tag angewiesen worden. Der zweite, an die Regierung zu Warschau: Bei der Posener Regierung schwebt zwischen dem Vormund der minderjährigen Michalina von Prusimska und dem Fürsten von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen ein Prozess auf Herausgabe der Brzycer Güter. Da das dortige Pupillenkollegium die Klage veranlasst hat und leitet und die meisten Posener Räte ihm angehören, soll die Sache in erster Instanz vom Plenum der Regierung zu Warschau entschieden werden. Die Regierung zu Posen ist benachrichtigt. Die Reskripte gingen am 13. zur Post. Der dritte, an den Oberappellationssenat des Kammergerichts: Die Sache soll in erster Instanz vom Plenum der Regierung zu Warschau und in zweiter Instanz vom Senat entschieden werden. Die Regierungen in Posen und Warschau sind angewiesen.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Three drafts of royal rescripts of 7 December 1800, one after another. The first, to the Government at Poznań: most of its councillors are members of the guardianship board there, which occasioned and directs the suit of Michalina von Prusimska's guardian against the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen for the recovery of the Brzyce estates. It is therefore laid down that the case will be decided in the first instance by the full bench of the Government at Warsaw and in the second by the Senate of Higher Appeal of the Kammergericht. Both courts have been instructed the same day. The second, to the Government at Warsaw: a suit for the recovery of the Brzyce estates is pending before the Government at Poznań between the guardian of the minor Michalina von Prusimska and the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen. Since the guardianship board there occasioned and directs the suit and most of the Poznań councillors belong to it, the case is to be decided in the first instance by the full bench of the Government at Warsaw. The Government at Poznań has been informed. The rescripts went to the post on the 13th. The third, to the Senate of Higher Appeal of the Kammergericht: the case is to be decided in the first instance by the full bench of the Government at Warsaw and in the second by the Senate. The Governments at Poznań and Warsaw have been instructed.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Berlin, 7 December 1800. To the South Prussian Government at Poznań.

ex officio

Friedrich Wilhelm, King, etc. Our, etc. Since most of the councillors of your court are members of the guardianship board there, and the suit of the guardian of the minor Michaelina von Prusimska against the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen for the recovery of the Brzyce estates was brought at the instance of the latter, and the lawsuit itself has been conducted, and will continue to be conducted, under its direction, We have found it necessary hereby to order that the said case shall be decided in the first instance by the full bench of the Government at Warsaw, and in the second instance by the Superior Appeal Senate of the Kammergericht. We hereby make this known to you for your information and observance, together with the fact that the necessary orders have accordingly been sent today to both the said courts; and are, etc. Berlin, 7 December 1800. By command.
