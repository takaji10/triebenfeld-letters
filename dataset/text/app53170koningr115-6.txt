Ad officium et Acta praesentia Castrensia Coninensia personaliter veniens Providus Nicolaus Krzepczyński de Myszaków Ministerialis Regni Generalis Sanus existens Recognovit Quia Ipse habitis Secum duobus Nobilibus Videlicet Generosis Antonio Wolski et Constantino Folwarski hic licet ad praesens absentibus in Loco tamen infrascripti Negotii pro tunc praesentibus ad Instantiam et Officiosam Requisitionem Magnifici Stanislai Scibor Chełmski Bonorum Łukom cum Attinentiis Haeredis Feria Secunda Immediate praeterita Fundum Bonorum Łukomia et Borrae Lusnie Ibidemque taliter existentes Viderunt ac Debite Conspexerunt et quidem Przybywszy do Stawiska w Boru Łukomskim Leżącego Widzieli Groblą rozkopaną i w niej Szarpali Czternaście Siekierami przyciętych Pogrodki z Słupami Stawidłami i rynami, do Szczętu Zniesione i Wodę z rybą wypuszczoną Tamże Kijów grubych Dwadzieścia nowo Ciętych przysposobionych do Boju a potym Zostawione. Item w Boru Luśnie nazwanym na Hollendrach w Chałupie Kaczmarskiej w Ścianach na Okół budowia wyciętych Dziur Osm w blochach do przykładnego Strzelania Okna trzy do jednej Szyby wycięte y wystrzelane Quae praemissa Is Idem Ministerialis Regni Generalis et quidem circa aggeris disiectionem per Subditos Trąmpczynenses plus quam Triginta Congregatos de nomine ex post Specificandos in praesentia Magnifici Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevensis in Trajectione vero Gazae per Subordinatos Homines Licentiosos Mandato et jussu Magnifici Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevensis Die eadem Facta et Illata esse asseruit de Quo praesens Relatio.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Bericht des Gerichtsboten Nicolaus Krzepczyński vom 20. Dezember 1771, für Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski. Mit zwei Adligen, Antoni Wolski und Konstanty Folwarski, hat er am vergangenen Montag den Grund von Łukomia und den Wald Lusnia besichtigt. Am Teichgrund im Łukomer Wald sahen sie den Damm aufgegraben, das Wehr mit Pfosten, Schützen und Rinnen mit Äxten zerhauen und völlig beseitigt, das Wasser mit den Fischen abgelassen; dort lagen zwanzig frisch geschnittene dicke Knüppel. Im Schankhaus bei den Olędern im Wald Lusnia waren ringsum acht Schießlöcher in die Wände gehauen und drei Fenster zerschossen. Nach seiner Angabe geschah der Dammbruch durch mehr als dreißig Untertanen von Trąbczyn in Gegenwart Prusimskis, das Durchschießen des Hauses durch seine Leute auf seinen Befehl, am selben Tag.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A report of the court messenger Nicolaus Krzepczyński of 20 December 1771, for Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski. With two noblemen, Antoni Wolski and Konstanty Folwarski, he inspected the ground of Łukomia and the Lusnia wood on the Monday before. At the pond-ground in the Łukom wood they saw the embankment dug through, the weir with its posts, sluices and channels hacked with axes and wholly removed, and the water let out with the fish; twenty thick clubs, freshly cut, lay there. In the tavern house among the Olęder settlers in the Lusnia wood eight loopholes had been cut in the walls all round and three windows shot out. He states that the breaking of the embankment was done by more than thirty subjects of Trąbczyn in Prusimski's presence, and the shooting through of the house by his men at his order, on the same day.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
On behalf of the same — Judicial Site Inspection of the Demolition of the Embankment at Łukom

To the office and present records of the Konin Municipal Court, appearing in person, the Honest Nicolaus Krzepczyński of Myszaków, Royal Court Summoner, General, being sound, certified — because he, having with him two noblemen, namely the Honourables Antoni Wolski and Konstanty Folwarski, although absent here from the court for the present, being nonetheless present at the time at the location of the below-written matter — at the request and official requisition of the Right Honourable Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, heir of the estates of Łukom with their appurtenances — on the immediately preceding Monday [went] to the estate of Łukomia and the Lusnia pine forest, and being there in such manner, they saw and duly inspected:

Having arrived at the fishpond [stawisko] lying in the Łukomia pine forest, they saw an embankment dug up, and in it, fourteen [elements] cut with axes — enclosure elements, posts, sluice gates, and channels — demolished completely, and the water let out with the fish. Also there: twenty thick clubs newly cut, prepared for combat, and then left behind. Also: in the pine forest called Lusnia, among the olędry, in the innkeeper's cottage, in the walls all around — eight holes cut in the structural timber for aimed shooting; three windows cut down to a single pane and shot through.

Which aforementioned things the same above-named Royal Court Summoner — namely, concerning the demolition of the embankment, [carried out] by more than thirty of the Trąbczyn subjects assembled, to be identified by name afterward, in the presence of the Right Honourable Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice; and concerning the shooting through of the house, [carried out] by licentious subordinate men, by the mandate and command of the Right Honourable Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — asserted to have been done and inflicted on the same day. Concerning which, the present report.
