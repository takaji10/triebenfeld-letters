283
Monseigneur
Il suffit que Votre Altesse Serenissime daigne me donner des ordres, pour
que je mʼempresse de Lui obeïr selon mes facultés, je ne suis pas assés
presomptueux pour oser croire quʼil soit possible que mon intervention
ajoute un prix à une lettre qui vient de la part de Vôtre Altesse
Serenissime ; Pour executer neanmoins Vos volontés Monseigneur, je remettrai
incessament au Prince Chancellier dʼEtat la lettre qui lui est addressée
je saisirai cette occasion pour lui mettre sous les yeux les desirs que Vôtre
Altesse Serenissime a daignée me temoigner. Trop heureux si en executant
les ordres du Prince Chancellier dʼetat je pouvois être lʼorgane dont il ſe
servit pour remplir les volontés de Vôtre Altesse Serenissime. Ce nʼest
quʼici à Vienne que jʼai appris à connoitre Mr. de Triebenfeld qui
mʼetoit entierement inconnu jusquʼà present. Il lui suffisoit du reste
de me parler de Vôtre Altesse Serenissime pour que je pretasse la
plus grande attention à tout ce quʼil avoit à me dire.
Daignéz agréer Monseigneur lʼexpression du très profond respect
avec lequel jʼai lʼhonneur dʼêtre
Monseigneur
de Votre Altesse Serenissime
Vienne le 26 Mars 1815.
le très humble & tres soumis
serviteur
Beguelin

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Beguelin sagt zu, den an den Fürsten Staatskanzler gerichteten Brief des Fürsten unverzüglich zu übergeben und ihm dabei die Wünsche des Fürsten vorzutragen. Er wäre glücklich, im Auftrag des Staatskanzlers diese Wünsche erfüllen zu helfen. Triebenfeld habe er vorher nicht gekannt und erst in Wien kennengelernt. Dass dieser sich auf den Fürsten berief, habe genügt, ihm volle Aufmerksamkeit zu schenken.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Beguelin promises to hand over the Prince's letter addressed to the Prince State Chancellor without delay and to put the Prince's wishes to him at the same time. He says he would be happy to help fulfil these wishes on the State Chancellor's instructions. He says he had not known Triebenfeld before and only met him in Vienna; that Triebenfeld referred to the Prince was enough for him to give him his full attention.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
283

My Lord, It is enough that Your Most Serene Highness deign to give me orders, for me to hasten to obey Him according to my faculties; I am not presumptuous enough to dare believe it possible that my intervention should add any value to a letter coming from Your Most Serene Highness. Nevertheless, in order to carry out Your wishes, My Lord, I shall without delay deliver to the Prince Chancellor of State the letter addressed to him; I shall seize this occasion to set before his eyes the desires which Your Most Serene Highness has deigned to communicate to me. Too happy should I be if, in carrying out the orders of the Prince Chancellor of State, I might be the instrument of which he made use to fulfil the wishes of Your Most Serene Highness. It is only here in Vienna that I have come to know Mr de Triebenfeld, who

was entirely unknown to me until now. It was moreover enough for him to speak to me of Your Most Serene Highness for me to lend the greatest attention to all that he had to say to me. Deign to accept, My Lord, the expression of the very profound respect with which I have the honour to be, My Lord, Your Most Serene Highness's, Vienna, the 26th of March 1815, most humble and most submissive servant

Beguelin
