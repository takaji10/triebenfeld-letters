--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Die Regierung zu Posen überreicht dem König eine Abschrift der Kabinettsordre, die in der Sache Prusimska gegen den Fürsten von Hohenlohe an sie ergangen ist. Die empfangende Behörde legt den Bericht am 23. Juni 1800 zu den Akten. Die beigelegte Abschrift ist die Kabinettsordre des Königs an die Regierung zu Posen, dort eingegangen am 16. Juni 1800: Durch eine beiliegende Beschwerde der von Prusimska veranlasst, fordert der König die Akten ihrer Sache gegen den Fürsten von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen wegen des mütterlichen Vermögens der Bittstellerin ein, so wie sie liegen, und befiehlt, sie sofort einzusenden.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The Government at Poznań submits to the King a copy of the cabinet order issued to it in the case of Prusimska against the Prince of Hohenlohe. The enclosed copy is the cabinet order of the King to the Government at Poznań: prompted by an enclosed complaint of von Prusimska, the King calls in the files of her case against the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen over the petitioner's maternal property, as they stand, and orders them sent in at once.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Most Serene and Most Mighty King, Most gracious King and Lord! Poznań, 18 June 1800. The Government most humbly submits a copy of a cabinet order. The cabinet order issued to us in the case of Prusimska against the Prince of Hohenlohe we most humbly submit to Your Royal Majesty in a copy enclosed, and remain, until death, in most devoted loyalty, Your Royal Majesty's most humble and faithfully obedient President, Vice-President and Councillors [uncertain: appointed] to Your Majesty's South Prussian Government. Hering Danckelman v Götz Diederichs Dannenberg Schwarz

Bormann

Fromme

Hoening Leuchert Richter v Fischer

Copy. Received 16 June 1800. His Royal Majesty of Prussia is moved by the enclosed complaint of von Prusimska to call in the files, as they stand, of the case conducted before the Government at Poznań in her suit against His Lordship the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen concerning the petitioner's maternal property, and therefore commands the said Government to send them in immediately upon sight of this. Charlottenburg, 14 June 1800. Friedrich Wilhelm

To

The Government at Poznań
