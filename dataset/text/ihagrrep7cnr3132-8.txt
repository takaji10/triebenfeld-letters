--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Zwei Konzepte vom 30. Mai 1803, Berlin, nacheinander geschrieben. Das erste, an den Staatsminister von Voss, ist ohne Unterschrift, mit einer als [H?] gelesenen Paraphe; der Schreiber ist offenbar Haugwitz, der im zweiten berichtet, dass Voss mitgezeichnet hat. Da der König dem ehemaligen Starosten von Prusimski und Xaver von Jezierski mit der Kabinettsordre vom 10. Mai die Rückkehr in ihr Vaterland erlaubt hat, teilt der Schreiber dies Voss in beiliegender Abschrift mit und stellt ihm anheim, das in Konzept und Reinschrift beigefügte Notificatorium an die Kammer zu Posen in dieser Sache mitzuzeichnen. Das zweite ist Haugwitz' Antwort an Goldbeck: Er dankt Goldbeck für sein Schreiben vom 11. Mai mit der Nachricht von der Rückkehr Prusimskis und Xaver von Jezierskis, gibt die originale Kabinettsordre mit den von ihm mitgezeichneten Konzepten und Reinschriften zurück und fügt hinzu, dass er mit Voss' Mitzeichnung der Kammer zu Posen bereits aufgegeben hat, die beiden nunmehr den Huldigungseid ablegen zu lassen. Unterzeichnet Haug[witz]. Ein Vermerk am Kopf der Antwort lautet "d. 6. ten Juny" (der Rest nicht gelesen).

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Two drafts of 30 May 1803, Berlin, written one after the other. The first, to the minister of state von Voss, is unsigned, with a paraph read [H?]; its writer is evidently Haugwitz, who reports in the second that Voss has countersigned. The King having granted the former Starost von Prusimski and Xaver von Jezierski leave to return to their fatherland by the cabinet order of 10 May, the writer informs Voss of it by an enclosed copy, and asks whether Voss will countersign the notificatorium on the matter to the Chamber at Poznań, enclosed in draft and fair copy. The second is Haugwitz's reply to Goldbeck: he thanks Goldbeck for his letter of 11 May with the news of the return of Prusimski and Xaver von Jezierski, returns the original cabinet order with the drafts and fair copies he has countersigned, and adds that, with von Voss's countersignature, he has already instructed the Chamber at Poznań to have the two now take the oath of allegiance. Signed Haug[witz]. A note at the head of the reply reads "d. 6. ten Juny" (the rest not read).

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Berlin, 30 May 1803

To His Royal Minister of State etc. von Voss, Excellency.

[H?]

His Royal Majesty having been pleased, by cabinet order of the 10th of this month, most graciously to grant the former Starost von Prusimski and Xaver von Jezierski the leave they asked for to return to their fatherland, I do not fail to bring this to Your Excellency's closer knowledge through the copy enclosed, and leave it most obediently to you whether you will be pleased to countersign the notificatorium on this matter to the Chamber at Poznań, enclosed in draft and fair copy.

Berlin, 30 May 1803

To His Royal Grand Chancellor, Minister of State, v. Goldbeck, Excellency.

[Signature M?]

d. 6th June both c. alltis ins.

I thank Your Excellency most kindly for the news given me in your most esteemed letter of the 11th of this month of the return of v. Prusimski and Xaver von Jezierski. With the original cabinet order kindly communicated to me I return to you

herewith the drafts and fair copies countersigned by me, and add most obediently that, with the countersignature of His Royal Minister of State etc. v. Voss, Excellency, I have also already instructed the Chamber at Poznań to have these subjects now take the oath of allegiance.

Berlin, 30 May 1803

Haug[witz]
