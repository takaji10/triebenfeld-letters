--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Das Kammergericht fordert Kircheisen als Testamentsvollstrecker der verstorbenen Fürstin von Sacken auf, Triebenfeld nichts von einem Legat auszuzahlen. Dies gilt, falls die Klage der General Invaliden Casse zutrifft, wonach Fürst Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen aus dem Nachlass 80000 Rthl erhalte und diese Triebenfeld zur Begleichung seiner Forderungen angewiesen habe. Gleichlautend ergeht das Schreiben an Cosmar. Das Ober Landes Gericht von Schlesien übersendet Triebenfeld in Konotop die Vorladung des Kammergerichts und verlangt eine Empfangsbescheinigung.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The Court of Appeal calls on Kircheisen, as executor of the will of the late Princess of Sacken, to pay Triebenfeld nothing from a legacy. This applies if the suit of the General Invalids' Fund is correct, according to which Prince Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen is to receive 80,000 Rthl from the estate and has assigned this sum to Triebenfeld in settlement of his claims. A letter in the same terms goes to Cosmar. The High Court of Silesia sends Triebenfeld at Konotop the Court of Appeal's summons and requires a receipt for it.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The Royal Minister of State and of Justice, Mr von Kircheisen, is hereby called upon, as executor of the will of the deceased Lady Princess von Sacken, in the event that the assertion advanced by the General Invalids' Fund in its complaint lodged with the Royal Superior Court of Justice on the 30th of last month — "that the Lord Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen was to receive a legacy of 80,000 Rthl out of the estate of the Prince von Sacken, and has assigned the same to the Councillor of War and Forests von Triebenfeld at Konotop near Zielona Góra in satisfaction of the latter's claims upon him" — should prove to be well founded, to pay out nothing of this sum to the said von Triebenfeld, at his own responsibility.

Berlin, the 1st of August 1811.

Royal Prussian Superior Court of Justice.

To the Royal Minister of State and of Justice, Mr von Kircheisen.

in the like terms

To the Royal Chief Medical Councillor Mr Cosmar here. To the Councillor of War and Forests Mr von Triebenfeld at Konotop.

No. 1103.

We herewith cause to be transmitted to you a summons of the Royal Superior Court of Justice at Berlin of the 1st of the current month in the matter of the General Invalids' Fund against you, with the command to render in all respects the obedience due to the contents thereof, and to certify in full the due receipt of the same upon the copy to be laid before you for that purpose. Głogów, the 6th of August 1811. Royal Prussian Superior Provincial Court of Silesia. Fülleborn
