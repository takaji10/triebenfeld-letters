Actum in Conin Feria Sexta post Dominicam Misericordiae proxima Die scilicet Decima Nona Mensis Aprilis Anno Domini 1782do
Ex Parte JM Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen~ contra MM Zakrzewskie et Judicium Terrestre Calissien~ Relatio
Videatur hoc loco Relatio ex parte Illustris Magnifici Antonii Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen~ Insignis Sancti Stanislai Equitis contra Magnificos Parentem et Filios Zakrzewskie atque Judicium Terrestre Calissien~ pro Judiciis Ordine~ Generalibus Tribunalis Regni Petricovien~ editt~ prout Copia in productis extat

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Registervermerk vom 19. April 1782: Bericht über die Ladung, die Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice, Ritter des St.-Stanislaus-Ordens, gegen Vater und Söhne Zakrzewski sowie gegen das Kalischer Landgericht vor das Krontribunal in Petrikau erwirkt hat.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A register note of 19 April 1782: the report of the citation that Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, knight of the Order of St Stanislaus, obtained against the Zakrzewskis, father and sons, and against the Kalisz land court, before the Crown Tribunal at Piotrków.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Done in Konin, on Friday, next after the Sunday of Misericordia, namely the 19th day of the month of April, in the Year of the Lord 1782.

On behalf of the Illustrious Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, against the Illustrious Zakrzewskis and the Kalisz Land Court — a Report

Let there be seen at this place a Report on behalf of the Illustrious Magnificent Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, Knight of the Order of Saint Stanislaus, against the Illustrious [Casimir] the father and the sons Zakrzewski, and [against] the Kalisz Land Court — for the Ordinary General Sessions of the Crown Tribunal of the Kingdom at Piotrków, issued — as the copy in the submitted documents stands.
