--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Die Regierung zu Poznań sendet dem regierenden Fürsten zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen eine Ausfertigung des Kaufvertrags über die Güter Kolno und Kamionna mit dem Bankrendanten Leixner, den sie am selben Tag bestätigt hat.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The Government at Poznań sends the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen an engrossment of the contract of sale of the estates of Kolno and Kamionna to the bank cashier Leixner, which it has confirmed the same day.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
By the Grace of God Friedrich Wilhelm King of Prussia. Our Royal grace, friendship and good will first of all! Most Serene Prince, dearest friend and dear faithful one. Your Lordship receives enclosed an official copy of the purchase contract concluded with the bank cashier Leixner concerning the estates Kolno and Kamionna, confirmed this day. We are well disposed towards you with Royal grace, friendship and favourable will. Given at Poznań, the 18th of March 1805. Royal South Prussian Government [illegible]raudchreig[illegible]
