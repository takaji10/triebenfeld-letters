--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Goldbeck an Haugwitz, Berlin, 22. April 1803, eingegangen am selben Tag. Die Regierung zu Posen hat die Akten des Konfiskationsprozesses gegen den ehemaligen Starosten Anton von Prusimski und den Kanonikus von Jezierski eingeschickt; Goldbeck gibt sie in 6 Bänden weiter, mit seinem Konzept des vom König verlangten gemeinschaftlichen Berichts. Hat Haugwitz gegen das Konzept nichts einzuwenden, so soll er es mitzeichnen, die Reinschrift anfertigen und Goldbeck zur Mitunterschrift zugehen lassen und das Konzept und die Akten zurückgeben. Unterzeichnet Goldbeck.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Goldbeck to Haugwitz, Berlin, 22 April 1803, received the same day. The Government at Poznań has sent in the files of the confiscation proceedings against the former Starost Anton von Prusimski and the canon von Jezierski; Goldbeck passes them on in 6 volumes, with his draft of the joint report the King has asked for. If Haugwitz has no objection to the draft, he is to countersign it, have the fair copy made and sent to Goldbeck for his co-signature, and return the draft and the files. Signed Goldbeck.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
received 22 April 1803

The Government at Poznań having sent in the files of the confiscation proceedings against the former Starost Anton von Prusimski and the canon von Jezierski, I do not fail to communicate them to Your Excellency herewith in 6 volumes, together with the draft of the joint report required by His Majesty the King, with the most obedient request that, should you have nothing to remark against the latter, you kindly countersign it, have the fair copy made and sent to me for my co-signature, and be so good as to return the draft and the files to me.

Berlin, 22 April 1803.

Goldbeck

To His Royal Actual Privy State, War and Cabinet Minister and directing Minister of Finance, Count von Haugwitz, Excellency
