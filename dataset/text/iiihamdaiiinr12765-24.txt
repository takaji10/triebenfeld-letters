--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Entwurf einer Antwort an die Witwe von Brehmer: Das Ministerium habe der Gesandtschaft zu Petersburg bereits mitgeteilt, dass die hypothekarischen Gläubiger Gefahr laufen, ihre Kapitalien zu verlieren. Mehr könne es für sie nicht tun; sie müsse ihre Rechte selbst wahrnehmen, falls der Kaiser dem Fürsten eine Entschädigung gewährt. Die am 25. Juli eingereichten Anlagen gehen zurück.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Draft of a reply to the widow Brehmer: the ministry says it has already informed the legation at St Petersburg that the mortgage creditors are in danger of losing their capital. It says it can do no more for her; she must look after her own rights herself should the Emperor grant the Prince compensation. The enclosures submitted on 25 July are returned.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Berlin, 10 August 1816. To the widow of Colonel von Brehmer, Right Honourable,

in

Sommerfeld

the 13th, to the post.

No. 6360. Already upon Your Right Honourable's letter of 26 April of this year I found occasion to bring before the Royal Legation at Petersburg the circumstance that, since the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen had allowed the estates of Trąbczyn with appurtenances to deteriorate and had burdened them with debts, the mortgage creditors stood in danger, through the confiscation of the estate which took place in the year 1807, of losing the capitals they had lent, so that use might be made of it, as circumstances allow, in supporting the compensation petition of the Prince of Hohenlohe. Since nothing further can be done on the part of the undersigned Ministry for Your Right Honourable, but it must rather be left to you, should His Majesty the Emperor of Russia be pleased to grant the Prince of Hohenlohe a compensation, to look after whatever rights you may have therein, I herewith return to you the enclosures submitted on the 25th of July. Berlin

Ministry of Foreign Affairs, 3rd Section.

Hoffm[ann], 12/8.

B[alan].
