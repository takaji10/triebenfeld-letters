Dokument Nr 795
Wladislaus dux regni Polonie etc. 1298 Sept. 9, Piysdir; concedit comiti Woyslao, ut villam Wrąbczyn iure Theutonico locare valeat.
Pergam. orygin., którego pieczęć przywieszona na niciach jedwabn., czerwonych, przedstawia godło Ziemi Kujawskiéj. Warszawa. Zbiory Rusieckich, Diplomata. - Rzyszczew. Muczkow. Codex diplom. Poloniae I, Nr. 90.
In nomine Domini amen. Multis incomodis prudenter occurrimus, cum ea que etatis nostre tempore peraguntur, literis ac testibus perhenniter confirmamus. Ea propter est quod nos Wladislaus, Dei gracia dux regni Polonie, Pomoranie, Cuyavie, Lanzicie ac Syradie, omnium presencium et futurorum memorie commendamus, quod Woyslao fideli nostro famulo et sue posteritati legittime damus potestatem, villam suam, que Vronztino Polonice nominatur, iure Teuthonico collocare, absolventes predictam villam et ipsius incolas a naraz, a povos, a prevod, a podvorove, a stroza, a bove, a vacca, a porco, a perna, a poramb, a stan, a falconibus, a venatoribus, ab urna mellis provincialis, a curru feni, a capete siliginis, ab opole, a poradlne, et a castri citacione ita, quod coram nullo per ministerialem citati debeant comparere vel respondere, sed coram nobis tantum, anulo nostro vel sigillo mediante litera provocati. Volumus eciam, ut iam dictus comes Woyslaus et sui successores omnes causas, maiores, mediocres et minores, que infra limites eiusdem hereditatis Vroncino emerserint, habeat iudicare, et penas iudicatas pro se recipere integraliter et complete. In cuius rei firmitudinem evidentem, presentes conscribi fecimus, nostro sigillo perpetuo roborantes. Acta sunt hec in Piysdir presentibus hys comitibus: Roscone venatore Kalisiensi, Swanthomiro castellano de Bechovia, Swanthoslao venatore, et aliis quam plurimis fide dingnis. Datum ibidem in crastino Nativitatis beate virginis Marie per manus domini Dominici notarii curie nostre, anno Domini MCC nonagesimo octavo.
Vronztino, Wrąbczyn. Piysdir, Pyzdry. Bechovia, Biechowo.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Herzog Władysław von Polen gestattet seinem treuen Diener Graf Wojsław und seiner rechtmäßigen Nachkommenschaft, sein Dorf Vronztino (Wrąbczyn) nach deutschem Recht auszusetzen, und befreit Dorf und Bewohner von allen polnischen Abgaben und der Burgladung, so dass sie vor keinem Amtmann, sondern nur vor dem Herzog selbst erscheinen müssen; Wojsław und seine Erben sollen alle Sachen, große, mittlere und kleine, innerhalb der Grenzen des Gutes richten und die Bußen selbst einziehen. Pyzdry, 9. September 1298. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski II, Nr. 795, nach dem Original, dessen Siegel das Wappen Kujawiens zeigt.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
At Pyzdry, on 9 September 1298, Duke Władysław of Poland allows his faithful servant Count Wojsław and his lawful posterity to locate his village of Vronztino (Wrąbczyn) under German law, and frees the village and its inhabitants from all the Polish payments and the castle's summons, so that they need appear before no official, but before the duke alone; Wojsław and his heirs are to judge all causes, greater, middling and lesser, within the bounds of the estate, and to collect the fines themselves.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 795

Władysław, Duke of the kingdom of Poland etc., 9 September 1298, at Pyzdry; allows Count Wojsław to locate the village of Wrąbczyn under German law.

Original on parchment, whose seal, hung on threads of red silk, shows the arms of the land of Kujawy. Warsaw. The Rusiecki collection. Diplomata. — Rzyszczew. Muczkowski, Codex diplomaticus Poloniae I, no. 90.

In the name of the Lord, amen. We prudently provide against many inconveniences when we confirm for ever with letters and witnesses the things done in the time of our own age. Therefore we, Władysław, by the grace of God duke of the kingdom of Poland, of Pomerania, of Kujawy, of Łęczyca and of Sieradz, commend to the memory of all present and to come, that we give to Wojsław, our faithful servant, and to his lawful posterity the power to locate his village, which is named Vronztino in Polish, under German law, freeing the said village and its inhabitants from the naraz, the povos, the prevod, the podvorove, the stroza, the ox, the cow, the pig, the ham, the poramb, the stan, the falcons, the huntsmen, the urn of provincial honey, the cart of hay, the head of rye, the opole, the poradlne, and the castle's summons — so that, cited, they need not appear or answer before any official, but before us alone, appeal being made to us through our ring or our seal by letter. We also will that the said Count Wojsław and his successors shall judge all the causes, greater, middling and lesser, which shall arise within the bounds of the same estate of Vronczino, and receive the penalties judged entirely and completely for themselves. For the evident firmness of this matter we have had these presents written, strengthening them for ever with our seal. This was done at Pyzdry in the presence of these counts: Roscone, huntsman of Kalisz, Świętomir, castellan of Biechowo, Świętosław, huntsman, and many others worthy of trust. Given there on the morrow of the Nativity of the blessed Virgin Mary, by the hand of the Lord Dominik, notary of our court, in the year of the Lord 1298.

Vronztino, Wrąbczyn. Piysdir, Pyzdry. Bechovia, Biechowo.
