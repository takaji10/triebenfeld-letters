--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Der Fürst drängt Triebenfeld, bei Fürst Hardenberg den Vorschuss von 112000 Rthl und die sofortige Administration der Majoratsgüter zu erwirken; damit ende Cosmars Macht. Ein Mandatarius habe gegen 12000 Rthl und eine sichere Anweisung auf hunderttausend Rthl die Auslieferung des Schuldinstruments zugesagt. Der günstige Bericht des Oberlandesgerichts Brzeg sei eingesandt. Zudem soll Triebenfeld die Sache Trąbczyn und Kamionna betreiben: Die vom König verliehenen Güter hätten die Polen eigenmächtig weggenommen, der Kaiser müsse die Rückgabe befehlen.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The Prince presses Triebenfeld to obtain from Prince Hardenberg the advance of 112,000 Rthl and the immediate administration of the entailed estates; with that, he says, Cosmar's power would end. He says an agent has promised to hand over the debt instrument in return for 12,000 Rthl and a secure order for a hundred thousand Rthl. The favourable report of the High Provincial Court at Brzeg has been sent in, he says. Triebenfeld is also to pursue the matter of Trąbczyn and Kamionna: the estates granted by the King were taken away by the Poles on their own authority, and the Emperor must order their return.

Right Honourable Sir,

Highly Honoured Councillor of War and Forests!

A few hours ago I received from a trusted friend the news that Austria, [uncertain: Vnß land] and Prussia had come to agreement concerning Saxony and Poland, and that the [uncertain: Begrif jezt schreib] may now be brought to a conclusion.

Since the 13th of January I have had no news from you; I live in great anxiety on that account. — After the repeated assurances on the part of His Serene Highness the Prince State Chancellor, I might count with the highest confidence upon rescue. But if the Congress is brought to an end without anything having been done for me, then I am irretrievably lost and must perish in the greatest distress; therefore exert everything, and lay my distress to the heart of Prince Hardenberg, who has after all always been my friend. — The report of the Brzeg Upper Provincial Court was already sent in on the 14th of last month to His Serene Highness the Prince Chancellor. — It is favourable to me and proves everything that has been said by me on the matter. The Prince can now help me with every right.

I ask for nothing further — take this well to heart — than that His Serene Highness the Prince grant the advance of 112,000 Rthl, and against it have all the entailed estates taken at once into administration. Then Cosmar's power is at an end. Only the day before yesterday the mandatary [uncertain: Kurz] gave this assurance, that as soon as he receives the 12,000 Rthl [uncertain: Baur] and a secure order to pay upon the hundred thousand Rthl, he will at once deliver up the debt instrument with a receipt; and this is indeed the only thing, if always the entailed estates [uncertain: einzinsicht]. Let the Prince only grant that, and I am already saved; the estates are of such consequence that the King will soon have the advance back again, everyone receives what is his, and I need not go hungry any longer. As for Cosmar, the matter then settles itself. He withdraws at once from the entailed estates, his executorship ceases, he must render account forthwith and give over into deposit all sums he has behind him, for so the report of the Brzeg Upper Provincial Court directs, and the matter rests upon right and equity. These you need at bottom only urge in passing, because here everything must follow of itself. Only, only beg His Serene Highness for the 112,000 Rthl, that he [uncertain: gemühet] this advance; then [uncertain: bey] I settled with the King and with the Cur. heirs, and am rescued out of Cosmar's [uncertain: Kleinen].

Next to this I beg that the Trąbczyn and Kamionna matter be now attended to. The King has (conferred these same estates upon me by means of the instrument which you have there; I have held the estates in quiet possession for over 12 years, and the Poles took the same away by their own authority only 14 weeks after the Peace of [uncertain: Tilsit]. Here the Kaiser would now have to command that the estates be restored to me and that the revenues drawn from them be reimbursed by the Miączyńska. I can add nothing more today, but only assure you, as ever, of my grateful friendship and high esteem, as

Your Right Honourable's

Sławięcice, the 29th of January

1815
