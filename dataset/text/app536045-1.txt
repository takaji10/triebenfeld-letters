17. Inter Magnificam Catharinam de Rozdrazewska Prusimska relicta viduam actricem et citatam tum Generosum Josephum Puchalski actorem per Generosum Albertum Zurawski et Magnificum Stanislaum Ścibor Chełmski ac Generosum Czajkowski citatos et actores per Generosum Ignatium Byszewski Judicium decidendo terminos partium mutuo sibi directos et controversiis exauditis combinatis earumque plurium actoratibus et reatibus necessariam esse inquisitionem per partes expediendam censet decernendo quatenus partes eandem inquisitionem testibus juratis super contenta protestationum
et terminorum durantibus judiciis suis educant et expediant et addit praesentibus eisdem Ministerialem ad pronuntiandam super incorporationem eorum testium juramenti rotham quod juramentum quoniam per partes sibi mutuo est indultum proinde eisdem partibus testes inducere mandat induxerunt eduxerunt et inquisitiones pro parte sui expediverunt ad quarum lectionem hoc idem judicium accessit quibus lectis quando quidem deducendum Magnificam Prusimska cum cineribus non constat quo tempore praeparatis in anno 1761 cineatores disposuisse, eosdemque Gedanum discedentes Magnificum Chełmski Posnaniam discedentem prope oppidum Pyzdry praevenisse, eisdemque cineribus sequestratis, eosdem in oppido Pyzdry in diversorio ibidem sitto locasse, arestum super eosdem cineres apud notarium Thaelonei imposuisse, post hac licet opponente se Notario Thelonei eosdem cineres de loco cineatores ad assistentiam Generosorum Wawrowski et Gotartowski movisse et Gedanum duxisse, quandoquidem inter has partes causa respectu limitum inter bona Trąpczyn et Łukom ac alia in Judicio Tribunalis Regni ventilatur indecisa in cuius nam vero fundo sunt cineres praeparati ex dislimitatione bonorum subsequenda modo deducendo pro inde negotii praesentis resolutionem ad decisionem causae fundi Judicium suspendit et inquisitiones partibus ex cancellaria terrestri in occluso rothulo extradendas debere jubet

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Dekret des Landgerichts in Konin aus dessen Sitzung von 1763 im Streit zwischen der Witwe Katarzyna Prusimska, geborene Rozdrażewska, mit Joseph Puchalski auf der einen und Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski mit einem Czajkowski auf der anderen Seite; beide Seiten klagen und sind zugleich geladen. Das Gericht hält eine Vernehmung beeideter Zeugen für nötig, lässt sie durchführen und die Aussagen verlesen. Danach hatte die Prusimska 1761 Pottaschebrenner mit Pottasche auf den Weg geschickt; Chełmski, der nach Posen reiste, traf sie bei Pyzdry, als sie nach Danzig zogen, ließ die Pottasche beschlagnahmen und in einem Wirtshaus in Pyzdry einlagern und legte beim Notar des Zollamts Arrest darauf. Die Brenner schafften sie trotz des Widerspruchs des Notars mit Hilfe der Adligen Wawrowski und Gotartowski fort und brachten sie nach Danzig. Weil zwischen den Parteien der Streit um die Grenze zwischen Trąbczyn und Łukom vor dem Krontribunal noch unentschieden ist und erst die Grenzziehung zeigen wird, auf wessen Grund die Pottasche hergestellt wurde, setzt das Gericht die Entscheidung aus und ordnet an, den Parteien die Zeugenaussagen aus der Kanzlei in verschlossener Rolle herauszugeben.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A decree of the land court at Konin, of its sitting of 1763, in the dispute between the widow Katarzyna Prusimska, born Rozdrażewska, with Joseph Puchalski on one side, and Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski with one Czajkowski on the other; each side is plaintiff and at the same time summoned. The court holds that sworn witnesses must be examined, has it done and has the testimony read out. It showed that in 1761 Prusimska had sent potash-burners off with potash; Chełmski, travelling to Poznań, came upon them near Pyzdry as they were making for Gdańsk, had the potash sequestered and stored at an inn in Pyzdry, and laid an arrest on it with the notary of the customs house. The burners, helped by the noblemen Wawrowski and Gotartowski, took it away over the notary's objection and brought it to Gdańsk. Because the dispute over the boundary between Trąbczyn and Łukom is still undecided between the parties before the Crown Tribunal, and only the fixing of the boundary will show on whose ground the potash was made, the court suspends its decision and orders the testimony handed to the parties from the chancery in a closed roll.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
17. Between the Right Honourable Catharina de Rozdrazewska Prusimska, widow and plaintiff, and the summoned Honourable Joseph Puchalski, plaintiff, through the Honourable Albert Żurawski; and the Right Honourable Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski and the Honourable Czajkowski, summoned and plaintiffs, through the Honourable Ignatius Byszewski — the court, in settling the matters directed mutually between the parties and having heard their disputes and joined the plaintiff claims and charges of the several of them, considers a sworn inquiry to be necessary, to be conducted by the respective parties, decreeing that the parties shall carry out the same sworn inquiry by sworn witnesses regarding the contents of the protestations

and of the terms, while its sittings last, shall produce and expedite it; and by these presents it assigns them the Court Summoner to pronounce the formula of the oath for the swearing-in of their witnesses — which oath, since the parties have mutually conceded it to one another, it therefore orders the same parties to bring in their witnesses. They brought them in and produced them, and expedited the sworn inquiries each on its own behalf; to the reading of which this same court proceeded. These having been read — whereas it is shown that the Right Honourable Prusimska, in the year 1761, dispatched the potash-burners with potash, prepared at what time is not established; and that the Right Honourable Chełmski, departing for Poznań, came upon them, as they were departing for Gdańsk, near the town of Pyzdry; and having seized the same potash, placed it in an inn situated in the town of Pyzdry; and imposed an arrest on the said potash with the notary of the customs post; and that thereafter, although the notary of the customs post opposed it, the potash-burners, with the assistance of the Honourables Wawrowski and Gotartowski, moved the said potash from the location and conveyed it to Gdańsk — since, however, between these parties a case regarding the boundaries between the estates of Trąbczyn and Łukom, and other matters, is being heard undecided before the Crown Tribunal, and on whose ground the potash was prepared will be shown only from the demarcation of the estates that is to follow — for that reason the court suspends the resolution of the present matter until the decision on the estate case, and orders that the sworn inquiries be handed out to the parties from the land chancery in a closed roll.

[Translator's note: The source reads "sitto", which is not a standard Latin form. The expected ablative of situs is "sito". Retained in the transcription as a scribal slip; translated as "situated."]

[Translator's note: The source uses two variant spellings for the customs post: "Thaelonei" at first mention and "Thelonei" at second. Both are scribal forms of Latin teloneum (customs post, toll house). Translated uniformly throughout.]
