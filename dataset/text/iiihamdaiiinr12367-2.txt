Je vous annonce en partant, mon cher Schmidt, que j'ai lu hier mon mémoire au Conseil sur l'affaire Weigel & Eisenhardt & qu'il y a été unanimement reconnu qu'il fallait payer. — Comment? voilà sur quoi on délibérera.
M[ohrenheim]

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Undatiertes Billett Mohrenheims an Schmidt, beim Aufbruch geschrieben: Er habe am Vortag im Rat seine Denkschrift über die Sache Weigel und Eisenhardt vorgelesen, und dort sei einstimmig anerkannt worden, dass gezahlt werden müsse. Wie, darüber werde man noch beraten.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
In an undated note to the Prussian consul general Schmidt, written as he was leaving, Baron Mohrenheim says that the day before he had read to the council his memorandum on the affair of the bankers Weigel and Eisenhardt, and that the council recognised unanimously that payment must be made. How it is to be made will be deliberated.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
I let you know as I leave, my dear Schmidt, that yesterday I read my memorandum on the Weigel & Eisenhardt affair to the Council, & that it was unanimously recognised there that payment must be made. — How? That is what will be deliberated.

M[ohrenheim]
