M Prusimski contra M Chełmski Manifestatur
Quoniam Informatio non est Data ad Requisitionem Partis post Elapsum Triduum Limitatur
Antoni na Kolnie Prusimski Starosta Nieszczewice manupropria

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Überschrift eines Protests von Prusimski gegen Chełmski, eingetragen unter der Sitzung vom 24. April 1777. Darunter vermerkt der Schreiber, dass die Angaben dazu nicht geliefert wurden und dass auf Verlangen der Partei nach Ablauf von drei Tagen „limitiert“ wird. Antoni Prusimski von Kolno, Starost von Niszczewice, hat unterschrieben.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A protest of Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice and owner of Trąbczyn, against Stanisław Chełmski, owner of Łukom, entered under the sitting of 24 April 1777, was never carried out: the clerk notes that the information for it was not supplied and that, at the party's request and three days having passed, it "is limited". Prusimski signed.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Right Honourable Prusimski against Right Honourable Chełmski — Protest Lodged

Since the Information has not been provided in response to the Requisition of the party within the expired three-day period, the matter is thereby restricted.

Antoni of Kolno Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — by his own hand.
