# 1363-03-16  Ląd

Dokument Nr 1491
Wyrzbentha castellanus Poznaniens. n. n. capitaneus Polonie general. 1363 Mart. 16, in Land; protestatur, Nicolaum subdapiferum episcopi Poznaniensis, medium dominium in villa Wrąbczyn monasterio Ląd resignavisse.
Pergam. orygin. Na skrawku pergamin. przywieszona pieczęć przedstawia na tarczy trójkątnéj godło rodu Niesobiów; napis zatarty. Warszawa. Zbiory Rusieckich. Diplomata.
Nos Wyrzbentha castellanus Poznaniensis nec non capitaneus Polonie generalis, omnibus et singulis tam presentibus quam futuris quorum auditibus presens scriptum fuerit recitatum palam recognoscimus et aperte: quod accedentes ad nostram aliorumque terre Polonie nobilium presenciam nobilis vir Nicolaus, subdapifer venerabilis in Christo patris domini episcopi Poznaniensis, una cum sua conthorali honoranda domina Czerska, non compulsi, non coacti nec alico scrupulo calumnie seducti, sed bona deliberacione sui animi prehabita, venerabili viro domino Iohanni divina paciencia abbati suoque conventui in Land Ordinis Cysterciensis, medium dominium in villa magnum Wronczino vulgariter nuncupata pro quatuordecim marcis grossorum paratorum vendiderunt, contulerunt, ascripserunt et resignaverunt iure hereditario perpetuis temporibus possidendum, tenendum, habendum, vendendum, comutandum et ad usus beneplacitos convertendum, secundum quod eorum placuerit voluntati; in cuius rei testimonium, sigillum nostri tytuli presentibus est appensum. Actum et datum in Land quinta feria post dominicam qua Letare in ecclesiis decantatur, sub anno Nativitatis Domini m.ccc sexagesimo tercio, presentibus hiis testibus: domino Lasczone subvenatore Landensi, domino Nicolao de Wysoczko, Andrea de Dreczcowo, Lascarzone de Slawosewo, Pyotrkone de Stralcowicze, Iohanne de Beganowo heredibus.
Magnum Wronczino, Wrąbczyn. Wysoczko, Wysokie. Dreczcowo, Drzeczkowo. Slawosewo, Sławoszew. Stralcowicze, Strzałkowo. Beganowo, Bieganowo.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Derselbe Wyrzbenta beurkundet, dass Mikołaj, subdapifer des Bischofs von Posen, mit seiner Ehefrau, der ehrwürdigen Dame Czerska, aus freiem Willen die Hälfte der Herrschaft (medium dominium) im Dorf Groß-Wrąbczyn dem Abt Jan und seinem Konvent in Ląd für 14 Mark fertiger Groschen verkauft haben. Ląd, 16. März 1363, am Donnerstag nach Laetare. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski III, Nr. 1491; das Siegel trägt das Wappen der Niesobia.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The same Wyrzbenta attests that Mikołaj, subdapifer of the bishop of Poznań, with his wife the honoured lady Czerska, of their free will sold the half of the lordship (medium dominium) in the village of great Wrąbczyn to Abbot Jan and his convent in Ląd for 14 marks of ready groschen. Ląd, 16 March 1363, on the Thursday after Laetare. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski III, no. 1491; the seal bears the arms of the Niesobia.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 1491

Wyrzbenta, castellan of Poznań and the same general captain of Poland, 16 March 1363, at Ląd; attests that Mikołaj, subdapifer of the bishop of Poznań, resigned the half of the lordship in the village of Wrąbczyn to the monastery of Ląd.

Original on parchment. On a scrap of parchment hangs a seal showing on a triangular shield the arms of the Niesobia family; the inscription effaced. Warsaw. The Rusiecki collection. Diplomata.

We, Wyrzbenta, castellan of Poznań and also general captain of Poland, acknowledge openly and plainly to all and singular, present as much as to come, whose ears the present document shall have been recited to, that there came before us and the other nobles of the land of Poland the noble man Mikołaj, subdapifer of the venerable father in Christ, the Lord bishop of Poznań, together with his consort the honoured lady Czerska; not compelled, not forced, nor led astray by any scruple of calumny, but with the good deliberation of their mind first had, to the venerable man Lord Jan, by divine patience abbot, and his convent in Ląd of the Cistercian Order, they sold, gave, assigned and resigned the half of the lordship in the village commonly called great Wronczino for fourteen marks of ready groschen, by hereditary right, to be possessed, held, had, sold, exchanged and turned to such uses as shall have pleased their will, for all time. In testimony of this matter the seal of our title is hung upon these presents. Done and given at Ląd on the fifth day after the Sunday on which Laetare is sung in the churches, in the year of the Lord's birth 1363, in the presence of these witnesses: the Lord Lasczone, under-huntsman of Ląd, the Lord Mikołaj of Wysoczko, Andrzej of Dreczkowo, Laskarz of Sławoszew, Piotrko of Strzałkowo, Jan of Bieganowo, heirs.

Magnum Wronczino, Wrąbczyn. Wysoczko, Wysokie. Dreczcowo, Drzeczkowo. Slawosewo, Sławoszew. Stralcowicze, Strzałkowo. Beganowo, Bieganowo.
