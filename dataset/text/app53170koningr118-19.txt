Pro Parte JM Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen[sis] Loci Visionis Oblata
Videatur hoc loco Oblata Visionis Loci busto ad Bona Trąpczyn J Magnifici Antonii Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen[sis] haeareditt~ per Duos Nobiles et Ministerialem in Papyro a Thaesauro Regni Gros~ unius argen~ Conscriptt~ manibusque Eorundem Nobilium propriis Subscriptt~ prout Originale extat in productis

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Registervermerk vom Juni 1782: Für Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice, wird die Besichtigung eines Ortes auf Trąbczyner Erbgrund zur Eintragung vorgelegt, von zwei Adligen und einem Gerichtsboten auf Stempelpapier zu einem Silbergroschen aufgesetzt und von den Adligen eigenhändig unterschrieben; das Original liegt bei den vorgelegten Schriftstücken.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
In June 1782 an inspection of a place on the hereditary ground of Trąbczyn, made for Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice and owner of Trąbczyn, by two noblemen and a court messenger, was brought in for entry at Konin; the noblemen signed it in their own hands.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
For the part of the Illustrious Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — Enrollment of a Judicial Site Inspection of the Location

Let there be seen at this place the Enrollment of a judicial site inspection of the location at the mound belonging to the hereditary estates of Trąbczyn of the Illustrious Magnificent Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — conducted by two Nobles and a Process Server, written on paper from the Treasury of the Kingdom of one silver grosz, and subscribed by the own hands of the same Nobles — as the original stands in the submitted documents.
