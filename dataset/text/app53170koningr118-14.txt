--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Bericht des Gerichtsboten Bartłomiej Wrzask von Sławsk, eingetragen in Konin 1782, für Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa. Er hat eine königliche Ladung zum Ortstermin auf dem Grund des Gutes Grab zugestellt, ausgestellt in Petrikau am 26. November 1781. Geladen sind Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice, sowie die Eheleute Bogdański: Ludwik, Landkämmerer von Kalisz, und Teresa, geborene Rozdrażewska, Erbin der Katarzyna Prusimska, geborene Rozdrażewska, Erbfrau von Grab. Sie sollen am 3. Juni 1782 vor dem Gericht des Ortstermins erscheinen, das ein Dekret des Krontribunals angeordnet hat. Chełmski verlangt die Zahlung der Summe, die der Kaufmann Tracholz ihm abgetreten hat, samt Zinsen; vier Untertanen aus Nowa Wieś sollen als Zeugen gestellt werden. Der Bote hat die Ladung am Freitag, dem Vorabend von Mariä Empfängnis 1781, in Trąbczyn hinterlegt.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A report of the court messenger Bartłomiej Wrzask of Sławsk, entered at Konin in 1782, for Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa. He has served a royal citation to the sitting on the ground of the estate of Grab, dated at Piotrków on 26 November 1781. Cited are Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, and the Bogdańskis, husband and wife: Ludwik, land chamberlain of Kalisz, and Teresa, born Rozdrażewska, heiress of Katarzyna Prusimska, born Rozdrażewska, the heiress of Grab. They are to appear on 3 June 1782 before the court of the sitting on the ground, which a decree of the Crown Tribunal ordered. Chełmski claims payment of the sum that the merchant Tracholz made over to him, with interest; four subjects from Nowa Wieś are to be produced as witnesses. The messenger left the citation at Trąbczyn on the Friday on the eve of the Immaculate Conception 1781.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
On behalf of the Illustrious Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, against the Illustrious Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — a Report

Having come in person, the Honest Bartłomiej Wrzask of the village of Sławsk, General Royal Process Server, sound [of mind], acknowledged that he had served authenticated Citation Letters for the Term of the Condescension in the settlement of the estates of Grab, being conducted, upcoming, below-written, of the following tenor:

Stanisław August, by the Grace of God, King of Poland — to you, the Illustrious and Magnificent Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, Knight of the Order of Saint Stanislaus, Heir of the estates of Trąbczyn and others; and to Ludwik, Land Court Chamberlain of Kalisz; and to Teresa of the Rozdrzewski [family], [uncertain: oto] the Illustrious Katarzyna, likewise of the Rozdrzewski [family], last [by] marriage Prusimska, Heiress of the estate of Grab; and [uncertain: oto] the Illustrious Magnificent, likewise of the Rozdrzewski [family], Molska, Castellaness of Rogoźno — having died without issue — to her successor, or mutually [Congb~is], Bogdański — concerning all your estates generally — we command: that before Us and Our Condescensorial Court, by the force of a Decree of the Crown Tribunal of the Kingdom at Piotrków issued between you, the Cited [parties], on one part, and the Plaintiffs and others on the other part, on the 3rd day of June in the present year 1782, by virtue of the last Limitation, the Condescension [session] to proceed — you appear in person and peremptorily — at the instance of the Illustrious Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, Plaintiff, who cites you, and indeed [the] married [party] under the competent authority of her husband, adhering to their documents supporting the matter, cites [you], or rather summons [you] to any prepared decrees [and] a prepared proceeding — to see and hear yourselves [obliged] to the payment of sums, whatever and however much shall appear from the documents to be due to the Plaintiff from you, together with provisions, to be bound [thereby]; the Plaintiff's claim regarding the payment of the sum transferred to the Plaintiff [as agent] from the Honourable Tracholz, Merchant — originally instituted [against] you before the Kalisz Land Court, and afterwards, via prosecutions, [transferred] to the Illustrious Magnificent [Antoni] and to the Illustrious Magnificent [Katarzyna] Prusimska to the Condescensorial Court by remission — to be resolved at the Term of Our Condescension, notwithstanding any of your oppositions and prosecutions; to decide [regarding] the payment of the transferred sum for the Plaintiff, together with provisions, bound to the chain of Documents, [Austacorum] Mercantile Contracts, and any other Transactions proving these, to be followed by you, to be found; the penalties in respect of the evocation to the Tribunal Courts for what is at stake to be extended; court expenses to be found compensable; all-encompassing satisfaction for the Plaintiff's interest, as great as it is, to be commanded; the summoning of the labourers Albert Siwy, Andreas Rataj, Casimir the Keeper, Albert the Shepherd, of Nowa Wieś, and the subjects of the Illustrious Magnificent [Katarzyna] Prusimska, who by [?ct] this Citation are detained at your [estate], to be decreed to give testimony; and other things which [by] the law of the cause and present matter shall be decided, resolved, and sentenced of the same [parties] — be equally ready to observe the Term and to respond judicially to these and other things which will be more fully proposed to you. Given at Piotrków, on the 26th day of the month of November, in the Year of the Lord 1781. In the estates of Trąbczyn, upon the table in the heated room, the Revenue Clerk being present, on Friday, the Eve of the Feast of the Immaculate Conception of the Most Blessed Virgin Mary, in the Year of the Lord 1781 — having placed [the citation] and having left notification.
