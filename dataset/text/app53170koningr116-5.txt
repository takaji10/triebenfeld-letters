N Rajewicz in Vim Diligentiae Manifestatur
Videatur hoc Loco Manifestatio Certa ex re et personali Comparitione N Petri Rajewicz prout se de nullo modo in Actum Granitialem Inter Bona i Łukom et Trąmpczyn Agitatum Involverat Cumque Ejus Assensum res Sunt gestae [de?] Diligenter Facta in Productis.
Petrus Rajewicz mp

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Registervermerk über einen Protest, eingetragen in Konin im September 1775. Piotr Rajewicz ist persönlich erschienen und erklärt vorsorglich, er habe sich in das zwischen Łukom und Trąbczyn betriebene Grenzverfahren in keiner Weise eingelassen. Er hat unterschrieben.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A register note of a protest, entered at Konin in September 1775. Piotr Rajewicz appeared in person and declares, as a precaution, that he in no way took part in the boundary proceedings conducted between Łukom and Trąbczyn. He signed.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
The Honourable Rajewicz — by force of due diligence — Protest

Let there be seen here the Formal Protest made in fact and upon the personal appearance of the Honourable Piotr Rajewicz, declaring that he in no way had involved himself in the Boundary Act being conducted between the goods of Łukom and Trąbczyn — yet things were done with his consent [?] — duly and diligently made, among the Produced Documents.

Petrus Rajewicz, by his own hand
