--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Registraturvermerk: Der Bericht ist unter Nr. 3278 C an die 3. Sektion des auswärtigen Departements abgegeben worden, gezeichnet Bever; er ist mit den Akten beigefügt, gezeichnet Strenger.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A note of 1816 records that a report in the matter of the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen's claim was passed to the third section of the Department of Foreign Affairs and attached to the file.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
This report has been handed over under no. 3278 C. to the 3rd Section of the Foreign Department. Bever. 3/5 — it is attached with the file. Strenger 5/5. [18]16.
