Decreti Condescensorialis Granicialis Inter Haereditatis Bonorum Trąpczyn Łukom et Alia prolati Oblata
Videatur hoc loco Oblata Decreti Condescensorialis Campestris Granicialis Inter haereditates Bonorum Trąpczyn cum Attinentiis Illustri Magnifici Antonii Prusimski Capitaneum Niesczevicensem et Łukom Itidem cum Attinen~ Magnifici Stanislai Chełmski Thaesaurarii Vschov~ haereditat~ prolati, per Officium[que?] Camerariale Graniciale Junivladislav~ expeditt~ pro ut Originale Ejusq[ue] Decreti in productis extat

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Registervermerk vom 8. Juni 1782: Eingetragen wird das Feldgrenzdekret des Ortstermins zwischen den Erbgütern Trąbczyn des Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice, und Łukom des Stanisław Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa, ausgefertigt vom Grenzkämmereramt Inowrocław. Das Original liegt bei den vorgelegten Urkunden; der Text des Dekrets ist hier nicht abgeschrieben.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
On 8 June 1782 the field boundary decree of the sitting on the ground between the hereditary estates of Trąbczyn, of Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, and Łukom, of Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, issued by the office of the boundary chamberlain of Inowrocław, was brought in for entry at Konin. The original stood among the documents produced; the entry does not copy the decree.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Enrollment of the Boundary Decree of the Sitting on the Ground pronounced between the Hereditary Estates of Trąbczyn, Łukom and Others

Let there be seen at this place the Enrollment of the Field Boundary Decree of the Sitting on the Ground pronounced between the hereditary estates of Trąbczyn with its appurtenances, of the Illustrious Magnificent Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, and of Łukom, likewise with its appurtenances, the hereditary estate of the Magnificent Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, and issued by the [uncertain: office] of the boundary chamberlain of Inowrocław — as the original of the same decree stands in the submitted documents.
