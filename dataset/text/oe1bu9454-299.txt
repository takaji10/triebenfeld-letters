--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Triebenfeld will in Pszczew mit Leixner in Międzyrzecz die aufgefundene Quittung geltend machen und die Schuld löschen lassen. Einer Aufhebung des Kontrakts, die Bürger, Bauern und Zerboni wünschen, will er nur unter Bedingungen zustimmen, darunter 11000 Rthl rückständiger Canon an den Fürsten. Aus Pszczew stünden dem Fürsten noch 99000 Rthl zu. Triebenfeld besteht darauf, dass die 72000 und 65000 Rthl auf Kamionna gehörten. Trzciński erhält Aufschub bis Johanni. Schenck will eine Generalvollmacht; Triebenfeld bittet um ein Schreiben, um ihn loszuwerden.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Triebenfeld intends to press the receipt that has been found with Leixner at Międzyrzecz while in Pszczew, and to have the debt struck off. He says he will agree to a cancellation of the contract, which the townspeople, the peasants and Zerboni want, only on conditions, among them 11,000 Rthl of canon arrears to the Prince. He says a further 99,000 Rthl from Pszczew are still due to the Prince. Triebenfeld insists that the 72,000 and 65,000 Rthl belonged to Kamionna. Trzciński is granted a postponement until Midsummer. Schenck wants a general power of attorney; Triebenfeld asks for a letter so as to be rid of him.

Most Serene Prince

Most gracious Prince and Lord!

My illness has ceased; only the great weakness does not permit me to undertake long journeys. The physician forbids exposing oneself at once to the weather, and therefore I must keep quarantine yet a few days. With this in view I shall await the return of this messenger. My estate affairs in Kohlen [uncertain: Poland] I have arranged — There remain we exactly 62,000 Rthl over — Schenck has leased out the estates to a Jew for me shamefully [uncertain: sendenhaft] and badly — yet I now obtain, by turning matters otherwise, enough that I shall be able to travel and to meet the manifold costs — 500 Rthl will now be [text lost] to me, and with that I go to Pszczew and from there to Berlin — the Emperor Alexander does not come to Berlin until the end of April, upon which, however, I still expect detailed information shortly from Boidelang.

In Pszczew my first business will be to come to an agreement with Leixner in Międzyrzecz and to see that I bring the receipt that has been found into validity — If that succeeds, then I shall have the debt struck out of the mortgage book, and seek out the profit that is to be drawn from Pszczew: The burgesses and peasants have declared themselves insolvent and are pressing for the cancellation of the contract — They prove the lesion, that they are [uncertain: ceded] beyond the half, since the estates were valued at only 64,000 Rthl. I prove that the Most High Provincial Authority itself examined everything and approved [uncertain: Ewr] Serene Highness's [uncertain: statement] — since, moreover, the Chief President Zerboni desires the cancellation of the contract, I shall consent to it, reserving however only quaevis competentia — that is to say, he, the burgesses and the peasants, forfeit the earnest money they have given —

2. They must at once pay the arrears of canon and of competence with about [uncertain: 7]000, so that the sequestration ceases forthwith.

3. They must at once render up to Your Serene Highness the arrears of canon with 11/m Rthl.

4. They must declare themselves how they intend to discharge the arrears of interest. Now it depends upon what Pszczew is still worth; the forest is totally devastated. At 64,000 Rthl it was valued — Queritur, will it be worth 72,000 Rthl? And how will it look with the other 65,000 Rthl standing upon it? They do indeed also stand in the receipt, but I do not yet know whether I shall be able to move Leixner to accept these as well. If that were to succeed, and Leixner were to take this sum upon Kolno and Kamionna, then all would be helped — Your Serene Highness would have, as said before, 99,000 Rthl to receive. From the estates themselves I [uncertain: nählen] the 72,000 Rthl, from the remainder with about 20,000 Rthl.

Lombardini, Mr Hahn and the good Bally were paid. Everything meanwhile depends on my being favoured with Leixner — The man is now destitute; I must support him and give money towards the outlay of costs in his business. The Chief President Zerboni is charged from higher quarters to sustain him; Kamionna is to remain his — I insist in the meanwhile that the sum of 72 and 65,000 Rthl entered upon Pszczew does not belong upon Pszczew but, according to the receipt of Meyer Bernhard, upon Kamionna — God grant me further good fortune — But may Your Serene Highness deign to write to me whether this altogether has Your approval — the compensation business, which has come to a standstill through the malicious delay, will nevertheless turn out very well. It is being worked diplomatically, and the Minister von Humboldt has great need of me now — Trzciński is here again — It is not he but the Major Trzciński who has been suffocated — He was with me the day before yesterday and has begged for forbearance until Midsummer, when he will receive 25,000 Rthl for an estate he has sold — Litigate I cannot; that costs money — I have granted him such, but assured him that I shall lay arrest not only upon the purchase moneys but upon all revenues. such as I requested in my last, so that I may be rid of him. With Zerboni he has fallen out, I know not upon what grounds — "The [illegible] President also wrote lately to the district administrator von Kurnatowski that he should, together with the Chief Forester Seager and with the Privy Councillor von Triebenfeld, for the present investigate the forest devastations in Pszczew, so that one may see clearly — Schenck is offended at it that the Government answered him merely that his submission could not be understood — He wishes, if he receives a general power of attorney and 500 Rthl together with free conveyance, to go to Poznań and work upon the matter — It will be hard for me to raise 10 Rthl, so I have given him no decision upon it — and in truth I do not need him — My tenant's brother is house factor and favourite of Zerboni, through whose hands pass all the offerings that are brought."
