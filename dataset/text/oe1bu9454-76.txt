--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Von Triebenfeld meldet, dass der Gütertausch jetzt im Gange ist und vielleicht bald abgeschlossen wird. Er will Anfang September nach Berlin reisen und bis Ende des Monats bleiben. Dort hofft er den Fürsten wiederzusehen und ihm persönlich über mehrere Geschäfte zu berichten.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Von Triebenfeld reports that the exchange of estates is now under way and may perhaps soon be concluded. He intends to travel to Berlin at the beginning of September and to stay until the end of the month. There he hopes to see the Prince again and to report to him in person on several pieces of business.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
76

Most Serene Prince,

Most gracious Lord!

The said exchange of estates is now under way, and perhaps the matter will shortly be settled. I shall go to Berlin at the beginning of September, and shall remain there until the end of that same month. The chief good fortune which I hope to partake of in Berlin is to see Your Most Serene Highness again in health, and to be able to lay myself personally at the feet of the same, whereupon I shall then render the most faithful report upon several matters of business.

In deepest reverence I remain, until death, Your Most Serene Highness's Wrocław, the 23rd of August 1798. truly most humble and obedient servant v Triebenfeld
