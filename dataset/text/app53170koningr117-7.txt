--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Protest von Antoni Prusimski von Kolno, Starost von Niszczewice, vom 15. April 1778, nach einer vorbereiteten Abschrift eingetragen und von ihm unterschrieben. Er richtet sich gegen Stanisław Chełmski und gegen Stefan Zielonacki, Unterrichter des Kalischer Landgerichts, wegen des vollen Landgerichts wie wegen des Ortstermins auf dem Grund von Trąbczyn. Prusimski verweist auf seinen Protest und seine Ladung gegen das Dekret des Landgerichts vom 20. Oktober des Vorjahres. Der Streit um die Herausgabe der Urkunden mit den Erben der Witwe seines Onkels Paweł Prusimski sei beim Tribunal anhängig; ohne diese Urkunden könne über die Forderung, die Chełmski von Jan Tracholz erworben hat, nicht entschieden werden. Der Unterrichter habe die Sache auf Betreiben Chełmskis an das volle Landgericht verwiesen, obwohl dieses vor das Tribunal geladen sei, und Prusimski damit unnütze Kosten verursacht; Prusimski erklärt diese Verweisung für nichtig.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A protest of Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice, of 15 April 1778, entered from a prepared copy and signed by him. It is directed against Stanisław Chełmski and against Stefan Zielonacki, sub-judge of the Kalisz land court, in respect both of the full land court and of the sitting on the ground of Trąbczyn. Prusimski refers to his protest and his citation against the land court's decree of 20 October of the year before. The suit for the surrender of the papers, against the heirs of the widow of his uncle Paweł Prusimski, is pending before the Tribunal; without those papers the claim that Chełmski acquired from Jan Tracholz cannot be judged. The sub-judge, at Chełmski's instance, sent the cause to the full land court although that court stands cited before the Tribunal, and so caused Prusimski useless costs; Prusimski declares that remission null.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Done at Konin on the Wednesday next after Palm Sunday, namely 15 April 1778.

The Honourable Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, against the Honourable Chełmski and the Land Court — Manifest Filed

Certified copy.

To the office and present records of the Konin Municipal Court, appearing in person, the Honourable Antoni of Kolno Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, hereditary owner of the estates of Trąbczyn with appurtenances — looking to avert any danger to his cause and fortune and to safeguard the integrity of the laws of the nobility, and referring himself to the manifest and prosecution term against the Kalisz Land Court decree issued at Konin prejudicially, made and lodged before the records of the Konin Municipal Court in the preceding year by permission of the law — solemnly, before the same office and its records, in and against the Honourables Stanisław Chełmski, current hereditary owner of the estates of Łukom with appurtenances, and Stefan Zielonacki, Deputy Land Judge of Kalisz, judge of the cause described below both in the full Kalisz Land Court and at the condescension term on the ground of the estates of Trąbczyn — manifests:

of the fact that the manifestant — observing, for the reasons set out in the manifest and prosecution term, that the Kalisz Land Court decree issued at Konin on the Monday after the Feast of Saint Hedwig, Widow and Chosen of God, namely 20 October of the preceding year, between the same Honourable Chełmski and himself the manifestant — is contrary not only to the prior decrees but also to the Commission of the Estates of the Realm issued in this cause, and likewise issued outside the proper forum — as much because without decision of the cause with the successors of the widow of the late Honourable Paweł Prusimski, his uncle, regarding the production and delivery of documents pertaining both to this cause and to his uncle's general estate — a cause pending and entrusted and ordered to be decided by land court decrees and ultimately by the Commission of the Estates of the Realm, and to this point undetermined, nay rather pending before the Tribunal

and due to be resolved there — and that without the documents received he cannot be adjudicated in the present cause with the Honourable Chełmski, the acquirer of the pretension or the most illegal claim acquired from the Honest Jan Tracholz, the assignor — (with whom also the cause and his pretension had been remitted together with this cause and the condescension, by reason of the connected nature of the matter, as recently as in the year 1766; the same Honest Tracholz having summoned and cited the successors of Katarzyna of Rozdrażewo Prusimska, the widowed survivor of his uncle, after her death, on that same pretension of his, at his own term) — he cannot be separately adjudicated; and [attentive to the fact] that he had already conceived that the evident danger to this cause, and also some entanglement of the manifestant's boundary cause and discrepant decree, had been premeditated by the Honourable Chełmski.

Accordingly, the manifestant — from these motives — was compelled by force of law to make and file the prosecutions, namely the manifest and prosecution term, against the above-written Kalisz Land Court decree and simultaneously against the court; and accordingly, standing on those same legal remedies — namely the manifest and prosecution term — staying the execution of that same Kalisz Land Court decree given at Konin and the carrying out of the act of that condescension, he laid the same prosecutions before the Honourable Sub-judge at the condescension term; and since the entire Kalisz Land Court had been incriminated — not only through the prosecution term but also through the timely manifest on grievance and for the review of their decree — and summoned to the Tribunal.

The manifestant, therefore, unable to pursue the matter in that same Kalisz Land Court as having already been prosecuted there, and its own decree being incapable of being reviewed by it — had sought and concluded that the remission of this cause to the Tribunal should be made by the Honourable Sub-judge of the Kalisz Land Court. For the same Honourable Land Judge of Kalisz, instead of making the remission to the Tribunal by virtue of the aforesaid reasons, then — although the aforesaid prosecutions, issued to the Tribunal and not to the Kalisz Land Court, stood in the way — improperly made that remission back to himself and the whole court, who were under prosecution and by force of law already bound to desist from adjudicating that cause, at the motion of the party, namely the Honourable Chełmski; and thereby added empty litigation costs to the manifestant and derogated from his legal rights; and also of the fact that the same act of the condescension could not be completed — for reasons to be given at the prosecution term and to be elucidated through a separate manifest if necessary —

in full force of his every explanation, and also as a certain process of the manifestant's honour and aggrievance, and simultaneously regarding the nullity and want of substance of the aforesaid remission made to the Kalisz Land Court as prosecuted, and the impossibility of being adjudicated together with the Honourable Chełmski in that same prosecuted court — and also of the remission sought there from the same court to the Tribunal — again and again, most solemnly, manifests;

offering to act lawfully in respect of all the foregoing, with all things preserved that ought to be preserved by law, and with full force reserved and safeguarded to himself of augmenting or diminishing the same manifest, or of making another manifest in supplement of this one.

Antoni Na Kolnie Prusimski
