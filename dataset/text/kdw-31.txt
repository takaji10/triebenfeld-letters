# kdw-31  31  attestation
# 1364-04-27  Ląd
# /documents/kdw/31/

Dokument Nr 1519
Wyrzbentha castellanus Poznaniens. n. n. capitaneus Polonie general. 1364 Apr. 27, in Land; protestatur, Dobrocham heram de Wrąbczyn recognovisse, omnem substantiam, quam possideret, esse abbatis de Ląd.
Pergam. orygin. Skrawek pergamin. od przywieszenia pieczęci. Warszawa. Zbiory Rusieckich. Diplomata.
Nos Wyrzbentha castellanus Poznaniensis nec non capitaneus Polonie generalis, universis et singulis quibus nosse fuerit oportunum palam recognoscimus et aperte, quod accedens ad nostram aliorumque terre Polonie militum presenciam nobilis domina nomine Dobrocha, hera de magna Wronczino, existens sana et incolumis per graciam Ihesu Cristi, recognovit publice protestando: quod omnem substanciam quam habet, tam iumenta indomita quam pecora, cum pecudibus et aliis universis que habet et possidet, hec non sunt eius, sed religiosi viri domini Iohannis divina miseracione abbatis in Land suique monasterii in Landa; ita tamen, quod post mortem eiusdem domine Dobroslawe, nec pueri nec aliquis amicorum suorum habet ius et respectum ad omnem substanciam iam dictam, nisi dominus Iohannes abbas Landensis cum suo monasterio prenotato. In cuius rei testimonium, sigillum nostri tytuli presentibus est appensum. Actum et datum in Land sabbato post diem beati Marci ewangeliste, sub anno Nativitatis Domini m.ccc sexagesimo quarto, presentibus hiis testibus et dominis: Laurencio castellano Landensi, Przesprawo vexillifero Calisiensi, Iohanne de Beganowo, Henrico de Wola heredibus, et aliis multis fidedignis.
Magna Wronczino, Wrąbczyn. Land, Ląd. Bieganowo, Wola, dziś tak samo; wsi ostatniego nazwiska znajduje się 5 w okolicy m. Konin.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Derselbe Wyrzbenta beurkundet, dass die edle Frau Dobrocha, Erbin von Groß-Wrąbczyn, gesund und unverwundet, öffentlich anerkannt hat, dass ihre ganze Habe, das ungezähmte Zugvieh so gut wie das Rindvieh, samt den Herden nicht ihr, sondern dem Abt Jan von Ląd und seinem Kloster gehört, und dass nach ihrem Tod weder die Kinder noch einer ihrer Freunde ein Recht daran haben soll; nur der Abt mit seinem Kloster. Ląd, 27. April 1364, am Samstag nach St Markus. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski III, Nr. 1519; am Ende nennt die Urkunde die Erbin "Dobroslawa", im übrigen durchweg Dobrocha.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The same Wyrzbenta attests that the noble lady Dobrocha, heiress of great Wrąbczyn, sound and unharmed, has acknowledged publicly that all her substance, the unbroken beasts of burden as much as the cattle, with the flocks, is not hers but Abbot Jan of Ląd's and his monastery's, and that after her death neither the children nor any of her friends is to have a right in it; the abbot with his monastery alone. Ląd, 27 April 1,364, on the Saturday after St Mark's day. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski III, no. 1519; at the end the document calls the heiress "Dobroslawa", elsewhere Dobrocha throughout.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 1519

Wyrzbenta, castellan of Poznań and the same general captain of Poland, 27 April 1364, at Ląd; attests that Dobrocha, heiress of Wrąbczyn, acknowledged that all the substance she possessed was the abbot of Ląd's.

Original on parchment. A scrap of parchment from the hanging of the seal. Warsaw. The Rusiecki collection. Diplomata.

We, Wyrzbenta, castellan of Poznań and also general captain of Poland, acknowledge openly and plainly to all and singular to whom it shall have been needful to know, that there came before us and the other knights of the land of Poland the noble lady named Dobrocha, heiress of great Wronczino, being sound and unharmed by the grace of Jesus Christ, and she acknowledged, protesting publicly, that all the substance she has, both the untamed beasts of burden and the cattle, with the flocks and all the rest that she has and possesses — these are not hers, but the religious man Lord Jan's, by divine mercy abbot in Ląd, and his monastery's in Ląd; yet so, that after the death of the said lady Dobroslawa, neither the children nor any of her friends shall have right or regard in all the substance aforesaid, but the Lord Jan, abbot of Ląd, with his monastery aforesaid. In testimony of this matter the seal of our title is hung upon these presents. Done and given at Ląd on the Saturday after the day of St Mark the evangelist, in the year of the Lord's birth 1364, in the presence of these witnesses and lords: Laurentius, castellan of Ląd, Przesprawo, banner-bearer of Kalisz, Jan of Bieganowo, Henryk of Wola, heirs, and many others worthy of trust.

Magna Wronczino, Wrąbczyn. Land, Ląd. Bieganowo, Wola, today the same; of villages of the last name there are 5 in the neighbourhood of the town of Konin.
