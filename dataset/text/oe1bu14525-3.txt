--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Abschrift einer Schenkungsurkunde König Friedrich Wilhelms, mit der Friedrich Ludwig, regierender Fürst von Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Generalleutnant und Gouverneur von Wrocław, für langjährige Dienste dem König anheimgefallene Güter erblich und zu freier Verfügung erhält, vorbehaltlich der landesherrlichen Steuern. Der Text der Urkunde nennt Kamionna und Kolno im Posener Kreis und Brzyce in Kujawien; die Aufschrift nennt dazu Trąbczyn, Szetlewek und die übrigen Dörfer im Koniner Kreis. Der Gouvernementsauditeur Richter beglaubigt die Abschrift.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A copy of a deed of gift of King Friedrich Wilhelm by which Friedrich Ludwig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, lieutenant general and governor of Wrocław, receives for long service estates fallen to the King, heritably and to dispose of freely, saving the sovereign's taxes. The text of the deed names Kamionna and Kolno in the Poznań district and Brzyce in Cujavia; its endorsement names Trąbczyn, Szetlewek and the other villages of the Konin district as well. Richter, judge advocate of the governor's court, certifies the copy.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
We Friedrich Willhelm, by the Grace of God King of Prussia, do hereby, by means of this Our open letter, certify and declare, for Ourselves and Our successors in the Crown, the Electorate, and Our remaining lands, and herewith graciously give to know to each and every one whom the same concerns, that as We, out of inborn favour and clemency, are much inclined to acknowledge and to requite the good and fruitful services rendered to Us and to Our Royal House by particular marks of Our Royal good will, We have weighed in Our Royal mind the important, distinguished and useful [text lost: service] rendered to Us and to Our Royal House for [uncertain: several] years by the High-born Prince, Our kindly beloved uncle, Friderich Ludewig, reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Our Lieutenant General of Infantry, Chief of a regiment of foot, Governor of the capital city of Wrocław, Knight of Our Orders etc., and have resolved, in order to give the said Prince a mark of the recognition of his excellent merits and of Our Royal gratitude, to confer and convey to the same, hereditarily and in full property, the hereinafter named estates fallen in to Our All-Highest disposition, namely: the town of Kamionna, and the market township of Kolno, in the district of Poznań, and the village of Brzyce together with its appurtenances in Cujavia, with all their appurtenances, by whatever name they may be called.

We also do the same hereby and by force of this present instrument, out of Royal power and plenitude, for Ourselves and Our descendants and successors in the Crown, the Electorate and Our remaining lands, in such manner and wise that the aforesaid Our kindly beloved uncle, the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, shall have, possess and enjoy the above-named estates with all rights and privileges attaching thereto, as such have hitherto been exercised or may lawfully be exercised, and no less with all their appurtenances and other belongings, by whatever name they may be called, just as the same have hitherto been used or may in law be used, as a well-acquired and unrestricted property — yet under reservation of the sovereign taxes and other public dues to be laid thereupon as upon the other estates of Our South Prussian vassals — for himself and his descendants and heirs, from now on for all eternity, irrevocably from Us and Our successors; and there shall likewise be conferred upon him the power to dispose thereof at his pleasure, unhindered by any man.

We therefore command and enjoin all Our South Prussian Governments and Chambers of War and Domains, as also all other authorities whom the same concerns, herewith as graciously as earnestly, that they in no wise hinder nor trouble the oft-mentioned Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen in the hereditary and proprietary possession of the above-named possessions and estates together with their appurtenances and belongings conferred upon him, nor him or those persons upon whom he, by virtue and in force of this Our letter of donation, may one day bequeath the same, or may otherwise lawfully transfer it by other acts inter vivos aut mortis causa, but rather let him, and eventually them also, [text lost] the same freely and quietly, and themselves do nothing against it, [text lost]

and of unfailing severe penalty. In witness whereof We have signed the present letter of donation with Our own All-Highest hand, etc. Friedrich Willhelm

Deed of donation, for the Reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, to the hereditary and proprietary possession of the town of Kamionna and of the market township of Kolno, in the district of Poznań, of the villages of Trąbczyn, Szetlewek, Nowawies, Łazy, Old and New Laske, Osiny, Hauland Trąbczyński, in the district of Konin, and of the village of Brzyce together with appurtenances in Cujavia.
