--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Entwurf einer Antwort an die Witwe von Brehmer: Ihre an Hardenberg gerichtete Vorstellung vom 26. April sei dem Ministerium zugefertigt worden. Wolle sie wegen des auf Szetlewek eingetragenen Kapitals von 5000 Rthl aus dem übrigen Vermögen des Fürsten Befriedigung suchen, so müsse sie ihn zuerst vor seiner Gerichtsbehörde belangen, um den Anspruch rechtlich festzustellen, falls der Kaiser von Russland sich geneigt erklären sollte, dem Fürsten eine Entschädigung zu gewähren. Auf derselben Seite folgt der Entwurf eines Schreibens an Schöler: Mit Bezug auf das Schreiben vom 29. April teilt das Ministerium mit, dass die hypothekarischen Gläubiger Gefahr laufen, ihre Kapitalien zu verlieren, da der Fürst Trąbczyn deterioriert und mit Schulden belastet hat; Schöler möge dies zur Sprache bringen. Er soll Nachricht geben, falls der Kaiser dem Fürsten eine Entschädigung gewährt, damit die Gläubiger ihre Gerechtsame verfolgen können.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Draft of a reply to the widow Brehmer: her petition of 26 April addressed to Hardenberg has been passed to the ministry. If she wishes to seek satisfaction for the capital of 5,000 Rthl registered on Szetlewek out of the Prince's remaining property, she must first sue him before his own court in order to establish the claim at law, in case the Emperor of Russia should declare himself willing to grant the Prince compensation. On the same page follows the draft of a letter to Schöler: with reference to the letter of 29 April, the ministry reports that the mortgage creditors are in danger of losing their capital, since the Prince has let Trąbczyn deteriorate and has burdened it with debts; Schöler is asked to raise this. He is to send word if the Emperor grants the Prince compensation, so that the creditors can pursue their claims.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Berlin, 29 June 1816.

To the Right Honourable widowed Mrs von Brehmer, Colonel's widow.

At Sommerfeldt, concerning: the compensation of the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen. Hg. 3 July, fair copy made.

On the 6th of July both to the post, No. 3997.

The representation addressed by Your Right Honourable to the State Chancellor, the Prince of Hardenberg, under date of the 26th of April of this year, has been forwarded to me for further action. If [uncertain: Ewohnfrau] therein declares the intention of seeking satisfaction, out of the remaining property of the Prince, her debtor, in respect of the capital of 5,000 Rthl inherited from her deceased brother, which he had lent to the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen and which was registered upon the latter's estate of Szetlewek, confiscated in the year 1807; then it will first of all be your affair to sue him before his own court of jurisdiction, in order to establish the claim at law, in case you, should His Majesty the Emperor of Russia be pleased to declare himself inclined to grant the Prince of Hohenlohe some compensation for the loss of Szetlewek and Trąbczyn, without consideration here, [text lost] desire thereby.

Berlin, 22 June 1816.

4277.

To the Royal Major General and Envoy at the Imperial Russian Court, Mr von Schöler, Your Right Honourable

at St Petersburg

[uncertain: Hg.] 3rd, fair copy made.

With reference to my letter of 29 April of this year concerning the intercession for the Prince Friedrich Ludwig of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen with His Majesty the Emperor of Russia, to procure some

compensation for the estates confiscated from him in the Kalisz department, I [uncertain: find myself moved] most respectfully to communicate to Your Right Honourable the information [illegible] received later, that, since the Prince of Hohenlohe has let the estates of Trąbczyn with appurtenances [uncertain: deteriorate] and has burdened them with debts, the mortgage creditors run the risk of losing their capital, leaving it to your discretion to bring this circumstance into the discussion, should you support the Prince's claim for compensation. At the same time I also most respectfully request [uncertain: Your] Right Honourable, in case His Majesty the Emperor should deign to grant the Prince some compensation, to send word, so that the said creditors may be moved to pursue their rights further.

Berlin, 22 June 1816.

Ministry of Foreign Affairs, 3rd Section.
