--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Entwurf der Resolution im Namen des Königs für Stanislaus von Chełmski zu Kalisch, Berlin, 21. Oktober 1799, wegen seiner Forderung an den Insurgenten Prusimski; auf Befehl gezeichnet, die Unterschrift nicht gelesen. Auf seine am 8. eingereichte Vorstellung müsse es bei der Resolution der Regierung zu Kalisch vom 10. September lediglich sein Bewenden behalten, da jeder Gläubiger mit Personalansprüchen an das ehemalige Vermögen eines Insurgenten sich selbst bescheiden werde, dass die zur Dämpfung der von den Insurgenten erregten Unruhen verwendeten Kriegskosten und die von ihnen verursachten Schäden die ganze Masse eines solchen Insurgenten weit übersteigen. In der linken Spalte Vermerke der Registratur: am 21. eingegangen, am 22. revidiert, zu den Generalakten über das Schuldenwesen der Insurgenten, am 30. zur Post.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Draft of the resolution in the King's name for Stanislaus von Chełmski at Kalisz, Berlin, 21 October 1799, concerning his claim on the insurgent Prusimski; signed by command, the signature not read. In answer to his representation submitted on the 8th, the resolution of the Government at Kalisz of 10 September must simply stand, since every creditor with personal claims on the former property of an insurgent will understand of himself that the costs of war spent on putting down the unrest the insurgents raised, and the damage they caused, far exceed the whole estate of such an insurgent. Registry notes in the left column: received on the 21st, revised on the 22nd, to the general files on the debts of the insurgents, sent by post on the 30th.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Berlin, 21 October 1799. Resolution for Stanislaus von Chełmski at Kalisz.

(concerning his claim on the insurgent

v. Prusimski.) Gr.

acc. on the 21st.

ad rev. on the 22nd.

Files general: concerning the debts of the insurgents, to the Reg[istry]:

on the 30th to the post. South Pr.

His Royal Majesty of Prussia etc., our most gracious Lord, has Stanislaus von Chełmski informed hereby, as answer to the representation he submitted on the 8th of this month concerning his claim on the former Starost von Prusimski, that the resolution of the Government there of 10 Sept.

this year must simply stand, since every creditor who has personal claims to make on the former property of an insurgent will understand of himself that the costs of war spent on putting down the unrest the insurgents raised, and the damage they caused, far exceed the whole estate of such an insurgent.

Signed: Berlin, 21 October 1799. By command.

[Signature]

[?] on the 22nd.

3426.
