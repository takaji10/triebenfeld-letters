Ex Parte M Chełmski Thesaurarium Vschoven[sem] contra JM Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen[sis] Relatio
Videatur hoc loco Relatio in ex parte Magnifici Stanislai Scibor Chełmski Thesaurarii Vschoven[sis] contra Illustrem Magnificum Antonium Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen[sis] pro Judicii Ordinarii Generalibus Tribunalis Regni Petricovien[sis] prout Copia in productis

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Registervermerk von 1781: Bericht über die Ladung, die Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa, gegen Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice, vor das Krontribunal in Petrikau erwirkt hat.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
In 1781 Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa and owner of Łukom, obtained a citation before the Crown Tribunal at Piotrków against Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice and owner of Trąbczyn; the report of it was entered at Konin.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
On behalf of the Illustrious Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, against the Illustrious Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — a Report

Let there be seen at this place a Report on behalf of the Illustrious Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, against the Illustrious Magnificent Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — for the Ordinary General Sessions of the Crown Tribunal of the Kingdom at Piotrków — as the copy in the submitted documents [attests].
