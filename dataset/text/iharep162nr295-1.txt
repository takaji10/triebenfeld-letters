--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Abschrift der Schuldverschreibung des Kriegs- und Forstrats Peter Friedrich August von Triebenfeld, Berlin, 28. Januar 1805. Das dritte Departement des Ober-Kriegs-Kollegiums hat ihm aus der General-Invalidenkasse ein Darlehen von 50.000 Talern Courant bewilligt und in Seehandlungs-Obligationen ausgezahlt. Er verspricht, es nach sechsmonatiger, beiden Teilen freistehender Kündigung zurückzuzahlen und bis dahin mit 5 Prozent in halbjährlichen Raten in Berlin zu verzinsen. Zur Sicherheit verpfändet er sein ganzes Vermögen und besonders die Obligation des Fürsten zu Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen vom 1. Juni 1804 über 250.000 Taler, die auf dessen südpreußische Güter Zagórów, Drzewce, Kopojno, Świątniki, Skokum, Oleśnica, Wrąbczyn und Grądzyń eingetragen ist. Für den Fall der Nichtzahlung tritt er der Kasse 50.000 Taler davon ab, mit Vorrang vor den ihm verbleibenden 200.000. Am selben Tag erkennt er die Urkunde vor dem ersten Kurmärkischen Justizamt in Berlin an.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A copy of the bond of the Councillor of War and Forests Peter Friedrich August von Triebenfeld, Berlin, 28 January 1805. The third department of the Supreme War College has granted him a loan of 50,000 Thaler Courant out of the General Invalids' Fund and paid it out in Seehandlung bonds. He promises to repay it after six months' notice, which either side may give, and until then to pay 5 per cent interest in half-yearly instalments at Berlin. As security he pledges his whole property and, specially, the bond of the Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen of 1 June 1804 for 250,000 Thaler, which is registered on the Prince's South Prussian estates of Zagórów, Drzewce, Kopojno, Świątniki, Skokum, Oleśnica, Wrąbczyn and Grądzyń. In case of non-payment he cedes 50,000 Thaler of it to the fund, with priority over the 200,000 that remain his. The same day he acknowledges the deed before the first Kurmark justice office at Berlin.

Copy.

(6 Groschen stamp)

Whereas the third department of the Royal Supreme War College has granted to me, the undersigned, out of the General Invalids' Fund a loan of 50,000. Rthl, in words Fifty Thousand Thaler in Courant according to the coinage standard of 1764, and has paid out this amount to me this day in Seehandlung bonds, I do hereby not only give receipt in the best form of law for the due reception of the said sum, but also promise to repay the same promptly after six months' notice, which either party is free to give, and until then to pay interest on it yearly at 5 per cent in half-yearly instalments, and to make this payment of interest here in Berlin, or to send it hither free of charge. For the greater security of this loan I pledge to the Most Praiseworthy General Invalids' Fund not only my whole property under a general mortgage, but I also pledge to the same besides, under a special mortgage, the bond annexed hereto dated Wrocław, the 1st of June 1804, which was made out to me by

the reigning Prince of Hohenlohe-Ingelfingen, Royal Prussian General of Infantry and Governor of Wrocław, for the sum of Two Hundred and Fifty Thousand Thaler in Courant, and made out upon his estates situated in South Prussia, the town of Zagórów, Drzewce, Kopojno, Świątniki, Skokum, Olesnica, Wrąbczyn and Grądzyń, and by virtue of a decree of the 5th of June 1804 is registered upon the said estates; eventualiter I cede of the same the sum of 50,000 Rthl as a property of the Most Praiseworthy General Invalids' Fund, in such manner that in respect of this sum I concede to it priority over the 200,000. Rthl still belonging to me in the said bond. I also consent to the sub-registration of this pledge and respective cession, and the Most Praiseworthy Invalids' Fund shall be free, in case of non-payment, to assert upon the said bond the right of property belonging to it in this cession.

In witness whereof I have subscribed the following bond in my own hand and judicially.

Thus done at Berlin, the 28th of January 1805.

I have received this loan of the fifty thousand Thaler.

signed: Peter Friedrich August von Triebenfeld (L. S.)
