--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Bericht des Gerichtsboten Bartłomiej Szepczyński von Trąbczyn vom 21. September 1782. Er hat drei Ausfertigungen einer königlichen Ladung vor das Krontribunal in Petrikau zugestellt, ausgestellt in Petrikau am Montag nach St. Bartholomäus 1782. Geladen sind Kazimierz Zakrzewski, Landrichter von Brześć Kujawski, und seine Söhne Nepomucen, Ignacy und Ludwik aus der Ehe mit der verstorbenen Marianna Prusimska; deren Söhne erster Ehe Paweł und Józef Tomicki; Franciszek Tworowicz und Elżbieta, Frau von Jakub Taczanowski, Kinder der verstorbenen Wiktoria Prusimska; und Kunegunda Kołaczkowska, Tochter der verstorbenen Konstancja Prusimska und des Jan Kowalski. Geladen sind ferner die Amtsträger des Kalischer Landgerichts: der Richter Stefan Leszczyc Zielonacki, der Unterrichter Andrzej Bogdański, der Notar Xawery Mikorski und der Kämmerer Ignacy Rapacki. Kläger ist Antoni Prusimski von Kolno, Starost von Niszczewice: Sohn des verstorbenen Stefan Prusimski und der Wiktoria Malczewska, Enkel von Krzysztof Prusimski und Teresa Golińska, Neffe von deren Söhnen Jan, Bogumił, Antoni und Paweł Prusimski und nach dem kinderlosen Tod von Antoni und Paweł deren einziger Rechtsnachfolger. Er beruft sich auf ein Tribunalsdekret von 1738, das den Eheleuten Kowalski die Erbfolge nach Jan und Bogumił Prusimski absprach, und auf dessen Bestätigung durch ein Dekret von 1760. Er verlangt, die Dekrete des Landgerichts aufzuheben, ihn von dem Anspruch auf die Erbfolge nach Antoni und Paweł Prusimski freizusprechen und den Geladenen ewiges Schweigen aufzuerlegen: Töchter, die der Vater ausgestattet hat, könnten von den Brüdern nichts fordern. Die Ausfertigungen wurden in den Gutshöfen von Zieleniec und Mostki und in Konin im Haus der Bürgerin Wiśniewska hinterlegt, wo die Kanzlei des Landgerichts ist.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A report of the court messenger Bartłomiej Szepczyński of Trąbczyn of 21 September 1782. He has served three copies of a royal citation before the Crown Tribunal at Piotrków, dated at Piotrków on the Monday after St Bartholomew 1782. Cited are Kazimierz Zakrzewski, land judge of Brześć Kujawski, and his sons Nepomucen, Ignacy and Ludwik by his marriage with the late Marianna Prusimska; her sons of her first marriage, Paweł and Józef Tomicki; Franciszek Tworowicz and Elżbieta, wife of Jakub Taczanowski, children of the late Wiktoria Prusimska; and Kunegunda Kołaczkowska, daughter of the late Konstancja Prusimska and Jan Kowalski. Cited too are the officers of the Kalisz land court: the judge Stefan Leszczyc Zielonacki, the sub-judge Andrzej Bogdański, the notary Xawery Mikorski and the chamberlain Ignacy Rapacki. The plaintiff is Antoni Prusimski of Kolno, Starost of Niszczewice: son of the late Stefan Prusimski and Wiktoria Malczewska, grandson of Krzysztof Prusimski and Teresa Golińska, nephew of their sons Jan, Bogumił, Antoni and Paweł Prusimski and, after Antoni and Paweł died childless, their sole successor. He relies on a Tribunal decree of 1738, which denied the Kowalskis the succession to Jan and Bogumił Prusimski, and on its confirmation by a decree of 1760. He asks that the decrees of the land court be quashed, that he be freed from the claim to the succession of Antoni and Paweł Prusimski, and that perpetual silence be laid on those cited: daughters whom their father has dowered can claim nothing from their brothers. The copies were left at the manors of Zieleniec and Mostki and at Konin in the house of the townswoman Wiśniewska, where the chancery of the land court is.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Done in Konin, on Saturday, on the very day of the Feast of Saint Matthew, Apostle and Evangelist, namely the 21st day of the month of September, in the Year of the Lord 1782.

On behalf of the Illustrious Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, against the Illustrious Zakrzewskis and others — a Report

Having come in person to the present Office and Records of the Konin Municipal Court, the Honest Bartłomiej Szepczyński of the village of Trąbczyn, General Royal Process Server, sound [of mind], acknowledged that he had served authenticated Citation Letters of one and the same essence, three in number, for the Ordinary General Sessions of the Crown Tribunal of the Kingdom at Piotrków below-written, of the following tenor:

The following: Stanisław August, by the Grace of God, etc. — to you, the Illustrious Casimir, Judge of the Brest-Kuyavia Land Court, father; Nepomucen, [official] of the Radziejów Municipal Court; Ignacy, Land Court Chamberlain; and Ludwik, [Judge] of Brest-Kuyavia — his sons — the Zakrzewskis; together with the late Illustrious Marianna Prusimska, first [wife] of the late Honourable Józef Tomicki, second [by] marriage wife of the same Illustrious Casimir Zakrzewski, Judge of the Brest-Kuyavia Land Court — the children begotten in the second marriage; and to Paweł and Józef Tomicki, sons begotten by the above-mentioned Honourable Józef Tomicki with the same Illustrious Marianna Prusimska in the first marriage, and other children of the same Honourable Tomicki; and also to Franciszek, Pantler of Podlachia, [and] Elżbieta, wife of the Illustrious Jakub Taczanowski, Huntsman of Trębowla — brother and sister among themselves, of the Tworowicz [family], son and daughter begotten by the late Honourable Michał Tworowicz with the late Illustrious Wiktoria Prusimska, first [by] marriage Wysocka, in the second marriage; and to the same Illustrious Jakub Taczanowski, husband of the Honourable Elżbieta Tworowicz, or to both the Taczanowski spouses jointly; and also to Kunigunda Kowalska, wife of the Honourable Piotr Kołaczkowski, daughter begotten by the late Honourable Jan Kowalski with the late Honourable Konstancja Prusimska, or rather both the Kołaczkowski spouses; and finally to the Illustrious Stefan Leszczyć Zielonacki, Land Judge, Andrzej Bogdański, Sub-judge, Ksawery Mikorski, Notary, and also Ignacy Rapacki, Chamberlain, then Sub-delegated Grievance Judges of the Kalisz Land Court — concerning your persons, estates, sums, and Office, we command [uncertain: ott~] that before Us and Our Ordinary General Court of the Crown Tribunal at Piotrków, when and as the present cause from its Register and appropriate inscription falls to be judged when called and proclaimed, you appear in person and peremptorily —

at the instance of the Illustrious Magnificent Antoni of Kolno Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, Knight of the Order of Saint Stanislaus, son of the late Illustrious Stefan Prusimski begotten with the late Illustrious Wiktoria Malczewska, grandson of the late Illustrious Krzysztof Prusimski and Teresa Golińska, spouses, [descendant] of the late Illustrious Jan, Bogumił, Antoni, and Paweł Prusimski, sons of the above-mentioned Krzysztof and Teresa Golińska, spouses, brothers among themselves and blood brothers of the late father of the Plaintiff, and blood paternal uncles of the Plaintiff himself, and sole natural and legitimate Successor of the Plaintiff after the sterile deaths of the Illustrious Antoni and Paweł —

who cites you, needing due legal assistance, adhering to his manifests made or to be made, and to the Tribunal Decree of the Year 1738, at Piotrków, on Tuesday after the Feast of All Saints, issued between the late Illustrious Jan and Konstancja of the Prusimski family, Kowalski spouses, on one part, and the Illustrious Antoni, Paweł, and Stefan Prusimski, father and uncles of the Plaintiff, on the other part — adjudicating against the late Kowalskis in the claimed succession after the late Illustrious Jan and Bogumił Prusimski who died without issue; [and also] the subsidiary Decree of the Year 1760, likewise at Piotrków, on Thursday, the eve of the Feast of the Presentation of the Most Glorious Virgin Mary, between the late Illustrious Antoni Prusimski, his

uncle, and himself the Plaintiff, and you the Cited — confirming, after the sterile death of the third brother namely Paweł, the said Decree of 1738 together with other Transactions and the paternal disposition, and approving it; and to the paternal disposition of the late Illustrious Krzysztof Prusimski [uncertain: Aci] his grandfather, and other documents supporting this cause; and also to the law of the neighbouring Prussian Territories, and the precedents in similar causes following, and the admitted motion —

cites [you], or rather summons [you] to the above-mentioned, to see and hear:

the above-mentioned Tribunal Decree of 1738 not having been vitiated even by the parties, but rather confirmed by the subsequent Decree of 1760, the same also again approved and declared to be stood by — [the Plaintiff seeks:] the Kalisz Land Court Decrees, first indeed ordering the sharing of documents regarding the successive substance devolved upon the Plaintiff after the late brothers and uncles, also without the Plaintiff's competence having first been resolved, contrary to law and usual practice, to be set aside; then another [decree] for the place of the remission, with the intervening Tribunal decree, the appropriate forum having been found, together with the grievance sentences regarding the accessories of the ordered proceeding and prosecution of the cause, and the grievance decrees following between you the Cited and himself the Plaintiff, and the threatening of the loss of the cause to the Plaintiff being relieved; and also the Kalisz Land Court Decree on the very day of the Feast of Saint Hedwig, Widow, Elect, in the Year 1781 — issued contrary to law, denying and indeed cutting off the delay lawfully due to the Plaintiff without heeding the Tribunal Decrees — and also the last [decree] by which the competent action and plaintiff-standing was found in favour of the Zakrzewskis and others — to be cassated; and penalties both upon you for having procured these decrees, and upon the Illustrious Judges for transgression of the law in their judging, to be imposed; and now any process unduly obtained against the Plaintiff from your part to be cassated, and the Plaintiff to be freed from the chain and rigour of the Kalisz Land Court Decrees, from your demand and the pretended undue succession after the late Illustrious Antoni and Paweł Prusimski, blood brothers of the late father of the Plaintiff and blood uncles of the Plaintiff himself, who died without issue; the incompetent action claimed by you regarding such succession after the late Antoni and Paweł, unduly claimed by you — as also after the late Illustrious Jan and Bogumił Prusimski, their earlier deceased brothers — according to the Tribunal Decree of 1738 and the paternal disposition and the precedents in similar causes following, and the law prohibiting daughters exdotated by their father to claim nothing from brothers, and also [the law] of the neighbouring Prussian Territories — to be declared [incompetent]; the right of action of the Honourable Tomicka rather regarding the improvement of the dowry than the claimed succession, as appointed without a term, to be declared; and perpetual silence to be imposed upon you in the present cause; and penalties for the resuscitation of a matter already decided, for undue harassment, for the issued and procured grievance decrees, and in the force of litigation expenses unduly caused to the Plaintiff, upon you [uncertain: Voles]

and the Kalisz Land Court to be extended — and as for the rest, all those things which shall be [decided] according to law and documents, to be decreed and sentenced against you. Be therefore equally ready to attend this Term and to respond judicially.

Given at [P]iotrków, on Monday, next after the Feast of Saint Bartholomew the Apostle, in the Year of the Lord 1782. The first of them, against the Illustrious Tomickis, in the manor of the estates of the village of Zieleniec, in the presence of the Cited party, on the Thursday most recently past. The second, against the Illustrious Zakrzewskis, in the manor of the estates of the village of Mostki, in the presence of the Cited party, on yesterday, the day preceding today's act. The third, against the Kalisz Land Court, in the town of Konin, in the house of the Renowned Wiśniewska, citizen of Konin, and in the Kalisz Land Court Chancellery existing there during the court sessions, before the same Renowned Wiśniewska, on this very day — having placed [the citations] upon the tables in the heated room at all three [locations] distinctly, and having left notification at every location.
