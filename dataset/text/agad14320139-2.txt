--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Der Jahresbericht des Pastors E. Erdmann für 1882 über das Filial Lissewo-Pyzdry, auf Deutsch, auf dieselbe Anordnung (30. Oktober, Nr. 267) und fast mit denselben Worten wie sein Bericht über die Gemeinde. Wie dieser ist er an den Generalsuperintendenten des Warschauer evangelisch-augsburgischen Konsistorialbezirks gerichtet, und das Blatt ist verbrannt, die Zeilenenden auf der ersten Seite und die Zeilenanfänge auf der zweiten sind verloren. Kirchengebäude: Das Filial hat weder Kirche noch Küsterhaus, und der Gottesdienst in Pyzdry wird in einem gemieteten Lokal abgehalten; es hat fünf Schulen mit Bethäusern, alle in gutem Zustande [das Wort vor „Schulen“ ist teilweise verloren], mit etwa 120 Kindern, und fünf Friedhöfe, alle umzäunt und mit Kreuzen versehen. Das kirchliche Leben ist befriedigend. Die Taufen werden von den Kantoren in den Bethäusern vollzogen, und der Satz fährt mit der Rolle des Pastors dabei fort [der Rest des Satzes ist teilweise verloren]. Die Kommunikanten werden mit 326 Männern und 408 Frauen, zusammen 734, angegeben, 131 mehr als 1881. Die Einwohner von Pyzdry würden gern eine eigene evangelische Schule einrichten, sind aber dazu zu arm, da der Flecken nur 9 Familien zählt [der Satz ist teilweise verloren, und die Lesung „zählt“ ist unsicher]; die Kolonien haben eigene Lehrer und Bethäuser. Die Missionskollekte, 3 Rubel 40 Kopeken, wurde an den Superintendenten Pastor Manitius in Warschau weitergegeben. Der Bericht sagt, es gebe keine Baptisten, Irwingianer und dergleichen, besondere Laster kämen in der Gemeinde nicht vor und Übertritte zu anderen Konfessionen fänden nicht statt, besondere Ereignisse hätten nicht stattgefunden, 23 seien konfirmiert worden (13 Knaben und 10 Mädchen), und in der Gemeinde lebten keine Kandidaten. Für die eigene Gemeinde wurde nichts gesammelt; zu anderen Zwecken wurde 1 Rubel 76 Kopeken für Gymnasiasten, die Theologie studieren wollen, gesammelt und an das Hohe Evangelisch-Augsburgische Konsistorium in Warschau gesandt, und für die Witwen- und Waisenkasse eine Summe, deren Rubel verloren sind, und 36 Kopeken; sie wurde an den Superintendenten von Boerner in Płock gesandt. Unterschrieben ist er von E. Erdmann, Pastor.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Pastor E. Erdmann's annual report for 1882 on the filial Lissewo-Pyzdry, in German, on the same order (30 October, No. 267) and in nearly the same words as his report on the parish. Like it, it is addressed to the General Superintendent of the Warsaw Evangelical-Augsburg consistorial district and its leaf is burnt, the ends of the lines lost on the first page and the beginnings on the second. Church buildings: the filial has neither a church nor a sexton's house, and divine service at Pyzdry is held in a rented room; it has five schools combined with prayer houses, all in good condition [the word before "schools" is partly lost], with about 120 children, and five cemeteries, all fenced and provided with crosses. Church life is satisfactory. The baptisms are performed by the cantors in the prayer houses, and the sentence goes on about the pastor's part in them [the rest of the sentence is partly lost]. The communicants are given as 326 men and 408 women, 734 in all, 131 more than in 1881. The inhabitants of Pyzdry would gladly set up an Evangelical school of their own but are too poor for it, the town counting only 9 families [the sentence is partly lost, and the reading "counting" is uncertain]; the colonies have their own teachers and prayer houses. The mission collection, 3 roubles 40 kopecks, was passed on to Superintendent Pastor Manitius at Warsaw. The report says that there are no Baptists, Irvingians and the like, that no special vices occur in the congregation and no conversions to other confessions, that no remarkable events took place, that 23 were confirmed (13 boys and 10 girls), and that no candidates live in the congregation. Nothing was collected for the congregation's own benefit; for other purposes 1 rouble 76 kopecks was collected for pupils intending to study theology, sent to the High Evangelical-Augsburg Consistory at Warsaw, and for the widows' and orphans' fund a sum of which the roubles are lost and 36 kopecks, sent to Superintendent von Boerner at Plock. It is signed E. Erdmann, pastor.

of the Warsaw Evangelical-Augsburg
Consistorial District.

The Pastor-Administrator [of the]
Evangelical-Augsburg [congregation of]

In consequence of [Your Reverence's] order
of the 30th of October [text lost],
No. 267, I have the honour to submit the following annual report
concerning the filial Lissewo-Pyzdry.

I. Church affairs.

a.) External church affairs.– The filial possesses neither a church [text lost] sexton's house.– Divine service in Pyzdry is held in rented premises.– In the filial there are five religious schools combined with prayer houses, all of them in good condition.– The number of children in the five schools of the filial comes to about 120.– There are five cemeteries in the filial, all fenced and provided with crosses.–

b.) Inner church affairs.– Church spirit and church attendance satisfactory.– Baptisms are performed by the cantors concerned in the prayer houses; the pastor, when he is present in the prayer houses, confirms the baptisms performed by the cantors, and holds the churching[?] with the women [text lost].– Open despisers of the Lord's Supper: none known.– The number of communicants: men 326, women 408, total 734, [text lost] compared with the year 1881, 131 more.– The inhabitants of Pyzdry would gladly set up an Evangelical school among themselves, [but] are too poor for it, since the little town counts only 9 families[?], and [text lost]

[text lost] lay people can be brought in for it, since the [text lost] colonies have their own teachers and prayer houses.–

For the mission to the heathen, 3 roubles 40 kopecks were collected in the course of the year at the mission services, which the Pastor-Administrator sent on to Superintendent Pastor Manitius at Warsaw.– Morality and domestic life are in general satisfactory.– Particular vices in the congregation not present.– Baptists, Irvingians etc. there are none.– Conversions to other confessions do not take place.–

Remarkable events.– None have taken place.–

Number of confirmed.– Boys 13, girls 10, together 23.–

Candidates living in the congregation.– None.–

Collections.– For the benefit of the congregation itself nothing was collected, but for other purposes the following: 1.) For the support of grammar-school pupils who intend to study theology, 1 rouble 76 kopecks, which the Pastor-Administrator sent to his High Evangelical-Augsburg Consistory at Warsaw.– 2.)
