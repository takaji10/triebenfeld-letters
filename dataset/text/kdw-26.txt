Dokument Nr 1044
Wladizlaws rex Polonie etc. 1324 Aug. 9, in Pyser; monasterio de Ląd locum molendini navalis in fluvio Warta sub castro de Pyzdry confert, et possessionem quarundam villarum confirmat.
Pergam. orygin. Na sznurze z nici jedwabn., zielon. przywieszona pieczęć Władysława króla; (pieczęć XLIV). Warszawa. Zbiory Rusieckich. Diplomata.
In nomine Domini amen. Sicut fragilitas humane conditionis mortem nequit vitare temporalem, sic nec ullum actum quod in fluctuoso geritur tempore robur firmitatis valet obtinere, nisi scripto reformetur et sigilli munimine roboretur. Hinc est quod nos Wladizlaws Dei gracia rex Polonie, Cracovie, Sandomirie, Syradie, Cuiavie Lancicieque dux, notum facimus tam presentibus quam futuris, quod ob honorem Domini nostri Ihesu Christi nec non pie ac gloriose genitricis sue semper virginis Marie, in remissionem peccaminum nostrorum omniumque progenitorum nostrorum, et propter nimiam desolacionem et destructionem quam fecimus domui Lendensi Ordinis Cysterciensis cum essemus cum exercitu in Polonia, contulimus eidem domui et .. fratribus locum molendini navalis sub castro Pysdrensi in fluvio Warta, qui nostro dominio et regno dinoscitur pertinere, iure hereditario possidendum, indulgentes fratribus de domo iam dicta omnimodam obcionem, in eodem loco molendinum construere pro libito ipsorum voluntatis, aut antiquum molendinum, quod pertinet ad ipsos, denuo reparare. Ne igitur hec nostra donacio per aliquem in posterum molestetur, volumus et mandamus omnibus capitaneis, castellanis, procuratoribus, advocatis, consulibus, scabinis et singulis civibus in civitate Pysdrensi constitutis, quatenus eosdem fratres in construendo vel reparando molendino, ubi velint in prefatis duobus locis, ullatenus impedire non debeant nec presumant. Item solo divine remuneracionis intuitu confirmamus eidem domui Lendensi has subscriptas hereditates, eis per nobiles Polonie hereditario iure collatas, vel ab ipsis fratribus racionabiliter emptas, in nostram nichillominus defensionem recipientes, videlicet Mynzelino, Zramlyz, Bronchowo, Cysowo, Wardosino, Wszonowo, Wronchyno cum lacu qui Seborowo dicitur. Volumus eciam, ut homines dictarum villarum eadem libertate gaudeant, que in privilegio fundacionis beate memorie proavi nostri Mesichonis magni, illustris ducis Polonie, plenius continetur. Datum et actum in Pyser sub testibus ydoneis: Clemente palatino Syradiensi, comite Petro castellano Poznaniensi, comite Slavanta castellano Kalisiensi, comite Nycolao castellano Bechoviensi, et Petro vicecancellario et canonico Cracoviensi, anno Domini M.CCC vicesimo quarto, in vigilia beati Laurencii martiris et Levite. In cuius rei testimonium, presens scriptum sigilli nostri munimine iussimus roborari.
Lendensis, de Ląd. Mynzelino, Mycielin. Zramlyz, Chrzamlice. Bronchowo, Bronki. Cysowo, Czyżew. Wardosino, Wardężyn. Wszonowo, Sczonów. Wronchyno, Wrąbczyn. Lacus Seborowo, dziś bagno na północ Wrąbczyna. Pyser, Pyzdry. Bechoviensis, de Biechów.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
König Władysław von Polen schenkt dem Haus Ląd zur Sühne der großen Verwüstung, die er mit seinem Heer in Polen an der Abtei verübt hatte, den Platz einer Wassermühle an der Warta unterhalb der Burg Pyzdry, erblich zu besitzen, mit voller Freiheit, dort zu bauen oder die alte Mühle wiederherzustellen, und verbietet allen Beamten der Stadt Pyzdry jede Störung; daneben bestätigt er dem Haus die Güter, die ihm polnische Adlige gegeben oder die Brüder redlich gekauft haben: Mycielin, Chrzamlice, Bronki, Czyżew, Wardężyn, Sczonów und Wrąbczyn (Wronchyno) mit dem See Seborowo, deren Leute die Freiheit des Gründungsprivilegs Mieszkos des Großen genießen sollen. Pyzdry, 9. August 1324. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski II, Nr. 1044.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
At Pyzdry, on 9 August 1324, King Władysław of Poland gives the Cistercian abbey of Ląd, in satisfaction of the great desolation he had worked upon it with his army in Poland, the site of a water mill on the Warta below the castle of Pyzdry, to be possessed hereditarily, with full liberty to build there or to restore the old mill, and forbids all the officials of the town of Pyzdry every hindrance. He also confirms to the abbey the estates given to it by nobles of Poland or honestly bought by the brothers: Mycielin, Chrzamlice, Bronki, Czyżew, Wardężyn, Sczonów and Wrąbczyn (Wronchyno) with the lake of Seborowo, whose people are to enjoy the liberty of the founding privilege of Mieszko the Great.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 1044

Władysław, King of Poland etc., 9 August 1324, at Pyzdry; gives the monastery of Ląd the site of a water mill on the river Warta below the castle of Pyzdry, and confirms its possession of certain villages.

Original on parchment. On a cord of green silk the seal of Władysław the King is hung (seal XLIV). Warsaw. The Rusiecki collection. Diplomata.

In the name of the Lord, amen. As the frailty of the human condition cannot escape temporal death, so no deed done in the shifting time can obtain the strength of firmness, unless it is renewed in writing and strengthened with the protection of a seal. Hence we, Władysław, by the grace of God King of Poland, Duke of Kraków, of Sandomierz, of Sieradz, of Kujawy and of Łęczyca, make known to present and to come, that, for the honour of our Lord Jesus Christ and of his pious and glorious mother, the ever-virgin Mary, in remission of our sins and of all our forefathers', and on account of the great desolation and destruction which we worked upon the house of Ląd of the Cistercian Order when we were in Poland with our army, we have bestowed on the said house and its brothers the site of a water mill below the castle of Pyzdry on the river Warta, which is known to belong to our lordship and kingdom, to be possessed by hereditary right, granting to the brothers of the said house every liberty to build a mill in that place at their own pleasure, or to repair anew the old mill that belongs to them. Lest therefore this our gift be troubled by anyone in the future, we will and command all the captains, castellans, procurators, advocates, consuls, aldermen and every citizen constituted in the town of Pyzdry, that they in no way impede, nor presume to impede, the said brothers in building or repairing the mill, wherever they wish in the two places aforesaid. Moreover, for the regard of the divine reward alone, we confirm to the said house of Ląd these estates written below, given to them by the nobles of Poland by hereditary right, or honestly bought by the brothers themselves, nevertheless receiving them into our defence, namely Mynzelino, Zramlyz, Bronchowo, Cysowo, Wardosino, Wszonowo, Wronchyno, with the lake which is called Seborowo. We also will that the people of the said villages enjoy the same liberty which is contained more fully in the founding privilege of our great-grandfather of blessed memory, Mieszko the Great, illustrious Duke of Poland. Given and done at Pyzdry with suitable witnesses: Klemens, palatine of Sieradz, Count Piotr, castellan of Poznań, Count Slavanta, castellan of Kalisz, Count Mikołaj, castellan of Biechów, and Piotr, vice-chancellor and canon of Kraków, in the year of the Lord 1324, on the vigil of the blessed Laurencjusz, martyr and levite. In testimony of which we have ordered the present document to be strengthened with the protection of our seal.

Lendensis, of Ląd. Mynzelino, Mycielin. Zramlyz, Chrzamlice. Bronchowo, Bronki. Cysowo, Czyżew. Wardosino, Wardężyn. Wszonowo, Sczonów. Wronchyno, Wrąbczyn. Lacus Seborowo, today a marsh north of Wrąbczyn. Pyser, Pyzdry. Bechoviensis, of Biechów.
