Pro parte JM Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen~ Hollandri Traditio
Videatur hoc Loco pro parte J Magnificis Antonii Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen~ Honesti Martini Hunt Hollandri Trąmpczynen~ Traditio, per M Josephum Łukaszewicz Commissarum Palatinatus Majoris Poloniae peracta prout in productis extat.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Registervermerk vom Dezember 1782: Für Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice, liegt die Übergabe des Trąbczyner Olęders Martin Hunt bei den vorgelegten Schriftstücken, vorgenommen von Józef Łukaszewicz, Kommissar der großpolnischen Woiwodschaften.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A register note of December 1782: for Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, the delivery of Martin Hunt, an Olęder settler of Trąbczyn, carried out by Józef Łukaszewicz, commissioner of the palatinates of Greater Poland, is among the documents produced.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
For the part of the Illustrious Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — Delivery of the Olęder

Let there be seen at this place, for the part of the Illustrious Magnificent Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice — the Delivery of the Honest Martin Hunt, Olęder of Trąbczyn, carried out by the Illustrious Józef Łukaszewicz, Commissioner of the Greater Poland Palatinate — as stands in the submitted documents.
