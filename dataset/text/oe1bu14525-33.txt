--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Entwurf Glencks, im Namen des Fürsten zu Hohenlohe, in zwei Fassungen, die eine an die Kammer zu Poznań, die andere an die Regierung gerichtet: Die Regierung zu Poznań habe die Berichtigung des Besitztitels der fürstlichen Güter binnen 14 Tagen gefordert und sonst mit dem Fiskus gedroht; der Fürst habe darauf die fehlenden Schenkungsurkunden im Original eingeschickt. Nun bittet Glenck um die schleunige Abtretung aller fürstlichen Güter in Kujawien und bei Pszczew durch den Fiskus.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
Glenck's draft, written in the name of the Prince of Hohenlohe, in two versions, one addressed to the Chamber at Poznań, the other to the Government: the Government at Poznań, he says, demanded that the title to the Prince's estates be put in order within 14 days, or the fisc would act; the Prince then sent in the missing deeds of gift in the original. Glenck now asks for the speedy formal surrender by the fisc of all the Prince's estates in Cujavia and at Pszczew.

To the Royal Chamber at Poznań.

Most Serene [Lord],

Already under the xxx th of xxx of last year a Royal Government at Poznań did already, according to the enclosed copy, demand of His Serene Highness the correction of the title of possession of the princely Hohenlohe estates as being so necessary and urgent that the treasury (fiscus) was to be moved against any longer delay [uncertain: renn] should the same not have been done within a set period of fourteen days; whereupon His Highness the Prince of Hohenlohe sent in on the 6[uncertain: 6] of March the original deeds of donation that had been wanting for this purpose. As much, then, as His Serene Highness has been pressed herein to the [uncertain: zug.] [uncertain: tilige] correction of the title of possession, so much has [he] since [seen? been] most concerned [uncertain reading] towards the conclusion of this matter; and I have now been charged, in the name of my Lord's Serene Highness, most urgently to beg Your Majesty for the speedy resignation of the treasury over the whole of the princely [uncertain: G]estates in Cujavien and Pszczew. Wrocław, the 10th of February 1802.

To Your Royal Majesty, Glenck
