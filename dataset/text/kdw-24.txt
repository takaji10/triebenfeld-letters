# 1291-08-01  Pyzdry

Dokument Nr 672
Premisl secundus dux Polonie 1291 Aug. 1, in Pisdri; confert Henrico civi de Pyzdry villam Wrąbczyn, iure Theutonico locandam.
Wilanów. Biblioteka. Rejestr Rewizorów do Wielkopolski, fol. 121 v.
In nomine Domini amen. Dum vivit litera vivit et accio commissa litere, cuius assercio nutrit memoriam et roborata suscipit incrementum. Nos igitur Premisl secundus, Dei gracia dux Polonie, notum facimus universis tam presentibus quam futuris presentem paginam inspecturis, quod prospicientes nos per experienciam ius Polonicale in dominio nostro nichil nobis vel parum affere utilitatis, volentes villam nostram iure Polonico locatam nomine Wronchino in meliorem et utiliorem conferre condicionem, ipsam contulimus Henrico, civi nostro de Pisdri, iure Theutonico locari secundum ius civitatis Pisdrensis, ita quod racione sue locacionis prefatus Henricus et sui successores et heredes optinebunt sextum mansum liberum, thabernam, piscinam (sic), molendinum, et cameram liberam sutorum. Donamus eciam silvas et paludes, que extirpari non possunt nec habiles sunt ad agriculturam, pro pascuis peccorum et scropharum; et quidquid de pratis est in districtu eiusdem ville, adiungimus eidem Henrico et successoribus ipsius cum omnibus utilitatibus et attinentiis. Et si apes, que in silva sunt eiusdem ville, perire contingat per melioracionem et locacionem, prefatum Henricum et heredes ipsius propterea non inculpabimus aliquo modo. Ut autem melius locari possit dicta villa, damus duodecem annorum libertatem ab omni exactione et solucione a festo sancti Martini proxime venturi; qua elapsa, singulis annis persolvent nobis incole dicte ville de singulis mansis sex mensuras silliginis, totidem avene, et fertonem denariorum, secundum quod erunt in Pisdri. Liberum eciam arbitrium habeat Henricus cum heredibus suis ibidem ecclesiam construere, et eidem pro decore mansum unum liberum assignare. Preterea dictam villam Wronchino adiungimus ville nostre que Sal nuncupatur, quod utraque unum districtum habeat et limitacionem. Insuper prenominatus Henricus et sui heredes liberum habeant arbitrium in districtu dicte hereditatis lepores venandi et perdices. Ut autem hec nostra donacio inviolabilis perpetuo perseveret, presentem paginam sigilli nostri munimine fecimus communiri. Actum in Pisdri in die Vincula beati Petri, anno Domini millesimo ducentesimo nonagesimo primo, datum per manus. Iasconis notarii curie nostre.
Wronchino, Wrąbczyn. Sal, nie odgadnione; pokazuje się wszakże z odnośnych dokumentów, że w powyżéj oznaczonéj okolicy, t. j. miasteczka Zagurów, wyrabianie soli było w dawnych wiekach pospolitem; cnfr. Nra. 20 i 192. Pisdri, Pyzdry.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Herzog Przemysł II. gibt sein nach polnischem Recht ausgesetztes Dorf Wronchino (Wrąbczyn) dem Henryk, Bürger von Pyzdry, zur Aussetzung nach dem deutschen Recht der Stadt Pyzdry: der sechste Hufe frei, eine Schenke, ein Fischteich, eine Mühle und die freie Schusterstube; die Wälder und Sümpfe, die nicht gerodet werden können, zur Schweine- und Sauweide, die Wiesen des Dorfes mit allen Nutzungen; wenn die Bienen des Waldes durch die Aussetzung zugrunde gehen, wird Henryk dafür nicht verantwortlich gemacht; zwölf Jahre frei von jeder Abgabe, dann von jeder Hufe sechs Scheffel Roggen, ebensoviel Hafer und ein Ferton, wie sie in Pyzdry gelten; Henryk darf mit seinen Erben eine Kirche bauen und ihr eine freie Hufe zuweisen; das Dorf wird der Herzogsstadt Sal zugeordnet, einen Bezirk mit ihr; Hasen- und Rebhuhnjagd frei. Pyzdry, 1. August 1291. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski II, Nr. 672, aus dem Rejestr Rewizorów in der Bibliothek von Wilanów; die Herausgeber können "Sal" nicht deuten, weisen aber auf den alten Salzbau bei Zagórów.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
At Pyzdry, on 1 August 1291, Duke Przemysł II gives his village Wronchino (Wrąbczyn), located under Polish law, to Henryk, citizen of Pyzdry, to locate under the German law of the town of Pyzdry: the sixth mansus free, a tavern, a fishpond, a mill and the free shoemakers' shop; the woods and marshes, which cannot be cleared, for the pasture of pigs and sows, the meadows of the village with all their uses; if the bees of the wood perish through the location, Henryk is not to be blamed for it; twelve years free of every payment, then six measures of rye, as much oats and one ferton from each mansus, as they count in Pyzdry; Henryk may build a church with his heirs and assign it a free mansus; the village is joined to the duke's town of Sal, one district with it; the hunt of hares and partridges free. The editors cannot interpret "Sal", but point to the old salt-working near Zagórów.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 672

Przemysł the Second, Duke of Poland, 1 August 1291, at Pyzdry; gives Henryk, citizen of Pyzdry, the village of Wrąbczyn, to be located under German law.

Wilanów. Library. The Register of the Reviewers of Greater Poland, fol. 121v.

In the name of the Lord, amen. While the letter lives, the deed committed to the letter lives, whose assertion nourishes memory and, strengthened, takes increase. We therefore, Przemysł the Second, by the grace of God Duke of Poland, make known to all, both present and to come, who examine this page, that, seeing by experience that the Polish law in our lordship brings us nothing or little use, and wishing to bring our village, located under Polish law, named Wronchino, into a better and more useful condition, we have bestowed it on Henryk, our citizen of Pyzdry, to be located under German law according to the law of the town of Pyzdry, so that by reason of his location the said Henryk and his successors and heirs shall have the sixth mansus free, a tavern, a fishpond (sic), a mill, and a free shoemakers' shop. We also give the woods and marshes, which cannot be rooted out and are not fit for tillage, for the pasture of pigs and of sows; and whatever of meadow there is in the district of the same village we join to the said Henryk and his successors with all its uses and appurtenances. And if the bees, which are in the wood of the same village, should perish through the improvement and the location, we shall not on that account blame the said Henryk or his heirs in any way. And that the said village may be the better located, we give a liberty of twelve years from every exaction and payment, from the feast of St Martin next coming; when that has passed, the inhabitants of the said village shall pay to us each year from each mansus six measures of rye, the same of oats, and a ferton of denarii, according as they shall be in Pyzdry. Henryk shall also have free authority with his heirs to build a church there, and to assign to it one free mansus for its adornment. Besides, we join the said village of Wronchino to our town which is called Sal, so that both have one district and one boundary. Moreover, the aforesaid Henryk and his heirs shall have free authority to hunt hares and partridges in the district of the said estate. And that this our gift may persevere inviolate for ever, we have had the present page strengthened with the protection of our seal. Done at Pyzdry on the day of the Chains of blessed Peter, in the year of the Lord one thousand two hundred and ninety-one, given by the hand of Jaszko, notary of our court.

Wronchino, Wrąbczyn. Sal, not identified; it appears nevertheless from the related documents that in the region marked above, that is the little town of Zagórów, the making of salt was common in the early centuries; cf. nos. 20 and 192. Pisdri, Pyzdry.
