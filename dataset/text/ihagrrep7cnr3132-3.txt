--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Haugwitz' Konzept seiner Antwort an Goldbeck, Berlin, 29. April 1803. In völligem Einverständnis mit Goldbecks Meinung über Prusimski und Jezierski hat er den ihm übersandten Bericht unterzeichnet und mundieren lassen und schickt die Reinschrift zur Mitzeichnung und Absendung, nebst dem Konzept. An der einzigen Stelle, wo der Bericht sagt, dass es bei der Vermögenskonfiskation unabänderlich bleiben müsse, hat er hinzugefügt, dass er sich einer Meinung enthält, weil der Punkt zu Goldbecks Ressort gehört und außerhalb seines eigenen liegt. Die Akten gibt er mit Dank zurück. Unterzeichnet Haug[witz]. Ein Vermerk unter dem Text lautet "d. 3ten May. ins: cum 2 vol: Actor:" (3. Mai, mit 2 Bänden Akten).

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
On 29 April 1803 Haugwitz drafted his reply to Goldbeck: in full agreement with Goldbeck's opinion on Prusimski and Jezierski, he had signed the report Goldbeck sent him and had it engrossed, and sent the fair copy for Goldbeck's countersignature and dispatch. At the one place where the report says that the confiscation of their property must remain unalterable, he had added that he refrains from expressing an opinion, because the point belongs to Goldbeck's department and lies outside his own. He returned the files with thanks.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
An d 29 Apr. 1803

To His [sub?] lit. Lord von Goldbeck, Excellency

An v Prusimski & Jezierski

In full agreement with Your Excellency's enlightened opinion on Prusimski and Jezierski, I have signed the report Your Excellency kindly communicated to me, and had the fair copy made; and I have the honour to send you most obediently the fair copy, for your kind countersignature and dispatch, together with the draft. At the one place where it says that the confiscation of property must remain unalterable, I have believed, since this point belongs solely to your department, that I had to add that I

d. 3rd May. ins: cum 2 vol: Actor: pLch:

refrain from expressing an opinion, since this matter lies outside my sphere of business.

The files kindly communicated to me I return herewith with thanks.

Berlin, 29 Apr. 1803.

Haug[witz]
