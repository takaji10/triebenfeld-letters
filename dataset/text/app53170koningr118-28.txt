Decreti Condescensorialis Granicialis Inter bona Trąmpczyn et Łukom Prolati Oblata
Videatur hoc loco oblata Decreti Condescensorialis Granicialis Inter Illustrem Magnificum Antonium Prusimski Capitanei Nieszczevicen~ et Magnificum Stanislaum Chełmski Thesauararium Vschovensem et alios Inter bona Trąmpczyn et Łukom cum descriptione scopulorum prolati in productis

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Registervermerk vom Dezember 1782: Das Grenzdekret des Ortstermins zwischen den Gütern Trąbczyn und Łukom, ergangen zwischen Antoni Prusimski, Starost von Niszczewice, Stanisław Chełmski, Schatzmeister von Wschowa, und anderen, mit der Beschreibung der Grenzhügel, wird zur Eintragung vorgelegt und liegt bei den vorgelegten Schriftstücken.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
A register note of December 1782: the boundary decree of the sitting on the ground between the estates of Trąbczyn and Łukom, given between Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, and others, with the description of the boundary mounds, is brought in for entry and is among the documents produced.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Enrollment of a Condescensorial Boundary Decree issued between the estates of Trąbczyn and Łukom

Let there be seen at this place the Enrollment of a Condescensorial Boundary Decree between the Illustrious Magnificent Antoni Prusimski, Starost of Niszczewice, and the Illustrious Stanisław Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa, and others — [issued] between the estates of Trąbczyn and Łukom — with the description of the boundary mounds — in the submitted documents.
