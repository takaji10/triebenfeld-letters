--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Hahn übersendet im Auftrag des Fürsten eine Charta blanche und einen Auszug aus Glencks Schreiben vom 22. März 1806. Der Fürst will Glenck noch einmal retten und fordert Triebenfeld auf, ebenso zu handeln und ihm das Schema zum Consens zu schicken. Lehnt Glenck die Bedingungen erneut ab, soll man ihn seinem Verderben überlassen. Glenck klagt über Not wegen der Wrąbczyner Güter, hat Wilhelmsruh vor der Subhastation gerettet und will mit dem Consens Ribbeck aus dem Kauf von Wrąbczyn bringen.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
On the Prince's instructions, Hahn sends a charta blanche and an extract from Glenck's letter of 22 March 1806. The Prince wants to rescue Glenck once more and calls on Triebenfeld to act in the same way and to send him the draft of the consent. If Glenck again rejects the conditions, he is to be left to his ruin. Glenck complains of hardship because of the Wrąbczyn estates, says he has saved Wilhelmsruh from forced sale, and wants to use the consent to get Ribbeck out of the purchase of Wrąbczyn.

190 By way of supplement I am, on the command of His Serene Highness the Prince, to send Your Right Honourable the enclosed charta blanche for the reply to Mr von Rewen[uncertain: Rewen], which you may be pleased to have made out according to your own view of the matter. I also enclose herewith the extract from a letter of the mining councillor Glenck, as His Serene Highness has commanded me. The folly of this man, his ever-active but all the more senseless spirit of speculation, his frivolity and his lady wife have led him to the brink of the abyss, on which he truly stands. It now rests with the magnanimity both of the Prince and of Your Right Honourable whether you will once more extend to him the hand of rescue.

His Serene Highness is ready for this, and hereby calls upon you to act in the same manner; and I should have to be ignorant of your magnanimous and readily giving heart if you were not willing to comply with the Prince's wish in this respect, should it be in any way possible. In order to bring this about, it is left to Your Right Honourable to bring Glenck at last to silence by sending the draft of the consent to His Serene Highness.
