Dokument Nr 228
Kasimirus dux Coavie et Landensis 1241 Jun. 17, in Copagnin; commutat haereditates suas Głowiew et Wrąbczyn pro haereditate monasterii de Ląd, dicta Koszanow.
Pergam. orygin. Na niciach lniannych biał. i niebiesk. przwieszona pieczęć, przedstawia Jeźdzca zbrojnego pędzącego w prawo. Na głowie hełm płaski, na lewicy tarcz trójkątna, prawica trzyma prostopadle włócznią z długim proporcem. Napis: S. Kazim.... ucis Cuyavie. Oprócz tego 4 wcięcia poprzeczne od przywieszenia drugiéj pieczęci. Warszawa. Biblioteka prywatna. Diplomata.
In nomine sancte et individue Trinitatis amen. Quod geritur in tempore, simul cum labente labitur tempore nisi vivacis littere memoria perhennetur. Hinc est quod ego Kasimirus Dei gracia dux Coavie et Landensis notum facio omnibus tam presentibus quam futuris, comutasse me cum abbate de Landa Iohanne et eius fratribus duas hereditates cum omnibus pertinentiis, videlicet Glovevo et Wronecino que olim venatoribus spectabat, pro una hereditate predictorum fratrum que dicitur Coscheno; et in predictis villis scilicet Glovevo et Wronecino, omnem eandem libertatem quam in aliis hereditatibus ecclesie sue predicti fratres hactenus habuerunt, concessimus. Et ne super hoc scrupulus alicuius dubietatis in posterum oriatur, sigilli nostri appensione roboravimus. Testes vero huius facti sunt: Boseus castellanus de Landa, Nycholaus thesaurarius, Gerardus thesaurarius, Lasch subiudex, Brezdreve subpincerna, Lancomirus subagazon, Nycholaus de Yrgove, Golug et Pribizlaus milites; Marcus, Cunrardus, Petrus et Mychael capellani; Iarozlaus, Iohannes, Mychael armigeri ipsius ducis; Henricus maior cellerarius, Nycholaus, Willelmus fratres predicte domus. Acta sunt hec in Copagnin anno Domini M.CC.XXXXI, XV Kalendas Iulii.
Coavia, Kujawy. Landensis, de Ląd. Glovevo, Głowiew. Wronecino, Wrąbczyn. Coscheno, Koszanów. Yrgove, Rzgów. Copagnin, Kopojno.

--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Herzog Kasimierz von Kujawy und Ląd tauscht mit Abt Jan von Ląd und seinen Brüdern seine Güter Glovevo (Głowiew) und Wronecino (Wrąbczyn), ehemals Land der Jäger, gegen deren Gut Coscheno (Koszanów) ein und gewährt den beiden Dörfern dieselbe Freiheit wie den übrigen Gütern ihrer Kirche. Kopojno, 17. Juni 1241, vor Bosek, Kastellan von Ląd, dem Schatzmeister Mikołaj und anderen. Kodeks Dyplomatyczny Wielkopolski I, Nr. 228, nach dem Original in einer Privatsammlung in Warschau; die Siegelbeschreibung zeigt einen berittenen Bewaffneten.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
At Kopojno, on 17 June 1241, before Bosek, castellan of Ląd, the treasurer Mikołaj and others, Duke Kazimierz of Kujawy and Ląd exchanges with Jan, abbot of Ląd, and his brothers his estates of Glovevo (Głowiewo) and Wronecino (Wrąbczyn), once the huntsmen's land, for their estate of Coscheno (Koszanów), and grants the two villages the same liberty as the other estates of their church.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Document no. 228

Kazimierz, Duke of Kujawy and Ląd, 17 June 1241, at Kopojno; exchanges his estates of Głowiewo and Wrąbczyn for the estate of the monastery of Ląd called Koszanów.

Original on parchment. On threads of white and blue linen a seal is hung, showing an armed rider riding to the right. On his head a flat helmet, at his left a triangular shield, the right hand holding upright a lance with a long pennon. Inscription: S. Kazim.... ucis Cuyavie. Besides this, 4 crosswise notches from the hanging of a second seal.

Warsaw. A private library. Diplomata.

In the name of the holy and undivided Trinity, amen. What is done in time slips away with the time that slips, unless it is made everlasting by the memory of the living letter. Hence it is that I, Kazimierz, by the grace of God Duke of Kujawy and Ląd, make known to all, both present and to come, that I have exchanged with Jan, abbot of Ląd, and his brothers two estates with all their appurtenances, namely Glovevo and Wronecino, which once belonged to the huntsmen, for one estate of the said brothers which is called Coscheno; and in the said villages, namely Glovevo and Wronecino, we have granted every same liberty which the said brothers have had hitherto in the other estates of their church. And lest any scruple of doubt about this arise in the future, we have strengthened it with the hanging of our seal. The witnesses of this deed are: Bosek, castellan of Ląd, Mikołaj the treasurer, Gerard the treasurer, Lasch the under-judge, Brezdreve the under-cupbearer, Lancomir the under-falconer, Mikołaj of Rzgów, Golug and Pribizlaus, knights; Marek, Konrad, Piotr and Michał, chaplains; Jarosław, Jan and Michał, the duke's squires; Henryk the chief cellarer, Mikołaj and Wilhelm, brothers of the said house. This was done at Kopojno in the year of the Lord 1241, on the fifteenth day before the Kalends of July.

Coavia, Kujawy. Landensis, of Ląd. Glovevo, Głowiew. Wronecino, Wrąbczyn. Coscheno, Koszanów. Yrgove, Rzgów. Copagnin, Kopojno.
