--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Das Konzept des Reskripts im Namen des Königs an die Kammer zu Posen, Berlin, 30. Mai 1803, unterzeichnet von Haugwitz, zur Mitzeichnung des Staatsministers von Voss in Konzept und Reinschrift bestimmt und am 8. Juni mit der Post abgegangen. Es übersendet eine Abschrift der Kabinettsordre vom 10. Mai, durch die dem ehemaligen Starosten Anton von Prusimski und dem ehemaligen Kanonikus Xaver von Jezierski die Rückkehr in ihr Vaterland erlaubt wird, und befiehlt der Kammer, beide nunmehr den Huldigungseid leisten zu lassen und zu berichten, dass es geschehen ist.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The draft of the rescript in the King's name to the Chamber at Poznań, Berlin, 30 May 1803, signed by Haugwitz, marked for the countersignature of the minister of state von Voss in draft and fair copy, and sent by post on 8 June. It sends a copy of the cabinet order of 10 May granting the former Starost Anton von Prusimski and the former canon Xaver von Jezierski leave to return to their fatherland, and commands the Chamber now to have both take the oath of allegiance and to report that it has been done.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Berlin, 30 May 1803

To the Chamber at Poznań.

[H.?]

for the kind countersignature of His Royal Minister of State etc. von Voss, Excellency, in draft and fair copy

8 June of this year, sent by post.

Friedrich Wilhelm

In the copy enclosed We send you the cabinet order issued on the 10th of this month, by which the former Starost Anton von Prusimski and the former canon Xaver von Jezierski are granted leave to return to their fatherland, and command you now to have both take the oath of allegiance, and to report here how it has been done.

Sind etc.

Berlin, 30 May 1803.

By command:

[M?] Haugwitz
