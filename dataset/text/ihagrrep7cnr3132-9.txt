--- SUMMARY (German) ---
Das Konzept eines Schreibens an den Staatsminister von Voss, Berlin, 30. Mai 1803, ohne Unterschrift, mit einer als [H?] gelesenen Paraphe; der Schreiber ist offenbar Haugwitz, der in Dokument 10 berichtet, dass Voss mitgezeichnet hat. Da der König dem ehemaligen Starosten von Prusimski und Xaver von Jezierski mit der Kabinettsordre vom 10. Mai die Rückkehr in ihr Vaterland erlaubt hat, teilt der Schreiber dies Voss in beiliegender Abschrift mit und stellt ihm anheim, das in Konzept und Reinschrift beigefügte Notificatorium an die Kammer zu Posen in dieser Sache mitzuzeichnen.

--- SUMMARY (English) ---
The draft of a letter to the minister of state von Voss, Berlin, 30 May 1803, unsigned, with a paraph read [H?]; the writer is evidently Haugwitz, who reports in document 10 that Voss has countersigned. The King having granted the former Starost von Prusimski and Xaver von Jezierski leave to return to their fatherland by the cabinet order of 10 May, the writer informs Voss of it by an enclosed copy, and asks whether Voss will countersign the notificatorium on the matter to the Chamber at Poznań, enclosed in draft and fair copy.

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION (draft) ---
Berlin, 30 May 1803

To His Royal Minister of State etc. von Voss, Excellency.

[H?]

His Royal Majesty having been pleased, by cabinet order of the 10th of this month, most graciously to grant the former Starost von Prusimski and Xaver von Jezierski the leave they asked for to return to their fatherland, I do not fail to bring this to Your Excellency's closer knowledge through the copy enclosed, and leave it most obediently to you whether you will be pleased to countersign the notificatorium on this matter to the Chamber at Poznań, enclosed in draft and fair copy.

Berlin, 30 May 1803
