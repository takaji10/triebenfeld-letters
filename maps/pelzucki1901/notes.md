# Pełzucki 1901 — the survey of the colony Nowy Trąbczyn's lands

The twenty-second map in the edition and one of the bulk batch of 2026-11-06
(docs/ADDING_A_MAP.md; the register's entry in `reference/maps.yml`). The
**odrys pomiaru** (the certified copy of the survey) of the lands of the
colony **Nowy Trąbczyn**, Słupca poviat, Kalisz Governorate, 1901 — APŁ
39/98/0/-/10091. The map of record is the unit's first image (the editor's
word: "001 only"; the folder's 002 is left out). The editor's MapWarper
georeference (75703, "Nowy Trąbczyn colony in the Słupecki poviat…") is on
the same image (9442 × 6629) — the points fetched directly.

## The labels

The editor marked **three labels**: the colony's name and its sections G
and F (`trabczyn`, `trabczyn-g`, `trabczyn-f` in the gazetteer). The
lettering is Russian, and the editor marked them with the Polish standard
in the form field — the same treatment as mapadrogi1890, though this map
was not named as the batch's exception. The OCR (2026-11-06, Claude's read
of the crops): **Тромбчинъ**, **Тромбчинъ Г**, **Тромбчинъ Ф**, all high
confidence — and the **б** of Тромбчинъ agrees with the editor's correction
of mapadrogi1890's Trąbczyn reading the same evening (the two maps
transliterate alike). `form:` carries the Russian, `form_standard:` the
editor's Polish, as on mapadrogi1890.

## The georeferencing

The editor's 10 MapWarper points plus the 3 marks: **order 1, RMS 42.7 m**
— good for a surveyed plan. One residual flagged for the editor's eye
(a misfit is a question, not a correction): **mk-pelzucki1901-003** (the
Trąbczyn mark, 130 m) — worth a look at the overlay
(takaji10.github.io/triebenfeld-maps/warp.html?map=pelzucki1901): the
survey covers the colony's lands, and the village's mark may sit where the
colony's label was drawn rather than at the village's modern point.
