# Trójwiorstówki 1880 (`trojwiorstowki1880`)

**Renamed 2026-11-14** (the editor) from `trojwiorstowki1914` / "Trójwiorstówki 1914":
the sheet is dated ca. 1880 (Mapster). The old addresses /maps/trojwiorstowki1914/ and
/de/karten/trojwiorstowki1914/ redirect; the tiles moved to
triebenfeld-maps/trojwiorstowki1880/. The register's title, maker and reference now
follow the library catalogues (see below): the Russian General Staff's *Военно-
топографическая карта Европейской России*, Râd XVII List D, the k.u.k. reprint; the
maker is no longer given as Schubert.

The sheet XVII D "Pyzdry, Konin" of the three-verst map, in the editor's file
`XVII-D_KuK_126K_Pyzdry-Konin.jpg` (Mapster 1761425, Archiwum Map WIG).

## What the sheet is (researched 2026-11-14)

- The lettering is German with Polish place names (Fähre, Friedhof, Meierhof...):
  the k.u.k. reprint ("KuK" in the file name) of a Russian three-verst (1:126 000)
  sheet. **Which Russian series is not settled (corrected 2026-11-14).** The sheet
  code XVII D is the Russian three-verst series' Râd/List scheme (RCIN: "Râd XVIII
  List V"...), not the Kingdom of Poland map's Kolumna/Sekcja scheme, so the
  original is probably the Russian "Voenno-topografičeskaâ karta" (Râd XVII, List
  D), not the *Topograficzna Karta Królestwa Polskiego* (1839/1843, Richter; Kol. I
  Sek. II Konin; legend on Kol. II Sek. VIII Sławków; Mapster has it). RCIN has no
  Râd XVII sheet of this area. An earlier version of this note said the Kingdom of
  Poland map: not supported.
- **Schubert is not confirmed** as the maker. A Habr article (habr.com/en/articles/205310)
  calls "Schubert's three-verst map" a misconception and credits Maj. Gen. P. A.
  Tuchkov (Glushkov, *Istoriâ voennoj kartografii v Rossii*; *Zapiski Voenno-
  topografičeskogo depo* 9-10, 1847); the Polish map is Richter's. Sources to
  check: Glushkov; the *Zapiski VTD*; *Kartografia Królestwa Polskiego 1815-1915*
  (PAN 2000).
- **The date is ca. 1880** (Mapster, "rok wydania ±1880"; the editor's ruling,
  2026-11-14). The short form and the slug were renamed to 1880 the same day (the editor).

## The abbreviations (abbreviations.yml, shown under the map)

Printed: M.H. (Meierhof, a folwark), H.H. (Herrenhof, a manor house) - both
probable; Fr. (Friedhof), Jsr. Fr. (Israelitischer Friedhof), Kol., Hol., Fähre.
Read from the sheet's lettering and where each stands; the printed key (Sławków
sheet) has not been read yet. **Unknown**: G. (G. Imielno, G. Dziadolina, both
beside parcelled fields and places the gazetteer calls abandoned or forest land),
Bn. H. (Bn. H. Kapczyk, in forest by Czarny Bród: the editor, 2026-11-14, would
label it a forester's house), Z.O. (inland, near Imielno and Bystrzyca; "Zoll" is
doubtful), Tr. P., F.W., Chol. (probably the Cyrillic Хол., Holendry).

## Catalogue records of the series (2026-11-14)

None of these names Schubert; all give the series title as *Военно-топографическая
карта Европейской России*, 1:126 000, by the General Staff's military topographic
department. **A citable form (the sheet itself is in no catalogue found):**
*Военно-топографическая карта Европейской России, 1:126 000* (Voenno-topografičeskaâ
karta Evropejskoj Rossii), Voenno-topografičeskij otdel Glavnogo štaba, Râd XVII
List D (Pyzdry, Konin), k.u.k. reprint, ca. 1880 (Mapster 1761425, Archiwum Map WIG).
- Library of Congress, "Topograficheskie karty evropeĭskoĭ chasti Rossiĭskoĭ Imperii
  masstaba 1:126 000" (Topographic maps of the European part of the Russian Empire),
  LCCN 2017588176, call no. G7010 s126 .R8; made by the Imperial Russian Army General
  Staff ca. 1865-1917, "some later editions ... by German and Polish mapping
  agencies" (loc.gov/item/2017588176; guides.loc.gov/maps-genealogy-eastern-europe/
  topographic-sets/russia).
- Russian Geographical Society geoportal, series "Военно-топографическая карта
  Европейской России (М 1:126 000)", e.g. Ряд XXI Лист 3 (Volhynia and Lublin),
  dated 1915, Военно-топографический отдел (geoportal.rgo.ru/record/652).
- RCIN (rcin.org.pl, IGiPZ PAN), the series with the Polish sheets lettered (Râd XVIII
  List V "g. kališskoj, petrokovskoj i varšavskoj", 1911; no Râd XVII sheet).
- Habr, habr.com/en/articles/205310 (title "Трехверстная военно-топографическая карта
  Западной России" of 1845; Tuchkov, not Schubert): an article, not a catalogue.
**Not found**: the sheet Râd XVII List D in any catalogue; a catalogue naming Schubert.

### The Library of Congress record, read in full (2026-11-14, loc.gov/item/2017588176)

*[Topograficheskie karty evropeĭskoĭ chasti Rossiĭskoĭ Imperii masstaba 1:126 000]*,
LCCN 2017588176, call no. G7010 s126 .R8, about 2000 maps in seven digital sets
(3 483 images), published "[Saint Petersburg?]: [General Staff of the Russian
Army], [1865?-1917?]". The cartographers it names are the **General Staff's
Cartographic Establishment** (Russia. Armiâ. Glavnyj štab. Kartografičeskoe
zavedenie) and the **Prussian Landesaufnahme**; Schubert is not named. Its notes:
sheets have row numbers (Râd / Reihe) and sheet numbers (List / Blatt) in the upper
margin; "Various editions of some sheets"; "Some sheets have been reissued and
reprinted by German ('Auflagedruck 1917') and Polish mapping agencies"; languages
German, Polish, Russian; titles devised by the cataloguer. LC does not catalogue the
sheets one by one, so Râd XVII List D is not itself listed there; its title says
the series is "Topographic maps of the European part of the Russian Empire".
The record uses the cataloguer's title, not the series' own (Военно-топографическая
карта Европейской России, as the RGO geoportal has it).
