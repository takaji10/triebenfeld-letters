## Persons

### Trąmpczyński / Otto-Trąmpczyński family (16th–17th c.)

- **Wojciech Trąmpczyński (16th c.)** — co-heir of Trąbczyn, uncle and guardian of his nephews Wawrzyniec, Walenty, Rachwał, and Stanisław; conducted the disputed 1589 *wizja* attested in 1592.
  - Spellings encountered: Wojciech (Justin); *Albertus Trąmpczyński Otha* (1589 corpus document); Wojciech Trąmpczyński (1776 source's later reconstruction).
  - **Open question (still open):** The 1589 corpus document names *Albertus Trąmpczyński Otha* as plaintiff; the 1776 source's reconstruction names Wojciech. Justin follows the 1776 reading. A footnote acknowledging the divergence is still the recommended treatment.
  - **NEW:** The 1592 *wizja* attestation would be enrolled in **Konin Gr. 29**, *Inscriptiones, relationes [inducta]* 1592–1593 — identified but not yet consulted.

- **Albertus Trąmpczyński Otha** — see Wojciech above. Possibly the same individual; possibly a parallel-action plaintiff in a related 1589 decree. The Otha designation marks him as Otto-line.

- **Wawrzyniec, Walenty, Rachwał, Stanisław Trąmpczyński (16th c.)** — four nephews of Wojciech; minors under his guardianship; co-heirs of Trąbczyn. Sons of the late Jan Trąmpczyński and his widow Szczęsna née Popów per the 1596 agreement (1776 source f. 677–677v).

- **Stanisław Otto-Trąmpczyński (early 17th c.)** — owner of Trąbczyn; married Anna of Słończyc; their sons (per 1644 corpus record): Marcin, Wojciech, Władysław, Andrzej Otto-Trąmpczyński.

- **Stanisław Otto-Trąmpczyński (18th c., the commissioner)** — **NEW, and distinct from the 17th-c. namesake above.** Confirmed from Kalisz Gr. 425 as one of the commissioners on the 1775 panel, signing the adjournment decree "Stanisław Otto-Trąmpczyński Kommissarz manu propria." Listed in the 1776 commission record among the commissioners "sitting jointly from both parties" (with Maciej Mierzewski and Ksawery Szczaniecki) — i.e. a neutral/shared commissioner, not aligned to either side. Appears in abbreviated form as **"Stanisław Trąbczyński."**
  - **Variant forms to watch:** Stanisław Otto-Trąmpczyński / Stanisław Trąbczyński (same person).
  - **Note of interest:** he bore the old Trąbczyn family name, making him a descendant of the family whose ownership preceded the Prusimskis.

- **Anna of Słończyc / Anna Trąpczyńska of Słończyce** — widow of Stanisław Otto-Trąmpczyński; *administratrix* of the family estate in 1644; lodged the 1644 Pyzdry manifest about the Cygany mounds. Variants: Słończyc (Latin), Słończyce (Justin's prose).

- **Marcin, Wojciech, Władysław, Andrzej Otto-Trąmpczyński (mid-17th c.)** — four full-blood brothers, sons of Stanisław and Anna of Słończyc. Plaintiffs in the 1644 Pyzdry vision against Jerzy Chełmski; defendants in the 1644 Konin Z. 36 decree brought by the same Jerzy Chełmski.
  - **RESOLVED:** The "Trąpczyński defendants" mislabel in the Pyzdry action has been corrected, and a new paragraph integrating the previously uncited 1644 Konin land court decree (Chełmski v. Otto-Trąmpczyński) has been added.

- **Andrzej, Jan, Władysław Otto-Trąmpczyński (late 17th c.)** — heirs of Trąbczyn at the 1693 partition (Pyzdry, 6 October 1693). Per Minakowski: Andrzej and Władysław (jr.) are sons of Władysław (sr.) and Anna Bojanowska; the genealogy of "Jan" of the 1693 partition is contested.

- **Anna Bojanowska / of her first marriage Trąbczyńska and thereafter Golińska** — widow of Władysław (sr.) Otto-Trąmpczyński; sold the Trąbczyn estate to Jan Prusimski for 90,000 Polish *złoty* in 1698 (Monday after Saint Margaret 1698).
  - **Still open:** the original 1698 sale instrument has not been located. Expected in a **Resignationes** volume of the Castrensia Posnaniensia series at APP Poznań, not in Gr. 387 (which holds primarily financial *inscriptiones*). Her Bojanowski brothers are named as Alexander, Karol, Krzysztof, and Bogusław. **Name correction:** the surname read as "Czetecki" in the pre-sale clearing instruments is correctly **Cielecki**.

### Chełmski family (17th–18th c.)

- **Jerzy (Georgius) Chełmski** — heir of Łukom mid-17th c.; plaintiff in the 1641–1644 Konin Z. 36 decree against the Otto-Trąmpczyński brothers; defendant in the 1644 Pyzdry vision by the same brothers.

- **Józef Chełmski (early 18th c.)** — **RESOLVED:** confirmed as both the co-plaintiff (with brother Franciszek) in the 1728 Konin Z. 40 Decree of Location against Krzysztof Prusimski **and** the father of Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski. The multi-generational bridge between the 1728 disputant and the 1760s–80s protagonist now runs through Józef, and is placed in the narrative at the Józef Chełmski paragraph rather than at Stanisław's introduction.

- **Franciszek Ścibor Chełmski (early 18th c.)** — Józef's brother, joint heir of Łukom in 1728.

- **Stanisław Ścibor Chełmski, Treasurer of Wschowa** — principal Łukomia heir from the early 1760s through 1782.
  - **RESOLVED (the 1766 question):** Boniecki's 1766 date records the **consolidation of a cousin's share**, not a first acquisition. Stanisław was already an active co-heir of Łukomia by 1761–62 (the 1762–65 potash dispute names him; the 1761 condemnation was obtained by both Chełmski brothers). The text now reflects consolidation rather than acquisition.
  - **NEW:** Died **21 August 1802** in the village of Łukom as its hereditary owner (Teki Dworzaczka entry 1653, Owińska Klasztor records).
  - Designations across corpus: "Right Honourable" / "Honourable" / "Treasurer of Wschowa" (1782).

- **Ignacy Stanisław Chełmski** — **NEW.** The Latin of Konin Gr. 119 names **Stanisław Chełmski and Ignacy Stanisław Chełmski as two separate individuals.** The two citations of 10 March 1783 against Prusimski were issued by *different* Chełmskis, not both by Stanisław. Any text attributing both to "Chełmski" singular needs disambiguating.
  - **Do not conflate with:** Ignacy Chełmski, Stanisław's cousin, from whom Stanisław bought shares of the four Łukomia-area estates in 1766 (per Boniecki). Whether Ignacy and Ignacy Stanisław are the same man is **not established** — flag as open.

- **Heliodor Chełmski, Canon of Chełmno** — Stanisław's brother. Confirmed against the 1776 corpus, which names "Reverend Heliodor, Canon of Chełmno, and Stanisław, brothers by birth of the Chełmski family."

### Prusimski family (18th c.)

- **Krzysztof Prusimski** — heir of Trąbczyn in the early 18th c.; lodged a 1713 manifest concerning the Łukom takeover of the *stawisko*; principal defendant in the 1728 Decree of Location. Died 12 May 1729 (1786 Prusimski settlement document).
  - Wife: Teresa Łoś-Golińska, daughter of Jan Łoś-Goliński, Burgrave of Konin, and Constancja Krusińska.
  - Sons: the elder Antoni, Paweł, Stefan, plus Jan and Theophilus who died sterile in 1729.

- **Antoni Prusimski (the elder, ?–c. 1761)** — son of Krzysztof; lodged the 1761 manifest; the 1761 condemnation in default was obtained against him; died later in 1761.
  - **RESOLVED:** He is **not** the source of the Trąbczyn inheritance. The chain runs through Paweł and Paweł's widow Katarzyna's life-tenancy. The corrected text avoids forward-referencing the 1776 commission.

- **Paweł Prusimski** — son of Krzysztof; uncle of the present Antoni; **founder of the Olędry on the Trąbczyn estate (1746)**. *Subcamerarius S.R.M.* Married Katarzyna née Rozdrażewska. Died before the 1754 site inspection (which names Katarzyna as widow). Per the 1740 division (Schedule II, 1786 source), Trąbczyn passed to him. Held the Goliński maternal estates, including the 1726 lease of Prusinowo to Mikołaj Trąmpczyński.

- **Stefan Prusimski** — son of Krzysztof; father of the present Antoni; owner of Kamionna, Kolno, and Trąbczyn per the PSB. Married Wiktoria Malczewska (1st v. Tomaszowa Wierusz-Kowalska).

- **Antoni Ostroróg-Prusimski (Kazimierz Józef Antoni), Starost of Niszczewice (1744–c. 1806)** — son of Stefan; Knight of the Order of Saint Stanisław (1778); Sejm deputy from Wschowa (1773–75), Brześć Kujawski (1778), Poznań (1780); organiser of the 1794 Kuyavian uprising at Śmiłowice; emigrated to Venice with his wife in late 1794.
  - Wife: Marianna Dąmbska (z Lubrańca), daughter of Józef, Castellan of Kowal, and Barbara z Komorowskich.
  - **NEW / correction:** His **only child was his daughter Michalina.** Any reference in the manuscript to "Antoni Prusimski's son" is wrong.
  - **RESOLVED:** the inheritance chain now runs Paweł → Katarzyna's life-tenancy → Antoni, not via "the elder Antoni."
  - **NEW (framing):** He was never formally declared an outlaw. What the documents show is decrees issued *under penalty of banishment* — a penalty for non-compliance, not an assigned status. The side-road title was revised from "Politician vs. Outlaw" to "Legislator Under Threat of Banishment" accordingly. The parish records calling the settlement *Łukomskie Olędry* reflect the court-ordered land transfer, not a personal status.

- **Catharina (Katarzyna) de Rozdrazewska Prusimska** — widow of Paweł; life-tenant (*Domina Reformatoria et advitalis*) of Trąmpczyn at the 1754 site inspection; plaintiff in the 1762–65 potash dispute.

- **Michalina z Prusimskich Dąbska, later Miączyńska** — only child of Antoni; recovered the estates in 1807 through the Komisja Rządząca of the Duchy of Warsaw. Married first **Józef Dąmbski** (widowed 1798), then **Stanisław Miączyński** — two sequential marriages, **not** a name discrepancy.
  - Estates recovered: Trąbczyn (Kalisz Department); Kamionno, Kolno, Pawłowo, Pawłówek, Wrocławek (Poznań Department).
  - Legal basis: Napoleon's decree of 5 June 1807 (Finckenstein) restoring Józef Wybicki's estates became the *prawidło* for the Komisja Rządząca's resolution of 21 July 1807 at Dresden, extended to Michalina, General Niemojewski, and Wichrowski.

## Places

- **Trąbczyn / Trąmpczyn / Trąpczyn** — the principal estate centre. Justin's prose uses "Trąbczyn"; Latin documents use "Trąmpczyn" or "Trąpczyn"; family names use "Trąmpczyński." **NEW German-era forms:** *Trombczin*, *Träbczyn*, **Trombschino-Hauland** (the Olęder colony), and **"Prosrzyner = Trąbczyner"** (confirmed equivalence in the Hohenlohe corpus).
- **Trąbczyn Mały / Nowa Wieś** — the *stawisko* and the Lusnia/Miąsykąt/Dziadulina pine forests lay within Nowa Wieś ground per the 1522 partition.
- **Trąbczyn Wielki** — the Trąbczyn Wielki / Biskupice boundary was deferred by the 1776 commission.
- **Łukom / Łukomia** — **standardisation rule confirmed:** *Łukomia* (and its declined forms) is the **estate** name, used in legal and tenurial contexts; *Łukom* is the **settlement** name, used in directional travel phrases. This distinction is the document's own and is maintained in translation.
- **Łomów / Łomowo, Imielno, Bukowe** — Łukom-area holdings. The corpus contests whether Bukowe had any independent boundary at the corner-mound junction.
- **Drzewce** — held by the Cistercian Convent of Ląd; third party to the corner-mound junction.
- **Osiny, Łazy, Maryanton, Porąbki, Folwark Huta** — adjacent settlements within the estate (1791 visitation).
- **Trąbczyńskie Stare Olędry** — the original 1746 settlement; also "Lusnia *olędry*," and "Łukomskie / Chełmskie Olędry" during the 1771–1781 annexation. Also appears as **Łazińsk / Łaziny**.
- **Trąbczyńskie Nowe Olędry** — first attested in metrical records 29 September 1776.
- **Łukomskie Olędry (post-1798)** — distinct from the temporary entity of 1771–81; a separate place northwest of Łukom on Perthées' 1798 map.
- ***stawisko*** — retained in Polish, italicised (Rule 15). "Mud pond" is non-standard.
- **Lusnia / Lusnie** — corpus standard is "Lusnia" (nominative); Justin uses "Lusnie."
- **Ostrowce, Miąsykąt, Dziadulina** — the three woodlands at the heart of the dispute.
- **Cygany mounds** — eight mounds and forty-one tree blazes north of Lusnia.
- **Studzianka / Studzienka** — southern end of the boundary; GPS-anchored to present-day Studzianka (52.0831191°N, 17.8680192°E).
- **Czarny Bród** — two words (corpus standard); Justin uses "Czarnybród."
- **Kaliski Ford (*Bród Kaliski*)** — south of the main road west to Łomów; on the 1766 Łukom estate map.
- **Michalinów Oleśnicki vs. Michalinów Olędry** — distinct places; do not merge.
- **NEW — Hohenlohe-era settlements:** the estate inventory lists 34 named settlements including the German-named Olęder colonies **Klein-Ingelfingen, Klein-Öhringen, Sophienthal, Ludwigslust**. Also **Szetlewek** (and the tentative identification of *Szeklejówek* as possibly *Szetlewek*), **Wrąbczyn / Wrąbczynek**, **Drzewice, Kopayno, Świątniki, Skokon, Olisnice**, **Zagórów / Zagórowo / Zagurowo** (not "Lagurowo" — a Z/L letterform error), **Witów**, **Kamionno / Kamień**, **Guhrwitz** (Rittergut bei Breslau, seat of the von Stillfried court — Stillfried's ownership there in 1805 remains unconfirmed).
- **Pyzdry, Konin, Kalisz, Bydgoszcz, Poznań, Piotrków, Brześć Kujawski (Brest Kuyavia)** — court venues.

## Confirmed terminology (binding)

- ***Sąd grodzki*** = municipal court; ***sąd ziemski*** = land court (Rule 16). *Castrensia Capitanealis* = Starost's Municipal Court. Not "Castle Court" or "Captain's Court."
- ***Wizja*** = judicial site inspection (Rule 17); distinct from *kondestensja* (field inspection, a full commissarial hearing in the field).
- ***Kopiec narożny*** = corner mound; ***kopiec ścienny*** = wall mound. "Border mound" is generic only.
- ***Pastor*** in Latin documents = **shepherd**, not "Lutheran pastor." **NEW — a confirmed prior mistranslation to keep corrected.**
- **"hippi~"** in service returns (e.g. *super Mensa hippi praesen~*) = *hypocaustorum / hypocausti*, genitive of *hypocaustum* (heated room / stove room). Full form confirmed from Konin Gr. 117. Correct rendering: "on the table in the heated room." **Any reference to a "chestnut table" is wrong.**
- ***Axialny / acialiter*** = a technical surveying term (from *axialis*), "at the convergence/corner point" — not a place name or common adjective.
- ***Roboracja*** = formal court enrollment giving a document binding force; distinct from mere contract-signing.
- ***Traditio*** = **delivery of possession**, not "tradition" and not "eviction."
- **Łochowski/Paciorkowski** — short-cite form throughout. True author is Stanisław Łochowski; plagiarised under Paciorkowski's name.
- **Standardisation decisions:** Trąbczyn (not Trąpczyn); Dąmbski (not Dambski); Tomice (not Tomie or Tomci); Gorzyński (not Garzyński).
